## Summary
## Selection Principles
Leadership in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]] is **competence‑tested, consensus‑validated, and recallable**. Most offices combine:
- **Merit Credentials:** craft mastery, stewardship records, or scholarly proofs.
- **Consensus Mandate:** multi‑chamber approval or guild endorsement.
- **Term Limits & Recall:** fixed terms with citizen‑initiated dissonance recall.

## Concord‑Level Roles (Galactic)
- **Concord Speakers ([[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]):** presiding facilitators, one per chamber; rotate annually.
- **Eidetic Coordinators:** chair the **Secretariats** (Energy, Transit, Health, [[culture]], Knowledge, Defense, Diplomacy).
- **Harmonic Ombuds:** guardians of [[Rights]]; can file a **[[Rights]] Veto** to suspend legislation.
- **High Arbiters:** apex judiciary panel for interspecies [[law]] and Accord interpretation.
- **Crisis Harmonists:** temporary directors during **Crisis Harmonics** (dual‑[[Key]] activation).

## Sector‑Level Roles
- **Regional Concordors:** sector facilitators coordinating ports, corridors, megastructure quotas.
- **Standards Masters:** lead cross‑[[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] safety & bioscale protocols.
- **Auditor‑General:** rotates between [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]; oversees the **Audit Mesh**.

## Species‑Level Roles
- **Conclave Orchestrator:** executive of a [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] polity; implements Conclave [[law]].
- **Steward College Prefect:** chairs ecology, health, education, habitat bureaus.
- **Chief Arbiter:** head of [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] judiciary; liaison to Regional Concord courts.
- **Guild Preceptors:** elected heads of major craft guilds within the [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]].

## Guild & Civic Roles
- **Lattice Wardens:** Dyson/energy lattice custodians; manage quotas and safety.
- **Corridor Marshals:** oversee beamed‑sail lanes, mass drivers, and gateway agoras.
- **Archivist‑Legates:** keepers of archives, memory looms, and cultural custodianship.
- **Sanctum Abbots:** lead resonance [[Temples]]; mediate cultural disputes (advisory).
- **Rescue Castellans:** command [[Sentinel]] constellations and relief depots.

## Selection & Tenure (Typical)
- **Secretariat Heads:** 6–12 standard years; staggered; renewable once.
- **High Arbiters:** 12–24 years; non‑consecutive terms; strict conflict‑of‑interest codes.
- **Ombuds:** 8–16 years; selected by sortition shortlist + chamber ratification.
- **Regional Concordors:** 6–10 years; sector chambers co‑elect.
- **Conclave Orchestrators:** per‑[[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] statute; commonly 8–12 years.
- **Guild Preceptors:** set by guild charter; often [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]–8 years, project‑bound.

## Recall & Accountability
- **Dissonance Petitions:** cross‑[[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] citizen petitions trigger review votes.
- **Performance Audits:** periodic, public scorecards; mandatory transparency.
- **Incompatibilities:** no simultaneous service in judiciary, ombuds, and executive.

## Notes
Leaders are expected to treat **freedom, pluralism, and ecological [[Continuity]]** as non‑negotiable. Any attempt to centralize power beyond Accord bounds triggers **[[Rights]] veto**, audits, and potential corridor denial within their jurisdiction.
