[[Narrative]]
C.U.C. Ascendant Resolve
	•	Type: Ark-Class Colony Carrier
	•	Length: 17 km
	•	Crew: ~400 awake personnel (Command, Engineering, Medical)
	•	Payload: ~110,000 colonists (90% in cryo; 10% in generational rotation)
	•	Cargo: Prefab shelters, industrial drills, terraforming test units, security mechs, biostorage, cryo-embryo vaults

Aurora Colonial Command 
Prefect Commander Dal Enver
High Surveyor-General Naera Yelvith


Transmission
The ship emerges from its final deceleration burn into Leslarut’s orbital plane. It immediately sends a formal docking request:
“To [[Aurora Station]] Command—this is C.U.C. Ascendant Resolve. Requesting authorization to approach L1 holding pattern and begin phased disembarkation protocol for civilian colonists. All [[Systems]] nominal. Payload integrity confirmed. Priority: Class One Colonial Transfer. Awaiting orbital corridor clearance.”

[[Aurora Station]] Response
Colonial Command Internal Response:
	•	Prefect Commander Dal Enver hesitates. The station isn’t fully prepared for influx—not in housing, security, or resource stability.
	•	Security Liaison advocates for full lockdown: the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|system]]’s instability, local anomalies, and Aurora’s lack of scalable [[Infrastructure]] make this a risk.
	•	High Surveyor-General Naera Yelvith counters—this was always the plan. Delaying docking violates multiple [[Colonial Union]] charters and risks morale collapse aboard the Resolve.
Outcome: Clearance is granted, but only to dock externally, not for planetary descent. Supplies will be ferried down in batches. Disembarkation must proceed under full C.U. supervision.
