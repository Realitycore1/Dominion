# (2270) Terradynamics revolution

## Canonical Event

Terradynamics turns fragmented recovery programs into coordinated planetary restoration.

Its major applications include:

- atmospheric repair;
- ocean deacidification;
- wetland and watershed reconstruction;
- continental reforestation;
- cryosphere stabilization;
- Arctic reicing;
- long-duration ecological monitoring.

The revolution restores planetary carrying capacity while creating new conflicts over extraction, relocation, habitat sovereignty, monitoring, and who owns a repaired ecosystem.

The Immortals advise these projects through their specialties but do not command them.
