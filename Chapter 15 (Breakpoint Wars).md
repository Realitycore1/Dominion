# Chapter 15 - Breakpoint Wars

## Timeline Function

War Years 10-12. Multiple theaters reach collapse points at the same time.

## Event Role

- Food reserves, orbital defense, rail corridors, archive continuity, and civil order fail in linked cascades.
- Commanders stop planning campaigns and begin managing breakpoints.
- Immune sabotage, regeneration poisoning, synthetic-organ rejection triggers, body-lock attacks, and friend-or-foe inversion make clinics and maintenance bays active fronts.
- Anti-Immortal doctrine targets legal continuity and witness authority as deliberately as bodies.
- Long-horizon artillery, tactical nuclear restraint, autonomous weapons, and deep-region mining all become decisive.
- Harmina's ecology turns some victories into delayed disasters.

## Consequence

The war becomes systemic. Winning a battle no longer matters if the system behind it breaks.
