It spans several degrees on the sky and ranks among the largest known structures.  ￼ ￼
	•	How it was found: by stacking SDSS quasar spectra and using intervening Mg II absorption as tracers of large-scale structure; statistical tests (clustering, Cuzick–Edwards, power spectrum) highlighted an extended, arc-like overdensity.  ￼
	•	Why it matters: its size exceeds the oft-quoted homogeneity scale (~1.[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Gly), so—taken at face value—it nudges at the cosmological principle (large-scale isotropy/homogeneity). Debate continues over a-posteriori statistics and selection effects, so it’s provocative rather than decisive.  ￼ ￼
