# RELIC Ω-01 — THE FIRST BLADE
## Classification: Pre-Causal Instrument
## Tier: Ω (Non-Origin, Non-Constructed)
## Status: Unstable / Event-Manifested

---

# I. PRIMARY ACCOUNT (FIRST-HAND RECORD)

> **Source:** Unverified Witness Log — Paradox Event Cluster (Designation: Storm-Delta-7)  
> **Observer State:** Cognitively intact (partial memory degradation post-event)

---

I did not see it arrive.

There was no descent, no formation, no sound. One moment the space between us held tension—like a decision waiting to be made—and the next, it was simply there.

The Blade was impossibly long.

Not large—long.

It extended beyond what distance should allow, its tip anchored in nothing I could identify. Its base intersected the ground, but did not rest on it. It passed through space the way a thought passes through a mind—without resistance.

It appeared crystalline at first.

But that was incorrect.

It was not made of material. It was made of **distinction**.

Edges existed only where I expected them to. When I tried to focus, the blade changed—not shape, but definition. It sharpened in response to attention.

The air around it fractured.

Not physically—causally.

I saw moments repeat incorrectly. My own stance adjusted before I moved. Shadows detached from their sources.

When contact occurred, there was no motion.

No swing.

No force.

The target did not fall.

Instead, it ceased to have been aligned with itself.

Its past no longer led to its present.

It remained standing—but wrong.

Disconnected.

I attempted to look away.

I could not.

Not because I was forced—but because there was no longer a “before” in which I had not been looking.

After its use, the Blade did not vanish.

It became irrelevant.

> “Before the first light, there was the cut that allowed it.”

- Interpreted as the act that separated existence from void  
- Blade seen as the **first division in reality**

---

### 2. **The Sword That Decides**
> “When all paths exist, it chooses one.”

- Associated with fate, inevitability, and finality  
- Believed to enforce a single outcome among infinite possibilities  

---

### 3. **The Silent Judgment**
> “It does not strike the body. It strikes the truth of a thing.”

- Suggests identity-level severance  
- Victims described as “present but no longer themselves”

---

### 4. **The Unheld Weapon**
> “No hand wields it. The world does.”

- Common across all interpretations  
- Reinforces non-ownership and non-agency  

---

### 5. **The Fracture Made Form**
> “It exists because something broke that was never meant to.”

- Often linked to:
  - Timeline fractures  
  - Cosmic interference events  
  - Artificial causality disruptions

# III. STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS (MOT COMPATIBLE)

## A. Ontological Nature

The First Blade is not:

- A constructed object  
- A technological artifact  
- A natural formation  

It is:

> A **system-level response** to irreconcilable causal contradiction.

---

## B. Functional Domain

### 1. **Causal Severance**
- Separates:
  - Event from consequence  
  - Identity from continuity  
  - Timeline from progression  

---

### 2. **Probability Collapse**
- Forces:
  - One outcome to dominate  
  - All alternatives to degrade or terminate  

---

### 3. **Non-Local Interaction**
- Effects propagate across:
  - Past states  
  - Present conditions  
  - Future branches  

---

## C. Manifestation Conditions

The Blade does **not persist**.

It appears only when:

- Multiple timelines overlap beyond stability thresholds  
- A system reaches **decision-lock** (no resolvable outcome)  
- Contradiction density exceeds local causal tolerance  

---

## D. Observer-Dependent Rendering

The Blade has no fixed appearance.

Observed forms include:

- Prismatic crystalline weapon  
- Void-lined stellar blade  
- Fragmented energy construct  
- Absence shaped like a blade  

Conclusion:

> The Blade is **interpreted**, not seen.

---

## E. Energetic / Physical Footprint

No measurable:

- Mass  
- Energy signature  
- Radiation emission  

However, measurable effects include:

- Temporal desynchronization  
- Memory discontinuity  
- Localized entropy spikes  
- Emergence of Paradox Storms
