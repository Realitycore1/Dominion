Classification

Yōkai Martial Sentients / Wind-Bound Ascetics / Pride-Wardens

⸻

CORE IDEA

Tengu are discipline weaponized by ego.

They are not demons of chaos, nor guardians of mercy. Tengu arise when mastery, isolation, and pride calcify into something predatory. They are beings who perfected a path—and decided that perfection entitled them to judge others.

They don’t tempt like demons.
They test.

⸻

ORIGIN

Tengu manifest where:
	•	mountains remain inaccessible
	•	martial traditions isolate from society
	•	ascetic orders abandon humility
	•	long-lived warriors outlast their purpose

They are born from:
	•	corrupted mountain kami
	•	ascended warriors who refused to return to humanity
	•	spirits that learned combat before compassion

Every Tengu was once something closer to mortal.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Tengu exist in two dominant forms, often within the same lineage.

Karasu-Tengu (Crow Tengu)
	•	Avian features: black wings, talons, beaks or sharp facial angles
	•	Lean, agile bodies built for aerial combat
	•	Feathers often scarred or ritual-marked

Daitengu (Great Tengu)
	•	More humanoid, tall and imposing
	•	Long red or pale faces with pronounced noses
	•	Vestigial or hidden wings
	•	Ornate robes, armor, and ritual masks

Both carry:
	•	bladed polearms or swords
	•	wind talismans
	•	mountain relics

Their presence bends air pressure subtly.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Tengu are proud, hierarchical, and confrontational.
	•	Demand respect before dialogue
	•	Punish arrogance instantly
	•	Humiliate before killing
	•	Respect strength, skill, and resolve

They view mercy as weakness—but earned restraint as honor.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Extremely high.
	•	Tactical and strategic brilliance
	•	Mastery of martial forms
	•	Deep philosophical frameworks (often flawed)
	•	Long memory for insults and victories

A Tengu never forgets being bested.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Wind Dominion: manipulate air pressure, flight, momentum
	•	Grace-neutral: angels do not command them
	•	Malice-reactive: corruption amplifies cruelty and pride
	•	Honor Binding: violating their own code causes degeneration

Broken Tengu become feral sky-demons.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Dwell in mountain ranges, cloud forests, isolated peaks
	•	Live in strict hierarchies or solitary watch-posts
	•	Rare reproduction; apprenticeship is more common
	•	Territories are defended through ritual combat

Mountains with Tengu are avoided—or revered.

⸻

ALPHA TENGU

An Alpha Tengu is called a Sky Lord or Wind Judge.

Traits:
	•	Command over entire wind systems
	•	Can ground flight with authority alone
	•	Enforce trials across regions
	•	Treat gods as equals, not superiors

Alpha Tengu often become living taboos—places you do not trespass.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (individual) / Severe (Alpha or warbands)

You don’t outmuscle a Tengu.
You either earn their respect or die embarrassed.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Tengu represent:
	•	mastery without humility
	•	tradition turned exclusionary
	•	pride as a predator
	•	discipline divorced from compassion

Where Kitsune rewrite truth,
Tengu enforce their version of order.
