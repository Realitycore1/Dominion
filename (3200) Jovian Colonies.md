# (3200) Jovian Colonies

The first permanent Jovian foothold colonies are recognized in 3200.

They are not yet a mature Jovian civilization.

The footholds center on:

- radiation-sheltered Callisto settlements;
- Ganymede orbital and subsurface installations;
- restricted Europa science and ocean-access stations;
- fuel, ice, magnetic-environment, and communications platforms.

Survival requires heavy shielding, local ice processing, fusion-drive convoys, redundant power, and rescue reserves measured in months.

Callisto becomes the main civilian and logistics base because it lies outside the worst of Jupiter's radiation environment.

During the First Solar War, the footholds serve as refuge, repair, and supply nodes. Their distance limits their military role and makes every diverted convoy a threat to permanent settlement.

By 3425, the Jovian system is one of the two major outer powers. Callisto and Ganymede support large civilian populations, shipyards, agriculture, convoy fleets, and system defense.

Jupiter co-founds the Outer Systems Compact with Saturn and demands independence from direct Colonial Union government.

The Jovian government does not speak automatically for every moon. Europa science communities, Ganymede districts, Callisto cities, and orbital stations remain divided over independence and war.
