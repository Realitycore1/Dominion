(The Dream-Saturated Substrate / Somnial Expanse)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Proto-Expressive / Pre-Formal Reality
	•	Authority Level: Non-sovereign, generative
	•	Relation to Axioms: Loosely constrained
	•	Relation to the Empty: by (neither pre- nor post-death)
	•	Relation to Aravoth: Symbolic overlap only

⸻

Core Nature

The Oneiric Plane is where meaning forms before rules harden.

It is not illusion.
It is unfinished reality.

Here:
	•	thought precedes structure,
	•	symbolism precedes causality,
	•	identity exists as narrative potential.

If the Axiomatic Stratum answers “what may exist”,
the Oneiric Plane answers “what could exist if believed hard enough.”

⸻

What Dreams Are (important correction)

Dreams are not personal hallucinations.

They are:
	•	localized intersections between mortal minds and the Oneiric Plane,
	•	temporary bindings of narrative fragments to identity,
	•	low-energy manifestations of proto-reality.

This is why:
	•	shared dreams occur,
	•	archetypes repeat across cultures,
	•	nightmares feel external,
	•	dream-entities persist after waking.

⸻

Structure of the Plane

The Oneiric Plane has no fixed geography, but it does have currents.

Common manifestations:
	•	endless shifting landscapes stitched from memory
	•	cities built from metaphor
	•	oceans of unfinished faces
	•	staircases that ascend into childhood
	•	skies that respond emotionally

Time is:
	•	non-linear,
	•	cyclical,
	•	or symbolic (events repeat until “resolved”).

Space is:
	•	associative, not metric.

⸻

Inhabitants

Unlike the Axiomatic Stratum, the Oneiric Plane is crowded.

1. Archetypes

Semi-stable narrative entities:
	•	the Mother
	•	the Hunter
	•	the King
	•	the Betrayer
	•	the Monster

They are not gods.
They are patterns that want expression.

⸻

2. Oneirogenic Entities

Beings formed entirely of dream logic:
	•	Night-terrors
	•	Memory Eaters
	•	Shape-Symbols
	•	Sleep Parasites

These entities:
	•	feed on emotion, belief, repetition,
	•	migrate between sleepers,
	•	sometimes escape into waking reality.

⸻

3. Tulpae

Artificially stabilized dream-entities.
	•	Created through belief, ritual, repetition
	•	Dangerous because they want continuity
	•	Often mistaken for spirits or minor gods

⸻

4. Shapeshifters

Many shapeshifters originate here.

They are beings whose identity never fully solidified under axioms.

This explains:
	•	skinwalkers,
	•	nagual,
	•	changelings,
	•	dream-born predators.

⸻

Interaction with Other Planes

With the Axiomatic Stratum
	•	The Oneiric presses against axioms
	•	Most dreams collapse when axioms reassert
	•	Some ideas survive and become real

This is where new monsters are born.

⸻

With Aravoth
	•	Angels interpret dreams symbolically
	•	Prophetic dreams originate here
	•	Heaven does not control this plane

Dreams inspire doctrine.
They do not obey it.

⸻

With Sheol-Gehenna
	•	Malice curdles dreams into nightmares
	•	Trauma creates stable horror-forms
	•	Hell exploits Oneiric weaknesses

This is why nightmares feel predatory.

⸻

With the Empty

There is no overlap.

When a soul enters the Empty:
	•	dreaming stops completely
	•	identity is suspended
	•	no narrative persists

This is why the Empty is feared by dream-entities.

⸻

Mortal Interaction

Mortals interact constantly, unknowingly.

States of access:
	•	sleep
	•	delirium
	•	ritual trance
	•	madness
	•	near-death experiences
	•	extreme creativity

Extended exposure causes:
	•	dream bleed
	•	hallucinated continuity
	•	identity instability
	•	spontaneous monster manifestation

⸻

Why the Oneiric Plane is dangerous (and necessary)

Without it:
	•	no creativity
	•	no myth
	•	no innovation
	•	no new forms of life

With it:
	•	reality is constantly at risk of becoming something else

It is the engine of novelty.

⸻

Relationship to the Dream-Wastes

Important distinction:
	•	Oneiric Plane = healthy dream substrate
	•	Dream-Wastes (Somnial Fractures) = damaged regions where dream logic detached from minds

The Dream-Wastes are wounds.
The Oneiric Plane is the bloodstream
