## Summary

GalFed military technology prioritizes detection, containment, disabling, and infrastructure control over spectacular destruction. When destruction is used, it is usually justified as quarantine or prevention.

## Details

Common systems:

- Gravitic disruption detectors: log unregistered mass-transition events and illegal FTL activity.
- Corridor interdiction nets: close or redirect transit routes.
- Harmonic pacification arrays: reduce mass panic, riots, or rebellion through noetic damping.
- Aether quarantine veils: isolate contaminated ruins, ships, or cities.
- Memory audit probes: compare local records against Archive Gate baselines.
- Dyson dampeners: regulate stellar output or impose strategic energy denial.
- Deterrent fleets: high-end ships held near route chokepoints and restricted worlds.
- Restoration cradles: medical containment systems that double as population control infrastructure.
- Chronometric embargo fields: delay or desynchronize forbidden research and transit.
- Noetic countermeasure suites: protect crews from cognition weapons, Aether ghosts, and hostile archives.

Forbidden or restricted systems:

- Planetary-scale consciousness weapons.
- Recursive Aether amplifiers.
- Autonomous singularity-seed devices.
- Unlicensed temporal distortion platforms.
- Memory collapse weapons outside Council authorization.

GalFed technology is most unsettling when it is dual-use. The machine that saves a damaged mind can also narrow a dissident one.

## Consolidated Lock - Terminal Continuity Correction Protocol

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Terminal Continuity Correction Protocol is a classified GalFed continuity doctrine authorizing limited strategic destruction against a civilization, polity, installation, or relay network judged to pose terminal recurrence risk.

Terminology by layer:

| Layer | Term |
| --- | --- |
| Public / diplomatic | Emergency Stabilization Mandate |
| Legal / classified doctrine | Terminal Continuity Correction Protocol |
| Operational phrase | Compassionate Terminal Correction |
| Harminan / Final Line term | Atomic Compliance |

Publicly, such operations are announced under an Emergency Stabilization Mandate.

Internally, the protocol is justified as Compassionate Terminal Correction: the destruction of selected command, transit, archive, military, or industrial systems to prevent greater civilizational collapse, followed by immediate humanitarian stabilization and restorative administration.

In practice, the protocol breaks independent command, destroys strategic self-sufficiency, preserves systems GalFed can later control, creates medical and logistical dependency, frames survivors as evidence of GalFed compassion, and turns post-strike aid into jurisdiction.

GalFed claims the protocol prevents Family-pattern recurrence and reduces total casualties. The claim is not always false. Its corruption lies in treating GalFed's authority as the only acceptable form of safety.
