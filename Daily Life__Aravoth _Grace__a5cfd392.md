I. Eternal Mount

Souls:

Rise each morning with new strength, joining pilgrimages upward.

Each step sheds corruption, revealing their truer self.

Pilgrims gather at way-shrines, share testimonies, and encourage one another.

[[Angels]] (Seraphim):

Guard higher slopes, ensuring only purified may pass.

Teach pilgrims songs and prayers that strengthen their ascent.

Their daily service is [[Fire]]: burning away shadows with radiant wings.

II. Zion of Light (Celestial Metropolis)

Souls:

Live in radiant homes that reshape to their needs.

Study at archives, work in communal halls, join processions of worship.

They are always learning, building, and sharing — their “jobs” are fulfillment, not toil.

[[Angels]] (Cherubim, Thrones):

Serve as guides, architects, and governors.

Oversee harmony of the city, balancing order and joy.

Their daily tasks resemble civic service: maintaining justice, inspiring progress.

III. Aeon Vault (Treasury of [[Archetypes]])

Souls:

Scholars, scribes, and seekers come here to study eternal truths.

Each “day” they meditate on archetypal glyphs, gaining clarity about themselves and creation.

Some are entrusted with learning True Names of virtues.

[[Angels]] (Ophanim):

Turn endlessly, each rotation rewriting glyphs into new harmonies.

Their daily duty is contemplation, translation of divine blueprints.

They exist as archivists of perfection, unceasing but never weary.

IV. Celestium ([[Choirs]] of Resonance)

Souls:

Join [[Choirs]] at their own pace; no voice is excluded.

They experience joy in being part of a greater whole.

[[Angels]] (Dominions):

Conduct [[Choirs]], balancing harmony across countless voices.

Each day they fine-tune creation’s cadence by guiding songs.

Their [[(2030) Existence]] is resonance — they are living instruments.

V. Gardens of Renewal

Souls:

Rest, heal, and feast beneath eternal orchards.

Each day is renewal: wounds fade, grief is soothed, joy deepens.

They tend flowers of light, share meals, and walk with eternal animals.

[[Angels]] (Mercy spirits):

Act as caretakers, gardeners, and healers.

Training is joyful, building strength without hatred.

VII. Hall of Judgement

Souls:

Newly arrived gather here, their daily experience one of reflection.

Memories and sins are revealed, weighed, and refined.

Once judged, they are sent to other [[Realms]] of [[Aravoth (Grace)]].

VIII. River of Living Waters

Souls:

Come to drink and bathe, restoring memory and clarity.

Their service is flow — ceaseless, guiding, always gentle.
