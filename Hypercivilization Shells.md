Hypercivilization Shells are cosmic-scale enclosures encapsulating galactic superclusters, harnessing their collective mass, energy, and topology. They function as habitats, computation substrates, and defensive shells — a civilization literally turning [[Regions]] of the cosmos into enclosed engines.
Scale

Encompass hundreds of millions of light-years (supercluster envelopes).

Walls/shells composed of [[Exotic Matter]], strange energy, or latticework woven from Spiralverse conduits.

Anchored by Matter Cores at nodal points for stability.

Structure

Outer Membrane: Vast, semi-permeable lattice capturing radiation, [[Particles]], and gravitic waves.

Inner [[Infrastructure]]: Populated with [[Computronium]] strata, [[Ark Platforms]], Dyson Fortresses, and corridor junctions.

Concordia Harmonics: The entire shell sings, maintaining stability across eons.

Functions

Energy Harvesting: Every star and black hole in the cluster becomes an internal resource.

Habitat & [[culture]]: Countless [[Banks Orbitals]], [[Shellworlds]], and [[Ark Platforms]] stud the interior.

Computation: The lattice itself becomes a [[Computronium]] meta-brain.

Defense: Encapsulates entire galactic volumes; no threat enters or leaves without sanction.
