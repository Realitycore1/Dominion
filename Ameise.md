![[7558e324-0df8-437a-9b38-9f8b8a562e85.png]]
Homeworld

World: vast desert planet with polar oceans (north/south).

Habitats: sub-sand tunnel cities around deep aquifers; polar littoral hive-ports; night-surface caravans using dew-condensers.

Climate hazards: thermal amplitude swings; silicate dust storms; periodic salt fogs at the poles.

Biology & morphology

Clade: arthropodal mammals—endothermic, with a chitinous exoskeleton and fur plumes emerging between plates (thermoregulation & micro-scent wicking).

Form: digitigrade hexapod ancestry expressed as four ambulatory limbs + two manipulator forelimbs (many hives dock the lower forelimbs during heavy work).

Senses: multimodal—chemosense (200+ pheromone channels), vibration/tarsal mechanoreception, polarization-sensitive vision, limited infrasound.

[[Key]] features: mandibular tools; antennae (dense chemo cilia) used for contact “concept-dumping.”

[[Social Structure]] & reproduction

Eusocial hardline: [[Queen]]–brood model.

[[Workers]]: all female, the majority; possess agency and self-sufficiency but adhere to the [[Queen]] for strategic coordination.

Males (drones): remain with the [[Queen]]; rarely leave brood chambers except for nuptial flights in founding cycles.

[[Queens]]: central immunological and memetic repository for the hive; regulate [[Worker]] development.

Development: larval → pupal → [[Worker]] (or specialized [[Worker]]). Pheromones + feed profiles can irreversibly steer physiology ([[Soldier]] build, forager, engineer, archivist, etc.).

Immunity (critical constraint)

[[Queen]]-anchored immune memory. [[Workers]] inherit trained immunity via [[Queen]]-mediated factors.

Generational gap: each new brood starts susceptible until the [[Queen]] updates the profile.

Failure Modes: sudden pathogen shift, [[Queen]] loss, or supply chain break → hivewide vulnerability. ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] policy: maintain [[Queen]] Sanctuaries and immuno-relay clinics at refugee sites.)

Communication (range = bandwidth)

Contact (very high bandwidth): antenna clasping for short bursts of concept-transfer (compressed sensory packets + intent).

Near-field (high): rich pheromone chords layered with tarsal tapping (syntax), enabling nuance and parallel tracks (who/what/urgency).

Mid-field (medium/low): substrate drumming and structural infrasound; robust through dust and bulkheads but low information density.

Cognition & [[culture]]

Decision habit: default to local autonomy with periodic synchrony ([[Queen]] pulses coordination pheromones on cycles).

Arts: scent-glyph libraries (porous stone inlaid with resins), drum poetry, sand-inlay mosaics whose channels wick meaning over hours.

Taboos: waste of water; sterilant fogging of public scent inscriptions; unauthorized interference with [[Queen]] signals.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Military/Technology|Technology]] & material [[culture]]

Materials: bio-composites (chitin-ceramic laminates), mycelial resins, high-toughness silicones; favored for heat and abrasion resistance.

Computing: hybrid mechanical/computational [[Systems]]—fluidic logic for harsh environments, low-power microcontrollers for coordination; “scent-hash” ledgers for audit.

Starship style: honeycomb bulkheads, sand-ceramic thermal batteries, vibration-isolated decks; corridors marked with scent rails and tactile code.

Medicine & augmentation

Augments: tolerated if they don’t impair chemo/tactile channels; common upgrades include thermal exchangers, dust-lung scrubbers, and concept-buffer implants (to prevent overload during dumps).

Allergies: reactive to broad-spectrum sterilants and some amines; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] facilities flag red-band cleansers as hostile for Ameise zones.

Public health: [[Queen]]-linked vaccine programs—[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] medics pair a [[Queen]]’s inoculation with staged [[Worker]] boosters.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] relationship

Status: Refuge & Service Compact—they accepted [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] protection (sanctuaries, medical, water logistics) in exchange for bounded service ([[Infrastructure]]1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Intrastellar/Luna Colonial Committee/Infrastructure]], logistics, engineering, and specific [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] support billets).

[[Rights]] under the Compact: bodily security, freedom of conscience, language/[[culture]] protection, labor compensation, protected access to [[Queen]] Sanctuaries.

Athearn & Harmonic posture

Athearn (hard-line shepherds): classify Ameise as Ward labor-guild elites (inspection swarms, archives). Major friction: Wayhouse sterilization of scent-glyph spaces and bans on “illicit proofs.”

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Harmonic Cooperative/Harmonic Cooperative]]: love them as array singers (phase-stable), scent-auditors for Open Tuning; many Ameise join Caretaker [[Choirs]] and logistics [[Choirs]].

Operations & [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] roles

Strengths: environment-hardy engineering, boarding sappers (tarsal drum nets, low-light optics), tunnel fortifications, disaster logistics.

Tools: foam-crete bursters, shield-web anchors (bolted like hive plates), drum-sync comms resilient to EW.

Weaknesses: [[Queen]] loss = cohesion and immunity crash; chemo-jamming can garble orders; heavy reliance on water and micro-nutrient logistics.

[[law]] & ethics ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] annex)

Protected: [[Queen]] sanctity; scent libraries as cultural heritage; right to concept-transfer in designated spaces.

Regulated: pheromone broadcast power in mixed spaces; concept-dumping with non-Ameise requires consent & medical clearance.

Prohibited: forced [[Queen]] separation; sterilant deployment without tribunal approval.

Language & writing

Modes: click-speech (serviceable), scent-glyph (primary literature), tap-code (technical).

Translation quirk: single Ameise “words” often bundle cause, posture, and duty into one scent chord—expect apparent bluntness in phonetic translation.

Spirituality & myth

[[Queen]]-Mother rites: brood blessings stored in amber-resin beads; funerary practice turns plates into library tiles (knowledge returns to the hive).

Aesthetics

Palettes: sandstone tan plates with dark fur rills; or (diaspora) lacquered matte blacks with jade resin inlays.

Built spaces: low arches, wind-quieted corridors, scent rails engraved at shoulder height, dew-catch courtyards.

Vulnerabilities & counters (for plots)

Chemo-jamming/sterilants: disrupt public communication & [[culture]]; often sparks unrest.

Concept-overload: unfiltered dumps can cause syncope or traumatic imprinting in both Ameise and [[Humans]].

Notables

[[Queen]] Oss-Of-Quiet-Pools — diaspora matriarch who negotiated the first [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Refuge & Service Compact; famed for “cold-shade” clinics.

Archivist-Engineer Tiktik-Grey-Row — designed scent-hash ledgers used by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] relief fleets.

Sapper-Marshal Rill-of-Nine-Drums — led the shield-web boarding that saved a hospital tender at O-19 (now teaches drum-sync to mixed crews).
