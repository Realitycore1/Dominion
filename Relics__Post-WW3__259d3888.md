I. CLASS I RELICS — PRE-FALL RELIGIOUS & ESOTERIC OBJECTS

These are real archaeological or cultural artifacts whose rediscovery produces memetic and political shockwaves.

⸻

1. The [[Ark of the Covenant]] (Rediscovered: 2094)

Location: A collapsed cavern beneath the Ethiopian Plateau
Nature: A gold-plated containment vessel storing:
	•	uranium traces,
	•	anomalous ionization residue
	•	ancient insulating materials

Effects in a low-tech world:
	•	bursts of Cherenkov-like light during exposure
	•	radiation burns interpreted as “punishment”
	•	EMP flash during oxygenation event
	•	spontaneous fires from ionized dust

Interpretation:
The First Sign of divine wrath or divine inheritance.
Causes wars between PMCs and desert cult-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]].

⸻

2. The [[Spear of Longinus]] (Rediscovered: 2140)

Location: Flooded vault beneath St. Peter’s Basilica
Nature: Iron and copper alloy with preserved ancient adhesives; no supernatural properties
Effects:
Used as a symbol by emerging militant theocracies in the European Archipelago.

⸻

3. Dead Sea Scroll Micro-Archive (Rediscovered: 2217)

Location: A dried fissure near Qumran
Nature: Digitized archives on early solid-state media encased in insulating clay jars
Effects:
Provides unprecedented [[Continuity]] of religious scholarship and becomes the backbone of the Jerusalem Scholar-Kingdoms.

⸻

4. The Sibylline Microtablets (Rediscovered: 2301)

Location: Collapsed hill-temple beneath the Apennine fault
Nature: Metal plates encoded with predictive models from a pre-fall quantum forecasting project
Effects:
Interpreted as prophecy; actually statistical simulations of collapse.
Used by Alpine Helveticon strategists.

⸻

⸻

II. CLASS II RELICS — GEO-POLITICAL TECHNOLOGICAL SURVIVORS

These are old-world technologies that become legendary in the Discordia Aera.

⸻

5. The Boreal AI Core (“Northmind”) — (Found 2234)

Location: Canadian Shield deep vault
Nature: A [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]]/hydro-grid AI sealed prior to the Exchange
Effects:
	•	intermittently awakens
	•	answers questions in fragmented logic
	•	worshipped by Skygrave Republics as an oracle
	•	feared by southern polities
	•	becomes the backbone of Arctic coordination

⸻

6. The Shinjuku Vault (Japan) — (Opened 2408)

Nature:
A preserved robotics lab containing:
	•	22nd century maintenance drones
	•	nanomaterial fabricators
	•	geothermal microreactor
	•	a language-preserving archive

Effects:
Forms the technological basis of [[The Neo-Kyoto Vault]]’s supremacy.

⸻

7. The Cascadian Quantum Stack (“Q-Stack”) — (Rediscovered 2587)

Location: Buried beneath the Cascades after a landslide
Nature:
A partially functional quantum processor with limited memory intact.

Effects:
It cannot compute new data but remembers old forecasts:
	•	warming models
	•	migration projections
	•	nuclear fallout dispersal
	•	resource deposit [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Locations/Locations|Locations]]

Becomes the equivalent of a holy codex for the Hydrocliffs.

V. CLASS V RELICS — THE UNINTENDED ANOMALIES

These are the ones with the strongest cultural impact.

⸻

14. The [[Nairobi]] Singularity Wall (Africa) — (Observed 2480)

Nature:
A mega-data center that partially melted and fused computational [[Layers]].

Effects:
Projects electromagnetic interference in stable pulses.
Locals see it as a “thinking mountain.”
Scientists of Alpine Helveticon consider it a [[Physics]] curiosity.
Tribals fear its “ghost radio.”

⸻

15. The Greenland Warm Gate (2570)

Nature:
A geothermal borehole from pre-Fall energy experiments.
Now exposed by ice retreat.

Effects:
Constant steam plumes.
Myth: “The Earth’s Breath.”
Reality: a dangerous geothermal vent complex near abandoned labs.

⸻

16. The Sahara Skyglass (2340)

Nature:
Vitrified desert glass created by MIRV saturation strikes.
Massive sheets of blackened, melted silica.

Effects:
Used as mirrors, weapons, religious symbols.

⸻

⸻

VI. RELICS THAT BECOME MYTHS (AND WHY)

Relics become sacred because:
	•	they are the last intact objects from the Old World
	•	they exhibit behaviors misunderstood by post-tech societies
	•	they interact with climate instability in dramatic ways
	•	they grant localized power
	•	their origins are [[Forgotten]] → myth emerges naturally

Examples of mythic evolution:
	•	“The Nails of God” (tungsten rods) → sky-punishment religion
	•	“The Breath of Earth” (Greenland geothermal vents) → creation myth
	•	“The Oracle Northmind” (AI core) → pilgrimage site
	•	“Metal Whales” (biopods) → protective ocean spirits
	•	“Spear of the Last King” (Longinus) → kingship symbol
