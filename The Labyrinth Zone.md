•	Nature: A vast belt where streets fold, loop, and trap travelers. Maps are useless — the City itself resists being charted.
	•	Feel: Infinite corridors, stairs that descend forever, plazas that repeat with subtle variations.
	•	Function: Acts as both barrier and test. Those who can traverse it earn prestige or die trying.
	•	Story Hook: A character could inherit an instinctual ability to “read” the Labyrinth from their Interstice parent.
