Mars never becomes the primary refueling site.

That would give the planet leverage.

Instead, fuel infrastructure is distributed.

⸻

Orbital Refueling Rings

Several smaller refueling platforms orbit Mars at varying inclinations.

They handle:
	•	hydrogen
	•	oxygen
	•	methane
	•	reaction mass
	•	emergency reserves

These stations are:
	•	semi-automated
	•	interchangeable
	•	replaceable

No single strike—or political crisis—can shut down Martian fuel flow.

⸻

Phobos Integration

Phobos supplies:
	•	bulk propellant
	•	reaction mass
	•	heavy orbital transfers

But refueling decisions are made elsewhere—primarily via Areopagus coordination.
