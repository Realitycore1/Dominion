Squad vitals

Reality Redaction Protocols: Information the user is not cleared for is actively masked or rewritten.

Primary AR Applications
Urban Control
Enforces order by digitally tagging suspects, zones, and curfews.
Citizens may receive moral scores, orders, or loyalty reminders live in their visual field.

 Bureaucratic Efficiency
City-[[Wide]] AR navigation of[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] departments, legal [[Systems]], and transport.
Personal AI agents appear as projected assistants—only seen by their assigned user.

Civil & Industrial Use
Engineering overlays, repair indicators, hazard warnings, and dynamic schematics.
Used in void construction, tesseract fabrication, and quantum machine calibration.

DreamBridge Mode: Seamless blending of waking reality and dreamlike simulation.

MetaJudication Protocols: Suspects judged partially in VR court while their real body remains in lockdown.

AR-VR Collapse Scenarios: Used in [[Domain]] warfare to destabilize enemy cognition—real and unreal blur until surrender.

Failure Modes & Hazards
Sim-Addiction: Citizens lose themselves in VR dreamloops.

AR Desync: Visual overlays contradict physical reality—leads to accidents or psychological breakdown.

Metaphysical Desaturation: Exposure to [[Aetheric]]-AR blending causes soul dissonance—some become unanchored.
Ghost Echo Imprints: Memories of past users linger in shared VR [[Systems]], resulting in anomalous emotional bleed.
