Core Bias:

Rotational convergence and centralization

This subtype is where instability organizes itself around centers of pull.

Traits:
	•	vortex behavior
	•	centralized destructive cores
	•	spiral convergence
	•	inward-drawing turbulence
	•	high instability around central singular structures

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	everything is being drawn toward a center
	•	instability has a shape
	•	motion is violent but not directionless

Common Expressions:
	•	attractor civilizations
	•	convergent war basins
	•	spiral collapse zones
	•	giant rotating pressure structures

Failure Mode:

Core collapse
The center becomes too unstable to sustain orbiting structure.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	Great Attractor-like systems
	•	Dominion-scale convergence cosmology
	•	vortex megastructures
	•	centralized violent empires
