## Summary

Economic: A
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Spatial Conduits are engineered corridors of bent spacetime, stabilized by [[Exotic Matter]] lattices and Concordia harmonics, allowing ships, signals, and even energy flows to travel vast distances in compressed “tubes” of reality. Unlike Horizon Arc Gates (edge-of-[[Verse]] gateways), Conduits are interior arteries — highways threaded through [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]-ruled space.
Structure

Throat Lattice: A tunnel of warped spacetime, lined with strange-matter anchors and [[Computronium]] nodes.

Concordia Mantle: Resonant fields that keep the corridor stable, often inscribed with glyph-patterns that sing as ships pass.

Entry Gates: Orbital fortress-stations housing the conduit mouths, bristling with [[Magnus Bastions]] and beam defenses.

MRO Swarms: Endless repair drones stitching the geometry, maintaining balance against cosmic tides.

Functions

Civil: Reduce travel times between core worlds; a trip of light-years becomes days or hours inside a conduit.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Provide rapid fleet redeployment — [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] step through hymnal [[Fire]] and emerge whole on the other side.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Arteries” — described as veins through which the Empress’s blood flows across creation.

Variants

Short-Span Conduits: Link nearby [[Systems]] (interstellar roads).

Long-Span Conduits: Thread across sectors, sometimes branching like vascular networks.

Sanctum Conduits: Forbidden arteries, reserved only for sacred pilgrimages or [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] bearing the Empress’s will.

Economic: Conduits make whole clusters function like single cities.
