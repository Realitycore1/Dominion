# Chapter 8 - ArcCells to Ashes

## Timeline Function

War Years 4-6. Arcology cells, shelter districts, habitat nodes, and strategic civilian infrastructure are destroyed.

## Event Role

- Demonstrates that clean tactical nuclear arms and precision weapons still create moral catastrophe.
- Turns reconstruction systems into battlefield objectives.
- Creates refugee flows that overwhelm route compacts.
- Makes civilian protection the central legitimacy test for every side.

## Consequence

The war loses any remaining illusion of clean separation between front and rear.
