# Domain Origins

## Post-Equilon Union Departure
- Cabal of techno-elites, politicians, and medical-industrial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Government/Leaders|leaders]] remain on [[Earth (Terra)]] after [[Equilon Union]]~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Equilon Union/Equilon Union]] departs.
- They reject outward expansion, focusing instead on remaking [[Humanity]].

## Methods
- Manipulation through **food, water, and medical supply [[Chains]].**
- Gene editing, vivisection, and covert experimentation.
- Slow generational editing first, then Prime Transfiguration.

## Breakthrough
- **First Thrall**: crude, obedient, monstrous proof of principle.
- **Prime Transfiguration:** discovered as thralls age and evolve intelligence/cruelty.

## Schism
- A biologist injects herself with Prime genetic material → becomes **First Sovereign Prime.**
- Establishes ruling caste above thralls.
- [[Domain]] goes underground during plague era, growing [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]]rse/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]]os Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Totum Verse/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] see [[Earth (Terra)]] fade into [[Silence]] → mythologized as tomb world.


# --- 02_Thralls.md ---

# Thrall Variants

## Brute Thrall (Juggernaut)
- Hulking warform, swollen muscle, bone plates, claw arm.

## Spindler Thrall (Infiltrator)
- Elongated, insect-like, shadow distortions, featureless mask.

## Larval Thrall (Unfinished)
- Half-[[Human]] cocoon stage, tendrils rooting body, twisted terror.

## Command Thrall (Overseer)
- Taller, armored with rune-lit chest.
- Controls thralls via resonance.


# --- 03_Primes_Sovereigns.md ---

# Primes & Sovereigns

## First Sovereign (The Biologist)
- Injected herself with raw Prime genetic material.
- Gained carapace armor like second skin.
- Proved voluntary Prime transfiguration → foundation of Sovereign rule.

## Malevolent Sovereign
- Gothic cathedral-like armor, faceless mask cracked with light.
- Seated on throne of bone.

## Ascendant Prime
- Semi-transparent crystalline carapace, shifting organs.
- Crown of glowing horn extrusions.

## Sovereign Enthroned (The Crown Ritual)
- Prime merges with living throne.
- Thralls kneel in resonance obedience.


# --- 04_Hives.md ---

# Hive Environments

## Transfiguration Chamber
- Vats with twitching bodies, half-[[Human]]/half-thrall.
- Pipes and ichor veins pumping into cocoons.

## Thrall Warrens
- Ribbed tunnels, fungal spores, ichor pools.
- Thralls swarm in unison, resonance-guided.

## Sovereign’s Cathedral
- Vast rib-like columns, throne at center.
- Glyphs glowing on walls, murals of transfiguration.

## Recycling Pits (Ouroboros)
- Living digestive pits for failed thralls and captives.
- Overseers carve Ouroboros glyph before victims are consumed.

## Corpus Table
- Periodic chart of organs.

## Ouroboros of Bone

- Inverted fetus with hollow chest.

## The Black Sun

- Chalice encircled by DNA-helix runes.

## The Veil of Silence
- Shattered eye with concentric crescents.
