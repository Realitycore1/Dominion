The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Aphotic Zone/The Ringed Symphonies/Overview|Overview]] Effect is a profound cognitive and emotional shift experienced by astronauts when they view [[Earth (Terra)]] from space. First described in the 1980s by space philosopher Frank White, it’s a transformative realization of [[Earth (Terra)]]’s unity, fragility, and interconnectedness—a perspective difficult or impossible to fully grasp from the surface.
