## Elevator
A world where the sky itself is a tessellated exoderm that periodically translates reality to new coordinates and planes, refreshing the threats above. Cultures learned to live with migrations of heaven.

## Tone pillars
- Strange made practical; [[Ritual]] meets engineering.
- [[Undead]] are techno‑integrated and mission‑capable.
- Magic is tiered for everyday logistics (Ferrous → Adamant), with special out‑of‑band classes ([[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Transcendent]]/Divine/Mythic).

## Recent History (working canon)
- Post‑Cataclysm integration of bio‑mechanical resurrection [[Systems]] tied to [[Yondor]] & the [[Abyss]].
- Emergence of non‑Euclidean civic architecture; castles threaded with energy coilworks.
- Institutionalization of tier‑gated expeditions and contracts.
