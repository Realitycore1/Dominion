## Summary

GalFed's economy is a recovery economy that became a dependency economy. It coordinates food, medicine, transit, archive access, credit, standards, and disaster relief across Odysseus.

## Details

The Federation economy began as emergency logistics after the Aether Singularity. Surviving worlds needed food, spare parts, cognition medicine, safe routes, and restoration technology. GalFed built the systems that kept them alive, then made those systems the foundation of membership.

Core mechanisms:

- Chronocredits indexed to energy, entropy, transit time, and long-term stability.
- Founders' Clearing Houses mediating interstellar trade.
- Relief eligibility based on compliance and risk classification.
- Corridor licenses for legal FTL movement.
- Archive certification required for cultural and legal recognition.
- Technology tiering based on stewardship status.
- Medical supply guarantees for member worlds.
- Debt restructuring in exchange for deeper administrative integration.

To loyalists, this is civilization-scale mutual aid.

To dissidents, it is velvet monopoly. If a world leaves GalFed, its ships lose corridor priority, its universities lose accreditation, its medicines lose guarantee, its exports lose certification, and its leaders become risk subjects.
