1. Common Vampire
	•	Definition: The baseline—[[Undead]] predators feeding on blood.
	•	Traits: Pale skin, fangs, nocturnal, vulnerable to sunlight.
	•	Symbolism: Parasitic immortality; fear of the predator disguised as kin.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Noble Vampires
	•	Definition: Aristocratic vampires ruling courts or clans.
	•	Traits: Regal bearing, charming allure, manipulative politics.
	•	Symbolism: The parasitic nobility; wealth and power feeding on the masses.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Feral Vampires
	•	Definition: Bestial vampires who lose all pretense of [[Humanity]].
	•	Traits: Batlike features, claws, snarling [[Hunger]].
	•	Symbolism: Civilization stripped away to raw appetite.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Dhampir (Half-Vampires)
	•	Definition: Hybrids born of vampire and mortal unions.
	•	Traits: Walk in daylight, partial powers, struggle between bloodlines.
	•	Symbolism: The liminal being; torn between [[Hunger]] and [[Humanity]].

⸻

5. Dread Vampires
	•	Definition: Elder, monstrous vampires steeped in centuries of power.
	•	Traits: Near-immortal, towering presence, vast magical powers.
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of eternity; the vampire as god-king.

⸻

6. Nosferatu
	•	Definition: Grotesque vampires warped beyond beauty.
	•	Traits: Bald heads, ratlike teeth, plague auras.
	•	Symbolism: Disease, rot, the hideous side of [[Hunger]].

⸻

7. Shadow Vampires
	•	Definition: Vampires attuned to shadow or abyssal powers.
	•	Traits: Intangible forms, necrotic breath, dark auras.
	•	Symbolism: [[Hunger]] turned spiritual; the devourer of life’s essence.

⸻

8. Psychic Vampires
	•	Definition: Feed on thought, emotion, or will instead of blood.
	•	Traits: Cold touch, draining gazes, domination powers.
	•	Symbolism: Energy drain as metaphor for toxic dependence.

⸻

9. Elemental Vampires
	•	Definition: Vampires tied to primal forces ([[Fire]]-drinkers, frostbitten, stormborn).
	•	Traits: Unique breath, elemental weaknesses and resistances.
	•	Symbolism: [[Hunger]] bound to the elements; nature consumed.

⸻

10. Vampire Lords (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary vampire rulers who embody the pinnacle of undeath.
	•	Traits: Immense magical and physical power, command entire [[Undead]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]].
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] enthroned; immortality as tyranny.
