## Summary

GalFed megastructures are built to prevent another collapse of consciousness and to bind worlds into a common logistical order. They are monuments, hospitals, archives, ports, weapons, and leashes.

## Details

Major structures:

- Beacon World Prime: capital archive world built around the ruins of the first Accord meeting site.
- Archive Gate System: galactic-scale memory repository and cultural certification network.
- Continuity Arrays: Aether monitoring webs that detect noetic instability across sectors.
- Corridor Rings: licensed FTL route anchors and customs choke points.
- Silent World Cradles: shielded habitats preserving damaged populations until restoration becomes possible.
- Dyson Dampener Nets: stellar regulation systems used in both disaster prevention and coercive embargo.
- Quarantine Halos: orbital containment systems around restricted planets or ruins.
- Restoration Moons: clinic-worlds where damaged minds are stabilized at population scale.
- Founders' Clearing Vaults: financial and data centers that mediate chronocredit settlement.
- Predictive Engine Nodes: distributed computation structures feeding the Central Predictive Engine.

The Federation prefers infrastructure that has dual use. A relief corridor can become an interdiction net. A clinic can become a census station. An archive can become an identity court. A trade hub can become a dependency lock.
