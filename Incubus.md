1. Common Incubus
	•	Definition: A [[Demon]] of lust who preys on mortals through dreams and seduction.
	•	Traits: Handsome or alluring male form, wings, hypnotic aura, drains vitality.
	•	Symbolism: Desire turned into exploitation; intimacy weaponized.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Dream Incubi
	•	Definition: Incubi who haunt sleep and weave nightmares into temptation.
	•	Traits: Appear in dreams, drain life through nocturnal visitations.
	•	Symbolism: The violation of safety in slumber.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Shadow Incubi
	•	Definition: Lurkers at the edges of vision, feeding on suppressed or forbidden desires.
	•	Traits: Half-corporeal, smoky forms, haunting whispers.
	•	Symbolism: Guilt and secrecy given flesh.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Noble Incubi
	•	Definition: Refined, charismatic seducers who thrive in courts and palaces.
	•	Traits: Elegant features, charming voices, master manipulators.
	•	Symbolism: The devil dressed as nobility; corruption beneath beauty.

⸻

5. War Incubi
	•	Definition: Incubi who turn lust into bloodlust, thriving on the mingling of desire and violence.
	•	Traits: Muscled physiques, cruel weapons, aura of frenzy.
	•	Symbolism: The link between passion and destruction.

⸻

6. Vampiric Incubi
	•	Definition: Demonic predators who drain blood as well as desire.
	•	Traits: Fanged smiles, crimson eyes, blend of [[Vampire]] and incubus traits.
	•	Symbolism: Consumption without limit; [[Hunger]] that never ends.

⸻

7. Elemental Incubi
	•	Definition: Incubi whose forms are bound to [[Fire]], storm, or water.
	•	Traits: Fiery hair, storm-veins, dripping aura of the sea.
	•	Symbolism: Desire as a [[Natural]] force; passion beyond [[Human]] control.

⸻

8. Fallen Angelic Incubi
	•	Definition: Once-holy beings twisted into [[Demons]] of lust.
	•	Traits: Burned halos, wings blackened but radiant, mournful yet predatory.
	•	Symbolism: The tragedy of purity corrupted into vice.

⸻

9. Parasite Incubi
	•	Definition: Monstrous incubi who implant seeds of corruption that grow inside mortals.
	•	Traits: Twisting forms, talons, parasitic offspring.
	•	Symbolism: The fear of unwanted legacy; violation of the body.

⸻

10. Abyssal Incubi (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary incubi who embody desire itself—more a force than a being.
	•	Traits: Ever-shifting forms, appear as the perfect object of desire.
	•	Symbolism: The endless [[Hunger]] of lust as a consuming [[Abyss]].
