I. The Committee Era (2330–2380)

Venus was the last of the inner worlds to be reached. Colonization began not on the surface but in the skies.

The Venus Colonial Committee (VCC) was established by the [[Colonial Union]] to oversee cloud-top habitats — vast aerostat cities floating at 50–60 km altitude, where temperatures were survivable and pressures near-Earthlike.

The VCC ruled with tight control: every cubic meter of breathable air was rationed, every descent forbidden. Colonists lived in pressurized spheres suspended in a world of endless storms, dependent on CU shipments for metals and rare tech.

They were nicknamed “Sky Wards” — colonists who lived, quite literally, at the mercy of the wind.

⸻

II. The Age of the Sky Cities (2380–2430)
	•	Habitat Expansion: Aerostat cities multiplied, linked by magnetic sky-rails and dirigible freighters. The greatest were Ishtar, Aphrodite, and Eosphoros, each with populations of 20,000–50,000.
	•	[[culture]] of Balance: Venusian life demanded constant vigilance — ballast checks, gas mixing, storm prediction. Balance and precision became cultural virtues, embedded into education and [[Ritual]].
	•	Surface Probes: Early terraforming attempts began: orbital mirrors to reflect heat, solar shades at L1 to dim insolation, and sulfur scrubbers seeded into the upper atmosphere.

The dream of “taming the Morning Star” grew stronger, but the Committee remained cautious and authoritarian.

⸻

III. The [[Schism]] of the Clouds (2430–2470)

The spark of independence came from Eosphoros City, when its citizens refused to obey a CU order to shut down local fusion rigs.

They declared that Venus was not a colony, but a world unto itself, and formed the Venusian Council of Cities, a rival to the Committee. Other aerostat habitats quickly aligned, tired of CU oversight.

The Committee attempted to sanction food and water shipments, but by then the sky cities had built enough closed-loop [[Systems]] to endure. The blockade failed.

⸻

IV. The Cloudborn [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] (2470–2500)

Venusians began to call themselves Cloudborn, seeing survival in the skies as their defining heritage.
	•	Cultural Traits:
	•	Reverence for balance, cycles, and the constant vigilance required to keep their cities afloat.
	•	Festivals of lightning, celebrated when storms surged beneath them.
	•	Oral histories blending science and myth, speaking of the Surface as Hell and the Clouds as Eden.
	•	Political Shift:
	•	The Venus Colonial Committee dissolved, replaced by the Council of the Clouds.
	•	Council operated as a confederation of sky cities, each with autonomy but bound by resource-sharing pacts.

⸻

V. Toward the Star of Morning (2500+)

Terraforming made halting progress:
	•	[[Star Shades]] reduced solar input by a fraction.
	•	Sulfur harvesters cleared swaths of sky.
	•	Sky-mirrors reflected more heat back into space.
