Reality remembers what was lost.

⸻

Environmental Effects:

* Ghost structures appear
* Past states overlay current ones
* Ruins become “whole” temporarily

⸻

Cognitive Effects:

* Emotional weight increases
* Memories (real or false) surface
* Individuals experience alternate pasts

⸻

Scientific Effects:

* Memory-based materials become unstable
* Memory Glass activates spontaneously
* Temporal anomalies increase

⸻

Combat Effects:

* Fallen entities may reappear
* Battlefield “echoes” interfere
* Attacks can hit past or future states

⸻

Social Behavior:

* Activity slows
* Rituals and reflection increase
* Some citizens withdraw entirely

⸻

Key Insight:

Mourning is not decay—it is recollection forced into the present
