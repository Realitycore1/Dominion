Today, BAO appear as a preferred spacing (~500 million light-years) between galaxies, acting as a cosmic standard ruler. By measuring these patterns, scientists can track the expansion of the universe and gain insights into dark energy, cosmic geometry, and the universe’s large-scale structure.
