Saudi Arabia is a country located in Western Asia, with a diverse geography and significant mineral resources. The country is bordered by Qatar and the Persian Gulf to the north, Kuwait and [[Iraq]] to the northeast, Jordan and [[Iraq]] to the east, and Yemen and [[Oman]] to the south. Saudi Arabia's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is heavily dependent on its oil and gas resources, which account for over 90% of its exports. The country has the world's largest proven oil reserves and one of the largest reserves of [[Natural]] gas. In addition to oil and gas, Saudi Arabia has significant mineral resources such as copper, zinc, phosphate, and gemstones. The country has also made efforts to diversify its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] through tourism and industrial development. The country's geography is characterized by desert [[Regions]] such as the Rub al-Khali desert, which covers over 60% of the country's territory. Saudi Arabia also has a coastal region along the Red Sea, which is an important transportation hub for trade with neighboring countries. Saudi Arabia's [[culture]] is heavily influenced by Islam, which is the predominant religion in the country. The country has a long [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] of scholarship and learning, with several universities and research institutions across the country. The [[culture]] of Saudi Arabia also includes traditional dress, music, and dance, as well as a strong emphasis on hospitality and generosity. Saudi Arabia has faced challenges related to social and economic inequality, political instability, and global competition for oil. However, the country has made efforts to diversify its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] and promote social reforms in recent years. Overall, Saudi Arabia remains a [[Key]] player in the global [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]], with its mineral resources and strategic location making it an important partner for businesses and governments around the world.
