## Summary

Interferome
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
SGL Constellations are vast networks of probes and stations placed at the focal points of multiple [[Stars]]’ gravitational lenses. Each lens magnifies and channels light, [[Gravitational Waves]], or other signals. In combination, they form cosmic-scale interferometers, capable of seeing or projecting across intergalactic distances.
Constellation Layout

Single-Star Arrays: Fleets at 550–1,000 AU beyond a sun, forming ring-shaped collector webs.

Multi-Star Constellations: Linked networks spanning multiple [[Stars]], weaving a galactic eye.

Functions

Observation: Peer across galaxies — resolve exoplanet surfaces, [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]], even Spiralverse harmonics.

Communication: Use [[Stars]] as amplifiers, transmitting encoded grav-wave or light signals across light-years.

Projection: Constellations can broadcast beams, making glyphs visible across entire sectors.

Weaponization: Overdrive an SGL with a Nicoll–Dyson Beam, turning the focused spot into a stellar-scaled [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] ray.

Variants

Civil Constellations: Used for mapping, navigation, and interstellar coordination.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Constellations: Apex recon [[Systems]] and potential weapons of focused annihilation.

Sacral Constellations: Positioned deliberately to form glyphic constellations, so that entire sectors look up and see the Empress’s script in the [[Stars]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide eyes and ears that span galaxies — no corner of creation unseen.

Economic: Aid navigation for Beam Corridors and [[Gravimetric Roads]], ensuring flawless interstellar trade.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Eyes” — myths say the Empress sees through every star, her gaze piercing the Omniverse.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Collector arrays shaped like ribbed cathedral webs at the focal points.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they appear as silent black spines in deep interstellar dark.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Arrays ripple in fractal mandalas of light, fields bending in recursive geometry.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The focused spot of each lens glows with glyphs — scripture written in starlight.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Awe — their Empress sees and speaks across galaxies.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Supreme reconnaissance and communication backbone.

To myth: Called the “Amber Eyes of Heaven”, each constellation a gaze of the divine Empress stitched into the fabric of [[Stars]].
