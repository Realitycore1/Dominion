	•	Internally, they operate via a hex-redundant organ structure—three hearts, two respiratory clusters, and dual thermal exchangers for high adaptability.

	•	Civil [[law]] is minimal—conflict is resolved via rite duels, and justice is considered a clan responsibility, not a civil right.
	•	[[I - Dominion Omniverse/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Military/Technology|Technology]] Use:
	•	Viewed as tools, not [[I - Dominion Omniverse/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]—machines are to be mastered, not respected. Their tech is brutal, unornamented, and optimized for utility in hostile conditions.

Weaponry:
	•	Favor plasma-disruption melee weapons, acid-spike launchers, and bonegrip scatterguns.
	•	Known for using flesh-memory explosives—mines that scream when approached, designed to unnerve the weak.
	•	All weapons are body-locked—genetically encoded to the wielder to prevent theft or cowardly usage.
	•	Armor:
	•	Jagged, overlapping plates that mimic [[Natural]] predators from their homeworld. Armor is blood-oiled before battle for [[Ritual]] bonding.
	•	Helmets are sculpted to show exaggerated bone ridges and tusks—intimidation is as functional as protection.
	•	Ships:
	•	Vessels resemble carapace-wrapped beasts, each one marked with sigils carved into the hull.
	•	Travel via multi-burn orbital jump—a chaotic but effective method of layered propulsion that leaves disruptive gravity waves.

The Narkerens are used primarily as shock enforcers along hostile or contested borders.
	•	Rarely stationed within inner Federation [[Systems]]—viewed as unstable, tho[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]]h them are sacred—they never betray a sealed oath, and are brutally loyal to their terms.
	•	Some Federation theorists believe the Narkerens represent a lost pre-galactic [[Warrior]] order, now reduced to instinctual echoes. The Narkerens neither confirm nor deny this.
