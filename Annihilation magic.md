Definition
Annihilation Magic is the art of unmaking. Unlike Destruction (which burns, crushes, or explodes matter into other states), Annihilation removes things from the ledger of being entirely. It erases not only matter, but often the memory, meaning, and axiom that supported it.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Non-Substitution – destruction converts matter to energy; annihilation converts it to nothing.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Pattern Erasure – annihilation targets the informational blueprint of a thing.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Untraceability – once annihilated, recovery is impossible without [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-level axiom rewriting.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Abyssal Resonance – annihilation often touches [[The Void]] between verses, using absence as fuel.

⸻

Techniques of Annihilation
	•	Unmaking Touch – erases flesh, stone, or steel with a gesture.
	•	Voidfire – conjured flame that consumes without residue.
	•	Soul Obliteration – permanent erasure of spirit; even necromancy cannot recall.
	•	Concept Stripping – removing an attribute (“this place no longer exists as a city”).
	•	Zone Nullification – creating fields where nothing persists — wards, spells, or even [[Time]] unravel on entry.
	•	[[Verse]]-Scale Erasure – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-tier: erasing laws, histories, or whole realities.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: Rumors say the World-Breaker dwarven machine used annihilation, not destruction — splitting “Sky Above and Stone Below” into absence rather than debris ￼.
	•	Concordia: Mythic-tier saints wield “white flames” that erase corruption utterly, leaving no ashes behind. [[Yondor]] [[Undead]] fear annihilation more than [[Fire]], since it severs both flesh and soul.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Weaponized in Oblivion Lances and [[Nulliton]] Bombs — ordnance that doesn’t just destroy targets but writes them out of causal Entire battles vanish as if they never occurred.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Abhors annihilation as a philosophical obscenity; but their harmonic scholars admit certain “discordant tones” can collapse resonance into [[Silence]].
	•	Gnostic Expanse: The Demiurge whispers annihilation as liberation: “To erase is to free.” Cities have willingly walked into void-chasms, believing annihilation ends their suffering.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Finality – nothing resists annihilation; even gods fall silent before it.
	•	Utility – erases corruption, infection, or cursed artifacts when no other method works.
	•	Terror – annihilation terrifies enemies; fear of ceasing to ever have been is unmatched.
	•	Balance – used sparingly, it can cleanse verses of threats beyond conventional destruction.

⸻

Perils
	•	Irreversibility – annihilation removes options; once wielded, all paths [[Close]].
	•	Collateral Erasure – annihilation spreads unpredictably, consuming more than intended.
	•	Memory Holes – survivors may forget what was lost, destabilizing and
	•	Contamination – annihilation scars linger: null zones where nothing grows, no magic functions, and even [[Time]] hesitates.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Anchors of Persistence – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] tech that “pins” matter and memory against erasure.
	•	Mythopoeic Resistance – strong stories can resist erasure; myths are harder to annihilate than facts.
	•	Revelation Wards – prophetic shields that “foresee” Existence and reassert it.
	•	Axiomatic Lockdown – sealing the underlying [[law]] of being, making annihilation illegal within a zone.
