Phase V: Independence (2300–2330)
	•	Trigger Events:
	•	Taxation/resource disputes with [[Colonial Union]].
	•	Growing Lunar pride in independence from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s cycles of collapse and repair.
	•	[[Skirmishes]] with CU patrols over SPA Basin mining [[Rights]].
	•	Declaration of the Cynthia Protectorate (c. 2320):
	•	Named for Selene/Cynthia, Greek goddess of the Moon.
	•	Framed as protectorate “under Luna’s own light.”
	•	Retained ties to CU in trade and defense but asserted political independence.
	•	Symbolism:
	•	Cynthia Protectorate embodied [[Humanity]]’s first permanent breakaway civilization.
	•	A direct descendant of the Ash Century ethos: survival through stewardship, not [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]].
	•	Became staging ground for further expansion into the Belt and Mars.
