This immediately signals:

These are not ghosts.
These are energy-adaptive organisms.

⸻

II. Dominion / Operational Term

PSA-Class Entities

(Pulsar-Symbiotic Anomalies)

Dominion deliberately avoids the word life.

Sub-designations:
	•	PSA-Passive – stable attachment
	•	PSA-Reactive – emission-responsive
	•	PSA-Shear – dangerous during stellar events

⸻

III. What They Actually Are

Spectral Wraiths are quasi-biological energy condensates that:
	•	Form within intense magnetic fields
	•	Anchor to pulsars / magnetars
	•	Feed on:
	•	EM radiation harmonics
	•	Neutrino flux
	•	Spacetime vibration induced by stellar rotation

They are born, not summoned.

⸻

IV. Morphology & Behavior

Appearance
	•	Luminous filament bodies
	•	Semi-transparent, phase-shifting
	•	Internal light pulses synchronized to stellar rotation
	•	“Tails” are field gradients, not limbs

Behavior
	•	Permanently orbit pulse stars
	•	Detach briefly during:
	•	Starquakes
	•	Magnetic pole shifts
	•	Binary interactions
	•	Die if removed from high-energy environments

They cannot survive planets.
They cannot survive ships.
They cannot survive quiet.

⸻

V. Intelligence (Important Boundary)

They are non-sentient in the human sense.
	•	No goals
	•	No memory
	•	No communication
	•	No reaction to observers except through field disturbance

What looks like awareness is resonance alignment.

This distinction is critical—because people will get it wrong.

⸻

VI. Cultural & Mythic Names

Humans cannot resist mythologizing them.

Common names:
	•	Spectral Wraiths (default)
	•	Star Ghosts
	•	The Watchers
	•	Light Souls
	•	The Burned Dead
	•	Neutron Angels (banned term in Dominion texts)

Religious sects believe:

Souls of extinct civilizations cling to the universe’s last lighthouses.

Dominion response:

“Incorrect, but understandable.”

⸻

VII. Why They Stick to Pulse Stars

Pulse stars provide:
	•	Stable rhythmic emissions
	•	Predictable energy gradients
	•	Long-term survivability (millions of years)

They are obligate symbionts of extreme astrophysical objects.

Remove the star, and the wraith collapses into incoherent radiation.
