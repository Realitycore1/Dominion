Birth: 2042 – Tokyo, Japan
Profession: Robotics specialist
Survival specialty: Robot preservation, automated maintenance [[Systems]]
Base: Neo-Kyoto Vault

Aya speaks little.
She thinks faster than she talks.
She is the only person alive who can reliably maintain pre-Fall robotics.
She has reactivated drones, refitted exosuits, and restored dead machines that everyone else believed were gods.

Known as:
The Sleepless Monk
The Perfect Form

She fears only one thing: losing her mind long before losing her life.
