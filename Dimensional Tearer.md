## Summary

## Details

A Dimensional Tearer is a type of 
starship that has the ability to tear holes in the fabric of space-[[Time]], allowing it to traverse dimensions and travel between different realities. Dimensional Tearers are incredibly powerful and rare ships that require adv[[Technology Portal|technology]]logy]] and knowledge to construct. They are often found in high-tech factions or empires, and are used as a means of exploring and understanding the mysteries of the universe. When a Dimensional Tearer is activated, it creates a wormhole that allows it to travel to another dimension or reality. This can be a risky process, as the ship may end up in an unstable or hostile dimension that poses a significant threat to its crew. The Dimensional Tearers can be used to access hidden areas of the galaxy or even other galaxies entirely. They can also be used to explore and understand the nature of the multiverse. However, using a Dimensional Tearer is not without its risks, as the ship can be lost or damaged in the dimensional tear, or even destroyed by the forces of reality itself. As such, those should approach its use with caution and only undertake it when they have the necessary resources and expertise to handle the potential dangers involved.
