Core Bias:

Cyclical force and recurrent return

This subtype is defined by rhythm.

Reality behaves in surges, pullbacks, recurring waves, and oscillatory pressure.

Traits:
	•	strong cyclicality
	•	return patterns
	•	repeated rise/fall behavior
	•	civilizations built around timing
	•	periodic access to structures or routes

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	nothing stays available forever
	•	timing matters as much as location
	•	opportunity and danger arrive in waves

Common Expressions:
	•	tide-synced civilizations
	•	recurring access corridors
	•	cycle-based metaphysical openings
	•	basin-scale seasonal epochs

Failure Mode:

Cycle lock
The basin becomes trapped in repetition instead of progression.

This subtype is very strong for:
	•	recurring history
	•	dream/ritual access systems
	•	seasonal or phase-based cosmology
