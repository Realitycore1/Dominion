To demoralize a nation, the initial step is to target the education system. This ensures that the younger generation is indoctrinated with a socialist ideology, which can lead to a loss of national identity and pride.
The second wave targets the economy, causing widespread economic instability and poverty. This can further erode public confidence in the government and increase social unrest.
The final wave targets the family, which is the fundamental building block of society. By disrupting family structures and values, the socialist ideology can be reinforced, and loyalty to the state becomes more important than loyalty to traditional institutions.
In summary, demoralizing a nation involves a three-pronged approach that targets education, the economy, and the family. These waves work in tandem to undermine societal values and create a socialist society that is subservient to the state.
