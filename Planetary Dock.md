## Summary

Sanctum Doc
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Planetary Docks are super-sized spaceport and shipyard complexes built into the surface, mantle, or orbital envelope of a planet, designed to host, repair, and construct fleets ranging from civilian convoys to Lozenge-class [[World Stations]]. They are equal parts factory, cathedral, and fortress-harbor.
Structure

Surface Basins: Continent-spanning drydock pits lined with launch spires and construction gantries.

Mantle Tethers: [[Deep-Well Geo-Taps]] and [[Mantle Pistons]] provide energy and structural support.

Orbital Crowns: [[Halo Habs]], Orbital Rings, and [[Mass Driver Rings]] integrated into the dock network.

[[Atlas Pillars]]: Support towers linking surface docks to orbital slipways, like titanic crane-spires.

Capabilities

Fleet Construction: Build everything from [[Frigates]] to capital fleets.

Megastructure Assembly: Fabricate Dyson panels, Clarke Cities, even [[Ark Platforms]] in planetary forges.

Maintenance: Service entire fleets at once, with automated MRO ecologies swarming across hulls.

Defense: Dock doubles as Magnus Bastion — planetary artillery, gravitic curtains, and kinetic nets protect the facility.

Variants

Civil Docks: Commercial hubs, colony shipbuilders, logistics centers.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Docks: Heavily fortified ship-factories, the hearts of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] fleet power.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide endless [[Infrastructure]] to colonize and supply sectors.

Economic: Anchor entire trade networks, exporting ships and modules to surrounding [[Systems]].

Religious: Known as “Harbors of Amber” — said to be the places where the Empress launches her [[Angels]] of steel into creation.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Surface docks shaped like ribbed cathedrals, each slipway a nave that births fleets.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, Docks look like black scars across continents, geometric and absolute.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Gantries and slipways spiral upward in fractal patterns, living geometry of industry.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Launch arcs traced with glyphs — every fleet leaves its dock accompanied by scripture written in [[Fire]].

From orbit, a Planetary Dock makes the world look like a cog in a galactic engine, glowing veins of construction stretching across its face.

Narrative Function

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic lynchpins — destroy a dock, and you cripple a sector; hold a dock, and you hold the [[Stars]].
ks: Religious pilgrimage-hubs, where fleets are consecrated before [[Crusades]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Absolute keystones — without Docks, fleets wither. They are the arteries of war.

To citizens: Pride — their world is the cradle of fleets.

To rebels: Terror — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s fleets are endless because the docks are endless.

To myth: Called the “Amber Harbors”, seen as sanctified slipways where the Empress’s children of iron and [[Fire]] are born.
