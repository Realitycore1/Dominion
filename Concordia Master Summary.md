# Concordia Master Summary (v1.0)

## Core Identity
Concordia is a high-magic world within the Eos multiverse (Aphotic Zone). It occupies a stage between High Renaissance and mid-Dark Ages: visually advanced but scientifically primitive. Its people understand magic deeply yet misinterpret physical law. Angels gatekeep cosmic truths, mortals depend on faith and myth, and the Dominion's eventual arrival will expose their false cosmology.

---

## Magic System

### Tiers of Power
**Ferrous → Adamant** form the standard scale:
- **Ferrous** – minor sparks and charms.
- **Russet** – practical magic for guilds and craftsmen.
- **Argent** – reliable spellcraft; battle and healing tier.
- **Amber** – continent-level power; control of storms, armies.
- **Platina** – legendary; city-shaping and miracle-making.
- **Adamant** – world-shaping; demigod threshold.

### Special Ranks
- **Transcendent** – evolution of magic itself; one per world, elusive.
- **Divine** – granted through surrender to gods or higher entities.
- **Mythic** – unique, story-made-real, appearing once per epoch.

### Density Ecology
Magic depends on **aether density**. Ferrous–Argent thrive anywhere; Amber+ require high-density zones. Adamant and special ranks bend or ignore these restrictions. Geography and politics revolve around controlling dense ley regions.

### The Aether Tables
Three “periodic tables” organize magical matter and flow:
1. **[[Elemental Aether Table]]** – base essences (Pyra, Hydra, Terran, Luxa, Tenebris, etc.).
2. **[[Channel Table]]** – ley-lines, nulls, foci, paradox wells.
3. **[[Transcendent Substrata Table]]** – rare, reality-defining materials like Paradoxium, Mythril Logos, and Anatheum.

---

## World Structure
Concordia’s layered reality includes ten major strata:

1. **Telluria** – surface world of kingdoms, guilds, and mortals.
2. **Depths** – vast subterranean networks and ley reservoirs.
3. **Deadlands** – null zones where magic collapses.
4. **Yondor** – necro-industrial unlife realm of soul-tech.
5. **Abyss** – consuming void where magic devours itself.
6. **Skyreach** – storm citadels and floating isles.
7. **Ley-Sea** – invisible lattice of ley-lines beneath all.
8. **Dreamveil** – realm of imagination and divine communion.
9. **Aether Crown** – high-resonance sphere of world-shaping forces.
10. **Mythic Expanse** – outer realm where history crystallizes into story.

---

## Knowledge and Society

### Knowledge Hoarding
Angels and elite guilds suppress forbidden truths (e.g., the Exoderm). Knowledge is treated as sacred property; mortals are drip-fed half-truths to maintain hierarchy.

### Scientific Blindness
Physical laws are misinterpreted through magical frameworks: gravity as moon-song, disease as Mortis imbalance. Mechanics, biology, and chemistry remain superstition-bound.

### Education
Guilds and temples control study. Secular scholarship is nearly extinct outside of null or Deadland zones.

---

## Dual Economy

### Coin Economy (Everyday)
- Used by Ferrous–Argent classes.
- Coin, barter, and material trade dominate.
- Collapses in null zones where magic ceases.

### Favor Economy (Elite)
- Used by Amber–Adamant classes.
- Favors, oaths, relics, and bound contracts replace currency.
- Managed through magical compacts, often enforced by the Myrrin Blood Guild.

---

## Guilds of Concordia
Guilds are both corporations and sects of magical specialization.

- **Illuminators’ Guild** – Pyra and Luxa light/heat masters.
- **Hydramancers’ Concord** – water management, irrigation, seafaring.
- **Argent Collegium** – standardized magical education and certification.
- **Cartographers of the Ley** – map magic density and sell partial charts.
- **Stormwardens** – Fulminis battle-magi; mercenary lightning corps.
- **Iron Veins Order** – Ferrum/Terran architects and siege-smiths.
- **Bloodpact Guild** – Myrrin contract magi using blood-bonds.
- **Dreamveil Navigators** – Eidolon dreamwalkers, semi-legal.
- **Necropolis Foundry** – Yondor necrotechs and soul-engineers.
- **Oblivion Cartel** – illegal traders of unmaking essence.
- **Mythographers’ Lodge** – collectors of Mythic relics and stories.

---

## Religion and Faith

### Popular Churches
- **Luxa** – light and purity.
- **Vitae** – life and healing.
- **Selenor** – moon, tides, and prophecy.
- **Fulminis** – storms and strength.

### Tolerated
- **Mortis** – endings and entropy.
- **Myrrin** – blood and sacrifice.
- **Choriel** – sound and inspiration.
- **Bestia** – beasts and nature.

### Banned
- **Tenebris** – void and gravity.
- **Oblivionists** – unmaking cults.
- **Dreamveil Extremists** – delusionist sects.
- **Yondor Necrotics** – necromantic cults.

### Angelic Oversight
The angelic hierarchy sanctions stable faiths and censors disruptive ones. Their goal is cosmic order, not mortal enlightenment.

### Prophecy of the Coming Darkness
An ancient vision misread by angels: “the shadow that devours the song of magic.” They interpret it as an Abyssal threat, unaware it foretells the Dominion’s arrival and the nullification of all magic.

---

## External Planes (Translocation Cycle)
Concordia periodically transits other planes, reshaping epochs:

- **Pharos** – plane of pure light, ages of miracles and drought.  
- **Noctis Fold** – shadow and revelation, secrets uncovered.  
- **Verdant Coil** – overgrowth and fertility, civilizations consumed by nature.  
- **Glass Sea** – shared dreams and prophecies.  
- **Crucible** – fire and chaos, ages of conquest.  
- **Null Continuum** – silence, loss of magic, collapse.  
- **Chorus** – story-realization, mythic eras.  
- **Maw** – entropy and ruin, civilization resets.

---

## Culture and Politics

- Knowledge = power, hoarded by angels, guilds, and monarchs.
- High-magic society with low scientific literacy.
- Wars fought for ley-line intersections rather than land.
- Magic-rich cities flourish, but null-zone cultures advance technology and reason.
- The Dominion’s future arrival will nullify magic, forcing an irreversible shift to scientific civilization.
