The Kraft suit is an white/grayish toned enhancement suit that consists of carbon nanotubes and diamond threads. 
The Kraft is a sleek form-fitting suit that is worn under armor. The Kraft also many useful functions such as 
life support 
temperature regulation
energy storage/regulation 
The Kraft is Equipped with a Phase Module Located on the upper back which allows for Combat Armor to be Instantly deployed.

Deep Space Enviro Suit
The Deep Space Enviro (D.S.S) Suit is designed to protect the user in deep space hazards. Such as solar radiation, space debris, temperature and it also helps the user in maintaining a functioning bodily state. It also provides combat support by using adaptive armor.

Deep Space Engineer suit
The Deep space Engineer Suit functions much like the (D.S.S) but is modified to handle extreme hazardous conditions and used during ship maintenance
