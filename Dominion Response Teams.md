# Dominion Response Teams

## Summary

Dominion Response Teams are Federal rapid-response units for disasters, infrastructure failures, epidemics, evacuation, severe weather, habitat breaches, and civilian emergencies.

They are not a secret police force and not a military substitute. Their legitimacy comes from showing up when ordinary people are in danger.

## Mandate

Response Teams handle:

- rescue operations;
- evacuation;
- fire, flood, storm, and habitat failures;
- field medicine;
- quarantine logistics;
- supply drops;
- temporary shelter;
- local government support;
- civilian search operations.

## Powers

They may requisition emergency equipment, control unsafe zones, coordinate local crews, and request military support when civil capacity fails.

## Limits

They cannot conduct criminal interrogations, suppress protests, or convert emergency shelter into detention.

## Public Face

Citizens know them as the crews on the train platform during evacuation, the medics in flooded streets, the engineers sealing a dome breach, and the exhausted volunteers at a shelter desk.

## Story Use

Use Response Teams to show competence and exhaustion. They are where the Dominion's promise of protection becomes a person carrying someone out of smoke.
