1. Common Pegasus

Definition: The baseline—winged horses, pure and noble.

Traits: White coats, feathered wings, swift fliers.

Symbolism: Freedom, purity, divine favor.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. War Pegasi

Definition: Winged steeds trained for battle.

Traits: Armored hides, disciplined flight, serve as mounts for knights or [[Angels]].

Symbolism: Nobility bound to warfare; the divine horse of battle.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Noble Pegasi

Definition: Larger, more intelligent Pegasi often bonded with royalty or heroes.

Traits: Golden manes, intelligent eyes, sometimes speech.

Symbolism: Kingship and destiny carried on wings.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Storm Pegasi

Definition: Pegasi tied to storms and thunder.

Traits: Dark coats, lightning-charged wings, stormrider speed.

Symbolism: The wild fury of the skies; freedom through chaos.

5. Frost Pegasi

Definition: Pegasi of tundras and icy mountains.

Traits: Pale-blue coats, wings rimmed in frost, cold-resistant.

Symbolism: Survival and purity in desolation.

6. Shadow Pegasi

Definition: Corrupted or abyssal winged steeds.

Traits: Black coats, skeletal wings or ghostly forms, ride through nightmares.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]’s steed; purity inverted into terror.

7. Firemane Pegasi

Definition: Pegasi infused with flame or solar power.

Traits: Manes of [[Fire]], radiant wings, burning hooves.

Symbolism: Solar nobility, destruction and renewal in light.

8. Sea Pegasi (Hippocamp-Pegasus Hybrids)

Definition: Winged horses that can dive and swim as well as fly.

Traits: Finned wings, amphibious breath, ocean wanderers.

Symbolism: Mastery of both sea and sky.

9. Spirit Pegasi

Definition: Pegasi tied to souls, dreams, or afterlives.

Traits: Ethereal forms, carry souls to other [[Realms]], ghostlike wings.

Symbolism: The steed as psychopomp; journey beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

10. Arch-Pegasi (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary winged steeds tied to gods, [[Stars]], or creation myths.

Traits: Colossal wingspans, radiant auras, thunderous flight.

Symbolism: The eternal symbol of freedom; sky’s divinity given flesh.
