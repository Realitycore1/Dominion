•	A sentient star, but not a creator god — instead, the mechanism that sustains [[The Nine Rings]].
	•	Polyform: appears differently depending on who observes it (golden sun, black hole, storm of magic, lantern, imperial seal, etc.).
	•	Quantum-Paradoxical: Schrödinger’s Cat writ large — it is all forms at once, collapsing to one when observed.
	•	Conductor Role: each ring is a string vibrating at a mythic frequency; the Starmaker keeps them in harmony and shields them from Noise (discordant chaos beyond).
