Classification: Infernal Constructs & Threshold Beasts
Subtype: Psychopompic Warden / Boundary Enforcer
Status: Active, Bound, Non-Migratory

⸻

Core Description

Cerberus manifests as a massive, multi-headed hound-beast with a body of corded muscle, ash-black hide, and eyes burning like banked coals. Its heads vary by manifestation—commonly three, but sometimes more—each expressing a distinct aspect of vigilance: past, present, and inevitable return.

Its presence induces:
	•	dread without panic,
	•	submission without coercion,
	•	and the overwhelming sense that turning back is impossible.

Cerberus does not chase prey.
It prevents escape.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Cerberus is not a naturally evolved creature. It is a purpose-forged entity, created to enforce boundaries where metaphysical leakage would otherwise occur.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Sheol-Gehenna (Malice, punishment)
	•	Secondary Influence: Echoic Plane (memory of guilt), Death-adjacent resonance
	•	Realm Interface: The Null Shores, Purgatoria, Infernal Gateways

It exists only at thresholds—never deep within a realm, never in open worlds unless the boundary itself has failed.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Cerberus possesses high, specialized intelligence.

It understands:
	•	intent,
	•	guilt,
	•	obligation,
	•	and metaphysical weight.

It does not understand:
	•	mercy,
	•	negotiation,
	•	or persuasion.

Cerberus does not judge innocence.
It judges direction.

If you are meant to pass, it allows you.
If you are meant to remain, it ends the attempt.

⸻

Behavior & Function

Cerberus enforces:
	•	entrances to afterlife realms,
	•	sealed infernal gates,
	•	ancient prisons,
	•	or collapsed planar breaches.

It does not attack indiscriminately.
It reacts only to:
	•	escape attempts,
	•	unauthorized crossings,
	•	threshold violations.

When active, it may:
	•	paralyze intruders with fear,
	•	physically restrain souls or bodies,
	•	annihilate entities attempting forced passage.

Cerberus is tireless.
Time has no meaning to it.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Mortals rarely encounter Cerberus alive.

Those who do experience:
	•	overwhelming compulsion to turn back,
	•	vivid resurfacing of guilt or unfinished acts,
	•	bodily refusal to proceed.

Direct combat is possible but catastrophic.
Defeating Cerberus usually:
	•	destabilizes the boundary it guards,
	•	releases worse entities,
	•	or triggers immediate infernal response.

Outwitting it requires authorization, not strength.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Bound to its assigned threshold
	•	Cannot pursue beyond its jurisdiction
	•	Severely weakened outside Malice-aligned zones
	•	Susceptible to divine writs, psychopomp seals, or rightful passage

Cerberus can be bypassed—but never tricked.

⸻

Variants
	•	Triarch Cerberus: Three heads, classic form
	•	Polycephalic Cerberus: Many-headed, guarding mass prisons
	•	Spectral Cerberus: Soul-only manifestation
	•	Chained Cerberus: Bound to collapsing gates or failing seals

Each head may function semi-independently, coordinating denial of passage.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Reapers / Psychopomps: Operational allies
	•	Demons: Subordinate or adversarial, depending on command hierarchy
	•	Angels: Rarely interact; Grace overrides Cerberus only by writ
	•	Wights & Undead: Actively prevented from improper escape
	•	Mortals: Irrelevant unless crossing boundaries

Cerberus does not serve evil.
It serves containment.
