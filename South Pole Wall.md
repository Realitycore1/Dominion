A massive, wall-like galaxy superstructure mapped just beyond the [[Laniakea]] Supercluster. It’s the largest contiguous feature identified in the local volume via velocity–density reconstructions.  ￼
	•	How it was found: Using Cosmicflows-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] galaxy distances and a Wiener-filter reconstruction of the 3D peculiar-velocity field; the map reveals a filament with a density peak near the celestial south pole.  ￼ ￼
	•	Where/scale: The structure’s core lies roughly 500 million ly away, wrapping around [[Laniakea]] “like an arm,” and spans an arc extending far into the northern sky. Reported total extent is ~1.[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] billion ly, with a ~220 Mpc rectilinear segment before it curves.  ￼ ￼ ￼
	•	Context: Comparable in scale to the [[Sloan Great wall]] but closer to us; parts are obscured by the Milky Way’s Zone of Avoidance, so its full extent remains uncertain pending deeper surveys
