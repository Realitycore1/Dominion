Specialize in subversion, sabotage, and annihilation — bypassing defenses, striking from darkness, leaving nothing but [[Silence]].

Recruitment drawn from the broken and damned — criminals, madmen, near-dead or resurrected thralls, molded into one seething mass.

Nature:

Cursed with blood-rage echoes, a frenzy restrained only by iron discipline.

Officers act as handlers, unleashing them like chained [[Leviathans]] upon the enemy.

Symbolism:

Banners of abyssal vortices, riven [[Chains]], and the eye of [[The Void]].

Fleets described as [[Leviathans]] in darkness, slipping silently through [[The Void]] until they strike.

Reputation:

Feared by foes and distrusted even by allies.

Whispers claim they sometimes “turn inward,” feeding the [[Abyss]] with their own kin.

Motto:

“From the [[Depths]] we rise, to drag all into [[Silence]].”
