## Summary

Symbolic: A
Core Features


![[Pasted image 20
250830161910.png]]
Mooncraft are weaponized moons transformed into defensive [[_[[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]]nlike [[Fortress Worlds]] (planetary-scale redoubts), Mooncraft serve as mobile citadel-wards for VIP targets — Empress reliquaries, StarForges, or [[Key]] Spiralverse nodes. Each Mooncraft is both guardian and executioner, its surface remade into weapons arrays, shield grids, and Concordia harmonic projectors.
Structure

Orbital Mass: Built from [[Natural]] moons or [[Artificial]] lunar-scale bodies.

Surface Saturation: Turrets, mass drivers, and energy lances embedded across the crust.

Shield Grids: Entire hemispheres layered with harmonic projectors, designed to absorb or redirect orbital [[Fire]].

Vaulted Core: Housing command sanctums, armada bays, and reliquary chambers.

Functions

Defense: Guarding high-value targets ([[World Stations]], StarForges, Empress Shrines).

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Capable of intercepting fleets with planetary-scale firepower.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Citizens see Mooncraft as protectors — celestial shields forever circling their homes.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Serve as armored escorts for [[_[[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]]nsuring that no enemy can strike the empire’s crown jewels.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Lunar surfaces carved into fortress walls, spires, and ribbed vaults.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they appear as black, geometric moons etched with golden [[Fire]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Weapon-[[Fire]] arcs and shield harmonics form fractal patterns across their surfaces.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyph-light inscriptions circle their equators, burning scripture into orbit.

From orbit, a Mooncraft looks like a scarred, fortress moon, bristling with golden-lit defenses, endlessly circling its charge like a bodyguard of stone and [[Fire]].
 visible reminder that even “minor” moons are reborn as cathedrals of war under [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] hands.

Religious: Called the “Amber [[Sentinels]]” — myths say they are guardian-[[Angels]] forged from moons, always watching, never sleeping.
