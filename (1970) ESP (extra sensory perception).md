1. Telepathy (thought reading)

In reality, this maps onto:

✔ Microexpression analysis

People detect tiny facial contractions subconsciously.

✔ Tone + breathing pattern reading

Humans can read emotional state changes in under 50 milliseconds.

✔ Pattern-based prediction

The brain constantly forecasts what someone will say or do next.

✔ Mirror neuron engagement

You model the internal state of the person you’re observing.

People who claim “telepathy” usually have extraordinarily sharp unconscious pattern models.

No magic necessary.

⸻

2. Clairvoyance (knowing information you “shouldn’t”)

This is:

✔ Thin-slice cognition

Judgments made in 1–3 seconds based on micro-level cues.

✔ Hypervigilance

Common in trauma survivors; they pick up “signals” others filter out.

✔ Bayesian inference

Your brain is a prediction machine—it fills in missing data and is often right.

✔ Memory + intuition fusion

Your brain retrieves similar past situations and produces an answer instantly.

The result feels like “I just knew.”

⸻

3. Precognition (seeing the future)

Your brain constantly simulates upcoming events.
Most don’t realize how aggressively predictive human cognition is.

✔ temporal processing

The brain works with ~80–120 millisecond prediction windows because raw sensory input arrives late.

✔ pattern forecasting

It notices trajectories, trends, microbehaviors.

✔ subconscious risk maps

You often sense danger before consciously recognizing it.

Precognition = the predictive brain functioning outside conscious awareness.

⸻

4. Psychometry (touch an object, get “information”)

This maps to:

✔ tactile recall

Touch triggers stored memories and associative networks.

✔ detail noticing

Wear patterns, temperature, weight, chemical residue.

✔ olfactory + microtexture data

Humans pick up more than we consciously report.

✔ emotional projection

Your brain generates meaning from subtle cues.

It feels like “the object is speaking,” but really it’s your processing engine unpacking data rapidly.

⸻

5. Empathy / Emotional ESP

The most common “ESP” is simply having:
	•	high interoception
	•	strong emotional inference capacity
	•	mirror neuron sensitivity
	•	trauma-tuned environmental scanning
	•	hyper-awareness of body language

People with this ability often read a room instantly.
In some, it borders on painful—like “feeling” the tension before anyone speaks.

It’s not magic.
It’s a high-resolution social radar.

⸻

6. Remote Viewing

Typically explained by:

✔ peripheral awareness

Sensing subtle vibrations, airflow changes, or background noise.

✔ cognitive mapping

Your brain builds spatial models automatically.

✔ unstated environmental memory

You unconsciously recall sensory details you didn’t actively focus on.

This gives the illusion of “knowing what’s behind you.”
