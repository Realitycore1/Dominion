# 🏙️ Craterheart Colony
**[[Colonial Union]] Settlement Type**: Hybrid Crash-[[Origin]] + Traditional Vertical Hive

---

## 🌌 Overview
Craterheart Colony is the first permanent extrasolar settlement founded by the Solar peoples. It is born from the wreckage of the ark ship *C.U.C. Deliverance* after the first interstellar mission reaches the selected Proxima colony world in 3608.

Damage accumulated during the voyage compromises deceleration. The crew avoids total loss through a controlled atmospheric descent and crash. The surviving colonists salvage the ark into a foundational industrial and civic zone.

Over decades, a structured upper city grows above it, giving rise to a dual-tiered settlement — part relic, part beacon of survival.

## Canonical Founding

- The *Deliverance* departs Pluto-Charon in 3600.
- Neptunian-produced antimatter and Uranian containment engineering support the drive.
- The mission carries human, uplifted, and synthetic colonists.
- The controlled crash in 3608 creates the colony's defining crater.
- The wreck becomes shelter, clinic, power plant, fabrication district, archive, and assembly hall.
- Craterheart becomes the founding settlement and emergency capital of the Proxima Commonwealth.

---

## 🧱 Structure

### 🌀 The Craterheart Hive (Lower Zone)
- **Location**: Within and around the crash site crater.
- **Functions**: Salvage operations, Deepcore mining, FABRIC plants, emergency infrastructure.
- **Key Facilities**:
  - MedSpire Pods
  - LEAF vertical farms
  - ArcCell capacitors
  - V.E.I.N. cable webbing
- **Culture**: Rugged, survivalist, familial — the "Descendants of the Womb."

### 🕊️ The Ascendant Ring (Upper City)
- **Location**: Constructed on raised platforms, towers, and monolithic supports above the crater.
- **Functions**: Government, diplomacy, cultural preservation, education.
- **Key Facilities**:
  - O.R.A.C.L.E. Tower
  - Archive Temple
  - Domus Civic Hub
  - S.O.L. Towers
- **Culture**: Hierarchical, ceremonial, aspirational — the "Stewards of the Flame."

---

## ⚡ Energy Infrastructure

- **V.E.I.N. Arrays**: Buried cabling around and under the crater to power fabrication and deep-industrial tools.
- **P.A.T.H. Spires**: Wireless energy emitters installed atop remaining ark pylons.
- **A.N.K.H. Nodes**: Smart switch relays coordinating energy demand between tiers.
- **S.O.L. Towers**: Solar overcharge stations ringing the upper city.

---

## 🔐 Defense Systems

- **VigilNet perimeter**: SentinelFrame turrets positioned along crater rim and upper walls.
- **Aether Drones**: Atmospheric recon and fauna tracking units.
- **Cerberus Bunker**: Deep vault in the wreck housing command protocols and gene archives.

---

## 🧠 Cultural Identity

- **Motto**: *“From Ruin, Order.”*
- **Holidays**:
  - Day of Landing (Crash remembrance)
  - Ember Rising (Celebration of Ascension Day when upper city was completed)
- **Rituals**:
  - Light Offering to the O.R.A.C.L.E.
  - Naming ceremonies held in the Archive Temple.

---

## 🧭 Strategic Notes

- Crater limits expansion, but provides [[Natural]] fortification.
- Salvaged tech jumpstarts development beyond standard colony kits.
- Strong social dichotomy between surface-dwellers and deepborn clans.
