•	Nature: Stabilized enclaves ruled by powerful guilds or [[Entities]] who have bent the City’s flux to their will.
	•	Feel: Palatial zones, each with its own [[Physics]] and laws: one district where sound replaces speech, another where light itself serves as currency.
	•	Function: Centers of authority. These Courts feud, negotiate, and sometimes collapse, giving the City its shifting “[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]].”
	•	Story Hook: The Silent Wardens (mysterious enforcers) may dwell here, stepping in when contradictions spiral too far.
