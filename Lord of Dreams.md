# Lord of Dreams

The Lord of Dreams is the sovereign custodian of the Oneiric Plane.

Its authority extends over:

- the Citadel of Dreams;
- the Council of Archetypes;
- Oneiric boundaries;
- dream manifestations that cross into shared space;
- incursions into the Material Continuum;
- disputes between archetypal domains;
- containment of self-propagating or invasive dream phenomena.

Its authority does not extend to ownership of dreams.

Dreams belong to the consciousnesses that generate them.

The Lord may observe, classify, redirect, quarantine, or prevent a dream from imposing itself upon external reality, but it cannot rightfully claim the dream itself as property.
