I. Founding (3420 – 3520 CE)

The Sudhakar expedition was conceived as a relief-agriculture colony meant to supply staple foods and genetic seed material to nearby industrial [[Systems]] such as [[Star Union]] and Kuroda.
Its colonists—agrarian cooperatives, displaced miners, and surplus labor contingents from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s recovery zones—arrived with minimal automation and little [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] oversight.

The early settlement established the First Cooperative Republic of Sudhakar Prime, promising democratic councils for every district.
C.U. oversight was light; the colony was designated a “nutritional reserve world,” dependent on external logistics for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Military/Technology|Technology]] and manufactured goods.

Population ≈ 9 million.

Governance = local [[Worker]] councils with seasonal congresses.

II. The Green Republics (3520 – 3750 CE)

As other [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] industrialized, Sudhakar’s role stagnated.
Famine cycles forced migration between dome belts; each cluster evolved into a self-declared [[Worker]] Republic managing its own crops and tugs.
To coordinate aid shipments, the republics signed the Sudhakar Compact (3621 CE)—a loose federation guaranteeing food-credit exchange and shared defense.

[[Infrastructure]] remained basic:

Greenhouse Belts around equatorial plains.

Slow Tugs linking orbiting silos.

Low-power beamers used mainly for data and emergency signaling.

Aid from Kuroda and [[Star Union]] kept the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] afloat, cementing its reputation as the breadbasket that feeds but never feasts.

III. Economic Decline and Dependence (3750 – 3920 CE)

The rise of synthetic-protein factories in richer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] undercut Sudhakar’s export prices.
By 3800 CE, the States survived primarily through seed-vault leasing—charging for genetic preservation and sample replication.
C.U. relief convoys arrived sporadically; when they stopped entirely during the Equilon buildup, local councils turned inward.

Militias formed not against [[Invasion]] but corruption: every republic maintained Anti-Corruption Brigades to audit grain quotas and punish hoarding.
Their motto—“The Storehouse Belongs to All”—became both [[law]] and prayer.

IV. The Tariff Crisis and Year 4000 CE

When the [[Star Union]] tariff spike (3984 CE) raised the cost of imported machinery and fertilizers, food-credit inflation triggered riots across the dome cities.
Several republics defaulted on inter-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] loans; grain convoys were seized by local militias.

The Recresh Mediation Mission arrived in 3991 CE under mandate from the [[Proxima Commonwealth]], brokering emergency credit relief and partial tariff suspension.
Though open warfare was avoided, the event left deep resentment toward industrial powers—especially Estela and [[Star Union]] financiers.

By 4000 CE:

Population ≈ 55 million.

[[Infrastructure]]: aging domes, slow-tugs, under-powered beamers.

Security: local militias; Anti-Corruption Brigades act as both auditors and constables.

Political cohesion: fragile but functional through annual Harvest Congress.
