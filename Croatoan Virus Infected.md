CROATOAN VIRUS

Classification

Hybrid Pathogen / Memetic Curse / Post-Biological Plague

Status

Active (Dormant Variants Exist)

⸻

CORE CONCEPT

The Croatoan Virus is not a traditional virus.

It is:
	•	Biological enough to spread through blood, saliva, aerosols
	•	Cognitive enough to rewrite behavior
	•	Metaphysical enough to persist after death
	•	Cultural enough to erase societies, not just individuals

It doesn’t just kill.

It empties.

⸻

ORIGIN (INTENTIONALLY UNCERTAIN)

Competing theories exist — all partially correct:
	1.	Failed Dominion-era bioweapon designed to erase hostile populations without leaving usable remains
	2.	Malice-adjacent contagion born from mass starvation, cannibalism, and ritual collapse
	3.	Echo of a vanished civilization — the virus is what’s left of them
	4.	A counter-Grace organism, designed to remove divine influence by deleting identity

No definitive answer survives.

Every archive referencing its origin ends abruptly.

⸻

TRANSMISSION VECTORS

The Croatoan Virus spreads through overlap, not proximity alone:
	•	Bodily fluids
	•	Consuming infected tissue
	•	Prolonged exposure to infected environments
	•	Psychological proximity (living among the infected long enough)
	•	Shared shelters, ration systems, communal eating

It thrives in:
	•	siege cities
	•	refugee camps
	•	quarantined zones
	•	ashlands
	•	abandoned urban districts

It spreads best where people depend on each other.

⸻

INFECTION STAGES

Stage I — The Hollowing
	•	Fatigue
	•	Memory gaps
	•	Emotional flattening
	•	Reduced pain response
	•	Loss of personal habits

Victims are still themselves — but less.

⸻

Stage II — The Silence
	•	Speech degradation (short phrases, then silence)
	•	Eye focus becomes fixed and distant
	•	Appetite becomes mechanical
	•	Sleep cycles break down

At this point, names stop mattering.

⸻

Stage III — The Infected

This is where monsters are born.

⸻

THE CROATOAN INFECTED

Physical Traits
	•	Skin pale, greyed, or waxy
	•	Veins visible, darkened
	•	Muscle tone preserved unnaturally
	•	Eyes cloudy or blackened
	•	Mouth slack unless feeding
	•	Bodies continue functioning despite trauma

They do not rot properly.
They do not heal properly.
They persist.

⸻

Behavior
	•	Slow, deliberate movement
	•	Operate in loose, silent groups
	•	No vocalizations except breath or feeding sounds
	•	Ignore pain, cold, fire unless fully destroyed
	•	Do not chase — they advance

They do not hunt individuals.
They consume areas.

⸻

Intelligence

Low individual cognition.
High collective pattern intelligence.

They:
	•	Block exits
	•	Funnel prey
	•	Learn safe routes
	•	Avoid hazards after first exposure

Not zombies.
Erase-and-replace organisms.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL PROPERTIES

This is where Croatoan becomes truly dangerous.
	•	Grace rejection: angelic influence weakens around large clusters
	•	Malice neutral: demons gain nothing from them
	•	Soul attenuation: victims do not pass cleanly into afterlife
	•	Faith decay: holy symbols lose efficacy over time

Croatoan doesn’t corrupt souls.

It disconnects them.

⸻

WHY CROATOAN IS TERRIFYING
	•	No rage
	•	No hunger frenzy
	•	No sadism
	•	No individuality

Just inevitable replacement.

Cities fall not in screams — but in silence.

⸻

VARIANTS

Urban Croatoan
	•	Found in subways, towers, arcologies
	•	Uses vertical movement
	•	Forms human-shaped barricades

Ashland Croatoan
	•	Hardened skin
	•	Slow, inexorable
	•	Resistant to heat and particulate storms

Hybrid Croatoan

When layered with:
	•	Beast Rot
	•	Vampirism
	•	Wiendigo curse

Results in unpredictable apex failures — often quarantined zones permanently sealed.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Catastrophic / Existential

Croatoan outbreaks don’t end.

They are:
	•	burned out
	•	buried
	•	sealed
	•	forgotten

Until someone opens the wrong door.

⸻

THEMATIC ROLE

Croatoan represents:
	•	the fear of being forgotten
	•	the horror of survival without identity
	•	civilization hollowed from the inside
	•	extinction without spectacle

Where zombies are loud,
Croatoan is quiet annihilation.
