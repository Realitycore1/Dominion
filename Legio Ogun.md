## **XIV. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Ogun**

**Title:** Legio Ogun – The Ironbreakers

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through indomitable strength, endurance, and mastery of war-forging. Legio Ogun embody the unyielding will of iron and steel, breaking enemies through raw power and relentless advance. Their creed: *“By iron, all is made and unmade.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Ogun**

* **Silhouette:** Massive and broad, armor bulked for resilience and heavy melee engagement. Plates hammered with forge-like textures, resembling cast metal straight from the anvil.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit sparks, molten embers, and metallic resonance—each step resounds like hammer upon steel.
* **Coloration:** Deep iron-black with glowing red-orange seams, as though the armor contains a living forge. Trimmed with bronze and steel-gray highlights.
* **Helmet:** Angular and heavy, shaped like a blacksmith’s mask reinforced with war-crest ridges. Eye-slits glow molten red like smoldering coals.
* **Specialization:** Forge Cores—reactors that convert battlefield energy into raw kinetic reinforcement, granting immense durability and melee strength. Can project localized seismic shockwaves through the ground.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Ogun Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Heavy Rifle and [[Close]]-quarters Carbine forms, often combined with melee attachments such as chain-blades or forge-hammers.
* **Modification:** Weapons reinforced with dense plating, some integrated with heat induction [[Systems]] that allow them to superheat ammunition or plasma channels.
* **Aesthetic:** Brutal, industrial look with heavy rivets, forged-metal etchings, and glowing ember seams.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Frontline juggernauts and siege-breakers. Ogun excel at grinding assaults and breaking fortified positions.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their advance resembles the march of living forges—foes hear the ring of hammers and see sparks raining before the clash.
* **Legacy:** Legio Ogun is remembered as the Ironbreakers, the unyielding anvil upon which the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s enemies are shattered.

LEGION OGUN — The Iron Forgemasters

Classification: Industrial War-Foundries • Siege Engineering
Status: Self-annihilated to prevent capture

Profile

Ogun were living factories. They built weapons mid-battle, converted cities into foundries, and forged entire armored divisions from raw crust. Their suits bore hammer sigils.

Final Fate

Ogun triggered their own starforge detonation to prevent Dracoshian capture.
