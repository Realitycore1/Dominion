## Summary

To citizens
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Launch Loop is a planet-to-orbit mass driver, essentially a [[Giant]] ribbon (thousands of kilometers long, ~80 km high at midpoint) carrying a stream of magnetically accelerated rotor elements. The momentum of the loop supports its own arch in the atmosphere, forming a permanent skybridge that can launch cargo or ships into orbit without [[Rockets]].
Structure

The Ribbon: Thousands of kilometers long, stabilized by counter-rotating streams of magnetized elements.

Anchor Stations: Colossal ground arcologies at each end, acting as counterweights and control hubs.

Midpoint Spine: Suspended ~80–200 km above the planet, serving as the launch platform.

Mechanics

Cargo attaches to the moving ribbon via mag-clamps.

Magnetic fields accelerate payloads along the loop until they achieve orbital velocity.

Loop constantly maintained by Autonomous MRO ecologies crawling along its length.

Power & Integration

Fed by [[Deep-Well Geo-Taps]], Cornucopia Machines, or Kugelblitz Plants.

Directly linked to [[Clarke Orbit Cities]] or [[Halo Habs]], serving as dedicated lifelines.

Can be paired with Beam Corridors — payloads leave the loop and ride light into deep space.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Cheap, endless access to orbit — moves people, goods, and whole modular habitats.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Launches fleets, drones, and kinetic weapons directly into orbit or beyond.

Economic: Acts as planetary arteries — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] with loops become major trade and industry hubs.

Religious: Known as “The Sky Ladders” — mythologized as literal bridges the Empress lays between [[Earth (Terra)]] and heaven.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Anchor stations like ribbed black cathedrals, hurling payloads skyward.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the loop appears as a seamless obsidian arc stretching from horizon to horizon.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Mid-loop platforms ripple with fractal geometry, curving like stained-glass ribbons through the sky.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The loop glows with inscriptions as cargo launches — fiery glyphs racing up the sky-ribbon.

From the ground, a Launch Loop looks like a black bridge of steel and [[Fire]] climbing to the heavens; at night, its path glows golden as payloads scream upward.

Narrative Function

To rebels: A chokehold — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] controls the loop, and therefore all access to space.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A tool of order — predictable, infinite transport arteries.

To myth: Called the “Amber Ladders”, imagined as the Empress’s hands reaching down to pull her people skyward.
: A symbol of prosperity and access — “our world is crowned by a ladder of [[Fire]].”
