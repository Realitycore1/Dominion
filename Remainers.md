The Remainers are a group of people who believe that the best course of actions is to stay within the [[Colonial Union]]. Over [[Time]] as the Equilon left the [[Colonial Union]] the group became more extreme in its beliefs until they were routed out and eliminated by the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]].
