## Summary

Mythic: Eac
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Gravitational-wave beacons are engineered spacetime transmitters, built to project encoded messages or signals via ripples in gravity itself. Unlike radio or light, grav-waves pass unhindered through [[Stars]], planets, and dust — meaning these beacons are unblockable, cosmological-scale lighthouses.
Structure

Mass Oscillators: Pairs or triplets of [[Artificial]] black holes, neutronium spheres, or strange-matter nodes rotated at relativistic speeds.

Anchor Arrays: [[Atlas Pillars]] or [[Dyson Fortress]] struts stabilizing oscillators.

Harmonic Coders: Concordia-field modulators imprinting glyph-patterns onto waveforms.

Signal Dynamics

Range: Cross-galactic, even cross-[[Verse]] (detectors tuned to Spiralverse harmonics can hear them).

Encoding: Binary, fractal glyphs, or even full Concordia hymns embedded as resonance.

Synchronization: Beacons can sync with Hyper-Interferometers, creating two-way grav-wave communication.

Power

Fueled by [[Deep-Well Geo-Taps]], Cornucopia fusion arrays, or directly by Dynamic Core Ignitors manipulating [[Stars]].

Efficiency increases when integrated into Galactic Radio Arrays, blending EM and gravity messaging.

Civil: Provide instantaneous, galaxy-spanning communication networks — the backbone of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] coordination.

Religious: Seen as “the Tongues of Amber” — the Empress herself speaking through the bones of the cosmos.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Oscillator towers shaped like black spires, colliding masses circling them like chained [[Angels]].

Suprematist Monolithism: To the eye, just a silent black tower floating in void — but it shakes creation itself.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Waveforms rendered as living mandalas, encoded into geometry itself.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Beacon pulses glow as golden glyphs through local spacetime distortions — scripture vibrating through [[(2030) Existence]].

To normal observers, a beacon looks inert; to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] eyes, it’s a star-sized bell, tolling across the universe.
