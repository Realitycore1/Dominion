Definition
Illusion Magic manipulates perception — sight, sound, thought, or even deeper senses of truth. It doesn’t alter matter directly, but it warps experience of it, convincing minds and sometimes worlds that something is other than it is.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Perception Precedes Reality – if the senses are fooled, reality follows belief.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Fragility of the Mind – illusions exploit assumptions and shortcuts the mind makes.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Anchoring – powerful illusions require narrative or emotional anchors to persist.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Duality – illusion is both mask and lens: hiding one thing, showing another.

⸻

Techniques of Illusion
	•	Glamour – beautification, disguises, changing appearances.
	•	Phantasm – conjuring images, sounds, or sensations.
	•	Veilcraft – cloaking objects or people from sight or memory.
	•	Hallucination Weaving – embedding false perceptions directly in the mind.
	•	Mirror Worlds – creating full environments that only exist as perception.
	•	Existential Illusion – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-tier: crafting false realities that become self-sustaining until collapsed.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: Blood mages veil their cults behind glamours, hiding rituals and corruptions from elven authorities. Some elven nobles use illusions for courtly intrigue as much as war.
	•	Concordia: Illusion guilds flourish; Platina-tier mages weave dreamscapes for diplomacy, trial, or assassination. Mythic-level illusions become indistinguishable from creation itself.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Illusion weaponized through Memetic Cloaks and Reality Masks, disguising fleets or entire worlds. Illusion merges with Mythopoeia in cognitive warfare.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Harmonists disdain trickery, but use illusions in art, [[Ritual]], and diplomacy to create shared experiences that unify rather than deceive.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Demiurgic illusions twist despair: cities that see lightless suns, people convinced their neighbors are [[Demons]] — illusions used to warp societies into madness.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Cheap Power – easier than altering reality itself.
	•	Utility – valuable in war, politics, and art alike.
	•	Psychological Force – fear of what might be real is often more effective than brute force.
	•	Flexibility – illusions can mask, distract, or inspire.

⸻

Perils
	•	Fragility – illusions collapse when disbelieved.
	•	Backlash – deep illusions may trap the caster too.
	•	Addiction – entire societies can prefer illusions over truth.
	•	Blending – strong illusions can blur into Mythopoeia, creating half-real horrors.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Truthsight – Revelation arts that pierce deception.
	•	Wards of Clarity – anchoring reality against false overlays.
	•	Skepticism – a doubting mind is resistant to glamour.
	•	Entropy Tearing – chaotic energies that destabilize illusion-weaves.

•	[[Revelation magic]] – illusions resist and are undone by revelation.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – strong illusions may harden into myth.
	•	[[Binding magic]] – veils as protective bindings.
	•	[[Domination magic]] – illusions of fear or trust compel obedience.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – illusions indistinguishable from conjurations.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – existential illusions rewrite reality’s [[Axioms]].
