Type: Relic Recovery & Archaeotech Conglomerate
Founded: 2037, Geneva (initially as a UN Cultural Heritage subsidiary)
Motto: “We restore what was promised.”

Origins

After the Ethiopian Ark Incident, global interest in “hyper-archaeological assets” exploded.
LXI was created to manage excavation [[Rights]], coordinate safety protocols, and prevent black-market looting.
Within a decade, they controlled nearly every active dig involving anomalous [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], ancient power sites, or quantum-archaeological signatures.

Structure
	•	Divisions:
	•	Cultural Assets Security (CAS) – field operations and artifact retrieval.
	•	Theological Verification Unit (TVU) – authenticates artifacts via AI spectral analysis.
	•	Subsurface Analytics Division (SAD) – uses quantum resonance scanners to locate “non-terrestrial isotopic anomalies.”
	•	Leadership: Director-General Solenne Ma’at, former UNESCO chair turned cultural pragmatist.

Influence
	•	Brokered excavation agreements with the [[Yamama Caliphate]]EOSMU[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Faction Profile/Alkebulan Union/Alkebulan Union|Alkebulan Union]]/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Pre-WW3/Faction Profile/Alkebulan Union/Alkebulan Union|Alkebulan Union]].
	•	Created the first Heritage Futures Exchange—a derivatives market trading ownership shares of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] not yet recovered.
	•	Lobbies the Directorate to classify [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] as “non-strategic cultural mass” (thereby exempt from disarmament treaties).

Reputation

To governments: indispensable experts.
To the public: spiritual profiteers.
They make headlines whenever another “divine object” is unearthed and disappear when it triggers violence.
Rumors persist that LXI deliberately leaks coordinates to PMCs like Aegis to create recovery opportunities.
