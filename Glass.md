Core Bias:

Overexposure and vitrified structure

This subtype forms when reality has been subjected to such intensity that it becomes:
	•	hardened
	•	transparent
	•	brittle
	•	and permanently altered

Traits:
	•	exposed and fused landscapes
	•	hyper-clarity with low resilience
	•	polished ruin conditions
	•	structural memory locked in place

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	fused wastelands
	•	burnt geometries
	•	irradiated or heat-scoured realities
	•	reality that has been “cooked”

Common Expressions:
	•	post-cataclysm verses
	•	ancient impact zones
	•	thermally scarred cosmologies
	•	high-energy disaster remnants

Failure Mode:

Brittle permanence
The basin survives only as locked ruin.

This is a very good subtype for:
	•	post-war megaverses
	•	weapon-scarred realities
	•	ancient cosmic gravefields
