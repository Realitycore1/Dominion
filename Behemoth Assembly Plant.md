## Details

A [[Planetcraft]] Assembly Plant is a 
large-scale facility that is designed to produce and construct entire planets, or "planetcrafts," from raw materials and resources. The [[Planetcraft]] Assembly Plant may be a highly automated facility that is capable of constructing planetary bodies of various sizes and types, including terrestrial worlds, gas giants, asteroids, and even entire moons. The plant may use a variety of advanced technologies, such as molecular fabrication, 3D printing, and automated robotic [[Systems]], to construct the various components and structures that make up the planet. The raw materials used to construct the planet may come from a variety of sources, including asteroid belts, nebulae, or even interstellar gas clouds. The plant may use sophisticated mining equipment to extract these materials and process them into the raw building blocks of the planet. Once the planetary body is constructed, it may be sent to its designated orbit or location in space, where it can be further outfitted with atmosphere, biospheres, and other features to support life or other forms of activity. The [[Planetcraft]] Assembly Plant may be used for a variety of purposes, from creating habitable worlds for colonization or terraforming efforts to developing new resources or habitats for industry or scientific research.
