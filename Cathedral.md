Core Bias:

Vertical sacred complexity

This is where growth becomes monumental, layered, and almost architectural.

Think less “trees” and more:

living hierarchies that grow upward into symbolic order

Traits:
	•	towering structures
	•	layered verticality
	•	sacred geometry in growth
	•	ecological stratification
	•	naturally emergent monumental systems

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	infinite canopies
	•	world-trees
	•	living pillars
	•	sacred towers grown rather than built

Common Expressions:
	•	living megastructures
	•	axis mundi worlds
	•	hierarchical biospheres
	•	towering cosmological organisms

Failure Mode:

Stratified rigidity
Upper layers dominate and choke lower growth.

This is great for:
	•	mythic cosmology
	•	world-tree structures
	•	Dominion-scale “grown but monumental” realities
