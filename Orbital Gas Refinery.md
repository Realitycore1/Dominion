## Details

An Orbital Gas Refinery is a type 
of space station that is used to process and refine raw gases or minerals into usable fuel or other valuable products. Orbital Gas Refineries are typically located in low [[Earth (Terra)]] orbit or other stable orbitals around a planet or moon, where they can benefit from the availability of raw materials such as hydrogen, oxygen, and methane. These gases are often extracted from [[Natural]] gas deposits on planets or moons, and then transported to the orbital refinery for further processing. The process of refining raw gases involves separating out the different components of the gas mixture, using advanced separation techniques such as fractional distillation or membrane [[Technology Portal|technology]]. The separated components can then be used as fuel for spacecraft, or as raw materials for other industries such as chemical [[Production]] or construction. Orbital Gas Refineries are an important component of interplanetary and interstellar transportation, as they provide a means of converting [[Natural]] resources into usable fuels that can power spacecraft and other vehicles. They are also useful for supporting larger space stations or habitats, as they can provide the raw materials necessary for manufacturing, building, and maintaining the [[Infrastructure]] of space-based settlements. The design of an Orbital Gas Refinery would depend on the specific raw materials being processed, as well as any existing regulations or safety protocols that need to be followed.
