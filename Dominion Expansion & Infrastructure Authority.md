# Dominion Expansion & Infrastructure Authority

## Summary

The Dominion Expansion & Infrastructure Authority plans and coordinates large-scale civilian expansion: new cities, orbital elevators, megastructure corridors, settlement grids, energy trunks, planetary development, and long-range infrastructure programs.

It builds the skeleton that other agencies inhabit.

## Mandate

The Authority oversees:

- settlement grid planning;
- orbital elevator coordination;
- megastructure logistics;
- planetary development corridors;
- energy trunk lines;
- strategic construction labor;
- frontier infrastructure standards;
- large civil works that cross provincial lines.

## Powers

It may certify major projects, coordinate multi-province construction, allocate federal construction priority, and pause projects that threaten safety or public finance.

## Limits

The Authority may not bulldoze local compacts, conceal ecological risk, displace communities without compensation, or launder Royal continuity projects through civilian budgets.

## Public Face

Citizens encounter the Authority through construction zones, relocation notices, new transit maps, housing expansions, orbital-elevator referendums, and jobs that last for generations.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show ambition at human scale: workers building impossible things while arguing over rent, pay, school capacity, and whether the future is worth the disruption.
