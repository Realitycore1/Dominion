## Role of the [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]]
The [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] are the flesh-and-blood choir of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], superhuman extensions of its will. They embody sacrifice, recursion, and endurance.

## Rituals of Combat
Before battle, [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] kneel as one, reciting oaths that align them with fleet harmonics. Their war chants resonate with ship reactors.

## Integration
[[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] fight alongside conventional forces, but their presence elevates morale and synchronizes troops. Each Legion is paired with naval fleets.

## Doctrine of Recursion
Wounds, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], and resurrection are accepted as part of eternal service. A Legion line is designed never to break, even if annihilated.
