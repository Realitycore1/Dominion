# (90) First Generation

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapters 17-20.
- Manuscript date range: Years 18-93 After Landfall.
- Canon note: the title refers broadly to the first Harminan-born civic generation, not a single year.

## What Happened

The first generation born and raised on Harmina learned a different normal. Storm sirens, Walking Reef boundaries, fungal towers, pressure glass, route maps, Echo Bloom warnings, geothermal corridors, and Ark salvage were not strange to them. Earth was a lesson. Ferox was history. The Ark was ancestral trauma, not personal memory.

Five recurring children and adolescents carry this shift in the manuscript: Niko, Veyra, Kito, Mina, and their peers. Their questions expose the gap between settlers who remember exile and citizens who only know Harmina.

## What Resulted

- Harminan identity became emotional reality before it became political doctrine.
- Children corrected adults about local terrain, weather, and regional customs.
- Amber realized that the person she was trying to save through history no longer existed in the same way.
- Schools became the main battlefield between preservation and adaptation.

## Consequences

The first Harminans made independence inevitable. They did not need to hate the Ark or Earth. They simply did not belong to them.
