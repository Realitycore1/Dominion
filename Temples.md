Their architecture and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures|Megastructures]] are designed to embody faith, enforce caste, and channel metaphysical power.

1. Urban & Planetary

Draconic Geometry Cities: Streets spiral like coiled spines; every city doubles as a [[Ritual]] diagram.

Caste Districts: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Draconid/Draconid]] barracks, Aldar [[Citadels]], Oracle temples. Clear hierarchies built into stone.

Possession Sanctuaries: Vast arenas where Draconids undergo bonding rituals. Survivors emerge as champions; failures are sacrificed to [[The Maw]].

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Orbital & Systemic

Resonance Lattices: Exotic orbital arrays that hum with acausal frequencies, used for prophecy and disruption of enemy fleets.

Mirror [[Choirs]]: Reflective [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures|Megastructures]] around [[The Maw]], designed to bend light and causality — equal parts weapon and shrine.

Bloodflow Rails: Energy distribution lines running like glowing veins through the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], symbolically called “the veins of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]].”

Resonance Dreadnought Yards: Shipyards where hulls are “sung into being” by Oracles, layering metaphysical bindings into every vessel.

[[The Maw]] as [[Infrastructure]]:

Oracle chambers project prophecies directly from its[[Infrastructure]]platforms drop heretics into its [[Depths]].

Reactors draw exotic energy from its turbulence.

Sacrifice Engines: Devices that convert [[Ritual]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] (burning mind, blood, or soul) into raw energy. Common in fortresses and fleets.

Memory Obelisks: [[Giant]] crystal pillars where histories, myths, and decrees are etched. Lit from within, they glow as propaganda beacons visible from orbit.

5. Cultural [[Infrastructure]]

Choral Halls: Cathedrals where myths are recited as polyphonic chants, reinforcing [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]].

Procession Routes: Roads, orbital paths, and even fleet routes that echo the “coil of the [[Dragon]]”, reinforcing cosmic order through motion.

Communal Vaults: Storehouses where goods are redistributed. Every vault is also a temple, reminding citizens that all belongs to the Host.
