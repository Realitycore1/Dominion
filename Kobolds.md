Domovoi

Classification

Domestic Spirits / Subterranean Tricksters / Threshold Sentients

⸻

CORE IDEA

Kobolds and Domovoi are maintenance spirits—entities born from long-term habitation, labor, and routine. They are not gods, not monsters, and not parasites. They are the intelligence of a place that has been lived in long enough to develop opinions.

They don’t want worship.
They want things done properly.

⸻

ORIGIN

These beings emerge where:
	•	families persist for generations
	•	mines are worked continuously
	•	workshops, forges, or homes develop ritual routines
	•	spaces are respected, repaired, and argued over

A house gains habits → habits gain memory → memory gains awareness.

Kobolds favor labor spaces.
Domovoi favor homes and hearths.

They are cousins, not duplicates.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Kobolds
	•	Small, hunched humanoids (0.6–1.2 m)
	•	Earth-toned skin: clay, ash, rust, stone
	•	Elongated noses, sharp teeth, bright eyes
	•	Often carry tools, keys, or mining implements
	•	Move quickly, skittering through tunnels and crawlspaces

Domovoi
	•	Short, thick-bodied figures
	•	Covered in coarse hair or soot
	•	Faces human-like but exaggerated
	•	Eyes reflective like embers in low light
	•	Often appear seated near hearths, ovens, or doorways

Both can appear:
	•	semi-corporeal
	•	shadow-like
	•	or fully physical for brief periods

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Shared Traits
	•	Territorial
	•	Easily offended
	•	Deeply observant
	•	Vindictive when ignored
	•	Protective when respected

They:
	•	hide tools
	•	break machinery
	•	tangle cords
	•	whisper warnings
	•	sabotage careless workers

Differences
	•	Kobolds value competence and safety
	•	Domovoi value respect, family harmony, and tradition

A pleased spirit prevents accidents.
An angry one causes many.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Moderate but extremely practical.
	•	Excellent spatial memory
	•	Understand structural weaknesses
	•	Anticipate failures before mortals do
	•	Limited abstraction—focused on “their” space

They do not plan globally.
They optimize locally.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Faith-neutral: belief not required
	•	Grace-compatible: angels tolerate them
	•	Malice-sensitive: corruption twists them into Boggarts or worse
	•	Place-bound: removing them destabilizes the structure

Killing one damages the location permanently.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Feed on routine, labor, and acknowledgment
	•	Reproduce by place-splitting (new rooms, new tunnels)
	•	Migrate only if driven out by disrespect or destruction
	•	Absence causes:
	•	increased accidents
	•	structural failures
	•	unexplained decay

They are the immune system of buildings.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Low individually / Moderate infrastructurally

A single Kobold or Domovoi is manageable.
Angering a colony can collapse a mine, ruin a factory, or make a home unlivable.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

They represent:
	•	respect for labor
	•	tradition as survival
	•	the cost of neglect
	•	small gods of practical order

Where Titans anchor continents,
Kobolds and Domovoi keep the lights on.
