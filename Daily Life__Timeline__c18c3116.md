	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Military/Technology|Technology]]: AI archaeology becomes a daily headline: each week brings whispers of “new discoveries.” Civilians crowd forums, watching leaks from university labs.
	•	[[culture]]: A TikTok generation starts memeing [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]]. 2040s: The Decade of Mobilization
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: Wartime rationing. In Crusader/Caliphate zones, industries shift toward relic protection, pilgrim logistics, and militarization. Black markets thrive: relic replicas, fake fragments, banned translations.
	•	Daily Survival: In Europe, families host Crusader volunteers; parades show off [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] in bulletproof glass. In Caliphate cities, daily sermons demand contributions of food and sons. In CRINK zones, factories run day and night producing ships and weapons framed as “Mandate of Heaven tools.”
	•	Faith in the Home: Prayer before meals, relic prints hanging on walls, relic prayers worked into school curriculum. In Jerusalem, some kids learn Hebrew letters with Zohar sefirot diagrams; in Mecca, kids memorize Picatrix fragments rewritten as “cosmic tafsir.”
	•	[[culture]]: Music, fashion, even slang revolve around [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]]. Shirts with [[Spear of Longinus]] motifs. Protest graffiti like “No hero but Ali” sprayed in Shi’a districts. Virtual pilgrimages offered for those too poor or restricted to travel.

2050s: The Decade of Collapse (WW3 proper)
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: Scarcity dominates. Food queues, barter markets. Farmers hoard grain; city folk burn relic posters for fuel. PMCs control supply lines. Civilians pay in relic tokens (crusader coins, Caliphate marks, [[Europolis]]MINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Overview/Faction Profile/Europolis]] “heritage credits”).
	•	Daily Survival: Air raids normal. Families shelter in subway stations while priests or imams lead relic-inspired prayers. Hospitals triage patients by “divine priority” in some zones (fighters first). Electricity rationed; water purified in shared cisterns.
	•	Faith in the Home: Every meal framed as sacred. Families display relic symbols as warding talismans: Menorah candles lit nightly in Israel, Sudarium veils painted on walls in Spain, Black Stone crescents stitched into clothing in Caliphate towns.
	•	[[culture]]: Folk songs revive — ballads about martyrs of relic battles. Children play “Crusader vs Jihadist” with sticks. Street art mixes relic symbology with despair: a Shroud overlayed with mushroom clouds.

2060s: The Decade of Fragmentation / Exile

Sectarian bitterness seeps into lullabies and children’s games. Some rebel by burning relic icons, others double down — relic shrines in basements, secret relic readings at night.
	•	[[culture]]: Diaspora [[Cultures]] emerge: Jewish refugees carrying Menorah replicas, Japanese enclaves with Kusanagi myths, CRINK exiles claiming Jade Seal destiny in space. Folk tales reframe [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] as both saviors and curses.
