hypothetical black holes that may have formed in the early universe, within the first fraction of a second after the Big Bang. Unlike stellar black holes formed from collapsing [[Stars]], PBHs could have arisen directly from extreme density fluctuations in the rapidly expanding primordial cosmos.

Depending on when they formed, PBHs could range from tiny, evaporating masses to supermassive black holes. They are of great interest because they might explain several cosmic mysteries: they could be a component of dark matter, act as seeds for early galaxy formation, or even produce gravitational wave signals. While none have been confirmed, they remain a powerful tool for exploring conditions in the infant universe.
