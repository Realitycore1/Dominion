I. Physical Definition

A Cyclic Star is a stellar-scale object that undergoes regular, repeatable transitions between different physical states, forming a closed or semi-closed loop in its evolutionary path. Unlike ordinary [[Stars]], which follow a single sequence from birth to [[New Readers Start Here/Dominion Omniverse/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/[[NEWREA~1/DOMINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Technology/Antimatter/Production]]lic star’s process restarts or loops indefinitely, either naturally or through engineering.

[[Key]] features:
	•	Periodic Core Reset — Energy [[Production]] and structure periodically revert to an earlier stable phase.
	•	Energy Storage & Release Cycles — Alternating between luminous output peaks and quiescent recharge phases.
	•	Material Regeneration — Core or envelope replenished from captured matter, reverse fusion, or dimensional inflow.
	•	[[Time]]-Locked Phases — In engineered types, certain states may be paused or extended arbitrarily.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Cyclic [[Stars]] are classified as Temporal Recurrence Nodes, serving as predictable energy and event markers for civilizations and Omniversal navigation [[Systems]].
