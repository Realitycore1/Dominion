# Story 10 - The First Harminan Meal

## Viewpoint

Mira Tal, serving food adapted from Harminan biomass for the first time.

## Setting

Years 3-17 AL, New Aurora communal kitchen.

## Story Shape

The meal is ugly. It smells metallic, fungal, and faintly sweet. Half the diners stare at it like a dare.

Mira watches people take the first bites and waits for illness, disgust, or relief. What comes instead is silence. Then someone laughs because the food is not good, but it is theirs.

## Harmina Element

The first local meal marks the shift from survival by stockpile to survival by relationship with the planet.

## Character Movement

Mira stops thinking of Harmina as poison that must be defeated. She begins thinking of it as danger that can be learned.

## Future Payoff

The First Meal becomes a settlement holiday whose later versions taste much better and mean much less unless someone remembers the original fear.
