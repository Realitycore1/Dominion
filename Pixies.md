1. Common Pixies
	•	Definition: The baseline—tiny winged humanoids, playful and capricious.
	•	Traits: Invisibility, glittering dust, minor [[Illusion Magic]].
	•	Symbolism: Innocent wonder mixed with mischief.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. [[Trickster]] Pixies
	•	Definition: Pixies that live to prank mortals.
	•	Traits: Shape-shifting, mocking laughter, lead travelers astray.
	•	Symbolism: Harmless play that reveals hidden truths—or causes harm.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Forest Pixies
	•	Definition: Pixies tied to trees, glades, and wild groves.
	•	Traits: Camouflage wings, speak with animals, guide or mislead travelers.
	•	Symbolism: Spirits of the untamed green; guardians of secret places.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Frost Pixies
	•	Definition: Winter-born pixies tied to ice and snow.
	•	Traits: Icy dust trails, mischievous with frost, love to freeze food and drink.
	•	Symbolism: Winter’s bite in playful guise.

⸻

5. Firelight Pixies
	•	Definition: Pixies born from sparks, embers, or candles.
	•	Traits: Flickering wings, warm glow, both guiding and deceiving.
	•	Symbolism: Light that reveals or misleads; warmth that burns.

⸻

6. Shadow Pixies
	•	Definition: Pixies born of twilight or curses.
	•	Traits: Dusky wings, haunting laughter, illusions of fear.
	•	Symbolism: Darkness in small doses; childlike glee turned sinister.

⸻

7. Noble Pixies
	•	Definition: Larger or more refined pixies who serve in [[Fae]] courts.
	•	Traits: Glamoured attire, etiquette training, minor diplomatic influence.
	•	Symbolism: The “small folk” woven into the politics of the great.

⸻

8. Healer Pixies
	•	Definition: Rare benevolent pixies who bless crops or cure illness.
	•	Traits: Soft glowing dust, gentle voices, require offerings of milk or honey.
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s small mercies; healing as a gift of whimsy.

⸻

9. Gremlin-Pixies
	•	Definition: Mischievous pixies that corrupt tools and machines.
	•	Traits: Metallic wings, knack for breaking gears or locks.
	•	Symbolism: Entropy as play; the hidden flaw in mortal craft.

⸻

10. Arch-Pixies (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary pixies who embody seasons or festivals.
	•	Traits: Larger, more powerful, radiating enchantments over entire [[Regions]].
	•	Symbolism: Whimsy writ large—play as cosmic [[law]].
