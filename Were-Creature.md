1. Werewolves
	•	Definition: The archetype—[[Humans]] who transform into wolves or wolf hybrids.
	•	Traits: Strength, regenerative powers, tied to the moon.
	•	Symbolism: Duality of [[Man]]/beast, fear of the wild.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Werebears
	•	Definition: Bear-shifters, often larger and more resilient than other were-creatures.
	•	Traits: Immense strength, thick hides, slower but unstoppable.
	•	Symbolism: Protection, primal ferocity, unstoppable force.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Wererats
	•	Definition: Rat-shifters tied to filth, disease, and cunning.
	•	Traits: Small, sneaky, swarm-like behavior, plague [[Carriers]].
	•	Symbolism: Corruption from within, weakness as strength.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Werecats (Panther, Tiger, Lion, Leopard)
	•	Definition: Feline [[Shapeshifters]], agile hunters of the night.
	•	Traits: Stealth, claws, predatory cunning, territorial.
	•	Symbolism: Elegance and savagery; predators cloaked in beauty.

⸻

5. Wereboars
	•	Definition: Boar or hog-shifters, feral and destructive.
	•	Traits: Tusks, berserk rages, destructive charges.
	•	Symbolism: Rage, stubbornness, reckless [[Hunger]].

⸻

6. Wereravens (or Werecrows)
	•	Definition: Avian [[Shapeshifters]] tied to omens and trickery.
	•	Traits: Feathered forms, prophetic sight, mimicry.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] omens, intelligence, [[Trickster]] nature.

⸻

7. Werehyenas (Gnoll Ancestors)
	•	Definition: Hyena-shifters, scavenger-predators often tied to gnoll myths.
	•	Traits: Laughing howls, scavenger cunning, relentless [[Hunger]].
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]-feasters, chaotic predators, cruelty.

⸻

8. Werecrocodiles
	•	Definition: Crocodile-shifters dwelling in rivers and swamps.
	•	Traits: Amphibious, ambush hunters, immense bite strength.
	•	Symbolism: Patient [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], lurking danger beneath calm waters.

⸻

9. Wereinsects (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Rare insectoid [[Shapeshifters]] (scarabs, spiders, locusts).
	•	Traits: Chitinous hides, hive instincts, venom or swarming forms.
	•	Symbolism: Alien corruption of the [[Human]] form.

⸻

10. Apex Were-Creatures (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary were-beasts that embody the primal archetype of their [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]].
	•	Traits: Towering hybrid forms, semi-divine, tied to cycles of nature or gods of the hunt.
	•	Symbolism: The beast within as ultimate destiny; [[Man]] subsumed by primal [[law]].
