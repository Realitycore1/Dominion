Cost: Prolonged use calcifies the bearer, until they too are mineral.

6. Gospels of Judas

Cost: Each use burns the caster’s flesh into constellations, until they are a map of scars.

Power: Prophecies carved into stone that can manifest reality if spoken in battle.

Cost: Speaking them drains years from the speaker, as the prophecy carves itself into their bones.

9. The Voynich Leaf

Power: A page that unravels alien botanical horrors—vines, spores, impossible [[Flora]] that devour armies.

Cost: Each summoning infects the summoner, turning their veins green and their breath into spores.

Cost: Each gate scorches the summoner’s eyes until blindness takes them.

11. The Dead Sea Shards

Power: Crystalline relics that store the screams of prophets; unleashed as walls of destructive sound.

Cost: The wielder becomes deaf, their ears bleeding until [[Silence]] consumes them.

12. The Crown of Thorns

Power: Grants invulnerability to [[Pain]] and suffering; the bearer becomes a juggernaut.

Cost: The thorns root into their skull; removal is impossible, and resurrection always brings the [[Pain]] back sharper.

The First Tear of the Demiurge

⸻

What It Is

Not a metaphor.

It is the first moment the Demiurge realized it was afraid — crystallized into a paracausal object.

The Tear embodies:
	•	doubt
	•	regret
	•	the awareness of Paradoxa Omnipotentiae

It is the only artifact that can force the Demiurge to confront its own contradiction.

⸻

Why It Is Buried Here

Because New Urusalim is:
	•	the strongest Law-anchor on Earth
	•	the most compliant city
	•	the place least likely to allow forgiveness

The Tear cannot activate under perfect obedience.

That is not coincidence.

⸻

Containment Method
	•	Triple conceptual null field
	•	Silence saturation
	•	Angelic presence (rotated to prevent empathy drift)
	•	Legal paradox seals (contradictory laws stacked endlessly)

It is not guarded by force.

It is guarded by procedure.

⸻

What Happens If It Activates

Not explosion.
Not apocalypse.

Instead:
	•	Archontes Law hesitates
	•	Chains loosen momentarily
	•	Fire fails to ignite
	•	Silence fractures
	•	The Demiurge Avatar destabilizes

For a few minutes…

choice exists without permission.

That is why it must never be used.
