•	Title: Primus Hierophant
	•	Position: Supreme spiritual leader of the Oneirotheon.
	•	Function: Living embodiment of the Oneirotheon’s core values — the Dream, the Myth, and the Pilgrimage.
	•	Authority: Guides the Consensus Slumbers, interprets the Dream Scripture, and declares the direction of the eternal pilgrimage.

⸻

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]
	•	The Primus Hierophant is considered a living archetype — a [[Synthesis]] of all mythic roles without being bound to any one of them.
	•	He knows every [[Human]] and alien myth, scripture, and faith recorded by the Oneirotheon, but without dogma. He holds stories as fragments of the Spark, not commandments.
	•	His body and mind are suffused with magenta [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]] bloom, granting him near-perfect memory recall and the ability to weave myths into living dream-visions.

⸻

Cultural Perception
	•	To the Oneirotheon, the Primus Hierophant is a walking scripture:
	•	The Memory of All Faiths.
	•	The Voice of the Dream.
	•	The Guide of the Pilgrimage.
	•	His proclamations during Consensus Slumber are taken as divine trajectory.
	•	Pilgrims describe his presence as both awe-inspiring and terrifying — as though he sees them not as individuals, but as [[Archetypes]] awaiting enactment.

⸻

Political Significance
	•	The Primus Hierophant does not rule in [[The Dominion]] sense — he guides rather than commands.
	•	His authority can redirect fleets, sanctify shrine-worlds, or end internecine wars by re-framing myths into unified cycles.
	•	Yet his neutrality is perilous: both the Dracoshian faction and the Seekers claim him as proof of their vision, forcing him to balance their zeal without fracturing the whole.

Current State
	•	The present Primus Hierophant is revered for holding all myths without falling into heresy or dogma.
	•	He is seen as the closest mortal step toward the Spark — but not the Spark itself.
