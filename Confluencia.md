### Question:  
**Can Confluencia Threads transmit matter or only information between Verses?**

---

### Answer:  
Confluencia Threads transmit **information, not matter.**  

They are fifth-dimensional filaments of **[[Aetheric]] strange-matter**, woven through the Omniversal manifold to maintain coherence between [[Verse]] Fields. Each Thread acts as a **non-local communication lattice**, carrying resonance data—quantum states, energy harmonics, and [[law]] synchronization—across unimaginable distances and dimensional [[Layers]].  

Matter cannot traverse a Thread because physical mass requires a continuous 4-dimensional spacetime metric, and the Threads operate in the fifth and higher dimensions where such metrics collapse. Attempting to push matter through results in immediate decoherence: the object’s pattern disassembles into raw informational noise before re-stabilization can occur.  

Instead, the Threads function as the **neural network of the Omniverse**, allowing verses to “know” each other’s presence, share [[VI - Q&A/Boundary|Boundary]] pressures, and remain in causal alignment. They relay only informational signatures—the *language* of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] itself.  

In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Physics]], this is described as **[[Aetheric]] Telemetry**, a process by which the Omniverse continually balances its internal geometry, ensuring that each [[Verse]] remains stable within the 11-dimensional structure.  

As the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] axiom states:  
> *“Matter divides; information unites.”*
