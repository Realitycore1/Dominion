Core Bias:

Collectivized recursive growth

This is the subtype you already implied, and it absolutely deserves to be formalized.

This is where Forest behavior merges with Hive logic.

Meaning:

Growth is no longer just branching — it becomes coordinated branching under distributed collective intelligence.

Traits:
	•	swarming life-information systems
	•	eusocial ecologies
	•	collective adaptation
	•	distributed command
	•	superorganism realities

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	forests that think as one organism
	•	entire ecologies functioning as minds
	•	life systems that are both wild and coordinated

Common Expressions:
	•	hive intelligences
	•	eusocial species dominance
	•	living network empires
	•	coordinated biomass civilizations
	•	basin-wide collective consciousness

Failure Mode:

Consensus lock
Individual novelty is consumed by collective pattern dominance.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	Symmorphica-type systems
	•	hyper-organized life basins
	•	bio-civilization megaverses
	•	“the jungle is one animal” cosmologies
