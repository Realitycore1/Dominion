# Mesic Era - Timeline Index

## Purpose

This folder tracks the Mesic Era using the manuscript as the primary timeline source. It covers the historical bridge from the Ark exile through GalFed discovery, Harmina landfall, New Aurora, the first Harminans, settlement divergence, proto-nation formation, strategic militarization, the Founder Clause, and the opening of the New World War.

## Dating Convention

- Pre-Landfall: Ark exile, GalFed discovery, Discovery Clock, Harmina selection.
- AL: After Landfall on Harmina.
- Legacy numbered files are preserved for vault continuity, even where the manuscript now treats a title as a period rather than a single year.

## Era Arc

The Mesic Era begins as flight, becomes settlement, becomes civilization, and ends with the political order that civilization built turning violent. The most important continuity thread is not technology or territory. It is the movement from survival identity to Harminan identity.
