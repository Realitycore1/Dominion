## Era Thesis

The Lithic Era charts humanity from alternate historical foundations through protoscientific discovery, political and ecological collapse, post-Exchange survival, reconstruction, Solar expansion, and the first interstellar systems. The pacing should feel like pressure accumulating for centuries, breaking in 2078, then rebuilding into a civilization capable of leaving Earth without fully escaping its old wounds.
