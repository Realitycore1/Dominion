supranational authority that keeps the early interstellar commons stitched together. It:

Operates the only FTL communications (PFN hub at Sol), schedules traffic, and enforces auditability.

Sets standards (metrology, safety codes, beamer protocols, ship transponders).

Runs arbitration/courts for inter-polity disputes and trade.

Licenses beamer corridors, recognizes jump-beacon crews (where they exist), and publishes star-hazard bulletins.

[[Earth (Terra)]]’s view of the Colonial Union

Self-image: the switchboard, customs house, and last ambulance of [[Human]] space.

Legitimacy story: democratic mandate via the Union Assembly; “we keep everyone talking and safe.”

Strategic value: keeps information sovereignty at Sol (PFN), lets [[Earth (Terra)]] de-escalate crises with throttles that never touch safety-of-life.

Economics: tariff skim pays for beamers, rescue, courts; [[Earth (Terra)]] industries win on standards they helped write.

Politics: a prestige project that unifies fractious [[Earth (Terra)]] blocs without an imperial flag.

[[Pain]] points [[Earth (Terra)]] admits: accusations of Sol-bias; backlog in courts; occasional heavy-handed PFN sanctions that hit markets hard.

Tone: “Partner and amplifier.”

Ask: More regional relay authority (PFN v2) with Sol synchronization; guaranteed P2 (governance) burst windows.

Security-first builders (e.g., [[Austro Guard]], [[Rythecroa union]], [[United Authority of Systems]])

Tone: “Necessary gatekeeper—keep corridors predictable.”

Why: Convoy insurance rates and [[Skyhook]] uptime depend on CU rules.

Ask: Faster PFN rulings on tariffs; formal anti-privateer protocols tied to comms sanctions.

Planned/party states (e.g., [[Star Union]], some Kuroda districts)

Tone: “Fair when audited, biased when convenient.”

Why: Party logistics hate delays; Sol metadata audits feel intrusive.

Ask: Hard utilization floors per PFN class; content-neutrality guarantees with published denial statistics.

Tone: “Leash from Sol.”

Why: PFN rate-limits sap propaganda and capital flows; court losses bite.

Ask: Direct colony↔colony PFN pairs (denied or [[Time]]-boxed by CU); more “sovereign discretion.”

Reality: Frequent sanctions; thin sympathy from neighbors.

Frontier pragmatists (e.g., [[Yauturn Sovereignty]], [[Sudhakar States]])

Tone: “Lifeline—don’t break it.”

Why: Disaster slots (P0/P1) and seed shipments save lives; PFN queues set crop and cryo-lab cycles.

Ask: Cheaper P4 off-peak; pre-approved disaster surge plans.

What the CU gets right (consensus)

Safety-of-life first: distress and public-safety traffic cannot be throttled.

Auditability: public dashboards, tamper logs, quarterly independent audits.

Due process: content seizure only with dual-court warrants; appeals clock.

What draws [[Fire]] (recurring grievances)

Sol-bias optics: [[Earth (Terra)]] firms over-represented in standards committees.

Comms as leverage: rate-limits on P3/P4 feel like economic coercion, even when legal.

Court backlog: arbitration delays turn small disputes into multi-year trade freezes.

Single-point politics: with PFN v1, one broken or throttled link chokes a whole colony’s voice.

Current fixes & reforms on the table

PFN v2 (multihoming): multiple Sol ports + CU-certified Regional [[Relays]]; per-class floor guarantees; Sol still signs routes.

Class floors: hard minimums for P2 (governance) to keep courts/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] functioning under stress.

Neutrality KPI: colony-facing scorecards—denials, wait times, sanction rationale—published per quarter.

Fast-track tribunals: 72-hour panels for tariff/propaganda emergencies to shorten sanction windows.

Commons covenant: a com
 mutual aid (famine relief, distress, epidemiology, debris warnings).

Red lines: no private FTL links; no unilateral weaponization of PFN (P0/P1 are sacrosanct).

[[Earth (Terra)]]’s elevator pitch: “We are the neutral backbone—your lifeline in distress, your referee in disputes, your calendar for civilization.”

Why: Arbitration + standards are their core business; PFN neutrality is revenue and legitimacy.
