The Starmaster Cloak was a symbol as much as a tool it serves as a mark of authority and serves to help the user by increasing efficiency in bodily processes such as regeneration, the cloaks were only given to those who commanded the seed ships to Harmonia
The only Cloaks ever made were given to the Captains of the Seed ships and then handed down through the generations
To own one was to be considered the highest honor bestowed upon due to the significance of the Seeder ships
The original families still own the cloaks as a tradition and to be used to show the pinnacle of one's devotion to one's country
