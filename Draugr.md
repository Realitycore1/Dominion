Classification

Corporeal Revenants / Grave-Strong Undead / Territorial Dead

⸻

CORE IDEA

Draugr are the dead who refuse to diminish.

Unlike ghosts or echoes, a Draugr does not fade, replay, or haunt. It occupies space. Its body persists because its will never loosened its grip on flesh, land, or legacy. Where Barrow-Wights administer jurisdiction, Draugr enforce presence through brute continuation.

They do not linger.
They stand their ground.

⸻

ORIGIN

Draugr arise from deaths steeped in possession and pride:
	•	warriors buried with arms, armor, and unresolved dominance
	•	chieftains interred to rule after death
	•	oathbound sentinels left without release rites
	•	environments saturated with cold, isolation, and ritual violence

They are not summoned.
They wake.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Draugr are unmistakably corporeal—heavy, cold, and wrong.

Common traits:
	•	Swollen or desiccated corpses with stone-dense musculature
	•	Skin darkened to ash-gray, blue-black, or frost-pale
	•	Eyes glowing dull amber, corpse-blue, or corpse-green
	•	Burial armor fused to flesh; weapons bound to grip
	•	Breath steaming in any temperature

Their mass feels greater than it should be, as if gravity favors them.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Draugr are aggressive, territorial, and brutally direct.
	•	Attack intruders without negotiation
	•	Guard graves, hoards, ships, halls, or borders
	•	Fight until physically destroyed
	•	Ignore pain, fear, and injury

They do not scheme.
They contest.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Moderate.
	•	Retain tactical memory and combat instinct
	•	Recognize enemies, rivals, and trespassers
	•	Capable of speech—often taunts, threats, or challenges
	•	Limited adaptability outside familiar territory

A Draugr remembers who it was—and why it should still matter.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Corpse Sovereignty: full control over its own remains
	•	Cold Pressure: temperature drops and air thickens nearby
	•	Grace-Resistant: divine light weakens but does not command
	•	Malice-Compatible: corruption increases strength and rage
	•	Weight of Death: strikes carry unnatural force

Fire disrupts cohesion. Cold reinforces it.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Found in burial mounds, ship-graves, frozen ruins, ancient halls
	•	Often solitary, sometimes in kin-groups
	•	Do not reproduce; new Draugr form independently
	•	Surroundings become hostile, heavy, and silent

Land ruled by Draugr does not forget the dead.

⸻

ALPHA DRAUGR

An Alpha Draugr is known as a Grave-Lord or Death-Chieftain.

Traits:
	•	Enlarged stature and increased mass
	•	Can animate lesser corpses temporarily
	•	Exerts dominance over burial territories
	•	May command Draugr retainers or dead crews

A Grave-Lord does not haunt a place.
It rules it.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (individual) / Severe (Alpha or clustered graves)

You don’t outlast a Draugr.
You overcome it completely—or not at all.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Draugr represent:
	•	pride that survived death
	•	strength without release
	•	burial as occupation
	•	the cost of dying unwilling

Where Death Echoes repeat,
where Barrow-Wights administer,
Draugr fight.
