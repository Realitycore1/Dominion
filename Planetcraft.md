Core Features


![[Pasted image 20
250830162951.png]]
Planetcraft are entire planets converted into weaponized [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] — vast [[Citadels]] of war that dwarf [[Mooncraft]] and rival [[Fortress Worlds]]. Unlike [[Fortress Worlds]], which are defensive redoubts, Planetcraft are mobile engines of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] will: armored planets equipped with propulsion [[Systems]], armada vaults, and enough firepower to wage war against whole star [[Systems]].

From a distance, a Planetcraft is a blackened world wreathed in [[Fire]], moving like a dreadnought, its surface alive with weapons, fleets erupting from vaulted bays, an entire planet reborn as war.

[[Megastructures Portal|Megastructures]]
Structure

Planetary Core Conversion: Original core hollowed and replaced with Matter Core reactors, providing mobility and weaponization.

Surface Saturation: Entire crust studded with mass drivers, beam cannons, artillery walls, and shield arrays.

Orbital Mantle: Planet-[[Wide]] shield domes and defense rings ensure redundancy.

Vaulted Interior: Armada bays, manufactoria, and reliquary sanctums hidden within planetary [[Depths]].

Functions

Mobile War-World: Capable of moving between [[Systems]] as a planetary-scale battleship.

Fleet Bastion: Houses entire armadas within its interior — launching fleets like spores from a furnace.

Planet-Cracker: Can unleash enough firepower to destabilize other worlds or strip entire orbital shells.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Terrifying symbols of the empire’s reach — an inhabited planet itself as war engine.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Serve as planet-sized [[Dreadnoughts]], first-strike or retaliatory weapons of total annihilation.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Surface carved into endless black fortress walls, with spire forests stretching across continents.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, Planetcraft appear as spheres scarred with cruciform defense grids glowing in gold.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Shields and weapon discharges ripple in fractal mandalas, visible across space.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Scripture etched into planetary crust, so entire continents glow as glyphs during activation.
Known as the “Amber Worlds of Judgment” — myths say each is the hammer of the Empress, planets reforged as her fists.
