# Cosmology Atlas - Pleroma Archive

The Cosmology Atlas is the public bridge between reader-facing orientation and the archive's deepest reality-structure material. It exists so readers can approach planes, realms, axioms, MOT, and Pleroma-level structure in a guided order instead of entering the specialist folders cold.

## Purpose Summary

This atlas organizes cosmology by reader difficulty. It does not replace the specialist portals or source notes; it points readers toward the safest sequence for understanding them.

It matters because cosmology in the Pleroma Archive is not a single subject. It includes metaphysics, planar geography, magical architecture, reality engineering, basin theory, axiom stability, and the terminal states of existence.

## Public Route

1. Read [[Advanced Cosmology FAQ Guide]] to establish the major terms.
2. Use [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]] for the existing question-and-answer foundation.
3. Move into [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Lore References|Lore References]] for the applied bridge between metaphysics and lived systems.
4. Browse the realm and plane anchors before entering broader cosmology indexes.
5. Enter [[Cosmology Portal]], [[Pleroma Portal]], and [[Master Omniversal Theory (MOT) Portal]] only after the reader can distinguish a realm, a plane, an axiom, a basin, and MOT.
