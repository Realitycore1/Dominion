# Story 13 - Lanterns Above Route Six

## Viewpoint

Pell Varek, a rail-yard child riding with a route maintenance crew.

## Setting

Years 18-40 AL, Route Six night camp.

## Story Shape

The crew sees lights moving in formation above the route and assumes drones, aircraft, or hostile machines. Pell is the first to notice that the lights pulse like feeding organisms.

The official report calls them colonial aerial fauna. The campfire version calls them Lantern Men. Both versions spread.

## Harmina Element

Lantern swarms create patterns in the night sky that feel intentional without being confirmed as intelligence.

## Character Movement

Pell learns that route people survive by respecting both data and rumor.

## Future Payoff

Route Six adopts lantern markings on shelters, tying transit identity to local sky mythology.
