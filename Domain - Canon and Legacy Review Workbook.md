# Domain - Canon and Legacy Review Workbook

> Superseded by [[Domain - Canon and Legacy Review Workbook - Completed v1.0]] and governed by [[Domain - Canon Bible v1.0]].

This file is retained as the original planning workbook and should not be used as the current Domain canon authority.

## Purpose

This workbook is for deciding what the Domain is, what existing lore should remain canon, what should be treated as legacy/raw concept material, and what needs to be developed before the Domain is used heavily in manuscripts.

It does not rewrite canon directly. It summarizes what exists and gives decision fields to fill in.

# I. Existing Domain Summary

## 1. High-Level Concept

The Domain is currently a hyper-intelligent bio-organic civilization/species/collective that assimilates organic matter, infrastructure, minds, and ecosystems into an evolving biological order.

It is not just a monster swarm.

It has:

- Primes;
- Sovereigns;
- Thralls;
- living tools;
- glyphs;
- rituals;
- hives;
- biomechanical ships;
- philosophical factions;
- psychological warfare;
- planetary-scale conversion doctrine.

The strongest current version of the Domain is:

> A bio-organic civilization that converts life, memory, infrastructure, and belief into a hierarchical-consensus living network governed by Primes and temporary Supremes.

## 2. Existing Origin Material

The master codex currently gives this origin:

- After the Equilon Union departs, a cabal of techno-elites, politicians, and medical-industrial leaders remains on Earth/Terra.
- They reject outward expansion and focus on remaking humanity.
- They manipulate food, water, and medical supply chains.
- They conduct gene editing, vivisection, and covert experimentation.
- The First Thrall proves the concept.
- Thralls evolve intelligence and cruelty, leading to Prime Transfiguration.
- A biologist injects herself with raw Prime genetic material and becomes the First Sovereign Prime.
- The Domain goes underground during a plague era while colonies watch Earth fade into silence and mythologize it as a tomb world.

Recommended treatment:

| Element | Suggested status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post-Equilon remnant cabal | Canon-candidate | Strong political origin; needs timeline placement. |

| First Thrall | Canon-candidate | Useful origin milestone. |
| Prime Transfiguration | Canon-candidate | Central concept; needs clearer mechanics. |
| First Sovereign biologist | Canon-candidate | Strong mythic/scientific origin figure. |
| Earth becomes tomb-world myth | Canon-candidate | Good Sol bridge; needs date and manuscript alignment. |

```text
  |

  |

  |
```

Existing concepts:

- The Primes are highest authorities within the Domain.
- Each Prime is a superintelligence and delegates or embodies part of the Domain.
- The Supreme is a Prime elevated temporarily above the rest to settle stagnated disputes.
- The hivemind is not pure flattening; it is a mix of hierarchy and consensus consciousness.

Recommended treatment:

> Keep the hierarchy/consensus tension. This is what makes the Domain more interesting than a simple hive.

Decision:

```text
Is the Domain one mind, many minds in one organism, or a civilization of linked biological sovereignties?

```

## 4. Existing Warfare Identity

The Domain does not always invade loudly. Existing timeline notes emphasize:

- infiltration;
- infrastructure rot;
- sensor confusion;
- biological spoofing;
- induced paranoia;
- atmospheric interdiction;
- terraforming spores;
- mimic organisms;
- dormant organisms in dead industrial zones;
- living ships and womb-worlds;
- conversion of entire planets and gas giants.

Best current war thesis:

> The Domain wins by making a civilization's own body, supply chain, architecture, memories, and fear collaborate in its conversion.

Decision:

```text
Is the Domain's primary horror biological, psychological, theological, civilizational, or all four?
```

1. The Domain originates from post-Equilon human remnant elites on Terra who tried to remake humanity through biological control.
2. It evolves from covert medical-industrial manipulation into a bio-organic civilization.
3. The First Thrall proves obedience; Prime Transfiguration proves intelligence; the First Sovereign proves rulership.
4. The Domain becomes a hierarchical-consensus hivemind governed by Primes.
5. The Supreme is a temporary apex Prime invoked when Prime disputes stagnate the whole.
6. The Domain's horror is assimilation without consent, but its strongest characters complicate this by integrating rather than simply dissolving.
7. Shaki Moha becomes the Socratic Prime: contradiction, cognition, dreamscape, philosophical assimilation.
8. Kharon becomes the Strategic Prime: pressure, battlefield logic, engineered survival.
9. Lunaris becomes the melancholic lunar Prime: memory, recursion, subtle surrender.
10. Jovaryn is Jupiter undergoing neural assimilation, separate from but related to Supernal world-form development.
11. Supernals are living planetary bioships and mobile gene factories.
12. The Domain is not just hunger. It is civilization made biological, ritualized, and predatory.
