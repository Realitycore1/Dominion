Core Bias:

Internal void and hidden interior structure

This subtype is extremely important because it stops Stone from becoming too “surface-heavy.”

Reality here is defined not only by what is solid—

but by what is inside the solid.

Traits:
	•	internal hollows
	•	hidden chambers
	•	buried civilizations
	•	subterranean ecologies
	•	concealed routes and pressure shelters

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the real world is inside the mountain, not on it
	•	hidden continuity survives within the mass
	•	what is preserved is often internal, not visible

Common Expressions:
	•	subterranean civilizations
	•	buried machine worlds
	•	inner archive vaults
	•	under-mountain ecologies
	•	hidden sacred refuges

Failure Mode:

Collapse inward
Internal support fails and the basin caves into itself.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	vault worlds
	•	hidden civilizations
	•	deep refuge systems
	•	understructure-based cosmology
