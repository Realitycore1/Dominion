1.1 Baseline Characteristics
	•	Scale: Hundreds to thousands of kilometers in diameter; mass measured in trillions of tons.
	•	Population: Tens of millions to billions.
	•	Purpose: Permanent, self-contained habitat and cultural center; a mobile nation-state.
	•	Propulsion: Slow but steady—fusion, [[Antimatter]], or beam-driven sail in baseline designs.
	•	Habitat Form: Can include O’Neill-type interiors, hollowed asteroids, or engineered shells.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].1 Baseline Characteristics
	•	Scale: Tens of kilometers to ~200 km.
	•	Population: Thousands to millions, depending on mission scope.
	•	Purpose: Long-term migration to a distant star, with crew living and dying aboard.
	•	Design [[Styles]]:
	•	[[O’Neill Cylinder]]-based: Two or more counter-rotating habitats.
	•	Hollowed Asteroid: Excavated and spun for gravity.
	•	Ring Cylinder Hybrid: Rotating outer hull with fixed inner core.
	•	Propulsion: Fusion torch, beam-pushed sail, or nuclear pulse drive.
