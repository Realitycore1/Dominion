Juno occupies a stable, well-understood orbit and becomes useful not for its resources—but for its predictability.

That makes it ideal for:
	•	coordination
	•	regulation
	•	arbitration
	•	contracts

Juno becomes the paperwork capital of the belt.

Primary Role
	•	Trade clearinghouse
	•	Shipping arbitration courts
	•	Insurance registries
	•	Cargo certification
	•	Labor contracts
	•	Dispute resolution

When something goes wrong between independent operators, Juno is where it’s settled.

Station Architecture
	•	Large cylindrical administrative habitats
	•	Extensive data infrastructure
	•	Secure legal and archival vaults
	•	Minimal heavy industry

Juno exports decisions, not materials.

Cultural Note

Juno stations develop a reputation for:
	•	rigid procedure
	•	dull efficiency
	•	low drama
	•	absolute reliability

It is not loved.
It is trusted.
