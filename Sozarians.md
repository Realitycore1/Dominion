Physiology:
	•	Sozarians evolved from glider-predators that inhabited the upper atmosphere of Vey’kaan. They retain hollow but reinforced bone structures, semi-hydraulic musculature, and gossamer-light wings reinforced with memory alloy filaments.
	•	Wings are not for powered flight, but rather glide stabilization and aerial deceleration in low gravity or dense atmosphere.
	•	Possess avian facial features with keratin-based crests, narrow eyes adapted for high-contrast storm vision, and beak-like lower jaws with minimal dentition.
	•	Skin-feathers are transparent crystal down—they refract solar and electrical light into rainbow-hued patterns during emotional surges.
	•	Height: 1.9 to [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] meters average
	•	Respiration: Dual oxygen-nitrogen respiration with air-sac buffering to survive rapid pressure shifts
	•	Neurology: Exceptional balance-control neural bundles, adapted for 3D navigation in driftspace

Core Philosophy:
“Above the cloud, every shadow is light.”
	•	Sozarians believe that wisdom exists only in perspective—one must see from above, between, and beyond.
	•	They revere verticality: height is status, both metaphorically and physically.[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] live on the highest sky-nests.
	•	Every important decision is made while gliding—drift-councils hang suspended during rulings to maintain impartiality.
	•	Art & Expression:
	•	[[culture]] dominated by motion-poetry: airborne dance-rituals where wings and limbs form glyphs visible only from altitude
	•	Writing is hologlyphic—projected crystal reflections from feather-etched lenses
	•	Memory is not stored in text but sky-sequences—specific aerial paths known only to the kinline

Governance:
	•	Led by the Cliffborn Guilds, merchant-philosopher collectives that maintain trade routes, interstellar archives, and diplomatic balance
	•	Elections occur during open-drift duels—nonlethal sky-dance competitions to determine leadership based on maneuver clarity and atmospheric harmony
	•	Caste Fluidity:
	•	One may move between societal [[Layers]] via challenge glides, ascending to higher “air brackets”
	•	Social shame is expressed through grounding—forced denial of glide-privilege

The Sozarians run the Cultural Vault Fleet—a caravan of massive interstellar museum-ships and [[Ritual]]-trade barges
	•	Their guilds act as diplomatic intermediaries between[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]] that cannot understand each other, using shared sky-symbolism
	•	Some believe they possess ancient navigational records predating the Federation, though they do not confirm this
	•	Their creed is one of grace without passivity—peaceful, but never stagnant
