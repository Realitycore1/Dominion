Core Bias:

Unregulated organic proliferation

This is the most “default” Forest subtype.

Reality behaves like a wild, ancient, overgrown ecological system with little imposed hierarchy.

Traits:
	•	explosive biodiversity
	•	uncontrolled branching lifeforms
	•	layered ecologies
	•	rampant adaptation
	•	ancient survival chains
	•	deep-time biological recursion

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	living wildernesses
	•	evolutionary jungles
	•	biospheric labyrinths
	•	overgrown cosmological canopies

Common Expressions:
	•	biologically dominant universes
	•	living continents / worlds
	•	naturally emergent intelligence webs
	•	semi-sentient ecologies

Failure Mode:

Ecological saturation
Too much complexity for stable continuity.

Good shorthand:

“Reality grows like old jungle
