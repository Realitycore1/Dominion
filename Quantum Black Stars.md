I. Physical Definition

A Quantum Black Star is a compact stellar remnant whose extreme gravitational field is stabilized and modified by quantum gravitational effects, preventing the formation of a traditional event horizon.
This allows the object to mimic many properties of a black hole while remaining luminous and information-accessible, avoiding the classical singularity paradox.

[[Key]] features:
	•	Horizonless Structure — Instead of an event horizon, the outer [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] is a quantum “firewall” or Planck-scale shell that prevents causal disconnection.
	•	Quantum Vacuum Stabilization — Internal spacetime is supported by vacuum fluctuation pressure, preventing total collapse.
	•	Information Transparency — Radiation carries retrievable information, preserving unitarity.
	•	Relativistic Photon Sphere — Light orbits [[Close]] to the surface, giving a black hole-like silhouette.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Quantum Black [[Stars]] are classified as Quantum-Gravitation Anchors, bridging compact object [[Physics]] with Omniversal information theory.
