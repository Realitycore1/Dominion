I. Royal Exo-Panoplies (Augmented Guards)
	•	Size: Still [[Human]]-scale, but sheathed in ornate tessellated exoderm.
	•	Role: Elite guards, ceremonial warriors, duelists.
	•	Features:
	•	Collapse inscriptions etched in precious alloys.
	•	Integrated resonance wards that resist psionic or metaphysical corruption.
	•	Cultural Note: Worn in palace processions and battlefield ceremonies; every strike carries dynastic authority.

⸻

II. Royal Legion Frames (Knight-Panoplies)
	•	Scale: 6–8m tall, larger than federal legion frames.
	•	Role: Vanguard shock troops and honor champions.
	•	Features:
	•	Winged resonant arrays projecting shimmering shields.
	•	Weapons forged as [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] (collapse-blades sanctified with golden harmonics).
	•	Painted in dynastic colors, bearing [[Iconography]] of lineage.
	•	Deployment: Typically only a few per battlefield, their appearance marks dynastic intervention.

⸻

III. Royal Hunter-Colossi (Paladin Walkers)
	•	Scale: 60–100m tall, twice the federal Hunters.
	•	Role: Anti-fortress and symbolic executioners.
	•	Features:
	•	Multi-layer resonance cannons that strike like judgment.
	•	Cloaked in veils of light and psionic shrouds.
	•	Semi-sentient: only functions properly when harmonized with a royal scion’s resonance signature.
	•	Cultural Role: Often named after Collapse martyrs; to see one stride is to [[Witness]] dynasty embodied.

⸻

IV. Royal Mechoid Cathedra (Walking Thrones)
	•	Scale: 300–500m tall, dwarfing federal colossi.
	•	Role: Mobile thrones and war-basilicas.
	•	Features:
	•	Carry oracle [[Choirs]] and dynastic courts inside.
	•	Function both as battlefield command and ceremonial seats of rule.
	•	Weapons include sanctified [[Graviton]] lances and orbital-scale resonance projectors.
	•	Presence: Not just machines — they appear as walking cathedrals, dripping with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]].

⸻

V. Royal Esoteric Behemoths (Final Liturgical Constructs)
	•	Scale: Kilometric, comparable to small cities.
	•	Role: Enact dynastic decrees of annihilation or sanctification.
	•	Features:
	•	Operate as both metaphysical [[Temples]] and siege [[Entities]].
	•	Once activated, act on Final Scripts that execute dynastic will across theaters.
	•	Beyond federal “non-recallable” — they function as doctrinal inevitabilities.
	•	Cultural Note: Rarely seen, whispered of in Collapse Canticles as “the Thrones of Wrath.”

⸻

[[Key]] Differences from Federal Suits
	•	Scale: Always larger, always more imposing.
	•	Symbolism: Every layer drips with dynastic [[Iconography]] — wings, halos, Collapse inscriptions gilded in precious metals.
	•	Pilotage: Restricted to dynastic bloodlines, sainted generals, or scions chosen by oracle decree.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: While Federal mechs are tools, Royal mechs are manifestations of divine legitimacy
