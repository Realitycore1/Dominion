# (3015) The Colonial Union

The Colonial Articles reform the Terran Federation into the Colonial Union in 3015.

This is constitutional continuity rather than conquest, secession, or regime collapse. Existing courts, civil service, public debts, treaty obligations, citizenship, personhood law, and the Immortal Concord remain in force.

## Constitutional Change

Earth becomes the founding planetary member of a multi-world polity rather than the sole territorial basis of federal power.

Luna and chartered orbital communities receive representation in their own right. Gilgamesh becomes the common seat of interworld government.

Member governments retain domestic civil law and local habitat authority within common guarantees of personhood, due process, and life-support access.

The Union controls:

- shared rescue and traffic coordination;
- interworld transport standards;
- external trade and treaty authority;
- common defense;
- infrastructure interoperability;
- recognition and admission of new settlements.

## Political Cause

The Lunar Colonial Committee is the principal institutional pressure behind the reformation. Its residents possess local government but remain dependent on a legislature in which Luna has no seat as a polity.

Orbital stations join the demand because federal regulations already govern their air, traffic, labor, and emergency systems without recognizing them as political communities.

## Political Cost

The name *Colonial Union* is contested from its first session.

Lunar delegates use *Colonial* to force the constitution to acknowledge off-world peoples. Critics argue that the term preserves the hierarchy the Articles claim to end. Later settlements inherit both meanings.

The Union's first major expansion appropriation authorizes the Mars Foothold Mission. Mars is promised a path to local self-government before launch, but Gilgamesh retains control of mission budgets, traffic windows, rescue thresholds, and colonial recognition.
