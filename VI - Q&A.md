# VI - Q&A

This is the public question-and-answer hub for the Pleroma Archive. It gives readers a softer entry point before they enter the deeper cosmology and specialist theory files.

## Purpose Summary

The Q&A section exists to answer the questions a reader is most likely to ask while entering the archive: where to begin, what the archive is, how canon works, what the Dominion is, how timelines should be read, and when to approach specialist cosmology.

## How To Use This Section

1. Start with [[New Reader FAQ]].
2. Use [[Reading Order FAQ]] if you know what kind of material you enjoy.
3. Use [[Archive and Canon FAQ]] when a page status, draft, or recovery stub is confusing.
4. Use [[World Orientation FAQ]] for the setting-level questions readers are likely to ask first.
5. Use [[Timeline FAQ]] before reading dated historical notes.
6. Use [[Advanced Cosmology FAQ Guide]] before entering the deep cosmology files.
