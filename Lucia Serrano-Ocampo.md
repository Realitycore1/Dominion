Birth: 2033 – Valencia, Spain
Profession: Naval architect
Survival specialty: Maritime engineering and archipelago diplomacy
Base: New Orleans Archipelago

Lucia has no illusions about [[Humanity]]—she just prefers to keep moving.
She built the first skimmer fleets of the Pan-Atlantic, charted new trade routes, and taught maritime [[Physics]] to people who could barely swim.

People call her:
The Tidewalker
The Lady of the Flood

She trusts no land, no throne, and no army.
But she trusts the sea.
