# Chapter 5 - Rise of the Equilon States

## Timeline Function

War Years 1-2. Equilon hardens from a founder-linked political inheritance into a wartime state system.

## Event Role

- Creates centralized war ministries, logistics boards, and emergency franchise rules.
- Aligns New Aurora loyalists, continuity courts, orbital watch assets, and civil-defense networks.
- Begins using Project Ra and skyview infrastructure as war-warning systems.
- Reveals that Project Ra's public asteroid-defense role does not explain its complete weapons orientation or observation envelope.
- Places Amber in conflict with custodians who extended founder-era external-defense programs without telling her.
- Forces other powers to treat Equilon as a full belligerent bloc.

## Consequence

Equilon's rise is both defensive and provocative. It can protect New Aurora, but every protection looks like empire to its enemies.
