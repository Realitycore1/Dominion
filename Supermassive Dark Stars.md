	•	Appearance: Extremely luminous in infrared, dim in visible light; can resemble vast glowing clouds more than compact [[Stars]].

In the Master Omniversal Theory, Supermassive Dark [[Stars]] appear in:
	•	Primordial Verses — Where dark matter density is high enough to sustain annihilation heating.
	•	Engineered Verses — Created deliberately to seed rapid supermassive black hole formation.
	•	Magic-Integrated Verses — Seen as “ghost suns,” vast and cold in visible light, yet blazing in the unseen spectrum.

A Supermassive Dark Star is a hypothetical stellar object from the early universe, forming before significant metal enrichment, in [[Regions]] where dark matter density was extremely high.
