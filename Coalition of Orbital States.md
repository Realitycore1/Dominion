# Coalition of Orbital States

## Nation Assignment

Assigned nation: Coalition of Orbital States.

Manuscript role: orbital sovereignty bloc, traffic-law power, watcher of Project Ra, and strategically necessary but internally divided war actor.

## Core Identity

The Coalition begins with Harmina orbital maintenance rotations that become permanent station communities. Weather observation, relay traffic, debris defense, docking control, orbital manufacturing, and the Harmina Lift make the stations essential long before they claim sovereignty.

Orbital identity forms around life-support immediacy. Surface law cannot govern a pressure failure quickly enough. In orbit, delay kills before ideology can explain itself.

## Geographic Core

Core locations:

- old Ark service platforms.
- weather-eye stations.
- orbital factories.
- relay habitats.
- debris-defense grids.
- transfer ports above New Aurora, Atlovaia, Ormova, and lift-support regions.
- Harmina Lift orbital terminals.
- station-to-station traffic corridors.

The Coalition's geography is not land but volume, timing, orbit, docking windows, and pressure envelopes.

## Origin From New Aurora

At first, crews rotate between New Aurora and orbital platforms. Then rotations stretch. Families move up. Children are born in orbit or raised between stations and surface transfer ports. Once orbital homes exist, surface administrators discover that station law is not merely a technical exception.

The Orbital Compact recognizes station self-governance in matters of life support, docking priority, internal civil law, traffic control, and debris defense after stations refuse unsafe surface-mandated transfers during a severe orbital risk period.

## Culture

Orbital culture is procedural, blunt, claustrophobic, and suspicious of symbolic politics. Its citizens live inside machines and know that no speech patches a hull.

Common stereotypes:

- cold-blooded.
- technical.
- blunt about mortality.
- loyal to station before surface nation.
- politically slippery.
- impossible to blockade without consequences.

Coalition children often measure safety in liters, seals, docking windows, and sleep-cycle alarms before they understand national flags.

## Government and Institutions

Important institutions:

- Station Councils: habitat governments organized around life-support reality.
- Seal Authority: can override civil law during pressure, contamination, or hull risk.
- Orbital Traffic Court: controls docking priority, safe windows, debris corridors, and transfer law.
- Water-Reclamation Trusts: treat water as civic inheritance.
- Coalition Assembly: begins as scheduling coordination and becomes political legislature.
- Parity Watch: classified monitoring of surface and orbital weapons.

Coalition sovereignty begins as the right to say no when ground orders would kill station citizens.

## Economy and Resources

Economic strengths:

- weather data.
- orbital traffic control.
- communications.
- surveillance.
- docking windows.
- microgravity manufacturing.
- debris defense.
- emergency relay services.
- orbital construction.
- Harmina Lift coordination.

Every surface state depends on orbital timing. This dependence makes Coalition neutrality powerful and never innocent.

## Military Doctrine

Coalition doctrine frames itself as life-support defense and orbital parity. The line between traffic control and military denial is thin because a denied docking window can decide a battle.

Primary programs:

- Debris Sovereignty Grid: dual-use debris defense able to disable hostile orbital objects.
- Traffic Lock Protocols: authority over docking windows, cargo priority, and emergency holds.
- Vacuum-Native Augment Lines: body-maintenance and adaptation programs for station-born citizens, later militarized.
- Kessler Insurance Swarms: autonomous capture and deflection drones that can become anti-satellite tools.
- Parity Watch: classified tracking of Project Ra, Atlovaian mirror arrays, and unauthorized launches.

The Coalition can honestly say it was deceived by Project Ra. It cannot honestly say it saw nothing.

## Internal Fault Lines

- strict neutrality versus contract dependence.
- station autonomy versus Coalition-wide authority.
- surface ancestry versus orbital identity.
- traffic law versus military leverage.
- fear of Atlovaia versus fear of Equilon centralization.
- whether Project Ra should be exposed, balanced, seized, or tolerated.

The Coalition's danger is believing neutrality is automatically moral when traffic control is already power.

## Role in the New World War

The Coalition becomes indispensable the moment war touches orbit, long-range communications, weather routing, or lift traffic. Its stations split over neutrality, contract obligations, and whether Project Ra violates orbital parity badly enough to justify confrontation.

War function:

- orbital logistics and surveillance bloc.
- traffic and docking authority.
- anti-satellite and debris-denial power.
- fractured neutral power under pressure.
- state most likely to decide tempo without officially choosing a side at first.

## Story Use

Use the Coalition to keep the war above the horizon: station councils arguing over docking windows, traffic officers seeing suspicious mass, orbital children learning emergency seals, and neutrality becoming a weapon one denied clearance at a time.
