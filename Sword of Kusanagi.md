🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: Kusanagi-no-Tsurugi (“Grass-Cutting Sword”)
Discovery Location: Traditionally enshrined at Atsuta Shrine, Nagoya; long hidden from public view. In 2040, seismic activity revealed a sealed subterranean chamberg beneath the shrine containing an ancient blade, distinct from known replicas.
Summary:
	•	Material: Bronze/iron alloy blade, ~80 cm, heavily corroded but structurally intact.
	•	Dating: Metallurgical analysis places forging between 3rd–6th century CE (Kofun period).
	•	Inscriptions: Weathered runes resembling archaic Yamato script; some fragments interpreted as references to Susanoo and the slaying of the eight-headed serpent (Yamata no Orochi).
	•	Anomalies:
	•	Trace remnants of serpent venom peptides identified in blade crevices, though chemically degraded.
	•	Magnetic resonance imaging reveals unusual forging [[Layers]] suggesting ritualistic smelting techniques.
	•	Archaeological Significance: Strong evidence of authentic artifact behind the Kusanagi legend; blurs line between Japanese mythology and historical weaponry.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-12 (Kusanagi)
Classification: HIGH SYMBOLIC / MODERATE STRATEGIC IMPACT
Custody: Japanese Imperial Household Agency and Atsuta Shrine; guarded by Self-Defense Forces. Display restricted to Emperor during state rituals.
