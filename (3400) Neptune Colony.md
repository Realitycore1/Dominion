# (3400) Neptune Colony

Permanent Neptunian colonies are recognized in 3400.

They center on Triton and high Neptunian orbit.

The colonies specialize in:

- large accelerator and particle-capture complexes;
- fusion-powered antimatter production;
- magnetic storage;
- Triton volatile processing;
- high-energy propulsion testing;
- Kuiper and interstellar navigation.

Neptune becomes the principal antimatter-production region. Its remoteness permits large exclusion volumes, while the Uranian and Neptunian systems already possess cryogenic, magnetic, and deep-route infrastructure.

Production workers live under strict inventory, movement, and maintenance surveillance because tiny discrepancies carry military and catastrophic implications.

Neptune joins the Outer Systems Compact and resists Colonial Union attempts to place production under permanent emergency command.

After the Second Solar War, Neptunian antimatter fuels the first interstellar mission under joint inner-outer authorization.
