This policy should be the respectable public-facing one.

It likely includes:

* tax benefits
* housing priority
* child-care support
* parental leave
* education guarantees
* inheritance protections
* family medical care
* multi-generational housing support
* fertility treatment
* adoption support
* civic prestige for stable households
* relocation packages for families settling frontier zones

This is not necessarily coercive.

It can be framed as:

The Dominion does not force families into existence; it makes family formation materially survivable and socially honored.

That is much stronger than making it cartoonishly authoritarian.

Why it exists

For a long-lived or biologically immortal civilization, family policy becomes more complicated.

If people live indefinitely, reproduction cannot be unrestricted everywhere. Otherwise stable worlds eventually choke.

So the Family Incentive has to be paired with:

* world capacity ratings
* migration rights
* settlement credits
* frontier family bonuses
* reproduction timing guidance
* regional demographic planning
* adoption and guardianship systems
* artificial gestation regulation if applicable

The incentive is not just “have more children.”

It is:

form durable households that can carry culture across time.

That makes it compatible with Dominion continuity themes.
