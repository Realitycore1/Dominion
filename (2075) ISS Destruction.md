# ISS Destruction

## Event

In 2075, a Europolis transport approaches the International Space Station carrying replacement filters, six crew, and a compact relay package.

Europolis registers the relay as civilian communications. North Korea identifies it as a western targeting node. The Order of the Spear calls it evidence that Christian orbit has been surrendered to secular powers.

Chairwoman Kim Yo-jong authorizes a North Korean interceptor launch.

Seven minutes later, an explosion opens the transport docking adapter from inside. Station telemetry records the internal pressure spike. Ground radar records the interceptor entering terminal approach. Corrected timing places the events within less than one second of one another.

The Order of the Spear releases a prepared martyrdom manifesto. North Korea claims the internal explosion proves its missile did not cause the emergency. Europolis claims an interceptor fragment triggered the blast. Neo Xia privately concludes that both events occurred and publicly blames western militarization.

No surviving record establishes which event made recovery impossible.

## Orbital Consequences

- The station breaks into modules, trusses, arrays, cargo, and a widening debris field.
- Defensive platforms misclassify fragments as attack vehicles.
- Interceptors create additional debris.
- Communications, navigation, weather observation, and civilian launch routes degrade.
- Mei Liang issues an unauthorized independent debris warning before her signal disappears.

## Ground Impact

A heavy station section remains in decaying orbit for twenty-eight days.

Governments fail to agree on a controlled descent. Neo Xia blocks Europolis tug access. Europolis rejects North Korean tracking corrections. Japan requests immediate action.

The wreck reenters over the Sea of Japan and strikes the Niigata freight district:

- two grain warehouses are destroyed;
- the coastal rail line is severed;
- fragments strike farms and a school field;
- forty-one people die on the ground.

The ground casualties are memorialized beside missing station crew before governments separate the lists by political attribution.

## Strategic Consequence

The incident demonstrates that fragmented command can make every technically defensible delay collectively catastrophic.

It also leaves orbital warning systems degraded and politically distrusted three years before the Exchange.
