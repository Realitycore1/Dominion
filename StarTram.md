## Summary

Civil: Proo
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
StarTrams are planetary-scale maglev launch [[Systems]], enclosed vacuum tubes running across landscapes or mountains, accelerating cargo and craft to orbital or even interplanetary velocities. They are the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s horizontal space ladders, built where Space Elevators or Orbital Rings aren’t feasible or as redundant “hymns of ascent.”
Structure

Launch Tube: Hundreds–thousands of km long, suspended or tunneled through mountains/land.

Superconducting Coils: Generate magnetic fields to accelerate payloads to orbital speed.

Vacuum Enclosure: Evacuated tube prevents drag, shimmering with faint auroras from Concordia stabilization.

Capstone Exit: Tower spire where the tube vents into the upper atmosphere, releasing craft like arrows of [[Fire]].

Functions

Civil:
– Launch colony craft, cargo, or habitats into orbit at minimal cost.
– Move billions of tons of goods into orbital [[Infrastructure]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– [[Fire]] mass payloads as kinetic bombardments, essentially railguns the size of countries.
– Serve as planetary fortress spines, doubling as artillery.

Economic:
– Anchor entire planetary economies by connecting surface industries directly to orbit.

Variants

Surface StarTrams: Stretch across continents, built into land like luminous rivers.

Mountain StarTrams: Tunnelled through highlands or cliffs, capped with cathedral spires at summits.

Orbital StarTrams: Space-based launch tubes firing between moons, stations, or habitats.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Terrifying siege weaponry — planets can defend themselves with continental railguns.

Economic: The engine of industrial throughput, feeding Orbital Rings and Planetary Docks.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Tubes ribbed like cathedral vaults, glowing with inner [[Fire]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, trams look like black scars etched across continents.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Fields ripple in fractal harmonics along their length, luminous mandalas of motion.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A marvel of belonging — their prayers and goods rise to heaven daily on rails of [[Fire]].

To rebels: A leash and a threat — their own land can be weaponized into a planetary cannon.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Pure pragmatism cloaked in scripture — logistics and terror in one.

To myth: Called the “Amber Arrows”, said to be bows strung by the Empress, firing matter into the firmament.
