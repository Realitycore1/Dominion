## **XV. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Quetzalcoatl**

**Title:** Legio Quetzalcoatl – The Serpent Sun

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through majesty, speed, and divine terror. Legio Quetzalcoatl embody the feathered serpent of radiant skies, descending upon enemies as living avatars of storm and flame. Their creed: *“From the heavens we descend, with [[Fire]] and feathered glory.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Quetzalcoatl**

* **Silhouette:** Sleek yet ornate, armor layered with feather-like plating that cascades along the pauldrons, greaves, and helm crest. Serpentine curves blend with avian elegance.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit radiant emerald and turquoise light trails with shimmering feathered projections. Movement accompanied by illusions of wings and serpents of light.
* **Coloration:** Emerald green and turquoise primary tones, with radiant gold trim and crimson feathered highlights. Armor glimmers as though enameled with precious stone.
* **Helmet:** Serpentine helm crowned with feathered crests flowing backward. Visor shaped like a serpent’s maw lined with radiant fangs of light. Eyes glow with shifting hues of emerald and gold.
* **Specialization:** Solar-Serpent Reactors—[[Systems]] channeling plasma into radiant whip-like lashes or serpent-shaped streams of energy. Capable of striking foes with both ranged plasma whips and radiant burst strikes.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Quetzalcoatl Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Carbine and Rifle forms, configured for speed and radiant plasma bursts. [[Close]] combat often enhanced with plasma whips projected from forearm mounts.
* **Modification:** Weapons shimmer with feathered energy motifs, firing bursts of plasma that trail like radiant feathers in the air.
* **Aesthetic:** Gilded alloy with emerald and turquoise enamel inlays. Engraved with serpent and feather motifs that shimmer in combat.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Skirmishers and divine shock troops, descending rapidly from orbital deployments. Quetzalcoatl excel in swift strikes, overwhelming foes with radiant majesty and chaos.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their descent is heralded by visions of feathered serpents in the sky, radiant plumes, and the blinding light of emerald and gold. Enemies often mistake them for gods in wrath.
* **Legacy:** Legio Quetzalcoatl is remembered as the Serpent Sun—feathered harbingers of radiant destruction who descend like divine storms upon the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s enemies.

LEGION QUETZALCOATL — The Sky Serpent Cohort

Classification: High-Altitude Combat • Bio-Aeronautics

Profile

Masters of upper-atmosphere warfare, they used serpent-flight frames and feathered plasma gliders. Their battles resembled [[Ritual]] thunder dances in the stratosphere.

Their final fleet spiraled into a Dracoshian gravity rift. A single fossilized serpent-frame remains.

## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch and Book III Function

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Founding Verarch: [[Xolotl Quenari]].

Xolotl begins in Bellator-associated early war service, but after Outer Artemis and the Aether Singularity wound, he becomes the founding Verarch of Legio Quetzalcoatl.

Book III doctrine reflects:

- high-altitude shock;
- orbital descent;
- Aether-storm navigation;
- relay assault;
- impossible-route pursuit;
- memory-contaminated reconnaissance;
- rapid intervention before GalFed can stabilize a corridor.

Core line:

> Xolotl Quenari is the founding Verarch of Legio Quetzalcoatl, the Legion born from the first realization that speed, sky, Aether, and memory had become one battlefield.
