Classification: Spirits & Household Entities
Subtype: Domestic Guardian Spirits / Threshold Wards
Status: Localized, Persistent

⸻

Core Description

Domovoi are small, unseen spirits bound to homes, hearths, workshops, and family lineages. They rarely manifest fully, instead appearing as fleeting shapes in firelight, shadows beneath furniture, or disembodied sounds—footsteps, knocks, muttering.

When seen, they resemble:
	•	small, wiry humanoids,
	•	bearded or animal-featured,
	•	soot-stained or ash-dusted,
	•	eyes glowing faintly like embers.

They are not cute.
They are territorial.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Domovoi emerge where human habitation, repetition, and emotional imprint overlap over time.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Echoic Plane (memory, imprint)
	•	Secondary Influence: Oneiric (belief), Material Continuum
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms (Umbrae), Hollow Veins (older structures)

They are not summoned.
They condense from lived-in space.

A house does not gain a Domovoi because someone believes in it.
A Domovoi forms because the house remembers.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Domovoi possess localized intelligence.

They understand:
	•	routines,
	•	family hierarchies,
	•	tools,
	•	and territory boundaries.

They do not understand:
	•	abstract morality,
	•	distant authority,
	•	or cosmic law.

Their loyalty is not to people—but to place-as-lived.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Domovoi act as:
	•	guardians against minor spirits,
	•	stabilizers against planar bleed,
	•	early-warning systems for intrusion.

Common behaviors include:
	•	hiding or returning objects,
	•	sabotaging harmful actions,
	•	disrupting intruders,
	•	causing accidents to drive threats away.

Neglected or abused dwellings may cause Domovoi to:
	•	turn hostile,
	•	sabotage occupants,
	•	or mutate into poltergeist-like variants.

A destroyed home usually kills the Domovoi—unless it migrates with the family.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Domovoi do not bargain in words.

They respond to:
	•	respect,
	•	maintenance,
	•	offerings of food or warmth,
	•	continuity of routine.

They despise:
	•	abandonment,
	•	cruelty within the home,
	•	chaotic restructuring,
	•	planar contamination.

When angered, a Domovoi will not attack directly—it will make the space unlivable.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Bound to a specific location or lineage
	•	Severely weakened by null fields or identity erasure
	•	Cannot survive long-term outside habitation
	•	Vulnerable during major renovations or destruction

However:

As long as a home remembers being a home, the Domovoi endures.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Kobolds: Often confused; Domovoi are non-industrial and non-tribal
	•	Poltergeists: Domovoi may degrade into these if corrupted
	•	Kami: Domovoi are the domestic analogue to nature spirits
	•	Demons: Actively resisted by Domovoi within their territory
	•	Vampires: Domovoi often detect them before mortals do

⸻

Variants
	•	Hearth Domovoi – Fire and warmth guardians
	•	Workshop Domovoi – Tools, craft, and machines
	•	Farmstead Domovoi – Animals, barns, granaries
	•	Urban Domovoi – Apartment blocks, stairwells, old utilities

Urban variants are more brittle—and more irritable.
