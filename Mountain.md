Core Bias:

Vertical mass and elevated permanence

This is one of the most archetypal Stone expressions.

Reality behaves as if structure wants to rise upward through accumulated mass and rooted foundation.

Traits:
	•	massive verticality
	•	sacred or dominant high points
	•	difficult ascent
	•	strong hierarchy of elevation
	•	isolation through height
	•	deep-rooted foundations

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	existence is built in ranges and peaks
	•	power resides in what stands above
	•	depth and height are linked through load-bearing permanence

Common Expressions:
	•	mountain-worlds
	•	citadel civilizations
	•	vertical empires
	•	sacred summit realities
	•	naturally fortified cosmologies

Failure Mode:

Avalanche collapse / overburden fracture
Too much accumulated mass destabilizes the whole.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	fortress civilizations
	•	sacred vertical cosmologies
	•	“climb to transcendence” structures
	•	hierarchical basin orders
