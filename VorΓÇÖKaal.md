Classification: Behemoth Class – Tier XIII / Field Designate “Vor’Kaal” (The Moon-Taken)

Observation:
Entity observed standing upright against a forest horizon under a planetary moonrise. Silhouette broad, musculature hypertrophic beneath ribbed exoskeletal plating. Cranial dome smooth, obsidian-black, flanked by four primary horn structures forming a crown configuration. Forelimbs terminate in sickle digits; caudal extension tipped with barbed bone filament. Estimated height: 8.[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]–9.1 meters. Thermal imagery records stable body temperature despite exposure to subzero nocturnal air, implying internal energy recycling.

Contextual Analysis:
The Vor’Kaal is catalogued as a high-order predator apex variant believed to have originated from accelerated environmental adaptation following the first atmospheric resonance harmonization. Habitual territory includes the northern boreal perimeters surrounding the [[Craterheart]] Expanse. Field [[Reports]] suggest partial nocturnal bioluminescence localized to ventral patterns and horn ridges during lunar zenith. Temporal interference readings recorded within 400 meters indicate possible distortion fields centered around the subject—[[Time]] displacement variance measured at 0.003 standard seconds.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
Preserved in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Sovereign Mutation. The Vor’Kaal is viewed by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] theologians as the final articulation of [[Harmina]]’s self-defense principle—a biological counterpart to the Empire’s planetary armor [[Systems]]. In metaphysical exegesis, its image represents [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] purity under strain: when the [[Natural]] order mirrors the Empire’s perfection in unplanned form. Within the Ehreian Archives, the creature is not demonized but sanctified; the horned silhouette is considered an omen of planetary vigilance, the world’s will made visible under celestial light.
