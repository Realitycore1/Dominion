Structure

Orbital Lattices: Webs of satellites and strange-matter tethers encircling the planet or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

Field Curtains: Energy sheets (plasma, gravitic veils, particle screens) that prevent entry or exit.

Anchor Spires: [[Atlas Pillars]] connect orbital net to ground stations for enforcement and atmosphere sealing.

Relay Nodes: Broadcast warnings, deterrents, and Concordia hymns.

Functions

Containment: Nothing leaves without [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] sanction — ships, signals, even radiation can be filtered.

Observation: Embedded [[Computronium]] monitors every heartbeat, thoughtstream, and transmission.

Neutralization: If needed, the curtain doubles as a weapon — collapsing inward to sterilize surface populations.

Symbolism: Visible from below as a permanent aurora, the “sky-veil” that proves isolation.

Variants

Planetary Curtains: Lock down single worlds.

Religious: Known as the “Amber Veils of Judgment” — seen as the Empress drawing her hair across the heavens to forbid contact with the impure.
