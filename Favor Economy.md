•	Medium: promises, contracts, oaths, favors, magical compacts.
	•	Scope: Amber, Platina, Adamant ranks, as well as [[Angels]] and divine casters.
	•	Features:
	•	At this level, coin is irrelevant — an Amber who can collapse a fortress doesn’t want gold, they want land, protection, knowledge, or political leverage.
	•	Barter becomes symbolic — exchanging [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|relics]], ley-charts, blood-oaths, or rare essences instead of coin.
	•	Myrrin guilds formalize this with blood contracts; breaking one risks your soul.
	•	Favors from Adamants are worth kingdoms; one promise from a Platina sage might stabilize an empire.
	•	Weakness: inherently unstable — favors can be [[Forgotten]], twisted, or betrayed. Entire wars begin when one side claims the other “failed their oath.”
