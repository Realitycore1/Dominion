## Summary

Containment
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Nanite Disassemblers are self-replicating swarms of nanoscale machines or hive-driven constructs programmed to break down matter into base atoms, quarks, or even Concordia-patterned substrates. They are both industrial recyclers and weapons of annihilation.
Modes of Deployment

Industrial: Stationary arrays, reducing derelicts, asteroids, or wreckage into raw feedstock for Cornucopia Machines.

Tactical: Swarms unleashed against enemy fleets or fortifications — reducing them to dust.

Strategic: Planetary-scale release, turning entire cities, biospheres, or worlds into raw slurry.

Mechanisms

Molecular Unzipping: Breaks chemical bonds sequentially.

Atomic Harvesting: Captures liberated atoms for storage or beam transfer.

Quantum Disassembly: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]-level nanites can split even [[Exotic Matter]] forms.

Control Protocols: Always tied to [[Computronium]] Nodes to prevent grey-goo runaway.

Countermeasures & Safety

Concordia Harmonization: Each swarm carries a “signature hymn,” shutting down if out of resonance with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] command.

Whipple-Swarm Guardians: Patrol against accidental spread.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Deployed as “Amber Locusts” — dissolving fleets, cities, and worlds into nothing.

Religious: Seen as “the Unmakers’ [[Choirs]]” — mythologized as the Empress’s breath that reduces all falsehood back to truth.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Hive-towers that spew forth rivers of liquid-black swarms, ribbed like cathedral throats.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, deployment sites look like perfect expanding black clouds.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Swarms ripple with fractal wavefronts — not chaotic, but patterned geometry.

From the ground, a disassembler event looks like shadows eating reality, matter simply falling apart into dust and light.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Useful, but terrifying — the machines that build can also unmake.

To rebels: Utter despair — every fortress can be eaten, every defense consumed.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Perfect control — they alone decide what persists and what dissolves.

To myth: Called the “[[Choirs]] of Undoing”, believed to be [[Angels]] of entropy, marching at the Empress’s command.
 Vaults: Disassemblers are stored in frozen stasis until deployed.

Civil: Used for recycling, construction, and planetary cleanup. Old cities are fed to Disassemblers, their material reborn as new.

Economic: Provide endless raw materials, ensuring nothing is ever “waste.”

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Disassembly trails glow faintly with glyphs, as though the matter being erased is singing its own prayer of dissolution.
