## **XII. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Morrigan**

**Title:** Legio Morrigan – The Dread Harbingers

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through fear, chaos, and the slaughter of foes. Legio Morrigan embody the black wings of war, descending upon enemies as omens of doom and inevitable [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]]. Their creed: *“We are the carrion crows of destiny.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Morrigan**

* **Silhouette:** Lean, avian-inspired with sharp, angular plates shaped like feathers or wings folded into the armor’s form.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit flocks of spectral black-feathered projections that circle the battlefield, creating the illusion of being surrounded by carrion crows.
* **Coloration:** Jet black plating with iridescent green and violet sheens that shift like oil on water. Trimmed in blood-red highlights.
* **Helmet:** Visors styled as bird-like masks with hooked beaks, eyes glowing crimson or ghostly pale white. Some helms bear winglike crests sweeping backward.
* **Specialization:** Harbinger Field Generators—emit auditory hallucinations of wingbeats and shrieking crows, disorienting enemies while amplifying fear responses.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Morrigan Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Carbine and Rifle forms for rapid, harrying [[Fire]]. Heavy Rifles are shaped with long, spear-like bayonets resembling talons.
* **Modification:** Weapons [[Fire]] shrieking sonic-charged projectiles or plasma bolts that leave trails like black feathers dissolving into smoke.
* **Aesthetic:** Jet black alloy with feather-etched engravings and violet energy conduits. Blades and bayonets shaped like talons.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Skirmishers and terror troops. Morrigan excel at sowing chaos, breaking enemy morale, and harrying fleeing forces.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their descent is heralded by the sound of wings and the cries of phantom crows. Enemies often rout before direct engagement.
* **Legacy:** Legio Morrigan is remembered as the Dread Harbingers, the carrion crows of war who feast upon the fallen and leave battlefields shrouded in dread and ruin.
