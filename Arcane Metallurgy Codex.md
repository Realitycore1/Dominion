VI. Alloy Interaction Types

Constructive Resonance

Alloy increases magical strength.

Example
Gold + Copper → increased magical conduction

⸻

Stabilizing Resonance

Magic becomes easier to control.

Example
Silver + Iron → stable enchantments

⸻

Destructive Resonance

Magical effects cancel out.

Example
Lead + Uranium → anti-magic material
