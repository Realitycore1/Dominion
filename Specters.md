Classification: Deathbound Predators & Corrupted Remnants
Subtype: Malice-Eroded Spirits / Phase-Shifted Undead
Status: Uncommon, Highly Dangerous

⸻

Core Description

Specters are disembodied undead entities formed when a ghost loses coherence but does not dissipate. What remains is a predatory, semi-sentient presence—often humanoid in outline, indistinct, flickering, or skeletal in silhouette.

A Specter is not someone who died.
It is what hunger, rage, or corruption left behind.

They are accompanied by cold, distortion, light dimming, and the sensation of being watched by something that no longer understands watching.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Specters are degraded ghosts, stabilized not by purpose (like revenants) but by negative resonance—malice, fear, hatred, or ambient corruption.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Shadow Realms (Umbrae)
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice, Null-edge erosion
	•	Realm Interface: Material ↔ Shadow bleed zones

They are not souls anymore.
They are functions of harm.

⸻

Formation Pathways

Specters form when:
	•	a ghost lingers too long without resolution,
	•	Malice saturates a haunting site,
	•	necromantic interference destabilizes a spirit,
	•	repeated trauma loops overwrite identity,
	•	Null erosion strips memory but not motion.

Ghost → Specter is decay, not evolution.

⸻

Intelligence & Behavior

Specters possess low to moderate cunning, but no empathy or memory.

They:
	•	recognize prey,
	•	learn patrol routes,
	•	avoid stronger entities,
	•	exploit fear responses.

They do not:
	•	converse meaningfully,
	•	remember names,
	•	hesitate.

Any apparent communication is mimicry.

⸻

Predatory Mechanisms

Specters feed on:
	•	fear,
	•	emotional distress,
	•	vital warmth,
	•	lingering soul-resonance.

They attack by:
	•	phasing through matter,
	•	draining vitality,
	•	inducing paralysis or panic,
	•	tearing at spiritual coherence rather than flesh.

Victims often feel “emptied” rather than wounded.

⸻

Physical & Metaphysical Traits
	•	Intangible or semi-solid
	•	Vulnerable during materialization
	•	Immune to mundane weapons
	•	Susceptible to light, fire, and Grace-aligned forces
	•	Disrupted by consecrated geometry

Specters cannot cross strong ward-lines without degradation.

⸻

Weaknesses & Termination
	•	Grace saturation
	•	Ritual purification
	•	Phoenix-grade cleansing
	•	Forced identity restoration (rare)
	•	Prolonged Null exposure (dangerous but effective)

Violence alone rarely works unless paired with containment.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Ghosts: Prey or raw material
	•	Wraiths: Sometimes subordinate, sometimes rivals
	•	Revenants: Destroy Specters incidentally
	•	Liches: Bind or harvest Specters
	•	Demons: Weaponize Specters
	•	Reapers: Actively cull them

Specters are undesirable even to Hell unless controlled.

⸻

Variants
	•	Haunt Specters: Location-bound predators
	•	Hunger Specters: Roaming drain-entities
	•	War Specters: Battlefield-born, aggressive
	•	Null Specters: Near-erasure forms
	•	Bound Specters: Controlled by necromancers

Null Specters are especially dangerous—they erase meaning, not life.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Ghosts: Specters lack identity
	•	vs Wraiths: Specters are less focused, more chaotic
	•	vs Poltergeists: Specters hunt, not disrupt
	•	vs Revenants: Specters lack purpose
	•	vs Shades: Specters are active predators

This preserves escalation clarity.
