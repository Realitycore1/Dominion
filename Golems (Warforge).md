1. Clay Golems
	•	Definition: The classic archetype, drawn from Jewish mysticism.
	•	Traits: Humanoid form sculpted from clay; inscribed with runes for life.
	•	Symbolism: Obedience, creation as burden, life from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Stone Golems
	•	Definition: Carved from rock, statues given animation.
	•	Traits: Immense durability, slow but unstoppable, often guardians of [[Temples]].
	•	Symbolism: Permanence, guardianship, tradition embodied.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Iron/Metal Golems
	•	Definition: Forged of iron, steel, or bronze.
	•	Traits: Armored hides, resistant to magic, battlefield juggernauts.
	•	Symbolism: Industry and war given flesh.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Flesh Golems
	•	Definition: Patchwork beings stitched from corpses, animated by alchemy or lightning.
	•	Traits: Grotesque bodies, unstable minds, immense strength.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]]’s fear of playing god; the cost of unnatural creation.

⸻

5. Crystal Golems
	•	Definition: Constructs made of crystal or gem, infused with resonance.
	•	Traits: Refracts light into beams, psychic resonance, fragile but potent.
	•	Symbolism: Purity of craft; the spirit trapped in stone.

⸻

6. Elemental Golems
	•	Definition: Golems infused with elemental power ([[Fire]], frost, storm, magma, shadow).
	•	Traits: Hybrid of substance and spirit, bodies shaped by primal forces.
	•	Symbolism: Nature harnessed by artifice.

⸻

7. Shadow Golems
	•	Definition: Animated statues of obsidian, smoke, or cursed essence.
	•	Traits: Shifting silhouettes, necrotic auras, cold guardians of the [[Abyss]].
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of creation; guardians of forbidden knowledge.

⸻

8. Warforged [[Soldiers]]
	•	Definition: Mass-produced humanoid constructs built for war.
	•	Traits: Metal or stone bodies with cores of magic, designed as [[Soldiers]].
	•	Symbolism: The dehumanization of war; life as weapon.

⸻

9. Warforged Scholars & Artificials
	•	Definition: Warforged who evolve beyond their battlefield roles.
	•	Traits: Independent thought, modified frames (scribes, artificers, scouts).
	•	Symbolism: The question of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]: tool or person?

⸻

10. Colossi (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary [[Giant]] constructs, city-sized golems or war machines.
	•	Traits: Move mountains, defend nations, powered by divine or lost magic.
	•	Symbolism: Civilization’s hubris; creation that rivals gods.
