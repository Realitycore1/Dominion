Titania is the largest moon of Uranus and becomes the primary human foothold in the system.

Why Titania Matters
	•	Large enough for long-term infrastructure
	•	Geologically fractured → easy subsurface access
	•	Stable orbit
	•	Lower micrometeorite risk than inner-belt bodies

Titania is not exciting.
That’s why it works.

⸻

Titania Station

Titania hosts a deep subsurface station, carved into ice-rock strata.

Structural Characteristics
	•	Vault-style habitation zones
	•	Thick natural radiation shielding
	•	Artificial gravity sections (slow rotation)
	•	Minimal surface exposure

Surface elements are sparse:
	•	comm arrays
	•	thermal exhausts
	•	navigation beacons
	•	cargo elevators

No skyline.
No sprawl.
Just buried permanence.

⸻

Function

Titania Station serves as:
	•	Uranian system administration
	•	logistics coordination node
	•	refueling and repair stop
	•	population stabilization site
	•	long-duration habitation testbed

If something in Uranian space goes wrong, Titania absorbs the shock.

⸻

Cultural Profile

Titania residents are:
	•	methodical
	•	introverted
	•	institutionally minded
	•	extremely patient

They are comfortable with:
	•	years between major arrivals
	•	long supply cycles
	•	delayed communication
	•	deep isolation

Titania culture resembles early submarine crews, scaled to a civilization.
