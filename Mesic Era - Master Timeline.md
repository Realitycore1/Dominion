# Mesic Era - Master Timeline

## Pre-Landfall: Ark Exile

### Ferox Aftermath and Ark Conversion

What happened: the surviving revolutionary population fled aboard a retrofitted O'Neill cylinder known as the Ark.

What resulted: the Ark Provisional Authority became the continuity government, and survival law replaced inherited state law.

### Ark Drift

What happened: communications failed to synchronize with old systems, rationing hardened, and births, riots, clinics, funerals, and religious practice adapted to shipboard life.

What resulted: the Ark became a society, not only a vessel.

### GalFed Discovery

What happened: intercepted transmissions revealed GalFed, a stable intergalactic order with advanced FTL, infrastructure, medical systems, schools, and patrol networks.

What resulted: GalFed became both temptation and threat. The Ark began planning around concealment.

### Discovery Clock

What happened: a classified Ark team spent forty-seven months decoding GalFed traffic.

What resulted: GalFed knowledge became the hidden root of later Harminan defense schools, orbital monitoring, Project Ra, and planetary defense thinking.

### Harmina Identified

What happened: GalFed navigation data revealed Harmina as a difficult but habitable world with low development priority.

What resulted: the Ark chose Harmina because it offered a chance to vanish.

## Year 0 AL: Landing

What happened: the first colonists landed in Aureth and established New Aurora under crimson fog, corrosive weather, and incomplete knowledge.

What resulted: Year 0 After Landfall became the Harminan dating anchor.

## Years 0-2 AL: Survival Doctrine

What happened: first deaths, burial failures, wall failures, storm weeks, fungal food production, hollow-crust discoveries, and titan sightings changed every assumption.

What resulted: New Aurora adopted flexible environmental doctrine and began moving critical life underground.

## Year 3 AL: First Survey Season

What happened: New Aurora chartered formal exploration beyond the settlement.

What resulted: surveyors, ecologists, cartographers, and weather technicians became the first interpreters of Harmina beyond the wall.

## Years 3-17 AL: Harmina Becomes Knowable

What happened: Glassleaf forests, Walking Reefs, Echo Bloom fields, Needlebacks, Lantern swarms, Hollow Runners, Watchers, Lantern Men, and Stone Pilgrims entered field reports and folklore.

What resulted: Harmina stopped being merely hostile and became mysterious, beautiful, and culturally generative.

## Years 18-40 AL: First Harminans

What happened: Harminan-born children grew up treating the planet as normal. Earth and Ferox became lessons rather than memories.

What resulted: Harminan identity began before formal nationhood.

## Years 41-93 AL: Slow Death of Earth Culture

What happened: language, holidays, schools, religion, food, maps, songs, and family practices drifted.

What resulted: Amber recognized that preservation could not prevent transformation.

## Year 95 AL: Expansionist Petition

What happened: settlement leaders and route communities pushed for expansion authority beyond New Aurora's central approvals.

What resulted: the first major autonomy dispute crystallized around routes, underways, faith, marriage, and local governance.

## Year 96 AL: Charter Debate

What happened: New Aurora debated whether settlement autonomy could be recognized without dissolving common survival law.

What resulted: political legitimacy began separating from emergency legitimacy.

## Years 101-108 AL: Convoy Years

What happened: convoys left New Aurora to build permanent lives across Harmina.

What resulted: geography began producing future nations.

## Year 122 AL: Letters and Second Hymnal

What happened: settlements stopped asking New Aurora for ordinary permission, and shared religious music split into local forms.

What resulted: cultural divergence became emotionally visible.

## Year 151 AL: Accords

What happened: settlements formed practical agreements around resources, routes, shelters, records, and apprenticeships.

What resulted: proto-nations began acting as power blocs without naming themselves nations.

## Year 185 AL: Skyview Day

What happened: children were shown the sky, orbital systems, and Harmina's larger place in space.

What resulted: the old GalFed fear was translated into civic education and planetary identity.

## Years 189-190s AL: Recognition and Body Question

What happened: welfare, family incentives, biological immortality, engineered beauty, pain reduction, muscle retention, clone labor, hybrid labor, and quality-of-life biology became political debates.

What resulted: the states began defining what kind of human future they wanted.

## Year 196 AL: Arms Race

What happened: autonomous weapons, power armor, tactical nuclear systems, Project Ra, skywatch infrastructure, deep mantle mining, super-soldier programs, and planetary defense systems accelerated.

What resulted: the old GalFed clock returned as justification for weapons that could also be used against other Harminans.

## Year 251 AL: Founder Clause

What happened: the Equilon state government attempted to draw Amber into active government as founder and last technically legitimate continuity body.

What resulted: Amber became a constitutional battlefield.

## Year 252 AL: First Strike

What happened: Atlovaian and F.S.S.R. forces struck New Aurora during the Founder Session.

What resulted: the New World War began.
