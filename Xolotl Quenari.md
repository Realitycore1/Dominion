## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch of Legio Quetzalcoatl

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Xolotl Quenari is the founding Verarch of [[Legio Quetzalcoatl]].

Continuity bridge:

> Xolotl begins in Bellator-associated early war service, but after Outer Artemis and the Aether Singularity wound, he becomes the founding Verarch of Legio Quetzalcoatl.

Xolotl becomes the wounded prophet of the relay age. During the Outer Artemis crisis, he briefly connects with the Aether Singularity and witnesses fragments of what came before GalFed:

- pre-GalFed civilizations;
- the true or partial origin of the Family Event;
- destruction of spark cognition;
- the seven founding survivor civilizations;
- first containment choices;
- buried guilt behind GalFed;
- the reason GalFed fears Aether;
- the reason GalFed benefits from that fear.

Symptoms of the wound:

- speaks names from dead pre-GalFed languages;
- recognizes symbols no human has seen;
- remembers seven skies around a dark center;
- knows the feeling of minds losing spark;
- reacts to Aether storms before instruments detect them;
- cannot distinguish testimony from contamination;
- carries memories GalFed would kill to suppress and Dominion courts cannot easily admit.

Legal problem:

> Can a memory be evidence if the witness did not live it?

Legio Quetzalcoatl doctrine grows from his wound: high-altitude shock, orbital descent, Aether-storm navigation, relay assault, impossible-route pursuit, memory-contaminated reconnaissance, and rapid intervention before GalFed can stabilize a corridor.

Core line:

> Xolotl Quenari is the founding Verarch of Legio Quetzalcoatl, the Legion born from the first realization that speed, sky, Aether, and memory had become one battlefield.
