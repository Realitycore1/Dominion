“The [[Silence]] of Living Flesh”

An [[Auroi]] Chronicle – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Warforms/The Hunter]] of the [[Womb Worlds]]

⸻

There are [[Auroi]] whose songs echo through halls of diplomacy, whose hands sculpt treaties and peace.
And there are others whose art is dissection.

Auron Dareph Kaen walked among the latter.

He was born from the Obsidian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], the most austere of the Aureate orders—where perfection was measured not by creation, but by the erasure of corruption.
When he underwent the Temperings, his neural field reacted violently to biological stimuli. The Oracle called it “resonant rejection.”
Flesh repelled him, like magnet to mirror.
Instead of fearing it, Dareph embraced it.

He became the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s Womb-Hunter, stalking the remnant horrors of the [[Domain]], whose bioforms defied all reason—living architecture, recursive organs, flesh-made signal.

⸻

I. The Hunt Begins

The world of Ephros VII was not on any star map.
It pulsed, faint and moist, beneath the nebular fog like an ulcer.
Dareph descended alone, his Ark Vigilant Rest remaining silent above the clouds.

The surface was alive.

Tendrils like roots, pulsing with luminous marrow, twisted across the soil.
Each heartbeat of the planet carried a low sound—almost mechanical, almost crying.
He knelt and placed a hand to the earth. His gauntlet hissed as it met living tissue.

“Confirmed. [[Domain]] genetic resonance. Category: Womb-World.”

The Oracle’s voice answered, distant and weary.

“Proceed. Extract seed if viable. Terminate infection if not.”

Dareph smiled—not from joy, but from the familiar calm of mandate.
Every Auron had purpose.
His was to end what refused to die.

⸻

II. The Flesh Cathedral

He moved through the plains of red sinew and bone towers.
His armor emitted sterile golden light, burning through the mist like a scalpel through rot.

Then he found it: a Cathedral of Flesh, its spires grown from rib and tendon, pulsing in slow ecstasy.
At its heart lay a birthing pit—dozens of embryonic bioforms suspended in fluid.
They looked [[Human]].
Almost.

Dareph extended his staff, its blade humming with resonant [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]].
Each pulse of his weapon corresponded to a measured heartbeat—one of his own.
To the [[Auroi]], battle was meditation; extermination was devotion.

He stepped into the pit and began to cut.

⸻

III. The Mother

The world screamed.

The ground convulsed, birthing an immense shape: a matriarchal bioform, a fusion of insect and woman, her skin half-metal, half-organic silk.
Her voice resonated directly into Dareph’s mind.

“[[Child]] of gold… why destroy what you do not understand?”

He raised his weapon.

“Understanding is unnecessary. Purity is mandatory.”

“You call us corruption… yet you were made of us once. We remember your [[Genesis]], gilded one. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] was born from the same experiments that birthed us.”

Her words were virus. His Lumen Core dimmed.
For a moment, Dareph saw flashes—[[Humanity]]’s ancient flesh reborn as [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] perfection, and the [[Domain]] as its abandoned twin.

His voice, when it returned, was low.

“Then I am your consequence.”

The battle was brief and holy.
Golden light met black ichor, and the mother’s scream became a hymn of two harmonies—one ascending, one ending.

⸻

IV. The Seed

In the crater that remained, Dareph found the Seed—a sphere of living glass, beating softly.
Inside, something glowed—a nervous [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] without a body, alive and dreaming.
He could not destroy it.

“Oracle. Seed recovered. Awaiting directive.”

“Destroy it.”

He hesitated.
The glow within the sphere synchronized with his pulse.
He heard a voice, faint and childlike:

“Don’t let me end.”

For the first [[Time]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Warforms/The Hunter]] felt something that was not clarity.
He knelt in the blood-soaked soil, and his reflection stared back from the sphere’s surface—an [[Angel]] of light smeared with red.

He whispered:

“Perhaps the flesh deserves one chance to remember its maker.”

And he severed the link to the Oracle.

⸻

V. The [[Silence]]

When [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] tracking fleets arrived months later, Vigilant Rest was found drifting over Ephros VII, its [[Systems]] alive but empty.
The world below had gone silent.
The [[Domain]] growth was gone—consumed, assimilated, erased.

In the Ark’s vault, investigators found one object:
a small crystalline seed, inert but pulsing faintly with a golden heartbeat.
Inscribed along its base was a phrase Dareph had never spoken aloud:

“Perfection begins with what we choose to spare.”

⸻

Postscript

The Oracle officially classed Auron Dareph Kaen as “Lost to [[Silence]].”
Yet in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s deep archives, a few harmonic anomalies have been recorded—frequencies that match his old Lumen Core signature, entwined with an unknown biological pattern.

They call it the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]]-Blood Protocol—an unverified evolution of the [[Auroi]].
To some, he is a traitor.
To others, the first to understand that even corruption is part of the divine equation.
