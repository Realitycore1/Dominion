# Domain Flesh Pits

## Classification

- **Type:** Biological reclamation and nutrient-processing infrastructure
- **Primary Function:** Breakdown, sorting, storage, and redistribution of organic and inorganic matter
- **Secondary Function:** Feedstock preparation for [[Biospawn Chambers]]
- **Command Organ:** [[Flesh Cores]]
- **Typical Locations:** Hive ships, Womb Worlds, Supernals, occupation sites, siege foundries, planetary digestion complexes
- **Domain Designation:** Flesh Pit
- **Technical Classification:** Proteolytic Reclamation Reservoir

---

# Overview

A **Flesh Pit** is a large biological processing pool filled with liquefied tissues, digestive acids, catalytic enzymes, suspended organelles, mineral solvents, and Wetwire-derived sorting organisms.

Its function is not simple disposal.

A Flesh Pit converts living and nonliving matter into standardized biological feedstocks that can be redistributed throughout Domain infrastructure or used to construct new bioforms.

Within the Domain, a dead body, ruined machine, shattered building, contaminated ecosystem, or captured organic structure may all be treated as recoverable material.

> **Nothing entering a Flesh Pit is discarded. It is translated into available life.**

Flesh Pits are controlled by [[Flesh Cores]], which regulate digestion, chemical separation, memory screening, contamination control, and distribution into nearby [[Biospawn Chambers]].

---

# Structure

## Surface Reservoir

The visible pit is a broad basin of dense, layered fluid.

Its surface may appear:

- black-red;
- oily gray;
- pale and opalescent;
- dark green;
- or nearly transparent,

depending on the materials currently being processed.

The reservoir contains:

- liquefied tissues;
- suspended connective fibers;
- digestive acids;
- base amino acids;
- lipids;
- nucleic acids;
- mineral salts;
- dissolved metals;
- synthetic polymers;
- Wetwire fragments;
- and microbial processing colonies.

Large pits can span hundreds of meters. Planetary or Supernal-scale reclamation basins may occupy entire valleys, caverns, or internal seas.

## Digestive Membrane

Beneath the surface lies a living membrane composed of:

- muscular filtration tissue;
- enzyme-secreting glands;
- immune nodes;
- ciliated transport channels;
- and chemical-recognition organs.

This membrane changes composition depending on incoming matter.

It can increase acidity, isolate contaminants, produce specialized solvents, or suspend digestion while a material is analyzed.

## Stratification Layers

Flesh Pits separate matter into functional layers.

### Upper Layer

Contains light oils, gases, foams, and microbial cultures.

### Organic Suspension Layer

Contains partially digested tissue, proteins, lipids, and cellular material.

### Acidic Reduction Layer

Breaks complex matter into simpler compounds.

### Mineral Sediment Layer

Collects metals, salts, ceramics, bone minerals, and other dense materials.

### Core Intake Layer

Routes processed feedstock toward the Flesh Core and connected biospawn infrastructure.

# Processing Sequence

## 1. Intake

Material enters through:

- feeding maws;
- conveyor tendrils;
- vascular channels;
- corpse sluices;
- excavation ducts;
- docking tubes;
- battlefield harvest organisms;
- or planetary ingestion cavities.

Matter may arrive intact, crushed, shredded, chemically softened, or pre-sorted.

## 2. Recognition

Sensory tissues determine:

- biological composition;
- toxicity;
- infection risk;
- genetic value;
- structural usefulness;
- technological content;
- and possible retained personhood.

Sapient remains or active minds are not automatically processed under orthodox Domain law. They are screened for continuity, recoverable memory, and legal status.

Militant or corrupted branches may ignore this safeguard.

## 3. Reduction

Digestive acids and enzymes break matter into:

- amino acids;
- fatty acids;
- sugars;
- nucleotides;
- mineral ions;
- useful polymers;
- conductive compounds;
- and stable chemical precursors.

Nonliving matter is dissolved, fragmented, or incorporated through specialized chelation and mineral-processing organisms.

## 4. Purification

Immune colonies remove:

- pathogens;
- incompatible proteins;
- unstable Wetwire;
- toxins;
- mutagenic residue;
- hostile nanotechnology;
- and corrupted command signals.

## 5. Classification

The Flesh Core assigns recovered matter to categories such as:

- structural biomass;
- neural substrate;
- vascular tissue;
- armor material;
- reproductive feedstock;
- metabolic reserve;
- conductive tissue;
- memory-compatible tissue;
- mineral reinforcement;
- or waste requiring further reduction.

## 6. Redistribution

Processed matter is pumped into:

- [[Biospawn Chambers]];
- repair organs;
- vessel tissues;
- Thrall foundries;
- Womb World nurseries;
- armor-growing chambers;
- nutrient arteries;
- and emergency biomass reserves.

# Flesh Cores

A **Flesh Core** is the command and regulatory organ of a Flesh Pit complex.

It functions as:

- chemical governor;
- metabolic scheduler;
- contamination authority;
- inventory system;
- biospawn allocator;
- and local command voice.

A Flesh Core continuously monitors:

- acidity;
- temperature;
- nutrient density;
- genetic diversity;
- contamination;
- throughput;
- heat generation;
- and chamber demand.

It decides what the pit digests, what it preserves, what it rejects, and what it sends onward.

## Relationship to Biospawn Chambers

The Flesh Core links the reclamation pit directly to nearby [[Biospawn Chambers]].

The chain is:

```text
Harvested Matter
â Flesh Pit
â Flesh Core Classification
â Nutrient and Genetic Feedstock
â Biospawn Chamber
â New Bioform
```

The Flesh Pit reduces matter.

The Flesh Core interprets and allocates it.

The Biospawn Chamber gives it form.

# Matter Sources

Flesh Pits can process:

## Living Matter

- livestock;
- plants;
- fungi;
- microbial cultures;
- damaged Domain organisms;
- captured ecosystems;
- and voluntary biological donations.

## Dead Organic Matter

- corpses;
- ruined Thralls;
- battlefield remains;
- dead forests;
- waste tissue;
- failed bioforms;
- and expired growth media.

## Nonliving Matter

- metals;
- plastics;
- concrete;
- ceramics;
- glass;
- composites;
- synthetic fibers;
- carbon structures;
- and machine components.

The Domain cannot convert every material with equal efficiency.

Some substances require:

- specialized acids;
- extreme heat;
- mechanical breakdown;
- symbiotic microbes;
- or long processing periods.

Flesh Pits remain constrained by chemistry, energy, heat rejection, and time.

# Ethical and Legal Status

## Orthodox Domain Doctrine

Orthodox hives distinguish between matter and personhood.

Before a body is reduced, the Flesh Core should determine whether it contains:

- active cognition;
- recoverable memory;
- continuity-bearing neural patterns;
- an integrated Domain mind;
- or a person eligible for restoration.

Deliberately processing a recoverable person may constitute:

- destruction of continuity;
- unlawful assimilation;
- memory murder;
- or heresy.

## Ambiguous Cases

Controversies include:

- bodies containing partial memories;
- Converted Thralls with uncertain selfhood;
- damaged synthetic-biological minds;
- corpses with reconstructable neural records;
- hostile bioforms carrying copied personalities;
- and persons requesting permanent dissolution.

## Militant Branches

Militant hives may classify enemies as biological resources once legal personhood is revoked.

This is one of the clearest points where Domain warfare becomes atrocity.

## Symmorphica-Corrupted Pits

Post-Severance pits influenced by the Symmorphica may:

- preserve suffering responses;
- recycle minds without continuity safeguards;
- cultivate unstable hybrid tissue;
- and deliberately retain hostile memory fragments.

Such pits are considered corrupted even by many surviving Domain factions.

# Operational Scales

## Field Flesh Pit

A temporary battlefield pool used to recycle casualties, wreckage, and local matter.

## Hive Flesh Pit

A permanent installation serving a city, vessel, or major colony.

## Womb Basin

A continental-scale reclamation reservoir feeding planetary biospawn systems.

## Supernal Digestive Sea

An internal ocean within a [[Domain Supernal]], capable of processing fleets, asteroids, and harvested planetary material.

## Planetary Reclamation Pit

A world-scale processing network used during conversion, reconstruction, or ecological replacement.

---

# Defensive Properties

Flesh Pits defend themselves through:

- corrosive fluid;
- immune organisms;
- submerged Thralls;
- pressure pulses;
- sealing membranes;
- toxic vapor;
- chemical sterilization;
- and emergency coagulation.

A threatened pit may harden its surface into armor, drain into lower chambers, flood nearby corridors, or release digestion organisms.

---

# Vulnerabilities

## Heat Accumulation

Digestive reduction produces substantial heat.

Overloading a pit can cause:

- enzyme collapse;
- tissue death;
- gas expansion;
- pressure rupture;
- and uncontrolled chemical reactions.

## Flesh Core Dependence

Destroying or isolating the Flesh Core may cause:

- misclassification;
- uncontrolled acidity;
- contamination;
- feedstock loss;
- biospawn deformity;
- and failure of connected chambers.

## Command Disruption

Without a valid command voice, a pit may:

- continue its last digestion cycle;
- reject all new matter;
- consume indiscriminately;
- enter dormancy;
- or develop a feral local ecology.

## Non-Corrupt Recall Systems

State-integrity restoration can prevent processed Domain matter from stabilizing into new biological forms or force recently altered tissue back toward an earlier configuration.

# Visual Aesthetic

From above, a Flesh Pit resembles a dark industrial reservoir whose surface moves with slow internal circulation.

Common features include:

- red fissures;
- black reflective fluid;
- pale floating membranes;
- ribbed retaining walls;
- feeding tendrils;
- bone sluices;
- vapor;
- submerged lights;
- and pulsing vascular outlets.

The environment should appear engineered and functional rather than randomly grotesque.

Every maw, gland, tube, and membrane has a clear role in digestion, filtration, transport, defense, or heat management.

> **A Flesh Pit is not a grave. It is a factory that refuses to recognize the permanence of waste.**

---

# Strategic Role

Flesh Pits allow the Domain to:

- recycle battlefield losses;
- exploit local environments;
- repair living ships;
- sustain invasions without conventional supply chains;
- convert wreckage into armor and tissue;
- preserve scarce chemistry;
- and supply continuous biospawn production.

A Domain force with an intact Flesh Pit and Flesh Core can turn a devastated battlefield into the raw material for its next army.

Destroying the enemy may therefore strengthen it unless the remains are sterilized, removed, or denied to the reclamation network.

---

# Canon Summary

- Flesh Pits are biological reclamation reservoirs.
- They process living and nonliving matter.
- They reduce matter into standardized chemical and biological feedstock.
- Flesh Cores govern digestion, purification, classification, and distribution.
- Biospawn Chambers use the processed material to construct new bioforms.
- The process is material, chemical, and biological rather than magical.
- Orthodox hives screen for recoverable personhood before reduction.
- Militant and corrupted branches may ignore those safeguards.
- Heat rejection and Flesh Core dependence are the primary weaknesses.
