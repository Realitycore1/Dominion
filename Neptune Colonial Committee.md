# Neptune Colonial Committee

The Neptune Colonial Committee coordinates inhabited settlements, remote power stations, accelerator arrays, navigation platforms, exclusion volumes, worker transit, and antimatter transfer. Its mature canonical role develops during 3300–3425.

Neptune manufactures antimatter; it does not mine it. Production remains physically separated from habitation. Government therefore operates through instruments, delayed communication, power schedules, maintenance disclosure, and clearly marked volumes no inhabited traffic may enter.

After a classified replacement delay contributes to a containment incident and two worker deaths, local worker review gains access to maintenance chains even when mission purpose remains secret. This becomes a defining committee responsibility: residents carrying strategic risk must be able to inspect the systems placing them at risk.

Neptune joins the Outer Systems Compact in 3425 and becomes a strategic target during the Second Solar War. The postwar settlement places antimatter infrastructure under joint safety control.

For mission-grade fuel production and Neptune’s contribution to the *Deliverance*, see [[Neptunian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
