I. The Infernal Core (True [[Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)]])

1. Ashen City
	•	Tormented:
	•	Wake to endless bureaucratic demands, forged contracts written in burning ink.
	•	Forced to build, only to see structures collapse back into ash.
	•	Daily routine: stand in lines that never end, labor for goals that erase themselves.
	•	Natives:
	•	Demonic scribes, overseers, and merchants thrive in the false [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] of [[Betrayal]].
	•	Power measured in oaths broken, deals enforced by [[Pain]].
	•	Life is a parody of a bustling metropolis — everyone busy, nothing real.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Nihilarium
	•	Tormented:
	•	Drift waist-deep in oily black [[Fire]] that burns hope instead of flesh.
	•	Consciousness erodes — waking each day feels emptier, memories dissolve.
	•	Routine is monotony: standing, staring, forgetting.
	•	Natives:
	•	Shades of shadow that feed on despair.
	•	They “fish” souls with [[Chains]], feeding on the moments when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] fades.
	•	Their lives are tides of despair, ebbing and flowing around the sea.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Abyssal Vault
	•	Tormented:
	•	Rare; only the most cursed souls are thrown here, experiencing [[Time]] collapse.
	•	A day can be an eternity; an eternity can feel like a second.
	•	Natives:
	•	Warden-[[Demons]] patrol halls of impossible geometry.
	•	Their duty is not freedom, but containment — even they fear the things chained here.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is vigilance, broken by shrieks of things that should not exist.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Broken Mount
	•	Tormented:
	•	Forced to climb forever, bodies crumbling into ash and regrowing at the base.
	•	Each “day” is another futile ascent.
	•	Natives:
	•	[[Demons]] of ambition climb alongside, whipping or lashing mortals.
	•	Hierarchs set endless contests: who can climb highest before the ground shatters.

⸻

5. Womb of Carrion
	•	Tormented:
	•	Live inside endless flesh corridors. Some wake in the stomach of beasts, some as tumors feeding the mass.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is digestion and regrowth, individuality dissolving into meat.
	•	Natives:
	•	Maggot-lords and carrion beasts thrive in putrefaction.
	•	Their society is [[Hunger]] — they eat, breed, and ooze.
	•	To them, life is abundance: everything is meat.

⸻

6. Plains of Iron
	•	Tormented:
	•	Wake already armed, already bleeding, forced to fight endless wars.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] is temporary: each “morning” you respawn, body aching, banners screaming.
	•	Natives:
	•	Demonic generals and infernal knights glory in strategy and slaughter.
	•	To them, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is [[Ritual]] battle, drilling troops, and rewriting [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] with blood.

⸻

7. Cavern of Echoes
	•	Tormented:
	•	Every thought they mutter returns amplified, tearing into flesh.
	•	Lies they once told in life now manifest as storm-wraiths that torture them.
	•	Natives:
	•	[[Demons]] of sound and language thrive in storms of speech.
	•	They spend each day twisting words into blades, hunting meaning itself.

⸻

8. Throne of [[Chains]]
	•	Tormented:
	•	Shackled as literal links in the throne. Each “day” is timeless servitude, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] flattened into a binding.
	•	Natives:
	•	Archdemons themselves are chained, ruling yet never free.
	•	Their daily [[(2030) Existence]] is paradox: absolute power, absolute enslavement.

II. Inner Peripheries (Cultural Refractions)
	•	Gehenna of [[Fire]]: Tormented boil in furnaces, while [[Fire]]-wraiths herd them like cattle. [[Demons]] treat this as livestock management.
	•	Tartarus: Mortals live in pits, digging endlessly; jailer-spirits chain them for sport. Natives live in hierarchies of punishment.
	•	Helheim: The dead shiver in gray monotony, chewing dust. Natives simply exist in gloom, their [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] as cold and joyless as their captives.
	•	Xibalba: Mortals face [[Trickster]] games — stairs collapsing, houses filled with endless knives. For the lords of Xibalba, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is amusement, wagering souls like dice.
	•	Naraka: Endless cycles of suffering; each day might be being flayed, the next frozen, the next boiled. [[Demons]] are administrators of suffering, rotating their charges.
	•	Kur/Irkalla: Souls sit in darkness, eating dust, without relief. Natives guard gates, enforcing [[Silence]].

⸻

III. Outer Peripheries ([[Verse]]-Specific Hells)
	•	[[Infernum Novem]]: Structured, regimented torments. Each circle has routines like bureaucratic offices of [[Pain]]. Natives are clerks of punishment.
	•	Blight Wastes: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is sickness — mortals cough, rot, and reanimate endlessly. Natives are blight-priests tending to the rot.
	•	Infernal Megalopolis: Endless paperwork, identification checks, work quotas. Mortals shuffle in bureaucratic torment. [[Demons]] act as administrators.
	•	[[Furnace Hells]]: Souls burn as fuel, becoming solar flares. Natives are custodians, feeding the furnaces with despair.
	•	[[The Maw]]: Mortals fall forever; daily [[(2030) Existence]] is falling and screaming. Natives are predators that drift alongside, feeding on terror.

⸻

IV. [[False Infinities]] (Illusionary Hells)
	•	Mirror Hells: Souls believe they are tormented but live in endless hallucination cycles. Natives are Archons harvesting resonance.
	•	Hollow Paradises: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] is bliss on the surface, but it never changes. Souls feel comfort but starve of growth. Natives are caretakers who enforce stagnation.
	•	Archonic Labyrinths: Souls wander through puzzle-worlds, every “day” a new unsolvable trial. Natives are jailer-[[Entities]] writing and rewriting mazes.
