## I. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

The **Aurath Tree** stands as the signature organism of the *Luminous Expanse*.  
Reaching heights of 50–90 meters, its trunk is composed of **Aurathene crystalline lattice**, a mineral-organic hybrid that conducts both electric and photonic currents.  
Each branch terminates in **photonic leaves** — translucent membranes capable of refracting Aetheric light into precise harmonic wavelengths.  

Under daylight, the canopy resembles a field of glass-fire; under nightfall, it glows from within, turning entire valleys into oceans of blue-violet flame.  
When touched, the bark is warm and faintly resonant — an indication of its dual biological and computational function.

---

## II. BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

Aurath Trees exhibit slow rhythmic luminescence pulses corresponding to planetary ion cycles.  
These pulses synchronize across kilometers of forest, forming a global frequency network.  
Each tree acts as both **transmitter** and **receiver**, communicating through light, vibration, and field resonance.  
When external stimuli are introduced (movement, electrical discharge, or psychic contact), the tree modulates its pulse pattern — effectively “speaking” to neighboring trees.  

Local fauna (Mirror Drakes, Seraphic Stags) rely on these pulses for orientation, emotional regulation, and mating synchronization.
