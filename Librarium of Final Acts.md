The Librarium of Final Acts is one of the most sacred institutions within [[The Dominion]]—a place where [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and divine justice converge. It is not merely a library; it is a vaulted sanctum of remembrance, consecrated to preserve the last moments of [[The Dominion]]’s enemies… and the final deeds of its heroes.
To record the last words, deeds, and judgments issued in battle by [[Exemplars]], Saints, Inquisitors, and other sacred agents.
To document moments of ultimate loyalty or [[Betrayal]], where [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] was irrevocably changed through action.
To act as a [[Ritual]] archive—every entry is considered a sacred rite, often read aloud during ceremonies of initiation, funeral pyres, or oaths of vengeance.
Housed deep beneath the Eternal Throneworld, the Librarium is built inside a hollowed mountain lined with living stone-veins that glow softly with the voices of the dead.
Endless rows of memory-obelisks and vocal sarcophagi store audio-visual projections of final acts. Each can only be accessed by those with clearance or undergoing spiritual trials.
The air is still. No dust gathers. All who enter must undergo a Veritas Confession before proceeding deeper than the outer nave.
The Canticle Vaults – Where the final sermons of [[Exemplars]] are sung by eternal [[Choirs]] of harmonic AI.
The Red Echo Well – A deep chamber that stores the emotional [[Pain]] and final cries of [[The Dominion]]’s martyrs, used to strengthen soulbound [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Doctrine/Relics/Relics]]s/Relics]].
The [[Ascension]] Walls – Carved with the names of those whose final acts elevated them to sainthood or posthumous divinity.
Oath Recorders – Devices carried by all Crusade [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]], which transmit their dying words directly to the Librarium in real-[[Time]].
Before every Crusade, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] visit the Librarium to study the final acts of previous commanders—to understand sacrifice, to inherit courage.
It is also where new oaths are sworn by high-ranking officers and Sanctus [[Choirs]], using fragments of older declarations engraved in radiant script.
