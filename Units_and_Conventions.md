# Units & Conventions

- **Base [[Physics]]:** SI units (s, m, kg, K, A).  
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] fields:** Φ, η, ℓᶜ, Ζₑ are reported in instrument-native units with calibration curves attached; always include instrument model and calibration date.  
- **Radiator area:** km² (projected).  
- **Corridor bandwidth:** standardized to **PFN-eq Mbps** (include error bars).  
- **Memetic load (M):** dimensionless (0–1 per participant) with method of estimation (e.g., validated choir model).  
- **Uncertainty:** quote 1σ and method (analytic vs Monte Carlo).  
- **Graphs:** include source hashes and timestamps; Obsidian wikilink back to the data note when possible.
