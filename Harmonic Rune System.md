Power Runes
	•	9 runes, each derived from a ring.
	•	They are the essence or fuel of a spell.
	•	Example: [[Ginnungagap]] = frost/void entropy, [[Inti]] = [[Fire]]/[[law]]/sovereignty.

Support Runes
	•	15 runes that give instructions to the spell.
	•	Examples: Self, Creature, Object, Area, Movement, Transform, Protect, Destroy, Summon, Bind, Release, Sense, [[Time]], Channel, Weave.

Channel Rune
	•	The amplifier rune.
	•	Multiplies the power of the spell, but increases instability.
	•	Maximum of 6 Channels in a 9-rune chord.

Chords (Spell Tiers)
	•	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]-rune chords → Minor spells (resistance, healing, bolts).
	•	5-rune chords → Rituals, enchantments, advanced works.
	•	7-rune chords → Mythic works, reshaping landscapes, binding great beings.
	•	9-rune chords → Forbidden harmonics: reality-warping, capable of altering rings or [[The Starmaker]]’s face
