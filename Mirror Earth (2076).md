Nature:

A parallel [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] fabricated by the Demiurge to taunt the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]].

Declared guilty as a whole [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]: “All are guilty.”

[[Invasion]] in 2076 by the [[Cavalieri di Inferno]], Archontes, [[Angelus]], and the risen Dead of [[Man]].

[[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] is transfigured into a literal Hell — its crust reshaped into burning battlefields.

The Curse:

No true [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]]. Every soul resurrects after [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], awakening randomly across the blighted planet.

This cycle ensures endless suffering and perfect harvest for the Demiurge.

Resistance and submission alike feed Him; [[Pain]] is the fuel of His design.

Theological Corruption:

Prayer manipulates [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]], but not as mercy — it draws from the Demiurge’s own essence.

At the heart of this is the [[Christus heterodoxus]], a cruciform avatar hung eternally in the burning sky, bleeding into the world.

Its blood empowers [[Humanity]], creating blood-anointed super [[Soldiers]] — weapons forged in both the name and damnation of the Demiurge.

The Manifest God of [[Pain]]:

Through synchronized prayer and [[Ritual]] suffering, mankind manifests the Christus Dolorum (Christ of [[Pain]]).

A god-among-men born from collective agony, towering and radiant with scars.

It fights the Demiurge’s hosts, but every act of resistance still feeds the Demiurge.

The War of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]]:

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|relics]] of [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s faiths take on divine power under the lens of the Demiurge’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]].

Belief weaponizes them into icons of war:

[[Spear of Longinus]] – pierces illusion, unravels veils.

[[Shroud of Turin]] – turns the dying into radiant martyrs.

Menorah Eternal – flames that empower warriors endlessly.

[[Book of Jubilees]] – rewrites laws of the battlefield.

[[Humanity]] turns relic-hunts into holy wars, forging Weapons of Divinity.
