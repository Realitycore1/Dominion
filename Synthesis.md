Synthesis is the means of altering the Genetic structure of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] to become both Synthetic and Organic, by allowing organic formations to occur alongside [[Artificial]] enhancements.

The Syl’an Genetic Structure after synthesis was altered in such a way that allowed the combination of Organic and Synthetic [[Entities]] which allowed the best of both worlds to flourish within one form.

The Synth Second Skin is a full body suit comprised of various metamaterials. The Skin fully integrate unto the wearer. The Skin is Equipped with particle beam emitters around the hands and forearms, It is also equipped with Holo Armor, Plasma Shielding. The Skin is also built with a Phasing module as well as a Replicator Module, the Skin is powered by a [[Antimatter]] core.
