Mass Star is a hypothetical object composed predominantly — or entirely — of [[Negative Mass]] matter, where both inertial and gravitational mass are negative.
This means:
	•	Repulsive Gravity: It accelerates away from positive-mass objects under mutual attraction.
	•	Inverted Dynamics: Forces act in the opposite direction to applied impulses.
	•	Self-Accelerating Pairs: When paired with positive mass objects, both accelerate indefinitely in the same direction without external input.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), [[Negative Mass]] [[Stars]] are gravitational inversion nodes — stellar-mass-scale objects that warp the [[Verseon]] lattice in reverse curvature patterns, creating repulsive gravity wells.
These [[Stars]] cannot form naturally in standard axiom-root verses but can appear in engineered universes or alternate-constant [[Regions]].
	•	Core State: Negative-mass plasma, possibly in a Bose–Einstein condensate form for stability.
	•	Radiation Profile: May emit standard photons if hybridized with positive-mass matter, or exotic luxon fields if pure.
	•	Appearance: May be visually indistinguishable from normal [[Stars]] but will gravitationally repel normal matter.

In the [[VFF]], [[Negative Mass]] [[Stars]] are strategic spacetime tools:
	•	Repulsion Shields — Used to clear [[Regions]] of positive-mass debris or fleets.
	•	Exotic Propulsion Nodes — Provide infinite acceleration pairing for mass-asymmetric [[Starships]].
	•	Wormhole Stabilizers — Counteract collapse forces in traversable wormhole mouths.
	•	[[Verseon]] Lattice Sculptors — Create large-scale repulsive voids to shape matter distribution in verses.

In the Master Omniversal Theory, [[Negative Mass]] [[Stars]] are found in:
	•	Engineered Verses — Designed as defense barriers or high-efficiency propulsion hubs.
	•	Meta-Stable [[Regions]] — Areas where exotic symmetry laws allow co-[[(2030) Existence]] of positive and [[Negative Mass]] matter.
	•	Magic-Integrated Verses — Seen as “cursed suns” that banish all who approach.
