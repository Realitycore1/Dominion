I. Marius Hills Dome Complex – Political & Cultural Capital

Founding: 2120s; first true colony dome, expanded into 6+ interconnected domes and lava tube burrows.

Function: Capital of [[The Cynthia Protectorate]].

Districts:

Civic Dome (Cynthia Assembly Hall): administrative heart, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Government|Government]] functions.

The Burrows: vast lava-tube habitats; mixed residential and industrial use.

University Ring: research institutes, engineering academies, legal courts.

Industries:

Administration and governance (colonial bureaucracy).

Education and R&D (early space medicine, closed-loop agriculture).

Cultural exports: Lunar literature, arts, and historical archives (“The First Step” schools).

II. Shackleton Crater Hub – Mining & Logistics Center

Founding: 2080s as an ice-harvest outpost, expanded into industrial city.

Function: Water, volatiles, propellant hub.

Districts:

Ice Pits: mining and processing of frozen volatiles.

Power Arrays: solar farms along crater rims, fusion cores underground.

Cargo Spines: maglev hubs sending supplies to orbit.

Industries:

Ice and volatile mining → propellant [[Production]].

Oxygen and hydrogen export to cis-lunar orbit.

Early fuel depots feeding orbital expansion.

III. Tycho & Copernicus Districts – Heavy Industry

Founding: mid-2200s during industrial expansion.

Function: Manufacturing hubs.

Districts:

Foundry Belt: alloy smelters, 3D-printing facilities, and nanofab centers.

Mass Driver Banks: railgun-style launchers for cargo and raw ore.

Labor Quarters: dense [[Worker]] habitats, more austere than Marius Hills.

Industries:

Heavy metallurgy, alloys for spacecraft and domes.

Mass driver [[Systems]] for exports.

Robotics and drone assembly.

IV. Lunar Orbital Yards (E-M Lagrange Stations) – Shipbuilding Crown

Founding: 2300s as refueling depots → by 2500s, largest shipyards after [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].

Function: Shipbuilding and repair for inner fleets.

Stations:

E-M L1: trade & logistics, hub for Terran–Lunar exchange.

E-M L2: heavy shipyards ([[Battleships]], freighters, colony craft).

E-M L4/L5: defense bastions and freeport stations.

Industries:

Spacecraft construction and maintenance.

Orbital defense arrays.

Logistics and long-range convoy organization.

V. The Burrows (Global Subterranean Network) – Residential & Redundant [[Infrastructure]]

Founding: carved from lava tubes from 2200 onward.

Function: Housing, agriculture, fallback zones.

Districts:

Hydro Farms: layered [[Hydroponics]], aquaponics for colony food base.

Vaults: water and oxygen storage, emergency bunkers.

Communes: multi-family residences organized around “Crews” or guilds.

Industries:

Agriculture → Lunar food self-sufficiency by 2350.

Light manufacturing, textiles, everyday goods.

Underground biotech labs (protected from solar radiation).

VI. Polar Rings (Aristarchus, Peary, Malapert Stations) – Energy & Relay Hubs

Founding: 2400s; smaller domes and stations on polar sites.

Function: Communications, relay, and energy.

Districts:

Relay Towers: high-bandwidth transmitters tying Luna to orbit and [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].

Solar Belts: [[Giant]] polar solar farms.

Observatories: astronomical and gravitic sensors.

Industries:

Energy [[Production]] (solar, fusion grid integration).

Communications and signal intelligence.

Research outposts for astrophysics and surveillance.
