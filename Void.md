Core Bias:

Reality shear and structural instability

This is one of the most important high-concept Storm subtypes.

It is where instability affects not just environment—but the continuity of reality itself.

Traits:
	•	rifts
	•	causal tearing
	•	discontinuity fronts
	•	nonlocal instability
	•	unstable passage or transit
	•	ontological weather

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	reality itself is under weather pressure
	•	boundaries tear under stress
	•	existence can be “stormed” at the structural level

Common Expressions:
	•	verse breach zones
	•	unstable inter-basin corridors
	•	reality fracture civilizations
	•	transit hazard cosmologies

Failure Mode:

Continuity shredding
The basin loses enough coherence that it cannot sustain stable structure.

This subtype is extremely important for:
	•	MOT / VFF integration
	•	basin transition zones
	•	cosmic war scars
	•	Dominion or anti-Dominion frontier regions
