## Summary

GalFed command is distributed across military, legal, medical, and predictive authorities. A fleet commander may hold weapons, but a quarantine jurist, Aether warden, or predictive executor can decide when those weapons become lawful.

## Details

Command hierarchy:

- Council of Primacy: authorizes existential interventions and permanent quarantines.
- Custodial High Command: coordinates fleets and sector-scale readiness.
- Continuity Courts: define legal status of rebellions, hazards, and restricted worlds.
- Aether Warden Corps: issues emergency field-safety orders.
- Predictive Executor Office: converts risk forecasts into operational directives.
- Sector Marshals: command regional Harmonium forces.
- Restoration Commanders: lead mixed medical-security missions.
- Corridor Admirals: control transit security and interdiction routes.
- Ordinate Prefects: oversee punitive campaigns and border pacification.

Standard command orders:

- Seal: close a corridor, gate, or transit window.
- Cradle: preserve a population under protective isolation.
- Quiet: suppress communications to prevent panic or memetic spread.
- Reframe: replace local leadership while preserving institutional continuity.
- Detune: disable hostile noetic, harmonic, or Aether systems.
- Cleanse: sterilize an Aether hazard or unrecoverable contamination site.
- Return: restore lawful transit and relief access after compliance.

GalFed command language is intentionally clinical. The vocabulary reduces the emotional visibility of coercion.
