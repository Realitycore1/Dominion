# Chapter VI - Chorus of Doubt

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Public resistance to unification grows across settlements, shelters, rail corridors, and surviving cities.

## Event Role

- Citizens ask whether unity means safety or captivity.
- Religious communities debate whether the covenant language is sacred, political, or manipulative.
- Veterans distrust leaders who now call former enemies partners.
- Dissident households test whether the new order will permit lawful refusal.

## Consequence

The United Syl'an project must earn legitimacy from people who have already survived too many promises.
