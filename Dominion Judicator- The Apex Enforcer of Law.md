Where others interpret, I enforce. Where others question, I decide. I am the final line—sharp, cold, and unbending."

I. Purpose & Role
Judicators are the walking embodiment of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[law]]. They are both [[Judge]] and executioner, deployed across civilian, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]], and interstellar [[Domains]]. Their purpose is not to seek justice abstractly—but to restore order and balance in real-[[Time]].
[[Key]] Functions:
Instant Field Sentencing for crimes of all classes.
Investigation, arbitration, and punishment in a single role.
Assign Restitution, Penal Legion service, or Execution.
Authorized to override provincial authorities if [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[law]] is in jeopardy.

II. Position in the Hierarchy
Judicators are a federal-level agency, operating above provincial [[law]], but below the Empress and Supreme Courts.
They are analogous to the Royal [[Inquisition]] in prestige and lethality, but distinct in mandate and method:
Judicators act publicly, by codified [[law]].
Inquisitors act secretly, by doctrinal purity.
“The [[Inquisition]] purges the hidden. We confront the visible.”
Vow of Purity in Judgment
“Let no kin blind me, no fear shake me, no favor touch me. I serve not men, nor titles—but [[The Dominion]] itself.”
Judicators are bound by the Lex Puritas, an unalterable code of legal conduct and ethical clarity.
Expected to maintain emotional neutrality and unwavering discipline.
They record and remember every sentence they carry out. It is a moral burden and a badge of precision.

Handle constitutional crises or multi-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] corruption.

Judicators preside over two types of trials:

1. Summons Judgment
Issued by state or Judicator command. Presumes wrongdoing.
Fast. Procedural. Adversarial.
Heavy on evidence and forceful enforcement.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Requested Judgment
Filed by citizen, [[Soldier]], or governor to clarify disputes or seek redress.
Reflective. Often includes duels, restitution negotiation, or symbolic reparations.

VI. Tools & Equipment
1. Vox Gavel
Multi-mode weapon and enforcement tool.
Fires sonic, kinetic, and gravitic pulses.
Used to issue sentences vocally, broadcast [[law]] updates, and trigger execution protocols.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Helm of Verdict
Encloses the head completely; biometrically sealed.
Equipped with truth-detection, AI co-analysis, case overlays, and instant connection to the Codex [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/Eternum/Eternum|Eternum]].
Never removed in public—a symbol of impartiality.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Codex Arbiterium
Embedded legal compendium.
Continuously updated from the Codex [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/Eternum/Eternum|Eternum]] AI core.
Contains [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[law]], ethics, rulings, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]].

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Veritas Node (True AI Companion)
A Class-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] autonomous intelligence built into every Judicator’s [[Systems]].
Assists with verdict simulation, [[law]] parsing, and behavioral assessment.
Can challenge or delay a Judicator's decision if legal precedent contradicts their action.

A. The Black Test
Final trial of a Judicator candidate.
Involves live-[[Fire]] moral dilemmas where mercy, bias, or delay leads to rejection or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]].

B. Ash Anniversary
Each Judicator recites the names and crimes of those they have executed on the day of their commission.
Failure to remember signifies unethical detachment and leads to reconditioning or removal.

C. Vigil [[Silence]]
One hour of stillness before deployment. During this [[Time]], Judicators receive any updates from the Codex [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/Eternum/Eternum|Eternum]] and review their moral alignment records.

VIII. Public Image
Feared by criminals, respected by officers, revered by the righteous.
Known for consistency, honor, and unflinching judgment.
Civilian children are taught “When the Judicator arrives, so too does the truth.”

“They root out rot. We sever the limb.”
Unofficial rivalry exists due to overlapping authority.
Judicators view Inquisitors as secretive zealots, while Inquisitors see Judicators as rigid automatons.
Cross-jurisdictional operations are heavily regulated under Crossfire Protocols.
Rare joint missions require a Judicator Arbiter and [[Inquisition]] Purifier to co-authorize actions.

X. Redemption Protocols
Judicators often serve as:
Final arbiters in determining Penal Legion eligibility.
Executioners if all restitution paths are denied.
Witnesses to a Redeemed's return and reintegration.
They are deeply involved in the cycle of crime, penance, and salvation, ensuring the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] is complete—from offense to forgiveness.
