13 Baghdad, the capital city of [[Iraq]], is a bustling metropolis with a rich [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and cultural heritage that dates back to ancient times. The city is located in a fertile valley in central [[Iraq]], surrounded by the Tigris and Euphrates Rivers. This geographical location has made Baghdad an important crossroads of trade and commerce throughout [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]], with merchants and traders from all over the world coming to the city to conduct business. The city's skyline is dominated by the ancient structures of the Islamic era, including the Al-Mansur Mosque, the Shia Imam Ali Mosque, and the Nasiriyah Mosque. These buildings are a testament to Baghdad's rich Islamic heritage and its role as a center of learning and scholarship throughout the centuries. However, Baghdad's modern [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] has been marked by conflict and instability, with the city being the target of several wars and invasions over the years. The 2003 [[Invasion]] by US forces resulted in extensive damage to the city's [[Infrastructure]] and historical landmarks, with many of its buildings and monuments being looted or destroyed. Despite these challenges, Baghdad continues to be a vibrant and dynamic city, with a thriving [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] and a diverse population. The city is home to a [[Wide]] range of cultural institutions, including museums, galleries, and theaters, as well as universities and research centers that attract scholars from all over the world. Baghdad's cuisine is also renowned for its rich flavors and diverse ingredients, with many of its restaurants serving up traditional dishes such as falafel, kabobs, and hummus. Overall, Baghdad is a city that has endured through centuries of conflict and turmoil, and continues to thrive as a center of commerce, [[culture]], and innovation.
