1. Common Trolls

Definition: The baseline—large, brutish humanoids with crude intelligence.

Traits: Long limbs, ugly features, appetite for flesh.

Symbolism: The savage predator in the wilderness.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Cave Trolls

Definition: Trolls dwelling in dark caverns and mountains.

Traits: Thick hides, nocturnal, slow but powerful.

Symbolism: Darkness given form; danger in hidden places.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Forest Trolls

Definition: Trolls of woods and thickets.

Traits: Mossy hides, ambush predators, camouflage.

Symbolism: The predator disguised as the forest.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Swamp Trolls

Definition: Trolls of marshes and bogs.

Traits: Warty, amphibious, toxic breath or bites.

Symbolism: Filth, decay, and corruption embodied.

5. Frost Trolls

Definition: Trolls of tundras and icy peaks.

Traits: White hides, cold resistance, brutal strength.

Symbolism: Cruel [[Hunger]] in desolation.

6. Regenerating Trolls

Definition: Trolls whose wounds heal unnaturally fast.

Traits: Flesh knits rapidly, limbs regrow, only [[Fire]]/acid halts them.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] denied; savagery made immortal.

7. Shadow Trolls

Definition: [[Abyss]]-touched or cursed trolls.

Traits: Black hides, glowing eyes, necrotic resilience.

Symbolism: [[Hunger]] corrupted into unending malice.

8. Bridge Trolls

Definition: Folkloric trolls who demand tolls at crossings.

Traits: Territorial, cunning, deceptive.

Symbolism: Greed as predator; danger at thresholds.

9. Noble Trolls

Definition: Rare trolls who rise above savagery to lead tribes or clans.

Traits: Cunning intelligence, warlords of their kind, stronger than common trolls.

Symbolism: Savagery given structure; might forged into rule.

10. Arch-Trolls (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans|Titans]] said to embody mountains or storms.

Traits: Colossal size, immune to sunlight, semi-divine.

Symbolism: The wilderness itself risen against mortals.
