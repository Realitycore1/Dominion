Core Definition

A Storm Basin is a megaversal region in which the host axiom preferentially expresses reality through:
	•	instability
	•	turbulence
	•	charge differentials
	•	violent transitions
	•	cyclical disruption
	•	release under accumulated tension

In simple terms:

A Storm Basin is a reality-space where existence tends to form, evolve, and break through energetic conflict, pressure imbalance, and recurring discharge events.

This does not have to mean weather.

Storm can express through:
	•	plasma or electromagnetism
	•	causality turbulence
	•	war-density
	•	information overload
	•	emotional or psychic pressure
	•	unstable physics
	•	reality-shear conditions

The key is:

Storm = structured instability

Not random disorder.

⸻

STORM BASIN — PRIMARY BEHAVIORAL RULES

A Storm Basin tends to produce realities where:

1. Tension accumulates before release

Things rarely remain calm for long.

2. Difference generates movement

Gradients, imbalances, and oppositions matter.

3. Violence is often formative, not incidental

Disruption is part of how the basin grows.

4. Cycles of calm and rupture are normal

Instability comes in waves, fronts, and surges.

5. Structures must survive repeated shock

Only systems that can endure turbulence persist.

That makes Storm Basins ideal for:
	•	warlike civilizations
	•	charged cosmologies
	•	unstable travel regions
	•	psychic storm zones
	•	conflict ecologies
	•	civilizations forged by repeated crisis

This is a very powerful basin class.

⸻

STORM BASIN — SUBTYPE LOGIC

A Storm subtype should answer:

What kind of instability or energetic unrest dominates the basin?

Not just “what kind of storm it looks like.”

That means Storm subtypes should define:
	•	what builds pressure
	•	what releases it
	•	what gets destabilized
	•	what survives the turbulence
	•	what kind of violent transition is normal
