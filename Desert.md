A Desert Basin tends to produce realities where:

1. Resources are limited or unevenly distributed

Nothing is guaranteed.

2. Persistence matters more than proliferation

What survives is what can endure.

3. Structures are exposed rather than buffered

Things are stripped down and visible.

4. Distance matters

Separation and isolation are real forces.

5. Failure is often slow, attritional, and cumulative

Not explosive—grinding.

This means Desert Basins are ideal for:
	•	survival cosmologies
	•	ancient ruin ecologies
	•	sparse but meaningful realities
	•	pilgrimage worlds
	•	attritional civilizations
	•	exposed metaphysical geometries
