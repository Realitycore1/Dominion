## Summary

Beam-powere
Core Mechanism

[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Definition

Power Beaming

High-energy microwave, laser, or Concordia plasma beams track vehicles along the route.

Vehicles have rectennas, superconductive coils, or Concordia harmonics that capture and convert the beam into thrust.

Guidance & Control

Beam lanes act like “magnetized light rails.”

Embedded gravitic or mag-field conduits keep traffic locked in pathing, preventing collisions.

Scaling

Ground-to-ground: Intercontinental highways, skipping over oceans or deserts.

Ground-to-orbit: Launch [[Systems]] using beam thrust instead of rocketry.

Interplanetary: Orbital arrays [[Fire]] beams across vacuum, powering sail-ships and couriers.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Style)

On the Ground: Gleaming black causeways stretching to the horizon, rimmed with Atlas Pillar towers every hundred kilometers, each firing coherent beams into the next.

In Orbit: Lattices of satellites creating glowing lanes between planets — literal ribbons of light in [[The Void]].

Aesthetic: At night, planets crisscross with glowing arterial beams, like cities with neon veins visible from orbit.

Advantages

Beams can double as weapons — overcharge them, and the highway becomes a planet-spanning kill-laser.

Civilizational Role

Logistical Arteries: Moving people, [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], and resources at scale.

Cultural [[Iconography]]: Citizens call them “roads of light,” symbolic proof that the Empress carries her people.

Control Mechanism: Shut off the beams, and no vehicle moves — power becomes sovereignty.

Narrative Resonance

For myth: “The Empress does not walk the [[Earth (Terra)]] — she lays light down upon it, and through it we move.”
