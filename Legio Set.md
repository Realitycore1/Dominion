## **XVI. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Set**

**Title:** Legio Set – The Red Storm

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through chaos, destruction, and the mastery of desert fury. Legio Set embody the howling sandstorm, the desert serpent, and the sudden violence of the storm’s heart. Their creed: *“Through chaos, we conquer.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Set**

* **Silhouette:** Jagged and imposing, armor shaped with sharp, angular edges resembling shifting dunes and storm-swept stone. Heavy, predatory form.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit whirling clouds of red dust, sand, and static, cloaking the Legionnaires in desert storms. Sparks of crimson lightning ripple through the haze.
* **Coloration:** Deep crimson and black, accented with bronze and ochre tones. Plates textured like scorched sandstone, cracked and jagged.
* **Helmet:** Set-inspired jackal-serpent hybrid helm with elongated, angular features and upward curving ridges. Eyes glow blood-red, often flickering like lightning in a storm.
* **Specialization:** Stormcore Reactors—[[Systems]] capable of generating localized sandstorms and static fields, obscuring vision and choking enemy advance while empowering the Legionnaires with storm energy.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Set Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Rifle and Carbine forms for chaotic, [[Close]]-to-mid range combat. Weapons often adapted with static-discharge coils and sand-propellant [[Systems]].
* **Modification:** Weapons [[Fire]] crimson-charged plasma bolts accompanied by bursts of sand and static. Bayonet and blade attachments shaped like serrated storm-fangs.
* **Aesthetic:** Brutal crimson and bronze alloy, engraved with serpent and desert-storm motifs. Conduits glow with shifting crimson light.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Masters of disruption and ambush in arid or unstable battlefields. They strike as chaotic storms, scattering enemies before overwhelming them.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their coming is heralded by red dust clouds and the hiss of static sandstorms, leaving foes blind, choking, and disoriented.
* **Legacy:** Legio Set is remembered as the Red Storm—embodiments of chaos and fury, breaking the ordered lines of battle with desert-born violence and serpent’s cunning.

LEGION SET — The Devourers of Storm and Sand

Classification: Desert Warfare • Ambush • Storm Suppression
Status: Destroyed

Profile

Set specialized in warfare across dune worlds, low-visibility atmospheres, and electromagnetically violent [[Regions]]. Their mirage cloaks and storm-spliced plasma made them the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s desert assassins, crushing empires that relied on harsh territory to survive.
