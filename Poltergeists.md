Classification

Psychokinetic Phenomena / Emotional Manifestations / Semi-Sentient Haunts

⸻

CORE IDEA

Poltergeists are violence without a body.

They are not ghosts in the traditional sense and not spirits seeking closure. A Poltergeist is emotion that learned how to push back—rage, fear, grief, or stress given kinetic expression. Something inside a place, a person, or a moment snapped, and the environment started responding.

They don’t appear.
They happen.

⸻

ORIGIN

Poltergeists emerge from sustained emotional pressure and instability:
	•	prolonged domestic conflict
	•	abuse, isolation, or suppressed trauma
	•	adolescent psychic surges
	•	malice-saturated locations
	•	failed containment or cleansing rituals
	•	Death Echo or Onirokinesis overlap zones

Some are tied to places.
Others are tethered to living anchors.

Not all Poltergeists require death to exist.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Poltergeists lack a fixed form.

Common indicators:
	•	objects thrown with precision
	•	doors slamming rhythmically
	•	scratches, dents, or fractures appearing spontaneously
	•	furniture shifting incrementally
	•	cold gusts or pressure changes
	•	audible knocks, footsteps, or impacts

When partially visible:
	•	distortions in air
	•	pressure ripples
	•	shadow displacement without source

They are perceived through effects, not appearance.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Poltergeists are volatile, reactive, and territorial.
	•	Escalate in response to attention or fear
	•	Focus activity around specific individuals or rooms
	•	Display patterns tied to emotional triggers
	•	Rarely pursue beyond anchor range

They are not malicious by intent.
They are uncontained.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Low to moderate.
	•	Reactive rather than strategic
	•	Capable of pattern repetition
	•	Can “learn” which actions cause stronger responses
	•	Rarely capable of communication

Advanced Poltergeists may mimic intent without possessing it.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Psychokinesis: direct force without mass
	•	Grace-Disruptive: angelic presence stabilizes but cannot command
	•	Malice-Amplified: corruption increases violence and radius
	•	Anchor Dependency: tied to person, place, or unresolved stressor
	•	Feedback Loop: fear strengthens manifestation

Destroying objects does nothing.
Removing the pressure source often ends it.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Common in enclosed living spaces
	•	Rare in open or emotionally neutral environments
	•	Do not reproduce, but can cascade into clusters
	•	May evolve into more defined haunt types

Buildings plagued by Poltergeists feel hostile, not haunted.

⸻

ALPHA POLTERGEISTS

An Alpha Poltergeist is known as a Kinetic Storm or Rage Node.

Traits:
	•	Multi-room or multi-building influence
	•	Can levitate heavy structures
	•	Generates sustained environmental damage
	•	May form a semi-visible core

Kinetic Storms are often mistaken for demonic infestation.
They are actually emotional catastrophes.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Moderate (localized) / Severe (Alpha or living-anchor)

You don’t exorcise a Poltergeist.
You decompress it—or contain it.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Poltergeists represent:
	•	violence without villain
	•	trauma externalized
	•	domestic horror
	•	the environment turning against you

Where Death Echoes replay the past,
where Onirokinesis Spirits rewrite perception,
Poltergeists weaponize emotion in real space.
