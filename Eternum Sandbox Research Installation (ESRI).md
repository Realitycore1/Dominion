9. Failure Modes & Emergency Response

Primary failure Modes
	•	Axiomatic Conflict Cascade — incompatible [[Axioms]] create runaway resource loops (entropy spike).
	•	Memetic Contagion — tested artifact encodes replicating memetic payload that pierces observation.
	•	Anchor Failure — one or more lattice cores lose sync; paradox dampers overloaded.
	•	Persistent Rewrite — artifact writes a persistent axiom into local [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]] lattice.

Immediate responses
	1.	Soft Abort (Magisterium) — throttle output, [[Close]] translation matrix, engage dampers.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Hard Kill — Magisterium fires containment hard-kill: field lock, Nullity Net engagement, local [[Time]] dilation.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Quarantine Surge — raise quarantine vaults, lock observation nodes, initiate memory-scrub on personnel.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	[[Infernariate]] Invocation — bring in [[Infernariate]] for memetic forensic analysis and possible interdiction.
	5.	[[Aftermath]] — sealed audit, artifact sequestration, possibility of sacrificial ley sink if lattice corrupted.
