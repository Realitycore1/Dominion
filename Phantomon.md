Within the [[VFF]], Phantomons are semi-material quanta occupying phase-displaced states in the ΨM field — the noetic substrate that overlays the physical [[Verse]] Lattice. They are hypothesized to mediate the projection of mental constructs into reality, as well as the absorption of physical events into mnemonic or metaphysical archives.
	•	Spin: Variable; depends on the cognitive or energetic “load” encoded in the particle.
	•	Mass: Effectively null in standard metric space; acquires apparent mass when anchoring to physical [[Systems]].
	•	Charge: None in EM terms; carries perceptual polarity, determining whether it manifests as constructive (clarifying) or destructive (obscuring).
	•	Interaction: Couples weakly with baryonic matter; strongly with [[Aetherion]], [[Nulliton]], and [[Chronon]] fields.

In the [[VFF]], Phantomons act as information–matter translators:
	•	Store and transmit cognitive blueprints between [[Entities]] or across spacetime.
	•	Allow consciousness imprints to persist as tangible environmental influences — the physical root of “haunting” phenomena in low-tech societies.
	•	Used in psychotronic [[Systems]] to stabilize pilot–machine neural coupling in advanced [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] craft.
