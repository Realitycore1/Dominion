## Current Canon Frame

The Dominion is a dual government divided by time horizon.

The Federal Government is the service-franchised constitutional republic that governs ordinary civil life: rights, courts, budgets, daily administration, public services, provincial law, and civic representation.

The Royal Government is the continuity government that guards the long future: existential threats, civilizational memory, succession, restoration after collapse, VFF and MOT risk, and dangers too slow or large for ordinary politics to manage.

The older service-franchised republic language on this page describes the Federal side of the Dominion, not the entire civilization.

See: [[Dual Government Model]]
