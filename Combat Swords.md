CQC weapons must prioritize durability, sharpness, and mass efficiency:
1.	Monomolecular Blades
•	Edges honed down to a single molecule thickness.
•	Must be supported by extreme materials like carbon nanotubes, diamondoid composites, or graphene-laminate alloys.
•	Effective against nearly all armors not designed with phase/dynamic countermeasures.
•	Fragility at the edge must be mitigated by structural micro-vibrations or reinforced edge matrix fields.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Hyperdense Core Blades
•	Incorporate [[Exotic Matter]] alloys (e.g., osmium-tungsten-ceramic composites).
•	Higher cutting momentum due to mass, useful in cleaving heavy armor or cybernetic limbs.
•	Risk of fatigue or recoil unless paired with powered suits (e.g., [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Kraft Suit [[Systems]]).

The following technologies align with plausible hard sci-fi applications:
1.	Vibroblades
•	Utilize high-frequency oscillation (typically ultrasonic: 20–40 kHz) to amplify cutting efficiency.
•	Allow blades to “saw” through hard surfaces (bone, armor plates).
•	Require built-in gyrostabilizers to prevent user harm and energy dampeners to minimize hand fatigue.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Electrothermal Edge Induction
•	Briefly superheats the edge on contact via capacitor pulse.
•	Useful for cauterizing wounds or slicing through frozen material.
•	Requires precision insulation and feedback [[Systems]] to avoid overheating.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Smart Blade Memory Metal
•	Blades that can change shape slightly (e.g., expand into a hooked or serrated form).
•	Uses shape memory alloys or programmable crystal structures.
•	Can be adapted to user intent via neural interface (common in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] bio-augmented forces ￼).
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Energy Sheath Edge
•	A thin energy field (e.g., plasma envelope or EM arc field) confined closely to the blade’s surface.
•	Enhances cutting but not designed to act like a Star Wars lightsaber; it’s a molecular or plasma edge augment.
•	Requires tight field stability and massive power efficiency—viable only for elite troops like [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Inquisitors
