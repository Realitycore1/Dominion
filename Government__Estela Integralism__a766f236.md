The Integral State

The [[Estela Integralism]] colony is ruled as a totalitarian dictatorship, modeled after National Socialist [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] but adapted to the [[Colonial Union]]’s frontier reality.
	•	[[The Supreme]] Leader – El Caudillo Estelar
	•	The head of state is styled El Caudillo Estelar (“The Stellar Leader”), worshipped as the living embodiment of Integralism.
	•	His image is everywhere—murals, holograms, and orbital banners project his face across the colony.
	•	Succession is not hereditary or electoral; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] are “chosen by destiny” through the ruling party’s inner circle. In practice, assassinations and purges are common.
	•	The Integral Party
	•	A single, state-absorbing party governs all spheres of life. Membership is mandatory for all officials and officers.
	•	The party has branches for youth, women, [[Workers]], and [[Soldiers]], ensuring cradle-to-grave indoctrination.
	•	Its symbol—a black sun flanked by twin comets—is burned into flags, uniforms, and even stamped on rations.
	•	The Council of Purity
	•	A shadow cabinet of party ideologues who enforce racial, cultural, and ideological “purity.”
	•	They wield power equal to or greater than ministries, capable of ordering purges, rewriting [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]], and denouncing officials.
	•	Centralized Ministries
	•	Ministry of Order: secret police, censorship, and surveillance.
	•	Ministry of Expansion: colonial settlement, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] conquest, and propaganda.
	•	Ministry of Unity: cultural indoctrination, festivals, and control of education.
	•	All ministries answer directly to the Caudillo.
	•	[[law]] and Justice
	•	Courts exist only as show trials. Sentences are predetermined by political need.
	•	Criminality is redefined as disloyalty to Integralism; corruption within the party is ignored if it serves expansion.
	•	Governance Style
	•	Hyper-centralized, but constantly paranoid. Party purges are routine.
	•	Loyalty is signaled through [[Ritual]] salutes, public oaths, and visible displays of devotion.
	•	Dissenters disappear into “Ash Camps”—orbital labor stations where survival is short and conditions brutal.
