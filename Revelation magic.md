Definition
Revelation Magic encompasses all arts of seeing the hidden, unveiling the concealed, and knowing the unknowable. It ranges from simple divination to omniversal axiom-scrying. Practitioners risk madness, corruption, or paradox, but kings, generals, and gods still seek their counsel.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Veils & Layers – reality hides itself in folds; revelation peels them back.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Sympathy – like calls to like; [[Fire]] reveals [[Fire]], blood reveals blood.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Observer’s Burden – knowledge changes the knower, sometimes the known.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Paradox – too much revealed collapses meaning itself (the “seer’s spiral”).

⸻

Techniques of Revelation
	•	Divination – casting bones, runes, or [[Stars]] to glimpse possibilities.
	•	Scrying – mirrors, pools, or crystals that reflect distant places or events.
	•	Prophecy – channeling visions of futures; true prophecy is rare and costly.
	•	Truthsight – stripping illusions, glamours, and disguises.
	•	Memory Echoing – reading the past of objects, places, or people.
	•	Axiomatic Vision – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-tier: perceiving the underlying logic of a [[Verse]] (its “root code”).
	•	Theophany – revelation by angelic or demonic beings, often overwhelming mortals.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: Seeress Ennalie warned [[Dwarves]] of the Abyssal uprising ￼; prophets are revered but feared, as their words shift wars.
	•	Concordia: Prophecy guilds bargain with visions; Adamant-tier seers can glimpse multiple possible outcomes and auction them to rulers.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Revelation is institutionalized in Oracle [[Choirs]], vast assemblies of minds linked through [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] to scan verses. Revelation here becomes both science and mysticism.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Practices harmonic “choral divination,” reading the resonances of whole populations to foresee political or cosmic shifts.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Revelation is poisoned — Demiurgic cults reveal twisted truths, showing mortals only despair or false inevitability.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Strategic Advantage – knowing enemy movements, weather, or plague patterns wins wars.
	•	Existential [[Hunger]] – mortals cannot resist the urge to ask “what comes next?”
	•	Religious Authority – prophets anchor faiths, turning revelation into divine mandate.
	•	Scientific Parallels – many “discoveries” blur into revelations, especially when logic roots are involved.

⸻

Perils
	•	Madness – too much knowledge overwhelms mind and soul.
	•	False Prophecy – visions are shaped by bias; wrong predictions cause ruin.
	•	Observer Effect – knowledge of a future may cause it, closing paths.
	•	Predation – [[Entities]] feed on revelation, whispering only to trap mortals ([[Demons]] of curiosity, [[Angels]] of zeal).

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Veilcraft – counter-revelation arts: cloaks, obscuring wards, false futures.
	•	[[Silence]] Sigils – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] runes that mute averse to prophecy by disrupting axiom access.
	•	Skepticism – the simplest but most effective; resist belief, and revelation loses power.
	•	Entropy Shrouds – chaotic fields that muddy probabilities, denying foresight.
