![[Pasted image 20250830135536.png]]
Planetary Strip Miners are mega-extraction infrastructures deployed to systematically consume entire planets, stripping away crustal [[Layers]], oceans, and biospheres. They convert matter directly into fuel, alloys, and feedstock for [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]], leaving behind nothing but a husk or core remnant.
Harvest [[Infrastructure]]

Atmosphere Siphons: Towers and orbiting collectors drain air, gases, and volatiles.

Oceanic Drains: [[Mantle Pistons]] and [[Chimney Towers]] pump seas into plasma refineries.

Crust Flayers: Strip belts peel kilometers of surface at a [[Time]] into orbital conveyors.

Orbital Feed Belts: [[Mass Driver Rings]] carry raw matter into orbital depots.

Processing [[Systems]]

Cornucopia Forges: Convert rock into alloys, ceramics, and fuels.

Nanite Disassemblers: Swarm across crust, reducing it to molecular feedstock.

Variants

Civil Miners: Target barren moons and uninhabited rocks for raw materials.

War Miners: Deploy on conquered or rebellious worlds — punishment by consumption.

Sacred Miners: Used ritually, dismantling worlds as offerings to the Empress, their material reforged into cathedrals of war.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide endless raw matter for Dyson Fortresses, [[Ark Platforms]], and [[Nested Dyson Swarms]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Serve as strategic terror weapons — stripping rebellious worlds until nothing remains.

Economic: A single strip-mined planet can supply material for centuries of expansion.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Scythes” — myths say the Empress harvests false worlds to sow new ones in truth.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Gigantic excavation towers like cathedral spires digging into continents.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the planet appears as a geometric wound, scarred in perfect symmetry.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Conveyor belts and plasma rivers flow in fractal spirals across the surface.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Stripped land glows with glyphic scars — scriptures burned into the planet’s bones.

From space, a strip-mined planet looks like it has been peeled like fruit, glowing veins of molten light carved into sacred geometry across its face.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Proof of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] power — even worlds can be dismantled and remade.

To rebels: Utter horror — resistance can doom their world to erasure.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic backbone — material extracted on planetary scales.

To myth: Called the “Amber Scythes”, seen as the harvest of false creation to build the Empress’s true [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]].
