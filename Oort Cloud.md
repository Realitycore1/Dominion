The Oort Cloud is a theoretical, distant region of icy planetesimals that surrounds the Sun at the outermost edge of the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]m|System]]. It is believed to extend from roughly [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]],000 astronomical units (AU) from the Sun (about 0.03 light-years) to as far as 50,000 AU or more (nearly a light-year).

It is divided into two parts:
	•	The inner Oort Cloud, also called the Hills Cloud, which is denser and closer in.
	•	The outer Oort Cloud, a vast, sparse spherical shell.

The Oort Cloud is thought to be the source of long-period comets (those with orbital periods greater than 200 years) that are occasionally perturbed inward by gravitational interactions — with passing [[Stars]], galactic tides, or [[Giant]] molecular clouds.

Estimated to contain trillions of icy bodies composed of water, ammonia, and methane, the Oort Cloud represents the leftover debris from the ea[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]gnty/System|System]]’s formation, scattered outward by the gravitational influence of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

Because of its extreme distance, no Oort Cloud objects have been directly observed; its [[(2030) Existence]] is inferred from the orbits of observed comets.
