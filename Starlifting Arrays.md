Starlifting Arrays are vast engineered [[Systems]] built around [[Stars]] to siphon plasma, gases, and heavy elements directly from the stellar surface. They allow [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]]niverse/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Multiverse/[[Local Cluster]]/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[Verse]]/[[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]/[[Ameise]]/Castes/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]] to refuel fleets,[[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]]ures]], or prolong [[Stars]]’ lives. To ordinary citizens, they are cathedrals of [[Fire]] — proof that the Empress farms suns as easily as mortals pluck fruit.
Methods of Extraction

Magnetic Funnels: Gigantic loops pulling stellar plasma into collection conduits.

Laser Sailors: Focused beams driving mass off the stellar surface for collection.

Thermal Wind Harnessing: Inducing solar winds and capturing them in orbital nets.

Structure

Array Rings: Orbiting magnetic collectors strung around the star in concentric circles.

Collector Swarms: Billions of harvest satellites guiding plasma flows into storage vessels.

Atlas Anchors: Mega-spires (sometimes tethered to Dyson swarms) holding extraction funnels steady.

[[Computronium]] Overseers: Simulate stellar dynamics in real-[[Time]] to avoid instability.

Uses

Civil: Extend star lifetimes by reducing fuel waste, ensuring [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] endure for billions of years.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Drain [[Stars]] into stellar weapons, powering Nicoll–Dyson Beams or collapse bombs.

Economic: Provides endless fuel for fusion, [[Antimatter]] [[Production]], and Dyson [[Infrastructure]].

Religious: Known as “The Amber Harvest” — myths say the Empress gathers suns like wheat, threshing [[Fire]] into order.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Keeps star [[Systems]] stable and prosperous — no colony fears a dying sun.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Weaponizable on a scale beyond terror — [[Stars]] themselves can be bled dry to fuel wars.

Religious: Viewed as divine agriculture — the Empress reaps [[Fire]], turning chaos into harvest.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Extraction towers ribbed like black cathedrals, spearing into stellar [[Fire]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the arrays appear as perfect black arcs encircling suns.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Plasma flows spiral into fractal ribbons, weaving luminous mandalas in orbit.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Harvest streams glow with glyphs — rivers of scripture flowing from the star itself.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Assurance — their suns will never dim; the Empress tends them like shepherded flame.

To rebels: Horror — their sun can be bled dry, extinguished as punishment.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: The literal agriculture of [[Fire]] — harvesting [[Stars]] to fuel eternal empire.

To myth: Called the “Amber Harvest”, sung as proof that the Empress plants galaxies and reaps their suns.
