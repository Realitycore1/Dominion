## **X. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Helios**

**Title:** Legio Helios – The Solar Wardens

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through illumination, incineration, and radiant guardianship. Legio Helios are the burning protectors of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], embodying the searing might of the sun. Their creed: *“None escape the light.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Helios**

* **Silhouette:** Towering and radiant, armor design emphasizes tall crests and flared plating like solar flares frozen in metal.
* **Illumination [[Systems]]:** Radiant corona projectors emit constant golden brilliance, surrounding the [[Warrior]] with an aura of blazing light.
* **Coloration:** Brilliant white armor with radiant gold and deep crimson trim. Plating glows faintly as though heat radiates from within.
* **Helmet:** Crowned with solar crests, visors resemble blazing sun-discs. Eyes radiate golden light, flaring brighter in combat.
* **Specialization:** Helios Cores—fusion-reactor nodes integrated into chest and back, enabling solar flare discharges, blinding pulses, and heatwave projection.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Helios Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Rifle and Heavy Rifle forms for radiant fusillades and [[Wide]]-area incineration.
* **Modification:** Weapons [[Fire]] concentrated solar plasma bolts or sustained beams, appearing as miniature suns on discharge.
* **Aesthetic:** Gilded plating with radiant engravings. Weapons glow as if containing caged [[Stars]].

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Guardians of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s most sacred places and spearheads of planetary purges, their presence equates to divine solar judgment.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their radiant advance overwhelms both sight and will—foes see them not as men, but as burning gods.
* **Legacy:** Legio Helios is remembered as the Solar Wardens—those who descend as burning suns, leaving scorched [[Earth (Terra)]] and purified ash in their wake.

Profile

Legion Helios commanded the “brightness lanes” — stabilized warp-light highways across the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s trillion-galaxy empire. Their fleets carried sun-core lances, flare engines, and illumination warheads capable of burning out entire dark-sector empires.
Helios embodied optimism, expansion, and radiance — the golden ideal of the First [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].
