## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch of Legio Ares

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Valerius Drakon is the founding Verarch of [[Legio Ares]].

He represents punitive war, frontal shock, anti-fortress doctrine, morale-breaking assault, the Dominion's refusal to be contained, the first transformation of defense into wrath, and the human answer to GalFed's compassionate violence.

His mature depiction preserves the existing visual canon:

- 22 feet tall;
- draconic helm;
- Aether-Breaker Spear;
- colossal warblade;
- crimson war-cloak;
- dragon-wing pauldrons;
- furnace eyes;
- phantom battlefields and war drums.

Moral line:

> Surrender given in witnessed terms is not prey.

Core line:

> Where Valerius leads, restraint ends - but only after judgment has failed.

Motto:
tte is unmistakable: shoulders crowned with serrated pauldrons shaped like [[Dragon]]-wings folded downwards.

Inspired by the Panoply set — overlapping plates, intricate engravings — but warped to his own essence: brutal elegance.

A great crimson war-cloak hangs from his shoulders, tattered yet monumental, dragging like a banner of blood.

The helm’s crest curves back like horns, marking his [[Dragon]] namesake.

Represents [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s wrath made flesh — the will to wage war without restraint until victory is absolute.

His Legion follows not just his commands, but his feral momentum: wherever Valerius leads, slaughter follows.

“War is the breath of [[Man]]; I am the [[Fire]] that makes him roar.”
