Core Bias:

Open continuity and distributed expanse

This is the baseline Ocean subtype.

Reality behaves like vast open medium with immense scale and low immediate obstruction.

Traits:
	•	broad connected space
	•	long-distance drift and travel
	•	sparse but meaningful nodes
	•	large roaming entities or civilizations
	•	expansive continuity between structures

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	open deep expanses
	•	civilizations floating in continuity
	•	endless routes through living medium
	•	freedom mixed with vulnerability

Common Expressions:
	•	widely dispersed aquatic or fluid civilizations
	•	current-based navigation systems
	•	migratory cosmic cultures
	•	basin-scale trade or thought currents

Failure Mode:

Drift dissolution
Things spread too thin to maintain coherence.
