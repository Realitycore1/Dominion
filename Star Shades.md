Star Shades are planetary- or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]-scale light filters, typically vast arrays of ultralight films, sail-like membranes, or coordinated swarms placed between a star and its planets. They regulate stellar flux for climate control, energy modulation, and symbolic projection.
Structure

Occultation Discs: Large membranes or panels (hundreds to thousands of km [[Wide]]) positioned at Lagrange points.

Swarm Arrays: Clouds of aligned reflectors or absorbers acting as programmable shutters.

Veil Nets: Semi-transparent sheets woven from metamaterials, filtering light selectively.

Control Swarms: MRO drones continually adjust alignment and replace panels.

Functions

Civil:
– Regulate planetary climate (cool hot worlds, warm cold ones).
– Prevent stellar flares or reduce long-term solar aging effects.
– Create “eternal twilight zones” for colonists.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Block light to cripple enemy solar power [[Infrastructure]].
– Create [[Artificial]] eclipses to plunge worlds into terror.
– Serve as staging grounds for orbital ambushes.

Religious:
– Align shades into glyph-light constellations, making the star appear to pulse scripture into [[The Void]].
– Used in rituals to create “holy eclipses” across entire worlds.

Variants

Planetary Shades: Protect or control individual [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] Shades: Swarms regulating whole planetary [[Systems]].

Sacral Shades: Arrays deliberately shaped into [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] hymns, visible across parsecs.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Proof of stewardship — the Empress literally tempers suns to make them gentle.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Fear weapon — “she can extinguish your sun.”

Economic: Enables colonization of worlds otherwise uninhabitable.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Shades ribbed like cathedral rose windows, vast black lace across suns.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they look like dark discs or crescents carved into stellar [[Fire]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Swarm-shades flutter in fractal spirals, forming shifting mandalas.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Light passing through them emerges as glyphs — the star literally shining scripture into the cosmos.

From a planetary surface, a Star Shade looks like a constant eclipse, a black rose cut into the face of the sun, glowing edges spelling patterns across the sky.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Comfort and awe — the sun itself obeys the Empress.

To rebels: Terror — their daylight can be stolen, their world veiled.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A symbol of mastery — not even suns shine without sanction.

To myth: Called the “Amber Veils of Heaven”, imagined as the Empress’s wedding veils, drawn across [[Stars]] to sanctify them.
Known as “The Amber Veils” — myths say these are the Empress’s veils, drawn across [[Stars]] to hide or reveal her light at will.
