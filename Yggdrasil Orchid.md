The Yggdrasil Orchid is a bio-mechanical megastructure grown in the likeness of a cosmic flower, fusing biotechnology, [[Artificial]] ecosystems, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] engineering. It serves as both habitat and reliquary, a living cathedral whose branching spines and petal-like vaults span hundreds to thousands of kilometers.

Religious: The Orchid is mythologized as the “Flower of the Empress,” each bloom a hymn unfolding across [[The Void]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the Orchid is a perfect fractal bloom against the black.
