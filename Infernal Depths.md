(The Sub-Hell / Pre-Oblivion Collapse Zones)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Degenerative Sub-Plane
	•	Authority Level: None (post-governance)
	•	Relation to Sheol-Gehenna: Below and outside its control
	•	Relation to the Empty: Adjacent but barred
	•	Relation to Axioms: Severely degraded, not voided

⸻

Core Nature

The Infernal Depths are where Hell stops working.

They are not ruled.
They are not ordered.
They are not meaningful.

If:
	•	Aravoth is authority through harmony
	•	Sheol-Gehenna is authority through malice

Then the Infernal Depths are what remains when authority collapses under its own cruelty.

This is where:
	•	punishment loses purpose,
	•	torment becomes mechanical,
	•	hatred forgets why it exists.

⸻

Fundamental Rule

Nothing in the Infernal Depths is “deserved.”

Damnation implies judgment.
The Depths have none.

⸻

Structure

There is no unified geography—only sinks, wounds, and pressure gradients beneath Hell.

Common manifestations include:
	•	Endless vertical drops that never land
	•	Crushing black strata layered with screaming fossils
	•	Caverns of fused bodies and architecture
	•	Oceans of boiling ichor where memories dissolve
	•	Gravity that pulls inward toward regret rather than mass

Time fragments here.
Events repeat without progress.

⸻

How the Infernal Depths Form

They form when:
	•	demonic hierarchies implode,
	•	Hell wars exhaust themselves,
	•	power accumulates without release,
	•	meaning is stripped from suffering.

Entire infernal cities have fallen into the Depths.

Demons fear this fate more than annihilation.

⸻

Inhabitants

1. Depthspawn

Not demons.
Not undead.
	•	Once-demonic entities whose identity eroded
	•	No ambition, no ideology
	•	Function as predatory reflexes

They hunt not for malice, but momentary coherence.

⸻

2. Punishment Husks

Souls abandoned mid-damnation.
	•	Torment continues without observers
	•	Pain persists without narrative
	•	Many eventually dissolve into environmental hazards

This is Hell’s greatest failure state.

⸻

3. Collapsed Princes

Former infernal rulers reduced to:
	•	immobile masses,
	•	screaming geological features,
	•	living labyrinths of regret.

Their names are erased even in Hell.

⸻

What Is Forbidden Here
	•	No contracts
	•	No bargains
	•	No ascension
	•	No ideology
	•	No redemption
	•	No escalation

Nothing improves.
Nothing concludes.

⸻

Relationship to the Empty

Critical distinction:
	•	The Empty = neutral custody, silence, rebirth potential
	•	Infernal Depths = endless malfunction, no exit

Souls do not pass through the Depths on the way to the Empty.

They are stuck.

This is why even demons avoid destroying souls completely—
better damnation than Depth-collapse.

⸻

Interaction with Other Planes

Sheol-Gehenna
	•	Officially denies the Depths’ existence
	•	Secretly dumps failures here
	•	Launches purges when Depth pressure rises

⸻

Aravoth
	•	Does not intervene
	•	Considers the Depths a consequence, not a sin
	•	Angels do not descend here; those who do do not return intact

⸻

Oneiric Plane
	•	Nightmares occasionally leak upward
	•	Depth imagery infects dreams as inescapable despair
	•	These nightmares do not “resolve”

⸻

Plane of Omens
	•	The Depths generate absence of omens
	•	When disasters produce no warning, Depth influence is suspected

⸻

Why the Infernal Depths Are More Terrifying Than Hell

Hell still:
	•	cares,
	•	judges,
	•	remembers.

The Infernal Depths do not.

They are:
	•	suffering without audience,
	•	torment without purpose,
	•	existence without justification.

This is why:
	•	demons fear falling,
	•	angels refuse rescue missions,
	•	mortals who glimpse the Depths lose faith in both Heaven and Hell
