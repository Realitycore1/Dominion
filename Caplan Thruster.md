## Summary

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Caplan Thruster is a stellar engine that uses a Dyson-like array of mirrors to focus a star’s own radiation and drive plasma exhaust jets, accelerating the star (and its planetary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]) through space.
It’s a refinement of the Shkadov Thruster (which only pushes via light pressure), except this version actively burns the star for thrust.
Structure

Mirror Arrays: Colossal light sails or lenses focus the star’s energy into propulsion beams.

Fusion Scoops: Capture stellar mass, fuse it, and eject as collimated plasma jets.

Balancing Thrusters: Smaller counter-thrust units maintain orbital stability of planets as the star drifts.

Performance

Acceleration: ~10⁻¹⁰ m/s² (tiny, but over millions of years it’s galactic-scale).

Travel: Can move a star thousands of light-years across galactic timescales.

Energy: Consumes part of the star’s luminosity and mass.

Safety

Requires [[Atlas Pillars]] to brace mirror swarms.

Autonomous MRO ecologies maintain mirror alignment and plasma nozzles.

Failsafe [[Systems]] can “vent” plasma in controlled arcs to prevent runaway.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civilizational Engineering

Used to migrate [[Stars]] away from dangerous [[Regions]] (gamma-ray bursts, galactic collisions).

Deployed as strategic re-alignment tools: moving entire [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] cores closer to Omniversal hubs.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Projection

The ultimate “fortress world” becomes a fortress [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], dragging its sun along like a war chariot.

Theological Dimension

Poets call them the “Chariots of Amber Light”, [[Stars]] yoked into her service.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Style)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Mirror towers shaped like cathedral spires, solar sails webbed with stained-glass light.

Suprematist Monolithism: The propulsion arrays appear as colossal black slabs, blotting parts of the sun.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Exhaust jets sculpted into spirals, glowing with Concordia harmonics.

Narrative Role

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] citizens: proof the Empress can move the heavens themselves.
