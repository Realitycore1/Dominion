[[Narrative]]
The Slow Burn: Rise of Discord on [[Aurora Station]]

Era: CU pre-Hubfall (Years 1–12 after Aurora’s arrival)
Scope: early integration pressures, faction formation, first flashpoints
Canon guards: PFN single paired link to CU hub; no colony↔colony FTL. Elevator remains pilot/test only. No mass colonist ark until ~Year 40; people referenced here are early civilian specialists, contractors, and dependents stationed in orbit to support ISRU.

Phase 1 — Arrival & Uneasy Integration (Years 1–[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]])
Initial frictions
Early civilian specialists arrive with surface-settlement expectations, but encounter:
Administrative choke points from CU’s orbital bureaucracy.
Rigid curfews, ID checkpoints, and surveillance corridors.
“Transit housing” in modular decks never meant for long-term habitation.
Official “environmental concerns” repeatedly delay general descent, even as CU-operated drones and short-stay specialists continue surface ISRU.
Cultural divide
CU staff occupy well-maintained core zones (private quarters, predictable rations, rec [[Time]]).
Civilians/contractors are pushed to peripheral sectors (older life-support trunks, rationed power windows).
Rumors spread of resource siphons, off-manifest shipments, and a quiet relay for privileged traffic.

Phase [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] — Structural Stress & Social Fragmentation (Years [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]–6)
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|System]] failures & scapegoating
Grid instability triggers a critical outage during a medical emergency—six civilians die.
CU command blames “improper civilian maintenance,” alienating station [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]].
Independent techs organize Grey Cells—cooperative workgroups that bypass official oversight to keep things running.
Black market emergence
Surface harvesters barter exotic Leslarut bio-organics for meds, parts, and favors.
An underground barter network takes root; CU crackdowns grow increasingly violent.
Food-quality and distribution privileges become a public scandal, spiking guard–civilian confrontations.

Phase [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] — Ideological [[Divergence]] (Years 7–10)
Faction formation
[[Equilon Union]] First (EUF)
Base: former [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]], civic planners.
Aim: full autonomy under a single executive to replace paralyzed bureaucracy.
Arc: begins reformist; hardens absolutist after repeated failed talks.
[[Remainers]] (CU loyalists)
Base: CU-aligned personnel and sympathizers.
Aim: preserve CU authority; brand dissenters as anarchists/secessionists.
Arc: collaboration with CU security → increasingly fanatical as tensions rise.
The Drifted (surface isolationists)
Base: surface miners, deep-water harvesters, independent [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]].
Aim: reject CU orbital politics; adapt to Leslarut itself.
Practice: reclaim old survey skiffs, run quiet ferries, seed unregistered subsurface outposts.
Faces that matter
[[William Zohair]] (EUF): visionary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] engineer; publicly pragmatic, privately uncompromising.
[[Emilia Navarro]] (Drifted): smuggler-turned-organizer; builds semi-nomadic island [[Chains]].
Remainer operatives: propagandists/saboteurs inside civilian sectors, feeding CU security.

Phase [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] — Irreversible Polarization (Years 11–12)
Breaking points
Aurora Riot (Deck-9 Incident): terraforming supplies are rerouted to CU command; protest spirals into violence. Five dead, dozens wounded; media blacked out.
High Surveyor-General assassinated by unknowns (strong signs of internal actors).
CU imposes Martial Protocol Gamma: civilian decks locked down, mass detentions, full surveillance grid lit.
Inception of revolt
Suppression catalyzes rebellion rather than quelling it.
Grey Cells become the technical/logistics spine of EUF.
[[Remainers]] attempt a failed grab for Aurora’s reactor controls, poisoning trust further.
Drifted sever from orbit—transponders dark, expanding rogue subsurface ecosystems.
Station & Surface (quick reference)
[[Aurora Station]]: [[O’Neill Cylinder]] in Leslarut orbit; 1 g at rim; sensitive labs rely on vibration isolation and acoustic baffling (no hand-wavy “dampeners”).
Surface posture: ISRU-first only.
Blackrock Chain: relay + sensor outpost (met/ionosphere/seismic + anomaly detection).
Equatorial Test Spire: tether pilot hardware; full elevator deferred.
Vanthe Seamounts: undersea mining [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Pilots]] (rare-isotope yield, hazardous conditions).
Comms (PFN): Single paired FTL link to CU hub; content E2E-encrypted; headers/byte counts auditable. No colony↔colony FTL.
How this dovetails with later canon
Sets the table for Hubfall and the Ghost Relay deception.
Establishes EUF/[[Remainers]]/Drifted as living factions before the first arkship wave (~Year 40).
Keeps tech inside early–moderate interstellar limits and preserves the ~200-year distance-lag established elsewhere.
