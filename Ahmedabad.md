13 Ahmedabad is a fast-growing and modern city located in the western Indian state of Gujarat. It is the capital of the Gujarat state and the sixth-largest city in [[India]]. Ahmedabad has a population of around 6 million people, making it one of the fastest-growing cities in [[India]]. Ahmedabad is an industrial and commercial hub, known for its textile, chemical, and leather industries. It is also a major center for agriculture, with crops like mangoes, oranges, sugarcane, and cotton being grown in the surrounding areas. The city has a vibrant cultural heritage, with its diverse population of Muslims, Hindus, and Parsis adding to its rich cultural tapestry. The Qutbuddin Mahal Palace and the Kankaria Lake are two iconic landmarks of the city that reflect its Islamic and colonial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. Ahmedabad is also known for its strong focus on education and healthcare. The city has several top-ranked universities and medical colleges, including Gandhi Medical College and Shaligram Medical College. The city's transport [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] includes an extensive network of roads, highways, and railways. Ahmedabad also has an international airport and is connected to other cities in [[India]] by air and road. The city is undergoing rapid urbanization and [[Infrastructure]] development, with new buildings, malls, and high-rise complexes rising up across the city. Ahmedabad has also been ranked as one of the most liveable cities in [[India]] due to its good quality of life, low crime rate, and high level of civic amenities.
