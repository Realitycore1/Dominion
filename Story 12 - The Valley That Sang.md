# Story 12 - The Valley That Sang

## Viewpoint

Iri Venn, a weather technician on her first independent storm survey.

## Setting

Years 18-40 AL, a Needleback migration valley.

## Story Shape

Iri enters the valley to record storm resonance and prove that the reports are exaggerated. Then the Needlebacks lift their crystalline spines into the wind, and the whole basin begins to sing.

The sound is biological, meteorological, and almost unbearable. A technician kneels without meaning to. Later, no one can agree whether the valley should be treated as a research site, a hazard zone, or a pilgrimage road.

## Harmina Element

Needlebacks make severe weather audible as landscape-scale music.

## Character Movement

Iri learns that explanation does not cancel awe.

## Future Payoff

The valley becomes one of the first places where Harminans argue over scientific access versus cultural reverence.
