1. True Dragons
	•	Definition: The baseline—the great winged, [[Fire]]-breathing reptilian giants of myth.
	•	Traits: Four limbs + wings, scales, elemental breath (usually [[Fire]]).
	•	Symbolism: Chaos, majesty, destruction, wealth hoarding.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Wyrms
	•	Definition: Serpentine, wingless dragons (older and more primal).
	•	Traits: Elongated bodies, burrowing or aquatic, ancient wisdom.
	•	Symbolism: Primordial chaos; serpents of knowledge and doom.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. [[Wyverns]]
	•	Definition: Lesser draconic beasts, with two legs + wings.
	•	Traits: Venomous tails, animal cunning, scavengers.
	•	Symbolism: Bestial echo of dragons; the raw predator.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Drakes]]
	•	Definition: Small or mid-sized dragons, often elemental-bound or beast-like.
	•	Traits: Flightless or short-winged, pack hunters, tied to environments.
	•	Symbolism: Lesser reflections of draconic might.

⸻

5. Elemental Dragons
	•	Definition: Dragons tied to primal forces.
	•	Variants: [[Fire]], Frost, Storm, [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]], Acid, Shadow, Radiant, etc.
	•	Symbolism: Personifications of nature’s fury.

⸻

6. Sea Dragons
	•	Definition: Oceanic or lake-bound dragons.
	•	Traits: Finned wings, breath of steam or crushing water jets.
	•	Symbolism: The terror of the deep; Leviathan archetype.

⸻

7. Celestial / Astral Dragons
	•	Definition: Dragons of the heavens, starlight, or cosmic [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Luminous scales, psionic or dream powers, constellation bodies.
	•	Symbolism: Immortality, fate, the unreachable sky.

⸻

8. Shadow / Abyssal Dragons
	•	Definition: Dragons infused with void, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], or infernal essence.
	•	Traits: Necrotic breath, blackened wings, skeletal features.
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of majesty into [[Hunger]].

⸻

9. Metallic Dragons
	•	Definition: Dragons with metallic scales, often noble or wise.
	•	Traits: Gold, silver, bronze, brass, copper variations; breath and magic vary.
	•	Symbolism: Guardianship, justice, the “noble” dragon archetype.

⸻

10. Gem Dragons
	•	Definition: Dragons tied to crystalline forms and psionic powers.
	•	Traits: Amethyst, sapphire, emerald, ruby, topaz scales; psychic or force breath.
	•	Symbolism: Knowledge, inner worlds, mind made manifest.

⸻

11. Imperial Dragons
	•	Definition: Serpentine, wingless Eastern dragons tied to balance and fortune.
	•	Traits: Four-legged serpentine bodies, cloud-dwellers, rain-bringers.
	•	Symbolism: Cosmic order, wisdom, harmony.

⸻

12. Primordial Dragons
	•	Definition: Firstborn dragons who embody aspects of creation.
	•	Traits: Titanic size, continent-shaping power, semi-divine.
	•	Symbolism: Creation myths, apocalypse bringers.

⸻

13. Lich Dragons (Dracoliches)
	•	Definition: Dragons who pursue undeath to extend power.
	•	Traits: Skeletal forms, necrotic breath, corrupted hoards.
	•	Symbolism: Greed carried beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

14. Mythic Dragons (World-Dragons)
	•	Definition: Dragons so vast they embody worlds, [[Time]], or eternity.
	•	Traits: Scales = continents, breath = storms, wings = skies.
	•	Symbolism: The dragon as the universe itself; Ouroboros archetype.
