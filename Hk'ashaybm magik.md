Hk'ashaybm magik comes from the strange [[Origin]] that these souls have to the Dreamlands.

To sever that connection would undo most of such magics that the host possess, as their soul slowly unravels.

There is something about the main 13 that seems to keep this connection, even if strained. And the 13 have the ability to retreat to the Dreamlands if under threat of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], however rituals are usually placed by them before [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] so that they swap to another body rather then back to their [[Domain]].
