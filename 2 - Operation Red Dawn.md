The focus of the Red Dawn operation is to achieve generational brainwashing by implementing a series of steps over several decades. The goal is to subvert the values and beliefs of a population and create a generation that is loyal to communist ideals. The first stage of the operation is demoralization, which involves undermining the confidence and morale of the target population. This is achieved through a variety of means, including propaganda, economic instability, and social unrest. The second stage is destabilization, which involves creating a crisis that causes further social and political upheaval. This stage may involve the use of violence, as well as propaganda and misinformation. Once the target population has been demoralized and destabilized, the third stage is normalization. This involves establishing a new social order that is more in line with communist ideals. This stage often involves the consolidation of power by the communist regime and the elimination of any opposition. The final stage is to establish a generational cycle of brainwashing, ensuring that future generations are also indoctrinated with communist ideals. This involves using schools, media, and other institutions to propagate communist propaganda. To prevent discovery and subterfuge from outside forces, cells under the Red Dawn operation maintain little to no contact with other cell networks. This helps to keep their activities secret and allows them to operate independently.
