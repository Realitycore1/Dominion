Architectural Solutions

[[Atlas Pillars]]: Used as gravitational stilts around black holes, redistributing tidal stresses.

Void Cradle Casings: Encasing smaller [[Artificial]] singularities inside entropic null zones for added stability.

Skimmer Belts: Harvesting accretion plasma while shielding habitats deeper within the structure.

MRO Ecologies: Permanently assigned to black hole constructs — drone swarms crawl endlessly to patch radiation scars and gravitational distortions.
