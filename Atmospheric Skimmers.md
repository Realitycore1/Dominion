## Summary

To locals, 
Core Functions

[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Atmospheric Skimmers are aero-[[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] or vessels designed to harvest, refine, or stabilize planetary atmospheres. They are the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s way of turning entire skies into resources, defenses, or living laboratories.

Resource Harvesting

Strip gases (hydrogen, helium, nitrogen, xenon) for starship fuel and industrial feedstock.

Skim water vapor and organics to feed orbital arcologies.

Deploy aerostat mines and drones to maintain constant “sips” from jetstreams.

Terraforming Support

Remove excess greenhouse gases, ozone-depleting compounds, or toxic smogs.

Seed atmospheres with engineered aerosols or microbes.

Skimmers can write a climate the way a farmer tills a field.

Defense & Control

Carry Concordia plasma capacitors, discharging lightning-storm barrages on surface or orbital targets.

Can whip up engineered storms as weapons, weaponizing the planet’s own sky.

In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] occupation, Skimmers act as constant reminders overhead — the sky itself bears the Empress’s seal.

Surveillance & Sovereignty

Bristling with sensor spires that read everything from wind currents to brainwave emissions.

Skimmers double as skyborne fortresses, monitoring entire [[Regions]].

Architectural Form

Design Language:

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Jagged keel-ribs like cathedral buttresses.

Suprematist Monolithism: Vast, smooth undercarriages like slabs gliding through clouds.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Shifting vent lattices that inhale/exhale airflows.

Visual Impression:

Imagine continent-sized manta rays made of metal and glass, drifting just below the stratosphere.

Ventral intakes glow faintly as they sip atmospheres, trailing aurora-like contrails.

Surface covered in towers and domes like a mobile city suspended in the clouds.

Scales

Aero-[[Corvettes]] (Minor Skimmers):

Smaller skimmers, used for single-biome harvesting or battlefield weather-control.

Stratospheric [[Leviathans]]:

Kilometers across, spanning continents. Serve as both city-ships and harvesters.

Planetary Mantles:

Entire planetary shields of interlinked skimmers, creating a “net” around a world’s atmosphere.

Both terraformers and fortresses.

Narrative Role
Atmospheric Skimmers are both terrifying and awe-inspiring — the sky itself made mechanical.

Resistance movements see them as monstrous occupiers: colossal, slow-moving targets that are impossibly hard to destroy.

For [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] citizens, they are security and providence: the machines that feed them, shield them, and remake hostile worlds into paradise.
