After Mao's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] in 1976, China embarked on a restructuring process that transformed its governing [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] from a cult of personality to one of Trotskyism. The transformation involved the gradual de-emphasis on Maoist ideology and an increasing focus on the works of Trotsky, a prominent Bolshevik leader and theorist. The roots of this restructuring can be traced back to the chaotic Cultural Revolution of the 1960s and 1970s. Mao's efforts to implement his ideas through the mobilization of the masses led to rampant chaos, corruption, and political infighting that ultimately destabilized the country. This had a profound impact on Chinese society, eroding public confidence in Maoist ideology and giving rise to a widespread [[Hunger]] for reform and stability. In response to this discontent, Deng Xiaoping, who had previously served as China's premier and foreign minister, began to implement reforms that aimed to address the country's economic and social problems. These reforms included a series of steps designed to gradually de-emphasize Maoist ideology and place greater emphasis on market forces, technological innovation, and scientific development. At the same [[Time]], Deng also began to place greater emphasis on Trotskyist theory. Trotsky was seen as a critical thinker who had pushed for more democratic forms of central planning and collective decision-making. This approach resonated with the reformist agenda of Deng Xiaoping and became the basis for a more participatory form of governance that would help build the new socialist society. Over [[Time]], Trotskyism became increasingly entrenched in Chinese political discourse, with many scholars and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] arguing that it provided a more balanced and practical approach to governance than the more authoritarian forms of Marxism-Leninism espoused by Mao. Today, Trotskyist thought continues to inform Chinese political philosophy and practice, providing a framework for democratic centralism and economic reform.
