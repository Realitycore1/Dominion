10.8 London, the capital city of the United Kingdom, is a bustling metropolis with a rich [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and [[culture]] that spans centuries. The city is located on the River Thames, which has played a crucial role in its development and growth over the years. The river has been a major transportation artery, and its port has been a hub of trade and commerce since ancient times. London's architecture is a mix of [[Styles]] and periods, from the Roman amphitheater at the Roman London Wall to the modern skyscrapers of Canary Wharf. The city is known for its iconic landmarks such as the Tower of London, the Big Ben clock tower, and the Buckingham Palace. The cultural scene in London is vibrant and diverse, with world-renowned museums such as the British Museum and the Tate Modern showcasing the country's rich artistic heritage. The city is also home to numerous theaters, galleries, and music venues that cater to all tastes and preferences. The city is also a hub for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] and innovation, with startups and tech giants thriving in areas such as Silicon Roundabout and Shoreditch. Despite its cosmopolitan nature, London remains steeped in tradition, with a strong sense of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].
