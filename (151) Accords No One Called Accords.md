# (151) Accords No One Called Accords

## What Happened

In Year 151 AL, practical agreements between settlements became too consistent to dismiss. They covered train rights, route shelter obligations, resource exchange, emergency response, pressure-zone maintenance, archive transfers, education credentials, and apprenticeship recognition.

## What Resulted

- Proto-diplomacy emerged under administrative language.
- Settlement blocs began acting like powers before they claimed nationhood.
- New Aurora's role shifted from parent city to first among negotiating centers.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Year 151 accords became the legal and cultural foundation for later recognized states.
