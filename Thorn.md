Core Bias:

Defensive and exclusionary growth

Growth exists, but it is armored, territorial, and painful to penetrate.

Traits:
	•	defensive ecologies
	•	self-protective complexity
	•	hostile perimeter behavior
	•	barriers, toxins, exclusion fields
	•	adaptive immunity at reality scale

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	reality does not want to be entered
	•	life is barbed, layered, and territorial
	•	every growth is also a defense system

Common Expressions:
	•	isolationist civilizations
	•	defensive biospheres
	•	anti-intrusion worlds
	•	highly immune ecological systems

Failure Mode:

Autistic closure / over-isolation
The basin becomes too defended to evolve further.

This subtype is extremely useful for:
	•	quarantine verses
	•	anti-xeno ecologies
	•	sacred or forbidden growth zones

Very strong subtype.
