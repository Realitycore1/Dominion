Wealth exists in [[Austro Guard]], but true status flows from honor capital—a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] of esteem tied to the Oath and one’s fulfillment of duty. Money buys comfort; honor buys respect, trust, and influence.
	•	Family Walls of Duty
Each family maintains a carved wall, shrine, or digital archive inscribing the names of members who have served as Oathkeepers. Visitors [[Judge]] a lineage by the thickness of its wall. A family with many names earns instant respect; one with few must prove itself through sacrifice or service.
	•	Service Ledgers
Beyond serving as an Oathkeeper, honor accrues through recorded deeds:
	•	Guard Service ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] duty, patrols, defense)
	•	Sacrifice (loss of life or limb in service)
	•	Burden-Taking (accepting undesirable posts or volunteering for hard labor in crisis)
	•	Oathkeeping Acts (exemplary fairness while in civic office)
These deeds are tracked in public ledgers, inscribed both physically in basalt tablets and mirrored in digital archives.
	•	Trade of Honor
Honor cannot be bought, but it can be traded socially.
	•	Families with surplus honor may “lend” their reputation to struggling kin through sponsorship in ceremonies or appointments.
	•	Marriages often involve negotiation over honor balance sheets, much as dowries once did on [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].
	•	Dishonor spreads through association—an oath-breaker can tarnish entire lineages if ties are not severed.
	•	Punishment Through Honor
Instead of fines or prisons, lesser crimes are punished by honor deduction: removal of a name from the ledger, erasure from a Wall, or public branding of dishonor. Severe dishonor marks one as a “Shadowed,” a pariah with no civic [[Rights]].
	•	Symbols of Esteem
	•	Steel Rings: Polished and plain at oath-swearing, rings gain notches or etchings for each major service recorded. A heavily scarred ring signals high honor.
	•	Line Banners: Families embroider small sigils for each Oathkeeper served. A banner bristling with sigils is a symbol of prestige when paraded during festivals.
	•	Honor Lanterns: Annual festivals display lanterns sized according to a family’s honor score, turning the city into a glowing hierarchy of esteem.

The honor [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] ensures that status cannot ossify into wealth or inheritance alone—every generation must renew its claim by serving, sacrificing, and adding to the Wall of Duty.
