The Fortress Worlds are Massive Bunker sized worlds that serve as bastions against invaders, and can hold massive Armadas within. The Fortress Worlds are heavily defended. For every turret there are two more turrets covering that one. And the surface is totally covered by Artillery and anti orbital weapons. The Fortress world itself is a complex maze designed to confuse the enemy. The Fortress Worlds are used as a First line of  defense, and are used as examples of the strength and the Iron-Will  of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]].
