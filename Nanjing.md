10.[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] Nanjing, also known as Nanking, is a major city located in the eastern province of Jiangsu, China. It is the capital of the Jiangsu province and one of the most significant cultural centers in China. Nanjing is a vibrant and historic city with a rich cultural heritage dating back to the Warring States period. The city is situated on the banks of the Yangtze River and the Qinhuai River, which have been important waterways for trade and commerce throughout [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. Nanjing has a population of around 8 million people, making it the fourth-largest city in China. It is known for its beautiful gardens, historic sites, and traditional Chinese cuisine. One of the most significant landmarks of Nanjing is its Xuanwumen Gate, which was built in 1653 and is the largest city gate in the world. The gate is part of the Ming dynasty city wall that encircled the city. Other significant historical sites include Nanjing Museum, which houses an extensive collection of Chinese artifacts and art, and Fucheng Shitang, a historic street that is home to many old-world shops and eateries. The city's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is diversified, with significant contributions from manufacturing, tourism, finance, education, and research. Nanjing is home to several multinational companies, including Alibaba Group, Walmart China, and Marriott International. Nanjing has a subtropical climate with warm summers and mild winters. The city is also home to several universities and colleges, including Nanjing University, Nanjing Agricultural University, and Nanjing Normal University.
