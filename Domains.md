[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]]

Magic that uses lifeforce, blood, and essence as currency.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Alteration magic]] – reshaping the body via blood essence.
	•	[[Binding magic]] – blood oaths and soul [[Chains]].
	•	[[Domination magic]] – blood-linked compulsions.
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – soul obliteration through blood sacrifice.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – life-forging through blood [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Aphotic Zone/Concordia/Concordia/Index/Templates|Templates]].

⸻

[[Alteration magic]]

Reshaping matter, form, or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] without creating or erasing.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]] – biological alteration via blood essence.
	•	[[Binding magic]] – stabilizing altered states with seals.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – uncovering hidden forms to alter them.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – archetypal transformations.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – alteration of laws themselves.
	•	[[Motion magic]] – enhancing speed or agility through form shifts.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – blending reshaping with manifestation.

⸻

[[Binding magic]]

Restraining, sealing, or compelling through oaths, [[Chains]], or runes.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]] – blood-bound contracts.
	•	[[Alteration magic]] – anchoring unstable forms.
	•	[[Domination magic]] – overlap with compulsions and oaths.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – wards against scrying and prophecy.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – social and narrative bindings.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – anchoring [[Axioms]] to prevent collapse.
	•	[[Motion magic]] – counterpoint: restraint vs. movement.

⸻

[[Revelation magic]]

Divination, prophecy, scrying, truthsight — uncovering hidden layers.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Binding magic]] – veils and wards conceal against revelation.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – prophecy often grows into myth.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – axiom-vision as revelation’s pinnacle.
	•	[[Domination magic]] – mental intrusion framed as “revelation.”
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – erased knowledge resists revelation.

⸻

[[Mythopoeia magic]]

Story and belief shaping reality, manifesting myths and [[Archetypes]].

Cross-References:
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – blurred line between prophecy and myth.
	•	[[Binding magic]] – myths bind societies and contracts.
	•	[[Domination magic]] – propaganda and narrative control.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – myth-born gods and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]].
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – stories resisting or rewriting [[Axioms]].
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – strong myths resist being erased.

⸻

[[Axiomatic Warping magic]]

Rewriting the root laws of [[(2030) Existence]].

Cross-References:
	•	[[Binding magic]] – axiomatic anchors are highest-level bindings.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – prophecy as glimpses of axiom roots.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – narrative can override axiom collapse.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – requires bending conservation [[Axioms]].
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – begins with axiom erasure.
	•	[[Motion magic]] – rewriting inertia or velocity as laws.

⸻

[[Motion magic]]

Manipulating vectors, momentum, and displacement across space.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Binding magic]] – [[Natural]] counterpoint to motion.
	•	[[Alteration magic]] – reshaping form for speed/agility.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – rewriting inertia and gravity.
	•	[[Creation magic]] – world-shaping through displacement.
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – displacing matter into void.

⸻

[[Domination magic]]

Bending will, thought, and emotion into obedience.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Binding magic]] – geasa and enforced oaths.
	•	[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]] – blood compulsions and soul-binding.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – mind intrusion as revelation.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – domination through shared stories.
	•	(→ later link: [[Necrosis Magic]]) – [[Undead]] thralls as domination’s endpoint.

⸻

[[Annihilation magic]]

Unmaking matter, energy, and even memory into absence.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Creation magic]] – annihilation’s perfect opposite.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] – erased truths leave prophetic blind spots.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – erasure through axiom collapse.
	•	[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]] – blood sacrifice as soul-erasure.
	•	[[Motion magic]] – void displacement.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – myths resist annihilation’s reach.

⸻

[[Creation magic]]

Bringing forth new matter, life, or laws.

Cross-References:
	•	[[Alteration magic]] – reshaping blended with creation.
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] – its opposite twin.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] – myths manifested as creations.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] – requires axiom-bending to allow “from nothing.”
	•	[[Vitality magic]] – [[Blood Magic]] – blood used as life-template.
	•	[[Motion magic]] – shaping landscapes or summoning worlds through displacement.
