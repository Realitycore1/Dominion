# Labor and Service Standards Commission

## Summary

The Labor and Service Standards Commission regulates work, service obligations, apprenticeship systems, public labor contracts, clone workforce protections, and occupational safety.

It is a central institution in a civilization where service can grant status, public duty is culturally honored, and exploitation can hide behind patriotic language.

## Mandate

The Commission oversees:

- employment contracts;
- service-track work placements;
- apprenticeship conditions;
- workplace safety;
- clone and hybrid labor protections;
- wage floors and benefit schedules;
- military-family employment rights;
- anti-coercion rules in public service;
- retirement and reassignment systems.

## Powers

It may inspect workplaces, suspend abusive contracts, fine employers, certify service credit, and refer forced-labor cases to Federal courts.

## Limits

It cannot compel lifelong work assignment, reduce engineered or cloned people to labor categories, or treat poverty as proof of low civic worth.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Commission through workplace audits, apprenticeship postings, service-credit disputes, hazard pay claims, and labor hearings.

## Story Use

Use this agency to explore the moral edge of Dominion meritocracy: when service becomes honor, burden, pathway, and trap at the same time.
