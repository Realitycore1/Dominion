I. Physical Definition

In conventional [[Physics]], neutrinos are extremely light, neutral leptons that interact only via the weak nuclear force and gravity. A neutrinonic plasma is a hypothetical high-density, high-energy collective state of neutrinos intermixed with a sparse charged component (electrons/positrons) to form a coherent plasma-like medium.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), neutrinonic plasma is a dual-[[Domain]] matter phase — existing partially in the weak-interaction lattice and partially in the baryonic plasma lattice.
