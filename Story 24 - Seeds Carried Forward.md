# Story 24 - Seeds Carried Forward

## Viewpoint

A future archive clerk assembling ordinary Harminan records into an exhibit.

## Setting

After the main collection timeline, before the later Dominion fully mythologizes Harmina.

## Story Shape

The clerk expects history to be speeches, battles, and founding documents. The exhibit boxes contain something else: a stained ration ledger, a child's Echo Bloom drawing, heatline repair chants, a second hymnal draft, route petitions, storm maps, clinic consent forms, and a train schedule annotated by three settlements.

The clerk realizes the Dominion's origins are not only in founders. They are in ordinary systems that kept people alive long enough to become a people.

## Harmina Element

Harmina is revealed as an archive of lived adaptation.

## Character Movement

The clerk stops looking for the single decisive moment and begins arranging the exhibit as a chorus.

## Future Payoff

This final story frames the anthology itself: not the official story of Harmina, but the records official history nearly overlooks.
