Core Bias:

Built monumental design

This subtype is where the basin feels less like “machine” and more like:

civilization as intentional, aesthetic, total architecture.

Traits:
	•	designed spatial hierarchy
	•	monumental built environments
	•	formalized geometry
	•	civic or sacred layout logic
	•	civilization-scale urbanity

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the world was master-planned
	•	structure exists because someone intended every line
	•	continuity is an act of architecture

Common Expressions:
	•	hypercities
	•	built paradise or empire basins
	•	world-shells
	•	ceremonial or state-designed realities

Failure Mode:

Rigidity of plan
The basin becomes too designed to adapt to unplanned novelty.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	Dominion-style megastructure realities
	•	planned empire worlds
	•	urban or palace cosmologies
	•	built-civilization basins
