General Clayton Voss
Party: Liberty and Sovereignty Caucus
Region: Midwest / Appalachian Axis
Faction: Constitutionalist Nationalist
Future Alignment: Isolationist [[Earth (Terra)]] Sovereignty / Domestic Federalist Revival

Background:
Former Chairman of Joint Operations, Voss was forced into early retirement after opposing biometric surveillance integration across the armed forces. He became a firebrand speaker and militia unifier, restoring fractured regional defense leagues into a cohesive political movement. Voss operates under a revived Articles of Confederation-based charter, rejecting all supranational accords and global digital [[law]].

Traits:
Decorated veteran of foreign conflict zones
Deeply distrustful of centralization and corporate-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] fusion
Spearheads the "Back to the Republic" movement
Supported by southern industrial zones, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] families, and digital libertarians

Quotes:
"This land doesn’t need reformation. It needs remembrance."
