After a total global collapse, societies reorganize not around ideology, but around mechanisms of survival.

By 2678, four core cultural models dominate the planet.

⸻

1. Archivist Cultures

Survival strategy: Preserve knowledge → become indispensable.

These are enclaves built around:
	•	libraries
	•	data vaults
	•	preserved servers
	•	power plants
	•	medical knowledge
	•	engineering guilds

Examples:
	•	Hydrocliff Consortium (Rockies)
	•	Neo-Kyoto Vault (Japan)
	•	Andean Sky-Steppes ([[South America]]~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Overview/Faction Profile/UAR/South America]])
	•	Antarctic Crown Cities

Cultural Traits:
	•	literacy is sacred
	•	apprenticeships last decades
	•	memory of the Old World becomes scripture
	•	meritocratic but rigid
	•	every tool is symbolic
	•	rituals resemble academic ceremonies turned tribal

Mythic archetype:

The Keepers of the Flame

They become the backbone of later intercontinental civilizations.

⸻

2. Warlord-Technocrat Cultures

Survival strategy: Control machines and [[Infrastructure]] → control people.

These societies form around whoever manages to keep:
	•	drones
	•	fusion batteries
	•	weapons
	•	armored vehicles
	•	desal plants
	•	local grids

Examples:
	•	Teuton Archipelago Warlords (Europe)
	•	Red Sea Arcology Union (Middle East)
	•	Gulfen Reach Clans with repurposed drones

Cultural Traits:
	•	authoritarian
	•	hierarchical
	•	valorize martial ability
	•	blend religion with machine-worship
	•	codes of honor based on maintenance skill and combat expertise

Mythic archetype:

The Lords of Iron

Feared and admired; their fall or assimilation later dramatically reshapes the post-Discordian world.

⸻

3. Eco-Survivalist & Bio-Adaptive Cultures

Survival strategy: Rewild, adapt, and live with the new Earth.

These emerge in [[Regions]] where [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] fails but biodiversity is rich.

Examples:
	•	Amazonian Mire Rovers
	•	Congo Verdant Confederacies
	•	Indonesian Swarm-Tribes
	•	Australian Outback Tribes

Cultural Traits:
	•	highly adaptive
	•	fluid social structures
	•	reverence for ecology
	•	extensive medicinal plant knowledge
	•	flexible gender roles
	•	oral traditions dominate
	•	often seen as “primitive” but in reality biologically advanced

Mythic archetype:

The Green-Walkers

The freest people in the Discordia Aera, often underestimated—but culturally resilient.

⸻

4. Maritime & Archipelagic Cultures

Survival strategy: The sea replaces the world.

Sea-level rise fractures continents, creating new maritime empires.

Examples:
	•	New Orleans Archipelago
	•	Skygrave Republics (Arctic)
	•	Mediterranean Neo-City Federations
	•	Philippine Shattered Isles

Cultural Traits:
	•	decentralized democracy (ship crews = families)
	•	piracy normalized as trade
	•	xenophilic (constant contact with outsiders)
	•	hybrid languages
	•	intricate navigation mythologies
	•	salvage-based economies

Mythic archetype:

The Tide-Born
