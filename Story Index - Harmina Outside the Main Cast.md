# Story Index - Harmina Outside the Main Cast

## Purpose

This index converts the old act-and-chapter spine into a short-story collection about Harmina as lived by people outside the main cast.

Each story should be readable alone, but together they form a cultural history.

## Cycle I - The Struggle

1. [[Story 01 - First Breath Day]] - A young suit tech watches the first settlers learn that breathable air is not the same as safety.
2. [[Story 02 - Bitter Kitchen]] - A ration cook discovers that local abundance can still mean starvation.
3. [[Story 03 - Wallwatch During Hunger]] - A tired perimeter guard faces a food riot without knowing whether the crowd is wrong.
4. [[Story 04 - The Fabricator Queue]] - A fabrication clerk becomes the face of scarcity when machines keep people alive but cannot make them feel secure.
5. [[Story 05 - The First Burial That Failed]] - Grave workers learn that even mourning needs new rules on Harmina.
6. [[Story 06 - Storm Week Lessons]] - A maintenance family survives the first long storm and invents household rituals from repair procedures.

## Cycle II - The Relearning

7. [[Story 07 - Soil That Bites Back]] - A failed farmer stops trying to copy Earth and learns to read Harmina on its own terms.
8. [[Story 08 - The Relearners]] - Biologists and engineers create microbial intermediaries while arguing over whether survival has become a religion.
9. [[Story 09 - The Day the Founder Did Not Speak]] - A meeting clerk watches colonists react to a founder who refuses to become their answer.
10. [[Story 10 - The First Harminan Meal]] - A kitchen crew serves the first adapted local food and realizes relief can become ritual.
11. [[Story 11 - Echo Bloom Homework]] - A school exercise in an Echo Bloom field turns history into a voice children can hear.
12. [[Story 12 - The Valley That Sang]] - Weather technicians document Needlebacks during a storm and accidentally create a local pilgrimage site.
13. [[Story 13 - Lanterns Above Route Six]] - A route crew mistakes Lantern swarms for machines and gives a settlement its first sky myth.
14. [[Story 14 - Hollow Runner Weather]] - A frontier child learns that some voices in the storm are animals, and some fears should still be respected.

## Cycle III - The Rebirth

15. [[Story 15 - The Choir of Survivors]] - A sanctuary singer helps transform collapse testimony into Harminan worship.
16. [[Story 16 - The Grave and the Cradle]] - A midwife and a memorial registrar share a shift during a birth beside the burial wall.
17. [[Story 17 - The Petition of Routes]] - A route family petitions for recognition and changes the meaning of home.
18. [[Story 18 - Convoy Season]] - A teacher leaves New Aurora with a convoy and realizes the city is no longer the whole world.
19. [[Story 19 - First Winter Away]] - A remote heatline settlement survives winter and begins to resent being called an outpost.
20. [[Story 20 - The Second Hymnal]] - Shared faith splits into regional song without anyone intending schism.
21. [[Story 21 - Accords No One Named]] - Train schedules, shelter obligations, and apprenticeship records become diplomacy before anyone says nation.
22. [[Story 22 - Skyview Day]] - Children learn about orbit, GalFed, and planetary defense as civic education rather than secret terror.
23. [[Story 23 - The Body Question]] - A clinic counselor watches beauty engineering, pain reduction, and clone labor become dinner-table politics.
24. [[Story 24 - Seeds Carried Forward]] - A future archivist assembles ordinary records and sees the Dominion forming in handwriting, stains, songs, and route maps.

## Collection Rule

Every story should answer one question:

What did Harmina make ordinary that would have seemed impossible to the first settlers?
