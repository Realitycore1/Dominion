# Story 03 - Wallwatch During Hunger

## Viewpoint

Esai Corven, on volunteer perimeter duty after three nights without proper sleep.

## Setting

Year 0 AL, New Aurora wall gate during the food riot.

## Story Shape

The crowd is not monstrous. It is hungry, frightened, and tired of being told that another committee is working on it. Esai stands between them and the supply depot with a stun baton he does not want to use.

People chant for the founder to intervene. Esai has never met her. He only knows that everyone wants one person to become the answer because the alternative is admitting no one has one yet.

## Harmina Element

The colony's first civic conflict is shown as ration pressure, public fear, and the emotional failure of emergency legitimacy.

## Character Movement

Esai expects order to mean obedience. He ends the story realizing order also has to mean food, explanation, and someone willing to be seen while people are afraid.

## Future Payoff

Wallwatch culture later becomes one of New Aurora's civic myths: the people who stood between panic and violence without pretending hunger was treason.
