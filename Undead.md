1. Skeletons
	•	Definition: Animated bones bound by necromancy or curses.
	•	Traits: Fragile but tireless, armed with crude weapons.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] stripped to its frame; inevitability made mobile.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Zombies
	•	Definition: Reanimated corpses, mindless or semi-aware.
	•	Traits: Decay, slow movement (or frenzied [[Hunger]]), overwhelming numbers.
	•	Symbolism: Fear of contagion, loss of self, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as plague.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Ghouls
	•	Definition: Flesh-eating undead that retain cunning.
	•	Traits: Elongated limbs, ravenous [[Hunger]], graveyard haunters.
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of appetite; [[Humanity]] reduced to carrion-feasting.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Wights
	•	Definition: Undead with preserved will and malice.
	•	Traits: Shadowed eyes, necrotic touch, command lesser undead.
	•	Symbolism: Hatred carried beyond the grave.

⸻

5. Wraiths & Specters
	•	Definition: Spirits torn from their bodies.
	•	Traits: Intangible, cold touch, shrieking voices.
	•	Symbolism: The restless dead, memory refusing to fade.

⸻

6. Ghosts
	•	Definition: Souls bound by unfinished business.
	•	Traits: Transparent forms, haunting powers, sorrowful or vengeful.
	•	Symbolism: The persistence of regret and memory.

⸻

7. Liches
	•	Definition: Mortals who achieved undeath through sorcery.
	•	Traits: Skeletal mages, phylacteries, mastery of necromancy.
	•	Symbolism: Knowledge pursued at the expense of life.

⸻

8. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] Knights
	•	Definition: Fallen warriors bound to unholy vows.
	•	Traits: Blackened armor, cursed weapons, unyielding hatred.
	•	Symbolism: Chivalry corrupted; the knight eternal in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

9. Mummies
	•	Definition: Preserved corpses bound in cloth and [[Ritual]].
	•	Traits: Curses, desert origins, divine or sorcerous guardians.
	•	Symbolism: Immortality as curse; kingship beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

10. Vampires
	•	Definition: Undead predators who drink blood.
	•	Traits: Immortal beauty, fangs, shapeshifting, sunlight weakness.
	•	Symbolism: Parasitic aristocracy; immortality through predation.

⸻

11. Revenants
	•	Definition: Mortals who return from [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] to complete a singular purpose.
	•	Traits: Burning eyes, unnatural resilience, bound to vengeance.
	•	Symbolism: Justice or vengeance persisting past mortality.

⸻

12. Banshees
	•	Definition: Spirits of grief, heralds of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].
	•	Traits: Wailing cries, pale visages, omens of passing.
	•	Symbolism: The voice of sorrow; inevitability sung aloud.

⸻

13. Shadows
	•	Definition: Undead formed from living shadows.
	•	Traits: Two-dimensional, strength-draining touch.
	•	Symbolism: The loss of substance, [[Man]] consumed by his own shadow.

⸻

14. Bone/Corpse Colossi (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary undead formed from mass graves or battlefields.
	•	Traits: [[Giant]] amalgam bodies, dozens of voices, catastrophic force.
	•	Symbolism: War’s dead given singular will; the grave as an army.

⸻

15. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] Lords (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Supreme undead rulers—kings, necromancers, or gods.
	•	Traits: Command [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], bend [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] to their will, often lich-emperors.
	•	Symbolism: The triumph of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as sovereignty; entropy enthroned.
