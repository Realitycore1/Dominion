1. Why They Can Be Drunk
	•	Each potion is built from axiom-stabilized fluids that align with [[Human]] (or compatible [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]) biology.
	•	The Arcanum Omniversalis [[Domains]] bind the substance to body, soul, and pattern.
	•	When ingested:
	•	The potion dissolves into the bloodstream.
	•	The glyphs encoded within the liquid activate, resonating with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] nodes embedded in cells.
	•	The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] method ensures the potion doesn’t destabilize the host’s [[Verse]]-[[law]] pattern — it harmonizes the body to accept the energy.
	
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Why the Colors
Each potion’s color is not cosmetic, but a visualization of its axiom resonance frequency.
	•	[[Mana]] Flask (Blue-Violet)
	•	Resonates with Cosmos and Arcane [[Domains]].
	•	Blue = harmonic stability (mind, calculation).
	•	Violet = liminal energy (bridge between physical and metaphysical).
	•	Effect: Restores connection to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] channels, replenishes spellcasting capacity.
	•	Stamina Flask (Green-Gold)
	•	Resonates with Motion and Vitality [[Domains]].
	•	Green = growth and kinetic renewal.
	•	Gold = rhythmic energy of motion and pulse.
	•	Effect: Restores physical endurance, floods muscles with stabilized bio-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]].
	•	Vitality Flask (Crimson)
	•	Resonates with Vitality and Creation [[Domains]].
	•	Red = life, blood, organic resonance.
	•	Occasional cell-like patterns = glyphs aligned with biological repair.
	•	Effect: Accelerates healing, regenerates tissue, harmonizes blood and flesh with its ideal form.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Why They Work Differently
	•	The glyph encoding inside the fluid acts as a program.
	•	Drinking the potion initiates the program in the body:
	•	[[Mana]] flask = reopens metaphysical pathways.
	•	Stamina flask = amplifies metabolic resonance.
	•	Vitality flask = rewrites damaged tissues to match ideal pattern.

Each potion is literally a liquid axiomcraft circuit — the color shows what field it resonates with, and ingestion runs it through the host’s biological [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] like a power conduit.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Why Ingestion Works Better than Other Methods
	•	Injection: Too fast, bypasses [[Ritual]] glyph activation, risks burn-out.
	•	Absorption/Contact: Inefficient, glyphs dissipate on skin.
	•	Drinking: Passes through digestive tract → [[Natural]] bio-symbolic “circle” where the glyphs can activate in sequence.
	•	To the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]], the body itself is a laboratory crucible: ingestion completes the [[Ritual]].
II. Codex Standard Flasks (Battlefield / Civic Use)
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Clarity Flask

	•	Color: Silver-Azure
	•	[[Domains]]: Revelation + Self
	•	Effect: Restores focus, purges illusions, calms mental disorder.
	•	Use: Scholars, Navigators, [[Infernariate]] interrogators.

5.	Aegis Flask

	•	Color: Gold-White
	•	[[Domains]]: Protection + Binding
	•	Effect: Temporary shielding of body and soul from physical/magical harm.
	•	Use: Common in field exorcisms and high-risk expeditions.

6.	Resonance Flask

	•	Color: Prismatic (shifting rainbow hues)
	•	[[Domains]]: Cosmos + Mythopoeia
	•	Effect: Harmonizes drinker to foreign [[Verse]]-laws; prevents “goo collapse.”
	•	Use: Essential for multiversal travel outside [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Anchors.

7.	Purity Flask

	•	Color: White-Silver with faint blue sparkles
	•	[[Domains]]: Vitality + Revelation
	•	Effect: Burns out toxins, curses, and corruptive influences.
	•	Use: Emergency antidote; required when dealing with demonic blight.

8.	Sanguine Flask

	•	Color: Deep Ruby, glows like living blood
	•	[[Domains]]: Vitality + Domination
	•	Effect: Temporarily increases strength and aggression; risks frenzy.
	•	Use: Issued only to shock troops and berserker units.

III. Experimental / Forbidden Flasks
	9.	Oblivion Flask

•	Color: Black-Violet
	•	[[Domains]]: Annihilation + Channel
	•	Effect: Erases poisons, curses, or memories entirely.
	•	Use: Classified; [[Infernariate]] use only.

10.	Thanatotic Flask

	•	Color: Emerald-Black
	•	[[Domains]]: Necrosis + Vitality
	•	Effect: Heals the drinker by siphoning nearby life force.
	•	Use: Forbidden except under sanctioned battlefield protocols.

	11.	Eclipse Flask

	•	Color: Gray-Black, rimmed with gold
	•	[[Domains]]: Domination + Revelation (Inverted)
	•	Effect: Renders drinker suggestible to command.
	•	Use: Banned by Orthodoxy, but rumored in heretical sects.

12.	Void Flask

	•	Color: Transparent (appears as plain water)
	•	[[Domains]]: Null + Absorb
	•	Effect: Strips axiom resonance, leaving drinker “hollow” — resistant to possession, but diminished.
	•	Use: Only in containment operations; permanent side effects common.

⸻

IV. Rare Legendary Flasks
	13.	Chorus Flask

•	Color: Violet-Gold with harmonic rings
	•	[[Domains]]: Mythopoeia + Revelation + Cosmos
	•	Effect: Synchronizes multiple casters’ resonance, enabling joint rituals otherwise impossible.
	•	Use: Rarely deployed; drinking without proper [[Ritual]] causes resonance collapse.

14.	Eidolon Flask

	•	Color: Pale Silver-Gray, misty interior
	•	[[Domains]]: Self + Creation + Binding
	•	Effect: Temporarily stabilizes soul-pattern after near-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], preventing dissociation.
	•	Use: [[Infernariate]] exorcists and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] explorers; prevents “soul-loss.”

	15.	Empyreal Flask

	•	Color: Radiant White-Gold, impossible to look at directly
	•	[[Domains]]: Creation + Protection + Cosmos
	•	Effect: Fully restores body and soul to idealized form; rumored to cleanse heresy itself.
	•	Use: Mythic; said to exist only in [[Aravoth (Grace)]], never replicated in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] labs.
