# National Foreign Security Council

## Summary

The National Foreign Security Council coordinates Federal policy toward external states, rival civilizations, alien polities, non-Dominion human powers, trade blocs, border regions, and hostile intelligence environments.

It is a coordinating council rather than a single field agency.

## Mandate

The Council coordinates:

- foreign threat assessment;
- diplomatic security;
- sanctions planning;
- border and transit security;
- external crisis briefings;
- trade-risk review;
- foreign military liaison;
- hard-contact preparation with specialized bodies.

## Powers

It may issue interagency security priorities, request intelligence assessments, coordinate emergency briefings, and recommend Federal policy to the executive and legislature.

## Limits

It cannot declare war, run secret diplomacy beyond statutory authority, or override courts by labeling all external contact a security threat.

## Public Face

Citizens see the Council indirectly through travel advisories, foreign crisis broadcasts, sanctions notices, and hearings after policy failures.

## Story Use

Use this council to show the Dominion thinking outward. It is where fear of outsiders, trade need, military planning, and diplomatic restraint collide.
