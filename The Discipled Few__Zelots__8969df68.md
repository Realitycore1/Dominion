Designation

The Discipled Few

Avenue

Zealots

Core Identity

The Discipled Few are not pirates who found religion.
They are believers who discovered piracy was useful.

Their creed is intentionally opaque. Outsiders know only fragments:
	•	rejection of station authority
	•	hostility to bureaucratic civilization
	•	reverence for “unmediated survival”
	•	belief that the Belt must remain hostile to remain human

They do not seek territory.
They seek correction.

Operational Characteristics
	•	No ransom negotiations
	•	No trade
	•	No arbitration
	•	No consistent targeting logic

They strike:
	•	symbolic cargo
	•	infrastructure nodes
	•	governance convoys
	•	cultural assets

And then vanish.

Loss tolerance is extreme.
Survivability is secondary to meaning.

Reputation

“You can’t bargain with them.
You can’t predict them.
You can only outlast them.”

Stations don’t track Discipled Few ships.
They track patterns of absence.
