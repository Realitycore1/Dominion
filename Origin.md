## The Transformation
- **Inside the Eternum**:  
  - Physical laws bent and broke; survival required reframing reality through shared dreams.  
  - Mythic archetypes manifested as survival tools: the Hero, the Betrayer, the Martyr became literal forms to inhabit.  
  - Magenta Aether seeped into their infrastructure, reshaping biology and thought until myth and dream governed all existence.  
- **Cultural Unification**: All old religions collapsed under the weight of survival; instead, they fused into a **universal Dream Scripture** — humanity’s total mythic inheritance, refracted through the Eternum lens.  
- **Time Distortion**: What felt like millennia inside may have been only centuries outside.  

---

## The Return
- **Re-emergence**: The system eventually bled back into normal spacetime, but permanently marked — a “scar system,” riddled with warped planets and magenta auroras.  
- **Exodus**: The survivors, now the **Oneirotheon**, abandoned their ruined home to become a pilgrimage civilization, convinced their ordeal was the first step in reuniting with the **Divine Spark**.  
- **Pilgrimage Fleet**: They converted ruined colonies into shrine-worlds and built **Dream-Arks**, cathedral-ships glowing with magenta resonance.  

---

## The Mythic Narrative
- They call themselves **“the People Who Passed Through the Dream of the Gods.”**  
- Their Eternum exile is viewed as a divine trial — proof that mankind was meant to seek beyond the Dominion, beyond dogma, toward the Spark itself.  
- Dominion doctrine is seen as a **mortal misinterpretation of eternity**, while they alone lived in its truth.  

---

## Dominion Reaction
- **Initial Response**: Dominion Infernariate scholars identified their magenta resonance as *foreign*, proof of their taint.  
- **Strategic Tolerance**: They were never assimilated because their fleets were too fractured, their purpose too narrow, and their survival too bound to Eternum anomalies for conquest to be cost-effective.  
- **Ongoing Status**: Watched warily, sometimes exploited for relic-hunting, always considered a **potential threat** if their Divine Hunt ever bears fruit.  

---

## Aesthetic Markers of Origin
- **Magenta Auroras**: Every shrine-world and Dream-Ark is surrounded by luminous Aetheric auroras — remnants of Eternum bleed.  
- **Architecture**: Their ships and cities carry jagged, dreamlike geometries that appear recursive or impossible to outsiders, but perfectly navigable to Oneirotheon.  
- **People**: Their physiology shows faint **magenta Aether bloom** — glowing veins, dream-scars on skin, eyes that shimmer as if lit from within.

The Oneirotheon are the descendants of a **lost [[Human]] colony engulfed in a [[Natural]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/Eternum/Eternum|Eternum]] bleed-through**.
