Classification: Malice-Bound Predators & Vengeful Undead
Subtype: Hatred-Stabilized Spirits / Purpose-Corrupted Remnants
Status: Uncommon, Highly Lethal

⸻

Core Description

A Wraith is a disembodied undead entity formed when a ghost or dying soul is saturated with sustained malice, betrayal, or injustice—and instead of eroding into a specter, coheres around a singular grievance.

A wraith remembers why it hates.
It no longer remembers who it was.

Their forms are tall, thin, and distorted—cloaked in shadow, smoke, or cold vapor—with eyes or cores burning with focused intensity. They do not flicker like specters; they advance.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Wraiths are malice-stabilized identity cores.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Shadow Realms (Umbrae)
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice (structured, not chaotic)
	•	Realm Interface: Material ↔ Shadow predation zones
	•	Null Interaction: Actively resisted (they care too much to erode)

They are not accidents.
They are what resentment becomes when it refuses to fade.

⸻

Formation Pathways

Wraiths form when:
	•	a ghost fixates on injustice without resolution,
	•	death occurs under betrayal or violation,
	•	malice is reinforced by external forces,
	•	a revenant’s purpose collapses but hatred remains,
	•	necromantic corruption “sharpens” a spirit.

Ghost → Wraith is corruption with focus.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Wraiths possess moderate to high intelligence.

They:
	•	recognize targets,
	•	plan ambushes,
	•	adapt tactics,
	•	avoid stronger threats.

They do not:
	•	empathize,
	•	forgive,
	•	relinquish grudges.

Some wraiths can speak—but only of what wronged them.

⸻

Predatory Behavior

Wraiths hunt:
	•	specific bloodlines,
	•	professions,
	•	ideological groups,
	•	locations tied to their grievance.

They feed on:
	•	fear,
	•	suffering,
	•	spiritual degradation,
	•	life-force stripped through terror.

A wraith does not kill randomly.
It kills symbolically.

⸻

Abilities
	•	Intangibility with controlled materialization
	•	Life-drain through contact
	•	Terror induction and paralysis
	•	Cold manifestation
	•	Resistance to mundane weapons

Some wraiths develop:
	•	spectral weapons,
	•	shadow armor,
	•	limited possession ability.

⸻

Weaknesses & Termination
	•	Grace-aligned rites (especially forgiveness-based)
	•	Resolution of grievance (rare)
	•	Phoenix-grade cleansing
	•	Reaper intervention
	•	Identity dissolution rituals

Destroying a wraith violently often strengthens it unless properly bound.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Specters: Wraiths dominate or consume them
	•	Ghosts: Raw material
	•	Revenants: Rivals or precursors
	•	Liches: Harvest wraiths as batteries
	•	Demons: Sponsor or weaponize wraiths
	•	Reapers: Actively hunt wraiths

Wraiths are tolerated by nothing except Hell—and even Hell prefers them controlled.

⸻

Variants
	•	Avenger Wraiths: Focused on retribution
	•	War Wraiths: Battlefield-born, aggressive
	•	Bloodline Wraiths: Hereditary hatred
	•	Ideological Wraiths: Belief-driven
	•	Bound Wraiths: Used as weapons or guards

Bloodline Wraiths can persist for centuries.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Specters: Wraiths have focus
	•	vs Revenants: Wraiths lack closure
	•	vs Wights: Wraiths are mobile
	•	vs Ghosts: Wraiths have intent
	•	vs Demons: Wraiths are former mortals

This keeps escalation clean and readable.
