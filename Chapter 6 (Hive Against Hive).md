# Chapter 6 - Hive Against Hive

## Timeline Function

War Years 2-3. Harmina's industrial societies mobilize as total systems rather than individual armies.

## Event Role

- Factories, clone labor pools, civil-defense hives, school networks, and archive systems become military assets.
- Propaganda reframes entire societies as hostile survival machines.
- Autonomous production accelerates faster than legal oversight can follow.
- The Body Question becomes militarized through enhancement, pain reduction, muscle retention, and engineered soldiers.
- Gene therapy, cybernetic recovery, AI combat, and proto-Panoply programs reveal requirements designed for threats no Harminan rival possesses.
- Anti-biological and anti-synthetic doctrines predate the attacks now used to justify them.

## Consequence

The war becomes a contest between organized ways of life. Civilians remain civilians, but every civilian system becomes strategically relevant and every survival technology acquires a possible military owner.
