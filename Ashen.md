Core Bias:

Storm after destruction

This subtype is not just active violence, but the continuing turbulence of aftermath.

Traits:
	•	particulate saturation
	•	obscured visibility
	•	unstable residue fields
	•	post-destruction turbulence
	•	long-lived environmental violence

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	destruction never fully ends
	•	the air itself remembers violence
	•	even after the blast, the storm continues

Common Expressions:
	•	nuclear or volcanic post-war basins
	•	fallout weather realities
	•	soot-clogged civilizations
	•	ash-choked travel systems

Failure Mode:

Permanent aftermath
The basin remains trapped in its own residue.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	post-war megaverses
	•	collapsed industrial civilizations
	•	dark-age atmospheres
	•	long-term scar zones
