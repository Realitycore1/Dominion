# Dominion Arts Council

## Summary

The Dominion Arts Council funds public art, cultural preservation, memorial work, theatre, music, local festivals, civic architecture programs, and cross-provincial cultural exchange.

It is a Federal agency because culture is part of public life, not only court ritual.

## Mandate

The Council supports:

- public murals;
- memorial commissions;
- local arts grants;
- archive-backed cultural restoration;
- youth arts programs;
- compact-region cultural festivals;
- public performance spaces;
- translation and preservation of endangered traditions.

## Powers

It may issue grants, certify cultural-preservation projects, fund artists, and preserve at-risk cultural records.

## Limits

It cannot require praise of the Throne, deny funding solely for lawful dissent, or turn cultural integration into aesthetic uniformity.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Council through festivals, school performances, station murals, memorial gardens, preservation grants, and bitter arguments over what counts as Dominion art.

## Story Use

Use this agency to humanize the state. The Dominion should not only build weapons and cities. It should sponsor songs, bad plays, beautiful monuments, and public fights over taste.
