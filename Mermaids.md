1. Common Mermaids
	•	Definition: The baseline—[[Humans]] with the lower half of a fish.
	•	Traits: Alluring beauty, aquatic adaptation, strong swimmers, siren songs (sometimes).
	•	Symbolism: The sea’s allure—beauty masking danger.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Sirens
	•	Definition: Mermaids who lure sailors with irresistible song.
	•	Traits: Enchanting voices, illusions of paradise, sudden predation.
	•	Symbolism: Temptation leading to ruin; the sea as seductress.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Deep-Sea Mermaids
	•	Definition: Mermaids adapted to abyssal [[Depths]].
	•	Traits: Bioluminescence, pale or monstrous forms, eerie calls.
	•	Symbolism: The unknown terrors of the deep ocean.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. River Mermaids (Naiads)
	•	Definition: Freshwater mermaids tied to rivers and lakes.
	•	Traits: Amphibious, gentle appearances, tied to fertility and water cycles.
	•	Symbolism: Life-giving waters, spirits of bounty and danger.

⸻

5. Coral Mermaids
	•	Definition: Mermaids tied to reefs and coastal shallows.
	•	Traits: Bright scales, symbiosis with reef life, guardians of coastal [[Regions]].
	•	Symbolism: The beauty and fragility of the ocean.

⸻

6. Storm Mermaids
	•	Definition: Mermaids born from tempests and shipwrecks.
	•	Traits: Wild hair like seafoam, command over storms, destructive songs.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos of the waves, wrath of the ocean.

⸻

7. Abyssal/Shadow Mermaids
	•	Definition: Corrupted or cursed mermaids tied to necrotic seas.
	•	Traits: Dark scales, skeletal features, chilling touch.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] beneath the waves; drowned souls.

⸻

8. Noble Mermaids
	•	Definition: Royal or aristocratic merfolk with structured kingdoms.
	•	Traits: Crowns of coral, courts beneath the waves, wielders of tridents or magic.
	•	Symbolism: Civilization beneath the sea; reflection of surface kingdoms.

⸻

9. Elemental Mermaids
	•	Definition: Mermaids infused with elemental forces.
	•	Variants: Flame-finned (volcanic seas), Frostmaidens (ice floes), Stormcallers (tempests).
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s primal forces given graceful form.

⸻

10. Leviathan Brides (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary mermaids said to consort with or embody sea gods and [[Leviathans]].
	•	Traits: Towering forms, aura of tides, voices that command the oceans.
	•	Symbolism: The sea as divinity; creation and destruction in equal measure.
