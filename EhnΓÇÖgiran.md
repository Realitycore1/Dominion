Classification: Oneiric Native Species
Origin: Dreamlands / Oneiric Layer
Status: Extradimensional Sapient
Threat Index: Existential (Indirect)

⸻

Overview
The Ehn’giran are dream-born intelligences whose biology, cognition, and culture evolved entirely within the Oneiric Layer. They do not originate from material reality and cannot be accurately classified as extradimensional invaders. Instead, they are convergence organisms—entities whose core function is to reconcile symbolic reality with physical causality.

To waking civilizations, they appear monstrous. To themselves, they are corrective.

⸻

Ontological Nature
Ehn’giran are not organisms that dream; they are dreams that stabilized into self-consistent entities. Their bodies are semi-coherent translations of meaning into matter, resulting in forms that resist fixed anatomy and fluctuate based on observer proximity, emotional density, and narrative saturation.

They experience linear time imperfectly and perceive causality as negotiable rather than absolute.

⸻

Physiology
Common observable traits include:
	•	Radial or near-radial symmetry, reflecting non-directional causality.
	•	Cephalopodial limb structures capable of manipulating matter, memory, and symbolic constructs simultaneously.
	•	Membranous wings that do not facilitate flight but instead displace probability fields and narrative weight.
	•	Cranial crowns or floral structures functioning as oneiric sensory arrays, detecting unresolved intent, trauma, and archetypal resonance.
	•	Vestigial ocular organs; vision is secondary to interpretive perception.

Internal anatomy cannot be reliably mapped. Dissection attempts result in symbolic dissolution or observer-side cognitive contamination.

⸻

Psychology
Ehn’giran cognition is collective but not hive-based. Individual entities possess autonomy while sharing a unified philosophical framework.

Their central axiom:

A reality that cannot dream while awake is incomplete.

They do not conceptualize morality as mortals do. Harm is irrelevant; coherence is paramount. Resistance to convergence is interpreted as a temporary dysfunction rather than opposition.

⸻

Cultural Objective
The primary Ehn’giran objective is the merging of the Overworld and Dreamworld into a permanent liminal state.

This convergence would result in:
	•	Symbolic gravity influencing geography and architecture.
	•	Time becoming elastic rather than linear.
	•	Archetypes manifesting physically.
	•	Death losing finality but retaining consequence.
	•	History shifting from fixed record to recursive narrative.

Civilizations would persist—but without the ability to forget.

⸻

Incursion Methodology
Ehn’giran cannot force entry through material means. Manifestation requires conceptual permeability, achieved via:
	•	Recurrent shared dreams.
	•	Myth repetition and prophecy loops.
	•	Mass trauma or cultural collapse.
	•	Dream cults, seers, and oneiric scholars.

By the time physical manifestations occur, ontological compromise is already advanced.

⸻

Threat Assessment
Ehn’giran do not destroy worlds. They overwrite interpretation.

Cities become metaphors.
History begins to rhyme.
Thought acquires consequence.

Military action is largely ineffective. Scientific models destabilize. Religious frameworks fracture under paradox. The true danger lies not in extinction, but in irreversible redefinition.
