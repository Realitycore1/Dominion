- **Integration with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]:** Provide perspectives alien to other client [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]—biological swarm-logistics, immune-modular troops, hive-logic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] designs.
