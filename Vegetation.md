1. Primary Canopy Producers (massive energy-harvesters)

These are the equivalents of [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s forests and jungles.
	•	Fur-Trees – colossal black-red trees with fur-like bark and purple-black photosynthetic tissue.
	•	Absorb [[Wide]]-spectrum light and even storm lightning.
	•	Provide fruits (bitter, alkaloid-rich) eaten by beasts and processed by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] into medicines.
	•	Crystalroot Towers – fungal-crystal hybrids that glow from radioactive decay.
	•	Their spongy interiors store sugars and exotic isotopes.
	•	Grazed upon by storm behemoths and harvested by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] for energy reagents.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Secondary Growth / Undercanopy (feeds herbivores, omnivores)
	•	Aurora Ferns – phosphorescent fronds that drink in storm-electricity.
	•	Used by radiant moths, glowfang wolves, and even [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] as energy snacks (fermented into aurora-brews).
	•	Glassleaf Vines – sharp-leaved vines, deadly if eaten raw.
	•	Some [[Fauna]] evolved silica-resistant guts to digest them (shardshell beetles, ashfang crawlers).
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] harvests them to create bio-glass fibers.
	•	Bloodfruit Shrubs – thorny bushes producing thick, iron-rich berries.
	•	Staple food for many mid-tier [[Fauna]].
	•	Processed into a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] nutrient paste (high in hemoglobin, sustaining in radiation).

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Ground and Wetland Plants (swamp, marsh, rift growth)
	•	Spore-Fangs – carnivorous fungal plants that trap smaller [[Fauna]], releasing nutrient-rich remains into the soil.
	•	Indirectly feed scavenger creatures and fungi-eaters.
	•	Blackreed Stalks – marsh plants that thrive in aurora marshes.
	•	High in starches and edible after [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] detoxification.
	•	Rad-lichen – moss-lichen hybrids living on radioactive stones.
	•	Small [[Fauna]] (insects, amphibians) feed on them directly.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] cultivates it in vaults as emergency food (nutritious, though carcinogenic without filtering).

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Specialized Extremophiles (lava, ice, storm zones)
	•	Ember Moss – grows on volcanic glass, glows red and stores thermal energy.
	•	Consumed by hollow spitters and used as fuel clumps by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] for survival rations.
	•	Cryoshards – crystalline shrubs in tundras, grow using geothermal cracks.
	•	Stores antifreeze sap used by both [[Fauna]] and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] as medicine.
	•	Stormblooms – small, flower-like plants that only open during lightning storms.
	•	Attract insectoid swarms which in turn feed predators.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] regards them as “omens” and collects the petals for [[Ritual]] use.
