These [[Stars]] are often found in the center of planetary [[Systems]], where they provide energy and heat to the planets that orbit around them. The term "Forge Star" is derived from the similarities between these [[Stars]] and a blacksmith's forge, which generates intense heat and light in order to shape and transform materials. In the same way, Forge [[Stars]] are believed to be able to heat and shape the planets in their [[Systems]], creating conditions that are conducive to the development of life and complex ecosystems. Forge [[Stars]] are typically bright and luminous, with a range of colors from white to yellow to blue depending on their temperature and composition. They can also emit powerful flares and bursts of energy, which can have significant impacts on nearby planets or spacecraft. The study of Forge [[Stars]] is an active area of research in astrophysics, as scientists continue to explore the relationship between these [[Stars]] and the development of life in the universe.
