# Dominion AI Accountability Council

## Summary

The Dominion AI Accountability Council regulates artificial intelligence systems used in public administration, infrastructure, courts support, health care, transit, defense-adjacent logistics, and private industry.

Its central principle is simple: no citizen should be ruled by a machine decision that cannot be explained, appealed, or traced to accountable human authority.

## Mandate

The Council oversees:

- AI licensing;
- public algorithm audits;
- automated decision appeals;
- model provenance;
- sentience and personhood review where applicable;
- infrastructure automation safety;
- military-adjacent AI transfer limits;
- anti-bias and anti-caste testing.

## Powers

It may suspend AI systems, require explainability reports, order retraining or rollback, and refer unlawful automated governance to courts.

## Limits

It cannot outlaw all useful automation, classify every model detail as secret, or treat emergent intelligence as property without review.

## Public Face

Citizens encounter the Council when a benefit denial is appealed, a school placement algorithm is challenged, a transit AI fails, or an automated security system harms someone.

## Story Use

Use this agency to ask who is responsible when a civilization lets machines make ordinary life faster than human oversight can follow.
