# National Spacecraft Security

## Summary

National Spacecraft Security regulates the civilian and commercial spacecraft security environment: registry, inspection, anti-hijacking standards, cargo screening, passenger safety, and orbital-port coordination.

It is closer to a transport-security agency than a navy.

## Mandate

The agency oversees:

- spacecraft registry;
- passenger screening;
- cargo inspection;
- anti-hijacking measures;
- port-security standards;
- hazardous cargo procedures;
- civilian vessel emergency beacons;
- coordination with military and port authorities.

## Powers

It may ground unsafe vessels, seize illegal cargo, issue security directives, require vessel transponder compliance, and coordinate emergency response.

## Limits

It cannot convert routine travel into indefinite detention, seize vessels without review, or treat all frontier crews as smugglers by default.

## Public Face

Citizens know it through boarding queues, cargo delays, safety briefings, port inspections, and the irritation of security theater when no one believes the threat is real until it is.

## Story Use

Use this agency to ground space travel in procedure. Ships should feel like workplaces and public vehicles, not only heroic vessels.
