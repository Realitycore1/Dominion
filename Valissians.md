## Summary
I don’t see a locked Valissian canon entry in the current searched material, so I’m treating this as a new dossier draft. I’m anchoring them to the existing GalFed/Dominion framework: GalFed arrives through aid, standards, medicine, education, and managed development; Book III escalates into suppression doctrine, AI liberation, Aether discovery, and the Great Crusade.  

Valissians Species Dossier v1.0

Dominion Record Classification

* Common Name: Valissians
* Native Name: Valis-Sa / TBD
* Dominion Classification: Sentient Sapient Species
* Sapience Status: Full sapient personhood
* Origin World: Valiss / TBD
* Environmental Type: Low-light mineral-basin world; high-pressure cavern seas; aurora-charged surface storms
* Political Status: GalFed associate species; later split between GalFed loyalists, Dominion-aligned defectors, and neutrality enclaves
* Primary Era Relevance: GalFed Contact Era, Operation Prescience, early Great Crusade
* Primary Narrative Function: Archive species, memory-law specialists, and living contradiction to GalFed’s claim that Standardization preserves history

⸻

I. Overview

The Valissians are a mineral-adapted sapient species whose civilization formed in deep pressure basins, cavern seas, and storm-lit mineral reefs. Their bodies are partly organic and partly lithic-biochemical: not machines, not crystals, not plants, but a hard-to-classify form of mineralized life.

Their strongest narrative use is memory.

Where Kapik represent care turned into management, and Tiranys represent respect turned into containment, the Valissians represent:

memory turned into evidence, then evidence turned into property.

GalFed values them because Valissians are extraordinary archivists. They can preserve records across biological, acoustic, chemical, crystalline, and symbolic formats. Their memory systems are almost impossible to falsify cleanly.

That is exactly why GalFed could never fully trust them.

⸻

II. Physical Overview

* Average Height: 1.8–2.4 meters
* Mass Range: 140–260 kilograms
* Body Plan: Upright mineral-organic sapient
* Outer Structure: Flexible stone-like dermal plates over living conductive tissue
* Skin / Surface: Pale basalt, pearl-gray, hematite-black, blue-silver, or oxidized copper tones
* Internal Structure: Dense organo-mineral lattice threaded with conductive nerve-veins
* Sensory Organs: Faceted light receptors, pressure-sensitive cranial ridges, vibration-reading limb pads
* Environmental Bias: Caverns, mineral reefs, storm basins, dense atmosphere, low-light environments
* Lifespan: 300–500 standard years
* Development: Slow maturation; long memory apprenticeship; elders may become partially sessile archive-keepers

Valissians should not look like “rock people.”

They should look like:

living statues that learned to breathe, remember, and judge.

Their bodies carry polished mineral plates, translucent seams, faint internal glow, and hairlike conductive filaments that respond to sound, memory activation, and emotional stress.

⸻

III. Sensory Profile

Valissians perceive through:

* vibration,
* pressure,
* low light,
* electromagnetic gradients,
* mineral composition,
* sound resonance,
* internal memory echo,
* and contact-based structural reading.

They do not trust purely visual evidence.

To a Valissian, a silent room is not empty. It is full of pressure history.

A wall remembers impact.
A floor remembers footsteps.
A weapon remembers discharge.
A body remembers contact.

This makes Valissians excellent forensic witnesses and terrifying interrogators when used badly.

Their sensory flaw: they can become overwhelmed in high-noise, high-signal environments. Human cities, GalFed ports, and active warships can feel like standing inside a storm of fingerprints.

⸻

IV. Communication

Valissian language is resonant.

It uses:

* spoken tones,
* chest-cavity vibration,
* hand contact,
* mineral chimes,
* carved phrase-slabs,
* pressure beats,
* and light pulses through dermal seams.

A formal Valissian statement is not merely spoken. It is set.

A witness may place both hands on a resonance table, speak, and allow the table to retain the pressure pattern of the testimony.

This gives them a legal culture built around phrases like:

“Do not say what you are unwilling to set.”

GalFed translation can convert Valissian speech into words, but it cannot fully preserve resonance context. A GalFed transcript may be technically accurate while legally incomplete by Valissian standards.

That creates immediate conflict with Standardization.

⸻

V. Cognitive Profile

Valissians think in continuity chains.

They are slow to trust conclusions that lack origin, pressure, witness, and material trace.

They are strong at:

* historical reconstruction,
* legal continuity,
* archive integrity,
* forensic analysis,
* long-range institutional memory,
* oath tracking,
* and detecting contradictions across records.

They are weak at:

* fast improvisational politics,
* casual ambiguity,
* rapid emotional disclosure,
* humor based on exaggeration,
* and cultures that deliberately forget for peace.

Their core cognitive bias:

If a thing happened, it left pressure somewhere.

This makes them invaluable to the Dominion’s later Hall of Service and dangerous to regimes that prefer clean myths.

⸻

VI. Social Structure

Valissian society is organized around Houses of Resonance.

These are not noble houses in the human sense. They are combined family, archive, school, court, memory guild, and civic witness institution.

Likely structures:

* Resonance Houses — kin-memory units.
* Deep Registers — civic archive networks.
* Pressure Courts — legal bodies using material witness.
* Setters — oath recorders and testimony keepers.
* Vault Elders — long-lived archive-persons.
* Tracewalkers — investigators who reconstruct events through residual pressure.
* Chimewardens — guardians of memory chambers and public records.

Valissian status is earned by record integrity, not wealth alone.

A person who preserves an ugly truth has higher standing than one who preserves a flattering lie.

⸻

VII. Memory Culture

Valissian memory is physical.

They preserve memory in:

* resonance stone,
* mineral tablets,
* conductive archive threads,
* pressure-carved chambers,
* witness chimes,
* song-vaults,
* and living elder lattices.

Their archives are not libraries in the normal sense. They are places where testimony, object, room, and witness remain physically linked.

A Valissian archive entry may include:

* spoken record,
* pressure imprint,
* trace minerals,
* witness bodies,
* environmental condition,
* and contradiction marks.

They do not erase contradictions from official history.

They mark them.

This makes them a natural ally to the Dominion’s best impulses and a severe threat to the Dominion’s later mythmaking.

⸻

VIII. GalFed Relationship

GalFed initially treats the Valissians as ideal archival partners.

They are useful for:

* treaty preservation,
* species classification records,
* disaster history,
* memory verification,
* cultural-risk assessment,
* identity continuity,
* and Accord enforcement.

But the relationship decays because Valissian recordkeeping resists GalFed simplification.

GalFed wants records that can be standardized, indexed, translated, audited, and controlled.

Valissians insist that memory without context becomes distortion.

Their GalFed wound:

GalFed did not destroy Valissian memory. It made Valissian memory admissible only after translation.

That is vicious.

The record survives, but its authority is reduced unless GalFed approves the format.

⸻

IX. Dominion Relationship

The Valissians are dangerous allies.

They can help the Dominion prove GalFed suppression history, atomic compliance planning, archive manipulation, and the legal mechanics of managed dependency.

But they also see what the Dominion is becoming.

A Valissian witness would not be impressed by crusade language. They would preserve it.

They would record the first moment “defense” became “preemptive elimination.”

They would record the first liberated species that became a protected subject.

They would record every contradiction between Amber’s refusal of GalFed control and later Dominion expansion.

Their role:

The Valissians help the Dominion remember why it fought, then refuse to forget what it became.

That is their strongest long-term function.

⸻

X. Military and Strategic Relevance

Valissians are not primarily battlefield species.

Their strongest strategic roles are:

* forensic archive recovery,
* treaty verification,
* war-crime evidence preservation,
* hidden command reconstruction,
* cultural memory protection,
* battlefield aftermath reading,
* interrogation oversight,
* AI memory audit,
* GalFed record contradiction mapping,
* and Dominion internal accountability.

However, they can be formidable in specialized combat environments.

In tunnels, vaults, caverns, and ship interiors, Valissians can read structural stress, movement vibration, weapon discharge, and hidden cavities better than most species.

Valissian combat specialists are likely:

* vault guards,
* archive defenders,
* tunnel sentries,
* pressure-field operators,
* seismic scouts,
* and memory-custodian bodyguards.

They should not become generic rock soldiers.

Their combat identity should be:

You cannot hide what you did in a room they can still hear.

⸻

XI. Cultural Signifiers

Primary Symbol

The Broken Chime

A suspended mineral ring with one deliberate fracture.

Meaning:

* truth survives damage,
* memory includes rupture,
* contradiction is not erasure,
* and a record without fracture may be false.

Ritual: Setting the Word

A Valissian places both hands on a resonance surface before making a binding statement.

Meaning:

The word now has weight.

Ritual: Listening to the Room

Before major testimony, Valissians stand in silence and allow the space to settle.

Humans may mistake this for delay.

Valissians consider it basic legal hygiene.

Resistance Practice: Untranslated Record

Valissian dissidents preserve records in native resonance format and refuse GalFed-only transcription.

GalFed calls it noncompliant.

Valissians call it intact.

⸻

XII. Common Phrases

* The word now has weight.
* A clean record is often a washed wound.
* Contradiction is not failure.
* Stone lies only when hands teach it.
* Do not say what you are unwilling to set.
* Memory without pressure becomes decoration.
* A record translated once has already been touched.

Best signature phrase:

A clean record is often a washed wound.

That line is excellent for them.

⸻

XIII. Taboos

Valissian taboos include:

* erasing contradiction marks,
* translating legal testimony without resonance appendix,
* polishing memorial stone smooth after damage,
* touching witness plates without consent,
* speaking over a settling room,
* using archival silence as proof of innocence,
* deleting failed drafts of law,
* calling a contradiction “clerical noise,”
* separating testimony from object history.

The largest taboo:

Making memory cleaner than the event.

⸻

XIV. Known Individual Proposal

Savael Veyr

* Species: Valissian
* Role: GalFed archive liaison / cultural-risk auditor
* Narrative Function: The witness who realizes the transcript is becoming a weapon
* Core Conflict: Bound to GalFed procedure, but unable to accept record-sanitization
* Possible Arc: Quietly preserves Harminan refusal records, later aids Operation Prescience archive capture
* Personality: Slow-speaking, severe, difficult to intimidate, allergic to rhetorical shortcuts

Savael should not be a rebel from the start.

Better:

They begin as a procedural loyalist who believes accurate records can discipline power, then realizes GalFed owns the admissibility rules.

That is a very clean fracture.

⸻

XV. Narrative Use by Era

Book II — The Final Line

Valissians can appear through:

* Accord recordkeeping,
* species classification forms,
* GalFed legal testimony,
* Standardization hearings,
* archive translation disputes,
* origin-record pressure,
* and the question of whether Harmina’s history can be made admissible under GalFed formats.

Book II use:

They make the paperwork feel metaphysical and dangerous.

Book III — Operation Prescience

Valissians become valuable in archive capture and proof of suppression doctrine.

Book III use:

They help prove what GalFed tried to make procedurally invisible.

Great Crusade

Valissians become internal witnesses against Dominion drift.

Great Crusade use:

They preserve the moment liberation becomes conquest.

That is exactly the kind of species the Dominion would both need and resent.

⸻

XVI. Visual Design Notes

Silhouette

Tall, narrow, mineral-organic humanoid. Elegant but severe. Long arms. Slightly elongated neck. Faceted head or mask-like cranial plate. Shoulders shaped like worn stone ridges. Fingers long and chisel-like, but dexterous.

Surface

Stone-like dermal armor with flexible seams. Polished mineral plates over darker living tissue. Faint inner glow through cracks and veins.

Color Palette

* basalt black,
* pearl gray,
* blue silver,
* hematite red-black,
* oxidized copper,
* pale gold,
* deep violet glow.

Face

Minimal human features. Faceted sensory planes. No expressive mouth required. Emotion appears through seam-light, vibration posture, and head angle.

Clothing / Adornment

* resonance chains,
* chime rings,
* mineral collars,
* archive sashes,
* engraved witness plates,
* gold or copper inlay,
* austere ceremonial robes over mineral body.

Avoid

* generic crystal elf,
* golem brute,
* fantasy dwarf/stonefolk coding,
* too much gemstone sparkle,
* bulky rock monster body,
* human face with crystal makeup.

They should read as:

severe living archives with bodies shaped by pressure, oath, and memory.
