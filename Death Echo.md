Classification

Post-Mortem Phenomena / Causal Afterimages / Non-Sentient–to–Sentient Haunts

⸻

CORE IDEA

A Death Echo is a death that didn’t finish happening.

It is not a ghost in the traditional sense. There is no soul seeking justice, no will demanding revenge. A Death Echo is the residual imprint of a violent or meaningful death, replaying because reality failed to close the loop.

It doesn’t remember being alive.
It remembers dying.

⸻

ORIGIN

Death Echoes form when death intersects with instability:
	•	sudden, traumatic deaths
	•	mass casualty events (battlefields, collapses, plagues)
	•	malice- or grace-saturated environments
	•	temporal or spatial distortion zones
	•	ritual interruption at the moment of death

Where ghosts are souls, Death Echoes are records.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Death Echoes are semi-visible, unstable projections.

Common appearances:
	•	Flickering humanoid silhouettes
	•	Afterimages that lag behind movement
	•	Repeating gestures (falling, grasping, shielding, screaming)
	•	Bodies that phase between intact and mortally wounded
	•	No consistent facial features—faces blur or fracture

They often appear out of sync with the environment by a fraction of a second.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Death Echoes are non-hostile by intent, lethal by proximity.
	•	Repeat the same action loops endlessly
	•	React when their “death moment” is approached or disturbed
	•	Do not pursue, but may intersect with the living
	•	Escalate in intensity when observed or acknowledged

They do not choose targets.
They replay coordinates.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Ranges from none to emergent.
	•	Most are purely reactive
	•	Some develop limited pattern recognition
	•	Rare Echoes become self-aware after prolonged exposure
	•	Intelligence correlates with emotional intensity of death

A self-aware Death Echo is often unstable and catastrophic.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Causal Looping: existence tied to a specific event-space
	•	Grace-Resistant: divine light does not absolve unfinished causality
	•	Malice-Amplified: corruption increases loop radius and lethality
	•	Perceptual Harm: witnessing one causes disorientation, dread, or time-slip
	•	Phase Damage: contact causes necrotic or temporal injury

Destroying an Echo physically does nothing.
You must resolve the loop.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Cluster in ruins, battlefields, hospitals, collapse sites
	•	Multiply when deaths overlap
	•	Can “stack,” forming Echo Storms
	•	Fade naturally only if the site stabilizes

Places with many Death Echoes feel crowded even when empty.

⸻

ALPHA DEATH ECHO

An Alpha is called a Resonant Event.

Traits:
	•	Multiple deaths merged into a single phenomenon
	•	Can replay different moments simultaneously
	•	Distorts time, sound, and perception in wide radius
	•	May manifest partial environments (trenches, rooms, fires)

Resonant Events are often mistaken for hauntings or curses.
They are actually historical failures.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Moderate (isolated) / Severe–Existential (cluster or Alpha)

You don’t fight a Death Echo.
You understand what reality is failing to say.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Death Echoes represent:
	•	trauma that never resolved
	•	history refusing to stay past
	•	death as data corruption
	•	the cost of unfinished endings

Where Barrow-Wights enforce burial,
Death Echoes expose where burial never truly occurred.
