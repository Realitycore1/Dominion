# (3007) Gilgamesh Federal Station opens

Gilgamesh opens as the orbital home of the Terran Federation before the first permanent Lunar colony.

It contains:

- chambers for the People's Assembly and Council of Representatives;
- federal courts;
- station and colonial ministries;
- Space Force civil command;
- public archives;
- Quiet Council hearing rooms;
- diplomatic facilities;
- ordinary civil-worker housing.

The move prevents one terrestrial Great City from controlling off-world administration. It also creates an orbital political center increasingly distant from ordinary life on Earth.

The 3080 Gilgamesh marker records the station's mature expansion rather than its founding.
