The Retrieval Crusades evolve from military expeditions into mythic continuity pilgrimages.

Entire generations are born aboard:

* anchor-carriers,
* bunker fleets,
* and crusade arks.

The Secundus identity becomes almost religious:

* not faith in gods,
* but faith in continuity restoration.
