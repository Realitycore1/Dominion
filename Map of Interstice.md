	•	Known only as the Cartomancer, a godlike being of order and madness, born from a dead [[Verse]] whose axiom no longer exists.
	•	Neither living nor dead, neither present nor absent. It cannot be interacted with, invoked, or altered — only its artifact remains.
	•	Scholars debate whether the Cartomancer still exists somewhere, endlessly drawing, or whether the map itself is the being.

⸻

Nature of the Map
	•	Medium: A techno-alchemical construct — not parchment, but a layered fusion of shifting crystal plates, metallic lattices, and organic filigree that pulses faintly with non-[[Time]].
	•	Appearance: To the untrained eye, it looks like an indecipherable mandala of shifting lines, colors, and glyphs. To one attuned, it “unfolds” into the current City’s layout.
	•	Behavior: Updates itself in real [[Time]], but not universally. Each viewing is relative to the local timestream of the reader.
	•	Singularity: There is only one Map. Copies, illusions, or attempts at replication dissolve into nonsense. The Map is unique and indivisible.

⸻

Use & Danger
	•	Navigation: The only true way to traverse deeper [[Layers]] (Labyrinths, Paradox Districts, [[The Fringe]]). Without it, you risk permanent dissolution or looping forever.
	•	Temporal Drift: Two travelers consulting the Map at different “moments” may find completely different layouts — and both will be correct.
	•	Toll: The Map demands a price. Reading it too long imprints fragments of the Cartomancer’s mind, causing visions, babbling prophecy, or episodes of deranged lucidity.
	•	Rumor: Some say the [[Omega Key]] and the Map are siblings — one opens, the other guides.
