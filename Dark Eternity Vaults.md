Dark Eternity Vaults are entropic sanctuaries built within Dark Eternity zones, where [[Time]] itself is slowed, halted, or inverted. They are designed to preserve artifacts, knowledge, and even entire populations outside the normal flow of causality.
To outsiders, they are tombs; to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], they are timeless fortresses of [[Continuity]].
Temporal Isolation

Inside, centuries outside pass as seconds within.

Anything stored remains unaged, uncorrupted, untouched by entropy.

Structural Form

Encased in [[Atlas Pillars]] configured as temporal buttresses, stabilizing the anomaly.

Interiors often paradoxically small — or infinitely recursive, depending on perspective.

Access requires Horizon Arc Gates tuned to specific Concordia harmonics.

Defense

Any approach destabilizes local [[Time]] — invaders risk aging millennia or rewinding into nothingness.

Guardian ecologies patrol entrances, drones and constructs unaffected by temporal dilation.

Myth holds that the Empress herself can “wake” sleepers selectively, plucking them from eternity at will.

Punishment: Enemies cast into vaults suffer eternal imprisonment, never aging, never released.

Religion: Regarded as “the still heart of the Empress” — [[Temples]] of eternal [[Silence]] where her will pauses the universe itself.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Interior vault chambers shaped like recursive spirals, endless mirrored geometries collapsing inward.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Inscribed with glyphs that glow only when observed, paradoxically unreadable outside the vault.
