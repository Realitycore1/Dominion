Classification: Deathbound Avatars & Vengeful Returns
Subtype: Purpose-Bound Undead / Will-Reanimated
Status: Rare, Temporary, Extremely Dangerous

⸻

Core Description

A Revenant is a dead being forcibly returned to physical existence by singular purpose—most often vengeance, justice, oath, or unresolved mandate. Unlike ghosts, revenants possess a body. Unlike liches, they possess no long-term plan.

A revenant does not want eternity.
It wants one thing finished.

Its body is often damaged, scarred, or partially decayed, yet animated with unnatural strength and endurance driven by will alone.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Revenants are purpose-stabilized undead.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Death (direct enforcement)
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice or Justice (contextual)
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms ↔ Material Continuum
	•	Null Interaction: Actively resisted by will

They exist because the system allowed an exception—or was forced into one.

⸻

Creation Conditions

A revenant forms when all of the following align:
	•	death was unjust, violent, or interrupted,
	•	purpose was clear and singular,
	•	identity was strong and focused,
	•	transition was denied or rejected.

Revenants are not created intentionally under normal circumstances.
Attempts to manufacture them usually fail catastrophically.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Revenants retain:
	•	memory,
	•	personality,
	•	tactical reasoning.

They lose:
	•	long-term planning,
	•	fear of death,
	•	concern for collateral damage.

A revenant’s cognition is tunnel-focused. Everything that does not serve its purpose is irrelevant.

⸻

Behavior & Methods

Revenants:
	•	pursue specific targets relentlessly,
	•	ignore pain, injury, and exhaustion,
	•	regenerate slowly through will,
	•	adapt tactics over time.

They do not:
	•	build armies,
	•	rule territories,
	•	bargain meaningfully.

A revenant walks straight through hell if that’s the shortest path.

⸻

Physical Capabilities
	•	Strength exceeds mortal limits
	•	Damage resistance scales with purpose
	•	Wounds close unnaturally
	•	Cannot be dissuaded psychologically

Destroying the body without resolving the purpose results in reconstitution.

⸻

Weaknesses & Termination

A revenant ends only when:
	•	its purpose is fulfilled,
	•	its target is destroyed or absolved,
	•	it is convinced its task is complete,
	•	its will is shattered by revelation or Grace.

Killing a revenant without closure only delays it.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Ghosts: Revenants consume or stabilize ghosts unintentionally
	•	Wraiths: Revenants dominate or destroy them
	•	Liches: Mutual hostility; philosophies incompatible
	•	Reapers: Tolerate revenants reluctantly
	•	Demons: Avoid unless weaponizing

Revenants are tolerated because they do not linger.

⸻

Variants
	•	Avenger Revenant: Pure vengeance
	•	Oathbound Revenant: Duty-driven
	•	Judicial Revenant: Enforces broken law
	•	Guardian Revenant: Protects a location or bloodline
	•	False Revenant: Artificial, unstable imitation

Guardian Revenants are the most morally complex.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Ghosts: Revenants have bodies and agency
	•	vs Wights: Revenants are not territorial
	•	vs Liches: Revenants are temporary
	•	vs Deathless Spirits: Revenants burn out

This keeps the undead hierarchy tight and clean.
