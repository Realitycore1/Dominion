# Luna

## Narrative Position

Luna is humanity's first industrial world and later its first permanent off-world colony.

The distinction defines Lunar culture.

## Industrial Phase

By 2650, Luna supports:

- automated polar extraction;
- regolith processing;
- vacuum manufacturing;
- cargo mass drivers;
- shielded technical habitats;
- rotating human crews;
- AI and robotic construction.

The installations belong to terrestrial governments, city blocs, worker cooperatives, and industrial organizations. Crew residency is tied to employment.

Luna is economically indispensable but not yet a civic home.

## Phaeton Phase

The Phaeton Program turns Lunar material into the foundation of orbital civilization.

Lunar cargo supports:

- Gateway;
- Welkin;
- Orpheus;
- Eurydice;
- Chulainn;
- Fionn;
- Oisin;
- Gilgamesh.

Lunar workers increasingly object that their world is financing settlements whose residents possess more political rights than Lunar crews.

## Colony Charter

The Lunar Colony Charter passes in 3005.

Permanent settlement requires:

- family-rated habitats;
- schools;
- clinics;
- local civil records;
- burial and inheritance law;
- elected representation;
- redundant rescue and supply;
- transition from federal administration to local self-government.

## First Permanent Colony

Gilgamesh opens in 3007.

The first permanent, self-governing Lunar colony is established in 3008.

For the first time, residents move to Luna expecting their children to remain.

## Cultural Conflict

Founding settlers do not consider themselves newcomers.

Their families maintained Lunar industry for generations while Earth treated the world as a worksite. This produces a lasting Lunar argument:

Earth did not settle Luna.

Lunar labor made settlement possible and then demanded the right to stay.
