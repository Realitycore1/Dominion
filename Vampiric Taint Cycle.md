1. Infection
	•	Method: Bite, blood contact, shadow exposure, or immersion in corrupted soil/water.
	•	Effect on Hosts:
	•	Mortals: Fever, pallor, increased [[Hunger]] → transformation.
	•	Animals: Predatory instincts override balance → rabid parasitism.
	•	[[Angels]]/[[Nephilim]]: Loss of radiance, halos fracture, wings blacken.
	•	Gods: Portfolios begin to consume instead of sustain.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Corruption
	•	Environmental Spread:
	•	Forests wither, rivers stagnate, skies darken.
	•	Faith, trust, or cultural vitality weakens in mortals — even belief can be drained.
	•	Social Spread: Thralls and cults emerge around vampires, either enslaved or “willing” worshippers.
	•	Magical Spread: Any spell cast near taint risks “souring,” twisting into necrotic reflection.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Collapse
	•	Ecosystems: Food webs die. Life feeds only on life. Entire landscapes fall silent.
	•	Civilizations: Mortals either die off, flee, or serve the vampires. Cities become hives of [[Husks]] and thralls.
	•	Divine Order: If [[Angels]] or gods fall, heavens fracture. Cosmic [[law]] breaks down — justice, fate, even [[Time]] can become consumable commodities.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Cleansing
	•	Burning Purge: [[Fire]], sunlight, and divine radiance cleanse but often at total cost — forests must burn, rivers must boil, cities must fall.
	•	Blood Purge Rituals: High-risk rites channel corruption into vessels or banish it to voids, but can backfire.
	•	Long Renewal: Even after cleansing, land is scarred. Recovery takes decades, centuries, or longer.
