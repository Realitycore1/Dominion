# (3608) Craterheart Colony founded

The *C.U.C. Deliverance* reaches the selected Proxima colony world in 3608 with deceleration systems damaged by the interstellar voyage.

The crew avoids total loss through a controlled atmospheric descent and crash.

The wreck creates the crater around which the survivors build Craterheart Colony.

The ark becomes:

- emergency shelter;
- the first power and fabrication district;
- medical and ecological support;
- gene, seed, and cultural archive;
- source of salvage and industrial feedstock;
- meeting place for the first emergency civil assembly.

Human, uplifted, and synthetic survivors form the Proxima Commonwealth because communication delay makes continued mission command inadequate for civil government.

The lower Craterheart Hive develops from the wreck and survival industries. The later Ascendant Ring grows above it through government, education, diplomacy, and cultural preservation.

Craterheart is the first permanent extrasolar settlement founded by the Solar peoples.
