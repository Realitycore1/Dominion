# (96) Charter Debate

## What Happened

The Year 96 AL charter debate forced New Aurora to decide how much authority could be released without endangering shared survival law. Delegates from Low Haven, Atlovaian pressure zones, Black Expanse stations, route communities, and frontier blocs argued from lived terrain rather than abstract ideology.

## What Resulted

- The vote made autonomy discussable in public.
- Settlement blocs learned how to negotiate as collective interests.
- The state learned that law written for emergency survival could become resented once communities matured.

## Long-Term Consequence

The charter debate became a precedent for recognition politics and later Founder Clause arguments.
