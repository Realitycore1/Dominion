# Chapter 17 - Price of Unity

## Timeline Function

War Years 12-14. Unity becomes militarily necessary but morally expensive.

## Event Role

- Emergency command demands concessions from states that still remember why they separated.
- Prisoner exchanges, war debt, occupation rules, and archive access become bargaining chips.
- Some communities accept unity for protection and hate themselves for needing it.
- Others reject unity because it looks too much like the thing they fought.

## Consequence

The future United Syl'an State is born from necessity before belief.
