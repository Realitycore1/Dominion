## Consolidated Lock - Crown Birch of Odysseus / New Harmina

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

The completed galactic megastructure is formally known as the Crown Birch of Odysseus.

Human popular / ideological name: New Harmina.

Common shorthand: the Crown Birch.

Archivist / historical name: Odysseus.

The Crown Birch of Odysseus is a single spherical layered megastructure composed of nested inhabited shells, built from the deconstructed matter of the Odysseus Galaxy over hundreds of millions of years.

It is one spherical world-body, many internal layers, a habitat complex, logistics engine, memory archive, prison system, industrial body, sovereign object, transit nexus, and eventually the primary physical seat of Odyssean Dominion civilization.

Key lines:

> New Harmina was the name humanity wanted. Odysseus was the name history refused to surrender.

> Odysseus ceased to be a galaxy and became a world.

## Crown Birch Strata

Old Odysseus Crown Shell names become major Birch layer families:

1. Crown Core - engine heart, deepest archives, Aether regulation, highest-security containment.
2. Mantle Strata - industry, fabrication, machine ecologies, mass processing.
3. Signet Strata - law, archives, civil registry, treaty chambers, governance interfaces.
4. Sceptre Strata - strategic command, intergalactic transit, fleet yards, expedition launch.
5. Reach Strata - mass habitation, artificial continents, cities, agriculture.
6. Fray Strata - hard-environment zones, reclaimed species zones, frontier experimentation.
7. Periphery Strata - customs, migration, docking, external commerce.
8. Halo Skin - armor, radiators, defense web, observatories, outer ports.

## Founder Species Fate

After GalFed collapse, the Seven Founder species are confined to their homeworld systems under the Founder Containment Mandate, commonly called the Sevenfold Imprisonment.

They are stripped of interstellar sovereignty, denied relay command, forbidden from external stewardship, surrounded by quarantine fleets, forced to preserve complete archives of the Family Event and GalFed crimes, allowed internal civil life under strict constraints, prohibited from political control over non-Founder species, and preserved as living witnesses rather than martyrs.

## Harmina, Crown Birch, and Akasha

Before Severance, Harmina is preserved within the Crown Birch of Odysseus as the Harminan Reliquary: a protected historical, ecological, archival, and civic sanctuary containing the preserved continuity of the Dominion's first homeworld in Odysseus.

While portions of Harmina's non-sacred mass and surrounding system infrastructure are incorporated into the Crown Birch over deep time, its living continuity, major historical sites, biospheric records, grave strata, and founding archives are protected from ordinary deconstruction.

During Severance, the universe's spatial and ontological order fractures. The Harminan Reliquary is torn from the Crown Birch, rewritten through Severance distortion, and reconstituted as Akasha.

Akasha is therefore neither merely old Harmina nor a wholly new world. It is Harmina transformed: a post-Severance homeworld made from preserved matter, archive-memory, civic continuity, and metaphysical scar tissue.

Key line:

> Harmina was preserved so it would never be lost. Severance proved preservation was not the same as safety.
