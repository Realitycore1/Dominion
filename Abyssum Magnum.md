## Summary
The Abyssum Magnum—literally “Great Abyss”—is the cosmic null expanse encasing the entirety of the Eos Multiverse. It is not a vacuum or mere emptiness but a meta-domain of anti-causality, where the normal frameworks of physics, spacetime, and even axioms of logic dissolve into silence. In the Dominion framework, it represents the outer boundary condition of creation—the unresolvable remainder left when all structure, law, and energy are stripped away.

At the metaphysical level, the Abyssum Magnum is where axiomatic decay occurs: the erosion of universal constants and metaphysical rules when verses drift too far from their generating root logic. It is a vast ocean of potential unbound by definition, sometimes described as the negative space of reality, or the “hollow mirror” of existence. Where the Eos Multiverse is symphonic—built from harmonic interplay of axioms—the Abyssum Magnum is atonal, swallowing order into unpatterned silence.

Entities that exist within it—if “existence” applies at all—are either remnants of collapsed verses or aberrant intelligences adapted to pure informational entropy. These beings are beyond ontology, often referred to as Abyssals or Nulliforms, sustained only by paradox or by clinging to fragments of broken reality. Travelers entering this realm experience conceptual corrosion: their identity, history, and physical constants begin to blur into abstraction.

Functionally, the Abyssum Magnum acts as the ultimate containment field of the Eos Multiverse. It is where horizon arc gates tether, where spiralverse arrays terminate, and where the outer logic of existence meets pure non-being. Beyond it lies no further verse—only the Atemporum Silence, the unreachable zone where even possibility fails.

The Abyssum Magnum is therefore both womb and tomb of all things: the space from which verses are born through rare Axiomatic Ignition Events, and the void into which dying verses collapse, dissolving back into the raw unpatterned null from which all realities arise.
