## Summary

Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Kinetic Interdiction Nets are vast, layered defense webs deployed across orbital shells, corridors, or megastructure perimeters. They are designed to intercept and neutralize kinetic projectiles, from micrometeoroids to relativistic kill vehicles, using a combination of massive tether webs, gravitic shears, plasma curtains, and sacrificial drone [[Layers]].
Web Architecture

Tether Lattices: Kilometers-to-AU [[Wide]] arrays of ultra-strong nanofiber strands, suspended by satellites or Atlas Pillar towers.

Gravitic Anchors: Strange-matter nodes pull projectiles off trajectory into controlled kill-zones.

Active Interception

Drone [[Layers]]: Swarms of interdiction drones fly along web-lines, intercepting targets with counter-mass.

Kinetic Mirrors: Redirect projectiles, slingshotting them back toward hostile vectors.

Detonation Nodes: Controlled plasma detonations unravel relativistic slugs before they reach the target zone.

Integration

Deployed around [[Clarke Orbit Cities]], [[Banks Orbitals]], Dyson Fortresses, [[Ark Platforms]], and Corridors.

Often woven into [[Distributed Whipple-Shield Swarms]], combining sacrificial armor with active nets.

Can stretch across [[Beamed-sail corridors]] to protect commerce lanes.

Economic: Secures trade and fleet lanes, reducing loss from hazards or deliberate sabotage.
