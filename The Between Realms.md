1. [[Purgatoria]] (The Scoured Threshold)
	•	Nature: Neither Heaven nor Hell, but a burning plain of refinement.
	•	Role: Souls pass here when they cannot yet ascend but are not wholly damned.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Wraiths of unfinished purification (Hollow Shades).
	•	[[Entities]] fused from [[Sin]]-burnt fragments of multiple souls.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Souls endure purges, [[Fire]] of memory, endless trials. Natives act as keepers or judges, but some mutate into tormentors.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. [[The Shadow Realms]] ([[Umbrae]])
	•	Nature: Liminal echoes of mortal worlds; half-dead, half-living landscapes.
	•	Role: Thin membranes between material worlds and higher [[Realms]].
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Vampires, born of the bleed between [[Hunger]] and mortality.
	•	Shadow-beasts feeding on fear in the overlap.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Mortals sometimes slip here in dreams or curses; natives hunt them.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. The Abyssal Middle ([[Chasms of Negation]])
	•	Nature: Not the Abyssal Vault proper, but countless smaller rifts between [[Realms]].
	•	Role: Cracks in creation where [[law]] unravels, but not fully destroyed.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Demonic chimeras stitched from broken [[law]].
	•	Behemoths that drift in gravityless black seas.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Nothing endures — everything falls, fuses, or mutates. Native [[Entities]] live only to devour and multiply.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Dream-Wastes]] ([[Somnial Fractures]])
	•	Nature: Overlap between the Oneiroverse (dream-rooted multiverse) and mortal souls.
	•	Role: Nightmares given autonomy, [[Forgotten]] [[Archetypes]] crystallizing into matter.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Night-terrors, [[Shapeshifters]], illusory predators.
	•	Sleep-parasites that feed on memory.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Constant shifting; there is no stable [[Time]], only cycles of dream logic. Natives thrive by confusion and misdirection.

⸻

5. [[The Bleeding Wilds]] ([[Feral Expanse]])
	•	Nature: [[Realms]] where creation’s primal life-force overflows without balance.
	•	Role: Borderlands between perfected Gardens of Renewal and corrupted Womb of Carrion.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	[[Chimera]], beast-gods, endless predator-prey cycles.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans|Titans]] and [[Leviathans]] — embodiments of raw vitality.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Survival, constant battle for dominance. Natives revere strength as [[law]].

⸻

6. [[The Hollow Veins]] ([[Forgotten Underworlds]])
	•	Nature: Abandoned tunnels of dead gods and extinct civilizations.
	•	Role: Subterranean echoes beneath other [[Realms]].
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	[[Undead]] constructs, golems whose masters never returned.
	•	Blind serpents, stone-beasts, scavenger shades.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Ceaseless wandering, scavenging in ruins. Some [[Demons]] mine the hollow veins to harvest resources.

⸻

7. [[The Tempest Realms]] ([[Elemental Chaos Zones]])
	•	Nature: Intersections where [[law]] is dominated by one [[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] ([[Fire]], storm, frost, etc.).
	•	Role: Border [[Regions]] between harmony and collapse.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Elemental giants.
	•	Storm-serpents, frost-wraiths, magma hulks.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Constant upheaval — storms never end, mountains collapse and rebuild. Natives embody their element, knowing nothing but force.

⸻

8. [[The Null Shores]] ([[Edge of the Empty]])
	•	Nature: Borderland of [[V - Beast Chronicles/Undead & Constructs/The Empty|The Empty]], where dead souls drift.
	•	Role: Transition between life, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], and oblivion.
	•	Monstrous Spawn:
	•	Hollow men — souls whose essence was stripped away.
	•	Null phantoms that consume resonance itself.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]]: Natives wander without [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], feeding only to stay coherent.
