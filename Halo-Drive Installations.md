Halo-Drive Installations are gravitational propulsion arrays built around compact-object binaries (black holes, neutron [[Stars]], or engineered mass pairs), designed to use their relativistic orbits as reactionless “slingshot engines.”
They are both transport hubs and weapons platforms — places where entire fleets can be launched at relativistic speeds.

Drive Mechanism

Fleets [[Fire]] laser/plasma beams into the binary’s orbital path.

Ships accelerate to fractional-c to relativistic speeds without expending their own fuel.

[[Infrastructure]]

Beam Projector Towers: Atlas Pillar–scale spires directing coherent beams into the binary.

[[Computronium]] Nodes: Run simulations of photon orbits (chaotic at relativistic regimes).

Gravitational Lens Rings: Arrays that amplify and stabilize beam return.
