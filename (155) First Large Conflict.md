# (155) First Large Conflict

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapters 24-29.
- Manuscript date range: Years 151-252 After Landfall.
- Canon note: this legacy file should now function as the long conflict buildup note. The first open military strike occurs in Year 252.

## What Happened

The first large conflict did not begin with armies. It began with incompatible legal memories. Every settlement bloc could point to a practical reason for autonomy, a historical reason for distrust, and a resource reason for leverage.

The buildup includes recognition disputes, body ethics, welfare pressure, family incentives, clone and hybrid labor, autonomous weapons, orbital infrastructure, deep mantle mining, power armor, secret programs such as Project Ra, and planetary defense projects hidden inside civilian systems.

## What Resulted

- The power blocs armed themselves while publicly claiming defensive necessity.
- GalFed observation remained the buried justification for orbital networks and strategic schools.
- New Aurora's symbolic centrality made it both legitimacy prize and target.
- The Founder Clause debate turned Amber from private founder into a contested constitutional instrument.

## Consequences

The first large conflict culminates in the Atlovaian and F.S.S.R. strike on New Aurora during the Founder Session in Year 252. This becomes the opening of the New World War.
