# Chapter XIV - Last Enclave

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The final holdout tests whether the new order will be founded on extermination, mercy, trial, or negotiated memory.

## Event Role

- The enclave contains hardliners, civilians, archives, damaged soldiers, and people with nowhere else to go.
- Calls for revenge collide with the need to build a state after victory.
- Surrender terms define what counts as guilt after systemic war.
- The future justice system begins in the treatment of the defeated.

## Consequence

The last enclave forces the United Syl'an project to decide whether unity means annihilating refusal or surviving it.
