Taxonomy of Magic [[Domains]]

The arts of magic across the Omniverse can be organized into five great Branches, each defined by how they interact with life, form, will, and [[law]].

⸻

I. Vital Branch – Life and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]]

Magic tied directly to vitality, blood, and decay.
	•	[[Vitality Magic – Blood Magic]] — life essence as fuel.
	•	[[Necrosis Magic]] — undeath, rot, and the breaking of the life–[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] cycle.

⸻

II. Formative Branch – Shaping and Story

Magic that reshapes, conceals, or manifests meaning.
	•	[[Alteration magic]] — reshaping form and matter.
	•	[[Creation magic]] — bringing forth new matter, life, or laws.
	•	[[Illusion Magic]] — deceiving the senses and cloaking reality.
	•	[[Mythopoeia magic]] — weaving story and belief into reality.

⸻

III. Restrictive Branch – Bonds and Barriers

Magic that fixes, restrains, and shields beings or concepts.
	•	[[Binding magic]] — oaths, seals, and restrictions.
	•	[[Protection Magic]] — wards, barriers, and sanctuaries.
	•	[[Domination magic]] — compulsions and control of will.

⸻

IV. Primal Branch – Unmaking and Foundations

Magic that deals with fundamental forces of [[law]], destruction, and causality.
	•	[[Annihilation magic]] — absolute erasure into nothingness.
	•	[[Axiomatic Warping magic]] — rewriting the root rules of [[(2030) Existence]].
	•	[[Motion magic]] — vectors, displacement, and translation across space.
	•	[[Revelation magic]] — prophecy, truthsight, and unveiling hidden layers.

⸻

V. Cosmic Branch – The Grand Canvas

Magic that operates at planetary, stellar, or [[Verse]]-spanning scales.
	•	[[Cosmos Magic]] — manipulation of [[Stars]], worlds, and cosmic harmonics.

⸻

Notes on Overlap
	•	[[Domains]] often blur at the edges. For example, [[Illusion Magic]] shades into [[Mythopoeia magic]] when a lie becomes a story, and [[Binding magic]] overlaps with [[Domination magic]] when an oath becomes compulsion.
	•	Higher orders of magic ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-tier, Demiurgic corruption, Mythic Concordian [[Ascension]]) often combine multiple branches at once.
	•	Scholars argue whether [[Revelation magic]] belongs in the Primal branch or deserves its own, since knowledge is both a tool and a foundation.
