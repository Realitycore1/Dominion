The Enthralling One is the creator of all things and is responsible for everything past, present and future. Hungry and alone the Old One ushered into [[(2030) Existence]] the [[Entities]] to provide it nourishment.

Millennia ago, when The Enthralling One was not mad with [[Hunger]], it is said that it had a great battle with one of its own creations, Roezong, The Embraced God of [[The Void]]. Roezong, jealous of The Old One's power of creation and unsatisfied with the boundaries of its vast void, decided to enter into the heart of creation. This angered The Enthralling One, for not only did Roezong defy its master by leaving [[The Void]], but it brought part of [[The Void]] into creation. For its transgression, The Old One battled with Roezong leaving the master of [[The Void]] severely weakened. To ensure this transgression never occurs again, The Old One created [[The Gate Guardian]] to guard the heart of creation from Roezong and its creeping void. To this day, [[The Gate Guardian]] has never failed in its duty to protect its master's creations. Though with its master now mad with [[Hunger]] there may come a day when Roezong's void enters into creation once more.
