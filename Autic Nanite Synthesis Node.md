## Details

An automated [[Orbital Factory]] that 
converts cosmic and stellar [[Particles]] into programmable nanite swarms.
	•	Mechanism: Ion harvesters collect charged [[Particles]]; quantum assembly arrays splice subatomic building blocks into [[Nanomachines]].
	•	Output: Nanites usable for orbital construction, ship repair, surface refurbishment, or fleet-level automatic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] enhancement.
	•	Strategic Role: Provides a low-cost, renewable supply of utility nanites—ideal for scalability and rapid response repair networks.
