1. Vessels
	•	Mortals are “clay vessels”, fragile but adaptable.
	•	[[Angels]] and [[Demons]] cannot manifest fully in material [[Realms]] without burning them apart.
	•	To descend, they need a host body to anchor their essence.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Consent vs Force
	•	Angelic Possession: Requires consent. The mortal must “open the door,” even if only for a moment. This makes it rare but far more stable.
	•	Demonic Possession: Can force its way in through fear, [[Sin]], or broken will. These are unstable, degrading both host and possessor.
	•	Special Case: Cults of [[Christus heterodoxus]] practice willing corruption, surrendering themselves to possession in exchange for blissful oblivion.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Manifestation
	•	Signs of Angelic Possession:
	•	Burning blue/white light in the eyes.
	•	Voice layered with harmony, like multiple [[Choirs]].
	•	Presence bends the room — shadows shrink, sound becomes sharper.
	•	Signs of Demonic Possession:
	•	Black or crimson eyes.
	•	Voice laced with distortion.
	•	Surroundings corrode: lights flicker, air grows heavy, whispers linger.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Strain on the Host
	•	Mortal bodies are not meant for divine/infernal essence.
	•	Angelic Strain: Skin cracks with light, lifespan shortened, body deteriorates with prolonged use.
	•	Demonic Strain: Madness, rot, and violent urges overwhelm the host.
	•	Some lineages (bloodlines, Nephorim remnants, cult initiates) can endure longer.

⸻

5. Conflict Inside the Vessel
	•	A vessel is not passive. The mortal’s will remains, even if suppressed.
	•	[[Angels]]: often cooperate, but tensions arise when mortal will diverges.
	•	[[Demons]]: often dominate, but strong mortals may resist or even imprison the [[Demon]] inside themselves.

⸻

6. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Theology
	•	Orthodoxy View: Angelic possession = sacred union; demonic possession = abomination.
	•	[[Infernariate]] Role: Exorcists specialize in purging [[Demons]], but also regulate angelic possession to prevent “unauthorized incarnations.”
	•	Heresy: Some cults claim possession is the true path to divinity, willingly opening themselves to [[Angels]], [[Demons]], or even false aeons.

⸻

7. Advanced Variants
	•	Dual Possession: Rare — when [[Angel]] and mortal perfectly align, the mortal may co-shape miracles.
	•	Overburn: If an [[Angel]] forces too much of itself through, the host immolates in holy [[Fire]].
	•	Hollow Hosts: Souls emptied by the Hollow Church are perfect vessels for possession — no resistance at all.



8. Angelic Multiplicity
	•	Definition: More than one [[Angel]] inhabits a single host.
	•	Result: Almost always catastrophic. Mortals aren’t built to channel multiple divine harmonics at once.
	•	Outcome:
	•	Best case: the [[Angels]] harmonize, the host becomes a living choir (blinding, awe-inspiring, body slowly consumed).
	•	Worst case: host burns out instantly, leaving only ash and echoes.
	•	Example Vision: Eyes glow with layered halos, voice is an overwhelming chorus, flesh fractals with light.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Demonic Multiplicity
	•	Definition: More than one [[Demon]] forces entry into a host.
	•	Result: Highly unstable, but survivable if the host is strong-willed.
	•	Outcome:
	•	Host becomes a battlefield; the [[Demons]] fight inside, spilling madness outward.
	•	In rare cases, the [[Demons]] merge into a single gestalt, creating a far more dangerous entity.
	•	Example Vision: Multiple eye-colors flicker, voice shifts between tones, body spasms with inhuman distortions.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Angelic + Demonic Possession
	•	Definition: Both angelic and demonic essence share a single vessel.
	•	Result: Paradoxical and extremely rare.
	•	Outcome:
	•	The vessel becomes a living war — two cosmic forces vying for control.
	•	If balanced, it can stabilize into something new: a hybrid avatar that channels both [[law]] and negation.
	•	If unstable, it rips the host apart from within.
	•	Example Vision: One eye glows blue-white, the other red-black; one half of the body radiant, the other corroded.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Triple or Higher (Heretical Fusions)
	•	Definition: Angelic + Demonic + Additional (false aeons, gnostic archons, etc.).
	•	Result: Considered heresy beyond comprehension.
	•	Outcome:
	•	The vessel becomes a shattered [[Locus]], radiating chaos across reality.
	•	Can collapse local physical laws as competing essences tear at the world around them.
	•	The Orthodoxy’s View: Such beings are walking abominations, to be annihilated at all costs.
	•	Example Vision: Host body fractures like glass, each shard showing a different reality.

⸻

5. Theological Interpretation
	•	Angelic Multiplicity = hubris; no mortal should try to contain multiple flames of the Holy Will.
	•	Demonic Multiplicity = corruption feeding on itself.
	•	[[Angel]] + [[Demon]] = proof of paradox — some theologians argue these are glimpses of the true nature of the Omniverse, where opposites coexist.
	•	Triple or Higher = direct evidence of [[The Gnostic Expanse]] bleeding through, where boundaries break down.
