Classification

Forest Warden / Territorial Nature Sentinel / Animistic Apex

⸻

CORE IDEA

Leshy are the will of old forests made personal. Not gods, not beasts—custodians. Where woodland persists long enough to develop memory, boundaries, and anger, a Leshy emerges to enforce them.

They don’t protect people.
They protect the forest’s right to exist.

⸻

ORIGIN

Leshy arise where:
	•	forests remain unbroken for centuries
	•	hunting and logging follow ritual limits
	•	paths are walked but not widened
	•	the land is remembered rather than owned

A forest gains rules → rules gain consequence → consequence gains a face.

They are born, not summoned.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Leshy are polymorphic, shifting with the forest’s mood.

Common traits:
	•	Tall, humanoid silhouettes (often 2–4 m)
	•	Bodies of bark, root, moss, and bone
	•	Antlers or branch-crowns grown unevenly
	•	Eyes glowing amber, green, or fungal blue
	•	Limbs that lengthen or shorten to mimic trees

When angered, they grow taller and more monstrous; when calm, they shrink and blend into the woods.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Leshy are territorial, cunning, and merciless when crossed.

They:
	•	mislead travelers
	•	rearrange paths
	•	lure intruders in circles
	•	separate groups quietly
	•	escalate from confusion to violence

They tolerate:
	•	respectful passage
	•	ritual offerings
	•	necessary harvest within limits

They do not tolerate:
	•	clear-cutting
	•	fire misuse
	•	Beast Rot
	•	Malice-tainted activity

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High, but non-human.

Leshy think in:
	•	territory
	•	cycles
	•	balance of predator and prey
	•	long-term consequence

They understand humans, but do not center them.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Domain Sovereignty: absolute control within forest bounds
	•	Grace-neutral: angels cannot command them
	•	Malice-hostile: corruption triggers immediate retaliation
	•	Illusion Mastery: forest geometry bends at will
	•	Regenerative: draws vitality from roots and soil

Killing a Leshy poisons the forest for generations.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Feed on growth cycles, decay, and seasonal transition
	•	Reproduce rarely—new Leshy form only after catastrophic loss
	•	Lesser forest spirits often serve or orbit them
	•	Their absence causes ecological collapse or feral overgrowth

A forest without a Leshy becomes either dead or monstrous.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Severe (localized) / Catastrophic (provoked)

Within its domain, a Leshy is effectively unbeatable without destroying the forest itself.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Leshy represent:
	•	nature’s memory
	•	consequence without malice
	•	guardianship without mercy
	•	the danger of treating land as inert

Where Kami are reverent,
Leshy are enforcement.
