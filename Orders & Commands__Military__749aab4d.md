## Summary
## Concord‑Level Commands
- **Concord Defense Mesh HQ (CDM‑HQ):** harmonizes multi‑[[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] operations; owns [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], comms, and cross‑training.
- **Strategic Reserve Command (SRC):** ark‑evac, deep‑range rescue, megastructure incident response.
- **Corridor Guard Command (CGC):** trade lane security, embargo enforcement, gateway control.
- **[[Sentinel]] & Rescue Command (SRC‑S):** pickets, patrols, SAR networks, depot triage.
- **Audit Enforcement Command (AEC):** executes Ombuds/court writs; boards & seizures under legal mandate only.
- **Logistics & Sustainment Command (LSC):** fuel/energy settlement, depot management, fleet maintenance.
- **Medical & Humanitarian Command (MHC):** hospital fleets, field clinics, relief stockpiles.
- **Training & [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] Command (TRADOC‑H):** standards, simulations, interspecies crew integration.

## Regional & Species Commands
- **Regional Concord Defense (RCD‑<Sector>):** sector operations, muster audits, port authority integration.
- **Species Defense Guilds (SDG‑<Species>):** organic fleet cores; provide cadres, cultural interfaces, and unique platforms.
- **Habitat Defense Wardens (HDW):** planetary/habitat security, evacuation, civil defense.

## Standing Operational Orders (examples)
- **Order of Corridor Denial (OCD):** authorize graduated interdiction and lane closure; requires court writ + CGC confirmation.
- **Order of Sanctuary (OOS):** establish protected transit for evacuees/relief; overrides embargo when lives at stake.
- **Order of Recalibration (OOR):** mandate de‑escalation, reparations, and habitat restoration after hostilities.
- **Order of Quieting (OOQ):** non‑kinetic dominance (ECM, deception, power decoupling) to prevent fighting.
- **Order of Shielding (OOSH):** raise planetary/habitat defenses and deploy rescue constellations.

## Command Authorities & Keys
 **Dual‑[[Key]] Triggers:** high‑risk orders require two independent signatories (operational + legal).
 **[[Rights]] Veto Pause:** Ombuds may suspend orders that threaten protected freedoms; AEC executes the pause.
**Telemetry Transparency:** command logs mirrored to audit mesh; post‑op summaries are public.
