1. Common Cockatrice

Definition: The baseline—rooster’s head and body with serpent’s tail and dragonlike wings.

Traits: Venomous bite or petrifying gaze, shrill crow of doom.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in absurd disguise; nature mocked into terror.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Serpentine Cockatrice

Definition: [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] leaning more toward snakes than birds.

Traits: Long serpentine tails, venomous strike, slithering gait.

Symbolism: The rooster crowned as serpent-king; parody of regality.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Avian Cockatrice

Definition: [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] more birdlike, with powerful wings.

Traits: Sharp talons, crowing shrieks that stun prey, limited flight.

Symbolism: The barnyard twisted into a monster.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Draconic Cockatrice

Definition: [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] infused with [[Dragon]] traits.

Traits: Scaly hides, fiery breath, miniature [[Dragon]] appearance.

Symbolism: The lesser [[Dragon]]—mockery of majesty.

5. Swamp Cockatrice

Definition: [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] adapted to wetlands and fetid places.

Traits: Mud-colored feathers, toxic breath, ambush hunters.

Symbolism: Decay and filth turned to life.

6. Frost Cockatrice

Definition: Cold-dwelling [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]].

Traits: Icy plumage, freezing breath, crystalline petrification.

Symbolism: Winter’s bite given absurd form.

7. Shadow Cockatrice

Definition: [[Abyss]]-touched [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]].

Traits: Black feathers, glowing eyes, necrotic gaze.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in parody; corruption of life’s crow.

8. War-Bred Cockatrice

Definition: [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] created or bred as battlefield horrors.

Traits: Armored hides, venomous spines, used as shock troops.

Symbolism: Mortals turning absurdity into terror.

9. Regal Cockatrice

Definition: Rare, larger [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] that claim territories like dragons.

Traits: Brilliant plumage, cunning intelligence, tyrant’s pride.

Symbolism: Absurdity elevated to nobility.

10. Arch-Cockatrice (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary [[V - Beast Chronicles/Mythic/Cockatrice/Cockatrice|Cockatrices]] that embody entropy and mockery itself.

Traits: Colossal rooster-serpents, crowing shatters stone, gaze petrifies nations.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and laughter entwined; creation’s parody become apocalypse.
