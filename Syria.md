Syria is a country located in Western Asia with a diverse geography and rich cultural heritage. The country is bordered by Turkey to the north, [[Iraq]] to the east, Jordan to the east, and Israel, Lebanon, and the Mediterranean Sea to the south. Syria's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is primarily based on agriculture, with a focus on crops such as wheat, barley, and olive oil. The country also has significant mineral resources, including phosphate and gypsum, which are used in various industries. The country's geography includes fertile plains in the north and highlands in the south, as well as a rugged coastline along the Mediterranean Sea. The country's major cities include Damascus, Aleppo, and Homs, which have a rich [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] dating back to ancient civilizations such as the Phoenicians and Assyrians. Syria's [[culture]] is a blend of Eastern and Western influences, with a strong Islamic heritage that dates back to the spread of Islam in the region in the seventh century. Arabic is the official language of Syria, with a rich literary tradition and poetry. The country has a diverse population, with ethnic groups such as Arabs, Kurds, Assyrians, and Circassians living in different parts of Syria. The Syrian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] has faced challenges in promoting greater social and economic inclusion for minority groups. In recent years, Syria has been affected by conflicts and political instability that have caused significant damage to its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] and [[Infrastructure]]. The country has been at war since 2011, with fighting between [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] forces and various rebel groups, including militant groups such as ISIS and al-Qaeda. Despite these challenges, Syria remains a culturally rich and historically significant country with significant potential for growth and development.
