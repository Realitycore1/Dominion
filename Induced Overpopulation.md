It does not mean the Dominion mindlessly overcrowds worlds until people starve.

It means the Dominion may deliberately create controlled demographic surplus in specific zones to force outward motion.

Useful phrasing:

Induced Overpopulation is the deliberate creation of population surplus beyond local comfort thresholds, but below systemic collapse thresholds, to generate migration, colonization, labor competition, military recruitment, and expansionary pressure.

This works especially well on:

* Hive Worlds
* Yield Worlds
* Military Worlds
* Refinery Worlds
* Frontier Worlds
* Postwar reconstruction zones
* New settlement belts
* Colony clusters near terraforming projects

The state allows, encourages, or engineers higher birth rates because it wants a population that cannot comfortably remain static.

That creates a built-in social pressure:

There are too many people for everyone to stay where they are, so some must build elsewhere.

That is harsh, but it is strategically coherent.

Risks

Induced Overpopulation can produce:

* poverty
* housing stress
* labor exploitation
* frontier dumping
* military over-recruitment
* social unrest
* family pressure
* ecological strain
* resentment toward Core Worlds
* black-market migration
* demographic caste systems

This policy should have critics inside the Dominion.

A solid internal criticism:

“A surplus citizen is still a citizen, not raw material for expansion.”

That line is strong.
