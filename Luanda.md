14.6 Luanda, the capital city of Angola, is a vibrant and cosmopolitan metropolis that has grown rapidly in recent years due to the country's oil wealth. The city is situated on the coast of the Atlantic Ocean and is known for its stunning beaches and bustling ports. Luanda's architecture is a mix of colonial and modern [[Styles]], with many of its buildings constructed during the Portuguese colonial era. The city's skyline is dominated by the "Diamant" tower, a modern high-rise that is one of the tallest buildings in Africa. Luanda is also a center for tourism, with its beaches and cultural attractions attracting visitors from around the world. However, Luanda also faces significant challenges, including poverty, unemployment, and [[Infrastructure]] shortages. The city's growing population and limited resources have led to overcrowding and a lack of basic amenities in many areas. Despite these challenges, Luanda remains a dynamic and energetic city, with a thriving arts scene and a vibrant nightlife. The city's markets are also a hub of activity, with vendors selling everything from traditional African crafts to imported goods.
