“The World Already Forgetting Itself”

Purpose

Introduce:

* Sachin,
* the Frayed condition,
* paradox storms,
* and continuity erosion.

Location

A Deep Aether frontier civilization:

* isolated,
* dimly lit,
* partially derealizing,
* existing inside unstable continuity pockets.

Think:

* worn megastructures,
* fragmented archives,
* skies with overlapping stars,
* people preserving history manually because records decay.

* Frayed strands
* Derealization
* Gabriel’s Horn dreams

A major paradox storm hits.

Reality partially overlaps with:

* another civilization,
* another timeline,
* or dead memories.

Sachin survives because:

he can hear the call clearly.

Others cannot.

This marks him as:

* dangerous,
* blessed,
* or doomed.
