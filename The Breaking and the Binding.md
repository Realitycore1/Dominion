The chamber reeked of steel, blood, and sanctity.

Kael knelt naked on the obsidian floor, his body trembling as [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] medicae bound him with [[Chains]] not to restrain, but to anchor. Before him loomed the sarcophagus of Fenrir — the wolf of apocalypse — etched in radiant script. The words glowed faintly, like veins in stone, pulsing in rhythm with his own heart.

“Your flesh will fail,” intoned the magister-surgeon, his voice echoing like iron through a hollow hall. “Your breath will fail. Your fear will fail. Only the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] endures.”

The procedure began.

Implants drove into Kael’s spine — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] cores humming with cold light. [[Aetheric]] couplers slid into his veins, flooding his body with liquid [[Fire]]. Bones shattered and were reforged with lattice-steel, each fracture sealed with searing light. He screamed until his voice broke, then screamed without sound as the [[Pain]] pushed past mortal limits.

And still, he did not break.

Hours stretched into something uncountable. His blood boiled and stilled, his muscles tore and healed, his lungs drowned and drew breath again. At last the medicae withdrew, their masks unreadable, their hands wet with his [[Rebirth]].

He was carried into the Hall of Wolves.

There, under banners torn from a thousand xenos wars, the Legion of Fenrir stood in silent ranks. Their armor was ash-grey and crimson, scarred but unbroken, each helm a wolf’s visage snarling against eternity. Their eyes — those who had eyes left — burned with gold [[Fire]].

And at the end of the hall, seated upon a throne of blackened iron, was the [[Verarch]] of Fenrir.

His armor was a mountain, his presence a gravity. His helm was no mask but a crown of snarling steel, his aura radiating both [[Hunger]] and command. He leaned forward, one clawed gauntlet resting on the haft of a great axe whose edge seemed to drink the light itself.

“Rise,” the [[Verarch]] growled.

Kael staggered, every nerve alight, every breath like swallowing glass. But he rose.

The [[Verarch]] descended from his throne. Each step reverberated like thunder. He circled Kael, a predator appraising prey. “You are broken,” he said, his voice low, dangerous. “But all flesh must break. Tell me — what remains?”

Kael’s throat was raw. His words came as a whisper, yet they carried into every corner of the hall:

“Only the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].”

The [[Verarch]] stopped. For a heartbeat, [[Silence]] reigned. Then the hall erupted.

Legionnaires slammed spear butts against the obsidian floor, a sound like a storm breaking. Their voices — one, unbroken, eternal — filled the air: “Fenrir! Fenrir! Fenrir!”

The [[Verarch]] raised his axe, its edge igniting with wolf-[[Fire]]. He touched it to Kael’s shoulder, then his brow. “Then you are no longer Kael,” he intoned. “You are Fang of Fenrir. You are the wolf unchained. You are Legion.”

Kael fell to his knees, not from weakness but from awe. His body was no longer his own. His name was no longer his own. He belonged to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

The [[Verarch]] raised his head, eyes burning gold. “March, Fang. The xenos wait. And we are hungry.”

The Legion roared. The banners flared. And the newborn wolf took his place among [[Immortals]].
