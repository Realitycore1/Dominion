Classification

Grave-Bound Revenants / Territorial Undead Sovereigns / Oath-Anchored Dead

⸻

CORE IDEA

Barrow-Wights are the dead who never relinquished ownership.

They are not restless spirits and not mindless corpses. A Barrow-Wight persists because something was promised, claimed, or buried incorrectly. The grave became a contract, and the contract is still enforced.

They do not haunt.
They administer.

⸻

ORIGIN

Barrow-Wights form under specific funerary failures:
	•	kings or champions buried with unresolved oaths
	•	cursed interments sealing power instead of releasing it
	•	mass graves sanctified incorrectly
	•	tombs designed as wards rather than memorials

Unlike ghouls or liches, Barrow-Wights do not seek immortality.
They inherit it.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Barrow-Wights appear as preserved dead nobility or warriors.

Common traits:
	•	Corporeal undead bodies clad in burial armor or grave-rusted regalia
	•	Flesh pallid, leathery, or desiccated but intact
	•	Eyes glowing cold blue, green, or corpse-white
	•	Breath frosting the air even in warmth
	•	Weapons fused with funerary magic

They smell of earth, old iron, and cold stone.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Barrow-Wights are territorial, methodical, and dignified.
	•	Rarely leave their burial grounds
	•	Challenge intruders formally or silently
	•	Kill efficiently, without rage
	•	Ignore the living who respect the dead

They do not pursue vengeance.
They enforce jurisdiction.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High.
	•	Retain memories of life and rulership
	•	Understand tactics, law, and hierarchy
	•	Capable of speech—often archaic and formal
	•	Cannot adapt to new eras easily

They are rulers frozen in time.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Grave Sovereignty: absolute control within burial territory
	•	Cold Dominion: temperature drops near their presence
	•	Grace-Resistant: angelic light weakens but does not command
	•	Malice-Compatible: corruption amplifies territorial hostility
	•	Oath Binding: sworn promises still compel them

Destroying a Barrow-Wight without resolving its oath destabilizes the land.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Anchor burial mounds, tomb-cities, and necropolises
	•	Surrounding land becomes eerily preserved or stagnant
	•	Lesser undead may form under their dominion
	•	Absence causes grave-collapse phenomena

Barrow-Wights turn graves into borders.

⸻

ALPHA BARROW-WIGHTS

An Alpha Barrow-Wight is a Tomb-King / Grave Regent.

Traits:
	•	Commands multiple burial sites
	•	Raises disciplined undead retainers
	•	Can impose ancient law on the living
	•	Treats modern authority as illegitimate

Tomb-Kings don’t conquer.
They reclaim.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (localized) / Strategic (Alpha)

You don’t rush a Barrow-Wight.
You negotiate, appease, or outlast it.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Barrow-Wights represent:
	•	power buried incorrectly
	•	tradition weaponized
	•	the cost of unfinished rites
	•	death as jurisdiction, not escape

Where Ghoul-Pires are collapse,
Barrow-Wights are order that never died.
