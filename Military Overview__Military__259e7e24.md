## Summary

GalFed's military is formally a custodial and disaster-response apparatus. Its fleets are designed to quarantine, rescue, interdict, stabilize, and, when necessary, erase threats before they can recreate Singularity-scale harm.

## Details

Primary force: The Custodial Harmonium.

The Harmonium began as rescue fleets protecting restoration teams and quarantine zones. Over time it became GalFed's armed expression of continuity law. It is not culturally framed as a war machine. It is framed as emergency medicine at galactic scale.

Strategic roles:

- Aether quarantine enforcement.
- Protection of relief corridors.
- Suppression of unauthorized FTL routes.
- Interdiction of Founder-grade weapons.
- Defense of Archive Gate infrastructure.
- Escort of restoration missions.
- Evacuation of collapsing worlds.
- Regime restructuring on Stability Risk worlds.
- Enforcement of Synod and Council mandates.

Military doctrine:

- Detect early.
- Isolate fast.
- Preserve infrastructure.
- Avoid martyr narratives.
- Disable leadership before mass combat.
- Control transit before controlling ground.
- Use relief access as leverage before kinetic force.

GalFed prefers wars that never look like wars. A rebellious world first loses certification, then corridor access, then credit stability, then leadership legitimacy, then military options.
