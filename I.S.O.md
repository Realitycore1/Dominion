ncept in science fiction that refers to objects or [[Entities]] that possess an advanced level of intelligence, comparable to that of [[Humans]] or other intelligent [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]]. These intelligent super objects may have evolved over [[Time]], either through [[Natural]] selection or through deliberate intervention by intelligent civilizations. They may have gained sentience, consciousness, and self-awareness, and may possess the ability to communicate, reason, and make decisions. In many stories, intelligent super objects play a central role, as they are often highly advanced and possess abilities and knowledge that far surpass those of their creators or other beings. They may be used as tools or allies, or they may pose a threat to other civilizations or even the entire universe. The concept of intelligent super objects explores the potential for machines and other inanimate objects to achieve a level of intelligence and consciousness that is comparable to that of living beings. It raises questions about the nature of intelligence and sentience, as well as the ethical implications of creating and interacting with such [[Entities]].
