1. Common Fairies
	•	Definition: The baseline—tiny winged humanoids, magical and mischievous.
	•	Traits: Insectlike wings, glowing auras, [[Trickster]] magic.
	•	Symbolism: The small spirits of wonder; nature in miniature.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Sprite Fairies
	•	Definition: Mischievous fairies tied to pranks and illusions.
	•	Traits: Invisibility, laughter-magic, quick flight.
	•	Symbolism: Harmless play masking hidden malice.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Pixie Fairies
	•	Definition: Kind-hearted or curious fairies tied to light and joy.
	•	Traits: Glittering wings, helpful charms, childlike personalities.
	•	Symbolism: Innocence and wonder; fragile beauty.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Brownies
	•	Definition: Household fairies who aid mortals in secret.
	•	Traits: Small, earthy, nocturnal helpers; turn malicious if disrespected.
	•	Symbolism: Reciprocity of gifts; hidden helpers turned foes.

⸻

5. Will-o’-Wisps (Fairy Lights)
	•	Definition: Ghostly fairies appearing as lights to lure travelers.
	•	Traits: Floating orbs, lead wanderers into swamps or safety depending on mood.
	•	Symbolism: Temptation, uncertainty, false hope.

⸻

6. Flower Fairies
	•	Definition: Fairies tied to blossoms, gardens, and growth.
	•	Traits: Petal-wings, colorful hair, seasonal ties.
	•	Symbolism: The fleeting beauty of life, cycles of bloom and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

7. Frost Fairies
	•	Definition: Fairies of snow, frost, and winter’s bite.
	•	Traits: Pale-blue wings, ice-dust trails, sharp tempers.
	•	Symbolism: The cruelty and beauty of winter.

⸻

8. Shadow Fairies
	•	Definition: Fairies bound to twilight or curses.
	•	Traits: Dark wings, eerie laughter, affinity for necrotic or shadow magic.
	•	Symbolism: The [[Trickster]] turned sinister; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in playful guise.

⸻

9. Noble Fairies (Faerie Aristocracy)
	•	Definition: Fairies who rise into leadership roles within courts or courts-of-summer/winter.
	•	Traits: Larger bodies, ornate wings, aura of authority.
	•	Symbolism: Power layered over whimsy; rulers of the “small folk.”

⸻

10. Arch-Fairies (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary fairies tied to seasons, moons, or great [[Natural]] phenomena.
	•	Traits: Semi-divine, their auras can alter weather, crops, or mortal fate.
	•	Symbolism: Playfulness writ cosmic; the tiny become titanic in scope.
