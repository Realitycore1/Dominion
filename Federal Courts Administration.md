# Federal Courts Administration

## Summary

The Federal Courts Administration keeps the Dominion's court system operating across worlds, habitats, fleets, restoration zones, and local compacts.

It does not decide cases. Judges do that.

It ensures that courts exist, records survive, appeals move, public defenders are assigned, evidence systems remain trustworthy, and emergency tribunals do not become permanent substitutes for lawful courts.

## Mandate

The Administration manages:

- court staffing;
- records preservation;
- public defender access;
- jury pools where required;
- remote hearing systems;
- evidence custody;
- warrant systems;
- emergency court logistics;
- translation and cultural-access services;
- interjurisdictional case transfer.

## Powers

It may discipline court staff, audit evidence systems, suspend corrupted record channels, and report judges or agencies for procedural misconduct.

## Limits

It cannot alter judgments, select political outcomes, conceal cases for administrative convenience, or allow classified claims to erase due process entirely.

## Public Face

To ordinary citizens, this agency is the counter, the hearing notice, the clerk, the translation terminal, the defender appointment, and the appeal deadline.

## Story Use

Use this agency to ground the Bill of Rights in daily procedure. Rights feel real when someone can find a clerk, file a challenge, and receive a hearing.
