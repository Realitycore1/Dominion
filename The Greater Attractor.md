"Citizens of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]. Children of the Eternal Flame. Scions of order forged in the crucible of entropy—hear me."
"For eons we have stretched our hands toward the [[Stars]], reaching for them as if they were fading embers scattered by cosmic winds. We carved paths through [[The Void]]. We made steel speak our name. We lit the heavens with engines, and we gave the darkness no quarter."
"Yet always we raced. Raced against [[Time]], against entropy, against the tide of unreason that would see all undone. We fought, not only to survive, but to bring purpose to an indifferent cosmos."
"But no more shall we chase the universe."
"Tonight, the [[Stars]] move to us."
"This, before you, is not merely a machine. It is not merely a shell. It is not even a weapon. It is the culmination of divine intent and boundless will. It is the Final Anchor—GRA-Ω, the hinge of destiny itself. Its lattice binds not just atoms, but spacetime. Its core is not merely a star, but a summons."
"From this moment forward, the fabric of reality will bend to a new axis. Not the indifferent drift of expansion, but the sovereign call of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]. A gravity of civilization so absolute, so inevitable, that all things must come home."
"Let the xenos gaze into [[The Void]] and see it fold. Let those who hide beyond the galactic rim feel the drift of their own [[Stars]] pulling away—toward us. Let the unworthy understand: distance is no longer sanctuary. There will be no more hiding. No more [[Time]]."
"The age of expansion ends. The age of consolidation begins."
"I am Amber Zohair. I speak with the voice of [[Origin]]. I act by the will of all humankind. And by this act, we bind the cosmos to the cradle of order."
"May the [[Stars]] bow."
(She extends her hand. A ripple of golden gravitic auroras ignite across the Anchor’s interior. The core begins to pulse with celestial rhythm as the Final Anchor enters full activation…)
