Classification: Colossus Class – Tier XII / Field Designate “Threxon” (The Ironhorn Behemoth)

Observation:
Recorded traversing the Harminan equatorial savannah basin, nocturnal cycle. Height: 18 meters at the shoulder; total length: 40 meters. Estimated mass exceeds 900 metric tons. Carapace composed of hyperdense bio-ferrite [[Layers]] woven with fibrous musculature capable of magnetic reinforcement under stress. Dorsal spikes function as atmospheric sensors, oscillating in resonance with local electrical fields — possibly for storm prediction or energy collection. The primary cranial horn serves dual purposes: a kinetic battering ram and [[Aetheric]] conductor capable of discharging plasma bursts during territorial defense.

Contextual Analysis:
Threxon represents the surface biome’s keystone stabilizer [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]]. It forages on silicate-rich [[Flora]], ingesting mineral matter to reinforce its exoskeleton through bio-alchemical conversion. Despite its overwhelming strength, it exhibits grazing patterns and non-hostile behavior unless provoked. Observed incidents of aggression coincide exclusively with seismic activity or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] terraforming disruption, suggesting heightened environmental sensitivity.
Field records from the [[Harmina]] Central Observatory note the rhythmic vibrations caused by its movement align with harmonic frequencies of the planet’s crustal lattice, implying the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]]’ migration contributes to tectonic stability.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
Within the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Enduring Flesh, the Threxon embodies [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] resilience and sanctified burden — a creature forged for suffering without collapse. It is seen as the patron symbol of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] fortification corps and planetary architects. The [[Infernariate]] refer to it as the Silent Atlas, believing each step it takes absorbs the planet’s [[Pain]] and redistributes it evenly, ensuring [[Harmina]]’s survival.
Religious depictions often portray the Empress astride the Threxon in murals of conquest and restoration, symbolizing [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] over endurance itself — strength not as dominance, but as the sacred duty of carrying the world upon one’s back.
