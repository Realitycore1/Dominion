Definition
Cosmos Magic manipulates stellar, planetary, and cosmic-scale phenomena. Unlike localized elemental or alteration arts, Cosmos Magic is concerned with astronomical forces: gravity, tides, orbital harmonics, stellar birth and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], even the movement of verses themselves.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Scale of Resonance – the larger the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], the more stable (and harder to move). Cosmos magic requires harmonizing with vast forces.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Correspondence – small rituals can echo into large bodies (a tide-pool spell linked to an ocean, a bonfire linked to a star).
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Anchor & Observer – cosmic acts ripple across verses; without anchors, the caster may vanish into their own resonance.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Patience – cosmos spells unfold slowly, sometimes over centuries.

⸻

Techniques of Cosmos Magic
	•	Astromancy – guiding comets, shaping constellations, altering orbits.
	•	Gravity Weaving – increasing or nullifying planetary pull; crushing enemies or granting weightless sanctuaries.
	•	Tidal Enchantment – manipulating oceans, storms, and planetary cycles.
	•	Solar Invocation – channeling stellar [[Fire]], flares, or radiance.
	•	Lunar Mirroring – moonlight spells for growth, madness, or tide-bonding.
	•	[[Verse]] Manipulation – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-tier: shifting entire worlds between universes, rethreading cosmic harmonics.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: Giants’ slings echo cosmic motion — their stone-sling rituals are said to mimic planetary trajectories ￼. [[Elves]] once practiced star-omens, tying their wars to celestial alignments.
	•	Concordia: Cosmos magic ties into [[The Nine Rings]] of Symphonies, planetary-scale harmonics that can shift entire ecosystems. Adamant-tier mages are revered as “world-singers” capable of bending skies.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Cosmos Magic is industrialized in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Engines, Exoderms, and Horizon Arc Gates — technologies that harness cosmic forces at [[Verse]] scale. What others call “magic,” the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] calls [[Infrastructure]].
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Their harmonic pluralism treats [[Stars]] as [[Choirs]]; their Cosmos magic is sung rather than cast, aligning stellar clusters into resonant symphonies of peace.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Demiurgic Cosmos magic corrupts heavens themselves — black suns, inverted moons, and starfields that induce despair. Entire skies are rewritten to enslave mortals through fear.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Spectacle – manipulating skies inspires awe and control.
	•	Utility – shifting storms, tides, or climates sustains civilizations.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] Power – cosmic weapons dwarf any mundane arsenal.
	•	Transcendence – mortals practicing cosmos arts often blur into godhood.

⸻

Perils
	•	Scale Backlash – tampering with [[Stars]] or planets often backfires catastrophically.
	•	Blind Ripples – cosmic changes ripple into unseen places; a shifted tide here might flood a continent elsewhere.
	•	Isolation – cosmos mages often detach from [[Humanity]], perceiving only celestial patterns.
	•	Entropy Drag – meddling with stellar cycles accelerates decay of entire [[Systems]].

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Planetary Anchors – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] wards bind tectonic and orbital [[Systems]] against alteration.
	•	Resonant Disruption – [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]] harmonics can break hostile cosmic songs.
	•	Mythic Interference – strong myths (e.g. “the sun must rise”) resist cosmos tampering.
	•	Axiomatic Barriers – rewriting the root laws of gravity or light cancels cosmos spells.
