(“The fear of emptiness made animate”)
	•	Ontological Status: Non-causal entity
	•	Reality Affiliation: Boundary-state only
	•	Temporal Relation: Non-linear, but not timeless
	•	Mass/Energy: Undefined until observed
	•	Origin: Emergent, not evolutionary

They do not evolve.
They condense.

⸻

III. What They Are (Plainly)

Void Horrors are self-stabilizing contradictions.

They form when:
	•	Reality is stretched
	•	Causality weakens
	•	Axioms thin
	•	Observation fails to fully resolve a region

In short:
When the universe hesitates, something fills the pause.

That something is a Void Horror.

⸻

IV. Why They “Lurk”

They cannot fully enter reality.

Reality is:
	•	Too rigid
	•	Too rule-bound
	•	Too hostile to contradiction

Void Horrors instead:
	•	Press against weak points
	•	Nest near unstable physics
	•	Accumulate around forbidden technologies
	•	Gather near failed universes, dead gods, collapsed timelines

They are pressure phenomena, not predators in the usual sense.

⸻

V. Appearance (Why They Look Like That)

Void Horrors have no true form.

What observers see is:
	•	A projection
	•	A cross-section
	•	A failure of interpretation

Common features recur because minds reuse familiar structures:
	•	Limbs (because agency is assumed)
	•	Eyes (because intent is feared)
	•	Jaws (because consumption is intuitive)

None of these are strictly accurate.

Different observers will see different horrors—and all are wrong.

⸻

VI. Interaction with Reality

Void Horrors do not attack civilizations directly.

They:
	•	Corrupt physics
	•	Distort probability
	•	Induce existential dread
	•	Collapse local meaning structures

Effects include:
	•	Time behaving “softly”
	•	Causation reversing briefly
	•	Objects losing historical consistency
	•	Sentient minds experiencing false memories of events that never occurred

A Void Horror’s presence is often recognized after it leaves.

⸻

VII. Why They Are Not Gods

Gods, even false ones:
	•	Operate within a framework
	•	Respond to belief
	•	Possess internal identity

Void Horrors:
	•	Do not care about belief
	•	Do not respond to worship
	•	Do not even recognize observers as entities

They are pre-symbolic.

Religion forms after encounters, not before.

⸻

VIII. Dominion Doctrine on Void Horrors

Dominion classification is blunt:

“Void Horrors are not enemies.
They are failures of containment.”

Standard policy:
	•	Seal, do not fight
	•	Stabilize axioms
	•	Reinforce causality
	•	Collapse observation windows

Weapons are largely useless.
Consistency is lethal to them.
