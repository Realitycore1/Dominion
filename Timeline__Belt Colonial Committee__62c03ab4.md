I. Outpost Era (2250–2350) – The Drifter’s Frontier

Population: a few thousand, mostly corpo miners + contract labor.

[[Infrastructure]]: crude mining habs, orbital depots, skeleton crew outposts.

Politics: none; “airlock [[law]]” rules. Whoever controls life support is [[Judge]], jury, executioner.

[[culture]]: transient [[Workers]], loners, outcasts. First “Chainborn” families appear (multi-generation Belt-born).

II. Corporate [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] (2350–2450) – The Age of Company Barons

Population: tens of thousands → hundreds of thousands.

[[Infrastructure]]: permanent habitats on [[Ceres]], Vesta, Hygiea. Corporate-built truss stations.

Politics: corporate executives act as feudal lords. PMCs enforce quotas.

Smuggling begins.

[[culture]]: Chainfolk [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] begins to grow, forged in resentment of corpo abuses.

III. Guild & Syndicate Rising (2400–2550) – The Belt Awakens

Population: crosses into millions by late 2500s.

[[Infrastructure]]: larger modular stations, rotating habitats, guild-owned hydroponic farms.

Politics:

Guilds (Oxygen Brotherhood, [[Ironhands]], Graincoil) → provide solidarity.

Syndicates (pirate clans, smuggler families) → enforce shadow [[law]].

[[Corporations]] → begin decline, some merging with guilds.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: Belt no longer just suppliers → now refiners, food producers, shipbuilders.

[[culture]]: Belt slang, tattoos, chain-rituals. Chainfolk pride emerges: “We keep what we dig.”

IV. [[First Solar War]] (2500–2650) – The Crucible

Population: 5–10 million scattered.

[[Infrastructure]]: fortified habitats, guild fleets, smuggler black routes.

Politics:

Belt becomes a proxy battlefield between Inners ([[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]/Luna) and Outers (Jovians/Saturnians).

Guilds and Syndicates profit, but at terrible [[Human]] cost (stations annihilated, famines).

[[culture]]: hardened Chainfolk [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]. Songs and sagas about massacres, betrayals, airlock justice.

Legacy: Corps shattered, guilds + syndicates dominant.

V. Chainfolk Confederacy (2650–2800) – The Shadow Empire

Population: 12–15 million.

[[Infrastructure]]: city-sized habitats on [[Ceres]], Vesta, Pallas. Shipyards, smelters, relay stations.

Politics:

No true “state” → but syndicates, guilds, and families form a loose web of contracts (“Chain Accords”).

Chainwrights Assembly enforces oaths, acting as Belt’s only arbitration council.

Belt becomes indispensable → the broker of war supplies, smuggling routes, and labor.

[[culture]]: Children raised as Chainfolk first, [[Human]] second. Feasts, tattoos, chain-rituals codify [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].

VI. [[Second Solar War]] (2800–2880) – The Golden Age of Syndicates

Population: ~20 million.

[[Infrastructure]]: sprawling multi-rock federations, mobile habitats, secret fortresses.

Politics: Syndicates dominate, ruling habitats like mafia families. Guilds provide backbone industries. Families enforce dynastic loyalty.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: Belt acts as the black-market heart of the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] — feeding both sides of the war.

[[culture]]: Legends of “Black Fleets” (unmarked ships crewed by Chainfolk mercs). Resentment grows from Inners and Outers who view Belt as parasites, but they can’t survive without it.

VII. Toward Acheron (2880–3000) – The Belt’s Final Shape

Population: 25–30 million.

[[Infrastructure]]: mega-habitats rival Luna domes, entire smuggler fleets operate openly.

Politics:

Belt is not a nation, but a network of syndicates, guilds, and families.

Outclans rely on Belt for survival; Inners/Outers are forced into uneasy truces with them.

[[culture]]:

Mythologized as “The Chain” — each station a link, each life an oath.

Chainfolk [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] absolute: Belt-born now proudly call themselves a people apart.

Legacy: Belt becomes indispensable to [[Acheron Station]] and interstellar expansion → the broker class of the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].
