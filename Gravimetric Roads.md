## Summary

Curvature S
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Gravimetric Roads are engineered spacetime corridors, where [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] constructs manipulate gravitational fields into fixed “lanes.” Ships traveling inside them require little to no propulsion — the road itself pulls them along. They are both transport networks and imperial bindings, because once you’re on the road, your destiny is already shaped.
Structure

Gravitic Anchors: Arrays of [[Artificial]] mass nodes, black hole seeds, or [[Confluencia]] strange-matter tethers.

Resonance Harmonization: Roads hum with Concordia frequencies, stabilizing them against collapse.

Travel Dynamics

Ships slide with minimal energy, “falling forward” along the curve.

Entry/exit gates provide controlled slingshot acceleration or braking.

Roads can bend light and matter alike, creating corridors hidden from casual observation.

Variants

Planetary Roads: Connect [[Clarke Orbit Cities]], [[Chimney Towers]], and deep-sea hubs via surface-to-orbit gravitic lifts.

Interplanetary Roads: Link moons and inner worlds of a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

Interstellar Roads: Spanning light-years, often braided with [[Beamed-sail corridors]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide effortless commerce lanes, migration routes, and cultural highways.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Entire fleets can be hurled across [[Systems]] with almost no fuel cost; roads can also be weaponized (collapsing them into gravitic traps).

Economic: Anchor trade; entire [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] live or die by their proximity to the nearest road.

Religious: Revered as “the Paths of Amber” — pilgrims see traveling them as walking within the Empress’s will, literally falling along her hand.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Entry gates shaped like colossal buttresses, glowing with golden gravitic fields.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Curvature fields ripple in fractal waveforms, making geometry itself look alive.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Concordia hymns encoded into the gravity — travelers hear the road sing.

Narrative Function

To rebels: [[Chains]] — the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] doesn’t just control [[Stars]], it controls how you fall.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: The most elegant tool of all: invisible highways binding worlds together in geometry.

To myth: Called the “Amber Paths”, eternal routes through the cosmos laid down by the Empress’s will.
haping: Spacetime is “tilted” along the route, creating a [[Natural]] flow.

Suprematist Monolithism: Roads themselves are invisible — only felt as [[Stars]] bending, ships sliding, [[Time]] flowing differently.

From the ground, Gravimetric Roads sometimes appear as auroral scars in the sky, faint golden arcs marking where space is bent.

To citizens: Proof that travel is effortless — “gravity itself carries us.”
