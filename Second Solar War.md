A War of Moons and Worlds

⸻

I. Origins
	•	The [[First Solar War]] (3500–3650) left the corpos broken but not erased, while [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] hardened into distinct polities.
	•	By the late 38th century, the Inners ([[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]-led [[Colonial Union]] remnants + Luna + Mars + Mercury + Venus) began consolidating, fearing the growing power of Jupiter’s Moonclans and Saturn’s Ringclans.
	•	Trade disputes over ice shipments from the Belt and helium exports from Saturn spiraled into direct confrontation.
	•	The spark: a skirmish at [[Callisto]] Shipyards, where Jovian guild militias destroyed an Inner patrol flotilla, killing thousands.

⸻

II. Factions
	•	Inner Worlds (CU Remnants):
	•	[[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]], Luna (Cynthia Protectorate), Mars (now firmly Red Clan [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] but allied with Inners), Mercury (Sunborn), Venus (Cloudborn).
	•	Present themselves as “united civilization” against fragmentation.
	•	Control most advanced shipyards, still strong in numbers and tech.
	•	Outer Rebels:
	•	Jovian Confederacy: Fireborn of Io, Divers of Europa, Citizens of [[Ganymede]], Bastions of [[Callisto]]. Decentralized but fierce.
	•	Saturnian Alliance: [[Titan]] domes, Enceladus divers, Ring Syndicates. Vast hydrocarbon and ice wealth funds the war.
	•	Both declare independence, claiming the Inners exploit their labor and resources.
	•	Quiet Fringe:
	•	Uranus Iceclans & Neptune Deepclans: too far, too few — fade into the background, maintain watch on the Kuiper lanes but play little role.
	•	The Outclans (Pluto–Charon):
	•	Neutral, but vital — they keep the Belt Confederacy and Kuiper [[Watchtowers]] supplied.
	•	Act as brokers and smugglers, ensuring trade doesn’t collapse completely.
	•	Outclans’ neutrality lets them quietly prepare [[Acheron Station]] as the post-war staging ground.

⸻

III. The War Itself (Traditional Phase)

Unlike the [[First Solar War]], this one is fought with open fleets and armies:
	•	Fleet Battles:
	•	Inners vs. Jovian/Saturnian flotillas in the Belt.
	•	Famous engagements: the Battle of [[Ganymede]] Orbits, the Siege of [[Titan]], the Second [[Callisto]] Blockade.
	•	Ground Combat:
	•	Dome sieges on [[Ganymede]], [[Titan]], and Enceladus.
	•	Boarding actions on orbital stations.
	•	Brutal use of railguns, nuclear mines, and drone swarms.
	•	Proxy Havens:
	•	The Belt becomes a secondary front — habs divided between Inners and Outers.
	•	Cartels and Syndicates thrive, selling [[Arms]], smuggling organics, and backing both sides.

⸻

IV. Duration & Attrition (3800–3880)
	•	Nearly a century of war, marked by cycles of escalation and fragile truces.
	•	The Inners are technologically superior but overstretched.
	•	Jupiter and Saturn can’t match numbers but fight like insurgent federations, using moons as fortresses.
	•	By the 3870s, both sides are exhausted.

⸻

V. Resolution & [[Aftermath]]
	•	Neither side achieves decisive victory.
	•	A fragile peace is negotiated at [[Acheron Station]], which emerges as the neutral ground.
	•	Pluto–Charon, by keeping the Belt and [[Watchtowers]] afloat, become the unexpected kingmakers of the war’s end.
	•	The Second Solar War leaves scars:
	•	Moons devastated.
	•	Rings stripped for resources.
	•	Entire generations lost to fighting.
	•	But it also cements the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] as a multipolar order: Inners, Outers, Belt/Kuiper, each too strong to dominate, too weak to erase.
