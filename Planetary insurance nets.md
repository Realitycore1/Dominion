[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Planetary Insurance Nets are mega-engineered orbital webs suspended around planets or moons, designed to absorb, redirect, or neutralize incoming hazards — [[Natural]] or hostile. They are both defensive [[Infrastructure]] and religious spectacle, protecting citizens while reminding them that their skies belong to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]].
Structure

Orbital Web: A lattice of strange-matter tethers, kilometers thick, spanning equatorial or full planetary coverage.

Anchor Spires: [[Atlas Pillars]] connect net segments to the surface, doubling as launch towers.

Shield Swarms: Whipple-shield drones patrol the net, filling in gaps.

Energy Curtains: Plasma/field [[Layers]] woven into the net for beam interception.

Functions

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Defense: Absorbs kinetic bombardments, relativistic slugs, or directed-energy attacks.

Climate Control: Nets regulate solar input, doubling as planetary “weather tuning” [[Systems]].

Symbolism: Visible from the ground as glowing arcs — a constant reminder of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] oversight.

Variants

Equatorial Nets: Partial defense rings, protecting [[Key]] population zones.

Global Nets: Fully wrap the world, creating a planetary halo.

Dynamic Nets: Movable, reconfigurable webs that thicken in areas of greatest threat.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Economic: Nets protect trade hubs, planetary docks, and agriculture zones from catastrophic loss.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Veils” — citizens see them as the Empress’s hair, flowing across the heavens to shield her children.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: [[Atlas Pillars]] rise from continents into the sky, ribbing the net like cathedral vaults.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, nets look like dark lace around the planet, perfect in symmetry.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Nets ripple with fractal reconfigurations, like living geometry.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: At night, the nets glow faintly with glyph-light — scripture visible in the skies above every city.

From the ground, the Insurance Net appears as a permanent aurora lattice — a glowing crown stretching across the firmament.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Comfort — they live under the safest skies in the universe.

To rebels: Dread — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] can deny them the net at will, stripping away heaven’s shield.

To myth: Called the “Veil of Amber”, said to be the Empress’s protective mantle over her children.
ction: Stops meteors, comets, and debris.

Civil: Insurance nets reassure citizens their planet is invulnerable — no rock, no warhead can touch them.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Strategic necessity — rebellious worlds are stripped of their nets, rendering them vulnerable.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Symbol and tool — nets are the literal guarantee of control, “insurance” written across the sky.
