Large spatial [[Regions]] where electromagnetic signals arrive systematically fainter than expected after standard distance/expansion corrections. In-world, these map to patches of raised impedance (Ζₑ), lowered coherence length (ℓᶜ), and/or ordinary astrophysical opacity.

Why they happen (drivers)

Standard [[Physics]]
	•	Geometric dimming (Tolman): surface-brightness ∝ (1+z)⁻⁴; baseline everywhere.
	•	Intervening opacity: dust in galaxies/filaments; neutral/partly neutral IGM (Lyα forest / Gunn–Peterson trough at high-z).
	•	Diffuse backgrounds: high-energy γ rays absorbed by the extragalactic background light (EBL); X-rays by warm–hot IGM.
	•	Weak-lensing demagnification: sightlines through voids slightly de-lens sources (opposite of strong magnification).
	•	Plasma scattering: free electrons (n_e) add small-angle scatter → SB loss and polarization tweaks.

Exotic/setting hooks
	•	Photon–[[Axion]](-like) conversion in large-scale B-fields → grey, frequency-dependent dimming without dust colors.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]] “weather”: local rises in Ζₑ and drops in ℓᶜ dissipate coherent EM; shows as broadband faintness + jitter.
	•	[[Verse]] interface fatigue / DE fringe: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] turbulence adds excess noise (η↑) and intermittent flux loss.

How they look (signatures)
	•	SB & color: fainter surface brightness at fixed size; dust leaves reddening, [[Axion]]/Ζₑ dimming is greyer.
	•	Spectral breaks: Lyman-series absorption at high-z; EBL cuts TeV spectra; soft [[X-Ray]] edges from WHIM.
	•	Anisotropy maps: coherent patches on degree–tens-of-degrees scales aligned with voids/filaments.
	•	Polarization: increased depolarization/rotation if plasma scattering dominates.

Disentangling causes (quick tests)
	•	Color [[law]]: dust ≈ wavelength-dependent; EBL hits γ-rays; [[Axion]]/Ζₑ ≈ grey over optical/NIR.
	•	Environment: demagnification lines up with void catalogs; IGM opacity with high-z.
	•	[[Time]] behavior: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]/DE-linked zones vary with η (“weather”); dust/EBL are stable on [[Human]] times.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] ops (do/don’t)
	•	Corridors & gates: avoid seating Horizon-Arc windows through strong demagnification/Ζₑ ridges; require a Window Proof Sheet showing Φ/ℓᶜ within bounds.
	•	PFN/comm: route around dimming zones; if unavoidable, raise power, add error-correcting codes, and log PFN hashes.
	•	Ports: post light-hygiene advisories; calibrate instrument zero-points nightly; keep [[Radiator Neutrality]]—Σ spikes can masquerade as dimming.
	•	Survey policy: maintain [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]-weather maps (η, Ζₑ); cross-match with void/EBL/WHIM [[Layers]].

KPIs

Mean flux deficit vs model • Color excess E(B–V) or grey-dimming index • Polarization change • Void-overlap fraction • η/Ζₑ variance • Corridor incident rate crossing the zone.
