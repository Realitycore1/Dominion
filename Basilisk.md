1. Common Basilisk
	•	Definition: The baseline—serpentine or lizardlike beast with a petrifying or lethal gaze.
	•	Traits: Snake or reptile body, venomous bite, gaze of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]/stone.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as inevitability; corruption in sight alone.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Serpentine Basilisks
	•	Definition: Basilisks resembling massive snakes.
	•	Traits: Coiling bodies, hypnotic gaze, constriction.
	•	Symbolism: The serpent as absolute predator.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Lizard Basilisks
	•	Definition: Reptilian, dragonlike basilisks with legs and claws.
	•	Traits: Crested heads, scaled armor, gaze that petrifies.
	•	Symbolism: The blending of serpent and [[Dragon]] [[Archetypes]].

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Cockatrice]] (Avian Basilisk)
	•	Definition: A hybrid basilisk with birdlike traits (rooster or [[Dragon]]-bird mix).
	•	Traits: Feathers, beak, wings; gaze or breath that petrifies.
	•	Symbolism: Folklore’s absurd horror; the unexpected predator.

⸻

5. Swamp Basilisks
	•	Definition: Basilisks adapted to marshes and wetlands.
	•	Traits: Camouflaged scales, toxic breath, lurking ambush predators.
	•	Symbolism: The unseen [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in stagnant places.

⸻

6. Desert Basilisks
	•	Definition: Heat-dwelling, dune-stalking basilisks.
	•	Traits: Sandy hides, venomous spit, mirage-like gaze.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in the wasteland; predator in the endless sun.

⸻

7. Abyssal Basilisks
	•	Definition: Corrupted, [[Abyss]]-born basilisks.
	•	Traits: Black scales, necrotic gaze, aura of despair.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as spiritual corruption; [[Abyss]] staring back.

⸻

8. Elemental Basilisks
	•	Definition: Basilisks infused with elemental forces.
	•	Variants: Frostgaze, Storm-Eyes, Emberfang.
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s primal fury carried in a killing gaze.

⸻

9. Colossal Basilisks
	•	Definition: Massive, [[Dragon]]-sized basilisks.
	•	Traits: City-crushing frames, mountain-splitting gaze, worshiped as gods.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as apocalypse; kingship of monsters.

⸻

10. Arch-Basilisks (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary basilisks tied to cosmic forces of entropy and petrification.
	•	Traits: Eyes that turn rivers to stone, forests to dust, armies to statues.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as universal [[law]]; the ultimate predator of creation.
