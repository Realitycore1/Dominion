Core Bias:

Collective survival under scarcity

This is where Desert behavior merges with Hive logic.

Meaning:

Scarcity does not eliminate coordination — it selects for highly efficient collective endurance.

Traits:
	•	eusocial survival structures
	•	hyper-efficient distribution
	•	low-waste civilizations
	•	communal adaptation
	•	distributed hardship management

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	anthills in a dead world
	•	collective life surviving where individuals would fail
	•	thin but incredibly resilient societies

Common Expressions:
	•	hive-cities in wastelands
	•	scarcity collectivism
	•	hyper-coordinated survival civilizations
	•	distributed desert intelligence

Failure Mode:

Resource authoritarianism
The collective becomes too rigid and survivalist to evolve.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	machine survival cultures
	•	desert insectoid or synthetic civilizations
	•	highly disciplined attritional societies
