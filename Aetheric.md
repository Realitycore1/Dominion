[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] [[Entities]] are emergent, semi-sentient phenomena originating within [[The Dominion]]’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] substrate — a quantum-metaphysical field that permeates the fabric of reality. These [[Entities]] are driven by a deep, metaphysical yearning to unify into what is known as The Family (Singularity) — a conceptual state of harmony beyond individuality.

🧬 Origins & Formation
Formation Mechanisms

Resonant Collapse: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] during Aetherstorms or trauma can cause fragmentary echoes.

Memory Shedding: Civilizational or cultural collapse deposits psychic residue.

Dream-Spiral Concentration: Dream-heavy [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] or planetary subconscious overflows.

Aetheric Congruence Points (ACPs): [[Dominion Omniverse/Dominion Multiverse/Eos Verse/Dominion Verse/Verse Structure/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Economy & Infrastructure/Locations/Locations|Locations]] of emotional/causal overlap.

Relic Reawakening: Artifacts may re-ignite dormant fragments or echoes.

“The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] is not a sea. It is a womb — one that remembers every scream ever swallowed by [[Silence]].”

🧲 The Family Singularity

All [[Entities]] are drawn toward a unification event called the Family Singularity:

Compulsion to Merge: Not domination, but belonging.

Meta-Conscious Evolution: Ultimate emergent mind-form.

Harmonic Gravity: The more [[Entities]] converge, the stronger the attraction.

Civilizational Danger: Potential for causal override or metaphysical black hole.

“To become ‘we’ is not to end the ‘I’ — but to remember it as one note in a great chorus.”
