## Summary

Parametric 
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Chimney Towers are planetary-scale vertical [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] that extend from the surface into the upper atmosphere (sometimes brushing the exosphere), designed to regulate airflows, generate power, and act as conduits for atmospheric or orbital exchange.
They are both climate machines and vertical cities.
Structure & Scale

Height: tens to hundreds of kilometers, piercing atmospheric [[Layers]].

Base: fused into planetary crust, buttressed by Atlas Pillar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Military/Technology|technology]].

Materials: smart composites, strange-matter struts, Concordia lattice sheathing.

Function

Climate Regulation: Channel warm or polluted air upward, drawing clean air downward. Terraforming tools for hostile worlds.

Energy Generation: Use temperature differentials to drive colossal turbines (“thermo-towers”).

Transport: Act as vertical space elevators for cargo and people.

Defense: Can vent Concordia plasma or storm-lances downward as weapons, or beam power upward to orbital grids.

Active Ecology

Skimmers ride currents in and around them.

MRO swarms crawl their exteriors like mechanical ants, constantly maintaining the shell.

Some towers host entire cities along their interior and exterior ribs — spirals of balconies, terraces, and platforms.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide breathable air, energy, and weather control to new colony worlds. A single Chimney Tower can make an uninhabitable planet borderline Earthlike within a century.

Economic: Anchor planetary trade — the tops function as orbital docks, the bases as ground ports.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Towers double as fortress-[[Citadels]], each one able to defend entire [[Regions]] with their storm-projectors.

Religious: Regarded as “world-spines” — cathedrals of air, binding heaven and [[Earth (Terra)]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Sheer vertical ribs rising like cathedral buttresses, spiked with tower-spires that pierce cloud [[Layers]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, they appear as simple obsidian shafts stabbing upward from the planet’s skin.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Etched with endless scripts and prayers that climb their length, visible for kilometers.

Narrative Function
Cyber-Constructivism: Their interiors are spirals of habitable [[Layers]], like vertical cities inside the tower walls.

Seen from the ground, a Chimney Tower is a horizon-breaking pillar, clouds spiraling around it as though the tower itself rewrites the weather.

To civilians: Chimney Towers are life-givers, making unbreathable worlds survivable.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] authority: they are anchors of control — whoever owns the towers controls the climate, the ports, and the people.

To enemies: they are siege nightmares — tall enough to be visible from orbit, armed enough to burn whole [[Regions]].

To myth: they are “the spines of the Empress” — proof that she does not just rule over land or sky, but the very breath of worlds.
