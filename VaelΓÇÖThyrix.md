Classification: Archon Class – Tier XIV / Field Designate “Vael’Thyrix” (The Voidborne [[Dragon]])

Observation:
Subject observed within the upper exosphere of [[Harmina]] during the Auric Night Cycle, approximately 80–120 kilometers above surface level. Wingspan estimated at 200 meters; total body length between 150–170 meters. Exoskeleton exhibits a semi-translucent carbon-ceramite weave embedded with micro-[[Aetheric]] veins that pulse with internal light during flight. Membranous wings double as energy collectors, harvesting radiation and cosmic particle flow. Capable of both atmospheric reentry and low-orbit maneuvering, the Vael’Thyrix is adapted to the vacuum through bio-sealed dermal plating and internal pressure-regulation organs that act as living void suits.

Contextual Analysis:
The Vael’Thyrix functions as [[Harmina]]’s aerial regulator — an apex predator of both sky and void. [[Reports]] from the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] orbital bastion Crown of Ecliptica record this entity’s flight path intersecting meteor trajectories, where it demonstrates predatory behavior toward inbound debris, disintegrating them with bursts of plasma-breath resembling directed energy discharge. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]]’ respiration [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] operates through quantum condensation, converting cosmic radiation into thrust and weaponry simultaneously. Its tail spines act as gravitational rudders, manipulating microfields for navigation within thin or nonexistent atmosphere.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
Within the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of the Upper Heavens, the Vael’Thyrix is sanctified as the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s celestial warden — guardian of thresholds between heaven and matter. The [[Infernariate]] describe it as the “Wing of Judgment,” the first and last [[Sentinel]] to move when worlds are threatened. Its [[(2030) Existence]] blurs the line between biological and divine mechanism; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] theologians teach that the Empress herself inscribed the pattern of its wings into the stratostructure of [[Harmina]]’s skies during the world’s sanctification.
To citizens and [[Soldiers]] alike, the sight of Vael’Thyrix descending from the [[Stars]] is interpreted as omen of both cleansing and renewal — a harbinger of divine reclamation when balance tilts toward chaos.
