## Summary

To citizens
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Partial Dyson Ring Swarms are incomplete orbital arrays of satellites, habitats, and collectors arranged in arcs around a star. Unlike complete Dyson Swarms, these structures emphasize directional energy harvesting and projection rather than total capture, often built for regional use, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] focus, or symbolic purposes.
Structure

Arc Arrays: Semi-circular or crescent-shaped arcs spanning fractions of an AU, resembling a crown or halo.

Collector Nodes: Solar collectors, fusion [[Relays]], and beam transmitters concentrated along arcs.

Habitat [[Chains]]: Bishop Rings, McKendree Cylinders, or O’Neill Cylinders integrated into the swarm.

Control Nodes: [[Computronium]] banks for synchronization, beam focusing, and orbital balancing.

Functions

Energy Collection: Capture a portion of stellar output for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] use.

Beam Projection: Focus energy into directed beams (for power transfer or weaponization).

Habitation: Populate the arc with trillions across linked habitats.

Symbolism: Serve as visible star-crowns, proof of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] presence without the expense of full shells.

Variants

Civil Swarms: Partial crowns built primarily for energy harvesting and habitats.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Swarms: Arcs focused to power Nicoll–Dyson Beams, solar lances, or defense nets.

Religious Swarms: Arcs deliberately shaped into glyphs, visible from light-years away as cosmic scripture.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide power and living space at reduced resource cost, a scalable step toward Dyson Fortresses.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Apex energy platforms, used to create focused [[Fire]] or project “star cannons” across space.

Economic: Anchor Beam Corridors and [[Beamed-sail commerce lanes]].

Religious: Known as “Amber Halos” — symbolic arcs of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], said to be the first step of crowning a star.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Each collector node shaped like a spire, ribbed and vault-like, glowing with solar [[Fire]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From a distance, a partial swarm looks like a black crescent across the star, stark and immense.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Node positions ripple in fractal spirals, like living geometry across orbital space.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The swarm glows with glyphic patterns — beams themselves become visible scripture.

From afar, a Partial Dyson Ring Swarm looks like a star with a burning crown, the arc of civilization stretched like a halo of [[Fire]] across its light.

Narrative Function

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A deliberate strategy — flexible, affordable, symbolic, and weaponizable.

To myth: Called the “Amber Halos”, seen as the first jewels the Empress places upon a sun before full enthronement.
: A beacon of progress — their star is being crowned piece by piece.

To rebels: A reminder of inevitability — the crown is incomplete, but its shadow already rules them.
