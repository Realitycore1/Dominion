## Summary

The Domain is a wholly human-origin bio-organic civilization descended from [[Domain Enterprises]] and its deliberate manipulation of Wetwire.

It begins as a corporate medical and continuity project, becomes a distributed biological network known as [[The Collective]], produces Prime command minds, and matures into a civilization in which people, organisms, infrastructure, worlds, and fleets can function as organs of a greater living order.

The Domain is neither a simple infection nor one universal mind. It is:

- a civilization of linked biological sovereignties;
- a living civilizational organism;
- a technological lineage;
- a political response to mortality;
- and an alternative continuity doctrine.

Core thesis:

> The Domain is humanity's attempt to make survival compulsory.

Dominion contrast:

> The Dominion preserves continuity by defending the person.
> The Domain preserves continuity by making the person impossible to lose.

Core active concepts:

- [[Domain Enterprises]]: human medical-industrial corporation and continuity cabal.
- Wetwire: human-origin survival mechanism industrialized into biological control substrate.
- [[The Collective]]: early linked Domain population before stable Prime authority.
- [[The Primes]]: major biological/cognitive sovereignties.
- [[The Supreme]]: temporary apex state when ID consciously acts through the network.
- [[ID]]: basal intelligence beneath the Domain.
- [[Morphic Covenant]]: post-Severance framework separating voluntary Domain lineages from coercive assimilation.
- [[Shaki Moha]]: Socratic Prime of contradiction, cognition, dreamscape, and philosophical assimilation.
- [[Prime Kharon Emerges|Kharon]]: Strategic Prime of pressure, battlefield logic, and engineered survival.
- [[Prime Lunaris]]: lunar memory Prime of subtle conversion, recursion, and melancholy strategy.
- [[Jovaryn]]: Jupiter undergoing neural assimilation.
- [[Domain Supernal]]: mobile planetary bioship and gene-factory.
