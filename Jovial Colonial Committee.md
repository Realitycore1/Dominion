# Jovian Colonial Committee

The Jovian Colonial Committee coordinates the first permanent settlements around and within Jupiter during the outer-foothold era of 3180–3250. The archive folder retains the older “Jovial” filename for link compatibility; “Jovian” is the canonical adjective.

Its responsibilities include radiation shelter, convoy schedules, medical authority, moon access, atmospheric-city safety, fuel extraction, and ecological protection.

The Committee never becomes an uncontested unitary government. Callisto, Ganymede, Europa, the atmospheric cities, and other settlements face different hazards and reject the assumption that one capital can speak for the entire system.

Jovian experience produces the Harmonium local-necessity principle: agreements must identify who may act before remote authority answers. It also produces moving biological corridors for native atmospheric life and strict non-extraction law around Europa’s ocean.

In 3425, Jovian authorities join the Outer Systems Compact while individual moons retain their own representation. For the complete development through the Second Solar War and *Deliverance*, see [[Jovian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
