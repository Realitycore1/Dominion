Classification

Death Herald / Psychopomp Enforcer / Fate-Bound Revenant

⸻

CORE IDEA

The Dullahan is death that has already signed the paperwork.

It does not hunt. It does not curse. It does not choose.
When a Dullahan rides, the decision has been finalized somewhere deeper than chance. What remains is delivery.

It doesn’t chase victims.
It arrives where they already are.

⸻

ORIGIN

Dullahans manifest where death becomes procedural:
	•	ancient compacts between mortality and inevitability
	•	executioners elevated into psychopomp roles
	•	noble warriors bound to carry death rather than suffer it
	•	roads that have witnessed too many final journeys

Some were once people.
Others were assembled by fate itself.

They are not raised.
They are appointed.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

The Dullahan is unmistakable and wrong in precise ways.

Common traits:
	•	Headless humanoid rider clad in dark, weathered armor or cloak
	•	A massive black horse with burning eyes and iron-shod hooves
	•	The Dullahan carries its severed head—often under one arm
	•	Head’s eyes glow like corpse-fire; mouth smiles or recites names
	•	A whip made of a human spine or sinew
	•	Lanterns fashioned from skulls or cold flame

Where the head looks, someone dies.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Dullahans are silent, exact, and unstoppable.
	•	Ride predetermined routes
	•	Stop without warning
	•	Speak a name once—never twice
	•	Kill instantly and cleanly

They do not savor the act.
They complete the task.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High, but narrowly scoped.
	•	Perfect knowledge of their assigned deaths
	•	No curiosity outside duty
	•	Cannot be bribed, persuaded, or delayed
	•	Retain memories of life without attachment

They are not cruel.
They are final.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Name-Sovereignty: speaking a name marks death immediately
	•	Grace-Independent: angels do not command them
	•	Malice-Neutral: corruption cannot redirect them
	•	Road Authority: paths bend subtly to accommodate passage
	•	Beheading Clause: decapitation is symbolic, not functional

Gold is one of the few things that can repel—but never destroy—a Dullahan.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Appear singly
	•	Follow ancient roads, bridges, thresholds, and borders
	•	Leave no physical trace after passing
	•	Animals flee in silence

A road a Dullahan favors becomes historically lethal.

⸻

ALPHA DULLAHANS

There are no Alphas—
but there are Processions.

When multiple Dullahans ride together:
	•	a massacre, plague, or war is imminent
	•	entire regions are marked
	•	names are spoken in sequence

A Procession means history is about to fold.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Absolute (Targeted) / Non-combatant otherwise

You cannot fight a Dullahan.
If it is for you, you are already dead.
If it is not, you are irrelevant.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Dullahans represent:
	•	inevitability without malice
	•	death as administration
	•	fate without drama
	•	the terror of certainty

Where Death Omens warn,
where Death Echoes replay,
the Dullahan executes.
