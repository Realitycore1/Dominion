## History
- **Apex Civilization:** Dominated the Myrmidon Galaxy until assaulted by the Hiren Coalition and an unknown [[Human]] faction.  
- **Fall:** Presumed extinct after their last battle—fought to the last drone.  
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Intervention:** Hidden [[Queens]] and [[Workers]] were secured, bred back, and reforged as auxiliaries.  
- **Reemergence:** Now serve as loyal [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] shock troops, their loyalty purchased by survival itself.
