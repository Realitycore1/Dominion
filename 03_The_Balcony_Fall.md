# Chapter 3 – The Balcony Fall
The garden balcony lay quiet, its night-blooming flowers glowing pale beneath the cylinder’s inner [[Stars]]. Amber pressed trembling hands against the balustrade, dragging air into her lungs. Her shame burned hotter than her father’s gaze — she wanted to rip the jeweled gown from her body, shed the Zohair name, vanish into [[Silence]].

Footsteps approached. She turned, startled.

“Running already?” The voice was Axel’s, smug and heavy. His shadow filled the archway. “A poor look for a future [[Queen]].”

Amber backed against the railing. “Leave me.”

“Or what? You’ll pout? You’ll sulk until someone notices?” His laugh was low, cruel. “You should be grateful. I could have had Evelynn. Instead, I will take you.”

Amber’s throat closed. She clutched the rail — then felt the world pitch.

A shove. Sudden. Hard. The marble edge vanished beneath her hands. She fell.

The impact tore the breath from her lungs, darkness crowding her vision. Somewhere above, the music still played. The court still danced.

Then — a face, not Axel’s. Not Evelynn’s. Not her father’s.

A boy — no, a [[Man]], though only just. His clothes were plain, his hands calloused. His eyes, steady.

“Stay with me,” he whispered. His voice carried no mockery, no command. Only concern.

Amber’s lips parted, but no words came. She had never been held this way before, cradled not as a daughter of kings but as a fragile, wounded girl.

“What’s your name?” she managed, faintly.

“Demetrius,” he said. “Orfano.”

Her thoughts blurred, her heart clinging desperately to that one kindness. She reached for his hand as the darkness pulled her under.
