# Environmental Protection Agency

## Summary

The Environmental Protection Agency protects biospheres, restored ecologies, inhabited atmospheres, water systems, and hazardous zones from industrial, military, and settlement damage.

It is often unpopular because it can tell powerful people that survival does not justify poisoning the ground beneath everyone else.

## Mandate

The Agency oversees:

- pollution control;
- water and air standards;
- ecological surveys;
- hazardous-material rules;
- wildlife and habitat protections;
- restoration-zone monitoring;
- industrial impact review;
- settlement exclusion zones;
- public environmental data.

## Powers

It may halt dangerous projects, fine polluters, publish hazard notices, quarantine contaminated terrain, and require remediation.

## Limits

It cannot freeze all development indefinitely, hide environmental data for political convenience, or treat frontier communities as acceptable sacrifice zones.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Agency through warning signs, water reports, settlement bans, industrial lawsuits, protected wildlands, and the smell of a river that either recovered or did not.

## Story Use

Use this agency to make geography politically active. Land, water, weather, and ecology should shape policy instead of serving as background scenery.
