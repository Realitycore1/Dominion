## Summary

Civil Topop
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Topopolis is a gigantic tubular habitat, kilometers [[Wide]] and millions of kilometers long, bent into loops or coils to remain gravitationally stable. It spins along its axis, generating [[Artificial]] gravity on its inner surface. In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] use, it is both colony-ribbon and cathedral-script, a literal scroll of civilization written across space.
Structure

Tube Spine: A hollow cylinder with an inner livable surface.

Rotational Gravity: Tube spins to generate centrifugal force for life inside.

Bending Loops: Tube coils around [[Stars]], planets, or orbits through void like a looped ring.

Support [[Systems]]: Solar collectors, thermal [[Layers]], and Whipple-shield nets protect the tube.

Habitability

Interior Worlds: Vast landscapes inside the tube — mountains, rivers, seas, forests — all curving overhead.

Lengthwise [[Cultures]]: Civilizations distributed along millions of km, separated like nations on a cosmic highway.

Climatic Bands: [[Artificial]] day/night cycles projected across tube walls.

Variants

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Act as mobile fortress-worlds, slowly coiling through [[Systems]] like colossal serpents.

Economic: Serve as interstellar trade spines — each loop a staging hub.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Tube ribs shaped like cathedral arches, spanning across the [[Stars]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Habitat lights ripple down the length of the tube like fractal calligraphy.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The exterior hull etched with glyphs, so the tube reads as scripture across parsecs.

Inside, the ground curves upward into the sky — a valley without end, an entire world wrapped into a ribbon. Outside, it looks like a cosmic scroll unrolled through heaven, black steel glowing with scripture.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Wonder — a world without horizon, only endless road and sky.

To rebels: A prison without escape — the scroll coils forever, every loop watched.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Both colonization tool and holy icon — order stretched across infinity.

To myth: Called the “Amber Scrolls”, said to be the Empress’s writings, each loop a stanza of her hymn.
