I. Physical Definition

The Singulon is a [[VFF]]-specific extreme-curvature boson that governs the formation, stability, and interaction properties of singularities — including black holes, [[Artificial]] micro-singularities, and certain exotic spacetime folds.

While no direct real-world analog exists, the Singulon shares traits with a high-mass [[Graviton]] condensate capable of locally amplifying curvature to near-infinite density. Within the [[Verse]] Field Framework, Singulons are bound excitations of the ΩS Horizon Lattice, a sub-layer of the [[VFF]] that governs causal [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] geometry.
	•	Spin: [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] (tensor boson) in standard gravitational Modes; can manifest fractional spin in engineered states.
	•	Mass: Extremely high in bound states; near-zero in free roaming curvature pre-cursors.
	•	Charge: Neutral in EM terms; carries Curvature Index (CI) defining its singularity-generation potential.
	•	Interaction: Strong coupling to gravitons, graviphotons, and Nullitons; weak coupling to baryonic matter except via curvature effects.
