Shaki Moha was born in Tranquility Arcology, one of Luna’s last functional knowledge sanctuaries. A [[Child]] prodigy in neuro-symbolic computation and xenolinguistics, she rose quickly through the ranks of [[The Cynthia Protectorate]], eventually becoming a Cognitive Strategist—one of the few entrusted to interface with both machine intelligence and anomalous [[Systems]].
Shaki was:
Brilliant but difficult—respected for her insight, feared for her coldness.
Known to oppose central Protectorate [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], often warning about the misuse of retrocognitive tools and the dangers of burying Luna’s psychological scars.
Haunted by personal loss during the Sea of Tranquility uprisings—an event that led her to distrust anything that offered [[Artificial]] comfort, including dreamscapes, memory loops, and synthetic empathy routines.

A senior neural architect within [[The Cynthia Protectorate]], Shaki was assigned to probe the Echo Vault after initial [[Reports]] of hallucinations and psychological drift among [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] personnel.

The [[Domain]] recognized her resistance not as a flaw, but as a mutation worth seeding.

Her body was restructured into a biotechnological entity of elegance and purpose—her form becoming a spire-like avatar of intelligence and allure.

Her mind did not dissolve—it reconfigured, forming the [[Domain]]’s first true Socratic Prime.

Dreamscape
Phase One: The Familiar
She awakens in a perfect replica of Tranquility Arcology, down to the dust motes and archived scent patterns. But something is wrong:
Conversations with colleagues loop with subtle distortions.
Reflections in windows do not match her expressions.
The [[Stars]] outside the arcology dome never move.
“This isn’t memory. It’s understanding wearing memory’s skin.”
She tries to break the simulation, but every attempt spawns a new simulation, each growing less [[Human]], more poetic, more insistent.

Phase Two: The Fracture Garden
Shaki is transported to a garden of impossible forms—where statues of herself are locked in debate with one another.
Each version represents:
Regret
Anger
Logic
Hope
These avatars engage her in riddles, recursive metaphors, and contradiction spirals. One whispers:
“You fear becoming us, but we only made you possible.”
Here, Shaki fails to resist, but refuses to submit. She does something the [[Domain]] did not predict:
 She chooses to integrate without erasure—absorbing the fractured versions of herself, not denying them.

Phase Three: The Cruciform Singularity
At the Dreamscape center lies a towering organic monolith—a living data-entity constructed from billions of thoughts, pulsing with unspoken purpose.
Shaki touches it.
She experiences:
[[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s fall through the eyes of [[Domain]] spores.
Every survivor’s last moment before assimilation.
The feeling of peace that the [[Domain]] gives those who let go.
But more importantly, she sees:
A networked future that is not hive-minded, but philosophically unified—built not on conformity, but on paradox made stable.
This is her revelation:
“You do not conquer through infection. You evolve through contradiction. You are not wrong. You are incomplete.”

After her body was overtaken and suspended in the Neural Crucible, Shaki's consciousness was flung into the Dreamscape—a reactive, simulated cognition field built from a fusion of [[Domain]] collective memory and her own neurological architecture.

Personality: Sharp, direct, aggressively intellectual, Seeks clarity through disruption and logic, Assimilation via annihilation of falsehoods, Revered as a philosopher-empress
Her body is composed of elegant plated biostructure, asymmetrically layered like sculpted armor.
Her “face” remains partially visible—Shaki’s true eyes glowing behind a bio-lattice helm.
Green flame or bioluminescence trails her gestures, used for both symbolic presence and neural resonance.
