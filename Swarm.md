Core Bias:

Collective engineered continuity

This is where Synthetic merges with Hive logic.

Meaning:

The basin is not merely built — it is collectively inhabited, maintained, optimized, and extended by distributed system intelligence.

Traits:
	•	swarm maintenance systems
	•	distributed machine or bio-machine labor
	•	collective architecture
	•	coordinated system upkeep
	•	civilization as self-repairing organism

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the machine is alive because the many sustain it
	•	every node exists to maintain continuity
	•	infrastructure is population, and population is infrastructure

Common Expressions:
	•	maintenance swarms
	•	machine hives
	•	self-repairing city ecologies
	•	distributed synthetic empires
	•	habitat-superorganism basins

Failure Mode:

Optimization lock
The collective becomes too system-oriented to generate novelty or transcend its own architecture.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	machine civilizations
	•	posthuman maintenance ecologies
	•	self-repairing shell worlds
	•	ultra-coordinated synthetic societies
