# (3005) Terran Federation

## Canonical Clarification

The Terran Federation was established in 2601. The 3005 marker records the Federation at the beginning of permanent Lunar settlement, not a second founding.

The Federation authorizes the Lunar Colony Charter under a constitution written for one inhabited world. Its legislature represents Earth's Great Cities and regional populations. Orbital communities and Lunar workers possess federal citizenship as individuals but no representation as off-world polities.

This distinction becomes untenable once the charter permits permanent residence, inheritance, burial, and elected local government beyond Earth.

Gilgamesh opens in 3007 as the Federation's orbital seat. The Lunar Colonial Committee's demands for appropriations, transport authority, and representation expose the limits of a planetary federation administering a multi-world society.

In 3015, the Colonial Articles reform the Terran Federation into the Colonial Union. The change preserves constitutional and institutional continuity while ending Earth's status as the Union's sole political center.
