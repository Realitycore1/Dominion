## Details

A Hoopworld is a type of planetary
habitable structure that is designed to provide a sustainable and habitable environment for [[Humans]] in space. It consists of a large, spherical habitat with an internal environment that includes an atmosphere, gravity, and other environmental features that mimic those found on [[Earth (Terra)]]. The interior of a Hoopworld is divided into multiple [[Layers]] or "zones" that simulate the different climates and environments found on [[Earth (Terra)]]. These zones could include forests, deserts, mountains, oceans, and other habitats. The different zones could be connected by elevators, transport [[Systems]], and other means of movement, allowing for the free movement of people and resources between them. In addition to providing a livable environment, Hoopworlds could also be used for various scientific and research purposes. They could be used for experiments in space exploration, planetary science, or environmental studies. They could also serve as a means of protecting valuable [[Natural]] resources from the effects of climate change or other environmental disasters on [[Earth (Terra)]]. While still in the conceptual stage, the design of Hoopworlds is inspired by the idea of creating a fully self-sustaining and sustainable space habitat that can support [[Human]] life over long periods of [[Time]].
