## Summary
Habitats and Living Environments

* Planetary‑scale and orbital habitats: Ecumenopolis, Shield World, Globus Cassus, Ringworlds, Banks Orbitals, Rungworld, Shellworlds, Disc Worlds, Birch Worlds, Topopolis, Shellworld Ringworld, Orbital Shell, Orbital Plate, O’Neill Cylinder, Stanford Torus, Bishop Ring, McKendree Cylinder, Kaplan Turbine Colonies, Worldhouses, Paraterraforming Roofs, World Stations.
* Moving habitats and fleet communities: Sky Trenches, Hive Cities, Under Cities, Orbitals Habs, Halo Habs, Clarke Orbit Cities, Deep Sea Cities, Bubble-Hab Clusters, Bubblehabs, Buckyhabs, Habitat Swarm, Hoopworld, Freesphere, Alderson Freesphere, Dyson Freesphere, Ark Platforms.
* Habitat megastructures built into or around stars/black holes: Matrioshka Brain, B.I.O Brain, A‑Brain, M‑Brain, N‑Brain, J‑Brain, S‑Brain, B‑Brain, C‑Brain, Galaxy Brain, W‑Brain, Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere, Void Realms, Hypercivilization Shells.

Resource Extraction and Terraforming

* Planetary and stellar resource extractors: Planetary Strip Mine, Planetary Strip Miners, Dynamic Core Ignitor, Stratosphere Network, Stratospheric Spire Node, Stellar Mass Extraction Array, Star Lifters, Penrose Sphere, Autic Nanite Synthesis Node, Nanite Disassembler, Gas Giant Lifter, Gas Giant Compressor, Gas Giant Shellworld, Stellarite Harvester, Stellarite Bottle, Stellarite Infuser, Solar System Disintegrator, Stellar Mass Extraction Array, Crystal Megabore, Deep‑Well Geo‑Taps, Mantle Pistons.
* Terraforming and environmental control: Terraforming Outpost, Macro‑Atmospheric Stabilizer, Atmospheric Purifier, Fusion Suppressor, Macro‑Engineering Testing Station, Deep‑Cryo Radiator Worlds, Statite Sunshades, Statite Mirror, Star Shades, Sunshields, Worldhouses, Paraterraforming Roofs, Micro Black‑Hole Power Generators (MBHPGs) used to manipulate planetary climates.

Agriculture, Food Production, and Life Support

* Dedicated agricultural megastructures: Yggdrasil Orchid and Yggdrasil Orchid Station (gas‑giant greenhouse/upper‑atmosphere farms), Orbital Hydroponics Station, Orbital Artificial Ecosystem, Orbital Elysium, Planetary Assembly Station and Planetary Cloning Station (supporting large‑scale biomass and food manufacturing), Habitat Bands for ring‑like agro‑zones, Water Features integrated into ringworlds and shellworlds, and Deep Sea Cities that include aquaculture.
* Food logistics and distribution: Many logistics megastructures (see below) serve to transport food supplies across systems.

Industrial Manufacturing and Construction

* Factories and production nodes: Forge Worlds, Forge Star, Orbital Foundry, Orbital Factory, Orbital Gas Refinery, Neutronium Gigaforge, Deep Well Industrial Zones, Stellar Cornucopia, Cornucopia Machine, Matrioshka Hypernode (computronium + industrial), Systemcraft Assembler, Hyper‑Structural Assembly Yard, Behemoth Assembly Plant, Ship Recycler, Autonomous MRO Ecology, Lunar Specular Reflector (manufacturing and energy).
* Megashipyards and craft builders: Hyperquasaric Craft, Hyperquasaric Teraforge, Hyperquasaric Megashipyard, Planetary Shipyard, Planetary Dock, Orbital Drydock, Fractal Giga Art Installation (also industrial/arts), Galactic Anvil, Demiurge Forge, Equatorial Shipyard, Behemoth Assembly Plant.

Power Generation and Energy Management

* Stellar and black‑hole energy systems: StarForge, Penrose Sphere, B.H.I.S. (Black Hole Industrial Siphon), Supermassive Black Hole Manipulator, Supermassive Black Hole Matrioshka Brain, Kugelblitz Power Plants, Vacuum Energy Transduction Array, Fusion Candle (interstellar system), Fusion‑Wind Stellar Engine, Caplan Stellar Engine, Kugelblitz Power Plants, G.L.U.E (energy storage), Dimensional Tearer.
* Dyson‑type collectors: Dyson Sphere, Nested Dyson Swarms, SMBH Dyson Swarm (“Black Hole Club”), Shellworld Dyson Sphere, Shellworld Dyson Swarm, Stellar Mirrors, Specular Orbital Reflectors, Solar Collection Station, Solar Gravitational Lens Relay, Solar Gravitational Lens Relay (SGL).

Scientific and Observation Facilities

* Observatories and research hubs: Local Galactic Group Observatory, Multi‑Galactic Observatory, Ultimate Telescope, Gravitational Wave Detectors (Hyper‑Interferometers), Galactic‑Scale Radio Arrays, Spatial Multiverse Research Facility, Thought Cluster (Multi‑System Megastructure), S.U.C.C (computational research), Grand Academy, Fauna Research Station, Experimentation Complex, Galactic Anvil, Mega‑Engineering Testing Station, Macroengineering Testing Station, Vacuum Energy Transduction Array (research into exotic physics).
* Computational/AI brains: Matrioshka Brain, B.I.O Brain and all alphabet‑brains (A‑Brain through W‑Brain, etc.), Neuronium Compressor, Tipler Oracle, Ancestor Simulation.

Transportation, Logistics, and Communication

* Interstellar and interdimensional travel: Warp Gates, Hyper Warp Gates, Wormhole Projector, Quantum Catapult, Spatial Conduits, Space Fountains, Launch Loops, Rotovators (rotating tethers), StarTram, Space Elevator, Skyhook, Mass Driver Rings, Beam‑Powered Highways, Beamed‑Sail Commerce Lanes and Beamed‑Sail Commerce Tolling, Halo‑Drive Installations, Warp Gate Network, Partial Dyson Ring Swarms (acting as transit hubs).
* Logistics and storage infrastructure: Asteroid Ore Processor, Gas Giant Shellworlds (converted into logistics hubs), Stellar Hoop Plasma Ring Habitat (large cargo assembly and distribution), Lesser/Greater Nexus (major transfer stations), Interstellar Habitats and Interstellar Ringworlds (serve as waystations), Thought Cluster and Systemcraft (network control), Thermal Logistics Layer (managing energy/material flows), Memory Glaciers (data storage), Quarantine Curtain and Quarantine Veils (biosecurity logistics).
* Communications and navigation aids: Mega‑Constellation Relays, Gravitational‑Wave Beacons, Gravitational‑Wave Resonators, Axion Comm Nets, SGL constellations, Quantum Brane Lattice Node (QBLN) (high‑dimensional comms), Vacuum Energy Transduction Array (long‑range signalling).

Defence, Fortification, and Military Systems

* Planetary and orbital fortresses: Fortress Worlds, Magnus Bastions, Mega Fortifications, Planetary Defense Nexus, Maginot World, Planetary Fortifications, Ion Cannon Fortification, Asteroid Fortification, Asteroid Artillery, Mega‑Constellation Relays doubling as early‑warning nets.
* Weapons and deterrents: Star‑Eaters, Supermassive Void Birch World (weaponized), Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere, Neutronium Gigaforge (for weapon construction), Kinetic Interdiction Nets, Orbital Fortress Chains, Dyson Fortress, Mass Driver Rings (can also be weapons), Starlifting Arrays.
* Strategic shield and quarantine: Maginot World, Planetary Insurance Nets, Quarantine Curtain, Quarantine Veils, Specular Orbital Reflectors as defensive solar weapons, Distributed Whipple‑Shield Swarms (protection from debris).

Culture, Society, and Miscellany

* Education and cultural centres: Grand Academy, Archeology Center, Fractal Giga Art Installation, Celestial Printer (arts and cultural creation), Covenant Rings (treaty habitats), Singing Architecture Arrays (art and culture).
* Religious or memorial structures: Void Eternity Vaults, Dark Eternity Vaults, Void Cradles, Verse Anchors, Verse Gate, Universal Gateway, Portal‑like constructs linking the Dominion across timelines.
* Trade and economics: Ark Stations, Ark Platforms, Ark Worlds, Deep Well Industrial Zones (economic centers), Supermassive Birch Worlds as resource banks.
* Miscellaneous mega‑engineering: Spatial Conduits, Systemcraft, Deep Well Industrial Zones, Vacuum Energy Transduction Array, Galactic Gateway, Quantum Catapult, Frame World, A’ME‑RIGΩ Node, Verse Gate, G.L.U.E, Spiralverse Array, Mega‑Constellation Relays, Computronium Nodes, Matrioshka Hypernode, Astroengineering Swarm, Planetary Mega‑Sculpting.
