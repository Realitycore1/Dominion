LATTICE VESSELS & THERMAL SAILS

Silicon life does not build metal ships. They grow vessels as crystalline structures.

Lattice Ships

• Hulls are grown from macro-crystal matrices
• Naturally radiation-resistant
• Can heal fractures from micrometeorite hits
• Internal structure acts like a massive photonic brain

A ship is halfway between a building and a living organism.

⸻

Thermal Resonance Drives

Instead of chemical or fusion propulsion, they use:

• heat-gradient sails
• photonic pressure manipulation
• resonance-field oscillators

Their ships “hum” through space.

FTL is achieved by:

• using [[Aetheric]] crystalline alignment
• phase-shifting the hull to create low-resistance traversal [[Layers]]

They don’t pierce space. They slide through tuned spacetime lattices.
