Role:

Teacher / Historical Archivist

This is probably your most important missing role.

⸻

Function:

Memory continuity.

Jordan becomes:

the battlefield between history and myth.

Perfect for your themes.

⸻

Personality

* patient,
* emotionally intelligent,
* quietly worried,
* protective of historical accuracy,
* increasingly uncertain what “accuracy” even means anymore.

⸻

What Makes Jordan Powerful

Jordan teaches:

* children who never saw Earth,
* Harminan-born generations,
* students who emotionally relate more to Harmina than humanity’s origin.

That creates incredible scenes automatically.

⸻

Core Internal Conflict

Jordan realizes:

memory changes when survival changes.

Not because people lie.

Because civilizations reinterpret pain.

This directly connects to Amber’s fears.

⸻

Best Use Scenes

* teaching children about oceans,
* students misunderstanding Earth,
* arguments over curriculum,
* Celestialist reinterpretations of migration history,
* adaptation ethics debates.

⸻

One Excellent Long-Term Dynamic

Jordan and Amber should agree intellectually—
while emotionally diverging.

That becomes powerful.
