# The Somnial Fractures

The Dream-Wastes are entered through:
## Somnial Fractures

These are ruptures between:
- waking reality
- dream-space
- subconscious fields
- and the deeper Oneiroverse.

Fractures appear:
- during mass trauma
- prolonged fear states
- collective dreaming events
- consciousness instability
- or heavy Oneirogenic pressure.

They may manifest as:
- impossible doors
- recurring hallways
- shifting fog
- dream-like storms
- sleeping cities
- or recursive locations.
