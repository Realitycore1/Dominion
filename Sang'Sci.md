## Summary
Sang’Sci biology on the exterior somewhat mimics the Syl’ans but with notable differences, Such as Four mandibles attached to the skull, two middle fingers along with two thumbs and both legs are backward facing with two large toes.
The Interior Biology also has its differences such as the blood which is a Cobalt-Iron mix which gives a purple like color which gives a distinct smell of Tar. There are also extra visceral muscles which enhance the Sang’Sci’s Strength which allows for Extreme speeds and Jumping Height.

Sang’Sci Armor is entirely based off the Syl’an combat Armor and uses the same materials which gives extreme Strength and durability.
Although Same in Design the appearance has changed to accommodate the different body structure.
Underneath the Armor is a body suit based off [[The Kraft Suit]] which allows Environmentally regulation, Energy storage/Regulation and Life support.

Sang’Sci wag Warfare similar to the Syl’ans but modified for [[Close]] quarters combat and they uphold an honorary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] in which if the opponent fights well they receive an honorary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] but if their opponent is unworthy then they are decapitated to strike a fear and intimidation into their enemies.

The Sang’Sci use the same weapons as the Syl’ans use but they most prefer the Hard Light Blades. The Sang’Sci also use a different form of the blade as it has two elongated blades unlike its counterpart.
