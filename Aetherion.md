I. Physical Definition

The Aetherion is a foundational [[VFF]] particle that bridges the baryonic, exotic, and noetic [[Domains]]. It acts as a carrier for energy, information, and structure across [[Layers]] of [[(2030) Existence]] that would otherwise remain causally or physically disconnected.

While there is no real-world particle with these properties, the Aetherion conceptually incorporates:
	•	The universal coupling potential of a [[Graviton]]–photon hybrid (like a [[Graviphoton]]).
	•	The nonlocal coherence of quantum entanglement [[Carriers]].
	•	The substrate-binding role of a lattice boson.

In [[VFF]] terms, Aetherions are resonance nodes of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]-Lattice Field (ALF) — a pan-[[Verse]] network of energetic filaments enabling synchronous interaction between physical and metaphysical structures.
