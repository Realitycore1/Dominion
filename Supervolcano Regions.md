1. The Caldera of Black Glass (Vorcalis Continent)
	•	Appearance: a continent-sized obsidian basin, perpetually shrouded in stormclouds.
	•	Eruptive Style: explosive super-eruptions that melt sand into endless obsidian plains.
	•	Ecology: glassleaf vines dominate the barrens; shard-beetles thrive here.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Role: site of major warform foundries — forges draw directly on lava flows.
	•	Cultural Meaning: “the crucible of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]” — all true weapons must taste its flames.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Ashfang Rift (Draelith Continent)
	•	Appearance: jagged canyon complex splitting the landmass for thousands of kilometers.
	•	Eruptive Style: long fissure eruptions releasing magma rivers and volcanic winters.
	•	Ecology: home of ashfang crawlers, storm behemoths migrate through to graze new growth.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Role: considered a testing ground for shock-troops.
	•	Cultural Meaning: “[[The Maw]] of [[Harmina]]” — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Soldiers]] sent here as rites of survival.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. The Ember Crown (Azeroth Plateau, near Ranham Megacity)
	•	Appearance: a ring of active calderas surrounding a central volcanic spire.
	•	Eruptive Style: pyroclastic storms, ash fall burying entire cities, frequent lava geysers.
	•	Ecology: ember moss and stormblooms flourish between eruptions.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Role: Ranham was built with fortresses shielding it from the Ember Crown’s fury.
	•	Cultural Meaning: “the throne-[[Fire]]” — seen as a divine mantle proving the capital’s right to endure.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. The Furnace Deeps (Subterranean, under Riftlands)
	•	Appearance: a hidden undercrust chamber so vast it rivals surface continents.
	•	Eruptive Style: when pressure builds, it cracks open new landmasses in explosive oceanic eruptions.
	•	Ecology: furnace serpents spawn here; fungal towers root in molten caverns.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Role: deep-vault incubators and experimental warforges are hidden here.
	•	Cultural Meaning: “the buried sun” — worshiped as both doom and renewal.

⸻

5. Crown of Actaeon (Auric Isles, Glasswake Sea)
	•	Appearance: a half-submerged caldera whose eruptions ignite tsunamis of boiling water.
	•	Eruptive Style: explosive submarine supervolcano, turning seas to steam.
	•	Ecology: aurora ferns and bioluminescent coral dominate once cooled.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Role: naval warforms tested in waters around the caldera.
	•	Cultural Meaning: “[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Warforms/The Hunter]]’s wound” — eruptions are linked to Actaeon [[Pulsar]]’s cycle.
