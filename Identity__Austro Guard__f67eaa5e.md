The [[Austro Guard]] think of themselves not as one colony among many, but as the bastion of the Union’s frontier, the stone wall that holds firm when others falter. Their oath-driven society breeds a collective character both admired and mistrusted by neighboring [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
	•	Self-Perception
	•	They call themselves the Burden-Bearers, proud of the idea that every citizen carries a piece of the colony’s survival on their shoulders.
	•	The phrase “We endure so others may falter” is both boast and bitter jest, reflecting their belief that their sacrifices enable softer [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] to flourish.
	•	View of Other [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]]
	•	They look upon wealth-oriented [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] as dangerously decadent, indulgent at the expense of vigilance.
	•	They respect [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] that emphasize hardship or frontier struggle, considering them kin in spirit.
	•	Diplomatic relations are formal and wary; the Guard prefers to trade resources and service rather than open themselves to cultural dilution.
	•	Collective Ethos
	•	Individual ambition is expected to bow before collective survival. A rising star is not celebrated for outshining peers but for adding strength to the wall.
	•	Honor binds more tightly than [[law]]. To be trusted as a “true Oathkeeper” is the highest compliment; to be doubted is social [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]].
	•	Symbols of Self
	•	Citizens often describe themselves as stones in a wall—individually insignificant, but together unbreakable.
	•	Basalt fragments worn as pendants are not just tokens; they are reminders that Eridani stone resists even stellar [[Fire]].
	•	Foreign Reputation
	•	To outsiders, the [[Austro Guard]] seem grim, humorless, and rigid.
	•	To allies who fight beside them, they are known as the Last Line—warriors who refuse to retreat even when logic demands it.
	•	To enemies, their reputation is of a people who cannot be bribed or broken, only destroyed.

In their own eyes, the [[Austro Guard]] embody the paradox of duty: they are free because they are bound, elevated because they bow, strong because they endure the burden that others would not.
