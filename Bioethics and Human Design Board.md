# Bioethics and Human Design Board

## Summary

The Bioethics and Human Design Board reviews human biological modification, genetic enhancement, engineered beauty, pain reduction, fertility alteration, clone-line stability, hybrid integration, and quality-of-life redesign.

It exists because the Dominion has the technical ability to improve bodies in ways that can also create caste, coercion, market pressure, and new forms of inequality.

## Mandate

The Board reviews:

- elective body modification;
- medical genetic repair;
- engineered attractiveness standards;
- permanent muscle-tone retention;
- pain-cycle reduction;
- clone-line health;
- hybrid legal and medical standards;
- fertility modification;
- military-adjacent enhancement drift;
- commercial enhancement advertising.

## Powers

It may approve, restrict, delay, audit, or ban biological design protocols. It can require informed-consent review and public labeling for enhancement categories.

## Limits

The Board may not define a single ideal human body, deny treatment based on moral disgust, or permit employers, schools, or service tracks to demand elective enhancement as a hidden entry fee.

## Public Face

Citizens encounter the Board through clinic disclosures, enhancement permits, youth consent rules, clone health audits, and public fights over whether beauty has become another form of taxation.

## Story Use

Use this agency for ethical dilemmas around engineered beauty, quality-of-life biology, clone labor, and the fear that an immortal civilization may redesign humanity until natural human variation becomes socially unaffordable.
