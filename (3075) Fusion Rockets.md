# (3075) Fusion Drives

Reliable fusion-electric and fusion-thermal drives enter sustained Colonial Union service in 3075.

They reduce travel time and increase cargo flexibility without making Solar distance trivial.

Fusion vessels still require:

- reaction mass;
- large radiator systems;
- scheduled maintenance;
- protected departure and arrival corridors;
- rescue reserves;
- long coasting or low-thrust periods for heavy cargo.

High-thrust fusion-thermal vehicles serve urgent crew and rescue missions. Efficient fusion-electric tugs move heavy industrial cargo over longer periods.

The technology makes the 3090 fueling network and permanent Belt logistics practical and supports later Venusian and Mercurian construction. It also concentrates power in operators able to finance reactors, radiators, fuel processing, and traffic access.

Fusion propulsion changes the frontier from unreachable to governable in principle. It does not make government timely.
