The Caste Dictatorship

The [[Eprea Hindutva]] regime rules as a centralized autocracy spread across five colony [[Systems]]. Unlike the [[Colonial Union]]’s oath-driven or guild-based models, Eprea operates on an iron pyramid of caste hierarchy, codified into [[law]] and upheld with technological enforcement.
	•	Supreme Dictator – The Pradhān
	•	A single ruler at the apex, styled as the Pradhān Eternal.
	•	Chosen not through elections or lineage but through Council Selection—the highest caste council convenes in secret and elevates one of their own. Once chosen, the Pradhān’s rule is absolute.
	•	Publicly presented as the “embodiment of Dharma among the [[Stars]].”
	•	Caste Councils
	•	Each caste maintains its own council, which governs internal affairs and represents its caste before higher authority.
	•	Councils are stacked: Brahmic → Kshatriya → Vaishya → Shudra → Dalit (the lowest, often silenced).
	•	Councils can advise, but only the Brahmic Council holds true influence, effectively forming the dictator’s inner court.
	•	[[law]] & Enforcement
	•	The caste order is enforced via biometric caste tags, implanted at birth. These tags regulate access to jobs, education, and even physical zones of cities.
	•	Inter-caste mobility is forbidden; violations are punished by exile or execution.
	•	Surveillance networks ensure obedience: drones patrol streets, monitoring caste interactions.
	•	Colonial Administration
	•	Each of the five colony [[Systems]] is administered by a Satrap, appointed directly by the Pradhān.
	•	Satraps rule with [[Wide]] autonomy but are watched closely by Brahmic “Observers” who ensure doctrinal and caste purity.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Purity
	•	The regime frames its caste order as the only safeguard against chaos, claiming that cosmic expansion demands social stability.
	•	Dissent is branded as “adharma,” a crime against the order of creation.
