“Reality is optional. Obedience is not.” — Dominus Class Indoctrination Module

Core VR Framework
Full Neural Immersion: Achieved via neural uplinks or cortical mesh interfaces embedded in [[The Kraft Suit]] or Omni-device.
Simulated Environments: Entire sensory streams are rerouted—vision, sound, smell, touch, [[Pain]], and [[Time]].
Cognitive Compression: Experiences in VR can be compressed or expanded—hours in simulation may be seconds in reality.

Causal Forking: Some simulations permit alternate histories or physical laws (compatible with the [[Verse]] Gate emulator [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]).

Primary VR Applications
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Indoctrination
Simulated battles, ideological reinforcement, and moral filtering.
Used to pre-condition [[Soldiers]] for real combat stress, loss, and obedience.
Failure in VR may result in retraining—or termination if loyalty thresholds are not met.

 Memory Reconstruction & Restoration
VR used to explore, edit, or relive memories—especially after trauma or consciousness migration.
“Memory Vaults” house idealized simulations of lost loved ones or past worlds.

 Civic Participation
Citizens vote, attend forums, and even participate in trials via secure VR channels.
Judiciary VR allows observers to [[Witness]] and simulate punishment before execution.

Synthetic Mind Incubation
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]-born AI or synthetic souls are tested and shaped in simulated environments before release into realspace.
