## Summary

Thanekhet Osiron is the founding Verarch of [[Legio Anubis]].

## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch of Legio Anubis

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Thanekhet Osiron is silent, exact, ritualistic, patient, emotionally sealed, and nearly impossible to intimidate.

He does not embody rage. He embodies aftermath.

Core belief:

> A civilization is not judged by whether it loses. It is judged by what it made necessary before it fell.

Moral line:

> No civilization may be condemned without witness.

Legio Anubis answers the question left after victory:

> What is to be done with the defeated?

Anubis evaluates conquered civilizations for purge, quarantine, uplift, protection, integration, or termination. Their arrival means the battle has ended and judgment has begun.

They are not merely executioners. They are the Dominion's funerary court in armor.

Legio Anubis is eventually erased from record because it begins judging the Dominion by the same standards it applies to conquered worlds.

Visual anchors:

- tall, austere silhouette;
- elongated jackal-headed helms;
- obsidian-black armor;
- dull gold trim;
- pale bone funerary script;
- violet-black pulses;
- emerald or violet eyes;
- black-gold particulate haze;
- atmospheric chill;
- subsonic funeral-dirge weapon hums;
- battlefields arranged in perfect burial order.

Best line:

> Ares ended resistance. Aurelis took the walls. Anubis decided whether the city deserved to wake.
