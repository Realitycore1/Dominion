Classification: Mythic Fauna & Allied Beasts
Subtype: Aerial Mounts / High-Altitude Herd Beasts
Status: Active, Rare, Semi-Domesticated

⸻

Core Description

Pegasi are winged equines, possessing powerful bodies, broad feathered wings, and reinforced skeletal structures capable of sustained flight. Their coats range from white and silver to storm-gray, dusk-blue, or ash-black, often reflecting the skies they inhabit.

They are graceful, but not fragile.
A Pegasus is built to endure wind, altitude, and exhaustion.

They are not symbols by default.
They are animals that mastered the sky.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Pegasi are naturally occurring mythic fauna, evolved (or stabilized) through long-term exposure to aerial ley currents, high-altitude environments, and Vital Expanse pressure.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Material Continuum
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (endurance, muscle density)
	•	Tertiary Resonance: Upper-atmospheric liminal zones

They are not celestial constructs and not summoned beings.

⸻

Intelligence & Temperament

Pegasi possess high animal intelligence with strong emotional sensitivity.

They understand:
	•	trust,
	•	danger,
	•	rider intent,
	•	territorial boundaries.

They do not tolerate:
	•	cruelty,
	•	reckless riders,
	•	prolonged exposure to Malice-tainted individuals.

A mistreated Pegasus will:
	•	throw its rider,
	•	flee,
	•	or deliberately ground itself in hostile terrain.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Pegasi inhabit:
	•	mountain ranges,
	•	high plateaus,
	•	cloud forests,
	•	sky-adjacent liminal zones.

They feed on:
	•	high-altitude grasses,
	•	mineral-rich lichens,
	•	atmospheric energy currents,
	•	condensed dew and cloud moisture.

They form:
	•	herds,
	•	migratory routes,
	•	seasonal sky-paths invisible from the ground.

⸻

Flight Capabilities

Pegasi are optimized for:
	•	long-distance gliding,
	•	rapid altitude changes,
	•	stable flight under load.

They are not built for:
	•	heavy combat,
	•	carrying siege equipment,
	•	sustained aerial warfare.

Pegasi excel as:
	•	scouts,
	•	couriers,
	•	messengers,
	•	exploration mounts.

⸻

Bonding & Use

Pegasi cannot be truly domesticated.

Bonding requires:
	•	patience,
	•	ritual respect,
	•	emotional compatibility.

Cultures that rely on Pegasi often:
	•	breed selectively but sparingly,
	•	impose strict rider codes,
	•	treat Pegasi as citizens rather than livestock.

Killing a Pegasus is taboo in most sky-adjacent cultures.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to grounded ambush
	•	Susceptible to wing injury
	•	Avoid Null-dominant airspace
	•	Severely affected by corrupted skies or pollution

Pegasi abandon regions where the sky becomes hostile.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Hippogriffs: Competitive cousins; Hippogriffs are hardier, Pegasi more agile
	•	Rocs: Avoid; Rocs hunt Pegasi opportunistically
	•	Ziz: Treated as existential threats
	•	Kirin: Rarely seen together; Pegasi become docile in their presence
	•	Angels: Sometimes mistaken as divine steeds, but not bound

Pegasi are often used as neutral messengers between sky factions.

⸻

Variants
	•	Mountain Pegasi: Strong climbers, cold-resistant
	•	Storm Pegasi: Lightning-adapted, aggressive flyers
	•	Ash Pegasi: Post-collapse regions, resilient lungs
	•	Night Pegasi: Nocturnal, star-navigators
	•	Royal Pegasi: Larger, selectively bonded, ceremonial

Royal Pegasi often serve as living symbols of legitimacy—but never authority.
