# (32) First Conflict

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapters 17-20.
- Manuscript date range: approximately Years 24-41 After Landfall.
- Canon note: treat this as first major civic conflict, not a formal war.

## What Happened

The first deep conflict came from interpretation: what children were being taught, what Earth meant, what Harmina meant, and whether New Aurora had the right to define danger for people whose lives depended on different terrain.

Students misremembered Earth, argued over maps, renamed hazards, and treated Ark and Ferox history as inherited study rather than living memory. Adults recognized that culture was slipping beyond their control.

## What Resulted

- The education system became a political battlefield.
- Regional map language began to matter: hazard, home, route, folklore, infrastructure, and sacred place could describe the same location.
- Amber saw that Earth culture was not being destroyed; it was becoming mythology.
- Harminan-born citizens began rejecting the idea that they were merely colonists.

## Consequences

This civic conflict created the emotional groundwork for later independence movements. The question was no longer "Can we survive here?" but "Who has the authority to define here?"
