# Tiered Reader Path - Pleroma Archive

This page is the archive's difficulty-based route map. It does not replace the subject portals or Q&A pages; it gives readers a pacing model for when to use them.

## Purpose Summary

The Tiered Reader Path exists to connect onboarding, Q&A, civilization material, infrastructure, and specialist cosmology into one public-facing progression. It matters because the Pleroma Archive is too large to read only as a folder tree.

## Recommended Progression

1. Start with [[New Reader FAQ]] and [[Tier 1 - New Reader Portal]] for orientation, character anchors, timeline pages, and public entry points.
2. Continue into [[World Orientation FAQ]], [[Timeline FAQ]], and [[Tier 2 - Intermediate Portal]] for civilizations, places, governments, military structures, and social context.
3. Move into [[Tier 3 - Advanced Portal]] for technology, megastructures, materials, and systems that change the world at scale.
4. Finish with [[Advanced Cosmology FAQ Guide]], [[Cosmology Atlas - Pleroma Archive]], and [[Tier 4 - Specialist Portal]] for cosmology, Pleroma-level structure, MOT, axioms, and deep reality architecture.
