## Details

🔹 Exotic Matter

General term for
any matter that exhibits non-standard properties—such as negative energy density, imaginary mass, or tachyonic phase states.

Includes:
	•	Casimir vacuum fields
	•	[[Tachyon]] condensates
	•	[[Aetheric]] phase matter ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] use)
	•	Quantum field constructs with energy below the local vacuum state

Property
[[Negative Mass]]
Exotic Matter
Inertial Behavior
Moves opposite to applied force
Variable—may be massless or imaginary mass
Gravitational Response
Repels positive mass; self-attractive
Depends on form—some may induce gravitic shear
Phase Stability
Requires constant energy input
Often metastable; anchored via quantum confinement
Interaction with Light
May bend light inward or outward
Can redshift/blueshift photons, enable cloaking
