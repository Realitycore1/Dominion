# Recurring Harminan Viewpoint Cast

## Purpose

These characters give the Harmina anthology continuity without relying on Amber, Emilia, or the central political cast.

They may appear as viewpoint characters, relatives, elders, teachers, rivals, or names on public records.

## First Settlement Generation

### Mira Tal

Role: ration cook, later settlement food archivist.

Function: shows food anxiety, local adaptation, first harvest ritual, and the emotional politics of hunger.

### Esai Corven

Role: perimeter mechanic and wallwatch volunteer.

Function: shows practical survival work, maintenance culture, class tension, and the first household repair rites.

### Sada Amri

Role: biolab apprentice, later microbial systems supervisor.

Function: shows science as daily labor, not only breakthrough genius.

### Tomas Revik

Role: burial registrar and memorial worker.

Function: shows how Harmina forces mourning, burial, identity records, and memory to change.

### Leira Cest

Role: sanctuary singer and later hymnal collector.

Function: shows religion changing through ordinary music, grief, and local pronunciation.

## First Harminan-Born Generation

### Niko Venn

Role: route child, later weather-cartography apprentice.

Function: treats storms, warning towers, and walking terrain as normal.

### Veyra Sol

Role: schoolchild, later Echo Bloom field interpreter.

Function: shows history becoming sensory, mythic, and educational.

### Kito Amri

Role: biolab child, later food-systems technician.

Function: bridges the first survival scientists and children who inherit their methods as custom.

### Mina Corven

Role: maintenance child, later heatline engineer.

Function: shows frontier competence and the pride of settlements beyond New Aurora.

### Pell Varek

Role: rail-yard child, later megatrain dispatcher.

Function: shows route identity, transit law, and the Commonwealth seed.

## Convoy and Divergence Generation

### Orsa Nadi

Role: convoy mother and contract keeper.

Function: shows family life in motion and the emotional cost of leaving New Aurora.

### Halan Jor

Role: teacher assigned to convoy children.

Function: shows Earth culture fading through lessons children cannot fully imagine.

### Iri Venn

Role: weather technician and storm-siren operator.

Function: shows how environmental knowledge becomes civic authority.

### Corin Aves

Role: dissident printer and route pamphleteer.

Function: shows lawful disagreement before later nationalism hardens.

### Rema Sorith

Role: clinic counselor during the Body Question years.

Function: shows how engineered beauty, pain reduction, clone labor, and welfare policy become intimate rather than abstract.

## Use Rule

Do not make these characters secret founders of everything.

Their value is that they are ordinary enough to show the world working.
