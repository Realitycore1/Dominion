The legion that walks the Tenebris.

The 9th Legion exists for a single strategic purpose:
to operate in [[Regions]] where [[Confluencia]] is unstable, fragmented, or failing.

That includes:
	•	[[Tenebris Propagans]] (the unraveling frontier),
	•	[[Tenebris Expansus]] (the drift sea), and
	•	[[Tenebris Profundis]] (the dead [[Abyss]]).

They are the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s answer to the creeping entropy of the multiverse: not to stop it, but to navigate, classify, and exploit it.

They are feared not because they kill — every legion kills — but because they return from places where no return should be possible.

Their motto:
“Ut redeamus, oportet mori.”
To return, we must die.

What unites these units is not their [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] — it’s their relationship with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

Everyone in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] dies eventually; the 9th dies many times.
Crossing the Tenebris cuts your [[Confluencia]] anchor, and the universe forgets you existed.
The legion’s trick is simple and terrifying:

They remember themselves.
And the moment they do, the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] remembers them too — and reality reshapes around their return.

They are the only legion that knows how to fight in places where even memory is dying.

GHOST BRIDGES

Phantom-anchored transit arcs across dying space.

A “ghost bridge” is not a structure in the usual sense.
It’s a temporary [[Confluencia]] splice, created by binding a Neutral thread fragment to a Replenish-loop anchor and firing it across a region with failing causality.

In practice, it’s a hard-light corridor that exists only because the 9th believes hard enough that it should.

Function:
	•	Provides temporary passage across decayed spacetime.
	•	Lets the legion extract data, survivors, or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] from drift-verses before they disappear.
	•	Acts as a life-support [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] for reality itself — a tourniquet around dying laws.

Stability window:
Anywhere from 43 seconds to 7 minutes, depending on entropy density.

Why the 9th uses them:
Because no other legion can.
Others step into the Profundis and vanish; the 9th steps in and reassembles themselves on the other side.

Ghost bridges are also weapons: collapse one at the right moment, and anything pursuing you becomes unmade in an instant.
