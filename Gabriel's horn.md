> Image unavailable after migration: `4F0585EB-5B65-4539-890E-849D916F56A3.webp`Gabriel’s Horn is a metaphysical-mechanical war-engine and ceremonial artifact used to inaugurate [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[Crusades]]. Inspired by the paradox of infinite volume within finite surface, the Horn collapses infinite potential into a single decree. When sounded, it resonates across realspace and metaphysical strata, announcing that [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] war-[[law]] has begun.

Core Features

Structure

Horn Geometry: Modeled after Gabriel’s Horn (torso of a trumpet + infinite mathematical taper), rendered as a colossal construct suspended in orbit or metaphysical space.

Resonance Lattices: Glyph-inscribed surfaces ripple with Concordia harmonics, able to project across real and [[Verse]]-space.

Anchor Spires: Atlas-class pylons stabilize the Horn’s mouth and throat, bracing against metaphysical stresses.

Infinity Coil: A taper that visually seems endless, vanishing into black-gold recursion.

Functions

Planetfall Herald: When activated above a world, its resonance shakes the atmosphere, causing fleets to descend through the “flare” of its sound.

Metaphysical Spearhead: Burns corridors through hostile [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Frameworks|Frameworks]], nullifying enemy psionics, oracles, and prayers.

Symbolic: The sounding of the Horn is the point of no return — crusade cannot be rescinded once declared.
