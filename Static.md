Core Bias:

Suspended tension and charged stillness

This subtype is extremely useful because it introduces a form of Storm that is not constantly exploding, but is always on the edge of it.

Traits:
	•	constant background charge
	•	low-motion but high-tension environments
	•	latent instability
	•	pressure without immediate release
	•	environments that feel “ready to rupture”

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	everything is holding its breath
	•	calm is not safety, only delay
	•	reality hums with unreleased violence

Common Expressions:
	•	militarized stand-off civilizations
	•	tense diplomatic or ideological basins
	•	charged atmospheric realities
	•	suspended war states

Failure Mode:

Delayed rupture
The eventual release is far worse because it was postponed too long.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	cold war realities
	•	pre-collapse civilizations
	•	ideological deadlocks
	•	highly pressurized peace zones
