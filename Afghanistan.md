Afghanistan is a country located in Central Asia with a diverse geography and rich cultural heritage. The country is bordered by [[Pakistan]] to the east, [[Iran]] to the west, [[Turkmenistan]] to the north, and Tajikistan and China to the northwest. The country also has significant mineral resources, including gold, copper, and gemstones, which have attracted foreign investors in recent years. The country's geography is characterized by rugged mountain ranges and fertile valleys, with the Kabul River running through the center of the country. Afghanistan has one of the world's longest and highest mountain ranges, the Hindu Kush, which stretches from [[Pakistan]] to China. Afghanistan's [[culture]] is deeply rooted in its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and traditions, with a strong Islamic heritage that dates back to the spread of Islam in the seventh century. The Pashto language is the most widely spoken in Afghanistan, with Arabic also being commonly used in religious and literary contexts. The country's diverse ethnic groups include Pashtuns, Tajiks, Hazara, Uzbeks, and Turkmen. These groups have their own languages and [[Cultures]], and have played a significant role in shaping Afghanistan's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]. Despite its [[Natural]] resources and cultural heritage, Afghanistan has faced ongoing conflicts and instability since the Soviet [[Invasion]] in the 1980s.
