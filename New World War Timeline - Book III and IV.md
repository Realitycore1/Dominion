# New World War Timeline - Book III and IV

## Purpose

This note converts the Book III and Book IV chapter stubs into a working timeline for the New World War.

For the active novel structure based on this timeline, see [[Amber Book II - The Final Line - Definitive Skeleton]]. The numbered Book III and Book IV entries below remain source chronology rather than active manuscript chapter numbering.

Book III is the war-proper timeline: the Founder Clause crisis, the strike on New Aurora, the rise of wartime blocs, siege warfare, failed accords, verified contemporary GalFed pressure, and the silent dawn after the old planetary order breaks.

Book IV is the late-war and unification timeline: debt, reconstruction, disputed admission into a new state, council politics, rebellion, orbital and deep-region campaigns, the Covenant of Syl'an, the first shape of the later Dominion, and formal GalFed contact after Harmina develops one authority capable of answering.

## Active Manuscript Structure

Amber Book II is organized into six parts:

1. Ash Assembly;
2. New World War;
3. Bodies and Spears;
4. Sovereign of Mankind;
5. Gifts and Standardization;
6. The Final Line.

The old Chapter 18 title, *First Contact*, now means the first verified contemporary evidence or administrative signal proving that GalFed pressure has reached Harmina. It is not the arrival of a GalFed delegation.

Formal contact begins in Chapter 38 of the definitive skeleton. Standardization and the Final Line continue through Chapter 44. Open GalFed war, Operation Prescience, Aether, the Primus Legions, and the Great Crusade continue in Amber Book III.

## Dating Rule

Exact years remain adjustable.

Use relative dating until the final chronology is locked:

- Prewar: before Year 252 AL.
- War Year 0: the strike on New Aurora during the Founder Clause debates.
- War Years 1-3: emergency mandates, uprisings, bloc formation, and opening campaigns.
- War Years 4-9: sieges, autonomous warfare, arcology destruction, and accord failures.
- War Years 10-15: breakpoint wars, contact pressure, and systemwide exhaustion.
- Late War / Unification Years: Book IV events that resolve the war into the United Syl'an State and early Dominion structure, followed by formal GalFed contact.

## War Premise

Atlovaia strikes the New Aurora Constitutional Assembly first during the Founder Clause debate. FreeStack independently launches its follow-on strike seconds later against New Aurora and suspected Equilon proto-legionary infrastructure. The two attacks do not share one command chain.

The attack does not simply begin a military conflict. It turns Amber, New Aurora, the Equilon state, and the meaning of Harminan legitimacy into war objectives.

The visible civil war also activates two older conflicts.

The hidden conflict concerns preparation for GalFed's return. Project Ra, orbital defense, the space elevator, proto-Panoplies, gene therapies, AI and cyborg doctrine, and biological and synthetic countermeasures have public Harminan purposes and compartmentalized external-defense purposes.

The internal conflict is fought inside bodies, memories, and identities. Anti-Immortal attacks target continuity and witness authority. Anti-synthetic attacks target agency, memory, perception, and control. Proto-legionary treatments preserve soldiers by making survival dependent on systems the state controls.

## Major Belligerents

Primary powers include:

- Equilon States;
- Atlovaian bloc;
- F.S.S.R.;
- Commonwealth of States;
- Ormova Replika;
- Estelia Scriptorium;
- Toldax Ascent;
- New Aurora loyalist institutions;
- route communities and compact settlements;
- autonomous and semi-autonomous military-industrial systems.

Book IV adds postwar or late-war formations:

- United Syl'an provisional structures;
- debt compacts;
- admission councils;
- Artemis-aligned orbital or strategic forces;
- rebel enclaves;
- early Dominion continuity factions.

## Book III Timeline - Wars of the Equilon States

### Part I - Ignition

1. [[Chapter 1 (Ashes of Harmina)]] - War Year 0. New Aurora is bombed during the Founder Clause debates. The city becomes both battlefield and symbol.
2. [[Chapter 2 (Equilon Mandate)]] - Equilon officials invoke emergency legitimacy and attempt to centralize authority around the survival of New Aurora.
3. [[Chapter 3 (Seeds of Discord)]] - Other states reject or reinterpret the mandate. Alliance language hardens into war language.
4. [[Chapter 4 (The First Uprising)]] - Local unrest, resistance cells, and first counterstrikes make the conflict impossible to contain as a single attack investigation.

### Part II - Expansion

5. [[chapter 5 (Rise of the Equilon States)]] - The Equilon bloc becomes a wartime state structure rather than a loose inheritance of New Aurora.
6. [[Chapter 6 (Hive Against Hive)]] - State systems mobilize against one another: factories, clone labor, data networks, civil defense hives, and propaganda blocs.
7. [[Chapter 7 (The Walking War)]] - The front spreads across Harmina through terrain, megatrain corridors, fungal jungles, red deserts, walking reefs, power armor, and autonomous platforms.
8. [[Chapter 8 (ArcCells to Ashes)]] - Arcology cells, shelter districts, and strategic habitation nodes are destroyed, proving that no rear area is fully civilian anymore.
9. [[Chapter 9 (Siege of New Aurora)]] - New Aurora enters siege conditions. The city becomes a test of legitimacy, logistics, and memory.

### Part III - Accord Pressure

10. [[Chapter 10 (Concordant Vanguard)]] - A coalition tries to prevent total collapse by forming a vanguard around shared survival interests.
11. [[Chapter 11 (Edge of Accord)]] - Negotiations almost work, but every settlement bloc reads survival differently.
12. [[Chapter 12 (Harmonic Synthesis)]] - Military, civic, scientific, and religious institutions attempt integrated command to survive the widening conflict.
13. [[Chapter 13 (Path of the Thousand Forms)]] - Warfare diversifies through clones, hybrids, proto-legionary treatments, autonomous weapons, power armor, cybernetics, orbital systems, and ecological theater warfare. Survival gains arrive with chronic medical and social costs.
14. [[Chapter 14 (Silent Chorus)]] - Anti-synthetic memory corruption and anti-Immortal continuity attacks join censored casualties, dead communication channels, and public grief.

### Part IV - Breakpoint

15. [[Chapter 15 (Breakpoint Wars)]] - Multiple fronts reach failure points at once. Biological, synthetic, medical, logistical, and civic failures turn campaigning into systemic collapse management.
16. [[Chapter 16 (Accord Fractures)]] - The remaining accords fracture under scarcity, revenge, delayed biological agents, dormant synthetic commands, and incompatible definitions of unity.
17. [[Chapter 17 (Price of Unity)]] - Unity becomes possible only through concessions no faction can fully defend later.
18. [[Chapter 18 (First Contact)]] - Contemporary observation and an administrative signal match sealed *Ardent Mercy* records. The chapter proves GalFed pressure without bringing a formal delegation to Harmina.
19. [[Chapter 19 (Silent Dawn)]] - The first war phase ends in exhausted silence rather than triumph. Harmina survives, but the prewar world is gone.

## Book IV Timeline - United Syl'an State

### Part I - Debt and Admission

20. [[Chapter I (Chains of Debt)]] - War debts, reconstruction burdens, refugee obligations, and military dependency bind settlements together before they agree ideologically.
21. [[Chapter II (Price of Admission)]] - Entry into the emerging United Syl'an order requires concessions on law, military command, archives, transit, and resource sharing.
22. [[Chapter III (Ledgers of Chains)]] - Debt records become political weapons. Who paid, who bled, and who was protected become arguments over citizenship.
23. [[Chapter IV (Whispers in the Dark)]] - Secret factions, intelligence leaks, and survival cults exploit the exhaustion after Book III's silent dawn.

### Part II - Provisional State

24. [[Chapter V (Council of Ashes)]] - A council forms among burned cities, damaged states, and surviving institutions.
25. [[Chapter VI (Chorus of Doubt)]] - Citizens and regional blocs doubt whether unity is protection or a new form of captivity.
26. [[Chapter VII (Spark of Rebellion)]] - A rebellion begins against the terms of unification, especially where debt, military occupation, and archive control overlap.
27. [[Chapter VIII (O.R.A.C.L.E. Divides)]] - Strategic advisory systems, predictive networks, or high command intelligence split over the future state.

### Part III - Strategic Gambits

28. [[Chapter IX (Artemis Gambit)]] - Artemis becomes the central orbital or strategic objective: command platform, defense network, shipyard, weapon lattice, or symbolic high ground.
29. [[Chapter X (Shadows of the Craterheart)]] - Deep Harminan geography, mantle infrastructure, buried weapons, or hollow-crust systems bring the war below the surface.
30. [[Chapter XI (Breaking the Spires)]] - Defensive spires, command towers, orbital tethers, or cultural monuments are broken to end an enemy's capacity to resist.
31. [[Chapter XII (Siege of Harmina)]] - The conflict expands from city siege to planetary siege, with Harmina itself treated as the prize.

### Part IV - Covenant and Dominion Seed

32. [[Chapter XIII (Battle for Artemis)]] - The Artemis theater decides whether the new order controls the sky, the archives, the weapons grid, or the exit routes.
33. [[Chapter XIV (Last Enclave)]] - The final holdout forces the new state to define mercy, punishment, surrender, and memory.
34. [[Chapter XV (Covenant of Syl'an)]] - The covenant creates the United Syl'an State as a war settlement, legal compromise, and survival pact.
35. [[Chapter XVI (Ascension of Dominion)]] - The states request Amber's ascension as a continuity solution. The early Dominion grows from the United Syl'an settlement while limiting the authority created to face GalFed.

### Source Continuation - Formal Contact

36. [[Chapter XVII (The Sky Came Wearing Laws)]] - Source stub for the formal-contact sequence expanded into Chapters 38–44 of [[Amber Book II - The Final Line - Definitive Skeleton]].

## Timeline Function

The New World War should not feel like a single battle chain.

It should feel like the moment Harmina's local identities, survival systems, biological redesign debates, GalFed fear, orbital infrastructure, and founder-legitimacy crisis all ignite at once.

Book III shows the world breaking.

Book IV shows the survivors deciding what kind of state can prevent it from happening again.

Formal contact then reveals the harder question: whether that state can resist domination without importing domination into its own design. Open war continues in [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience - Development Spine]].
