Classification: Elementals & Primals → Infernal-Aligned Elementals
Subtype: Sovereign Fire Entity
Status: Active, Realm-Bound

⸻

Core Description

Efreeti are towering, humanoid entities composed of condensed flame, molten brass, and living smoke, often crowned with burning sigils or horn-like crests. Their bodies radiate oppressive heat not merely as temperature, but as ontological pressure—reality near them behaves as if it expects to burn.

Unlike lesser fire elementals, Efreeti possess:
	•	stable identity,
	•	long memory,
	•	and rigid internal hierarchy.

They are not chaotic beings of flame. They are authoritarian embodiments of combustion.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Efreeti originate from regions where Fundamentum Elementalis overlaps with Sheol-Gehenna (Malice).
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Fundamentum Elementalis (Fire-Dominant Zones)
	•	Secondary Influence: Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)
	•	Realm Interface: Tempest Realms (Elemental Chaos Zones)

Their fire is not purely elemental—it is weaponized intent.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Efreeti organize themselves into:
	•	dynasties,
	•	courts,
	•	and war-hosts.

They believe fire is the proper end state of all structures—physical, political, and spiritual.

Common behaviors include:
	•	enslaving lesser fire entities,
	•	binding mortals through contracts,
	•	constructing burning fortresses in unstable realms.

They do not destroy indiscriminately.
They conquer, rule, and refine through annihilation.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Efreeti are infamous for bargains—but these are not tricks.

Efreeti contracts are:
	•	literal,
	•	brutally precise,
	•	and intentionally asymmetrical.

They will:
	•	grant power,
	•	reshape fate,
	•	or annihilate enemies—

—but always in a way that strengthens their influence footprint.

A mortal who “wins” an Efreeti bargain often becomes:
	•	a political accelerant,
	•	a war catalyst,
	•	or a future vessel.

⸻

Known Manifestations
	•	As fire-kings ruling volcanic cities
	•	As living infernos bound into weapons or engines
	•	As sovereigns appearing during planar breaches

In low-saturation worlds, they appear as:
	•	“demons of flame,”
	•	wrathful spirits,
	•	or mythologized tyrants.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Efreeti lose cohesion in:
	•	Null-aligned zones,
	•	Dream-dominant realms,
	•	or areas stripped of symbolic meaning.
	•	Their power is tied to recognition of authority—defiance weakens them.
	•	They cannot exist long in planes dominated by Grace without degradation.

⸻

Relationship to Other Beings
	•	Djinn: Efreeti see them as weak cousins.
	•	Demons: Allies of convenience, not equals.
	•	Angels: Ideological opposites; Grace suppresses Efreeti authority.
	•	Mortals: Tools, fuel, or future assets
