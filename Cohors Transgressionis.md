“The Cohort of Breaking Boundaries.”

If the [[9th Legion]] is the revenant body, Cohors Transgressionis is its blade.

This cohort specializes in [[law]]-violation operations — exploiting the weaknesses in [[Confluencia]] for tactical advantage.

Not “breaking the [[law]]” as in disobedience —
literally breaking the laws of [[Physics]] where the Tenebris already weakened them.

What they do:
	•	Causal inversion raids (events happen in reverse from the enemy’s perspective).
	•	Phase-stepping through incomplete metrics.
	•	Probability leveraging (nudging outcomes in thin-[[law]] [[Regions]]).
	•	Harvesting Non-Causal Remnants — fragments of dead verses used in experimental weaponry.

Why they’re feared:

Where they fight, rifles jam not from mechanical failure but because probability changes.
Detonations happen before grenades are thrown.
Enemies die and then realize they were shot.

Required enhancement:

All members undergo the [[Ritual]] of Dissolution, temporarily severing their personal [[Confluencia]] anchor.
This makes them partly “unreal,” giving them the ability to operate where reality itself gives up.

Their unofficial nickname:
“The Broken.”
