## Summary

Tempestor Veyrun is the founding Verarch of [[Legio Aurelis]], the Golden Bulwark.

## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch of Legio Aurelis

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Where Ares breaks enemy lines and Quetzalcoatl descends through storm and sky, Aurelis captures, seals, converts, and holds.

Tempestor Veyrun represents:

- siege inversion;
- citadel capture;
- relay bastion conversion;
- fortress-world seizure;
- infrastructure preservation;
- protected occupation;
- conversion of enemy dependency systems into Dominion strongholds.

Tempestor Veyrun should be tied to the first major occupation dilemma. His victories save populations by preserving infrastructure, but they also create early Dominion control problems.

Core line:

> Valerius breaks the enemy's shield. Tempestor Veyrun turns the enemy's fortress into Dominion ground.

Drafting pressure:

Tempestor should not feel like a simple conqueror. He is the officer who knows exactly how to preserve a hospital, archive, relay, or city wall - and therefore knows why giving it back may be strategically impossible.
