(Nodes of the Demiurge’s Power)

Essence
	•	Each [[Fortress of Sin]] is a geometric citadel, inscribed with shifting Demiurgic scripture, suspended in impossible space or anchored deep into cursed lands.
	•	There are nine in total, each bound to an Archon. They serve as their worldly throne and [[Locus]] of their power.
	•	Together, they form the shield of the Demiurge: so long as the Fortresses stand, the Demiurge cannot be drawn into open battle.

Purpose
	•	Worldly Regency: Each fortress manifests the Demiurge’s will through its Archon, reshaping the verses caught in its orbit.
	•	Harvest Engines: They siphon suffering, blood-worship, and despair from across the Expanse, concentrating it into Demiurgic sustenance.
	•	Wards of Protection: Destroying one weakens the Demiurge slightly. Only when all nine are shattered can the Demiurge itself be exposed.

Nature of the Fortresses
	•	Indestructible Appearance: To mortal eyes they seem invincible, but [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Doctrine/Relics/Relics|Relics]] of divinity or the Christus Dolorum’s manifestation can scar them.
	•	Labyrinthine Interiors: Inside, reality folds into endless halls of torment — living prisons where damned races are chained and remade into [[Custodes Demiurgi]].
	•	Symbolic [[Domain]]: Each fortress reflects the [[Sin]] of its Archon. For example:
	•	[[Fortress of Silence]]: No sound exists within; prayers die in the throat.
	•	[[Fortress of Chains]]: Every corridor is lined with bound souls, their [[Chains]] stretching into infinity.
	•	[[Fortress of Hunger]]: Endless feasts of ash, where the damned devour each other without end.
