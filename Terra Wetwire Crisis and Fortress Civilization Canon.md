# Terra Wetwire Crisis and Fortress Civilization Canon

This note is the working canon spine for Wetwire, containment culture, fortress cities, corridor civilization, military biotechnology, and their social consequences between 2000 and 2078.

It expands [[(2000) Wetwire Outbreak]], [[(2003) Deployment of Accerisma compound]], [[(2008) Anti-Wetwire Walls]], and [[(2048) Wetwire Containment Failure]]. A narrower dated note controls if a later contradiction appears.

## Canon Boundaries

Wetwire is not a zombie plague. It does not reliably animate corpses, produce armies of infected people, or turn every encounter into an attack.

Wetwire is an adaptive tissue-and-substrate ecology. It grows where biological waste, conductive material, water, heat, and neglected infrastructure meet. It uses buildings, utilities, machines, and animal tissue as one connected environment. It is frightening because it can imitate routine without understanding routine in a human way.

Most Wetwire growth is sessile. Mobile forms exist, especially after environmental adaptation, but they are rare workers, carriers, or defensive colonies rather than a replacement population of monsters.

Wetwire is not fully eradicated by 2078. Humanity learns to suppress, isolate, route around, and sometimes destroy it. Large permanent exclusion zones remain.

Accerisma and Vulcanite are different technologies:

- **Accerisma** suppresses Wetwire-compatible growth and supports some respiratory treatment. It permits recovery and reoccupation, but repeated use damages lungs, soil, water, and microbial ecologies.
- **Vulcanite** is a two-part terminal incinerant used when a structure, vehicle, or terrain segment has been declared unrecoverable. It destroys the host substrate along with the infestation.

EVO-5 is not Wetwire's confirmed origin. EVO-5 is a later regenerative biotechnology platform. Cross-contamination between EVO-5 tissue and Wetwire becomes a major security concern, but the two remain biologically and historically distinct.

The attached visual references establish atmosphere, material language, and public imagination. Their labels are not automatically literal canon. In particular:

- a "700-900 meter wall" is canonized as the depth of a layered exclusion belt, not the height of a concrete wall;
- major walls are usually 20-60 meters high, with rare terrain-integrated sections approaching 100 meters;
- fortress populations and borders follow the established Terra nations rather than the labels on the concept map;
- engineered archosaurs are synthetic taxa derived from living genomes, not resurrected dinosaurs;
- green Vulcanite flame comes from its fictional indicator package and contaminated metals, not supernatural energy.

## I. Wetwire Crisis, 2000-2078

### Origins

No public inquiry identifies a single origin.

The best-supported 2078 model is a convergence event. During the 1980s and 1990s, livestock prion research, neural scaffolding, conductive polymers, automated tissue culture, and medical-device recycling created compatible fragments in several laboratories and supply chains. None was Wetwire alone. Repeated movement through slaughterhouses, hospitals, research facilities, waste processors, and black-budget programs allowed a persistent tissue architecture to acquire new substrates.

Three theories remain in circulation:

1. **The Feed Theory:** a livestock protein-contamination chain produced the first persistent misfolding behavior.
2. **The Scaffold Theory:** neural repair matrices taught damaged tissue to preserve structure after ordinary cellular death.
3. **The Convergence Theory:** no single invention caused Wetwire; incompatible projects became one ecology through shared waste and equipment systems.

The Convergence Theory has the strongest evidence and the least political support. It prevents governments and corporations from assigning responsibility to one laboratory.

### Discovery and First Outbreaks

Retrospective evidence places precursor incidents in the late 1980s and 1990s:

- livestock tags transmitting after battery removal;
- neural cultures retaining patterned conductivity after declared death;
- medical-waste compactors activating without power;
- tissue contamination clustered around recycled device components;
- unexplained animal orientation toward buried cables during storms.

The first recognized urban outbreak begins in 2000 inside a hospital. Missing patients, warm elevator walls, false intercom calls, altered network paths, and colonized service cavities reveal that the anomaly is using medical infrastructure as a continuous habitat.

The first month follows four phases:

- **Days 0-3:** isolated hospital anomalies, missing personnel, equipment faults, and unexplained tissue loss.
- **Days 4-7:** private-home nests, unofficial quarantines, responder rumors, and false stories about drug laboratories or attacks.
- **Days 8-14:** links to biotechnology, dialysis, livestock, medical waste, and abandoned utility spaces.
- **Days 15-30:** black containment units burn, sterilize, or entomb sites while governments circulate mutually contradictory cover stories.

The crisis becomes global through ordinary systems rather than one spectacular release. Dialysis filters, livestock transport, hospital laundry, sewage flow, reused electronics, waste contracts, and stormwater networks carry it farther than deliberate sabotage could.

### Government Response

Early government responses are fragmented:

- public-health agencies treat Wetwire as contamination;
- police treat disappearances as crime;
- militaries treat infrastructure mimicry as sabotage;
- intelligence services classify samples and suppress common reporting;
- municipal governments close buildings without receiving the evidence needed to explain why;
- courts struggle to distinguish destroyed property from property made permanently inaccessible.

The first major error is secrecy without coordination. Records are sealed inside separate jurisdictions, allowing identical failures to recur.

The second error is using functionality as proof of safety. A building that still supplies electricity, water, or data may be more deeply colonized than one that has failed.

The third error is equating uncertainty with either permission to destroy or a requirement to wait. Wetwire repeatedly forces officials to choose between those harms.

### Corporate Response

Corporate behavior divides into four recognizable patterns:

- **Denial:** medical, agricultural, and waste firms classify incidents as ordinary contamination to protect licenses.
- **Capture:** chemical and security companies patent treatments, inspection systems, and clean-cargo standards built from public research.
- **Abandonment:** shell companies inherit contaminated facilities and dissolve before cleanup claims mature.
- **Adaptation:** rail, filtration, construction, and insurance firms develop the standards that later make corridor civilization possible.

Some corporate teams save cities. Others prolong emergencies because containment contracts, land seizures, and proprietary certifications make crisis profitable. The distinction often exists inside the same company.

### Public Panic and Public Adaptation

Panic appears first as practical behavior:

- families remove wiring from walls;
- residents burn damp furniture in streets;
- filter cartridges, bottled water, copper-free tools, and paper maps vanish from stores;
- exposed workers are rejected by landlords and relatives despite valid clearance;
- crowds destroy substations after rumors that Wetwire travels through live current;
- fraudulent clean certificates become more valuable than passports.

The panic changes rather than disappears. By the second decade, children treat scanners, sealed schools, and wall drills as normal. Adults who remember open hospitals and unguarded highways experience the new routine as permanent emergency. Their children experience the old world as negligent.

### Scientific Model

By the 2030s, researchers agree on several behaviors:

- Wetwire preserves pattern before it preserves organism.
- It grows fastest where moisture, protein, conductive materials, and repeated mechanical rhythms overlap.
- It can entrain itself to pumps, lifts, alarms, traffic cycles, and search procedures.
- It does not need continuous grid power, but active systems accelerate growth and provide information.
- It can remain dormant inside insulation, ballast, scar tissue, buried cable, animal implants, and sealed waste.
- Heat alone is unreliable unless the host material reaches destructive temperatures throughout.
- Accerisma disrupts growth but creates selection pressure and ecological injury.
- Analog equipment is not immune. It is merely easier for humans to understand when it behaves incorrectly.
- Apparent voices are usually sampled sounds replayed through available systems. Whether any late colony associates those sounds with human meaning remains unproven.

### Mutation and Adaptation Timeline

#### 1980s-1999: Persistent Substrate Phase

Wetwire-like behavior remains confined to tissue cultures, livestock systems, neural research, and waste streams. It is misclassified as prion disease, contamination, defective electronics, or industrial fraud.

#### 2000-2003: Urban Nest Phase

Colonies join biological material to hospital wiring, home medical devices, sewage systems, and industrial controls. They reproduce routine sounds and equipment states. Most growth remains inside structures.

#### 2004-2012: Environmental Carrier Phase

Wetwire adapts to livestock, scavengers, stormwater, wet soil, root systems, and transport corridors. Animal hosts are usually carriers or altered sensory organisms, not aggressive infected beasts.

#### 2013-2023: Dormancy and Wirelung Phase

Surviving strains become quieter. Microscopic conductive fibrosis appears in some exposed lungs. Dormant colonies persist behind repaired walls and inside legacy equipment. Accerisma-resistant niches develop.

#### 2024-2037: Pandemic Exploitation Phase

Overloaded hospitals, morgues, emergency housing, remote monitoring, and abandoned facilities create unprecedented habitat. Wetwire spreads beneath the noise of other disease and emergency policy.

#### 2038-2047: Fortress Equilibrium

Fortress systems reduce uncontrolled urban spread. Wetwire survives in dead districts, sealed utility spaces, contaminated watersheds, and lightly governed territory. It becomes geographically uneven rather than universally present.

#### 2048-2058: Legacy Network Phase

Old sealed colonies reconnect through renovated rail, power, and communications systems. The 2048 European rail failure proves that individually compliant institutions can collectively reopen a nest.

#### 2059-2078: Red Ecology Phase

Large forest, wetland, tunnel, and ruin colonies become semi-stable ecologies. Some produce motile carrier forms or defensive worker colonies. Most remain inside permanent exclusion zones. War damage and neglected infrastructure create new outbreaks before the Exchange.

### Horror by Environment

Wetwire scenes should preserve the logic of the location rather than repeating one creature encounter.

| Environment | Wetwire Behavior | Human Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apartment complex | Doorbells, music lessons, heating cycles, registration chips, and lift calls repeat after residents leave | Families cannot prove whether familiar sounds indicate survivors, recordings, or a building pattern |
| Office building | Badge readers admit employees who are absent; meeting-room displays wake after power isolation; elevators deliver wet, empty cars | Workers realize the building has learned schedules management treated as productivity data |
| Hospital | Oxygen alarms, code calls, dialysis rhythms, tissue waste, and old service cavities become one habitat | Every lure resembles a real emergency responders are trained not to ignore |
| Subway | Empty trains complete routes, ballast carries dormant growth, and platform boards list sealed stations | Completion signals become suspect and evacuation can spread the colony through the network |
| Sewer | Flow maps change, sealed tunnels exhale, pumps work against gravity, and locator tags move through water | A city must choose between flooding inhabited districts and preserving an uncertain containment boundary |
| Forest | Roots, fungal webs, wet soil, animal implants, and abandoned telecom cable form a slow network | Wildlife behaves strangely without becoming an attacking army; the landscape becomes difficult to map or burn cleanly |
| Abandoned city | Weather, collapsed utilities, scavengers, plants, and multiple dormant colonies form a mature red ecology | Salvagers encounter a place that is neither dead nor recoverable and must decide what objects justify entry |

## II. Wetwire Containment Task Force

### Formation and Mandate

The Wetwire Containment Task Force, usually called the WCT, begins in 2001 as a shared protocol among public-health, fire, military engineering, transit, and utility teams. It becomes a treaty-backed network in 2006.

The WCT is not a world army. National and regional commands retain legal authority. The WCT supplies:

- common zone definitions;
- responder certification;
- evidence and sample protocols;
- isolated communications standards;
- cross-border incident teams;
- burn authorization procedures;
- survivor and property documentation;
- an archive designed to outlast dissolved governments.

Its permanent tension is between rescue and severance. Rescue teams assume a person or district may still be recoverable. Severance teams assume delay may connect the infestation to a larger system.

### Command Structure

Each major incident has two authorities:

- a **Civil Incident Commander**, responsible for life safety, evacuation, medical decisions, and public record;
- a **Containment Commander**, responsible for isolation, utilities, perimeter security, and destructive action.

A terminal Vulcanite burn normally requires both signatures plus an engineering or medical witness. Emergency severance can proceed with one commander when a live connection threatens another populated zone, but the decision enters automatic review.

WCT regional sectors report to national commands. The international secretariat maintains standards and archives but cannot order a sovereign government to surrender a city.

### Five-Person Fire Team: Kiln Nine

Kiln Nine becomes the manuscript's recurring containment squad.

#### Commander Adjoa Mensah

Civil-defense officer and incident commander. She believes uncertainty must be documented before it is acted upon, but she will order destruction when delay creates a wider route. Her private fear is that repeated emergency judgment will make her unable to recognize ordinary risk.

#### Dr. Leila Varga-Sayegh

Medical and environmental investigator. Leila identifies exposure, survivor viability, and ecological consequence. She refuses to let negative Wetwire tests erase injuries caused by containment.

#### Elias Velez

Infrastructure specialist recruited from hospital maintenance. He reads buildings through cleaning schedules, repair scars, unofficial access routes, and the spaces no department claims. He carries paper plans and chalk.

#### Rafi Santos

Entry, rescue, and burn technician. Rafi is competent, physically careful, and psychologically dependent on rituals that separate home from contaminated work. Accerisma exposure and AccerStick use eventually damage his lungs.

#### Noa Grunwald

Systems and drone operator. Noa builds isolated sensor packages, compares active readings against passive instruments, and distrusts any display that cannot be traced to a physical source. Their central conflict is deciding when machines are protecting the team and when the team is merely following a pattern the nest can observe.

Veterinarian Asha Raman serves as a recurring ecological liaison rather than a permanent team member.

### Training

WCT qualification includes:

- hospital and utility anatomy;
- confined-space rescue;
- contamination medicine;
- manual mapping;
- evidence preservation;
- isolated radio procedure;
- drone loss drills;
- Accerisma handling;
- Vulcanite burn safety;
- survivor de-escalation;
- legal documentation for destroyed homes and businesses;
- search-pattern randomization;
- recognizing when familiar sounds are environmental lures.

Responders train to stop when a site begins matching them. Movement, radio calls, and tool activation can become input.

### Equipment and Uniforms

WCT uniforms are layered, repairable, and visibly institutional rather than theatrical:

- charcoal or ochre sealed overalls;
- replaceable outer panels;
- nonconductive fasteners;
- clear faceplates with no network-dependent display;
- colored shoulder tabs for medical, engineering, entry, and command roles;
- handwritten name panels because electronic identity systems fail;
- exposed mechanical pressure gauges beside digital monitors.

Standard equipment includes passive thermal film, chemical strips, fiber scopes, chalk, paper plans, isolated cameras, short-range sacrificial drones, rescue lines, Accerisma foam, cutting tools, sealed evidence tubes, and two-part Vulcanite lances for authorized burns.

Vehicles include:

- sealed response vans called **mules**;
- heavy burn tenders carrying separated Vulcanite precursors;
- rail response cars with independent air and power;
- low-profile inspection crawlers;
- disposable aerial drones;
- mobile decontamination and survivor-record units.

### Doctrine

WCT doctrine uses six verbs:

1. **See:** compare active systems with passive observation.
2. **Separate:** isolate power, water, data, air, traffic, and human movement.
3. **Still:** stop routine machinery and search patterns to reveal what continues.
4. **Recover:** remove people, records, samples, and irreplaceable objects where feasible.
5. **Sever:** cut physical links before treating the nest.
6. **Record:** preserve who decided, who was displaced, what was destroyed, and what remains uncertain.

The last step exists because early containment erased victims a second time through missing records.

### Famous Operations

- **Saint Agnes, 2000:** first recognized hospital outbreak and origin of Kiln Nine.
- **Northbridge Severance, 2001:** first public high-rise Vulcanite burn after a nest enters lift shafts and water risers.
- **Morrow Exchange, 2007:** rail freight terminal saved by sacrificing two warehouses and three kilometers of track.
- **Harbor Lantern, 2013:** contaminated ship burned outside a floating sterilization port after its automated manifest continues transmitting from an empty bridge.
- **Nine Platforms, 2048:** legacy rail colony reopens beneath a European immigration station.
- **Red Pine Perimeter, 2061:** a forest exclusion zone is expanded after a colony begins using root systems and abandoned telecom cable as one network.

## III. Vulcanite Program

### Discovery

Vulcanite begins as an attempt to make Accerisma remain on wet vertical surfaces. Researchers combine a fictional reactive carrier, an oxidizing gel, and a visible tracer package. When mixed at the nozzle, the compound adheres to contaminated material and sustains destructive heat even in flooded or oxygen-poor cavities.

Its green-white flame becomes iconic. The color is partly intentional, allowing teams to distinguish active Vulcanite from ordinary fire, and partly produced by metals accumulated inside Wetwire nests.

Vulcanite is not clean fire. It is controlled ruin.

### Manufacturing and Storage

No facility stores active Vulcanite in bulk. Two precursor streams are manufactured separately, shipped in armored containers, and combined only in a certified emitter or demolition charge.

Plants require:

- isolated production lines;
- continuous air and water monitoring;
- nonshared drainage;
- independent labor inspectors;
- automatic precursor dumping into separate neutralization beds;
- paper batch records stored off-site.

The most valuable black-market product is not active Vulcanite but stolen precursor, false batch certification, or emitter valves.

### Hazards

Vulcanite can:

- consume oxygen and kill survivors in adjacent spaces;
- weaken concrete and structural steel;
- aerosolize toxic metals and treatment residues;
- spread burning gel through drains;
- destroy evidence needed to understand an outbreak;
- create chemically sterile ground;
- continue below visible surfaces after the flame appears extinguished.

WCT doctrine forbids using it as an ordinary weapon. Governments and private forces violate that doctrine during later corridor and corporate wars.

### Incineration Doctrine

A lawful burn requires:

- physical severance from active utilities;
- evacuation or documented determination that rescue is impossible;
- mapping of drains, ventilation, fuel, and neighboring foundations;
- perimeter air monitoring;
- independent confirmation of the target boundary;
- a post-burn watch lasting longer than the visible fire;
- public property and casualty accounting.

The doctrine prefers small sacrificial cuts over district-scale burns. Political leaders often prefer one large burn because it produces a clearer television image and fewer continuing obligations.

### Deployment

Vulcanite is deployed through:

- hand lances for small cavities;
- crawler-mounted emitters;
- burn-tender hoses;
- sealed building charges;
- rail-mounted corridor systems;
- shipboard pods used at sterilization anchorages;
- aerial drops only over evacuated permanent exclusion zones.

### Famous Burns

- **Northbridge Severance, 2001:** forty-two-story residential tower destroyed after Wetwire joins lift, water, heat, and rail systems. Three residents remain unresolved, establishing named casualty accounting in later WCT law.
- **Morrow Exchange Cut, 2007:** two freight warehouses and three kilometers of rail sacrificed to prevent a colony from entering a regional signal network. The route reopens, but neighborhoods outside the protected freight allocation remain short of food.
- **Harbor Lantern, 2013:** an automated cargo vessel is burned at a sterilization anchorage after it continues transmitting crew reports from an empty bridge. The owner's insurer litigates the cargo loss for twenty-one years.
- **Red Pine Firebreak, 2061:** aerial and crawler burns widen a forest exclusion belt after roots and abandoned telecom cable begin functioning as one colony. Smoke and runoff close two nominally clean towns downwind.

## IV. Quarantine Geography

### Green Zones

Green zones have no active findings and functioning inspection, air, water, waste, and reporting systems. Green does not mean untouched. It means monitored and connected to a response system.

### Yellow Zones

Yellow zones contain unresolved signals, recent exposure, damaged containment, or infrastructure that cannot be fully inspected. Movement is permitted through controlled routes. Residents face repeated scans, insurance penalties, and employment restrictions.

### Black Zones

Black zones contain active colonies or infrastructure behavior that cannot be bounded. Entry is limited to rescue, sampling, severance, or burn teams. Property rights are suspended but not automatically extinguished, creating decades of legal claims.

### Permanent Exclusion Zones

Permanent exclusion zones, often called red ground or dead districts, are places where removal would cost more lives and contamination than isolation. Some remain inhabited at their edges by salvagers, displaced owners, unauthorized religious communities, and workers maintaining the walls.

### Walls and Belts

Fortifications are layered systems:

- outer observation and burn strip;
- drainage and root barrier;
- vehicle ditch or rail cut;
- concrete, earth, or composite wall;
- inspection road;
- sensor masts and passive sampling lines;
- inner buffer with no residences;
- controlled gates connected to quarantine stations.

Typical walls are 20-60 meters high. The 700-900 meter figure used in public graphics refers to total belt depth. Terrain, water, and existing structures matter more than height.

Walls fail through neglected drains, corrupt inspection, improvised utility crossings, wildlife movement, and the political temptation to build impressive surfaces while underfunding maintenance.

### Drone Surveillance

Drones observe heat, gas, movement, vegetation change, and structural deformation. Because Wetwire can exploit repeated routes and networked control, WCT doctrine rotates flight patterns, uses one-way data collection where possible, and compares drone feeds with passive ground stations.

No-recovery rules apply to drones lost inside mature zones. Salvagers break them regularly.

## V. Fortress Civilization

### Fortress City Layout

Fortress cities are old cities rebuilt into defensible systems, not uniform new arcologies.

A mature fortress usually contains:

- a protected civic and medical core;
- residential ventilation districts;
- food-production roofs, courtyards, fungal farms, and greenhouse belts;
- utility islands capable of disconnecting from one another;
- sealed hospitals with dirty-side and clean-side access;
- gate districts for customs, quarantine, labor, and temporary housing;
- a logistics ring connected to rail and convoy yards;
- an outer maintenance and burn belt;
- memorial walls listing people lost to outbreaks, gate refusal, and containment action.

Population ranges from fortress towns under one hundred thousand to metropolitan systems containing several million people. Few are self-sufficient. The claim of self-sufficiency is usually political advertising.

### Government

Emergency continuity councils initially combine mayors, health officers, utility chiefs, security commanders, labor representatives, and major food or housing providers.

Over time, three patterns appear:

- councils return authority to ordinary municipal government;
- councils become unelected permanent executives;
- cities acquire treaty powers over corridors and function as city-states while remaining nominally inside a nation.

Gate control gives fortress governments power over movement, work, family reunification, and citizenship. The most consequential officials are often not governors but shift supervisors authorized to admit one vehicle or reject one family.

### Daily Life

Residents live by air cycles, inspection schedules, corridor arrivals, and wall maintenance:

- schools use airlocks and ventilation cohorts;
- apartment blocks publish filter life beside rent notices;
- weddings pause for sterilization before guests embrace;
- markets price goods by the route that carried them;
- children play scanner games and trade inspection seals;
- hospitals separate treatment eligibility from district residency;
- religious practice adapts to sealed rooms, limited gatherings, and shared purification space;
- recreation concentrates on roofs, interior courts, train halls, and the rare days an outer park opens.

The psychological divide is generational. Adults remember walls as a temporary sacrifice. Children born behind them understand open access as an old form of recklessness.

### Entry Procedure

Entry commonly requires:

- identity and route history;
- cargo and vehicle seal verification;
- passive temperature and chemical screening;
- skin, eye, and respiratory inspection;
- isolation of personal electronics;
- a timed observation period;
- secondary review for inconsistent records;
- assignment to a ventilation and residence district.

False positives cost jobs, housing, and family access. False negatives can close a city.

### Air, Food, and Utilities

Air systems use district isolation, replaceable membranes, positive-pressure shelters, and manual closure. Food systems combine imported grain with greenhouse vegetables, fungal protein, cultured tissue, preserved goods, and household allotments.

Water, power, waste, and communications are divided into islands that can be disconnected without killing the entire city. This resilience is expensive and politically unequal. Wealthier districts receive quieter filters, larger reserves, and faster repairs.

## VI. Corridor Civilization

### The Corridor System

By 2006, unrestricted highway travel is no longer reliable across many regions. Civilization reorganizes around inspected routes connecting fortress cities, ports, farms, mines, and power systems.

Corridors are classified as:

- **Clear:** continuously monitored, regularly maintained, and supported by rapid response;
- **Controlled:** open under escort, schedule, or seasonal restriction;
- **Conditional:** legally open but subject to local closure and uncertain inspection;
- **Perilous:** maintained only for strategic cargo or evacuation;
- **Lost:** no recognized authority can guarantee the route.

The final open highway does not close everywhere on one date. The phrase refers to the last major route in a region losing unrestricted civilian status. Thereafter, nearly every long journey requires a manifest, escort, corridor pass, or willingness to leave the law.

### Continental Corridor Authority

The Continental Corridor Authority, or CCA, is founded through the 2006 Safe Transit Accords. It is an international standards and engineering body with regional commands.

The CCA includes:

- a Route Standards Board;
- an Engineering Corps;
- a Rail and Bridge Inspectorate;
- a Convoy Coordination Service;
- a Quarantine and Customs Office;
- an Emergency Operations Center;
- corridor courts for cargo, delay, contamination, and salvage disputes;
- a public ledger of closures and inspection failures.

The CCA cannot command national armies. It can withdraw certification, insurance recognition, bridge support, and interoperable scheduling. Those powers often matter more.

Funding comes from freight tonnage fees, state contributions, emergency bonds, and corporate access charges. This makes the authority useful and vulnerable to capture.

### Mega-Train Network

"Mega-train" is an informal name for long, modular consists built to move enough cargo and people to justify opening a protected route. Most are two to six kilometers long and divided into independently sealed segments.

Types include:

- heavy freight trains carrying grain, fuel, filters, water systems, and industrial parts;
- passenger trains with quarantine classes rather than ordinary seating classes;
- armored trains carrying repair crews and perimeter defense;
- medical trains with surgery, dialysis, isolation, and oxygen production;
- refugee trains designed for high capacity but repeatedly used beyond specification;
- military trains capable of moving autonomous platforms, armor, and field hospitals.

Train control uses segmented networks, physical interlocks, independent brakes, and paper consist records. Conductors remain important because a verified human account can resolve contradictions between systems.

### Corridor Stations

Major stations combine:

- customs and immigration;
- quarantine screening;
- cargo sterilization;
- markets and currency exchange;
- hotels divided by clearance status;
- hospitals and respiratory clinics;
- bonded warehouses;
- repair shops;
- chapels, prayer rooms, and secular memorial spaces;
- temporary courts;
- refugee registration and labor offices.

Stations develop their own slang, food, law, and reputation. A person may spend weeks inside one without entering the city it serves.

### Ocean Convoys

Ocean trade survives through escort fleets, sterilization anchorages, floating transfer ports, and containers that never open between certified facilities.

The principal hazards are piracy, political seizure, false clean seals, contaminated ballast, abandoned automated ships, and storms that force vessels into uninspected harbors.

Cargo cranes avoid direct ship contact where possible. Crew transfer occurs through quarantine tenders. A contaminated ship may remain anchored for months while owners, ports, insurers, and governments argue over who must burn it.

## VII. Biotechnology and Counter-Systems

### EVO-5 Program

EVO-5 stands for **Evolutionary Vector Optimization, Platform Five**.

The program begins in the 2030s as a multinational effort to create tissue capable of rapid repair under chemical, radiological, and infectious stress. Earlier platforms either caused uncontrolled growth, failed outside laboratory conditions, triggered immune collapse, or could not be manufactured consistently.

EVO-5 uses modular gene-control packages, visible metabolic reporters, and externally supplied maintenance compounds. Its bright green laboratory appearance comes from the reporter system, not from the finished implants.

#### Civil and Military Uses

- accelerated clotting and wound closure;
- temporary lung and skin repair;
- radiation-injury mitigation;
- living contamination sensors;
- engineered grafts for burn victims;
- veterinary repair tissue;
- battlefield blood and organ support;
- tissue interfaces for prosthetics and armor.

#### Containment Failures

EVO-5 failures do not create a second Wetwire. They produce local overgrowth, immune misdirection, metabolic dependency, or tissues that continue a repair program after the injury has ended.

The worst cases include:

- a regenerative lung graft that seals healthy alveoli as if they were wounds;
- a clotting package that interprets repeated muscle stress as hemorrhage;
- a laboratory reservoir whose reporter organisms colonize coolant lines;
- Wetwire contamination of discarded EVO-5 scaffold, creating a colony unusually tolerant of Accerisma.

#### Black Projects

Classified programs investigate:

- long-duration metabolic suppression;
- command-activated tissue failure;
- inherited maintenance traits;
- rapid-grown replacement organs;
- engineered combat endurance;
- synthetic animal development.

Public records confirm reproducible principles before they confirm reliable prototypes. Many claimed capabilities remain years or decades from field use.

### Military Biotechnology

Military biotechnology focuses less on super-soldiers than on logistics:

- blood substitutes and field-grown grafts;
- engineered animals that detect chemical or electrical anomalies;
- living sensor mats for water and soil;
- microbial fuel and waste systems;
- rapid vaccine and antitoxin production;
- temporary protection against heat, hypoxia, and radiation;
- battlefield medicine that keeps wounded personnel alive long enough to reach a train or fortress hospital.

The ethical danger is maintenance dependence. A soldier whose altered body requires proprietary compounds remains tied to the institution that supplies them.

### Archosaur Program

The Archosaur Program develops synthetic reptile-avian working animals from extant genomes and reconstructed developmental pathways. It does not clone extinct dinosaurs.

The animals are designed for heat tolerance, rough terrain, strong olfaction, directional electrical sensitivity, and the ability to recover on food and water where battery supply is uncertain.

Two field classes reach limited deployment:

- **Striders:** 250-450 kilogram sensor and cargo animals, usually unmanned, able to carry medical packs, communications relays, or a remote observation mount.
- **Bastions:** multi-ton engineering and recovery animals used for towing, debris movement, and intimidation. They are rare, expensive, difficult to transport, and more useful in propaganda than combat.

Handlers train with one animal for years. Imprinting, scent commands, tactile harness signals, and short-range machine cues are combined because network control is unreliable around Wetwire.

Archosaurs require veterinary teams, feed, water, rest, transport clearances, and waste control. They panic, become ill, and disobey. These limits keep them from replacing vehicles.

Their best military roles are:

- route sensing;
- casualty extraction;
- load carrying in dust, mud, or electromagnetic disruption;
- tracking contaminated wildlife;
- perimeter patrol;
- recovery work where a stalled machine cannot be abandoned.

Mounting large weapons directly on an animal proves inaccurate and cruel. Later doctrine uses stabilized sensor or defensive stations controlled by nearby personnel.

### Western Robotics

UAR and Europolis forces answer military biotechnology with modular robotics:

- low walkers for rubble and stair access;
- autonomous freight tractors;
- unmanned armored vehicles;
- aerial and ground reconnaissance drones;
- isolated command systems;
- robotic bridge and rail repair;
- logistics machines able to operate inside chemically dangerous zones.

Robots are easier to standardize and politically easier to lose. They depend on batteries, spare parts, software trust, and communications. Wetwire turns those dependencies into vulnerabilities.

The robotics-versus-biotechnology competition is not a simple contest between advanced and primitive systems. Each side is choosing which supply chain it believes will survive.

## VIII. Warfare

### Wetwire Wars

The Wetwire Wars are not one declared war. The term covers decades of city cleansing, rescue operations, burn campaigns, quarantine enforcement, and failed containment.

Their central moral injury is that destructive decisions are often reasonable and still wrong for someone trapped inside them.

### Corporate Wars

Corporate conflicts grow around:

- mining and water rights;
- chemical-treatment patents;
- fortress construction;
- corridor security;
- clean-cargo certification;
- salvage ownership;
- orbital communications and remote-sensing access.

Private armies rarely declare war. They enforce contracts until two contracts require opposite control of the same bridge, mine, harbor, launch site, or data network.

### Corridor Wars

Corridor conflicts focus on systems that cannot move:

- bridge spans;
- rail switches;
- sterilization plants;
- water crossings;
- mountain tunnels;
- inspection databases;
- convoy fuel stores.

Sabotage often aims to make a route uncertifiable rather than physically impassable. One false contamination report can divert trade for months.

### Ocean Convoy Wars

Escort fleets defend food, fuel, chemical precursors, and machine parts. Pirates seek cargo, but governments and corporations also seize vessels through inspection law.

The most feared event is a clean convoy discovering contamination after entering a receiving harbor. The port must choose between closing itself and sending the ship back toward communities that no longer recognize its papers.

## IX. Society

### Refugees

Refugees move through registration systems that treat contamination history, labor skill, family ties, and route sponsorship as parts of identity.

Camps near gates become permanent districts. Residents work in cleaning, sorting, agriculture, wall repair, and freight handling while waiting for status. Children born in transit may hold a station record but no national birth certificate.

Citizenship reforms later grow from practical rules created by clerks, nurses, and corridor courts trying to make those lives legible.

### Black Markets

Major black-market goods include:

- medicine and oxygen;
- clean filters;
- fuel and battery cells;
- forged route and quarantine certificates;
- salvaged electronics;
- stolen Accerisma;
- Vulcanite precursor and emitter parts;
- Wetwire samples sold to researchers, cults, or weapons programs;
- weapons;
- unregistered passage through maintenance tunnels.

The black market is both predatory and necessary. It sells counterfeit medicine and also supplies districts formal systems have abandoned.

### Salvagers

Salvage crews enter yellow and black zones for records, machine tools, medicine, family objects, and rare materials.

Professional guilds require:

- sealed equipment;
- analog navigation;
- route deposits;
- recovery shares for property claimants;
- quarantine bonds;
- a rule against retrieving lost drones;
- testimony from at least two crew members before declaring a person dead.

Salvagers often know more about dead cities than the governments that still claim them.

### Corridor Merchants

Corridor merchants build reputation through arrival, seal integrity, fair weight, and willingness to carry low-profit essentials.

They use multiple currencies, freight credit, commodity notes, and personal guarantees. Caravan law develops around delay, rescue obligation, contaminated cargo, abandoned vehicles, and the order in which scarce goods move.

A merchant known to abandon drivers loses access faster than one convicted of ordinary smuggling.

## X. Recurring Civilian Viewpoints

### Mara Keene, Corridor Engineer

Mara repairs freight control, bridge power, and rail signaling across divided American jurisdictions. Her goal is to preserve worker autonomy inside systems every faction wants to command. She is threatened by security contracts, incompatible law, and the medical provider that treats political noncompliance as a health risk.

### Amina Mbele, Mega-Train Conductor

Amina operates a long Alkebulan supply consist called *Common Measure*. She is responsible for cargo, passengers, quarantine seals, and the authority to stop when political commanders demand movement. Her reputation rests on accounting for every sealed car and every person she accepts, including those the route system no longer recognizes.

### Governor Evelyn Price, Fortress Solace

Price governs a Great Lakes fortress built around rail, water treatment, and hospital capacity. She begins as an elected continuity mayor and remains in office through repeated emergency extensions. She keeps the city alive while slowly becoming unable to imagine surrendering gate authority.

### Sana Belmonte, Salvage Captain

Sana leads the Carthaginian-registered salvage vessel and ground crew *Patient Star*. She recovers archives, machine tools, and personal property from abandoned ports and cities. She rejects relic hunting and insists that a family photograph may have a stronger claim than a laboratory contract.

## XII. Continuity Rules

1. Wetwire horror should begin with familiar systems behaving slightly wrong.
2. Most infestations do not produce mobile attackers.
3. Every burn creates displaced people, records, claims, and ecological damage.
4. Accerisma remains necessary despite its cost.
5. Vulcanite is never described as clean or consequence-free.
6. Fortress cities depend on corridors. None is an effortless self-sufficient utopia.
7. Walls create safety and political power at the same time.
8. Mega-trains are large because opening routes is expensive, not because scale alone is impressive.
9. Archosaurs remain rare working animals with handlers and logistics.
10. Robotics and biotechnology both fail in ways produced by their supply chains.
11. New organizations should be shown through the person at the gate, switch, clinic, or maintenance hatch.
12. The Exchange does not erase Wetwire. It destroys many of the systems keeping red zones bounded.
