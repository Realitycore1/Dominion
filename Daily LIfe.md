## Summary

Daily life inside GalFed is safe, standardized, medically supported, and quietly monitored. Most citizens experience the Federation as a competent civilization that keeps food moving, transit safe, diseases controlled, and history available.

## Details

Common features of life:

- Universal disaster education.
- Routine cognition health checks.
- Public archive access through approved memory interfaces.
- Subsidized translation and interspecies mediation.
- Corridor travel by license, class, and risk status.
- Civic rituals remembering the Aether Singularity.
- Strong trust in relief fleets and restoration clinics.
- Deep unease around silence, isolation, and unmonitored Aether experiments.

Most citizens are not oppressed in an obvious way. They have housing, medicine, work placement, education, and legal recourse. Many are grateful. GalFed saved their worlds or their grandparents' worlds.

The pressure appears when someone refuses classification. A citizen who rejects archive submission, avoids cognition checks, uses forbidden language codes, or questions Founder authority may first encounter care teams, then counselors, then legal review, then containment.

Everyday saying:

"A free mind is a tended mind."

Dissident reply:

"A tended mind is not always free."
