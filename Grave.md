Core Bias:

Memorial permanence and death-bound continuity

This is one of the most thematically powerful Stone subtypes.

Reality preserves not just structure, but:
	•	the dead
	•	memory
	•	legacy
	•	endings made permanent

Traits:
	•	tomb architecture
	•	funerary landscapes
	•	memorialized civilizations
	•	preserved remains
	•	continuity through death and remembrance

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	history is a mausoleum
	•	civilization survives as monument and grave
	•	the dead still load-bear the living world

Common Expressions:
	•	necropolis worlds
	•	funerary empires
	•	ancestral archive systems
	•	memorial machine civilizations
	•	death-preservation cultures

Failure Mode:

Necrotic fixation
The basin preserves the dead so well it cannot fully support the living.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	tomb cosmology
	•	ancestral civilizations
	•	Yondor-adjacent themes
	•	post-collapse memorial worlds
