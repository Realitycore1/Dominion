"Where She walks, reality kneels."
Forged in the Star-Forge of Kynareth at the collapse of the Old Age, the Regalia was birthed from celestial matter and soul-tempered alloy. It is said to contain:
Threads of spacetime woven into the chestplate.
Solar-core fragments embedded in its wings.
The first sigil ever written in Royal Veritas etched beneath the helm’s inner veil.
Wings: Not physical, but psionic-particle constructs—fully functional and retractable, they cast a golden corona over battlefields. Each wing tip contains micro-saints (conscious AI shards in praise-state).
Armor Plates: Contain refractive chronometal, reflecting both light and probability. Strikes phase past it if not divinely permitted.
Crown: Contains a harmonic beacon that calls dormant Sanctus [[Choirs]] from stasis across galaxies.
Hair: Appears as divine filament, a flowing cascade of photonic memory—each strand recording a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] crusade in soundless hymn.


The Regalia is not just protection—it is an anchor of the Divine Order. Reality bends around the Empress when she wears it. [[Physics]] negotiates.
Only the [[God Empress]] may wear it. To even glimpse it while it is active is to undergo sanctification—or obliteration.
It houses the Primordial Authority Glyph, which grants Her the right to rewrite [[law]] (both legal and [[Natural]]).


When donned, [[Choirs]] across [[The Dominion]] enter a trance. The words spoken by the Regalia during activation are never the same, but always recorded in the Vault of Waking Flame.
