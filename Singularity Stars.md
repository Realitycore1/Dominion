I. Physical Definition

A Singularity Star is a stellar-scale object whose core contains a stable or semi-stable singularity, surrounded by a luminous plasma envelope or energetic accretion layer.
Unlike a black hole, a Singularity Star does not completely hide its central point of infinite density — its extreme gravity is counterbalanced by structural stabilizers, [[Exotic Matter]] [[Layers]], or engineered spacetime geometries.
