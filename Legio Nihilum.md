## **XIII. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Nihilum**

**Title:** Legio Nihilum – The Voidborn

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through nothingness, erasure, and absolute negation. Legio Nihilum embody the cold [[Silence]] of [[The Void]], unmaking all before them. Their creed: *“From nothing, into nothing.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Nihilum**

* **Silhouette:** Tall and featureless, armor design stripped of ornament, smooth and angular as if sculpted from absence itself. Their presence feels incomplete, like gaps in reality.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit null-fields that dampen sound and light, creating zones of [[Silence]] and shadow around each [[Warrior]].
* **Coloration:** Pure matte black, darker than surrounding shadow, with faint edges shimmering in negative light. No color save for faint, shifting void patterns that resemble collapsing [[Stars]].
* **Helmet:** Faceless helms, smooth with only a narrow slit of shifting voidlight where eyes should be. Looking into the visor feels like staring into a black hole.
* **Specialization:** Null Cores—reactor [[Systems]] that absorb energy attacks and distort reality fields. Capable of destabilizing nearby matter and shorting enemy power [[Systems]].

### **Legion Standard Arms – Nihilum Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Rifle and Heavy Rifle forms for cold precision. Weapons appear as seamless extensions of [[The Void]], with no glow save for faint distortions.
* **Modification:** Weapons [[Fire]] black-pulse projectiles and void-lances that seem to erase matter, leaving burn-scars like collapsed space.
* **Aesthetic:** Featureless matte black, outlined only by void distortions. Conduits shimmer faintly in inverted light.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Nihilum are shock troops of negation, deployed to annihilate high-value targets and erase strongholds with cold efficiency.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their presence alone inspires existential dread—foes describe them as “the [[Soldiers]] who are not there.”
* **Legacy:** Legio Nihilum is remembered as the Voidborn—heralds of nothingness who leave behind not ruin, but erasure, as though their enemies never existed.
