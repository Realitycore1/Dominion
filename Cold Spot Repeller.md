A large basin of repulsion (divergent flow region) in the reconstructed peculiar-velocity field of the nearby Universe, lying roughly toward the CMB Cold Spot direction. In flow maps it behaves like a [[Giant]] underdensity (supervoid) that pushes matter away.  ￼

How it was found.
Using Cosmicflows-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] galaxy distances/peculiar velocities and a Wiener-filter reconstruction out to z≈0.1, researchers identified two major repellers: the known [[Dipole Repeller]] and a second one—the Cold Spot Repeller—approximately aligned with the CMB Cold Spot. Streamlines diverge from this region, consistent with a vast void.  ￼ ￼

Why it matters.
If real, such an underdensity could contribute (at least partly) to the CMB Cold Spot via the integrated Sachs–Wolfe/Rees–Sciama effect; later analyses note it as the most prominent repulsive basin in the z≈0.1 web, strengthening the supervoid link.  ￼

Caveats.
The connection to the CMB Cold Spot remains suggestive, not decisive: reconstructions depend on data coverage/priors, and independent surveys still debate how much of the Cold Spot a supervoid can explain.
