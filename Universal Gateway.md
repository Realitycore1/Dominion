## Details

A Universal Gateway is a fictional
device or [[Technology Portal|technology]] that allows for instantaneous travel between different realities or dimensions in science fiction. The Universal Gateway typically functions by creating a wormhole or dimensional rift that allows for travel between two or more points in space-[[Time]]. It may be powered by advanced technologies such as [[Exotic Matter]], gravitational manipulation, or other esoteric means. The Universal Gateway may be designed as a stationary device or a portable device, allowing for flexible use and transport. In some stories, it may be powered by a central authority or governing body, which controls access to the gateway for security or regulatory reasons. Others suggest that it may be a free-for-all, with anyone who can operate the [[Technology Portal|technology]] being able to use it to travel between realities. The Universal Gateway may also have a variety of other functions beyond simply allowing for travel between dimensions. It may be capable of manipulating the fundamental laws of [[Physics]], creating [[Time]] paradoxes or even alternate timelines. Some stories may depict it as a source of great power or knowledge, with those who can access it gaining an advantage over their rivals or enemies. Overall, the Universal Gateway is a compelling and dynamic concept that explores the potential implications of travel between different dimensions and realities in science fiction settings.
