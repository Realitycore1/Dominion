Classification: Fey & Otherworldly Courts
Subtype: Harmonized Fey Sovereigns / Custodians of Living Order
Status: Active, Realm-Bound, Selectively Permeable

⸻

Core Description

The Seelie are a collective of fey entities organized into courts, bound by ancient compacts governing beauty, balance, reciprocity, and consequence. They appear radiant, graceful, and inviting—but this is a functional disguise, not kindness.

Their forms vary:
	•	humanoid figures of impossible symmetry,
	•	antlered nobles and winged courtiers,
	•	living silhouettes woven of leaves, moonlight, frost, or blossom.

They are not illusions.
They are optimized expressions of vitality and rule.

⸻

Ontological Nature

The Seelie arise where Vital Expanse, Oneiric resonance, and Echoic continuity stabilize into long-lived ecosystems of meaning.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Vital Expanse (balanced life-force)
	•	Secondary Influence: Oneiric (dream-symbol logic), Echoic (tradition)
	•	Realm Interface: The Bleeding Wilds (border regions), Dream-Wastes (controlled sectors)

They do not exist everywhere nature exists.
They exist where nature has rules.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Seelie cognition is deeply alien but highly structured.

They understand:
	•	symbolism,
	•	obligation,
	•	reciprocity,
	•	aesthetic law.

They do not understand:
	•	forgiveness without restitution,
	•	morality without consequence,
	•	intent divorced from action.

A Seelie who spares you has not shown mercy.
They have accepted a debt.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

The Seelie:
	•	maintain sacred groves, hidden courts, and living palaces,
	•	cultivate species, bloodlines, and myths,
	•	cull what disrupts long-term balance.

They intervene rarely—but decisively—when:
	•	planar saturation destabilizes life,
	•	Primordial residues corrupt ecosystems,
	•	mortal civilizations expand without restraint.

Their “kindness” often leads to:
	•	generations-long consequences,
	•	selective prosperity,
	•	or quiet extinction of threats.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Seelie interactions are ritualized and binding.

They may:
	•	bless lands,
	•	grant longevity,
	•	alter fate subtly.

But every gift carries:
	•	obligation,
	•	limitation,
	•	or future claim.

Breaking a Seelie compact is not punished immediately.
The punishment is perfectly timed.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Bound by ancient compacts they cannot violate
	•	Lose authority in Null-dominant or heavily industrialized zones
	•	Weaken when symbolism collapses into abstraction
	•	Cannot act directly in realms saturated with Malice

They are powerful—but not free.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Unseelie: Oppositional but necessary counterpart
	•	Kami: Respected equals in localized domains
	•	Domovoi & Brownies: Beneath Seelie notice unless boundaries are crossed
	•	Demons: Actively opposed
	•	Angels: Philosophical rivals—Grace vs Vital Balance

Seelie enforce living law.
Angels enforce transcendent law.

⸻

Variants & Courts
	•	Court of Spring: Growth, beginnings, fertility
	•	Court of Summer: Power, dominance, vitality
	•	Court of Autumn: Harvest, transition, decay
	•	Court of Winter: Preservation, silence, endurance

Seasonal courts rotate dominance, not allegiance.
