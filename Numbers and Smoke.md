Athens, 2037
[[Elena Volkova]] tapped her chalk against the board.
“The [[Shroud of Turin]] is not divine proof,” she told the classroom. “It is cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. A symbol, not a miracle.”

A hand shot up. A boy with sharp eyes asked, “But what if science proves it’s real?”

She hesitated. The room felt suddenly heavy. “Then it will still be a symbol,” she said. “But the meaning will belong to us, not to heaven.”

That night, she scrolled through headlines: Shroud confirmed first-century linen. Crowds wept in Rome. Militias formed in chatrooms. She set the chalk down and poured a drink.

She did as told. Each report bled into propaganda, her translations stripped of nuance, fed to crowds hungry for certainty.

One evening, she walked home past a parade. Secularists waved banners of the [[Pythagorean Tablets]], chant[[Europolis]]s if they were prayers. “Three, five, seven — the holy primes!”

Elena laughed, bitter. Even numbers burned like scripture now.

[[Europolis]], 2063
Bombs thundered in the east. Refugees crowded the streets, clutching relic tokens stamped on ration cards. Elena’s daughter, Sofia, grew restless, sketching graffiti across cracked walls: relic symbols twisted into warnings — a Menorah melting into a mushroom cloud, a hexagram split in half.

“Careful,” Elena warned. “They’ll arrest you.”

“Better they arrest me than believe this madness,” Sofia spat. “You tell us numbers are reason, Mama. But numbers are killing us too.”

Elena had no reply.

Athens, 2075
She stood in a crowd staring skyward as the ISS broke apart, [[Fire]] scattering across orbit like beads of glass. A voice shouted over the broadcast: “False science is destroyed! Heaven rejects idols!”

Around her, some wept, some cheered, some [[Europolis]]y. Elena only felt her stomach turn. The ISS had been the last place where reason still lived, the last fragile bridge between nations. Now it was debris, tumbling endlessly.

Sofia painted it that night on a wall: the station cracking apart, captioned: “We fell with it.”

⸻

[[Europolis]], 2078
The sirens screamed too late. Elena sat at her desk, pen scratching across paper. She wrote not [[Reports]], not propaganda, but testimony.

We made [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] into weapons. We made reason into scripture. We worshiped proof until it consumed us. And now we burn.

Outside, the sky turned white. She kept writing until the light took the ink, the page, the world.
