# (2997) Mass Driver and Skyhook Network

The Phaeton Program integrates Lunar mass drivers, terrestrial cargo accelerators, and rotating momentum-exchange skyhooks.

Lunar drivers move industrial cargo into cislunar assembly orbits. Earthside systems launch hardened cargo along controlled tracks. Short industrial drivers are not used for passengers.

Early skyhooks are rotating orbital tethers rather than ground-reaching elevators. They capture compatible vehicles at scheduled windows and transfer momentum into higher orbit.

The network lowers launch cost while creating choke points and kinetic risks. Cargo infrastructure can become a weapon through velocity, timing, or denial of access.
