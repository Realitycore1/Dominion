# (3120) Areopagus Station

Areopagus Station becomes the principal Colonial Union registry, market, arbitration venue, inspection exchange, and rescue-coordination node for the asteroid belt in 3120.

It records:

- prospecting and extraction claims;
- habitat charters and resident rolls;
- cargo custody and mass-driver declarations;
- rescue calls and salvage disputes;
- public ice and propellant reserves;
- inspection records and unresolved labor claims.

Areopagus is not the capital of a unified Belt government. Belt habitats elect their own councils, mobile crews change jurisdiction with their work, and many disputes concern whether the Union has authority over a claim at all.

Its power comes from recognition. Insurers, postal crews, courts, tugs, and markets use its records to decide which cargo can move and which settlement exists in law.

Its weakness is delay. A correct ruling may arrive after a habitat has moved, a company has dissolved, or the disputed water has already been consumed.
