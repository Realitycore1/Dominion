# (108) First Winter Away

## What Happened

The first winter away from New Aurora tested whether convoy settlements were communities or extended work camps. Heatline settlements, pressure stations, fungal basins, mobile shelters, and frontier camps survived through repair rituals, shared food, local warning systems, and improvised law.

## What Resulted

- Local hardship became local pride.
- Settlement stereotypes began forming.
- Children born or raised away from New Aurora treated regional hazards as normal.

## Long-Term Consequence

The first winter away taught frontier populations that New Aurora could help them, but it could not define them.
