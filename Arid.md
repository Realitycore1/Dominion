Core Bias:

Classical material scarcity

This is the baseline Desert subtype.

Reality behaves as though:
	•	energy
	•	matter
	•	life-support conditions
	•	or structural continuity

…are simply hard to sustain.

Traits:
	•	sparse life or infrastructure
	•	hard environmental conditions
	•	limited growth capacity
	•	highly adaptive survivors
	•	long barren intervals between viable nodes

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	dry planetary expanses
	•	hard vacuum survival corridors
	•	isolated worlds under stress
	•	ancient thin ecologies

Common Expressions:
	•	low-biomass verses
	•	sparse civilizations
	•	isolated city-worlds
	•	survivalist cultures

Failure Mode:

Total exhaustion
Nothing can regenerate fast enough.
