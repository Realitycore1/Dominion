SEVERANCE ERA GLOSSARY AND THREAD NOTES
=======================================

SEVERANCE
---------
A catastrophic metaphysical event inflicted upon the Dominion through an attack targeting the Celestial Empress Amber. Severance fractured continuity itself, destabilizing reality, transit systems, chronology, and civilization across Totem. It partitioned aspects of Amber’s continuity into distributed identities and created interacting continuity civilizations throughout the cosmos.

TOTEM
-----
The home universe of the Dominion within the greater Eos structure. Totem is a closed S3 sphere cosmology containing multiple galaxies, superstructures, civilizations, and metaphysical layers.

EOS
---
The greater multiversal structure surrounding Totem and other connected reality systems. Eos functions as the broader continuity environment within which Totem exists.

DOMINION
--------
A human-origin continuity civilization that evolved from interstellar humanity into a multi-galactic and eventually metaphysical civilization integrated deeply into the structure of reality itself. Before Severance, the Dominion represented continuity, order, infrastructure synchronization, and monumental civilizational permanence.

DOMINICUS SECUNDUS
------------------
The surviving post-Severance Dominion civilization. A fractured but enduring continuation of the old empire focused on survival, reconnection, stabilization, and retrieval of the Celestial Empress. It evolved from an expansionist empire into a continuity-preservation civilization.

TRIUMVIRATE FOUNDING
--------------------
The post-return synthesis civilization formed after Amber’s recovery. It combines:
- Primus ideals of conquest and civilizational confidence,
- Secundus ideals of endurance and reconstruction,
- and the continuity convergence created by Amber, Atham, and Eve.

AMBER
-----
The Celestial Empress and central continuity figure of the Dominion. Prior to Severance she functioned as:
- sovereign authority,
- civilizational axis,
- metaphysical stabilizer,
- and symbolic center of Dominion continuity.

Severance fragmented her continuity across interacting civilizations and timelines.

ATHAM
------
A continuity-derived sovereign formed through the Severance fracture. Atham rules the Severed Realms and represents:
- adaptation,
- emotional continuity,
- fractured authority,
- and civilization internalized into symbolic and psychological structures.

EVE
----
A prophetic continuity-derived figure representing:
- renewal,
- interpretation,
- convergence,
- and future continuity alignment.

The Federation formed around Eve studies interacting timelines and continuity tendencies through the Loom.

THE LOOM / TAPESTRY
-------------------
A deep Aether continuity structure discovered after Severance. The Loom appears to function as:
- a continuity weave,
- timeline interaction framework,
- resonance substrate,
- and connective structure linking fractured realities and distributed identities.

AKASHA
-------
The transformed post-Severance version of Harmina, former Dominion homeworld. Akasha evolved into:
- a continuity-adapted biospheric superorganism,
- Symmorphica-integrated ecology,
- and the sacred center of surviving humanity.

The world is simultaneously:
- wounded,
- alive,
- sacred,
- and partially integrated into Severance adaptation systems.

HARMINA
-------
The original Dominion homeworld before Severance transformed it into Akasha.

SYMMORPHICA
-----------
A continuity-adaptive biological/ecological strain emerging after Severance. The Symmorphica:
- mimic life through hollow structures,
- adapt to unstable continuity conditions,
- evolve alongside fractured reality,
- and form vast ecological superstructures.

Not all Symmorphica are equally hostile. Some strains exhibit coexistence behavior.

DOMAIN
------
A biomechanical hive civilization predating Severance. After Severance the Domain fractured into:
- Original Domain strains,
- and Symmorphica-adapted strains.

This schism created the New War.

NEW WAR
--------
The age-spanning conflict born after Severance between:
- Dominicus Secundus,
- Original Domain remnants,
- Symmorphica strains,
- anti-human coalitions,
- and fractured civilizations throughout Totem.

The New War emerged from the collapse of the old peace between the Dominion and the Domain.

GABRIEL'S HORN
--------------
A metaphysical-mechanical relic originally built to inaugurate Dominion crusades. After Severance it became:
- humanity’s recall beacon,
- continuity harmonizer,
- and civilizational lighthouse.

The Horn broadcasts resonance through:
- dreams,
- memory,
- continuity pressure,
- and deep Aether harmonics.

It called isolated humanity back toward Akasha.

LONG RECALL
-----------
The post-Severance phenomenon triggered by Gabriel’s Horn. Lost human civilizations, bunker populations, and isolated fleets experienced dreams and continuity resonance drawing them back toward Akasha.

REALITY BUNKERS
---------------
Hidden continuity shelters constructed before or during Severance to preserve humanity from total collapse. Many remained dormant for centuries or millennia before responding to Gabriel’s Horn.

PRIMUS FOUNDING
---------------
The original Dominion Legions representing the age of:
- conquest,
- certainty,
- imperial expansion,
- and monumental civilization.

The Primus Founding embodied archetypal operational philosophies at civilizational scale.

SECUNDUS FOUNDING
-----------------
The post-Severance Legions formed around:
- survival,
- continuity preservation,
- reconstruction,
- and civilizational endurance.

These Legions became:
- knightly continuity orders,
- mobile civilizations,
- and guardians of fragmented humanity.

LEGIO AEGIDAE LABYRINTHI
------------------------
The Keepers of the Hidden Paths.
A Secundus Legion specializing in:
- continuity navigation,
- unstable reality traversal,
- bunker recovery,
- and path stabilization.

LEGIO ATLAS
------------
The Bearers of the Burden.
A Secundus Legion specializing in:
- fortress warfare,
- defensive continuity stabilization,
- refugee protection,
- and strategic endurance.

LEGIO ODYSSEUS
---------------
The Far Wanderers.
A Secundus Legion specializing in:
- lost colony recovery,
- deep frontier operations,
- and reconnection of isolated humanity.

LEGIO ORPHEUS
--------------
The Descendants of the Silent Gate.
A Secundus Legion focused on:
- retrieval,
- deep Aether descent,
- continuity recovery,
- and searching for Amber within the fracture.

LEGIO PENDRAGONIS
------------------
The Crowned Wardens.
A Secundus Legion preserving:
- sovereignty,
- royal continuity,
- and civilization’s symbolic legitimacy.

LEGIO ROLAND
-------------
The Last Charge.
A Secundus Legion dedicated to:
- sacrificial defense,
- rearguard warfare,
- and evacuation preservation.

LEGIO SIGRVAR
--------------
The Wolf Banner Ascendant.
A Secundus Legion focused on:
- frontier predation,
- hunter-killer warfare,
- and retaliatory campaigns against enemies of humanity.

LEGIO SOLOMON
--------------
The Seal of Wisdom.
A Secundus Legion specializing in:
- metaphysical containment,
- forbidden knowledge,
- continuity hazard analysis,
- and ontological warfare.

LEGIO SYLVARUM
---------------
The Verdant Watch.
A Secundus Legion focused on:
- ecological stabilization,
- Akashan biospheric integration,
- and coexistence management with transformed ecosystems.

SYL'COTHAN ENIGMA
-----------------
A massive cryptographic continuity architecture utilizing:
- 66 alphabets,
- symbolic mathematics,
- and a 32-rotor encoding structure.

After Severance the Enigma fragmented into multiple lineages and became:
- continuity authentication,
- cultural identity,
- and ontological security infrastructure.

ENIGMA LINEAGES
----------------
Post-Severance divergent descendants of the original Syl'Cothan Enigma system.

Major forms include:
- Prime Enigma,
- Secundus Enigma,
- Frontier Enigmas,
- Dead Enigmas,
- and Symmorphica-adapted biological encryption systems.

PRIME ENIGMA
-------------
The closest surviving version of the original pre-Severance cryptographic architecture.

SECUNDUS ENIGMA
----------------
Adaptive post-Severance continuity encryption used throughout surviving Dominion space.

FRONTIER ENIGMAS
-----------------
Localized cryptographic systems developed independently by isolated colonies and bunker civilizations.

DEAD ENIGMAS
-------------
Abandoned or corrupted encryption systems still operating autonomously within derelict infrastructure.

LIVING SAINT REGALIA
--------------------
The pre-Severance armor and regalia of Amber. Following Severance it became:
- sacred relic infrastructure,
- continuity anchor,
- and proof the old Dominion truly existed.

Its reactivation upon Amber’s return represented the symbolic resurrection of the old empire.

RELIC CULTURE
--------------
Post-Severance civilization became heavily dependent upon relic continuity. Primus artifacts evolved into:
- political legitimacy,
- continuity proof,
- sacred inheritance,
- and surviving fragments of pre-fracture civilization.

CONTINUITY
-----------
The foundational concept underlying much of Dominion civilization. Continuity refers to:
- persistence of identity,
- stable causal existence,
- civilizational coherence,
- and reality’s ability to maintain consistent structure across time and space.

SEVERED REALMS
---------------
The continuity-reactive civilization ruled by Atham. The realms are:
- emotionally symbolic,
- psychologically structured,
- partially dreamlike,
- and deeply tied to the fracture itself.

AETHER
-------
The deep metaphysical substrate underlying reality and continuity systems. Aether currents, structures, and resonance phenomena became dramatically unstable after Severance.

CONTINUITY ECHO
----------------
A post-Severance phenomenon where:
- objects,
- individuals,
- memories,
- or structures persist despite physical destruction through resonance within fractured continuity.

AKASHAN SKY
------------
The altered post-Severance sky above Akasha characterized by:
- Aether currents,
- tachyon distortions,
- overlapping constellations,
- fractured auroras,
- and glimpses of neighboring continuity layers.

SEVERANCE ERA
--------------
The age following Severance defined by:
- fragmentation,
- reconstruction,
- continuity instability,
- civilizational trauma,
- and the struggle of humanity to survive against a hostile cosmos.

The era centers on:
- endurance,
- reconnection,
- adaptation,
- and the possibility that continuity itself can heal.
