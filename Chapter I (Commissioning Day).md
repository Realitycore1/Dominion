Leslarut

World: Leslarut (fourth planet) — oceanic, tectonically active, volcanic island [[Chains]]
Strategic distance: ~150 ly from the nearest CU forward yard (→ ~200-year one-way fleet ETA at 0.9c incl. accel/brake)
Settlement posture: orbital-first; surface used for targeted ISRU only

Comms (PFN rules): One paired FTL link to the CU hub (Hadranil Relay). No colony↔colony FTL. Traffic classed P0–P4; P0/P1 inviolate, P3/P4 metered. CU audits headers/bytes; content E2E-encrypted.

The Arrival of [[Aurora Station]] (Year 0)
Mission designation: Operation Aurora Ascent
Objective:
Stand up a fully orbital colonial [[Infrastructure]] ahead of any mass surface settlement.
Secure ISRU routes (volatiles/metals) and logistics control.
Establish long-range staging for future expansion deeper into the Orion Arm.
The Station — Aurora
Class: [[O’Neill Cylinder]]
Dimensions: ~32 km long × 8 km diameter (r = [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] km)
Spin: ~1 g at rim; period ≈ 127 s (~[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].1 min)
Initial crew: ~1,200 CU personnel ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]], admin, science)
Command lead: Prefect Commander Dal Enver

Primary functions:
Orbital traffic control, logistics planning, and mission authority for the Leslarut Initiative
Surface relay management for resource lifts and returns
Tether program (pilot): equatorial anchors and counterweight studies; full [[Space Elevator]] deferred pending material/planetary-rotation clearance
The Leslarut [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|System]] (Hadranil Prime)
Star: K5 V (stable output; flare activity low–moderate).
World: Leslarut — oceanic, active plate boundaries, extensive volcanic arcs.
Initial assessment:
Not fit for mass settlement without heavy [[Infrastructure]] and strict biohazard controls.
Rich seabed organics and rare-[[Earth (Terra)]] deposits along undersea ridges.
Ionospheric/magnetospheric noise complicates deep-range sensing; requires local towers and orbital constellations for coverage.
Stage One Operations (Years 1–5)
Construction & deployment
Aurora completes auto-unfold and stabilization in the Hadranil L1 region.
Drone swarms assemble tether hubs (anchor pads/counterweight trials); full elevator remains experimental.
Fabricator barges mine the inner belt; in-situ smelting prints trusswork, radiator panels, and tankage.
Surface [[Infrastructure]] (targeted ISRU)
Mining outposts on select volcanic islands/undersea ridges (short-stay crews, strict evac windows).
Modular geothermal skids on active seamounts with corrosion-resistant housings.
Sensor towers along major island arcs for met, ionospheric, and seismic data; dual-use for anomaly detection (foreign tech signatures).
Station [[culture]] & Command
Daily routine
Regulated rotations: 6-hour work blocks, [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]-hour mandatory instruction (safety/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]), 16-hour cycle.
Facilities: 1 g rim zones; vibration-isolated labs and intel bays (acoustic baffling, not hand-wavy “dampeners”).
Stratification
Core Habitat Ring 1: CU officers/advisors (controlled climate, botanical galleries, single cabins).
Peripheral Rings: technical labor/specialists (denser berthing, utilitarian fit-out, solar glare control).
Governance
Prefect Commander + Orbital Control Council hold final authority.
Planning files classify inbound civilian waves as “assets” pending status adjudication at full planetary integration (not yet granted civic franchise).
Long-Range Plan
Reception [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Overview/Timeline|timeline]]: ~40 years (orbit-first buildout before any broad surface footprint)

Surface expansion conditional on:
Elevator/tether program graduating from pilot, or robust shuttle lift capacity,
Biohazard & geohazard thresholds met,
CU audit sign-off under PFN governance.
