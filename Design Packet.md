1) Core Visual Summary (one-sentence)

Half-cleric, half-[[Titan]]: ceremonial monastic silhouettes in sculpted carapace and flowing robes, gilded glyphs and red-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] veins, helmets like sealed visors with elongated crests; they carry [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] that are part-liturgical, part-weapon.

2) Color + Material Palette (practical)
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] Red — deep carmine, luminous glow (accent). HEX #B71C1C (glow: #FF4B3E)
	•	Obsidian Black — matte structural base. HEX #0B0B0B
	•	Gilded Filigree — warm gold trim. HEX #C69C33 (specular metal)
	•	Platinum Pearl — pale reflective plate highlights. HEX #D7D9DE
	•	Jade Inset — small bio-tech lenses/eyes. HEX #0FAF7B

Materials: shot-peened obsidianized alloy for plates, electro-gilded gold filigree, red-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] luminophore veins beneath semi-porcelain mask, opaline glass gems (signal lenses).

⸻

3) [[Iconography]] & Heraldry
	•	Symbol: Triune sigil — a vertical lance-like spine with a central circlet (Manus/Vox/Axiolith trinity). Use triangular negative space motifs.
	•	Banner motif: black field, gold thin border, central red-ember vertical stripe with repeating sigils.
	•	Unit IDs: small gold chevrons on pauldrons and sleeve hems; field color indicates function (judicant = crimson edge, enforcement = steel edge, relic-wardens = jade edge).

⸻

4) Armor + Costume Breakdown (visual & practical)

A. Helmet — The Veilhelm
	•	Shape: full sealed visor with elongated crest or “splash” (varies by rank). Smooth porcelain-like faceplate with a narrow T-visor or multi-lens. Crest is either a single curved fin (judges) or segmented crown (high inquisitor).
	•	Detailing: gold inlay glyphs across cheek/temple plates; small jade “eyes” embedded along forehead crest (data readouts).
	•	Function: filters, neural dampeners, limited vision augmentation (red [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] HUD).

B. Torso & Pauldrons
	•	Form: layered carapace plates (platinum pearl) over cloth surcoat. Pauldrons angular and ceremonial — sometimes elongated to form a silhouette (for intimidation).
	•	Ornament: gilded filigree framing chest glyph, vertical seams where red-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] veins run (lit from below).
	•	Cloak/surcoat: heavy cloth, black with inner crimson lining, trimmed with gold script.

C. Gauntlets & Greaves
	•	Hands armored but articulated; slender fingerplates with runic etches used in rites. Boots heavy, with silent soles (ceramic tread).
	•	Ornament halos for relic-wielders: small floating gold rings that orbit wrists when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] channel is active (visual effect).

D. Civilian Variation (Archivists)

Less plate, more layered robes and jewelry, still with gilded motifs and jade lenses. Compact helmet or open hood.

⸻

5) Weapons & [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] (functional + ceremonial)

6) Eye of Veritas (Orb)
	•	Appearance: burnished orb with white luminescent veins, sits in gilded cradle.
	•	Use: truth-scan / memory resonance; used in tribunal rituals.
	•	Visual effect: veins pulse along orb to replay causal echoes as holographic glyph ribbons.

7) Scepter of Correction (Staff)
	•	Appearance: long shaft densely inscribed; top cluster holds a floating, rotating cage/orb motif.
	•	Modes: Resonant Bind (stasis), Purge Beam (localized [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] burn), Geo-seal (temporary causality anchor).
	•	[[Ritual]]: planting the scepter into ground aligns a local lattice for adjudication.

8) Logos Blade (Sword)
	•	Appearance: long, almost sacramental blade with gilded fuller inscribed in pale luminescent runes.
	•	Use: execution, metaphysical severance (cuts metaphysical bindings/wards).
	•	Visual: blade hums and emits micro-fracture glyphs when active.

⸻

6) Rank & Unit Structure (visual cues)
	•	High Inquisitor (Triune Crown): tall crest helmet, gilded full-plating, extra gold filigree, highest jewel count. Wields scepter + orb.
	•	Judicant ([[Judge]]): midcrest helmet, heavier robes, carries Eye of Veritas in case.
	•	Praetor (Enforcer): more practical armor, cross-harness for sword; pauldrons larger, crimson trim.
	•	Archivist (Scholar): robe-dominant, half-helm, jade lenses; responsible for Manus/Vox decoding.
	•	Warden (Relic Keeper): guardian armor plated with Isoplex ward sigils (small green badges).

Insignia placement: left chest for rank, right forearm for unit sigil, back of cloak for house/brotherhood emblem.

⸻

7) [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], [[Ritual]] & Behavior (short)
	•	Sequence in Tribunal: Eye scan > Scepter binding (if truth fails) > Verdict > Logos (finality)
	•	Ethos: “Order as Mercy.” [[Inquisition]] views its instruments as purification — ritualized, legalistic, never arbitrary.
	•	Secrecy: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] are split (Arcanum) and physically separated; only a Triad of [[Inquisition]] may convene all three.

⸻

8) Practical Design Notes for Artists / Modelers
	•	Keep silhouette iconic: helmet crest + long cloak = immediate silhouette recognition.
	•	Use negative space triangular motifs repeatedly.
	•	Allow for active [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] states: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] veins glow and slowly ripple when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] are used.
	•	Armor should feel slightly organic (not raw [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]]). Curved plates, seam-veins, and lit runes.
