Classification: Spirits
Subtype: Domestic Service Spirits / Labor Imprints
Status: Localized, Conditional

⸻

Core Description

Brownies are small, humanoid household spirits bound not to ownership or territory, but to acts of care, maintenance, and routine labor. They are rarely seen directly, appearing instead through the results of their work: cleaned floors, repaired tools, tended fires, prepared food.

When glimpsed, they are described as:
	•	squat or wiry humanoids,
	•	earth-toned skin or fur,
	•	oversized hands and feet,
	•	simple clothing or rags,
	•	eyes reflecting candlelight rather than emitting it.

They are not guardians by default.
They are caretakers.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Brownies form where:
	•	repetitive domestic labor,
	•	gratitude without dominance,
	•	and continuity of place
overlap long enough to condense intention into presence.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Echoic Plane (habit, imprint)
	•	Secondary Influence: Oneiric (belief reinforcement)
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms (Umbrae), inhabited Material spaces

Unlike Domovoi, Brownies are not born from the memory of a place, but from the pattern of service within it.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Brownies possess simple but adaptive cognition.

They understand:
	•	tasks,
	•	tools,
	•	expectations,
	•	social tone.

They do not understand:
	•	abstract law,
	•	hierarchy beyond task delegation,
	•	ownership in a legal sense.

Their identity is tied to usefulness.
A Brownie without work will fade or become erratic.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Brownies:
	•	clean, mend, organize,
	•	maintain hearths, kitchens, workshops,
	•	tend animals or gardens quietly at night.

They avoid attention and despise:
	•	praise directed at them,
	•	overt rewards,
	•	being thanked publicly.

Brownies prefer:
	•	discreet offerings,
	•	consistency,
	•	respectful distance.

If insulted or exploited, they will:
	•	leave without warning,
	•	sabotage tasks,
	•	or in rare cases degrade into spiteful domestic spirits.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Brownies do not bargain.

They respond to:
	•	unspoken respect,
	•	clean working conditions,
	•	indirect offerings (food left out, warmth, order).

Direct payment or coercion often results in:
	•	immediate departure,
	•	or long-term household dysfunction.

A Brownie that feels owned will not stay.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Cannot survive in neglected or chaotic environments
	•	Fade if labor becomes automated or meaningless
	•	Vulnerable to null fields and Echoic suppression
	•	Can be displaced by aggressive Domovoi

However:

As long as work is honest and continuous, a Brownie endures.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Domovoi: Sometimes coexist; Domovoi guard, Brownies serve
	•	Kobolds: Opposites—Kobolds industrialize, Brownies domesticate
	•	Fey: Fey mock Brownies for their humility
	•	Poltergeists: Failed Brownies may degrade into these
	•	Humans: Preferred companions, provided respect is maintained
