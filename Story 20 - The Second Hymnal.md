# Story 20 - The Second Hymnal

## Viewpoint

Leira Cest, older now, collecting regional versions of the same survival hymn.

## Setting

Year 122 AL, letters and recordings arriving from multiple settlements.

## Story Shape

Leira expects errors. She finds adaptation.

The route singers lengthen the walking verses. The heatline settlements add a repair refrain. The abyssal settlements lower the melody to match their work chants. No one has formally broken away, but the same faith no longer sounds the same.

## Harmina Element

Cultural divergence appears through music before flags or constitutions.

## Character Movement

Leira grieves the loss of unity until she realizes sameness was never the point of the hymn.

## Future Payoff

The Second Hymnal becomes the emotional proof that Harmina is producing multiple peoples.
