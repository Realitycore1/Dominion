# Martian Colonial Committee

The Martian Colonial Committee develops from the mission administration governing Mars’s first permanent colony in 3020. It allocates water, pressure, energy, rescue, construction access, and communications while the colony remains legally subordinate to the Colonial Union.

Distance turns local scheduling into political authority. Martian districts cannot wait for remote answers when pumps, seals, clinics, and dust systems require immediate action. Maintenance councils, water districts, guilds, uplifted settlements, and household assemblies gradually carry more legitimacy than permanent emergency command.

The Committee era establishes [[Red Synthesis]]: pressure-stone and basalt construction, canyon cities, buried districts, reactor temples, and dust-resistant geometry.

In 3230 the Meridians Ascendant movement unites local institutions into the sovereign [[The Kindred of Mars|Kindred of Mars]]. The Committee ends as a colonial office, while its engineering standards, rescue functions, water arbitration, and archives continue inside the Kindred.

For the complete canonical development through the Solar wars and the *Deliverance* launch, see [[Martian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
