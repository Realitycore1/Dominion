# (2998) Federation Space Force

The Federation Space Force is established as a uniformed federal orbital service.

Its founding missions are:

- rescue;
- traffic control;
- debris interception;
- station inspection;
- skyhook and mass-driver safety;
- kinetic defense;
- federal orbital-law enforcement;
- civilian evacuation protection.

It possesses military capacity but is not founded as a conquest fleet.

Its central political question is whether the force controlling orbital rescue and traffic can avoid becoming the force controlling every off-world settlement.
