# (190) Body Question

## What Happened

The Body Question gathered around engineered beauty, pain reduction, maintenance-cycle relief, muscle tone retention, fertility incentives, family welfare, clone labor, genetic hybrid labor, and the quality-of-life redesign of human biology. Biological immortality made the debate sharper because bodies lasted longer while family growth and labor needs still remained real.

## What Resulted

- Each state began defining acceptable human alteration differently.
- Welfare systems had to absorb overpopulation pressure and incentivized family growth.
- Clones and hybrids became part of daily labor ethics rather than distant speculation.
- Amber saw a new form of control emerging through improvement instead of punishment.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Body Question becomes one of the moral fault lines beneath the New World War and later Dominion culture.
