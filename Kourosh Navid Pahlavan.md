•	Project Name: Varjam Project (“fortress” in Persian)
	•	Conception: Created in secret during the Caliphate’s rise, funded by a network of monarchist exiles, Shia clerics, and sympathetic Western/CRINK scientists.
	•	Design Goal: To embody “ideal Persian traits” — endurance, charisma, intellect, and physical resilience.
	•	Birth Date: ~2030s (so by the 2050s–2060s, they are in their 20s–30s, prime for leadership).

⸻

	•	Name: Kourosh Navid Pahlavan
	•	Kourosh = Cyrus (evoking Cyrus the Great).
	•	Navid = “good news, promise.”
	•	Pahlavan = “champion,” a nod to Persian epic tradition (Shahnameh).
	•	Title: Shah-e-Now (“The New Shah”) and Protector of the Republic.
	•	Symbol: The Faravahar (Zoroastrian winged emblem), stylized alongside the Lion and Sun.

⸻

🧠 Personality
	•	Public Face: Charismatic, calm, cultured. A master of oratory who invokes [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] as easily as quoting poetry. Presents himself as both monarch and citizen.
	•	Private Self: Haunted by his designer origins — wonders if he is truly Persian or just a project. Deeply distrustful of those who “engineered” him.
	•	RAD-like Traits: Amber-style instability doesn’t fit here, but paranoia does. He has difficulty with intimacy, tends to push people away, but rallies the masses with passion.
	•	Strengths: Unifier, symbolic power, genuine intelligence.
	•	Flaws: Control issues, suspicion of advisors, possible drift into authoritarian instinct if unchecked.
