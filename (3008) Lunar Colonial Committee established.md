# (3008) Lunar Colonial Committee established

The Lunar Colonial Committee is established with the first permanent, self-governing Lunar colony in 3008.

Habitat districts elect delegates. Organized labor and the civil service hold guaranteed transition seats because industrial systems and public survival systems remain physically inseparable.

The committee's first major ordinance makes legal domicile independent of employment. A terminated contract can no longer cancel a resident's housing status, civil identity, family unity, or right to remain on Luna.

## Local Authority

The committee governs:

- civil records and elections;
- schools and clinics;
- inheritance and burial;
- municipal habitat rules and budgets;
- labor inspection and resident-status appeals;
- allocation of locally produced water, oxygen, and emergency shelter.

## Retained Federal Authority

The Terran Federation retains:

- interworld transport and traffic control;
- Space Force rescue and defense;
- external trade and treaty authority;
- recognition of additional colonies;
- common life-support and emergency standards.

The divided settlement makes Luna locally self-governing but materially dependent on federal infrastructure. Disputes over appropriations, transport priority, and off-world representation turn the committee into the principal institutional pressure for the 3015 reformation of the Terran Federation into the Colonial Union.
