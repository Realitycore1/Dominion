We, the people of Persia, heirs to a civilization older than empire and older than conquest, do declare that our nation endures not by kings nor prophets alone, but by the spirit of Persia itself.
Through faith and wisdom, through science and heritage, we unite to establish a Republic that honors our past while securing our future.

We reject tyranny whether in the guise of dynasty, dogma, or foreign domination.
We affirm liberty, dignity, and justice for all Persians.
So shall we endure, as we have for millennia.

⸻

Article I – The Shah
	1.	The Shah shall serve as the symbolic Head of State, guardian of Persian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], and commander of ceremonial forces.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	The Shah shall not dictate policy but shall represent [[Continuity]] between past and present, embodying the unity of the Persian people.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Shah shall serve for life but may be removed by a two-thirds vote of the Majlis-e-Now and approval of the Council of Elders.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	The Shah’s duties shall include:
	•	Preserving and promoting Persian [[culture]], language, and heritage.
	•	Endorsing laws passed by the Majlis-e-Now.
	•	Representing Persia before foreign nations.

⸻

Article II – The Majlis-e-Now (People’s Assembly)
	1.	The Majlis-e-Now shall be a republican, elected assembly of the people.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Members shall be elected every four years by universal suffrage.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Assembly shall legislate, approve budgets, and oversee the ministries of state.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	The Assembly may impeach ministers or propose constitutional amendments with a two-thirds majority.

⸻

Article III – The Council of Elders
	1.	The Council of Elders shall consist of:
	•	Ten representatives from Shia clerical schools.
	•	Ten representatives from academic and scientific institutions.
	•	Ten representatives appointed by cultural and artistic guilds.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	The Council’s role shall be advisory, ensuring that laws uphold Persian cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], [[Human]] dignity, and historical [[Continuity]].
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Council may delay legislation it deems dangerous but cannot veto outright; a two-thirds vote of the Majlis-e-Now overrides the Council’s objections.

⸻

Article IV – [[Rights]] of the Persian Citizen
	1.	All Persians are equal before the [[law]] regardless of faith, ethnicity, or class.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Citizens shall have the [[Rights]] to:
	•	Free expression, thought, and belief.
	•	Fair trial under independent courts.
	•	Education in both modern sciences and Persian heritage.
	•	Access to air, water, food, and shelter as [[Human]] necessities.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	No citizen shall be deprived of citizenship except for treason in armed service of a foreign power.

⸻

Article V – The Armed Forces of Persia
	1.	The Armed Forces shall defend the sovereignty of Persia and the [[Rights]] of its people.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	No foreign power shall command Persian forces.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Shah is the ceremonial Commander-in-Chief; operational command lies with the Defense Minister appointed by the Majlis-e-Now.

⸻

Article VI – Faith and Heritage
	1.	The official faith of Persia shall be Shia Islam, but freedom of conscience is guaranteed to all.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Zoroastrian heritage, Persian literature, and pre-Islamic traditions shall be preserved as national treasures.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Religious institutions shall not control the state but may advise through representation in the Council of Elders.

⸻

Article VII – Amendments and [[Continuity]]
	1.	Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by the Majlis-e-Now and must be ratified by both the Council of Elders and a national referendum.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	In times of crisis, emergency powers may be granted to the Shah, but only for six months, after which the Commons must re-authorize.

⸻

Closing Declaration

Persia is eternal not in blood alone, nor in creed alone, but in the resilience of her people.
We proclaim this Republic not as a denial of our past, but as its continuation, so that our children may stand proud and free when tyrants fall.
