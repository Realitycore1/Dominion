## Summary

Treaty Engi
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Covenant Rings are ring-shaped orbital habitats dedicated to diplomacy, treaty-making, and vassalage management. They are neutral-seeming spaces, but in practice, they are [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]-controlled sanctuaries of [[law]], where contracts are written into both paper and [[Physics]].
Structure

Ring habitats (100–500 km diameter), rotating to provide Earthlike gravity.

Atmosphere controlled, sealed against tampering — neutral “air of trust.”

Core spine running through the ring houses communication [[Relays]], archives, and defensive [[Systems]].

Function

Diplomatic Hubs: Meeting places for interstellar polities, alien envoys, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] vassals.

Cultural Exchange: Neutral markets, shared sanctuaries, [[Ritual]] spaces where factions interact under [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] oversight.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ring-rims shaped with spires and arches, every treaty chamber like a vaulted cathedral.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the ring appears as a stark obsidian band — simple, undeniable.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Meeting halls dynamically reshape to balance factions — roundtables literally reconfigure into harmonious geometry.

Seen from orbit, a Covenant Ring glows like a halo of judgment above the planet below.

Narrative Function

To allies: A place of hope — the arena where empires join the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] instead of dying by it.

To rebels: A gilded cage — they see it as proof that the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] only allows peace on its own terms.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] itself: A living contract — each Ring a monument to [[law]], order, and inevitability.

To myth: Known as “Halos of Oath” — rings where spoken words bind reality itself.

Rings bristle with hidden weaponry — corridor beams can sever them in half if treachery is attempted.

Surveillance is omnipresent; nothing said on a Covenant Ring is truly private.

Custodian AIs (“Oathkeepers”) enforce rules — impartial in appearance, utterly [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] in loyalty.

Symbolic Neutrality: They appear as fair meeting grounds but are unmistakably [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] artifacts.

Political Leverage: All treaties signed on a Covenant Ring are logged into the Empress’s Concordia archives — defying them is not just political [[Betrayal]] but theological heresy.

Cultural Myth: For vassals and allies, these Rings are the places where oaths gain cosmic permanence.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Interior walls inscribed with flowing treaty-script in a thousand languages, glowing faintly when pacts are ratified.
