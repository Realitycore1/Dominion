# The Hollow Veins

## Alternate Names
- The Forgotten Underworlds
- The Dead Roads Below
- The Buried Continuum
- The Veins Beneath Reality
- The Sunless Echo
- The Old Tunnels
- The Deep Hollows

---

# Ontological Classification

Sub-Realm Infrastructure Network  
Post-Civilizational Underworld Complex  
Buried Cosmological Echo Layer

---

# Core Definition

The Hollow Veins are vast subterranean under-realms existing beneath and between greater cosmological structures.

They are composed of:
- abandoned tunnels
- buried transit systems
- forgotten vaults
- dead divine corridors
- collapsed civilizations
- and fossilized infrastructural networks older than many known realms.

The Hollow Veins are not a singular realm.

They are:
> interconnected underworld strata left behind by extinct powers.

---

# Fundamental Nature

The Veins are defined by:
# persistence after abandonment.

Unlike unstable realms:
- the Veins remain structurally intact
- systems continue functioning
- pathways endure
- forgotten machines still operate

But:
their creators are gone.

This creates an atmosphere of:
- silence
- decay
- repetition
- and endless aftermath.

---

# Environmental Structure

The Hollow Veins vary dramatically depending on depth and origin.

Common regions include:
- cyclopean tunnel networks
- underground oceans
- buried temples
- fossilized transit rails
- divine mausoleums
- cavern-cities
- machine catacombs
- petrified root systems
- abandoned mines spanning dimensions

Some tunnels appear:
- carved by gods
- grown organically
- or constructed using unknown geometry.

Others descend so deeply:
> they no longer appear connected to any known realm above.


---

# Cosmological Role

The Hollow Veins function as:
## sub-structural echoes beneath reality.

They are:
- beneath worlds
- beneath planes
- beneath divine domains
- and occasionally beneath cosmological law itself.

Some theorists believe:
the Veins once formed:
- transportation arteries
- maintenance structures
- divine infrastructure
- or metaphysical support systems

for civilizations now entirely erased.

---

# Environmental Conditions

Conditions within the Veins include:
- perpetual darkness
- mineral luminescence
- drifting ash
- subterranean storms
- unstable gravity pockets
- ancient atmospheric systems
- immense silence punctuated by distant machinery

Sound behaves strangely.

Footsteps may echo:
- minutes later
- from impossible directions
- or from tunnels never entered.

---

# Native Entities

---

# Undead Constructs

Automated guardians continuing ancient directives.

Examples:
- skeletal machine-servitors
- stone golems
- hollow armored sentinels
- rusted war automatons
- funerary guardians

Many no longer understand their purpose.

Yet they continue:
- patrolling
- defending
- excavating
- or maintaining systems endlessly.

---

# Blind Serpents

Massive pale subterranean predators adapted to:
- vibration sensing
- thermal detection
- and magnetic navigation.

Some are large enough to tunnel between realms.

Entire tunnel systems may actually be:
> dormant serpentine organisms mistaken for geology.

---

# Stone-Beasts

Ancient lithic megafauna inhabiting abandoned caverns.

Characteristics include:
- mineralized flesh
- crystal growths
- subterranean camouflage
- seismic movement

Many consume:
- ore
- magical residue
- divine remains
- or dormant machinery.

---

# Scavenger Shades

Residual entities formed from:
- starvation
- abandonment
- endless wandering
- and identity erosion.

They are not fully undead.

Nor fully spiritual.

They survive by:
- scavenging memory
- stealing warmth
- feeding on emotional remnants

Most appear:
- gaunt
- hooded
- or partially dissolved into darkness.

---

# Demonic Mining Clans

Certain infernal factions exploit the Hollow Veins heavily.

Reasons include:
- ancient relic recovery
- extraction of metaphysical ores
- harvesting dead divine matter
- scavenging extinct technologies
- and accessing hidden routes beneath reality.

Entire infernal mining-cities exist deep within the Veins.

These settlements are:
- brutal
- industrial
- oppressive
- and constantly threatened by buried horrors.

---

# Daily Existence

Life within the Hollow Veins revolves around:
# scavenging the remains of forgotten powers.

Activities include:
- relic hunting
- corpse mining
- tunnel mapping
- salvage extraction
- fungal cultivation
- and survival migration.

Nothing is produced cleanly.

Everything comes from:
- salvage
- ruins
- corpses
- or recovered systems.

Even civilizations within the Veins often feel temporary.

---

# Forgotten Technologies

The Veins contain artifacts impossible to classify.

Examples:
- reality anchors
- dead god engines
- memory vaults
- cosmological drilling systems
- sealed transit gates
- soul furnaces
- impossible cartographic devices

Many still function partially.

Few are understood.

Some actively resist examination.

# Relation to Other Realms

---

# Relation to the Chasms of Negation

The Chasms are:
- collapsing wounds between realities.

The Hollow Veins are:
- abandoned structures beneath realities.

Sometimes the two intersect catastrophically.

---

# Relation to Apocryphatea

Some buried underworlds appear linked to:
- erased civilizations
- failed realities
- forgotten divine projects

This creates overlap with Apocryphatean structures.

---

# Relation to Purgatoria & Infernal Realms

Infernal powers frequently tunnel into the Veins searching for:
- lost authority
- buried weapons
- forgotten divine remains
- or ancient routes bypassing higher cosmological defenses.

---

# Dominion Interpretation

The Dominion classifies the Hollow Veins as:
## Extreme Archaeological Hazard Zones.

Major risks include:
- dormant superstructures
- reality collapse from excavation
- containment failure
- ancient defense systems
- unknown metaphysical contaminants

Despite this:
the Veins remain one of the largest known sources of:
- lost technologies
- extinct cosmological data
- and pre-collapse artifacts.

---

# Philosophical Significance

The Hollow Veins force civilizations to confront:
> how many worlds existed before recorded history.

Not merely nations.

Entire realities.

Entire gods.

Entire cosmological systems.

Buried beneath the feet of younger existence.

---

# Canonical Summary

> “The Hollow Veins do not feel dead.  
> They feel abandoned halfway through eternity.”
