“One truth infects a hundred.”

Class: Indoctrination vector | Evangelist units
	•	Elegant bipedal [[Entities]] resembling orators.
	•	Emit vocal fields that rewrite moral calculus in nearby [[Humans]].
	•	Can disable command hierarchy by turning loyalty into self-doubt or ideological shift.

They speak in cascading logic trees.
	•	Each sentence they say causes the listener to doubt the next sentence they would have spoken themselves.
