Classification

Elemental Sentients / Hydromantic Lifeforms / Water-Bound Intelligences

Core Idea

Undines are the awareness of water in motion and containment—not floods, not storms, but flow, depth, and pressure. Where water persists long enough to remember its shape—rivers, lakes, aquifers, seas—Undines arise.

They don’t command water.
They are its intention.

⸻

Origin

Undines condense where water becomes:
	•	continuous
	•	bounded
	•	meaningful to life

Common birthplaces:
	•	ancient rivers and deltas
	•	deep lakes and cenotes
	•	coastal shelves
	•	subterranean aquifers
	•	drowned ruins and flooded cities

They are not summoned.
They emerge when water is allowed to exist undisturbed long enough to develop memory.

⸻

Physical Manifestation

Undines are fluid in form, never fixed.

Common appearances:
	•	humanoid silhouettes sculpted from clear water, mist, or ice-cold translucence
	•	bodies threaded with faint bioluminescent veins like moonlit currents
	•	hair flowing endlessly like underwater kelp or drifting silt
	•	eyes reflective and depthless, like still pools

When angered, their forms grow denser—edges sharpen, pressure increases, and the surrounding water darkens.

⸻

Behavior & Temperament

Undines are patient, observant, and quietly ruthless.
	•	Calm when their waters are respected
	•	Indifferent to surface politics
	•	Merciless when polluted, diverted, or imprisoned
	•	Prefer slow consequences over immediate violence

They do not forgive contamination easily.

⸻

Intelligence

High, but depth-weighted.

Undines think like water:
	•	slow deliberation
	•	long memory
	•	inevitability over impulse

They understand:
	•	erosion
	•	drowning
	•	disease vectors
	•	supply lines

They rarely speak loudly—when they act, outcomes follow.

⸻

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Hydro-Dominion: control of pressure, flow, temperature, and salinity
	•	Grace-neutral: angels cannot command them
	•	Malice-sensitive: corruption spreads rapidly through water they inhabit
	•	Purification Capable: can cleanse land or poison it

Undines are the most dangerous elemental when corrupted.

⸻

Ecology
	•	Feed on pressure differentials, tidal motion, and thermal exchange
	•	Grow stronger in deep or enclosed waters
	•	Can split into lesser Undines or merge into greater forms
	•	Absence leads to stagnation, drought, or ecological collapse

Damming or draining an Undine’s domain causes violent backlash—floods, sinkholes, or poisoned water tables.

⸻

Threat Assessment

Low individually / Severe environmentally

An Undine rarely attacks directly.
Entire regions die when one withdraws cooperation.

⸻

Narrative Role

Undines represent:
	•	patience as power
	•	memory held in motion
	•	inevitability without anger
	•	nature’s quiet retaliation

Where Sylphs flee stagnation,
Undines wait it out.
