# Office of Dimensional Containment

## Summary

The Office of Dimensional Containment manages civilian-facing dimensional hazards: unstable portals, tesseract failures, anomalous spatial effects, cross-reality contamination, and laboratory breaches that do not yet require full Royal continuity command.

It exists at the border between Federal law and existential risk.

## Mandate

The Office handles:

- containment-zone setup;
- dimensional hazard mapping;
- civilian evacuation;
- lab breach response;
- spatial anomaly classification;
- port stability coordination;
- evidence preservation;
- referrals to Royal continuity authorities when threshold is crossed.

## Powers

It may establish exclusion zones, close affected facilities, quarantine contaminated materials, and require technical reporting from laboratories and port operators.

## Limits

It cannot classify entire communities out of legal existence, conduct human experimentation, or suppress all public knowledge of an incident without court-recognized hazard basis.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Office through sealed corridors, evacuation warnings, impossible geometry in rumor, and compensation hearings after a district is declared temporarily unsafe.

## Story Use

Use this office when the strange enters civic life. The tone should be procedural dread: forms, barricades, detectors, and officials trying to make impossibility obey law.
