## Details

A Hyper-Structural Assembly Yard i
s a highly advanced facility that is used to construct and assemble complex and intricate space structures, such as space stations, ships, and entire cities. The Hyper-Structural Assembly Yard is often located in orbit around a planet or within a stable nebula, where it can benefit from a constant supply of raw materials and resources. The facility may be capable of building entire structures from scratch, using a variety of advanced technologies and processes to rapidly assemble and fabricate complex parts and components. One of the most distinctive features of the Hyper-Structural Assembly Yard is its ability to use advanced 3D printing and fabrication techniques to construct complex structures directly in space.
