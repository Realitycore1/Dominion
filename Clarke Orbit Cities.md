## Summary

Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Clarke Orbit Cities are permanent megacities built in geostationary orbit, hanging eternally above a single longitude on the equator. They serve as hubs where planetary, orbital, and interstellar infrastructures converge. Unlike transient stations, they’re true urban spines, each hosting hundreds of millions of people.
Orbital Position

Fixed at ~35,786 km altitude above equator.

Always visible from the same point on the ground — making them both celestial beacon and political overseer.

Structure

Anchored by space elevators or [[Chimney Towers]] that tether city to surface.

Suspended superstructure shaped like orbital arc-rings or floating [[Citadels]].

Reinforced by Atlas Pillar dynamics — pillars of gravitational and magnetic support.

Functions

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Bastions: Housing [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], orbital defense grids, and Concordia artillery.

Civilian Metropolises: Habitats, universities, cathedrals, and marketplaces.

Cultural Arks: Museums, genetic vaults, relic repositories — Clarke Cities preserve civilization itself.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civilization Spine: Every core [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] world has at least one Clarke Orbit City, a fusion of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]], commerce, and temple.

Strategic Node: Control the Clarke Cities and you control surface-to-orbit transit, atmosphere-to-void trade, and planetary defense.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Towering buttresses arc from tether to city-rim, spines like cathedral ribs.

Suprematist Monolithism: From below, the city appears as a flawless black disc haloed in golden light.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Surfaces fractalized into reflective lattices, reconfiguring for climate or defense.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Outer hull inscribed with prayers, visible from the ground as luminous bands across the night sky.

Narrative Function
ays: Every Clarke City is a customs node for planetary import/export.

Religious Symbol: They are seen as “crowns of the [[Earth (Terra)]]” — divine halos suspended above worlds, reminders of Empress’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]].

Seen from a planet’s surface, a Clarke Orbit City is a constant star fixed in the heavens — unmoving, radiant, an eternal presence.

To citizens: Clarke Orbit Cities are cosmic capitals — the closest a planet has to touching the divine.

To rebels: they are overwatch fortresses — untouchable eyes of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], always watching.

To the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: they are anchors of sovereignty — each world crowned by light, bound by orbit and [[law]].

To myth: Clarke Cities are “the Thrones Above” — eternal palaces suspended between heaven and [[Earth (Terra)]].
