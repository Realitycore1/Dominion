# (3180) The Cynthia Protectorate

Luna reorganizes as the Cynthia Protectorate in 3180 after the Sea of Tranquility Corporate Conflict demonstrates that colonial civil government cannot protect residents while rival corporations command armed traffic infrastructure.

The Protectorate is domestically independent within the Colonial Union umbrella.

## Cynthia Authority

Cynthia controls:

- domestic law and taxation;
- industrial and labor regulation;
- local courts and policing;
- Lunar traffic, docking, and mass-driver safeties;
- internal security and the Cynthia Protection Service;
- education, medicine, inheritance, burial, and residency.

## Colonial Union Compact

The Colonial Union retains or shares:

- common defense;
- interworld traffic standards;
- external treaty authority;
- postal and rescue interoperability;
- Union personhood and due-process guarantees;
- emergency command under published, reviewable limits.

The name *Protectorate* describes the mutual-defense compact. It does not imply Union ownership of Luna.

The Lunar Colonial Committee becomes the Cynthia Assembly. The Lunar Civic Guard becomes the Cynthia Protection Service.

Military deployment beyond Lunar jurisdiction requires both Cynthia and Union authorization except under time-limited emergency rules.

Cynthia remains inside the Union by gaining the autonomy necessary to consent to it.
