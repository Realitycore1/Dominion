Reality questions itself.

⸻

Environmental Effects:

* Structures appear slightly misaligned
* Shadows behave independently
* Distance feels inconsistent

⸻

Cognitive Effects:

* Memory uncertainty
* Decisions feel incorrect regardless of choice
* People question their own continuity

⸻

Scientific Effects:

* Experiments produce ambiguous results
* Measurements fluctuate
* Observers disagree on outcomes

⸻

Combat Effects:

* Targeting becomes unreliable
* Actions may partially fail
* Illusions and misinterpretations increase

⸻

Social Behavior:

* Debate intensifies
* Hierarchies weaken temporarily
* Challenges are more frequent

⸻

Key Insight:

Doubt doesn’t destroy reality—it destabilizes confidence in it.
