# Chapter XVI - Ascension of Dominion

## Timeline Function

End of Book IV. The United Syl'an settlement becomes the seed of Dominion civilization.

## Event Role

- Continuity doctrine hardens from wartime necessity into constitutional and sacred-civic structure.
- Dual-government logic deepens: present-facing administration and future-facing continuity authority separate more clearly.
- The states ratify and transform Amber's earlier emergency coronation rather than creating her authority for the first time.
- The permanent office becomes a continuity solution rather than a restoration of inherited monarchy.
- Amber defines the office against Ferox's model of protective control and refuses the premise that long life makes her judgment sovereign.
- The New World War becomes founding memory, legal warning, and political wound.
- Amber's later imperial role gains context from the failure of ordinary states to survive long-horizon threats alone.

## Consequence

The Dominion does not appear from grandeur. It ascends from debt, fear, compromise, and the memory of a world that nearly destroyed itself after surviving exile. Its first external test is whether it can answer GalFed without reproducing GalFed's logic at home.
