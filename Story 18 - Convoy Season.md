# Story 18 - Convoy Season

## Viewpoint

Halan Jor, now a teacher assigned to a convoy school car.

## Setting

Year 103 AL, departure yards outside New Aurora.

## Story Shape

Halan packs slates, storm maps, hymn sheets, soil charts, and children's drawings of a city some of them are leaving forever.

The convoy departure is quieter than history will make it. Machines roll into rain. Parents count bags. Children ask whether the new settlement will have Echo Blooms. Halan realizes the emotional center of Harmina is moving.

## Harmina Element

Expansion is shown through families, school materials, route logistics, and portable customs.

## Character Movement

Halan stops teaching New Aurora as the center of the world. He begins teaching it as the first chapter.

## Future Payoff

Convoy schools become the carriers of cultural drift.
