Dark Eternities (DE) are trans-axiomatic [[Domains]] anchored in the [[Ultis]] Root: anti-causal, contradiction-permissive, entropy-seeking. They don’t just invade space; they overwrite rule-sets. Where they touch [[Primis]]-aligned reality, causality frays, memory unmoors, and conservation classes misprice.

Intent vector: maximize reachable state volume by dissolving constraints (cause, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], continuity). That “[[Hunger]]” presents as growing pockets of usable paradox and heat sinks that never quite settle.

Ontic profile (diagnostics)
	•	Root: [[Ultis]] (anti-causal, contradiction-tolerant).
	•	Field signatures: Φ ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]) becomes high but incoherent; η (noise) surges chaotically; ℓᶜ (coherence length) drops in pulses; Ζₑ (impedance) alternates between frictionless and absolute.
	•	Topology: 𝒦 (sheath curvature) exhibits non-metric “folds”; ER-like threads refuse stable anchoring; N_c (microconnections) spikes then collapses.
	•	Chronotaxy: local τ_c ([[Chronon]]) drifts; post-selection (𝒫) becomes “cheap” but leaves loop debt that can’t be paid without nulling.

[[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]] (threat classes)
	•	DE-G (Graze): transient [[Ultis]] eddy brushes a port; jitter, minor oath failures.
	•	DE-F (Fray): sustained edge contact; local conservation misprices; small paradox basins form.
	•	DE-B (Breach): stable pocket with reverse causality leaks; null rooms required to traverse.
	•	DE-S (Sovereign Beachhead): self-anchoring realm attempting re-root of the region.

Interaction rules (what works)
	•	Null first. In verified Veil (Φ≈0, ℓᶜ≈0), DE advantages crash.
	•	Reimposition beats improvisation. Anchors/Motes/Bridge of Writ reset [[Primis]] locally; ad-hoc rites amplify contradictions.
	•	Reason > [[Ritual]]. Athearn reason sheets provide paradox-free plans; [[Choirs]] run only in sanctified rooms with tight M-load caps and exit-keys.
	•	[[Radiator Neutrality]] holds. Mark radiators; they’re lifelines during Σ spikes.
	•	Light-hygiene saves cities. [[Ultis]] motifs ride flicker/music; ports enforce strict spectra.
