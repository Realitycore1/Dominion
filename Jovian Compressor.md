## Summary

Fuel Banks:
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Jovian Compressor is a macro-engineered apparatus designed to strip, compress, and transform Jupiter-class gas giants into usable matter reserves, exotic fuel cores, or controlled stellar embryos. They are the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s equivalent of quarries and foundries — except their raw stone is entire planets of hydrogen and storms.
Atmospheric Harvest

Orbital Siphons: Ring structures skimming upper clouds, pulling hydrogen, helium, and noble gases into space refineries.

Storm Vents: Towers drilled into persistent cyclones, channeling atmospheric mass upward.

MRO Ecologies: Drone swarms that stabilize turbulence and repair harvest [[Infrastructure]].

Compression [[Systems]]

Strange-Matter Collars: Encircle the planet, increasing internal pressure and density.

Gravitic Pressors: Atlas Pillar–scale constructs focusing [[Artificial]] mass to squeeze the planet’s heart.

Fusion-Assists: Dynamic Core Ignitors planted in the metallic hydrogen layer to regulate burn rates.

Transformation Outcomes

Mini-[[Stars]]: Ignited into [[Brown Dwarfs]] or sub-solar furnaces, lighting entire [[Systems]].

Collapse Weapons: Destabilized into runaway fusion events, used as planetary bombs.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide vast reserves of fuel and industrial isotopes for local sectors.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Converted into strategic furnaces for war fleets, or collapse-bombs aimed at rebellious [[Systems]].

Economic: Serve as the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s heavy industry backbone, like [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]-scale steel mills.

Religious: Revered as “The Anvils of Storms” — myths describe the Empress squeezing storm-lords into jewels of [[Fire]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Vast buttress towers plunging into storm bands, lit with molten glow.

Suprematist Monolithism: Orbital rings appear as smooth obsidian halos strangling the planet.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Jet streams shaped into spirals, storm belts reorganized into fractal geometries.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Entire aurorae bent into script across the poles, visible prayers written in plasma.

From orbit, a Jovian under compression looks unnatural: its storm bands twist into regular glyphs, lightning veins forming patterns as if the entire planet were being inscribed.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Proof of power — the Empress doesn’t just rule worlds, she reshapes giants.

To rebels: Horror — planets themselves turned into siege engines.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Foundation — without compressed giants, fleets, fortresses, and corridors would starve of fuel.
 The Jovian is compacted into a denser, more stable “fuel vault” world.

To myth: Called the “Storm Anvils” — where gods of gas are hammered into suns and jewels.
