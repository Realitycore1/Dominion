Birth: 2035 – Accra, Ghana
Profession: Microgrid electrician
Survival specialty: Power distribution, scavenged energy networks
Base: African Green Belt / Sahara Salt Kingdoms

Djimon lived three different lives in one: survivor, nomad, engineer.
He built the first post-Fall microgrids in West Africa and carried knowledge across deserts full of raiders, zealots, and biomechanical beasts.

People call him:
The Sunbringer
The Lightning Shepherd

He laughs often. Too often. Because laughter is how he keeps despair from hardening inside him.
