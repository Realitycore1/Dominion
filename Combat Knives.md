1.	Standard Infantry:
	•	Combat Knife: Hypercarbon or diamond-laced ceramic; 10–15 cm fixed blade; with vibro module.
	•	Utility Sword: Compact, machete-style, often non-powered for reliability.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Kraft Suit Infantry:
	•	Vibro-enhanced blades (both knives and swords) designed to integrate into forearm mounts or fold into armor.
	•	Enhanced user strength supports heavier, denser blades.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Inquisitors / SpecOps:
	•	Phase-Edge Rapiers: Lightweight monomolecular fencing blades with neural interface and shape memory modulation.
	•	Twin Blades: Synchronized pulse-vibro daggers designed for high-speed engagements and quick neutralization of armored targets.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Ceremonial or Empress Guard Blades:
	•	Oversized Greatswords or Falx-style blades, reinforced by gravitic dampening fields to reduce user strain.
	•	Sheathed in energy binding matrices that “sing” or “chant” when drawn, a mix of intimidation and psychoacoustic warfare.
IV. Non-Powered Stealth Knives
In environments where stealth and power [[Silence]] are [[Key]]:
	•	Silicon-carbide composite blades: Non-metallic, non-reflective.
	•	Edge field dampers or friction-absorption coatings prevent glint and noise.
	•	Sheathes built with static discharge absorbers to prevent electrical signature tracking.

Reflecting [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[culture]] and hierarchy:
	•	Citizen’s Honor Knife: Every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] citizen might carry a compact blade as both utility and symbolic tool, inscribed with genetic sigils.
	•	Syl’an Duel Blades: Designed for ritualized combat with collapsible diamond-thread blades, paired with kinetic feedback sensors for real-[[Time]] telemetry (used in trials or honor duels).
