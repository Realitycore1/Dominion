Ganymede is where humanity finally gets a stable outer-system world.

Why Ganymede Is Different
	•	Largest moon in the Solar System
	•	Has its own magnetosphere
	•	Lower radiation exposure
	•	Thick ice shell with layered oceans
	•	Strong gravitational stability

If Europa is dangerous knowledge, Ganymede is safety.

Role in the System

Ganymede becomes:
	•	Jovian administrative center
	•	Population hub
	•	Military logistics anchor
	•	Long-term habitation testbed

Where Europa experiments, Ganymede consolidates.

Settlement Model
	•	Deep subsurface cities
	•	Ice-mined construction
	•	Large rotating habitats integrated into caverns
	•	Centralized governance and infrastructure

Ganymede hosts:
	•	families
	•	long-term residents
	•	children

It is where humanity proves it can live normally beyond Mars.

Cultural Identity

Ganymede settlers are:
	•	conservative
	•	stability-oriented
	•	security-minded
	•	skeptical of experimentation

They see Europa as reckless.
They see Titan as strange.

They see themselves as the center of gravity.
