1. Common Minotaurs
	•	Definition: The baseline—towering humanoids with the head of a bull.
	•	Traits: Massive strength, horns for goring, brutal [[Close]] combat.
	•	Symbolism: Bestial rage, [[Man]] trapped in instinct.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Labyrinth Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Minotaurs tied to mazes, ruins, or underground fortresses.
	•	Traits: Sense of direction, ambush hunters, territorial.
	•	Symbolism: The terror of being lost; predator as guardian of mystery.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. War Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Minotaurs bred or trained for battle.
	•	Traits: Heavy armor, war-axes, berserker charges.
	•	Symbolism: The unstoppable wall of flesh and steel.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Tribal Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Clans of minotaurs living in plains, mountains, or savannahs.
	•	Traits: Nomadic raiders, beast-herders, war drummers.
	•	Symbolism: The herd as kin; strength in tribal unity.

⸻

5. Abyssal Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Minotaurs corrupted by demonic or infernal energies.
	•	Traits: Blackened horns, glowing eyes, [[Chains]] for weapons.
	•	Symbolism: The enslaved beast; wrath turned into damnation.

⸻

6. Noble Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Civilized minotaurs who build cities, serve as guardians, or take roles in courts.
	•	Traits: Ornate horns, disciplined warriors, oathbound.
	•	Symbolism: Strength yoked to honor; the beast as protector.

⸻

7. Elemental Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Minotaurs infused with primal forces.
	•	Variants: Stonehorns ([[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]), Firebloods (flame), Stormcallers (lightning), Frosthorns (ice).
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s fury given form in flesh and horn.

⸻

8. Seafaring Minotaurs
	•	Definition: A rare branch of minotaurs adapted to coasts and oceans.
	•	Traits: Horns used as rams, affinity with storms, pirate-[[Warrior]] cultures.
	•	Symbolism: The bull as sailor; chaos of sea and storm.

⸻

9. Shadow Minotaurs
	•	Definition: Twisted minotaurs who lurk in ruins and cursed places.
	•	Traits: Dark fur, spectral horns, ambush predators in gloom.
	•	Symbolism: Fear of the [[Forgotten]] and abandoned.

⸻

10. Arch-Minotaurs (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary bull-lords who embody primal or divine strength.
	•	Traits: Colossal size, command of labyrinths, semi-divine auras.
	•	Symbolism: The bull as god; raw creation and destruction incarnate.
