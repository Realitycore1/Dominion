Iraq is a country located in Western Asia, with a diverse geography that includes fertile plains, deserts, and mountain ranges. Its resources include oil and [[Natural]] gas, which have been its primary economic drivers for decades. Iraq is also rich in agricultural products such as wheat, barley, and various fruits and vegetables. The country's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is heavily dependent on oil and gas exports, which account for the majority of its revenue. Iraq is a member of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries (OPEC) and has one of the largest proven oil reserves in the world. In addition to oil and gas, Iraq has significant mineral resources such as coal, copper, zinc, and iron ore. The country has also made strides in renewable energy, with a focus on solar and wind power. Iraq's geography has a significant impact on its [[culture]] and traditions. The country's ancient Mesopotamian heritage can be traced back thousands of years, with many ancient cities and monuments located in southern Iraq. The country's diverse religious and ethnic groups have also contributed to its rich cultural heritage, including Shia Islam, Sunnism, Christianity, and Yazidism. Iraq's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] is marked by political instability and conflict, with several wars and invasions throughout its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. Overall, Iraq remains a complex and dynamic country with significant economic potential and cultural diversity.
