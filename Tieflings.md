1. Common Tiefling
	•	Definition: The baseline—[[Humans]] with infernal heritage manifesting in horns, tails, and other marks.
	•	Traits: Horns, tails, odd skin tones (reds, blues, purples), faint sulfur scent.
	•	Symbolism: The visible mark of forbidden power and stigma.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Hellfire Tieflings
	•	Definition: Descendants of [[Fire]]-dominant devils.
	•	Traits: Ember eyes, smoke from pores, warm skin, affinity with flame magic.
	•	Symbolism: The destructive allure of [[Fire]], passion and ruin.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Shadow Tieflings
	•	Definition: Bloodlines steeped in night, shadows, or the lower [[Depths]] of the Hells.
	•	Traits: Dark skin with glowing marks, affinity for stealth and necromancy.
	•	Symbolism: Fear of the unseen, the predator in the dark.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Ironblood Tieflings
	•	Definition: Tieflings descended from warlike, militant fiends.
	•	Traits: Metallic horns, iron-hued skin, martial instincts.
	•	Symbolism: War as destiny; strength as heritage.

⸻

5. Serpentine Tieflings
	•	Definition: Marked by serpentine devils or [[Demons]].
	•	Traits: Slitted eyes, forked tongues, scaled patches of skin.
	•	Symbolism: Deception, temptation, and hidden danger.

⸻

6. [[Abyss]]-Touched Tieflings
	•	Definition: Tieflings marked more by chaos and [[Demons]] than devils.
	•	Traits: Wild mutations (extra eyes, claws, unpredictable voices).
	•	Symbolism: The uncontrollable side of corruption.

⸻

7. Noble Tieflings
	•	Definition: Infernal-blooded who embrace refinement and leadership.
	•	Traits: Subtle horns or marks, striking beauty, silvered tongues.
	•	Symbolism: Power veiled in charm; the devil as a ruler.

⸻

8. Celestial-Blood Tieflings (Fallen)
	•	Definition: Tieflings whose lineage is tangled with both infernal and celestial blood.
	•	Traits: Contradictory features—dark horns but radiant eyes, an aura of sorrow.
	•	Symbolism: The eternal pull between corruption and redemption.

⸻

9. Plagueborn Tieflings
	•	Definition: Tieflings warped by disease and pestilent fiends.
	•	Traits: Sickly pallor, necrotic touch, flies or miasma around them.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]]’s fear of contagion and decay given flesh.

⸻

10. Ancient-Blood Tieflings
	•	Definition: Rare lines said to descend directly from arch-devils or primeval fiends.
	•	Traits: Monumental horns, molten veins, voices that echo.
	•	Symbolism: The living reminder of ancient pacts between mortals and the pit.
