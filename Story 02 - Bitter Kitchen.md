# Story 02 - Bitter Kitchen

## Viewpoint

Mira Tal, a ration cook tasked with turning shrinking stores into meals that do not look like panic.

## Setting

Year 0 AL, New Aurora ration kitchen.

## Story Shape

Mira hears the scientists explain right-handed amino acids and acid-based metabolism through a speaker that keeps cutting out. The language is technical, but the meaning is simple: Harmina is full of life and almost none of it can feed them.

She waters soup until it insults everyone. She labels meals by protein count instead of name. She watches parents trade their own portions to children and realizes hunger has its own etiquette.

## Harmina Element

The food crisis is shown through taste, smell, portioning, queue behavior, and kitchen labor rather than a council briefing.

## Character Movement

Mira begins by trying to preserve dignity through presentation. She ends by keeping a private ledger of who is skipping meals, because survival depends on noticing what people are too ashamed to say.

## Future Payoff

Mira later becomes one of the first food archivists, preserving recipes that began as ration fraud, desperation, and mercy.
