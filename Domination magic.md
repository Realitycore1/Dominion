Definition
Domination Magic is the manipulation of thought, emotion, and will. Its goal is not to kill or alter the body, but to compel obedience, loyalty, or [[Silence]]. It is enchantment sharpened into a weapon.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Resonance of the Mind – every mind has frequencies; domination “tunes” them to override autonomy.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Consent vs. Compulsion – willing subjects bind easily, but forced domination breeds rebellion.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Continuity of Self – the more deeply domination burrows, the more it risks destroying the target’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Reflection – to control another, the caster must risk exposing themselves; domination is always two-way, however slight.

⸻

Techniques of Domination
	•	Charm & Enchantment – soft influence; winning trust, lowering defenses.
	•	Compulsion – hard commands enforced by will or word.
	•	Fearcraft – overwhelming a mind with terror until obedience is reflexive.
	•	Geas & Oathbinding – magical promises that enforce specific behaviors.
	•	Memory Weaving – implanting or erasing memories to shape loyalty.
	•	Dream Control – invading sleep to command from within.
	•	Hive Binding – mass domination; turning armies or cities into a single will.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: The Divine Crown cult thrives on subtle domination, infiltrating politics with enchanted promises and honeyed lies ￼. Blood mages occasionally weave domination into their rituals to keep [[Soldiers]] bound to their cause.
	•	Concordia: Domination is seen in favor economies — a Platina-tier geas can bind a lord more securely than coin or steel. At Adamant level, entire guilds function under collective mind-links.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Institutionalized as Cognitive Warfare. FAID agents carry domination scripts to subvert enemy commanders mid-battle. On larger scales, memetic domination blends with Mythopoeia, rewriting belief itself.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Rejected in theory — harmonic pluralism abhors enforced control — but in practice, “resonant compliance fields” are used to keep dissonant factions aligned.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: The Demiurge delights in domination, not just of mortals but of whole verses, pressing them into puppet-worlds where free will is a lie.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Power – nothing is more efficient than controlling someone to fight for you.
	•	Stability – rulers use it to prevent rebellion.
	•	Subtlety – unlike fireballs or axes, domination can change outcomes without bloodshed.
	•	Utility – it scales from interrogations to mass populations.

⸻

Perils
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] Collapse – subjects may lose all individuality, becoming hollow [[Husks]].
	•	Backlash – broken domination can rebound, enslaving the caster.
	•	Addiction – the thrill of command often corrupts even benevolent casters.
	•	Social Collapse – once discovered, domination magic poisons trust across entire societies.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Wards of Autonomy – sigils that strengthen willpower.
	•	Truth Anchors – binding the soul to its own essence, resisting external control.
	•	Collective Resistance – groups sharing wills together are harder to dominate than individuals.
	•	[[Aetheric]] Interference – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] agents often flood battlefields with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] static to sever domination links.
	•	Null Faith – skepticism, disbelief, and sheer stubbornness are [[Natural]] shields
