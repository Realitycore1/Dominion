Classification: Aberrations & Degenerative Life
Subtype: Amorphous Biochemical Predators / Entropic Organisms
Status: Active, Environment-Dependent

⸻

Core Description

Oozes and Slimes are amorphous, semi-organic entities composed of corrosive protoplasm, unstable enzymes, and partially digested matter. They lack fixed anatomy, instead flowing, pooling, or creeping in response to stimuli.

Their forms vary wildly:
	•	translucent gels,
	•	opaque tar-like masses,
	•	acidic slurries,
	•	semi-sentient puddles filled with dissolved remnants.

They are not undead.
They are not constructs.
They are life reduced to function.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Oozes emerge where structure collapses faster than life can die.

Common genesis points include:
	•	abandoned ruins,
	•	collapsed magical laboratories,
	•	ancient dungeons,
	•	planar bleed-zones,
	•	sites of alchemical failure.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Material Continuum (degenerate biology)
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (unregulated metabolism)
	•	Realm Interface: Hollow Veins, Chasms of Negation (minor), industrial ruins

They are often mistaken for magical creatures, but most oozes are purely biochemical—magic merely accelerates their formation.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Most Oozes possess no true intelligence.

They operate via:
	•	chemical gradients,
	•	heat,
	•	vibration,
	•	nutrient density.

However, prolonged existence or exposure to planar forces can result in:
	•	pattern recognition,
	•	rudimentary problem-solving,
	•	territorial behavior.

Rare elder oozes may develop proto-consciousness, mimicking decision-making without understanding it.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Oozes function as:
	•	decomposers,
	•	waste processors,
	•	environmental sterilizers.

They consume:
	•	organic matter,
	•	metals,
	•	stone,
	•	magical residue.

They do not hunt.
They expand.

Oozes thrive where:
	•	nothing cleans,
	•	nothing maintains,
	•	and nothing asserts order.

They are common in places civilization has forgotten.

⸻

Reproduction & Spread

Oozes reproduce through:
	•	fission,
	•	budding,
	•	fragmentation.

Attempting to kill an ooze improperly often:
	•	creates multiple smaller oozes,
	•	accelerates infestation,
	•	contaminates the environment.

They are difficult to eradicate permanently without environmental remediation.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Highly sensitive to extreme cold or desiccation
	•	Vulnerable to sustained fire (not bursts)
	•	Disrupted by harmonic or structured energies
	•	Collapse rapidly in Grace-saturated zones

Oozes cannot exist in clean, ordered, well-maintained environments for long.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Undead: Sometimes coexist; oozes consume corpses undead ignore
	•	Kobolds: Farm or weaponize oozes in tunnels
	•	Constructs: Often dissolve into raw material
	•	Elementals: Fire elementals annihilate oozes; water may spread them
	•	Domovoi: Prevent ooze formation in inhabited spaces

Oozes are tolerated by some monsters because they clean what others leave behind.

⸻

Major Categories
	•	Gray Ooze: Stone and metal dissolvers
	•	Black Pudding: Aggressive, fast-dividing predators
	•	Gelatinous Cube: Corridor-filling, environment-shaped ooze
	•	Acid Slime: Alchemical failure product
	•	Living Tar: Industrial or post-collapse variant
	•	Elder Slime: Semi-sentient, territorial mass

“Slime” is a descriptor, not a taxonomy.
