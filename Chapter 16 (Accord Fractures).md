# Chapter 16 - Accord Fractures

## Timeline Function

War Years 11-12. The surviving accords fracture under scarcity, revenge, and incompatible settlement promises.

## Event Role

- Route compacts split over military transit.
- Food-sharing agreements collapse under refugee pressure.
- Archive exchange becomes hostage leverage.
- Delayed biological agents and dormant synthetic commands continue after ceasefire orders, making compliance difficult to distinguish from attack.
- Neutral settlements discover neutrality has become impossible to maintain.

## Consequence

The language that once held settlements together becomes the language used to accuse one another.
