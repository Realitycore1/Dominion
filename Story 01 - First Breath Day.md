# Story 01 - First Breath Day

## Viewpoint

Esai Corven, a young pressure-suit technician assigned to the first perimeter crew.

## Setting

Year 0 AL, New Aurora landing perimeter, Aureth.

## Story Shape

Esai expects the first breath on Harmina to feel like triumph. Instead he spends the day replacing filters, scraping acid residue off suit joints, logging corrosion, and trying not to look at the first stretcher crews.

The air is breathable, but the rain eats seals. The soil stains boot soles. Floodlights turn crimson fog into walls. A child asks if they can take their helmet off now, and no adult can answer without checking a gauge first.

## Harmina Element

Harmina is introduced as a world that permits life without welcoming it.

## Character Movement

Esai begins the story thinking survival is a completed engineering problem. He ends it understanding that every ordinary act will need a procedure before it can become safe.

## Future Payoff

First Breath Day later becomes a small household observance: checking seals, touching the ground with a gloved hand, and thanking the people who made breathing boring.
