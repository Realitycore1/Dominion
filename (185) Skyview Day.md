# (185) Skyview Day

## What Happened

The first Skyview Day in Year 185 AL began as an educational event for children. It connected Harmina's surface settlements to orbital systems, planetary weather, navigation, defense, and the larger cosmic danger represented by GalFed.

## What Resulted

- A shared planetary holiday briefly crossed settlement divisions.
- Children learned to see Harmina as a whole world, not only their region.
- GalFed preparedness became public education without revealing every classified origin.

## Long-Term Consequence

Skyview Day helped transform hidden fear into civic identity. It also normalized orbital infrastructure that later supported both defense and war.
