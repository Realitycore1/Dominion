# Three Great Circulations

## Summary

The Dominion's survival state is sustained by three great circulations: provision, energy, and memory.

Because continuity means the preservation of mankind at any cost, the Dominion cannot be understood only through throne, court, and creed. It must also be understood through the systems that keep mankind alive when ordinary conditions fail.

## I - Provision

Provision includes food, water, medicine, seedstock, atmospheric repair, habitat materials, terraforming substrates, emergency shelter, and refugee support.

This is the most human circulation. Citizens feel it directly.

Possible institutions:

- Ministry of Provision
- Annona of the Stars
- Office of Habitat Sustenance
- Frontier Relief Command
- Harmina Agricultural Compact

Public rite:

The Rite of First Bread marks the first formal supply of seed, water, medicine, and memory tablets to a restored or newly settled world. It declares that the Dominion has not merely claimed the world. It has accepted responsibility for keeping life possible there.

Story uses:

- A convoy delay becomes a political crisis.
- A governor falsifies provision records.
- A frontier world accuses the center of taking more than it returns.
- Emilia challenges a sacrifice that looks efficient from the court and monstrous from the ground.

## II - Energy

Energy includes stellar capture, orbital rings, fusion grids, antimatter or exotic storage, VFF-stabilized transit corridors, habitat power, and military reserves.

This is the imperial circulation. It makes the Dominion visibly vast.

Possible institutions:

- Stellar Tithe Authority
- Ring Maintenance Directorate
- Office of Transit Power
- Imperial Grid Conservatory
- Emergency Heat and Light Reserve

Public rite:

The Lighting of the Continuity Grid briefly synchronizes worlds, habitats, and transit nodes into a visible map of dependence.

Story uses:

- A sector withholds energy tithe from the center.
- A ring failure threatens multiple habitats.
- A commander diverts civilian grid power during an emergency.
- MOT engineers warn that a stable route is beginning to distort under VFF stress.

## III - Memory

Memory includes archives, legal identity, ancestral records, cultural works, treaties, disaster testimony, ecological baselines, genetic registries, and the names of the dead.

This is the sacred circulation. It makes continuity more than survival.

Possible institutions:

- Continuity Archives
- Sanctuary of Witness
- Office of Names
- Imperial Memory Courts
- Archive Funeral Corps

Public rite:

The Procession of Names enters the dead, missing, displaced, and restored into public memory. No person is fully lost while their name, testimony, and obligations remain witnessed.

Story uses:

- Archive corruption creates a succession dispute.
- A rescued population refuses Dominion memory registration.
- A hidden crime is revealed by preserved disaster testimony.
- Amber must preside over a funeral that implicates her own government.

## Interdependence

The circulations conflict with one another.

- Provision may require energy that the military wants.
- Energy projects may endanger local ecology.
- Memory may preserve legal claims that block expansion.
- Emergency survival may require actions that become future memory crimes.

This is where Dominion scale becomes story pressure.
