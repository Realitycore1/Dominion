Core Bias:

Harmonic and aesthetically ordered growth

This is the “beautiful” Forest subtype.

Still recursive and life-rich, but more balanced, luminous, and symphonic.

Traits:
	•	elegant biosystems
	•	stable mutualisms
	•	patterned natural geometry
	•	high consciousness compatibility
	•	low-chaos life harmonics

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	sacred groves
	•	radiant ecosystems
	•	ordered living worlds
	•	beauty as structural function

Common Expressions:
	•	highly stable life-bearing universes
	•	consciousness-compatible ecologies
	•	“garden worlds” on cosmic scale
	•	quasi-spiritual biospheres

Failure Mode:

Fragile idealization
Too dependent on equilibrium to withstand disruption.

Best use:

Good for:
	•	“higher” or noble biospheric cosmologies
	•	civilizations like Unitas-adjacent harmonic ecologies
