Oman is a country located in Southwestern Asia, with a diverse geography and significant mineral resources. The country is bordered by the [[United Arab Emirates]] to the north, [[Saudi Arabia]] to the west, and Yemen to the south. The country has significant oil reserves in the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, making it a [[Key]] player in the global energy industry. In addition to oil and gas, Oman has significant mineral resources such as copper, zinc, lead, and marble. Oman's geography is characterized by a long coastline along the Arabian Sea and the Gulf of Oman, as well as several mountain ranges and deserts. The country's climate is semi-arid, with temperatures ranging from hot and dry to cooler and more humid in coastal areas. Oman's [[culture]] is deeply rooted in its Arabic heritage and Islamic traditions. The country has a long [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] of trade and diplomacy, which has influenced its [[culture]] and society. The country's cuisine features a blend of Arabic, Indian, and Persian influences, with popular dishes including kebabs, flatbreads, and seafood. Oman also has a rich literary tradition, with several poets and writers contributing to the country's cultural heritage.
