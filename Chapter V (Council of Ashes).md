# Chapter V - Council of Ashes

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. A provisional council forms from burned cities, broken states, surviving institutions, route authorities, and military representatives.

## Event Role

- Builds a temporary governing table before a permanent state exists.
- Forces enemies to sit with the people who bombed, besieged, or abandoned them.
- Separates emergency coordination from conquest.
- Creates the first legal outline for the United Syl'an State.

## Consequence

The council proves that survival politics can assemble a government, but not yet a nation.
