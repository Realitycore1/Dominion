I. Founding (3390–3500 CE)

The Estela expedition was launched as a Corporate–Party consortium, part of the C.U.’s industrialization drive in the outer Orion [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].
Estela Prime was chosen for its extensive heavy-metal asteroids and ammonia-rich moons, ideal for chemical and propellant industries.
The initial colonists were drawn from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s political-industrial blocs—technocrats, commissars, and corporate logisticians—who saw themselves as architects of order in [[The Void]].

They formalized under the Integralist [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], a hybrid ideology blending corporate efficiency, state planning, and quasi-religious civic unity.
Early settlements orbited rather than terraformed, forming factory belts connected by magnetic rail spines and orbital tethers.
Population (by 3500 CE): ~12 million [[Workers]], all state-employed.

II. The Corporate Party Ascendancy (3500–3700 CE)

The colony’s ruling Integralist Party absorbed its corporate founders, nationalizing industries while retaining a shareholder hierarchy within the state itself.
Its core principle: “Work is faith; the Party is reason.”
All citizens are both employees and shareholders in the Estela Integral, the sole legal political entity.

By 3650 CE, Estela’s orbital [[Infrastructure]] had become a self-sustaining megafactory cluster producing:

Armor plate and bulk composites for the C.U. navy.

Propellants and industrial chemicals for regional exports.

Media/Agitprop services, spreading Integralism as a rational alternative to colonial anarchy.

The colony’s social model exported propaganda and political advisers to smaller [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]; Integralist cells began appearing throughout nearby [[Systems]].

III. Expansion and Militarization (3700–3900 CE)

As the Tharsis Closure legacy waned and outer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] pushed for autonomy, Estela filled the power vacuum by offering “political advisers” and corporate security detachments.
These Expeditionary Brigades, technically civilian contractors, operated as ideological missionaries and economic enforcers.

[[Infrastructure]] expanded:

Two 1–[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] km drydocks capable of refitting destroyer-class vessels.

Ammonia ISRU plants on the outer moons for propellant [[Production]].

A dense Habitat Ring Network supporting 100+ million inhabitants in orbital complexes.

The Integralist Party declared the colony’s mission was not conquest but integration—bringing disordered economies and ideologies into alignment with the Integralist model.

IV. The Propaganda Wars (3900–4000 CE)

During the C.U.–Equilon tensions, Estela Integralism became an indispensable but dangerous ally.
It supplied armor and munitions to the C.U. fleets while simultaneously exporting its ideology to rival [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].

Estela’s Propaganda Directorate operated psyops and union-busting programs in Kuroda ports and Recresh trade hubs, injecting Integralist slogans into entertainment and labor newsfeeds.
Dozens of “Information Exchange Missions” served as fronts for covert operations, ensuring dependency on Estelan consultants and materials.

By 4000 CE:

Population: ~120 million.

Legacy

By the dawn of the 5th millennium, Estela Integralism had become both indispensable and feared—a colony whose factories armed fleets and whose words shaped politics.
Its Integralists considered [[The Dominion]]’s eventual rise not as a threat but as the logical culmination of their ideals: unity, discipline, and purpose sanctified by faith.
