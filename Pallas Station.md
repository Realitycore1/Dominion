Pallas has:
	•	a highly inclined orbit
	•	unusual composition
	•	complex gravitational relationships

It is difficult to reach and difficult to leave—making it perfect for things that should not be disturbed.

Primary Role
	•	Long-baseline astronomy
	•	Deep-system sensor arrays
	•	Physics experimentation
	•	Systems modeling
	•	Early warning infrastructure

Pallas becomes the observation brain of the belt.

Station Architecture
	•	Deeply embedded research vaults
	•	Long-range sensor spines
	•	Autonomous observatories
	•	Minimal civilian presence

Pallas stations are quiet by design.

Cultural Note

Personnel are:
	•	long-term
	•	introspective
	•	research-driven
	•	socially isolated

Pallas develops the first true deep-time thinkers—people comfortable watching systems evolve over centuries.
