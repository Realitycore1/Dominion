# Story 14 - Hollow Runner Weather

## Viewpoint

Niko Venn, a child in a weather-station family.

## Setting

Years 18-40 AL, frontier weather station near a glassleaf forest edge.

## Story Shape

Niko hears human voices outside during storm watch. His mother tells him not to answer. Later, trackers explain the sound: Hollow Runners have resonant body cavities that mimic voice-like tones under pressure changes.

The adults laugh too hard because they were afraid too.

## Harmina Element

Hollow Runners are predators, but the story centers on misrecognition, sound, weather, and family safety procedures rather than attack.

## Character Movement

Niko learns that knowing the cause of a fear does not always remove the fear.

## Future Payoff

Weather families develop the saying: if the storm calls your name, check the latch.
