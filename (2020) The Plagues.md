Genetically engineered plagues are a hypothetical and highly controversial form of bioweaponry that involve deliberately modifying the genetic makeup of existing pathogens to create new and more deadly strains. These plagues are designed to target specific populations or groups of people, and could be used as a tool of mass destruction or as a means of economic or political manipulation. The creation of genetically engineered plagues would require access to advanced genetic engineering technology and expertise, as well as the willingness and ability to carry out unethical and potentially illegal research. The genetic modifications to existing pathogens could involve altering the pathogen's infectivity, virulence, or transmissibility, in order to create a more effective and deadly strain. For example, researchers could modify the genes that regulate a pathogen's ability to survive outside of a host, allowing it to persist in the environment and spread more easily. Some genetically engineered plagues may also be designed to target specific groups of people based on their genetic makeup or immune system responses. For example, a genetically engineered strain of influenza virus could be designed to preferentially infect people with certain genetic markers, such as those with a weakened immune system. Overall, the creation and use of genetically engineered plagues would be an immoral and unethical act, and would pose a grave threat to public health and security. Such weapons would be difficult to control once released into the environment, and could have devastating consequences for millions of people around the world.
