Classification: Internal Doctrine
Applies To: All Aspirants, Cadre, Instructors
Origin: Post–Black Cell Reconstitution Period
Purpose: Unit Cohesion, Psychological Stability, Operational Integrity

⸻

I. Foundational Principle

The body is not identity.
The body is not currency.
The body is equipment.

Training environments exist to strip false meaning from physical form so that competence, discipline, and reliability can be assessed without distortion.

Shame is a liability.
Sexualization is a threat.
Indifference is the goal.

⸻

II. Operational Rationale

Historical analysis confirms that:
	•	Cultures which ritualize shame produce brittle soldiers.
	•	Cultures which eroticize the body produce compromised units.
	•	Cultures which normalize the body without commentary produce discipline.

Asteroid facilities adopt body-neutral protocols not for ideological reasons, but because they work.

⸻

III. Attire States

Training facilities recognize three sanctioned bodily states. No others are permitted.

1. Unwrapped State (Restricted)

Permitted only during:
	•	Medical intake
	•	Injury assessment
	•	Decontamination
	•	Aquatic endurance drills

Duration is limited.
Exposure is functional.
No commentary is allowed.

2. Training Wraps (Standard)
	•	Opaque
	•	Layered
	•	Uniform knotting
	•	No ornamentation
	•	No rank markers

Wraps remove cultural signals while preserving function.

3. Field Uniform (Operational)

Issued only after qualification.
Contains identity markers, tools, and armor.

⸻

IV. Conduct Regulations

Absolute Prohibitions
	•	Commentary on bodies (positive or negative)
	•	Prolonged visual fixation
	•	Physical contact outside sanctioned drills
	•	Humor referencing anatomy or sexuality
	•	Pair-bonding that interferes with readiness

Violations are not moral failures.
They are compatibility failures.

⸻

V. Sexual Conduct Policy

Sexual arousal is involuntary and not punishable.

Behavior is.

Training Phase
	•	No sexual relationships permitted
	•	No physical intimacy outside drills
	•	No hierarchical pairings

Post-Qualification
	•	Relationships permitted between peers
	•	Disclosure required
	•	Any coercion results in immediate removal

This policy exists to preserve trust and focus, not to enforce morality.

⸻

VI. Boundary Violations

A. Sexualization of the Unit

Repeated behaviors indicating sexual interpretation of peers (staring, joking, fixation) result in:
	1.	Observation
	2.	Correction
	3.	Removal

No spectacle.
No humiliation.
No debate.

B. Unauthorized Touch

Any physical contact outside drills or explicit consent triggers immediate instructor intervention.

Standard response:
	•	Verbal correction
	•	Public clarification of boundary
	•	Formal notation

Repeat violations result in expulsion.

⸻

VII. Shame Response Protocol

Shame behaviors (freezing, dissociation, avoidance, panic) are treated as operational risks, not weaknesses.

Response includes:
	•	Temporary reassignment
	•	Modified exposure pacing
	•	Instructor monitoring

Shame is not confronted theatrically.
It is starved through neutrality.

⸻

VIII. Instructor Conduct

Instructors are forbidden from:
	•	Using exposure as punishment
	•	Commenting on aspirant appearance
	•	Creating spectacles around correction
	•	Leveraging discomfort for compliance

Any instructor violating these rules is removed.

The Resistance does not replicate the methods of its enemies.

⸻

IX. Psychological Outcome (Observed)

Aspirants completing the program demonstrate:
	•	Reduced sexualization response
	•	Increased focus under proximity stress
	•	Stable mixed-sex cohesion
	•	Neutral body perception
	•	Improved consent awareness

These outcomes correlate strongly with:
	•	Reduced internal conflict
	•	Higher unit trust
	•	Lower post-conflict maladaptive behavior

⸻

X. Doctrine Summary

“We do not break people.
We remove the lies they were taught about their bodies.”

The body is inevitable.
Meaning is optional.
Discipline is chosen.
