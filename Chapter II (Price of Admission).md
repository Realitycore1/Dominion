# Chapter II - Price of Admission

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Settlements and damaged states negotiate entry into the emerging United Syl'an order.

## Event Role

- Admission requires shared law, archive access, military standardization, transit priority, and resource obligations.
- Local leaders fear losing the identities formed during the convoy and proto-nation years.
- Refugee protection and reconstruction funds become leverage.
- Some communities join because the alternative is abandonment.

## Consequence

The new state is not born from shared enthusiasm. It is born from the cost of staying outside it.
