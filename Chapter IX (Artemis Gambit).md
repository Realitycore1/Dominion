# Chapter IX - Artemis Gambit

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Artemis becomes the decisive strategic objective.

## Event Role

- Artemis may function as orbital command, defense grid, shipyard, weapon lattice, archive node, or symbolic high ground.
- Control of Artemis means control of skywatch, orbital transit, and planetary-defense legitimacy.
- The provisional state launches a risky operation to prevent hardliners from seizing it.
- Enemy factions frame the move as proof that unity means military domination.

## Consequence

The war moves upward. Whoever controls Artemis can define the planet below.
