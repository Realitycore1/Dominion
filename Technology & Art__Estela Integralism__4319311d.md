[[Estela Integralism]]’s science and engineering bend to the service of ideology, with no distinction between function and propaganda.
	•	Militarized Innovation:
	•	Most research is devoted to weapons, fortifications, and ships. Civilian tech is always designed with dual use—tractors that can haul artillery, transports that can become troop [[Carriers]].
	•	Centralized Control:
	•	The Ministry of Expansion dictates all technological direction. Independent research is outlawed; scientists are state functionaries.
	•	Spectacle Engineering:
	•	Warships are deliberately massive and angular, emblazoned with black suns and comet sigils. Many [[Systems]] are redundant or oversized, not for efficiency but to project awe.
	•	Genetic Screening & Social Tech:
	•	Citizens undergo purity tests at birth, using genetic scanners to classify families into tiers of privilege.
	•	Surveillance networks track facial expressions during rallies; dissent can be detected by algorithms.
	•	Disposable Tech:
	•	Lower castes and labor brigades are given unsafe, short-lived equipment. Loss of life is considered acceptable if quotas are met.

Artistic Expression
For Estela, art is propaganda first, beauty second. It exists to glorify the Caudillo and terrorize the enemy.
	•	Architecture:
	•	Cities are monumental grids, dominated by colossal plazas for mass rallies.
	•	Towers rise like obelisks, topped with black sun emblems. Interiors are stark and militarized, with propaganda screens embedded in walls.

	•	Visual Arts:
	•	Murals show muscular [[Workers]] and [[Soldiers]] lifting banners beneath the Caudillo’s gaze.
	•	Sculptures are monumental—heroes three times life-size, placed in plazas to loom over citizens.
	•	Private art is outlawed; possession of unapproved works is a political crime.
	•	Music & Performance:
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] marches dominate the soundscape—drums, brass, and [[Choirs]] timed for rallies.
	•	Chants of loyalty are synchronized with holographic projections of the Caudillo.
	•	“Purity Operas” dramatize the founding of the colony, staged in orbital amphitheaters.
	•	Symbols & Motifs:
	•	The Black Sun as omnipresent emblem.
	•	Twin Comets arcing upward, symbolizing inevitable conquest.
	•	[[Chains]] & Eagles recurring in [[Iconography]], binding citizens into a shared myth of destiny.
