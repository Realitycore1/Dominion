I. The Committee Years (2550–2700)

The Pluto–Charon Colonial Committee (PCCC) was the last major body created by the [[Colonial Union]] before its decline. It was tasked with overseeing the binary dwarf [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] of Pluto and Charon, and smaller outposts on Styx, Nix, Kerberos, and [[Hydra]].
	•	Pluto: underground bases beneath nitrogen ice, warmed by reactors, served as the administrative seat.
	•	Charon: orbital yards and prison [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] — the “black stone” where dissenters were often sent.
	•	Minor moons: refueling depots, comm [[Relays]], and listening posts for [[Kuiper Belt]] operations.

The PCCC had almost no direct support from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] or the inner worlds. Colonists quickly saw themselves as abandoned exiles at the edge of the map.

⸻

II. The Age of Exiles (2620–2700)
	•	Pluto Burrowers: colonists dug vast ice caverns, turning them into pressurized cities. They developed strong communal ties, valuing secrecy and patience.
	•	Charon Gaolers: prison [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] evolved into [[Worker]] societies, their descendants proud of surviving under stigma. “Born of [[Chains]], freed by cold.”
	•	Kuiper Drifters: independent prospectors and outlaws ranged beyond [[Hydra]] into the Belt, often smuggling and raiding.

Culturally, Pluto–Charon colonists began calling themselves Outwalkers — those who lived beyond the light of the [[Inner System]].

⸻

III. The Charon Revolt (2700–2730)

The spark came when the PCCC attempted to enforce stricter quotas on Charon’s shipyards, threatening to cut off life support if demands weren’t met.

Charon’s “Gaol Crews” revolted, seizing orbital stations and declaring themselves free. They appealed to Pluto for aid, and for the first [[Time]] the binary acted as one.

The Charon Revolt spread into a full [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] uprising. The PCCC’s authority crumbled, its administrators forced to flee back to Neptune.

⸻

IV. The Outclan [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] (2730–2800)

From revolt to unity, the people of Pluto and Charon declared themselves Outclans of the Kuiper.
	•	Symbols: a split-circle sigil — half black (Charon), half white (Pluto), joined by a line of [[Stars]].
	•	[[culture]]:
	•	Pride in exile and endurance.
	•	Storytelling traditions steeped in myth of the underworld (Pluto) and prison (Charon).
	•	Reverence for distance — to be Outclan was to walk beyond all others.
	•	Politics: Pluto and Charon governed as a binary union, each with representation in the Council of Shadows.

⸻

V. Toward the Kuiper Freeholds (2800+)

By the 28th century, Pluto–Charon had become the gateway to the [[Kuiper Belt]].
	•	Pluto: hub of governance and archives, a “memory vault” for [[Humanity]]’s frontier.
	•	Charon: shipyards, training grounds, and stronghold of hardened crews.
	•	Kuiper Expansion: Outclan prospectors spread to [[Eris]], [[Sedna]], and scattered Kuiper habitats, planting seeds of further civilizations.

Together they formed the Kuiper Freeholds: independent, decentralized, but bound by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] as Outwalkers — the last heirs of the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], and the first to look beyond it.
