Beamed-sail commerce lanes are dedicated interplanetary and interstellar trade routes where cargo vessels ride immense sails pushed by coherent energy beams (laser, microwave, or Concordia plasma-beam harmonics). Unlike highways, they’re free-floating in [[The Void]], but the effect is the same: roads of light etched across the darkness.
The Sails

Gigantic, ultrathin sails (kilometers across), made of metamaterials that perfectly reflect or phase-lock to the driving beam.

Can be rigid parametric lattices or flexing “solar-membranes” that ripple like silver wings.

The Lanes

Defined by beam projectors:

Orbital beam arrays, tied into [[Atlas Pillars]] or [[Banks Orbitals]].

Solar-synchronous mirrors to keep beams aligned across months/years.

For long lanes: [[Chains]] of relay stations that pass the beam onward like cosmic lighthouses.

In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] style, the lanes appear as threads of light stretching between planets — visible even to the naked eye from the ground.

Commerce Function

Cargo pods ride the sails, stripped of heavy reactors or drives (just storage + comms).

Huge convoys drift along lanes, riding beamed thrust one way, braking on counter-beams the other.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] craft lurk at nodes — trade lanes can be weaponized by overloading beams.

Advantages

Efficiency: Ships don’t need onboard fuel — maximized cargo fraction.

Scalability: Lanes can push tiny couriers or continent-sized ark-[[Carriers]] alike.

Control: Whoever controls the beam projectors controls the flow of commerce — the ultimate chokepoints.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Style)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Beam projectors shaped like cathedral spires in orbit, throwing lances of light across [[The Void]].

Suprematist Monolithism: Smooth, obsidian relay towers — nothing but a single glowing aperture facing the next node.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Sails rippling with shifting lattice patterns, like glowing fabric sculpted by alien winds.

Civilizational Role

Economic: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] trade arteries, binding entire sectors together.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: First targets in wars — disable a lane, you strangle commerce.

Narrative Resonance

To myth: they’re the rivers of the sky — the [[Stars]] connected by shining threads, the Omniverse itself a loom upon which [[Humanity]] sails.
