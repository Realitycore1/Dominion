# MOT — Advanced Systems (Spec Pack)

**Scope.** Seven interlocking systems extending the MOT canon. Each entry has **Purpose**, **Core Objects (notation)**, **Operation**, **Failure Modes**, **Governance & Safety**, **KPIs**, and **Narrative Seeds**. All notation aligns with earlier packs: **Φ** (aether density), **η** (noise), **ℓᶜ** (coherence length), **Ζₑ** (impedance), **Ψ** (verseon), **𝒦** (sheath curvature), **𝒞** (choir operator), **Σ** (entropy budget), **τ_c** (chronon), **N_c** (connection count), **T_t** (tether tension), **κ** (consent key).

---

## 1) Horizon Arc Gate Mechanics
**Purpose.** Open high-throughput, low-shear corridors by seating a window along a **horizon arc**—a locus where sheath curvature **𝒦** and local aether gradients produce a minimal phase-shear geodesic.

**Core Objects.**
- **R_a** — arc radius through which the gate is inscribed.  
- **α** — alignment angle between the arc tangent and principal **𝒦** direction.  
- **b_arc** — gate bandwidth (PFN-eq Mbps).  
- **G_arc** — gate budget (throughput × time) before **spool/cool**.  
- **Θ_safe** — safe envelope in (Φ, η, ℓᶜ, Ζₑ) space.

**Operation (outline).**
1. **Survey:** map **Φ, η, ℓᶜ, 𝒦**; solve for **R_a, α** that minimize phase shear.  
2. **Sanctify:** RAC sets a window with **κ**; post scope/time.  
3. **Keel & Coupler:** deploy keel struts, **Ψ-coupler**, **τ_c** gate; verify **Θ_safe**.  
4. **Run:** admit traffic under **Radiator Neutrality**; log PFN hashes.  
5. **Spool/Cool:** on budget exhaustion, ramp down, cool, and re-attune.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Arc drift:** shift in **𝒦** or **Φ** moves the arc; jitter, packet loss.  
- **Shear bite:** misaligned **α** → hull/rite stress, crew fatigue.  
- **Arc knotting:** too many arcs intersect → outages.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC permits; DVAA can damp. **Null test port** required. **Light-hygiene** enforced.

**KPIs.**
- **b_arc**, **jitter**, **duty cycle**, **spool/cool time**, incident rate per 10⁶ hops.

**Narrative Seeds.** A stealth fleet rides an off-axis arc; an earthquake-level **𝒦** surge forces an emergency shut; pirates wage **permit war** over the best arc windows.

## 2) Verse Collision Protocols
**Purpose.** Detect, classify, and manage interactions between verse boundaries (grazes, piercings, or merges) without catastrophic axiom drift.

**Core Objects.**
- **Class G/P/M** — Graze, Pierce, Merge.  
- **Δ𝒦** — change in sheath curvature; **ΔS** — axiom stability drop.  
- **R_i** — reimposition operators (Anchor, Mote, Bridge of Writ).  
- **Z_quar** — quarantine impedance setting.

**Operation (incident ladder).**
1. **Detect:** aetherometers flag **η spike + Δ𝒦**; raise **Class G/P/M** alert.  
2. **Quarantine:** deploy dampers to **Z_quar**; institute **light-hygiene** and radiator shields.  
3. **Stabilize:** install temporary Anchors; tune **τ_c** gates to eliminate loop debt.  
4. **Assess:** run **Reason Sheet**; if **ΔS** persists, schedule **Bridge of Writ**.  
5. **Restore:** reopen windows gradually; publish postmortem with PFN hashes.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Axiom rot:** slow law drift; devices “haunted.”  
- **Pinch-off:** windows collapse; stranded assets.  
- **Paradox basin:** time ops stall until **𝒫** (post-selection) resolves.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC leads; Athearn demands **reason sheets** for shared lanes; DVAA may interdict. Medical sancta prioritized.

**KPIs.**
- Time to quarantine, **ΔS** to baseline, asset loss %, loop debt residual.

**Narrative Seeds.** A Graze masks a covert Merge; choosing between rescue and sealing a Pierce; a **Bridge of Writ** burns a city’s festival ground to save the lane.

## 3) Spiralverse Arrays & Confluencia Fields
**Purpose.** Arrange many minor verses around a stable attractor in a logarithmic spiral array, using **Confluencia fields** to distribute brane tension and maximize safe window density.

**Core Objects.**
- **Λ_s** — spiral pitch parameter;  
- **ρ(v)** — verse radial position;  
- **F_c** — Confluencia field strength (tethering bias);  
- **d_w** — window density;  
- **K_net** — network stability index.

**Operation.**
1. **Layout:** choose **Λ_s** to space verses so **d_w** is high but **knot risk** low.  
2. **Tethering:** apply **F_c** to bias microthreads, distributing **T_t** away from choke nodes.  
3. **Wayhouses:** seed hubs at spiral arms with NLPG banks and **Aether Anchors**.  
4. **Routing:** pathfinding prefers outward-inward sweeps along arms to minimize **Δ𝒦**.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Arm resonance:** synchronized traffic causes **η** surges along an arm.  
- **Spiral snap:** losing an inner hub collapses outer access.  
- **Overbias:** excessive **F_c** freezes topology; reconfiguration impossible under load.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC charter defines **F_c** caps; Harmonic enforces port norms; DVAA forbids covert re-biasing in civilian arms.

**KPIs.**
- **K_net**, average hop count, outage minutes per arm, NLPG reserve margin.

**Narrative Seeds.** A saboteur tunes **F_c** to strand a fleet; a new arm seeded at wartime pace; a hub sacrificed to keep the outer arms alive.

## 4) Expansion Limits & Verse Fatigue
**Purpose.** Model how corridor overuse and excess sanctification lead to **verse fatigue**—decline in **ℓᶜ** and rise in **η/Ζₑ**, capping growth.

**Core Objects.**
- **F_v(t)** — fatigue index from duty cycles and sancta density.  
- **θ_sat** — local sanctification saturation.  
- **Ω_c** — cooldown policy (window duty limits).

**Operation.**
- Compute **F_v = Σ (duty_i · load_i) + κ_s · θ_sat**.  
- When **F_v > F_thresh**: throttle bandwidth, extend cool periods, or migrate traffic to sublight.  
- Publish **aether weather** forecasts; adjust launch cadence **λ**.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Brownouts:** jitter spikes; rites misbind.  
- **Cracks:** permanent **ℓᶜ** loss; window bans.  
- **Community fatigue:** choir burnout; memetic over-bloom.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC/Harmonic set **Ω_c**; Athearn requires proof sheets before relaxing throttles.

**KPIs.**
- **F_v** trend, ℓᶜ recovery time, η variance, choir duty hours.

**Narrative Seeds.** A frontier port ignores **Ω_c** to win a contract—then collapses; a festival triggers city-wide **verse fatigue**; smugglers use the quiet period to move.

## 5) Entropic Zones & Invasive Realities
**Purpose.** Identify and neutralize high-entropy pockets where foreign axiom patterns gain foothold (“invasive realities”).

**Core Objects.**
- **Σ_excess** — measured entropy above baseline;  
- **I_inv** — invasive signature (pattern hash);  
- **R_clean** — remediation plan (nulling, reimposition).

**Operation.**
1. **Detect:** watch for **Σ_excess**, anomalous **ΔS**, and nonlocal correlations.  
2. **Isolate:** raise **Ζₑ**, drop **Φ** (dampers), declare **Null cells**.  
3. **Diagnose:** compare **I_inv** against registry; determine origin.  
4. **Remediate:** counter-chorus → **Bridge of Writ** if needed; re-open gradually.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Bloom:** invasive pattern rides public light/music (light-hygiene breach).  
- **False negative:** slow drift passes as fatigue; later catastrophic shift.  
- **Collateral nulling:** over-damping wrecks lawful port traffic.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Domain (DVAA/ACA) leads field ops; RAC authorizes Writs; Harmonic polices light-hygiene; civil notices required.

**KPIs.**
- Time to isolate, residual **I_inv**, collateral downtime.

**Narrative Seeds.** A touring show spreads an invasive cadence; a Domain team forms **quietseeds** to pre-tune a district; ethics war over city-scale nulling.

## 6) Void Cradles & Temporal Cryo-Divisions
**Purpose.** Provide long-span survival/staging via **Void Cradles** (engineered Null sancta) and units trained for **temporal cryo** logistics.

**Core Objects.**
- **n₀** — nulliton density; **Φ≈0, ℓᶜ≈0**;  
- **τ_span** — planned time-out;  
- **Δχ** — chronotaxy drift on thaw;  
- **C_div** — Cryo-Division unit with SOPs and legal authority.

**Operation.**
1. **Cradle build:** seed nullitons (**n₀**) to form a stable Null pocket; verify with aetherometers.  
2. **Archive:** snapshot consent logs, memory hashes; store in **Wayhouse** mirrors.  
3. **Freeze:** stage priority stacks (medical, seed banks, kernels); drop to **τ_span**.  
4. **Thaw:** raise Φ gently; run **Δχ** checks; re-certify personhood/keys.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Null creep:** cradle expands; neighboring systems fail.  
- **Memory skew:** hash mismatch on thaw.  
- **Loop debt:** re-entry collides with corridor schedules.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC charter for cradle sites; medical/ethics boards supervise; Pentarchs may sign **continuity writs** for mass cryo.

**KPIs.**
- Survival %, Δχ drift, re-integration time, null creep rate.

**Narrative Seeds.** A lost Cradle thaws out of schedule; a Cryo-Division hijacks a cradle during a coup; a thaw triggers an invasive reality from an old pattern.

## 7) Rebirth‑Through‑Collapse Framework
**Purpose.** Lawful dissolution and refounding of institutions/regions to reset axiom drift and corruption—**continuity through collapse**, supervised by the Aeternis Core and the Empress’s writ.

**Core Objects.**
- **Trigger set** **Τ** — corruption/rot metrics (ΔS, K-index, audit failures).  
- **Collapse plan** **Π** — staged nulling, unbinding, asset freeze.  
- **Reimposition suite** **R** — Anchor/Mote/Writ operations.  
- **Continuity archive** **ℵ** — memory, law, and claim registry.  
- **ΔC** — change impact (lives, heat, throughput).

**Operation.**
1. **Audit trip:** **Τ** crosses thresholds; Aeternis Core convenes **Conclave of Five**.  
2. **Unbind:** declare **Null Shock** windows; suspend GEZs; protect medical/radiator sancta.  
3. **Collapse:** execute **Π** (asset freeze; window pause; choir off).  
4. **Reimpose:** apply **R**; reload **ℵ**; re-open corridors with new permits.  
5. **Refound:** new charters; reissue keys; publish proofs and PFN hashes.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Shadow fuse:** a faction attempts a sovereign fuse in the chaos.  
- **Shard schism:** archives fork; two claimants emerge.  
- **Aether aftershock:** η spikes for weeks; traffic throttled.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Empress issues writ; Pentarchs hold **κ-Council** shares; Harmonic witnesses; Athearn reviews reason sheets for shared lanes.

**KPIs.**
- Time to refound, ΔC, rights restoration %, corridor uptime by week 4.

**Narrative Seeds.** A city accepts rebirth to purge a Domain infestation; a Verarch resists and is ejected by the sanctum backfire clause; two shards of **ℵ** disagree on who owns the past.
