Classification: Mythic → Celestial & Primal Hybrid
Alternate Titles: The Feathered Serpent, Wind-Bearer, Dawn-Bringer, The Returning God
Status: Dormant / Cyclical Manifestation

⸻

Core Description

Quetzalcoatl manifests as a vast serpentine entity clad in iridescent feathers, its body coiling through air as if wind itself had learned to breathe. Each feather refracts light like living jade, gold, and sky-blue glass. Its presence bends weather, gravity, and morale simultaneously.

Unlike purely draconic or celestial beings, Quetzalcoatl is neither conqueror nor destroyer by default. It embodies ordered renewal: the idea that civilization, knowledge, and life must periodically be undone and remade to remain viable.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Quetzalcoatl is not permanently resident in any single plane.
	•	Primary Affinity: Vital Expanse + Aravoth (Grace)
	•	Secondary Interfaces: Oneiric (Dream), Material Continuum
	•	Realm Echoes: Bleeding Wilds (as a civilizing counterweight)

In the Cosmologia Omniversalis, it appears as a cyclical stabilizer entity—one that enters reality when civilizations reach either stagnation or collapse.

Importantly:

Quetzalcoatl does not rule realms.
It corrects trajectories.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology
	•	Appears during transitional epochs: endings, dawns, cultural rebirths.
	•	Avoids direct domination; prefers teaching, testing, or withdrawing.
	•	When opposed violently, it responds with catastrophic but measured force—storms, tectonic shifts, atmospheric collapse.

It may:
	•	uplift species,
	•	seed knowledge,
	•	or erase entire cultures deemed irredeemably corrosive.

Beasts and mortals alike often mistake its restraint for mercy.

⸻

Known Manifestations
	•	As a storm-serpent crossing continents
	•	As a dream-figure instructing priests, engineers, or kings
	•	As a cataclysmic weather event interpreted as divine judgment

Some chronicles describe fragmentary avatars—lesser feathered serpents acting as extensions rather than full incarnations.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Quetzalcoatl does not grant power freely.

Those who interact with it may receive:
	•	knowledge that destabilizes their society,
	•	technologies their world cannot yet sustain,
	•	or mandates that doom them if misinterpreted.

Cultures that worship it too rigidly often collapse faster than those that treat it as a warning rather than a savior.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Bound to cyclical emergence; cannot remain indefinitely.
	•	Loses coherence in realms dominated by pure malice (e.g., deep Sheol-Gehenna).
	•	Can be wounded by axiom-breaking weapons or corrupted by forced worship.
