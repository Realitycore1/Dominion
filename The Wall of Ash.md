They told him his name no longer mattered.

The helmet sealed, and with it, his face was gone. The armor clamped tight, and with it, his flesh was [[Forgotten]]. He was Federal Branch now — one stone in the endless wall.

The field was a wasteland of ash and [[Fire]]. The xenos had come in numbers beyond count, a swarm like locusts, blackening the horizon. Federal lines stretched for miles, shields raised, rifles braced, banners of the Empress flickering faintly in the smoke.

He did not know the names of the [[Soldiers]] beside him. They did not know his. It did not matter. They were the wall.

The first wave struck like a storm. He fired until his [[Arms]] burned, until the rifle shuddered with heat. The [[Soldier]] to his left fell. Another stepped forward without hesitation. The wall did not break.

Hours became days. The sun was hidden, the sky a blur of smoke and shrieking alien wings. They ate standing, they bled standing, they slept not at all. When the xenos pushed too [[Close]], they drew blades and became a phalanx of steel, faceless helmets pressing forward, silent, unyielding.

And then — light split the sky.

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]] descended. [[Angels]] of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]], burning, radiant, terrible. The [[Verarch]] of Ogun himself led them, his hammer a sunburst that shattered the enemy lines. The swarm faltered, cracked, broke under the strike of gods.

The Federal [[Soldiers]] did not cheer. They did not weep. They held.

As the battle ended and the [[Angels]] marched on, the Federal line stood in the ash, unmoving. Their armor was cracked, their bodies broken, their souls already forfeit. But the wall remained.

In the [[Silence]], he remembered what the drillmasters had whispered when the mask first sealed:

“You are already dead. Your only task is to hold long enough that [[Humanity]] lives.”

And so he did.
