# Chapter I - Chains of Debt

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The first post-Book III settlement begins with debt rather than reconciliation.

## Event Role

- Tracks reconstruction obligations, refugee burdens, military loans, food credits, and orbital-defense costs.
- Shows how wartime rescue creates dependency.
- Makes economic memory as politically dangerous as battlefield memory.
- Establishes why unification is practical before it is ideological.

## Consequence

The United Syl'an State begins as a ledger of who owes survival to whom.
