I. Founding (3360–3450 CE)

The Eprea Expedition was launched as a terraform-labor concession funded by South-Asian reconstruction blocs.
C.U. planners expected the migrants—mostly agrarian and industrial castes—to provide cheap manpower for nearby colonial projects.
Upon arrival, the settlers discovered two oxygen-poor but soil-rich worlds. Lacking full support from Sol, they reorganized under the Hindutva Mandate Council, reviving caste-based administration to maintain order during decades of terraforming hardship.

Early governance hardened into caste ministries controlling labor, rationing, and migration. The first domed cities—Ayodh on Eprea Prime and Sarnath Ring on Vashma—became seats of the Dharma Administratum, theocratic bureaucracies interpreting every directive as divine duty.

II. Theocratic Consolidation (3450–3650 CE)

Isolation deepened devotion. [[The Mandate]] Council proclaimed that “work is worship, and worship is [[law]].”
Each ministry evolved into both religious order and labor directorate:

Karma Jati: Oversees population mobility and birth permits.

Bhumi Jati: Manages terraforming crews and agricultural quotas.

Agni Jati: Controls defense, internal policing, and the Temple Guard.

Shunya Jati: Priest-scientists maintaining life-support and scrub towers.

The social contract fused theology and bureaucracy; dissent equated to apostasy. Terraforming domes and atmospheric-scrub towers dotted both worlds, creating pockets of breathable land within seas of dust and acid fog.

Population passed 70 million; off-world contact dwindled as the C.U. tightened the post-Closure embargoes.

III. Industrial Maturity (3650–3900 CE)

By the late 37th century, Eprea Hindutva exported bio-organic materials, low-cost hab modules, and processed organics to nearby [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].
However, prosperity remained stratified: upper ministries monopolized education and FTL passage; lower castes remained under dome indenture.

The regime’s security posture shifted inward—thousands of Temple Guards and surveillance drones patrolled every settlement.
Eprea maintained only token off-world forces—small patrol flotillas built from refitted cargo craft—but remained politically stable through repression and religious unity.

IV. The Caste Flights (3900–4000 CE)

FTL communication revived awareness of other [[Human]] [[Systems]]. Stories of freer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] ignited unrest.
Beginning 3925 CE, dissident [[Workers]] attempted escape via humanitarian corridors organized by merchants from Recresh Space.

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] denounced the exodus as heresy and treason, ordering Operation Ash Curtain—mass internments and purges within transit domes.
Still, thousands reached Recresh, where their testimonies provoked interstellar outrage and limited sanctions by the C.U.

By 4000 CE:

Population stabilized near 150 million under rationed growth permits.

Both worlds remained marginal but self-sufficient.

Trade limited to nearby [[Systems]] needing cheap labor modules or terraforming bio-mass.

The caste-flight crisis persisted as a simmering humanitarian flashpoint.
