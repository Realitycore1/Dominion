Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Statite Sunshades are ultralight metamaterial sails suspended in space by radiation pressure, positioned to cast shadow across planets, habitats, or even entire orbital zones. Unlike orbital shades, these can hover in non-Keplerian orbits — static veils suspended like dark icons against the sun.
Structure

Shade Sails: Kilometer- to continent-scale ultrathin sheets absorbing or scattering starlight.

Support Frames: Spines etched with glyph-light, acting like cathedral tracery across the dark pane.

Stability Nodes: [[Computronium]] swarms adjusting sail tilt against stellar radiation.

Swarm Nets: Dozens or hundreds of shades coordinated into veils of shadow.

Functions

Civil:
– Global climate tuning (cool overheated worlds, regulate greenhouse effects).
– Create twilight sanctums or shield [[Regions]] from stellar flares.
– Terraform Mars-like worlds by shading specific [[Regions]].

Religious:
– Deployed as scripture-shadows, casting glyph-shaped eclipses on planetary surfaces.
– Used in [[Ritual]] to veil suns during holy days.

Variants

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: When in fleets, veils ripple like fractal mandalas, shadows dancing in sacred geometry.

From the ground, a Statite Sunshade looks like a false eclipse frozen in the sky, a black rose glowing faintly gold at its edges, veiling the sun with sacred geometry.

To myth: Called the “Amber Veils of Mourning”, believed to be the Empress drawing her face away in judgment.
