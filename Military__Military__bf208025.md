1. Military Philosophy

The Attean military is built on a single doctrinal axiom:

“War is the final expression of survival.”

Because their civilization was born from catastrophe, the Atteans developed a strategic [[culture]] defined by:
	•	absolute efficiency
	•	overwhelming force
	•	rapid mobilization
	•	disciplined hierarchy
	•	genetic optimization
	•	conduit integration
	•	zero tolerance for failure
	•	strict civilian protection

The military is not a separate institution —
it is an extension of civilizational [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]].

⸻

2. Purpose of the Military

The military exists to protect:
	1.	The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]]
	2.	The Monarch
	3.	The Great Houses
	4.	The [[Continuity]] of Attea
	5.	The Blue Galaxy Holdings

It is both a sword and a shield — the proactive and reactive arm of the state.

⸻

3. Structural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Overview|Overview]]

The Attean military is divided into six primary branches:
	1.	Attean Grand Army
	2.	Void-Naval Command
	3.	Conduit Battlemage Corps
	4.	Rune [[Warrior]] Phalanxes
	5.	Psionic Cartography & Navigation Corps
	6.	House Militias & Auxiliaries

Each branch is integrated into a unified strategic command structure.

⸻

4. Chain of Command

4.1 The Grand Monarch (Supreme Commander)

Holds absolute authority during wartime under the War Mandate Clause.
Personal commands from the Monarch override all other channels.

4.2 High Marshal of Attea

Equivalent to a supreme general or admirals’ council head.
Oversees all branches during peacetime and orchestrates multi-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]]m|System]] operations.

4.3 House Marshals

Great Houses maintain their own elite forces, each led by a Marshal who answers to the High Marshal but controls regional operations.

4.4 Specialist Commanders

Lead conduit regiments, psionic cartography wings, and rune [[Warrior]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]].

4.5 Standard Officers

Professionally trained from youth; graduates of the Aerin [[Griffin]] Command Academy.

8. Rune [[Warrior]] Phalanxes

8.1 What They Are

Rune Warriors are gene-augmented shock troops etched with:
	•	bio-reactive tattoos
	•	neural expansion runes
	•	conduit-proximal enhancers

8.2 Capabilities
	•	quadrupled strength and agility
	•	metabolic override
	•	survival past lethal trauma
	•	energy amplification
	•	berserker-mode combat

8.3 Role

Used for:
	•	breach operations
	•	enemy command decapitation
	•	ship boarding
	•	impossible ground assaults

Rune Warriors do not retreat.
Retreat physiology is literally removed.

9. Psionic Cartography & Navigation Corps

Unique among [[Human]]-descended civilizations.

Purpose

Psionic navigators interpret:
	•	dimensional shear
	•	fluctuation fields
	•	topological distortions
	•	superluminal currents
	•	anomalous gravitational signatures

They allow Attean fleets to conduct near-instant traversal of the Blue Galaxy without catastrophic FTL accidents.

They are revered and guarded assets.
