I. Physical Definition

The Chiralon is a speculative particle postulated within the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework) to be the fundamental mediator of parity preference — the bias toward left-handed or right-handed configurations in both physical and meta-physical [[Systems]].

While real-world [[Physics]] already observes parity violation in weak interactions (notably in neutrino helicity), the Chiralon extends this concept to multi-[[Domain]] handedness, affecting:
	•	Subatomic spin alignment in fermions and bosons.
	•	Crystallographic handedness in condensed matter [[Systems]].
	•	Biological molecular chirality (e.g., amino acids, sugars).
	•	Extra-physical asymmetry in metaphysical or axiomatically skewed verses.
	•	Spin: 1 (vector mediator) in most configurations; can appear as spin-0 scalar in condensed states.
	•	Mass: Extremely small but nonzero; scales with the [[Verse]]’s Parity Asymmetry Constant (PAC).
	•	Charge: Neutral under EM but carries a parity charge, determining its interaction sign with left- or right-handed structures.
	•	Interaction: Couples weakly to all matter but disproportionately to chiral-sensitive [[Systems]].
