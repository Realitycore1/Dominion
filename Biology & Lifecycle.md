## Biology & Lifecycle
- **Egg Stage:** Genetically engineered by [[Queens]]. Each egg carries dormant genetic pathways for all castes.  
- **Larval Stage:** Fed specific nutrient “jellies” infused with pheromones to activate caste pathways.  
- **Caste Development:** Determined by jelly + [[Queen]]’s pheromone imprint. Ensures precise control over hive composition.  
- **Lifespan:** [[Workers]], [[Soldiers]], and most specialized castes live **10–20 years**, often shortened by disease.  
- **Immune Bottleneck:** Castes adapt to pathogens individually but die quickly when new ones spread. [[Queens]] generate **new immune [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Aphotic Zone/Concordia/Concordia/Index/Templates|Templates]] each generation**, re-breeding the hive with adapted immunity.  
- **[[Queens]]:** Vastly longer lived (centuries), functioning as both breeders and living immune archives.
