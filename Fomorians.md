Classification

Cursed Giants / Abyss-Touched Colossi / Anti-Form Sentients

⸻

CORE IDEA

Fomorians are giants that reality rejected but failed to erase.
Where Titans stabilize and Cyclopes shape, Fomorians warp. They are not merely ugly or monstrous—their bodies contradict the rules that made them.

They do not represent chaos for its own sake.
They represent what happens when primordial power survives deformation.

⸻

ORIGIN

Fomorians originate from failed or corrupted giant lineages, shaped by forces that should not coexist:
	•	abyssal pressure
	•	malformed creation rituals
	•	Grace/Malice interference
	•	curse-layering across generations
	•	exposure to pre-cosmic or extra-cosmic environments

In older myths, they predate ordered giants entirely—remnants of a world that almost formed differently.

They are survivors of incorrect existence.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

No two Fomorians are alike, but all are wrong.

Common traits:
	•	Gigantic bodies (4–9 meters) with grotesque asymmetry
	•	Limbs mismatched in size, number, or function
	•	One eye, many eyes, or eyes in incorrect places
	•	Skin stretched, knotted, or fused with stone, coral, or bone
	•	Organs visible beneath semi-translucent flesh
	•	Growths resembling tumors, armor, or embryonic limbs

Their bodies look mid-transformation, frozen between states.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Fomorians are bitter, territorial, and brutally pragmatic.
	•	Deep resentment toward “beautiful” beings
	•	Violence is purposeful, not frenzied
	•	Prefer domination, extortion, and fear
	•	Rarely retreat once engaged

They remember humiliation across centuries.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High, but warped.
	•	Capable strategists
	•	Deep cultural memory
	•	Cynical worldview
	•	Limited empathy, immense spite

They understand hierarchy and power—but see them as weapons, not virtues.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Curse-Stabilized Physiology: killing them is difficult; bodies resist clean death
	•	Grace-repellent: holy influence destabilizes their flesh
	•	Malice-compatible: corruption integrates smoothly
	•	Reality Abrasion: prolonged presence degrades local order (weather, fertility, geometry)

Some Fomorians emit passive metaphysical distortion.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Thrive in border zones: coasts, deep caverns, ruins, abyssal plains
	•	Often rule over lesser creatures (Redcaps, corrupted giants, enslaved mortals)
	•	Reproduce rarely; offspring often more deformed
	•	Dead Fomorians leave tainted land

Settlements near Fomorians slowly rot or twist.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Severe / Strategic

They are not world-enders—but they corrupt everything they hold.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Fomorians represent:
	•	primordial resentment
	•	power without harmony
	•	survival through bitterness
	•	the cost of rejecting flawed creation

Where Ettins argue and Cyclopes focus,
Fomorians endure through spite.
