The resurgence of communism in Russia is shaped by the country's prolonged and interconnected proxy wars, starvation, plagues, and economic collapse. These crises have left the population in dire straits, with limited resources, a shattered [[Infrastructure]], and a growing sense of hopelessness. The country has been embroiled in numerous proxy wars across the globe, often on behalf of its allies like China and [[Iran]]. These wars have exacted a heavy toll on Russia's already weakened [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]. Not only has it expended significant resources and manpower in conflicts far away from its borders, but it has also been targeted by hostile actors in its own territory, resulting in increased civilian casualties and displacement. Starvation has also become a pressing issue in many [[Regions]] of Russia, exacerbated by economic collapse and a loss of [[Infrastructure]] to support farming. The changing climate has further compounded the problem by disrupting crop yields and increasing the risk of droughts and floods. This has led to widespread malnutrition and health crises, particularly among children and the elderly. The pandemic has also ravaged Russia, killing hundreds of thousands of people and adding to the country's economic woes. The weakened healthcare [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], lack of public education campaigns, and unreliable supply [[Chains]] have made it difficult for the country to contain the spread of various plagues effectively. With no end in sight to these crises, many Russians have turned to the Communist Party for stability and hope. Its ideology of nationalism, central planning, and collective prosperity resonates with a populace that is tired of war, poverty, and uncertainty.
