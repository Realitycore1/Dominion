# (101) Petition of Routes

## What Happened

By Year 101 AL, route communities, mobile camps, weather technicians, trade families, and frontier maintenance crews petitioned for formal recognition of route-based life. Their documents included shelter maps, food-sharing contracts, family lists, and maintenance obligations rather than ideological manifestos.

## What Resulted

- Mobility became a civic category.
- Mega-train access and shelter rights became political resources.
- The Commonwealth of States began forming from carried law and mutual obligation.

## Long-Term Consequence

Route law made it possible for a people to belong to a network rather than a single city.
