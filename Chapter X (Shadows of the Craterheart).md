# Chapter X - Shadows of the Craterheart

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The conflict descends into Harmina's deep geography.

## Event Role

- Craterheart becomes a deep-region theater tied to hollow-crust routes, geothermal power, mantle mining, hidden weapons, or survival archives.
- Old mining projects and wartime shelters become strategic terrain.
- Titan-like creatures, unstable geology, and ecological unknowns complicate military planning.
- Combatants discover that deep Harmina is not empty infrastructure.

## Consequence

The war is no longer only fought over cities and skies. It is fought inside the planet that made Harminans who they are.
