**Type:** Supra-conscious Entity  
**Realm:** [[V - Beast Chronicles/Undead & Constructs/The Empty|The Empty]]  
**Aspect:** [[Silence]], Equilibrium, Mercy without Attachment

The Eidolon is the mind of [[V - Beast Chronicles/Undead & Constructs/The Empty|The Empty]] — the caretaker of the universal cycle.  
It has no name beyond what mortals give it: *The Silent Shepherd*, *The Constant*, *The Dreamless One*.  
It enforces the laws of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and rest but never judges.

When perceived, it manifests as a [[Human]] silhouette of perfect shadow with one glowing ember-like eye.

### **Function**
- Maintains the flow of consciousness from life → [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] → rest → [[Rebirth]].  
- Delegates local manifestations of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] to compatible verses.  
- Corrects any disturbance in the cycle — undeath, immortality, or soul theft.

> “You mistake peace for mercy. I am the sound of your last breath, continuing.”
