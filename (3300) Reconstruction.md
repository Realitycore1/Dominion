### What, When, Where, How

- **What:** A rebuilding marker that shows how post-collapse humanity turns survival infrastructure into durable civilizational design.
- **When:** 3300, positioned inside the Solar Colonization and Solar Wars phase rather than as an isolated date.
- **Where:** the Solar System, especially Earth, Luna, Mars, the Belt, and the outer colonial routes.
- **How:** It happens through rationing systems, civic compacts, durable infrastructure, ecological repair, inter-city diplomacy, and long-horizon planning. The population learns to treat resilience as culture rather than emergency procedure.
- **Primary actors:** surviving city councils; engineer-priests and technical guilds; refugee administrators; immortal advisers where applicable; ecological restoration crews.
