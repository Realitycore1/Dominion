Designation: Cathedra Submareus – “The Throne Beneath”
General Specifications:
	•	Length: [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].1–[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].7 kilometers
	•	Displacement: Variable; equivalent to orbital-class [[Dreadnoughts]]
	•	Depth Rating: Can descend into crustal trench zones or breach oceanic sub-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] fields
	•	Hull Armor: Ferro-trinitium plating layered with anti-pressure hymnal dampers
	•	Command Core: Operated by an Exemplar-Aquaticum and supported by a Choir of Deep [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
Deployment Roles:
	1.	Planetary Shoreline and Undersea Assault
	•	Launches subaqueous drop pods, “Tide Coffins”, through trench deployment hatches
	•	Breaches coastlines with hydro-thrust emergence, often using the [[Angel]]’s Scourge variant—floodfire propulsion burns entire harbors
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Deep-Sea Surveillance and Xenoform Suppression
	•	Capable of tracking abyssal hive biostructures or eldritch sub-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] anomalies
	•	Fires rune-encoded depth harpoons, Pyrthrite tide bursts, and phase torpedoes into Leviathan-class targets
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Stealth Interdiction & Siege Subversion
	•	Cloaked via abyssal phase lenses and gravitonic sound shielding
	•	Capable of laying in wait beneath oceans for decades, rising only upon coded activation
