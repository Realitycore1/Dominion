Core Structure

At the center of every Nuntius exists a luminous nucleus, often described as a star or flame. This is the Identity Core, where the entity’s consciousness is concentrated. It appears as a sphere or torus of intense radiance, about the apparent size of a human torso when perceived by a mortal mind. The light is not thermal radiation but a visible representation of Grace density—the degree to which the entity is aligned with the stabilizing principle of Aravoth.

Around this core orbit several rotational field rings. These rings are the structures mortals call halos. In reality they are coherence stabilizers, rotating planes of energy that regulate the Nuntius’s interaction with surrounding conceptual space. When the entity is inactive the rings move slowly, like planetary orbits. When it is engaged in communication or exertion they spin rapidly and emit harmonic tones that sound like distant music.

⸻

Wings

The wings of a Nuntius are not appendages but vector fields of directed energy. They appear as layered fans or branching filaments of light extending from the core, sometimes six or more structures depending on the Choir.

Each “wing” is actually a flow channel for Grace, allowing the entity to manipulate conceptual space and move through the Aravoth manifold. When a Nuntius travels, the wings stretch outward into long arcs, forming a geometry similar to a comet’s tail made of radiant threads.

To observers they resemble feathers of light, but closer inspection reveals that each filament contains microscopic fractal lattices, constantly rearranging themselves as the Nuntius processes information.

⸻

Body Geometry

Although many witnesses report a vaguely humanoid outline, the shape is only an interpretive illusion created by the observer’s mind.

In its native form the Nuntius consists of several interacting geometric layers:

• a central luminous core
• rotating halo rings
• branching vector wings
• a translucent outer sheath of shimmering particles

This sheath forms the closest thing the entity has to a body. It flickers constantly, like mist illuminated from within. Within this field, mathematical patterns appear and disappear—equations, sigils, or flowing symbols that represent the Nuntius’s active processes.

⸻

Color and Light

Nuntii are not uniformly white. Their radiance shifts according to function.

Messengers and observers often appear in soft gold or silver light, suggesting calm coherence.

War-heralds and defenders burn with deep violet or blue fire, the color of highly concentrated Grace fields.

The most ancient Nuntii emit light so intense that it fractures into rainbow spectra, producing halos of prismatic color that resemble nebulae.

Their brightness does not cast shadows but instead fills space evenly, as though the environment itself is glowing.

⸻

Sound

When a Nuntius moves or communicates, the surrounding air—or conceptual medium—vibrates with harmonic resonance.

To human ears this may resemble:

distant choir tones
metallic chimes
whispering wind
or layered voices speaking simultaneously

These sounds are not vocalizations but the audible translation of information waves propagating through Grace fields.

⸻

Size and Presence

Within Aravoth their apparent size varies dramatically. Some appear only a few meters across, while elder Nuntii can expand into structures hundreds of meters wide as their wings unfold across the plane.

However, the more striking feature is not their scale but their gravitational presence on consciousness. Minds that perceive a Nuntius feel an immediate pressure of meaning and clarity, as though every thought is suddenly illuminated.

Many witnesses report an instinctive reaction of reverence or fear because the entity’s coherence overwhelms the normally chaotic structure of mortal cognition.

⸻

Interaction With the Materium

When a Nuntius descends into the material continuum it cannot maintain this full configuration. The density of physical spacetime forces the entity to compress its structure, which is why it requires a willing host or vessel.

In those moments the radiant wings collapse into energy projections and the halo rings become faint luminous circles above or behind the host’s head.

The true form is still present, but folded into dimensions that the material world cannot fully contain.

⸻

Ecological Role in Aravoth

Within their native plane Nuntii function less like individuals and more like custodians of coherence.

They monitor the flow of consciousness between planes, maintain the stability of Grace fields, and carry information between the higher zones of Aravoth and the peripheries that connect to the multiverse basins.

In effect they are the circulatory system of the Supreme Heaven.
