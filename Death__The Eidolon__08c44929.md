# The Hierarchy of Death

When a [[Verse]] manifests a local Empty, it gains a governing entity: **a Death.**

| Entity | Function |
|---------|-----------|
| **[[The Eidolon]]** | Oversees the universal cycle. |
| **Death (Local)** | Configures life, death, and undeath within its [[Verse]]. |
| **[[Reapers]]** | Agents or fragments of Death enforcing those laws. |

### **Universal Laws**
1. **Return:** All consciousness must rest.  
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. **Rest:** None may disturb another’s [[Silence]].  
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. **[[Rebirth]]:** The cycle must continue.

Only Death and [[The Eidolon]] comprehend [[V - Beast Chronicles/Undead & Constructs/The Empty|The Empty]] itself; [[Reapers]] know only its borders.

> *[[Reapers]] know the door. Death knows what lies beyond it.*

### **Concordia’s Death**
**Name:** The Lady of Quiet Ash  
**Appearance:** Pale woman in gray robes; her shadow burns faintly like cooling embers.  
**[[Reapers]]:** Pale Shepherds  
**Symbol:** A sinking crescent within still water.
