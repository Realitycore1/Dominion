# Dominion Faction Profile

## Function

This folder tracks the major Harminan nation-states that shape the Dominion-era political foundation and the New World War. Each profile is assigned to one nation or recognized power bloc and should explain how that power grows from the Mesic Era settlement process into a mature faction.

Use the manuscript as the active continuity source. Older files may preserve legacy spellings such as "Equilion"; manuscript-facing files should use "Equilon" unless an archival variant is being discussed.

## Core Principle

Harmina's powers do not begin as flags on a map. They emerge because geography teaches different people different survival ethics.

By the New World War era, every major faction protects a real lesson:

- [[Equilon States]]: continuity, archives, central medicine, restraint, and founder legitimacy.
- [[Atlovaia Exarchate]]: pressure literacy, precision, coastal discipline, and the danger of beautiful weapons.
- [[Coalition of Orbital States]]: life-support sovereignty, orbital traffic law, parity, and the politics of neutrality.
- [[Toldax Ascent]]: pass obligation, route memory, highland rescue, and witnessed war.
- [[FreeStack Socialist Republics]]: ration transparency, labor dignity, child commons, and mass mobilization.
- [[Commonwealth of States]]: carried law, distributed logistics, mobile responsibility, and route survival.
- [[Ormova Replika]]: heatline industry, technical legitimacy, fabrication, and armor-age production.
- [[Estelia Scriptorium]]: record integrity, education, certification, translation, and counter-secrecy.

The tragedy of the New World War is that no major power defends a meaningless value. The war becomes possible because each survival ethic hardens into strategic doctrine.

Low Haven is an Equilon city with a strong frontier-autonomy culture. It may oppose New Aurora and Equilon policy, but it is not a separate sovereign power in the active war roster.

## Current First Strike Continuity

The active manuscript continuity is that Atlovaia fires first at the New Aurora Constitutional Assembly during the Founder Session. The strike is intended to destroy the constitutional process and kill Amber before she can become the hinge of planetary unity. FreeStack follows seconds later with an independently authorized mass drone and ballistic strike against New Aurora and Equilon's suspected proto-legionary infrastructure. No stable alliance or joint command joins Atlovaia and FreeStack. They attack from different fears:

- Atlovaia reads Project Ra, the Founder Clause, and Equilon continuity as orbital centralization.
- FreeStack reads the same system as elite violence hidden inside public infrastructure.

This convergence begins the New World War.
