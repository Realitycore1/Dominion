1. Department of Magical [[Physics]] (DMP)
	•	Studies the root [[Axioms]] of foreign verses and how they generate magical fields.
	•	Researches equivalencies between spell-laws and physical constants.
	•	Runs collider-like experiments with “[[Mana]]-packets” and resonance [[Particles]].
	•	Motto: “Every spell has a conservation [[law]].”

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Department of Energy [[Systems]] & Channels (DESC)
	•	Focuses on how different magic [[Systems]] channel and store energy.
	•	Designs hybrid conduits: ley capacitors, soul-batteries, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] dynamos.
	•	Calibrates efficiency ratings ([[Mana]] per joule, will per erg).
	•	Specialty: simulating hostile-[[Verse]] energy scarcity conditions.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Department of Artifact Engineering (DAE)
	•	Designs and prototypes spell-guns, ley artillery, resonance shields.
	•	Integrates [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] into deployable weapon platforms.
	•	Hosts the Arcane Fabrication Bay with rune printers and etheric forges.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Department of Paradox & Stability (DPS)
	•	Studies Axiomatic Conflict Cascades and paradox storms.
	•	Maintains paradox dampers, anchor lattice, and nullity nets.
	•	Runs “crash tests” of incompatible [[Systems]] (e.g. divine faith + necromancy).

5. Department of Biothaumaturgy (DBT)
	•	Focuses on magical lifeforms, symbiotic weapon-organisms, necrotech.
	•	Manages containment of Abyssal Ghosts, Kriton behemoths, [[Silentium]] hive-forms.
	•	Oversees hybridization projects (rune grafts, spell-organ implants).

6. Department of Memetics & [[Ritual]] Dynamics (DMRD)
	•	Analyzes [[Ritual]] patterns, myth resonance, symbolic contagion.
	•	Studies memetic payloads as both weapons and defense [[Systems]].
	•	Works closely with the [[Infernariate]] liaison to flag heresy vectors.

7. Department of Temporal & Acausal Studies (DTAS)
	•	Experiments with [[Time]]-looped spellcasting and pre/post-selection rituals.
	•	Maintains [[Time]]-dilated ranges for stress-testing delayed-effect spells.
	•	Specializes in hostile-[[Verse]] overlays where causality runs backwards.

8. Department of Field Applications & War [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] (DFAWD)
	•	Bridge between R&D and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Strategic Command.
	•	Designs [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] packages for real-world deployment of arcane weapons.
	•	Trains simulation crews on hostile-[[Verse]] war games.
	
Department of Field Acquisition & Integration (FAID)

Mandate
	•	Serves as ESRI’s logistical and expeditionary branch.
	•	Works with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] operating in hostile verses to secure artifacts, magical organisms, and environmental samples.
	•	Deploys its own Acquisition Agents (arcane specialists trained for hostile-[[Verse]] survival) when high-risk retrievals require precision or discretion.

Functions
	1.	Acquisition Orders – Departments inside ESRI file requests (e.g., Magical [[Physics]] wants a shard of ley-core basalt from a collapsing [[Verse]], Biothaumaturgy wants a living [[Voltosh]] specimen). FAID drafts the mission package.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Legion Liaison – Coordinates with deployed [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], attaching FAID officers or embedding retrieval directives into the Legion’s operational objectives.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Direct Action Teams – Sends out elite Acquisition Agents when an item is too politically sensitive, unstable, or dangerous to entrust to regular Legion forces.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Quarantine Transport – Operates Nullity Crates and Containment Skiffs to move [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] and lifeforms safely back to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]].
	5.	Integration Protocols – Hands over secured material to the proper ESRI department, with full telemetry, provenance logs, and quarantine certifications.

Specialized Units
	•	The Axiom Crows – covert retrievers who infiltrate hostile-[[Verse]] sanctums, disguised as locals.
	•	The Reliquarians – heavy transport specialists who secure massive artifacts, sometimes whole shrines or war engines.
	•	Soulbinders – specialists who negotiate or bind living [[Entities]] (like [[Aetheric]] [[Fauna]] or memetic spirits) for safe transfer.

Governance
	•	Headed by the Magister of Acquisition, who sits on the ESRI Council.
	•	All FAID missions must carry a Legionary Seal (authorization from [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] command) and an [[Infernariate]] Waiver if the material is heretical or memetic.
	•	Success metrics include retrieval integrity, paradox avoidance, and compliance with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]].
