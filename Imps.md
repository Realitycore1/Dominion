1. Common Imp
	•	Definition: The baseline—small, mischievous, often winged minor [[Demon]].
	•	Traits: Red or black skin, tiny horns, bat wings, needle claws.
	•	Symbolism: Temptation and mockery in its smallest form.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. [[Fire]] Imps
	•	Definition: Imps born of embers, volcanic cracks, or infernal flame.
	•	Traits: Smoldering skin, smoke breath, leave sparks wherever they go.
	•	Symbolism: Destruction made playful; the flicker of corruption.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Shadow Imps
	•	Definition: Imps dwelling in dark corners, feeding on fear.
	•	Traits: Half-visible, whispering voices, eyes like coals.
	•	Symbolism: The parasite of the night, the fear in the corner of vision.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Trickster]] Imps
	•	Definition: Illusion-weaving imps that delight in deception.
	•	Traits: Shape-shifting, mockery, illusions of treasure or safety.
	•	Symbolism: False promises, lies in small packages.

⸻

5. Pestilent Imps
	•	Definition: Swarm-bringers of plague and vermin.
	•	Traits: Buzzing flies, diseased scratches, toxic ichor.
	•	Symbolism: Contagion, corruption that spreads from the smallest cut.

⸻

6. War Imps
	•	Definition: Imps bred for battle as scouts, saboteurs, or fodder.
	•	Traits: Spined bodies, sharpened teeth, pack hunters.
	•	Symbolism: Disposable evil—armies made from gnats of hell.

⸻

7. Contract Imps
	•	Definition: Imps that act as scribes, messengers, and brokers for greater [[Demons]].
	•	Traits: Scrolls, quills, cunning speech, delight in legal trickery.
	•	Symbolism: Bureaucracy of damnation; evil by fine print.

⸻

8. Gremlin Imps
	•	Definition: Mischief-makers that corrupt tools, machines, and crafts.
	•	Traits: Twisted fingers, ability to rust, jam, or break devices.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos in the mundane; entropy with a grin.

⸻

9. Familiar Imps
	•	Definition: Imps bound to sorcerers, witches, or warlocks as servants.
	•	Traits: Animal-like forms (cat, raven, toad) but with infernal twist.
	•	Symbolism: Dependency, the price of forbidden power.

⸻

10. Ascendant Imps
	•	Definition: Rare imps who devour or betray their masters, growing into larger fiends.
	•	Traits: Swollen bodies, new mutations, [[Hunger]] for promotion.
	•	Symbolism: Ambition even in the smallest evil; corruption seeks growth.
