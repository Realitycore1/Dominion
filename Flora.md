[[Harmina]]’s plants are highly adaptive, often blending metallic compounds, radiation tolerance, and bioluminescence.

🌳 Major Plant Types
	1.	Fur-Trees
	•	Towering black-red trunks with fibrous, fur-like bark.
	•	Photosynthesize with purple-black pigments tuned to Artemis’ spectrum.
	•	At night: faint bioluminescent moss coats them in blue-green glow.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Spore Fangs
	•	Carnivorous fungi-trees, shaped like massive jaws.
	•	Release clouds of corrosive spores that dissolve prey before absorbing nutrients.
	•	Often cultivated by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] warforms as “bio-traps.”
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Glassleaf Vines
	•	Transparent leaves, sharp as glass when dried.
	•	Thrive along volcanic vents.
	•	Used as living barbed wire around [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] installations.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Aurora Ferns
	•	Grow in irradiated meadows beneath storm-belts.
	•	Fronds pulse in bioluminescent rhythm, absorbing electromagnetic flux.
	•	Considered sacred and dangerous — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] uses them as storm-harvesters.
	5.	Crystalroot Towers
	•	Gigantic fungi that bond with radioactive crystals underground.
	•	Glow internally with eerie green light.
	•	Roots bore into undercrust honeycomb, stabilizing it.
