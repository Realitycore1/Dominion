Core Bias:

Opacity and low-visibility immersion

This subtype is one of the most dangerous.

Reality is continuous—but hard to perceive correctly.

Traits:
	•	low visibility
	•	signal attenuation
	•	hidden predators or structures
	•	perception uncertainty
	•	psychological or informational darkness

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	you are surrounded by continuity you cannot see
	•	navigation is possible but uncertain
	•	the medium itself conceals truth

Common Expressions:
	•	anti-memetic current zones
	•	low-signal civilizations
	•	hidden megafauna or entities
	•	dreamlike / obscured cosmologies

Failure Mode:

Orientation loss
Systems lose the ability to distinguish direction, truth, or continuity.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	anti-memetic realities
	•	dream oceans
	•	hidden civilizations
	•	abyssal psychological zones
