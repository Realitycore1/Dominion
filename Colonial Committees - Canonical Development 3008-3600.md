# Colonial Committees of Sol

This note is the controlling archive chronology for the Solar colonial committees from the first permanent Lunar colony to the launch of the *C.U.C. Deliverance*. It supersedes conflicting committee dates inherited from earlier development notes.

Craterheart does not yet exist during this period. The *Deliverance* launches from Acheron in 3600; the settlement called Craterheart is founded after the failed landing in 3608.

## What a colonial committee is

A colonial committee begins as a practical answer to delayed authority. It allocates air, water, power, shelter, transport, rescue, medical access, and industrial risk in a place where an answer from Earth may arrive too late to matter.

The committees are not identical governments. Each reflects the danger that makes its colony possible:

- Luna governs domicile beside military infrastructure.
- Mars turns mission administration into local civic time.
- Venus makes buoyancy a public utility.
- Mercury places shade, cooling, and cold storage under public control.
- The Belt attempts to impose portable law on moving stations, fleets, and claims.
- Jupiter must coordinate moons, radiation shelters, convoys, atmospheric cities, and living cloud ecologies.
- Saturn distributes authority through routes, moon councils, and reciprocal household obligations.
- Uranus organizes containment, cryogenic handling, and slow-failure engineering.
- Neptune separates inhabited government from remote antimatter production.
- Pluto–Charon joins archives, shipyards, Kuiper traffic, and interstellar departure.

The committee model repeatedly produces the same political question: if local people must act before the central government can answer, how much sovereignty already exists in that action?

## Canonical chronology

| Period | Committee development |
|---|---|
| 3008 | Luna receives the first permanent colonial charter. The Lunar Colonial Committee controls domicile, schools, clinics, graves, inheritance, local utilities, and elections. |
| 3015–3020 | The Terran Federation reforms into the Colonial Union as permanent settlement moves beyond Earth. Mars receives its first permanent colony in 3020. |
| 3050–3180 | Fusion drives, fueling stations, O’Neill cylinders, orbital traffic control, and the resource economy connect the Inner Sphere. Belt administration expands unevenly through concessions, claim courts, station councils, and rescue law. |
| 3160 | Venus establishes its Colonial Committee after employers use docking and reserve lift during a labor dispute. Buoyancy becomes a public utility. |
| 3180 | Mercury places shades, radiators, polar reserves, and cold stores under public authority. The committee’s work develops into the Mercurian Directorate under First Warden Deka Nuru. |
| 3180 | Luna becomes the Cynthia Protectorate, domestically independent beneath the Colonial Union umbrella. |
| 3180–3250 | Permanent Jovian and Saturnian footholds become multigenerational homes. The first interplanetary Harmonium Council recognizes that local necessity can outrun remote authority. |
| 3230 | The Kindred of Mars gains independence through the Meridians Ascendant movement. |
| c. 3230 | Belt mega-stations obtain semi-independent charters governing schools, air, rescue, and local courts. No single Belt authority controls the mobile fleets. |
| 3250 | The First Solar War tests every committee’s control over infrastructure. The armistice preserves Cynthia, Martian, and Belt autonomy while enlarging Union emergency route powers. |
| 3300–3425 | Uranus develops containment and cryogenic handling; Neptune manufactures mission-grade antimatter. Outer committees become strategic governments rather than remote administrative offices. |
| 3425 | Jupiter and Saturn demand independence. Their moons resist being absorbed into planetary identities. The Outer Systems Compact forms around convoy, defense, trade, and antimatter control. |
| 3450 | The Second Solar War divides Inner and Outer systems. Its settlement recognizes outer independence, creates a joint antimatter safety regime, and gives the Watchtower network a joint charter. |
| 3500–3600 | Pluto–Charon and Acheron become the interstellar staging civilization. The committees and successor governments contribute people, law, archives, fuel, ecology, and engineering to the *Deliverance*. |
| 3600 | The *Deliverance* departs Acheron’s outward Charon yard. |
| 3608 | The vessel reaches another system and the survivors establish Craterheart. This event begins the post-Sol colonial history. |

## Shared institutional development

The committees begin beneath the Colonial Union but do not remain simple branch offices. Their ordinary responsibilities create political identities:

1. Emergency allocation becomes local administration.
2. Local administration acquires elections, courts, schools, and archives.
3. Infrastructure control becomes constitutional power.
4. Multigenerational residents reject the legal fiction that their homes are temporary assignments.
5. Committees either become governments, are absorbed into successor governments, or survive as coordinating bodies inside larger compacts.

The [[Harmonium Council]] becomes the first interplanetary diplomatic council because no single legislature can credibly govern all of these systems. Its local-necessity principle requires agreements to identify who may act before remote authority answers.

By 3600, the committees’ greatest shared achievement is not political unity. It is interoperability. Their descendants can exchange mail, recognize emergency authority, reserve neutral rescue lanes, transfer fuel, certify pressure systems, carry multiple citizenships, and construct one interstellar vessel without requiring every world to become culturally alike.

The *Deliverance* is therefore a product of committee civilization: Luna’s shield doctrine, Martian pressure architecture, Venusian lift engineering, Mercurian thermal control, Belt repair culture, Jovian fuel and ecological restraint, Saturnian route obligations, Uranian containment, Neptunian antimatter, and Pluto–Charon’s archive-and-yard economy assembled into one moving settlement.
