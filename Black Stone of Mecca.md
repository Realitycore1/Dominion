🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: The Black Stone (al-Ḥajar al-Aswad)
Discovery Location: Embedded in the Kaaba, Mecca, Arabia. Revered since pre-Islamic times, central to the Islamic hajj.
Summary:

Material: Irregular fragment, ~30 cm diameter, currently held in silver frame.

Composition: Basaltic and meteoritic properties debated. Recent isotopic tests (Yamama-controlled labs, 2038) claim it is an extraterrestrial meteorite, dated to ~6000 BCE.

Structural Condition: Fragmented and held together by resin and silver; historical accounts confirm repeated damage and repairs.

Anomalies:

Micro-magnetic irregularities, unexplained localized radiation bursts.

Hypothesis that stone may contain trapped gases or isotopes — suppressed in Caliphate publications.

Archaeological Significance: Confirmation of meteoritic [[Origin]] reframes it as both a sacred relic and a cosmic artifact. For the faithful, proof of God’s sign. For scientists, a [[Natural]] but extraordinary object that intersects with religious myth.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-02 (Black Stone)
Classification: EXTREME POLITICAL & RELIGIOUS IMPACT
Custody: [[Yamama Caliphate]], absolute control. Access restricted; foreign archaeologists denied since 2037.
Strategic Assessment:

Internal Role: The Caliphate proclaims the Black Stone as the primary proof of divine legitimacy, framing it as God’s covenant on [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]. Every sermon ties jihad to its preservation.

External Role: Framed as the counterweight to Christian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] (Shroud, Spear, Ark). Used in propaganda: “They have cloth and wood; we have the Stone of Heaven.”

Risks: Any damage to or theft of the Black Stone would trigger catastrophic mobilization across the Muslim world. Western or Persian propaganda emphasizing its [[Natural]] meteoritic nature risks violent backlash.
