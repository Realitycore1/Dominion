The Edda fairy is a magical creature that inhabits the [[Fahi forest]]. The fairy wonders the across the forest using their healing properties to mend the forest of any damage. And according to the [[Fahi’an]] the Edda fairy is a fragment of their Goddess [[Fahila]]. The Edda fairy is also used as the banner for the [[Fahi’an]] race.
