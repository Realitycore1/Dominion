**Article II — Transparency of Influence**  
All persuasive media, funding, and algorithmic curation must disclose their source, method, and intent. **Memetic jamming** (false-source or coercive loops) is a felony.

**Article III — Reciprocity in Aid**  
Members pledge baseline aid (food, water, shelter, medical) to others within logistic reach; aid lanes are neutral spaces.

**Article IV — Chord Councils**  
Deliberation by **resonant voting**. A measure passes when amplitude ≥ **θ** and persists across **τ**. Minority report is archived and funded for review.

**Article VI — Open Standards**  
Transit, comms, and health run on open protocols; certification bodies are elected and recallable. Gate rents are not permitted.

**Article VII — Arms & Engagement**  
No first strike on sealed infrastructure; escalation ladder favors **de-escalatory modulation**. Sovereign coercive AIs prohibited.

**Article VIII — Accession & Exit**  
Joining is by local chord referendum; exit requires the same + settlement of shared debts and a six-month safety period.
