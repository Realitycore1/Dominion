Eros is not in the main belt—it’s a near-Earth asteroid with:
	•	extremely low gravity
	•	irregular, elongated shape
	•	frequent Earth–Mars transfer windows
	•	minimal delta-v requirements

Eros becomes the inner system’s most efficient transfer and testing node.

Primary Role
	•	Shipyard testing and calibration
	•	Experimental propulsion trials
	•	Navigation systems validation
	•	Near-Earth logistics buffering

If Luna is Earth’s warehouse, Eros is its proving ground.

Station Architecture
	•	External truss-based stations anchored directly to the asteroid
	•	Minimal excavation (Eros is structurally fragile)
	•	Large rotating habitats tethered nearby
	•	Heavy reliance on automation

Humans don’t live on Eros.
They live around it.

Cultural Note

Eros crews are known for:
	•	short rotations
	•	extreme technical specialization
	•	high accident tolerance
	•	low attachment to place

Eros culture is transient, sharp, and technical—closer to test pilots than settlers.
