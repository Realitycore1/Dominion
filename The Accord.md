The Accord is a Statement That Each Colony is considered a state, which has the ability to local [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]. Each will conduct its own Enforcement, and has the ability to create and pass laws as long as it does not violate Federal [[law]] and the Constitution. The Accord also States that Each World is to pay its due through manpower when called upon.
