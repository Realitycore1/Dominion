1. Remnant Anglic (North America + Oceanic + Some Africa)

The old lingua franca survives—but as dozens of dialects.

Sub-branches:
	•	High Anglic (Hydrocliff Consortium)
Clean, technical, archivist-influenced English.
Similar to Late Middle English meeting MIT engineering jargon.
	•	Marrow-Speak (Gulfen Reach Clans)
Broken syntax, heavy slang, swamp metaphors, river vocabulary.
	•	Rigshore Creole (New Orleans Archipelago)
A beautiful fusion of English + Cajun French + Caribbean pidgins.
The “pirate tongue” of the new seas.
	•	Skygrave Trade Anglic (Arctic Federation)
Clipped, simple, trade-oriented.
Words borrowed from Inuktitut, Russian, Dutch sailors.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Remnant Anglic becomes the merchant tongue of Discordia—rough, flexible, unpretentious, shaped by maps and salvage.

⸻

2. Neo-Nordic / Teuton Langs (Europe)

Germanic languages degrade, hybridize, and re-emerge.

Sub-branches:
	•	Helveticon Cant (Swiss Alps)
Clean, minimal, influenced by mathematic notation and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] structure.
	•	Teuton Archipelago Speech
Harsh consonants, simplified grammar.
A warlike, terse tongue.
	•	Eurospeak Commonwealth Creole
A hybrid of English, French, and German used by traders in drowned Western Europe.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

A mix of broken romance languages and hardened Germanic remnants.
Think Old Norse + Dutch + survival slang.

⸻

3. Neo-Romance Rings (South Europe + Latin America)

Spanish, Portuguese, French, and Italian mingle, fracture, and reform.

Sub-branches:
	•	Loirecrest Canton
French-rooted but simplified phonetics (think Picard + Haitian French + isolation slang).
	•	Andean High Romance
Spanish + Quechua hybrid.
Rich, oral, chant-driven language.
	•	Pan-Mire Creole (Amazon)
Portuguese base with indigenous grammar and swamp terminology.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Soft, melodic, but structurally altered by centuries of oral tradition.

⸻

4. Indo-Cant Languages (South Asia)

Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and others diverge as the Indian subcontinent fractures.

Sub-branches:
	•	Deccan Monarchic Tongue
A royal court language blending Sanskritized Hindi + Dravidian loanwords.
	•	Bengal Sea Creole
Grammatically simplified, sea-themed vocabulary after Bengal floods.
	•	River-Caste Varna-Speak
A ritualized, caste-coded dialect used by priest-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]] maintaining waterworks.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Deeply preserved mythically, heavily altered structurally.

⸻

5. Sino-Fractured Languages (China + Korea + Japan)

China becomes linguistically unrecognizable after regional fragmentation.

Sub-branches:
	•	Plateau-Mandic
A Tibetan-influenced Mandarin descendant.
Tonality weakened. Syntax simplified.
	•	Yangtze Isles Tongue
Softened Mandarin with Austronesian flavor.
	•	Neo-Kyoto Sect Speech
Hyper-preserved Japanese, technical vocabulary intact.
A “frozen” language inside the Vault.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Mandarin doesn’t disappear—it fractures into multiple post-Mandarin macro-languages.

⸻

6. Afric Tongues (Africa)

Africa sees the least linguistic collapse due to its diversity and oral tradition strength.

Sub-branches:
	•	Horn-Tech Cant
Amharic + Somali + English + Rift Valley engineering jargon.
	•	Sahara Salt-Speech
Tuareg + Hausa + Berber + route-trader slang.
	•	Congo Verdant Tongue
A Bantu creole with strong tonal heritage.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Africa becomes the strongest linguistic supercontinent in Discordia—plural, stable, adaptive.

⸻

7. Polar & Frontier Hybrid Languages (Arctic, Antarctic)

Fragmentary but critical.

Sub-branches:
	•	Skygrave Polar Trade
English + Russian + Inuktitut.
The lingua franca of the Arctic trade belt.
	•	Antarctic Crown Cant
Upper-class academic speech mixed with technical jargon.
Prestige language for centuries.

⸻

⭐ III. THE NEW TRADE LINGUA FRANCA BY 2500–2600

By late Discordia, a new global trade language emerges:

“High Trade” (H-Trade)

A deliberate reconstruction of English + French + simplified grammar, created by:
	•	Alpine Helveticon
	•	Neo-Kyoto Vault
	•	Andean Sky-Steppes
	•	Red Sea Arcology Union

It becomes:
	•	the diplomatic tongue
	•	the merchant tongue
	•	the scholar tongue
	•	the proto-colonial lingua franca

It’s similar to Early Modern English stripped of irregularities and infused with loanwords from all continents.
