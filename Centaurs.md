1. Common Centaurs
	•	Definition: The baseline—[[Humans]] fused with horses from the waist down.
	•	Traits: Swift, strong, archers or raiders, torn between instinct and reason.
	•	Symbolism: The duality of [[Man]] and beast; civilization atop primal power.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Plains Centaurs
	•	Definition: Nomadic centaurs roaming grasslands and steppes.
	•	Traits: Masters of horseback archery, tribal organization, raiding [[culture]].
	•	Symbolism: Freedom of the plains, storm of hooves.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Forest Centaurs
	•	Definition: Woodland centaurs tied to druidic traditions.
	•	Traits: Camouflage, nature affinity, [[Close]] bonds with [[Fae]].
	•	Symbolism: Harmony of [[Man]] and nature; the wild protector.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Mountain Centaurs
	•	Definition: Rugged centaurs adapted to rocky slopes and alpine terrain.
	•	Traits: Stout builds, endurance climbers, wield hammers and axes.
	•	Symbolism: Strength and survival in hardship.

⸻

5. Desert Centaurs
	•	Definition: Centaurs shaped by arid wastelands.
	•	Traits: Light builds, long-distance endurance, water-hoarding [[culture]].
	•	Symbolism: Resilience in desolation.

⸻

6. War Centaurs
	•	Definition: Centaurs bred or trained for battle.
	•	Traits: Armored bodies, heavy weapons, shock cavalry tactics.
	•	Symbolism: Warhorse and [[Warrior]] fused; living war machines.

⸻

7. Shadow Centaurs
	•	Definition: Centaurs bound to cursed forests, graveyards, or the [[Abyss]].
	•	Traits: Blackened hides, spectral auras, necrotic archery.
	•	Symbolism: The fall of nature into corruption.

⸻

8. Noble Centaurs
	•	Definition: Centaurs with structured societies, kings, and councils.
	•	Traits: Ornate armor, code of honor, philosophy alongside combat.
	•	Symbolism: Order reigning over wild instinct.

⸻

9. Elemental Centaurs
	•	Definition: Centaurs infused with primal forces.
	•	Variants: Firemanes, Stormhoof, Frostmanes, Stonehoof.
	•	Symbolism: The wild given elemental expression.

⸻

10. Arch-Centaurs (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary centaurs said to embody the Hunt, the Storm, or the Wild itself.
	•	Traits: Colossal stature, semi-divine auras, command of beasts and nature.
	•	Symbolism: The eternal hunter; [[Humanity]]’s shadow as predator-god.
