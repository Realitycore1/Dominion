•	Type: Metaphysical Civilization of [[Verse]]-Rulers.
	•	Rulers: A Council of Empyrearchs, each a sovereign entity who has devoured, bound, or subsumed an entire [[Verse]] as their [[Domain]].
	•	Power Source: The more verses an Empyrearch subjugates, the greater their authority and metaphysical stature grows.
	•	Comparison: The [[God Empress]] of [[The Dominion]] could be described as a rogue Empyrearch, existing outside their order, a singularity of will not bound to their council.

⸻

Structure
	•	Council of the Crowned: The assembly of Empyrearchs; membership requires absolute control of at least one [[Verse]].
	•	Throne-Verses: Each Empyrearch sits upon the core of their conquered [[Verse]], weaving its [[Axioms]] and laws into their body of power.
	•	[[Chains]] of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: When they subjugate a new [[Verse]], they rewrite its metaphysical constants to echo their own essence.
	•	Empire of Verses: Not a federation, but a web of enslaved realities, each stripped of autonomy yet still functioning as tributaries of power.

⸻

[[culture]] & Philosophy
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of [[Ascension]]: To exist is to dominate; to grow is to conquer. Verses are steps on the ladder to absolute metaphysical supremacy.
	•	No Singular Dogma: Each Empyrearch embodies their own metaphysical creed (entropy, flame, dream, [[Silence]], storm, recursion). Together, their council functions as a parliament of absolutes.
	•	Civilizational [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]: [[The Empyrearchy]] is not made of citizens in the traditional sense — its “people” are echoes of the subjugated verses, stripped avatars bound in service to their overlords.
	•	Mythic Memory: Conquered verses retain a distorted memory of their original [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], twisted into allegories that glorify the ruling Empyrearch.

⸻

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] & Expansion
	•	[[Verse]]-Armadas: Fleets formed of collapsed [[Axioms]] and condensed metaphysical armies, each [[Soldier]] an echo of a subjugated [[Verse]].
	•	Council Wars: Though united against outsiders, Empyrearchs secretly vie with each other, stealing tributary verses or undermining rival [[Domains]].
	•	Conquest Method: Not physical warfare but axiomatic war — rewriting [[Physics]], metaphysics, and logic of the targeted [[Verse]] until its fabric aligns with the Empyrearch’s will.

⸻

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Empyrearchs: [[Entities]] similar in magnitude to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]], but wholly separate. Where [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]] embody archetypal forms of transcendence, the Empyrearchs embody axiomatic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] itself.
	•	Power Growth: Each conquest strengthens them in a feedback loop. They are not eternal — but as long as conquest continues, they verge closer to it.
	•	Immortality: They cannot die in the traditional sense; to kill an Empyrearch, one must sever every [[Verse]] that sustains them.
