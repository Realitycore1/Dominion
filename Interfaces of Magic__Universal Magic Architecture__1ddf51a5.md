Definition
An interface is the means by which magic is applied. [[Domains]] describe what magic does; interfaces describe how beings access, control, and express it.

⸻

I. Biological Interfaces – Flesh and Soul

Magic worked directly through the body or essence.
	•	Bloodletting / Vital Sacrifice – cutting the flesh to fuel spells ([[Vitality Magic – Blood Magic]]).
	•	Soul Projection – detaching spirit to act independently ([[Necrosis Magic]] + Concordian [[Yondor]] [[Undead]]).
	•	Innate Resonance – [[New Readers Start Here/Dominion Omniverse/Dominion Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Eos Verse/Dominion Verse/Species/Species|Species]]-born talents (elven glamour, [[Giant]] motion vectors, angelic [[Domains]]).
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Sensitivity – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]-trained individuals whose nervous [[Systems]] are wired into [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] currents.

⸻

II. Cognitive Interfaces – Will and Thought

Magic channeled through mental structures.
	•	Word and Rune – shaping reality by inscribing or speaking.
	•	Geasa / Oaths – promises encoded in soul-logic ([[Binding magic]], [[Domination magic]]).
	•	Meditative Focus – trance, prayer, or chant aligning the mind with arcane flows.
	•	Mythopoeic Projection – belief itself as interface: stories so strong they alter reality ([[Mythopoeia magic]]).
	•	Dreamcraft – using the dream state as interface to illusion and prophecy ([[Illusion Magic]], [[Revelation magic]]).

⸻

III. Material Interfaces – Tools and Artifacts

External implements that focus or stabilize power.
	•	Staffs, Wands, Rods – common conduits for Alteration, Creation, or Cosmos work.
	•	Sigils, Runes, Glyphs – symbols carved, painted, or tattooed, acting as anchors.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] and Icons – mythopoeically charged objects ([[Creation magic]], [[Protection Magic]]).
	•	Blood-etched Weapons – blades bound with vitality, echoing life essence.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Crystals – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] refinement, storing essence for large-scale effects.

⸻

IV. Environmental Interfaces – Land and Sky

Magic harnessed through world or [[Verse]] features.
	•	Ley Lines – [[Natural]] conduits of planetary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] ([[Cosmos Magic]]).
	•	Sacred Sites – groves, shrines, volcanoes, or battlefields carrying lingering resonance.
	•	Tidal / Lunar Cycles – moons and [[Stars]] as timing interfaces for Cosmos and Revelation.
	•	Blightfields – corrupted ground amplifies Necrosis.
	•	Exoderm Overlays – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] planetary grids, artificially tessellating magic use.

⸻

V. Collective Interfaces – [[Choirs]] and Guilds

Magic magnified by group effort.
	•	Choir Casting – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Oracle [[Choirs]] link minds for Revelation.
	•	Guild Rituals – Concordian bartering guilds use shared sigils for Binding or Creation.
	•	Harmonic Chants – [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]’s communal resonance interfaces.
	•	Cultic Rites – Gnostic Expanse Demiurgic cults channel despair into Necrosis or Domination.
	•	Battlefield Enchantments – armies chanting as one to erect Protection domes.

⸻

VI. Technological Interfaces – Engineered Conduits

Where magic and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Military/Technology/Technology|Technology]] fuse.
	•	Spell-guns / Arcane Platforms – Concordian sandbox weapons ([[Motion magic]], [[Annihilation magic]]).
	•	Exoderm Anchors – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s axiomatic rewriting engines ([[Axiomatic Warping magic]]).
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Drives – ships that ride cosmos flows through stabilized resonance.
	•	Cyber-[[Undead]] Nodes – [[Yondor]]’s -machines linking Necrosis with circuitry.
	•	Memetic Codexes – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] “programming” of Mythopoeia into hostile verses.

⸻

VII. Axiomatic Interfaces – Highest Order

Rare and dangerous, used by the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]], Demiurge, or mythic saints.
	•	[[Verse]] Anchors – rewriting root [[Axioms]].
	•	Null Zones – suppressing laws as an interface of control.
	•	Paradox Sculpting – contradictions stabilized to force outcomes.
	•	Mythic Resonance – saints and heroes as living interfaces; their story itself channels cosmic [[Domains]].
	•	Cosmic [[Choirs]] – civilizations acting as a single instrument, harmonizing [[Stars]] and galaxies.
