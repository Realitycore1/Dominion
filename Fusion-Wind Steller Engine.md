A Fusion-Wind Stellar Engine is a stellar propulsion [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] that harnesses, redirects, and amplifies the [[Natural]] plasma outflows of a star (solar wind) to generate thrust. By magnetically capturing stellar wind and venting it directionally, the entire star (and its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]) can be pushed through space.
Capture Arrays

Magnetic Scoop Fields: Vast magnetic arcs tens of AU across, sweeping up solar wind.

Plasma Lattices: Parametric nets woven from strange matter, guiding plasma into conduits.

Atlas Pillar Anchors: Stabilize the collector structures against drift and collapse.

Processing Core

Additional Dynamic Core Ignitors can enhance stellar wind by boosting solar activity.

Energy harvested simultaneously powers surrounding [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Directional Thrusters

[[Giant]] plasma exhausts channel outflows as relativistic jets, pushing the star along.

Some [[Systems]] include beam reinforcement — lasers or microwaves to further accelerate expelled mass.

Performance

Thrust: Higher than Shkadov engines (which use only photon pressure).

Efficiency: Lower than Caplan engines (which “burn” the star directly), but safer long-term.

Use Case: Suited for slow stellar migration, or as hybrid [[Systems]] paired with Dyson shells.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Used to reposition [[Stars]] for megastructural arrays, or shepherd [[Systems]] into Spiralverse corridors.

Economic: The captured stellar wind provides infinite plasma feedstock for Cornucopia Machines and [[Computronium]] Nodes.

Religious: Regarded as “the Leashes of Suns” — proof the Empress not only cages [[Stars]] (Dyson Fortresses) but leads them through creation.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Cathedral-like magnetic towers rising millions of km, glowing like stained glass arcs in plasma.

Suprematist Monolithism: Seen from afar, the collector arcs appear as solid black crescents eclipsing the sun.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Plasma lattices ripple like luminous fabrics, woven into geometric spirals.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Exhaust plumes inscribed with visible Concordia glyphs, fiery prayers written in the solar wind.

From a distance, a Fusion-Wind Engine makes the star look winged — luminous plasma streams stretching like cosmic feathers, the sun itself transformed into a radiant seraph steed.

To myth: Called the “Sun-Wings of Amber”, each plume seen as a banner of her unstoppable march.
