# Chapter 11 - Edge of Accord

## Timeline Function

War Years 7-8. Negotiators come close to a settlement but cannot agree what survival requires.

## Event Role

- Revisits the Year 151 accords as legal memory.
- Creates ceasefire maps, prisoner exchanges, and disputed resource guarantees.
- Exposes incompatible definitions of autonomy, continuity, debt, and justice.
- Shows how close Harmina comes to stopping before the worst phase.

## Consequence

The failed accord makes later unity harsher because everyone remembers the chance they rejected.
