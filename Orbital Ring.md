## Summary

Suprematist
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
An Orbital Ring is a rigid ring encircling a planet at low [[Earth (Terra)]] orbit height, held aloft by magnetic tethers and anchored towers. It serves as a permanent platform for space launch, trade, defense, and habitation, effectively turning a planet into a crowned jewel of [[Infrastructure]].
Structure

Main Ring: Encircles entire equator or a band around the planet.

Anchor Towers: [[Atlas Pillars]] tether the ring down to surface megacities.

Lift Spines: Maglev elevators carry cargo and people seamlessly between ground and orbit.

Stability Nodes: Gravitic stabilizers and counter-rotating sections keep the ring balanced.

Functions

Transport: Permanent launch [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]; no [[Rockets]] required.

Habitat: Cities, farms, and sanctuaries line the ring’s spine, supporting millions to billions.

Defense: Ring can mount fortress bastions, beam arrays, and kinetic launchers.

Energy: Acts as a planetary solar collector, beaming power down to the surface.

Variants

Equatorial Rings: Continuous world-girdling crowns.

Partial Rings: Arc-segments over [[Regions]], usually around capital cities.

Nested Rings: Multiple stacked rings at different orbital heights, forming latticed skies.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: A crown of prosperity — seamless access between ground and space, anchoring planetary economies.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: A fortress-halo — bristling with launch tubes, shield nodes, and orbital artillery.

Economic: Turns a planet into a hub of interplanetary commerce; every trade artery flows through the ring.

Religious: Known as the “Amber Crowns”, each ring seen as a physical halo placed on the world by the Empress herself.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Tower buttresses rise from the planet to brace the halo, like ribs of a celestial cathedral.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Ring cities spiral upward with fractal towers, glowing with shifting geometry.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs inscribed along the ring’s outer hull blaze at night, visible from the ground as scripture written across the sky.
