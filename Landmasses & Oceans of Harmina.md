Oceans

[[Harmina]]’s oceans aren’t calm or [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]-like — they’re hyperactive chemical seas:
	•	The Black Expanse – a global equatorial ocean, thick with mineral-rich waters that shimmer purple-black. Constant storms churn its surface, generating kilometer-high waves and lightning supercells.
	•	The Vermilion Deeps – deep abyssal trenches in the southern hemisphere where hydrothermal vents belch red, metallic-rich plumes. Life here is bioluminescent and predatory, feeding directly on chemical outflows.
	•	The Glasswake Sea – northern hemisphere ocean studded with volcanic island [[Chains]], where molten flows frequently hit the water, creating vast obsidian shelves.

These oceans are highly acidic in places and occasionally carry radioactive currents due to runoff from volcanic activity and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] vault structures.

🏔️ Continents & Major Landmasses

[[Harmina]]’s land should be broken into three super-continents (powerful seats of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] civilization) and several fractured satellite landmasses.
	1.	Azeroth Plateau (Central Continent)
	•	Largest landmass, located in the equatorial zone.
	•	Host to New Aurora Megacity ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] capital).
	•	Geology: dominated by basalt plains, aurora-lit mountains, and deep honeycomb caves.
	•	Function: the administrative and industrial heart of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]].
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Draelith (Southern Supercontinent)
	•	Harsh volcanic land, covered in red deserts and storm-cracked mountains.
	•	Perpetual auroras from southern magnetic pole.
	•	Rich in radioactive crystal fields → main [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] extraction zone.
	•	Function: resource engine of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]].
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Vorcalis (Northern Supercontinent)
	•	High plateaus, jagged cliffs, scarred tundra.
	•	Storm plains and “flaming tornado alley.”
	•	Function: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military|Military]] proving grounds and bioforges.
	•	Home to several warform spawning pits and ancient Matter Core anchors.

⸻

🏝️ Secondary Landmasses
	•	The Shattered Archipelago – volcanic isles scattered across the Vermilion Deeps. Used for war-game trials and naval shipyard zones.
	•	The Auric Isles – glowing crystal islands in the Glasswake Sea, rumored to house sacred vaults.
	•	The Riftlands – broken continent-fragments east of Azeroth Plateau, fractured by megavolcanic eruptions.

⸻

🌐 Planetary Layout
	•	Equatorial Belt: dominated by The Black Expanse and Azeroth Plateau.
	•	South Hemisphere: Draelith supercontinent + Vermilion Deeps.
	•	North Hemisphere: Vorcalis + Glasswake Sea.
	•	Faultlines: continents fractured by tectonic instability, leaving [[Harmina]] riddled with active volcanic rifts.
