Core Bias:

Collective permanence through rigid infrastructure

This is where Stone merges with Hive logic.

Meaning:

Structure is no longer merely static — it becomes collectively inhabited, maintained, and extended as one persistent body.

Traits:
	•	colony architecture
	•	eusocial megastructures
	•	collective tunneling or building
	•	rigid communal spatial logic
	•	distributed permanence

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the city is the organism
	•	permanence is achieved through collective occupation and maintenance
	•	structure and population are inseparable

Common Expressions:
	•	hive cities
	•	tunneling civilizations
	•	eusocial fortress worlds
	•	ant-colony megastructures
	•	communal machine-architecture ecologies

Failure Mode:

Architectural lock
The collective becomes too rigidly embedded in its own infrastructure to adapt.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	hive-city empires
	•	machine collectives
	•	insectoid or eusocial civilizations
	•	built-world basin expressions

This should absolutely be one of your strongest additive subtypes.
