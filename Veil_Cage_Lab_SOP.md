# Veil Cage — Lab SOP

**Objective.** Safe operation of Null (veil) rooms for testing and counter-chorus.

## Roles
- **Keeper:** owns keys, posts hashes, runs checklists.  
- **Operator:** runs experiments.  
- **Auditor:** reads logs, signs off.

## Open/Close
- Pre-open: instrument check, hash post, Null verification.  
- During: light-hygiene enforced; no flicker sources; all rites disabled.  
- [[Close]]: counter-chorus sweep; archive logs; rotate keys.

## Safety
- [[Radiator Neutrality]] unless declared total war.  
- [[Silentium]]/[[Kapik]] protocols enforced (no strobe, no sharp transients).  
- Emergency nulling phrase posted at the door.
