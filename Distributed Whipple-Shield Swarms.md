Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Distributed Whipple-Shield Swarms are vast, dispersed clouds of autonomous shielding units deployed around [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]], fleets, or orbital networks. They act as sacrificial armor, intercepting micrometeoroids, debris, and even weapons [[Fire]] before it can reach the protected asset.
In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] practice, they are living clouds of defense — half-shield, half-drone ecology.
Swarm Composition

Micro-Shields: Thin wafers of composite/ablative material, free-flying in formation.

Drone-Shepherds: Larger units projecting magnetic, plasma, or gravitic fields to hold micro-shields in place.

Deployment

Laid in layered clouds around Clarke Cities, [[Banks Orbitals]], Lozenge Stations, [[Ark Platforms]].

For fleets, swarms travel with capital ships, stretching ahead like bows of light.

Can be “woven” into corridors of protection along Beamed-Sail Lanes.

Dynamics

Shields can sacrifice themselves in cascades — hundreds vaporizing at once to absorb the energy of a kinetic slug or beam strike.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Prevent catastrophic micrometeorite impacts on orbital habitats and trade lanes.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: First line of defense against kinetic kill vehicles and beam saturation — swarms act as ablative armor for entire fleets.

Religious: Referred to as “the Halo Veils” — veils of protection cast by the Empress, invisible to citizens but omnipresent.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Shepherd drones resemble flying buttresses, massive armored struts gliding through the swarm.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the swarm looks like a perfect black haze, blotting the [[Stars]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Micro-shields move in flowing lattices, fractal swarms adjusting like living script.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Impact shockwaves ripple through the swarm like luminous prayers — glowing patterns of protection.

Seen from a distance, a Whipple-Shield Swarm looks like a ghostly shroud, faintly glimmering as it drinks the starlight, always shifting but never dispersing.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Invisible guardians; they may never know they live beneath a ceaseless defensive cloud.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Proof of foresight: no empire endures without armor against the random cruelty of space.

To myth: Called the “Sleepless Host”, said to be the wings of [[Angels]] watching eternally above.

Constantly reconfiguring, denser against expected threats, thinner where efficiency is needed.

To rebels: A symbol of futility — even their fastest weapons are swallowed by the Empress’s veils.
