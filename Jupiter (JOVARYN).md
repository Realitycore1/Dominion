# Jovaryn  
*The Mantle Throne – Jupiter in Active Domain Conversion*

---

## Overview

Jovaryn is not a hollowed Jupiter.  
It is a **planet undergoing neural assimilation**.

The Domain does not remove mass or collapse structure.  
It exploits conductivity, pressure, and magnetohydrodynamic behavior to grow a planetary-scale bioelectric organism within Jupiter’s natural layers.
