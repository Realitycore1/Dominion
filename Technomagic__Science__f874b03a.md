## Summary

GalFed does not officially call its Aether applications magic. It uses terms like noetic engineering, harmonic fieldcraft, cognitive architecture, and cross-domain systems. Outsiders often call it technomagic because the results blur science, ritual, medicine, and metaphysics.

## Details

Common applications:

- Memory lattice therapy: restoring damaged identities through Aether-supported recall structures.
- Harmonic stabilization: using resonant tones, light, or field pulses to prevent cognition drift.
- Archive embodiment: allowing cultural records to be experienced as guided memory simulations.
- Consent wards: legal fields that verify whether a species-specific signal counts as agreement.
- Quarantine veils: containment membranes around unstable Aether sites or infected ruins.
- Transit hymns: synchronization patterns that calm field turbulence around corridor gates.
- Null anchor beads: portable tools used by restoration teams to preserve selfhood in hazardous zones.
- Grief engines: memorial systems that hold the names and sensory echoes of unrecoverable populations.

Military and coercive uses exist but are less public:

- Harmonic pacification.
- Memory narrowing.
- Identity desynchronization.
- Crowd resonance damping.
- Aether-guided interrogation.
- Predictive loyalty conditioning.

GalFed technomagic is strongest when it looks gentle. A clinic, school, courthouse, or memorial may all contain systems capable of measuring and shaping the interior life of a population.
