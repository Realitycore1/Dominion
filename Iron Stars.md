I. Physical Definition

An Iron Star is a stellar-scale object in which all nuclear fusion processes have ceased, and the majority of its mass has been converted into iron-56 or similar isotopes — the most tightly bound nuclei according to nuclear [[Physics]].
In [[Natural]] universes, this is a heat-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] timescale object, forming over 10¹⁵–10¹⁵⁰ years via nuclear reactions, proton decay, or neutron capture equilibria.
In engineered or altered verses, this state may be achieved deliberately and rapidly for structural stability or containment purposes.

[[Key]] features:
	•	No Fusion Output — Iron nuclei cannot release energy via fusion, marking the terminal point of stellar burning.
	•	Maximal Nuclear Stability — Resistant to decay under most astrophysical conditions.
	•	Degenerate Matter Pressure — Remnant may be supported by electron, neutron, or exotic degeneracy pressures.
	•	Cold, Dark Surface — Emits negligible radiation unless externally heated.
