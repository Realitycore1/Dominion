# FreeStack Socialist Republics

## Nation Assignment

Assigned nation: F.S.S.R. / FreeStack Socialist Republics.

Manuscript role: worker republic bloc, food and labor power, ideological rival of elite continuity states, and second strike participant in the First Strike on New Aurora.

## Core Identity

The FreeStack Socialist Republics grow from stacked worker settlements created to relieve New Aurora overcrowding and expand food, salvage, repair, and fabrication capacity. "FreeStack" begins as a practical term for modular habitation stacks made from Ark surplus, emergency frames, stormproof brackets, and whatever could be made habitable.

Its politics grow from ration anger. FreeStack citizens notice that central institutions praise hazard labor while better districts remain warmer, cleaner, and better supplied. Their ideology is not imported whole from Earth. It grows from Harmina's shared air, shared heat, shared food, shared childcare, and shared risk.

## Geographic Core

Core regions:

- stacked worker settlements beyond New Aurora overflow districts.
- Red Fen Echo Basin.
- Marishan food basins.
- fungal agriculture towers.
- salvage yards.
- modular industrial cooperatives.
- worker cargo terminals along mega train food routes.

FreeStack geography is dense, practical, improvised, food-centered, and politically loud. Its settlements often look ugly to outsiders because beauty is not allowed to outrank function.

## Origin From New Aurora

The early FreeStack population includes displaced worker families, agricultural technicians, salvage crews, fabricators, childcare cooperatives, ration clerks, and organizers shaped by years of crowding and unequal risk.

The Stack Referendums federate worker settlements into the F.S.S.R. after multiple communities refuse privatized allocation contracts and vote to coordinate ration systems, labor defense, and public childcare. New Aurora initially calls this illegal collective bargaining across settlement jurisdictions. FreeStack answers that people cannot be jurisdictionally separate when they share the same failing heat grid.

## Culture

FreeStack culture values labor dignity, ration transparency, cooperative ownership, public childcare, useful ugliness, and suspicion of inherited office.

Common stereotypes:

- argumentative.
- communal.
- bluntly moral.
- suspicious of luxury.
- proud of systems that work even when ugly.
- able to turn a meal queue into a constitutional debate.

FreeStack children often eat before speeches. This is both custom and doctrine: bodies first, rhetoric second.

## Government and Institutions

Important institutions:

- Stack Councils: elected by habitation stack, labor unit, and district.
- Ration Transparency Boards: public accounting of food, heat, water, medicine, and grow-light access.
- Cooperative Foundries: worker-controlled industrial and repair collectives.
- Child Commons: shared childcare systems that become ideological centers.
- FreeStack Congress: federation of worker republics and stack settlements.
- Labor Defense Councils: emergency bodies that convert production networks into militia systems.

FreeStack law recognizes clones as citizens in active manuscript continuity. Its slang and social habits take longer to catch up, which creates useful tension between principle and lived prejudice.

## Economy and Resources

Economic strengths:

- food towers.
- fungal starter systems.
- bioreactors.
- salvage.
- mass labor.
- cooperative foundries.
- repair crews.
- public-health logistics.
- childcare systems that keep adults available for work.

FreeStack's greatest leverage is that everyone needs food and labor, but FreeStack's weakness is that its own people can become overcounted as capacity. Bad policy turns bodies into infrastructure.

## Military Doctrine

FreeStack doctrine treats defense as mass participation. It distrusts elite weapons, but it is not anti-technology. It favors rugged systems that can be repaired publicly and deployed by trained workers.

Primary programs:

- Common Body Program: public endurance, injury recovery, militia readiness, and maintenance-cycle relief.
- Cooperative Armor Frames: rugged powered suits adapted from agriculture, rescue, and maintenance.
- Child Commons Continuity: demographic and welfare system linking family support to public responsibility.
- Fungal Bioreactor Defense Grid: hardened food and medical production against blockade or blight.
- Labor Militia Mobilization: rapid conversion of councils into defense networks.

FreeStack also uses drone and ballistic distribution swarms because swarm logic fits its political imagination: many small units acting together.

## Internal Fault Lines

- public welfare versus demographic pressure.
- cooperative pride versus surveillance by committees.
- clone citizenship law versus everyday prejudice.
- family incentives versus counting births as state capacity.
- anti-elite politics versus the need for skilled technical leadership.
- moral certainty versus the temptation to justify mass violence as defense.

FreeStack's danger is turning collective survival into compulsory virtue.

## Role in the New World War

FreeStack reads the Founder Clause, Project Ra, and Equilon continuity claims as elite violence hidden inside public infrastructure. It also fears Atlovaian precision weapons because "clean" strikes often leave workers to bury the results.

During the Founder Session, FreeStack does not operate as Atlovaia's ally. It detects Atlovaian movement, reads the mirror shift as the start of an Equilon-Atlovaian bargain, and launches its own strike plan. Its independently authorized drone and ballistic distribution swarms hit New Aurora and Equilon's suspected proto-legionary infrastructure seconds after the first Atlovaian lance.

War function:

- mass labor and militia power.
- food security and bioreactor defense.
- ideological rival to elite and coastal powers.
- second-strike participant in the First Strike.
- state most likely to turn defensive war into revolutionary language.

## Story Use

Use FreeStack to show the nobility and danger of collective survival. Its people should be sympathetic when feeding children, infuriating in meetings, and frightening when convinced violence is the only language elites understand.
