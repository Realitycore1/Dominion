## Details

A Freesphere is a type of planetar
y structure. It is a large, open-air structure that is designed to accommodate and support various forms of life and ecosystems. Freespheres are usually found in areas of the galaxy that are rich in resources or that have a unique ecosystem. They are often used by players as a way to study and explore these environments without harming the [[Natural]] balance. The interior of a Freesphere is typically divided into different sections, each with its own unique climate and ecosystem. These sections can be customized to suit specific [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]] or habitats, allowing players to create a [[Wide]] range of living spaces within the structure. Freespheres are typically constructed on a massive scale, with the structures sometimes stretching hundreds of kilometers across. They are often equipped with advanced environmental control [[Systems]] that can regulate temperature, pressure, and other environmental factors within the sphere. Freespheres can be used as a way to explore and study the unique ecosystems and biomes found in different parts of the galaxy. They can also serve as a hub for scientific research and discovery, allowing those to learn more about the universe and its inhabitants.
