Iran, also known as Persia, is a country in Western Asia with a rich [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]], [[culture]], and resources. Its geography is diverse, with rugged mountain ranges, fertile plains, and coastal [[Regions]]. Iran is located between the Caspian Sea in the north and the Persian Gulf in the south, with its capital city of [[Tehran]] located in the central region of the country. Iran's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is largely dependent on oil and gas, which make up around 60% of its exports. The country also has significant reserves of [[Natural]] gas, copper, iron ore, and other minerals. In addition to oil and gas, Iran is a major producer of fruits, vegetables, and other agricultural products. Iran's [[culture]] is deeply rooted in its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and religion. The country's official language is Persian, which has a rich literary tradition dating back to ancient times. Islam is the dominant religion in Iran, with Shia Muslims making up the majority of the population. Iran's cuisine is also diverse, with influences from both Persian and Middle Eastern [[Cultures]]. Iran's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] is marked by periods of political instability and conflict, including multiple invasions by foreign powers throughout its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. The country has also had a complex relationship with the United States and its allies, which has resulted in economic sanctions and tensions between the two countries. Despite these challenges, Iran remains a vibrant and dynamic country with a rich heritage and potential for growth and development.
