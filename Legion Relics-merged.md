######## Absolution Engine

Type: Blood-reactive Chain-Glaive Cannon
Dual-mode weapon: retractable rotating blade on one end, integrated explosive bolter on the other.
The chainblades sharpen as they drink enemy blood.
Runes along the weapon determine who is “worthy” of a clean [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]—and who isn’t.
Considered half-weapon, half-judgment.

Naval Boarding Variant
Jet-propulsion integrated into the wrist for lunge-kills in void combat.
Primary strike claw is serrated, secondary claw injects ionic overload into enemy armor.
Also functions as a mag-seal manipulator for tearing open bulkheads.
Rune Function: Glows only in vacuum—activating lock-break glyphs and void-seal bypass sequences.


######## Chalice of Silence

Type: Anti-Psyker Repeater
Fires containment crucibles lined with anti-neural dampeners and blessed [[Antimatter]] shards.
Silences all psionic activity in a large radius.
Used during exorcism campaigns and battles against [[Chapter XXV (Dracoshia)|Chapter XXV (Dracoshia)]]-touched horrors.
Emits no sound—only the soft hum of collapsing thought.



######## Clavis Mortem – The Deathkey Gauntlet

Type: Divine Power Claw (Exo-Gauntlet)
Design:
A brutal, oversized gauntlet forged from interlocked blackened alloys and sanctified neutron-steel.
The clawed fingers each terminate in shimmering white-gold talons, capable of vibrating at frequencies that sever matter on a molecular level.
Engraved across the plating are burning runes in Veritas, spelling out a continual litany of judgment and unmaking.
A halo-shaped emitter sits above the wrist, casting a shimmering energy arc that suspends anti-kinetic fields.
Powers:
Rend-Protocol: Can tear through armor, vehicles, or fortifications as though peeling fabric.
Edict Resonance: Each strike carries a digitized decree—causing reality to “agree” with the wielder. Against heretics or traitors, flesh willingly parts.
Apostate Bane: Deals exponentially increasing damage against corrupted or [[Chapter XXV (Dracoshia)|Chapter XXV (Dracoshia)]]-touched foes.
Hand of Writ: Can imprint [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[law]] into physical surfaces, or even into the body of a victim as a sacred execution method.
Lore:
Only given to Exemplar executioners or high-ranking Inquisitorial officers.
Created in the Vault-Forges beneath the [[Librarium of Final Acts]] using alloy retrieved from a starship swallowed by a temporal wound.
The first wielder was said to have ended an entire rebellion by slamming the gauntlet into the ground—causing all rebels within a hundred kilometers to suffer simultaneous cardiac arrest.

######## Sarcophagus Engine

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Warframe Codex – Sarcophagus Engine

⸻

Designation: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]] Exoform / Sarcophagus Engine

Type: [[Ascension]]-class warframe / semi-autonomous revenant construct
Height: 4.2–6.3 meters (varies by Legion pattern)
Mass: 11–19 metric tons
Power Source: Sarcophagus Core ([[Aetheric]] singular micro-reactor)

⸻

I. Purpose and Concept

The Sarcophagus Engine serves as the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s counterpart to ancient [[Dreadnoughts]] of other civilizations—a fusion of sacred resurrection and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] engineering. Unlike crude preservation of the dying, the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s design is an act of transfiguration. The fallen Legionnaire is reborn within an armored form where mind, soul, and matter converge.

Each engine carries the essence of a single [[Warrior]]—physically preserved, neurally mapped, and merged with the containment field of a singularity reactor. The result is a conscious weapon, simultaneously alive and dead, eternally bound to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

These warforms are revered not as machines but as Relic Revenants—extensions of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] will and [[Continuity]] beyond mortality.

⸻

II. Structure and Function

Chassis:
Built from layered Sanguinite-carbon composites and armored with [[Ritual]] plate in the image of the pilot’s Legion. Frames exhibit self-healing through internal nanitic myomer reknitting.

Core [[Systems]]:
	•	Sarcophagus Core: A sealed [[Aetheric]] singularity coupled to the enshrined pilot’s neural lattice.
	•	Cognition Bridge: Synchronizes the pilot’s mind-state with the warframe. Memory bleed often manifests as battlefield murmurs or hymnal echoes.
	•	Resonant Choir Node: Emits a harmonic pulse unique to each engine—used for battlefield coordination and [[Ritual]] identification.
	•	Containment Hierarchy: Threefold tomb-field architecture ensuring stability of the soul-reactor interface: matter tomb, energy tomb, and veritas tomb.

Mobility:
Servo-cluster actuators allow near-[[Human]] fluidity despite weight. Many exhibit motion patterns recalling their living combat [[Styles]].

⸻

III. Operational [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]

Roles:
	•	Shock Vanguard: Frontline assault units capable of annihilating entrenched enemy lines.
	•	Reliquary Guardian: Protectors of sacred [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] sites and [[Infernariate]] archives.
	•	Judicial Enforcer: Instruments of final punishment, deployed where heresy or treachery take root.

Combat Enhancements:
	•	Direct [[Aetheric]] discharge through melee [[Systems]] (plasma claws, gravitic hammers, or radiant halberds).
	•	Integrated weapon ports accept all LSA configurations.
	•	Adaptive armor harmonics permit deflection of plasma, kinetics, and metaphysical attack.

⸻

IV. Psychological and Spiritual Profile

Although stripped of ordinary mortality, most Sarcophagus [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Pilots|Pilots]] retain emotional fragments. They dream in static; they recall wars centuries dead. Some whisper to the living, offering counsel or warning.

Communication occurs through Choir resonance, a layered blend of machine-speech and harmonic vocalization that carries both data and emotion.

Despite their reverence, containment protocols are strict—should a Sarcophagus Engine experience core fracture or memory cascade, the [[Infernariate]] performs immediate tomb-stasis termination to prevent spiritual corruption.

⸻

V. Legion Patterns

Each major Legion maintains its own Sarcophagus pattern variant:
	•	Ares Pattern – “Pyric Tomb”
Furnace-core exoform with vented plasma exhausts; converts reactor bleed into weaponized flame.
	•	Amaterasu Pattern – “Dawn Reliquary”
Emits radiant photonic auras; capable of blinding entire assault formations.
	•	Anubis Pattern – “Necrotic Crypt”
Generates necrotic entropy fields that degrade organic tissue and machine cohesion alike.
	•	Enlil Pattern – “Tempest Catacomb”
Ion discharge architecture; lightning arcs cascade across the armor during high-output engagement.
	•	Hades Pattern – “Abyssal Vault”
Armored for attrition and psychological domination, suffused with subsonic dread projectors.
	•	Fenrir Pattern – “Iron Howl”
Integrates feral servo musculature for berserker-level melee performance; reactor syncs to adrenaline resonance.
	•	Helios Pattern – “Solar Sepulcher”
Houses a radiant core that mimics stellar fusion—burns brighter as the pilot’s rage intensifies.
	•	Quetzalcoatl Pattern – “Serpent Sun Reliquary”
Decorated with feathered plasma fins, releases whip-like tendrils of [[Aetheric]] lightning in circular arcs.
	•	Set Pattern – “Red Storm Catacomb”
Utilizes magnetic particulate fields to weaponize desert dust into metallic shrapnel clouds.
	•	Vritra Pattern – “Abyssal Coil”
Employs localized gravitic constriction fields to crush enemies or seal rifts.

⸻

VI. [[Ritual]] Significance

Each activation is a sanctification: the [[Infernariate]] performs rites of [[Ascension]] Requiem, binding the pilot’s essence into the engine through song, blood, and luminous code.

The Sarcophagus Engine thus stands as both [[Warrior]] and reliquary—a walking cathedral of willpower, bound in eternal service to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

Final Inscription:

“We are not entombed. We are exalted. We stand beyond the grave, that the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] may never fall.”

######## The Anastael

The Word Made Steel"
Appearance
Length: Taller than a standard [[Soldier]]. A hand-and-a-half blade with celestial proportions.
Material: Forged from Relic-Aurum, a radiant alloy retrieved from the vaults beneath the Throneworld—believed to hold echoes of the Empress’s first decree.
Coloration: Burnished gold core with white-hot channels that pulse with divine syllables in Veritas. These glyphs shift depending on the Exemplar’s oath and crusade.
Edge: Monoatomic along the outer layer, able to sever not only matter but “unworthy conviction.”
[[Iconography]]
Crossguard: Styled like twin wings folded inward, inscribed with the phrase “Vatum Vel Anor” in Royal Veritas.
Pommel: A circular seal bearing the Empress’s keyhole sigil, set in a disc of unbreakable obsidian. It is said to “whisper” across voidspace when an Exemplar draws the blade.
Fuller: Channels holy charge during battle, glowing brighter with each enemy felled by divine mandate.
Function & Lore
Sentient Layer: The blade is not AI in the traditional sense. It remembers. When an Exemplar falls, the blade returns itself to the Sanctum of Names and weeps silently for nine days before choosing another.
Soul-[[Witness]]: Every Oathblade records the final words of the enemies it slays.
