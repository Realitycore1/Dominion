I. Physical Definition

In some theoretical [[Physics]] proposals, the chronon represents the smallest indivisible unit of [[Time]] — a discrete “tick” of the universe’s temporal progression. While mainstream [[Physics]] treats [[Time]] as continuous, chronon-based models propose that [[Time]] advances in quantized steps, each mediated by a particle-like exchange.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), chronons are resonant excitations of the τL (Temporal Lattice), forming the fundamental bridge between cause and effect. They govern the rate and granularity of all processes, from quantum transitions to cosmic-scale evolution.
