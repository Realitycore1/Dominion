Birth: 2028 – Marseille, France
Profession: Linguist & diplomat
Survival specialty: Languages, treaties, conflict mediation
Base: Loirecrest / Mediterranean Arcologies

Helena is the oldest immortal in appearance—white hair, deeply lined face—not because she ages, but because she deliberately altered herself to appear mortal for centuries.

She speaks 40+ languages and created the early [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Frameworks|Frameworks]] of High Trade.

Known as:
The Silver Voice
The Treaty-Maker

She is haunted by the number of wars she prevented—and the ones she couldn’t.
