# Division of Relic Intelligence

## Summary

The Division of Relic Intelligence identifies, studies, catalogs, and secures dangerous artifacts, abandoned technologies, anomalous ruins, pre-Dominion systems, alien devices, and legacy weapons discovered inside Federal jurisdiction.

It is a knowledge agency with security powers, not a license to make every discovery disappear.

## Mandate

The Division handles:

- relic surveys;
- artifact chain of custody;
- anomaly classification;
- technical translation;
- public hazard notices when possible;
- coordination with scientific institutions;
- containment referrals;
- provenance disputes.

## Powers

It may secure relic sites, restrict unsafe access, classify specific technical details, and coordinate with courts when ownership or public disclosure is disputed.

## Limits

It cannot seize ordinary cultural artifacts under false security claims, erase local memory, or deny all public knowledge of discoveries merely because they complicate official history.

## Public Face

Most citizens encounter the Division through sealed dig sites, museum delays, university access disputes, and rumors that something old was found under a city.

## Story Use

Use this agency for wonder under restraint. Relics should tempt curiosity, ambition, fear, and institutional secrecy without every artifact becoming an apocalypse.
