# (3608) Proxima Commonwealth

The Proxima Commonwealth is founded in 3608 by the survivors of the *C.U.C. Deliverance* crash.

Its first settlement and emergency capital is Craterheart Colony.

The Commonwealth begins as a civil compact among human, uplifted, and synthetic colonists whose mission command cannot adequately govern crash survival, salvage, family law, ecological triage, and permanent settlement.

Its first institutions include:

- an emergency assembly;
- medical and ecological triage authorities;
- common salvage and fabrication rules;
- civil records and household recognition;
- ration, shelter, and work-allocation appeals;
- a public archive preserving the mission and crash record.

The Commonwealth inherits law and representatives from the Colonial Union, Cynthia, the Kindred of Mars, Belt polities, and Outer Systems Compact.

It does not remain subordinate to any one of them. Communication delay and local survival make political autonomy immediate facts before formal Solar recognition arrives.

Craterheart becomes the first durable political community beyond Sol.
