•	Nature: Zones where causality frays. Entire neighborhoods exist in [[Time]] loops, backward cause-effect [[Chains]], or infinite mirrors.
	•	Feel: A tavern where patrons relive the same night forever; a cathedral where prayers are answered before they’re spoken.
	•	Function: Dangerous but valuable — paradox energy is a prime Interstice export.
	•	Story Hook: Rebels or fugitives hide here, because pursuers may literally lose the trail of events.
