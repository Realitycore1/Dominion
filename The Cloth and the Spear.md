Turin, 2037
[[Father Luca Moretti]] pressed his hands together so tightly the knuckles ached. On the table before him, under sterile lights, the [[Shroud of Turin]] stretched like a veil between past and present. The machine ticked, analyzed, whispered its verdict.

“First century,” the technician said at last.

The words struck Luca like bells. He wanted to weep, to laugh, to run into the streets. Proof. Not faith whispered, but faith measured. God’s signature, confirmed by science. His journals filled that night with trembling script: The cloth speaks truth. Surely the world will believe.

⸻

Rome, 2055
The square thrummed with voices. Crusader brigades knelt, armor polished, relic-banners snapping in the wind. Luca stood at the dais, robes heavy, hands outstretched.

“Defend the faith,” he intoned. “Defend the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]]. Defend the truth.”

The crowd roared, chanting relic hymns like war songs. Behind him, the [[Spear of Longinus]] gleamed, displayed for the first [[Time]] in centuries.

Yet Luca’s stomach knotted. When he had first seen the Shroud authenticated, he imagined a world united in wonder. Now he saw boys with rifles raised, faces painted with blood and scripture. He blessed them anyway. His hands shook.

⸻

Jerusalem, 2065
The air stank of smoke and iron. Luca walked carefully through the field hospital, relic banners hanging above rows of wounded. Nurses whispered psalms as they dressed burns.

He stopped beside a woman tending children — her apron soaked in blood, her eyes hollow with exhaustion. She glanced at him, briefly, and turned back to her work.

Luca raised a trembling hand and made the sign of the cross. The children stared up at him. One whispered, “Will God save us?”

He opened his mouth, but no words came. He touched the Shroud fragment stitched into his sash and felt only ash.

⸻

Rome, 2078
The Vatican lay silent, a tomb of marble and echo. Sirens had stopped hours ago. The city above was [[Fire]]. Luca knelt in a crypt, clutching the Spear against his chest.

He prayed — not for victory, not even for mercy, but for forgiveness. His faith had turned [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] into banners, proof into weapons. He had blessed the march into hell.

The walls shook. White light seared the edges of the crypt door. Luca whispered one last confession.

“Lord, I believed. And in believing, I destroyed.”

Then the world fell silent.
