The chamber was hewn from the bones of a dead star. Its walls glimmered with the faint light of imprisoned nebulae, its floor etched with runes that marked every crusade waged since the birth of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]. At the center stood the Great Table, not wood or stone but forged from a single plate of dreadnaught hull, scarred by xeno claws and seared by plasma [[Fire]].

The Verarchs assembled.

First came Ares, his armor burning with crimson authority, helm under one arm, eyes like smoldering coals. He moved as a general, each step measured, each glance weighing victory and loss.

Opposite him sat Fenrir, wolf-helmed and massive, his gauntlets still stained from his last campaign. He laughed low, a growl in his chest, as if war was both meal and jest to him.

Ogun entered next, his frame crowned with iron and flame, sparks trailing from his forge-bound pauldrons. His voice rumbled like anvils striking, every word a labor hammered into form.

[[Atham]] the [[Judge]] followed, clad in black and white, his presence colder than void. His scales of [[law]] hung at his belt, and his [[Silence]] weighed more than any sword.

One by one the others came — sisters radiant and terrible, brothers grim and resplendent — until the table was ringed with the full circle of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]’s chosen.

For a [[Time]], they did not speak of crusade. They spoke as kin.

Ares asked Fenrir how many fangs he had broken on the last swarm; Fenrir grinned and said not enough. Ogun spoke of new engines of war he had tempered in the fires of [[Harmina]]’s moons. [[Atham]] reminded them, without mirth, of the cost of overreach, his gaze falling heavy on all of them in turn.

They laughed, they argued, they remembered the brothers and sisters who had fallen into eternity. They were rivals in method, but blood and steel bound them. None doubted that when the word was given, they would march as one.

And then — [[Silence]] fell.

A light filled the chamber, white and blue, calm yet unyielding. The air itself seemed to kneel as She entered. The [[Celestial Empress]] moved not as mortal nor as [[Angel]], but as inevitability incarnate. Her gaze carried the weight of every dawn and every judgment.

The Verarchs rose as one.

Her voice was not sound but gravity in the soul:

“The tide rises. A new crusade must be written. The xenos will not yield, and therefore neither shall we. Tell me, my children — where shall [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] be carved next?”

The Verarchs lowered their heads, not in fear, but in reverence. They were wolves and lions, hammers and flames, judges and executioners — but before Her, they were sons and daughters, waiting for the axis of mankind to turn.

And as the banners unfurled, radiant script began writing itself in the air — the opening lines of a new crusade.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] would follow.
