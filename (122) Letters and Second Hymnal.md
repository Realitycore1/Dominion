# (122) Letters and Second Hymnal

## What Happened

By Year 122 AL, letters from distant settlements stopped asking New Aurora for permission in the same tone. They reported, negotiated, requested supplies, and sometimes refused language imposed by central maps. The Second Hymnal made the same change audible: shared faith had developed regional cadence.

## What Resulted

- Cultural autonomy became visible in writing and worship.
- The Celestialist network adapted to frontier distance and local grief.
- Amber and Emilia saw that unity would survive only if it allowed difference.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Year 122 shift is one of the clearest signs that Harmina had become a civilization rather than a colony.
