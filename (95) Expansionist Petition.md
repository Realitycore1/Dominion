# (95) Expansionist Petition

## What Happened

In Year 95 AL, expansionist pressure gathered around underways, route rights, frontier settlements, and the argument that New Aurora could no longer manage every local condition from the center. The petition did not begin as treason. It began as exhaustion with delayed approvals, distant risk assessments, and rules written for a city that was no longer the whole world.

## What Resulted

- Local leaders gained language for autonomy.
- New Aurora faced the difference between coordination and control.
- Amber saw the Ark's old logic repeating: people asking to leave because staying inside one system felt like slow suffocation.

## Long-Term Consequence

The petition became one of the first clear steps toward proto-national divergence.
