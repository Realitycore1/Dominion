Simple. Brutal. Descriptive.
	•	Vacuum- → native to hard vacuum, not merely tolerant of it
	•	-amoeba → plastic morphology, no fixed anatomy

This tells scientists immediately:

“Stop expecting organs. Stop expecting symmetry.”

Formal Species Variant

Vacuumamoeba dispersa
(widely distributed, non-territorial)

Optional subtypes if you want taxonomy depth later:
	•	V. predatoria – actively hunts energy gradients
	•	V. symbiotica – attaches to ships, stations, or void fauna
	•	V. lenta – interstellar drifters with century-long metabolic cycles

⸻

II. Dominion / Operational Terminology

AM-Class Entities

(Amorphous Matter)

Cold. Reductionist. Intentionally strips agency.

Variants:
	•	AM-1 – passive drift forms
	•	AM-2 – reactive / ship-interactive
	•	AM-3 – hazardous / consumption-capable

Dominion commanders never say “lifeform” in reports.

⸻

III. Cultural & Spacer Names (These Matter Most)

These are what make them real in-setting:
	•	Void Slimes (dismissive, early explorers)
	•	Star Amoebae (civilian science media)
	•	Hull Leeches (engineers hate them)
	•	The Soft Things (crews who’ve seen one up close)
	•	Cold Feeders
	•	Breathers-without-Breath
	•	The Living Fog (when encountered in swarms)

A common saying among long-haul crews:

“If it has edges, it isn’t thinking. If it doesn’t, be careful.”

⸻

IV. Behavior That Makes Them Believable

To avoid “space blob” syndrome, give them rules:
	•	No centralized brain
	•	Intelligence emerges only at scale or contact
	•	Respond to:
	•	EM fields
	•	Waste heat
	•	Ion trails
	•	Can survive inside:
	•	Comet tails
	•	Debris fields
	•	Reactor plumes

They are not hostile.
They are indifferent.

That’s more unsettling.
