## Details

A Fusion Suppressor is a piece of 
advanced [[Technology Portal|technology]] that is designed to prevent or control the fusion reactions that occur within [[Stars]] and other celestial bodies. Fusion reactions are a critical process in the lives of [[Stars]], which rely on these reactions to produce the energy that powers their light and heat. However, if left unchecked, these reactions can lead to dangerous and unstable conditions within the star, including explosive events such as supernovae. A Fusion Suppressor is designed to prevent or control these fusion reactions by modulating or interfering with the conditions that enable them to occur. This may involve using magnetic fields, electromagnetic pulses, or other means to manipulate the fusion processes within the star.
