# Amber Book III - Operation Prescience - Remaining Questions Workbook

## Purpose

This workbook collects the remaining unanswered decisions for *Amber Book III - Operation Prescience* before the next scene-architecture or drafting pass.

It excludes questions already answered by the consolidated locks unless a more specific scene-level decision is still needed.

Use this document as a fill-in workbook. Each question includes:

- the decision to make;
- why it matters;
- a blank answer field;
- optional drafting notes.

## Current Book III State

Book III currently covers the ignition phase of the GalFed-Dominion War, approximately War Year 0-15.

The wider war lasts roughly three centuries, but this book should end at the point where:

1. GalFed's first major local structure is broken.
2. Outer Artemis proves Aether and GalFed history are tied.
3. Xolotl survives with the Aether Singularity wound.
4. The first six campaign legions are authorized.
5. One liberated world asks Dominion to stay.
6. Another world asks Dominion to leave.
7. Amber says *Dominion* as self-rule.
8. Someone else repeats *Dominion* as destiny.

# XII. Drafting Quality Tests

Use these after the chapter outline and again after the decompressed draft.

## GalFed Test

- [ ] Does at least one GalFed character act from sincere care?
- [ ] Is GalFed frightening because its systems work?
- [ ] Does the Aether Singularity trauma remain intelligible?
- [ ] Is there at least one GalFed institution worth preserving?
- [ ] Does a GalFed citizen have a rational reason to fear Dominion liberation?

## Dominion Test

- [ ] Does each new Dominion institution preserve something real?
- [ ] Does each new Dominion institution also create a risk of control?
- [ ] Can a citizen question the war without becoming an enemy?
- [ ] Do Amber and Emilia disagree in ways that both protect the future?
- [ ] Does the Final Line remain accountable to witnesses and civilians?

## Operation Prescience Test

- [ ] Is every objective more complicated than "steal the thing"?
- [ ] Does the operation prove GalFed suppression is systemic?
- [ ] Does the operation protect defectors as people, not assets?
- [ ] Does it reveal a truth that creates a larger moral burden?
- [ ] Does victory create obligations the Syl'Cothan did not plan for?

## Legion Test

- [ ] Does the reader understand why the legions are needed?
- [ ] Does the reader fear what they may become?
- [ ] Is every legionary still legally and emotionally a person?
- [ ] Is adult consent treated as ongoing rather than permanent?
- [ ] Are medical dependency and military usefulness kept in visible tension?

## Odysseus Test
