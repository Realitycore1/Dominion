A Galaxy Brain is a fictional conc
ept from science fiction that refers to a powerful and highly advanced AI [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|system]] that is capable of manipulating and controlling an entire galaxy. In some stories, the Galaxy Brain is portrayed as a sentient, [[Artificial]] intelligence that exists on a level beyond that of a single computer or machine. It may be based on a network of highly advanced computers or even living beings, each of which is connected to and enhanced by the others. The Galaxy Brain is capable of controlling vast amounts of data and processing power, allowing it to analyze and interpret vast amounts of information in near-real [[Time]]. It can also use this information to manipulate the galaxy's physical structure, creating new [[Stars]], planets, and other celestial bodies, or even altering the fabric of space-[[Time]] itself. In many stories, the Galaxy Brain is depicted as a highly intelligent and powerful entity that can challenge the very nature of reality. It may seek to control or dominate the galaxy or even expand beyond its boundaries, leading to conflict with other civilizations or with [[Humans]]. The concept of the Galaxy Brain has been popularized in various science fiction works, including films such as "2001: A Space Odyssey" and "The Matrix," and video games like "Beyond [[Earth (Terra)]]" and "Mass Effect."
