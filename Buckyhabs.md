A Buckyhab is a geodesic or polyhedral dome-habitat made of reinforced structural nodes and smart-matter panels, built for longevity, self-repair, and protection against environmental extremes.

The name is derived from Buckminster Fuller, the architect of geodesic domes, fused with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] engineering scale and adaptive material sciences.Component
