🔹 Mechanism:

Disjunction Drives allow ships to exit local spacetime entirely, entering a disjunct medium (e.g., hyperspace, non-Euclidean volume, or extradimensional fault) and reemerge elsewhere. Unlike traditional wormhole travel, Disjunction drives create severed causal tunnels with no continuous path.

🔹 How It Works:
	•	Engages dimensional detachment protocol: ship becomes a non-represented object in reality
	•	Navigates Confluencia vectors, shadow Realms, or higher-brane tunnels
	•	Emerges by resolving a target position via metaphysical lock, often aided by Divine Engine lattices

🔹 Use Cases:
	•	Preferred by Legio Nihilium, Crusade Exo-Choirs, and Null-Ships
	•	Used in ambush, strategic reinsertion, or Domain evasion
	•	Often leaves behind Time shadows, psycho-spatial afterimages, or false echoes

🔻 Risks:
	•	Can become stranded in Unreality if emergence fails
	•	Crews may suffer Simulacrine Syndrome or existential bleed
