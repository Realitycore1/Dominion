# How to Navigate

This archive is organized by scale and location, not by traditional topic alone.

## The Basic Method

Pick a large structure, then zoom inward.

Useful starting scales include:

- a basin
- a multiverse
- a verse
- a superstructure
- a civilization
- a codex category

Each level opens into the structures, regions, systems, civilizations, or phenomena inside that location or scale.

## Reading Shape

You are not expected to read everything linearly.

You are meant to:

- select a place, structure, subject, or question
- open the nearby portal or index
- move deeper only when your curiosity narrows

Think of the vault less like a bookshelf and more like a nested atlas.
