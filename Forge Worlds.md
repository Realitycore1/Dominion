Forge Worlds are entire planets converted into factory-cathedrals, their surfaces dominated by manufactoria, refineries, and assembly vaults. Unlike ordinary industrial worlds, Forge Worlds require no external input besides raw matter streams and design codices — they are autonomous [[Production]] engines, capable of fabricating everything from personal [[Arms]] to [[Starships]] and [[Ark Platforms]].

Mantle Feed [[Systems]]: The planet’s mantle and crust are mined directly into [[Production]] streams.

Vaulted Manufactoria: Kilometer-high assembly lines where [[Starships]] and war engines are birthed.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Strategic keystones; the loss of one is a catastrophe, the capture of one a near impossibility.

Religious: Known as the “Amber Anvils” — each is sanctified as a living altar where industry itself becomes a hymn. [[Workers]] are priests, machines are scripture, and every starship is a liturgy.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Every factory wall is inscribed with glyphs — [[Production]] runs emerge already marked with the Empress’s seal.
