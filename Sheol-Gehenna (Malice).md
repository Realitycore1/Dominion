Core Realm

The Pit of Gehenna

The opposite of [[Aravoth (Grace)]].

A city of ash and molten iron, towers collapsing endlessly, rebuilt by slave-[[Demons]] in parody of Heaven’s crystal spires.

Centered around a Throne of [[Chains]] — no ruler can sit upon it for long without being consumed by suspicion and [[Betrayal]].

Major Zones of Hell (Inversions of Heaven’s [[Realms]])

Olam Tachton – The Lower World (mirror of Olam Elyon)
A bureaucracy of torment. Endless caverns of scribes who record damnation.
Ledgers are written in blood and erased in screams.

The Ashen City (mirror of Zion of Light)
Streets of dust and bone, rivers of black sludge.
A parody of Zion: mansions crumble into ash the moment they’re built.

The Nihilarium (mirror of Celestium)
Instead of libraries of light, it is an archive of annihilation.
Scrolls are blank; books whisper madness when opened.
Knowledge exists only as fragments that corrode the mind.

The Pyre-Sphere (mirror of Empyrean)
A burning orb of smoke and bloodfire, with no song, only screams.
[[Angels]] inverted into Ashen Seraphs dwell here, wings of charred cinder.

The Abyssal Vault (mirror of Aeon Vault)
Holds the Aeonic [[Demons]] — [[Hunger]], Madness, Oblivion.
Constellations reversed into voids, fractals that erase memory by sight.

Irivash (mirror of Irivata)
Palaces of cacophony, where every surface screams and weeps.
[[Choirs]] here sing only to unmake.

The Broken Mount (mirror of Eternal Mount)
A jagged black mountain, inverted and hollow.
Pilgrims ascend not to purify, but to be consumed.
Its summit is a pit descending infinitely downward.
