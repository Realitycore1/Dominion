1. Common Phoenix

Definition: The baseline—immortal firebird reborn from its own ashes.

Traits: Flaming plumage, radiant aura, song of healing or sorrow.

Symbolism: [[Rebirth]], eternity, hope rising from destruction.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Solar Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes tied to the sun.

Traits: Golden feathers, solar flares, light that purifies or incinerates.

Symbolism: The eternal cycle of day and night.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Storm Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes born of lightning and tempests.

Traits: Thunderous cries, wings of stormfire, lightning trails.

Symbolism: Renewal through chaos; destruction birthing life.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Frost Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes reborn in ice instead of flame.

Traits: Icy plumage, cold-[[Fire]] [[Rebirth]], bringer of frozen dawns.

Symbolism: [[Rebirth]] through stillness; purity of frost.

5. Shadow Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and darkness.

Traits: Black flames, necrotic [[Rebirth]], rise from ash as omen of endings.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as renewal; corruption of immortality.

6. Ember Phoenix

Definition: Lesser phoenixes tied to volcanic [[Fire]] and magma.

Traits: Molten feathers, smoke trails, explosive bursts at [[Rebirth]].

Symbolism: Creation and destruction in volcanic cycles.

7. Spirit Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes that embody souls and afterlives.

Traits: Ethereal flames, guide spirits to [[Rebirth]], ghostly songs.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as passage, soul’s eternal return.

8. Noble Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes revered as divine emissaries or companions of gods.

Traits: Vast wingspans, songs that heal nations, immortal wisdom.

Symbolism: Immortality as divine gift; flame of eternity.

9. Elemental Phoenix

Definition: Phoenixes infused with primal forces beyond [[Fire]].

Variants: Crystalwing ([[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]), Tempestfire (air), Tideborn (water), Aetherflame (pure magic).

Symbolism: Renewal in every element; universality of [[Rebirth]].

10. Arch-Phoenix (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary phoenixes embodying [[Stars]], epochs, or universes.

Traits: Stellar [[Fire]]-birds reborn as novas, wings spanning heavens.

Symbolism: Cosmic renewal; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and [[Rebirth]] of worlds.
