[[Space Fountains]] are hypothetical devices that could potentially generate large amounts of usable energy by harnessing the flow of cosmic rays or other forms of high-energy [[Particles]] in space. The concept of [[Space Fountains]] was first proposed in the 1960s and 1970s as a means of producing energy for space exploration and defense. The idea is that by installing a device on the surface of the sun or another large celestial body, one could capture and channel high-energy [[Particles]] that are constantly streaming through space, converting them into usable energy. There are several possible mechanisms for creating [[Space Fountains]]. One approach involves the use of particle colliders, which could generate high-energy [[Particles]] that could be used to power spacecraft or defensive [[Systems]]. Another approach involves using magnetic lenses to focus and channel cosmic rays into beams that could be harnessed for energy [[Production]]. While [[Space Fountains]] remain a theoretical concept, they represent an exciting and potentially transformativ[[Technology Portal|technology]]]] that could revolutionize space exploration and defense. If ever developed, they would have far-reaching implications for the energy needs of [[Human]] spaceflight and the ability to explore and colonize distant worlds.
