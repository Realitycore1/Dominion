🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: Heirloom Seal of the Realm (Jade Seal of China)
Discovery Location: Xi’an, Shaanxi Province, 2039, in a hidden chamber beneath the mausoleum complex of Emperor [[Qin]] Shi Huang, discovered during subsurface mapping.
Summary:

Material: Carved from Heshibi jade, pale green with golden flecking. Size: ~9 cm square, [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] cm high.

Inscription: “受命於天, 既壽永昌” (“Having received [[The Mandate]] from Heaven, may [the emperor] live long and prosper eternally”).

Dating: Spectroscopic analysis matches quarry sources from Chu state (4th century BCE). Tool marks consistent with Warring States craftsmanship.

Preservation: Minor weathering, but inscription legible; sealed environment kept artifact intact.

Archaeological Significance: Confirms the survival of the Seal thought lost during the Yuan dynasty. Reinforces [[Continuity]] of imperial legitimacy claims across millennia.
