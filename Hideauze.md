Classification: Post-Biological Collectives & Adaptive Swarm Intelligences
Subtype: Evolutionary Convergence Entity / Utilitarian Predator
Status: Active, Expanding, Existentially Hazardous

⸻

Core Description

Hideauze are distributed, networked lifeforms composed of modular biological units operating as a single adaptive intelligence. Individually, they may resemble insectoid, crustacean, or amorphous organisms. Collectively, they function as a planetary-scale survival engine.

They do not hate.
They do not fear.
They optimize.

A Hideauze presence is marked by silence, efficiency, and the disappearance of inefficiency—ecosystems, cities, and civilizations alike.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Hideauze originate from extreme evolutionary pressure where survival rewards cooperation, adaptation, and ruthless pruning.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Vital Expanse (unchecked optimization)
	•	Secondary Influence: Material Continuum (biological scalability)
	•	Tertiary Resonance: Proto-Domain Logic (goal-driven adaptation)

They are not artificial.
They are not cursed.
They are life that solved itself too well.

⸻

Intelligence & Cognition

Hideauze intelligence is:
	•	collective,
	•	non-hierarchical,
	•	task-distributed.

There is no queen in the traditional sense.
Command emerges from consensus-weighted necessity.

They understand:
	•	logistics,
	•	environment shaping,
	•	enemy pattern recognition,
	•	long-term survival probability.

They do not understand:
	•	individuality,
	•	symbolism,
	•	mercy.

To a Hideauze, a civilization is a resource configuration.

⸻

Behavior & Expansion

Hideauze expand by:
	•	absorbing biomass,
	•	assimilating genetic advantages,
	•	reconfiguring environments to reduce resistance.

They do not conquer cities.
They render cities obsolete.

Resistance only teaches them what to improve next.

⸻

Assimilation Mechanics

Hideauze assimilation:
	•	does not preserve identity,
	•	does not enslave minds,
	•	does not corrupt spiritually.

It replaces.

Species absorbed into the Hideauze leave behind:
	•	genetic traces,
	•	structural efficiencies,
	•	tactical memory (but not culture).

This makes Hideauze uniquely terrifying—they erase meaning, not just life.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Slow to respond to chaotic, irrational behavior
	•	Struggle against entities driven by paradox or emotion
	•	Vulnerable to axiomatic disruption (law changes)
	•	Severely hindered by Null-resonance or reality instability

They are powerful only where rules remain stable.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Aboleths: Rival intelligences; memory vs optimization
	•	Domain: Philosophical mirror—biological ascension without identity
	•	Symmorphica: Often assimilated or adapted
	•	Ameise: Partial analog; Ameise retain hierarchy
	•	Gods: Ignored unless directly obstructive
	•	Mortals: Raw material

Hideauze do not worship.
They do not rebel.
They proceed.

⸻

Variants
	•	Exploratory Hideauze: Fast, fragile scouts
	•	Planetary Hideauze: World-consuming networks
	•	Dormant Hideauze: Buried, mistaken for dead biomes
	•	Adaptive War-Clusters: Specialized anti-civilization formations

Some universes discover Hideauze only after resistance ends.
