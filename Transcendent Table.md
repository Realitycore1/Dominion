# Transcendent Substrata Table (v0.1)

*The rare and dangerous essences beyond the normal ladder, touched only by Adamant or Special ranks.*

## Anomalous Substances
- **Paradoxium** — Allows mutually exclusive states to coexist.
- **Anatheum** — Essence of negation, cancels or devours magic.
- **Mythril Logos** — Story-substance; reality rewritten as narrative.
- **Divinal Concords** — Embodiments of divine compacts, unstable outside of godhood.

## Extreme Substrata
- **Chronovore** — [[Time]]-consuming essence, devours sequence.
- **Eschaton Dust** — End-state matter, entropy crystallized.
- **Inversium** — Essence of reversal; turns flow against itself.
- **Singularity Core** — Collapsed essence; infinite density of will.

## Mythic Rarities
- **Epitome** — The final form of a story condensed into substance.
- **Aureum** — Golden-thread essence; connects mortal and divine fate.
- **Noctis Veil** — The shadow of creation, fragment of the first dark.
