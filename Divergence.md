## Summary
After the civil war the monarchy is the became the legitimate form of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] within the newly formed [[Equilon Union]]. Now under the untested leader [[William Zohair]] the newly formed union  

After a couple of years the first wave of C.U ships arrived then immediately broadcasted a transmission that the colony must stand down and submit to the C.U or face punishment.

After the transmission, there was panic looming and n the air. Shortly after the [[Queen]] issued a National address and submitted to the people that should have the right to decide if they want to return to colonial rule. After the vote 90% of the populace refused to submit. Soon after the [[Queen]] announced a national emergency and issued a state of war.

For the last 200 years the [[Equilon Union]] has prepared for a possible colonial fleet and had built fleets of ships to help protect them. Soon after the state of war declaration the war [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] sprang to life and vast numbers of equipment began [[Production]]. Not to long after the declaration National Unity was at its highest ever recorded.

After a few months the C.U and Equilon fleets engaged which resulted in many [[Close]] calls as the C.U had a 200 year advantage of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]], which had heavily sawed the battle in favor of the C.U. But as the union had prepared for such an event they made sure they would have superior firepower. Which in the end granted the union a temporary victory. 

After the battle, there was much devastation as the C.U fleet was tasked to not only engauged hostile fleets but to cause as much damage as possible, which resulted in the loss of innocent life in nearby population centers.

After the battle another war ensued within the people. There now was indecisiveness on what to do next. Some wanted to return to the C.U, others wanted to continuously fight them off. The uncertainty soon began to take over as the different groups would attack one another, although nothing major had taken place it wasn't until the idea was proposed to colonize another world. 

The Arks are a nation [[Wide]] construction project of enormous proportion scaling up to 15 km. Many Arks are set to be built and can house up to 100k people max along with another 100k of frozen embryos.

The First Enviro suits were used in the Abreise Initiative and was considered much more cumbersome in regards to how much space was left for the user, and the design is a much loved classic but quickly became outdated once the Arche made landed 

As the arks are under construction, the Pioneers program is initiated to find those with the necessary skills to develop a new colony, along with backups for emergencies. The program is highly competitive and a high standard must be maintained in order to remain eligible. The seats remaining after the program are given through a lottery.

After several years dozens of arks are built and ready for departure. The union is in celebration for the achievement as they prepare for the departure.

Soon after the the last of the arks were built the second wave of C.U warships arrived in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]. With almost triple the ship count from the first wave,  which eclipses the union fleet in total firepower. As soon as they arrived they began to bombard stations and outposts without hesitation.

Soon after the C.U opened fired on the union emergency boarding procedures began. The arks then move into position to jump out of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] while the warships are preoccupied.

As a last stand the union used every last [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] assets to assist the front line to give as much [[Time]] as possible. The line could only last for a few hours as the C.U was relentless in their pursuit to reach the arks.

As the arks began to depart the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] many colonists took one last look at their home as it was being subjugated by the C.U, and many felt Sorrow and remorse as they looked upon their home for the last [[Time]].

As the jump drive is spinning a massive power surge with in the arks power grid drains into the drive itself and becomes highly unstable, And unable to stop the drive, it goes into catastrophic overload.

After the Drive core malfunctioned, the ark was propelled 1 billion light years away from home. With the drive core damaged beyond repair the ark is stranded until it can find a habitable world

Life on the ark was complicated, traditional lifestyles were destroyed due to food rationing and limited space which slowly reformed how society functioned in order to survive. During the crisis martial was declared and gained complete control in order to avoid a disastrous situation.

The arks banned firearms not as a population control but as a safety method for the ship as a whole. Not all weapons were banned anything that did not qualify as a firearm was legal, as it did not present a danger to the ship.
