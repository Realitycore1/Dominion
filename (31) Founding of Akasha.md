A coalition of surviving continuity fleets locates a naturally stable convergence region near Harmina.

The site becomes:

Akasha Prime Anchor.

This is the first successful large-scale:

* continuity-secured human settlement,
* reality bunker nexus,
* and stable civilization node after Severance.

Akasha is initially subterranean and shielded beneath:

* anchor lattices,
* VFF dampening arrays,
* and causal stabilization towers.

The Dominion no longer expands.

It survives.
