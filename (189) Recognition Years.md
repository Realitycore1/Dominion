# (189) Recognition Years

## What Happened

By Year 189 AL, the settlement blocs had become too large, distinct, and infrastructurally important to remain informal. Recognition debates formalized identities that had already emerged through geography, labor, and culture.

## What Resulted

- Equilon States, Atlovaia Exarchate, Ormova Replika, Estelia Scriptorium, Toldax Ascent, F.S.S.R., Commonwealth of States, and related blocs gained clearer institutional shape.
- Recognition did not erase dependence; it renamed it diplomacy.
- Amber's founder status became more politically valuable as states searched for legitimacy.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Recognition Years set the board for the New World War.
