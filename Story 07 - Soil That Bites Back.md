# Story 07 - Soil That Bites Back

## Viewpoint

Halan Jor before he becomes a teacher, when he is still part of a farming trial crew.

## Setting

Years 3-17 AL, New Aurora agricultural test plots.

## Story Shape

Halan has inherited a library of Earth and Ferox agriculture that is almost useless. The first crops die. The tools pit and flake. The soil is not barren; it is chemically alive in ways the settlers keep misreading.

An older technician tells him to stop asking what the ground lacks and start asking what it is already doing.

## Harmina Element

Alien soil is shown as active ecology rather than hostile dirt.

## Character Movement

Halan stops treating Harmina as a failed version of older worlds. He begins treating it as a text he cannot read yet.

## Future Payoff

This attitude becomes central to Harminan education: the world is not wrong because it does not answer old questions.
