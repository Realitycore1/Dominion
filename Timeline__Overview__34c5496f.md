## Summary
The Collective doesn’t want a loud [[Invasion]]. It wants to turn Luna’s own [[Infrastructure]] against itself—to rot it from within, letting fear, decay, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] collapse deliver it into [[Domain]] control.

1. Stranglers (Atmospheric Interdiction)
Floating, vine-like jellyfish that drift through the upper atmosphere. Their tendrils can emit high-density EM static, jamming comms and radar. Used to black out regional communication grids and snare low-altitude escape vehicles.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Burrowers ([[Infrastructure]] Killers)
Worm-like, armored organisms equipped with enzyme-weaponized drills. Burrow into underground cities, power lines, and command bunkers. Can enter a hibernation state for decades before reawakening.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Dollers (Psyops / Infiltration)
Bio-constructs shaped to mimic [[Human]] appearance—but always slightly off. Emit pheromones and neurochemical fogs that induce trust, then panic. Used to infiltrate survivor enclaves during the fall of major cities.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Bloomers (Terraformers)
Rooted organisms the size of buildings. Release atmospheric restructuring spores, mutating local [[Flora]]/[[Fauna]] into [[Domain]]-compatible biomes. Turn [[Regions]] into spawning grounds for more advanced forms.
5. Wombborns (Troop Transports / Breeder Class)
Massive, bloated forms that carry swarms of smaller drone bio-forms. Capable of aerobic lofting—floating in upper atmospheres before descending like a plague cloud.

Phase 1: The [[Silence]] Bloom
[[Domain]] agents on Terra(deep biosleep cells) awaken and construct an organic signal bloom.
This "flower" pulses a falsified distress signal from an old Feration mining vessel near Luna.
Phase [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]: The Spore Spear
A biological mass driver, disguised as a deorbited satellite, launches from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].
Inside: Stranglers, Dollers, and Bloomers adapted for lunar conditions.
Phase [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]: The Cyst Seed
Once the initial bloomers terraform a viable patch of lunar regolith, a gestation vault arrives: a modified Wombborn, containing a juvenile Prime mind (possibly derived from Kai-Hera).
This cyst begins forming the first [[Domain]] foothold beyond Terra.

Luna is a fortress-sarcophagus: miles-thick plating, subterranean warrens, atmospheric processing towers, orbital defense lasers, kinetic interceptors.
But it’s vulnerable:
Maintenance lag due to resource breakdown.
Power grid rationing.
Oversaturation of sensor clutter from overlapping civilian-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] [[Systems]].
Deep internal corruption in Protectorate command.

Delivery Vector: Ghost Burn Pods
Organic delivery vehicles—small, oblong pods built to mimic space debris.
Coated in thermal-disruptive biofilm that confuses long-range sensors.
Launched from the edge of Terra’s gravity well using repurposed magnetic slings crafted from ancient orbital junk.
Land in Luna’s [[Forgotten]] zones: industrial dead sectors, decompression zones, off-grid lower levels.
“Initial impact registered as micrometeorite. No immediate power rerouting. Residual biosignature went unnoticed beneath local reactor bleed.”
 — Lunar Deepwatch Report #771-A (Redacted)

Mycoseeds (Terraformer/Master [[Key]] Units)
Fungal-rooted organisms that infiltrate [[Infrastructure]].
Break down polymer walls, interface with old data lines, "taste" local power [[Systems]].
Start spreading neuromorphic signal roots to establish a covert [[Domain]] network.
Pulse Mites (Sensor and Grid Suppressors)
Crustacean-like organisms no larger than a fist.
Crawl into ventilation and sensor ducts.
Emit microburst jammers that mimic radiation spikes or decay flashes, confusing surveillance [[Systems]].
Spindle Lurkers (Organic Reconnaissance Units)
Elongated spider-like forms with bioluminescent "eyes" capable of mapping heat and EM grids.
They remain dormant for days until signaled by distant [[Domain]] bio-nodes.
Can split apart into swarms to infiltrate multiple tunnels at once.

Once secure footholds are made in Luna's [[Forgotten]] underlayers:
Lurking Bloomers
Miniaturized versions of surface-scale terraformers.
Secrete biosphere modulators into the recycled air—introducing molecular chaos into Luna’s CO₂ scrubbers, O₂ balance, and fluid reclamation.
Create spore fogs that cause hallucinations and “phantom infections,” making early discovery appear as civil breakdown or cybernetic error.
First Wombborn (Gestation Class)
A single bioform the size of a shipping container arrives dormant in a buried slag heap.
Begins secreting egg-[[Husks]] that will hatch into combat organisms and [[Infrastructure]] disruptors.

Infiltration & Adaptation
Crackmouthers
Flat-bodied, gelatinous creatures with ultra-thin bodies that slip through seams in bulkheads, even active mag-seals.
Feed on plastics and electronics, secreting hallucinogenic lubricants into ventilation shafts that induce paranoia and vertigo in [[Human]] crews.
Chimerettes
Grown using samples of Lunar civilian tissue harvested by Spindle Lurkers.
Appear as disoriented, semi-recognizable people: old crew members, lost miners, abandoned children.
Emit neural mimicry waves that temporarily override facial recognition [[Systems]] and confuse biometric locks.
Shardbroods
Pack-minded crystalline organisms that nest inside fiber-optic trunks and cooling veins.
Communicate via photonic pulses, relaying [[Domain]] data in a way that mimics routine maintenance traffic.
Create glass-silk cocoons to hatch new tech-interference organisms.

Borewasps
Drill-like appendages allow them to burrow into reactor shielding and water reservoirs.
Lay eggs in zero-pressure coolant tanks; when the eggs rupture, they release temperature-reactive fog that destabilizes [[Artificial]] gravity regulators.
Void Antlers
Massive root-creatures that grow out of walls and floors.
Appear suddenly—grow overnight in dead zones.
Act as bio-hubs, projecting [[Domain]] signals and biome distortion fields that warp camera feeds and disrupt pathfinding AI.
Neurochrysalids
Cystic lifeforms attached to deep-rooted [[Domain]] data trees.
Designed to fuse with captured [[Humans]] who show high stress or trauma response.
Indoctrinate through shared hallucination—indoctrinating through nightmare.

The [[Domain]] is a hyper intelligent bio-organic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] that assemelates all organic matter to create and improve its collective.
