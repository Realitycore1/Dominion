(2079–2678)
“The continents did not drown—they changed their clothes.”

The sea-level rise you’ve chosen—roughly 20–60m depending on region—reshapes Earth into something recognizable but uncanny.
Think Late Cretaceous silhouettes meeting post-industrial ruin.

Below is the full map catalog. We’ll go region by region.

⸻

1. NORTH AMERICA — “THE BROKEN TEMPLE”

Sea-level Rise: 35–55 m
Climate: Temperate core, subtropical coasts, hyper-wet north.

Major Geography Changes

• The Mississippi Basin floods into a vast inland sea called the Gulfen Reach.
• The Atlantic seaboard from Boston to Miami is underwater.
• The Appalachian Mountains become a spine of islands and highland refuges.
• California’s Central Valley becomes the Pacific Sound, a warm channel splitting the state.
• Hudson Bay merges with surrounding lowlands into a New Inland Arctic Sea.
• The Great Lakes overflow and connect to the Atlantic.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Hydrocliff enclaves built atop former Rocky Mountain cities.
• Nomadic technobarbarian tribes along the Gulfen Reach.
• Archivist-State remnants in the high Appalachian arcology vaults.
• New Orleans Archipelago—pirate city-states built around oil rigs and skyscraper reefs.

The region feels like a flooded cathedral—grand and fractured.

⸻

2. [[South America]]~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Overview/Faction Profile/UAR/South America]] — “THE GREEN CONTINENT”

Sea-level Rise: 20–40 m
Climate: Hyper-tropical; wet interior; Andean rainshadow desert expands.

Geographic Shifts

• The Amazon Basin becomes an enormous swamp-ocean called the Panthalassic Mire.
• The Pantanal becomes a true inland sea.
• Coastal Brazil moves hundreds of kilometers inland.
• The Andes become the backbone of all stable civilization.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Sky-steppe kingdoms in the Andes, solar-powered and trade-dominant.
• Jungle-barbarian federations navigating the swamp using boats and restored diesel-hybrids.
• Biotech enclaves experimenting with aggressive tropical rewilding.

This is the world’s ecological powerhouse—but unstable, beautiful, and lethal.

⸻

3. EUROPE — “THE ARCHIPELAGIC RUIN”

Sea-level Rise: 40–60 m
Climate: Northern Europe warms, the Mediterranean becomes more humid.

Geographic Shifts

• [[London]]/DOMINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Economy & Infrastructure/Locations/Cities/London]], Amsterdam, Copenhagen vanish beneath the North Sea.
• France becomes two islands divided by the expanded Loire Basin.
• Germany becomes the Teuton Archipelago.
• Italy fractures into several island [[Chains]].
• The Black Sea climbs and floods the Balkans.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Powerful city-states around Paris Heights and Alpine fortresses.
• Mediterranean heroic republics based on desalination mastery.
• Eastern warlords holding the Danube Iron Belt.
• Ruins of northern megacities turned into “vault-harvest zones.”

Europe feels like a drowned museum where each island guards a different era.

⸻

4. AFRICA — “THE SALT THRONE”

Sea-level Rise: 20–40 m
Climate: Sub-Saharan belt shifts south; Sahara blooms partially due to atmospheric changes.

Geographic Shifts

• The Nile Delta is submerged; Cairo becomes a cluster of fortified islands.
• The Sahara gains intermittent green belts (monsoon rebalancing).
• West African coast retreats markedly.
• Madagascar becomes wetter and more habitable inland.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Salt Kingdoms of the Sahara, caravan empires controlling rehydrated oases.
• Horn of Africa tech enclaves built on geothermal power.
• Central African jungle confederacies, violent but innovative.
• Southern megacities near old Johannesburg rise as continental hubs.

Africa becomes a crucible of migration, trade, and ruin—resilient and culturally explosive.

⸻

5. MIDDLE EAST — “THE SCORCHED SPINE”

Sea-level Rise: 20–30 m
Climate: Hyper-arid inland, wet along shifting Mediterranean winds.

Geographic Shifts

• Persian Gulf massively expands.
• Red Sea rises, splitting parts of Arabia further.
• Israel / Lebanon / Gaza almost entirely submerged except highlands.

[[Human]] Landscape

• City-state fortresses built around desalination and arcology water vaults.
• PMC successor states left over from the 2040–2070 proxy wars.
• Pilgrim-technocratic enclaves controlling relic sites—especially the Ark-rediscovery zones.

The region becomes a chessboar[[India]]ater, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], and ideology intersect.

⸻

6. [[India]] — “THE MONSOON KINGDOMS”

Sea-level Rise: 30–50 m
Climate: Stronger monsoons, hotter dry seasons.

Geographic Changes

• Bengal is underwater—Bengal Se[[India]]Deccan Plateau becomes prime refuge land.
• Sri Lanka merges partially with the Indian mainland due to sediment shifts.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Neo-Vedic monarchies dominating riverland agriculture.
• Technocratic city-states surviving via fusion batteries.
• Rewilded tiger territories across old urban [[Regions]].

[[India]] becomes mythic—dense, humid, resurrected by water.

⸻

7. CHINA — “THE THREE SEAS”

Sea-level Rise: 30–60 m
Climate: Northern deserts expand; southern rains intensify.

Geographic Shifts

• North China Plain heavily submerged.
• Yangtze River Delta becomes the Southern Inland Sea.
• Beijing becomes a fortified hill-peninsula.

[[Human]] Landscape

• Warlord-technocrat dynasties controlling highland data-vaults.
• Floating-barge nomads patrolling the Eastern Archipelago.
• Hyper-fortress cities along the Tibetan Plateau.

China becomes fractured but powerful—like a broken [[Dragon]] whose pieces still breathe [[Fire]].

⸻

8. SOUTHEAST ASIA — “THE DROWNED LABYRINTH”

Almost everything below 50 meters is underwater.

New Geography

• Indonesia becomes scattered archipelagos.
• Mekong Delta gone.
• Philippines = survivalist island kingdoms.

Civilization

• Boat empires.
• Pirate republics.
• Coastal dynastos controlling geothermal rigs.

⸻

9. AUSTRALIA — “THE RED ARCH”

Sea-level Rise: 30–40 m
Climate: Interior desert expands; north becomes hyper-tropical.

Geography

• Northern Australia fractured into islands.
• Sydney is partially submerged.
• A new inland sea forms in the low interior basin.

Civilization

• Coastal fortresses.
• Motor-tribes of the Outback.
• Archivist Orders preserving old biotech.

⸻

10. ANTARCTICA — “THE GREEN CONTINENT”

As ice collapses, the coastline retracts dramatically, exposing vast land.

New Geography

• Large Antarctic Rift Lakes.
• Temperate northern coastlines.

Civilization

• Refuge cities built by northern elites before the fall.
• Isolationist, quasi-utopian, but increasingly authoritarian.

⸻

11. THE ARCTIC — “THE CROWN SEA”

Ice-free summers.
Massive fisheries.
New archipelagos.

Civilization:
• Northern Rus enclaves
• Inuit federations
• Bio-arctic research states

⸻

12. PAN-OCEANIC WORLD — “THE BLUE EMPIRE”

With coastlines up to 50–60m inland, more than 1/3 of all [[Human]] land is lost.

New Ocean Geography

• Mid-ocean floating towns
• Storm-belt migratory fleets
• Remnants of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] seasteads
• Coral-cities built on skyscraper ruins
