10.8 Pune, formerly known as Poona, is a thriving metropolitan city located in the western Indian state of Maharashtra. It is home to over 6 million people, making it the fifth-largest city in the country. Pune is situated on the banks of the Mula and Raigad rivers, at an elevation of around 500 meters above sea level. Pune is known for its pleasant climate, green landscapes, and historic landmarks. The city has a rich heritage, with its colonial architecture and fortifications bearing [[Witness]] to its past. Pune has been an important strategic location for centuries, and its strategic location on the Konkan coast has made it a significant trading hub for spices and cotton. The city has a well-developed [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]], with a focus on high-tech manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, and medical tourism. Pune has a large number of educational institutions, including several reputed medical colleges, universities, and technical institutes. Pune's rich cultural scene includes many [[Temples]], ghats, forts, palaces, and monuments that date back to the 16th and 17th centuries. The city also hosts several cultural festivals throughout the year, including the vibrant Ganesh Chaturthi festival. Pune is also home to the national headquarters of several Indian organizations such as Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and Swadeshi Jagran Manch (SJM).
