The Grey Expanse

Between Propagans and Profundis lies a transitional ocean of partial coherence, where isolated Confluencia strands drift like kelp in tidewater.
Here, rogue verses can survive for a time as Drift Realms—cold, silent universes moving slowly toward dissolution.  Their skies appear empty except for faint ghost-arches, relics of severed threads.

To enter the Grey Expanse is to walk among dying gods.
