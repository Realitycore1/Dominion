1. What Existence Actually Means

The cleanest, most universal definition across every framework:

Something exists if it has a pattern stable enough to influence or be influenced.

Existence isn’t “being real.”
Existence is having effects.

An electron exists because it interacts.
A memory exists because it shapes identity.
A spell exists because it alters Aether.
A Dominion axiom exists because it constrains the universe.
An extinct species exists through its imprint on the present.

Existence = footprint + persistence.

⸻

2. The Three Layers of Existence (Your Cosmology-Compatible Framework)

A. Ontic Existence

Physical, concrete, measurable.
Atoms, energy, spacetime, biological bodies.

This is the lowest, most rigid layer.

B. Noetic Existence

Mental, conceptual, emotional, identity-based.
Memories, desires, dreams, intentions.

These exist by affecting the mind and behavior.

C. Axiomatic Existence

Rules, structures, metaphysical laws, Aether logic, cosmological boundaries.
This is the top layer in the Dominion’s view.

A Dominion scientist would say:

“Matter is an aftereffect.
Mind is an expression.
Axiom is existence itself.”

These layers interlock.

⸻

3. Existence Is Relational

Nothing exists alone.

Something exists if:
	•	it can be measured
	•	it can be remembered
	•	it can be changed
	•	it can cause change

Existence is not a property—
it’s an interaction.

This is why Aether Entities “exist” even when they have no physical form—they exert influence on identity and resonance.

It’s why Severance memories “exist” even when they contradict time—they distort experience.

It’s why the Dominion’s axioms “exist” even when nobody understands them—they shape reality’s behavior.

⸻

4. Consciousness and Existence

Consciousness is a structure that:
	•	perceives
	•	predicts
	•	binds time into narrative
	•	generates identity
	•	exerts intention
	•	creates significance

A conscious being is not “alive.”
It is locally coherent existence.

Your entire cosmology already leans into this:
	•	Psychometry detects residue of past existence
	•	Psycho-genesis produces new existence (new selves)
	•	Ethereal Plane is existence-before-collapse
	•	Aether Singularity is hyper-existence
	•	Dark Eternities are existence held in rigid stasis
	•	Light Eternities are existence dissolved into potentiality
	•	Dominion is existence consciously engineered
	•	Severance is existence fractured and contradictory

Consciousness is existence that knows it exists.

⸻

5. Existence and Time

Time doesn’t make things exist.

Existence makes time necessary.

You need time when you have:
	•	patterns that persist
	•	changes that matter
	•	sequences of events
	•	memory
	•	causality
	•	desire
	•	resistance

In your cosmology:
	•	Dark Eternity: Pattern → infinite stability → no time
	•	Light Eternity: Pattern → total instability → time dissolves
	•	Aether Singularity: Time loops around pattern intensity
	•	Ethereal Plane: Pre-time, uncollapsed possibility
	•	Dominion: Time as managed resource
	•	Severance: Time fails → existence becomes unstable

Time isn’t fundamental.
Pattern stability is.

⸻

6. Existence and Identity

Identity is a specific type of existence:

A self-sustaining pattern that references itself.

The moment a structure becomes self-referential, it becomes a mind.

Identity is:
	•	persistent
	•	recursive
	•	emotionally weighted
	•	narrative-forming
	•	partitioned
	•	symbolic
	•	anchored in memory

Identity is existence experiencing itself.

This is why identity failure (Severance, trauma, temporal fracture) feels like annihilation:
the pattern can’t hold itself together.

⸻

7. Existence and Non-Existence

Non-existence isn’t “nothing.”
Nothing doesn’t exist.

Non-existence is:
	•	loss of pattern
	•	loss of influence
	•	loss of memory
	•	loss of identity
	•	loss of agency

Death is not non-existence—it is the end of physical persistence.
Memory is not non-existence—it is a weakened form of noetic persistence.
Aether Echoes are not non-existence—they are fragmented persistence.

Dominion annihilation (true erasure) means:
	•	removing all traces
	•	removing all effects
	•	removing all possibility of return

That is non-existence.

⸻

8. Existence in Your Universes

Dominion

Existence is axiomatic structure + harmonic coherence.
Dominion controls existence itself via pattern manipulation.

Concordia

Existence is grace + malice + Aether resonance.
Magic is an interface with existence’s flexible layers.

Post-Severance Earth

Existence becomes unstable:
	•	memories contradict
	•	time loops
	•	identity fragments
	•	reality bleeds
	•	ethereal leakage smears causality

Existence here is fragile and incomplete.

Aether Entities

Existence is yearning + resonance.
They form patterns and dissolve, driven by desire to unify.

Xeno Civilizations

Each defines existence differently:
	•	Krexolites: scent-memory persistence
	•	Draconids: emotional resonance
	•	Synarchate: harmonic unification
