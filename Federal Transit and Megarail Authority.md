# Federal Transit and Megarail Authority

## Summary

The Federal Transit and Megarail Authority governs the Dominion's primary civilian ground-transport networks.

In dense settlements, frontier worlds, and restored planets, the Authority is often more visible than the military. Citizens know its schedules, stations, evacuation drills, fare waivers, cargo windows, and strike disruptions.

## Mandate

The Authority oversees:

- megatrain networks;
- intercity transit corridors;
- freight priority schedules;
- evacuation lines;
- station security standards;
- transit labor licensing;
- accessible transportation;
- public transit subsidies;
- strategic reserve rail capacity.

## Powers

It may allocate track priority, suspend unsafe routes, requisition emergency capacity, regulate private rail operators, and coordinate with military logistics during declared emergencies.

## Limits

The Authority may not use transit access as political punishment, strand dissident districts, or hide military movement inside civilian schedules without lawful authorization.

## Public Face

For ordinary citizens, the Authority is the morning train, the delayed supply convoy, the school commute, the pilgrimage route, the refugee evacuation, and the station market.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show how the Dominion physically holds itself together. Transit is where social classes mix, frontier settlements stay alive, and war planning touches ordinary life before anyone admits war is coming.
