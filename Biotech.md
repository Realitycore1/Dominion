Core Bias:

Engineered life continuity

This subtype is one of the most important, because it allows Synthetic to merge with Forest and Ocean behaviors in powerful ways.

Reality here is built through:
	•	grown design
	•	gene logic
	•	programmed ecosystems
	•	synthetic organics
	•	wet infrastructure

Traits:
	•	engineered ecologies
	•	biofabricated habitats
	•	living tools or environments
	•	programmable tissue systems
	•	designed evolutionary pathways

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	biology has been turned into infrastructure
	•	life is no longer merely natural
	•	ecosystems have architecture

Common Expressions:
	•	grown cities
	•	synthetic forests or oceans
	•	biocoded habitats
	•	flesh-machine civilizations
	•	wetwire-adjacent realities

Failure Mode:

Mutation escape
The designed biology becomes its own uncontrolled basin logic.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	biotech civilizations
	•	transhuman ecologies
	•	post-biological life systems
	•	living machine worlds
