## Summary

GalFed science is organized around frameworks that measure whether a civilization, technology, ecosystem, or mind can remain coherent under stress. Its models are medical, physical, legal, and political at the same time.

## Details

Primary frameworks:

- Aether-Noetic Continuity Model: measures how Aether fields support or destabilize conscious identity.
- Standard Cognitive Divergence Index: scores cultural, psychological, and memetic deviation from recognized stability ranges.
- Civilizational Load Index: estimates whether a world can survive without GalFed logistics.
- Restoration Viability Scale: ranks silent or damaged populations by recovery probability.
- Archive Integrity Lattice: checks whether submitted histories are internally coherent or politically altered.
- Transit Hazard Matrix: classifies FTL routes by field stress, causality drift, and noetic risk.
- Founder-Grade Technology Tiering: restricts dangerous technologies by social maturity and Council permission.
- Predictive Rebellion Model: estimates probability of uprising, collapse, or ideological contagion.

These frameworks are treated as neutral science. In practice they are also instruments of authority. A world can be denied trade, delayed in autonomy, or placed under stewardship because a model says its freedom is statistically premature.

GalFed's scientific culture values repeatability, caution, and archive-backed evidence. Its blind spot is novelty. Unmodeled phenomena are often treated as threats before they are understood.
