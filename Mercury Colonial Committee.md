# Mercury Colonial Committee

Mercury’s committee government becomes decisive in 3180 after a corporate shade schedule preserves refining output while making an exterior worker route lethal. The committee places solar shades, radiators, polar reserves, and cold stores under public authority.

Mercury’s settlements are industrial systems: mirror fields, heat exchangers, forge cities, magnetic freight tunnels, buried reservoirs, and pressure districts following survivable thermal seams. Every surface has a thermal history.

Because light, cold, transport, rescue, and production cannot be scheduled independently, the committee develops into the Mercurian Directorate. Seats represent settlements, worker councils, engineering offices, and production districts. Every decision requires a thermal consequence statement.

Deka Nuru becomes the First Warden of Mercury. The Warden does not symbolically rule territory; the office is responsible for keeping heat survivable. The Directorate controls common light and shade infrastructure while the Forge Lords retain much of private production.

For Mercury’s culture, war experience, and contribution to interstellar engineering, see [[Mercurian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
