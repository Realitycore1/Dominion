t in science fiction that refers to a type of [[Artificial]] intelligence that operates on the principles of the moon. The moon brain is designed to draw its power and computing capacity from the gravitational forces generated by the moon's mass. It is typically a highly advanced form of AI that can operate independently of other [[Systems]] or even from a central computer. In some stories, the moon brain is used to monitor and control lunar operations or to manage other space-based activities. It may also be used to assist in the exploration of the lunar surface or in the development of new technologies for space travel. However, the moon brain also presents risks, as it is susceptible to environmental factors such as temperature fluctuations and cosmic radiation. It may also be vulnerable to attacks from other [[Systems]] or outside forces, making it important to ensure that it is protected and secure. The concept of the moon brain is interesting because it explores the potential for harnessing the resources of celestial bodies such as the moon for the purposes of computing and [[Technology Portal|technology]].
