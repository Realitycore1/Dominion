## Summary

![[Pasted image 20250830143455.png]]
A Planeta
ry Computing Complex is a planet-sized or planet-integrated supercomputer, optimized for:
Simulating civilizations, universes, or abstract realities
Hosting massive AIs (god-minds, strategia cores, or memetic warengines)
Crunching cosmological data, weapons simulations, or predictive societal models
Serving as the central nervous [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] for interstellar empires
Subterranean PCC	Built beneath the crust to avoid detection and shield from cosmic events.
Surface-Integrated PCC	Embedded across planetary surfaces, often disguised or hardened.
Shell-Based PCC	A concentric planetary shell packed with quantum cores, thermal sinks, and logic clusters.
Tesseract-Extended PCC	Internally recursive—larger inside via dimensional engineering; may house civilization-scale minds.
Quantum Computation Hives – vast linked clusters using zero-point or topological qubits.
Chrono-Simulation Chambers – localized [[Time]]-manipulated zones for running accelerated or slowed models.
AI Cradle Vaults – secure memory zones where AIs evolve, fragment, or specialize into recursive roles.
Memetic Lattice Engines – run social, linguistic, or ideological simulations at [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]]-level scales.
[[Verse]] Modeling Nodes – used by the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] to predict [[Verse]] pressure gradients or simulate future timelines.
