Core Bias:

Entropy after destruction

This subtype is defined by what remains after something once larger has burned down.

Traits:
	•	lingering decay fields
	•	collapsed ecosystems
	•	dead symbolic or biological strata
	•	powder-like, low-coherence remnants
	•	fragile post-collapse persistence

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	burnt-out histories
	•	aftermath worlds
	•	ruin fields where continuity failed
	•	silent remains of great systems

Common Expressions:
	•	collapsed civilizations
	•	burned symbolic orders
	•	post-religious or post-cultural basins
	•	dead archive zones

Failure Mode:

Dissolution
Even memory of structure begins to fail.

This subtype is extremely good for:
	•	failed cosmic empires
	•	archive collapse
	•	post-memetic wastelands
	•	late civilizational ruin
