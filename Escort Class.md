## Details

Corvette – smallest warship, rapid
-response [[Patrol Craft]]. Swarm tactics, kinetic + laser loadouts.
Hull: needle-like diamond, prow elongated like a spear tip.
Detailing: minimal engravings, smooth black plating with faint crimson veins.
Spires: small dorsal fins, tuned for resonance scans.
Feel: a dart of black iron, humming faintly, meant for pursuit and interception.
![[Pasted image 20250826103822.png]]
Frigate – balanced small warship, anti-piracy, escort for larger ships. Often specialized into hunter-killer roles.
Hull: slightly broader lozenge, still sleek but with layered armor.
Detailing: etched runes along prow and flanks, glowing faint gold when reactors peak.
Spires: two dorsal, one ventral, forming a triadic harmony field.
Feel: a faithful shield-ship, protective, its hum deeper than a corvette’s.
