Beam-Push Arrays: Stellar beam projectors (or [[Beamed-sail corridors]]) focused onto super-reflective atmospheric sails.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Gas giants can be weaponized:

Slingshot as gravity bombs against enemy [[Systems]].

Moved into orbits where they act as shield-worlds for Dyson Fortresses or Covenant Rings.

Economic: Converted into mobile fuel arks, dragging their mass into industrial cores.

Religious: Known as “the Strong [[Arms]] of the Empress” — proof she can lift worlds with her hands.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Enormous buttress-towers rising out of storms, glowing red with plasma exhaust, like cathedral ribs piercing the clouds.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the thruster ring appears as a seamless obsidian halo binding the planet.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Propulsion exhaust plumes shaped into fractal spirals, painting geometry across [[The Void]].

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The Lifter’s tethered beams glow with script, so that entire aurorae spell prayers as the planet moves.

Narrative Function

To citizens: The ultimate reassurance — even planets obey the Empress.

To rebels: The ultimate terror — whole giants can be dragged down as weapons.
