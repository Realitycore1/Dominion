# Dominion_Glossary_Rail_v1

This glossary rail gives new readers a short route through the terms they are most likely to meet first. It is intentionally smaller than the full [[VII - Term Glossary/VII - Term Glossary|Term Glossary]].

## Purpose Summary

The rail exists to prevent early reader overload. It points to stable portals and index pages before sending readers into specialist definitions.
