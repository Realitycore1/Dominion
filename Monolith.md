Core Bias:

Singular massive form and centralized permanence

This subtype favors:
	•	singularity of structure
	•	centralized load-bearing authority
	•	minimal but overwhelming presence

Traits:
	•	giant singular constructs
	•	oppressive simplicity
	•	few but immense structures
	•	authority through sheer permanence
	•	civilization concentrated into monumental nodes

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	one thing dominates the horizon
	•	meaning is condensed into singular forms
	•	permanence comes from concentration, not distribution

Common Expressions:
	•	megastructure basins
	•	singular capital worlds
	•	machine obelisks
	•	tomb monolith civilizations
	•	reality anchored by a few colossal structures

Failure Mode:

Central point failure
Too much continuity is tied to too few load-bearing forms.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	authoritarian or sacred states
	•	god-engine worlds
	•	central throne-civilization aesthetics
	•	Dominion-adjacent monumental infrastructure
