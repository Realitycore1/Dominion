Classification

Elemental Sentients / Pre-Human Intelligence / Contract-Bound Beings

Core Idea

Djinn are beings of will and volatility, born of smokeless fire and shaped by intention. They are not angels, not demons, not undead. They predate human moral frameworks and operate by oath, consequence, and elemental logic rather than good or evil.

They don’t tempt.
They negotiate.

⸻

Origin

Djinn emerged from the early world’s elemental excess—deserts, storms, volcanic vents, lightning fronts—where raw forces stabilized into awareness.

They are not created; they condensed.

Some bind themselves to places.
Others to bloodlines.
Others to objects, words, or laws.

⸻

Physical Manifestation

Djinn are selectively corporeal.

Common forms:
	•	Tall humanoid silhouettes formed of flame, ash, smoke, glass, or lightning
	•	Eyes like embers, stars, or voids
	•	Bodies that ripple as if heat-distorted
	•	Limbs that fade into vapor or sparks

They may appear regal, monstrous, or abstract depending on temperament.

They are never fully solid unless bound.

⸻

Behavior & Temperament

Djinn are proud, literal, and dangerously fair.
	•	They respect contracts absolutely
	•	They despise coercion and sloppy wording
	•	They remember insults across centuries
	•	They value cleverness over virtue

They will grant miracles…
…but always in the shape you asked for, not the one you wanted.

⸻

Intelligence

Exceptionally high.

Djinn think in:
	•	cause-and-effect chains
	•	loopholes
	•	symbolic equivalence
	•	elemental balance

They are master linguists and metaphysical lawyers.

⸻

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Faith-neutral: belief does not empower them
	•	Grace-resistant: angelic authority struggles to compel them
	•	Malice-compatible but not submissive
	•	Bound by true names, seals, sigils, and precise oaths
	•	Can be imprisoned in objects, locations, or states of being

Their power increases dramatically when unbound.

⸻

Contracts & Binding

Djinn contracts are sacred mechanics.

A valid binding requires:
	•	a true name or sigil
	•	consent, trickery, or legal metaphysical loophole
	•	a vessel (ring, lamp, blade, city, body, idea)

Breaking a contract does not anger a Djinn.
It unleashes one.

⸻

Djinn vs Other Powers
	•	Angels: view Djinn as anarchic anomalies
	•	Demons: attempt domination; often fail
	•	Loa: parallel intermediaries, but more rigid
	•	Kami: place-bound cousins
	•	Tulpa: artificial and fragile by comparison

Djinn represent freedom constrained only by words.

⸻

Threat Assessment

Variable → Catastrophic

A calm Djinn is manageable.
An insulted Djinn reshapes regions.

⸻

Narrative Role

Djinn embody:
	•	power without alignment
	•	the danger of poorly chosen words
	•	freedom as a volatile force
	•	ancient intelligence that owes humanity nothing

They are not monsters.
They are terms and conditions given form.
