# Chapter XI - Breaking the Spires

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Strategic spires fall.

## Event Role

- Spires may be command towers, orbital tethers, defense pylons, sanctuary monuments, or data relays.
- Their destruction ends enemy capacity in one theater while creating cultural trauma.
- The provisional state learns that victory over symbols can radicalize survivors.
- The line between necessary strike and desecration becomes disputed.

## Consequence

Breaking infrastructure is easier than replacing the meaning people attached to it.
