# (3160) Venus Colonial Committee established

The Venus Colonial Committee is established with the first permanent Venusian aerostat colony in 3160.

Habitat districts elect delegates by resident population. Lift-cell workers, water-system workers, educators, and medical staff receive transition representation because a floating city's industrial and civic systems cannot yet be separated.

## Local Authority

The committee governs:

- lift-cell and pressure-envelope certification;
- altitude bands, drift corridors, and local docking priority;
- water, breathing mixture, ballast, and emergency buoyancy reserves;
- school, clinic, inheritance, burial, and household law;
- surface-robot licensing and environmental containment;
- local elections and resident-status appeals.

No employer may control enough lift, water, or docking access to make termination equivalent to expulsion from the sky.

## Union Authority

The Colonial Union retains:

- interplanetary traffic coordination;
- common rescue and pressure-system standards;
- defense and external treaty authority;
- interworld trade regulation;
- recognition of additional Venusian cities.

The division makes the floating cities self-governing without making them materially independent. Belt ice, imported hydrogen, precision machinery, and Union traffic access remain survival requirements.
