# Pursuer-Class Warform (v1.0)
> Image unavailable after migration: `eaeed648-1da6-4853-a89c-f9389b8f88d4.png`
The Pursuer is the standard quadrupedal machine warform deployed by the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]]. Inspired by predator physiology but elevated through [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]], it embodies relentless pursuit, terror projection, and Collapse remembrance. Highly modular, the Pursuer-class exists in multiple variants ranging from civic pacification to battlefield [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans]].

---

## I. Core Chassis Features

- **Locomotion:** Quadrupedal, digitigrade design optimized for sprints, leaps, and terrain dominance.
- **Armor:** Resonant alloy laminate plating, inscribed with Collapse Histories. Trim glows under thermal and harmonic stress.
- **Powerplant:** Compact fusion or antimatter-hybrid core, with dorsal venting ridges for excess heat.
- **Senses:** 
  - Gravitational distortion sniffers for jump/warp tracking.
  - Resonant harmonic scanners to disrupt enemy warforms.
  - Thermal, EM, and optical sensor suite housed in a faceted skull-plate.
- **Cognition:** Semi-autonomous pack-AI, informed by Collapse Histories. Overrides possible via legion command or Verarch link.

---

## II. Variants

### 1. Policing Pursuer (Urban Pacifier)
- Lightweight armor, non-lethal outfitting.
- Equipped with resonance stun projectors, net launchers, suppression fields.
- Deployed by Infernariate and civic patrols for riot control and law enforcement.

### 2. Standard Pursuer (Legion Hound)
- Balanced warform for field operations.
- Modular loadouts: plasma cutters, claw-blades, resonant cannons.
- Deployed in packs alongside infantry; fear projection as integral as physical combat.

### 3. Bunker-Buster Pursuer (Siege Variant)
- Heavily armored, slower design.
- Fitted with seismic claws, micro-fusion breachers, resonance drills.
- Used to tear through fortifications, collapse bunkers, and breach void citadels.

### 4. Void Pursuer (Boarding Beast)
- Adapted for zero-G and shipboard assault.
- Features magnetic limbs, hull-cutters, gravitic anchors.
- Dropped en masse into enemy vessels for shock boarding operations.

### 5. Colossus Pursuer (Titan-Hound)
- Macro-chassis, titanic in scale.
- Armored like a fortress; weapons include anti-legion harmonic projectors.
- Used to annihilate enemy armor columns or spearhead siege operations.

---

## III. Doctrine & Deployment

- Pursuers are the **most common machine-form in Dominion legions**, ubiquitous across theaters from civic suppression to interstellar war.
- Always deployed in **packs**, their AI builds emergent pack-logic that mirrors biological predators but resonates with Collapse memory.
- Serve as both **weapon and reminder**: every Pursuer is inscribed with Collapse verses, binding them to doctrine as much as to war.

---

## IV. Cultural Role

- **Children’s Toys:** Miniature Pursuers carved in wood or crystal are common protective charms.
- **Symbols of Vigilance:** Poets describe them as “choirs of iron teeth.”
- **Fear Projection:** Their resonant roar destabilizes enemy morale and can collapse lesser warforms tuned to discordant harmonics.
