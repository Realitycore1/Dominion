Amber is recovered.

But:

* not unchanged,
* not singular,
* and not fully human anymore.

Exposure to:

* the Loom,
* Deep Aether,
* and continuity partitioning
    has altered her fundamentally.

Some witnesses report:

* multiple overlapping versions of her speaking simultaneously,
* partial temporal displacement,
* and emotional resonance effects across nearby personnel.

Official Dominion records simply state:

“The Empress returned.”
