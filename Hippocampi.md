Classification: Mythic Fauna & Allied Beasts
Subtype: Pelagic Mounts / Semi-Sapient Oceanic Steeds
Status: Active, Rare, Culturally Bound

⸻

Core Description

Hippocampi are hybrid marine beings with the forequarters of powerful horses and the scaled, finned bodies of sea-serpents or large fish. Their hides shimmer with nacreous sheen, bioluminescent veining, or scale-patterns resembling coral and wave foam.

They move through water with effortless momentum, able to outrun ships and maneuver through reefs, currents, and abyssal pressure layers.

A Hippocampus does not obey blindly.
It chooses its rider.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Hippocampi are naturally occurring mythic fauna, shaped by the long-term overlap of the Material Continuum, Vital Expanse, and oceanic spiritual resonance.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Material Continuum
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (endurance, speed)
	•	Tertiary Resonance: Liminal Sea-Spirit Ecology

They are not elementals, not constructs, and not summoned beings.

They are born of the sea’s need for mobility.

⸻

Intelligence & Temperament

Hippocampi possess high animal intelligence with empathic sensitivity.

They understand:
	•	intent,
	•	danger,
	•	territorial boundaries,
	•	rider emotional states.

They do not tolerate:
	•	cruelty,
	•	coercion,
	•	riders driven by malice.

A Hippocampus bonded unwillingly will sabotage its captor at the first opportunity.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Hippocampi inhabit:
	•	warm and temperate seas,
	•	coral shelves,
	•	Marid-controlled waters,
	•	myth-saturated coastlines.

They feed on:
	•	fish,
	•	kelp,
	•	mineral-rich plankton,
	•	magical residue from ley-tides.

They form:
	•	pods or shoals,
	•	long-term bonds,
	•	migratory routes known only to sea cultures.

⸻

Bonding & Use

Hippocampi are used as:
	•	mounts for sea gods’ emissaries,
	•	messengers between oceanic realms,
	•	ceremonial steeds,
	•	scouts in deep waters.

Bonding rituals often involve:
	•	offerings,
	•	oath-binding,
	•	emotional resonance rather than dominance.

Bonding failure can be fatal.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Cannot survive long on land
	•	Vulnerable to pollution and corrupted waters
	•	Avoid Null-dominant zones
	•	Severely affected by Malice-tainted seas

A dying Hippocampus is often an omen of ecological collapse.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Marid: Revered and protected
	•	Undines: Close allies
	•	Sea Serpents: Avoidance or competition
	•	Kraken: Flee on sight
	•	Leviathans: Treat as environmental hazards
	•	Adaro: Prey or rivals

Hippocampi are often used as neutral ground in aquatic diplomacy.

⸻

Variants
	•	Royal Hippocampi: Larger, heavily ornamented, ceremonial
	•	Deepwater Hippocampi: Pressure-adapted, darker coloration
	•	Reef Hippocampi: Agile, smaller, territorial
	•	Storm Hippocampi: Fast, lightning-aligned currents
	•	Ashwater Hippocampi: Rare, aggressive, post-collapse variants

Royal Hippocampi sometimes serve as living symbols of legitimacy.
