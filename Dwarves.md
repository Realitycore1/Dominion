1. Mountain Dwarves
	•	Definition: The classic archetype—dwarves dwelling in high peaks and cavern-fortresses.
	•	Traits: Hardy, excellent smiths and miners, culturally rigid.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance, tradition, mastery over stone and metal.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Deep Dwarves (Duergar)
	•	Definition: Subterranean dwarves warped by the dark and isolation.
	•	Traits: Pale or ashen skin, grim temperaments, resistant to magic, often cruel or paranoid.
	•	Symbolism: Survival in eternal night; dwarves twisted by secrecy.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Hill Dwarves
	•	Definition: Surface-dwelling dwarves in rolling hills, closer to [[Humans]] and agriculture.
	•	Traits: Broader builds, skilled in craft and farming, less isolationist.
	•	Symbolism: Grounded, earthy, the balance of toil and hearth.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Ember Dwarves
	•	Definition: Dwarves born to volcanic [[Realms]] and forges powered by magma.
	•	Traits: [[Fire]]-forged skin tones (ruddy, obsidian), mastery of [[Fire]] magic, weaponsmiths.
	•	Symbolism: Creation and destruction bound together.

⸻

5. Frost Dwarves
	•	Definition: Cold-dwelling dwarves adapted to tundras, glaciers, and permafrost.
	•	Traits: Pale or blue-tinged skin, fur-lined armor, attuned to ice and stone.
	•	Symbolism: Resilience against a merciless world.

⸻

6. Rune Dwarves
	•	Definition: Scholars and mystics among dwarves who etch magic into steel and stone.
	•	Traits: Covered in rune-tattoos, wield enchanted forges, blend craft and magic.
	•	Symbolism: The unbroken bond of craft and arcane [[law]].

⸻

7. Sky Dwarves
	•	Definition: Rare dwarves who dwell on mountain-tops, airships, or sky-[[Citadels]].
	•	Traits: Leaner builds, affinity for runic engines, stormforged weaponry.
	•	Symbolism: Ambition beyond stone—dwarves who refuse to be bound underground.

⸻

8. Exiled Dwarves
	•	Definition: Clans cut off from their kin, forced into wandering life.
	•	Traits: Pragmatic, mercenary-minded, distrustful of tradition.
	•	Symbolism: Dwarves without roots; survivalists stripped of heritage.

⸻

9. Stoneborn Dwarves
	•	Definition: Mythic dwarves said to be carved from rock or born of the [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] itself.
	•	Traits: Stone-like skin, near-immortal endurance, speak the “tongue of stone.”
	•	Symbolism: Living embodiments of [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s permanence.

⸻

10. Deep-Forge Dwarves (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary dwarves who tend the world-engines, primordial smiths of gods.
	•	Traits: Immense size, golden or glowing eyes, wield forges that shape reality.
	•	Symbolism: Creators of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], bridges between mortals and divine.
