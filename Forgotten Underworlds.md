# The Forgotten Underworlds

The deepest regions are collectively known as:
## The Forgotten Underworlds

These are:
- collapsed subterranean civilizations
- buried metaphysical kingdoms
- dead divine empires
- and sealed abyssal domains

Many contain:
- functioning systems without inhabitants
- empty thrones
- dormant war machines
- endless archives
- and automated guardians still obeying extinct rulers.

Some regions appear frozen in the exact moment of collapse.
