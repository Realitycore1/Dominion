## Details

An Orbital [[Artificial]] Ecosystem (O
AE) is a self-contained and self-sustaining ecosystem that exists in space, typically in low-[[Earth (Terra)]] orbit. OAEs are designed to simulate the conditions of [[Earth (Terra)]]'s ecosystems and serve as a testing ground for new technologies and ideas related to space exploration, life support [[Systems]], and long-term [[Human]] settlement in space. OAEs typically consist of multiple interconnected modules that are housed within a space station or other type of orbital platform. These modules include habitats for plants, animals, and microorganisms, along with various support [[Systems]] such as water recycling, energy generation, and waste processing. The idea behind an OAE is to create a closed-loop ecosystem that is entirely dependent on its own resources, with minimal dependence on external Inputs or support from [[Earth (Terra)]]. By studying the functioning of an OAE, scientists can better understand how complex ecosystems work and how they might be adapted for use in space. OAEs can also be used as a testing ground for advanced technologies such as [[Artificial]] intelligence, robotic [[Systems]], and autonomous navigation. For example, researchers can use OAEs to test new ways of controlling plant growth or studying the behavior of animal populations in microgravity. The development of Orbital [[Artificial]] Ecosystems is a growing area of research and innovation, with several private companies and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] [[Federal Agencies]] currently exploring the potential applications of these [[Systems]].
