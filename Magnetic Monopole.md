I. Physical Definition

In real-world [[Physics]], a magnetic monopole is a hypothetical particle that carries a net magnetic charge, existing as an isolated north or south pole rather than as a dipole. It arises naturally in certain grand unified theories (GUTs), as well as in some quantum field theory extensions and Dirac quantization arguments. Its [[(2030) Existence]] would symmetrize Maxwell’s equations, introducing magnetic current density alongside electric current.

Within the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), the magnetic monopole is defined as a topological defect particle — a stable knot in the electromagnetic field lattice that anchors magnetic flux lines to a single point in spacetime.
