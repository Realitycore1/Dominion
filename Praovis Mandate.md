## Summary

![[IMG_5197.jpg]]
Type: Xenophobic National–Authoritarian Mandate
Location: Praovis [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], ≈55 ly from Sol; one marginal terrestrial world (Praovis Prime) and several airless industrial moons.
Population (c. 4000 CE): 60–90 million
Founding Charter: C.U. Outer Defense Initiative Concession (3378 CE)

I. Founding (3370–3470 CE)

The Praovis Colonization Mission was commissioned by the [[Colonial Union]] as part of its Outer Defense Initiative, aiming to create hardened manufacturing [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] capable of sustaining frontier fleets.
Praovis Prime was chosen for its dense mineral crust and nearly oxygenless atmosphere—ideal for underground bunkers and vacuum metallurgy but unfit for open habitation.

Initial colonists were [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] contractors, penal [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], and ideological loyalists from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s nationalist enclaves. Their settlement charter included [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] autonomy in exchange for continual [[Production]] of war steel and kinetic weapon parts.

By 3470 CE, the colony had evolved into a self-contained garrison-industrial world. The Praovis Mandate Council declared itself “executor of the frontier,” operating as both [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] command.

Population: ~6 million, almost entirely within subterranean depots and domes.

II. Consolidation and Militarization (3470–3700 CE)

As the C.U.’s grip weakened after the Closure Era, [[The Mandate]] entrenched itself as an authoritarian state defined by isolation and martial [[culture]].

[[Infrastructure]] built during this period included:

Vacuum foundries and orbital crucibles producing high-tensile “war steel.”

Missile silos and hardened bunker-cities tunneled beneath the crust.

Fortified depots on airless moons, each garrisoned by thousands of infantry.

[[The Mandate]]’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] emphasized infantry fanaticism, indoctrinated from youth in survival drills and siege warfare. Every citizen received weapons training; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] service equaled citizenship.

Trade remained minimal. Excess materials were sold only through gray-market intermediaries, often rebranded as “recycled industrial alloy.”

III. The Age of Suspicion (3700–3900 CE)

Praovis propaganda portrayed the outer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] as “contaminated” by alien sympathizers and moral decay.
[[The Mandate]] launched a Fortress Program, walling itself off politically and economically.
Outsiders were forbidden to settle without “purity certification.”

To sustain its labor force, however, the regime quietly imported nonhuman laborers—semi-sapient alien clones bred by smugglers.
These [[Workers]] were confined to sealed compounds and used in hazardous foundries.

Scandal of 3886 CE: leaked comms from defectors exposed the [[(2030) Existence]] of “second-class” alien labor. Neighboring [[Systems]] demanded sanctions and threatened quarantine. [[The Mandate]] denied the charges, claiming all [[Workers]] were “modified [[Humans]].”

The incident fueled both xenophobia and isolationism; foreign observers were expelled, and border raids against “traitor [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]” increased.

IV. Toward 4000 CE

By the [[Time]] of the Equilon–C.U. conflicts, the Praovis Mandate functioned as a heavily armed fortress-state operating under perpetual emergency [[law]].
Its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] centered on:

War steel and vacuum fabrication.

Small [[Arms]] manufacturing for black and gray markets.

Paramilitary “export brigades” hired covertly by isolationist [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].

Population: ~70 million (official), with an estimated 15 million unregistered laborers.
Society divided between citizen-[[Soldiers]] and bonded [[Workers]].
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] headed by the Praovarch, a hereditary “Custodian of Purity,” supported by the Security Conclave.

Security posture: extreme OPSEC, encrypted comms, no external broadcasting.
Neighboring [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] report suspected raids and abductions near Praovis space but lack proof.

V. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] & [[culture]] (c. 4000 CE)

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: National–authoritarian Mandate ruled by Custodian (Praovarch) and Security Conclave.

Ideology: Purity [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] — [[Humanity]] must remain untainted by alien influence.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: War steel, vacuum industries, gray-market [[Arms]].

[[Infrastructure]]: Hardened depots, missile silos, airless rock fortresses, subterranean cities.

Security: Fanatical infantry, rigorous operational secrecy, networked point-defense grids.

Symbol: A black sun over a closed gate.

Language & [[culture]]: Militarized dialects of Standard; constant propaganda broadcasts extolling vigilance and purity.

Legacy

By 4000 CE the Praovis Mandate was both feared and pitied—a mirror of [[Humanity]]’s darkest survival instincts.
Its isolationist [[Policies]] and fortress mentality preserved it through countless wars, yet condemned it to paranoia and stagnation.
