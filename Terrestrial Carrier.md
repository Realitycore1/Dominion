Castrum-Class Terrestrial Carrier
Designation: Thronus Mobilis Terrae – “The Walking Throne of [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]”
General Specifications:
	•	Length: 1.6–5.[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] km
	•	Height: 180–300m (depending on suspension platform)
	•	Chassis Configurations:
	•	Tread-Fortress (iron-crawling cathedral)
	•	Hexapedal Walker (for extreme terrain)
	•	Grav-Lifted “Sanctum Hauler” (slow but invulnerable to mines and chasmic faults)
	•	Armor: Ferro-cathedralite—an alloy of adamantine, sanctified concrete, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]]-warded reinforcement
Deployment Roles:
	1.	Continental Crusade Support
	•	Deploys [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans]], armored convoys, and battlefield priests via central breach bays
	•	Functions as a mobile command cathedral, war hospital, forge, and staging ground
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Siege Advancement Platform
	•	Supports artillery lances, anti-fortification rail guns, and multi-layered flak domes
	•	Used to push through shielded lines and corrupted cities with prayer-powered impact drills
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	War Procession Anchor
	•	Accompanies crusade convoys as a [[Ritual]] centerpiece, carrying the choir-binders, Exemplar statues, and psalmic war banners
	•	May broadcast planetary-[[Wide]] Vox Veritas as it rolls, demoralizing enemies over weeks of rumbling advance
Core Features:
	•	Pulpitum Gate – Grand procession ramp used for mass deployment
	•	Choral Spires – Resonance towers that emit sanctified audio shockwaves to confuse enemy infantry
	•	Inner Sanctum of Command – War cathedral inside the hull, where high command communes with divine machine spirits
	•	Pyrthrite Vent Lances – Used to clear terrain ahead, or burn trenches behind
Censurium Cannons
Fires relic casings inscribed with Royal Veritas, destabilizing psychic terrain
Tombramp Gantries
Transports [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans]] and siege beasts offloading from the inner crucible halls
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] Seal Generator
Creates a local null-warp halo protecting the carrier and its spiritual [[Systems]] from corruption
Subordinate Vehicles and Escorts:
	•	Psalter Tractors – Mobile artillery batteries that follow in procession
	•	Flame-Knights – Piloted armored walkers with integrated Pyrthrite spears
	•	Veritas Tenders – High-speed utility crawlers for logistics and holy oil distribution
	•	Crux-Guardians – Auto-turret walking bunkers mounted with scripture-fed rotary cannons
Battlefield [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:
	•	Deployed only when a region is to be claimed, sanctified, or erased
	•	Presence alone can alter weather patterns, cause tectonic shifts, or draw [[Aetheric]] attention
Field Quote – Inscribed on All Castrum-Class Thrones:
“This world shall remember the thunder of faith, and the tremor of its unmaking.”
