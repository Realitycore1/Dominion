Core Bias:

Radiant overactivity and star-scale discharge

This subtype expresses instability through:
	•	radiation
	•	stellar flares
	•	solar pressure
	•	intense radiative environments

Traits:
	•	radiant hazard
	•	energy overload
	•	high photonic or plasma activity
	•	intense outer-layer instability
	•	star-dominant environmental shaping

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	light itself is dangerous
	•	stars are not background, but active violence
	•	civilizations must survive under radiant aggression

Common Expressions:
	•	flare-heavy star systems
	•	radiation-adapted species
	•	plasma navigation civilizations
	•	luminous but dangerous realities

Failure Mode:

Radiative sterilization
Energy becomes too active for layered continuity to survive.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	hard-sci-fi stellar basins
	•	radiant empire zones
	•	solar cult or engineering civilizations
	•	dangerous luminous cosmologies
