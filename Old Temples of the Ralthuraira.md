The Old [[Temples]] of the Ralthuraira, a holy site of the Merfolk that has long believed to have been lost in the eyes of both Celestial and Merfolk alike.

Theories among the Ralthuraira speculate that this was the location of the fracture of the Merfolk and the Celestials, though strange artifacts and symbols appear around the place through artistic interpretation and oral tradition, have led to a more mystical [[Origin]] in nature.

The place has been broken and slowly crumbling due to the pressure of the sea and the active lack of preservation, but maybe it has hidden treasures stored inside should it's location ever be unveiled again.
