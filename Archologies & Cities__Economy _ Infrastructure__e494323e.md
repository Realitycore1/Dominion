## Summary

GalFed cities are designed as civic therapy. They merge housing, clinics, archives, transit, education, and surveillance into environments that constantly reassure citizens that continuity is being maintained.

## Details

Common city types:

- Restoration Arcologies: dense medical-civic complexes for recovering populations.
- Archive Cities: cultural preservation districts built around memory courts and public history gardens.
- Corridor Cities: ports arranged around FTL customs, quarantine docks, and migrant flows.
- Harmonized Capitals: member-world capitals rebuilt to meet GalFed safety, law, and communication standards.
- Silent Preserves: low-stimulation districts for partially restored minds.
- Founder Enclaves: high-security administrative zones where local law yields to Primacy law.
- Null Towns: settlements in low-Aether regions used for research, exile, or mental recovery.

Design features:

- Soft lighting and slow public soundscapes to reduce noetic stress.
- Omnipresent memorials to the Aether Singularity.
- Public translation veils that make multilingual cities feel unified.
- Archive kiosks where citizens access approved history.
- Medical gates that look like transit gates.
- Transit gates that look like civic temples.

Citizens experience GalFed cities as safe, orderly, and beautiful. Dissidents experience them as places where comfort itself has administrative intent.
