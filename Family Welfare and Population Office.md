# Family Welfare and Population Office

## Summary

The Family Welfare and Population Office manages family support, child benefits, parental leave, fertility incentives, adoption systems, elder care, and population-pressure forecasting.

The Dominion's long-lived population makes family policy politically sensitive. Bodies still require care, children still require time, and immortality does not remove housing limits, school capacity, food demand, or emotional burden.

## Mandate

The Office administers:

- child allotments;
- household support credits;
- parental leave;
- adoption and guardianship support;
- fertility incentives;
- population-density reports;
- child-care cooperatives;
- elder and dependent care;
- family relocation support;
- welfare protections for military and frontier households.

## Powers

It may issue benefits, forecast demographic pressure, recommend housing expansion, fund care networks, and trigger review when a region faces induced overpopulation.

## Limits

It cannot force reproduction, punish childlessness, remove children for political belief, or treat family structure as a single cultural template.

## Public Face

Citizens know this office through birth grants, school placement support, crowded clinic forms, foster placement reviews, and arguments over whether the state is helping families or designing them.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show the intimate side of policy: population growth, engineered longevity, welfare, family incentives, and the quiet fear that civilization may ask more children from people already carrying too much.
