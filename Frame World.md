## Details

In the context of science fiction,
[[Artificial]] habitats orbiting a black hole. These habitats are typically designed to provide a safe and stable environment for [[Humans]] or other intelligent [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]] to live and work in the extreme conditions of the vicinity of a black hole. They may be constructed from advanced materials and technologies, capable of withstanding the powerful gravitational forces and intense radiation generated by the black hole. In some stories, these habitats may also have advanced computer [[Systems]] and [[Artificial]] intelligence that allow them to perform complex tasks and manage their operations. They may serve as a source of energy or resources for other civilizations, or they may be used as a place to study and explore the [[Physics]] and properties of black holes. However, the extreme conditions surrounding a black hole make these habitats extremely difficult to maintain and operate. They may require constant monitoring and maintenance to prevent catastrophic failures or accidents. Overall, [[Artificial]] habitats orbiting a black hole are a fascinating concept that explores the possibilities of creating sustainable environments in extreme environments and expanding [[Human]] reach into previously uncharted territories.
