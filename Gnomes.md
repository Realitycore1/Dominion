1. Rock Gnomes
	•	Definition: The classic tinkerers and burrow-dwellers, closest to the folkloric “[[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] spirits.”
	•	Traits: Curious inventors, stonecraft affinity, alchemical leanings.
	•	Symbolism: Industriousness, hidden wisdom, the secret power of the small.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Forest Gnomes
	•	Definition: Nature-bound gnomes who live in woodland glades.
	•	Traits: Masters of illusion, speak with animals, shy but clever.
	•	Symbolism: Innocence, trickery, the laughter of the forest.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Deep Gnomes (Svirfneblin)
	•	Definition: Gnomes adapted to the underdark or deep subterranean [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Grey skin, cautious temperaments, [[Natural]] stealth and psionic affinity.
	•	Symbolism: The small survivor in vast darkness.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Tinker Gnomes
	•	Definition: Highly inventive gnomes obsessed with devices, gears, and contraptions.
	•	Traits: Brilliant but erratic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], prone to explosive failures.
	•	Symbolism: The marriage of curiosity and chaos.

⸻

5. River Gnomes
	•	Definition: Rare waterbound gnomes tied to rivers and wetlands.
	•	Traits: Amphibious tendencies, excellent swimmers, herbalists.
	•	Symbolism: Resourcefulness, adaptability, liminality (between land and water).

⸻

6. Sky Gnomes
	•	Definition: Dreamers and astrologers among gnome-kind.
	•	Traits: Obsession with [[Stars]], navigation, weather-lore, often balloon/airship cultures.
	•	Symbolism: The small who reach for the infinite.

⸻

7. Clockwork Gnomes
	•	Definition: Semi-mythic gnomes tied to constructs, gears, and magical automatons.
	•	Traits: Mechanized prosthetics, symbiotic with machines, half-living puzzle-boxes.
	•	Symbolism: The blurring of craft and life.

⸻

8. Feyblood Gnomes
	•	Definition: Gnomes whose lineage leans more heavily into faerie magic.
	•	Traits: Illusionists, pranksters, uncanny charm.
	•	Symbolism: Trickery, whimsy, the strange light-hearted side of the unseen world.

⸻

9. Ember Gnomes
	•	Definition: Gnomes tied to volcanic or desert [[Regions]].
	•	Traits: [[Fire]]-resistant, glassworkers, smiths of miniature but deadly weapons.
	•	Symbolism: The spark of resilience in unlikely places.

⸻

10. Stoneheart Gnomes
	•	Definition: Legendary gnomes said to be born from gemstones or crystals.
	•	Traits: Gem-like eyes, innate elemental magic, guardians of hidden vaults.
	•	Symbolism: Rarity, treasure, and the indestructible spark of the [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].
