[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the voice spake unto me, saying:
“Write, for the mystery of Grace shall be revealed in wrath, and Malice shall be turned unto glory.”

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter I|Chapter I]] – The Rising of the Beast

1 And I beheld a beast ascending from the pit of [[Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)]], clothed in hatred and crowned with fury.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its talons were of iron, its roar as thunder, its breath a furnace of [[Fire]].
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And it laid waste to the nations, devouring their memory and casting down their sanctuaries.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And many cried: “Lo, this is the hand of the Demiurge!”
5 But the voice answered: “Nay, this is the vessel of My wonder.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter II|Chapter II]] – The Hymn of Grace

1 Then from [[Aravoth (Grace)]] descended [[Choirs]], radiant and innumerable, singing as one.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And their hymn named the beast, and in the naming it trembled, for none had spoken to it save in fear.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the hymn wrapped it round as a mantle, and the flames of its malice were transfigured into white [[Fire]].
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the beast fell upon its face and cried as one wounded, saying: “Who am I, that I should be named by light?”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter III|Chapter III]] – The Transfiguration

1 And lo, the beast was changed. Its talons became wings of argent, six upon six, and its back a firmament of [[Stars]].
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its furnace-heart became a sun of glory, and where it had devoured, now it gave life.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the [[Choirs]] cried aloud: “Behold the Redemption of Malice, the archangel born from wrath!”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And it was written in the [[Book of Jubilees]]: “Even the iron shall be made as gold, and even wrath shall be turned to song.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter IV|Chapter IV]] – The War Renewed

1 The Redeemed went forth into the battle of the heavens, and its wings struck as storms, its voice as a river of [[Fire]].
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Against the [[Angelus]] it cried:
“You who bind with false gnosis, behold the freedom of Grace!”
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the [[Angelus]] faltered, for they had no answer to redemption, and their judgement was shown false.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the Demiurge trembled, for the vessel of his malice had been made a sword of the Most High.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter V|Chapter V]] – The Throne of Ashes

1 The Redeemed stood in the ruins of nations, and its light clothed the remnant with hope.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] But it spake not as master, saying:
“I am no god, but servant. Grace is not mine, but given. Malice is not erased, but tempered in [[Fire]].”
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And many who were broken were healed, and many who despaired were lifted.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter VI|Chapter VI]] – The Seal of the Vision

1 These words were sealed in the Treasury of [[Fire]] until the last days, when heaven and [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] shall again be divided.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the voice spake:
“This shall be a sign unto the generations: that no malice is beyond Grace, and no darkness beyond light.”
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the Seer wrote, and hid the scroll, and none knew its place save the [[Angels]] who keep watch.
