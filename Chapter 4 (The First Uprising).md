# Chapter 4 - The First Uprising

## Timeline Function

War Year 1. Local unrest erupts behind the formal front lines as ration controls, emergency arrests, occupation rumors, and counter-bombing campaigns fracture public trust.

## Event Role

- Shows the war entering streets, factories, schools, shelters, and rail yards.
- Distinguishes lawful dissent from sabotage.
- Forces Federal and emergency authorities to decide how hard they will police their own people.
- Creates the first martyrs claimed by multiple sides.

## Consequence

The New World War stops being a conflict between governments only. It becomes a civilizational crisis inside ordinary households.
