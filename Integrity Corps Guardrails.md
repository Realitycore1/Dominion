# Integrity Corps Guardrails

## Summary

The Inquisition, publicly called the Integrity Corps, is a continuity-threat institution, not ordinary police and not a lawful tool for suppressing dissent.

It exists because the Dominion faces threats ordinary civil agencies may not be built to handle: systemic corruption, constitutional subversion, hostile memetics, ontological instability, forbidden technologies, archive sabotage, infiltration, and continuity-scale security breaches.

Its danger is obvious. A state that treats continuity as sacred can call almost anything a threat if no one can force proof.

These guardrails define what prevents the Integrity Corps from becoming secret police in current canon.

## Mandate Boundary

The Integrity Corps may investigate only when there is a demonstrable continuity risk.

Valid thresholds include:

- organized corruption that can compromise governance;
- constitutional subversion;
- continuity archive tampering;
- hostile foreign or metaphysical infiltration;
- prohibited ontological or relic technology;
- memetic structures capable of mass harm;
- existential security breaches;
- sabotage of provision, energy, transit, or memory systems;
- cultic or ideological networks attempting to seize governing power unlawfully.

Invalid grounds include:

- lawful dissent;
- unpopular speech;
- private worship;
- criticism of Amber;
- criticism of the Royal Government;
- refusal to participate in public ritual;
- minority culture;
- investigative journalism;
- ordinary crime without continuity implications.

## Legal Requirements

Core requirements:

- warrants for searches, seizures, and surveillance;
- probable cause or continuity-risk showing;
- court review for classified evidence;
- counsel access for detained persons;
- detention time limits;
- individualized suspicion;
- minimization of unrelated private data;
- audit logs for every exceptional authority;
- after-action review for emergency measures.

Emergency actions may move first only when delay would create immediate continuity harm. They still require after-the-fact judicial review.

## Oversight Bodies

The Integrity Corps is checked by multiple bodies:

- Federal courts for warrants, due process, and rights review;
- Classification Court for secrecy and public-interest disputes;
- legislative oversight committee with subpoena power;
- Inspector-General with indictment authority;
- protected whistleblower channel;
- Continuity Archives for action records;
- Imperial Memory Courts for continuity crimes committed by the Corps itself.

No single Royal office may permanently shield the Corps from all review.

## Prohibited Practices

The Integrity Corps may not:

- torture;
- use indefinite detention;
- operate parallel courts;
- erase memory as punishment;
- conduct mass surveillance without individualized warrants;
- police lawful political expression;
- infiltrate legal parties without court authorization;
- classify embarrassment as security;
- punish families for the accused person's lawful associations;
- use Amber's seal to bypass Federal review without witness-chain validation.

Memory alteration, identity editing, or record suppression is treated as a continuity crime unless narrowly authorized for containment of a proven ontological hazard and reviewed afterward.

## Political Dissent Rule

Lawful opposition is not a continuity threat.

A dissident becomes a valid target only when there is evidence of:

- violent conspiracy;
- foreign or hostile metaphysical direction;
- sabotage;
- unlawful seizure of governing organs;
- deliberate continuity-system compromise;
- targeted incitement to imminent mass harm.

The Dominion must be able to survive being criticized.

## Religious Rule

Faith Without Franchise means religion may not govern, but belief itself is protected.

The Integrity Corps may investigate religious factions only when they cross into:

- coercive hierarchy seeking state power;
- violence;
- predatory control;
- hostile memetic activity;
- foreign subversion;
- unlawful paramilitary organization;
- metaphysical containment risk.

Private worship is not evidence.

## Failure Indicators

The Integrity Corps is becoming secret police if:

- citizens fear criticism more than crime;
- warrants become automatic;
- courts receive only summaries, not evidence;
- whistleblowers disappear;
- "continuity risk" is used for embarrassment;
- dissenters are charged under security language without concrete acts;
- records are sealed forever;
- families avoid public petition because attention itself feels dangerous.

If these occur, the story should treat them as institutional corruption or crisis, not normal Dominion law.

## Story Use

Useful conflicts:

- an investigator believes a dissident cell is lawful but a Royal minister wants a case opened;
- a court rejects a continuity-risk warrant for lack of individualized evidence;
- a whistleblower exposes unauthorized surveillance of religious minorities;
- the Corps uncovers a real ontological threat hidden inside lawful activism;
- Amber forces a public review of an emergency action taken in her name;
- Emilia argues that a population cannot be protected by making it afraid to speak;
- Vel discovers a forged seal inside an Integrity Corps operation.

## Canon Rule

The Integrity Corps is legitimate only while it can be resisted, audited, witnessed, and judged.

If it cannot be challenged, it has stopped defending continuity and has become a threat to continuity.
