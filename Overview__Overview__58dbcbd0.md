Umbrae Res Impura is a Throne Realm civilization within the dark Aether sea of Severed Totum, centered upon a primary sovereign domain and expanded through the stabilization, conquest, and forced integration of adjacent broken realms.

It exists to survive against all threats and ascend toward apotheosis, imposing coherence upon hostile reality through paradoxic order, sovereign authority, and continuous war at its borders.

Bound to Atham, the Ancestral Majesty and Lord of the Severed Realms, it is not merely a state but a living field of imposed civilization—tragic, triumphant, unstable, and sublime.
