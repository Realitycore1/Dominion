Core Bias:

Distributed hidden connectivity

This is one of the strongest and most useful Forest subtypes for your setting.

Growth is not obvious, but deeply interconnected beneath the surface.

Traits:
	•	hidden network intelligence
	•	subterranean / substructural connectivity
	•	delayed but massive coordination
	•	distributed memory
	•	fungal / neural / root-like logic

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	everything is connected underground
	•	isolated things are secretly one organism
	•	knowledge and life spread invisibly

Common Expressions:
	•	networked civilizations
	•	distributed consciousness fields
	•	hidden reality roots
	•	deep substrate ecologies
	•	antimemetic or latent intelligence ecosystems

Failure Mode:

Substrate takeover
The hidden network becomes more real than the visible system.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	anti-memetic ecologies
	•	hidden verse-spanning biological intelligence
	•	“forest hive” crossover zones

This is probably one of your most important subtypes
