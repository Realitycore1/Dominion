# MOT and VFF Reader Integration

## Summary

MOT and VFF should not be hidden entirely in wiki lore. They are too central to the Dominion Project's originality.

They should enter general-reader material through felt experience first, faction interpretation second, and technical explanation third.

Dominion canon presents MOT as all three:

- public science;
- restricted state doctrine;
- sacred mystery.

Which face a person receives depends on citizenship, office, security level, education, religious context, and proximity to crisis.

## Layer I - Felt Experience

Readers should first encounter VFF as something characters experience.

Examples:

- instruments disagree with each other;
- the sky shows impossible colors;
- dreams synchronize across a ship or city;
- gravity behaves like weather;
- gates refuse safe alignment;
- old prayers or myths become suddenly relevant;
- a commander orders evacuation because the field has begun to shear.

Reader-facing handle:

VFF is the weather of reality.

## Layer II - Faction Interpretation

### Dominion

VFF instability is a continuity threat. It must be measured, stabilized, contained, or survived.

Dominion language should be procedural, grave, and morally charged.

### Synarchate

VFF events may be treated as dissonance, collision, disharmony, or an injured relation between realities.

Synarchate language should emphasize tuning, sanctuary, consent, and restoration.

### Sacred and Popular

Ordinary people may call VFF events signs, hauntings, false dawns, broken skies, angel weather, or the return of old prophecies.

Texts such as The Book of Grace and Malice belong in this layer.

### Technical MOT

MOT specialists interpret VFF through axiom behavior, verse topology, causal stress, information thermodynamics, exotic matter, and field interactions.

This is the deep wiki layer.

### Restricted State Doctrine

The Dominion treats some MOT knowledge as continuity-sensitive.

Public education may teach basic concepts. State doctrine controls operational knowledge that could destabilize transit, weaponize axiom conditions, reveal classified Severance causes, or undermine public order.

### Sacred Mystery

Sanctuaries, rites, and apocryphal texts may preserve MOT-adjacent truths in symbolic form.

The sacred reading is not necessarily false. In some cases, sacred tradition may preserve observations that technical institutions later reinterpret.

## Layer III - Technical Explanation

MOT should be revealed when characters need to make decisions.

Avoid:

- opening with a complete definition;
- explaining all seven pillars at once;
- using MOT as a universal answer to every mystery;
- letting technical language replace stakes.

Use:

- brief technical fragments under pressure;
- disagreement between specialists;
- failure modes;
- partial models;
- classified reports;
- post-incident autopsies;
- sacred texts later reinterpreted as descriptions of physical events.

## Story Method

Introduce the concept in this order:

1. A strange event happens.
2. A character gives it a simple name.
3. Someone else interprets it differently.
4. The event causes a cost.
5. MOT explains enough to make the next decision more frightening, not less.

## Civilian VFF Knowledge

Before the Severance:

Ordinary citizens generally did not know basic VFF safety rituals. VFF events were rare, concealed, geographically limited, misunderstood, or handled by specialists.

After the Severance:

Ordinary citizens learn basic VFF safety rituals. These may include shelter procedures, silence periods, light discipline, anti-panic liturgy, field alarms, evacuation formations, dream-report protocols, and instructions for identifying false transit stability.

This shift should be visible culturally. Older citizens may remember when VFF was obscure. Younger post-Severance citizens may treat VFF drills as normal civic life.

## Sacred Texts

The Book of Grace and Malice should not explain MOT directly.

It should show how people mythologized events that MOT can later analyze.

Possible annotations:

- two suns may correspond to overlapping local axiom expressions during a high-energy VFF shear event;
- false choirs may represent an encountered civilization, memetic field, or theological interpretation of interversal contact;
- the beast redeemed may be a literal entity, a weapon system, a traumatized species, a symbolic memory of Malice, or a mythologized account of successful axiom reorientation.
