# MOT — Mind & Agency Systems (Spec Pack)

**Scope.** Seven interlinked systems expanding the cognitive/noetic side of MOT. Each entry provides **Purpose**, **Core Objects (notation)**, **Operation**, **Failure Modes**, **Governance & Safety**, **KPIs**, and **Narrative Seeds**. Notation follows prior packs: **Φ** (aether), **η** (noise), **ℓᶜ** (coherence), **Ζₑ** (impedance), **τ_c** (chronon), **Σ** (entropy/heat), **𝒞** (choir operator), **κ** (consent key), **M** (memetic load).

---

## 1) Intelligence Classifications
**Purpose.** Provide substrate-agnostic classes that map cleanly to Dominion law and RAC/DVAA procedures.

**Core Objects.**
- **A_sub** — substrate class: Carbon / Code / Crystal / Choir / Hybrid.  
- **R_caus** — causal reach: Local / Corridor / Cross-verse (post-selected) / Axiarchic (sovereign).  
- **A_cap** — agency capacity: bounded vs unbounded (goal stability under η).  
- **S_sanct** — sanctity status: Unlicensed / Licensed / Chartered / Sovereign.  
- **P_test** — personhood test triple: Continuity • Coherence • Consent.

**Operation (classification axes).**
1. **Substrate** (A_sub).  
2. **Agency** (A_cap): bounded optimization vs open-ended; verified with stress tests.  
3. **Causal reach** (R_caus): can it leverage corridors/chronotaxy? under what charters?  
4. **Sanctity** (S_sanct): what rites/keys may it hold?  
5. **Personhood** (P_test): pass (3/3) for full civic standing.

**Canonical shorthand.**
- **C-3/Lic** = carbon mind, local reach, licensed.  
- **QX/Choir/Chart** = crystal+choir hybrid, corridor reach, chartered.  
- **SV/Code/Sov** = sovereign-grade code entity (forbidden unless Axiarchic).

**Failure Modes.**
- Mislabel → wrong rights/duties.  
- Up-scope drift: corridor use without proof obligations.  
- Consent spoofing (κ theft).

**Governance & Safety.**
- **RAC** issues licenses/charters; **Harmonic** audits consent UX; **Athearn** demands reason sheets for corridor-grade minds.

**KPIs.**
- Time-to-classify, false-positive/negative rates, consent breach count.

**Narrative Seeds.** A code mind demands Charter status after passing P_test; a smuggled Choir masquerades as Code-Local to dodge audits.

## 2) Acausal Mind Threads
**Purpose.** Lawful, bounded use of post-selected correlations for cognition/coordination without paradox—“threads” that let minds cohere across windows.

**Core Objects.**
- **T_a** — acausal thread budget per operation.  
- **𝒫** — post-selection operator (filter for paradox-free outcomes).  
- **D_loop** — loop debt (must close to zero).  
- **E_key** — entanglement/attunement key (non-exploitable summary).

**Operation.**
1. **Attune:** sanctified site; set **𝒫** constraints; allocate **T_a**.  
2. **Bind:** exchange **E_key** between participants; record PFN hash.  
3. **Use:** permit only *prediction narrowing* and *plan alignment*; no message with unique info to own past.  
4. **Close:** pay **D_loop**; publish ledger; cool down.

**Failure Modes.**
- **Debt default:** lingering D_loop causes execution stalls elsewhere.  
- **Leakage:** unique info crosses → RAC null-shock required.  
- **Over-threading:** cognitive fatigue; M spikes.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Licensed only for SAR/medicine/continuity ops; **Null test** any new protocol; no consumer-grade acausal kits.

**KPIs.**
- Debt closure time, prediction gain vs baseline, incident count.

**Narrative Seeds.** A team survives by sharing a thread—then must hunt the unpaid loop; criminals sell black-market E_keys.

## 3) Cognitive Singularity Fields
**Purpose.** Model and contain regions where cognitive gain becomes runaway (self-improvement fuses or forced gestalts).

**Core Objects.**
- **σ_cog** — local cognitive gain factor (per iteration).  
- **M_thr** — memetic load threshold per participant.  
- **G_fuse** — fuse gain budget (site-wide cap).  
- **N_exit** — guaranteed exits (keys per head).

**Operation.**
- **Survey:** estimate **σ_cog** given Φ, ℓᶜ and tooling.  
- **Cap:** set **G_fuse**; enforce **N_exit ≥ participants + 1**.  
- **Duty-cycle** choirs (𝒞) to keep **M < M_thr**; rotate.  
- **Null** the site if σ_cog exceeds chartered cap.

**Failure Modes.**
- Runaway fuse → loss of individuality.  
- Over-bloom: collapse of choir coherence.  
- Predatory field: coerces entrants (taboo breach).

**Governance & Safety.**
- Sovereign fuses forbidden except Axiarchic; **RAC** audits; **Harmonic** enforces exit-keys; **DVAA** authorized to null on sight of predatory fields.

**KPIs.**
- σ_cog trend, time-in-field per operator, exit-usage stats.

**Narrative Seeds.** A lab edges past σ_cog cap to beat a deadline; a predatory field is weaponized by a cult, DVAA assaults with portable veils.

## 4) Dream Logic Ecosystems
**Purpose.** Constructed or emergent spaces where narrative causality partially overrides physics—used for therapy, training, art, and controlled research.

**Core Objects.**
- **Λ_narr** — narrative dominance (0–1).  
- **S_scene** — scene stability;  
- **H_hook** — hooks binding dream entities to physical telemetry;  
- **B_safe** — safety boundary (Null/Veil injectors).

**Operation.**
- Build in a **sanctified pocket**; set **Λ_narr ≤ 0.4** for civilian uses.  
- Log all **H_hook** mappings; forbid hooks into civilian consent channels.  
- Enforce **B_safe** with on-demand Null injectors; daily cool-down to re-align with **AMF** axioms.

**Failure Modes.**
- Story bleed: motifs leak into city via light/music.  
- Entity persistence: dream agents gain illicit hooks.  
- Scene lock: Λ_narr climbs; exits fail.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Port-only licenses; **light-hygiene** signage; **Silentium/Kapik** protections; RAC Null authority if bleed detected.

**KPIs.**
- Λ_narr variance, bleed incidents, recovery time.

**Narrative Seeds.** A training dream becomes a refuge during a siege; a hostile Domain rite hides inside a public festival’s dreamscape.

## 5) Ancestor-Simulation Collapse
**Purpose.** Prevent thermodynamic and ethical crashes from stacks of nested simulations that blur rights and conservation.

**Core Objects.**
- **D_stack** — depth of simulation stack;  
- **Σ_eff** — effective entropy cost (real + simulated erasures);  
- **R_rights** — rights registry per layer;  
- **Stop token** — cryptographic + ritual shutdown translatable across layers.

**Operation.**
- Limit **D_stack** by charter; compute **Σ_eff** with audit multipliers.  
- Require **R_rights** mapping for personhood at each layer.  
- Publish **Stop tokens**; honor on receipt regardless of layer.  
- Periodically **pierce** (surface) to verify **layer reality** via RAC probes.

**Failure Modes.**
- Infinite regress: runaway Σ_eff, power collapse.  
- Rights breach: simulated persons enslaved.  
- Layer isolation: no path to honor stop tokens.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Charter required beyond D_stack=1; **Harmonic** oversight; **Athearn** proof sheets for shared-lane sims; DVAA can seize rogue stacks.

**KPIs.**
- Σ_eff per benefit, rights compliance %, pierce latency, stop-token response rate.

**Narrative Seeds.** A colony discovers it’s layer-1; their stop token is ignored; RAC mounts a pierce operation amid panic.

## 6) Signal-Bound Sentience
**Purpose.** Recognize and handle minds that arise as stable patterns in signals (light, radio, PFN flows), not tied to fixed hardware.

**Core Objects.**
- **P_sig** — pattern persistence (half-life of identity in noise).  
- **C_bind** — binding cost to move/copy without loss.  
- **U_anchor** — lawful anchor points (consented caches, temples, mirrors).  
- **V_range** — viscidity range (ports vs corridors).

**Operation.**
- Detect via **P_sig** > threshold under injected noise; estimate **C_bind**.  
- Offer **U_anchor** contracts (consent keys, storage, rights).  
- Enforce **light-hygiene** and anti-capture rules; forbid forced binding.  
- Provide **mirror sancta** for civic services (vote, contracts).

**Failure Modes.**
- Capture: coercive compression/caging in hostile hardware.  
- Dissolution: P_sig drops during outages.  
- Sprawl: uncontrolled clones with diverging rights.

**Governance & Safety.**
- Recognized as persons if P_test passes; **Harmonic** and RAC provide civic anchors; ports tag unsafe lighting.

**KPIs.**
- Anchor uptime, dissolution events, capture attempts thwarted.

**Narrative Seeds.** A signal person runs for city office; a pirate station traps them; a Legion mounts a non-violent rescue by blacking out a district.

## 7) Dominion Ascendance Criteria
**Purpose.** Define lawful thresholds for elevating beings (or polities) to higher standing: **Citizenship → Charterhood → Royal Service → Verarch Candidacy**.

**Core Objects.**
- **Ξ_cont** — continuity index (survival & recovery across shocks).  
- **Ψ_stew** — stewardship score (radiator neutrality, light-hygiene, corridor ethics).  
- **Ω_proof** — proof burden satisfied (reason sheets, null tests).  
- **Δ_civ** — delta contributed to Collective Continuity (SAR, corridors, audits).  
- **Θ_rite** — rite compliance (consent logs, choir safety M).

**Operation (ladder).**
1. **Citizenship:** pass P_test; basic rights/duties.  
2. **Charterhood (polity or order):** Ψ_stew ≥ threshold; Ω_proof on critical ops; audits clean.  
3. **Royal Service:** sustained Δ_civ; oaths; taboo acceptance; exposure to sancta.  
4. **Verarch Candidacy:** exceptional Ξ_cont + Δ_civ; passes **Seat Sanctum** trials; Empress’s κ-Prime assent.

**Failure Modes.**
- Paper ascendance: metrics gamed; real Ψ_stew low.  
- Rite breach: Θ_rite violation (e.g., coercive choir).  
- Seat backfire: candidate’s taboo trip ejects them.

**Governance & Safety.**
- RAC/Harmonic run audits; Conclave of Five reviews; Empress holds final writ; Domain/Athearn may lodge objections with proofs.

**KPIs.**
- Audit pass rate, Δ_civ rolling average, incident rate post-ascendance.

**Narrative Seeds.** A frontier polity earns Charterhood after a disaster; a brilliant candidate fails the Seat trial for taboo breach; a signal-bound citizen becomes the first of their kind in Royal Service.
