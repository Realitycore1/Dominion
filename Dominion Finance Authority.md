# Dominion Finance Authority

## Summary

The Dominion Finance Authority manages federal revenue, public budgets, debt instruments, currency stability, interprovincial transfer payments, welfare funding, and major fiscal review.

It is the agency that turns moral promises into funded programs or exposes them as rhetoric.

## Mandate

The Authority oversees:

- federal taxation;
- budget certification;
- public debt;
- currency stability;
- infrastructure financing;
- disaster relief funds;
- welfare disbursement;
- anti-corruption audits;
- interprovincial fiscal equalization.

## Powers

It may freeze suspicious funds, audit public contracts, certify budgets, issue federal bonds, and block unlawful spending.

## Limits

It cannot starve political opponents of lawful funds, hide military or Royal spending inside civilian accounts, or let core-world stability consume frontier survival.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Authority through taxes, benefit payments, public grant notices, budget scandals, inflation reports, and the hard question of who pays for continuity.

## Story Use

Use this agency whenever idealism hits cost. The Dominion can promise survival; Finance decides what that promise does to rent, food, schools, and salaries.
