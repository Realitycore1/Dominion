1. Common Drakes
	•	Definition: The baseline—smaller, wingless or short-winged dragonkin.
	•	Traits: Quadrupeds, scaled hides, beastlike intelligence.
	•	Symbolism: A “tamed” fragment of dragonkind, danger in a manageable form.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. [[Fire]] Drakes
	•	Definition: The classic elemental drake, born of volcanic wastes or dragonfire.
	•	Traits: Ember breath, scorched scales, pack hunters in ashlands.
	•	Symbolism: Flame embodied in lesser form; destructive loyalty.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Frost Drakes
	•	Definition: Icebound drakes roaming tundras and glaciers.
	•	Traits: Frost breath, pale-blue hides, thrive in arctic storms.
	•	Symbolism: The biting [[Hunger]] of cold; relentless pursuit.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Storm Drakes
	•	Definition: Skybound drakes riding thunderclouds.
	•	Traits: Electric breath, wings sparking lightning, affinity with storms.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos of the skies; living tempests.

⸻

5. [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] Drakes
	•	Definition: Burrowers and mountain-dwellers with stony hides.
	•	Traits: Tremor-causing charges, rocklike scales, subterranean nests.
	•	Symbolism: The crushing inevitability of stone.

⸻

6. River Drakes
	•	Definition: Semi-aquatic drakes of rivers, swamps, and wetlands.
	•	Traits: Amphibious, acidic breath, scaled like eels or crocodiles.
	•	Symbolism: Corruption and ambush; predators of thresholds.

⸻

7. Winged Drakes
	•	Definition: Larger drakes closer to dragons, capable of limited flight.
	•	Traits: Small wingspans, gliding rather than soaring, agile in combat.
	•	Symbolism: The stepping stone to true dragons.

⸻

8. Pack Drakes
	•	Definition: Social drakes that hunt in coordinated groups.
	•	Traits: Wolf-like tactics, bonded calls, territorial packs.
	•	Symbolism: The blending of [[Dragon]] ferocity with animal instinct.

⸻

9. War Drakes
	•	Definition: Domesticated or bred for battle as mounts or siege beasts.
	•	Traits: Heavily armored, disciplined, loyal when bonded.
	•	Symbolism: Mortal attempt to control dragonkind through force.

⸻

10. Mythic Drakes
	•	Definition: Legendary drakes rumored to rival lesser dragons in power.
	•	Traits: Massive, ancient, and cunning; often revered as guardian beasts.
	•	Symbolism: The reminder that even “lesser dragons” can grow into awe.
