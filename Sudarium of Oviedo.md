🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: Sudarium of Oviedo (face cloth of Christ)
Discovery Location: Oviedo Cathedral, Spain — long preserved, subjected to new forensic study in 2037 parallel to Shroud testing.
Summary:
	•	Material: Linen cloth, 84 × 53 cm, bloodstained, with no visible image.
	•	Dating: Radiocarbon testing revised — places [[Origin]] ~1st century CE ± 60 years.
	•	Forensic Comparison:
	•	Blood type matches [[Shroud of Turin]] (AB).
	•	Stain patterns correspond to facial wound placements; overlay analysis shows congruence with Shroud facial structure.
	•	Pollen samples include [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] native to Jerusalem region, absent from Iberia.
	•	Anomalies:
	•	Traces of myrrh and aloe detected, consistent with Jewish burial practices.
	•	Cloth fold patterns suggest it was wrapped tightly around a head with crown-like puncture wounds.
	•	Archaeological Significance: Strong forensic correlation with [[Shroud of Turin]] elevates both [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]]’ authenticity. Provides a chain of custody narrative from Jerusalem → North Africa → Spain.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-06 (Sudarium)
Classification: EXTREME SYMBOLIC IMPACT (Secondary Corroborating Relic)
Custody: Shared oversight by Spanish Church and Vatican; moved to Rome in 2038 for joint display with Shroud during Crusade mobilization.
Strategic Assessment:
	•	Vatican: Frames Sudarium as forensic confirmation of the Shroud, transforming speculation into “double evidence.” Central to Papal messaging: “Two witnesses to the Passion.”
	•	[[Europolis]]: Supports but resents Vatican dominance; Spain presses for recognition of Sudarium as national heritage.
	•	Caliphate: Condemns as forgery; propaganda mocks Christians for venerating “bloodstained rags.” Still worried by psychological effect on Crusader recruits.

	•	Combined Shroud–Sudarium displays generate mass enlistments and fervor.
	•	Splinter sects claim Sudarium is more authentic than Shroud, risking minor fractures inside Crusader bloc.
