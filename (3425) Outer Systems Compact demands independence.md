# (3425) Outer Systems Compact demands independence

Jupiter and Saturn form the Outer Systems Compact in 3425 with Uranian and Neptunian participation and Kuiper observers.

The Compact demands:

- independent taxation and domestic law;
- local command of rescue and defense fleets;
- control of outer-system resource policy;
- a veto over Colonial Union seizure of antimatter, fuel, ships, and Watchtower infrastructure;
- equal treaty status rather than colonial representation;
- continued postal, traffic, rescue, trade, and personhood interoperability.

Jupiter and Saturn lead because their moon systems contain the largest outer populations, shipyards, convoy fleets, agriculture, and fuel industries.

The Compact does not speak automatically for every moon. Local governments, labor organizations, scientific stations, and mixed inner-outer households divide over independence.

The Colonial Union offers expanded autonomy but rejects full independence because the outer systems control antimatter production, deep-system warning, and the route for future interstellar flight.

The constitutional dispute becomes the immediate cause of the Second Solar War.
