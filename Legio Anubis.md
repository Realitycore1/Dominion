## Consolidated Lock - Founding Verarch and Judgment Role

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

Founding Verarch: [[Thanekhet Osiron]].

Legio Anubis was one of the first six campaign Legions of the early Dominion.

Where other Legions broke fleets, seized corridors, inverted fortresses, or strangled enemy supply, Anubis answered the question left after victory:

> What is to be done with the defeated?

Anubis evaluated conquered civilizations for purge, quarantine, uplift, protection, integration, or termination. Their arrival meant that the battle had ended and judgment had begun.

They were not merely executioners. They were the Dominion's funerary court in armor.

Legio Anubis was erased from record because it began judging the Dominion by the same standards it applied to conquered worlds.

Best line:

> Ares ended resistance. Aurelis took the walls. Anubis decided whether the city deserved to wake.

Legacy: Legio Anubis is remembered as the Legion of silent doom. Their coming heralds despair; their [[Aftermath]] is fields of the dead arrayed in perfect order, as though prepared for burial by unseen hands.
