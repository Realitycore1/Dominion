Designation

The Jade Association

Avenue

Syndicate

Core Identity

The Jade Association is not a cartel.
Not a mafia.
Not a cult.

It is criminal professionalism.

They sell:
	•	interdiction
	•	asset seizure
	•	deniable enforcement
	•	corporate sabotage
	•	information extraction

If someone needs a problem solved quietly, they call Jade.

Operational Characteristics
	•	Contract-only operations
	•	No visible branding
	•	No permanent territory
	•	Highly specialized crews
	•	Exceptional legal literacy

They operate in the spaces between jurisdictions, using ambiguity as armor.

Reputation

“If Jade is involved, someone paid for this—and they paid well.”

Jade Association crews are rarely intercepted.
Not because they’re invisible—but because nobody wants to admit they exist.
