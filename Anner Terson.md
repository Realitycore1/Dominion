Role:

Senior Floodwall Maintenance Chief

Function:

Civilian continuity anchor.

Anner is the person who:

* keeps infrastructure alive,
* understands New Aurora physically,
* notices structural failure before systems do,
* distrusts politicians,
* survives through routine competence.

⸻

Personality

* dry humor,
* emotionally restrained,
* practical,
* exhausted without self-pity,
* deeply superstitious despite pretending otherwise.

Not ideological.

Not philosophical.

But after decades on Harmina:
he has rituals.

Example:

* taps wall plating before storms,
* refuses to work during specific resonance frequencies,
* leaves thermal stones outside maintenance doors.

Never explains why.

⸻

Why He Matters

He embodies:

civilization as maintenance.

Which fits your themes perfectly.

He also gives you:

* grounded civilian perspective,
* working-class continuity,
* infrastructure realism,
* decompression scenes naturally.

⸻

Best Use Scenes

* repairing storm damage,
* mentoring younger workers,
* arguing with engineers,
* casually discussing death while fixing rail systems,
* drinking fungal tea during storms.

⸻

Hidden Emotional Weight

He remembers when New Aurora was temporary.

That matters.

One line from him could hit hard:

“You know the strange thing? We stopped building like we expected to leave.”

That’s his function.
