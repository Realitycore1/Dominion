The Veyrth’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] blends theology, philosophy, and war strategy into one unified worldview. It is recited in councils, drilled into [[Soldiers]], and carved into the walls of their sanctums.

[[The Maw]] is Truth

The rupture is not a wound but a divine mouth — it reveals the acausal and guides the faithful.

To question [[The Maw]] is heresy.

[[The Dominion]] is the Devourer of Ages

All myths of apocalypse, all legends of evil, are fulfilled in mankind and their [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]].

To resist them is to preserve creation; to ignore them is to doom it.

Caste is Destiny

Each race and subrace fulfills the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]]’s design:

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]] = divine will.

Aldar = interpreters and administrators.

Draconids = vessels and warriors.

To step outside one’s caste is to reject salvation.

Communion is Strength

No private property, no private destiny.

The Host is all; the individual is nothing without it.

War is Worship

Every campaign is a holy act.

To fight is to sing praise; to die in battle is to transcend.

The Promise of Transcendence

The faithful believe that through sacrifice, possession, and eternal war, they will ascend beyond mortality.

In truth, this is impossible — but the lie binds them tighter than [[Chains]].
