## Summary

## Details

An Orbital [[Hydroponics]] Station is 
a type of space station that is designed to grow plants in a closed and controlled environment, using [[Hydroponics]] techniques and [[Technology Portal|technology]]. These stations are typically located in low [[Earth (Terra)]] orbit or other stable orbitals around a planet or moon, where they can take advantage of the unique growing conditions that are available in space. Orbital [[Hydroponics]] Stations use advanced techniques and equipment to grow plants in a nutrient-rich solution, without the need for soil or other traditional growing media. This allows for the [[Production]] of crops that are more efficient, sustainable, and controllable than traditional farming methods. The plants grown in an Orbital [[Hydroponics]] Station can be used to produce food for [[Human]] consumption, as well as to provide a source of oxygen and other vital resources for the crew of space stations or habitats. They can also be used to produce medicinal herbs and other crops that are necessary for the [[Production]] of medications or other pharmaceuticals. The design of an Orbital [[Hydroponics]] Station would depend on the specific crops being grown, as well as any existing regulations or safety protocols that need to be followed. They typically consist of a series of interconnected modules, each containing specialized equipment and technologies for growing plants in a controlled environment. Overall, Orbital [[Hydroponics]] Stations represent a vital component of modern space exploration, providing a sustainable and reliable source of food and other essential resources for [[Human]] settlement beyond [[Earth (Terra)]]'s atmosphere.
