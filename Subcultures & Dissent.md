## Nature of Dissent
Variation tolerated; dissonance crushed. Dissent = existential threat.

## [[Subcultures|Subcultures]]
- **Whisper [[Choirs]]**: counter-harmonies.  
- **Grey Markets**: black-market augmentations.  
- **Children of [[Silence]]**: reject harmony worldview.  
- **Obscura**: parodists and grotesque artists.

## Heresy Classes
- Disharmony – eccentricity.  
- Discord – destabilizing.  
- Corruption – sabotage.  
- Silencing – total erasure.

## Integration vs Suppression
Eccentric sects sometimes absorbed; destabilizers annihilated.
