Classification: Leviathan Class – Tier XII / Field Designate “Abyssal Seraph”
Observation:
The visual records a colossal serpentine organism breaching storm waters amid violent surf. Dermal structure smooth but armored with repeating organic ridges; cranial dome glossy, uniformly opaque, with ventral mandibles extended in display posture. Scale ratio analysis places total body length between [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]–[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].1 kilometers. Wake displacement measurable across several nautical sectors, accompanied by electromagnetic disturbances in the cloud strata above.

Contextual Analysis:
This entity’s first confirmed sighting occurred during the “Harminan Hydrospheric Mapping Campaign.” Orbital telemetry registered subsurface thermal anomalies and localized gravitational fluctuations before emergence. The Abyssal Seraph exhibits both hydrodynamic and gravitational manipulation traits—its movement appears to displace density rather than traverse it. Genetic correlation studies identify it as a marine offshoot of the Vellum genus, exhibiting full mesopsionic field control and oceanic sovereignty behavior.

Filed in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] scripture under the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Vastness. The Abyssal Seraph is regarded as the living metaphor for the planet’s subconscious—a being that does not defend, but defines the sea itself. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] philosophers liken it to the manifestation of planetary will: creation that has transcended scale and motive. Within religious canon, the creature symbolizes divine equilibrium—immense power without aggression, presence as control. Its recorded stillness in later observations became the baseline image for Harminan serenity.
