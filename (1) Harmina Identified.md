# (1) Harmina Identified

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part II, Chapter 12.
- Timeline function: explains why Harmina is chosen and why GalFed remains the hidden strategic clock.

## What Happened

While drifting in the Void, the Ark discovered that it was not alone. Intercepted transmissions revealed GalFed: an intergalactic power with mature faster-than-light networks, automated logistics, medicine, schools, patrol routes, and enough stability to make surrender look attractive to exhausted refugees.

For forty-seven months, a classified listening and analysis program decoded GalFed traffic. The work eventually became known as the Discovery Clock. The Ark did not prepare for a war it could win. It prepared to avoid being found.

Harmina emerged from intercepted GalFed navigation data as an undesirable but habitable world: costly to colonize, ecologically difficult, lightly trafficked, and therefore useful as a place to disappear.

## What Resulted

- GalFed became the external threat model behind Harmina's long-term defense culture.
- Specialized GalFed knowledge programs began: language, biology, logistics, orbital detection, camouflage, and future defense planning.
- Harmina was selected not because it was ideal, but because it was difficult enough that GalFed avoided heavy development.
- The Ark population divided between those who wanted contact with GalFed and those who feared dependency.

## Consequences

The Discovery Clock became the buried secret of Harminan civilization. Later generations would remember the schools, the drills, and the orbital systems more clearly than the fear that created them.
