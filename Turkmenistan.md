Turkmenistan is a landlocked country located in Central Asia, with a diverse geography and significant mineral resources. The country is bordered by Kazakhstan to the north, Uzbekistan to the west, [[Afghanistan]] and [[Iran]] to the south, and the Caspian Sea to the east. The country has also made efforts to develop tourism and agriculture, with a focus on promoting the country's cultural heritage and [[Natural]] landscapes. Turkmenistan's geography is characterized by deserts, mountains, and steppes, with the country's northern [[Regions]] featuring more arid landscapes while the southern [[Regions]] are more fertile. The country has significant mineral deposits located throughout its territory, with the rich resources of the northern Caspian region being particularly valuable. The country's [[culture]] is heavily influenced by its nomadic past and Islam, with the Turkish language being spoken by the majority of the population. Turkmen [[culture]] is characterized by its traditional dress, music, and food, as well as its emphasis on hospitality and generosity. Despite its significant mineral resources and cultural heritage, Turkmenistan has faced challenges related to political instability, corruption, and environmental degradation. The country has made efforts to address these challenges through reforms and economic diversification efforts.
