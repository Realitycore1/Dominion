Motto
io Caelestis — legion of aerial, orbital, and stellar supremacy.

Armor: Sky-forged panoply with flowing, radiant cerulean and argent tones. The plates shimmer faintly as though reflecting nebulae. The design is winglike and ascendant, with sweeping forms suggesting lift and grace.

Helm: Crowned with a radiant halo-crest shaped like a starburst, visor glowing with gold light. His gaze is said to burn like a sun over the battlefield.

Secondary: Wings of [[Aetherion]] — immense energy-sails that can unfold from his back, allowing him to “fly” across the battlefield or descend like a comet.

Wherever he appears, the skies themselves ignite. [[Soldiers]] describe seeing constellations realign above him, as if the heavens bend to his presence.

His arrival is often heralded by blazing streaks of light, as though meteor-showers attend his coming.

He is the sovereign of the skies, the embodiment of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s rule not only over worlds but over the space between them.

His very presence is a crown of [[Stars]], showing [[Humanity]]’s claim to heaven’s throne.

“The sky is no sanctuary. The [[Stars]] are ours.”
