1. Common Gnolls
	•	Definition: The baseline—hyena-headed humanoids, savage scavengers and raiders.
	•	Traits: Lean builds, yellowed fur, bone weapons, feral laughter.
	•	Symbolism: Cruelty of the scavenger; [[Hunger]] that never ends.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Plains Gnolls
	•	Definition: Nomadic gnoll tribes that roam grasslands and savannahs.
	•	Traits: Hunters and raiders, hyena pack tactics, expert trackers.
	•	Symbolism: The predator as a [[Natural]] force; survival by the pack.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Desert Gnolls
	•	Definition: Gnolls dwelling in arid wastes and salt flats.
	•	Traits: Sun-bleached fur, thirst-hardened, ambush predators in dunes.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance of [[Hunger]] and thirst; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in wastelands.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Jungle Gnolls
	•	Definition: Hyena-folk of deep forests and tropics.
	•	Traits: Darker fur, ambushers from treetops, venom-coated weapons.
	•	Symbolism: Abundance twisted into cruelty; predators where life thrives.

⸻

5. Abyssal Gnolls
	•	Definition: Gnolls corrupted by demonic influence (often Yeenoghu archetype).
	•	Traits: Red eyes, shadowy auras, [[Hunger]] for flesh beyond reason.
	•	Symbolism: [[Hunger]] as a curse; cruelty without end.

⸻

6. Bone Gnolls
	•	Definition: Gnolls who wear bones as armor and wield necromantic rites.
	•	Traits: Skeletal adornments, bone-shamans, carrion-eaters of warfields.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] recycled into life; feast of the dead.

⸻

7. Shadow Gnolls
	•	Definition: Gnolls tied to graveyards and cursed lands.
	•	Traits: Gaunt frames, spectral howls, affinity for necrotic magic.
	•	Symbolism: The scavenger made into omen; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] laughing in the dark.

⸻

8. Noble Gnolls
	•	Definition: A rare breakaway—gnolls who organize into structured, disciplined societies.
	•	Traits: Tribal monarchs, armored warbands, loyalty forged in blood.
	•	Symbolism: The predator given [[law]]; savagery tamed into [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

⸻

9. Pack-Lord Gnolls
	•	Definition: Greater gnolls that dominate whole tribes through sheer power.
	•	Traits: Larger builds, iron-willed, terrifying howls.
	•	Symbolism: Alpha predator; [[law]] of strength alone.

⸻

10. Arch-Gnolls (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary gnolls said to embody famine, war, or unending [[Hunger]].
	•	Traits: Colossal forms, laughter that splits stone, [[Hunger]] for souls.
	•	Symbolism: [[Hunger]] as apocalypse; predator elevated to cosmic threat.
