## Summary
Classification: Dimensional Hazard – Tier Ω (Cross-Verse Intrusion Event)
Department of Record: Directorate of Axiomatic Integrity, Division of Reality-Field Containment
Status: Active Monitoring Across 112 Known Loci

⸻

1. Definition

A Verse Breach Point (VBP) is a localized rupture in the Verse Field Framework—the membrane of Axiomatic cohesion that defines the Dominion’s physical-metaphysical constant set.
A VBP occurs when an external field, often from a foreign verse, universe, or higher-order structure, exerts pressure or resonance sufficient to force phase intrusion into Dominion spacetime.

“A verse breach is not a doorway; it is a wound.”
— Dr. Selian Vaert, Dominion Aetherics Directorate

⸻

2. Genesis and Cause

Primary Triggers
	1.	Axiomatic Pressure Gradient: External verse exerts higher logical or energetic density, overwhelming local constants.
	2.	Resonance Collision: Frequency overlap between Dominion Verse signature and another verse’s basal harmonic.
	3.	Technological Provocation: Artificial experimentation (e.g., NLPC overreach, warp-core harmonic collapse, or forbidden Aether-Anchor projects).
	4.	Cosmic Stress Event: Gravitational or causal distortions caused by collapsed realities (Chain Collapse Events, Matter-Core implosions).
	5.	Intentional Invasion: Directed incursion by non-native intelligence or omniversal faction using verse-piercing devices or metaphysical anchors.

⸻

9. Strategic Interpretation

Verse Breach Points demonstrate that Dominion Reality is semi-permeable—its constants are strong but not absolute.
Each intrusion reaffirms that the Verse Field Framework is a negotiated boundary, not an immutable wall.
Exploitation potential remains under study: controlled micro-breaches could yield cross-verse energy extraction—but at existential risk.
