# (2250) Animal uplift programs begin

## Canonical Event

Animal-uplift programs produce new persons among elephants, distinct great-ape lines, ravens and other corvids, octopuses, squids, and additional successful species.

These peoples develop independent cultures and settlements:

- Elephant Memory Houses;
- separate chimpanzee, bonobo, gorilla, and orangutan Canopy Compacts;
- Raven Moots;
- cephalopod Littoral Assemblies;
- mixed human-uplift communities.

Some cooperate with humanity. Some limit contact. Some reject human settlement entirely.

Created persons cannot be owned by laboratories, families, corporations, or governments. Full constitutional recognition is codified by the Terran Federation in 2601.

See: [[Uplift Charter & Legal Timeline]]
