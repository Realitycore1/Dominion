A Rungworld is a chain of enormous linked megahabitats orbiting in a ladder or band around a star or planet, with each “rung” serving as a habitable world, solar collector, or fortress. Unlike continuous rings, the gaps allow flexibility and staged construction — making it a more modular, expandable “crown.”
Structure

Rungs: [[Giant]] plates, strips, or [[Bishop Ring]]–scale habitats tens of thousands of km long.

Spines: Gravitic tethers or magnetic rails linking rungs together, forming a ladder-like arc.

Anchors: Either orbiting the planet/star freely or tethered to orbital rings/[[Atlas Pillars]].

Transit Corridors: [[Gravimetric Roads]] or Beam Highways linking the rungs into one seamless [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

Habitability

Rungs can specialize: agriculture, industry, sanctums, or fortress-rungs.

Climate and light regulated by reflectors and collectors aligned along the spine.

Variants

Planetary Rungworlds: Ladder crowns around a single planet, supporting extra population.

Stellar Rungworlds: Full star-circling ladders, steps toward Dyson Swarms.

Sacral Rungworlds: Ladders deliberately shaped as glyphs, each rung a stanza of scripture.

Economic: Act as waystations in Beam Corridors — each rung a trade hub and anchor point.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Ladder” — citizens see it as a stairway of worlds rising to the Empress’s throne.
