Type: Private [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] & [[Infrastructure]] Contractor
Founded: 2029, Denver Free Economic Zone
Motto: “Order is its own mercy.”

Origins

Aegis began as a coalition of logistics-defense firms hired to protect American and Eurasian supply [[Chains]] during the first water and energy rationing waves.
They built automated convoy escorts, drone [[Sentinels]], and modular base [[Systems]] for conflict zones.
When the Directorate formed, Aegis won contracts to operate “peacekeeping logistics”—effectively merging the roles of army, police, and shipping company.

Structure
	•	Divisions: Orbital Security, Humanitarian Response, Ground Operations, Cognitive Warfare.
	•	Manpower: 2.3 million active contractors ([[Human]]), 6.1 million autonomous units.
	•	Leadership: Board of 9 directors, each representing a major bloc client; chair rotated annually by Directorate mandate.

Influence
	•	Owns 40% of global drone defense networks.
	•	Controls access to most arid-region desalination facilities, using “security maintenance” as leverage.
	•	Operates under legal immunity granted by the Directorate’s Crisis Mediation Authority—meaning they can wage undeclared wars.

Reputation

Feared but respected; their insignia (a white shield split by a black sunburst) becomes shorthand for “stability at gunpoint.”
Populists call them the Janitors of Empire—they clean up messes by burning the room.
