The Integral War Machine

The [[Estela Integralism]] colony treats war not as necessity but as destiny. Expansion, conquest, and spectacle of force are written into their ideology. Their armies and fleets are as much propaganda as they are weapons.
	•	The People as [[Soldiers]]
	•	Every citizen is considered part of the army. Conscription is universal; even factory [[Workers]] are regarded as [[Soldiers]] in uniformed labor battalions.
	•	Military salutes, chants, and drills bleed into civilian life—citizens march to work in parade formation.
	•	Structure of [[Arms]]
	•	Integral Guard: the professional standing army, organized into armored divisions and orbital infantry.
	•	Stellar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]]: elite units sworn directly to the Caudillo, indoctrinated to fanatical loyalty.
	•	Youth Brigades: teenage conscripts deployed as auxiliaries, often in dangerous shock roles.
	•	[[Worker]] Battalions: labor units that build fortresses, mine asteroids, and march in parades; treated as military corps in their own right.
	•	Naval [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	The Fleet of Destiny is vast but theatrically designed: warships are angular, emblazoned with black suns and comets, meant to terrify as much as fight.
	•	Naval [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] emphasizes overwhelming frontal assault—fleet charges accompanied by propaganda broadcasts, drowning enemy comms in chants of loyalty.
	•	Captains are expected to die with their ships; retreat without permission is punishable by execution.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of War
	•	Expansionism: conquest of new [[Systems]] is framed as fulfilling the Integral destiny.
	•	Total Mobilization: every citizen, building, and machine must serve the war effort.
	•	Purity of Blood and Steel: military campaigns are ritualized as purification acts, enemies labeled “parasites” or “degenerates.”
	•	Symbols of Power
	•	The Black Sun Standard: flown from every warship and fortress, its rays depicted as [[Chains]] binding the galaxy.
	•	Victory Parades: after campaigns, entire cities march in militarized celebration, with captured enemies displayed as living trophies.
	•	Oath of the Legion: all [[Soldiers]] recite a pledge of loyalty directly to the Caudillo before every deployment.
	•	Internal Enforcement
	•	The military doubles as secret police. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]] of Purity root out dissent and purge “disloyal elements.”
	•	Ash Camps are guarded by military units, ensuring fear is part of [[Dominion Omniverse/Dominion Multiverse/Eos Verse/Dominion Verse/Verse Structure/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]].
