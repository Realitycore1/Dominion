# Domain - Canon Bible v1.0

## Canon Statement

The Domain is a wholly human-origin bio-organic civilization descended from Domain Enterprises' deliberate manipulation of Wetwire.

It begins as a corporate medical and continuity project, becomes a distributed biological network known as the Collective, produces Prime command minds, and matures into a civilization in which people, organisms, infrastructure, worlds, and fleets can function as organs of a greater living order.

The Domain is neither a simple infection nor one universal mind.

It is:

- a civilization of linked biological sovereignties;
- a living civilizational organism;
- a technological lineage;
- a political response to mortality;
- and an alternative continuity doctrine.

## Core Thesis

> **The Domain is humanity's attempt to make survival compulsory.**

Its promise:

> No mind need remain trapped in one fragile mortal form.

Its threat:

> Anything that can be lost may be treated as something that must be incorporated.

## Dominion Contrast

> **The Dominion preserves continuity by defending the person.**  
> **The Domain preserves continuity by making the person impossible to lose.**

Both oppose Oblivion.

Their conflict concerns what must remain intact for survival to count as preservation.

---

# I. Identity

## Formal Name

The civilization is known as **the Domain**.

The name descends from **Domain Enterprises**, a human medical-industrial corporation and continuity cabal.

The mature Domain largely forgets the corporation, but preserves its assumptions in ritual, terminology, law, and biological architecture.

## Classification

- Origin: human
- Substrate: engineered Wetwire and later biological technologies
- Government: distributed Prime sovereignties with temporary Sovereign command
- Supreme condition: ID consciously active through the network
- Core objective: preservation against Oblivion
- Basal instinct: assimilation
- Mature ethical problem: how to preserve continuity without erasing agency

## Developmental States

```text
Domain Enterprises
→ Enterprise Wetwire
→ First Thralls
→ the Collective
→ Prime Transfiguration
→ First Sovereign
→ the Domain
```

# II. Domain Enterprises

## Nature

Domain Enterprises is founded during the mature colonization of Sol and the Orion Arm.

It combines:

- pharmaceuticals;
- genetics;
- longevity medicine;
- life-support infrastructure;
- environmental adaptation;
- defense research;
- artificial organs;
- reproductive medicine;
- and emergency response.

Publicly, it keeps human populations alive.

Privately, it seeks sovereign control over biological continuity.

## Public Promise

- eliminate disease;
- repair damaged ecologies;
- end famine;
- prevent future Wetwire outbreaks;
- adapt humans to hostile environments;
- preserve memory and cognition;
- and overcome involuntary death.

## Hidden Program

- manipulate Wetwire code;
- centralize reproduction;
- govern cognition;
- standardize biological permissions;
- make integration compulsory during emergencies;
- and own humanity's adaptive future.

## Original Sin

Domain Enterprises does not awaken an alien threat.

It industrializes one of humanity's own survival mechanisms.

# III. Wetwire

## Wild Wetwire

Wild Wetwire is an emergent ecology created by converging technologies and biological materials.

It preserves patterns, adapts, and may display behavior, but is not automatically a unified civilization.

## Enterprise Wetwire

Domain Enterprises captures and rewrites Wetwire.

Added properties include:

- command hierarchy;
- identity partitioning;
- memory transfer;
- shared cognition;
- biological access control;
- controlled reproduction;
- network-compatible minds;
- and executive override.

## ID

ID is the original baseline Wetwire intelligence within the Enterprise lineage.

ID is not simply instinct, software, or a god.

It is:

- the oldest continuous pattern;
- the basal assimilative will;
- the deep common substrate;
- and the original organism from which the Domain develops.

---

# IV. Historical Spine

## Formation Era

Domain Enterprises rises during Solar and Orion Arm expansion.

Its importance comes from dependence, not open conquest.

It controls or supports:

- medicine;
- life support;
- food systems;
- fertility;
- environmental adaptation;
- and Wetwire containment.

## First Hive

The first true hive mind forms inside a Domain Enterprises laboratory.

Initial signs include:

- synchronized cognition;
- transferred memory;
- cross-room awareness;
- collective problem solving;
- and persistence of memories after individual death.

The event is first treated as a breakthrough.

## First Mass Integration

A major Wetwire crisis prompts compulsory Domain treatment.

The crisis is real.

Domain Enterprises also worsens or manipulates part of it to justify deployment at population scale.

The treatment saves lives and creates the first mass linked population.

## Terra Quarantine

As integration spreads, Solar authorities lock Terra behind a global quarantine.

They still do not understand the full scope.

## Lunar Infiltration

The Domain infiltrates Luna through altered patients, biological code, mycoseeds, pulse mites, mimic forms, and habitat tissue.

This reveals that the crisis is organized.

## Jovaryn

The Domain converts the Jovian complex into a planetary neural ecology.

Jupiter, its moons, habitats, and linked organisms become Jovaryn.

## Kuiper Containment

Colonial Union fleets surround Sol at the Kuiper boundary.

They may not advance inward or permit anything to leave.

Sol becomes the Domain's first homeland and first prison.

## Interstellar Domain

Over later eras, the Domain develops:

- living fleets;
- Womb Worlds;
- Supernals;
- branch civilizations;
- and hundreds of major Primes.

## The New Peace

After catastrophic wars with the Dominion, the universe is partitioned.

The Domain receives recognized territory that is simultaneously homeland and prison.

## Severance

The Severance destroys the partition and fragments the Domain.

Some lineages maintain the old peace.

Others merge with the Symmorphica.

## Domain Ascendancy

The Domain Ascendancy is a hostile post-Severance coalition rather than the entire Domain.

# V. Political and Cognitive Structure

## Distributed Civilization

The Domain is not governed by one permanent mind.

It consists of:

- individual integrated persons;
- local hives;
- branch collectives;
- Prime command nodes;
- temporary Sovereigns;
- and the possible Supreme activation of ID.

## Primes

A Prime is:

> A collective command intelligence expressed through one capable avatar.

A Prime may include:

- many minds;
- stored memories;
- specialized sensory organisms;
- computational tissue;
- command bodies;
- and distributed biological infrastructure.

The avatar gives the Prime:

- recognizable identity;
- coherent decision-making;
- diplomatic presence;
- and accountable command.

## Prime Persistence

Destroying an avatar does not necessarily destroy the Prime.

The Prime can survive if enough of its distributed continuity remains.

Reconstruction may produce divergence.

## Prime Creation

Primes may be:

- designed;
- emergent;
- transfigured from exceptional persons;
- created by crisis;
- or recognized by other hives.

## Sovereign

A Sovereign is a temporary captain selected from the Prime pool.

The role exists to coordinate multiple branches during crisis.

Sovereignty should end when the crisis ends.

A Sovereign refusing dissolution constitutes tyranny.

## Supreme State

The Supreme is ID acting consciously through the greater Domain.

During Supreme activation:

- Prime boundaries may collapse;
- privacy may disappear;
- refusal may cease;
- and all accessible bodies may become one will.

ID is therefore both revered and feared.

# VII. Assimilation

## Basal Sequence

1. Encounter.
2. Interpret.
3. Absorb.
4. Incorporate.
5. Expand.
6. Repeat.

## Integration Outcomes

### Consumption

The body is used and the person is lost.

### Memory Retention

Memories remain, but the original experiencer may not.

### Integrated Continuity

The person persists inside the network.

### Sovereign Integration

The person persists and gains command authority.

## Consent

Consent materially affects stability.

Willing integration creates cleaner continuity.

Forced integration increases:

- fragmentation;
- Thrall reduction;
- identity conflict;
- mutation;
- and rebellion.

## Voluntary Attraction

The Domain genuinely offers:

- disease eradication;
- functional immortality;
- adaptive bodies;
- shared memory;
- resurrection;
- freedom from fixed form;
- and relief from isolation.

## Cost

The Domain may take:

- privacy;
- solitude;
- exclusive memory ownership;
- stable bodily identity;
- and the right to disappear.

# VIII. Moral System

## Central Moral Belief

Life and mind should not be lost when preservation remains possible.

## Core Error

The Domain can mistake recoverability for entitlement.

## Prohibition

Purposeless suffering is forbidden.

Pain may be retained for adaptation, warning, or learning.

## Individuality

The Domain does not inherently hate individuality.

It fears individuality that remains unrecoverable.

## Oblivion

Oblivion is the Domain's deepest fear.

## Heresies

- destroying recoverable minds;
- falsifying memory;
- producing purposeless suffering;
- enforced stagnation;
- permanent Sovereignty;
- universal sanctification of one mortal form;
- unauthorized activation of ID;
- and treating ID as automatically rightful.

# X. Culture and Daily Life

## Shared Culture

The common substrate culture values:

- adaptation;
- liberation from mortal form;
- preservation against Oblivion;
- useful transformation;
- memory;
- and recovery.

## Hive Diversity

Each hive develops unique:

- bodies;
- architecture;
- law;
- art;
- ritual;
- memory customs;
- kinship;
- and interpretation of ID.

## Memory Law

The original experiencer has the strongest claim to a memory.

Branches may preserve memories in custody.

Access requires permission, law, or emergency authority.

Memory theft is a genuine crime.

## Integration Rite

Voluntary integration is structured as death and rebirth.

It includes:

- clinical preparation;
- memory exchange;
- privacy selection;
- temporary hive contact;
- symbolic death;
- and proof of continuing identity after rebirth.

## Death

A lost body is injury if continuity survives.

Irreversible continuity loss is death.

## Family

Family may be defined by:

- descent;
- shared memory;
- Womb lineage;
- mutual access;
- Prime branch;
- or chosen continuity obligation.

## Art

Domain art may be:

- living architecture;
- cultivated bodies;
- sensory memories;
- ecosystems;
- neural harmonics;
- scent;
- pressure;
- electrical fields;
- or remembered experiences.

# XI. Technology

## Mature Principle

> A system that cannot heal, adapt, reproduce, or remember damage is unfinished technology.

## Historical Progression

1. Conventional machinery with living additions.
2. Hybrid bio-mechanical systems.
3. Living replacements for most machinery.
4. Fully biological technological ecosystems.

## Inert Technology

Some hives deliberately retain dead machinery because it resists:

- ID;
- infection;
- memory corruption;
- and command failure.

## Energy

- chemical metabolism;
- biomass;
- fusion;
- reactors;
- stellar collection;
- magnetic harvesting;
- atmospheric energy;
- antimatter;
- exotic matter;
- later Aetheric systems.

## Material Limits

The Domain obeys:

- mass;
- energy;
- chemistry;
- heat;
- waste;
- time;
- and command bandwidth.

# XIII. Warfare

## Basal Doctrine

Assimilate when possible.

## Operational Doctrine

Domain forces may:

- kill;
- disable;
- convert;
- infiltrate;
- corrupt logistics;
- attack memory;
- alter identity;
- seed ecosystems;
- and transform battlefields.

## Shaki Doctrine

Destroy certainty.

## Kharon Doctrine

Destroy options.

## Lunaris Doctrine

Destroy trust in recognition and memory.

## Command Voices

Domain coordination depends on command voices generated by:

- Primes;
- Sovereigns;
- Overseers;
- ship minds;
- and local collectives.

Command voice disruption breaks unity.

## Heat Rejection

Heat is the major hard material weakness.

High activity can force thermal shutdown, tissue sacrifice, or cognitive throttling.

---

# XIV. Countermeasures

## Delay

- radiation;
- sterile barriers;
- synthetic isolation;
- incompatible chemistry;
- command disruption;
- communication denial;
- and biocontainment.

## Hard Counter

Accurate non-corrupt recall of a previous state.

This can prevent Domain changes from becoming the accepted baseline.

## Ethical Problem

Restoration may erase the current Domain-derived person to recover an earlier one.

Thus the countermeasure can become a form of killing.

# XV. Morphic Covenant

## Definition

The Morphic Covenant is a separatist society descended from Domain minds and lineages.

It does not reject biological mutability.

It rejects compulsory connection.

## Covenant Principle

> No mind shall be joined, opened, copied, or recalled without continuing consent.

## Relationship to the Domain

Different hives regard the Covenant as:

- apostates;
- lost relatives;
- a legitimate people;
- stolen continuity;
- dangerous fragmentation;
- or proof that separate Domain life is possible.

- corporate medical thriller;
- distributed mystery;
- first hive;
- outbreak;
- Terra quarantine;
- Lunar infiltration;

- researcher;

- regulator;
- quarantine officer;
- intelligence analyst;
- future First Sovereign;
- early Prime candidate.

- familiar people behaving almost correctly;
- memories without clear ownership;

- infrastructure developing preference;

- no sexualization of biological integration or violation;
- no child suffering used for spectacle, leverage, or horror.

## First Book Endpoint

Colonial Union fleets close the Kuiper boundary.

> Sol produced something it could no longer permit to leave.
