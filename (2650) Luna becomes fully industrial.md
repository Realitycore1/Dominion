# (2650) Luna becomes fully industrial

## Canonical Event

Luna becomes an industrial world before it becomes a colony.

By 2650, automated mines, regolith processing, vacuum manufacturing, polar extraction, mass drivers, and rotating human crews support the Terran economy.

Workers remain legally and domestically based on Earth or orbital contract habitats. Luna does not yet possess permanent families, local schools, independent civil records, burial law, or self-government.

This distinction becomes central to later Lunar identity: the world was worked for centuries before its workers were allowed to call it home.

See: [[Sol Second Space Race and Phaeton Program]]
