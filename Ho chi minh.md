13 Ho Chi Minh, also known as Saigon, is the largest city in Vietnam and the former capital of South Vietnam. It is located in the southwestern region of the country on the banks of the Saigon River. Ho Chi Minh City has a population of around 9 million people, making it one of the largest metropolitan areas in Southeast Asia. The city is a bustling economic and cultural hub with a thriving manufacturing, trading, and tourism sector. Ho Chi Minh City has a vibrant cultural heritage, with its French colonial past evident in the city's architecture and cuisine. The city's cultural scene includes museums, parks, [[Temples]], and gardens, with Notre Dame Cathedral and Ben Thanh Market being some of its most famous landmarks. The city's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is diverse, with significant contributions from services, manufacturing, tourism, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]]. Ho Chi Minh City is a major center for manufacturing, particularly garments and electronics. The city also has a booming software development sector, with numerous companies based in the city's tech hubs. Ho Chi Minh City is known for its hot and humid climate with heavy rainfall throughout the year. The city has several major rivers and canals that contribute to its transportation [[Infrastructure]]. The city is also well connected by air and land to other cities in Vietnam and neighboring countries like Cambodia and Thailand.
