Localized minima in the density of Planck-scale micro-topology (the ER-foam above the [[Verse]] field). In troughs, vacuum fluctuations are quieter/sparser, changing how phase, light, and rites propagate. In [[MOT]] dials: η↓ (noise), ℓᶜ↑ (coherence length), Ζₑ↓ (impedance)—until the foam gets too sparse to anchor microthreads.

⸻

Why they happen
	•	[[Natural]]: long void sightlines, destructive interference of vacuum Modes, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Doctrine/Relics/Relics]]s/Relics]] of earlier [[Verse]] collisions, GW “polishing” after strong transients.
	•	Engineered: quietseeds ([[Domain]] dampers), tunable Casimir lattices, or wayhouse sanctification that bleeds high-k Modes.

⸻

How they look (signatures)
	•	Metrology: atom-interferometer phase noise drops; clock Allan deviation improves; Lamb-shift/Casimir drifts trend low.
	•	Propagation: lower scintillation/jitter; weak-lensing–like de-speckling; PFN error rates fall at same SNR.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]] dials: η maps show cool patches; ℓᶜ is elongated along the trough axis; Ζₑ dips gently.
	•	GW & RF: reduced small-angle scattering; polarization holds longer.

⸻

Benefits vs risks

Good (within bounds):
	•	Horizon-Arc gates run with less shear; window jitter drops.
	•	NLPG/NLPC efficiency ↑ (less entropy lost to phase noise).
	•	[[Choirs]]/threads: lower memetic over-bloom; acausal loop debt closes cleaner.

Bad (if too deep or [[Wide]]):
	•	Anchor scarcity: microthreads thin → window pinch-off / knotting.
	•	Return imbalance: Σ budgeting goes off (under-radiation during ops, overpay later).
	•	Sanctum fragility: GEZ budgets fluctuate; backfire clauses trigger more easily on edges.

⸻

Detection & mapping (toolkit)
	•	Aetherometers: joint Φ/η/ℓᶜ survey; look for long ℓᶜ ridges.
	•	Casimir stacks: drift sensors as passive trough meters.
	•	Atom interferometers / optical clocks: rolling baselines for phase noise floors.
	•	PFN telemetry: corridor error spectra; burst tests under permit.

⸻

Ops & governance
	•	Seat gates on the shoulders (moderate η↓, strong ℓᶜ↑), not at the deepest floor.
	•	Window Proof Sheet required: publish b_arc, jitter, and spool/cool in trough and out.
	•	Throttle Ω_c during storms; never co-locate multiple sancta at a single deep minimum.
	•	RAC licenses; DVAA audits quietseed use; Harmonic posts public “[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]] weather.”

⸻

KPIs

Jitter (ps) across window • PFN BER at fixed SNR • ℓᶜ ridge length/width • Anchor failure rate • Σ (heat) reconciliation after ops • Choir M-load vs exit-[[Key]] usage.
