10.6 Tehran, the capital city of [[Iran]], is a bustling metropolis with a rich [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and [[culture]] that dates back to the Achaemenid Empire. The city is located in a mountainous region, surrounded by the snow-capped Elburz Mountains to the north and the Alborz Mountains to the west. This [[Natural]] barrier has helped protect the city from invasions and attacks throughout its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. The streets of Tehran are filled with a mix of modern and traditional architecture, reflecting the city's long and varied [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. The city's central area, known as the Grand Bazaar, is home to hundreds of shops and markets selling everything from spices to textiles. The city's centerpiece is the Shahyad Tower, which was constructed during the Qajar dynasty in the 19th century. The tower is a popular tourist attraction and offers stunning views of the city from its observation deck. Tehran's mosques and religious buildings are also significant landmarks, with the Masjid-e Shah, or Holy Shrine of the Prophet Hazrat Ali, being one of the most important sites for Shia Muslims. In recent years, Tehran has undergone significant economic growth and development, with new skyscrapers and [[Infrastructure]] projects transforming the cityscape. However, political tensions and sanctions imposed by the United States have made it difficult for [[Iran]] to fully realize its potential. Despite these challenges, Tehran remains a vibrant and dynamic city that continues to draw visitors from all over the world.
