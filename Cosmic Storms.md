I. Definition

A cosmic storm is a large-scale astrophysical disturbance that propagates through space, driven by extreme energy release, spacetime distortions, or [[Exotic Matter]] interactions. These storms can be purely physical, energy-based, or meta-ontological (affecting the underlying [[Verse]] Field). They often span light-years to galactic scales and can persist for millennia or longer.
