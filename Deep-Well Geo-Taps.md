## Summary

Atlas Pilla
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Deep-Well Geo-Taps are planetary-scale geothermal extraction [[Systems]], bore-shafts that plunge tens to hundreds of kilometers into the mantle, tapping magma, pressure, and gravitational stress to generate near-limitless power.
They are equal parts energy plants, fortress-roots, and shrines to the Empress’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] beneath the [[Earth (Terra)]].
Structure

Mantle Shafts: Vast, reinforced conduits bored into planetary crust with strange-matter drills and gravity-stabilized walls.

Pressure Vaults: Contain superheated steam, magma, or exotic plasmas for controlled release.

Energy Harvesting

Supercritical Turbines: Driven by mantle heat to generate planetary-scale electricity.

Seismic Resonance: Vibrations tuned into energy fields, sometimes even modulating planetary tectonics.

Safety & Control

Cooling Towers: Chimney-like spires vent excess heat to the atmosphere or orbit.

Failsafes: Can deliberately collapse shafts to seal runaway magma events.

MRO Ecologies: Autonomous repair swarms crawl the shafts, reinforcing constantly.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Geo-Taps supply cities and arcologies with inexhaustible power.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Used as planet-killer engines — an overdriven tap can trigger controlled volcanic eruptions or planetary quakes.

Religious: Treated as “wells into the world-soul.” Priests oversee their operation, reciting litanies as if drawing from sacred [[Fire]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Massive shafts rimmed with rib-arches descending into abyssal red glow.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the Geo-Tap’s surface facility looks like a stark black circle etched into continents.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Internal shaft walls ripple with refractive lattice, redirecting magma flows like flowing stained glass.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Shafts inscribed with endless spiraling glyphs, glowing brighter the deeper they go.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Life-giving — cities powered by the eternal heart below.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A reminder that even the deepest fires of creation belong to the Empress.

To myth: Called “the Wells of Her Hands”, where the Empress grips the roots of worlds themselves.

Concordia Conversion: Magma heat is harmonized into plasma flows usable as weaponry or beam-power sources.

Economic: Extract rare minerals and isotopes directly from mantle flows.

To stand above one is to look into the open throat of a world, heat shimmering upward like divine breath.
