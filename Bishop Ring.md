A Bishop Ring is a rotating orbital megastructure ~1,000 km in diameter (and ~500 km [[Wide]]), generating gravity through spin. It’s essentially a habitat ring the size of a continent — a manageable cousin to the vast Banks Orbital.
Scale & Habitability

Circumference: ~[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]],000 km, with ~[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]],000,000 km² of usable surface area.

Supports tens of billions of people.

Construction

Built from asteroid belts or planetary crust mass.

Reinforced with carbon nanotube megacomposites, strange-matter lattices, or [[Computanium]] cores in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] versions.

Actively stabilized by Atlas Pillar anchors and MRO ecologies.

Environment

Habitable interior surface includes oceans, forests, and cities stretched along a band of perpetual horizon.

Sunlight provided by orbital mirrors or beam redirection.
