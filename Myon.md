I. Physical Definition

The Myon in the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework) is not simply a heavier cousin of the electron, as in real-world particle [[Physics]]’ muon. Instead, it is an engineered or naturally enhanced leptonic state designed to act as a transitional carrier between baryonic, exotic, and [[Aetheric]] [[Domains]].

It retains baseline leptonic properties — spin-½, lepton number conservation — but possesses an intrinsic coupling channel to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]-Lattice Field (ALF). This grants it limited interaction with non-baryonic quanta such as Aetherions, Phaseons, and Phantomons.
	•	Spin: ½ (fermion).
	•	Mass: Variable; default ~200× electron mass in physical-only [[Domains]], but tunable via [[Aetheric]] resonance in [[VFF]] [[Systems]].
	•	Charge: –1 EM charge; carries [[Aetheric]] Polarization Index (API) as a secondary coupling metric.
	•	Interaction: EM, weak nuclear, gravitational, and partial [[Aetheric]] coupling.

In the Master Omniversal Theory, Myons occur in axiom-roots where standard leptons require an adaptive bridge to interact with non-physical matter:
