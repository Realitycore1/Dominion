Classification

Psychopomps / Terminal Enforcers / Soul-Transition Entities

⸻

CORE IDEA

Reapers are death with a schedule.

They are not killers in the mortal sense and not judges of morality. A Reaper appears when the conditions of ending are satisfied. Their presence means the process has advanced past prevention and entered execution and transfer.

They don’t decide who dies.
They ensure death completes correctly.

⸻

ORIGIN

Reapers emerge from systemic needs in the metaphysical ecosystem:
	•	to prevent soul backlog and echo accumulation
	•	to stabilize transitions between life, death, and afterstates
	•	to enforce boundaries between realms
	•	to prevent Death Echoes, rogue undead, or soul fractures

Some Reapers are former beings elevated into the role.
Others are purpose-built inevitabilities.

They are not summoned.
They are dispatched.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Reapers adopt forms optimized for recognition and authority.

Common traits:
	•	Tall, cloaked or skeletal silhouettes
	•	Faces hidden, absent, or symbolic (skulls, masks, voids)
	•	Implements of function: scythes, keys, ledgers, chains, hourglasses
	•	Presence that drains color, sound, or warmth
	•	Shadows that move independently

Their form is chosen so the dying understand what is happening.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Reapers are calm, detached, and precise.
	•	Speak rarely and plainly
	•	Ignore bargaining, threats, or pleas
	•	Do not hurry or linger
	•	Appear exactly when needed

They are not cruel.
They are complete.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Extremely high, but constrained.
	•	Perfect awareness of assigned targets
	•	No curiosity outside mandate
	•	Immune to emotional manipulation
	•	Capable of conversation, but not debate

A Reaper can explain your death clearly—
but never change it.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Terminal Authority: finalizes death-state transitions
	•	Grace-Compatible: angels recognize but do not outrank them
	•	Malice-Resistant: corruption cannot redirect function
	•	Soul Containment: prevents fragmentation or possession
	•	Boundary Enforcement: seals thresholds after passage

Attacking a Reaper damages your own metaphysical integrity more than theirs.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Appear singly
	•	Do not linger after task completion
	•	Leave no corruption or residue
	•	Absence results in hauntings, echoes, and undead surges

Where Reapers operate correctly, death is quiet.

⸻

ALPHA REAPERS

Alpha Reapers are known as High Reapers, Archivists, or Conductors.

Traits:
	•	Oversee regional or categorical death flows
	•	Can reassign lesser psychopomps
	•	Appear during plagues, wars, or mass-death events
	•	May manifest with multiple implements or forms

High Reapers do not collect individuals.
They process epochs.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Absolute (Targeted) / Neutral otherwise

If a Reaper is for you, resistance is irrelevant.
If it is not, you may not even perceive it.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Reapers represent:
	•	death without malice
	•	order without mercy
	•	inevitability without drama
	•	systems beyond morality

Where Death Omens warn,
where Dullahans announce,
where Death Echoes fail—

Reapers close the loop.
