Classification: Pre-Mythic → Foundational Entities
Subtype: Ontological Progenitors / Law-Seeding Forces
Status: Dormant, Fragmented, or Bound Beyond Comprehension

⸻

Core Description

Primordials are entities that predate planes, gods, and defined law. They are not creatures in the conventional sense, but acts of existence that never fully resolved into concepts.

Where gods embody rule, and Titans embody force, Primordials embody conditions.

They are:
	•	not born,
	•	not created,
	•	not summoned.

They are what reality crystallized around.

Their forms—when perceivable at all—are vast, abstract, and contradictory:
	•	continents that breathe,
	•	stars that bleed memory,
	•	oceans that remember being thought.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Primordials originate before planar differentiation.

They existed when:
	•	Grace and Malice were undivided,
	•	Elements were not discrete,
	•	Identity was optional.

They are tied to no single plane, but resonate faintly with all of them.
	•	Plane Interaction: Universal (Passive)
	•	Realm Echoes: Bleeding Wilds, Chasms of Negation, R’lyeh-adjacent zones
	•	Compatibility: Extremely low — most verses cannot sustain their presence

A Primordial entering a verse does not invade.
The verse fails around it.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Primordial cognition is non-anthropomorphic.

They do not think in:
	•	morality,
	•	intent,
	•	or narrative.

They operate through:
	•	persistence,
	•	pressure,
	•	repetition,
	•	and inevitability.

Some appear hostile.
Some appear benevolent.
Both interpretations are errors of scale.

A Primordial does not want anything from mortals.

It does not know they exist.

⸻

Behavior & Influence

Primordials rarely act directly.

Instead, they influence reality through:
	•	fragments,
	•	residues,
	•	progeny,
	•	corrupted derivatives.

Many Titans, Leviathans, and Colossi are:
	•	distant descendants,
	•	stabilized offshoots,
	•	or failed attempts to contain Primordial essence.

Some planes themselves may be:
	•	constructed atop Primordial remains,
	•	or shaped to suppress them.

⸻

Known Types (Conceptual, Not Taxonomic)

Primordials are categorized by effect, not species:
	•	Foundational Primordials – Seeded existence (time, matter, continuity)
	•	Devourer Primordials – Consume structure and meaning
	•	Shaper Primordials – Warp form, identity, and causality
	•	Still Primordials – Arrest motion, entropy, or time
	•	Echo Primordials – Repeat realities endlessly

These are scholarly labels, not true distinctions.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Direct interaction is nearly impossible.

Mortals encounter Primordials through:
	•	dreams,
	•	relics,
	•	corrupted ecosystems,
	•	impossible geometry,
	•	inherited curses.

Those who survive contact often become:
	•	prophets,
	•	monsters,
	•	or foundational myths.

Most go mad—not from fear, but from ontological overload.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints

Primordials cannot be “killed” in any meaningful sense.

However, they can be:
	•	fragmented,
	•	sealed,
	•	displaced,
	•	or buried beneath layers of law.

Planes, realms, and gods may exist specifically to keep Primordials inert.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Gods: Latecomers; often usurpers or caretakers
	•	Titans: Stabilized echoes of Primordial force
	•	Elemental Colossi: Failed containment events
	•	Leviathans: Biological expressions of Primordial excess
	•	Shoggoths: Unfinished Primordial biology

Some demons and angels may unknowingly channel Primordial residues.
