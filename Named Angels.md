•	Ulyandian, [[Angel]] of Zeal – patron of relentless fervor, inspires holy armies.
	•	Ainelayre, [[Angel]] of Confidence – grants courage and assurance in the face of despair.
	•	Ulaena Azaran, [[Angel]] of Purity and Freedom – symbol of liberation, dissolves corruption.
	•	Michael – archangel of war and protection, “Who is like God?”
	•	Gabriel – herald and messenger of revelation.
	•	Raphael – healer and guide, companion of Tobias in apocrypha.
	•	Uriel – [[Angel]] of wisdom, flame of God, often guiding with divine light.
	•	Metatron – celestial scribe, sometimes seen as Enoch transformed.
	•	Sandalphon – twin of Metatron, associated with prayers and song.
	•	Azrael – [[Angel]] of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], compassionate psychopomp.
	•	Sariel – [[Angel]] of judgment and guidance ([[Book of Enoch]]).
	•	Raguel – [[Angel]] of harmony, justice, and vengeance on corruption.
	•	Remiel – [[Angel]] of hope and resurrection.
	•	Abaddon/Apollyon – “[[Angel]] of the [[Abyss]],” destroyer.
	•	Israfel (Islamic) – [[Angel]] of the trumpet, herald of the Last Day.
	•	Malahidael (folk tradition) – [[Angel]] of courage and serenity.
