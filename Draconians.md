1. Common Draconians
	•	Definition: The baseline—humanoids with draconic traits, often servants or descendants of true dragons.
	•	Traits: Scales, claws, reptilian eyes, partial wings, breath affinity.
	•	Symbolism: The bridge between [[Man]] and [[Dragon]]; diluted majesty.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Dragonspawn
	•	Definition: Mortals corrupted or transformed by [[Dragon]] blood or magic.
	•	Traits: Patchy scales, uncontrollable rages, unstable forms.
	•	Symbolism: The danger of mingling mortal and draconic essence.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Noble Draconians
	•	Definition: Stable dragonborn, proud [[Warrior]] or sorcerer lineages.
	•	Traits: Fully scaled, disciplined, loyal to [[Dragon]] clans.
	•	Symbolism: Honor-bound hybrids; living echoes of draconic nobility.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Winged Draconians
	•	Definition: Draconians with fully functional wings, often ruling their kin as elites.
	•	Traits: Flight, aerial combat superiority.
	•	Symbolism: Mortal envy of true [[Dragon]] majesty, made flesh.

⸻

5. Elemental Draconians
	•	Definition: Draconians whose forms reflect [[Dragon]] elements ([[Fire]], frost, lightning, acid, shadow).
	•	Traits: Elementally aligned scales, breath weapons.
	•	Symbolism: Mortals fused with nature’s primal forces.

⸻

6. Cursed Draconians
	•	Definition: [[Soldiers]] warped by draconic rituals, often unstable or doomed.
	•	Traits: Explode, petrify, or release curses upon [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] (classic Dragonlance style).
	•	Symbolism: Power as a curse, the expendable [[Soldier]] of [[Dragon]] cults.

⸻

7. Shadow Draconians
	•	Definition: Corrupted dragonkin tied to the [[Abyss]] or void.
	•	Traits: Blackened scales, ghostly wings, necrotic breath.
	•	Symbolism: The fallen majesty of dragonkind; draconic [[Hunger]] given to darkness.

⸻

8. Astral Draconians
	•	Definition: Rare hybrids attuned to cosmic or celestial dragons.
	•	Traits: Scales that shimmer like [[Stars]], psionic affinity, dream-walkers.
	•	Symbolism: Mortals reaching beyond the world, borne on [[Dragon]] wings.

⸻

9. Warforged Draconians
	•	Definition: Artificially bred or alchemically forged dragonkin.
	•	Traits: Hardened scales like armor, mechanical breath, disciplined [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]].
	•	Symbolism: Dragons turned into weapons of war through mortal ingenuity.

⸻

10. Ascendant Draconians (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary draconians who evolve toward becoming true dragons.
	•	Traits: Gradual growth in size, wingspan, and intellect; near-immortal.
	•	Symbolism: The mortal path to dragonhood.
