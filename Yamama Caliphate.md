If the Middle East were under control of a Caliphate, it would be governed by Islamic [[law]] (Sharia) and Islamic theology, with the Caliph acting as [[The Supreme]] ruler of all Muslims. The Caliphate would be a hierarchical structure, with the Caliph at the top, followed by regional governors and local [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]]. The Caliph would be responsible for maintaining order and stability in the region, enforcing Sharia [[law]], and expanding the reach of Islamic values and beliefs. The Caliphate would likely have a strong [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] force, with troops dedicated to protecting the region from outside threats and imposing Islamic values on non-Muslim populations. This could lead to conflict with neighboring states and countries, as well as with religious and ethnic minorities within the region. Economically, the Caliphate would be focused on promoting Islamic values and principles, such as charity, generosity, and redistribution of wealth. This could lead to changes in the structure of economic [[Systems]], with Islamic banking and trade practices becoming more prevalent. In terms of social issues, the Caliphate would likely enforce strict rules and regulations related to family, marriage, and sexuality, as well as clothing and other cultural practices. Women would likely be restricted in their roles and opportunities, and there could be greater enforcement of violence against non-Muslims and those who deviate from Islamic teachings. Overall, a Caliphate in the Middle East would represent a return to an Islamic past and a departure from secular or Western influences, creating a more conservative and restrictive social and political order.
