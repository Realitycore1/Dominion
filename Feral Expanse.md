# VII. The Feral Expanse

The deepest known naturally accessible layer of the plane.

A realm where identity itself becomes unstable under adaptive pressure.

Nothing remains static.

Not even concepts like:
- species
- individuality
- ecosystem
- anatomy
- reproduction

The Feral Expanse is less a biome and more a self-evolving biological stormfront.

### Properties
- ecosystems consume and rewrite each other constantly
- organisms may exist as distributed evolutionary patterns
- predator/prey distinctions collapse
- biological laws mutate regionally

Some entities within the Feral Expanse no longer resemble life as understood by lower realities.

They behave more like:
- evolutionary algorithms
- adaptive disasters
- living extinction events
- roaming ecological singularities

### Common Inhabitants
- apex evolutionary intelligences
- recursive megafauna
- world-consuming organisms
- self-replicating adaptive swarms
- conceptual predators

Even high-tier civilizations avoid deep penetration into the Feral Expanse.
