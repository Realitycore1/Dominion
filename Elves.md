1. High Elves
	•	Definition: The archetypal “noble” elf, refined in [[culture]] and magic.
	•	Traits: Tall, graceful, long-lived, arcane affinity.
	•	Symbolism: Superiority, enlightenment, detachment.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Wood Elves
	•	Definition: Forest-dwellers tied to nature and druidic traditions.
	•	Traits: Agile, camouflaged, masters of bows and stealth.
	•	Symbolism: Harmony with nature, wild independence.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Dark Elves (Drow/Shadow Elves)
	•	Definition: Elves of the underworld, cursed or adapted to shadow [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Pale or obsidian skin, silver hair, mastery of shadow and poison.
	•	Symbolism: Corruption, [[Betrayal]], exile, but also resilience.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Snow Elves
	•	Definition: Elves of frozen lands, adapted to harsh winters.
	•	Traits: Pale or icy blue skin, fur-lined garb, frost magic.
	•	Symbolism: Isolation, survival, purity of the cold.

⸻

5. [[Fire]] Elves
	•	Definition: Elves bound to flame, volcanoes, or elemental [[Fire]].
	•	Traits: Ruddy skin tones, ember-like eyes, volatile tempers.
	•	Symbolism: Destruction and creation through passion.

⸻

6. Water Elves
	•	Definition: Aquatic or riverine elves, attuned to tides and oceans.
	•	Traits: Amphibious lungs, sea-green hues, fluid movement.
	•	Symbolism: Adaptability, mystery, shifting currents of fate.

⸻

7. Avariel (Winged Elves)
	•	Definition: Rare skyborne elves with angelic wings.
	•	Traits: Flight, keen eyesight, [[culture]] tied to freedom and [[Stars]].
	•	Symbolism: Aspiration, spiritual transcendence.

⸻

8. Low Elves
	•	Definition: Common or lesser elves, closest to [[Humanity]] in form and [[culture]].
	•	Traits: Shorter-lived, less magically potent, more pragmatic.
	•	Symbolism: The everyman’s elf—bridges between men and [[Fae]].

⸻

9. Wild Elves
	•	Definition: Untamed elves, feral and tribal, dwelling in uncharted lands.
	•	Traits: Savage grace, ritualistic [[culture]], primal magic.
	•	Symbolism: The untamed spirit of elf-kind.

⸻

10. Rune Elves
	•	Definition: Elves steeped in ancient glyphs and magical language.
	•	Traits: Skin etched with runes, [[Natural]] spellcraft, often seen as mystics.
	•	Symbolism: Guardians of [[Forgotten]] knowledge.

⸻

11. Star Elves / Astral Elves
	•	Definition: Elves connected to starlight, [[The Void]], or cosmic [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Celestial auras, dream-walking, spacefaring traditions in some worlds.
	•	Symbolism: Immortality, destiny, reaching beyond the mortal plane.

⸻

12. Shadowkin Elves
	•	Definition: Elves who dwell between worlds, neither wholly dark nor light.
	•	Traits: Blurred features, shifting voices, liminal [[(2030) Existence]].
	•	Symbolism: Ambiguity, balance, [[Trickster]] archetype within elvenkind.
