## Summary
A post-scarcity [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] exists when nearly all physical needs (food, shelter, energy, health, education, information, tools) can be met at near-zero marginal cost due to technological and resource abundance.
Enablers:
Unlimited energy (e.g., fusion, [[Antimatter]]INI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Technology/Antimatter/Antimatter]])
Adv[[Production]]on and robotics
[[Nanotechnology]] and molecular assemblers
Full recycling and matter conversion
AI-managed logistics and distribution
Digital [[Production]] (e.g., software, art, design)

A. Resource Allocation without Scarcity
AI-guided algorithms optimize distribution of goods and services.
Needs are forecast and fulfilled automatically (like supply [[Chains]] run by a divine logistics [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]).
No prices needed for basic goods—entitlements are default.

 B. Value Shift from Material to Intangible
Scarcity migrates from physical goods to intangibles:
Social status
Reputation
Creative expression
Cultural capital
Access to experiences, leadership, or influence
Economies shift to post-material value [[Systems]] (e.g., prestige, purpose, ideology).

 C. Work Becomes Purpose-Driven
No need to "work to survive" → People engage in:
Exploration
Research
Art, [[culture]], or ideology
Defense and civil service (if threats still exist)
Merit becomes the new currency—as seen in [[The Dominion]]’s meritocratic stratocracy.

 D. Merit-based Access Hierarchies
In fully post-scarcity societies with social hierarchies:
Basic needs are guaranteed
Access to high-risk roles, influence, command, and cultural legacy is earned
This aligns with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s citizen vs civilian divide and strategic access design

-Access replaces ownership (e.g., why own a ship when you can summon one?)

-Obsolete Concept / Why It Fades
-Wages for Survival
People no longer need money to live
-Mass Labor Markets
Automation handles nearly all [[Production]]
-Resource Hoarding
Infinite replication removes incentive
-Poverty
Baseline conditions are guaranteed
-Basic Commerce
Barter and most buying/selling are unnecessary

Governments shift from regulators to curators of purpose, ideology, and [[Continuity]].
Laws regulate ideological and cultural integrity, not economic efficiency.
Crimes shift from theft to ideological corruption, privacy violation, or sabotage.
This is reflected in [[The Dominion]]’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]:
No prisons, only restitution or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]].
Service to earn redemption (penal [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]]).
Surveillance is tolerated due to stability and efficiency.

-Theft (material goods), Anything basic—food, shelter, tools—is universally accessible or replicable.
-Property crime, With abundant and often non-exclusive access to tools and spaces, property is less defensible or emotionally "owned."
-Survival-driven crime, Starvation, homelessness, or economic desperation are eliminated.
-Petty economic fraud, Currency is tightly integrated into [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]-based [[Systems]] (Omni-devices), and surveillance discourages low-tier scams.

-Crime Type / Evolution in Post-Scarcity Context
-Violent crime, Still occurs—now often driven by ideology, status challenge, trauma, or [[Ritual]].
-Cybercrime, Grows in sophistication—AI sabotage, ideological subversion, false [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] crafting, or synthetic consciousness manipulation.
-Ideological subversion	Seen as existential threats—e.g., trying to corrupt loyalty, disrupt meritocracy, or spread [[Domain]] influence.
-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]/merit fraud, Forging one’s record to gain access to restricted roles or prestige.
-Biotech/memetic sabotage, Altering one's genes illegally, spreading dangerous memes or psychotropics.
-Cultural taboo violation, Even with tolerance, some acts (like violating constitutional order or aiding enemies) become high crimes.

A. Post-Material Hacking
Goal: Subvert social capital [[Systems]] (e.g., merit score), AI protocols, command authority [[Chains]].

Example: Forging battle honors to gain entry into elite [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]].

B. Ideological Rebellion
Goal: Not material gain, but narrative control or memetic warfare.

Example: Infiltrating loyalist media to spread anti-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] or [[Domain]] sympathies.

C. Omnidevice Subversion
Goal: Hijack [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]-linked devices for control over access [[Rights]], travel, or economic leverage.

D. Ritualized or Honor-Based Violence
Goal: Settle ideological or personal grievances in controlled or unregulated duels.

In some [[Regions]], this could be tolerated or even encouraged within specific bounds.

post-scarcity society like [[The Dominion]], where automation and replication meet centralized meritocracy, “work” is no longer about survival or wages. Instead, it becomes a declaration of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], allegiance, and function. Purpose replaces profit; contribution replaces consumption.

	1.	Survival is Guaranteed
	•	No one works for food, shelter, or health.
	•	This removes coercion and allows for total vocational redirection.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Work is Purpose-Driven
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] citizens pursue paths that align with their talents, convictions, or doctrinal assignments.
	•	All work is meaningful, even if menial tasks are simulated for [[Ritual]] or psychological need.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Stratocratic Meritocracy
	•	Citizens can ascend ranks by fulfilling challenging or high-value roles.
	•	Every action, project, or creation contributes to one’s merit profile, influencing access to leadership, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] rank, or honorific roles.
