## Summary
Filamentary conduits linking the [[Verse]] to external [[Regions]]. They carry exotic energy or mass (strange matter, dark flux) for stabilization or extraction. Improper use risks “tether inversion,” collapsing sections of the [[Verse]].

The siphoning of strange matter from neighboring verses or dead universes to fuel or reinforce one’s own. This is both an act of survival and parasitic aggression.
