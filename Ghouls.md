Classification: Undead & Degenerate Predators
Subtype: Necrophagic Humanoids / Hunger-Devolved
Status: Active, Common in Collapse Zones

⸻

Core Description

Ghouls are corpse-eating humanoids—once living beings—whose bodies and minds have degraded through sustained necrophagy and death-saturated environments. Their physiques are wiry, crouched, and elongated, with pallid or gray skin stretched tight over bone, sharpened teeth, and eyes adapted to darkness.

They are fast, quiet, and unnervingly familiar.

A ghoul does not feast for pleasure.
It feeds because nothing else sustains it anymore.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Ghouls arise from biological and spiritual degradation, not a single curse.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Death-adjacent Material Continuum
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice (low-grade), Null Shores bleed
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms, grave-sites, Hollow Veins

They are not animated corpses.
They are living beings who crossed a line and could not cross back.

⸻

Creation & Transmission

Ghoulification occurs through:
	•	prolonged consumption of corpses,
	•	starvation paired with exposure to death-saturated zones,
	•	scavenging battlefields or plague pits,
	•	survival cannibalism focused on the dead rather than the living.

Importantly:

Ghouls do not spread by bite.

Becoming a ghoul is a process, not an infection.

⸻

Intelligence & Psychology

Ghouls retain limited sapience.

They understand:
	•	pack hierarchy,
	•	ambush tactics,
	•	burial customs,
	•	fear responses.

They lose:
	•	long-term planning,
	•	empathy,
	•	individual ambition.

Some early-stage ghouls remember names, routines, and even shame—but this fades.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Ghouls inhabit:
	•	cemeteries,
	•	mass graves,
	•	ruined cities,
	•	battlefields,
	•	plague zones.

They prefer:
	•	corpses over living prey,
	•	the freshly dead over the old,
	•	isolation and darkness.

However, when starved, ghouls will attack the living.

They often trail wars like scavenger shadows.

⸻

Physical Adaptations
	•	Enhanced night vision
	•	Heightened smell for decay
	•	Extreme flexibility and crawling locomotion
	•	Rapid healing when feeding
	•	Resistance to disease and toxins

They are difficult to kill permanently unless burned or dismembered.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to fire
	•	Weakened by Grace-aligned zones
	•	Disrupted by sanctified burial grounds
	•	Cannot tolerate prolonged daylight

Clean environments starve ghouls out naturally.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Wendigo: Despised; Wendigo hunt ghouls
	•	Vampires: View ghouls as vermin or failed stock
	•	Ghosts: Often consumed unintentionally
	•	Necromancers: Sometimes exploit them
	•	Domovoi: Actively repel ghouls from homes

Ghouls are bottom-tier predators, but dangerous in numbers.

⸻

Variants
	•	Feral Ghouls: Fully degraded, animalistic
	•	Grave Ghouls: Cemetery-bound, territorial
	•	Urban Ghouls: Sewer and ruin dwellers
	•	Ash Ghouls: Post-collapse zones, aggressive
	•	Alpha Ghoul: Rare leaders with retained memory

Alpha Ghouls can organize large packs and become regional threats.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Zombies: Ghouls are alive and adaptive
	•	vs Vampires: No blood fixation or hierarchy
	•	vs Wendigo: No spiritual hunger escalation
	•	vs Wights: No territorial binding
	•	vs Cannibals: Sustained transformation has occurred

This distinction keeps your undead taxonomy clean.
