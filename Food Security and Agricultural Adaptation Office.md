# Food Security and Agricultural Adaptation Office

## Summary

The Food Security and Agricultural Adaptation Office protects the Dominion's food supply across planets, habitats, frontier ecologies, and restoration zones.

It exists because no civilization survives on ideology, weapons, or architecture alone. Someone must make sure people eat.

## Mandate

The Office oversees:

- crop adaptation;
- soil and substrate testing;
- fungal and microbial food systems;
- ration reserve standards;
- seed vault coordination;
- livestock and synthetic-protein regulation;
- ecological contamination rules;
- famine forecasting;
- food transport priority with transit agencies.

## Powers

It may quarantine contaminated food chains, approve adaptive crop lines, redirect emergency stores, and require local food-security plans.

## Limits

It cannot conceal famine risk for political convenience, privilege core worlds at the expense of frontier survival without emergency justification, or release engineered food systems without safety review.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Office through ration labels, agricultural schools, food recalls, frontier greenhouse inspections, and the taste of whatever crop survived the last bad season.

## Story Use

Use this agency to ground big politics in material reality. Food policy reveals class, settlement priority, environmental risk, and whether the Dominion actually values the people it claims to preserve.
