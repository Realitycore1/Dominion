# (3180) Mercury Colony

The first permanent Mercurian colony is established in 3180 in shielded polar terrain, excavated galleries, and lava-tube systems.

The settlement is an artificial thermal environment rather than an open surface colony or early terraforming project.

## Managed-Shadow Engineering

Mercurian life is supported by:

- a station-kept thin-film reflector and shade swarm near the Sun-Mercury L1 region;
- local horizon shades, reflective berms, and movable surface screens;
- regolith-buried pressure galleries;
- liquid-metal and high-temperature heat loops;
- radiator fields placed in permanent or scheduled shadow;
- polar cold-sink vaults and thermal stores;
- autonomous sunward mines and smelters;
- electromagnetic mass drivers coordinated with shade and inhabited orbits.

The shade swarm does not cool the entire planet. It reduces incident energy over designated settlement, transit, radiator, and industrial windows.

## Extraction Economy

Mercurian industry uses abundant solar energy to mine and refine iron and nickel feedstock, silicon, sulfur-bearing material, ceramics, reflector film, radiator components, and high-temperature machinery.

Polar water deposits are protected as public life-support and emergency-cooling reserves. Belt volatiles and Venusian carbon and nitrogen reduce pressure to consume them.

Mercury exports refined metals, solar arrays, shade film, radiators, and mass-driver components.

## Civil Government

The Mercury Colonial Committee is established with the colony. It governs shade guarantees, heat budgets, radiator access, polar reserves, mining permits, mass-driver schedules, and local civil law.

No employer or creditor may withdraw shade, cooling, water, or radiator access to enforce a contract or debt.
