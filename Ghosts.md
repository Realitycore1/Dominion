Classification: Deathbound Entities & Transitional Failures
Subtype: Disembodied Remnants / Identity Echoes
Status: Active, Common, Highly Variable

⸻

Core Description

Ghosts are disembodied manifestations of identity—the residue of a mind, will, or memory that failed to complete the transition from life to death. They are not corpses, not souls in full, and not beings meant to persist.

A ghost is what lingers when letting go fails.

They may appear as:
	•	translucent humanoid forms,
	•	partial silhouettes,
	•	environmental distortions,
	•	repeating sensory imprints.

Their presence is often accompanied by cold, static, or temporal dissonance.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Ghosts form when the death process is interrupted, resisted, or malformed.

Common causes include:
	•	sudden or violent death,
	•	unresolved emotional anchors,
	•	cursed locations,
	•	Malice or Null interference,
	•	improper funerary rites,
	•	planar misalignment.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Shadow Realms (Umbrae)
	•	Secondary Influence: Null Shores (Edge of the Empty)
	•	Realm Interface: Material ↔ Death Thresholds

Ghosts are not inherently corrupted—but they are unstable.

⸻

Identity & Consciousness

Ghost cognition varies widely:
	•	Echo Ghosts: No awareness; repeating moments
	•	Aware Ghosts: Retain personality and memory
	•	Fragmented Ghosts: Partial identity, erratic behavior
	•	Degraded Ghosts: Memory erosion, emotional fixation

Over time, most ghosts lose coherence, becoming hostile or mindless unless stabilized.

⸻

Behavior & Manifestation

Ghosts are bound to:
	•	places,
	•	objects,
	•	people,
	•	unresolved events.

They do not travel freely unless:
	•	tethered to a moving anchor,
	•	forcibly bound,
	•	elevated into a higher undead state.

Ghosts often:
	•	repeat actions,
	•	attempt communication,
	•	influence emotions,
	•	distort local reality subtly.

They rarely seek violence initially.

⸻

Escalation Paths

If unresolved, ghosts may evolve into:
	•	Poltergeists (kinetic fixation)
	•	Wraiths (predatory resentment)
	•	Barrow-Wights (territorial undead)
	•	Death Echoes (temporal hauntings)
	•	Null Phantoms (identity eroded entirely)

Ghosthood is usually a temporary condition—unless something interferes.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to ritual resolution
	•	Disrupted by Grace-aligned rites
	•	Dissolve in Null-dominant zones
	•	Cannot cross consecrated thresholds easily

Violence against ghosts often worsens instability.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Reapers: Seek to complete their transition
	•	Domovoi: Prevent ghost formation in protected homes
	•	Necromancers: Exploit or bind ghosts
	•	Demons: Corrupt ghosts into weapons
	•	Phoenix Fire: Erases ghosts completely

Ghosts are prey to many forces—but belong to none.

⸻

Variants
	•	Restless Ghosts: Emotionally bound
	•	Guardian Ghosts: Voluntary anchors
	•	Haunt Ghosts: Location-locked
	•	Memory Ghosts: Non-sentient echoes
	•	Lost Ghosts: Drifting toward the Null Shores

Guardian Ghosts are rare and require conscious sacrifice.
