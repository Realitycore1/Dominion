The Throne of Equilon is the center of authority within the Equillion Union. The Throne is held by the Zohair family and was established after the declaration of independence. The current monarch is [[Armand Zohair]] who is known as the insular king.

The Zohair family, running on its fourth generation of rule is currently under four immediate members. For the last [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] centuries the union has known prosperity under the monarchy. The fourth generation under Armand has now began to show signs of fracture within its foundation as the crown and the populace began to differ in views.
