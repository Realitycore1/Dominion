(The Elemental Foundation / The World-Bearing Substrate)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Foundational Material–Energetic Plane
	•	Authority Level: Structural, non-sovereign
	•	Relation to Axioms: Manifestation layer
	•	Relation to Aravoth / Sheol-Gehenna: Neutral substrate
	•	Relation to the Empty: Pre-disengagement only

⸻

Core Nature

Fundamentum Elementalis is where the raw components of reality first stabilize into forces.

It is not the origin of existence.
It is the origin of composition.

If:
	•	the Axiomatic Stratum defines what may exist,
	•	the Oneiric Plane imagines what could exist,

then Fundamentum Elementalis determines what existence is made of.

It answers:

“What does reality weigh, burn, flow, and resist with?”

⸻

What “Elemental” Means Here (important)

Elements are not substances in the mortal sense.

They are:
	•	persistent energetic behaviors,
	•	stable interaction modes,
	•	repeatable force patterns.

Examples:
	•	Fire = propagation and consumption
	•	Water = cohesion and adaptation
	•	Earth = inertia and persistence
	•	Air = transmission and motion

Secondary and composite elements emerge where these overlap.

⸻

Structure of the Plane

Fundamentum Elementalis is layered and stratified, not unified.

The Deep Strata
	•	Pure elemental seas
	•	No form, no life
	•	Impossible to inhabit

These layers are:
	•	crushing,
	•	burning,
	•	dissolving,
	•	dispersing beyond identity.

⸻

The Stable Bands

Where elements achieve self-consistency.
	•	Elemental domains
	•	Crystalline fire-fields
	•	Oceanic pressure-realms
	•	Stone-masses the size of continents

Most elemental beings originate here.

⸻

The Turbulent Interfaces

Zones of elemental collision.
	•	Storm belts
	•	Magma oceans
	•	Ash skies
	•	Tectonic tempests

These interfaces are where:
	•	monsters form,
	•	colossi emerge,
	•	worlds are seeded or destroyed.

⸻

Inhabitants

1. Elementals

Pure expressions of a single elemental mode.
	•	Lack morality
	•	Follow instinctual physics
	•	Can be bound, not reasoned with

⸻

2. Elemental Sovereigns

(Ifrit, Marid, Djinn Lords, Earth-Kings)
	•	Stable identities
	•	Political behavior
	•	Domain control

They rule regions, not the plane.

⸻

3. Elemental Colossi

Continental-scale entities.
	•	Walking volcanoes
	•	Living hurricanes
	•	Moving mountain ranges
	•	Oceanic titans

They are environmental events, not rulers.

⸻

4. Primordials

Extremely rare.
	•	Early stabilizations of elemental force
	•	Not creators
	•	Not gods
	•	Often mistaken for both

Their existence predates most planes but does not supersede axioms.

⸻

Interaction with Other Planes

With Aravoth
	•	Elements are interpreted as divine instruments
	•	Angels do not rule here
	•	Order is imposed from above, not native

⸻

With Sheol-Gehenna
	•	Corrupted elements form infernal matter
	•	Fire becomes torment, earth becomes prison
	•	Hell depends on stolen elemental stability

⸻

With the Oneiric Plane
	•	Dreams influence elemental mutation
	•	Mythic beasts often emerge from this overlap

⸻

With the Axiomatic Stratum
	•	Axioms constrain how elements behave
	•	Direct axiom damage causes elemental collapse

This is why Verse Severance events often begin with elemental disasters.

⸻

Mortal Interaction

Mortals encounter Fundamentum Elementalis indirectly:
	•	Elemental magic
	•	Natural disasters
	•	Summoning rituals
	•	Cataclysms

Direct exposure causes:
	•	disintegration,
	•	transformation,
	•	or permanent elemental bonding.

⸻

Why This Plane Is Necessary

Without Fundamentum Elementalis:
	•	reality has rules but no substance,
	•	dreams have ideas but no mass,
	•	gods have law but nothing to shape.

It is the load-bearing layer of existence.
