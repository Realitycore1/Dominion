Life in the Integral State

[[Dominion Omniverse/Dominion Multiverse/Eos Verse/Dominion Verse/Verse Structure/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Post-WW3/Daily life|Daily life]] in [[Estela Integralism]] is less about survival or tradition, and more about constant performance of loyalty. The colony is an ideological theater, where citizens are both actors and audience in the cult of the Caudillo.
	•	The Citizen as [[Soldier]]
	•	Every Estelan is considered a [[Soldier]] of Integralism, whether wielding a rifle, working a lathe, or teaching children.
	•	Uniforms are ubiquitous: even [[Workers]] and farmers wear standardized coveralls marked with the party symbol.
	•	Individual expression is discouraged; collective [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] is exalted.
	•	Family as Cell of the State
	•	Families are treated as miniature party units.
	•	Parents are responsible for the ideological purity of their children—failure to indoctrinate can result in removal to state-run orphanages.
	•	Marriage is regulated: only unions deemed to “strengthen the race” are permitted, often requiring genetic screening.
	•	Education & Indoctrination
	•	Children enter Youth Leagues at age 6, drilled in party chants, orbital marches, and loyalty salutes.
	•	By adolescence, they train in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] basics and racial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], preparing for either conscription or administrative service.
	•	Schools double as propaganda centers, decorated with murals of the Caudillo and victories of the party.
	•	Propaganda & [[Ritual]]
	•	Citizens are bombarded with visual symbols: black suns, comet motifs, and stylized eagles.
	•	Radios and public screens broadcast endless hymns to the leader and denunciations of enemies.
	•	Mass rallies are common—hundreds of thousands gathered in plazas, chanting in unison, saluting as holographic banners of the Caudillo sweep overhead.
	•	[[culture]] of Fear
	•	Surveillance drones hover over streets, recording salutes and facial expressions.
	•	Neighbors are encouraged to denounce one another for insufficient zeal.
	•	Families keep portraits of the Caudillo not only as loyalty symbols, but as shields against suspicion.
	•	Festivals of Unity
	•	Holidays revolve around ideological milestones: the founding of the colony, victories in war, anniversaries of the Caudillo’s reign.
	•	Day of the Black Sun: a mass rally held annually, marked by synchronized salutes and the release of black-lantern drones across the sky.
	•	The Purity Parades: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] marches through cities, where entire lower districts are compelled to attend or face fines.
	•	Art & Expression
	•	Art is bombastic, monumental, and propagandistic: statues of the Caudillo, massive murals of heroic [[Workers]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] parades choreographed as aesthetic displays.
	•	Literature is censored; only state-approved poetry and prose circulate, filled with rhetoric of destiny and purity.
	•	Underground resistance art exists but is ruthlessly hunted—small coded songs, graffiti comets with broken tails.
