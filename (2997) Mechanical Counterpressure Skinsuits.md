# (2997) Mechanical Counterpressure Skinsuits

Phaeton standardizes close-fitting mechanical counterpressure suits for routine orbital and Lunar exterior labor.

The suits press directly against the body and retain separate breathing, cooling, helmet, glove, thermal, micrometeoroid, radiation, and emergency-pressure systems.

They improve mobility and reduce fatigue without making vacuum safe.

Their adoption creates disputes over:

- individual fitting;
- body privacy;
- medical surveillance;
- skin injury;
- maintenance quality;
- disability accommodation;
- employer responsibility.
