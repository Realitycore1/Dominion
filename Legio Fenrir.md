Armor Variant – Panoply of Fenrir

Silhouette: Broad-shouldered, muscular armor profile, evoking beastlike presence. Wolf-head motifs across pauldrons and greaves.

Adaptive Plating: Reinforced to endure melee strikes, claw-like gauntlets integrated for tearing [[Close]] combat.

Coloration: Dark iron-gray with blood-red accents and scar-like markings etched across the plating.

Helmet: Bestial visors shaped like snarling wolves, glowing crimson eyes. Audio projectors emit lupine howls during charges.

Specialization: Berserker Protocols—adrenal stimulants and reactor overdrive linked directly to Panoply [[Systems]], allowing Legionnaires to fight with frenzied speed and strength when the pack surges together.

Legion Standard [[Arms]] – Fenrir Configuration

Modification: Weapons reinforced for melee integration—bayonets, shock-blades, and chain attachments common.

Aesthetic: Rugged, scarred metal finish with wolf-etched designs. Weapons roar with guttural discharge like beast growls.

Doctrinal Role

Deployment: Shock-hunters—Fenrir [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] thrive in [[Close]]-quarters, urban, and wilderness combat where ferocity and pursuit overwhelm the foe.
Prefers Carbine and Pistol forms for brutal [[Close]]-range skirmishing, though Heavy Rifles are used as “hunting spears.”

Psychological Warfare: The sound of their howls and the sight of wolf-faced helms charging en masse breaks enemy morale before contact.

Legacy: Legio Fenrir is remembered as the Legion of the Hunt. They embody the untamed predator, leaving nothing but shredded remains where their packs descend.

LEGION FENRIR — The Wolf Host

Classification: Frontier Assimilation • Predator Warfare
Status: Active (feral remnants)

Profile

Fenrir warpacks hunted across lawless frontiers far beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] logistics. Their linked instincts, augmented senses, and predator formations made them ideal for converting wildspace into civilization.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Pack infiltration
	•	Deep-frontier dominance
	•	Instinct synchronization
	•	Long-hunt eradication campaigns

[[culture]] & Symbol

Twin wolf jaws interlocked — a sign of consuming the impossible.

Final Fate

Fenrir never died. Their remnants roam the post-Severance wild sectors, feral yet intact. Some believe they wait for the Empress’s return.
