1. Common Dryads
	•	Definition: The baseline—tree-bound [[Nymphs]] who embody the spirit of a single tree.
	•	Traits: Humanoid beauty, bark-like skin, hair of leaves, life tied to their tree.
	•	Symbolism: The soul of the forest, beauty interwoven with fragility.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Oak Dryads
	•	Definition: Dryads of oak trees, strong and long-lived.
	•	Traits: Dark bark-skin, great strength, protective temperaments.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance, guardianship, timeless strength.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Willow Dryads
	•	Definition: Dryads of willow trees, mournful and graceful.
	•	Traits: Flowing hair, affinity for water, soothing or sorrowful voices.
	•	Symbolism: Sorrow, reflection, patience.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Birch Dryads
	•	Definition: Pale-skinned dryads tied to birch groves.
	•	Traits: White bark-like skin, renewal magic, youthfulness.
	•	Symbolism: [[Rebirth]], purity, resilience.

⸻

5. Thorn Dryads
	•	Definition: Fierce protectors of brambles and thorn thickets.
	•	Traits: Barbed vines, cruel tempers, ambush predators.
	•	Symbolism: Beauty and danger intertwined.

⸻

6. Blossom Dryads
	•	Definition: Dryads of flowering trees (cherry, apple, etc.).
	•	Traits: Petal-hair, enchanting fragrance, charm and illusion.
	•	Symbolism: Fleeting beauty, cycles of growth and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

7. Grove Dryads
	•	Definition: Dryads bound not to a single tree, but to a grove or forest as a whole.
	•	Traits: Collective consciousness, command over many trees, stronger magic.
	•	Symbolism: The forest as a singular spirit.

⸻

8. Shadow Dryads
	•	Definition: Corrupted or cursed dryads bound to dark woods or necrotic groves.
	•	Traits: Black bark-skin, hollow eyes, withering touch.
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s corruption; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in the heart of the wild.

⸻

9. Elemental Dryads
	•	Definition: Dryads infused with elemental powers.
	•	Variants: Flamebark ([[Fire]]), Stonebark ([[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]), Frostleaf (ice), Stormbranch (lightning).
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s adaptability; primal forces through arboreal forms.

⸻

10. Arch-Dryads (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary tree-[[Queens]], embodiments of ancient forests or world-trees.
	•	Traits: Towering, semi-divine, tied to ley-lines and world-roots.
	•	Symbolism: Creation, fertility, apocalypse and [[Rebirth]] through the green.
