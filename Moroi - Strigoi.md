Classification

Proto-Vampires / Post-Mortem Predators / Transitional Undead

⸻

CORE IDEA

[[Moroi]] and [[Strigoi]] are vampirism before refinement.

They are not elegant immortals or courtly predators. They are the raw, folkloric truth of vampirism: hunger, fear, and unfinished death. Where later vampires become systems, Moroi and Strigoi remain personal catastrophes.

They don’t rule the night.
They terrorize it.

⸻

ORIGIN

Moroi and Strigoi arise from corrupted birth–death boundaries:
	•	children born with caul, tail, or irregular signs
	•	suicides, unbaptized dead, or improper burial
	•	victims of vampiric predation who resist full conversion
	•	malice-tainted bloodlines or ancestral curses

Moroi often begin alive.
Strigoi return after death.

The difference is timing—not destiny.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

They appear mostly human, but decay leaks through.

Common traits:
	•	Pallid or gray-tinged skin
	•	Distended or sunken eyes, glowing faintly at night
	•	Sharp teeth, sometimes uneven or excessive
	•	Bloated or corpse-heavy appearance
	•	Shadow behaving incorrectly
	•	Breath frosting even in warm air

They lack the composure of true vampires. Their bodies betray them.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Moroi and Strigoi are erratic, obsessive, and intimate predators.
	•	Feed on family, neighbors, or familiar places
	•	Exhibit fixation on homes, livestock, bloodlines
	•	Attack impulsively rather than strategically
	•	Capable of cruelty but driven primarily by hunger

They do not build courts.
They empty villages.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Moderate.
	•	Retain memories and emotions
	•	Poor impulse control
	•	Strong attachment to former life
	•	Easily enraged or panicked

They know who they were—and resent it.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Blood–Breath Hybrid Feeding: drain blood, vitality, and sleep
	•	Grace-Reactive: angelic presence causes agony, not obedience
	•	Malice-Amplified: corruption accelerates transformation
	•	Corpse Instability: bodies degrade without feeding
	•	Ritual Vulnerability: folklore protections genuinely work

Stakes, decapitation, fire, and exposure are all effective—because these beings are unfinished.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Appear in rural, isolated, or tradition-heavy regions
	•	Often cluster in bloodlines
	•	Rarely travel far from origin
	•	Cause livestock deaths, sickness, and nightmares

A Moroi infestation feels like slow social collapse.

⸻

ALPHA MOROI / STRIGOI

An Alpha is called a Strigoi Viu or Night-Elder.

Traits:
	•	Retains higher cognition
	•	Can command lesser undead or proto-vampires
	•	Begins developing true vampiric traits
	•	Acts as a bridge between folklore and aristocratic vampirism

Night-Elders are transitional monsters—the missing link.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (local) / Severe (Alpha or lineage spread)

Moroi and Strigoi are dangerous because they are close—emotionally and physically.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

They represent:
	•	vampirism as curse, not power
	•	the horror of familiar faces
	•	superstition that’s right for the wrong reasons
	•	monsters born from community fractures

Where Ghoul-Pires show collapse,
where Ascendant Vampires show control,
Moroi and Strigoi show origin trauma.
