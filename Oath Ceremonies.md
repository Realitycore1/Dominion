The Oath of Guardianship is not a mere recitation of words; it is a carefully staged rite of passage that fuses [[Ritual]], spectacle, and binding civic contract.
	•	Age of Oath: Citizens swear the Oath at 18 standard years. No citizen is considered an adult until they have sworn it. Those who refuse are classed as “stateless” and cannot hold property, marry legally, or inherit. Refusal is rare and carries social shame.
	•	Setting: Ceremonies are held in open plazas, often built in concentric rings around a central monolith known as the Pillar of Duty. Each Pillar is inscribed with the names of every Oathkeeper who has served since the colony’s founding.
	•	The [[Ritual]]:
	1.	The initiate steps barefoot onto a stone dais at the base of the Pillar.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	A family elder presents the initiate’s Line Banner—a standard marked with sigils of their ancestry.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The initiate places their hand on the Pillar of Duty, then recites the Oath aloud, promising to “guard, serve, and endure.”
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	A shard of basalt from the Eridani [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] is pressed against the initiate’s brow, symbolizing the weight of the frontier.
	5.	The newly sworn citizen receives a steel ring etched with their name and lineage, to be worn at all times as proof of their Oath.
	•	Communal Aspect: Oaths are sworn in cohorts of dozens, reinforcing that adulthood is not an individual journey but an entry into collective responsibility. The entire city gathers to [[Witness]], turning the event into a civic festival.
	•	[[Aftermath]]: Following the ceremony, families host feasts where the new Oathkeeper is formally welcomed into adult life. They may now serve in the militia, hold land, and—most importantly—become eligible for selection as an Oathkeeper in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]].
	•	Cultural Weight:
	•	To break the Oath is unthinkable; oath-breakers are ritually stripped of their steel ring, their names struck from the Pillars.
	•	Citizens measure the worth of their neighbors by how faithfully they embody the Oath in their daily lives.

The Oath Ceremony isn’t just a political foundation—it’s a religio-civic rite, a binding contract that fuses [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], family honor, and the colony’s survival.
