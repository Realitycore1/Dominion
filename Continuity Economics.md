Most operate in:

	•	food cultivation
	•	entertainment
	•	small-scale biotech
	•	robotics
	•	cybernetic augmentation
	•	material crafting

However, the Market Sector cannot influence:
	•	national security
	•	genetic policy
	•	[[Infrastructure]] integrity
	•	food chain resilience

	•	mineral flux
	•	water purity
	•	atmospheric composition
	•	genetic library integrity
	•	industrial load

3.2 Redundancy [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]

After the Fall, Attea learned that one failure can kill a civilization.

Thus, every critical resource has:
	•	a planetary backup
	•	an orbital backup
	•	a deep-vault genetic backup
	•	a cryo-archive
	•	a psionic memory-stone archive

Every colony maintains enough reserves to survive at least a century without external support.

7. Money, Wealth, and Value

Currency:

A hybrid [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] of:
	•	Energy-backed credits
	•	Genetic capital licenses

Wealth is measured not in material goods but in:
	•	genetic quality
	•	conduit lineage
	•	historical prestige

	•	knowledge mastery

Luxury exists, but excess is frowned upon — it implies misallocation of resources.

8. Construction Philosophy: Bio-Integrated Engineering

Attean architecture merges:
	•	synthetic materials
	•	engineered stone
	•	adaptive alloys
	•	semi-organic composites
	•	rune-layer conduits
	•	psionic harmonics

Buildings “breathe”:
	•	self-repairing
	•	temperature-regulating
	•	gravity-resistant
	•	anti-contaminant surfaces

Some structures are partially alive — vascular walls distribute power and coolant.
