A McKendree Cylinder is a rotating orbital megastructure, shaped as a colossal hollow cylinder, with its inner surface turned into habitable land. Unlike the smaller O’Neill design, a McKendree can be hundreds to thousands of kilometers in length, capable of housing billions to trillions in a single habitat.
Structure & Scale

Length: up to 1,000–10,000 km, diameter 100–1,000 km.

Walls made of exotic composite lattices ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] uses strange-matter alloys or [[Computronium]]-reinforced shells).

Rotational Gravity: Spin generates Earthlike gravity on inner surface.

Atlas Pillar Caps: Towering end structures stabilize the rotation and double as docking ports.

Habitability

Landscapes: Inner walls lined with oceans, mountains, forests, and arcologies — an “endless band of horizon.”

Sunlight: Redirected via orbital mirrors or plasma-light conduits.

Atmosphere: Contained by sheer walls and dynamic magnetic fields.

Ecologies: Can host layered climates: deserts, jungles, polar belts — all stacked around the inner curve.

Variants

Civil Cylinders: Residential worlds for billions.

Industrial Cylinders: Endless strips of forges, fields, and manufactories.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Cylinders: Fortress-worlds in a can, bristling with artillery and hangars.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Used as standardized settlement modules — entire sectors seeded with fleets of McKendrees instead of terraforming planets.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Some serve as mobile bastions — rotated into fleets as fortress-cylinders.

Economic: Their size and stability make them ideal hubs for commerce corridors and beamed-sail routes.

Religious: Known as “Amber Wheels” or “Amber Bands”, mythologized as the Empress’s bracelets — each one a jewel orbiting a sun.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ribbed struts rising like cathedral arches across the interior sky.

Suprematist Monolithism: From space, they look like seamless black cylinders drifting with faint light at the ends.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Interiors sculpted into fractal landscapes, repeating in recursive symmetry.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs inscribed along the rim walls — prayers visible from anywhere inside.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A paradise — endless land, light, and air, carefully tuned.

To rebels: A trap — no way out but through [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] airlocks.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A practical jewel — modular, repeatable, eternal.

To myth: Called the “Amber Bands”, said to be the bracelets of the Empress, worn around the suns.
