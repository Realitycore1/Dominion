# (2601) Terran Federation formally established

## Canonical Event

The Terran Federation forms from the twelve Great City power blocs and their regional federations.

Its federal structure includes:

- a population-based People's Assembly;
- a Council of Representatives with equal Great City delegations;
- a limited federal executive;
- federal constitutional courts;
- the advisory Quiet Council.

The twelve cities preserve transformed traditions from older civilizations rather than restoring pre-Exchange borders unchanged.

The global constitution recognizes full personhood regardless of species, engineered origin, body form, communication method, or lifespan. Uplifted persons possess voting, officeholding, family, property, labor, movement, and self-government rights.

The Quiet Council guides but does not rule.
