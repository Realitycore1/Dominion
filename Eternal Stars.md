I. Physical Definition

An Eternal Star is a stellar-scale object whose energy generation, mass retention, and structural stability are balanced in such a way that its luminosity and physical integrity can persist indefinitely, without collapse, fusion burnout, or runaway mass loss.

This is achieved by one or more of the following mechanisms:
	•	Self-Sustaining Exotic Fuel Cycle — Continuous particle replenishment via matter-energy conversion (e.g., [[Verseon]]–[[Aetherion]] cycling).
	•	Perfect Energy Recycling — Stellar envelope and surrounding [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures|megastructures]] return radiated energy back into the star for reuse.
	•	Vacuum Energy Draw — Permanent energy feed from quantum vacuum fluctuations (Quantum Foam Star integration).
	•	Entropy Regulation — Stellar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|system]] actively reverses or halts entropy increase within its own matter field.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Eternal [[Stars]] are classified as Perpetual Luminous Anchors — fixed luminous nodes capable of surviving from the earliest epochs of a [[Verse]] until its terminal cosmological phase, or even beyond if trans-[[Verse]] stabilized.
	•	Core State: Variable — may be fusion, exotic particle decay, or vacuum draw.
	•	Lifespan: Effectively infinite in its operational phase.
	•	Appearance: Ma[[Infrastructure]]y ordinary to distant observation; distinction lies in never aging or evolving.
