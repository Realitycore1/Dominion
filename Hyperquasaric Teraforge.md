A Hyperquasaric Teraforge is a megastructure complex tethered to a quasar, engineered to harness its relativistic jets as industrial crucibles. Instead of simply building ships, it refines raw cosmic matter ([[Stars]], gas giants, asteroid fields, even dark matter) into usable exotic alloys, [[Computronium]] substrates, and Concordia-infused artifacts.
Anchoring & Structure

Accretion Lattices: Vast struts and [[Atlas Pillars]] suspending the forge across quasar jets.

Magnetogravitic Funnels: Capture and compress plasma streams into crucibles the size of planets.

Dyson Mantles: Panels redirect quasar radiation into controlled reservoirs.

Forge [[Systems]]

Plasma Crucibles: Entire moons or planets suspended, melted, and recombined under jet [[Fire]].

Quark-Press Chambers: [[Exotic Matter]] lattices compressed under relativistic pressures.

Outputs

Exotic Alloys: Stronger than neutronium composites, used for [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] and [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].
