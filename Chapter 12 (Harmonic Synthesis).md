# Chapter 12 - Harmonic Synthesis

## Timeline Function

War Years 8-9. Civic, military, scientific, religious, and intelligence systems attempt integrated survival command.

## Event Role

- Combines medical logistics, food production, rail control, orbital watch, and sanctuary networks.
- Uses predictive systems without fully trusting them.
- Blurs the line between Federal coordination and continuity emergency.
- Creates the institutional ancestor of later dual-government thinking.

## Consequence

Harmina learns that integration can save civilization and also make every part of life governable by emergency.
