# Oneirotheon Fleet Doctrine  
**“The Pilgrimage Must Survive Its Own Myths.”**

The Oneirotheon do not possess territory, worlds, or capitals.  
They exist as a **nomadic civilization-organism**, distributed across interstellar fleets that migrate, fracture, recombine, and adapt as the Dream requires.

Their fleets are not merely logistical structures.  
They are **mythic organs**, each fulfilling a distinct survival function.

The fleet doctrine is deliberately non-centralized, asymmetrical, and resilient to schism.

---

## Fleet Stratification Overview

The Oneirotheon fleet is divided into three interdependent macro-fleets:

1. **The Living Fleets** — survival, population, continuity  
2. **The Heavy Fleets** — defense, deterrence, mythic enforcement  
3. **The Salvager Fleets** — adaptation, reclamation, reinterpretation  

No single fleet type can exist independently for long.  
Each is designed to **fail gracefully without collapsing the whole**.

---

## I. The Living Fleets  
**Role:** The Body of the Pilgrimage  
**Primary Function:** Survival, continuity, dream cohesion

The Living Fleets carry the population, culture, and ongoing mythic life of the Oneirotheon. They are not civilian fleets in the conventional sense; they are **biocultural arks**.

### Core Characteristics
- Massive, slow-moving, and internally complex  
- Designed for generational habitation and population drift  
- No single Living Fleet is ever indispensable  

### Internal Structure

**Habitat Arks**
- Vast habitation rings, spindles, or layered internal cities  
- Artificial gravity optimized for long-term skeletal and neurological health  
- Light and time cycles synchronized to Consensus Slumber, not stellar norms  

**Myth-Agriculture Modules**
- Food production is ritualized and symbolic  
- Crops grown under controlled Aether fields to reinforce shared dream-states  
- Certain foods are restricted to specific archetypal cycles (fasting, martyrdom, renewal)

**Consensus Slumber Cathedrals**
- The true core of each Living Fleet  
- Immense dream-chambers where populations enter guided slumber  
- Myths are not taught; they are *experienced*  
- Conflicting myth-currents are spatially separated to prevent dream-feedback collapse  

**The Pilgrim Census**
- Population is tracked by narrative state rather than rank  
- Each individual is indexed by:
  - Active archetype  
  - Dormant archetypes  
  - Narrative instability risk  
- Census data influences migration routes and ritual timing  

### Doctrine of Vulnerability
Living Fleets are intentionally **lightly armed**.

This prevents:
- Military hostage-taking by internal factions  
- Consolidation of power through force  
- The conversion of survival infrastructure into political leverage  

Dependence on other fleets is **by design**.

---

## II. The Heavy Fleets  
**Role:** The Immune System  
**Primary Function:** Defense, deterrence, mythic enforcement

The Heavy Fleets exist to absorb violence so the Living Fleets do not have to. They do not conquer space; they **occupy threat vectors**.

### Core Characteristics
- Modular, overbuilt, and sacrificial by doctrine  
- Optimized for attrition and containment rather than elegance  
- Capable of operating independently for extended periods  

### Primary Asset Types

**Dream-Bastions**
- Capital-scale war platforms  
- Equipped with relic-weapons, Aether resonance lances, and myth-locking arrays  
- Can impose narrative quarantine on regions or fleets  

**Pilgrimage Escorts**
- Mid-weight warships operating within close proximity of Living Fleets  
- Focused on interception, electronic warfare, and decoy projection  
- Crews often drawn from Guardian or Penitent archetypes  

**Severance Screens**
- Semi-autonomous or disposable defense formations  
- Deployed when Living Fleets must traverse hostile or unstable space  
- Designed to die visibly and decisively, buying time and symbolic weight  

### Internal Tension
Factional differences are most visible here:

- **Dracoshian-aligned elements** favor decisive annihilation of “false cycles”  
- **Seeker-aligned elements** prioritize containment, relic capture, and intelligence  

This conflict is never resolved—only managed.

---

## III. The Salvager Fleets  
**Role:** The Metabolism  
**Primary Function:** Adaptation, reconstruction, heresy recycling

The Salvager Fleets are the most mutable and most dangerous component of the Oneirotheon.

They do not merely recover ships.  
They **reinterpret them**.

### Core Characteristics
- Constantly changing composition and structure  
- Crews include engineers, myth-interpreters, and sanctioned heretics  
- No Salvager Fleet retains a stable configuration for long  

### Primary Roles

**Claiming**
- Reclamation of battlefields, dead systems, abandoned megastructures  
- All recovered material is treated as potential scripture  
- External insignia and ideological markers are stripped and recontextualized  

**Retrofit & Rebinding**
- Captured vessels are rebuilt to align with Oneirotheon myth-logic  
- Systems are renamed, ritualized, and repurposed  
- Former warships may become dream-temples; habitats may become reliquaries  

**Heresy Containment**
- Dangerous doctrines are not destroyed  
- They are isolated, studied, and metabolized  
- Salvager Fleets function as ideological digestion chambers  

### Unspoken Reality
Most internal assassinations, disappearances, and “ritual accidents” occur here.

Adaptation has a cost.

---

## Fleet Doctrine Axioms

The Oneirotheon adhere to three unbreakable principles:

1. **No Fleet Is Sacred**  
   Any fleet may be sacrificed to preserve the Pilgrimage.

2. **No Myth Is Final**  
   Even victories are provisional interpretations.

3. **No Structure Is Permanent**  
   Ships, hierarchies, and fleet roles evolve—or dissolve.

These axioms are why the Oneirotheon survive schisms that annihilate other civilizations.

---

## Role of the Primus Hierophant

The Primus Hierophant does not command fleets in a bureaucratic sense.

He:
- Adjusts migration arcs  
- Reassigns archetypal emphasis  
- Declares when fleets must separate or reunite  

A single dream-pronouncement may:
- Convert a Salvager Fleet into a new Living Fleet  
- Condemn a Heavy Fleet to sacrificial exile  
- Force population transfers without a spoken order  

This authority remains mythic, not administrative—and therefore uncontestable.

---

## Closing Note

The Oneirotheon fleet is not a navy.  
It is a **distributed pilgrimage engine**, engineered to endure ideological fracture, external threat, and internal contradiction without collapsing.

Their ships do not merely travel through space.  
They carry the Dream forward—whether it survives them or not.
