[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Interplay Between Scientific & Magical Tables

Here’s where it gets interesting: magic doesn’t just sit beside [[Physics]] — it alters the periodic behavior of known matter.

a. Influence on Matter Table
	•	Magically Active Isotopes: Some isotopes resonate with magical [[Domains]] (e.g., iron holds necromantic weight; silver resonates with Lux).
	•	Arcane Conductors: Copper, gold, and crystal lattices carry Aetherium better than others.
	•	Elemental Transmutations: Under magical fields, elements can spontaneously change states (stone “remembering” [[Fire]] → volcanic glass that bleeds heat).

b. Influence on [[Antimatter]] Table
	•	Stabilized [[Antimatter]]: Infused with Lux or Binding [[Domains]], preventing annihilation until triggered.
	•	Resonant Annihilation: Annihilation events release not just energy but magical effects (e.g., [[Antimatter]] + Vitae → radiant healing burst, [[Antimatter]] + Mortis → necrotic implosion).

c. Influence on [[Dark Sector Table]]
	•	Occult Gravity: Dark elements infused with Somnium or Umbra create “dream gravity,” bending not matter but perception.
	•	Void Alloys: Fusions of Dark Sector + Nullium → unobservable materials (functionally absent yet structural).
	•	Dark-Magic Fusion: Acts as seeds for pseudo-verses, microcosms embedded in material reality.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Emergent Properties Under Magic Influence

New Physical Behaviors
	•	Resonance States: Atoms can hum with magical frequency, producing light, heat, or illusions spontaneously.
	•	Living Matter: Materials infused with Vitae gain regenerative, self-healing properties.
	•	Morphic Matter: Terra + Somnium in alloys allows materials to shift shape by thought-command.
	•	Entropic Fuels: Mortis + Dark Matter becomes decay engines, consuming entropy into usable power.

New Magical Behaviors
	•	Rune Isotopes: Certain isotopes act as [[Natural]] runes — their atomic resonance embodies specific magical [[Domains]].
	•	Arcane Half-Lives: Magical infusions decay not by radiation but by conceptual exhaustion (e.g., a ward fading as memory of it dims).
	•	Quantum Spells: Matter + Logos (Veritas) allows atoms to “speak themselves,” creating self-casting enchantments.
