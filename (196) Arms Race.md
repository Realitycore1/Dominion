# (196) Arms Race

## What Happened

By Year 196 AL, the states accelerated military and strategic projects while claiming defensive necessity. Programs included robotic forces, mobile autonomous weapons platforms, mass-produced power armor, clean tactical nuclear arms, long-horizon artillery, planetary defense systems, secret programs such as Equilon's Project Ra, deep mantle mining, super-soldier programs, and orbital communications inherited from Skyview infrastructure.

## What Resulted

- Civilian systems and military systems became harder to separate.
- GalFed preparedness justified weapons that could be used against other Harminans.
- Space elevator construction continued despite rising tension, proving that civilization-building and militarization happened at the same time.
- The New World War became materially possible decades before it became politically unavoidable.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Arms Race weaponized the forgotten Discovery Clock.
