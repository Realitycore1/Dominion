1. Protoscience is “science before it becomes science.”

Not pseudoscience.
Not superstition.
Not junk philosophy.

It’s a domain where:
	•	someone sees a pattern
	•	they don’t have the tools to test it
	•	the ideas are ahead of the available instrumentation
	•	the theory is incomplete but not incoherent
	•	the data points in a direction no one can fully explore yet

Think:
Chemistry before chemistry existed, when it was still alchemy.
Evolution before Darwin, as natural philosophers noticing odd similarities.
Germ theory before microscopes, as doctors noticing contagion but not seeing pathogens.

It’s the scaffolding before the building goes up.

⸻

2. What separates protoscience from pseudoscience

Protoscience lacks evidence because the tools don’t exist yet.

Pseudoscience lacks evidence because the claims are unfocused, unfalsifiable, or incoherent.

The key difference:

✔ Protoscience = testable eventually

✘ Pseudoscience = untestable even in principle

Protoscience is embryonic science.
Pseudoscience is intellectual taxidermy.

⸻

3. Examples of historical protosciences

A. Alchemy → Chemistry

Alchemy was incorrect in many places, but it generated:
	•	laboratory methods
	•	distillation
	•	acids/bases
	•	early atomic theories
	•	systematic experimentation

It wasn’t wrong—it was early.

B. Astrobiology (before 1990)

We didn’t have exoplanet data yet, but the frameworks were there.

C. Continental Drift (Wegener)

He was right about tectonics but lacked the mechanism.

D. Cognitive science (pre-1970)

Before computers existed, we didn’t have a way to model the mind algorithmically.

E. Quantum foundations before QM

Planck, Einstein, and others were feeling the edge of something they couldn’t describe.

⸻

4. Modern fields that are in protoscientific stages

These aren’t pseudoscience—they’re early, messy, and awaiting better tools.
	•	Consciousness studies
	•	Brain uploading research
	•	Aether simulations (in-universe, like your Dominion’s field models)
	•	Cold fusion / LENR (not pseudoscience, just insufficiently reproducible)
	•	Exotic propulsion studies (NASA has real papers on Alcubierre metrics)
	•	Quantum gravity unification
	•	Linguistic-memetic evolution modeling
	•	Dark matter interaction physics
	•	Biomechanical senses beyond the classic five

These fields have potential, not certainty.

⸻

5. Why protoscience matters in your universe

Your worldbuilding thrives on high-tier tech and metaphysics (Axioms, Aether, Severance, Dominion physics). These systems wouldn’t present as fully mature everywhere—they would have protoscientific periods on various worlds and civilizations.

Protoscience is the cultural scaffolding before the cosmic truth arrives.

In Dominion-era physics:
	•	early civilizations misinterpret Aether as magic
	•	axiom fluctuations become “omens”
	•	Dracoshian manifestations become “dragons”
	•	Light/Dark Eternities become “gods” or “curses”
	•	temporal bleedthrough becomes prophecy

Protoscience is how primitive civilizations brush against the real mechanics long before they understand them.

It gives cultures texture, ideology, and mythic resonance.

⸻

6. In an alt-Earth context

Protoscience marks the boundaries between:
	•	alchemy and chemistry
	•	spiritism and early psychology
	•	parapsychology and neuroscience
	•	aether theories and electromagnetism
	•	astrology and astronomy
	•	ESP/clair senses and cognitive science

Each field contains a kernel of truth buried under layers of symbolic interpretation.

In your timeline—where engineered pandemics, political collapse, relic rediscovery, and pre-WW3 ideological warfare dominate—protoscience becomes a weapon:
	•	religious protoscience → movements interpreting relic radiations as divine signals
	•	political protoscience → conspiracy theories rooted in partially true black-ops projects
	•	technological protoscience → PMCs experimenting with half-understood biotech
	•	metaphysical protoscience → cults emerging from Severance echo-zones

Protoscience is the front line of misunderstanding that shapes public perception.
