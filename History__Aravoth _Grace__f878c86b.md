# Aravoth — Early History

Aravoth is the Supreme Heavenly Manifold, created and sustained by the Source, also known as Light Eternity, the Holy Will, the Grace-Fount, and the Heart of Aravoth.

The Source is not the creator of the Omniverse. The Omniverse predates it. Rather, the Source is the origination point of Aravoth itself: the infinite light at the realm’s center, endlessly emanating Grace.

Before recorded history, before mortal scripture, before the first archived cosmologies, Aravoth existed in silence.

It was empty, but not dead.

Its first native inhabitants were the Nuntii: luminous coherence-beings formed within Aravoth’s native Grace-field. The Nuntii did not discover Aravoth. They awoke with it. They became its messengers, stabilizers, custodians, and circulatory intelligence.

All other souls and consciousnesses discovered Aravoth later.

As more beings entered, Aravoth expanded. This expansion was not conquest, colonization, or territorial acquisition. Aravoth grows by reception. Every compatible consciousness adds depth, relation, memory, architecture, and possibility to the heavenly manifold.

Thus Aravoth is not a fixed palace or singular afterlife. It is a living heaven-field whose scale increases with communion.

The Source speaks rarely, and only to those compatible with its purposes. Its speech is not ordinary language but Grace-alignment: vision, clarity, harmonic instruction, direct knowing, or revelation mediated through the Nuntii.

The Shadow claims by ownership.

Aravoth receives by Grace.

This is the oldest distinction between false heaven and true heaven.
