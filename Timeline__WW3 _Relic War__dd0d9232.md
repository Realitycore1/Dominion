Suburban warfare is a form of urban warfare that takes place in suburban areas, or areas immediately outside of cities and metropolitan areas. It typically involves conflicts between organized criminal groups, paramilitary organizations, and rival factions over territory, resources, and control. Suburban warfare is often characterized by heavy firepower, explosive devices, and intimidation tactics. Gangsters and militias use the cover of residential areas to conduct ambushes, drive-by shootings, and other forms of violent crime. One of the primary reasons for suburban warfare is the increasing blurring of the line between cities and suburbs. In many cases, suburban areas have become more densely populated and integrated with cities, leading to increased competition for resources and control over criminal enterprises. Conflicts in suburban areas often have a significant impact on civilians living in the area. They can experience high levels of violence, forced displacement, and a decline in the quality of life. The most common victims of suburban warfare are innocent bystanders caught in the crossfire of gang violence or collateral damage from attacks. Children, women, and the elderly are particularly vulnerable during these conflicts.

The detonation of a 100Megaton fusion bomb in a major city would have catastrophic consequences, with the potential to cause widespread destruction and casualties on a global scale. Firstly, the energy released from a 100Megaton fusion bomb is estimated to be equivalent to approximately 120,000 times the energy released by the atomic bombs dropped on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. The blast would release a huge amount of heat, radiation, and kinetic energy that would incinerate everything within its radius. Secondly, the blast wave would be so powerful that it could level buildings, uproot trees, and cause massive fires. The resulting shockwave could also produce seismic waves that could damage [[Infrastructure]] and trigger tsunamis in nearby bodies of water. Thirdly, the release of radioactive material from the bomb would have severe long-term health effects on anyone exposed to it. The radioactive fallout could contaminate food sources, water supplies, and the environment for decades or even centuries, posing a significant threat to public health. Finally, the destruction of major cities and loss of life and [[Infrastructure]] could destabilize global governance and economic [[Systems]], leading to political instability and chaos. In conclusion, the detonation of a 100Megaton fusion bomb in a major city would be a catastrophic event with potentially devastating consequences for the world.

2075 – Orbital Catastrophe

Kessler Cascade: Years of satellite warfare between CRINK, Vatican, and Caliphate forces culminate in massive debris storms. Entire orbital shells become unusable. Communications fragment; GPS collapses; weather prediction fails.

The ISS Destroyed:

A splinter Crusader extremist group, the Order of the Spear, hijacks a [[Europolis]] orbital transport.

They ram it into the International Space Station, claiming it was “a temple of false science” that rejected divine [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]].

The station shatters into debris, its final transmissions broadcasting astronauts’ frantic prayers in multiple languages before [[Silence]].

Global Reaction:

Vatican: Condemns the attack publicly but secretly benefits, as it discredits secular relic-skeptic factions.

Caliphate: Propaganda celebrates ISS destruction as “heaven cleansed of idols.”

[[Europolis]]: Outrage — calls it the “murder of reason.” Protests erupt in burned-out capitals demanding revenge.

CRINK: Declares ISS attack proof that relic mania has doomed the planet; accelerates Mandate orbital weapons development.

Civilian Impact:

The ISS had been one of the last symbols of global unity — its fall convinces millions that reconciliation is impossible.

Panic spreads: rumors claim [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] caused the debris storm, that God himself “cast down” the station.

Space programs collapse; surviving [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] retreat into secrecy.

Cultural Fallout:

Artists in ruined cities paint the ISS breaking apart across building walls, captioned: “We fell with it.”

Refugee camps nickname the year “When Heaven Fell.”
