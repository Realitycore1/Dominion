TULPA

Classification: Cognitive Entity / Thought-Construct / Memeform Sentient
Status: Rare, Emergent

Core Concept

A Tulpa is belief given persistence. Not illusion, not spirit—consensus hallucination made real. They arise when enough minds imagine the same thing with sufficient intensity, repetition, and emotional charge.

Physicality
	•	Form varies wildly; often humanoid but unstable
	•	Features may “glitch” or simplify when not observed
	•	Bodies appear semi-solid, like matter struggling to agree on itself
	•	Details sharpen when watched; degrade when ignored

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Highly intelligent, but psychologically fragile
	•	Obsessed with maintaining belief in themselves
	•	Actively manipulate narratives, rumors, cults, or myths
	•	Fear disbelief more than death

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Sustained by attention, belief, and repetition
	•	Can be wounded by skepticism or contradiction
	•	Faith-based relics can stabilize or destroy them
	•	Dangerous in mass media zones or ideologically volatile populations

Narrative Role

Tulpa represent the weaponization of belief. They are what happens when ideas refuse to stay abstract.

VETALA (Blood-Drinkers)

Classification: Possessive Undead / Blood Parasite Sentients
Status: Active, Hidden

Core Concept

Vetala are corpse-occupiers, not revenants. They inhabit dead bodies and animate them through blood consumption, using blood as both fuel and memory substrate.

They are closer to parasites than vampires.

Physicality
	•	Appear corpse-like but mobile
	•	Bodies often hung in unnatural postures when inactive
	•	Facial expressions are wrong—too still or too alert
	•	Blood visibly circulates unnaturally within them

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Extremely intelligent and cruelly curious
	•	Speak fluently; enjoy riddles, manipulation, humiliation
	•	Prefer psychological games over brute force
	•	Can abandon a body if damaged

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Blood contains experiential memory for them
	•	Drinking from victims gives insight into fears and secrets
	•	Resistant to normal undead banishment
	•	Fire and consecration disrupt possession

Narrative Role

Vetala are predators of identity, not just flesh—perfect infiltrators, interrogators, and corrupters.

LAMIA

Classification: Hybrid Predator / Curse-Bloodline Entity
Status: Stable Lineages

Core Concept

Lamia are matriarchal serpentine humanoids, often created through curses, bloodline corruption, or divine punishment. They are not inherently evil—but are unapologetically predatory.

Physicality
	•	Upper torso humanoid; lower body serpentine
	•	Skin patterned with scales, often iridescent
	•	Eyes slit-pupiled, hypnotic
	•	Fangs or venom glands present

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Highly intelligent and socially strategic
	•	Prefer seduction, manipulation, and territorial control
	•	Strong familial and lineage loyalty
	•	Often rule hidden enclaves or undercities

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Resistant to poisons and toxins
	•	Venom often has paralytic or hallucinogenic effects
	•	Some variants drain vitality or fertility
	•	Faith artifacts react unpredictably—attraction or repulsion

Narrative Role

Lamia embody temptation without apology and the power of inherited curse turned into culture.

RAKSHASA

Classification: Shapeshifting Predator / Inverted Moral Entity
Status: Active, Highly Dangerous

Core Concept

Rakshasa are sentient deceivers who wear morality as camouflage. They are not demons—but their ethics are reversed or alien.

They enjoy corruption as an art form.

Physicality
	•	True form often bestial or monstrous
	•	Can perfectly imitate humanoid forms
	•	Minor tells: reversed palms, shadow distortion, scent anomalies
	•	Eyes reflect predatory amusement

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Extremely intelligent, theatrical, patient
	•	Feed on social collapse, betrayal, and fear
	•	Prefer long-term manipulation over violence
	•	Often embed themselves in power structures

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Illusions resist detection magic
	•	Lies reshape reality locally
	•	Grace reacts violently to prolonged exposure
	•	Killing one often releases psychic backlash

Narrative Role

Rakshasa are corruption with intent—villains who don’t conquer worlds, they ruin them.

⸻

OKAMI

Classification: Divine Beast / Guardian Spirit
Status: Rare, Declining

Core Concept

Okami are wolf-like divine guardians, spirits of land, oath, and balance. They are not angels—but serve a similar stabilizing role at a regional scale.

Physicality
	•	Wolf or wolf-humanoid forms
	•	White, silver, or ash fur
	•	Glowing markings like calligraphy or constellations
	•	Eyes luminous, calm, judgmental

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Highly intelligent, non-verbal or selectively verbal
	•	Protect territories or bloodlines
	•	Intervene only when balance is threatened
	•	Can be merciful or merciless

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Empowered by harmony and rightful order
	•	Weakens in corrupted or Malice-saturated zones
	•	Grace-adjacent but independent
	•	Can sanctify or curse land

Narrative Role

Okami represent natural law without dogma—the world correcting itself when gods fail
