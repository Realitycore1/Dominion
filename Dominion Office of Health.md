# Dominion Office of Health

## Summary

The Dominion Office of Health governs public health, hospitals, longevity medicine, mental health access, epidemic response, trauma recovery, genetic medicine, and medical emergency coordination.

In an immortal or near-immortal civilization, health policy is not about avoiding death alone. It is about maintaining livable bodies and minds across centuries.

## Mandate

The Office oversees:

- public clinics;
- hospitals;
- mental health systems;
- epidemic response;
- vaccination and immune updates;
- longevity maintenance;
- trauma rehabilitation;
- genetic disease repair;
- medical licensing;
- biowarfare detection with security agencies.

## Powers

It may quarantine outbreaks, issue medical standards, fund clinics, require health reporting during emergencies, and coordinate disaster medicine.

## Limits

It cannot force elective enhancement, deny care to dissidents, conceal public-health failures, or treat mental collapse as moral weakness.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Office through clinic queues, body-maintenance schedules, therapy access, child health visits, outbreak alerts, and long-form consent screens.

## Story Use

Use this agency to keep immortality grounded. Bodies still hurt, minds still break, clinics still run late, and care still has to be delivered by people.
