# Terra Rough Draft - Continuity and Expansion Notes

## Current Draft

- Complete compressed narrative draft: prologue, 4 parts, and 28 chapters.
- Current scale: approximately 29,000 words.
- Intended full-novel scale: 100,000-130,000 words.
- Current ending: first nuclear winter, bridging into [[Lithic Manuscript 2 - Sol]].

## Existing Canon Characters Used

### Abdulrahman bin Ali Al-Saud

- Descendant of the Saudi royal family.
- Designed before implantation for disease resistance, oxygen efficiency, physical retention, stress recovery, memory stability, and broad dynastic attractiveness.
- Military logistician and strategic commander.
- Moderate coalition-builder during regional radicalization.
- First caliph of the Yamama Caliphate.
- Central internal conflict: whether any choice can be free when his body and education were designed for rule.

### General Clayton Voss

- Constitutionalist nationalist.
- Opposes biometric military integration and supranational authority.
- Becomes a principal leader of the American restoration movement and Operation Home Run.
- His victory restores formal government but cannot prevent new continuity systems from escaping accountability.

### Senator Adrienne Solace

- Global-renewal federalist.
- Supports integrated governance, AI-assisted constitutional systems, and universal resource access.
- Supports emergency authority after the capital bombing, then loses control of the institutions using it.
- Survives Operation Home Run and becomes a constrained witness to the old regime.

These names and exact relationships are provisional until promoted into character profiles.

## Provisional Timeline Decisions

- 1889: Continental Atlas Congress in Constantinople creates the unauthorized layered atlas.
- 1958: Daniel and Ruth identify the Stability Index's internal-collapse branch.
- 2000: Leila becomes a frontline Wetwire investigator.
- 2004: Nadiya is born.
- 2028: Abdulrahman is born; the capital bombing destroys most of Congress and the judicial assembly.
- 2032: Operation Home Run retakes the American capital.
- 2036: Axum Reliquary incident; eight-year-old Abdulrahman discovers the implications of his engineered design.
- 2040: Fourth International publicly forms.
- 2048: European Union dissolves and Europolis consolidation begins.
- 2068: Abdulrahman accepts appointment as first caliph of Yamama.
- 2078: Paris is destroyed and the Exchange cascades globally.
- 2079: First-winter enclaves begin forming civic rules and reconnecting by radio and caravan.

## Expansion Priorities

To reach the planned novel length, expand lived scenes rather than adding more historical markers.

1. Add 8,000-12,000 words to the atlas-congress arc through delegate relationships, family consequences, and the atlas's suppression.
2. Add 15,000-20,000 words to Daniel, Ruth, Miriam, and Hana through Korea, RAND, Berlin, Cuba, Vietnam, cognition trials, and family strain.
3. Add 20,000-25,000 words to Leila's medical arc through Wetwire cases, Accerisma dependence, SOIS conflict, and the Shadow Pandemic.
4. Add 15,000-20,000 words to Nadiya, Voss, and Solace through election reporting, the capital bombing investigation, martial law, and Operation Home Run.
5. Add 15,000-20,000 words to Abdulrahman through childhood, military service, famine logistics, the formation of Yamama, and the burdens of the caliphate.
6. Add 8,000-12,000 words to the final decade through the Taiwan Strait, Equilibrium Pact, deep-state investigation, cold fusion, and the Exchange.

## Continuity Guardrails

- Treat partisan claims as factional claims unless physical evidence confirms them.
- Do not make the Exchange the work of one villain or ideology.
- Keep Wetwire frightening through biology, environment, and uncertainty rather than constant creature violence.
- Keep Accerisma genuinely useful as well as harmful.
- Preserve the difference between cognitive inference and literal mind reading.
- Keep Abdulrahman's designed traits advantageous but never equivalent to moral superiority.
- Do not confirm who launched the first 2078 weapon unless later canon settles attribution.
- End Terra with survival, not recovery; reconstruction belongs to Sol.
