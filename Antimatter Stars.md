An [[Antimatter]] Star is a stellar-mass object composed primarily of [[Antimatter]] — anti-protons, anti-neutrons, and positrons — undergoing the same thermonuclear fusion processes as ordinary [[Stars]], but with reversed baryonic charge.

In isolation, an [[Antimatter]] star behaves identically to a normal star in terms of structure, fusion chain reactions, and luminosity.
The [[Key]] difference is in interactions with normal matter:
	•	Direct matter–[[Antimatter]] contact results in complete annihilation, releasing γ-ray photons and other high-energy [[Particles]].
	•	Even minute accretion of normal matter leads to intense bursts of gamma radiation from the star’s surface.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), [[Antimatter]] [[Stars]] are considered Matter-Sign-Inverted Stellar Engines, often arising in [[Antimatter]]-dominant [[Regions]] of a [[Verse]] or deliberately seeded for high-energy weaponization or controlled annihilation power plants.
	•	Core State: Standard proton–proton chain or CNO cycle, but with anti-nuclei.
	•	Radiation Profile: Normal stellar spectra, with possible high-energy gamma spikes from annihilation events.
	•	Stability: Highly stable in isolation, catastrophic in mixed-matter environments.

II. [[VFF]] Integration

In the [[VFF]], [[Antimatter]] [[Stars]] serve as strategic and energetic extremes:
	•	Annihilation Energy Wells — Harvest annihilation output by feeding controlled streams of matter.
	•	Deep-Space Defensive Barriers — Emit lethal radiation fields to deter approach.
	•	Baryon-Sign Control [[Systems]] — Balance matter/[[Antimatter]] distribution for large-scale [[Verse]] stabilization experiments.
	•	Annihilation Beacons — Extreme-range Omniversal navigation markers detectable via γ-ray signature.

	•	Engineered War Zones — Used as annihilation triggers in Omniversal-scale conflicts.
	•	Magic-Integrated Verses — Regarded as “cursed suns” or “the opposite fires” of creation.
