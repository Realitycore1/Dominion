The City That Lives Beside the Fire

Location:
On the western rim of the former Pacific coast, built into an extinct-looking but still venting volcanic complex.

Anchor:
A colossal Fire-Siphon Cathedral that bleeds Archontes Fire into controlled basalt channels.

Doctrine:

“Fire is not evil. It is correction. Learn its rhythm.”

Society:
	•	Fire-priests and engineers
	•	Citizens trained to read flame patterns
	•	Children taught silence through heat meditation

Relationship to Archontes:
Transactional.
They don’t rebel. They negotiate thermodynamics with divinity.

Narrative Role:
Shows that survival through adaptation is possible — but at a cost.
