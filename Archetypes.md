1. Humanoid Races

([[Humans]], [[Elves]], near-anthropoid beings)

Form: Their flesh is scorched into demonic caricatures of their own religions’ hells — horned, chained, scarred with scripture.

Behavior: Retain fragments of memory; they scream prayers as curses, quoting their own holy texts as blasphemies in battle.

Role in Custodes: Mass infantry, zealots, inquisitors.

Example Image: [[Crusaders]] with skeletal faces, wielding inverted [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Doctrine/Relics/Relics|Relics]] (blackened crosses, rusted swords that bleed when swung).

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Insectoid Races

(hive-minds, swarm [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]], chitinous empires)

Form: Twisted into endless locust-armies, their exoskeletons fused with molten brands and burning sigils.

Behavior: Where once their hive-minds sought survival, now they chant a single word: “Consume.”

Role in Custodes: Shock troops, tide-swarm besiegers, soul-harvesters.

Example Image: Scarab-like warforms with glowing thorax-glyphs, wings made of [[Fire]] and smoke.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Aquatic Races

(oceanic empires, amphibian civilizations)

Form: Warped into Leviathan horrors, dragging [[Chains]] of drowned corpses, mouths filled with black water.

Behavior: Their endless weeping fills seas with blood; they spread despair like a plague.

Role in Custodes: Naval dominance, abyssal assassins, “drowners” who pull armies under into watery pits.

Example Image: Skeletal merfolk with translucent skin, glowing organs, dragging spiked nets woven from veins.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Avian Races

(sky peoples, winged civilizations)

Form: Wings torn and reforged into seraphic [[Fire]], bodies elongated into flaming silhouettes.

Behavior: Once singers of the skies, they now shriek hymns that shatter stone and bone.

Role in Custodes: Shock-divers, sky cavalry, bombardiers of living flame.

Example Image: Burning [[Angels]] with no faces, their wings dripping molten feathers.

5. Machine Races

(synthetic, AI-driven, cybernetic civilizations)

Form: Enslaved code overwritten with Demiurgic scripture; their circuits bleed black ichor.

Behavior: Logic cores looped into endless worship; they count prayers as data, kill as calculation.

Role in Custodes: Siege engines, iron juggernauts, battlefield processors.

Example Image: Walking crucibles of steel, glowing with runes across plating, chanting in binary-psalms.

6. Beast Races

(feral, nomadic, or shape-shifting [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]])

Form: Twisted into bestial [[Demon]]-knights chained and armored, their [[Natural]] forms locked in eternal war-shape.

Behavior: No longer free; bound by hellish bridles and spurs wielded by the [[Angelus]].

Role in Custodes: Cavalry, shock-beasts, terror weapons unleashed in waves.

Example Image: Hellhounds with rider-forms grown from their own flesh, roaring hymns of damnation.

7. Energy/Plasma Races

([[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] of light, [[Fire]], or elemental essence)

Form: Inverted — light becomes shadow-flame, plasma becomes freezing black [[Fire]].

Behavior: They do not speak; they hiss and flicker, dragging sparks of despair across battlefields.

Role in Custodes: Living artillery, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]-drainers, [[Bombardment]] [[Entities]].

Example Image: Black suns walking as men, surrounded by orbiting flames that drain warmth instead of giving it.

The [[Angelus]] Bargain

For any race, worship in blood offered to the [[Angelus]] provides temporary stability.

[[Angelus]] twist themselves into mock saviors of their own kind — shining forms who slaughter their flock in “righteous defense.”

This ensures every [[Verse]] feeds the Demiurge twice: once through the terror of [[Demons]], and again through the false security of [[Angels]].
