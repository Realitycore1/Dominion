an extreme form of resource extraction that involves fracturing, dismantling, or wholly destroying a planetary body to harvest its internal materials on a massive scale. It represents one of the most violent and technologically advanced methods of resource acquisition, reserved for civilizations at or beyond Type II on the [[Kardashev Scale]] 
Strategic Purpose
	•	To extract core-level resources inaccessible by traditional mining: neutronium alloys, exotic isotopes, heavy metals, fusion Catalysts.
	•	To repurpose planetary mass for megastructure construction (e.g., [[Forge Worlds]], Dyson Swarms, Shield Worlds).
	•	To deny enemy factions access to strategic worlds or biospheres (scorched void policy).

⸻

II. Planet Cracking Methods

1. Gravitic Shearing
	•	Uses gravitational manipulation to rip apart planetary strata.
	•	Applied via orbital [[Graviton]] generators or micro-singularity arrays.
	•	Produces massive tectonic disruption followed by layered implosion.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Thermal Core Detonation
	•	A planet’s core is superheated or artificially destabilized, causing global fracture.
	•	Typically achieved with penetrator-class fusion lances, [[Antimatter]] cores, or induction fields.
	•	Exploits thermal expansion to crack the mantle outward like an overpressurized shell.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Fracture Seeding
	•	Implants core disruptor nodes deep into the planet using impact drills or bore cannons.
	•	Nodes generate harmonic frequencies that resonate with the lithosphere, triggering planetary-scale fissures.
	•	Useful for precision cracking—e.g., to preserve a moon while destroying its host planet.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Dimensional Phase Collapse
	•	The most exotic method: reality anchors are inverted to destabilize spacetime within a planetary volume.
	•	The planet doesn’t explode—it ceases to hold cohesion in its current dimensional state.
	•	Leaves behind raw, phase-fractured material for harvesting.

⸻

III. Extraction Process

After cracking, autonomous gravity-lift harvester swarms collect debris and transport it to orbital refineries or nanoforges. This process includes:
	•	Mass Displacement Tethers: haul continent-sized chunks of core material.
	•	Resource Codex Scanners: categorize and process elements during lift.
	•	Molecular Recyclers: break matter down into base elements for resequencing.
