## Summary
Definition: A semi-permeable, hyperdimensional [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] that regulates exchange between the [[Verse]] and extra-versal [[Domains]].
## Details
Properties:

Maintains one-way or restricted causality into/out of the [[Verse]].

Filters or converts [[Exotic Matter]] and energy entering from adjacent [[Regions]].

Can flex or thin in localized zones, creating "lacunae."

Risks: Breach events can allow unstable phenomena such as strange matter or non-causal [[Entities]] to enter.
