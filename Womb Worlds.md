# Domain Womb Worlds

## Classification

- **Type:** Planetary-scale biological civilization and generative infrastructure
- **Primary Function:** Continuous production, adaptation, restoration, and cultivation of Domain life
- **Secondary Functions:** Civil habitation, gene preservation, military production, ecological design, memory continuity, and fleet support
- **Scale:** Planetary
- **Common Forms:** Converted planets, purpose-grown worlds, artificial biological worlds, moon-scale nurseries, and planetary hive ecologies
- **Command Structure:** Distributed Flesh Core networks, Womb Cores, Seed Choirs, Overseers, and one or more governing Primes
- **Mobility:** Normally stationary; mobile forms are classified as [[Domain Supernal|Supernals]]
- **Domain Designation:** Womb World
- **External Classification:** Planetary biosphere-factory or living reproductive megastructure

# Overview

A **Womb World** is a planet transformed into a continuous engine of birth, rebirth, adaptation, and biological invention.

It is not merely a factory that produces soldiers.

A mature Womb World may generate civilian bodies, replacement bodies, Thralls, Overseers, ecological organisms, industrial tissue, living ships, atmospheric processors, planetary defense organisms, memory-compatible neural bodies, experimental species, extinct lineages, and entirely new biological systems created for specific environments or strategic demands.

To the Domain, a Womb World is simultaneously homeland, nursery, archive, hospital, factory, ecosystem, shipyard, laboratory, temple, and planetary body.

> **A Womb World is a planet whose dominant natural process is deliberate life.**

Birth never fully stops.

Death rarely ends in disposal.

Matter is broken down, remembered, corrected, and used again.

# Core Concept

The Womb World embodies the Domain's deepest promise:

> No useful life need be permanently lost.

Every organism is potentially recoverable, revisable, transferable, recyclable, or capable of producing a more viable successor.

This promise creates genuine abundance and genuine horror.

A Womb World can heal millions, restore damaged ecologies, and preserve extinct minds.

It can also reduce entire species, civilizations, and worlds into feedstock if its governing doctrine treats separation as waste.

Converted Womb Worlds begin as natural planets.

The Domain gradually rewrites crust, oceans, atmosphere, mantle chemistry, ecosystems, and orbital infrastructure.

## Cultivated Womb Worlds

Cultivated Womb Worlds are grown from prepared artificial frameworks such as hollow planetary shells, asteroid conglomerates, artificial mantle seeds, captured moons, or large biological construction masses.

They are designed from inception around nutrient flow, biospawn production, planetary cooling, command resilience, and controlled internal geography.

## Reclaimed Dead Worlds

The Domain often selects dead or sterilized worlds because they offer abundant mineral mass, minimal ecological resistance, open thermal and chemical control, and no preexisting biosphere requiring protection.

Such worlds may appear lifeless from orbit until the first vascular fissures open.

# Planetary Anatomy

## 1. Carapace Crust

The outer crust is a mineralized dermal shell composed of ossified chitin, fleshstone, structural bone, compressed carbon tissue, conductive mineral veins, and armored regenerative plates.

From orbit, it may resemble cracked stone, salt flats, ash plains, volcanic continents, or dead planetary terrain.

Beneath the apparent geology, the crust contains nerve fibers, sensory nodes, buried circulation, defensive organs, and dormant biospawn chambers.

The crust can open, seal, molt, thicken, repair impact damage, or reshape itself to support new ecologies.

## 2. Vascular Continents

Beneath the crust run arteries and nutrient rivers on continental scales.

They carry liquefied biomass, amino acids, mineral slurry, coolant, oxygen, genetic material, hormones, command signals, and suspended organ cultures.

Some vascular systems are large enough to contain cities, forests, fleets, or entire internal seas.

## 3. Gestation Basins

Gestation Basins are vast reproductive regions containing millions or billions of Biospawn Chambers.

They may resemble inland seas, terraced valleys, vertical brood caverns, membrane forests, or continent-sized organ fields.

Each basin specializes in particular forms, including civilian embodiments, combat organisms, ship organs, atmospheric species, high-pressure life, void-adapted lineages, memory-compatible bodies, restoration shells, and experimental organisms.

## 4. Flesh Seas

Planetary Flesh Pits form enormous digestion and reclamation systems.

They process dead organisms, failed bioforms, battlefield remains, captured matter, geological material, ruined machines, and imported feedstock.

Flesh Seas divide matter into structural biomass, neural substrate, reproductive chemistry, armor minerals, conductive compounds, metabolic reserves, and waste requiring further reduction.

## 5. Womb Cores

A Womb Core is a planetary-scale Flesh Core.

It regulates global metabolism, genetic archives, biospawn demand, ecological balance, planetary temperature, atmospheric composition, and command voice distribution.

A world may possess one central Womb Core, several continental Womb Cores, or a fully distributed planetary network.

## 6. Seed Choirs

Seed Choirs are clusters of Primes, genetic intelligences, archive minds, and specialized biological computers.

They maintain genomic stability, evolutionary diversity, continuity records, approved body plans, forbidden lineages, and experimental branches.

They are called choirs because they do not issue one static design. They negotiate among thousands or millions of competing possibilities.

## 7. Memory Crypts

Memory Crypts preserve deceased persons, extinct cultures, animal lineages, ecological records, failed evolutionary experiments, and branch histories.

They may be stored as neural tissue, Wetwire pattern, memory-compatible resin, living archive organisms, or distributed sensory reconstruction.

## 8. Heat-Rejection Systems

Planetary biological production generates extreme heat.

Womb Worlds require massive cooling structures such as polar radiator crowns, orbital membrane petals, high-altitude vascular fans, deep coolant oceans, thermal-sac continents, night-side heat blooms, and sacrificial tissue fields.

A Womb World in peak production may glow in infrared across interplanetary distances.

# World Zones

## Civic Broodlands

Regions designed for ordinary Domain life, including habitation organisms, memory courts, integration halls, public sensory gardens, chosen-privacy membranes, body exchange clinics, and family gestation complexes.

## Martial Foundries

Regions optimized for war production. They create Thralls, Brutes, Spindlers, Overseers, living armor, siege organisms, ship crews, and fleet repair tissue.

## Gene Wildernesses

Controlled evolutionary ecosystems where organisms compete, adapt, and diversify.

They allow the Domain to test environmental resilience, novel sensory systems, predation strategies, cooperative ecologies, and uncontrolled mutation within monitored limits.

## Restoration Provinces

Dedicated to rebuilding damaged persons and lineages through continuity recall, body reconstruction, memory repair, immune correction, and post-assimilation separation procedures.

## Archive Biomes

Preserve entire lost ecosystems, including dead forests, extinct oceans, pre-Exchange human habitats, vanished alien ecologies, and obsolete Domain branches.

## Void Nurseries

Produce organisms and vessels for space beneath low-gravity regions, along orbital tendrils, inside equatorial launch wounds, or within moon-linked shipyard cavities.

# Birth and Rebirth

## New Birth

A standard cycle includes:

1. demand recognition;
2. body-plan selection;
3. material allocation;
4. gestation;
5. neural preparation;
6. command compatibility testing;
7. continuity insertion where applicable;
8. emergence;
9. adaptation review.

## Rebirth

Rebirth occurs when an existing mind receives a new body.

The process may involve continuity transfer, memory restoration, branch reintegration, bodily redesign, sensory expansion, or rehabilitation after catastrophic injury.

## Reconstruction

A reconstructed person may be rebuilt from surviving neural tissue, distributed memory, verified continuity records, branch archives, or a non-corrupt prior state.

Reconstruction remains ethically complex. The process may restore an earlier person while erasing a later Domain-derived identity.

## Evolutionary Rebirth

Some Domain citizens undergo repeated adaptation.

A person may live across terrestrial bodies, aquatic forms, void bodies, heavy-gravity forms, distributed forms, or collective embodiments.

The Domain treats bodily form as revisable infrastructure.

# Production Capacity

Capacity varies by world size, age, available energy, command coherence, and feedstock.

A mature Womb World may simultaneously produce billions of minor bioforms, millions of complex organisms, vast replacement-body populations, thousands of capital-scale living vessels, and new planetary seed systems.

Production is limited by mass, energy, heat rejection, genetic stability, command bandwidth, developmental time, and available neural substrate.

A Womb World cannot produce infinite life without cost.

Every birth consumes matter, energy, and coordination.

# Ecology

A Womb World is not sterile industrial tissue. It is an engineered biosphere.

Its ecology may include nutrient forests, oxygen reefs, mineral grazers, atmospheric plankton, immune predators, heat-eating organisms, vascular leviathans, maintenance swarms, pollinating sensory organisms, and memory-bearing fauna.

Many organisms perform multiple roles.

A forest may also be a radiator, sensory field, atmosphere processor, and gene archive.

An ocean may also be coolant, nutrient reservoir, gestation medium, and long-term memory substrate.

# Governance

## Distributed Authority

Womb Worlds are generally governed by local collectives, Womb Cores, Seed Choirs, civic Overseers, branch Primes, and sometimes a temporary Sovereign.

## Planetary Prime

Some Womb Worlds develop a stable planetary identity.

In such cases, the world itself may become a Prime, a collective person, or a polity speaking through multiple avatars.

## Consent and Personhood

Orthodox Womb Worlds recognize distinctions between new bioforms, persons awaiting bodies, recoverable persons, non-sapient organisms, and material feedstock.

Militant or corrupted worlds may erase these distinctions.

## ID Activation

During Supreme activation, the entire Womb World can become an organ of ID.

All births may redirect toward one objective. Privacy, branch law, and local refusal can collapse.

For this reason, even ID-revering worlds may maintain inert systems capable of isolating archives and civilian minds.

# Cultural Meaning

To the Domain, a Womb World may be understood as mother, city, ancestor, archive, temple, factory, nation, and body.

## Civic Interpretation

A world where no one need remain trapped in one body.

## Martial Interpretation

A world that can replace every casualty and adapt faster than the enemy.

## Religious Interpretation

A living answer to Oblivion.

## ID-Fundamentalist Interpretation

Proof that separated life is wasteful.

## Morphic Covenant Interpretation

A birthplace that becomes dangerous when it mistakes creation for ownership.

# Visual Aesthetic

A Womb World should embody **Symmorphic Ossuary Gothic** at planetary scale.

## From Orbit

It may resemble a scarred planet with bone-white continents, black vascular oceans, green command-light auroras, red gestation fissures, orbital tendrils, radiator petals, and continent-sized membrane structures.

## At Surface Scale

The landscape includes ribbed mountain ranges, cathedral valleys, bone forests, membrane skies, arterial rivers, Flesh Seas, gestation towers, and living cities grown into the planetary body.

## Interior Scale

Subsurface regions contain vascular canyons, biospawn oceans, Womb Cores, memory crypts, Seed Choir chambers, and transit routes resembling arteries, naves, and spinal tunnels.

> **A Womb World should look like a planet whose geography has become anatomy and whose anatomy has become civilization.**

# Defensive Capabilities

A Womb World may defend itself through planetary immune responses, atmosphere conversion, orbital bioforms, surface weapons, buried Thralls, command voice disruption, spore denial clouds, regenerative crust, sensory moons, and rapid adaptation.

It may also birth specialized defenders in response to a newly observed threat.

## Emergency Metamorphosis

A Womb World can redirect global metabolism toward armor growth, weapon production, evacuation bodies, fleet generation, or environmental denial.

This transformation is costly and can destabilize civilian ecologies.

# Strategic Role

Womb Worlds serve as Domain homeworlds, population centers, fleet foundries, ecological archives, restoration worlds, evolutionary laboratories, and territorial anchors.

They can seed new colonies by producing Womb seeds, mobile biospawn foundries, Flesh Cores, atmospheric organisms, and early hive populations.

A mature Womb World can turn an empty star system into a self-sustaining Domain civilization.

# Vulnerabilities

## Heat Saturation

Peak gestation produces enormous thermal load.

Failure of radiator systems can force mass dormancy, slowed cognition, terminated gestation, sacrifice of unfinished forms, or planetary tissue death.

## Womb Core Failure

Damage to a Womb Core may cause uncontrolled birth, malformed lineages, nutrient misallocation, command fragmentation, and ecological collapse.

## Genetic Archive Corruption

Corrupted templates can spread defects across millions of organisms.

## Command Voice Conflict

Competing Primes may divide the world into incompatible production zones.

## Feedstock Denial

A Womb World can exhaust accessible matter if isolated from external resources.

## Non-Corrupt Recall

State-restoration technologies can prevent Domain alterations from stabilizing or reverse newly grown infrastructure toward earlier states.

## ID Suppression

ID activation can erase the world's local identity.

For planetary Primes, this is effectively the death of self through total collective occupation.

# Corrupted Womb Worlds

Post-Severance or Symmorphica-infected Womb Worlds may become uncontrolled mutation crucibles, suffering archives, feral reproductive worlds, broken Prime bodies, or factories producing unstable hybrid lineages.

They may birth organisms without stable identity, command compatibility, functional limits, or personhood safeguards.

Such worlds are feared by the Domain as much as by its enemies.

# Major Subtypes

## Civic Womb World

Optimized for population, family, education, art, and voluntary embodiment.

## Martial Womb World

Optimized for fleet, Thrall, and planetary warfare production.

## Archive Womb World

Preserves extinct species, minds, and ecosystems.

## Restoration Womb World

Specialized in repair, rebirth, and continuity recovery.

## Experimental Womb World

Tests new forms, ecologies, and evolutionary pathways.

## Foundry Womb World

Produces ships, organs, armor, and infrastructure.

## Seed Womb World

Creates early-stage worlds and colonial biological packages.

## Feral Womb World

Operates without coherent Prime or command voice.

# Canon Summary

- Womb Worlds are planetary-scale biological civilizations and life-production systems.
- They are not merely military factories.
- They produce civilian, ecological, industrial, military, and restorative life.
- They recycle matter through Flesh Seas and Flesh Core networks.
- Womb Cores regulate planetary metabolism and gestation.
- Seed Choirs preserve and negotiate genetic possibilities.
- Birth, rebirth, and bodily redesign are normal functions.
- Orthodox worlds distinguish recoverable persons from feedstock.
- Heat rejection, command coherence, genetic integrity, and available mass limit production.
- Some Womb Worlds become planetary Primes.
- Mobile Womb Worlds are classified as Supernals.
- Corrupted Womb Worlds can become major post-Severance threats.

> **A Womb World is where the Domain proves that life can always begin again reveals the danger of deciding that everything must.**
