# Dominion Vault Cleanup Audit

## Purpose

This note tracks the first cleanup pass after the Dominion canon was clarified as a dual government: Federal Government for the living present, Royal Government for the endangered future.

## Completed In This Pass

- Created [[Dominion Overview - Canon Spine]] as the definitive current overview.
- Created [[Government Hierarchy]] to clarify throne, Federal Branch, courts, ministries, provinces, states, and local compacts.
- Rewrote [[Dominion]] as a clean entry point.
- Rewrote [[Daily Life]] from the ground level.
- Rewrote [[Social Cohesion & Control]] to align cohesion with rights and guardrails.
- Created [[Integrity Corps Guardrails]].
- Rebuilt [[Federal Agencies]] into a full agency map for civic, material, technology, security, and response bodies.
- Added missing ground-level Federal agencies for registry, elections, courts, transit, public works, labor, family welfare, food security, migration, bioethics, and oversight.
- Expanded existing Federal agency stubs into current-canon notes with mandates, limits, public-facing functions, and story uses.
- Added current-canon scope notes to [[Continuity Code]], [[Political Structure]], [[Government Structure]], and [[Bill of Rights]].
- Linked [[The Dominion as Survival State]] and [[Dominion Institutions]] to the new canon spine.

## Remaining Cleanup Targets

These files still need later mechanical or canon-alignment cleanup:

- [[Government Structure]]: contains useful Federal Government material but also long legacy text and broken links.
- [[Bill of Rights]]: strong content but needs formatting cleanup, encoding repair, and duplicate-block removal.
- [[Political Structure]]: needs a clean rewrite after the current-canon scope note.
- [[Economy Overview]]: conceptually useful but has encoding damage, duplicated links, and post-scarcity claims that should be reconciled with provision/energy/memory.
- [[Archologies & Cities]]: contains useful arcology categories but heavy migration damage and duplicated fragments.
- [[Military Doctrine]]: strong aesthetic but should be reconciled with continuity doctrine and lawful oversight.
- [[Honor System]]: strong social logic but should be checked against rights, disability protections, and anti-abuse safeguards.
- [[Inquisition]]: current guardrail is now at top, but the body still contains older phrasing that should be harmonized in a later pass.

## Cleanup Rule

Do not delete old lore blindly. First determine whether it is:

- current canon;
- legacy canon;
- archival contradiction;
- duplicate migration artifact;
- broken-link damage;
- prose needing rewrite.

The goal is not to flatten the Dominion. The goal is to make the strongest version easier to find.
