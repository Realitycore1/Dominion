# Advanced Cosmology FAQ Guide

This page bridges public reader questions into the existing deep Q&A material.

## Should I read this first?

Answer:
No. Read this after [[New Reader FAQ]], [[Reading Order FAQ]], and the basic tier path. This guide is for readers who are ready for specialist cosmology.

Read next:
- [[Tier 4 - Specialist Portal]]
- [[Cosmology Atlas - Pleroma Archive]]

## What is the Omniverse?

Answer:
The Omniverse is the highest known structure of reality in the current Q&A. It is the total framework in which realities emerge, stabilize, decay, and are replaced.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is a Basin?

Answer:
A Basin is the large-scale expression of a foundational rule set called an Axiom. It is the environment in which related multiverses, verses, and reality structures are generated.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is an Axiom?

Answer:
An Axiom is the deepest operating rule space of a reality family. It determines what can exist, how continuity behaves, and what counts as a viable reality expression.

Read next:
- [[Axiomatic Stability]]

## What is a Severance Event?

Answer:
A Severance Event occurs when a timeline becomes too unstable to remain safely attached to the larger Verse and is cut loose.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is the Void?

Answer:
The Void is the low-density outer medium outside and between Verses. It is not pure nothingness; it still permits limited existence, movement, and continuity.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is Oblivion?

Answer:
Oblivion is the terminal failure state beyond Unreality. In the existing Q&A, full collapse into Oblivion means permanent nonexistence.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## Can Confluencia Threads transmit matter?

Answer:
No. The existing Q&A states that Confluencia Threads transmit information, not matter.

Read next:
- [[Confluencia]]

## How is Many-Worlds different from the Dominion multiversal structure?

Answer:
The existing Q&A separates quantum branching from dimensional or multiversal structure. Many-Worlds concerns possible outcomes within a wavefunction; Dominion cosmology treats Verses and Multiverses as larger enclosed structures.

Read next:
- [[Many Worlds]]

## What is the observable boundary of a Verse Field?

Answer:
The existing Q&A names this boundary the Mercy Horizon. It marks a limit where a Verse's spacetime geometry folds back into the Aetheric substrate.

- [[New Readers Start Here/VI - Q&A/Boundary]]

Answer:
The existing Q&A says yes, but as informational pressure rather than physical force.
