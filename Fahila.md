Fahila (one of many celestials) is the Goddess of the [[Fahi’an]] race. While as the central figure of the faith Fahila cannot manifest into the world physically. While lacking the ability to manifest the Goddesses influence can still be felt within the [[Fahi forest]], most notably the Edda tree. While the Goddess cannot physically exist she exerts her influence through whispers and dreams.
