Profession: Explosives engineer
Survival specialty: Demolition, mining, subterranean architecture

Nadiya is small, sharp, fiercely intelligent, and utterly unafraid of the dark.
She taught tribes how to tunnel, cities how to mine, and warlords how not to blow themselves up.

Known as:
The Iron Widow
The Lady of Ruins

People fear her because she never forgets a war crime.
