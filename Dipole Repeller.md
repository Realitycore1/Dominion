•	What it is: a large basin of repulsion (i.e., a deep underdensity/supervoid) in the nearby Universe that helps set the Local Group’s bulk motion; in reconstructed flow maps, streamlines diverge from it.  ￼
	•	How it was found: by Wiener-filter reconstructions of the [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]-D peculiar-velocity field using Cosmicflows galaxy distance data, revealing both attractors (overdensities) and repellers (voids). The analysis isolated one dominant repeller plus the Shapley attractor.  ￼ ￼
	•	Location/scale (inferred): roughly opposite the Shapley Concentration and near the anti-apex of the CMB dipole, at a distance of ~16,000 km s⁻¹ in redshift units (≈ 220–240 Mpc).  ￼
	•	Dynamics: the Local Group’s bulk flow (dipole) is closely anti-aligned with this repeller, indicating a push–pull picture: pushed from [[The Void]] and pulled toward Shapley.  ￼
	•	Update/related: later Cosmicflows-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] work confirmed a prominent repulsive basin in this general direction and also discussed a separate [[Cold Spot Repeller]] nearer the CMB Cold Spot line of sight.  ￼
	•	Interpretation & caveats: the repeller is predicted to coincide with a large void, but mapping voids is hard (they’re underluminous), and reconstructions carry data/priors sensitivities—so continued surveys are used to refine its extent.  ￼ ￼
