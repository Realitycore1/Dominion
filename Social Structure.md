# Social Structure
[[culture]]
## Merit as the Spine
Status is earned, never inherited. Families may pass opportunity, but not rank. All advancement is measured in sacrifice, loyalty, and achievement.

## Social Strata
- **Unaugmented Citizens**: majority, respected bedrock.  
- **Augmented Citizens**: technicians, artisans, officers.  
- **Recursive**: post-biological resilient [[Humans]].  
- **Legionary**: martial superhumans, living saints.  
- **Nobles / High Merit Houses**: sustained contributions; titles lapse without merit.  
- **Divine-Tiered**: rare [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Transcendent]] anomalies.

## Family & Legacy
Family is sacred; children are extensions of sacrifice. Families rise and fall on the deeds of their members.

## Mobility
Fluid meritocracy: low-born may ascend, nobles may fall. Treachery erases all merit.
