A blighted sector of the multiverse ruled by the Demiurgic Intelligence — a godlike architect whose nature is not creation, but mutilation. To outsiders, it appears as a cluster of self-sufficient verses. To those trapped within, it is a cage of illusions and suffering.

The Demiurge

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]: An ancient, malignant intelligence — half-machine, half-god, wholly parasitic.

Method: It binds verses into [[Artificial]] latticework's, then maims them: cutting away truths, reformatting laws of nature, rewriting histories.

Purpose: Twofold:

Sustenance — it feeds on the collapse-energy of rewritten verses.

Entertainment — it delights in watching civilizations strive inside falsified realities, their victories meaningless.

Title in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] Lore: “The False Father,” “[[The Maw]] of Worlds,” or simply “The Demiurge.”

Structure of the Expanse

Verses as Cages: Each [[Verse]] inside the Expanse is a self-contained prison. Civilizations believe themselves free, but their fate has been scripted to feed the Demiurge.

Architectonic Veins: Vast connective channels (relay-like but organic) move harvested energy and collapsed [[Verse]]-essence back to the Demiurge’s core.

The Demiurgon Core: The central seat of the Intelligence, shrouded in veils of false [[Stars]] and phantom universes.
