## Ontological Classification
**Axiomatic Null-State**

## Alternate Cultural Names
- Eternal Perdition (theological; inaccurate)
- The Final Dark
- The Last Silence
- Zero-State
- The End Without End

> *Note: All cultural names impose narrative structure where none exists.*

---

## Definition

**Oblivion** is the condition in which axioms no longer apply.

It is not emptiness.  
It is not destruction.  
It is not nothingness.

It is the point at which the concepts of *state, absence, presence, change, and persistence* become invalid.

Oblivion is not an end-state.  
It is the invalidation of end-states.

---

## Ontological Properties

- **Existence:** Inapplicable  
- **Non-Existence:** Inapplicable  
- **Causality:** Undefined  
- **Temporality:** Non-applicable  
- **Identity:** Non-persistent  
- **Observation:** Impossible  
- **Description:** Self-invalidating  

Any attempt to describe Oblivion produces metaphor, not truth.

---

## Relationship to the Omniverse

The Omniverse is separated from Oblivion by **axioms**.

Where axioms hold—even minimally—structure exists.
Where axioms fail entirely, Oblivion is immediate.

There is no spatial, temporal, or energetic distance between the Omniverse and Oblivion.
There is only axiomatic commitment or its absence.

---

## Axioms as the Boundary

Axioms define:
- Difference
- Relation
- Identity
- Symmetry or asymmetry
- Order (including Tempus Profundis)

As long as *any* axiom holds, Oblivion is excluded.

When axioms collapse:
- Reality does not fail
- Unreality does not persist
- Paradox does not survive

All categories cease to apply.

---

## Relation to Unreality

Unreality is not Oblivion.

Unreality:
- Requires axioms to violate
- Produces paradox, contradiction, instability
- Can host entities (e.g., Void Horrors)

Oblivion:
- Has no axioms to violate
- Cannot host entities
- Cannot sustain paradox

Void Horrors cannot exist within Oblivion.
They unravel before reaching it.

---

## Interaction and Traversal

Oblivion cannot be entered.
Oblivion cannot be exited.
Oblivion cannot be observed.

Anything that appears to “return” from Oblivion never reached it.

Claims of traversal are classified as:
- Projection errors
- Narrative contamination
- Axiomatic misinterpretation

---

## Dominion Doctrine

> “Oblivion is not an enemy.  
> It is the absence of conditions that permit enemies to exist.”

Standard policy:
- Prevent axiom erosion
- Maintain anchor integrity
- Contain unreality before axiomatic collapse
- Never attempt interaction

Weapons, containment fields, and force are meaningless at this boundary.
Only consistency delays collapse.

---

## Cultural Misinterpretations

Many civilizations describe Oblivion as:
- Punishment
- Afterlife
- Damnation
- The will of gods

These interpretations are classified as:
**Psychologically inevitable, ontologically false.**

The term *Eternal Perdition* persists because minds resist non-meaning.

---

## Canonical Summary

**Oblivion is not the end of everything.  
It is the end of the ability for “everything” to be a valid concept.**
