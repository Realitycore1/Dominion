•	[[Oath Ceremonies]] – The oath-swearing is the most sacred [[Ritual]] in the colony. Citizens stand beneath the banners of their families and declare loyalty not to rulers, but to the Guard itself.
	•	Guardian Ethos – “I guard, therefore we endure” is a common proverb. Social honor is tied less to wealth and more to fulfilling the Oath—service is the currency of esteem.
	•	Family Lineages – Families take pride in how many of their members have served as Oathkeepers, inscribing names on Stone Walls of Duty in each city.
