2037–2039: The Discovery Years
	•	Shroud authenticated (2037):
	•	Luca in Turin — priest-scientist on the team, jubilant.
	•	Elena in Athens — lectures that [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] are cultural, not divine; clashes with Luca in academic debates.
	•	Miriam hears news during market day in Jerusalem — sees crowds argue violently.
	•	Jade Seal revealed (2039):
	•	Wei presents lab data to CRINK officials; propaganda co-opts his words.
	•	Akira watches broadcast in Japan; shrine youth treat it as sign Shinto [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] will be next.
	•	Black Stone re-validated (2039):
	•	Yasin attends mass rally in Riyadh; chants slogans, feels swept up.
	•	Broadcast of rally seen by Luca and Miriam, both horrified at scale.

⸻

2045–2047: Early Flashpoints
	•	Menorah rediscovered in Rome (2045):
	•	Miriam takes children to first parade in Jerusalem; pride mixed with fear.
	•	Luca comments in Vatican press release: “Our faith grows stronger when tested.”
	•	First riots in Jerusalem (2047):
	•	Miriam caught in chaos; shields her children.
	•	Yasin deployed with Caliphate youth brigade; unknowingly fights near Miriam’s district.
	•	Elena follows newsfeeds, writes angry essays about relic mania poisoning politics.

⸻

2055–2059: Lock-In and War Declaration
	•	Papal Crusade declared (2055):
	•	Luca blesses Crusader recruits — uneasy, but compliant.
	•	Elena sees friends enlist; [[Europolis]]MINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Overview/Faction Profile/Europolis]] fracturing.
	•	Caliphate Jihad formalized (2056):
	•	Yasin conscripted into jihadist battalion.
	•	Wei pressured to provide tech to CRINK ally Caliphate.
	•	[[Sword of Kusanagi]] unveiled (2058):
	•	Akira watches Emperor broadcast; shrine youth seize his rice harvest for “relic tithe.”
	•	First global clashes (2059):
	•	Miriam tending wounded in Jerusalem siege.
	•	Yasin fighting in same city; they nearly cross paths, both saving children during shelling.

⸻

2063–2065: The Relic Wars Intensify
	•	Limited nuclear strike (2063):
	•	Wei in command bunker as CRINK scrambles to respond.
	•	Elena translates intercepted communiqués; realizes relic politics are pushing toward Armageddon.
	•	Siege of Jerusalem (2065):
	•	Miriam runs a field hospital.
	•	Yasin deserts his unit after seeing a [[Child]] killed; hides in rubble.
	•	Luca sent as Papal envoy, blesses Crusader units — sees Miriam’s hospital but avoids speaking.

⸻

2070–2072: Collapse and [[Schism]]
	•	[[Angel]] cult militias rise (2070):
	•	Elena’s daughter joins graffiti resistance, mocking “[[Angel]] battalions.”
	•	Miriam’s son joins a Zoharist light militia; family fractures.
	•	Limited nuclear exchanges (2072):
	•	Wei’s daughter in orbital division, horrified by escalating brinkmanship.
	•	Akira’s eldest son killed in Pacific relic fleet battle; Akira stops listening to Emperor’s relic speeches.

⸻

2075: The Orbital Catastrophe
	•	I[[Europolis]]by extremists:
	•	Wei’s daughter watches debris storm from CRINK control center.
	•	Elena’s daughter sketches ISS breaking apart on [[Europolis]] wall, captioned: “We fell with it.”
	•	Yasin hears Caliphate imams call ISS’s fall “God’s will” — feels only despair.

⸻

2078: Armageddon
	•	Global nuclear exchange:
	•	Miriam dies shielding grandchildren in Jerusalem bunker.
	•	Luca prays alone in Vatican catacombs, confessing he has lost his faith.
	•	Yasin dies on desert road, looking at [[Stars]] one last [[Time]].
	•	Wei watches daughter’s station vaporized in first strikes.
	•	Elena records final testimony: “We worshiped [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], and they consumed us.”
	•	Akira survives in a ruined Japanese village, praying not to Kusanagi but to his lost son.
