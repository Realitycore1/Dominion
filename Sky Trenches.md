Structure

Orbital Girdle: A colossal trench-structure encircling the planet like a segmented ring, kilometers thick and thousands long.

Fleet Vaults: Hangar caverns and dockyards embedded into the trench walls, large enough to berth dozens of fleets.

Shield Mantles: Multi-layered shield arrays projected both inward (planetary protection) and outward (orbital defense).

Artillery Lattice: Mass driver batteries, beam emplacements, and torpedo bastions saturate every section of the trench.

Functions

Civil Defense: Protects planets of high value — political capitals, [[Forge Worlds]], or reliquary worlds.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Acts as an orbital fortress, capable of annihilating entire fleets before they reach the atmosphere.

Symbolic: Its sheer visibility in the sky makes it a perpetual reminder of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] supremacy to all below.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provides reassurance — a city, continent, or world feels eternally guarded under its trench-shadow.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Strategic keystones — one Sky Trench can hold off sector-scale invasions.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Trench walls ribbed with fortress spires, cathedral buttresses lining docking bays.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the trench looks like a stark black scar circling the world.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Shield grids shimmer as fractal mandalas rippling across orbital space.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyph-light inscriptions blaze across trench segments, visible from the planet’s surface as fiery scripture in the sky.

A Sky Trench is a supersized orbital battlestation, constructed as a continuous trench-like megastructure girdling a planet. It is both armada vault and shield lattice, capable of housing dozens of fleets while projecting layered defenses that envelop the world below. It functions as a celestial moat — an orbital fortress encircling its charge in [[Fire]] and scripture.

Religious: Known as the “Amber Moats” — myths say they are the Empress’s ramparts, celestial walls she dug across the heavens.
