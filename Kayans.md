Type: Carbon-silicate insectoids
[[Origin]] Planet: Ka’thurell – a tidally-locked moon orbiting a cold gas [[Giant]], marked by hypercarbon deserts, subterranean geode forests, and atmosphere-rich caverns
Federation Role: Silent infantry masters, memory keepers, and swarm-strategy tacticians

	•	Kayans evolved from shallow reef ambush predators that gradually adapted to high-pressure diving and surfaced-based cognition.
	•	Possess dual respiratory systems: one for water (gill-lung hybrid), one for air (biofluid-cooled lungs).
	•	Their skin is translucent and bioluminescent, revealing flowing vascular patterns that pulse with emotion—used in non-verbal communication.
	•	Internally, they host a neural-symbiotic organ: a silica-crystal matrix that grows over their cerebellum—this structure resonates with specific harmonic frequencies and aids in data storage, deep learning, and sonic projection.
	•	Highly advanced sonar echolocation—can ‘sing’ structures into resonance, detecting flaws, or assessing composition.
	•	Eyes adapted to broad spectrum light, including polarized UV reflections beneath water.
	•	240 Earth years average. Aging is marked not by senescence, but by tonal degradation—the loss of harmonic clarity in their “soul song.” This is considered a sacred transition.

Core Philosophy:
The Harmonic Spiral—a belief that all consciousnesses are echoes of a prime waveform originating at the birth of the universe. Each act must be “in tune” with one’s harmonic fingerprint to retain spiritual coherence.
Every diplomatic issue is settled with a Resonance Council, where both sides express grievances through ritualized sonic debates. Victory is not declared by consensus, but by harmonic stabilization—when both sides create a sustained harmonic resonance.
	•	Music and architecture are one. Their cities are designed as [[Giant]] resonant chambers, where each hall and dome reflects songs of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and [[Dominion Omniverse/Dominion Multiverse/Structure of Eos/Dominion Verse/Ephyrain Galaxy/Synarchate/Culture & Society/Daily LIfe|daily life]].
	•	Instruments are not tools but living [[Entities]]—bio-sonic coral that respond to emotional frequency.
	•	Sculpture is underwater performance: floating kinetic installations of silk-mineral composite.
Meditation involves submerging into tonal baths of various resonances. High clerics (known as Lumaeh) can “dissolve” into harmonic echoes temporarily, returning days later with new insight.

Governance:
	•	Polycentric: Cities operate as autonomous [[Choirs]]—each governed by a Chord Council of three roles: The Voice (Speaker), The Drone (Historian), The Pulse ([[Judge]]).
	•	The Federation Delegates are chosen based on their mastery of dissonance—a [[Ritual]] where one maintains peace through chaotic environments.
	•	Conducted almost entirely in sung frequency [[Layers]], children are immersed in harmonic fields that encode memory and concept clusters.
	•	All citizens undergo a Tone Trials at 40 [[Earth (Terra)]] years—tests of mental, emotional, and harmonic stability to determine societal roles.

Regarded as the spiritual glue of the Federation—capable of calming even the [[Kulavens]] in moments of rare introspection.
	•	Lead diplomatic delegations, serve as arbiters in ideological conflicts, and are often granted temporary neutrality status during galactic standoffs.
	•	Their Song Archives are considered a Galactic Cultural Treasure, storing thousands of years of interspecies harmonics, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]], and recorded griefs.
