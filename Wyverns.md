1. Common Wyverns
	•	Definition: The baseline—two-legged, winged dragonkin with venomous tails.
	•	Traits: Animal intelligence, fast flyers, brutal predators.
	•	Symbolism: The savage echo of true dragons, chaos without majesty.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Venom Wyverns
	•	Definition: Wyverns known for deadly poisons.
	•	Traits: Barbed stingers, venomous fangs, toxic breath.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in small doses; nature’s assassin in [[Dragon]]-shape.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Frost Wyverns
	•	Definition: Wyverns adapted to arctic and mountain biomes.
	•	Traits: White or pale-blue scales, ice breath or frozen stingers.
	•	Symbolism: The biting [[Hunger]] of winter given wings.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Desert Wyverns
	•	Definition: Heat-dwelling wyverns found in sands and rocky wastes.
	•	Traits: Sand-colored scales, scorching breath, ambush predators.
	•	Symbolism: Mirage and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in the wastelands.

⸻

5. Jungle Wyverns
	•	Definition: Canopy-stalking wyverns in tropical [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Camouflage scales, smaller wingspans for maneuvering, ambush tactics.
	•	Symbolism: Predation where life is most abundant.

⸻

6. Storm Wyverns
	•	Definition: Wyverns that haunt thunderclouds and coastal cliffs.
	•	Traits: Lightning-charged wings, thunderous screeches, dive-bomb attacks.
	•	Symbolism: Sudden chaos, the predator in the storm.

⸻

7. Bone Wyverns
	•	Definition: [[Undead]] or skeletal wyverns, often bound to necromancy.
	•	Traits: Exposed bone wings, necrotic venom, eerie flight.
	•	Symbolism: The persistence of [[Hunger]] even beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

8. Siege Wyverns
	•	Definition: Bred or tamed for war, used by [[Orcs]], hobgoblins, or tyrant-kings.
	•	Traits: Heavily armored hides, strong tails for smashing fortifications.
	•	Symbolism: Domesticated ruin; the warlord’s [[Dragon]] substitute.

⸻

9. Abyssal Wyverns
	•	Definition: Wyverns twisted by infernal or void-taint.
	•	Traits: Red or black scales, shadowfire breath, glowing eyes.
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of nature into tools of damnation.

⸻

10. Mythic Wyverns
	•	Definition: Legendary wyverns grown to dragonlike stature.
	•	Traits: Towering over castles, cunning intelligence, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Government/Royal Branch/Leaders/Leaders|Leaders]] of wyvern broods.
	•	Symbolism: The line between “lesser [[Dragon]]” and true [[Dragon]] blurred.
