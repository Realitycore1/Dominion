# Mana, Magick, and Tiered Energy Scaling  
**Mathmagicks, Quality Tiers, and Non-Aligned Ranks**

---

## Purpose

This document defines how mana and magick are quantified, stored, converted, and scaled across:
- standard tier progression (Ferrous → Adamant)
- non-aligned advancement paths (Transcendent, Mythic, Divine)

The system preserves:
- physical plausibility
- narrative weight
- monumental tier gaps
- compatibility with both high-fantasy and high-sci-fi verses

---

## Foundational Separation

Magic is not raw energy.

Three layers are always distinguished:

1. **Raw Arcane Potential (RAP)**  
   - Wild, symbolic, intent-laden  
   - Not directly measurable  

2. **Refined Mana Medium (RMM)**  
   - Neutralized, stabilized, intent-stripped  
   - Measurable and storable  

3. **Effect Expression (EE)**  
   - Spells, enchantments, rituals  
   - Depends on symbols, efficiency, and coupling  

Only **RMM** is quantified.

---

## Canonical Energy Unit

### Arcane Standard Unit (ASU)

**1 ASU**  
= the amount of refined mana required to sustain  
a stable, non-symbolic arcane field of **1 m³ for 1 second**  
under standard axiomatic conditions.

ASU is a *bridge unit*, not raw joules.

---

## Joule Conversion (Approximate)

- **1 ASU ≈ 4–6 joules** under ideal conditions
- Typical systems operate at **60–80% efficiency**
- Wild-mana refinement loses **20–40%** to entropy bleed

This variability is intentional.

---

## Liquid Mana as a Carrier

Liquid mana is a **suspension medium**, not liquid energy.

### Standard Baseline
- **1 fluid ounce of stabilized liquid mana**
  - ≈ **10–15 ASU**
  - ≈ **30–60 J usable**, depending on efficiency

Examples:
- **Vial (4 fl oz)** ≈ 240 J (baseline ferrous)
- **Flask (16 fl oz)** ≈ 960 J (baseline ferrous)

These numbers are *small by design*.

---

## Tier Scaling (Main Path)

Tiers represent **refinement quality**, not brute force.

### Tier Effects
Each tier modifies three parameters:

1. **Energy Density (E)**  
   - Stored energy per volume  
   - Scales modestly (×2 per tier)

2. **Efficiency (η)**  
   - Fraction of stored energy that becomes intended effect  
   - Rises gradually

3. **Coupling (κ)**  
   - How much ambient / structural energy may be unlocked  
   - Rises steeply but safely

### Effective Work Formula

\[
\textbf{Effective Work} = E \times \eta \times \kappa
\]

Stored energy remains modest.  
Impact comes from **structure and permission**, not raw joules.

---

## Example (Russet Tier, Main Path)

- Stored energy (flask): 1,920 J
- Efficiency (η): 0.35
- Coupling (κ): 5

\[
W = 1{,}920 \times 0.35 \times 5 = 3{,}360\ \text{J-work}
\]

---

## Non-Aligned Ranks (Alternative Paths)

Non-aligned ranks do **not** increase stored energy.

They modify **how aggressively mana couples to reality**.

These ranks represent:
- exceptional conditions
- mythic alignments
- divine permissions
- non-repeatable circumstances

---

## Rank Scaling Law

For a given tier with coupling κ:

### Transcendent
\[
\kappa' = \kappa^2
\]
- Minor efficiency boost
- Requires extraordinary but attainable conditions

### Mythic
\[
\kappa' = \kappa^3
\]
- Larger efficiency boost
- Requires world-scale or narrative-scale circumstances

### Divine (choose tone)

**Option A (Controlled):**
\[
\kappa' = \kappa^3 \times C
\]
Where C is a fixed divine constant.

**Option B (Unstable):**
\[
\kappa' = \kappa^4
\]
Reality actively resists at this level.

---

## Example (Same Russet Flask, Ranked)

Baseline:
- E = 1,920 J
- η = 0.35
- κ = 5

### Transcendent
- κ' = 25
- η ≈ 0.45

\[
W \approx 21{,}600\ \text{J-work}
\]

### Mythic
- κ' = 125
- η ≈ 0.55

\[
W \approx 132{,}000\ \text{J-work}
\]

Stored energy unchanged.  
Impact increases **~40×**.

---

## Why This Works

- No vial becomes a nuclear weapon
- Conservation laws remain intact
- Higher tiers feel monumental
- Alternative paths punch far above their volume
- Infrastructure and containment still matter

Mana remains **currency**, not raw power.

---

## Containment and Risk Scaling

Exponentiating κ increases instability.

| Rank | Stability Demand | Typical Failure |
|----|----|----|
| Main Path | Baseline | Inefficiency |
| Transcendent | ×10 | Paradox residue |
| Mythic | ×100 | Axiom drift |
| Divine | ×1000+ | Reality rejection |

High-rank mana **demands environment**, not just skill.

---

## Extraordinary Prerequisites

### Transcendent
- Stable axiom-anchored site
- Specialized containment
- Cognitive symbol tolerance
- Clean ley intersection

### Mythic
- Mass ritual or myth engine
- Sacrifice term (irreversible cost)
- World-scale alignment

### Divine
- Prime invariant access
- Angelic / relic / axiom license
- Legal–ontological containment
- Reality consent mechanism

---

## Final Principles

- **Tiers** scale refinement and stability  
- **Ranks** scale permission and coupling  
- **Energy remains small**  
- **Effect scales through structure**  

> Mana is not power.  
> Mana is authorization.

The universe does the rest—  
if it agrees to let you.
