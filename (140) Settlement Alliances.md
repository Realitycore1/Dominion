# (140) Settlement Alliances

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapter 24.
- Manuscript date: Year 151 After Landfall.

## What Happened

By Year 151, the settlements had begun forming agreements that no one wanted to call nation-state diplomacy yet. Train schedules, resource corridors, emergency shelters, water rights, geothermal claims, pressure-zone maintenance, archive exchange, and apprenticeship recognition became the language of alliance.

The accords were practical and deliberately unromantic. Their importance came from repetition. People followed them because they worked.

## What Resulted

- New Aurora stopped being the only legitimate negotiating center.
- Regional blocs began forming around shared dependency and mutual leverage.
- Future powers became visible: Atlovaia, Ormova, Estelia, the Commonwealth, Toldax, the F.S.S.R., and the Equilon States.
- Diplomacy emerged from infrastructure before formal nationalism.

## Consequences

The Year 151 accords mark the transition from settlement network to proto-national order. They also create the legal memory later weaponized before the New World War.
