## Summary

The GalFed Imperial Truth is the civic doctrine that gives moral shape to Federation rule. It is not presented as conquest. It is presented as the only mature response to a galaxy that once lost nearly all conscious awareness.

## Details

Central axiom:

Consciousness without continuity becomes catastrophe.

GalFed teaches that the Aether Singularity proved civilization cannot treat mind, memory, technology, and sovereignty as separate domains. A society may govern itself only if its freedom does not endanger the noetic stability of others.

Core principles:

- Continuity is the first right.
- Memory must be preserved before autonomy can be trusted.
- Dangerous knowledge must be sequenced, not suppressed.
- Uplift is a duty of survivors toward the damaged.
- Sovereignty is earned through stability.
- Aether freedom without oversight is civilizational negligence.
- Rescue justifies temporary authority.

The quiet corruption of the doctrine lies in the word temporary. Most stewardship never ends. Every recovered world contains some instability, every archive contains some missing truth, every culture contains some divergence, and every divergence can be framed as the first vibration before another Singularity.

Loyalists call the Imperial Truth mercy disciplined by memory.

Dissidents call it trauma made into law.
