1. Foundational Principle: Survival as Moral Imperative

Attean [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] begins with one truth:

“We survived the Fall. Therefore we must endure forever.”

Survival is not instinct.
Survival is morality.
A [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] that nearly died once becomes a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] that refuses to die again.

This foundational [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] shapes:
	•	[[law]]
	•	warfare
	•	science
	•	economics
	•	social policy
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]

Nothing is permitted to threaten the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] — internally or externally.

⸻

2. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Continuity

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] defines their long-term civilizational planning.

“Every choice must preserve our tomorrow.”

[[Core Tenets]]:
	1.	Decisions must be evaluated on century-scale impact, not immediate benefit.
	2.	Genetic stability and improvement are obligations, not privileges.
	3.	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] must never endanger the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] again.
	4.	Governance must be centralized enough to act, distributed enough to adapt.
	5.	Attea must remain united across worlds, [[Time]], and crises.

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] prohibits:
	•	reckless experimentation
	•	political fragmentation
	•	uncontrolled AI autonomy
	•	uncontrolled genetic drift

And it mandates:
	•	selective reproduction
	•	continual bio-optimization
	•	planetary resource oversight
	•	redundancy in all critical [[Systems]]
	•	long-term cultural preservation

⸻

3. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of the Strong

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] evolved after the Fall destroyed public trust in technocrats.

“Strength protects. Weakness endangers.”

Strength is defined not as brute force but as:
	•	physical competence
	•	mental discipline
	•	genetic robustness
	•	emotional stability
	•	conduit potential
	•	civic loyalty

Conversely, weakness is a risk factor for civilizational collapse.

Weakness is treated, corrected, or engineered out —
never shamed, but never allowed to persist.

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] produces:
	•	disciplined society
	•	high professional standards
	•	strong population health
	•	elite [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] [[culture]]

⸻

4. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Unified Purpose

“The individual lives for the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]], and the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] protects the individual.”

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] balances collectivism and individual excellence.

[[Key]] Tenets:
	1.	The state must protect its people.
	2.	The people must serve the unity of the state.
	3.	Division is [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].
	4.	Cooperation is survival.
	5.	Conflict within must never exceed threat from without.

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] gave rise to the Great Houses, who serve as semi-autonomous pillars of unity.

⸻

5. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Scientific Stewardship

After the technocracy caused the Fall, Attea reshaped its scientific worldview.

“Knowledge serves survival — not curiosity.”

Science is sacred but must be:
	•	regulated
	•	purposeful
	•	ethical (Attean ethics, not [[Human]] ethics)
	•	beneficial to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] continuity

Prohibitions:
	•	unsupervised dimensional research
	•	unrestricted AI sapience
	•	unbounded biological mutation

Allowed — even encouraged:
	•	genetic improvement
	•	psionic enhancement
	•	conduit amplification
	•	neural optimization
	•	planetary engineering

Every research project must pass the Continuity Test:

*“Will this benefit Attea 100 years from now?

1,000 years from now?
100,000 years from now?”*

If not → denied.

⸻

6. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of the Monarch (The [[Griffin]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]])

The monarch is not divine — but doctrinally necessary.

“One will guides the many. One line endures. The Monarch safeguards tomorrow.”

Responsibilities:
	•	interpret and execute Continuity [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	lead [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] forces
	•	resolve Great House conflicts
	•	steward genetic purity of the royal line
	•	preserve cultural unity
	•	act with absolute power in existential crises

The Monarch is the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]]’ living memory and shield.
Failure is not conceivable.

⸻

7. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Conduit Sovereignty

Conduit abilities are not magic — they are evolved national assets.

“A Conduit is a weapon of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]], not of the self.”

Conduits must:
	•	serve the state
	•	train rigorously
	•	maintain emotional discipline
	•	undergo genetic monitoring
	•	preserve their lineage

Conduits are to Attea what Axiomatic Warriors are to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] —
but evolved through biology, not metaphysics.

The Griffins, with multi-gene conduits, embody this [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] completely.

⸻

8. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of War

“War is a failure of diplomacy — but diplomacy must never become weakness.”

War is justified if:
	•	the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] is threatened
	•	unity is at risk
	•	resources essential to survival are contested
	•	hostile powers encroach
	•	precursor anomalies pose danger

In war:
	•	civilian casualties must be minimized
	•	enemy leadership must be neutralized
	•	overwhelming force is preferred
	•	conduit battlemages lead front-line engagements
	•	genetic infantry and rune warriors provide backbone

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] enforces:
	•	decisive strikes
	•	targeted destruction
	•	avoidance of protracted conflicts

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] allows outcomes as extreme as:

Niklaus [[Griffin]]’s destruction of an entire rival solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]]m|System]].

⸻

9. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Memory

“The past instructs the living. The dead remain with us.”

Memory-Stones (crystallized remains) preserve:
	•	genetic patterns
	•	neural residues
	•	personal [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]
	•	cultural lineage

Every family maintains Continuity Vaults containing thousands of stones across millions of years.

The Atteans do not forget —
because forgetting nearly killed them.

⸻

10. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of the Blue Horizon

The Blue Galaxy is sacred geography and existential truth.

“We are the Children of the Unchosen Sky.”

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] dictates:
	•	reverence for their cosmic exile
	•	gratitude for survival
	•	vigilance toward dimensional instability
	•	caution in deep FTL experimentation

The galaxy is both home and warning —
a reminder that the universe is not stable.

⸻

11. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of External Powers

Atteans classify foreign civilizations into three categories:

1. Threats ([[Domain]])

Powers capable of mind-control or axiomatic rewriting.

2. Rivals ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]])

Empires strong enough to challenge Attea, but not inherently hostile.

3. Dependencies (lesser Blue Galaxy powers)

Nations requiring Attean stabilization or protection.

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] ensures:
	•	vigilance
	•	preparedness
	•	strategic expansion
	•	controlled diplomacy
