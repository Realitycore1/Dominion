1. Common Demons
	•	Definition: The baseline—manifestations of corruption, cruelty, or chaos.
	•	Traits: Horns, claws, fangs, warped humanoid forms.
	•	Symbolism: [[Sin]] and destruction given flesh.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. [[Imps]]
	•	Definition: The smallest and weakest demons, often tricksters or familiars.
	•	Traits: Tiny, mischievous, [[Shapeshifters]].
	•	Symbolism: Temptation in small things; annoyance that corrodes.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Succubi & Incubi
	•	Definition: Demons of lust, manipulation, and dreams.
	•	Traits: Alluring beauty, shadowy wings, dream-walking seduction.
	•	Symbolism: Corruption through desire, love twisted into [[Hunger]].

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Brutes (Baalspawn, War Demons)
	•	Definition: Demons of raw violence and rage.
	•	Traits: Towering, muscle-bound, armored hides, burning weapons.
	•	Symbolism: The beast of war and slaughter.

⸻

5. [[Trickster]] Demons
	•	Definition: Liars, gamblers, whisperers, masters of illusion.
	•	Traits: Shifting faces, silver tongues, smoke-and-mirror forms.
	•	Symbolism: Lies as power, reality bent through deception.

⸻

6. Shadow Demons
	•	Definition: Demons born of voids, darkness, or the [[Abyss]].
	•	Traits: Amorphous, incorporeal, eyes like embers in the dark.
	•	Symbolism: The terror of the unseen.

⸻

7. Plague Demons
	•	Definition: Demons of disease, rot, and corruption.
	•	Traits: Pestilent breath, rotting flesh, swarms of flies.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] that spreads silently; decay made sacred.

⸻

8. Infernal Nobility
	•	Definition: Lords and Dukes of Hell, ruling [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] with terrible authority.
	•	Traits: Grand, regal, monstrous but dignified; wield contracts and armies.
	•	Symbolism: Power through tyranny, the eternal hierarchy of damnation.

⸻

9. Abyssal Horrors
	•	Definition: Chaotic, eldritch demons—part Lovecraftian horror, part living nightmare.
	•	Traits: Tentacles, eyes in impossible places, gibbering mouths.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos as the true face of evil; corruption that cannot be reasoned with.

⸻

10. Fallen [[Angels]] ([[Demon]] Princes)
	•	Definition: [[Angels]] turned to rebellion, now ruling hellscapes or wandering as broken seraphs.
	•	Traits: Wings scorched black, halos shattered, powers intact but inverted.
	•	Symbolism: Pride, hubris, tragedy—the corruption of perfection.

1.	Common Demons
	•	Twisted mockeries of [[Angels]].
	•	Roles: tempters, tormentors, parasites on mortal will.
	•	Traits: humanoid with grotesque exaggerations (horns, scars, inverted wings).
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Seraphic Inversions (“Ashen Seraphs”)
	•	Once Seraphim, now [[Fire]] burned cold.
	•	Symbolism: false purity, consuming ash instead of light.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Chthonic Cherubim
	•	Corrupted guardians, now gatekeepers of forbidden places.
	•	Appear as many-eyed, but their eyes drip shadow.
	•	Symbolism: keepers of corruption, jailers of the damned.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Archdemons
	•	High generals of Hellish hosts, mirroring Archangels.
	•	Often named: Asmodeus, Belial, Beelzebub, etc.
	•	Traits: embody a specific vice or [[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] (lust, pride, [[Hunger]]).
	5.	Thrones of [[Chains]]
	•	Parody of Thrones: burning wheels shattered into barbed rings.
	•	Symbolism: judgment turned to tyranny.
	6.	Dominators
	•	Corrupted [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Angels]]; enslave rather than guide.
	•	Traits: wield seals of bondage, mock scepters.
	7.	Tormentor Demons (Anti-Guardians)
	•	Instead of protecting, they prey upon mortals.
	•	Manifest as shadows at the bedside, whispers in dreams.
	8.	Fallen Lords
	•	Original rebels, like Satan, Samael, Azazel.
	•	Traits: once radiant, now scarred by rejection.
	9.	Elemental Demons
	•	Parasitic reflections of elemental [[Angels]].
	•	[[Fire]] → wild infernos, Water → stagnant corruption, etc.
	10.	Aeonic Demons (Mythic Class)

	•	Beings of absolute negation: [[Hunger]], Despair, Madness, Oblivion.
	•	Manifest as incomprehensible abysses or living nightmares.
