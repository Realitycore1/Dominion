1. Common Fae
	•	Definition: The baseline—spirits of nature, beauty, or mischief.
	•	Traits: Slightly inhuman features (glowing eyes, pointed ears, unnatural grace), minor magic.
	•	Symbolism: The liminal “in-between” of [[Human]] and supernatural.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Seelie Fae (Court of Light)
	•	Definition: The “noble” fae, tied to beauty, light, and harmony.
	•	Traits: Radiant forms, charm magic, lawful in their own way.
	•	Symbolism: Order and benevolence—but alien and self-serving.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Unseelie Fae (Court of Shadow)
	•	Definition: The “dark” fae, cruel and predatory.
	•	Traits: Twisted forms, glamours hiding horror, delight in mortal suffering.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos, cruelty, the shadow of joy.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Trickster]] Fae
	•	Definition: Mischief-driven fae who toy with mortals for amusement.
	•	Traits: [[Shapeshifters]], illusionists, pranksters with cruel streaks.
	•	Symbolism: Humor as cruelty; unpredictability of nature.

⸻

5. Nature Fae
	•	Definition: Fae bound to specific aspects of nature (streams, flowers, storms).
	•	Traits: Physical traits linked to their [[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]]—leaf-hair, skin of bark, flowing voices.
	•	Symbolism: Fragmentation of nature into countless spirits.

⸻

6. Shadow Fae
	•	Definition: Fae who lurk in twilight, graveyards, or [[Forgotten]] ruins.
	•	Traits: Pale forms, hollow voices, necrotic glamour.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] as part of nature’s cycle.

⸻

7. Noble Fae
	•	Definition: High lords and ladies of faerie courts.
	•	Traits: Impossibly beautiful, radiate authority, wield vast magic.
	•	Symbolism: Power veiled in elegance; rulers who see mortals as pawns.

⸻

8. Elemental Fae
	•	Definition: Fae infused with primal forces.
	•	Variants: [[Fire]] dancers, frost maidens, storm-callers, stone-lords.
	•	Symbolism: Raw forces of nature cloaked in glamour.

⸻

9. Wild Fae
	•	Definition: Feral, savage fae outside the courts.
	•	Traits: Beastlike, unpredictable, primal hunters.
	•	Symbolism: The chaos of untamed nature.

⸻

10. Arch-Fae (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary fae who embody entire seasons, [[Realms]], or laws of nature.
	•	Traits: Immense size or power, semi-divine, shape shifting with the seasons.
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s eternal cycles—creation, destruction, [[Rebirth]].
