3650 CE: Corporate expansion into Jovian moons; brutal mortality, rampant profiteering.

3800–3950 CE: C.U. consolidates power, merges [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] and [[Corporate Fleets]] into “Unified Colonial Command.”

3950–4100 CE: [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] of Saturn and the [[Kuiper Belt]] appear.

4100–4200 CE: Corporate wars drain resources; the C.U. nationalizes major [[Corporations]], turning them into state directorates.

The First Rebellion and the Tharsis Closure
“They built the [[Stars]], and then sealed them.”

4420 CE: Growing resentment on Mars and the Jovian frontier leads to the Tharsis Uprising.

4427 CE: The rebellion spreads; corporate cartels secretly fund it, hoping to gain leverage.

4430 CE: The C.U. invokes Closure Protocols — full [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] quarantine of rebellious sectors.

4432 CE: Closure Fleets deployed. Entire orbital zones are sterilized; Tharsis Plateau bombarded. 60% of Martian [[Infrastructure]] lost.

4438 CE: Final resistance crushed at the Valles Citadel.

4440 CE: The Concord of Obedience signed — bans private militaries, independent FTL or energy research, and enforces mandatory communications oversight.

[[Aftermath]]: “To be Closed” becomes synonymous with extermination. Even corporate factions refuse to oppose the C.U. again.

The Long Peace (C.U. Hegemony)

4500–4700 CE: Two centuries of uneasy order. The C.U. expands cautiously, maintaining monopoly on interplanetary drives and communications.

Colonists develop underground [[Cultures]] and myths about the Ships that Burned Red—Closure Fleet vessels remembered as omens of divine punishment.

Individual dissent manifests as sabotage or migration rather than open war.

4300–4700 CE: Slow-ship expansion begins under strict C.U. oversight. Many [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] fail due to isolation and rationing.

4700 CE: First fully stable extrasolar colony reported; C.U. censors its independence movement immediately.

4900 CE: ~14 viable [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] exist; none autonomous.
