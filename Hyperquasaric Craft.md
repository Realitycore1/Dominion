Hyperquasaric Craft are vessels powered and propelled by artificially induced quasar emissions — jets of relativistic plasma drawn from supermassive black holes or engineered micro-[[Quasars]]. They are fusion of starship and cosmic singularity, moving at near-relativistic speeds and wielding power on galactic scales.
Core Drive

Quasar Heart: A bound micro-black hole or collapsed mass-core, surrounded by controlled accretion disks.

Jet Nozzles: Plasma collimators that channel relativistic jets as thrust or directed energy.

Hull & Frame

Atlas Pillar Spines: Colossal supports, ribbed like cathedral arches, bracing against tidal stresses.

Parametric Geometry: Hull form flexes to redirect jets, folding like wings of plasma.

Weaponry

Relativistic Jet-Lances: Focused plasma streams capable of sterilizing entire [[Systems]].

Gravitic Whips: Oscillations in the craft’s quasar heart generate spacetime shockwaves.

Concordia Harmonics: Jets inscribed with hymns — each blast a literal psalm of war written in light.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Deployed as apex flagships or sector-killers. A single Hyperquasaric Craft can anchor fleets like a god among mortals.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ribs, spines, and glowing veins bracing an impossible mass.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they look like pure black silhouettes, with only their plasma wings visible.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Exhaust jets curl in fractal spirals, like luminous stained-glass flames.

To an observer, a Hyperquasaric Craft appears like a winged [[Angel]] of [[Fire]], its jets painting [[The Void]] with cosmic light, leaving behind trails that last centuries.
