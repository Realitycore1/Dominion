Classification: Curseborn & Identity Predators
Subtype: Profane Shapeshifters / Malice-Bound Mimics
Status: Active, Highly Dangerous, Rare but Persistent

⸻

Core Description

Skinwalkers are sapient predators who steal form through violation—wearing the skins, bones, or identities of animals and people to move unseen among the living. Their transformations are grotesque, imperfect, and often unsettling even when convincing.

A Skinwalker does not become something else.
It hollows something out and moves in.

Their presence is marked by:
	•	distorted movement,
	•	wrong proportions,
	•	delayed expressions,
	•	voices that imitate but do not understand.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Skinwalkers are created when ritual shapeshifting is severed from restraint and ethics, binding Malice directly to form manipulation.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Malice-dominant resonance
	•	Secondary Influence: Shadow Realms (Umbrae), Dream-Wastes (identity erosion)
	•	Realm Interface: Liminal thresholds, cursed territories, ritual sites

They are not a species.
They are a crime against identity.

⸻

Origins

Skinwalkers arise through:
	•	forbidden rituals intended to steal power,
	•	murder paired with form-binding rites,
	•	corrupted Nagual traditions,
	•	deliberate rejection of spiritual balance.

The act of becoming a Skinwalker is always intentional.

This is important:

Skinwalkers are not victims. They chose this.

⸻

Intelligence & Psychology

Skinwalkers retain human-level intelligence, but their psychology is deeply fractured.

They understand:
	•	social behavior,
	•	trust cues,
	•	emotional manipulation.

They do not experience:
	•	empathy,
	•	stable identity,
	•	long-term attachment.

Most Skinwalkers develop:
	•	paranoia,
	•	compulsive imitation,
	•	escalating violence as their borrowed selves decay.

⸻

Transformation Mechanics

Skinwalker shapeshifting:
	•	requires physical trophies (skins, bones, blood),
	•	degrades with each use,
	•	becomes more painful over time,
	•	results in increasingly distorted forms.

They may:
	•	partially shift,
	•	fuse traits,
	•	lose control mid-transformation.

Eventually, most Skinwalkers can no longer return fully to human form.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Skinwalkers operate as:
	•	infiltrators,
	•	serial predators,
	•	ritual hunters.

They prefer:
	•	isolated communities,
	•	borderlands,
	•	culturally fragmented regions.

They kill not only to feed, but to renew form integrity.

A Skinwalker who cannot steal identities will deteriorate rapidly.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable during transformation
	•	Susceptible to Grace-aligned wards
	•	Disrupted by identity-anchoring rituals (names, lineage, memory)
	•	Cannot maintain form in Null zones

Exposure and recognition are deadly to them.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Nagual: Mortal enemies; Nagual hunt Skinwalkers relentlessly
	•	Beastfolk: Viewed as abominations; often exterminated on sight
	•	Wendigo: Mutual hostility; hunger vs identity
	•	Domovoi & Land Spirits: Actively expose or banish Skinwalkers
	•	Vampires: Occasionally exploit them, rarely trust them

Skinwalkers are universally despised across cultures.

⸻

Variants
	•	Bone-Walkers: Use skeletal trophies; fragile but terrifying
	•	Skin-Thieves: Highly convincing, socially dangerous
	•	Feral Walkers: Identity collapse, bestial forms
	•	Urban Skinwalkers: Psychological predators, rare but lethal

Urban Skinwalkers are among the hardest to detect.
