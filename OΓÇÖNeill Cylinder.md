## Summary

Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
An O’Neill Cylinder is a pair of counter-rotating orbital habitats, each a hollow cylinder (typically ~30–50 km long, 5–10 km [[Wide]]), rotating to generate gravity on its inner surface. With mirrors to direct sunlight inside, each cylinder becomes a living valley rolled into the sky.
Structure & Scale

Length: 30–50 km; diameter: ~6–10 km.

Two cylinders rotate in opposite directions, canceling gyroscopic wobble.

Interior surface lined with oceans, forests, fields, and arcologies.

Mirrors at the sides reflect sunlight inward to simulate day/night cycles.

Habitability

Supports hundreds of thousands to millions of people.

Climate controlled by adjustable mirrors and internal Concordia harmonics.

Can host specialized ecologies — farms, biomes, or cultural sanctuaries.

Variants

Civil Cylinders: Agricultural and settlement habitats.

Industrial Cylinders: Entirely devoted to forges, factories, or [[Computronium]] banks.

Sanctum Cylinders: Cultural or religious vaults, storing memory and tradition.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Serve as mid-scale seed habitats — often used in frontier [[Systems]] or as population overflow from [[Clarke Orbit Cities]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Can be hardened into fortress-cylinders, bristling with weapons.

Economic: Function as trade-stations and supply vaults along Beam Corridors.

Religious: Known as “Amber Hollows” — mythologized as miniature thrones, each one holding a sliver of the Empress’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] in [[The Void]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Interior ribs shaped like cathedral arches, dividing valleys into sacred segments.

Suprematist Monolithism: From outside, they look like seamless black tubes, quiet but immense.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Cityscapes spiral upward across the inner horizon, glowing in fractal bands.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs line the mirror bands, projecting scripture as sunlight itself.

Narrative Function

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: A workhorse habitat — mass-produced, reliable, eternal.
