Civil: Skyh
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Skyhook is a long orbital tether [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] anchored in orbit and extending down into a planet’s upper atmosphere. Vehicles latch onto the hook to transfer cargo and passengers directly to orbit, bypassing [[Rockets]] or mass drivers. In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] hands, Skyhooks are scaled up into monumental orbital umbilicals — lifelines, fortresses, and symbols of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] over gravity itself.
Structure

Tether: Thousands of kilometers long, built of [[Exotic Matter]] composites (carbon nanotube lattices, strange-matter threads).

Counterweight: Balances the tether, anchored in higher orbit or linked to Orbital Rings.

Anchor Towers: Surface megaspire arcologies that serve as docking and cargo hubs.

MRO Ecologies: Nanodrone swarms constantly repair, reinforce, and realign the tether.

Functions

Civil: Move cargo and people cheaply into orbit.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Act as choke points — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] fleets can strangle a planet simply by seizing or severing its Skyhook.

Economic: Anchor trade, providing constant flow between surface and orbital habitats.

Symbolic: Skyhooks appear as celestial cords, connecting planets to the heavens.

Variants

Low-Orbit Skyhooks: Extend only into upper atmosphere — efficient but fragile.

Geostationary Skyhooks: Full tether reaching ground, with anchor arcologies and orbital docks.

Nested Skyhooks: [[Chains]] of tethers between multiple orbits, forming ladder-like [[Systems]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Targets for sieges; severing a hook can cripple planetary access.

Economic: The cheaper the lift, the more viable planetary industry becomes.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Anchor towers ribbed like cathedral spires, tethers braced in rib-vault arches.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the tether looks like a perfect black line cutting from planet to void.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Luminous cargo pods spiral up the tether, glowing like beads on a rosary.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The tether itself glows faintly with glyph-light, a line of scripture stretching from [[Earth (Terra)]] to sky.

From the ground, a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Skyhook looks like a celestial cord descending from heaven, dotted with glowing stations climbing eternally upward.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A lifeline, tying them to the [[Stars]].

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Practical, scalable, symbolic — the simplest crown-thread binding a planet.

To myth: Called the “Amber Cords”, said to be spun by the Empress herself, stitching creation into order.
ooks are the veins of trade and colonization, turning planets into connected jewels.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Cords” — myths say they are threads the Empress spins, tying worlds into her cosmic loom.

To rebels: A leash — their world is literally tethered to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] oversight.
