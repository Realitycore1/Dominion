I. Early Foundations (2070–2150)

Population:

2080s: <200 (rotating scientists, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], security).

2100s: ~1,000 (Shackleton outpost + orbital support).

2150: ~[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]],500 permanent residents across multiple sites.

Infrastructure:

Shackleton Crater outpost: ice mining, solar farms.

First pressurized dome at Marius Hills.

Surface transport: crawler trucks, small maglev lines between bases.

Reliance on [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] shipments for machinery, medicine, and food.

II. The First Expansion (2150–2250)

Population:

2200: ~25,000 (first real colony generation).

2250: ~75,000 across [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] main settlements.

Infrastructure:

Marius Hills Dome Complex:

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] domes linked by lava tube burrows.

Agricultural domes ([[Hydroponics]], aquaponics).

Shackleton expands into a fuel hub exporting water → orbit for propellant.

Orbital depots and refueling stations established at [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]-Moon Lagrange points.

Small local manufacturing begins — plastics, simple alloys, tools.

III. Custodial Era (2250–2350)

Population:

2300: ~150,000

2350: ~250,000

Births outnumber migration; Lunars are now multi-generational.

Infrastructure:

Expansion into The Burrows (lava tubes turned into hab-cities).

First mass driver railguns for orbital cargo launches.

Power grid: [[Fusion Reactors]] supplement solar.

CU administrative quarter in Marius Hills — unpopular, seen as colonial oversight.

Orbital shipyards in construction, limited to small craft.

IV. Cynthia Protectorate (2350–2450)

Population:

2400: ~500,000

2450: ~1 million

Largest off-world population to date.

Infrastructure:

Full city-arcologies in Marius Hills & Shackleton.

Orbital elevators considered but too costly; replaced by high-capacity mass drivers.

Cis-lunar network: hundreds of satellites, comm [[Relays]], and defense platforms.

Lagrange-Point stations expanded into trade hubs.

Luna becomes the shipyard of the Inners, building transports and warships.

V. Solar Wars Era (2500–2800)

Population:

2500: ~[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] million

2650: ~[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] million (despite [[First Solar War]] losses).

2800: ~7 million

Infrastructure:

Fortified domes: Marius Hills and Shackleton turned into bunker-cities.

Massive orbital shipyards (second only to [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]).

Maglev loops spanning equatorial lines for cargo transfer.

Large [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] garrisons; Luna is CU’s frontline stronghold.

Extensive underground vaults to protect from kinetic [[Bombardment]].

VI. Post-[[Second Solar War]] (2880+)

Population:

2900: ~10 million

Growth stabilizes as Luna focuses on being an industrial/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] hub rather than a settlement frontier.

Infrastructure:

Cynthia Assembly: semi-autonomous [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Government|Government]] body.

Megashipyards building Kuiper freighters and warships.

Joint CU–Lunar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] fleets stationed at Lagrange points.

Luna exports both ships and administrators to other [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].
