# Chapter VII - Spark of Rebellion

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The unification settlement triggers armed resistance.

## Event Role

- Rebellion begins where debt enforcement, military occupation, archive seizure, and admission requirements overlap.
- Rebels claim the council has turned wartime survival into permanent rule.
- The provisional state must decide whether rebellion is treason, grievance, or both.
- Old New World War weapons return to use before they can be decommissioned.

## Consequence

The war's second phase begins because peace terms feel like surrender to too many people.
