1. Common Satyrs
	•	Definition: The baseline—goat-legged, horned humanoids tied to revelry and mischief.
	•	Traits: Horns, cloven hooves, panpipes, carnal appetites.
	•	Symbolism: Untamed joy, indulgence, chaos of the wilderness.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Bacchic Satyrs
	•	Definition: Revelers tied to festivals, wine, and endless celebration.
	•	Traits: Drunken resilience, infectious laughter, aura of euphoria.
	•	Symbolism: Pleasure without limit; joy that slips into madness.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. [[Trickster]] Satyrs
	•	Definition: Mischief-makers and pranksters, often harassing mortals with illusions or riddles.
	•	Traits: Quick reflexes, [[Fae]] magic, mocking wit.
	•	Symbolism: The chaos of play, laughter as weapon.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Hunter Satyrs
	•	Definition: Savage satyrs of deep forests who stalk mortals like prey.
	•	Traits: Predatory cunning, sharpened horns, mastery of ambush.
	•	Symbolism: The predator behind the mask of merriment.

⸻

5. War Satyrs
	•	Definition: Rare satyrs who turn their wild nature to violence and bloodshed.
	•	Traits: Larger horns, brutish frames, wield crude weapons.
	•	Symbolism: The berserker spirit; war as frenzied revel.

⸻

6. Shadow Satyrs
	•	Definition: Satyrs twisted by curses, shadow [[Realms]], or infernal bargains.
	•	Traits: Blackened horns, glowing eyes, aura of unease.
	•	Symbolism: The corruption of joy into malice.

⸻

7. Elemental Satyrs
	•	Definition: Satyrs infused with elemental energies.
	•	Variants: Flamehorns ([[Fire]]), Stormcallers (lightning), Frosthoof (ice).
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s revelry personified in elemental form.

⸻

8. Noble Satyrs (Courtiers of the [[Fae]])
	•	Definition: Satyrs who rise into faerie nobility, serving in the courts of the Seelie or Unseelie.
	•	Traits: Refined beauty, subtle horns, glamoured clothing.
	•	Symbolism: The wild clothed in courtly grace; chaos made political.

⸻

9. Beast Satyrs
	•	Definition: More monstrous cousins with greater beastly traits.
	•	Traits: Full bestial faces, goatlike or bull-like bodies, near-feral strength.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]] abandoned; instinct triumphant.

⸻

10. Arch-Satyrs (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary satyrs tied to gods of revelry, fertility, or chaos.
	•	Traits: Immense size, aura of irresistible revelry, command over [[Fae]] beasts.
	•	Symbolism: The eternal festival; creation and destruction blended.
