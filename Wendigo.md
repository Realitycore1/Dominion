Classification: Curseborn & Hunger Entities
Subtype: Cannibal-Spirit / Malice-Manifest Predator
Status: Active, Highly Contagious (Conceptually)

⸻

Core Description

Wendigo are emaciated humanoid entities, unnaturally tall, with elongated limbs, exposed bone structure, pallid or frost-burned flesh, and eyes hollowed by endless hunger. Their presence is accompanied by cold, silence, and the sensation of being watched by something that understands you as meat.

A Wendigo is not defined by its body.
It is defined by what it has chosen to consume.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Wendigo arise when Malice binds itself to hunger, using cannibalism as the ignition point.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Malice-dominant resonance
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (survival instinct)
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms (Umbrae), Bleeding Wilds (cold regions), Dream-Wastes (memory erosion)

They are not undead.
They are not demons.
They are moral collapse made predatory.

⸻

Creation & Transmission

A Wendigo is born through:
	•	cannibalism under desperation,
	•	prolonged starvation paired with moral surrender,
	•	consumption of Malice-tainted flesh,
	•	ritualized hunger in isolation.

Importantly:

Not all cannibals become Wendigo.
The curse requires acceptance, not accident.

This allows tragedy: people may begin transforming without knowing why.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Wendigo retain human-level intelligence, but stripped of empathy and restraint.

They understand:
	•	deception,
	•	mimicry,
	•	social manipulation,
	•	hunting patterns.

They do not feel:
	•	guilt,
	•	loyalty,
	•	compassion.

They often lure victims by:
	•	imitating voices,
	•	crying for help,
	•	recalling shared memories.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Wendigo inhabit:
	•	frozen forests,
	•	remote wilderness,
	•	abandoned settlements,
	•	famine-struck regions.

They hunt:
	•	humans almost exclusively,
	•	sometimes animals when starving,
	•	never enough to feel sated.

The more they feed, the more their hunger grows.

They are self-sustaining catastrophes.

⸻

Physical Adaptations
	•	Supernatural endurance
	•	Resistance to cold
	•	Enhanced strength disproportionate to mass
	•	Regenerative capabilities fueled by consumption
	•	Height increases with each kill (in many variants)

Their bodies become monuments to hunger, not efficiency.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to fire and extreme heat
	•	Disrupted by Grace-aligned rituals
	•	Weakened by communal presence (they thrive in isolation)
	•	Cannot cross certain warded thresholds

Destroying a Wendigo often requires symbolic as well as physical acts.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Vampires: Mutual hostility; vampires see Wendigo as wasteful
	•	Rugaru: Overlapping territory, frequent conflict
	•	Ghouls: Inferior imitators
	•	Dream-Wastes Entities: Sometimes overlap through memory erosion
	•	Taniwha / Land Spirits: Actively repel Wendigo infestations

Wendigo do not form societies.
They erase them.

⸻

Variants
	•	Feral Wendigo: Early-stage, animalistic
	•	Elder Wendigo: Massive, calculating, mythic
	•	Urban Wendigo: Modern isolation, subtle predation
	•	Wiendigo: Hybridized or culturally altered variants (your setting-specific evolution)

Elder Wendigo often become the nucleus of regional legends.
