# Story 08 - The Relearners

## Viewpoint

Sada Amri, now assigned to microbial conversion work.

## Setting

Years 3-17 AL, New Aurora biolab annex.

## Story Shape

Sada's team builds microbial intermediaries that can make parts of Harmina's biomass usable. The work is repetitive, wet, fragile, and morally strange. They are not farming yet. They are negotiating with chemistry.

Outside the lab, people begin calling the scientists Relearners. Sada dislikes the reverence at first. Then she watches a child eat without ration panic for the first time.

## Harmina Element

Survival science becomes ritual because people need to name the labor that keeps them alive.

## Character Movement

Sada accepts that technical work can become sacred without becoming false.

## Future Payoff

The Relearners become a cultural category: scientists, farmers, cooks, and teachers who translate Harmina into human life.
