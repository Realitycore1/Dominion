1. Common Unicorn

Definition: The baseline—slender horse with a single spiraled horn.

Traits: White coats, healing powers, purity auras.

Symbolism: Innocence, untamed beauty, divine favor.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. War Unicorns

Definition: Larger, armored unicorns trained as steeds for heroes or knights.

Traits: Muscular builds, horns like lances, radiant charges.

Symbolism: Purity turned militant; the divine weaponized.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Noble Unicorns

Definition: Regal unicorns bound to kingship or divine courts.

Traits: Golden horns, jeweled manes, speech or telepathy.

Symbolism: Authority, destiny, divine right.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Black Unicorns (Nightmares)

Definition: Corrupted unicorns, often infernal or [[Abyss]]-touched.

Traits: Black coats, fiery or shadowed horns, nightmare steeds.

Symbolism: Innocence inverted; corruption of the divine.

5. Forest Unicorns

Definition: Unicorns tied to wild woodlands and [[Fae]] [[Realms]].

Traits: Green or mossy manes, antlerlike horns, affinity with [[Dryads]]/[[Fae]].

Symbolism: The wild’s beauty; harmony of beast and forest.

6. Storm Unicorns

Definition: Sky-dwelling unicorns born of lightning and thunder.

Traits: Crackling horns, storm-colored hides, wings of electricity.

Symbolism: Power and purity in chaotic form.

7. Frost Unicorns

Definition: Unicorns of frozen tundras and glaciers.

Traits: White-blue hides, icy horns, breath of frost.

Symbolism: Purity preserved in desolation.

8. Sea Unicorns (Narwhal-Unicorns)

Definition: Aquatic unicorns tied to oceans and rivers.

Traits: Finned tails, narwhal-like horns, amphibious grace.

Symbolism: The horn as a compass; guidance across seas.

9. Celestial Unicorns

Definition: Unicorns tied to [[Stars]], moons, or cosmic forces.

Traits: Starry manes, radiant horns, dream-walking powers.

Symbolism: The divine made visible; hope in darkness.

10. Arch-Unicorns (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary unicorns embodying primal forces of creation.

Traits: Colossal size, horns of light, voices like [[Choirs]].

Symbolism: Purity as apocalypse or salvation; eternity crowned.
