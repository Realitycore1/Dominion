# Elemental Aether Table (v0.1)

## Ferrous (common & stable)
- **Pyra** — [[Fire]] essence, heat and combustion.
- **[[Hydra]]** — Water essence, flow and absorption.
- **Terran** — [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] essence, solidity and growth.
- **Aeron** — Air essence, movement and sound.
- **Luxa** — Light, illumination and purity.
- **Umbra** — Shadow, concealment and coolness.

## Russet (slightly volatile, more specialized)
- **Florin** — Plant-life essence, growth and fertility.
- **Bestia** — Animal-life essence, vigor and instinct.
- **Ferrum** — Metal essence, structure and conductivity.
- **Glacien** — Cold essence, frost and slowing.
- **Fulminis** — Lightning essence, charge and speed.

## Argent (complex harmonics, reliable for mid-tier)
- **Choriel** — Resonant sound essence, vibration and music.
- **Eidolon** — Dreamstuff, imagination, illusion.
- **Mortis** — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] essence, entropy, endings.
- **Vitae** — Life essence, healing, continuity.
- **Selenor** — Lunar essence, tides, reflection, mystery.

## Amber (unstable, immense power, requires mastery)
- **Veyra** — [[Time]]-tide essence, probability, sequence.
- **Astralis** — Starfire essence, radiant fusion, cosmic flame.
- **Tenebris** — Deep-shadow essence, void, gravity-like pull.
- **Myrrin** — Blood-essence, sacrifice, binding.
- **[[Aetherion]]** — Pure [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]], raw magical substrate itself.

## Platina (high resonance, extremely volatile)
- **Oblivion** — Unmaking essence, erasure, nullification.
- **Animae** — Collective soul essence, memory, echoes.
- **Corona** — Solar corona essence, flares, overwhelming brilliance.
- **Chasmor** — Abyssal essence, deep oceans, crushing weight.
- **Eclipta** — Union of opposites, balance of light and dark.

## Adamant (world-shaping, rarest known)
- **[[Genesis]]** — Creative essence, birth of new forms, shaping life.
- **Cataclysm** — Destructive essence, quakes, volcanic fury, planetary scale ruin.
- **Continuum** — Spacetime fabric essence, bending distance and reality.
- **Logoth** — Word-essence, language and truth becoming [[law]].
- **Myrrhos** — World-heart essence, binding the land and ley-lines together.
