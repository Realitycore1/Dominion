Vesta is:
	•	differentiated (core, mantle, crust)
	•	metal-rich
	•	structurally solid
	•	large enough to support major excavation

If Ceres provides water, Vesta provides structure.

Primary Role
	•	Heavy metal extraction
	•	Structural fabrication
	•	Armor and hull materials
	•	Mass-driver exports
	•	Large-scale manufacturing

Vesta is where things get built.

Station Architecture
	•	Extensive surface and subsurface mining
	•	Industrial caverns
	•	Rail-based mass drivers
	•	Rotational habitats integrated into excavated voids

Unlike Ceres, Vesta can support limited surface activity.

Cultural Note

Vestan society is:
	•	industrial
	•	unionized
	•	physically demanding
	•	culturally blunt

They see themselves as the muscle of the belt.
