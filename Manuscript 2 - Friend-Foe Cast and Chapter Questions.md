# Manuscript 2 - Friend-Foe Cast and Chapter Questions

## Purpose

This workbook identifies the cast already supported by the Pleroma Archive, separates allies from opponents and uncertain loyalties, and lists the decisions required before prose drafting.

The central cast principle is:

> Every major faction needs a human face, and every important friend needs a believable reason to oppose Amber at least once.

The novel should not divide into good nations and evil nations. It should distinguish:

- people Amber trusts;
- people who protect Harmina through methods she distrusts;
- direct wartime opponents;
- conditional allies whose loyalty depends on one issue;
- institutional actors who may help or harm without personal hatred;
- GalFed officials whose benevolence is part of the threat.

## Pleroma Reference Findings

GalFed is the external administrative antagonist.

### Continuity Issues Requiring Decisions

- Low Haven is a city inside the Equilon States. Its frontier-autonomy culture may oppose Equilon policy, but it is not a ninth sovereign war faction.
- Toldax Ascent is the active Pleroma name and belongs in the design-level faction cast.
- The Coalition of Orbital States remains the eighth active Harminan power and must not disappear from an orbit-centered war.
- The active First Strike continuity says Atlovaia fires first and FreeStack follows seconds later. They are not a stable alliance and must have separate motives and separate operational faces.
- “Dr. Adams” is Dr. Augustus Adams.
- Maxine Hyden is described as an agricultural ecologist in her character note but also performs revolutionary intelligence work in earlier manuscript material. Decide whether both belong to one long career or whether one role should pass to another character.
- Exemplar Vel belongs to later Dominion continuity and is not born yet during this novel.

## Alignment Categories

### Inner Friend

Emotionally trusted even when politically opposed.

### Civic Ally

Works toward the same immediate outcome without belonging to Amber's private circle.

### Conditional Ally

Supports Amber or unification only while a specific interest remains protected.

### Internal Adversary

Belongs to Amber's side but threatens her values, autonomy, or legitimacy.

### Direct Foe

Actively attacks her people, position, institutions, or war objectives.

### External Antagonist

Operates outside Harmina's civil conflict and attempts to define Harmina through administrative control.

## Minimum Recurring Cast Target

Aim for eighteen to twenty-four recurring named characters:

- five central anchors: Amber, Emilia, Adams, Kieran, Sera;
- eight faction faces, one from each Harminan power;
- three body-war faces: proto-legionary, synthetic medic or soldier, Immortal witness;
- two ordinary family anchors from earlier Harminan lines;
- two GalFed officials;
- optional additional strike commander, archivist, or orbital officer.

Not every recurring character needs a POV. Every recurring character needs:

- a private want;
- a public role;
- a relationship crossing faction lines;
- a truth they possess;
- a truth they misunderstand;
- a decision that changes their alignment.

- every faction has at least one recurring named face;
- Atlovaia and FreeStack have separate strike chains;
- Amber has an inner friend, civic ally, conditional ally, internal adversary, and direct foe;
- one enemy performs an unmistakably humane act;
- one ally commits an unmistakably harmful act;
- Kieran and Sera have complete identities;
- the survival status of the older Pleroma characters is settled;
- the proto-legionary, synthetic, and Immortal continuity arcs each belong to named people;
- Chapter 18 and Chapter 36 have different contact functions;
- at least three family lines connect prewar Harmina to the New World War;
- every major alignment change has a specific scene rather than an explanatory summary.
