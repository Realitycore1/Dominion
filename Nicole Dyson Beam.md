A Nicoll–Dyson Beam is a stellar-scale directed energy weapon, created by outfitting a Dyson Swarm or Sphere with phased mirrors, lenses, and emitters that focus the entire output of a star into a single coherent beam. It is both civilizational [[Infrastructure]] (power transmission) and apex weapon (star-lance).
