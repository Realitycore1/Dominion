# Chapter XII - Siege of Harmina

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The conflict expands from city siege to planetary siege.

## Event Role

- Artemis, Craterheart, spires, rail networks, ports, and orbital defenses become linked objectives.
- Civilian evacuation and military movement compete for the same infrastructure.
- External-contact fear makes every faction argue that planetary control is species survival.
- Harmina itself becomes the prize rather than any single capital.

## Consequence

The war reaches its final scale: not state against state, but competing claims over the whole planet's future.
