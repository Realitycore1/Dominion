In the VFF, Oblivion Particles are meta-structural weapons and reset agents:
	•	Used as irreversible seals against dangerous anomalies or hostile Entities.
	•	Form the “cutting edge” of final-state weaponry capable of bypassing regeneration, recursion, and meta-restoration.
	•	Serve as reality sanitizers, excising corrupted or paradoxical Regions of spacetime.

In the Master Omniversal Theory, Oblivion Particles are rare and dangerous constants, appearing in only the most entropy-maximized or metaphysically decaying verses:
	•	Dead Verses — Oblivion lattices spread naturally, dissolving the last remnants of structure.
