# Government Hierarchy

## Summary

The Dominion's hierarchy is not a simple chain of command. It is a dual structure divided between present-facing governance and future-facing continuity.

The Federal Government governs daily civic life.

The Royal Government guards the long horizon.

The two governments overlap by design, and their conflict is lawful when each side is protecting its proper domain.

## Highest Principle

Continuity is the highest civilizational mandate, but continuity does not erase ordinary law.

The living present has rights.

The endangered future has claims.

The Dominion's government exists to keep those truths from destroying each other.

## Royal Government

### Celestial Throne

The Celestial Throne is the sovereign center of continuity authority.

Functions:

- final emergency authority;
- long-horizon continuity judgment;
- succession legitimacy;
- witness-chain protection;
- civilizational memory;
- final appeal in existential disputes;
- sacred-civic focus of the Dominion.

Limits:

- should not govern ordinary daily matters by default;
- emergency acts require witness, record, naming, and review;
- Amber's image may not be treated as equal to Amber's living judgment.

### Inner Witness

The Inner Witness protects the person inside the sovereign office.

Likely members:

- [[Emilia Navarro]];
- [[Voice of the Throne]];
- [[Shield of Continuity]];
- medical or longevity steward;
- memory witness;
- security custodian.

Function:

To ensure that the sovereign's will is not captured by illness, ritual machinery, bureaucracy, panic, court myth, or forged authority.

### Royal Ministries

Royal ministries handle continuity-scale systems.

Examples:

- Ministry of Provision;
- Stellar Tithe Authority;
- Continuity Archives;
- Office of Rites;
- Emergency Continuity Command;
- Imperial Memory Courts;
- relic and forbidden-technology containment offices;
- VFF and MOT risk bodies.

These ministries may coordinate with Federal institutions, but they should not absorb ordinary administration unless continuity is directly threatened.

## Federal Government

### Federal Branch

The Federal Branch is the present-facing government.

Functions:

- legislation;
- courts;
- public budgets;
- public services;
- rights protection;
- franchise and elections;
- civil security;
- agency oversight;
- provincial and state coordination;
- routine military authorization.

The Federal Branch is not anti-Royal by default. Its resistance right exists because the Royal Government needs a lawful counterweight.

### Federal Legislature

The legislature governs ordinary law, appropriations, public accountability, provincial representation, and civil policy.

It may resist Royal action by:

- refusing ordinary funding;
- demanding testimony;
- preserving dissenting records;
- holding public review;
- referring disputes to courts;
- triggering emergency sunset processes.

### Federal Courts

Courts preserve the rights floor and review federal, provincial, ministerial, and emergency action.

Important functions:

- warrants;
- due process;
- classification review;
- emergency-power review;
- rights enforcement;
- anti-corruption cases;
- continuity-law disputes when ordinary jurisdiction is implicated.

Courts may not simply overrule an immediate species-level continuity action in the moment, but they can require record, review, reparation, and future limits.

### Federal Agencies

Federal agencies handle ordinary governance.

Examples:

- education;
- health;
- finance;
- environmental protection;
- infrastructure;
- science and technology;
- civil defense;
- public administration.

Agencies become dangerous when they hide behind Royal continuity language to avoid Federal oversight.

See: [[Federal Agencies]]

## Provinces, States, and Local Compacts

### Provinces

Provinces coordinate large regional territories, sectors, worlds, habitats, or restoration zones.

They implement Federal law and may receive Royal continuity mandates during long-horizon projects.

### States

States govern more specific political units inside provinces.

They handle local law, ordinary courts, schools, infrastructure, public health, and civic administration unless superseded by higher law or continuity emergency.

### Local Governments

Local governments handle immediate civic reality:

- water;
- streets;
- transit;
- schools;
- local courts;
- safety inspections;
- local festivals;
- household registration;
- ordinary emergency response.

Local government is where citizens most directly experience the Dominion.

### Local Compacts

Local Compacts are agreements between Dominion authority and local populations, especially frontier worlds, restored worlds, habitats, and culturally distinct settlements.

They allow autonomy inside continuity.

A compact may define:

- local rites;
- environmental obligations;
- resource rights;
- school requirements;
- militia limits;
- archive duties;
- religious protections;
- evacuation obligations;
- Federal and Royal jurisdiction boundaries.

Harmina-style settlement diversity makes Local Compacts essential.

## Chain of Conflict

When governments disagree:

1. Local authority acts if the issue is immediate and local.
2. State or province escalates if resources, law, or regional impact exceed local capacity.
3. Federal Government acts if rights, budget, representation, or ordinary civil law are implicated.
4. Royal Government acts if continuity, existential risk, succession, civilizational memory, VFF/MOT instability, or long-horizon survival are implicated.
5. If Federal and Royal authority conflict, the act must be witnessed, recorded, reviewed, and assigned to the correct time horizon.

## Failure Modes

### Federal Overreach

Federal overreach occurs when present-facing politics blocks necessary future preparation.

Examples:

- underfunding continuity infrastructure;
- dismissing slow threats;
- treating VFF or MOT risks as political inconvenience;
- refusing necessary sacrifice for public popularity.

### Royal Overreach

Royal overreach occurs when future-facing authority consumes present life.

Examples:

- permanent emergency;
- secrecy without review;
- local autonomy erased by abstract planning;
- citizens treated as continuity material;
- Amber's image used to bypass law.

## Canon Formula

Throne: continuity and final burden.

Federal Branch: ordinary law and rights.

Courts: review, due process, warrants, and rights floor.

Ministries: specialized execution.

Provinces and states: regional administration.

Local compacts: autonomy inside continuity.

The Dominion is healthy only when this hierarchy remains contested but lawful.
