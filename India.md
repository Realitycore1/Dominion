India is a vast and diverse country located in South Asia, bordered by [[Pakistan]], China, Nepal, and Bhutan. It is home to over 1.[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] billion people, making it the second most populous country in the world. India's population is spread across vast plains and mountains, making it one of the most densely populated countries on [[Earth (Terra)]]. The Indian political [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] is a federal constitutional republic with a parliamentary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]. The President of India is the head of state, while the Prime Minister and his cabinet form the executive branch. The legislative branch consists of two houses - the Lok Sabha (lower house) and the Rajya Sabha (upper house). Economically, India is a fast-growing market with significant opportunities in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]], agriculture, healthcare, and other sectors. It has become a global leader in manufacturing, information [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]], and services. Despite its significant economic growth, India continues to struggle with poverty and inequality, particularly in rural areas. India's social fabric is marked by diverse cultural traditions and religions. The country has a large Hindu majority, followed by Muslim, Christian, Sikh, and Buddhist minorities. Despite these differences, Indian society is generally harmonious and tolerant. The country has been shaped by a complex [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] of empires, conquests, invasions, and migrations that have left an indelible mark on its cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]. It is home to some of the oldest civilizations in the world, including the Indus Valley Civilization and the Mauryan Empire.
