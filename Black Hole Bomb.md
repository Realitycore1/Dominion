The two components that are needed for the B.H.B are a spinning black hole and a mirror similar in shape to a [[Dyson Sphere]].
The trigger for the bomb is to [[Fire]] electromagnetic waves at the black hole at light speed. As the waves hit the Ergosphere a small portion falls into the black hole, The rest is amplified by the rotational energy of the black hole itself. The waves begin Superradiant Scattering. This process continues to build up until the energy is released.
