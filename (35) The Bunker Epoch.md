Humanity exists in disconnected enclave-realms.

Communication between bunker systems is:

* dangerous,
* delayed,
* and often fatal.

Many expeditions never return.

This period forms the psychological foundation of the Secundus Dominion:

* paranoia,
* continuity obsession,
* militarized preservation,
* and distrust of uncontrolled reality.

The surviving Royal Legions reorganize into:

The Secundus Founding Chapters.

Not conquerors.

Recovery orders.
