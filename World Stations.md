## Summary

Magnus Bast
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
World Stations are planet-mass or near-planetary-mass stations, often diamond, cube, or monolithic in form, used as occupation platforms, logistical hubs, and symbols of imperial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]. Unlike Dyson crowns or StarForges, they are focused, singular icons of power — black monuments drifting against the [[Stars]].
Structure

Geometric Hulls: Massive diamond, cruciform, or cubic bodies kilometers to hundreds of kilometers across.

Core Spine: Anchored by a Matter Core or [[Confluencia]] tether to stabilize its gravitational influence.

Habitat Mantles: [[Layers]] of vault-like living quarters and reliquaries for millions.

Glyph Crown: Surfaces inscribed with Concordia scripture, glowing across [[The Void]].

Functions

Civil:
– Anchor hubs for trade, orbital [[Infrastructure]], and colony oversight.
– Provide living space equivalent to cities or continents.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Occupation platforms used to enforce planetary compliance.
– Bristle with orbital weapons, planetary suppression arrays, and fleet command decks.
– Can project Quarantine Curtains or Insurance Nets over whole worlds.

Religious:
– Known as “Amber Thrones” — mythologized as celestial [[Temples]] floating in the firmament.
– Serve as pilgrimage sites: citizens ascend from planets to pray in the shadow of their world’s station.

Variants

Civil Thrones: Trade and habitation [[Citadels]], less militarized.

War Thrones: Fortress-stations for planetary dominance.

Sacral Thrones: Stations built purely as monuments, reliquaries, or “floating [[Temples]].”

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Serve as the visible hand of order, reminding every world they orbit who rules them.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Keystone in planetary control — one World Station can keep entire continents subdued.

Economic: Function as cosmic ports, where entire fleets dock and redistribute resources.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Stations ribbed with buttress-like ridges, cavernous interiors like vault cathedrals.

Suprematist Monolithism: Pure geometric silhouettes — cubes, diamonds, pyramids — sharp against starlight.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Outer surfaces shift with fractal glyph-light, living mandalas of [[Fire]].

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Central cores glow with scripture-light, a radiant “eye” or sigil staring into [[The Void]].

From below on a planet, a World Station looks like a dark sun eclipsing the heavens, a geometric shadow with a glowing script-heart — the weight of empire hovering above.

Narrative Function

To myth: Called the “Amber Thrones”, described as seats of heaven itself, set into orbit to remind mortals of their Empress’s gaze.

Religious: Called the “Amber Thrones of the Sky” — myths say each one is an extension of the Empress’s will, placed to watch over her children.

To citizens: Awe and security — their world is literally overseen.

To rebels: Terror — the station is both overseer and executioner.
