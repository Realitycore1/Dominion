# (3400) Antimatter Drives

Industrial antimatter production and practical antimatter-supported drives are demonstrated in 3400.

Antimatter is manufactured in high-energy accelerator and capture complexes. It is not mined.

Production requires:

- fusion-scale power generation;
- accelerator time;
- particle capture and cooling;
- magnetic confinement;
- remote handling;
- distributed storage;
- large exclusion and emergency-ejection volumes.

Uranian systems provide cryogenic containment, cartridge standards, and transfer engineering. Neptunian and Triton facilities become the principal production centers.

Small quantities catalyze fusion ignition and high-energy maneuvers. The *C.U.C. Deliverance* uses a larger antimatter-supported interstellar drive designed for sustained acceleration and deceleration.

No inhabited station stores the mission's full inventory in one place. Fuel cartridges move through isolated robotic transfer systems and remain politically controlled from production through launch.

Control of antimatter becomes a cause and constraint of the Second Solar War.
