Classification: Leviathan Class – Tier XI / Field Designate “Sol’Drath” (Solar Burrower)
Observation:
Depiction captures a colossal serpentine organism partially emerged from desert terrain, its form glistening under intense solar exposure. Outer integument composed of concentric ridges resistant to abrasion and heat dissipation. Cranial dome smooth and reflective, concealing sensor organs beneath layered chitin. Ventral cavity expels fine particulate streams, indicating subdermal filtration [[Systems]] capable of converting sand and mineral particulate into metabolizable compounds. Environmental context confirms equatorial latitude under peak irradiance.

Contextual Analysis:
Sol’Drath [[Entities]] dominate [[Harmina]]’s desert belts, functioning as thermodynamic regulators. Seismic readings indicate their migration patterns correspond to planetary temperature stabilization cycles. Colonists recorded them only at dawn or dusk, moments when surface temperatures shift rapidly. Despite size estimates exceeding two kilometers in length, their energy signature remains minimal—absorptive dermal fields mask biological emissions entirely. Remnant tunnels discovered post-[[Ascension]] serve as subterranean conduits for atmosphere exchange and mineral flow.
