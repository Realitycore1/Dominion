Core Bias:

Modeled or emulated continuity

This subtype is where reality behaves like:

a simulation, representation, or deliberately constrained version of something else.

Traits:
	•	abstraction layers
	•	emulated ecosystems or laws
	•	parameterized reality
	•	high controllability
	•	subtle or overt “designedness”

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	this place was built to imitate another place
	•	reality has settings, boundaries, and assumptions
	•	continuity feels curated

Common Expressions:
	•	simulated worlds
	•	training or test basins
	•	emulation cosmologies
	•	controlled afterlife or educational environments

Failure Mode:

Model divergence
The simulation drifts too far from what it was meant to represent.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	artificial heavens / hells
	•	educational or containment verses
	•	AI-run realities
	•	“built but not original” cosmologies
