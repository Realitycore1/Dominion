Tier I – Sub-Conceptual (Fragmentary / Demigod Level)
	•	Scope: Below a full [[Domain]]; partial, broken, or dependent [[Entities]].
	•	Examples: Demigods, fallen or crippled gods, incarnated aspects, saint-like figures bound to one myth-thread.
	•	Nature: Limited power; often tied to geography, lineage, or a single story. Vulnerable to being consumed or subsumed by higher tiers.
	•	Analogy: A spark of divinity without the full [[Fire]].

⸻

Tier II – Single Concept (Pure [[Domain]] Deities)
	•	Scope: One absolute, indivisible concept.
	•	Examples: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], Undeath, [[Fire]], Harvest, Rivers, Memory.
	•	Nature: Unshakable within their lane, but blind outside it.
	•	Analogy: A perfect circle — total mastery of one thing, but nothing beyond.
	•	Historical Echo: Many Mesopotamian or Egyptian gods, who ruled a single defined power.

⸻

Tier III – Multi-Concept (Composite / Paradoxical Deities)
	•	Scope: Two or more [[Domains]], blended or paradoxically unified.
	•	Examples: Athena (Wisdom + War/Strategy), Hecate (Magic + Night + Crossroads), Shiva (Destruction + Renewal).
	•	Nature: Complex and paradoxical; their power comes from [[Synthesis]]. Often the most “relatable” to mortals because they embody contradictions of life.
	•	Analogy: A woven braid — multiple strands of concept bound together.

⸻

Tier IV – All-Encompassing (Totalizing Deities)
	•	Scope: Encompass all things, or claim supremacy over all [[Domains]].
	•	Examples: Odin, Yahweh, Allah, the Source, Great Mother [[Archetypes]].
	•	Nature: All [[Domains]] are nested within them. They are not “of” concepts; concepts are “of” them.
	•	Analogy: The canvas on which all the paintings are drawn — or the painter itself.
