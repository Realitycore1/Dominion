# MOT — Expansion (Footprint, Corridors, Energy)

**Aim.** Model how civilizations spread in space and [[Time]] under corridor constraints, finite ship speeds, and S-curve technologies. Deliver knobs a writer can turn without breaking plausibility.

## 1) Regimes of Expansion
- **Sublight Frontier (early):** sphere growth limited by cruise speed \(v\) and launch cadence \(\lambda\). Reach \(R(t)\approx v t\).  
- **Corridor-Bound FTL (Gen-I):** effective reach dominated by **window graph** \(G=(V,E)\); travel [[Time]] is hop-sum, not Euclidean.  
- **Mixed Fleet:** bulk freight remains sublight/high-Isp; FTL used for people, command, SAR, high-value cargo.

## 2) Growth Models (rule-of-thumb)
- **Footprint ([[Systems]] settled):** \(\dot N \propto d\_w\,\lambda\, t^{\alpha}\), with \(\alpha\in2,[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]\) for frontier-limited growth; \(d\_w\) = window density.  
- **Energy (Kardashev):** \(\dot K \sim \beta\, (\text{radiator area}) - \gamma\, (losses)\).  
- **Corridor Throughput:** \(C\_{corr} = \sum\_{e\in E} b\_e\) with per-edge bandwidth \(b\_e\) capped by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] weather (η) and sanctity.

> **Translation:** increase windows (|E|), raise bandwidth (b_e), or more launches (λ) → faster growth. But geometry and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] weather cap you.

## 3) Rails by Era (canon-aligned)
- **1980–2300:** stacked exponentials on [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]; first slow interstellar; K~0.8–0.83.  
- **2300–3000:** geometric spatial growth; PFN comms; high-Isp arks (0.05–0.2c); K~0.88–0.92.  
- **3785–4000 (Gen-I FTL):** corridors come online; hop speeds 1.1–5c on primes; spool/cool mandatory; mixed fleets persist; K~1.0–1.1.

## 4) Corridor Economics
- **Slots & Permits:** windows are scheduled; **RAC/DVAA** allocate slots; sabotage is mostly **permit warfare**.  
- **Wayhouses:** cache heat, spare parts, and proofs; raise local Φ and ℓᶜ.  
- **Externalities:** each new window perturbs η and \(\mathcal{K}\); too many in a cluster → **knotting** and outages.

## 5) KPIs (keep the world consistent)
- **Windows open (|E|), window bandwidth (∑b_e)**  
- **[[Systems]] in union (N), corridor diameter (graph radius)**  
- **Population off-world (%), radiator area (km²)**  
- **ΔP(survival)** from Continuity audits

## 6) Scenarios to plug in
- **Corridor-First:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] floods standards & window builds → fast political integration along rails; hinterlands lag.  
- **Slowline Resilience:** communities stick to sublight; [[Domain]] looks fast but brittle; revolt travels the old lines.  
- **Chokepoint War:** a prime corridor goes down; **quarantine geographies** appear overnight; SAR becomes strategy.

## 7) Safety & Norms
- **[[Radiator Neutrality]]** except in declared total war.  
- **Light-Hygiene** in ports and festivals (protect [[Silentium]]/[[Kapik]]).  
- **Sanctity Budgets:** don’t oversanctify; keep η manageable.
