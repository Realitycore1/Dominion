# Government Structure

## Current Canon Frame

The Dominion is a dual government divided by time horizon.

The Federal Government is the constitutional, representative, service-franchised government of the living present. It is well suited for courts, ordinary law, rights, budgets, public services, provincial administration, infrastructure maintenance, and day-to-day civil order.

The Royal Government is the continuity government of the endangered future. It is well suited for long-horizon planning, civilizational memory, existential defense, VFF and MOT risk, succession integrity, restoration after collapse, and threats that ordinary politics is too short-sighted to sustain.

This means the Dominion can be both a constitutional republic in daily civic life and a sacred survival monarchy in long-horizon continuity matters.

The Federal Government is ill equipped for centuries-long planning because it is responsive to elections, public pressure, provincial bargaining, and immediate needs. The Royal Government is ill equipped for ordinary daily governance because its horizon can become abstract, ceremonial, and too willing to overshadow local life.

The system works only when both governments are strong enough to resist each other.

See: [[Dual Government Model]]

## Federal Agency Layer

The Federal Branch governs daily civilization through reviewable agencies rather than royal ministries.

Core agency responsibilities include:

- civil registry and franchise;
- education;
- health;
- public works;
- megatrain and port transit;
- family welfare;
- labor standards;
- finance;
- resource allocation;
- environmental protection;
- science and AI accountability;
- court administration;
- emergency response;
- foreign and domestic security within legal limits.

Federal agencies are legitimate when they remain budgeted, audited, appealable, and subject to court review.

See: [[Federal Agencies]]

A Democratic Stratocratic Meritocracy is a form of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] that combines the principles of democracy, stratocracy, and meritocracy. In a Democratic Stratocratic Meritocracy, the people have the power to choose their [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] through a democratic process, but the leadership positions are reserved for those who have demonstrated the necessary skills, abilities, and competencies to fulfill those roles. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] in such a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] are typically stratocrats, who are drawn from a meritocratic pool of qualified individuals. This pool is composed of people who have undergone rigorous testing and selection processes to determine their abilities and suitability for leadership roles. Once selected, these [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] serve in a stratocratic manner, meaning that they are responsible for executing their duties with the utmost efficiency and effectiveness. They are held accountable for their actions and decisions by the people, who have the power to revoke their leadership positions through democratic means if they are found to be incompetent or unfit for the role. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] in a Democratic Stratocratic Meritocracy is structured to encourage and reward merit, rather than favoritism or nepotism. This means that individuals who demonstrate exceptional talent, intelligence, and hard work are rewarded with opportunities to rise to leadership positions.
