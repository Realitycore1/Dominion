I. The Committee Years (2350–2520)

The Jovial Colonial Committee (JCC) was established by the [[Colonial Union]] as the administrative body for the Jovian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]. Its focus: regulating claims on the “big four” moons — Io, Europa, [[Ganymede]], and [[Callisto]].
	•	Io: mining colony, source of sulfur and exotic volcanics, but plagued by radiation.
	•	Europa: ice tunnels and ocean probes, tantalizing for its subsurface sea.
	•	[[Ganymede]]: chosen as administrative hub for the JCC — magnetic field offered partial radiation shielding.
	•	[[Callisto]]: deep-space staging ground, ideal for low-radiation [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].

The JCC was bureaucratic and stretched thin. It couldn’t manage the sheer scale and diversity of the moons, relying heavily on corpo fleets and PMCs to enforce order. Colonists resented being treated as expendable labor for “resource labs.”

II. The Age of Moons (2460–2520)
	•	Io Guilds: [[Workers]] on Io, hardened by radiation storms, formed guilds to resist corpo exploitation. They saw themselves as “fireborn,” forged in volcanism and danger.
	•	Europa Divers: Colonists bored tunnels into ice crust, living in pressurized caverns. They became spiritual about the subsurface ocean, speaking of it as “the deep mother.”
	•	[[Ganymede]] Cities: With its magnetosphere and relative safety, [[Ganymede]] became home to the first permanent Jovian megacities — vast domes, ports, and trade hubs.
	•	[[Callisto]] Bastions: Fortified [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]], staging points for expansion into the [[Outer System]]. Became havens for independents and dissidents fleeing Committee rule.

Each moon developed its own [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]], straining against the Committee’s centralized control.

⸻

III. The Storm [[Schism]] (2520–2560)

The turning point came when a JCC directive forced Io guilds to increase sulfur exports to fuel starshade projects back in the [[Inner System]]. [[Workers]] revolted, sabotaging mining convoys. Radiation storms made suppression nearly impossible.
	•	Europa Divers joined in protest, threatening to flood ice tunnels and abandon bases unless given autonomy.
	•	[[Ganymede]] citizens staged mass strikes, halting port traffic.
	•	[[Callisto]] bastions declared outright independence, refusing to recognize the JCC.

This [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]-[[Wide]] unrest became known as the Storm [[Schism]], named for the great red spot — a storm as eternal as Jovian unrest.

	•	Io Fireborn, Europa Divers, [[Ganymede]] Citizens, and [[Callisto]] Bastions all kept their own traditions, but recognized shared kinship: children of a [[Giant]]’s moons.
	•	Symbols: braided banners of four colors ([[Fire]], ice, steel, shadow), representing each great moon.
	•	[[culture]]:
	•	Pragmatic, but spiritual — seeing Jupiter’s storms as omens, the moons as siblings.
	•	Highly decentralized, with each moon governing itself but united under a Council of Clans.

V. Toward the Jovian Confederacy (2600+)

By the mid-27th century, the JCC collapsed. Corpo fleets withdrew after years of guerrilla strikes and sabotage. Out of the chaos, the Jovian Confederacy formed:
	•	A loose alliance of moonclans, guilds, and city councils.
	•	Bound not by [[law]] but by survival and shared heritage.
	•	[[Ganymede]] remained the trade hub, Europa the spiritual heart, Io the forge, and [[Callisto]] the shield.

The Confederacy became the Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]’s largest outer power, rivaling Mars and the Belt in population and influence.
