# Story 21 - Accords No One Named

## Viewpoint

Corin Aves, a printer hired to duplicate route schedules and apprenticeship recognition forms.

## Setting

Year 151 AL, a settlement conference no one wants to call diplomacy.

## Story Shape

Corin notices that the documents look boring only if no one reads them closely. Train priority, shelter obligations, pressure-zone repair, water access, archive transfer, and school credential recognition are all acts of power.

The delegates insist they are not forming blocs. Then they fight over wording like states.

## Harmina Element

Proto-national politics emerges through infrastructure.

## Character Movement

Corin begins amused by administrative vanity. He ends afraid of how much future conflict is being hidden inside practical language.

## Future Payoff

The accords become the legal memory later powers invoke before the New World War.
