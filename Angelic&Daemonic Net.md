## Summary

In fiction, Angelic and Daemonic Networks are 
advanced forms of communication and information [[Technology Portal|technology]]nology]] that are typically used by celestial beings or beings with supernatural abilities. Angelic Networks are networks that allow [[Angels]] or other celestial beings to communicate and share information across vast distances. They can also be used to manipulate or control other beings or events. Daemonic Networks, on the other hand, are often associated with [[Demons]] or other malevolent forces. They are darker and more sinister versions of the Angelic Networks, designed to spread corruption, manipulation, and chaos. Both Angelic and Daemonic Networks are often depicted as highly advanced and powerful technologies that require a significant amount of energy and resources to maintain. They are typically accessed through specialized devices or rituals, making them less accessible to ordinary beings. In many stories, Angelic and Daemonic Networks play an important role in shaping the narrative, as they allow the protagonists or antagonists to gain an advantage over their opponents.
