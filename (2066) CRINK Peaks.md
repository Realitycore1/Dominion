# (2066) CRINK Peaks

## Definitive Event

CRINK reaches maximum aggregate industrial and military output in 2066.

The peak consists of:

- integrated freight and programmable currency;
- hardened rail, food reserves, and continuity districts;
- shared surveillance and targeting data;
- orbital factories and the Mandate Fleet;
- biotechnology and designed-service cohorts;
- North Korean launch forces supplied through the common network.

The peak does not mean political unity.

India and China continue disputing sovereign command after the concealed 2041 border war. China increasingly describes itself as the Neo Xia continuity and places Mandate doctrine above the alliance's nominal equality. Russia keeps independent strategic release systems. North Korea uses CRINK guidance while Chairwoman Kim Yo-jong rejects joint approval of her launches.

A North Korean missile crosses Japanese airspace during the 2066 exercises without joint authorization. India suspends one tracking relay. China restores the data through a military channel. Russia suppresses the dispute until the exercises end.

The event establishes the central contradiction of the CRINK Peaks:

**The bloc can coordinate production faster than it can agree who commands the result.**

This contradiction directly prepares the 2073 fracture and leaves dangerous shared systems active through the 2078 Exchange.
