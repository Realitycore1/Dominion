Nagoya Prefecture, 2039
[[Akira Sato]] rose before dawn, as his father had, and his grandfather before him. He bent his back to the paddies, water cold around his ankles, dragonflies darting between the stalks.

On the radio, an announcer’s voice trembled with excitement: the Jade Seal of China authenticated, paraded as proof of Heaven’s Mandate. Across the sea, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] were returning. Akira shut it off. Rice did not grow faster for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]].

⸻

Atsuta Shrine, 2058
He stood among thousands when the Emperor unveiled the [[Sword of Kusanagi]]. [[Soldiers]] raised banners, youths raised fists, the shrine bells thundered.

“Behold the [[Dragon]]-slayer!” priests cried. “Japan’s destiny is reborn!”

Akira clapped politely, but unease gnawed at him. He felt the eyes of his eldest son, Haruto, burning with pride. That night Haruto told him he would enlist. “Father, this is our purpose.”

Akira said nothing. The paddies still needed planting.

⸻

Nagoya Prefecture, 2064
The shrine militias came in trucks, stamping serpent insignia onto every sack of rice. Half the harvest, seized “for the Emperor and the Sword.” Akira signed the tithe papers with a steady hand, but rage burned in his chest.

At night, he found Haruto’s boots by the door, caked with mud from some distant camp. His boy no longer came home. His wife placed the boots on a shrine shelf, whispering prayers to Kusanagi.

Akira whispered none.

⸻

Nagoya Prefecture, 2068
News came like rain on a dry field: Haruto dead, killed in a relic fleet battle over the Pacific. His wife wailed. The shrine priest visited with solemn words: “Your son lives forever in the Sword.”

Akira bowed, silent. Later, alone, he touched [[V - Beast Chronicles/Undead & Constructs/The Empty|The Empty]] boots. He prayed not to the Sword, but to the memory of his boy’s laughter.

⸻

Nagoya Prefecture, 2075
He sheltered deserters in his barn — gaunt young men with haunted eyes, fleeing shrine militias. They knelt, ashamed, and begged forgiveness. Akira gave them rice and [[Silence]]. He knew they were all someone’s sons.

The Emperor’s broadcasts still thundered through loudspeakers: “Japan is the [[Dragon]]-slayer nation!” Akira stopped listening. He weeded the paddies. The soil was honest, at least.

⸻

Nagoya Prefecture, 2078
The flash came from the east. A light brighter than dawn, rolling like thunder across the sea. His wife screamed. The [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] trembled.

Akira knelt in the flooded field, water around his knees, watching the sky burn. He thought of Haruto’s boots, still by the door.

He prayed, not to Kusanagi, not to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], but to his lost son.

Then the horizon turned to [[Fire]].
