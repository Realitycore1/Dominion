# Story 09 - The Day the Founder Did Not Speak

## Viewpoint

Nera Voss, a meeting clerk responsible for recording public questions after the food crisis.

## Setting

Early New Aurora, a civic assembly hall.

## Story Shape

Everyone arrives expecting the founder to settle the colony's fear by command. Nera prepares blank pages for decisive words.

Instead, the public answer is procedural, limited, and unsatisfying. The founder refuses to become the colony's substitute for institutions. The crowd hates it before slowly understanding what it means: if they want law, food, schools, and repair schedules, they will have to build them.

## Harmina Element

Political legitimacy is shown through paperwork, public disappointment, and the birth of local civic responsibility.

## Character Movement

Nera begins as a recorder waiting for history to speak. She ends knowing that sometimes history is the empty space where people expected command.

## Future Payoff

This story keeps the main cast offstage while showing why Harmina's later government values lawful counterweights and administrative competence.
