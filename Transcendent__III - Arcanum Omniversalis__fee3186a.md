Transcendent [[Domains]] are meta-axiomatic forces — they govern not just effects within a [[Verse]], but the framework of [[(2030) Existence]] itself. They are rarely wielded directly; most mortals and even [[Angels]] only touch them through rituals, artifacts, or god-tier [[Entities]].

⸻

1. Nullity ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Absence)
	•	Essence: Erasure, void, the space “between verses.”
	•	Use: Suppression of [[law]], annihilation of essence, passage through the gaps of creation.
	•	Risks: Exposure collapses [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]; prolonged use creates Hollow states.
	•	Wielded by: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Navigators (Null Anchors), Archons of [[The Gnostic Expanse]].

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Intention ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Drive)
	•	Essence: The will that crosses Nullity; the ability of a being to impress purpose upon reality.
	•	Use: Powers [[Verse]] travel, constructs, or miracles; creates bridges where no [[law]] exists.
	•	Risks: Overextension dissolves the self into pure motive spirit.
	•	Wielded by: [[God Empress]], rare prophets, Empyrearchs.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Axiom ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Root [[law]])
	•	Essence: Control over the fundamental “roots” that generate verses.
	•	Use: Warping base [[Axioms]], rewriting constants, birthing pocket realities.
	•	Risks: A misstep collapses a [[Verse]]; even minor changes ripple infinitely.
	•	Wielded by: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]] of the Master Omniversal Theory, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Axiomcraft labs.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Dream ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Oneirogenesis)
	•	Essence: The collective unconscious, [[Archetypes]], possibility.
	•	Use: Creation of verses based on archetypal resonance, reality through dream-logic.
	•	Risks: Inconsistency; dream-made [[Realms]] may collapse if [[Forgotten]].
	•	Wielded by: The Oneirotheon, archetypal [[Entities]], Dreamwalkers.

⸻

5. Eternity ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Continuum)
	•	Essence: [[Time]] beyond [[Time]]; stability past heat [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].
	•	Use: Stasis fields, [[Dark Eternities]], preservation of [[Verse]]-cores.
	•	Risks: Creates causal deadlocks; mortals trapped in eternity cannot grow or change.
	•	Wielded by: Aeternal Custodians, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Eternal Anchors.

⸻

6. Logos ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of Word / Pattern)
	•	Essence: Speech, true names, resonance — the code of reality itself.
	•	Use: Commanding essence through [[Words of Power]], rewriting with utterance.
	•	Risks: Mispronunciation corrupts [[law]]; forbidden lexicons open pathways to [[Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)]].
	•	Wielded by: Seraphic [[Choirs]], [[Infernariate]] black linguists, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Codex Masters.

⸻

7. Apotheosis ([[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] of [[Ascension]])
	•	Essence: The path from mortal to divine, elevation beyond scale.
	•	Use: Empowering souls to transcend, fusing with motive spirits, birthing new [[Archetypes]].
	•	Risks: False [[Ascension]] leads to monstrosity; soul collapse common.
	•	Wielded by: Empyrearchs, ascended saints, failed god-kings.
