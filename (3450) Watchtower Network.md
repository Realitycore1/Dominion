# (3450) Watchtower Network

The Kuiper Belt Watchtower Network becomes operational in 3450.

Its civil mission is to detect and characterize:

- long-period comets;
- perturbed Kuiper objects;
- inbound interstellar bodies;
- asteroid fragments placed on hazardous transfer paths;
- debris and failed spacecraft approaching through outer routes.

The network uses:

- long-baseline optical and infrared telescopes;
- occultation surveys;
- distributed trajectory correlation;
- autonomous survey craft;
- local radar at inner warning nodes;
- deflection-tug and evacuation coordination;
- redundant warning archives.

Observation creates warning time. It does not guarantee interception.

## Wartime Conflict

Inner-system governments demand real-time Union access and command authority.

Outer-system governments argue that a network built and maintained by Kuiper communities cannot become surveillance for their shipping and defense movements.

During the Second Solar War, both sides use Watchtower data to track fleets. Sensor stations become targets while crews continue natural-hazard duty.

The postwar settlement places Watchtower under a joint hazard authority with public logs, divided command keys, and limits on military tasking.

Watchtower navigation archives later guide the *C.U.C. Deliverance* to Proxima.
