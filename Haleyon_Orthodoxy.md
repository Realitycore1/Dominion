### Character
Faith and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Culture & Society/Daily Life|Daily Life]] are fully integrated; services and study multiple times per day. The Holy Will speaks via visions (golden magic); the Angelic Empyrean enforces rarely, but decisively.

### Doctrine (public catechism, abridged)
- The Holy Will exists; debate is interpretation, not [[(2030) Existence]].
- [[Angels]] do not fall; [[Nephilim]] are not born of [[Angel]]‑mortal unions.
- Heresy [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]] maintained by the **[[Infernariate]]** (inquisitorial body).

### Operations
- City bells set by liturgical hours; market schedules co‑tuned.
- Investigative arm: [[Infernariate]] (see 09_Organizations/[[Infernariate]].md).
