Classification: Elementals & Primals
Subtype: Martial Fire-Elementals / Incarnate Conflagrations
Status: Active, Semi-Sapient

⸻

Core Description

Ifrit are living incarnations of violent fire, appearing as towering humanoids or bestial forms composed of flame, embers, obsidian, and molten sinew. Their bodies constantly shed sparks and ash, as though reality itself is burning away around them.

Where Efreeti rule fire, Ifrit are fire in motion.

They are not refined, patient, or political. They are combustion given intent.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Ifrit originate primarily from Fundamentum Elementalis (Fire) but are often contaminated or accelerated by Malice when near Sheol-Gehenna or Infernal breaches.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Fundamentum Elementalis (Fire)
	•	Secondary Influence: Sheol-Gehenna (Malice), Tempest Realms
	•	Realm Interface: Elemental Chaos Zones, Volcanic Thresholds

Ifrit do not form through birth. They ignite—often triggered by:
	•	mass death by fire,
	•	collapsed fire rituals,
	•	uncontrolled elemental engines,
	•	shattered Efreeti bindings.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Ifrit possess focused but narrow cognition.

They understand:
	•	combat,
	•	dominance,
	•	targets,
	•	and escalation.

They do not understand:
	•	mercy,
	•	patience,
	•	long-term planning beyond immediate conquest.

Some elder Ifrit develop:
	•	personal vendettas,
	•	crude honor codes,
	•	or battlefield identities.

But they never lose their core nature: to burn forward.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Ifrit behave like shock troops of the elemental planes.

Common patterns:
	•	appearing in swarms or warbands,
	•	overwhelming defenses through brute force,
	•	igniting landscapes to deny territory,
	•	hunting entities that suppress fire.

They are drawn instinctively to:
	•	cities,
	•	fortifications,
	•	forests,
	•	libraries,
	•	anything structured.

Ifrit do not hate life.
They hate containment.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Ifrit rarely bargain.

When they do, it is:
	•	direct,
	•	brutal,
	•	and temporary.

An Ifrit pact usually means:
	•	serving as a weapon,
	•	being pointed at an enemy,
	•	or feeding it destruction.

Mortals who attempt to command an Ifrit often become:
	•	fuel,
	•	collateral,
	•	or trophies.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to cold-dominant harmonics and null fields
	•	Lose cohesion in Dream-Wastes or Echoic realms
	•	Can be destabilized by symbolic suppression (fire without meaning weakens them)
	•	Overexertion leads to fragmentation into lesser fire elementals

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Efreeti: View Ifrit as expendable soldiers or dangerous assets
	•	Elemental Colossi: Ifrit may swarm Colossi like parasites or worship them
	•	Demons: Temporary allies; Malice resonates but does not control
	•	Mortals: Obstacles or ignition points

Efreeti command through authority.
Ifrit obey only momentum.
