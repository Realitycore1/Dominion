Type: Reptilian xeno-carnivores
[[Origin]] Planet: Kal’Tarak – a tidally locked, superheated planet featuring scorched “sunplateaus,” obsidian valleys, and sacred volcanic calderas
Federation Role: Reluctantly allied shock troops, [[Ritual]] enforcers, and border-world purifiers

Physiology:
	•	Evolved in extreme solar radiation and thermal instability, the Kulavens developed dense, heat-resistant scale armor layered with reflective silica strands.
	•	Quadratojugal skull structure enables crushing bite force with triple-layered jaws—adapted for fracturing bones, metal, and exoshells alike.
	•	Internal circulatory [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] incorporates copper-based hemolymph, giving them bright green blood and enhanced heat transfer capacity.
	•	Height: [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].5 to [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] meters on average.
	•	Limb Design: Digitigrade with powerful leaping tendons and hook-ended claws; four-fingered manipulators capable of fine movement, though often used for gripping prey.
	•	Senses:
	•	Visual spectrum includes thermal and polarized light
	•	Nasal clusters detect not just scent, but emotional pheromones and electrical field disturbances (allowing for low-light ambushes)
	•	Reproduction:
	•	Lays heat-reactive eggs in [[Ritual]] nesting pits lined with obsidian glass. Hatchlings undergo solar exposure trials to determine caste.
gid tribal-theocracy—sacrifice is seen as sacred; they consume sentient prey ceremonially.

Core Philosophy:
“The Sun Does Not Forgive.”
	•	The Kulavens are sun-worshipping zealots, viewing solar radiation as the purifying force of the cosmos.
	•	Their theology is built on the principle that heat is divinity, and that the cold (literal and symbolic) is heresy.
	•	Rituals & Beliefs:
	•	Pre-battle consecrations involve standing immobile beneath the midday sun until the outer scales harden and crack.
	•	Consume enemies ceremonially—not out of [[Hunger]], but to absorb their “unworthy heat.”
	•	Sacred texts are burn-etched into skin or obsidian tablets, written in a calligraphy of melted grooves.
	•	Language:
	•	Harsh hisses and bone-snapping clicks, with a sideband of infrasonic pulses for command [[Relays]]
	•	Written form resembles scorched mandalas—radial heat-crack patterns representing spoken ideograms

Hierarchy:
	•	Tribal-theocratic clans ruled by Solar Hierophants and Battle-Singers
	•	[[Ascension]] is tied to heat endurance—those who survive exposure to solar flares or plasma surges are reborn as Sunborn
	•	Dissenters are ritually “cooled”—immobilized and left in shadow-caves until their internal flame fades
	•	Social Order:
	•	[[Warrior]] caste dominant, but supported by Artisan-Forgers (heat-smiths), Sunpriests, and Ashborne (those who survived being consumed by [[Fire]])
	•	Justice:
	•	Based on thermal balance. Theft or [[Betrayal]] is seen as stealing warmth; the punishment is branding, cauterization, or [[Ritual]] scorching

Weaponry:
	•	Favor radiant combustion blades, solar-charged mass drivers, and heat-lash whips
	•	Many weapons incorporate sunstone capacitors—crystals that absorb solar energy and release it in controlled detonations
	•	Armor is ablative and black-glass plated, designed to refract radiation and focus plasma bursts
	•	Craft:
	•	Ships resemble flying obelisks with molten-core drives.
	•	Their vessels emit continuous heat and often radiate visible firestorms in atmo-entry.
	•	Unshielded internal design reflects their belief in exposure—no coolant, minimal protection, only resilience
	•	Engineering:
	•	Refuse to use AI or cold logic [[Systems]]—rely on [[Ritual]] codices and living interface-oracles (bioengineered reptiles that function as processors)

Viewed as dangerous but useful allies—the Federation keeps them [[Close]] but heavily monitored
	•	Serve as shock vanguard forces in environments inhospitable to other [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]] (plasma storms, irradiated worlds, fusion wars)
	•	Most conflicts involve negotiating with Kulaven Sunsingers to control their tendency toward religious purification campaigns
	•	Despite their brutality, their code of solar honor makes them rigidly reliable—if a pact is made under sunflare, it cannot be broken
