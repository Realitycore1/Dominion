## **XI. Legion Variant Entry: Legio Izanami**

**Title:** Legio Izanami – The Veilwalkers

**[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] through entropy, [[Silence]], and inevitability. Legio Izanami embody decay and the slow embrace of [[The Void]], fighting as shadows that unravel both body and spirit. Their creed: *“All returns to the Veil.”*

### **Armor Variant – Panoply of Izanami**

* **Silhouette:** Slender yet ominous, armor designed to appear spectral. Flowing plating layered like burial shrouds, trailing edges that dissolve into drifting motes.
* **Atmospheric Projectors:** Emit wisps of pale mist that cling to the ground, interlaced with faint violet light. Enemies perceive them as wraiths walking from another realm.
* **Coloration:** Bone-white and pale ash plating, trimmed with violet and void-black. Faint cracks glow with eerie purple light, as though the armor itself is decaying.
* **Helmet:** Masked visors shaped like serene [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]]-masks, eyes glowing dim indigo. Some helms feature elongated features evoking Noh masks or funerary effigies.
* **Specialization:** Veil Generators—armor [[Systems]] that distort visual and sensory perception, allowing Izanami to appear and vanish within mist. Capable of disorienting enemies with spectral afterimages.

### **Legion Standard Arms – Izanami Configuration**

* **Form Bias:** Prefers Carbine and Pistol forms for [[Close]] ambushes, with Rifles for precision veil-[[Fire]].
* **Modification:** Weapons emit pale violet plasma bolts that unravel matter on contact. Energy trails linger like ghostly streaks.
* **Aesthetic:** Bone-white and void-black alloy with violet conduits. Engraved with flowing script resembling funeral sutras.

### **Doctrinal Role**

* **Deployment:** Masters of ambush and attrition. Izanami strike from concealment, dissolving into mist before retaliation.
* **Psychological Warfare:** Their presence brings despair—foes describe them as fighting against phantoms, each volley of [[Fire]] accompanied by whispers of the dead.
* **Legacy:** Legio Izanami are remembered as the Veilwalkers—phantoms of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] whose coming heralds the silent unraveling of all things.
