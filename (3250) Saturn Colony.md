# (3250) Saturn Colony

The first permanent Saturnian foothold colony is recognized in 3250 as the First Solar War begins.

It centers on:

- Titan settlements supporting atmospheric chemistry and hydrocarbon industry;
- Rhea transfer, shielding, and logistics stations;
- Enceladus ice, ocean-science, and propellant installations;
- long-duration navigation and rescue platforms.

The foothold depends on fusion-drive convoys, local ice processing, deep emergency reserves, and infrastructure distributed across several moons.

War refugees and damaged ships arrive before the colony possesses mature housing, medicine, or repair capacity.

The Colonial Union must decide whether scarce convoy capacity supports the inner-system war or preserves a settlement whose return route is already uneconomical.

Saturn therefore begins not as a triumphal outer frontier but as a test of whether expansion remains a public obligation during civil conflict.

By 3425, Titan, Rhea, Enceladus, and their supporting stations make Saturn the second major outer power.

Titan's population, chemistry industry, agriculture, and civic institutions combine with Rhea's shipyards and Enceladus's ice and propellant infrastructure.

Saturn co-founds the Outer Systems Compact with Jupiter and demands independence from direct Colonial Union government.

Its moon governments remain divided over whether the Saturnian system government can speak for them in independence negotiations and war.
