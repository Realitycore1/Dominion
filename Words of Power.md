1. Root Principle
	•	Speech is not just vibration — it is resonance encoded in intent.
	•	Each spoken word is a micro-invocation of axiomcraft, carried through the medium of voice, soul, and will.
	•	Power depends on three factors:
	1.	Language (structure of resonance — runes, syllables, tones).
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Authority (who speaks — mortal, [[Angel]], heretic, Empyrearch).
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Anchor (the [[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] or axiom the word binds into).
