Spiralverse Arrays are superstructures built along the spiral filaments of the [[Verse]] Field Framework ([[VFF]]). They act as harmonic routers, shaping energy, information, and spacetime flows across intergalactic scales. The arrays are literally civilizational latticework strung across the bones of the cosmos — each filament turned into a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] instrument.
Structure

Filament Anchors: Matter Cores and Atlas Pillar–scale struts lock arrays into supercluster filaments.

Harmonic Nodes: [[Computronium]] colossi resonate with [[VFF]] harmonics, stabilizing local reality.

Concordia Choir Arrays: Emit resonance patterns that regulate energy and information flow.

Relay Crowns: Substructures that tie into Horizon Arc Gates, [[SGL constellations]], and Hyperciv Shells.

Functions

Cosmic Routing: Direct energy, signals, and fleets across intergalactic filaments.

Reality Stabilization: Maintain [[VFF]] coherence, preventing collapse or hostile interference.

Projection: Emit glyph-light across millions of light-years — scripture etched into filaments visible across void.

Weaponization: Can destabilize filaments into collapse cascades — cutting entire galaxies off from each other.

Scale

Stretch across tens to hundreds of millions of light-years, braided into the skeleton of the cosmos.

Operate in recursive harmony, like a choir of galaxies singing in unison.
