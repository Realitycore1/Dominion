## Arcane Method

A structured process for manipulating reality through the alignment of materials, energies, and conceptual states.

### Steps:
1. Identification
2. Correspondence Mapping
3. State Calibration
4. Structured Invocation
5. Observation
6. Iteration

### Core Principles:
- Meaning has causal power
- Alignment determines effectiveness
- Intent is a measurable variable
- Symbols can substitute for systems
- Misalignment produces deviation

### Note:
Repeatability requires not only identical physical inputs, but identical conceptual and intentional states.
