Here’s the Military backbone of the [[Austro Guard]], the steel edge of the Oath.

⸻

Military

The [[Austro Guard]]’s defense force, known collectively as the Eridani Watch, is both sword and shield. It is not an army standing apart from society, but the living extension of the Oath that every citizen swears.
	•	Universal Reservists
	•	Every oath-sworn adult is considered a reservist.
	•	Training begins in youth cohorts, ensuring citizens can handle [[Arms]], survival drills, and squad coordination.
	•	Reservists rotate through Burden Patrols—week-long militia duties patrolling mines, shipyards, or frontier settlements.
	•	Standing Forces
	•	Professional cadres form the colony’s permanent guard:
	•	The Line Guard – infantry and planetary defense troops.
	•	[[The Void]] Guard – naval crews and orbital defense personnel.
	•	The Stone Guard – fortress [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]] and heavy siege operators.
	•	Each cadre is commanded by Duty Marshals, appointed by the Council of Oaths for a single six-year term.
	•	The Eridani Watch Fleet
	•	The colony maintains a small but ferociously disciplined navy.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: fight to the last hull, prioritizing denial of territory over survival.
	•	Ships are utilitarian—slab-sided, heavily armored, with oversized reactor cores to maintain railgun [[Fire]] for days.
	•	Flagships often carry basalt fragments from the Pillars of Duty, symbolically binding them to the colony’s soil.
	•	Strategic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Shield First: Defense of Eridani [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] assets takes precedence over expeditions.
	•	Duty to the Union: In Union-[[Wide]] conflicts, [[Austro Guard]] commits full fleets but insists on maintaining a core defensive garrison at home.
	•	Burden [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: Better to lose ships and lives in honorable defense than retreat dishonorably and risk the colony.
	•	Honor and Discipline
	•	Military service accrues the highest honor in the ledgers, especially if tied to sacrifice.
	•	Cowardice or desertion is considered oath-breaking—punished by exile and erasure from family Walls.
	•	Veterans of the Eridani Watch are revered, their rings etched with deep notches for each campaign endured.
	•	Symbolism in [[Arms]]
	•	Standard sidearms are engraved with oath-phrases; to drop one in battle is an unthinkable shame.
	•	Warships fly banners stitched with the Line Banners of their crew families, turning every fleet into a web of kinship.

The Eridani Watch is less an army than a ritualized duty-force, every battle both practical defense and sacred enactment of the Oath. They are the [[Colonial Union]]’s bulwark in Eridani, feared not for their numbers but for their absolute refusal to yield.
