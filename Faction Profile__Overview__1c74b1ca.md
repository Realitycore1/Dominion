## Summary

GalFed is a post-catastrophe federation built by the surviving conscious powers of Odysseus after the Aether Singularity. It presents itself as the custodian of galactic recovery and the guarantor that no world will fall into silence again.

## Details

Type: Custodial interstellar federation with permanent founder primacy.

Primary territory: The Odysseus Galaxy, with dense control over coreward and mid-galactic transit corridors and looser authority along hazardous outer regions.

Founding crisis: The Aether Singularity, which collapsed conscious continuity across most of the galaxy.

Founding body: The Accord of Seven Harmonies, later formalized into the Galactic Federation.

Capital complex: Beacon World Prime and the Archive Gate System.

Core promise: "No World Stands Alone."

Core contradiction: No world stands alone because no world is permitted to become fully independent from the systems that keep it standing.

GalFed's legitimacy comes from real achievement. It ended famine on collapsing worlds, restored partial cognition to shattered populations, stabilized transit routes, prevented secondary Aether plagues, and preserved entire species whose home cultures had been reduced to ruins. Its threat comes from the same infrastructure. Food, medicine, credit, law, language, memory, and movement all pass through Federation standards.

Political posture:

- Friendly to compliant member species.
- Protective toward damaged or recovering civilizations.
- Suspicious of unsupervised Aether research.
- Hostile to autonomous military buildup.
- Intensely alarmed by unclassified FTL systems.
- Quietly imperial toward worlds deemed unstable.
