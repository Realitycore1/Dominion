When a [[Vampire]] has fed beyond mortality and broken its dependence on blood, it ascends. These are not parasites but predatory principles.

Traits of Ascendants
	•	No [[Hunger]]: They no longer need to feed, but often still do for pleasure or symbolism.
	•	Embodiment of Taint: Their presence spreads vampirism passively. Entire [[Regions]] bend to their [[(2030) Existence]].
	•	Cosmic Inversion: Mortals, [[Angels]], or even gods may “dream of them,” as reality frays around their essence.

Forms of Ascendants
	1.	The Red Sovereigns (Mortal Ascendants):
	•	Former lords and empresses who fed until their [[Hunger]] became authority itself.
	•	They don’t drain blood anymore — they drain obedience and loyalty.
	•	Cities bend to their will without coercion.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	The Black Seraphs (Angelic Ascendants):
	•	[[Angels]] who transcended feeding to embody faith inversion.
	•	They devour prayers and sanctuaries, making worship a form of nourishment.
	•	Their wings bleed shadow-light that enthralls whole congregations.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Eclipsed (Divine Ascendants):
	•	Gods who outgrew [[Hunger]] and now exist as entropy.
	•	They no longer feed — the cosmos feeds them simply by existing.
	•	Their [[Chapter XXI (Domain)|Chapter XXI (Domain)]] becomes inverted eternally: sun into eclipse, war into endless slaughter, sea into desiccation.
