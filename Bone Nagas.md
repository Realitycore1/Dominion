Classification

Undead Serpentine Sentients / Ritual Guardians / Skeletal Liches (Non-Ascendant)

⸻

CORE IDEA

Bone Nagas are memory given a spine.

They are not mindless undead and not failed liches. A Bone Naga is what happens when a guardian dies without being dismissed. The flesh rots away; the duty remains. What’s left is a thinking skeleton animated by oath, pattern, and ritual inertia.

They do not crave life.
They continue the job.

⸻

ORIGIN

Bone Nagas form through intentional or accidental ritual outcomes:
	•	sacred guardians bound to temples or tombs
	•	Nagas slain while under binding geas
	•	necromantic preservation of elite sentients
	•	curse-layered burial rites that stabilize bone but not soul

Unlike liches, Bone Nagas do not externalize their soul.
Their consciousness is diffused through sigils etched into bone.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Bone Nagas are immense skeletal serpents with vestigial humanoid features.

Common traits:
	•	Massive serpentine skeleton, often dozens of meters long
	•	Prominent rib structures forming natural armor cages
	•	Skull elongated, fanged, often crowned or rune-etched
	•	Residual spectral glow in eye sockets (blue, green, pale violet)
	•	Occasional remnants of armor, jewelry, or temple regalia fused to bone

Their movement is smooth and deliberate, bones articulating with unnatural precision.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Bone Nagas are coldly methodical and intensely territorial.
	•	Do not pursue beyond assigned boundaries
	•	Issue warnings through posture, sound, or magical pressure
	•	Engage intruders with calculated force
	•	Ignore beings recognized as “authorized”

They are not cruel.
They are procedural.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High.
	•	Retain tactical knowledge from life
	•	Capable of speech, often formal and archaic
	•	Understand ritual law and hierarchy
	•	Limited creativity outside assigned function

A Bone Naga can debate theology while killing you.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Oath-Driven Animation: purpose sustains structure
	•	Grace-Resistant: angelic presence weakens enchantments but not resolve
	•	Malice-Neutral: corruption increases aggression radius, not intelligence
	•	Bone Sigil Network: damage to runes disrupts coordination
	•	Soul Residue: faint echoes of the original self persist

If released from duty, many Bone Nagas simply… stop.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Found in tombs, temples, vaults, and deep ruins
	•	Do not feed or reproduce
	•	Surroundings remain unnaturally preserved
	•	Often coexist with lesser undead as subordinate guards

A Bone Naga’s lair is a frozen moment in history.

⸻

ALPHA BONE NAGAS

An Alpha Bone Naga is known as a High Ossuary Warden.

Traits:
	•	Oversees multiple tomb complexes
	•	Can reassign lesser undead tactically
	•	Maintains centuries-long continuity of defense
	•	May interpret ancient law more strictly over time

High Wardens become inflexible catastrophes when contexts change.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (localized) / Severe (Alpha complex)

You don’t exhaust a Bone Naga.
You invalidate its authority—or die.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Bone Nagas represent:
	•	duty beyond death
	•	ritual inertia
	•	guardianship without mercy
	•	the danger of eternal orders

Where Barrow-Wights rule graves,
Bone Nagas guard the rules of the dead.
