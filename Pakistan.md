Pakistan is a country located in South Asia, with a diverse geography and rich cultural heritage. The country is bordered by [[Afghanistan]] to the west, [[Iran]] to the east, [[India]] to the south, and China to the north. The country has significant mineral resources, including gas, coal, and iron ore, which are used in various industries. The country's geography is characterized by mountain ranges, deserts, and fertile plains. Pakistan has a long coastline along the Arabian Sea, with the city of Karachi being its largest port. Pakistan's [[culture]] is heavily influenced by its [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] and traditions, with the predominant religion being Islam. The country has a diverse range of ethnic groups and languages, including Punjabi, Pashto, and Sindhi. The country's cuisine is also diverse, featuring a range of dishes from across the country's [[Regions]]. Popular dishes include biryani, kebabs, naan, and curries. Pakistan has faced challenges related to political instability, poverty, and terrorism in recent years. The country has experienced several crises in recent years, including [[Natural]] disasters such as floods and earthquakes. Despite these challenges, Pakistan remains a culturally rich and historically significant country with significant potential for growth and development. The country's [[Natural]] resources and diverse population make it an important player on the global stage.
