(The Law Beneath Meaning)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Meta-Structural Plane
	•	Authority Level: Pre-expressive, Post-foundational
	•	Relation to Core Authority: Subordinate, non-jurisdictional
	•	Accessibility: Extremely limited; never entered whole

⸻

Core Nature

The Axiomatic Stratum is not a realm of beings.

It is a plane of constraints.

Where:
	•	Aravoth governs what should be
	•	Sheol-Gehenna governs what can be broken
	•	The Empty governs what must be released

The Axiomatic Stratum governs what is allowed to exist at all.

It is the layer where:
	•	logic crystallizes,
	•	causality acquires direction,
	•	identity gains persistence,
	•	contradiction becomes impossible or lethal.

This is not morality.
This is permission.

⸻

What the Axioms Are (important distinction)

Axioms are not laws enforced.

They are assumptions reality cannot violate without collapsing.

Examples:
	•	Cause precedes effect (unless explicitly broken)
	•	Identity persists through time (unless degraded)
	•	Something cannot fully exist and not exist simultaneously
	•	Power must propagate through a medium

Aravoth interprets these.
Sheol-Gehenna exploits their edge cases.
The Empty ignores them entirely.

⸻

Structure of the Plane

The Axiomatic Stratum has no geography in the traditional sense.

It manifests as:
	•	lattices of glowing equations,
	•	infinite glyph-strings folding into themselves,
	•	recursive geometries that define space rather than occupy it,
	•	frozen causal chains visible as crystalline threads.

Observers describe it as:

“Standing inside the rule that allows standing.”

Most who glimpse it do not return sane.

⸻

Inhabitants (or lack thereof)

Strictly speaking, nothing lives here.

However, three classes of phenomena may be observed:

1. Axiom Sentinels

Not beings, but stabilization nodes.
	•	Appear as faceless geometric forms
	•	React instantly to paradox
	•	Erase violations rather than punish them

They do not negotiate.
They do not pursue.
They correct.

⸻

2. Fracture Echoes

Remnants of realities that violated core axioms.
	•	Broken timelines
	•	Impossible creatures
	•	Self-contradictory entities

They exist only as frozen errors, unable to propagate.

Many eldritch horrors originate as escaped Fracture Echoes.

⸻

3. Observer Artifacts

Not native, but left behind by:
	•	Aeonic Angels
	•	Dominion-level intelligences
	•	Failed gods
	•	Ancient civilizations that tried to “edit” reality

Most are catastrophic if activated.
