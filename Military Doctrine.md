## Strategic Philosophy
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] warfare is the harmonization of overwhelming force. Fleets, [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], and civic sacrifices are all tuned to act as one choir. War is not chaos, it is resonance — every unit a note in the greater symphony.

## Fleet Composition
- Escorts ([[Corvettes]], [[Frigates]]) act as shields and scouts.  
- Line ships ([[Destroyers]], [[Cruisers]]) form the backbone.  
- Capitals ([[Battlecruisers]], [[Carriers]], [[Dreadnoughts]]) project overwhelming presence.  
- Apex ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans]], sanctum flagships) anchor fleets as spiritual and martial cores.

## Battle Formations
- **Chord Line**: fleets advance in layered diamond ranks, resonating with each other.  
- **Harmonic Envelopment**: coordinated pincer, fleets singing on different frequencies.  
- **Crescendo Assault**: escalating firepower in rhythmic waves.
