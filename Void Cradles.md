A Void Cradle is a constructed null zone of spacetime, an engineered region where entropy, motion, and external noise are suppressed into near-perfect stillness. They function as cosmic incubators — vaults where things can rest unchanged for eons, shielded from [[Time]], causality, or decay.
Structure

Cradle Walls: Lattices of strange-matter and [[Verse]] Anchors defining a closed null volume.

Entropic Membrane: Concordia harmonics suppress thermal noise, quantum fluctuation, and causal flow.

Anchor Spines: Atlas-class struts connect the cradle to surrounding filaments or [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Silent Choir Nodes: [[Computronium]] strata run at near-zero entropy, whispering only when called.

Functions

– Black reserves of fleets, hidden until summoned.
– Causal denial zones — weapons that erase local causality within enemy lines.

Variants

Preservation Cradles: Seed vaults, [[Memory Glaciers]], relic reliquaries.

Sacral Cradles: Cosmic reliquaries, forbidden vaults where even [[Angels]] fear to look.
