Classification: [[Chimera]] Class – Tier X / Field Designate “Aethorion” (The Centaur Warden)

Observation:
Entity exhibits a fusion of humanoid and quadrupedal morphology—torso upright, fused seamlessly to a lower body of equine configuration. Exoskeleton glossy, carbon-black, ribbing defined by symmetrical striations. Cranial dome polished and elongated, devoid of facial features save for recessed mandibular slit. Appendages extend from the dorsal plane in tendril-like ridges capable of limited motion. Subject captured in rearing stance under low lunar illumination, environmental scans confirming dense forestry with multiple subspecies present in the background.

Contextual Analysis:
The Aethorion is hypothesized to have emerged during the Third Terraforming Epoch when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] resonance harmonics began influencing the genetic field across northern biomes. Detected only in forests bordering mycelial zones, these hybrids demonstrate coordinated pack behavior and advanced territorial organization—suggesting transbiological intelligence. Preliminary vivisections revealed dual circulatory [[Systems]]: one organic, one plasmatic, hinting at integration between biological and residual [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] field [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Military/Technology|Technology]]. Specimens exhibit brief but measurable bursts of psionic coherence during lunar alignment.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
Archived under the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Living Hierarchies. The Aethorion embodies the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] ideal of [[Ascension]] through [[Synthesis]]: [[Humanity]] and beast conjoined not by domination but by resonance. Scholars interpret it as an omen of evolutionary obedience—life aligning itself to the Empire’s geometry. Within the Ecclesiarch’s exotheological texts, its equine foundation represents strength in service, while the humanoid upper form symbolizes consciousness elevated by structure. In [[Ritual]] lexicons, it is referred to as the “Custodian of Equilibrium,” guardian of the forests that remember the Empire’s first breath.
