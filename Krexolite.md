# The Krexolite Civilizational Codex

This file compiles the full evolutionary, cultural, governmental, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]], linguistic, and religious [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] of the Krexolite [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Species/Species|Species]] from their Stone Age origins through the Unveiling Severance and into the fractured post-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] era.

---

## 1. Government Systems by Era

### Stone Age — Nest Bands
- Led by a dominant matriarch with physical and pheromonal control.
- No formal law; disputes resolved through ritual combat and exile.

### Early Clan Era — Warrens and Scent Councils
- Clusters of nests form burrowed villages.
- Councils of elder females manage territory and alliances.

### Bronze Age — Matriarch Dynasties
- City-states ruled by dynast matriarchs legitimized by ritual consumption.
- Established castes: hunters, resin-workers, flesh-keepers.

### Iron Age — Scent Leagues
- Military alliances between dynasties.
- Emergence of formal law carved in bone and lacquered with scent tags.

### Pre-Industrial — Unified Scent Dominion
- Absolute monarchy under the first Grand Matriarch.
- Ministries: Resin, Carrion, Talons, Echoes.

### Early Space Age — Technocratic Imperium
- Deep-state technocrats control orbital infrastructure.
- Monarchy persists but with significantly reduced autonomy.

### Dominion Contact — Puppet Regimes & Collapse
- Splinter factions: appeasers vs. purists.
- Dominion briefly uses them as scouts then dismantles their government.

### Post-Severance — Echo Warrens
- Civilization collapses into fragmented, subterranean warrens.
- Echo Queens rule with inherited memory-meat lineages.

## 2. Religion & Metaphysics by Era

### Stone Age — Carrion Animism
- Spirits reside in anything that rots.
- Ritual consumption releases agony and knowledge.

### Early Clan Era — Scent-Echo Cult
- Identity believed to be a scent pattern carried in flesh.
- Burial becomes taboo.

### Bronze/Iron Age — Structured Death Theology
- Pantheon represents entropy, stars, and memory flow.
- Temples are ossuaries for ritual butchery.

### Pre-Industrial — First Beak State Cult
- Grand Matriarch deified as steward of the Scent-River.
- Heresies emerge: Dry-Bone Sects, Rot Ascetics.

### Early Space — Cosmic Carrion Theology
- Stars interpreted as divine bone-fires.
- Alien species seen as stray carrion without lineage.

### Dominion Contact — Apocalyptic Integration
- Dominion framed as cosmic predator or false carrion-lord.
- Collaborationist religious sects arise.

### Post-Severance — Fractured Faiths
- Severance Cults, Necro-Dominion worship, Scent-Continuists.

---

## 3. Language & Writing Evolution

### Proto-Language
- Clicks, croaks, hisses, and antenna gestures.

### Early Writing — Scratch-Glyphs
- Claw marks on bone/clay with pheromone tags.

### Bronze/Iron — Quill-Script
- Angular glyphs plus scented lacquer.

### Pre-Industrial — Dual Script
- Formal glyphic script and everyday phonetic script.

### Early Space — Compressed Code
- Encoded radio-clicks and scent-relay messages.

### Post-Severance — Fragmentation
- Some warrens abandon writing; others obsessively preserve it.

---

## 4. Military Evolution

### Stone Age
- Ambush raiders with stone talon-blades.

### Early Clan Era
- Coordinated night raids with silent, antenna-based command.

### Bronze Age
- War-Flocks with bronze armor and tunnel-siege tactics.

### Iron Age
- Professional Tunnel Legions; battles in total blackout.

### Pre-Industrial
- Gunpowder spine-guns and infrasonic resonant cannons.

### Early Space
- Zero-g boarding frigates and bioengineered void beasts.

### Dominion Contact
- Fleets destroyed; pivot to guerrilla tactics.

### Post-Severance
- Scavenger militias with salvaged Dominion gear.

---

## 5. First Contact Narrative (Excerpt)

The Krexolites observe a disturbance in the “Bone Sky.” A Dominion vessel appears with no detectable approach. A smaller vessel docks at their orbital station and communicates telepathically:

> “You harvest the dead. So do we.”

Half the crew disappears without trace. The Dominion symbol burns itself into the station wall. Later revelations show the Dominion harvesting their dead for husks.

---

## 6. Post-Severance Evolution

- Rapid adaptation to broken physics environments.
- Antennas mutate to detect axiom fractures.
- Descent into scavenger–apex populations.
- Emergence of feral aristocracies ruled by Echo Queens.
