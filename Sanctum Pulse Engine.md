Function: The Regalia’s primary power core, a collapsed fragment of a star housed in a stasis-prism, beating like a second heart.
Supplies energy for wing projection, mass reformation, atmospheric cleansing, and matter unbinding.
Surges divine plasma through the joints during combat, increasing reaction [[Time]] to near pre-cognitive levels.
Failsafe: If the Empress falls, the engine will overload in a controlled radiant burst to deny corruption or capture.
