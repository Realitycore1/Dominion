II. Inner Peripheries (Cultural Heavens)

These are the first refractions, where mortal religions and mythologies attach their visions of paradise.

Elysium (Greek) – a field of eternal peace and music, aligned with the Gardens of Renewal.

Valhalla (Norse) – eternal hall of warriors, mirroring the Plains of Radiance.

The Pure Land (Buddhist) – serene realm of enlightenment, resonant with the River of Living Waters.

Gan Eden (Jewish mysticism) – orchard of light, a reflection of the Gardens and Zion.

Tlalocan (Aztec) – paradise of water and abundance, tied to the River of Living Waters.

Summerland (Celtic/Neopagan) – a twilight meadow, echo of the Gardens of Renewal.

Each of these connects to a “core truth” but seen through mortal cultural lenses.

III. Outer Peripheries ([[Verse]]-Specific Heavens)

These are [[Verse]]-level heavens, constructed by their own [[Axioms]] but tethered to Aravoth.

The Crystal Spheres – sci-fantasy heavens, radiant [[New Readers Start Here/I - Dominion Omniverse/Megaverse Types/Forest/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Totum Verse/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Ho'oleilana/Boötes Supercluster/Odysseus Galaxy/Dominion/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures/Megastructures|Megastructures]] where souls live in harmony with stellar light.

Dream Paradises – in dream-rooted verses, heavens take form as lucid dreamscapes where thought manifests instantly.

The Eternal Republics – political utopias; souls live in perfected societies modeled after mortal ideals.

The Star Courts – judicial heavens, eternal cosmic halls of [[law]], paralleling the Hall of Judgement.

Solar Thrones – heavens where suns are seats of divinity, radiant monarchs presiding in eternal flame.
