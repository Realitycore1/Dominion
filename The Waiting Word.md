The harbor was already shaking before dawn.

Ranham’s orbital elevators fed thousands of [[Soldiers]] up into the black, their lines coiling like steel rivers across the plazas. The banners of the Federal Branch snapped above them, gray and gold, faceless helmets turning as one whenever a command was barked. The city hummed with inevitability.

Jalen stood at the edges, a civilian pressed between pilgrims and merchants, craning his neck for a glimpse of the true gods of the march: the [[Angels]] of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

They came late, as always, because they did not need to hurry. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]] were already assembled, the dreadnaught already primed, the crusade already decided. And then the air split like parchment — not from thunder, but from song.

The Legion of Ares descended, armor white-hot with aureate light. Their spears burned in unison, each identical, yet each terrible in its certainty. They marched without sound, the world’s noise bending around their advance. Behind them trailed banners not of cloth, but of radiant script that wrote itself across the sky — the Empress’s will in letters of [[Fire]].

Jalen’s breath caught. He had seen them before, from a distance, but never this [[Close]]. [[Angels]], not in the soft tongues of the Orthodoxy, but in their true form: flawless, merciless, impossible. To look on them was to know how small he was, how inevitable their war was, how fragile his own bones were. And yet—

He wanted nothing more.

Children whispered that sometimes — rarely — a Legionnaire’s gaze would fall on a mortal, and a word would be spoken. Not in speech, but as a seal impressed on the soul. That mortal would be taken into the ranks, remade in steel and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]], carried into [[The Void]] to serve where no Federal line could reach. Most never even glimpsed such a miracle.

But Jalen’s heart hammered as the [[Angels]] passed, and he thought — just maybe — one helm turned.

The crowd surged. The dreadnaught loomed, a mountain of black metal studded with light. One by one, the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]] crossed its threshold, vanishing into its endless decks. The banners burned brighter, the air filled with the smell of ozone, and the civilians fell to their knees in prayer.

Jalen remained standing, trembling, straining against the tide of kneeling bodies. His eyes locked on the [[Angels]]’ faceless visors, searching for anything, anything that might resemble recognition.

The last ranks marched past. The dreadnaught’s gates began to [[Close]].

For a heartbeat, a figure broke formation. An [[Angel]] — taller, more radiant, spear resting across its shoulder. It stopped. Its head tilted, ever so slightly, as though regarding the sea of kneeling mortals. Its voice — if it was a voice — pressed directly into Jalen’s bones:

“Stand.”

And then the gates sealed.

The dreadnaught roared, rising into the heavens, carrying its immortal army into the [[Abyss]].

Jalen fell to his knees at last, not from reverence, but because his body could no longer bear the weight of what had just been carved into his soul.

The word had been spoken.
