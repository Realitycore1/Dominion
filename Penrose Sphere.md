## Summary

Habitation:
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
![[Pasted image 20250830160115.png]]
A Penrose Sphere is a Dyson-like enclosure around a Kerr (rotating) black hole, built to harvest energy from the Penrose process — extracting rotational energy from the black hole’s ergosphere. Instead of solar collectors, the Sphere manages plasma, accretion flows, and relativistic [[Particles]], transforming them into usable power.
Structure

Collector Swarm: Panels, magnetic funnels, and habitats orbiting in complex relativistic geometries.

Accretion Harness: [[Artificial]] disks engineered to channel matter streams into controlled plasma flows.

Mirror Arrays: Reflectors and beamers capture energy and redirect it across [[Systems]].

Stability Nodes: Gravitic anchors and [[Computronium]] control webs counter tidal stresses.

Functions

Energy Harvest: Taps the black hole’s spin — potentially greater than the output of many [[Stars]] combined.

Computation: Outer shells serve as [[Computronium]] strata, cooled by deep-space.

Weaponization: Beams redirected as stellar-scale lances; destabilized flows can trigger quasar-like outbursts.

Variants

Civil Spheres: Powerhouses for Dyson Fortresses, [[Ark Platforms]], or sector grids.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Spheres: Dark fortresses weaponizing relativistic plasma.

Sacred Spheres: Religious sanctums, treating the caged black hole as the Empress’s hidden heart.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: A Penrose Sphere can power entire clusters — black suns turned into lanterns.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: A single misaligned release can sterilize star [[Systems]]. Economic: Anchor entire sectors’ power and trade grids.

Religious: Known as “The Black Crowns” — myths say the Empress cages not only [[Stars]], but night itself.

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Collector towers ribbed like cathedral spines, glowing against [[The Void]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the Sphere appears as a black star haloed by light, a perfect eclipse.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyph-light inscribed into accretion jets, making the black hole itself a pulsing hymn.
