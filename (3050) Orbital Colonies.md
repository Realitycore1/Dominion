# (3050) Orbital Colonies

The 3050 marker records the beginning of sustained O'Neill-cylinder expansion through the inner-system transport network.

The colonies are placed in:

- Earth-Luna L4 and L5 regions;
- Sun-Earth and Sun-Mars halo-orbit complexes;
- Venusian and Mercurian transfer regions;
- high-traffic staging orbits;
- later Belt gateway routes.

The cylinders provide rotational gravity, agriculture, schools, clinics, manufacturing, shipyards, and permanent family housing.

Their radiation shielding uses water, slag, and low-value regolith imported from Luna and the Belt. Spin sections, stationary docks, mirrors, radiators, and shielding layers remain separate systems whose failures create different evacuation problems.

The colonies turn Lagrange regions and managed halo orbits into inhabited jurisdictions. Control of docking, shielding mass, spin access, and rescue craft becomes political before many stations possess full representation.

By the First Solar War, the cylinders are ordinary cities and strategic infrastructure.
