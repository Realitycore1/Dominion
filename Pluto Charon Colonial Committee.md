# Pluto–Charon Colonial Committee

The Pluto–Charon Colonial Committee coordinates the binary system that becomes Sol’s final harbor. Its mature canonical role belongs to 3500–3600.

The Committee balances two worlds and the inhabited infrastructure between them. Pluto holds buried archives, cold laboratories, and vault communities. Charon holds shipyards, foundries, military approaches, and outward docks. Barycentric guideways connect universities, farms, trade districts, fleet quarters, diplomatic houses, and permanent families.

Its government must reconcile Colonial Union law, Outer Systems Compact agreements, Kuiper clan precedent, Watchtower operations, and the requirements of an interstellar mission whose passengers carry multiple citizenships.

Pluto preserves the records and reference sets for the next vessel. Charon builds the *C.U.C. Deliverance*. Acheron becomes the staging civilization that joins every Solar committee’s material and institutional inheritance.

The *Deliverance* launches in 3600. Craterheart is founded after its failed landing in 3608 and is not governed by this Committee. For the full transition, see [[Pluto-Charon Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
