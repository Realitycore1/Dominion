1. Common Hippocampus
	•	Definition: The baseline—front half of a horse, hind half of a fish.
	•	Traits: Aquatic speed, graceful swimmers, intelligent eyes.
	•	Symbolism: The union of land and sea; nobility beneath the waves.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. War Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Bred or trained for aquatic battle, often by sea empires or gods.
	•	Traits: Armored scales, ramming charges, mounts for aquatic knights.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] of the sea made militant.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Coral Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Hippocampi with coral-encrusted scales and reef-colored hides.
	•	Traits: Symbiotic with reefs, vibrant, guardians of underwater groves.
	•	Symbolism: Ocean as sanctuary and splendor.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Abyssal Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Deep-sea variants adapted to crushing [[Depths]].
	•	Traits: Bioluminescent hides, lantern-like eyes, eerie songs.
	•	Symbolism: Mystery of the [[Abyss]]; beauty laced with terror.

⸻

5. Storm Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Born of sea tempests and tied to Poseidon/sea-god [[Archetypes]].
	•	Traits: White or grey hides, foaming manes, lightning-like speed in waves.
	•	Symbolism: The sea’s fury; chaos embodied in steeds.

⸻

6. Frost Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Cold-sea hippocampi adapted to ice floes and glaciers.
	•	Traits: Pale scales, frost-breath, endurance in freezing waters.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance and survival in merciless seas.

⸻

7. Shadow Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Sea-horses warped by abyssal or necrotic powers.
	•	Traits: Dark fins, skeletal tails, necrotic aura around their wake.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] beneath the waves; the drowned steed.

⸻

8. Noble Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Regal breeds used by merfolk royalty or sea gods.
	•	Traits: Larger frames, shimmering scales, high intelligence.
	•	Symbolism: Authority of the oceans; living symbols of rule.

⸻

9. Winged Hippocampi
	•	Definition: Mythic crossbreeds between hippocampi and [[Pegasus]] [[Archetypes]].
	•	Traits: Fish-tails with wings; capable of skimming between sea and sky.
	•	Symbolism: Freedom across both [[Realms]]; unchained steed of creation.

⸻

10. Leviathan Hippocampi (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Colossal hippocampi, worshiped as sea gods or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans|Titans]].
	•	Traits: Whale-sized, control tides, voices like crashing surf.
	•	Symbolism: The sea itself given form; apocalypse in equine shape.
