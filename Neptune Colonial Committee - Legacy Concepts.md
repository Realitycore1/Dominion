I. The Committee Years (2500–2650)

The Neptune Colonial Committee (NCC) was formed as the [[Colonial Union]]’s most ambitious charter, tasked with managing settlements across Neptune, [[Triton]], and its scattered moons.
	•	[[Triton]]: became the NCC’s administrative hub — icy crust, geysers, and thin atmosphere gave it promise as both research base and mining colony.
	•	Nereid & Proteus: small outposts, often penal or exile communities.
	•	Orbital Stations: helium-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] harvesting rigs skimming Neptune’s upper atmosphere.

The NCC was underfunded, undermanned, and essentially abandoned by the [[Inner System]]. Colonists quickly learned they were on their own, treated as resource-exporters rather than citizens.

⸻

II. The Age of the Frontier (2580–2650)
	•	[[Triton]] [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]: grew into underground cities warmed by nuclear reactors, their people proud of surviving “beneath the frost.”
	•	Nereid Drifters: nomadic communities aboard slow, eccentric orbits; scavengers and traders who carried news between moons.
	•	Neptune Skimmers: high-risk crews running helium harvesters across the [[Giant]]’s blue skies, often lost to storms.

Culturally, Neptunians began to see themselves as Outriders — explorers at the edge of the [[Human]] map, where only [[Silence]] and [[Stars]] surrounded them.

⸻

III. The [[Triton]] Rising (2650–2690)

The turning point came when the NCC ordered mass conscription of [[Triton]] colonists to crew helium-skimmer fleets, citing “defense of the [[Outer System]].”

Colonists resisted, staging sit-ins and sabotaging skimmer launches. When the NCC attempted to enforce its order with PMC troops, the colonists fought back with mining lasers and ice-buried charges. The [[Skirmishes]] escalated into the [[Triton]] Rising:
	•	NCC authority collapsed after its orbital HQ was seized.
	•	Colonists declared [[Triton]] independent, expelling NCC staff.
	•	Other moons followed suit, with Nereid Drifters intercepting supply convoys to starve out loyalists.

⸻

IV. The Deepclan [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] (2690–2740)

	•	Reverence for mystery — Neptune’s storms, [[Triton]]’s geysers, and the Kuiper beyond.

	•	Politics: loosely allied Clans of the Deep, centered on [[Triton]] but with Nereid Drifters serving as connective tissue.

V. Toward the Neptunian Freeholds (2740+)

By the 28th century, the Neptunian [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] was no longer a committee colony but a frontier civilization:
	•	[[Triton]] Freeholds: strongholds of industry and survival.
	•	Nereid Drifters: messengers and traders, keeping the Deepclans linked.
	•	Neptune Skimmers: independent guilds, still risking storms for energy but now for their own profit.

Together, these became the Neptunian Freeholds, a confederation of the most distant, most stubborn colonists [[Humanity]] had yet produced.
