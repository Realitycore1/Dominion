(The Time-Bearing Medium / The Flow Beneath Events)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Structural–Temporal Medium
	•	Authority Level: Regulative, non-sovereign
	•	Relation to Axiomatic Stratum: Constrained by axioms
	•	Relation to Plane of Omens: Downstream (after significance forms)
	•	Relation to the Empty: Post-identity only
	•	Relation to Aravoth / Sheol-Gehenna: Utilized, not ruled

⸻

Core Nature

The Chronal Substrate is what time is made of.

Not time as measurement.
Not time as destiny.
But time as continuity-bearing substance.

If:
	•	the Axiomatic Stratum defines what may exist,
	•	Fundamentum Elementalis defines what existence is made of,
	•	the Plane of Omens defines what is becoming significant,

then the Chronal Substrate answers:

“How does one moment become the next?”

⸻

What Time Is (in your Omniverse)

Time is not a dimension you move through.

Time is a medium:
	•	events displace it,
	•	causality flows through it,
	•	identities carve grooves into it.

Every action leaves a wake.

⸻

Structure of the Plane

The Chronal Substrate appears as an immense layered flow, often described as:
	•	braided rivers of light and shadow,
	•	vast sheets of translucent crystal drifting past one another,
	•	pressure currents that drag memories forward,
	•	knots where timelines intersect or grind together.

There is no “past” or “future” here—only density gradients:
	•	dense regions = heavily recorded moments
	•	thin regions = unstable, mutable sequences

Time does not loop naturally.
Loops require damage.

⸻

Chronal States

1. Laminar Flow

Stable chronology.
	•	Cause precedes effect
	•	Memory aligns with sequence
	•	History remains coherent

Most realities exist here.

⸻

2. Turbulent Flow

High-stress chronology.
	•	Wars
	•	Cataclysms
	•	Massive death events
	•	Planar interference

Time frays, events accelerate, coincidences multiply.

⸻

3. Eddies

Localized distortions.
	•	Déjà vu
	•	Temporal echoes
	•	Repeating events
	•	“Time ghosts”

Often mistaken for hauntings or curses.

⸻

4. Knots

Critical failures.
	•	Two or more incompatible sequences collide
	•	Timeline coherence collapses
	•	Verse Severance risk increases sharply

Most multiversal disasters begin here.

⸻

Inhabitants (Rare and Dangerous)

1. Chronal Residua

Not beings, but temporal debris.
	•	Afterimages of erased events
	•	People who “almost existed”
	•	Battles that never happened but still scar reality

They are hazardous to memory and identity.

⸻

2. Temporal Predators

Extremely rare.
	•	Feed on unresolved sequences
	•	Appear near knots or eddies
	•	Erase causal consistency around them

Often misidentified as eldritch horrors.

⸻

3. Aeonic Interfaces

Not rulers—anchors.
	•	Used by Aeonic Angels
	•	Maintain laminar flow
	•	Sacrifice identity to stabilize time

They do not command time.
They hold it together.

⸻

What the Chronal Substrate Is NOT
	•	It does not decide fate
	•	It does not allow free time travel
	•	It does not permit rewriting history without cost
	•	It does not supersede the Empty

This prevents:
	•	paradox abuse,
	•	prophecy collapse,
	•	timeline power inflation.

⸻

Interaction with Other Planes

Plane of Omens
	•	Omens form before chronal commitment
	•	Once an event enters the Substrate, it must resolve
	•	This is where inevitability begins—not destiny

⸻

Oneiric Plane
	•	Dreams skim thin chronal regions
	•	Some dreams remember futures that never occur
	•	Prophetic dreams are partial knot perceptions

⸻

Echoic Plane
	•	Strong deaths thicken time locally
	•	Mass death creates chronal turbulence
	•	Ghost outbreaks often follow temporal stress

⸻

Aravoth
	•	Angels read time, not control it
	•	Divine foresight is probabilistic
	•	Excess manipulation is forbidden

⸻

Sheol-Gehenna
	•	Exploits turbulence
	•	Manufactures endless war to keep time unstable
	•	The Infernal Depths form where chronal collapse persists

⸻

The Empty

Critical distinction:

The Empty is not in time.

When a soul enters the Empty:
	•	its timeline terminates cleanly
	•	no echo persists
	•	no paradox is generated

This is why resurrection from the Empty triggers Eidolon intervention.

⸻

Mortal Interaction

Mortals encounter the Chronal Substrate via:
	•	time anomalies,
	•	impossible survivals,
	•	prophetic déjà vu,
	•	near-miss catastrophes,
	•	cursed locations where “time feels wrong.”

Extended exposure causes:
	•	memory fragmentation,
	•	age desynchronization,
	•	identity erosion,
	•	becoming chronally displaced (alive, but unmoored).
