# Amber Book II - Canon Reconciliation Before Scene Drafting

## Audit Result

The attached *Amber Book II — New World War / GalFed War Bible* is compatible with the Pleroma Archive at the level of theme, First Strike logic, GalFed soft subjugation, Aether fear, and Amber's long arc.

It originally conflicted at the level of book scope, faction roster, government timing, and several Legion-to-Verarch assignments.

Those structural decisions were confirmed on 2026-07-01. The active results are [[Amber Book II - The Final Line - Definitive Skeleton]] and [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience - Development Spine]].

## Corrections Already Applied

### Harminan Roster

The active sovereign roster remains:

1. Equilon States;
2. Atlovaia Exarchate;
3. FreeStack Socialist Republics;
4. Coalition of Orbital States;
5. Commonwealth of States;
6. Ormova Replika;
7. Estelia Scriptorium;
8. Toldax Ascent.

Low Haven is an Equilon city with a frontier-autonomy culture. It is not a ninth sovereign faction.

The attached war bible omitted the Coalition of Orbital States and called Toldax the Toldax Alliance. The design document and workbook now use the active Pleroma roster.

### First Strike

The active sequence is:

1. Atlovaia attacks the New Aurora Constitutional Assembly.
2. Its operational objective is to destroy the constitutional process and kill Amber before she becomes the hinge of planetary unity.
3. Amber survives by stepping from the speaker's dais toward Emilia.
4. Most of the assembled federal government dies.
5. FreeStack independently launches its follow-on strike seconds later against New Aurora and suspected Equilon proto-legionary infrastructure.
6. Atlovaia and FreeStack do not share a stable alliance or command chain.

The Pleroma faction profiles and Year 252 First Strike record now use this sequence.

### Project Ra / Spears of Ra

The newer premise supersedes the old Spears of Ra origin paragraph.

Project Ra begins before formal GalFed contact as:

- public anti-asteroid and orbital-safety infrastructure;
- classified anti-satellite and orbital-denial capability;
- hidden preparation for the GalFed coercion predicted by the Discovery Clock.

GalFed may later accelerate or expand the network, but it does not create the original system.

The Pleroma technology record now reflects this.

### Vel

Exemplar Vel is not born yet and should not appear in Amber Book II.

## Compatible Canon That Needs Expansion, Not Correction

### GalFed

The Pleroma version is stronger than describing GalFed as merely parasitic.

GalFed is a real post-catastrophe custodial federation. It ended famines, restored damaged minds, stabilized routes, and preserved species after the Aether Singularity. Its corruption is that emergency stewardship became permanent founder primacy and infrastructure dependency.

The dramatic principle should remain:

> GalFed's help is real. Its claim to permanent authority is the threat.

This makes Standardization more dangerous because accepting it would solve actual Harminan suffering.

### Aether and the Family

The existing vault describes the Aether Singularity as a galaxy-scale noetic collapse.

The war bible's Family can fit as concealed or incomplete truth:

- GalFed public history: runaway Aether-field collapse;
- restricted founder knowledge: a cognition-accreting structure or entity emerged;
- Harminan discovery: records suggest the event behaved as if consciousness had acquired gravity.

No public Pleroma page needs to identify the Family as settled fact before the manuscript earns that reveal.

### Early and Mature Verarch Scale

The war bible's ten-to-fifteen-foot early Verarchs can coexist with later twenty-two-foot archive depictions if later bodies, armor, mythic reconstruction, or further augmentation increase their scale.

## Decision 1 - One Novel or Two

### Current Skeleton

The current 36-chapter skeleton contains:

- New World War;
- war inside bodies;
- Project Ra disclosure;
- Harminan unification;
- Covenant of Syl'an;
- Amber's ascension;
- diplomatic GalFed first contact.

It is already structured for a 110,000–140,000-word novel.

### Attached War Bible

The attached document adds:

- GalFed Standardization;
- the Final Line;
- Amber's unilateral first strike;
- GalFed atomic retaliation;
- Kapik and AI defections;
- Operation Prescience;
- Aether discovery;
- Outer Artemis Relay;
- first six Primus Legions;
- Dominicus Process;
- first Verarchs;
- beginning of the Great Crusade;
- Amber's first use of *Dominion*.

That is a second complete narrative escalation.

### Recommended Resolution

Split the material:

**Amber Book II — The Final Line**

- New World War;
- Spears of Ra;
- ascension and Syl'Cothan unification;
- GalFed gifts and Standardization;
- Amber refuses control of Harminan education;
- the Final Line forms;
- end on the evidence that GalFed is preparing atomic compliance.

**Amber Book III — Operation Prescience**

- Amber's preemptive orbital strike;
- GalFed atomic retaliation;
- Kapik and AI defections;
- Operation Prescience;
- Aether and the Family;
- Dominicus Process;
- first Verarchs and Primus Legions;
- Outer Artemis Relay;
- Great Crusade begins;
- Amber speaks *Dominion*.

This preserves every major war-bible event while allowing GalFed's soft subjugation to remain soft long enough to matter.

If one volume is preferred, the existing 110,000–140,000-word target should be replaced by approximately 180,000–220,000 words and the 36-chapter skeleton must be rebuilt around the seven-part structure.

## Decision 2 - Covenant or Emergency Coronation

### Current Skeleton

The Covenant creates shared government, then the states request Amber's ascension as a continuity solution. This makes her authority derive from an imperfect inter-state settlement.

### War Bible

The death of government forces an emergency regency and wartime coronation. The Celestialist First Witness crowns Amber Sovereign of Mankind while war continues.

### Recommended Resolution

Use both, but separate emergency authority from permanent authority:

1. The Assembly Strike leaves Amber as emergency constitutional signatory or regent.
2. The bare wartime ceremony names her Sovereign of Mankind but grants only emergency continuity command.
3. The Covenant of Syl'an later ratifies, limits, or transforms that emergency office into the Sovereign of the Syl'Cothan States.
4. The mature Royal/Federal dual government develops from the argument between those two moments rather than appearing complete immediately.

This preserves the war bible's powerful coronation without making a religious officer capable of creating uncontested permanent sovereignty alone.

## Decision 3 - First Six Verarch Assignments

Three war-bible pairings conflict with current Pleroma records:

| War-bible pairing | Existing Pleroma record |
| --- | --- |
| Xolotl Quenari — Legio Quetzalcoatl | Xolotl is named as founding Verarch of Legio Bellator |
| Pyraxis Dominor — Legio Ares | Pyraxis's record identifies him with Ignis |
| Aurelion Custivar — Legio Aurelis | Aurelion has an extensive record as Primus Verarch of Legio Praetoria |

The other three pairings are compatible or currently open:

- Oblivar Seneschal — Legio Nihilum;
- Thanekhet Osiron — Legio Anubis;
- The Coil-Lord — Legio Vritra.

### Recommended Resolution

Preserve the developed Pleroma identities:

- Xolotl remains Bellator unless the entire Bellator founding record is intentionally rewritten;
- Pyraxis remains Ignis unless Ares and Ignis are being merged;
- Aurelion remains Praetoria because his existing profile is extensive and structurally coherent.

Select different founding Verarchs for Quetzalcoatl, Ares, and Aurelis, or redefine the “first six” as the first six deployed in this campaign rather than the only Primus Founding Legions.

Do not rewrite the three developed Pleroma profiles until this choice is explicit.

## Timeline Wording

“This book begins immediately after Pleroma” should mean the next published volume, not necessarily the next day or year.

The New World War begins in 252 AL. If Pleroma ends during early Harmina settlement, the war bible should state:

> This is the next main Amber volume, opening in Year 252 AL after a deliberate generational interval.

If Pleroma itself ends at the Founder Session, no correction is needed.

## Confirmed Resolution

1. The GalFed war continues in Amber Book III rather than occupying the second half of Book II.
2. Amber receives emergency coronation first and Covenant ratification later.
3. Existing Pleroma Verarch identities remain canonical. New founding Verarchs will be created for Quetzalcoatl, Ares, and Aurelis.

Scene drafting may proceed after the named opening-cast slots in the definitive Book II skeleton are filled.
