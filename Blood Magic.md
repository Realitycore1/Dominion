[[Vitality magic]]

Entry: Blood Magic

Definition
Blood Magic is the manipulation of life-essence — whether physical blood, metaphysical lifeforce, or soul-anchored vitality — as a direct fuel for power. Unlike other [[Systems]] that draw on external conduits ([[Angels]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]], elemental cores), Blood Magic is internalized. It burns the self and others to bend reality.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Sacrificial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Economy|Economy]] – life is currency; more blood means more power.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Sympathetic Bond – blood carries [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]; to have someone’s blood is to hold a tether to their soul.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Corruption Risk – the closer one works with blood, the more the caster risks binding themselves to primal or demonic currents.

⸻

Techniques of Blood Magic
	•	Vital Expenditure – cutting one’s own flesh to release raw energy (simple firebolts, shields).
	•	Hemomancy – controlling blood outside the body: ripping it from foes, freezing it into weapons, boiling it into steam.
	•	Sanguine Forging – enchanting steel, leather, or bone with blood; often binding soul-resonance into weapons.
	•	Blood Puppetry – enslaving another by threading their veins like strings.
	•	Life Extension – draining vitality from victims to prolong one’s own life or restore youth.
	•	Resurrection (forbidden art) – forcing lifeforce into a dead vessel; often creates abominations rather than true revival.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: outlawed after the Blood Mage cult rebellion; now synonymous with heresy. The Calastine Order still tries to break the Veil with it ￼.
	•	Concordia: tied to [[Yondor]] and the [[Abyss]], where [[Undead]] use their own corrupted blood-cores to persist.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]] Labs: studied under containment, used in bioweapons and regeneration platforms. FAID agents are engineered with failsafes against it.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: largely absent; harmonic pluralism views it as a “disharmonic resonance.” Those caught practicing it are often sealed in crystal resonance prisons.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: embraced; Archontes weave entire cults around sacrificial rivers of blood to appease Demiurgic [[Entities]].

⸻

Why it Persists
	•	Accessible – every creature has blood; no need for rare reagents.
	•	Immediate Power – unlike study-based [[Systems]], blood magic works as soon as blood is spilled.
	•	Fearsome Reputation – power born from blood creates terror, and fear itself strengthens its aura.

⸻

Perils
	•	Addiction – the rush of [[Vitality magic]] creates dependence; practitioners often escalate to mass sacrifices.
	•	Dehumanization – overuse erodes empathy; some become cold predators.
	•	Demonic Whisper – voices slip through the Veil when blood is spilled ritually, guiding mortals to serve external [[Entities]].
	•	Blight – repeated use leaves scars in the land: cursed fields, forests of bone, waters that flow red.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Wards of Purity – angelic sigils repel hemomantic control.
	•	Salt & Iron – common elements that ground spilled lifeforce, disrupting rituals.
	•	[[Fire]] – burning blood denies it to mages; cremation is a preferred anti-blood magic measure.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Interference – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] field agents often use controlled Aetherstorms to destabilize large rituals.
