LEGION AMATERASU — The Dawnbearers

Classification: Cleansing Warfare • Atmospheric Purity • Terraforming Offense
Status: Destroyed

Profile

Amaterasu turned atmospheric ignition and environmental cleansing into weapons of war. They could transform toxic worlds into radiant habitats as easily as they could scour enemy populations from orbit.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Atmosphere ignition
	•	Radiant cleansing
	•	Terraform-warfare hybrid operations
	•	Planetary purification

[[culture]] & Symbol

A stylized rising sun with prismatic rays.

Final Fate

Many became “burning silhouettes” trapped in collapsing atmospheric [[Layers]] during the Severance.
