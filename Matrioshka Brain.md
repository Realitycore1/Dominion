	•	Goal: Achieve god-tier computation by running at maximal energy efficiency (Landauer limit).
	•	Power Budget (Sol-like star):
	•	~[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].8 × 10²⁶ W → ~5 × 10⁴⁵ operations/sec (assuming 1 kT per bit operation at ~[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] K).
	•	Heat Management:
	•	Inner computational [[Layers]] emit heat absorbed by outer [[Layers]], which radiate at progressively cooler temperatures.

	•	[[Verse]]-linked parallelism—run computation across multiple [[VFF]]-connected universes to exceed local thermodynamic limits.
	•	Can channel waste heat into [[Dark Eternities]] ([[Time]]-inverted [[Regions]]), effectively removing it from local space.
	•	Central star replaced with Matter Core reactor to extend lifespan beyond [[Natural]] stellar evolution.

A Matrioshka Brain is a theoretical megastructure concept proposed in speculative astrophysics and transhumanist circles. It represents the ultimate computational megastructure, consisting of nested Dyson shells around a star, each layer harvesting energy and performing computation. It is named after Matryoshka dolls (Russian nesting dolls), referencing its concentric shell architecture.
