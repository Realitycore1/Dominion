## Summary

Corridors a
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Beamed-sail corridors are permanent, militarized, and stabilized networks of beamed propulsion routes, creating dedicated trade and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] arteries across space. Unlike ad hoc lanes, they are fixed, fortified pathways — engineered highways of light threading between worlds and [[Stars]].
Stabilized Beams

Corridors aren’t just “one shot” beams — they’re arrays of synchronized projectors, mirrors, and [[Relays]] that maintain a continuous ribbon of energy.

Beams phase-locked into harmonic corridors, forming literal luminous pathways across space, visible to the naked eye as star-spanning shafts.

Multi-Lane Architecture

Corridors can host multiple bands:

Cargo lanes (slow push, high mass).

Courier lanes (fast acceleration, lighter sails).

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] priority lanes (high-energy overdrive capacity).

Atlas Pillar [[Relays]]

Massive spires (stationary or mobile) that act as both beam amplifiers and defensive fortresses.

Navigation Protocols

Inside the corridor, ships can coast safely, shielded from outside interference by the beam’s protective magnetic sheath.

Economic Function

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] coffers draw tolls at every gate; trade depends entirely on corridor access.

Peripheral [[Systems]] fight to be “on the corridor” — worlds left outside become economic backwaters.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] Function

Corridors double as strategic chokeholds:

Shut one down and entire sectors collapse into isolation.

Overcharge one and it becomes a colossal kill-beam.

Cultural / Mythic Role

Poets describe them as “roads of glass-[[Fire]], written across the heavens.”

Visual Form ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Relay spires shaped like black cathedrals, glowing apertures hurling beams into [[The Void]].

Suprematist Monolithism: Corridors themselves appear as stark, unwavering shafts of obsidian light, straight as [[law]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Sails inside the corridor ripple with living lattice patterns, glowing like script in a cosmic scroll.
