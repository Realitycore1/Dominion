# Story 06 - Storm Week Lessons

## Viewpoint

Mina Corven as a small child, filtered through the adults repairing the shelter around her.

## Setting

Years 0-2 AL, a New Aurora habitation block during the first long storm week.

## Story Shape

The storm does not attack like an enemy. It simply continues. Water finds seams. Static ruins sensors. Children sleep in maintenance corridors because those are the warmest places left.

Mina watches adults turn repair tasks into call-and-response habits: count the seals, touch the heatline, check the latch, pass the light. By the final night, the children know the sequence better than the exhausted engineers.

## Harmina Element

Storm survival becomes household culture.

## Character Movement

Mina learns that fear can be organized into routine.

## Future Payoff

These repair chants later become domestic rituals across frontier settlements.
