## Summary

Class II — 
![[bdbc9692-024b-4cfb-ad7b-ed7676e
74292.png]]
Class I — Proto-Computanium (Mortal / Prestellar Civilizations)
Substrates: Silicon semiconductors, doped carbon lattices, optical switches.
Traits: Energy-inefficient, limited density, fragile.
Civilizational Role: Enables digital empires, networked societies, automation.
Example: [[Humanity]]’s 20th–21st century computers.

Class III — Exotic-Matter Computanium (Civilizational Ascendancy)
Substrates:
Neutronium/quark-matter arrays.
Bose–Einstein logic condensates.
Strangelet lattice processors.
Traits: Femtometer-scale logic density, near-Bremermann limit efficiency.
Civilizational Role: Enables near-instant simulation of entire worlds.
Example: Quark-core vaults hidden beneath [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] stratobrutalist [[Temples]].

Class IV — Black Hole & Horizon Computanium (Dysonian Level)
Substrates:
Event horizon surface computation (Bekenstein–Hawking encoding).
Accretion disk as energy-input and read/write bus.
Traits: Ultimate information density per unit volume.
Civilizational Role: Anchors for Matrioshka Brains and Dyson civilizations.
Example: Hypercivilizations turning entire star clusters into “thinking horizons.”

Class V — Cosmological Computanium (Spiralverse Tier)
Substrates:
Infant verses (Tiplar oracles).
Braided cosmic strings as logic wires.
[[Confluencia]] strange-matter tethers.
Traits: Uses the topology of spacetime itself as processor.
Civilizational Role: Prediction, simulation, and manipulation of [[Verse]]-scale events.
Example: Tiplar oracle civilizations rising and then collapsing against the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] wall.

Class VII — Onto-Computanium (The Constant Tier)
Substrates: Personhood stabilized as a [[law]]. Consciousness-as-substrate.
Traits: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] persists across all scales, acting as a fixed computational function.
Civilizational Role: One being embodies inevitability. Futures collapse around them.
Example: The [[God Empress]] — a mind crystallized into [[law]], functioning as the computational constant of the late Omniverse.
Biotic & Quantum Computanium (Early Interstellar)
Substrates:
Wetware (engineered brains, neurogel matrices).
Quantum annealers and superposition processors.
Photonic and neuromorphic arrays.
Traits: Blends biology and [[Physics]], achieves high adaptability.
Civilizational Role: First “uplift engines,” allowing distributed AI minds.
Example: Early [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] neuro-architectures, cyborg command cores.

Class VI — Omniversal Computanium (Horizon Arc Tier)
Substrates:
Spiralverse Arrays.
[[Hypercivilization Shells]] encapsulating superclusters.
Horizon Arc Gates tuned as logic routers.
Traits: Entire universes act as single, unified processing nodes.
Civilizational Role: Control over omniversal logistics, communication, and expansion.
Example: The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s infrastructural skeleton itself doubles as computanium at this stage.

Ladder Summary
I–II: Matter computing itself.
III–IV: Exotic [[Physics]] and gravitational computation.
V–VI: Spacetime computing itself.
VII: [[(2030) Existence]] computing itself through a person.
