10.6 Chengdu is a vibrant and modern city located in the Sichuan province of China. It is one of the most significant economic, cultural, and industrial hubs in the country. Chengdu is home to over 15 million people, making it one of the largest metropolitan areas in China. The city is situated on the Minjiang River, which has been a major waterway for transportation and trade for centuries. The city's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is diversified, with significant contributions from manufacturing, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]], tourism, and financial services. Chengdu is known for its advanced [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] sector, with companies like Tencent, Alibaba, and Kingsoft headquartered there. Chengdu has a reputation for being one of the greenest cities in China, with numerous parks and public spaces throughout the city. The city is also home to several animal reserves and wildlife parks, such as the Chengdu Research Base of [[Giant]] Panda Breeding, which is dedicated to preserving the [[Giant]] panda population. The city has a unique cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] that blends Chinese traditions with Western influences. Chengdu is home to several famous landmarks and attractions, including Wenshu Monastery, Baoguo Fengdi Grottoes, and Shuimei Gate. The city's food scene is also notable, with spicy Sichuan cuisine being a popular draw for foodies. Chengdu has a humid subtropical climate with warm summers and cold winters. Despite its large size, Chengdu is known for its relatively low crime rate and high quality of life, making it an attractive destination for business travelers and expats.
