# Shield of Continuity

## Summary

Shield of Continuity is Vel's proposed formal office: protector, commander, judge, and consort of the Throne.

The office exists to defend not only Amber's body, but the continuity chain around her: witness, command legitimacy, emergency procedure, archive record, and the distinction between Amber's living will and the machinery that speaks in her name.

## Functions

### Protector

Vel protects Amber's person, movement, ritual exposure, and vulnerability in public.

### Commander

Vel may command elite continuity defense forces, transit guardians, or exemplars assigned to existential continuity crises.

### Judge

Vel may sit, testify, or render judgment in continuity review when the sovereign person, witness-chain, emergency authority, or imperial seal has been compromised.

### Consort

Vel's intimacy with Amber is not merely private. It carries public meaning. His role must acknowledge that private love and public legitimacy can collide.

## Legal Meaning

The Shield of Continuity is one of the few offices authorized to distinguish Amber's living will from the bureaucracy, ritual apparatus, security machinery, or court myth acting in her name.

## Story Uses

- A ministry issues an order using Amber's seal while she is incapacitated.
- A rite alters Amber's image to make her appear more divine and less human.
- Emilia and Vel disagree over whether protecting Amber means protecting the person or the office.
- A frontier population trusts Vel more than the court because he bled beside them before he stood beside the Throne.
- An enemy targets the witness-chain rather than Amber herself.

## Open Design Points

- Was Vel appointed by Amber, acclaimed by the military, chosen by rite, or inherited from an older office?
- Which force does Vel command?
- Can Vel judge Federal Branch resistance, or is that outside his authority?
