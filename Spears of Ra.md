Project Ra was conceived before formal GalFed contact during the Harminan arms race. Publicly, the Spears of Ra were anti-asteroid laser satellites and orbital safety infrastructure. Their classified design also supported anti-satellite fire, orbital denial, strategic area denial, and deterrence against the future GalFed coercion predicted by the Discovery Clock.

The New World War exposed those hidden capabilities. GalFed contact later accelerated and expanded the network, but GalFed did not create its original collectors, targeting assumptions, or military purpose.

⸻

🔋 Power Source
	•	Solar Overload Arrays: [[Giant]] heliotropic mirrors collect and focus stellar radiation.
	•	Nuclear Backfeed Reactors: For eclipse or hostile-star [[Systems]], using high-yield mini-reactors.
	•	ArcCell Integration: Direct power transfer from Artemis [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]’s ArcCell banks.

Each reactor generates continuous yields equivalent to ~40 atomic bombs per second.
	•	Primary Beam: Focused coherent [[Antimatter]]-laced photonic lance.
	•	Effect: Melts fortresses, vaporizes armored columns, slices through warships.
	•	Pulse [[Fire]]: Quick high-intensity bursts, capable of blasting hardened bunkers or shield nodes.
	•	Scatter Sweep: Beam diffusion into [[Wide]]-area strike, enough to glass entire square kilometers per pass.
⸻

⚔️ Weapon [[Systems]]
	•	Solar Lances: Focused beams capable of vaporizing warships, piercing orbital fortresses, or glassing surface zones.
	•	Scatter Mode: Beams diffract into [[Wide]]-area sweeps — devastating against armies or cities.
	•	Pulse Strikes: Short, high-intensity bursts used to cripple enemy energy grids.
	•	Overburn Protocol: All stations combine [[Fire]] on a single target, enough to breach even GalFed capital ships.

⸻

🛰️ Deployment
	•	Orbital Network: Dozens of Spears placed in polar and equatorial orbits for full planetary coverage.
	•	Linked Array: ORACLE-AI synchronizes their targeting, turning them into a “sky shield” or concentrated spear.
	•	Hidden Reserves: Some Spears are kept in solar orbit, disguised as standard relay satellites.

⸻

	•	Symbol of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Will: Colonists view them as divine [[Fire]] raining from the heavens, tying back to solar cult imagery.
	•	GalFed Nightmare: Forces GalFed to fight from [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] edges, buying [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Time]].

Planetary Siege Breaker: Spears devastate enemy landing forces before they can establish beachheads.
	•	Orbital Denial: Capable of destroying capital ships in low orbit.
	•	Psychological Warfare: The aurora effect ensures everyone knows the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] has fired.
