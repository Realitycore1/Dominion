After landfall the first city was New Aurora which is also when the first established [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] took hold and the building of the new home started. New Aurora was the first city and home of the volkshalle the first of five capital buildings. It also holds the largest population in the world ranging up to 100 billion, and was founded on 12282 H.E.

[[The Dominion]] has five capitals New Aurora, Freiheit, Endlos, Albertaich and Manahawkin which were the first Five settled cities of Harmonia.
This is before [[The Dominion]] Expansion, After which [[Harmina]] Becomes the Capital For the Whole [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] and will eventually move to the Globus Causses after it’s construction.
