# VI. The Bleeding Wilds

A realm where evolution and violence have fused into a permanent ecological war-state.

The environment itself appears wounded.

The terrain:
- bleeds
- regenerates
- spasms
- mutates under stress

Predation here has escalated beyond ordinary ecological function into perpetual biological warfare.

### Environmental Features
- rivers of oxygen-rich blood
- regenerative flesh plains
- forests of exposed muscle-fiber growth
- airborne parasite storms
- predator swarms numbering in the billions

### Common Inhabitants
- apex pack organisms
- bio-artillery beasts
- parasitic war fauna
- carnivorous supercolonies
- hyper-aggressive megafauna

The Bleeding Wilds are believed to be one of the primary origins of:
- world-eaters
- extinction predators
- apocalyptic beast archetypes

Many civilizations interpret this realm as “hellish.”

It is not evil.

It is simply evolution stripped of restraint and empathy.
