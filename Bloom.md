Core Bias:

Rapid cyclical flourishing

This subtype is defined by bursts.

Reality grows in spectacular waves, blooms, and cascading life surges.

Traits:
	•	seasonal or phase-based expansion
	•	sudden ecological eruptions
	•	fast diversification
	•	unstable but fertile complexity
	•	periodic resets

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	flowering explosions of existence
	•	periodic life cascades
	•	epochs of sudden growth and collapse

Common Expressions:
	•	rapidly mutating realities
	•	cycle-based civilizations
	•	recurrent golden ages and die-offs
	•	basin-scale “spring” events

Failure Mode:

Bloom-burn collapse
Growth happens too fast to stabilize.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	unstable but beautiful cosmologies
	•	cyclical empires
	•	relic emergence environments
	•	civilizations prone to renaissance-collapse loops
