Core Bias:

Large-scale turbulent motion

This is the baseline Storm subtype.

Reality behaves as though massive unstable systems are constantly in motion.

Traits:
	•	high turbulence
	•	unstable fronts and moving pressure systems
	•	large-scale disruption
	•	violent but patterned motion
	•	no stable center for long

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	everything is always in weather
	•	reality is in motion under unstable force
	•	no region remains untouched forever

Common Expressions:
	•	migratory war zones
	•	unstable plasma or current fields
	•	moving civilizations forced to adapt
	•	large-scale causal turbulence

Failure Mode:

Permanent churn
Nothing stabilizes long enough to mature.
