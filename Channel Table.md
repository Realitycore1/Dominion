# Channel Table (v0.1)

*The hidden conduits and voids that govern magic density across Concordia.*

## Common Channels
- **Ley-Lines** — Vast rivers of magical current, visible or hidden.
- **Foci** — Localized nodes where power condenses and magnifies.
- **Pools** — [[Natural]] reservoirs of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]], often sacred or contested.
- **Wards** — Artificially created boundaries to shape and control flow.

## Restrictive Zones
- **Nulls** — Dead zones, barren of any magical current.
- **Bleeds** — Fractured [[Regions]] where power leaks and dissipates.
- **Drains** — Predatory zones that consume nearby essence, destabilizing casters.

## Advanced Matrices
- **Crystalline Conduits** — Structures ([[Natural]] or built) that stabilize and amplify flows.
- **Resonant Matrices** — Harmonized constructs that lock density into predictable rhythms.
- **Paradox Wells** — Zones where conflicting currents coexist, usable only by high-tier mages.
