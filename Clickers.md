Classification

Fungal-Infected Post-Humans / Sensory-Rewired Predators / Collapse-Era Monsters

⸻

CORE IDEA

Clickers are people whose senses were rewritten before their identities finished dying.

They are not undead in the classic sense and not mindless zombies. Clickers are alive—but life has been subordinated to a parasitic intelligence that optimized them for hunting in a ruined world.

They don’t see.
They map you in sound.

⸻

ORIGIN

Clickers emerge from advanced-stage fungal infection (Cordyceps-class or analogous bio-parasitic agents).

Key conditions:
	•	airborne or bloodborne fungal spores
	•	prolonged survival past initial infection
	•	neural tissue partially preserved
	•	environment favors stealth predation

Early infected die or degrade.
Clickers are the ones who adapted successfully.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Clickers are humanoid, but catastrophically altered.

Common traits:
	•	Head split open by fungal growths, facial structure destroyed
	•	Mycelial plates erupting from skull, shoulders, and chest
	•	Eyes nonfunctional or entirely overgrown
	•	Skin desiccated, cracked, and pale
	•	Jaw partially fused or distorted
	•	Jerky, deliberate movements tuned for ambush

They emit constant clicking sounds—echolocation pulses.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Clickers are hyper-aggressive and proximity-reactive.
	•	Hunt via echolocation
	•	Attack anything that produces sound
	•	Exhibit pack clustering in enclosed spaces
	•	Display extreme violence once contact is made

They do not retreat.
They finish the interaction.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Low individual intelligence, high system efficiency.
	•	No reasoning or planning
	•	Excellent spatial awareness
	•	Strong memory of territory layouts
	•	Coordinated behavior emerges through environmental cues

The fungus doesn’t think.
It routes behavior.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS (SETTING-ADAPTIVE)

In your framework, Clickers slot cleanly as biological analogues to Malice-driven spread, without being supernatural.
	•	Biological Grey-Goo Parallel: replication through consumption
	•	Grace-Inert: angelic energy has no authority
	•	Malice-Compatible: corruption increases aggression, not intelligence
	•	Sound-Sovereignty: silence becomes survival

They demonstrate how monsters can exist without myth.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Thrive in dark, enclosed ruins
	•	Avoid open sunlight not from fear, but inefficiency
	•	Consume biomass to sustain fungal networks
	•	Eventually ossify into spore-dissemination nodes

Areas with Clickers slowly become biological traps.

⸻

ALPHA CLICKERS

Advanced variants exist (often called Bloaters, Shamblers, or regional analogues).

Alpha traits:
	•	Reinforced fungal armor
	•	Spore-based ranged threat
	•	Environmental control (choking clouds, corrosive growths)
	•	Territorial dominance

Alphas are ecosystem anchors, not leaders.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (individual) / Extreme (cluster or Alpha)

You don’t fight Clickers loudly.
You outthink them quietly—or die.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Clickers represent:
	•	infection without ideology
	•	humanity erased biologically, not morally
	•	survival horror stripped of symbolism
	•	the horror of adaptation succeeding

Where Ghoul-Pires are a failure of vampirism,
Clickers are a success of infection.
