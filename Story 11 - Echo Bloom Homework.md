# Story 11 - Echo Bloom Homework

## Viewpoint

Veyra Sol, a Harminan-born student assigned to collect sound records from an Echo Bloom field.

## Setting

Years 18-40 AL, supervised school fieldwork beyond New Aurora.

## Story Shape

The adults warn the children not to call the blooms ghosts. Veyra does anyway, because the field repeats a dead technician's laugh from a storm season she never lived through.

The teacher insists on scientific language. The children make a game of identifying which sounds are weather, animals, machines, and people.

## Harmina Element

Echo Blooms store and replay sound, turning environment into accidental archive.

## Character Movement

Veyra learns that history can be true without being fully understood.

## Future Payoff

Echo Bloom fields become contested places: science sites, memorial sites, children's dare grounds, and informal shrines.
