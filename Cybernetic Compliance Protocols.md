# Cybernetic Compliance Protocols

## Summary

Cybernetic Compliance Protocols are the Federal standards governing implants, prosthetics, neural interfaces, military-adjacent augmentations, civilian cybernetics, workplace-required enhancements, and machine-body integration.

The protocols are administered jointly by health, labor, AI, and bioethics regulators.

## Mandate

The protocols cover:

- implant safety;
- neural-interface consent;
- prosthetic access;
- workplace augmentation limits;
- cybernetic security updates;
- military-to-civilian downgrade rules;
- data privacy in body-linked systems;
- failure reporting and recall standards.

## Powers

Federal regulators may certify devices, recall unsafe systems, suspend clinics, require patch disclosures, and block coercive augmentation contracts.

## Limits

Cybernetic compliance cannot be used to strip autonomy from disabled citizens, force upgrades for ordinary employment, or classify a person as equipment because their body includes machine systems.

## Public Face

Citizens experience these protocols through clinic forms, device recalls, workplace waiver disputes, prosthetic grants, and security warnings that arrive directly inside their nervous systems.

## Story Use

Use this note for body autonomy, disability rights, military reintegration, corporate pressure, and the question of how much machinery a person can carry before the state starts treating them as infrastructure.
