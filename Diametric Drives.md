🔹 Mechanism:

Diametric Drives operate using mass polarity generation—the ship hosts a positive-mass and a negative-mass segment in quantum entanglement. As the [[Negative Mass]] moves “away,” it pulls the positive mass with it, creating a reactionless, inertialess acceleration beyond relativistic limits.

🔹 How It Works:
	•	A bifurcated drive chamber contains stabilized positive and [[Negative Mass]] [[Regions]]
	•	Engine exploits the runaway acceleration paradox: [[Negative Mass]] “chases” the positive
	•	The craft’s net motion accelerates without limit (regulated by internal spacetime cohesion fields)

🔻 Risks:
	•	Instability if mass polarity fluctuates—can implode into null-pocket
	•	Gravitational lensing reveals the ship’s trail to advanced observers
	•	Mental dissonance affects unprotected crews due to inertial disconnection
