Classification

Primordial Colossi / Pre-Divine Sentients / World-Scale Entities

Core Idea

Titans are not gods and not beasts.
They are structural intelligences—beings formed when the world itself needed anchors. Before angels codified law and before demons learned hunger, Titans existed to hold reality in place.

They don’t represent ideals.
They represent load-bearing existence.

⸻

Origin

Titans emerged during the earliest stabilization of the world:
	•	when mountains first resisted collapse
	•	when continents stopped drifting
	•	when oceans needed basins
	•	when the sky required weight and tension

They were not created with intent.
They condensed out of necessity.

When the divine hierarchy later formed, Titans were already there—too big, too old, too embedded to remove.

⸻

Physical Manifestation

Titans are colossal beyond human scale.

Common traits:
	•	Bodies formed of stone, metal, crystal, bone, or fused terrain
	•	Limbs like cliffs, spines like mountain ridges
	•	Faces eroded, partially abstract, often unreadable
	•	Eyes glowing faintly like magma vents, starlight, or tectonic pressure

Many Titans appear indistinguishable from landscape until they move.

Some have cities, forests, or ruins growing on them.

⸻

Behavior & Temperament

Titans are slow, deliberate, and catastrophic without malice.
	•	They move rarely
	•	When they do, geography changes
	•	They do not hunt, rule, or conquer
	•	They respond to imbalance, not offense

A Titan does not attack a city.
A city collapses because the Titan stood up.

⸻

Intelligence

Ancient and alien.

Titans think in:
	•	epochs
	•	pressure accumulation
	•	structural integrity
	•	cause-and-effect over millennia

They do not speak often.
When they do, it is through earthquakes, storms, or tectonic shifts.

⸻

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Grace-resistant: angels cannot command them
	•	Malice-inert: demons cannot corrupt them directly
	•	Aether-anchoring: reality stabilizes around them
	•	Anti-Transience: they slow time and decay locally

Destroying a Titan risks cosmological failure.

⸻

Ecology
	•	Titans do not reproduce conventionally
	•	New Titans form only after mass extinction or planetary trauma
	•	Dormant Titans may appear “dead” for ages
	•	Lesser beings often worship them unknowingly

Some Titans are chained, buried, or partially dismantled to prevent movement.

⸻

Threat Assessment

Civilizational / Existential

Not because they are hostile—
but because they are unavoidable.

⸻

Narrative Role

Titans represent:
	•	inevitability over intent
	•	power without morality
	•	the cost of foundational stability
	•	the danger of waking ancient solutions

Where gods argue over purpose,
Titans simply endure.
