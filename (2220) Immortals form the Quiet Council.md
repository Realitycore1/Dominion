# (2220) Immortals form the Quiet Council

## Canonical Event

The thirteen original Immortals form the Quiet Council as an advisory and investigative body.

The Quiet Council may:

- publish long-horizon assessments;
- request and review public records;
- issue scientific and historical findings;
- testify before city governments;
- publish named dissents.

It may not:

- govern;
- command armed forces;
- veto legislation;
- compel a legislative agenda;
- grant hereditary office to an Immortal's family.

Families already begin becoming informal dynasties around their unaging ancestors. This influence remains socially and politically significant despite lacking constitutional status.

See: [[Sol Immortal Ensemble and Twelve Dynasties]]
