# (3180) Mercury Colonial Committee established

The Mercury Colonial Committee is established with the first permanent Mercurian polar colony in 3180.

Settlement districts elect delegates. Shade control, radiator operations, polar-reserve management, mining labor, medicine, and schools receive transition representation because thermal infrastructure determines whether any other civil right can be exercised.

## Local Authority

The committee governs:

- shade allocation and minimum public-shadow guarantees;
- settlement heat budgets and radiator access;
- polar water and emergency cooling reserves;
- mining claims and thermal-discharge permits;
- local mass-driver schedules coordinated with Union traffic control;
- elections, schools, clinics, inheritance, burial, and household law.

No employer or creditor may withdraw shade, cooling, water, or radiator access to enforce a labor contract or debt.

## Shared Emergency Authority

The committee and Colonial Union share emergency command over the shade swarm and mass-driver corridors because a failure can affect multiple settlements and orbital routes.

Emergency control is limited by:

- published activation thresholds;
- automatic expiration;
- public system logs;
- independent technical review;
- compensation for local resources commandeered during the response.

The Colonial Union retains external traffic, common safety standards, defense, interworld trade, and recognition of additional Mercurian settlements.
