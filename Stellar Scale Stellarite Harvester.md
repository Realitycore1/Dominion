## Summary

A Stellar Scale Stellarite Harvester is a high
-tech massive mining platform that is designed to harvest Stellarite from the surface of planets, asteroids, or other celestial bodies. The Stellar Scale Stellarite Harvester is an incredibly advanced piece of [[Technology Portal|technology]] and is capable of extracting vast quantities of Stellarite in a relatively short amount of [[Time]]. It is equipped with powerful mining lasers and other tools that allow it to break down the surface of planets and harvest the valuable resources within. The Stellar Scale Stellarite Harvester is also capable of processing the raw materials it gathers and converting them into useful resources. This can include everything from energy to rare commodities that are difficult to find elsewhere. Niobium
