All planes possess dual expression: an omnipresent aspect that overlays reality as latent law, and a localized dominion where authority, hierarchy, and origin reside.

No plane may assert its dominion into a verse without invitation, whether through ritual, alignment, sanction, or catastrophic breach.

Invitation alone is insufficient. A verse must also be compatible with the plane’s axioms, energies, and conceptual load. Where compatibility is absent, manifestation fails or deforms.

Thus, many worlds know the planes only as ideas, while others drown in their presence
