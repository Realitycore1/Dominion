Soon after the first strike a new [[Arms]] race began between nations. Each attempting to outpace the other as was looms over the horizon.

As war grew ever closer [[The Equilon States]] shifted gear into a war machine that quickly encompassed the other nations in firepower and tech. Soon [[The Equilon States]] would build new weapon [[Systems]] that would alter the course of the war.

In orbit around [[Harmina]] is one of the Equilon [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] stations that developed the first power armor. The armor would full enclose the wearer in a steel casing, the armor was designed to negate as much energy from kinetic projectiles without harming the water and with minimal damage to the unit. 

Power Armor is a armored exoskeleton.

Stylization

Soon the Power armor would be masses produced with the Equilon [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]. The armor was also modified to negate energy damage as energy weapons emerged. 

The first energy based weapon was constructed and developed by the Atlovaia Empire which pushes the [[Arms]] race more towards war.

The first field test of the energy weapon was conducted in [[Harmina]] aerospace just over international waters. The test proved successful as the weapons results were above expectations.

Eventually the weapons were scaled down to a compact frame for individual use, which tipped the scales of the [[Arms]] race.

The first strike came between the Atlovaia Empire and the Socialist Republic. Which started a local war that would soon spiral out of control.

As the war broke out more nations began to attack one another. [[The Equilon States]] abstained from the conflict as they believed it was of no concern to them.

This war was unlike the last as new weapons and new technologies has changed the way war is conducted. The new war was also not constricted by the geneva convention that was placed on [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].

As the war continued more sophisticated weapons were introduced from handheld systems to orbital platforms. The buildup included robotic forces, mobile autonomous weapons platforms, power armor, clean tactical nuclear arms, long-horizon artillery, planetary defense arrays, and secret counterforce systems.

Project Ra, also known as the [[Spears of Ra]], is a secret Equilon orbital weapons platform developed as a strategic counterforce and planetary-defense system. Public doctrine describes it as an intercept and deterrence network. Its classified capacity makes it capable of striking hardened military infrastructure from orbit, which turns it into one of the most dangerous escalation systems of the era.

Despite the arms race, civilian and mixed-state populations begin construction of the first Harminan space elevator. The elevator is framed as infrastructure rather than a weapon, but every major power understands its military value for lift capacity, orbital logistics, rapid deployment, and planetary defense support.
