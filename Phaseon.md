I. Physical Definition

The Phaseon is a hypothetical particle responsible for mediating phase transitions — not just in conventional states of matter (solid, liquid, gas, plasma) but across exotic, high-energy, and interdimensional phase boundaries.

In real-world condensed matter [[Physics]], phase transitions are described in terms of symmetry breaking, critical points, and order parameters. In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), the Phaseon is the quantized excitation of the ΨP Phase-Lattice, responsible for reconfiguring the structural state of fields, matter, or spacetime itself.
	•	Spin: Variable; often scalar (0) for bulk transitions, but can adopt vector/tensor Modes for directional phase control.
	•	Mass: Dependent on the phase energy gap; near-zero in phase-neutral states.
	•	Charge: None in EM terms; carries Phase Charge, defining which states it can transition between.
	•	Interaction: Couples strongly to [[Aetherion]], [[Nulliton]], and [[Chiralon]] fields; interfaces with baryonic and [[Exotic Matter]] alike.
