I. Physical Definition

A Phase-Transition Star is a stellar-scale object whose evolution is dominated by sudden, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]-[[Wide]] shifts in matter phase or energy generation mode, often triggered by critical thresholds in pressure, temperature, density, or particle interaction balance.
Rather than evolving smoothly like typical [[Stars]], these objects experience catastrophic or cyclical state changes that redefine their physical structure and energy output.

[[Key]] features:
	•	Core-State Conversion — Interior matter may shift between plasma, degenerate matter, quark-gluon plasma, or exotic phases.
	•	Abrupt Luminosity Changes — Energy output can jump orders of magnitude in seconds to days.
	•	Thermodynamic Fronts — Moving boundaries between old and new phases propagate through the star.
	•	Potential for Recurrence — Some phase-transition [[Stars]] cycle repeatedly between states.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Phase-Transition [[Stars]] are Energy-Regime Anchors, serving as [[Natural]] triggers for large-scale thermodynamic changes in their surrounding [[Regions]].
