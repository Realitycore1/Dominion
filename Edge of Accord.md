## Summary
Emerged during the later Tirany decline, when resource collapse and civil fracture forced brutal choices.
	•	Fringe philosophers declared that [[The Accord]] was not about acceptance but about purification.
	•	Their rallying cry: “A unity that tolerates discord is no unity at all.”
	•	Originally heretics to both Orthodoxy and Harmonics, but survived through sheer fanatic cohesion.

⸻

📜 [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Purity of Accord – unity means absolute oneness; difference is a flaw to be excised.
	•	Accord is a Blade – harmony is not peaceful but violent: the crushing out of dissonance.
	•	Sacrifice is Salvation – individuals, [[Cultures]]OMINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Post-WW3/Cultures]], even [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] may be destroyed if their [[(2030) Existence]] fractures the whole.
	•	The Edge is Eternal – conflict is not an aberration but [[The Accord]]’s truest expression.

⸻

⚔ Practices & Rituals
	•	Rites of Severance – symbolic or literal cutting-away of “discordant” parts: amputations, exiles, purges.
	•	Chants of One – spoken in unison, not to create resonance but to erase individual voice.
	•	Unity by Blood – members pledge to kill or die for [[The Accord]] without hesitation.
	•	Sacrificial Concords – in extreme cases, entire communities are destroyed in [[Ritual]] purges framed as holy offerings to perfect Accord.

⸻

🛡 Organization
	•	Blades of Accord – zealot warriors who believe [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] is mercy for the unharmonized.
	•	Severants – shadowy inquisitors rooting out dissent within [[New Readers Start Here/Dominion Omniverse/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Totum Verse/Totum/Verse Structure/Supercluster[[NEWREA~1/DOMINI~1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Post-WW3/Cultures]]yrain Galaxy/Synarchate/Synarchate]] ranks.
	•	Cleansers – demolitionists who destroy “false unities” such as hybrid [[Cultures]] or [[Artificial]] accords.
	•	Edge Prophets – mystics who claim to hear [[The Accord]] as a voice demanding sacrifice.

⸻

⚔ Role in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]]
	•	Exist at the border of heresy — tolerated only when external threats loom, despised otherwise.
	•	Feared as the “dark mirror” of all unity — they are proof that Accord, pushed too far, becomes tyranny and slaughter.
	•	Sometimes deployed as “last resort” shock forces, where their fanaticism serves a practical purpose.
	•	Criticism: even their allies admit they may be more dangerous to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]] than to its enemies.

⸻

🜏 Symbolism
	•	The Blade in the Circle – [[The Accord]] ring cut by a knife, showing unity enforced by violence.
	•	Colors – black (purge), red (sacrifice), silver (blade).
	•	Architecture – fortress-[[Temples]], stark and unadorned, built like guillotines of stone.

⸻

[[The Accord]] is not peace.
[[The Accord]] is the [[Silence]]
after all others are gone.”
