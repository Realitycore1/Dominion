Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Halo Habs are ring-shaped orbital habitats (partial or full circumference) suspended around planets, [[Stars]], or [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]]. They serve as mid-scale population centers, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]] stations, or trade ports, forming a “crown” around their anchor body.
Scale & Structure

Diameter: hundreds to thousands of kilometers.

Can be partial arcs (crescent belts) or full rings around a planet.

Anchored via [[Atlas Pillars]] or gravitic tethers to planetary crust.

Rim towers rise outward, doubling as docking spires and defense emplacements.

Habitability

Gravity provided via rotation or gravitic field generators.

Integration

Linked to [[Clarke Orbit Cities]] as orbital capitals.

Often fused into Beam Corridors and [[Gravimetric Roads]] — forming living trade junctions.

MRO ecologies patrol continuously, crawling like insects across the halo’s spine.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide cheap, fast-deployed living space for billions, especially in frontier [[Systems]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Act as orbital fortresses — each hab armed with shield arrays, fleet docks, and planetary [[Fire]] support.

Economic: Serve as hubs for [[Beamed-sail commerce lanes]], toll gates, and storage depots.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ribbed struts and towers rising from the ring like cathedral buttresses.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, Halos look like seamless black bands, perfectly geometric.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Interiors sculpted into sacred spirals and fractal geometries.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Surfaces inscribed with light-script, visible from planets below as a glowing halo of glyphs.

From a planetary surface, a Halo Hab appears as a permanent band of light across the sky, glowing at night like a celestial crown.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Home, safety, and spectacle — to live in the sky, always looking down on the world below.

To rebels: A reminder of control — the Empress’s crown looming above, impossible to ignore.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Practical and symbolic — mass habitation and perpetual display of power.

To myth: Known as the “Sky Crowns”, proof that no world stands without wearing her mark.
