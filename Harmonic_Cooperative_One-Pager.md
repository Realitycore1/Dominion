# Harmonic Cooperative — Snapshot

**Type:** Polyphonic network commonwealth (confederal, protocol-first)  
**Creed:** *“Where we resonate, we rise.”* Alignment is voluntary; uplift is invited, not imposed.  
**Core ethic:** Personal autonomy, transparency of influence, reciprocity in aid — the **Three Harmonics**.

## Governance
- **Chord Councils:** multi-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]] assemblies; proposals are vectorized preferences (“phase & amplitude”). Consensus forms when the **resultant amplitude** crosses a threshold for a set dwell [[Time]].  
- **Conductor (rotating):** emergency coordinator selected by Chord vote for a strict window (e.g., 30–90 days). Cannot legislate; only orchestrates.  
- **[[Choirs]]:** [[Domain]] cooperatives (health, transit, archives) with open ledgers; membership is opt-in and portable.

## Tech flavor
- **FTL:** **Resonant Lattice** — standing-wave corridors created by synchronizing multiple nodes (“array singing”). It cannot be monopolized by a single gateyard; requires **consenting arrays**.  
- **Comms:** **Phase-locked [[Relays]]** with strong authentication and open telemetry.  
- **Materials:** phononic/photonic crystals; meta-ceramics; iridescent films that shift with load (diagnostics as art).  
- **AI stance:** symbiotic decision aids (“Tuning Spirits”) allowed; sovereign coercive AIs banned.

## Economy
- **Dividend of Resonance:** credits accrue when [[Entities]] contribute to public goods (array [[Time]], health capacity, archives).  
- **Open-standards licensing:** revenue via certification, not rent; anti-monopoly clauses enforced by Chord courts.

## Military & ROE
- **Choir Fleets:** modular swarms; shield webs; **de-escalatory modulation** (dampers, dazzlers, dephase lances).  
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** **No First Breach** of closed rings; **Quiet War** playbook — jam coercion, escort aid, keep channels open.

## Posture
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]:** cooperative on humanitarian and existential crises; PFN↔Lattice bridging projects.  
- **Athearn:** strategic rival — oppose compulsory uplift and gate rent-seeking; build bypass corridors.  
- **GalFed:** friendly, sometimes impatient with their non-interference.

## Aesthetics
Iridescent greens/teals/purples; choral motifs; cities with wind-harp façades; ships with ribbed metamaterial petals that flex under load.
