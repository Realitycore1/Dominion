## Summary

GalFed legions are specialized orders within the Custodial Harmonium. Each combines military function with legal, medical, or noetic authority.

## Details

Major legions and orders:

- Archive Wardens: defend Beacon archives, recover stolen histories, and seize illegal memory stores.
- Corridor Lancers: rapid-response fleets for route interdiction and pirate suppression.
- Silent Guard: protect Silent Worlds and prevent exploitation of vacant populations.
- Restoration Escorts: mixed medical-security units assigned to cognition recovery missions.
- Null Choirs: low-Aether troops trained for Singularity scar zones and noetic hazard sites.
- Reclamation Lords: Vadiasen-descended commanders assigned to rebellious or collapsed sectors.
- Stellar Ordinate: punitive force used against worlds declared spiritually or noetically stagnant.
- Quarantine Phalanxes: blockade forces with medical, customs, and weapons authority.
- Harmonic Suppression Cohorts: Soa'lain-derived units that disable mass unrest through resonance control.
- Predictive Intervention Teams: small units deployed before rebellion or collapse becomes visible.

Legions vary widely in public reputation. Restoration Escorts are beloved on many worlds. The Stellar Ordinate is feared even by loyal member species. Archive Wardens are respected until they arrive with seizure orders.
