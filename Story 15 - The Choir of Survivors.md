# Story 15 - The Choir of Survivors

## Viewpoint

Leira Cest, a sanctuary singer asked to set survivor testimony to a form people can repeat.

## Setting

Years 41-93 AL, early New Aurora sanctuary.

## Story Shape

Leira is given transcripts of collapse histories, landfall deaths, and food-crisis testimony. She is told to make them singable without making them pretty.

The first rehearsal fails because everyone treats grief as performance. The second succeeds because an old cook interrupts and corrects a line about hunger.

## Harmina Element

Ritual forms from testimony, correction, and communal memory rather than doctrine alone.

## Character Movement

Leira learns that sacred music is not what rises above ordinary life. It is what can survive being corrected by people who were there.

## Future Payoff

The Choir of Survivors becomes the root of later regional hymnals.
