In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), quark [[Stars]] are classified as transitional hyperdense stellar engines. Their Quark Lattice Core (QLC) is stabilized via [[Phaseon]]–[[Graviphoton]] coupling, preventing runaway collapse into a [[Singulon]]-dominated singularity. This makes them both [[Natural]] astrophysical objects and deliberately engineered high-yield energy cores for Omniversal [[Infrastructure]]1/EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Intrastellar/Luna Colonial Committee/Infrastructure]].
	•	Core State: Deconfined up, down, and potentially strange quarks.
	•	Density: ~10¹⁸ kg/m³ (between neutron and preon star densities).
	•	Radiation: Extremely hot [[X-Ray]]/gamma emissions from thin outer [[Layers]]; exotic particle emissions in [[VFF]] models.

In the [[VFF]], quark [[Stars]] function as extreme energy density anchors:
	•	Serve as power cores for Dyson-tier civilizations through direct quark–gluon plasma tapping.
	•	Act as particle foundries, naturally producing exotic hadrons and rare matter states.
	•	Stabilize high-curvature transit hubs via their intense gravitational fields.
	•	Provide material conversion nodes — turning standard matter into [[Exotic Matter]] feedstock for advanced [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures|megastructures]].
