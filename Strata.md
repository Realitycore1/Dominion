Core Bias:

Layered time and buried history

This subtype is all about:

reality preserving itself in stacked layers of accumulated past.

Traits:
	•	visible temporal layering
	•	buried civilizations or truths
	•	geological memory
	•	sedimented identity
	•	civilizations literally built atop older ones

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	every age is still present beneath the current one
	•	history is a physical thing you can excavate
	•	truth is buried in load-bearing layers

Common Expressions:
	•	archive planets
	•	buried empire basins
	•	fossilized reality systems
	•	civilizations that live atop forgotten predecessors

Failure Mode:

Stratigraphic lock
The basin becomes unable to evolve beyond accumulated burden.

Best use:

Excellent for:
	•	deep-time settings
	•	relic-heavy realities
	•	civilizations of inheritance and excavation
	•	Dominion archaeology and continuity themes
