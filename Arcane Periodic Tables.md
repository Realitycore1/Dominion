1. Core Magical Elements (Base Row)
	•	Aetherium – raw [[Mana]] essence, foundation of all spellcraft.
	•	Vitae – life-force essence (blood, growth, vitality).
	•	Mortis – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]-force essence (entropy, necrosis, shadow).
	•	Ignis – [[Fire]], destructive energy.
	•	Aqua – water, memory, flow.
	•	Aer – air, breath, voice.
	•	Terra – [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]], stability, endurance.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Higher Magical Catalysts (Second Row)

These are like “noble gases” — rare, stable, but powerful anchors.
	•	Lux – light, revelation, truth.
	•	Umbra – shadow, concealment, secrecy.
	•	Anima – soul-essence, the spark of selfhood.
	•	Spiritus – motive spirit, archetypal drive.
	•	Tempus – [[Time]] essence, moments crystallized.
	•	Somnium – dream essence, archetypal and Oneiroverse anchor.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Transcendent]] Magical Substances (Third Row)

Like superheavy unstable elements — rare, volatile, often mythic.
	•	Nullium – void essence, erasure, nothingness.
	•	Intentionis – distilled will, drives [[Verse]]-crossing.
	•	Veritas – truth-word essence, Logos crystallized.
	•	Eternium – stasis, immortality, [[Dark Eternities]] anchor.
	•	Mythium – narrative crystallization, story becoming matter.
	•	Chaosium – primal flux, root of change and destruction.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Hybrid Compounds (Magical Alloys)

Just as chemistry has molecules, magic has compound essences:
	•	Ignis + Vitae = Pyrosang (bloodfire, berserker magic).
	•	Mortis + Aqua = Noxmire (swamps of decay, plague magic).
	•	Lux + Tempus = Solchron (light of eternity, holy [[Time]]-magic).
	•	Umbra + Somnium = Nyxveil (nightmare shrouds, illusion).
	•	Terra + Spiritus = Animastone (living golems, elemental guardians).
