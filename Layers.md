1. Root Axioms

A Root Axiom is the first principle through which a system defines cause and effect.
Examples:
	•	Thought shapes the real (Cognitive Paradigm)
	•	All power demands equivalent cost (Conservation Paradigm)
	•	Harmony sustains existence; discord corrodes it (Resonance Paradigm)

Each verse or civilization begins with one or more Root Axioms.
UMA registers these as A₀ Constants, the immutable basis of the local metaphysics.

⸻

2. Field Mediums

A Field Medium is the energy substrate that enables action.
Examples: Aether, Mana, Leyflow, Resonance, Faithlight, Dark Energy, Quantum Foam.
Fields define transmissibility and decay rate.
They are measurable by saturation (Φ) and coherence (κ).
Fields may coexist or interfere through harmonic superposition.

⸻

3. Interfaces

Interfaces translate will into structured interaction.
Types:
	•	Somatic – gestures, runes, motion
	•	Cognitive – thought, emotion, intent
	•	Technological – engines, circuits, algorithms
	•	Biological – bloodlines, organs, symbionts
	•	Ritual – composite synchronization of multiple forms

Interfaces operate as the translator nodes between the user and the Field Medium.

⸻

4. Expressions

Expression is the final observable effect.
It can be continuous (a sustained field) or discrete (a single cast or pulse).
UMA formalizes expression energy as:

Where Φ is field saturation, κ is coherence, and I is interface efficiency.
High-E systems produce macro-scale phenomena (storms, miracles).
Low-E systems yield minor or subtle outcomes (illusions, minor kinetic effects).

⸻

5. Governance

Governance defines constraints:
	•	Entropy Costs: energy loss during translation.
	•	Ethical Codes: socially imposed prohibitions.
	•	Systemic Boundaries: hard-coded conservation limits.

Governance prevents total collapse of axiomatic balance.
Violating governance introduces paradox instability (Ψₚ).

⸻

6. Translation Matrix

The Translation Matrix allows direct comparison or hybridization of systems.
Every paradigm may be expressed in a coordinate space of:
	•	Root Axiom (A₀)
	•	Field Constant (Φ)
	•	Interface Category (I)
	•	Governance Constraint (G)

A translation occurs when two systems share sufficient harmonic overlap to produce a stable solution:
S = (A₀_1 \cap A₀_2) \neq \varnothing
Where S is shared symmetry between systems.

Layer 7 – Intent–Reality Coupling

Definition

The Intent–Reality Coupling (IRC) defines the relationship between subjective will and objective world-logic.
It quantifies how effectively a user’s internal model of causality interfaces with the universe’s actual causal lattice.

⸻

1. Conceptual Principle

Every act of magic is a negotiation between belief and law.
The clarity of that negotiation determines its stability.
UMA treats intent as measurable by informational precision, not faith or emotion.

⸻

2. Parameters
	•	Conciseness (C): informational density and internal consistency of intent.
	•	Comprehension (U): degree of user understanding of true physical/metaphysical rules.
	•	Alignment (A): resonance between the user’s paradigm and the verse’s objective constants.

⸻

3. Core Equation

The probability of successful expression is:
P = f(C \times U \times A)
Where P approaches 1 when the user’s mental model is concise, accurate, and harmonically aligned with the environment.
Low-C or low-U events create noise and paradox.

⸻

4. Interpretive Notes
	•	High C: Symbolically optimized systems (mathematical magic, computational rituals).
	•	High U: Scientific understanding of metaphysical structure.
	•	High A: Spiritual resonance or genetic attunement.

Different systems favor different parameters:
Faith magic maximizes A, technomagic maximizes U, linguistic sorcery optimizes C.

⸻

5. Consequences of Mismatch
	•	Low C → uncontrolled chaos, imprecise casting.
	•	Low U → physical contradiction, self-damage.
	•	Low A → dissonance, failed manifestation.

Dominion research treats these as measurable error rates during Aether interaction.

⸻

6. Function within UMA

Intent–Reality Coupling is the objective correction to subjective magic.
It acknowledges belief but evaluates its efficiency.
This enables simulation and hybridization without narrative contradiction.

Layer 8 – Energy Transformation & Cross-Field Translation

Definition

Energy Transformation governs the exchange of power between different metaphysical systems.
It ensures conservation of total energy across paradigms, converting incompatible field types through controlled translation.

⸻

1. Core Axiom

Energy is singular; all distinctions between “magic,” “technology,” or “divinity” are interpretive encodings.
Conversion is achieved through axiomatic refactoring rather than creation.
2. Conversion Equation 
E_{out} = (E_{in} \times \eta_T) \times (A_c \times U_c \times C_c)
Where:
	•	E_{in} = input energy.
	•	\eta_T = thermodynamic or entropic efficiency.
	•	A_c = alignment constant between field axioms.
	•	U_c = comprehension constant of the converter or user.
	•	C_c = conciseness of translation algorithm.
Loss manifests as paradoxic radiation or entropy discharge.

⸻

3. Conversion Types
	•	Mag → Tech: Runic converters encode mana into electromagnetic form.
	•	Tech → Mag: Reactors generate resonant frequencies equivalent to divine energy.
	•	Mag → Alt Mag: Harmonic anchors reconcile incompatible metaphysical signatures.
	•	Bio ↔ Field: Living entities act as natural transducers of life-force and psionic current.

⸻

4. Infrastructure Standards
	1.	Axiomatic Converters: nano-ritual or computational cores rewriting field logic.
	2.	Field Couplers: resonance stabilizers bridging divergent energy constants.
	3.	Null Filters: safety lattices preventing runaway polarity inversion.
	4.	Calibration Scripts: adaptive algorithms calculating C_c, A_c, U_c in real time.
