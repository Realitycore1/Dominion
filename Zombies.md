Classification: Undead Mass & Necrotic Failures
Subtype: Flesh-Bound Reanimates / Low-Order Undead
Status: Common, Highly Dangerous in Numbers

⸻

Core Description

Zombies are reanimated corpses retaining flesh, animated by crude necromancy, curses, pathogens, or ambient death saturation. Their movement is stiff or shambling, their cognition minimal or absent, and their persistence unnerving.

A zombie does not think.
It advances.

Individually weak, collectively overwhelming.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Zombies are biological undead, animated at the level of tissue rather than soul.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Death (low-order application)
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice (ambient), Decay
	•	Realm Interface: Material Continuum, Shadow bleed zones

Most zombies contain:
	•	no intact soul,
	•	no identity,
	•	no will.

They are motion without meaning.

⸻

Creation Pathways

Zombies arise from:
	•	mass necromantic events,
	•	cursed battlefields,
	•	plague-like death magic (e.g., Croatoan-type vectors),
	•	failed resurrection rituals,
	•	environmental death saturation.

Unlike ghouls or vampires:

Zombies do spread—through curse, pathogen, or reanimation cascade.

⸻

Intelligence & Behavior

Zombies exhibit:
	•	stimulus-response behavior,
	•	attraction to movement, sound, or life,
	•	no fear, no tactics.

Variants may:
	•	follow simple commands,
	•	cluster instinctively,
	•	repeat last-life motions (doors, tools).

Any zombie displaying strategy is misclassified or possessed.

⸻

Physical Capabilities
	•	High durability through indifference to damage
	•	No pain response
	•	Slow reaction time
	•	Limited coordination
	•	Vulnerable to dismemberment and fire

Zombies are strongest when:
	•	packed tightly,
	•	advancing relentlessly,
	•	overwhelming defenses by attrition.

⸻

Weaknesses & Termination
	•	Fire (most effective)
	•	Total cranial destruction (context-dependent)
	•	Consecrated ground
	•	Environmental cleansing
	•	Phoenix-grade purges (absolute)

Isolated zombies are trivial.
Unchecked outbreaks are catastrophic.

⸻

Variants
	•	Shamblers: Classic slow undead
	•	Runners: Recently dead, high threat
	•	Plague Zombies: Infectious vectors
	•	Ash Zombies: Burned but active
	•	Armored Zombies: Battlefield remnants
	•	Necrotic Swarms: Hive-like masses

Necrotic Swarms are extinction-level threats in cities.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Skeletons: Often preferred by necromancers
	•	Ghouls: Feed on zombies opportunistically
	•	Wights: Use zombies as domain fodder
	•	Liches: Consider zombies inefficient
	•	Phoenixes: Erase zombies completely
	•	Reapers: Overwhelmed by outbreaks

Zombies are nobody’s preferred undead—but everyone’s problem.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Skeletons: Zombies retain flesh
	•	vs Ghouls: Zombies are not alive
	•	vs Revenants: Zombies lack purpose
	•	vs Vampires: Zombies lack hierarchy
	•	vs Constructs: Zombies rot

This preserves undead stratification.
