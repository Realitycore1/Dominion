1. Common Hydra

Definition: The baseline—serpentine monster with multiple heads and regenerative powers.

Traits: Amphibious, venomous breath, each head semi-independent.

Symbolism: Problems multiplied; chaos made flesh.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Swamp Hydra

Definition: Hydras lurking in bogs and wetlands.

Traits: Camouflaged hides, fetid breath, ambush predators.

Symbolism: Corruption and stagnation breeding horror.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. River Hydra

Definition: Hydras adapted to flowing waters.

Traits: Sleek forms, aquatic speed, water-jet breath.

Symbolism: The river as predator; danger in currents.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Desert Hydra

Definition: Sand-dwelling hydras.

Traits: Burrowing bodies, dust-breath, yellowed scales.

Symbolism: Multiplicity in emptiness; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] in the dunes.

5. Frost Hydra

Definition: Cold-born hydras of tundras and glaciers.

Traits: White scales, icy breath, snow-blending.

Symbolism: [[Hunger]] in desolation; chaos in purity.

6. Storm Hydra

Definition: Skybound hydras infused with lightning.

Traits: Crackling heads, thunderous roars, storm-chasing.

Symbolism: Multiplicity of chaos in the skies.

7. Abyssal Hydra

Definition: Hydras corrupted by the underworld or [[Abyss]].

Traits: Blackened scales, necrotic venom, endless [[Hunger]].

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] multiplied; corruption without limit.

8. Elemental Hydra

Definition: Hydras with heads embodying different elements.

Traits: [[Fire]], frost, lightning, acid, shadow—all in one body.

Symbolism: The chaos of nature’s forces warring in flesh.

9. Regal/Noble Hydra

Definition: Larger, more intelligent hydras that rule territories like dragons.

Traits: Dozens of heads, cunning strategies, treasure-hoards.

Symbolism: Monstrosity elevated into [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].

10. Arch-Hydra (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary hydras embodying apocalypse.

Traits: Hundreds of heads, continent-sized coils, immortal regeneration.

Symbolism: Chaos incarnate; the unslayable doom.
