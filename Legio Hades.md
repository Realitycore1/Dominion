Classification: Subterranean Siege • Necro-Tech Suppression
Status: Destroyed

Profile

Hades handled cavern-world empires, necro-mechanical infestations, and deep-strata wars. Their “Cerberus frames” were triple-limbed excavation warforms.

They fell in a crust-break collapse triggered during the rupture. Their tomb-world remains a forbidden shrine.
