# Terra Narrative Continuity Ledger

## Purpose

Track the elements that make the manuscript feel like one novel across 190 years:

- recurring objects;
- family and mentorship inheritance;
- locations that change;
- ordinary professions;
- infrastructure;
- cultural drift;
- decisions whose consequences cross generations.

## Secondary Nation Canon Register

These nations complicate the major-bloc map without becoming peer great powers. By 2046, each possesses enough recognized authority to issue documents, administer local infrastructure, and negotiate at the Constantinople corridor conference.

Some are sovereign states. Some are federations, autonomous republics, maritime commonwealths, or treaty nations. Cooperation with a major bloc does not transfer sovereignty.

Shared guardrails:

- Do not turn the seventeen nations into a formal sixth bloc.
- Do not assign exact borders, wars, rulers, or constitutions until a dedicated canon note settles them.
- Do not treat ancient names as proof of uninterrupted ancient government or exclusive ethnic inheritance.
- Do not let major-bloc maps erase their passports, taxes, schools, courts, emergency services, or consent over infrastructure.
- Use the Constantinople Corridor Protocols as functional recognition, not a mutual-defense treaty.

## Cultural Drift Timeline

### 1889-1890s

- Children know multiple legal geographies.
- Food and taxes cross different jurisdictions.
- Printed atlases create national authority.
- Celestialism begins as method, not religion.

### 1950s-1960s

- Civil-defense drills enter schools and birthdays.
- Shelter food becomes family ritual.
- Alliance language turns populations into variables.
- War trauma enters kitchens and workplaces.

### 1970s-1990s

- Classified science and antiwar distrust reshape family conversation.
- Television makes war appear precise.
- State objects become street-market goods after political collapse.
- The hidden exchange professionalizes secrecy.

### 2000s

- Containment architecture shapes apartments, schools, and property values.
- Children learn scanner posture.
- Air becomes a purchased household service.
- AccerStick use becomes worker and survivor ritual.

### 2019-2032

- Remote school, oxygen delivery, and health seals define adolescence.
- Designed interventions become family contracts.
- Cognitive scores enter employment and politics.
- Campaign identity enters grocery stores, clinics, and generators.

### 2033-2048

- Relic replicas enter homes before authentication settles.
- Pilgrimage creates markets, lodging, transit priority, and custody conflict.
- Designed children grow under different national ownership languages.
- Europolis and CRINK turn heritage into civic branding.
- Secondary nations use shared standards without surrendering local sovereignty; ordinary forms become a contest over who counts as a state.

### 2055-2068

- Religious insignia becomes work uniform and enlistment category.
- Family compensation makes mobilization economically ordinary.
- Relic tokens become permits, bribes, and ration currency.
- Schools begin teaching approved interpretations.

### 2069-2078

- Angel names become drone identifiers.
- Sacred and secular slogans compete over empty food offices.
- Orbit becomes a cultural frontier and battlefield.
- People distinguish private relic meaning from public mobilization.

### 2079

- Propaganda becomes fuel.
- Household tokens remain private.
- School teaches observation, verification, and claim as separate layers.
- Shared recipes and repair knowledge matter more than former citizenship.
