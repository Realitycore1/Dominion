I. Founding (3330–3400 CE)

Austro Guard began as a slow-ship convoy depot.
When the C.U. Vigilant arrived after a 160-year transit, its crew established orbital yards above the water-rich moon Austro Minor II.
Officially chartered as a defense manufacturing colony, its goal was to assemble escort hulls for other slow-ships en route farther into the Orion Arm.

Initial population: ~60 000 [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], miners, and naval artificers.
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: direct [[Colonial Union]] administration with corporate oversight from the Orbital Mechanics Guild (an [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]-era consortium).

II. The Guild Confederacy (3400–3600 CE)

Isolation and light-year-long response times forced autonomy.
When supply convoys stopped arriving after the Tharsis Closure echoed through policy, the colony reorganized into a guild confederacy—each yard, refinery, and hab cylinder electing masters who met in a rotating Grand Assembly.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] stabilized around:

Escort-class hulls and [[Armored Transport]] fabrication.

Tug and convoy services between neighboring [[Systems]].

Export of compressed deuterium slugs fired by a lunar mass driver for regional trade.

Population passed two million; the phrase “Guard your Guard” became civic creed.

III. The Defensive Golden Age (3600–3850 CE)

With FTL still unreliable, Austro’s yards became crucial stop-points for slower arks and courier craft.
They built a [[Skyhook]] + Cislunar Beamer Pair that lifted raw materials from Austro Minor II to orbit.
A Depot Ring grew around the beamer array, acting as both refinery and convoy-refuel hub.

Security [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:

Maintain pickets at every jump beacon.

Issue escort licenses through the Guild Council.

Enforce “Neutral Lane” policy—[[Pirates]] entering Austro lanes vanished.

By 3800 CE the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] held ~50 million citizens and the reputation of the most secure corridor in its quadrant.

IV. Trade Wars and the Equilon Front (3850–4000 CE)

The [[Colonial Union]]’s first FTL fleets began appearing in 3975 CE.
Austro’s guilds adapted instantly, converting depot rings into jump-refit cradles.
Neighboring [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] demanded toll-free access through Austro space; the Guilds imposed tariffs to fund defense.
Disputes escalated into brief Convoy Blockades (3982–3989 CE), ending with Austro’s victory after disabling three corporate [[Cruisers]] without lethal [[Fire]]—cementing their image as calm but unbreakable.

During the Equilon Conflict (≈4000 CE), Austro Guard declared armed neutrality yet quietly serviced both Union and Equilon convoys.

V. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] & [[culture]] (c. 4000 CE)

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: Defensive confederacy; Guild Councils (industrial and escort), Civil Assembly (habitation and trade).

Religion/Philosophy: Civic humanism—“No vessel left adrift.”

Security: High-readiness fleets, contract escorts, drone mines at every mass point.

Symbols: Three-ring emblem representing yard, guild, and convoy.

Anthem: “We built the ships that guard the [[Stars]].”

Legacy

By 4000 CE, Austro Guard stood as one of the most disciplined and technically capable [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] in the Orion Arm:

Trusted by merchants, feared by raiders.

A reluctant but vital node in the [[Colonial Union]]’s logistics web.
