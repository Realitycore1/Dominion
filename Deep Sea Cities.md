Deep Sea Cities are habitats and fortresses built into oceanic abyssal zones, designed for population housing, research, defense, and as cultural sanctuaries. They are part of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s strategy to colonize all environments — land, orbit, void, atmosphere, sea, and eternity itself.
Structural Design

Pressure Shells: Multi-layered diamondoid and strange-matter domes capable of withstanding tens of thousands of atmospheres.

Atlas Pillar Roots: Cities anchored into seabeds by colossal spires, preventing drift.

Bio-Integration: Coral-mechanical hybrid growths, symbiotic with ocean ecologies.

Functions

Habitat: Millions can live in arcologies shielded from storms, war, or orbital attack.

Research: Study of abyssal life, xenobiology, and pressure-based technologies.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Sub-ocean fortresses invisible to orbital scans, armed with toroidal plasma cannons and abyssal drone swarms.

Energy [[Systems]]

Geothermal taps from hydrothermal vents.

Tidal turbines harnessing deep currents.

Concordia reactors embedded in pressure vaults.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Colossal cathedral domes, ribbed supports arching like frozen [[Leviathans]].

Suprematist Monolithism: Outer hulls appear as smooth, black stones half-buried in the seabed.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Interior lights ripple like bioluminescent patterns, mimicking deep-ocean organisms.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Every corridor etched with glowing script, prayers rising like bubbles through the darkness.

From the outside, a Deep Sea City looks like a glowing jewel in eternal night — luminous towers rising from the seabed like sacred spires.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Places of mystery and wonder — a fusion of safety and alien strangeness.

To rebels: Infiltration points, if they can survive the abyssal approach.

To myth: Called the “Sunken Thrones”, believed to be reflections of the Empress’s cities above, mirrored in the deep.
