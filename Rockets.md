The [[Antimatter]] rocket is new engine designed to be superior to fusion engines which is predicted to be used by the union for interstellar voyages. The [[Antimatter]] engine produces hundreds of times more propulsion which allows more coverage and is more fuel efficient. Due to the penning trap designs [[Antimatter]] fuel is not economically feasible for everyday use.

Not a bomb; a heater or particle source you tame for thrust. Choice = thrust vs. Isp vs. radiation.

1) [[Antimatter]]-thermal (AM-NTR)

Use: antiprotons heat a working fluid (H₂) which expands through a nozzle.
Pros: decent thrust, simpler plumbing; propellant dilutes radiation & smooths power spikes.
Isp: ~1,000–[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]],000 s (solid-core), up to 5,000–10,000 s (gas-core).
Notes: think “nuclear thermal rocket, but the reactor is a tiny annihilation hot spot.”

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]) [[Antimatter]]-catalyzed fission/fusion (ACMF)

Use: nanograms of antiprotons trigger micro-pellets (fissile or D-T/D-He³).
Pros: sips [[Antimatter]]; pulsed operation (Orion-mini vibe) without big bombs.
Isp: 10,000–100,000 s (design dependent).
Notes: your go-to for deep-space fast ships without carrying dangerous AM mass.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]) Beamed-core [[Antimatter]]

Use: proton–antiproton annihilation at (near) rest → lots of charged pions; magnetic nozzle “beams” them for thrust.
Pros: ultra-high Isp (exhaust a big fraction of c).
Cons: low thrust, brutal fields, heavy shielding; nozzle efficiency matters.
Isp: up to ~10⁶–10⁷ s (effective exhaust ~0.1–0.[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] c with losses).
Notes: interstellar sprint stages or probes; not a lifter.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]) Photon/γ-rocket (theoretical)

Use: rely on gammas from annihilation for thrust.
Reality: mirrors don’t reflect γ well → terrible coupling. Keep this for exotic corner cases, not mainline craft.

How much [[Antimatter]] (sane budgets)

Station-to-Mars era fast transfers (AM-thermal): micrograms total AM + tens of tons H₂ propellant → weeks-scale transits with multiple km/s margins.

Outer [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] fast couriers (ACMF): 10–100 µg AM to “spark” hundreds of pellets, propellant mass modest; Isp 20–60 km/s exhaust velocities (10⁴–[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]×10⁴ s).

Interstellar precursor (beamed-core): milligram-class AM (handled only at dedicated depots), wafer-scale payload, multi-year sprint/coast profiles.

Worldbuilding guardrail: ships never haul gram-class AM. That lives in heavily shielded depots with “dump blocks,” remote scuttles, and no line-of-sight to habitation.

Quick numbers & rules of thumb

Useful energy fraction: p–p̄ at rest → much energy goes to π⁰→γ and to neutrinos; usable with nozzle/thermalization is ~30–60% depending on design.

Radiation & safety (story-relevant, non-operational)

Prompt fields: gammas, fast pions → heavy shadow shields and nozzle biocon zones.

Activation: keep annihilation zones sacrificial and modular.

Controls: triple-redundant field monitors; AM inventory air-gaps; PFN-logged state of traps; strict “no-carry” through habitats.
