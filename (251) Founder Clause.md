# (251) Founder Clause

## What Happened

In Year 251 AL, Equilon state actors began pushing legal interpretations that positioned Amber Zohair as the last technically legitimate continuity body: revolutionary founder, Ark continuity figure, landfall witness, and living symbol of the original settlement compact.

For Amber and Emilia, the debate was not only political. It threatened the private life they had built across decades.

## What Resulted

- Amber became a constitutional instrument others wanted to claim.
- The Founder Clause debate exposed unresolved tension between survival legitimacy and state legitimacy.
- Emilia became central to Amber's private stability as public pressure returned.
- New Aurora became the symbolic arena for legitimacy.

## Long-Term Consequence

The Founder Clause created the political gathering that Atlovaia and the F.S.S.R. targeted in the First Strike.
