## Summary

Civil: Blac
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]![[Pasted image 20250830175131.png]]
A Supermassive Black Hole Manipulator is a megascale [[Infrastructure]] array built in orbit around a galactic-center black hole, designed to control, harvest, and weaponize the energies of an SMBH — accretion disk radiation, relativistic jets, spin energy, and even spacetime curvature itself. They are black crowns, cages of scripture forged around night itself.
Structure

Accretion Harness: Colossal magnetic funnels and Dyson-like nets capture and redirect the accretion disk’s plasma.

Jet Lenses: Quasar Lens arrays collimate and steer relativistic jets into usable beams or signals.

Ergosphere Couplers: Penrose-process harvesters draw rotational energy directly from the black hole.

Stability Mantles: Strange-matter anchors and [[Computronium]] overseers prevent collapse or runaway quasar ignition.

Functions

Civil:
– Provide nearly inexhaustible power for entire superclusters.
– Act as computation hubs: SMBHs can anchor [[Computronium]] strata, cooled by [[The Void]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Weaponize relativistic jets — sterilize star [[Systems]] with beams spanning kiloparsecs.
– Trigger quasar eruptions on demand, burning sectors into ash.
– Collapse spacetime locally as a denial weapon (a “black hymn” strike).

Religious:
– Serve as sacred thrones, mythologized as prisons or [[Temples]] of [[The Void]].
– Used as pilgrimage sites — places where mortals approach the Empress’s power, staring into the [[Abyss]].

Variants

Harvest Crowns: Civil/industrial harness arrays.

War Crowns: Weaponized quasar-control hubs.

Sacral Crowns: Forbidden reliquaries, worshiped more than used.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Economic: Anchor Spiralverse Arrays and [[Hypercivilization Shells]], stabilizing reality at galactic scales.

Religious: Known as the “Ebon Crowns” — myths say the Empress does not merely cage suns, she sits enthroned upon black holes.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ribbed spires the size of star [[Systems]] bracing against tidal forces.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, only a black void remains, haloed by impossible symmetry.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Accretion flows spiral in fractal mandalas, sculpted by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] harmonics.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The jets themselves blaze with glyph-light — entire galaxies see scripture scrawled across the dark.

Narrative Function

To rebels: Utter despair — what can defy an empire that bends galactic hearts into lanterns?

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic necessity and sacred demonstration — the crown upon galaxies.

To myth: Called the “Ebon Crowns” or the “Amber Thrones of Night”, said to be where the Empress sits when she contemplates eternity.
k Hole Manipulators are the anchors of civilization — the deepest wells turned into fonts of life.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: They are the final deterrent — galactic doomsday weapons and fleet-killers.

To stand near one (if such a phrase has meaning) is to [[Witness]] night caged in golden ribs, a hole in reality singing with hymns of [[Fire]] and [[Silence]].

To citizens: Awe and assurance — even the [[Abyss]] itself is ruled.
