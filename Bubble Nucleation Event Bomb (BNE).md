## Summary

[[Technology Portal]]
A Bubble Nucleation Event Bomb,
also known as a BNE Bomb, is a fictional weapon found in some science fiction works. It is a powerful explosive device that causes a localized explosion that creates a large, stable bubble in the fabric of space-[[Time]]. When the BNE Bomb explodes, it causes a rupture in the continuum, creating a spherical area of disrupted space-[[Time]]. This bubble can trap anything within its boundaries, including ships, planets, or even entire galaxies. Once caught in the bubble, it becomes nearly impossible to escape. The bubble walls are nearly impenetrable, and any attempts to pass through them can result in disastrous consequences. The BNE Bomb is often used by villains in stories as a way to trap their enemies or to cause havoc across vast [[Regions]] of space. It is a powerful tool for those who seek to control or destroy vast areas of space-[[Time]].
