# Story 22 - Skyview Day

## Viewpoint

Veyra Sol as an adult field interpreter helping teach children about orbit.

## Setting

Year 185 AL, settlement school observatory.

## Story Shape

Children are shown Harmina from above: storm belts, dark seas, settlement lights, orbital systems, and skywatch infrastructure. The lesson includes GalFed because hiding the secret clock from every generation has become impossible.

The children are fascinated instead of terrified. That frightens the adults more than fear would have.

## Harmina Element

Planetary identity expands from ground survival to orbital awareness.

## Character Movement

Veyra realizes the next generation will inherit GalFed preparation as curriculum, not secret dread.

## Future Payoff

Skyview Day becomes a bridge between exploration culture and planetary defense culture.
