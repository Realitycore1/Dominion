I. What the C-Vac Plague Is (and Is Not)

The C-Vac Plague is not an infection.

It is a runaway ecological cascade involving Chthonovermis vacuumalis—void worms—when normal population, migration, or energy-balance constraints collapse.

Think less “virus” and more:

A feedback loop where the universe’s circulatory system tears itself open.

⸻

II. Mechanism of the Plague

Normal State

Void worms:
	•	Migrate slowly along gravity gradients
	•	Redistribute excess mass, radiation, and spacetime stress
	•	Exist in extremely low densities

Plague State

Triggered when one or more conditions occur:
	1.	Excessive spacetime disruption
	•	Repeated FTL tears
	•	Megastructure failures
	•	High-energy weapons testing
	2.	Artificial energy wells
	•	Dyson swarms
	•	Dark-matter manipulation
	•	Failed containment of exotic matter
	3.	Population compression
	•	Worms converge instead of dispersing
	4.	Loss of migratory cues
	•	Navigation signals drowned out by artificial emissions

Result:

Void worms stop passing through regions
and begin dwelling in them.

⸻

III. Symptoms of a C-Vac Plague Zone

Observed in stages:

Phase I – Silent Accretion
	•	Increased “background distortion” readings
	•	Navigation drift
	•	Sensor ghosts
	•	Minor disappearances

Phase II – Structural Interference
	•	Worm paths intersect:
	•	Trade lanes
	•	Orbits
	•	Habitable zones
	•	Planets experience:
	•	Tidal anomalies
	•	Seismic resonance without tectonics
	•	Stations begin to shear or desync

Phase III – Topological Collapse
	•	Multiple Chthonovermis bodies overlap
	•	Spacetime becomes viscous
	•	Matter is:
	•	Redistributed
	•	Pulverized
	•	Unrecognizable
	•	Entire regions become uninhabitable void scars

At no point is there intent.
At no point is there communication.

⸻

IV. Why It’s Called a “Plague”

The term is deliberately anthropomorphic—and inaccurate—but it stuck because:
	•	It spreads along trade routes
	•	It follows human expansion
	•	It punishes density
	•	It renders regions taboo long after “activity” ceases

Early Dominion texts tried “C-Vac Event” and “Vermiform Cascade.”

Crews kept saying plague.

The name stayed.

⸻

V. Dominion Doctrine on the C-Vac Plague

Official stance:

“The C-Vac Plague is not an enemy to be defeated,
but a consequence to be avoided.”

Unspoken doctrine:

If a region develops C-Vac symptoms, it was already overbuilt.

Dominion response hierarchy:
	1.	Evacuation
	2.	Signal silence
	3.	Energy drawdown
	4.	Abandonment

There is no military solution.

⸻

VI. Cultural & Mythic Interpretations

Across human space:
	•	“The Worm Fever” – spacer slang
	•	“The Long Hunger” – frontier religions
	•	“The Punishment of Excess” – post-collapse philosophy
	•	“God’s Intestines” – heretical theology
	•	“The Price of FTL” – banned academic paper

Some cultures mark plague zones on star charts with black spirals and refuse to name the systems.
