## Summary

Thin to non
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Deep-Cryo Radiator Worlds are planetary bodies transformed into colossal cooling [[Systems]], their surfaces engineered to maximize emissivity and channel waste heat from Dyson shells, [[Computronium]] nodes, [[Ark Platforms]], and other [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].
They are, in effect, planet-sized heat sinks.
Surface Engineering

Blackbody Mantles: Surfaces coated with ultra-dark metamaterials, maximizing infrared radiation.

Cryogenic Mirrors: Layered frost-lattices directing heat upward into [[The Void]].

Fractured Topography: Designed for maximum surface area — ridges, spires, and fractal plains.

Subsurface [[Systems]]

Coolant Seas: Oceans of liquid helium, hydrogen, or engineered cryofluids absorbing heat cycles.

Pump Cathedrals: Atlas Pillar-like towers that draw waste heat into conduits and vent it outward.

Vaulted Cores: Buried [[Computronium]] banks that require extreme cooling.

Atmosphere

When atmosphere remains, it glows faintly as auroral veils, shimmering with waste-heat spectra.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civilizational [[Infrastructure]]: Radiator worlds keep [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] functional, preventing cascade-failures of heat.

Strategic Targets: Taking or sabotaging one can cripple entire star [[Systems]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Towering radiator spires like cathedral ribs, stretching kilometers skyward, glowing faintly red with heat.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the planet appears matte-black, an obsidian jewel of absolute darkness.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Vast fractal plains reflecting infrared like frozen oceans of glass.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Surfaces inscribed with glyphs visible only in thermal bands, prayers written in heat.

Seen from afar, a radiator world looks dead and black, until sensors pick up its true glow — a faint red ember, like a star that refuses to shine.
