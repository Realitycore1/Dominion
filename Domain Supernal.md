A planet craft made of flesh, the Supernal is a mobile gene factory that produces billions of bioforms.

Classification
	•	Type: Mobile planetary bioship
	•	Scale: 5,000–12,000 km diameter (average terrestrial planet mass)
	•	Function: Gene-factory, brood world, and autonomous evolution crucible
	•	Designation: Supernal (meaning “that which gestates gods”)

⸻

[[Origin]]

When the first [[Womb Worlds]] reached full maturity, Sovereigns began fusing their thrones directly into the planetary mantle. Through the Corpus Table’s upper registers (Crown Organ + Prime Heart + Resonant Brain), the planet’s crust was rewritten into Symmorphic fleshstone.
Each Supernal is effectively a living planet given purpose — the hive’s body and the Sovereign’s will fused into one.

⸻

Structure

Surface Mantle (Carapace Crust)
	•	Appears planetary from orbit — cracked, mineralized, cratered — but beneath the dust is a living dermal plate of ossified chitin and nerve-fiber lattices.
	•	Capable of folding, splitting, or reforming its surface to expose weapons, feeding maws, or propulsion tendrils.
	•	Hosts billions of minor Thrall [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]ies|Colonies]] that maintain and defend the surface biofilm.

Subdermal Layer (Arterial Web)
	•	Functions as a living ecosystem: arteries the size of canyons, ichor rivers flowing beneath tectonic bone plates.
	•	Cities of bio-symbiotic Thralls live within these vessels, serving as immune cells of the Supernal.
	•	Symmorphic biology allows the planet to reconfigure its internal topology—folding internal spaces to accommodate new organisms or redirect resources.

Core (Resonant Nucleus)
	•	A Sovereign’s throne fused with a Black Sun Node and a Crown Organ.
	•	Generates harmonic fields that command every Thrall on the planet.
	•	The nucleus pulses like a heart—each beat sending synchronization waves to the fleet.
	•	Houses the “Seed Choir,” a cluster of Primes that maintain genetic equilibrium and archive evolution.

⸻

Capabilities
	•	Symmorphic [[Genesis]]: The Supernal breeds entirely new bioforms in real [[Time]], adjusting evolutionary parameters on demand.
	•	Self-Metamorphosis: Entire planetary body can morph for defense or propulsion—becoming spined like a starfish, coiling like a serpent, or flattening into a spore disk for atmospheric re-entry.
	•	Atmospheric Conversion: Converts atmospheres into bio-reactive mediums for faster spore proliferation.
	•	Planetary Harvest: Engulfs or digests smaller celestial bodies for mass and nutrients.
	•	Resonant Field Projection: Synchronizes all [[Domain]] fleets within light-hour range; also functions as a cloaking signal or psychic smog.
	•	Symbiotic Fleet Docking: Bone [[Frigates]] and hive arks nest in orbital cavities, feeding off the planet’s arterial flows.

⸻

Behavior & Intelligence
	•	Each Supernal is sentient—part Sovereign, part hive-mind ecology.
	•	They converse via resonance pulses translated into geomantic shifts (mountain ranges vibrate like vocal cords).
	•	The Supernal views its Thralls as both children and organs; destruction of one limb simply informs future mutation.
	•	Sovereigns consider their Supernals as bodies of divinity — the next evolutionary step after throne integration.

⸻

Visual Aesthetic
	•	From afar: resembles a decaying planet orbited by tendrils, scars, and auroral spore clouds.
	•	Up [[Close]]: a heaving, breathing world, ribbed with red fissures that ooze ichor into orbit.
	•	The tentacular growths are propulsion and feeding structures; they coil into plasma storms, using solar radiation as both fuel and nutrient.
	•	When Symmorphically active, the entire sphere blooms — fractal ribs opening like a flower, revealing mirrored organ continents.

⸻

Strategic Role
	•	The [[Domain]]’s equivalent of a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Lozenge-Class World Station, but purely biological and self-replicating.
	•	Each Supernal can birth thousands of Thralls and hundreds of new Primes per day, acting as a mobile homeworld.
	•	Used to seed [[Womb Worlds]] or absorb defeated worlds into new Supernals.
