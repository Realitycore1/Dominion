## Details

An Orbital Shell is a type of spac
e station that is designed to resemble a hollow sphere with rings surrounding it. Like the [[Orbital Plate]], it would orbit around a planet or other celestial body, but would have a more spherical and complete shape than the plates. The Orbital Shell design provides a similar set of benefits to the [[Orbital Plate]], including the ability to leverage the gravitational pull of the planet for energy conservation and efficiency. However, the shell's spherical shape would offer additional advantages in terms of structural stability and ease of construction, as well as potentially greater flexibility in terms of size and scale. In some implementations, the shell could be constructed in multiple [[Layers]] or segments, each containing different types of facilities and equipment. This would allow for greater modularity and scalability, as new segments could be added as needed to accommodate increasing demands or [[Production]] requirements. The design challenges associated with an Orbital Shell are similar to those of the [[Orbital Plate]], but with additional considerations related to structural integrity and the management of centrifugal force within the structure. Nonetheless, Orbital Shells continue to be explored as a promising concept for future space-based industrial and commercial applications.
