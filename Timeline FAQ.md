# Timeline FAQ

This page helps readers understand how to approach dated history notes and timeline folders.

## How should I read the timeline?

Answer:
Start with [[History Portal]], then read broad overview pages before individual dated notes. For the Lithic Era, use the whole timeline and impact matrix before reading every event in order.

## What is the Lithic Era?

Answer:
The Lithic Era is an origins-era timeline folder. It organizes early historical divergence, foundational worldbuilding events, and long-range pacing for later civilization development.

Read next:
- [[Timeline Spine - Pleroma Archive]]
- [[Lithic Era Timeline Index|Lithic Era Timeline Index]]

## Do I need to read every dated note?

Answer:
No. Many dated notes function as anchors or placeholders. Readers should use indexes, matrices, and summaries first, then open individual dates when they need detail.

Read next:
- [[History Portal]]
- [[Timeline Spine - Pleroma Archive]]

## What does a dated note represent?

Answer:
A dated note represents a timeline anchor: an event, divergence, concept, polity, technological step, crisis, or cultural shift placed at a specific year for pacing and continuity.

Read next:
- [[Timeline FAQ]]

## Are all dated notes equally important?

Answer:
No. Some dates are major causal anchors, while others are supporting references, placeholders, or unresolved lore prompts.

Read next:
- [[Lithic Era World Impact Matrix|Lithic Era World Impact Matrix]]

## Are empty timeline pages broken?

Answer:
Not automatically. Some are unfinished, but their title and placement still show intended pacing. Empty pages should be treated as draft anchors unless marked otherwise.

Read next:
- [[Archive and Canon FAQ]]

## What are Lore Questions To Resolve?

Answer:
They are development prompts, not public canon answers. They identify points where the timeline needs a decision before the page can be treated as complete.

Read next:
- [[Archive and Canon FAQ]]

## What is the difference between an alternate timeline and a separate Verse?

Answer:
An alternate timeline is a branch or variation within a Verse. A separate Verse is a complete enclosed reality structure with its own boundary and continuity.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is the most important turning point in the Lithic Era?

Answer:
The Exchange is the major visible break in the current Lithic route because it divides pressure-building origin history from post-collapse recovery and reconstruction. The whole era, however, is paced as a chain: alternate foundations, protoscience, wetwire and political crises, the Exchange, reconstruction, Solar expansion, ansibles, and warp deployment.

Read next:
- [[Whole Timeline|Lithic Era Whole Timeline]]
- [[Lithic Era World Impact Matrix|Lithic Era World Impact Matrix]]

## How does the Lithic Era lead toward the Dominion?

Answer:
The Lithic Era establishes the conditions that later civilizations inherit: fractured political memory, post-Exchange survival, reconstruction networks, Solar colonization, interstellar systems, and warp capability. It does not fully explain the Dominion by itself; it gives the long origin pressure that makes later Dominion-scale civilization possible.
