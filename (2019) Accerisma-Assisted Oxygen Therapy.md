1. Maintains oxygenation
	•	Prevents immediate hypoxia
	•	Keeps the patient alive long enough for intervention

2. Delivers micro-aerosolized Accerisma
	•	Targets alveolar surfaces directly
	•	Disrupts Wetwire biofilm / integration seams
	•	Forces partial collapse of the infection structure

3. Induces controlled damage
	•	Denatures infected tissue
	•	Also damages healthy tissue
	•	Triggers aggressive scarring (fibrosis)

⸻

Outcome

Best case:
	•	Wirelung growth is suppressed or partially purged
	•	Patient survives the acute phase

Cost:
	•	permanent reduction in lung capacity
	•	chronic respiratory limitation
	•	long-term dependence on support systems

Worst case:
	•	patient dies during treatment
	•	or survives with catastrophic lung failure
