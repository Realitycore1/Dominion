1. Cryonics is not about bodies. It’s about data.

A human isn’t the flesh; it’s the pattern encoded in a ~100 billion-neuron network with specific microtubule and synaptic states.

Cryonics tries to:
	•	halt entropy
	•	preserve that pattern
	•	keep information loss minimal
	•	prevent irreversible biochemical decay

If the pattern survives, the person survives.
If the pattern is scrambled, they die, even if the body looks “fine.”

Cryonics is fundamentally early-stage connectome preservation, not “ice sleep.”

⸻

2. The real enemy is ice, not cold.

Free water expands → tears cells → shreds membranes → disrupts synapses.

This is why:
	•	freezing is fatal
	•	vitrification (glass-like solidification) is the only viable method
	•	antifreeze cryoprotectants are essential

Modern cryonics uses chemicals like:
	•	DMSO
	•	glycerol
	•	ethylene glycol

to replace water and prevent crystal formation.

But even vitrified, the brain is still suffering thermal stress and protein damage.
