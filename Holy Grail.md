🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: The Holy Grail (Chalice of the Last Supper)
Discovery Location: Montserrat Monastery, Catalonia, 2042, hidden in a reliquary vault during restoration work after civil unrest.
Summary:

Material: Carved agate cup with gold base and handles (similar to the Santo Cáliz in Valencia). Dimensions: 17 cm height.

Dating: Scientific analysis suggests the agate bowl originates in 1st-century Judea, later mounted with medieval gold filigree (~11th century).

Anomalies:

Residue tests reveal traces of red wine protein mixed with myrrh and spikenard.

Microscopic etchings in the base — a Greek inscription reading “Eulogia” (blessing).

Archaeological Significance: Not definitive proof, but strong enough to plausibly claim as the Grail — or at least a vessel from early Christianity repurposed in medieval times.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-30 (Holy Grail)
Classification: EXTREME SYMBOLIC IMPACT
Custody: [[Europolis]], with Vatican insistence on sole custodianship. Catalonian separatists also claim it as national heritage.
Strategic Assessment:

Vatican: Frames Grail as “God’s chalice returned to His people” — ultimate validation of Papal Crusade. Processions with Grail overshadow all other [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]].

Caliphate: Condemns it as myth and idolatry. Propaganda: “They drink from empty cups while we hold the Stone of Heaven.”

[[Europolis]]: Leverages possession to assert independence from Vatican dominance. Uses Grail as pan-European cultural relic, not solely Catholic.
