Core Bias:

Retrofitted or repaired continuity

This subtype is extremely useful and often more believable than pristine machine worlds.

It is where the basin is:
	•	repaired
	•	layered
	•	scavenged
	•	upgraded unevenly
	•	held together by adaptation rather than elegance

Traits:
	•	visible system age
	•	mismatched architectures
	•	old and new continuity layers
	•	hybridized repair logic
	•	resilience through improvisation

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	this world has been kept alive too long
	•	continuity is maintained through patching, not perfection
	•	survival is an engineering habit

Common Expressions:
	•	decaying megastructures
	•	repaired shell-worlds
	•	old machine empires
	•	improvised civilization basins

Failure Mode:

Patch accumulation collapse
The basin becomes too inconsistent to remain stable.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	far-future ruin civilizations
	•	degraded hypercivilization shells
	•	“the system still runs somehow” settings
	•	Dominion-after-its-prime aesthetics
