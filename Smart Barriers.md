Smart Barriers consist of 100 ft tall metallic beams spaced 1 ft from each other and are attached to metallic beams vertical of those in front and are attached by motors. The barrier itself can adjust to any terrain and can expand or contract when ordered. The barrier can be used if the Emancipation grid cannot cover the area.
