# Luna Colonial Committee

Established with Luna’s first permanent colonial charter in 3008, the Lunar Colonial Committee governs domicile, schools, clinics, graves, inheritance, elections, and local utilities. The Colonial Union initially retains defense, interworld transport, and trade.

The Committee develops beside Fortress Luna: polar shipyards, deep command centers, missile fields, maglev tunnels, shelters, mines, and mass drivers. Its authority over the civilian interface expands through the Fortress Compact.

After the Sea of Tranquility Corporate Conflict reveals that local government cannot control weapons embedded in Lunar industry, the Committee leads the autonomy settlement that creates the [[The Cynthia Protectorate|Cynthia Protectorate]] in 3180. Cynthia assumes domestic courts, utilities, security, and domicile while remaining beneath the Colonial Union defense umbrella.

For the full canonical history through the *Deliverance* launch, see [[Luna Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
