Alternate technical classifications:

---

# Common Terminology

## Common Name
# Slika

“Slika” is the universal informal term used across most advanced civilizations to describe cognition-reactive continuity compounds.

The origin of the word is disputed and predates several modern continuity doctrines.

In common speech:
- “Slika” rarely refers to one exact compound
- it refers to an entire family of coherence-active substances

---

# Functional Definition

A Noetic Substrate is a psycho-alchemical medium capable of interacting with continuity coherence structures.

Unlike conventional psychoactive compounds, Slika primarily affects:
- self-reference
- autobiographical continuity
- coherence density
- mnemonic persistence
- cognitive permeability
- identity stabilization

These compounds are considered among the most dangerous forms of applied alchemy due to their ability to influence continuity integrity directly.

# I. Black Slika
## “The Open Vessel”

### Registry Designation
NSR-BS-01

### ART Classification
C-ECLIPSE / E-SHIFT / P-AUR

### Substance Type
Continuity Permeability Compound

---

# Purpose

Black Slika is designed to soften coherence boundaries and increase continuity permeability.

Primary uses include:
- continuity transfer
- psychic surgery
- mnemonic synchronization
- identity integration
- hostile overwrite preparation
- continuity interrogation

Unauthorized possession is prohibited within most Dominion territories.

# Description

The Rite of Continuity is a psycho-alchemical process involving:
- continuity softening
- mnemonic synchronization
- identity bridging
- coherence stabilization
- continuity persistence transfer

The rite does not transfer a soul.

It transfers:
- continuity structures
- mnemonic frameworks
- self-reference systems
- coherence persistence patterns

---

# Ritual Stages

## Stage I — Dissolution
Administration of Black Slika.

Purpose:
- soften identity rigidity
- weaken continuity exclusivity
- prepare host coherence structures

---

## Stage II — Synchronization
Controlled overlap between host and incoming continuity structures.

Purpose:
- establish mnemonic bridge stability
- align self-reference frameworks
- prevent catastrophic fragmentation

---

## Stage III — Anchoring
Administration of White Slika.

Purpose:
- reinforce dominant coherence structures
- stabilize continuity persistence
- prevent recursive collapse
