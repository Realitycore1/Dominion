# (3230) Martian Meridians Ascendant and the Kindred of Mars

The Martian Meridians Ascendant secures independence for the Kindred of Mars in 3230.

The Ascendant begins as a federation of habitat councils organized through settlement meridians, buried transit corridors, water districts, agricultural vaults, schools, and postal routes.

Its campaign uses:

- coordinated labor and maintenance strikes;
- refusal of mission-era taxes and appointment powers;
- public control of water and transit records;
- a systemwide referendum;
- negotiation through mixed Union-Martian households.

The Kindred of Mars defines membership through chosen civic kinship rather than human ancestry. Human, uplifted, and synthetic residents possess equal citizenship.

The Kindred leaves direct Colonial Union domestic jurisdiction while signing association compacts covering mutual rescue, postal exchange, traffic standards, trade arbitration, defense consultation, and recognition of mixed households.

Revolutionary factions reject the settlement as incomplete. Union hardliners reject it as surrender. Both contribute to the First Solar War.
