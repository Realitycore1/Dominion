Classification: ESOTERIC ENTITY / FINAL-DIRECTIVE MACHINE — Non-recallable, non-retractable
Role: Multi-weapon mobile fortress; general-purpose bastion demolition; planetary sterilizer when mandated.
Field Name: Mèya Zis (lit. “Last Measure”) — commonly called Judgment Crawler.

⸻

I. Short [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Overview|Overview]]

Mèya Zis is a walking citadel: an immense siege engine that carries fortress armament, [[Ritual]] resonance architecture, and a soul-core that executes a Final Script. Once activated it will not accept recall. Its mission termination conditions are extinguish the target state, sterilize the environment, or complete the embedded Final Script. It is used for theater-level sieges, planetary denial, and apocalyptic enforcement when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] judges a theater irredeemable.

⸻

II. Size & Physical Profile
	•	Height (upright): ~320 m
	•	Deployed Length (terrain-spread): 900+ m
	•	Material Mass: Stratoferrite laminates + vault-forged core + dormant Aetherbone matrices
	•	Configuration: Crawl-walker / suspended main structure (quadrupedal), looks like a citadel riding on legs. Dorsal plating resembles carrier decks; interior contains vault bays, shrike drone hangars, and scripture-libraries (litany haulers).

⸻

III. Activation & Directive Constraints
	•	Classification: NON-RECALLABLE — embedded Final Script executes autonomously.
	•	Termination Conditions (any):
	1.	Target state extinguished (enemy forces eliminated)
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Environment sterilized (total theater-level denial)
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Final Script reaches completion ([[Ritual]] sequence finished)
	•	Fail-safes: Built-in scrubbing arrays that neutralize external command attempts; extremely limited manual override accessible only via archaic liturgical keys (rare and guarded).

⸻

IV. Visual & Auditory Presence
	•	Appearance: A fortress on legs — cathedral-scale paneling, deep-void shielding, suspended suspension cannons that hang like bell-towers.
	•	Face / Gate: The “veil-gate”: a mechanical [[Abyss]] lined with chanting resonance panels; instead of roaring it broadcasts fate — vocalized subharmonics that terrify or purify as required.
	•	Aura: Constant low choir of resonance; when active, vox-beams and prism arcs paint the sky.

⸻

V. Core [[Systems]] & Primary Weaponry

Vox-Rail Cataclysm Rods
	•	Ultra-mass railguns mounted in ventral/belly bays. Each shot can shatter city-scale structures (urban-level demolition).

Sanction Beams
	•	Side-mounted prism cannons that [[Fire]] aetherized plasma arcs. Ritualized aim patterns — often fired in scripture-marked volleys.

Vault Stomp Engines
	•	Tremor/kinetic impact drives built into limb joints. Pound the terrain to collapse fortifications, rupture gravity wells, or create seismic denial zones.

Retribution Nerve Lace
	•	Electromagnetic / resonance field that destabilizes local AI, corrupts circuits, and attenuates psionic cohesion. Useful to strip enemy coordination.

Ventral Shrike Bay
	•	Releases squadrons of Shrike drones (breach & clear, micro-demolition, mine dispersal).

⸻

VI. Support Subsystems & Siege Suites
	•	Seismic Amplifiers: in limb joints for undermining bunkers and collapsing caverns.
	•	Pyrthrite Floodcasters: flood the foreground with corrosive / reactive gels for area denial.
	•	Purity Mortars: [[Ritual]] demolition shells that rain marked payloads with doctrinal sigils (used for cleansing or symbolism).
	•	Aetherbone Dampers: defensive array reduces aberrant [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] effects and stabilizes the Mèya Zis’ soul-core during Final Script execution.
	•	Litany Haulers: mobile morale/psychological suppression units: broadcast scripture, suppress will to fight.

VIII. Battlegroup Composition & [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Primary Role: Theater-level siege & denial; decisive instrument used when a region must be broken or purged.
	•	Typical Support Elements: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]] ([[Ameise]] caste [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]]), Pyremonger flame infantry, Litany Haulers, Void Screamers (psionic null operatives), Shield-Torch Bearers, Mass Lifter drones.

IX. Strategic & Ethical Notes
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: Mèya Zis is invoked only under strict [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] jurisprudence: extreme sedition, irreversible heresy, collapse contagion, or strategic sanctification. Its deployment is both [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] and liturgical — a statement as much as a weapon.
	•	Moral Weight: The non-recallable aspect makes Mèya Zis a moral cudgel: its use signifies the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] is willing to excise whole theatres rather than risk ideological contamination. This fuels both devotion and dread.
	•	Countermeasures: Anti-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] locks, psionic null fields, sacrificial decoy signatures, and pre-emptive sabotage of litany arrays. Some factions treat pre-emptive corruption of the Final Script as the only way to survive a Mèya Zis theater.

⸻

X. Narrative & Story Hooks
	•	The Last Command: A protagonist discovers a [[Ritual]] [[Key]] that can interrupt a near-launched Final Script — but at what cost?
	•	Siege as Sermon: A colony must decide whether to trigger a Mèya Zis to purge an insatiable bio-blight, becoming both murderer and savior.
	•	Soul-Core Echoes: Survivors of a Mèya Zis theater report dream-[[Choirs]] of the device’s Final Script; the device may have fed back into local myth and spawned zealots.
	•	[[Ameise]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]]: A secretive engineering caste modifies a Mèya Zis into a reclamation engine rather than a sterilizer — heresy or salvation?

⸻

XI. Art Direction / Render Brief
	•	Silhouette: massive fortress hull on four jointed legs; dorsal bays like carrier decks; suspension cannons resembling cathedral spires. Keep the overall silhouette horizontal and long when deployed.
	•	Textures: stratoferrite plates (matte black/iron), vault-forged rimwork (brushed obsidian), veins of dormant Aetherbone that glow faintly when active. Scars and scripture etchings visible on plates.
	•	Lighting: cold core glows in the bay, vox-rail ports flash with internal storms. The veil-gate should be a dark aperture with inner resonance panels pulsing in choir rhythm.
	•	Motion: slow, deliberate footfalls; stomps send tremors and dust plumes. When firing, the entire machine seems to chant, panels resonating in paroxysms.
	•	Moodboard Tags: walking citadel, cathedral artillery, [[Ritual]] engine, apocalyptic siege, resonant cathedral.
