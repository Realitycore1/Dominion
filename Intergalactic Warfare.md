Intergalactic Warfare — Quick Reference (one page)

1 — Strategic goals (the constants)
	•	Control mobility & access (gates/[[Relays]]).
	•	Cripple opponent industry & logistics.
	•	Achieve intelligence superiority (see-first, decide-first).
	•	Build resilience (redundancy, distributed industry).
	•	Enforce strategic deterrence where possible.

2 — Force multipliers (pick 2–3 per [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]])
	•	Gate/relay control — hold or deny [[Horizon Arc Gate|Horizon Arc Gates]].
	•	Distributed nanofabrication / mobile shipyards.
	•	Autonomous/proliferating swarm fleets (cheap, expendable).
	•	Nonlocal sensors / quantum scouts.
	•	Topological weaponry (metric disturbers, gravitic lattices).
	•	Memetic & cognitive ops to fracture command cohesion.
	•	Hidden sanctuaries & [[Production]] — e.g., [[Void Cradles]] and Lozenge caches.

3 — High-impact technologies (short list)
	•	Stabilized Topological Gates & Relay Arrays.
	•	Self-replicating micro-fabricators & swarms.
	•	Relativistic impactors & mass-driver batteries.
	•	Gravitic field projectors / metric distorters.
	•	Quantum/axiomatic authentication for command channels.
	•	[[Aetheric]] noise generators (for settings with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]] storms).
	•	Autonomous avenger [[Systems]] (survivable automated retorsion).

4 — Campaign patterns & counters
	•	Chokepoint control → Counter: dispersed bypass [[Relays]], black-market arcs.
	•	Logistics strikes → Counter: mobile fabrication, mobile caches.
	•	Swarm saturation → Counter: layered area defenses, intelligent filtering AIs.
	•	Sensor deception → Counter: sensor diversity, nonlocal sensing, AI pattern detection.
	•	Memetic attack → Counter: offline authentication, [[Human]]-in-loop checks.

5 — Defensive hardening checklist (quick)
	•	Disperse [[Production]] into many small/mobile nodes.
	•	Harden command/authentication (multi-channel + analog fallbacks).
	•	Layered defences (point / area / phase / metric).
	•	Obfuscate signatures and vary transit patterns.
	•	Keep [[Human]] oversight for critical escalation paths.
	•	Maintain civil caches: food, med, replicator seeds, spare cores.

6 — Tactical checkboxes (op campaign)
	•	Identify and map chokepoints (gates/[[Relays]]).
	•	Prioritize critical industry nodes (fabricators, fuel, spares).
	•	Deny enemy sensor accuracy (deception + jamming).
	•	Route swarm [[Production]] to forward nodes.
	•	Seed proxy actors and covert ops where politics forbid overt war.
	•	Prepare contingency: safe-houses in [[Void Cradles]] / Lozenge stations.
	•	Set up autonomous retaliation triggers (with strict constraints).
Ex: 
Faction: House Zohair Expeditionary Wing
Strategic priority: Industry (replace & outproduce)
Force multipliers:
  - Mobile nanofabricators (fleet-as-factory)
  - Covert relay network (black-market arcs)
  - Autonomous escort swarms for convoy defense
Unique countermeasures: Hidden Lozenge caches for resupply; memetic inoculation program for command AIs.
Political limit: No use of population-scale biochemical terraforming.
Logistical weakness: Heavy reliance on rare isotope mines at Ferox Node-3.
Tactical posture: Attrition + industrial strike
Reserve & recovery: Two Lozenge-class [[World Stations]] with emergency fabrication bays.
