I. Core Reframe (The Big Correction)

Sky Dragons do not originate on planets.
They are vacuum-native macro-organisms whose life cycle intersects with planetary environments just enough to confuse observers.

They are:
	•	Born in deep space
	•	Adapted to microgravity and radiation
	•	Incapable of sustained planetary life
	•	Frequently seen near atmospheres, storms, or gravity wells

Planets are feeding grounds, rest zones, or navigational landmarks—not habitats.

The mistake is anthropocentric.

⸻

II. Scientific Classification

Draco astravagus

(“Wandering star-dragon”)
	•	Domain: Non-Terran Vacuum Biota
	•	Substrate: Baryonic matter (exotic-biological composites)
	•	Native Environment: Interplanetary / interstellar space
	•	True Habitat: Dust clouds, radiation belts, heliospheres
	•	Planetary Status: Incidental / hazardous

They are not atmospheric organisms.
They merely survive atmospheres briefly.

⸻

III. Why People Think They’re Planetary

Because humans keep seeing them at the wrong moment.

Sky Dragons are most visible when they:
	•	Skim magnetospheres
	•	Feed on ionized upper atmospheres
	•	Pass through auroral zones
	•	Dive into storm layers to shed heat or parasites
	•	Use gravity wells for slingshot navigation

Ancient observers see:
	•	A colossal serpent descending from the sky
	•	Storms forming around it
	•	Cities destroyed afterward
	•	Then it leaves

Conclusion: storm god / dragon / punishment from heaven.

Reality: a migratory organism refueling and correcting course.

⸻

IV. True Physiology (Space First, Always)

Structural Design
	•	Dense, radiation-hardened dermal layers
	•	Internal voids for thermal expansion
	•	Crystalline-organic bone analogs
	•	Electromagnetic sensory ridges

Metabolism
	•	Feeds on:
	•	Solar wind
	•	Ionized gases
	•	Dust clouds
	•	Magnetospheric plasma
	•	Planets provide concentrated snacks, not sustenance

Locomotion
	•	Reaction-mass expulsion (ionized particles)
	•	Magneto-gravitic field surfing
	•	Solar radiation pressure manipulation

Wings, when present, are thermal radiators and control surfaces, not for lift.

⸻

V. Planetary Interaction = Near-Death Experience

From the Sky Dragon’s perspective:
	•	Gravity is oppressive
	•	Dense air causes overheating
	•	Terrain damages external membranes
	•	Microbes are invasive contaminants

They do not like planets.

They endure them.

Many Sky Dragons die because they misjudge gravity wells.

Those deaths become legends.

⸻

VI. Intelligence & Awareness

Sky Dragons are non-sapient but highly aware.

Think:
	•	Migratory intelligence
	•	Pattern memory across centuries
	•	Stellar navigation instincts
	•	Environmental threat avoidance

They do not plan civilizations’ destruction.
They barely notice civilizations at all.

A city flattened by one is collateral damage—like ants under a falling tree.
