# (2750) First off-world colonies established

## Canonical Clarification

The 2750 marker refers to the first legally recognized off-world work communities and orbital settlements.

These communities contain households and local civic institutions but remain contract-dependent and cannot yet sustain permanent multi-generational settlement without Earth.

They are called colonies in contemporary records. Later Lunar law disputes that label and reserves permanent colonization for communities with self-government, schools, clinics, civil records, inheritance, and the right to remain after employment.
