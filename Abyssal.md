Core Bias:

Extreme depth and pressure

This is one of the most important Ocean subtypes for your setting.

Reality becomes stranger, denser, and more hostile the deeper you go.

Traits:
	•	hidden lower strata
	•	crushing environmental or metaphysical pressure
	•	strange deep-adapted intelligences
	•	ancient things below perception
	•	deep-time concealment

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	there is always something deeper
	•	the bottom is never truly known
	•	reality becomes more alien with descent

Common Expressions:
	•	deep consciousness zones
	•	hidden abyssal civilizations
	•	pressure-adapted megafauna or entities
	•	buried cosmic archives
	•	Yondor / Abyss analog structures

Failure Mode:

Pressure singularity
Depth becomes too intense for continuity to survive.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	horror cosmology
	•	hidden ancient intelligences
	•	under-reality structures
	•	abyssal civilizations
