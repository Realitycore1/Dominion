Classification

Fallen Angelic Order / Post-Celestial Sentients

Origin

Pre-Fall angelic observers assigned to monitor mortal development. Unlike demons, the Grigori did not rebel out of hatred or ambition—they refused withdrawal. When commanded to abandon the world, they stayed.

That single act severed them from Aravoth.

⸻

PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

Grigori retain an angelic baseline but show structural decay and adaptive mutation:
	•	Tall, humanoid silhouettes (2.5–3m)
	•	Wings present but altered: molted feathers, asymmetrical growth, or bone-exposed frames
	•	Skin ranges from pale alabaster to ash-grey, often cracked or scarred with sigil burns
	•	Eyes unnervingly intelligent—gold dulled to amber, blue to steel, sometimes ringed with black
	•	Halos, if present, are broken phenomena: fragmented light rings, hovering glyphs, or faint gravitational distortions
	•	Some exhibit extra sensory organs (vestigial eyes, rune-scars, auditory fronds) from prolonged exposure to the material world

They look angelic, but no longer pristine.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & ECOLOGY

Grigori are isolationist observers, not conquerors.
	•	Prefer ruins, high places, deserts, ashlands, or abandoned cathedrals
	•	Rarely gather in numbers greater than three
	•	Intervene only when patterns repeat catastrophically (mass extinction events, curse cascades)
	•	Do not rule, do not preach, do not hunt unless threatened

They are watchers still—just without permission.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE & CULTURE

Highly sapient. Deeply contemplative.
	•	Think in systems, causality, and probability, not morality
	•	Keep records: stone carvings, light-etched glyphs, memory reliquaries
	•	Some act as cryptic guides; others as cold experimenters
	•	View mortals as variables, not children

They are capable of empathy—but choose restraint.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS

This is where Grigori become truly dangerous.
	•	Grace Residue: still present, but thin and unstable
	•	No Malice Core: unlike demons, they are not fueled by hatred
	•	Aether-Adaptive: bodies and minds slowly rewrite themselves to local metaphysics
	•	Soul Density: extremely high—feeding on a Grigori (vampirically or ritually) causes catastrophic mutation

They are living anomalies—Grace that no longer knows its place.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Regional → Catastrophic (context dependent)

A single Grigori is rarely hostile.
But:
	•	Killing one releases uncontrolled Grace shockwaves
	•	Corrupting one creates something far worse than a demon
	•	Studying one too closely risks metaphysical contamination

Aravoth considers them heresies that still remember Heaven.
Sheol-Gehenna considers them unfinished prey.

⸻

THEMATIC ROLE

Grigori embody:
	•	Disobedience without malice
	•	Knowledge without guidance
	•	Immortality without belonging

They are proof that not all falls are betrayals
