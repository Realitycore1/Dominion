The [[Eprea Hindutva]]’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Military/Technology|Technology]] reflects its caste divisions—who may build, who may wield, and who must suffer.
	•	Caste Stratification in Tech:
	•	Brahmic control advanced sciences (FTL navigation, biogenetics, orbital AI oversight). Access to knowledge is restricted by caste [[law]].
	•	Kshatriya receive cutting-edge weapons, armor, and cybernetics, optimized for dominance and spectacle.
	•	Vaishya handle industrial automation, keeping factories running, but are forbidden from tampering with high [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] or spiritual tech.
	•	Shudra labor with rugged, obsolete tools designed to last but never empower.
	•	Dalit are deliberately given unsafe, jury-rigged equipment for disposable tasks (e.g., mining plasma vents without shielding).
	•	Tech as Control:
	•	Biometric Caste Tags are implanted at birth—locking citizens into jobs, access zones, even oxygen rations.
	•	Surveillance Drones patrol streets, watching caste boundaries with machine precision.
	•	Purity Locks ensure tech cannot be operated outside one’s caste; a Dalit touching a Brahmic console might trigger alarms.
	•	Industrial Focus:
	•	Orbital shipyards churn out ritualized warships—designed not just for battle but for parade. Gilded prows and tiered decks mimic caste pyramids in space.
	•	Agricultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Military/Technology|Technology]] is advanced but segregated: lush gardens for Brahmic palaces, nutrient gruel processors for Shudra barracks.

Artistic Expression
Art in [[Eprea Hindutva]] is not free—it is propaganda made beautiful, enforcing hierarchy at every level.
	•	Visual Arts:
	•	Hierarchical Geometry: Triangles, pyramids, concentric tiers dominate murals and architecture.
	•	Sacral Ornamentation: Brahmic [[Temples]] gleam with gold leaf and fractal mandalas, while Shudra districts are plain concrete blocks.
	•	Dalit Graffiti: Forbidden but persistent—crude murals of broken pyramids, hidden in tunnels and airshafts.
	•	Music & Performance:
	•	State-sanctioned music is grand and layered—choral hymns and temple bells rising tier by tier, voices of lower castes always subordinated under Brahmic solos.
	•	Drum Processions mark Kshatriya marches, their rhythms echoing across cities to remind the lower castes of their place.
	•	Underground ballads circulate among Shudra and Dalits, sung in coded language about escape and rebellion.
	•	Architecture:
	•	Cities are concentric pyramids in stone and steel: [[Temples]] on the heights, fortresses in the middle, labor quarters sprawling at the edges.
	•	Space habitats mirror this—tiered orbital stations where caste dictates altitude and access.
	•	Public squares always feature Flames of Purity, ceremonial fires where offerings are burned in honor of caste and Pradhān.
	•	Symbols & Motifs:
	•	The Pyramid: hierarchy eternal.
	•	The Flame: suffering below fuels brilliance above.
	•	The Sigil Brand: the body itself marked as both tool and art.
