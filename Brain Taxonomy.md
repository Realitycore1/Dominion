[[Brain Taxonomy]]
[[Physics]]
Brain [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]] refers to the classification of different types of [[Artificial]] intelligence based on their capabilities, structures, and functions. It is an attempt to categorize AI [[Systems]] and to provide a common framework for understanding and discussing them. The brain [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]] framework has evolved over [[Time]], with different models and classifications being proposed by various researchers and experts in the field. However, there are some common elements that are typically included in most brain taxonomies. One of the most commonly used taxonomies is the four-level [[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]], which classifies AI [[Systems]] into four main categories: weak, narrow, strong, and superintelligent. The weak AI category includes [[Systems]] that are limited in their ability to understand and interact with the world around them. These [[Systems]] are typically designed for specific tasks and are not capable of complex problem-solving or adaptability. The narrow AI category includes [[Systems]] that are capable of solving a specific type of problem or task, but are not as general-purpose as the strong AI category. Narrow AI [[Systems]] may have specialized knowledge or skills that allow them to perform their tasks more effectively. The strong AI category includes [[Systems]] that are capable of complex problem-solving and are capable of making decisions based on their own reasoning and understanding of the world around them. Strong AI [[Systems]] can also learn and adapt over [[Time]]. The superintelligent AI category includes [[Systems]] that are far beyond the capabilities of existing strong AI [[Systems]]. These [[Systems]] are highly advanced and may be capable of generating their own consciousness or exhibiting sentience.
