Classification: Fey & Otherworldly Courts
Subtype: Entropic Fey Sovereigns / Predators of Excess
Status: Active, Realm-Bound, Hostile-Permeable

⸻

Core Description

The Unseelie are a collective of fey courts embodying decay, hunger, cruelty, winter, and inevitable loss. Where the Seelie cultivate and prune, the Unseelie strip, starve, and cull. They are not chaos incarnate—they are selective annihilation.

Their forms are unsettling rather than monstrous:
	•	gaunt, elongated nobles,
	•	antlered or skeletal silhouettes,
	•	frostbitten wings,
	•	skin like bark, bone, or shadowed ice,
	•	eyes glowing with cold amusement or predatory patience.

They do not hide their nature.
They glory in honesty through harm.

⸻

Ontological Nature

The Unseelie arise where Vital Expanse collapses into scarcity and where Oneiric symbolism tilts toward fear and inevitability.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Vital Expanse (degenerative phase)
	•	Secondary Influence: Oneiric (nightmares, dread), Malice-adjacent resonance
	•	Realm Interface: Dream-Wastes, Bleeding Wilds (predatory zones), Shadow Realms

They do not exist to destroy life indiscriminately.
They exist to ensure nothing grows without consequence.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Unseelie cognition is razor-sharp and cruelly precise.

They understand:
	•	weakness,
	•	inevitability,
	•	debt through suffering,
	•	how long something should have lived.

They do not care about:
	•	fairness,
	•	consent,
	•	proportionality as mortals define it.

An Unseelie act is never random.
It is perfectly timed.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

The Unseelie:
	•	hunt overgrown ecosystems,
	•	break civilizations swollen beyond sustainability,
	•	harvest fear, despair, and desperation as resources.

They prefer:
	•	famine over fire,
	•	cold over flame,
	•	rot over explosion.

Where the Seelie intervene rarely, the Unseelie intervene quietly and often, leaving worlds weakened long before collapse becomes visible.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Unseelie bargains are predatory traps.

They offer:
	•	survival at the cost of others,
	•	power through cruelty,
	•	certainty instead of hope.

They never lie—but they never explain the full cost.

Mortals who deal with Unseelie often:
	•	survive catastrophes that kill everyone else,
	•	become monsters themselves,
	•	or inherit curses that persist for generations.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Bound by the same ancient compacts as the Seelie
	•	Cannot act freely in zones saturated with Grace
	•	Lose cohesion in Null-dominant regions
	•	Can be repelled by collective resistance rather than heroism

The Unseelie fear unity more than weapons.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Seelie: Necessary opposites; eternal political tension
	•	Demons: Occasional allies, often rivals
	•	Angels: Philosophical enemies; Unseelie see Grace as stagnation
	•	Kami: Respected but hunted when ecosystems rot
	•	Vampires & Predators: Sometimes tolerated as tools

Unseelie do not rule hells.
They create conditions where hell becomes inevitable.

⸻

Courts & Variants
	•	Court of Winter: Cold, silence, preservation through death
	•	Court of Hunger: Famine, predation, desperation
	•	Court of Thorns: Pain, punishment, correction
	•	Court of Night: Fear, secrecy, inevitability

Courts cooperate when collapse is required.
