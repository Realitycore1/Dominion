A Soviet cell with six “controllers” who each run a sub cell of agents with various tasks overseen by the controllers. The Soviet spy ring, known as the Red Fist, was a highly sophisticated and secretive organization that had been infiltrated into the United States during the Cold War. Its goal was to transform the United States into a communist regime within a century. The Red Fist was led by a group of six primary spies, each with their own unique skill set and expertise. These six primary spies were responsible for overseeing the activities of smaller cells, each of which had a specific sub objective to ensure the transformation of the United States. Together, these six primary spies formed a tight-knit and highly disciplined organization that was dedicated to achieving its ultimate goal: transforming the United States into a communist regime within a century. Their methods were ruthless and effective, and they were willing to do whatever it took to achieve their goals, even if it meant sacrificing their own lives.
