# Uranus Colonial Committee

The Uranus Colonial Committee coordinates orbital yards, moon tunnels, cryogenic industry, rescue, communications, and the containment chain carrying Neptunian antimatter toward the rest of Sol. The current canon places its mature development during 3300–3425 without assigning one exact founding year.

Uranian industry specializes in layered containment, cryogenic handling, known-fracture materials, inspection access, sacrificial failure paths, and reserves measured against years-long replacement intervals.

The Committee’s political unit is safety margin: how much time remains between detecting a fault and losing the ability to answer it. Heat, vibration, maintenance history, and local worker review therefore become civic concerns rather than private industrial data.

Uranian settlements retain separate moon councils and reject automatic subordination to Jupiter or Saturn. They join the Outer Systems Compact through dependency and consent rather than a single “Outer Sphere” identity.

For the system’s culture, role in the Second Solar War, and contribution to the *Deliverance*, see [[Uranian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
