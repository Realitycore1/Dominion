# Chapter IV - Whispers in the Dark

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Secret factions exploit exhaustion after the silent dawn.

## Event Role

- Intelligence leaks, survival cults, hardliner cells, and foreign-contact panic circulate in damaged cities.
- Rumors claim the new state is a prison, a GalFed surrender plan, or an Equilon restoration under another name.
- Some rumors are false. Some are partly true.
- Public trust weakens before the provisional council can stabilize the peace.

## Consequence

The next phase of conflict begins in whispers before it returns to weapons.
