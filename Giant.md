1. Common Giants
	•	Definition: The baseline—massive humanoids towering over men.
	•	Traits: Immense size and strength, tribal or solitary lives.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]]’s struggle against overwhelming power.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Hill Giants
	•	Definition: Crude, gluttonous giants of lowlands and hills.
	•	Traits: Massive stomachs, dull minds, strength through bulk.
	•	Symbolism: Excess and indulgence made monstrous.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Stone Giants
	•	Definition: Giants of mountains and caves, carved like the rock they inhabit.
	•	Traits: Grey, stony skin, affinity with [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] and stone.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance, patience, permanence.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Frost Giants
	•	Definition: Giants of frozen lands, tied to Norse Jötnar [[Archetypes]].
	•	Traits: Blue or pale skin, affinity for cold and ice weaponry.
	•	Symbolism: The merciless cruelty of winter.

⸻

5. [[Fire]] Giants
	•	Definition: Black-skinned, flame-wielding giants of volcanic [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Obsidian armor, forges of war, affinity with magma.
	•	Symbolism: Creation and destruction through [[Fire]].

⸻

6. Storm Giants
	•	Definition: Sea and sky giants tied to tempests.
	•	Traits: Towering frames, lightning auras, deep-voiced speech like thunder.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos of storm and sea, divine wrath of the skies.

⸻

7. Desert Giants
	•	Definition: Giants of dunes and wastelands.
	•	Traits: Sun-baked skin, masters of survival, sand-shaping powers.
	•	Symbolism: Resilience against desolation.

⸻

8. Shadow Giants
	•	Definition: Giants twisted by the underworld or [[Abyss]].
	•	Traits: Dark, hollow eyes, necrotic magic, haunting presence.
	•	Symbolism: The great dead looming over the living.

⸻

9. Noble Giants
	•	Definition: Giants who embrace civilization, ruling as kings or sages.
	•	Traits: Regal armor, weapons like fortresses, wisdom tied to age.
	•	Symbolism: Strength balanced with [[law]]; the ruler scaled to myth.

⸻

10. Primordial Giants (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Titanic beings embodying fundamental forces (Chaos, [[Time]], Cosmos).
	•	Traits: Mountain-sized, able to shape worlds, rivaling gods.
	•	Symbolism: The first enemies of heaven; creation itself given opposition.
