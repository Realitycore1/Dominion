# (3180) Belt Mega-Stations and Charter Polities

Beginning in 3180, Ceres and other major Belt settlements receive semi-independent charter-polity status.

Ceres becomes the largest Belt mega-station. Vesta, Pallas, Hygiea, and major asteroid-moon systems develop buried pressure districts, rotating residential cylinders, refineries, shipyards, public ice reserves, local courts, customs, rescue services, and militia.

Charter polities control:

- local taxation and civil law;
- extraction and processing claims;
- habitat policing and courts;
- public ice, air, and emergency reserves;
- internal traffic and docking;
- local militia and rescue.

The Colonial Union retains:

- interregional traffic standards;
- common defense;
- personhood and due-process guarantees;
- inter-polity arbitration;
- external treaty authority.

Semi-independence recognizes an existing fact: the Union cannot administer the Belt without the stations, records, and fleets controlled by these settlements.

The status does not make the Belt uniform. Corporate enclaves, mining cooperatives, mafia houses, cartels, gangs, pirates, and mobile households continue operating between and inside the charter polities.
