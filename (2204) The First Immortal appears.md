# (2204) The First Immortal appears

## Canonical Event

Dr. Elian Voss of Morrow Refuge becomes the first person to complete stable biological immortalization.

Immortality prevents ordinary senescence but does not make Voss invulnerable. He remains vulnerable to injury, disease, deprivation, and lethal violence.

The treatment is politically controversial because it consumes scarce regenerative capacity and delays ordinary patient care. Voss's consent is publicly documented as real but constrained by institutional expectation and historic pressure.

Voss becomes the first of thirteen stable Immortals.

See: [[Sol Immortal Ensemble and Twelve Dynasties]]
