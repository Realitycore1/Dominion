I. Physical Definition

A Quantum Star is a highly speculative stellar object whose macrostructure is governed by large-scale quantum coherence rather than purely classical thermodynamics and gravity.
In such [[Stars]], quantum entanglement, superposition, and tunneling operate across the entire stellar mass, resulting in behaviors impossible for conventional [[Stars]]:
	•	Instantaneous state changes across the star’s body.
	•	Stable superpositions of different fusion rates or compositions.
	•	Energy transfer without heat loss via coherent field Modes.
