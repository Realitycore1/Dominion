## Summary
# Zek’razzar Veyrth

## Overview
The Zek’razzar Veyrth are a draconic civilization centered in the Carneliam System. They are composed of three major castes:
- **Dracoshia**: Metaphysical dragon-like entities, rarely entering the Verse, but worshiped as divine overlords.
- **Aldar**: The oligarchic administrators, priests, and specialists of the civilization, divided into functional sub-races.
- **Draconids**: The lowest caste but the most vital, as they can travel freely and act as vessels for Dracoshia possession.

The Veyrth are locked into an eternal holy war with the Dominion, believing them to be the **Devourer of Ages**, the fulfillment of every apocalypse myth across creation. Their civilization is structured as a fusion of **theocracy, oligarchy, militarism, and communism**.

---

## The Holy Edict of the Dracoshia
> *“From the Maw we were called, by the Dracoshia’s flame we were shaped, to guard the Veins of Creation from the Devourer of Ages.”*  
> — Opening Canticle of the Calamar Council

### The Decree
- Humanity and the Dominion are the **Prime Heresy**, a metaphysical blight that would consume creation itself.
- The Breach (the Carneliam rupture) is not a wound but a **Divine Maw**, a blessing of guidance from the acausal Dracoshia.
- The Zek’razzar Veyrth are chosen as **Wards of Creation**, bound to eternal war.

### The Creed
1. The Breach is a blessing.  
2. Caste is salvation.  
3. Communion is strength.  
4. War is worship.  
5. Myth is truth.  


---

## The Carneliam System
The Carneliam System is both fortress and shrine, built around the rupture.

- **Draakhorn Zar:** Stabilized sanctum-world within the gateway. Capital **Draconyx Betus** features non-Euclidean spires and ritual streets.
- **Resonance Lattices:** Orbital exotic megastructures functioning as prophecy-beacons.
- **Mirror Choirs:** Arrays of exotic crystal, used for divination and weapons.
- **Possession Bastions:** Orbital shrines for Draconid bonding rituals.
- **Bloodflow Rails:** Exotic plasma conduits distributing power system-wide.
- **Spine-Forges:** Factories resembling dragon vertebrae.
- **Wyrmgates:** Non-Euclidean transit hubs, ritual as much as physical.
- **The Maw:** Power source, oracle, and execution ground.

---

## The Military Machine
- **Draconid Host:** Core legions. Possession Legions and Vessel-Knights serve as sacred champions.
- **Aldar Command:** Synarchs as generals, Oracles as battlefield priests, Forgekin as logisticians.
- **Fleets:**  
  - Scale-Engines: Capital ships with draconic silhouettes.  
  - Possession Carriers: Enable mid-battle bonding.  
  - Wyrmgate Cruisers: Causal-slip vessels.  
  - Resonance Dreadnoughts: Acausal disruptors.  
- **Warfare as Worship:** Battles are liturgies; sacrifice-strikes power weapons; victories are sermons.

---

## Propaganda & Myth-Weaving
- **The Dominion as Myth:** Cast as the Devourer of Ages, the False Flame, the universal apocalypse.  
- **Recruitment:** Alien myths are rewritten so the Dominion embodies every culture’s ancestral nightmare.  
- **Internal Propaganda:** Scribes harmonize all myths into the *Creed of the Maw*. Oracles reinforce faith with battlefield prophecy.  
- **Suppression of Dissent:** Doubt = heresy = treason. Suspected heretics are offered as vessels; if rejected, they are unwoven.  

---

## Fatal Shortcomings
- **The Dracoshia Lie:** Transcendence is impossible for Veyrth minds. Possession is parasitism, not ascension.
- **Myth as Trap:** They cannot admit the lie without collapsing their entire society.  
- **Rigid Faith:** They cannot adapt politically, economically, or strategically.  
- **False Efficiency:** Exotic holy infrastructure is awe-inspiring but inefficient compared to Dominion pragmatism.  

---

## The Dominion vs. Veyrth
- **Dominion:** Reality-driven consolidation, exponential acceleration of conquest, survival-of-the-fittest at cosmic scale.  
- **Veyrth:** Faith-driven interdiction, locked into myths, burning resources to fulfill a false prophecy.  

**Result:**  
- The Veyrth hold the line with zeal, but the Dominion spreads faster with each passing age.  
- To the Veyrth, the Dominion is the greatest evil.  
- To the Dominion, the Veyrth are tragic pawns of parasitic gods, destined to burn out.  

---

## Narrative Role
The Zek’razzar Veyrth are **tragic zealots**:  
- To themselves: guardians of creation, chosen by gods.  
- To [[The Dominion]]: deluded slaves of acausal liars.  
- To outsiders: terrifying [[Crusaders]] who demand absolute submission to the holy war.  

They are an empire of faith, blood, and myth, waging a losing battle against the inevitable spread of [[The Dominion]].
