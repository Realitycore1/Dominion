Classification: Predator Class – Tier X / Field Designate “Wendralith”
Observation:
Depicted entity stands bipedal with elongated limbs and exaggerated talons optimized for grasping or impalement. Cranial dome smooth and opaque, absent ocular structures; bioluminescent undercarapace emits faint cyan reflections consistent with adaptive camouflage. Dorsal protrusions mimic antler formations composed of keratinized carbon lattice, possibly for thermoregulation or intimidation signaling. Background analysis places subject in boreal terrain beneath full satellite illumination.

Contextual Analysis:
The Wendralith was first recorded during the Second Expansion phase when [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] settlers breached [[Harmina]]’s northern tundra forest line. Behavioral telemetry indicates ambush predation, targeting biosignature emissions rather than movement. Autopsy records show partial psychic sensory development—rudimentary psionic perception, rare among baseline [[Fauna]]. Genetic sequencing links this variant distantly to [[Rift Stalker]] stock, altered by atmospheric composition and regional radiation gradients.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
Filed under the [[Infernariate]]’s “[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of Inversion.” The Wendralith symbolizes nature’s response to encroachment—beauty and terror fused into one efficient counterforce. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] exotheologians later reclassified it as an ecological guardian, a product of planetary equilibrium enforcing sacred order. Philosophically, it represents the inevitability of reflection: that every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] creation casts an equal and opposite shadow within the biosphere it conquers.
