## Summary
Two [[Verse]]-[[Domains]] with different (or drifting) axiom roots come into causal contact. Their fields and topology interact—Φ ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]] density), η (noise), ℓᶜ (coherence length), Ζₑ (impedance), 𝒦 (sheath curvature)—and the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] tries to pick a rule-set. That contest can graze, puncture, merge, or collapse.

The collision [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Timeline|Timeline]] (field model)
	1.	Forefeel (pre-contact): long-range Δ𝒦 and η ripple; window jitter rises; arc gates detune.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Contact: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] layer forms; η↑, ℓᶜ warps, Ζₑ seesaws; micro-links N_c spike.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Axiom contest: stronger root imposes; contradictions pool as paradox basins; conservation can misprice (temporary “free” work with later debt).
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Resolution path (classified):
	•	G — Graze: superficial shear, small ΔS (axiom stability drop).
	•	P — Pierce: puncture with null pockets, pinch-off risk.
	•	M — Merge: laminated or unified axiom set (often with taboos/quirks).
	•	Overwrite (rare): one realm re-roots the other (e.g., [[Dark Eternities]] beachhead).

Local symptoms you see
	•	[[Verse]] memory bleed (people remember incompatible pasts), reverse-causality leaks, and [[Time]] loop debt that won’t [[Close]] without nulling.
	•	Corridors knot; windows pinch off; Seat Sancta lose reliable GEZ budgets near the seam.
	•	Devices/rites misbind; NLPG/NLPC output “spikes then payback” unless Return Balancers are oversized.

What the [[MOT]] playbook says to do

Incident ladder ([[Verse]] Collision Protocols):
	1.	Detect & classify (G/P/M) from η spike + Δ𝒦 and ΔS.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Quarantine: raise Ζₑ, enforce light-hygiene and [[Radiator Neutrality]], cordon with portable Veil cells (Φ≈0).
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Stabilize: drop temporary Anchors/Motes; tune τ_c gates; ban ad-hoc [[Choirs]] (medical/SAR only, strict M-caps).
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Assess: file an Athearn Reason Sheet; if ΔS won’t recover, execute a Bridge of Writ (formal re-imposition).
	5.	Restore: gradual reopen, spool/cool windows, publish PFN hashes and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]-weather.

Don’t: run acausal threads, cut fresh Horizon-Arc gates through the seam, or violate [[Radiator Neutrality]] (it’s your heat sink during Σ spikes).

Outcomes (decision tree)
	•	Clear & open: Graze resolved; revert duty cycles and lift dampers.
	•	Laminated coexistence: document new local [[Axioms]]; taboos/limits codified.
	•	Pinch-off & reroute: retire the lane, ride sublight around the scar.
	•	[[Rebirth]]-through-collapse: if rot spreads, lawfully unbind → null-shock → refound under new permits.

KPIs to watch

ΔS(t) back to baseline • η variance falling • ℓᶜ recovery [[Time]] • window jitter and incident rate • loop-debt residual ≈ 0 • PFN compliance.
