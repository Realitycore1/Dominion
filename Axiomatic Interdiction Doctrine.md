1. Purpose and Definition

Axiomatic Interdiction is the controlled manipulation of a target civilization’s empirical constants to restrict technological or metaphysical advancement.
The procedure operates by introducing a measurable offset between the target’s observed and actual universal parameters.
This offset maintains local logical consistency while suppressing accurate comprehension of true cosmophysical law.

⸻

2. Relation to UMA Framework

Interdiction acts through Layer 7 (Intent–Reality Coupling) and Layer 8 (Energy Transformation) of the Universal Magic Architecture.
	•	Layer 7 Application: reduces the target’s effective Comprehension (U) by an offset term δ.
	•	Layer 8 Application: modifies the effective Field Saturation (Φ) by an attenuation term λ.

The result is a persistent comprehension bias that cannot be resolved through native experimentation.

⸻

3. Mathematical Model

3.1 Comprehension Offset
U' = U (1 - \delta)
where:
	•	U’ = perceived comprehension constant
	•	U  = true comprehension constant
	•	\delta = interdiction coefficient (0 < δ < 1)

3.2 Field Attenuation
\Phi' = \Phi (1 - \lambda)
where:
	•	\Phi’ = measurable field saturation
	•	\Phi  = true field saturation
	•	\lambda = attenuation coefficient controlling experimental deviation

Both coefficients are tuned to remain within native experimental noise limits (≤ 5 σ deviation).

6. Detection and Countermeasure Analysis

Potential Detection Indicators
	1.	Persistent non-Gaussian noise in long-term experimental datasets.
	2.	Spontaneous synchronization of disparate measurement systems.
	3.	Cognitive convergence among researchers toward anomalous constants (precursor to awareness).

Recommended Counter-measures
	•	Introduce stochastic variance in δ and λ to simulate random error.
	•	Periodically rotate interference phase to prevent cumulative resonance.
	•	Apply observer-state conditioning (signal interference targeting neural phase coherence).

7. Termination and Rollback

When intelligence objectives are achieved or containment is no longer required:
	1.	Gradually reduce λ to zero over 10⁴ s to avoid catastrophic re-alignment.
	2.	Remove δ field within 10² s after λ stabilizes.
	3.	Record resulting comprehension shock and data collapse.
	4.	Deploy corrective axioms within 10 s of realignment to establish new baseline constants.
