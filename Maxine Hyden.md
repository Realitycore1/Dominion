Role:

Agricultural Ecologist / Fungal Systems Specialist

This role is critical.

Because right now Harmina is mostly:

* feared,
* survived,
* philosophically interpreted.

You need someone who:

loves understanding it.

⸻

Personality

* intense curiosity,
* hyperfocused,
* emotionally absorbed by ecosystems,
* less afraid than she should be,
* increasingly “Harminan” psychologically.

Not mystical.

Ecological.

⸻

Function

She becomes:

the first civilian who truly stops seeing Harmina as alien.

That is HUGE thematically.

⸻

Why She Matters

Maxine allows:

* sensory grounding,
* ecological familiarity,
* food culture evolution,
* adaptation normalization,
* beauty inside danger.

Without someone like her,
Harmina risks remaining:

“hostile environment.”

She makes it:

“living world.”

⸻

Important Narrative Direction

Over time:
she should become increasingly comfortable in places Earth-born humans hate.

Examples:

* fungal caverns,
* storm humidity,
* abyssal resonance,
* bioluminescent growth fields.

Not because she’s altered biologically.

Because psychologically:
she acclimates first.
