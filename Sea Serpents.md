Classification: Mythic Fauna → Maritime Predators
Subtype: Pelagic Apex Hunters / Oceanic Megafauna
Status: Active, Widespread but Rare

⸻

Core Description

Sea Serpents are elongated, massive marine predators resembling colossal serpents, eels, or draconic reptiles adapted for open ocean and deep coastal waters. Their bodies range from tens to hundreds of meters in length, armored with scales, ridges, or plated hide hardened by pressure and age.

Unlike Kraken, Sea Serpents are mobile roamers, not territorial anchors.

They are not gods.
They are oceanic predators perfected by time.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Sea Serpents are natural megafauna, shaped by evolutionary pressure amplified by residual planar energies and ancient ecosystems.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Material Continuum
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (size, endurance)
	•	Realm Interface: Abyssal Middle (edges), Tempest Realms (surface overlap)

They are not cursed, summoned, or constructed.
They exist because the ocean allows them to.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Sea Serpents possess high animal intelligence.

They understand:
	•	prey migration,
	•	ship movement patterns,
	•	currents and tides,
	•	when to flee superior threats.

They do not:
	•	hoard territory like Kraken,
	•	engage in symbolic dominance,
	•	recognize worship.

Some elder Sea Serpents display:
	•	learned avoidance of naval weapons,
	•	recognition of specific ships or flags,
	•	retaliatory behavior if wounded.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Sea Serpents inhabit:
	•	deep pelagic zones,
	•	continental shelves,
	•	island chains,
	•	cold and temperate oceans.

They hunt:
	•	whales and megafauna,
	•	large fish schools,
	•	ships (mistaken for prey clusters),
	•	occasionally coastal settlements during famine.

They surface frequently—making them far more visible than Kraken.

⸻

Feeding & Growth

Sea Serpents grow continuously but slowly.

Their diet includes:
	•	marine megafauna,
	•	armored prey,
	•	occasionally abyssal carrion.

Older specimens:
	•	move slower,
	•	strike with devastating precision,
	•	possess scarred, nearly impervious hides.

Dead Sea Serpents often become entire reef systems.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable during surface breaches
	•	Susceptible to concentrated artillery or lightning
	•	Disoriented by sonic disruption
	•	Avoid Grace-saturated waters instinctively

They cannot survive prolonged exposure to shallow or enclosed seas.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Kraken: Avoid or clash; Kraken usually win in territory
	•	Leviathans: Flee; Leviathans are existential threats
	•	Marid: Sometimes hunted or bound
	•	Adaro: Prey or scavengers
	•	Undines: Coexist peacefully in most cases

Sea Serpents are respected predators, not rulers.

⸻

Variants
	•	Great Sea Serpent: Classic open-ocean hunter
	•	Ice Serpent: Cold-water, slow metabolism
	•	Storm Serpent: Appears during tempests
	•	Reef Serpent: Smaller, aggressive, coastal
	•	Alpha Sea Serpent: Rare, near-Leviathan scale

Alpha Sea Serpents are often misidentified as gods or world-beasts.
