A Multimetric Wormhole is a spacetime conduit that does not bend, tear, or violate the stress-energy tensor of General Relativity.
Instead, it operates by overlaying or shifting between coherent but distinct spacetime metrics that remain valid in their respective frames.

🔬 How It Works (VFF Interpretation)
Principle	Description
Multiple Metrics	The spacetime manifold is partitioned into distinct geometric zones, each with its own curvature solution (metric)
Geodesic Transitioning	Objects transition between geodesics across those metrics—effectively changing paths while remaining within allowed Physics
Conformal Matching	Regions are “stitched” together at surfaces where physical laws remain consistent, avoiding discontinuity
Metric Folding / Twisting	Space is not warped inward but layered, like origami—path shortening via spatial redefinition rather than compression

Entire bubble is moved between metric Regions
