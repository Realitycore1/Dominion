# Chapter III - Ledgers of Chains

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. Debt records become political weapons.

## Event Role

- War ledgers rank sacrifices, loans, casualties, salvage, and reconstruction claims.
- Factions dispute whether debt is obligation, punishment, or proof of belonging.
- Archive clerks become targets because records determine future power.
- Black-market debt forgiveness and forged service records spread.

## Consequence

The war continues through accounting after major campaigns pause.
