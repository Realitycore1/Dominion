Types of Paradox Storms

To make this usable, you want classification.

⸻

1. Causal Storms

Conflicting cause-and-effect chains collide.

Effects:
	•	actions produce multiple outcomes
	•	sequences loop or skip
	•	events occur before their cause

⸻

2. Identity Storms

Multiple versions of the same entity overlap.

Effects:
	•	individuals exist in conflicting states
	•	personality fragmentation
	•	memory overwrite or duplication

⸻

3. Historical Storms

Different pasts attempt to occupy the same space.

Effects:
	•	ruins from different timelines coexist
	•	cities appear in multiple architectural states
	•	wars “echo” across time

⸻

4. Probability Storms

Outcome distributions collapse violently.

Effects:
	•	extreme coincidence spikes
	•	impossible events become briefly possible
	•	survivability fluctuates wildly

⸻

5. Continuity Shear Zones

The most dangerous type.

Effects:
	•	reality tears along timeline boundaries
	•	entire regions detach or recombine incorrectly
	•	localized timeline collapse
