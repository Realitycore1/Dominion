12 Surat is a vibrant and bustling city located on the west coast of [[India]] in the state of Gujarat. It is a major industrial and commercial hub, with a significant port that serves as a gateway to international trade. Surat has a population of around [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].8 million people and is known for its rich cultural heritage and diverse population. The city has a significant Muslim minority, and its many mosques and bazaars are a testament to its Islamic heritage. Surat is also known for its textile industry, which is the third largest in the world. It is home to many garment factories, export houses, and weaving mills that produce cotton, silk, and polyester fabrics for the global market. The city's [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] is diversified, with significant contributions from the banking, information [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]], education, and real estate sectors. Surat is also home to some of [[India]]'s largest hospitals, including Apollo Hospitals, which has one of its largest branches in the city. Surat is an important center for commerce and trade, with its bustling streets teeming with vendors selling everything from traditional sweets to the latest fashion trends. The city's vibrant cultural scene includes music festivals, dance performances, and food fairs that attract visitors from all over the country.
