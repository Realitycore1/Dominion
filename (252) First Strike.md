# (252) First Strike

## What Happened

In Year 252 AL, Atlovaia struck the New Aurora Constitutional Assembly during the Founder Session. The attack landed while delegates were debating Amber's role, continuity authority, and the future legitimacy of the state. Its operational objective was to destroy the constitutional process and kill Amber before she could become the hinge of planetary unity.

Amber survived by chance after stepping away from the speaker's dais toward Emilia Navarro. Someone else died where she would have stood. Most of the assembled federal government did not survive.

Seconds later, FreeStack launched an independently authorized drone and ballistic strike against New Aurora and Equilon's suspected proto-legionary infrastructure. FreeStack was not acting under Atlovaian command. The two powers struck from different fears.

New Aurora became the first target because it represented the oldest claim: Ark survival, landfall memory, New Aurora law, and Amber herself.

## What Resulted

- The New World War began.
- The political debate became a military crisis.
- Strategic systems built for GalFed preparedness entered Harminan war logic.
- Amber's private resistance to being used by the state collided with the public need for wartime meaning.

## Long-Term Consequence

The First Strike marks the end of the Mesic Era's settlement arc and the beginning of Harmina's first planetary war.
