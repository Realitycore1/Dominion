The line that holds against oblivion.

If [[Cohors Transgressionis]] is the blade, the Phalanx Reveans is the shield — and the shield is made of ghosts.

The Reveans are the legion’s heavy infantry, but “heavy” means something different where mass and force fluctuate.

Signature traits:
	•	Slow, deliberate, unstoppable.
	•	Anchored by triple [[Confluencia]] braids woven directly into their armor.
	•	Capable of manifesting [[Aetheric]] afterimages — copies of themselves displaced by milliseconds or meters.

These afterimages aren’t illusions.
They’re echo-states, partial selves running parallel timelines in the thinning field.
Enemies strike one form and find the true Revean behind them, already mid-blow.

Battlefield role:
	•	Hold defensive lines in decayed spacetime.
	•	Stabilize ghost bridges.
	•	Anchor extraction operations against [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Umbraxis/Timeline|Timeline]] drift.
	•	Serve as the legion’s “bulkheads” during Profundis incursions.

Why their name matters:

Reveans derives from revenire — “to return.”
They are the part of the legion that ensures someone always comes back to report what they found.

Where the legion ventures, the Reveans stand like pillars in a collapsing temple.
