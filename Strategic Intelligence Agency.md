# Strategic Intelligence Agency

## Summary

The Strategic Intelligence Agency produces long-range threat forecasts, foreign intelligence assessments, strategic warning, covert collection plans, and high-level risk models for Federal leadership.

It is built to notice danger before the legislature wants to pay for it.

## Mandate

The Agency handles:

- foreign intelligence;
- strategic forecasting;
- long-horizon threat modeling;
- external black-network mapping;
- hostile-state analysis;
- alien-contact risk assessment where assigned;
- classified briefings for Federal leadership;
- coordination with military intelligence.

## Powers

It may collect foreign intelligence under law, conduct authorized covert operations, protect sources, and brief classified findings.

## Limits

It cannot run domestic political operations, assassinate political opponents, overthrow lawful Federal processes, or use secrecy to escape all review.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Agency through redacted reports, budget fights, foreign crises, and leaks that either save the state or damage it.

## Story Use

Use this agency for the real secret clock: outside threats, GalFed preparation, forgotten warnings, and the cost of knowing too much too early.
