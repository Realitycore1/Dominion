[[Fauna]] Research Stations are dedicated facilities for the study, preservation, and manipulation of alien and native animal lifeforms. They are hybrids of laboratory, zoo, cathedral, and arsenal — where biology becomes both data and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]].
Location & Scale

Often sited in orbit, tethered habitats, or sequestered wilderness zones.

Scale ranges from planetary outposts to Ark-sized biosphere sanctuaries.

Functions

[[III - Arcanum Omniversalis/Taxonomy]] & Cataloging: Documenting every [[Fauna]] trait, from genes to behavior.

Genetic Uplift: Splicing Concordia harmonics into animals for utility or [[Ritual]] symbolism.

Bioweapons Division: Breeding or hybridizing [[Fauna]] into war-beasts for [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]].

Religious: [[Ritual]] hunts, sacrifices, or consecration of beasts associated with the Empress.

[[Infrastructure]]

Habitat Vaults: Biosphere domes, climate chambers, microgravity aviaries.

[[Computronium]] Nodes: Run ecological simulations, predict invasive potential, design gene-maps.

Custodian Ecologies: Drone swarms that feed, heal, and monitor thousands of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] simultaneously.
