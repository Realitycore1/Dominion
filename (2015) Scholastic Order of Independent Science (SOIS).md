Phase I — The Fracture (1930–1945)

Science loses its innocence.

* Nazi programs and imperial research atrocities fracture the global scientific community.
* Some scientists refuse participation and go underground.
* These are not idealists—they are defectors and survivors.

Result:

* First informal networks form
* No structure, no shared doctrine
* Just one rule:
    “Never let knowledge be owned again.”

⸻

Phase II — The Shadow Exchange (1945–1965)

Cold War forces collaboration without trust.

* Operation Paperclip, Soviet equivalents—scientists see governments recycle the same abuses.
* Black-budget research expands (psychic programs, early neural work, etc.)
* Independent scientists begin quietly sharing:
    * research notes
    * warnings
    * safe methodologies

This is your proto-lodge phase.

Important detail:
They don’t trust each other fully yet.

⸻

Phase III — The Silent Accord (1965–1985)

The first real structure appears.

* Repeated close calls:
    * unsafe experiments
    * near-disasters
    * unethical trials
* Forces cooperation

They form:

* shared codes
* internal vetting
* early membership sponsorship

This is where your lodge model actually begins.

⸻

Phase IV — The Convergence (1985–2000)

They realize fragmentation is the real threat.

This aligns perfectly with your timeline:

* Deep web emergence
* Neural research
* Early Wetwire groundwork
* Accerisma precursors

They unify into a loose but global network.

Key shift:

They stop being “scientists sharing info”
and become a system controlling access to knowledge

⸻

Phase V — The First Crisis (2000–2015)

Wetwire changes everything.

From your timeline:

* 2000 → Wetwire outbreak begins
* 2003 → Accerisma deployed
* 2005 → AccerSticks emerge

This is the turning point.

They realize:

This is not theoretical anymore.
This is existential.

⸻

What they do:

* Begin field operations
* Develop containment doctrine
* Start identity protection protocols
* Establish blacklist policies for governments

They are no longer hidden observers.

They become active operators.

⸻

Phase VI — Formalization (2015–2030)

They step into the open—but carefully.

This is where your idea locks in.

* Shadow Pandemic (2019) forces global acknowledgment
* Governments cannot handle relic + Wetwire complexity alone
