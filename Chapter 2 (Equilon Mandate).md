# Chapter 2 - Equilon Mandate

## Timeline Function

War Year 0. Equilon authorities respond to the bombing by declaring an emergency mandate around New Aurora, continuity law, and founder legitimacy.

## Event Role

- Centralizes defense, rationing, transit priority, and emergency courts.
- Claims the right to speak for Harmina's continuity.
- Sparks fear that the Equilon state is using the attack to absorb other settlements.
- Establishes the first major wartime legitimacy crisis.

## Consequence

The mandate saves lives and creates the legal grievance that enemy blocs use for recruitment.
