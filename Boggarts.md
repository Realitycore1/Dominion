Classification

Liminal Trickster / Fear-Parasite / Domestic Haunt Sentient

Core Idea

Boggarts are small, malicious intelligences born from irritation, neglect, and low-grade fear. They don’t arise from terror or worship—only from places where things are slightly wrong for a long time. A door that never closes right. A house no one quite maintains. A neighborhood everyone tolerates but no one loves.

They don’t want your soul.
They want your peace.

⸻

Origin

Boggarts condense where:
	•	minor frustrations repeat daily
	•	spaces are lived-in but unloved
	•	routines erode into resentment

Common birthplaces:
	•	old houses and apartments
	•	barns, sheds, and crawlspaces
	•	ship holds and engine rooms
	•	abandoned classrooms or offices
	•	alleyways and service corridors

They are not summoned.
They accumulate.

⸻

Physical Manifestation

Boggarts are inconsistently visible.

Typical traits:
	•	Small, hunched silhouettes (child-sized or smaller)
	•	Limbs too long or joints bent wrong
	•	Faces partially obscured—features never fully align
	•	Eyes bright with mean curiosity
	•	Skin like soot, bark, dust, or shadow

They prefer half-light and corners. Full illumination weakens them.

⸻

Behavior & Temperament

Boggarts are petty, cruel, and patient.

They:
	•	hide objects
	•	spoil food
	•	tangle cords and tools
	•	create unexplained noises
	•	escalate arguments subtly

They love households under stress.

If ignored long enough, they may escalate to:
	•	physical tripping
	•	scratching
	•	whispering
	•	sleep disruption

They flee direct confrontation.

⸻

Intelligence

Low-to-moderate, but extremely cunning.
	•	Excellent memory for routines
	•	Understand human habits
	•	Learn boundaries quickly
	•	Cowardly alone; bold in familiar territory

They cannot plan long-term—but they can ruin weeks.

⸻

Metaphysical Traits
	•	Fear-feeding: thrive on irritation, anxiety, and anger
	•	Grace-averse: holy presence makes them skittish
	•	Malice-compatible: prolonged exposure mutates them
	•	Bound to place: removal requires cleansing or abandonment

Boggarts multiply when not addressed.

⸻

Ecology
	•	Feed on emotional leakage, not trauma
	•	Reproduce by fission of annoyance—one becomes two when ignored
	•	Die when environments are reclaimed, repaired, or loved
	•	Absence leaves spaces unnaturally quiet

Iron, cleanliness, laughter, and acknowledgment disrupt them.

⸻

Threat Assessment

Low individually / Moderate collectively

One Boggart is an annoyance.
A nest becomes a psychological hazard.

⸻

Narrative Role

Boggarts represent:
	•	decay through neglect
	•	the cost of ignoring small problems
	•	domestic horror
	•	the supernatural born of stress

Where Titans move mountains,
Boggarts move keys—and that’s worse.
