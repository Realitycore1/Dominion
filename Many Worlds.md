### Question:  
**How does the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Verse]] distinguish between the Many-Worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics and its own multiversal structure?**

---

### Answer:  
In the Dominion cosmology, *dimensionality* and *quantum branching* are not the same phenomenon. The Many-Worlds interpretation in physics describes the **mathematical structure of a single universe’s wavefunction**, not a set of physically separate universes.

In quantum mechanics, the wavefunction’s Hilbert space contains an **infinite number of possible outcomes**—each a valid solution to the same equation. These appear as “threads” or “branches,” but they are *informational*, not *spatial*; they coexist within one quantum framework rather than forming distinct, physical worlds.

The Dominion’s **Verse-Fractal Framework**, by contrast, models reality as a network of **Aetheric bubbles**—finite, self-contained universes stabilized within the larger Aether field. Each bubble (or Verse) has its own constants, causal laws, and boundaries. These are connected through **Confluencia Threads**, forming the tangible multiverse of the Dominion.  

Mathematically, the Aetheric field still holds **infinite potential solutions**, but only those meeting the criteria for **Axiomatic Stability**—self-consistent conservation and causal closure—manifest as actual Verses. The rest remain uncrystallized potentials, much like unrealized snowflakes within a supersaturated cloud.

Thus:
- The *infinite threads* seen in mathematics represent **possibility space**.  
- The *finite bubbles* within the Verse Field represent **realized, causal universes**.

In short, the **Many-Worlds** framework describes infinite *potential states* inside a single universe’s equations, while the **Dominion multiverse** describes multiple *physical universes* born from those potentials but separated by law and causality.
