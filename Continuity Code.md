# Continuity Code

## Summary

The Continuity Code is the Dominion's central civil doctrine: mankind must be preserved at any cost.

All other Dominion virtues exist inside this mandate. Mercy, beauty, law, ritual, memory, ecological repair, and restraint matter because they keep mankind from surviving as a hollow biological remnant. The doctrine is intentionally dangerous. Its humane form depends on sovereign discipline, federal resistance, witness, and memory.

## Governmental Role

The Continuity Code belongs primarily to the Royal Government's long-horizon mandate.

It does not replace the Federal Government's authority over ordinary civil life. Instead, it defines the future-facing obligations that ordinary politics is structurally poor at sustaining: existential defense, memory preservation, restoration after collapse, VFF and MOT risk, succession integrity, and species-level survival.

When continuity power enters daily governance, it must be witnessed, named, archived, and reviewed so the future does not consume the present.

See: [[Dual Government Model]]

## Public Formula

The Empress bears the burden of continuity.

The state bears the labor of continuity.

The people bear the memory of continuity.

All power, mercy, law, and sacrifice are judged by whether mankind's future continues.

## Articles

### I - The Burden of Sovereignty

Amber Zohair's authority is absolute in law, ritual, emergency, and myth. She is not weakly limited by the state. She willingly accepts forms, witnesses, procedures, and institutions because unshaped absolute power can endanger the continuity it exists to defend.

Her public image should therefore communicate burden rather than indulgence. The throne is not an escape from cost. It is the place where cost becomes visible.

### II - Provision Before Glory

No project of beauty, conquest, prestige, or expansion outranks provision.

Food, water, habitat integrity, medicine, energy routing, transit safety, archive preservation, and evacuation capacity take priority over display.

### III - Memory Is a Civic Substance

The Dominion treats memory as legal, spiritual, historical, and logistical infrastructure.

Archives preserve names, claims, inheritance, treaties, ecological baselines, disaster records, succession evidence, and testimony from the dead.

To destroy memory is not mere vandalism. It is a continuity crime.

### IV - Mercy Is Strategic

Mercy is the refusal to create future enemies, ghosts, and collapses when repair remains possible without imperiling mankind.

When repair is possible, mercy serves continuity. When repair is impossible, Dominion doctrine can become severe.

### V - Frontier Worlds Are Not Fuel

Frontier colonies, restored biospheres, damaged habitats, and dependent worlds cannot be consumed as raw material for the imperial center without formal review.

This principle creates recurring conflict between central planners, military emergency command, and frontier communities.

### VI - Emergency Power Requires Witness

Amber may act outside ordinary procedure when continuity is immediately threatened.

Every emergency act should be witnessed, archived, named, and later reviewed. Witness does not erase absolute authority. It gives that authority memory, accountability, and institutional shape.

### VII - The Person Is Not the Symbol

Amber's body, image, voice, and grief may be used in public ritual only under recognized forms.

This protects the person from being completely consumed by the icon.

[[Emilia Navarro]], as [[Voice of the Throne]], may speak with exemplar-to-throne level authority when Amber's personhood, judgment, mercy, or humanity is at stake.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Characters/Dominion/Vel]], as [[Shield of Continuity]], protector, commander, judge, and consort, defends both the sovereign person and the witness-chain that proves the sovereign's will has not been captured by machinery, panic, or court myth.

### VIII - Succession Is Continuity, Not Possession

No successor owns the Dominion. A successor receives the burden.

Succession rites emphasize inheritance of duty, archive confirmation, military recognition, sanctuary witness, and lawful continuity rather than victory over rivals.

### IX - Lawful Federal Resistance

The [[Workshop/Work On/Sort/Federal Branch]] has the right to resist Amber without being treated as an enemy.

This resistance is not rebellion. It is the lawful counterweight by which Amber's chosen restraint becomes institutional reality.

The Federal Branch may challenge procedure, demand witness, force review, preserve dissenting record, and slow the machinery of total power when continuity is not immediately at existential risk.

See: [[Lawful Federal Resistance]]

## Mottoes

- Continuity before comfort.
- Provision before glory.
- Memory before triumph.
- Mercy where repair remains.
- Mankind must continue.

## Story Pressure

The Continuity Code should create conflict, not remove it. It asks who may decide the cost of mankind's preservation, who must pay that cost, and which institutions are allowed to say that survival has become domination.
