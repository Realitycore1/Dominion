## Summary

Civilizatio
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Galactic-Scale Radio Arrays are superstructures spanning thousands of light-years, composed of countless synchronized antennas, satellites, and lattice-webs. They act as communication spines, omniversal sensor webs, and resonance projectors.
In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], they are not passive instruments — they are tools of rule.
Structure

Distributed Lattice: Antenna [[Chains]] stretched across entire spiral [[Arms]].

Anchor Nodes: Clarke Cities, [[Banks Orbitals]], and [[Computronium]] Nodes serve as the array’s “stations.”

Synchronicity: Beams phase-locked across thousands of light-years, their precision akin to a single mind.

Functions

Detection: Sense civilizations, anomalies, and [[Verse]]-Field fluctuations millions of light-years away.

Communication: Provide real-[[Time]] [[Ansible]]-like networks using exotic [[Carriers]] (entangled [[Particles]], [[Axion]] beams, Concordia harmonics).

Weaponization: Array focus can fry star [[Systems]] with coherent radio/plasma bursts.

Metaphysical: Used to “sing” into the [[Verse]] — Concordia hymns broadcast as physical harmonics across the cosmos.

Resonance Ecology

Array nodes act like neurons; [[Computronium]] Nodes “think,” [[Atlas Pillars]] “anchor,” Whipple-Swarms “shield.”

Autonomous MRO ecologies crawl and adjust millions of nodes.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: The foundation of Omniversal governance — ensures instantaneous coordination across impossible distances.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: First strike detection, sector control, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]-scale directed-energy capabilities.

Religious: Regarded as “the Choir of the Empress” — the galaxy itself bent into an organ that sings her will.

Economic: Guides [[Beamed-sail corridors]] and commerce lanes, ensuring trade arteries never drift.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Towers and nodes shaped like cathedrals suspended in space, linked by light-bridges.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, arrays form stark straight-line constellations, black threads against the [[Stars]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Transmission patterns ripple like mandalas across the galactic plane.

From within, a world under an array sees “[[Stars]]” that move in rhythm, forming constellations that shimmer with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] script.

Narrative Function

To myth: Known as “The Choir of a Thousand Suns”, said to be the music of the cosmos itself.
ns living within arrays often see them as sky-bridges or constellations, unaware they are part of a galactic machine.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Broadcasts themselves are encoded in glyphic harmonics, visible in aurorae, plasma flows, even dreams.

To citizens: The array is invisible [[Infrastructure]], the reason their messages and prayers are heard instantly.

To rebels: The array is a cage — every whisper, every rebellion, is overheard by the Empress.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: The array is voice and ear both — the organ by which the empire thinks, hears, and commands.
