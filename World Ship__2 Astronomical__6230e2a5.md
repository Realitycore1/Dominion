## Summary

Civil: Guar
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
World Ships are self-contained megavessels the size of cities, continents, or even planets, designed to carry entire populations across interstellar or inter-versal distances. They are arks and fortresses, combining habitation, industry, and weaponry into one vessel.
Structure

Hull Geometry: Often pyramidal, cruciform, or cathedralic — designed as much for symbolism as engineering.

Core Spine: A mantle piston / reactor hub that powers propulsion and life-support for millennia.

Habitat Vaults: Internal [[Layers]] contain oceans, farmland, cities, and reliquaries.

Armored Rind: Outer hull several kilometers thick, ribbed with weapon arrays and glyph-light.

Functions

Civil:
– Serve as arks to move whole [[Cultures]] or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]].
– Mobile colony-seeds, able to terraform or deploy [[Infrastructure]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Function as fortress-fleets — each one bristling with [[Magnus Bastions]], carrier decks, and planetary-killer arrays.
– Capable of serving as command nodes for entire [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]].

Religious:
– Treated as sacred vessels — “Amber Arks”, [[Temples]] that travel through [[The Void]].
– Used for pilgrimages, carrying billions through Concordia hymnal routes.

Variants

Civil Arks: Primarily habitats, colony transports, and trade caravans.

War Arks: Vast fortress-ships, keystones of crusading fleets.

Sacral Arks: Reliquary-ships, housing scripture, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/1890-2078/Pre-WW3/Doctrine/Relics/Relics]], or Empress-icons.

Economic: Mobile hubs, carrying industry, agriculture, and populations in one package.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Hulls ribbed with cathedral buttresses, vault-like interiors.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they look like pure geometric forms — pyramids, cubes, cruciforms.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Hull glyphs shift in living fractal patterns.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Outer plates blaze with scripture, visible even across planetary skies.

Seen in space, World Ships resemble sacred monuments drifting between [[Stars]] — black pyramids, cruciform [[Citadels]], or cathedral-ships blazing with glyph light.
