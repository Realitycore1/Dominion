(Rocky Mountains, North America)

Type: Technocratic trade league
Population: ~300,000 (fragmented into cliff-cities)
Tech: Hydroelectric, geothermal, microfusion [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], wind-rail transport

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

The world’s first stable post-collapse polity. Built around cliffside dams, valley hydroplants, and mountain redoubts. Their [[culture]] values:
	•	engineering as sacred [[Ritual]]
	•	strict resource accounting
	•	archivist-priests who maintain old-world [[Infrastructure]]
	•	rigid birth quotas

They see themselves as the spine of civilization.

Enemies: Gulfen Reach tribes, Pacific Sound Nomads
Exports: turbines, microgrids, medicines, alloy scrap
