(The New “God Wills It”)
Issued from the Holy See of Rome, Anno Domini 2055

By the Grace of Almighty God,

We, the Bishop of Rome, Servant of the Servants of God, entrusted with the Keys of Peter, do address the faithful across all nations.

⸻

I. The Hour of Trial

Behold, a great darkness rises in the East.
The Caliphate of Yamama has declared holy war, seeking not only lands and riches but the very soul of [[Humanity]]. They wage their jihad against the Church, against Christendom, against every [[Man]], woman, and [[Child]] who dares profess freedom in the light of the Gospel.

This is no mere conflict of borders — it is an assault upon faith itself.

⸻

II. The Signs Given Unto Us

In these days of peril, the Lord has revealed His providence through sacred wonders long hidden:
	•	The [[Shroud of Turin]], proven by both faith and science to bear the imprint of our Savior.
	•	The [[Spear of Longinus]], unearthed from the soil of Jerusalem, which pierced the side of Christ and testifies to His sacrifice.
	•	The ancient Menorah of Israel, restored from [[Silence]], symbol of God’s eternal covenant.
	•	Scriptures once veiled — the [[Book of Enoch]], the Dead Sea Scrolls, the lost Gospels — now rise as torches in the darkness.

These are no accidents. They are reminders: God’s hand moves in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]. His call is urgent.

⸻

III. The Call to [[Arms]]

Therefore, in the authority entrusted to us, we proclaim a Crusade of the Faithful:
	•	Not for conquest, but for defense.
	•	Not to enslave, but to preserve.
	•	Not to glorify men, but to glorify God and protect His children.

We call upon every Christian soul — Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant alike — to rise.
Let each enlist in the defense of his homeland. Let each take up [[Arms]] under the banners of the Cross.
Let none stand idle while the enemies of Christ seek [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] over the world.

⸻

IV. The Blessing of the [[Crusaders]]

We bless all who answer this call with the full protection of the Church.
We grant indulgence to those who fight in defense of the faith, as our forebears once granted to the [[Crusaders]] of old.
We sanctify the banners of every brigade, the [[Arms]] of every defender, the hearts of every martyr.

Know this: if you fall in this struggle, you fall not in vain. You fall in the light of Christ, defending His people.

⸻

V. The Promise

This war shall not be fought alone. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] themselves march with you:
	•	The Shroud, a cloak of [[Witness]].
	•	The Spear, a sign of triumph over [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].
	•	The Menorah, flame unquenched.
	•	The Scriptures, voice of prophecy fulfilled.

With these, God reminds us: Christendom shall not perish.

⸻

Conclusion

Therefore we say unto all: Deus vult novum — God wills it anew.
Rise, O faithful. The trumpet has sounded. The Cross must shine brighter than the Crescent of war.
Stand firm, for this is the day of trial, and in your steadfastness lies the salvation of generations.

Given in Rome, from the Chair of Saint Peter, on this day of ___, in the year of Our Lord 2055.

✠ Pontifex Maximus, Servant of the Servants of God
