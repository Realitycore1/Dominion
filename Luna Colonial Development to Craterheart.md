# Luna: from company ground to Humanity’s shield

## Permanent settlement, 3008

Luna’s first permanent colony begins in 3008 because industrial occupation has already created facts that temporary contracts cannot explain: children, schools, graves, retired workers, family kitchens, clinics, and residents whose lives cannot be transferred with a mining schedule.

The Lunar Colonial Committee receives authority over domicile, education, clinics, graves, elections, inheritance, and local utilities. The Colonial Union retains interworld defense, transport, and trade. This division is Luna’s founding contradiction. The Committee governs the people who live beside systems it does not fully command.

## Fortress Luna

Luna becomes Humanity’s shield through infrastructure rather than rhetoric:

- military bunkers and deep command centers survive damage to orbital systems;
- missile silos and kinetic interceptors defend Earth and divert dangerous bodies;
- polar military shipyards launch without crossing terrestrial weather;
- maglev tunnels connect civilian districts, shelters, mines, and defense zones;
- autonomous mines and mass drivers feed orbital construction;
- buried utilities allow settlements to endure radiation, impact, and siege.

The Fortress Compact does not give the Committee command over strategic weapons. It gives it authority over the civilian interface: evacuation order, warning systems, shelter capacity, maglev access, worker safety, and compensation when military closures interrupt ordinary life.

Lunar children consequently learn two maps. The civil map shows homes, clinics, gardens, grave walls, markets, and public tunnels. The defense map shows permitted evacuation routes and omits the weapons.

## Society and economy

Lunar culture is shaped by scheduled movement. Residents clip tools before releasing them, plan routes before walking, finish conversations when launch warnings change color, and organize celebrations around transit and air budgets. Schools teach pressure response beside ordinary civics.

The economy rests on regolith and helium-3 extraction, polar ice, vacuum manufacturing, mass-driver freight, shipbuilding, communications, and military support. Luna’s proximity to Earth makes it the hinge between planetary recovery and orbital industry.

## Sea of Tranquility and Cynthia, 3180

The Sea of Tranquility Corporate Conflict exposes the weakness in the founding charter. Rival companies arm infrastructure under old federal concessions. The Committee orders both fleets to stand down; neither accepts local control over weapons licensed before the permanent colony existed. Fighting closes three docks and kills forty-seven residents before Union traffic command intervenes.

The Committee responds by proposing domestic independence beneath a protectorate rather than complete separation. In 3180 the Cynthia Protectorate assumes local courts, utilities, security, and domicile while remaining beneath the Colonial Union umbrella. Defense and interworld obligations remain shared.

Cynthia is not the abolition of the Lunar Committee. It is the Committee’s transformation from delegated colonial administration into a domestic government with a negotiated external shield.

## Solar wars and the interstellar era

During the First Solar War in 3250, Cynthia’s strategic position makes it both shield and target. The armistice preserves its autonomy while expanding Union emergency authority over system routes.

During the Second Solar War in 3450, Cynthia remains an Inner-System power but cannot treat shared infrastructure as politically neutral. Its defense corridors, shipyards, and trained crews support the postwar settlement and later Watchtower operations.

By the *Deliverance* launch in 3600, Luna contributes military engineering, traffic scheduling, buried-habitat expertise, archive procedure, and citizens who understand that a settlement becomes permanent when law recognizes the people who cannot simply be reassigned. Cynthia participates in the departure as its own polity beneath the wider interplanetary order.

Luna does not found Craterheart. It helps make the vessel capable of carrying a city there.
