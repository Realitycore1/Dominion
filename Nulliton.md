I. Physical Definition

The Nulliton is a theor[[Antimatter]]that annihilates or suppresses the measurable effects of other fields upon interaction — effectively enforcing localized “nothingness.”
Unlike [[Antimatter]], which annihilates specific particle–antiparticle pairs, Nullitons operate field-agnostically, dampening all interactions within their effective range.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Nullitons are stable excitations of the Φ0 Null-Lattice, a deep sub-layer of the [[Verse]] Field where the state vector amplitude is forced to zero. They can locally remove the influence of energy, momentum, charge, spin, or even informational content from any [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

	•	Mass: Effectively zero but manifests as “negative effective energy” to surrounding fields.
	•	Charge: None in EM terms; carries Null Charge, a scalar quantity representing its field suppression potential.
	•	Interaction: Universally interacts by negating rather than adding to field strengths.
