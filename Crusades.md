The Scouring of Ninefold Barrows
A thousand heretic ships veiled in shadow descended upon the citadel-world of Theragon, chanting profane echoes from their fractured god-machines. [[The Dominion]]’s line held, but barely. Wards faltered. Palisades melted. Faith wavered.
Then the Exemplar descended.
Wreathed in flame and orbit, wings of sanctified gold outstretched, she fell from orbit like a judgment hurled. The impact carved a crater that rang like a bell, and all across Theragon’s plains, the heretics fell to one knee—not in worship, but in despair.
She rose from the light, her voice like a cathedral collapsing:
“Your oaths are ash.
 Your gods are drowned.
 You stand before the Spear of Her Will.”
With a single command, Anastael ignited—its runes burning a line through reality. The Exemplar charged the heretic host alone, and for seventy-seven seconds of sacred fury, nothing could touch her.
Even the enemy's seers wept, their visions consumed by gold.
 The battlefield afterward was described only as:
“Consecrated [[Silence]].”
Anastael was buried to the hilt in the final war-priest’s heart. It sang in the voice of the Empress.
