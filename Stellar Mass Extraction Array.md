## Details

A megastructure equipped with orbi
tal-scale gravitational manipulators and plasma phase-field drags that progressively dismantle uninhabited celestial bodies.
	•	Mechanism: Uses high-powered gravity lenses to disrupt planetary structural integrity and plasma torches to subliminate material into raw elements.
	•	Output: Converts dismantled matter directly into feedstock—metallic isotopes and construction-grade minerals—for large-scale industrial use.
	•	Implications: Enables empires to recycle planetary mass without launching fleets—useful for resource-limited or remote [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
