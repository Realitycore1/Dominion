🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: The [[Book of Enoch]] (Ethiopic 1 Enoch; apocryphal Jewish text)
Discovery Location: Axum, [[Ethiopia]], 2036 — a sealed monastery archive, opened after flood damage. Manuscript fragments also found near Qumran caves during renewed excavations.
Summary:

Material: Goat-skin parchment scrolls, ink analysis consistent with ~2nd century BCE origins.

Content: Expanded apocalyptic visions of [[Angels]], watchers, and final judgment. Includes “Astronomical Book” describing cosmological cycles.

Scientific Notes:

Radiocarbon dating: 210 ± 30 BCE.

Parchment DNA: identified as Nubian goats.

Astronomical calculations surprisingly accurate for pre-modern period (lunar cycles, solar anomalies).

Archaeological Significance: Confirms that Jewish apocalyptic traditions were wider and older than previously thought. Offers [[Continuity]] between Old Testament prophecy, New Testament eschatology, and later Islamic angelology.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-11 ([[Book of Enoch]])
Classification: HIGH PROPAGANDA IMPACT
Custody: Shared — primary manuscripts under Ethiopian Orthodox custody, but digital scans widely disseminated. Fragments in Israeli and Vatican hands.
Strategic Assessment:

Christian Use: Vatican seizes on apocalyptic imagery (“The Lord comes with ten thousand of His saints to execute judgment”) as validation of Crusade rhetoric. Sermons cite it as prophecy of the Caliphate’s defeat.

Caliphate Use: Frames Enoch as a corrupted pre-Islamic text, citing Islamic tradition that Idris (identified with Enoch) was an early prophet — twisting the narrative to claim true prophecy belongs to Islam.

Broader Impact:

Surge of millenarian sects across Africa and Europe.

“Watcher cults” emerge in North America, interpreting drones and satellites as modern [[Angels]] fulfilling Enoch’s vision.

Fringe Persian intellectuals reinterpret Enoch as evidence of a third prophetic stream independent of Rome and Mecca.

Risk Factors: Highly accessible text (digitized), easy to weaponize in propaganda. Potential to fuel violent apocalyptic movements beyond Vatican or Caliphate control.
