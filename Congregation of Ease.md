[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]
	•	Known among the faithful as the Hollow Church or the Congregation of Ease.
	•	They claim to follow the true Christ, one who brings salvation without suffering.
	•	Their rituals parody the Orthodoxy, reworking its sacraments into false gospels.

⸻

Symbols & Rites
	1.	The Inverted Halo
	•	Instead of a circle of light, their symbol is a halo turned inward — a ring of shadow or void.
	•	Represents salvation collapsing into self, rather than radiating outward.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	The Hollow Eucharist
	•	Bread and wine are replaced with tasteless ash and bitter water.
	•	It grants blissful visions, but slowly erodes will and individuality.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	The Gospel of [[Silence]]
	•	Followers chant inverted scripture: words that comfort but carry no truth.
	•	Example: “Suffering is the lie, meaning is the chain, freedom is the sleep.”
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	The Seal of Embrace
	•	Cultists mark themselves with two crossed [[Arms]] around a circle, symbolizing “universal acceptance.”
	•	[[Angels]] interpret this as binding into oblivion.

⸻

Hierarchy of the Hollow Church
	•	Apostles of Ease: High priests who speak with the “voice” of [[Christus heterodoxus]].
	•	Preachers of [[Silence]]: Missionaries who spread the Hollow Gospel across verses.
	•	Keepers of Eden: Overseers of false paradises, ensuring souls remain pacified.
	•	The Blissful: Followers who surrender will entirely, existing in perpetual trance.

⸻

Practices
	•	False Baptism: Immersion into waters that erase memories of [[Pain]] — and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].
	•	Communal Bliss: Mass visions induced by hymns of Hollow Light.
	•	The Embrace: Final rite where the follower willingly dissolves into nothing, believing they’ve ascended.

⸻

Spread Across Verses
	•	Thrives in worlds broken by war, famine, or despair — where the Orthodoxy’s discipline feels too harsh.
	•	Appeals to those who seek escape, not meaning.
	•	False paradises seeded by the cult often mimic [[Aravoth (Grace)]]’s Zion of Light — but hollow, filled with stagnant bliss.
