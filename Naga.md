1. Common Naga
	•	Definition: The baseline—humanoids with serpentine lower bodies.
	•	Traits: Forked tongues, scaled hides, venomous fangs, often magical.
	•	Symbolism: Guardianship, serpentine wisdom, hidden danger.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Guardian Naga
	•	Definition: Serpents who protect sacred places, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], or treasures.
	•	Traits: Bright scales, benevolent yet stern, wield divine or arcane magic.
	•	Symbolism: The protector at the threshold; the serpent as holy [[Sentinel]].

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Spirit Naga
	•	Definition: Corrupted or malevolent naga, tied to curses or dark magic.
	•	Traits: Twisted forms, venomous spells, necrotic or abyssal influence.
	•	Symbolism: Wisdom turned to corruption; serpents as deceivers.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. River Naga
	•	Definition: Naga tied to rivers, lakes, and fertility cycles.
	•	Traits: Amphibious, affinity with floods and water control.
	•	Symbolism: Water as both life-giving and destructive.

⸻

5. Desert Naga
	•	Definition: Naga adapted to sands and ruins.
	•	Traits: Pale scales, venomous spit, ambush predators beneath dunes.
	•	Symbolism: Survival in barrenness; serpent as lurking [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

⸻

6. Jungle Naga
	•	Definition: Naga that haunt rainforests and swamps.
	•	Traits: Camouflaged scales, constrictor bodies, ambushers among foliage.
	•	Symbolism: The serpent hidden in abundance; danger in paradise.

⸻

7. Multi-Headed Naga
	•	Definition: Naga with two, three, or seven heads (drawn from Hindu/Buddhist myths).
	•	Traits: Each head may embody different powers; [[Hydra]]-like resilience.
	•	Symbolism: Multiplicity of wisdom—or multiplicity of danger.

⸻

8. Shadow Naga
	•	Definition: Naga tied to the underworld or cursed [[Domains]].
	•	Traits: Blackened scales, spectral eyes, mastery of necromancy.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]’s serpent; corruption beneath the world.

⸻

9. Noble/Celestial Naga
	•	Definition: Semi-divine naga, often linked to gods or cosmic forces.
	•	Traits: Golden or radiant scales, immense wisdom, guardians of cosmic order.
	•	Symbolism: The serpent as divinity; immortality and cosmic balance.

⸻

10. Primordial Naga (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Titanic serpent-beings from the dawn of creation.
	•	Traits: Vast coils that shape rivers or mountains; breathe storms or [[Fire]].
	•	Symbolism: Creation, apocalypse, eternity in coils.
