# Venus Colonial Committee

The Venus Colonial Committee is established in 3160 after employers withhold docking and reserve lift during a labor dispute. Its founding charter makes buoyancy a public utility.

The Committee begins as a lift-allocation board and develops into an elected government for floating cities. It separates civic voting from technical command, funds cross-altitude certification, and prohibits housing assignment solely by certification band.

It governs settlements made from suspended platforms, pressure balloons, sulfur-resistant metals, enclosed gardens, and atmospheric docks. Every city must continuously adjust altitude. Venusian law therefore treats lift, ballast, docking, storm warning, and emergency rise as public survival systems.

Venusian caste develops through altitude, engineering specialization, pressure certification, and habitat responsibility rather than formal hereditary nobility. Unequal access to training nevertheless allows privilege to pass through households.

The Committee later sends multiple altitude and technical delegations to the first interplanetary [[Harmonium Council]]. For its history through the Solar wars and the *Deliverance* launch, see [[Venusian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
