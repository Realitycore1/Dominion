# Tribus-Class Warform (v1.0)
> Image unavailable after migration: `e9a0c000-e5a8-4280-bc29-0008d38d4643.png`
The Tribus is a compact [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] machine-form designed for scouting, infiltration, and [[Close]]-quarter suppression. 
At only ~[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] ft tall, it combines unsettling insectoid movement with geometric precision. 
Based on tripod locomotion, it serves as both a practical swarm-unit and a psychological weapon.

---

## I. Core Design

- **Chassis:** Hexagonal central body, armored but lightweight.
- **Height:** ~2 ft tall.
- **Appendages:** Three articulated limbs (tripod stance), each capable of serving as leg, blade, or grappler.
- **Mobility:** Agile and unnervingly organic in its motion, echoing biological predators while maintaining strict geometric form.
- **Power:** Micro-fusion core with limited endurance; designed to be expendable but efficient.

---

## II. Armament

- **Paralyzer:** Emits resonance-based harmonic shocks that forcibly disrupt biological nervous systems and destabilize machine warforms.
- **Pulse Phaser:** Small, rapid-fire directed energy weapon effective against infantry or light cover.
- **Melee Function:** Appendages double as piercing or slashing implements, making the Tribus deadly in close combat.

## IV. Variants

1. **Scout Tribus:** Minimal armament, maximized sensors and stealth coatings.
2. **Shock Tribus:** Enhanced paralyzers, reinforced limbs for melee combat.
3. **Relay Tribus:** Climbs structures to act as resonance repeaters and comms relays.
4. **Shrieker Tribus:** Projects resonance-noise fields to disorient, terrorize, or break morale.
