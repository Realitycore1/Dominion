## Summary
the Deep Darkness

Definition:
The zone where the Confluencia lattice reaches its terminal attenuation—threads stretch until their harmonic frequency can no longer sustain coherent verse anchoring.
Inside, causality still flickers, but as a dim afterglow.

Physics:
	•	Aether density \rho_A \to 0.
	•	Confluencial tension T_c drops below the critical binding threshold; verses begin to delaminate from the multiversal field.
	•	Information propagation slows; even light loses phase integrity after a few thousand kilometers.
	•	Vacuum energy inverts: random negative fluctuations dominate, producing anti-space pockets—local voids that consume curvature.

Perceptual character:
	•	To sensors: black static with occasional spikes of phantom energy.
	•	To consciousness: a profound muting—thought itself feels dampened, as if the medium no longer echoes.
	•	Time behaves viscously; a second may stretch into years relative to anchored verses.

Mythic view:

“Where the song ends, silence remembers.”
Dominion archivists call it The Still Sea, believing that Tenebris Profundis is not dead, merely asleep—potential awaiting a new harmonic ignition.

Scientific hazard:
Vessels that drift here cannot maintain Verse-Field alignment; navigation systems collapse because spacetime metrics no longer form closed loops.  Rescue is mathematically improbable.
