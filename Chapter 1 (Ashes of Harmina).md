# Chapter 1 - Ashes of Harmina

## Timeline Function

War Year 0. The Atlovaian bloc and the F.S.S.R. bomb New Aurora during the Founder Clause debates over Amber's role as founder and technically legitimate continuity body.

## Event Role

- Begins the New World War.
- Turns New Aurora from political center into wounded symbol.
- Makes Amber's legitimacy a military objective even if she is not the battlefield commander.
- Forces neutral settlements to choose whether the strike was liberation, aggression, or unforgivable escalation.

## Consequence

The war begins as an attack on a city, but its real target is the right to define Harmina's future.
