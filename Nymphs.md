1. Common Nymphs
	•	Definition: The baseline—spirits of [[Natural]] places, often taking [[Human]]-like forms.
	•	Traits: Ethereal beauty, tied to a location, minor magical powers.
	•	Symbolism: The personification of nature’s allure and fragility.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Naiads (Water Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs of rivers, lakes, and springs.
	•	Traits: Flowing hair, affinity with fresh water, healing and drowning powers.
	•	Symbolism: Life-giving waters—gentle and deadly.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Oceanids (Sea Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs of the oceans.
	•	Traits: Seafoam hair, affinity with tides, allies of sea gods.
	•	Symbolism: Mystery and vastness of the deep.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. [[Dryads]] (Tree Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs bound to trees (your dryad list fits here as a subtype).
	•	Traits: Bark-like skin, leafy hair, life tied to their tree.
	•	Symbolism: Fertility and fragility of forests.

⸻

5. Oreads (Mountain Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs of mountains and rocky crags.
	•	Traits: Stronger builds, stone-colored skin, echoes in their voices.
	•	Symbolism: Endurance, timelessness, the untamed peaks.

⸻

6. Nereids (Seafoam Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs of waves and coastal waters.
	•	Traits: Often depicted riding dolphins or hippocampi, protective of sailors.
	•	Symbolism: The sea’s shifting mercy.

⸻

7. Lampads (Underworld Nymphs)
	•	Definition: Nymphs of the Underworld, attendants of Hecate.
	•	Traits: Dark beauty, torchbearers, eerie laughter.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]’s hidden allure; guides between life and shadow.

⸻

8. Star Nymphs (Asteriads)
	•	Definition: Nymphs tied to the night sky, starlight, or constellations.
	•	Traits: Skin like night, glowing eyes, dream-speaking.
	•	Symbolism: Wonder of the cosmos; unreachable beauty.

⸻

9. Shadow Nymphs
	•	Definition: Nymphs corrupted by curses, abandoned groves, or necrotic magic.
	•	Traits: Withered forms, blackened veins, draining touch.
	•	Symbolism: Nature fallen into decay.

⸻

10. Arch-Nymphs (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary nymphs who embody entire landscapes or rivers (e.g., the spirit of the Nile, a world-tree, or an entire mountain range).
	•	Traits: Semi-divine forms, colossal presence, shape-shifting with seasons.
	•	Symbolism: Nature enthroned; the eternal muse and destroyer.
