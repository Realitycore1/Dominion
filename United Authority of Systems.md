I. Founding (3400 – 3490 CE)

The United Authority of [[Systems]] was established by the [[Colonial Union]] to manage the exponential growth of frontier trade following the first Orion-Arm settlements.
Rather than a single world, the UAS began as three partner ports—Hollis Station, Marduk Depot, and Virella Free Port—each specializing in customs, arbitration, or warehousing.
A Parliament of [[Systems]] met aboard the rotating capital station Concordia One, with seats apportioned by tonnage handled rather than population.

Early governance emphasized transparency, customs uniformity, and freedom of movement.
The motto adopted in 3458 CE—“Commerce Is Civilization.”—encapsulated its philosophy.

Population ≈ 18 million by 3490 CE; [[Infrastructure]] included bonded warehouses, early jump-beacon refueling points, and a nascent port-authority corps.

II. The Transit Age (3490 – 3730 CE)

With the outer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] expanding, the UAS became the primary transshipment node between Sol-proximate and frontier routes.
Its Trade Ministry coordinated docking fees, registry standards, and merchant guild charters; its Customs Tribunal evolved into a respected venue for resolving tariff and contract disputes.

[[Infrastructure]]:

High-throughput depots capable of servicing multiple thousand-ton freighters simultaneously.

Bonded warehouse [[Chains]] operated jointly by state and cooperative guilds.

Tug cooperatives ensuring safe transfer of heavy cargo through dense traffic lanes.

By 3730 CE:

Population ≈ 80 million.

III. The Public–Private Era (3730 – 3930 CE)

As convoy traffic diversified, the UAS developed a hybrid [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] Guard Service, merging public patrol fleets with private security companies licensed under parliamentary oversight.
The result was one of the most efficient—if least militarized—security [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Frameworks|Frameworks]] in the sector.

[[Key]] developments:

Customs Computorium Network automating cargo tracking across thousands of manifests.

Commercial Arbitration Exchange, settling inter-corporate lawsuits without C.U. intervention.

Free-Dock Statute (3812 CE) granting neutrality to all registered ports; even Estelan and Rythecroan vessels berthed without incident.

The UAS became known as the Port that Serves All, its neutrality underpinning the regional [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]].

IV. Praovis Incident and Year 4000 CE

Neutrality met its hardest test when a [[Praovis Mandate]] “aid” convoy (3996 CE) requested emergency berthing at Marduk Depot.
Routine inspection uncovered concealed munitions within the humanitarian cargo—missiles and combat suits bound for insurgent markets.
The parliamentary Security Committee ordered immediate seizure and public disclosure, igniting a diplomatic storm.

Praovis denounced the act as piracy; Estela-aligned media praised it as “a defense of lawful commerce.”
Despite threats of retaliation, the UAS maintained its stance, citing Article 7 of the Free-Dock Statute: “All cargo is subject to truth before entry.”

After the scandal:

Security protocols tightened; random beacon inspections became mandatory.

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] Guards expanded by 30 %, funded jointly by merchant cooperatives.

Public trust in the Parliament rose—the rare colony where transparency became prestige.
