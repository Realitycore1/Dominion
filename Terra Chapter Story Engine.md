# Terra Chapter Story Engine

## Standard

Every chapter must contain:

- a personal objective;
- a reader question;
- a threatened value;
- a decision;
- a visible cost;
- a forward bridge.

The question should be emotional or practical, not merely historical. The definitive manuscript structure is the prologue and forty-seven chapters below.

## Current Assessment

Every chapter now has a functioning story engine. The weakest remaining engines are Chapters 7 and 9 because their outcomes are quieter than their surrounding history, not because they lack character stakes. Preserve that restraint.

The manuscript should not be expanded merely to equalize chapter lengths. Chapters 15, 16, 25, 29, 44, 45, 46, and 47 need their present breadth. Shorter chapters can remain short when one decision and its consequence are complete.
