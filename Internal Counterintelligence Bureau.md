# Internal Counterintelligence Bureau

## Summary

The Internal Counterintelligence Bureau investigates espionage, sabotage, infiltration, compromised officials, hostile networks, and foreign intelligence activity inside Federal institutions.

It is not the Integrity Corps. It protects Federal operations from hostile penetration. It does not police ideology.

## Mandate

The Bureau investigates:

- foreign spies;
- infiltrated agencies;
- sabotage cells;
- compromised contractors;
- classified-data theft;
- hostile influence operations tied to material acts;
- insider threats in security-cleared offices.

## Powers

It may conduct counterintelligence investigations, seek warrants, monitor cleared personnel under lawful standards, and refer cases for prosecution.

## Limits

It cannot investigate lawful dissent as espionage, use religious minority status as suspicion, run indefinite detention, or bypass courts by claiming that disagreement is hostile influence.

## Public Face

Most citizens never meet the Bureau directly. They know it through security-clearance interviews, scandal headlines, and occasional warnings that a familiar official was working for someone else.

## Story Use

Use this agency for paranoia with legal boundaries. The danger should be real, but the state must still prove the difference between opposition and infiltration.
