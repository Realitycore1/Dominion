## Current Canon Scope

The Bill of Rights defines the Federal Government's rights floor for ordinary civil life inside the Dominion's dual government.

These rights bind federal, provincial, state, and local authorities. They also constrain Royal Government intervention unless continuity faces an immediate existential threat. Even then, emergency derogations must be narrow, witnessed, archived, sunsetted, and reviewed after the crisis.

See: [[Dual Government Model]]
