## Summary

GalFed education is universal, multilingual, interspecies, and built around continuity. Students learn that civilization survives through memory, restraint, technical competence, and trust in shared systems.

## Details

Core curriculum:

- Aether Singularity history.
- Cognitive safety and noetic hygiene.
- Interspecies law and consent.
- Archive literacy.
- Standard trade language.
- Disaster logistics.
- Transit etiquette.
- Founder history.
- Restoration ethics.
- Technology tier law.

Major academy types:

- Continuity Academies: civil service, law, logistics, and disaster governance.
- Restoration Institutes: cognition medicine and noetic recovery.
- Corridor Schools: navigation, transit control, and route law.
- Archive Colleges: history certification, translation, and memory preservation.
- Aether Conservatories: field physics and hazard containment.
- Harmonium Officer Schools: military, quarantine, and intervention command.

Education is generous but directional. Students are taught to ask questions within approved frameworks. A brilliant student who questions the framework itself is often recruited, watched, or classified.
