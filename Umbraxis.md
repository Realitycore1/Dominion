Umbraxis is the center of the Oneiroverse, where all twelve [[Archetypes]] manifest directly. It is the axis of shadow and dream, the world upon which their eternal cycles of alliance, rivalry, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], and [[Rebirth]] play out.

Geography
	•	Divided into twelve [[Realms]], each shaped by an archetype (Vale of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], Archive of [[Witness]], Hollow of [[Forgotten]], etc.).
	•	Boundaries between [[Realms]] are unstable — one may bleed into another when [[Archetypes]] clash.
	•	At the literal Axis, the [[Realms]] meet in a shifting city-temple called the Hall of Masks, where mortals and [[Archetypes]] can parley.

Function
	•	What happens in Umbraxis ripples outward into the rest of the Oneiroverse.
	•	When [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] falls here, endings weaken everywhere.
	•	When [[Judge]] allies with [[Witness]], dream-[[law]] hardens across [[Realms]].
	•	When [[Trickster]] reigns in Umbraxis, chaos spreads outward in waves.

Society
	•	Mortals here are not passive: they become avatars, masks, or vessels for fallen [[Archetypes]].
	•	Entire [[Cultures]] align with [[Archetypes]] ([[Judge]]-cities, [[Trickster]]-markets, [[Lover]]-[[Temples]]).
	•	Wars and alliances are as much mythic dramas as geopolitical events.

Symbolism

Umbraxis is both stage and fulcrum:
	•	A stage where [[Archetypes]] act out their eternal conflicts in personal form.
	•	A fulcrum where dream-[[law]] tilts, setting balance across the Oneiroverse.
