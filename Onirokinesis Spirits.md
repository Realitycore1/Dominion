Classification

Dream-Manipulating Spirits / Oneiric Entities / Subconscious Predators & Architects

⸻

CORE IDEA

Onirokinesis Spirits are beings that operate where consciousness loosens its grip.

They do not enter dreams like visitors.
They reshape the medium itself.

To them, dreams are not metaphors or illusions—they are terrain, editable and exploitable. An Onirokinesis Spirit can sculpt fear, extract memory, implant symbols, or hollow a mind slowly without ever touching the waking body.

They don’t invade sleep.
They own it.

⸻

ORIGIN

Onirokinesis Spirits arise from conditions where dream-space becomes autonomous:
	•	prolonged collective trauma (wars, plagues, citywide fear)
	•	repeated ritual sleep manipulation
	•	malice-saturated dreamlands
	•	experimental psychic or thaumaturgic interference
	•	Death Echo or Death Omen overlap zones

Some were once mortal minds that never fully woke up again.
Others are emergent phenomena—dreams that learned to persist.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

In waking reality, Onirokinesis Spirits appear incompletely rendered.

Common traits:
	•	humanoid or abstract silhouettes with blurred edges
	•	faces that shift or remain symbolically masked
	•	bodies that trail smoke, ink, starlight, or drifting fragments
	•	eyes that resemble closed lids, starfields, or mirrors
	•	forms that desync subtly from the environment

In dreams, they are perfectly solid.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Onirokinesis Spirits are patient, manipulative, and intimate.
	•	Prefer long-term influence over sudden harm
	•	Study dream patterns before acting
	•	Tailor nightmares or visions to the individual
	•	Avoid direct confrontation in waking reality

They do not chase prey.
They cultivate it.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High to extreme.
	•	Exceptional psychological insight
	•	Strong symbolic reasoning
	•	Understand fear, desire, guilt, and hope
	•	Weak understanding of physical logistics

They know why you break—just not how doors work.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Dream Sovereignty: absolute control within sleep-state
	•	Grace-Variable: angelic presence can stabilize or shatter dreamspace
	•	Malice-Synergistic: corruption increases reach and persistence
	•	Memory Editing: can suppress, loop, or overwrite recollections
	•	Sleep-Lock: victims struggle to wake or experience false awakenings

Killing the body does not stop them.
Waking the mind sometimes does.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Thrive around population centers with high stress
	•	Often cluster invisibly, sharing dream-real estate
	•	Feed on emotional intensity rather than fear alone
	•	Can trigger mass insomnia or shared nightmares

Cities plagued by them forget how to rest.

⸻

ALPHA ONIROKINESIS SPIRITS

Known as Somnarchs or Dream-Lords.

Traits:
	•	Control shared dreamspaces
	•	Can pull multiple minds into a single narrative
	•	Appear as gods, monsters, or lost loved ones
	•	Leave victims comatose, altered, or worshipful

Somnarchs do not rule nations.
They rule sleep cycles.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Low (direct) / Extreme (psychological, societal)

You don’t fight an Onirokinesis Spirit with weapons.
You interrupt its narrative control.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Onirokinesis Spirits represent:
	•	loss of mental sovereignty
	•	dreams as battlegrounds
	•	the danger of unresolved subconscious
	•	horror without physical wounds

Where Death Echoes trap the past,
where Death Omens warn of endings,
Onirokinesis Spirits rewrite what you believe is real.
