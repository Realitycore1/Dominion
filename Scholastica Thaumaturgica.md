# Concordia Timekeeping & Calendar Systems

> *Time in Concordia is not singular.  
It is layered, negotiated, and often contradictory.*

This document defines the **canonical calendar structure** used across Concordia, separating **world age**, **epochal reference**, and **civil standard timekeeping**.

---

## I. World Age Index (Cosmological Time)

The World Age Index is an **era-based framework** used to describe the state of the world itself.  
It does **not** use years and is not intended for daily use.

This index is primarily used by:
- Scholars
- Elven archivists
- Deep historians
- Certain religious or extra-civic institutions

### World Ages

1. **The Untime**
   - Pre-existence state
   - No causality, no chronology
   - Not measurable or accessible
   - Serves as a placeholder for pre-verse conditions

2. **Creation / Eldar Age**
   - Formation of Concordia
   - Tessellated Exoderm fully intact
   - World Trees function as planetary infrastructure
   - Magic is ambient, subtle, and systemic
   - Elves act as long-horizon stewards

3. **Age of Ruin**
   - Systemic collapse of custodial balance
   - War in Heaven (cascading conflicts)
   - Starfall
   - Soul fragmentation begins
   - The Abyss stabilizes as a domain
   - Damage is irreversible

4. **Age of Swords & Sorcery**
   - Power becomes institutionalized
   - Magic is expressive, personal, and weaponized
   - Orders, schools, and heroic myth dominate
   - Decline continues but is largely unrecognized

5. **Modern Age**
   - Magic becomes regulated and tiered
   - Tessellated Exoderm visibly degrades
   - Maintenance drones persist without replacement capacity
   - Translocation efficiency declines
   - Ends in catastrophic failure

6. **The Fracture Event**
   - Exoderm failure and uncontrolled release
   - Continental fragmentation
   - Floating landmasses form
   - Permanent cloud mantle establishes

7. **The Shattered Age (Current)**
   - Vertical world
   - Airships as primary transportation
   - Subairships operate beneath cloud cover
   - Realms become hostile and specialized
   - Survival replaces restoration

## II. Epoch Calendars (Age-Based Reference)

Each World Age may be referenced internally as an **epoch** for historical clarity.

Epoch calendars:
- Are used retroactively
- May contain gaps or disputed spans
- Are not synchronized across cultures

They exist primarily for:
- Comparative history
- Cross-cultural scholarship
- Long-form archival work

Epoch calendars do **not** replace civil calendars.

---

## III. Standard Academic Calendar (BA / AA)

### Scholastica Thaumaturgica Standard

The most widely accepted **functional calendar** in Concordia is maintained by the **Scholastica Thaumaturgica**.

#### Calendar Designation
- **BA** — *Before Academy*
- **AA** — *After Academy*

#### Epoch Marker
- Year 0 AA is defined as the **founding of the Scholastica Thaumaturgica**

This calendar persists because the Scholastica:
- Standardizes thaumaturgical measurement
- Maintains long-term archives
- Certifies spell tiers and safety margins
- Serves as neutral ground between factions
- Requires reproducible timestamps for research

The BA / AA system does **not** claim cosmic authority.
It claims **utility**.

---

## IV. Civil & Cultural Calendars

In addition to BA / AA, many cultures maintain their own calendars, including but not limited to:

- **VCA** — Veldar Creation Age (Elven)
- **AR** — After Rebellion (Human)
- **BGLE / AGLE** — Before / After Great Lutharian Eruption (Dwarven)
- Post-Fracture local counts (Floating city-states, airship clans)

These calendars are:
- Politically motivated
- Culturally biased
- Often mutually incompatible

They may reference World Ages but do not define them.

---

## V. Practical Usage Conventions

- **Scholarship:** World Age + BA / AA
- **Trade & Treaties:** BA / AA exclusively
- **Local Law:** Civil calendar of the ruling polity
- **Myth & Religion:** Often ignore standardized time entirely

Example notation:
> *214 AA (Late Modern Age)*  
> *Recorded during the final harmonic drift, Eldar Age*

---

## VI. Canon Principle

> **The world has ages.  
Institutions have calendars.  
People live in the overlap.**

Timekeeping in Concordia reflects power, survival, and memory—not universal truth.
