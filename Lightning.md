Core Bias:

Sudden high-energy discharge

This subtype is defined by:

brief but extremely consequential release events.

Traits:
	•	sudden energy spikes
	•	catastrophic but localized transformation
	•	charge buildup and release
	•	intense event-driven change
	•	moments of extreme consequence

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	most of history is waiting for strikes
	•	change comes suddenly and violently
	•	one event can redefine an entire region

Common Expressions:
	•	strike-prone realities
	•	high-risk innovation zones
	•	civilizations built around anticipation of discharge
	•	relic or weapon activation regions

Failure Mode:

Flashover cascade
Too many discharge events destabilize the basin permanently.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	relic activation zones
	•	war-tech civilizations
	•	event-driven cosmologies
	•	“history moves in strikes” realities
