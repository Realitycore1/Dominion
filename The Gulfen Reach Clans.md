(Drowned Mississippi Basin)

Type: Technobarbarian confederation
Population: ~150,000 scattered
Tech: hybrid diesel-solar boats, jury-rigged railguns, reclaimed drones

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]]:

Nomads of the new inland sea.
Tribes formed from climate refugees, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] remnants, and coastal survivors.

They raid, trade, and worship the ruins—literally.

Their scripture:
“The Old World drowned itself for its sins. We take what floated.”

Enemies: Hydrocliffs, New Orleans Archipelago
Exports: scrap metal, swamp crops, salvaged pre-war electronics
