## Summary

GalFed society contains loyalists, skeptics, recovery communities, frontier cultures, and illegal memory movements. Most subcultures form around how they remember the Aether Singularity and how much control they believe survival requires.

## Details

Major subcultures:

- Wound-Keepers: citizens who dedicate life to Singularity remembrance and Silent World care.
- Corridorfolk: port families, pilots, customs workers, and migrant lineages shaped by transit culture.
- Return-Kin: restored persons and their descendants, often politically loyal to restoration institutions.
- Archive Doubters: scholars who suspect Beacon histories have been altered.
- Nullists: low-Aether communities seeking mental independence from Federation monitoring.
- Relief Born: populations descended from worlds saved by GalFed famine or disaster fleets.
- Border Apostates: frontier cultures that accept GalFed trade but reject Founder moral authority.
- Silent Rights Movements: activists arguing that silent populations are persons, not projects.
- Canon Artists: creators who work inside official translation standards while hiding layered meanings.
- Free Corridor Cells: illegal navigators preserving routes outside GalFed control.

Subcultures are tolerated when they enrich the Federation narrative. They become suspect when they create parallel memory, parallel transit, or parallel legitimacy.
