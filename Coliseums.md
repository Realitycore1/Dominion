The coliseum is a Tetrahedron Shaped structure ranging in 100 miles in length, width and height. And it can hold up to 500 billion viewers each segregated between different shows.

The Games
The Games are a series of events that consist of (Hand to Hand, Ground/Air Vehicle, Space Combat and Competitive Racing).
The games are a form of entertainment on the Syl'an homeworld, as it consists of all forms of combat. And much resembles the Gladiatorial battles of the romans.
