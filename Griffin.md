1. Common Griffin

Definition: The baseline—lion’s body, eagle’s head and wings.

Traits: Predatory hunters, territorial, intelligent.

Symbolism: Majesty of sky and land united; vigilance.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. War Griffins

Definition: Griffins bred or trained for battle.

Traits: Armored hides, disciplined riders, cavalry mounts.

Symbolism: Civilization’s attempt to harness nobility of the wild.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Royal Griffins

Definition: Larger, more regal griffins associated with kings and divine authority.

Traits: Golden plumage, immense size, awe-inspiring presence.

Symbolism: Kingship and sovereignty embodied.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Frost Griffins

Definition: Griffins adapted to tundras and frozen peaks.

Traits: White plumage, icy breath, snowstorm camouflage.

Symbolism: Winter’s majesty; predator cloaked in purity.

5. Desert Griffins

Definition: Griffins adapted to dunes and wastelands.

Traits: Sand-colored feathers, mirage-hunting tactics, drought endurance.

Symbolism: Survival against desolation; predator as wanderer.

6. Shadow Griffins

Definition: Griffins corrupted by curses or underworld energies.

Traits: Black feathers, glowing eyes, [[Silence]] in flight.

Symbolism: Nobility turned sinister; guardian of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]].

7. Storm Griffins

Definition: Skyborne griffins tied to tempests.

Traits: Lightning in feathers, thunderous cries, storm-chasing flights.

Symbolism: Sky’s fury given wings.

8. Sea Griffins

Definition: Rare griffins adapted to coastal cliffs and islands.

Traits: Webbed talons, affinity for fishing, control of waves.

Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] over shorelines; hybrid of sea and sky.

9. [[Chimera]]-Griffins

Definition: Hybrid griffins with additional beast traits (serpent tails, [[Dragon]] wings).

Traits: Fierce mutations, unstable temperaments.

Symbolism: Nobility warped into chaos.

10. Arch-Griffins (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary griffins who embody the sun, sky, or primal guardianship.

Traits: Colossal wingspans, radiance of dawn, voices that shake mountains.

Symbolism: The eternal guardian; sky-lion enthroned.
