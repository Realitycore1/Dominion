🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: The Ring of Solomon (Seal of Solomon)
Discovery Location: Jerusalem, 2039, during an underground excavation near the City of David, found inside a basalt reliquary sealed with wax impressions in Paleo-Hebrew script.
Summary:
	•	Material: Gold signet ring, inset with a carved green stone (possibly emerald or peridot).
	•	Inscription: Hexagram/star symbol surrounded by Paleo-Hebrew lettering reading: “Shlomoh ben David” (“Solomon son of David”).
	•	Dating: Gold composition analysis matches Judean sources ~10th century BCE. Toolmarks on the gem consistent with Iron Age Levantine techniques.
	•	Anomalies:
	•	Gem contains microscopic metallic inclusions that form geometric patterns under magnification.
	•	Residue analysis shows traces of frankincense and bitumen, substances used in ancient temple rituals.
	•	Archaeological Significance: Extremely strong candidate for a high-value royal artifact; whether it was Solomon’s personal ring is debated, but physical evidence is compelling enough to ignite belief.

🛡 Intelligence / Security Brief

Relic Code: RELIC-32 (Seal of Solomon)
Classification: EXTREME SYMBOLIC / EXTREME STRATEGIC IMPACT
Custody: Initially claimed by Israeli Antiquities Authority; immediately contested by both Vatican and [[Yamama Caliphate]]. Israel refuses to cede possession. Current status: guarded in Jerusalem under Mossad authority.
Strategic Assessment:
	•	Israel: Frames the Ring as proof of divine kingship and [[Continuity]] of Jewish covenantal authority. Hardline movements demand restoration of Solomonic monarchy.
	•	Vatican: Argues the Seal prefigures Christ’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] over creation. Some factions propose it be used in Papal coronations as “Ring of the New Solomon.”
	•	Caliphate: Claims the Ring belongs to Islam, citing Qur’anic traditions of Solomon (Suleiman) as prophet and ruler of [[Djinn]]. Propaganda warns Israel “has stolen the power to command [[Demons]].”
	•	Persia: Reframes the Ring as evidence of Zoroastrian angelic-[[Djinn]] cosmology influencing Abrahamic lore. Uses this to argue Persia is the intellectual root.
	•	[[Europolis]]: Nervous — fears Ring could destabilize Christian-Jewish unity if Israel monopolizes it.
	•	Broader Impact:
	•	Surge in magical-mystical cults claiming the Ring grants supernatural control.
	•	Underground markets flooded with “Seal of Solomon” talismans; militias brand themselves with the hexagram.
	•	Rising paranoia in the Caliphate — sermons claim Israel can now “command the [[Djinn]] armies.”
	•	Risk Factors:
	•	Extreme ideological volatility. Any public “use” of the Ring (e.g., staged [[Ritual]]) could spark uncontrollable panic or fervor.
	•	Theft or destruction could destabilize Jerusalem beyond recovery.
	•	Recommendation: Treat as Tier 0 Relic — capable of reorienting entire blocs. Monitor propaganda [[II - Companion Narratives]] aggressively.
