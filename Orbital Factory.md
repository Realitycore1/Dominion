An Orbital Factory is a type of sp
ace station that is designed for the manufacturing and assembly of large-scale spacecraft or other complex structures. These factories are located in low [[Earth (Terra)]] orbit or other stable orbitals around a planet or moon, where they can take advantage of the resources and facilities available in space. Orbital Factories are typically much larger and more complex than Orbital Research Stations or other space stations, as they are designed to produce large and complex spacecraft such as space stations, deep-space probes, and even entire space habitats. They are equipped with a range of advanced manufacturing and assembly technologies, including robotic [[Arms]], 3D printers, and other automated equipment designed to produce intricate and precise components. The manufacturing process in an Orbital Factory is typically automated and highly efficient, with each stage of [[Production]] carefully controlled and monitored by computer [[Systems]] and remote operators on the ground. The completed spacecraft or other structures are then transported to their final destinations using other types of spacecraft or vehicles. Orbital Factories are a vital component of modern space exploration, as they allow for the [[Production]] of large and complex spacecraft that would not be feasible to build on the ground. They are also essential for the construction and maintenance of space stations and other habitats, providing a means of producing and repairing the necessary components and equipment needed to sustain [[Human]] life in space. The design of an Orbital Factory would depend on the specific needs of the spacecraft being produced, as well as any existing regulations or safety protocols that need to be followed.
