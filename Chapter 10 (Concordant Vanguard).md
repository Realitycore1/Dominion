# Chapter 10 - Concordant Vanguard

## Timeline Function

War Years 6-8. A coalition forms to prevent total planetary collapse.

## Event Role

- Draws together survival moderates, route authorities, neutral military planners, and exhausted settlement leaders.
- Protects evacuation corridors, archive transfers, and food routes.
- Becomes accused of treason by hardliners on multiple sides.
- Introduces the first practical shape of postwar unity.

## Consequence

The Concordant Vanguard proves that coalition is possible, but only under pressure severe enough to make everyone suspicious of it.
