# (3002) Gateway & Welkin Stations

## Canonical Event

Gateway and Welkin become the first major Phaeton stations.

### Gateway

- low-orbit passenger and cargo interchange;
- customs and medical processing;
- rescue and crew transfer;
- primary link to launch and skyhook windows.

### Welkin

- higher-orbit construction yard;
- traffic control;
- vehicle and habitat assembly;
- AI astronaut maintenance;
- Space Force rescue support;
- orbital engineering education.

Gateway moves people. Welkin builds the places they are going.
