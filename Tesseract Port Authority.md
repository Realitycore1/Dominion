# Tesseract Port Authority

## Summary

The Tesseract Port Authority regulates high-capacity transit gates, dimensional ports, interstellar transfer terminals, customs corridors, freight gates, passenger gates, and emergency transit locks.

It is where migration, trade, security, and physics meet.

## Mandate

The Authority oversees:

- port licensing;
- gate scheduling;
- customs procedures;
- freight inspection;
- passenger safety;
- quarantine channels;
- emergency transit priority;
- port labor standards;
- dimensional-stability reporting.

## Powers

It may close unstable ports, seize contraband, redirect freight, impose quarantine, and coordinate with security agencies during credible threats.

## Limits

It cannot block lawful travel for political belief, conceal port instability to protect commerce, or allow security screening to become indefinite detention.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Authority through crowded gates, customs queues, freight delays, port strikes, refugee processing, and the anxiety of stepping through impossible infrastructure.

## Story Use

Use this agency as the Dominion's doorway. Every port can carry trade, family reunion, disease, espionage, exile, pilgrimage, and war.
