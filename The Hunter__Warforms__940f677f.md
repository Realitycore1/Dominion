•	Height: 120 ft (≈ 36 meters).
	•	Role: Anti-fortress hunter-killer, also used for heavy infantry suppression.
	•	Classification: AFI (Anti-Fortress/Infantry).
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: The Hunter is a fast-striking siege biped, deployed where Pursuers and [[Tribus]] cannot crack defenses. It moves like a predator, a blend of fortress-demolisher and infantry exterminator.

⸻

Design & Profile
	•	Chassis: Bipedal frame with digitigrade legs, inspired by predatory reptiles. Reinforced for rapid bounding and high stability.
	•	Silhouette: Sleek, hunched-forward stance — evokes menace and speed, not slow plodding.
	•	Armor: Stratoferrite plates layered with resonance seams that glow under stress.
	•	Head/Face: Angular “shark-like” skull plate (Metal Gear RAY inspiration) with a central resonance eye. Panels open to reveal integrated weapons.

⸻

Weapons & [[Systems]]
	•	Primary Anti-Fortress Loadout:
	•	Resonant Breach Cannons: Twin arm-mounted plasma rail-casters, optimized to punch through fortress walls.
	•	[[Graviton]] Shear Emitters: Mounted along shoulders; collapse structural supports by destabilizing gravity anchors.
	•	Secondary Anti-Infantry Loadout:
	•	Floodcasters: Multi-directional pyrthrite sprayers, clearing entrenched infantry.
	•	Pulse Scatter Phasers: Short-range area denial beams, effective against infantry and light armor.
	•	[[Close]]-Quarters:
	•	Claw-blades mounted on forearms, capable of tearing through bunkers.
	•	Tail-like stabilizer doubling as a whip weapon ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] design twist).

⸻

Mobility
	•	Digitigrade legs allow bounding leaps over fortifications.
	•	High stability even when firing heavy weapons.
	•	Amphibious variant exists (like RAY), though standard Hunter is land-optimized.

⸻

Deployment & [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Hunters are dropped ahead of sieges to soften strongholds, or unleashed when Pursuers/[[Tribus]] swarms have scouted defenses.
	•	They excel at shock and awe, collapsing fortifications while broadcasting [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] collapse-chants through their resonance seams.
	•	Units rarely operate alone — deployed in pairs or trios under [[Verarch]] command.

⸻

Cultural Resonance
	•	To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Soldiers]], the Hunter embodies relentless vigilance: a [[Giant]] striding executioner.
	•	To enemies, it is a nightmare symbol — towering, swift, and merciless. Its resonance broadcasts are said to echo the voices of collapsed civilizations.
	•	Civilians whisper “when the Hunter strides, the walls fall.”
