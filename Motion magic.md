Definition
Motion Magic is the manipulation of momentum, position, speed, and trajectory across physical, metaphysical, or axiomatic space. It governs how things move — from the flick of an arrow to the translation of entire fleets across verses.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Force – every action has its reaction; motion magic cheats this balance.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Vectors – direction is as important as magnitude; altering either changes destiny.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Anchoring – to move something, you must know what it moves relative to.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Continuity – motion spells often need stabilizers, or objects rip apart mid-translation.

⸻

Techniques of Motion Magic
	•	Kinetics – accelerating or halting movement (speeding arrows, freezing foes mid-stride).
	•	Levitation & Flight – suspending gravity, granting aerial motion.
	•	Trajectory Control – bending paths of weapons, guiding projectiles mid-air.
	•	Teleportation – instant relocation across space (short hops, world-crossings, [[Verse]]-jumps).
	•	Phase Shifting – slipping through matter by displacing into parallel space.
	•	Momentum Steal – draining speed from one object and granting it to another.
	•	Mass Translation – moving entire armies, cities, or planets across distances.

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: Giants wield crude but devastating motion magic in their slings — hurling stones with magically multiplied force ￼. Elven mages use trajectory control in their musket formations.
	•	Concordia: Motion magic becomes critical at Platina and Adamant tiers, where heroes can leap continents, redirect storms, or bend rivers with gestures. Travel guilds monopolize teleportation networks.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Motion is industrialized — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Drives warp fleets across verses, while FAID field-agents use momentum-transfer to turn enemy artillery back on itself.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Resonant “choral vectors” let their fleets ride harmonic slipstreams, moving entire civilisations without friction.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Demiurgic corruption warps motion into impossibility — armies march endlessly but never arrive, or fall upward into black skies forever.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Universality – every being needs to move.
	•	Utility – transport, war, communication, even agriculture benefit from motion spells.
	•	Scalability – works at every level: from nudging a coin to shifting a galaxy.

⸻

Perils
	•	Displacement Errors – teleportation gone wrong fuses bodies into walls or scatters them across dimensions.
	•	Relativity Collapse – altering speed without regard to reference frames tears momentum apart, causing explosive backlash.
	•	Paradox Loops – phase-shifting into wrong spaces creates recursive infinities.
	•	Overextension – moving masses too great (continents, [[Stars]]) destabilizes surrounding [[Systems]].

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Anchoring Sigils – runes that lock an area, preventing teleportation or levitation.
	•	Inertia Wards – maintain [[Natural]] motion; arrows and bullets unaffected by speed spells.
	•	Gravitic Nets – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] tech that pulls motion mages down by reinforcing local gravity.
	•	Entropy Fields – corrupts vector stability, so teleporters scatter instead of land.
