# Dominion Institutions

## Summary

The Dominion does not function only because Amber wills it. A durable survival state needs institutions thick enough to carry continuity when the sovereign is absent, constrained, contested, or forced into emergency rule.

Amber's power is absolute in law, ritual, emergency, and myth. The state does not make her powerful by permission. Instead, the state receives forms of restraint that Amber willingly upholds so absolute power does not become total domination.

## Dual Government Frame

Dominion institutions are divided by time horizon.

The Federal Government governs the living present. It handles ordinary law, representation, public services, budgets, courts, rights, provincial administration, and the daily political needs of citizens.

The Royal Government guards the endangered future. It handles continuity, existential planning, civilizational memory, succession integrity, VFF and MOT risk, long-horizon defense, restoration after collapse, and threats ordinary politics cannot reliably sustain attention toward.

The system is not meant to eliminate tension. It is meant to make tension lawful.

If the Federal Government dominates completely, the Dominion becomes short-sighted and vulnerable to slow catastrophes. If the Royal Government dominates completely, the Dominion becomes ceremonial autocracy and begins treating present citizens as material for future survival.

See: [[Dual Government Model]]

## Sovereign Center

### Celestial Throne

The office of Amber Zohair as sovereign-symbol of continuity.

Functions:

- final emergency authority;
- continuity oath keeper;
- public focus of civil religion;
- last appeal in existential disputes;
- embodiment of imperial memory and responsibility.

Risks:

- over-centralization;
- mistaking Amber's image for Amber's judgment;
- bureaucrats invoking her name without her consent.

### Inner Witness

The private and semi-public circle permitted to see and protect the person inside the office.

Likely members:

- [[Emilia Navarro]];
- [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Characters/Dominion/Vel]];
- medical or longevity steward;
- memory witness or confessor;
- security custodian.

Function:

To prevent the sovereign from being consumed by the symbol.

### Voice of the Throne

Emilia Navarro's formal office.

Authority level: exemplar-to-throne.

Functions:

- speak from unusual proximity to Amber's person and judgment;
- humanize the Throne in council, ritual, crisis, and review;
- carry trusted speech between Amber, the [[Workshop/Work On/Sort/Federal Branch]], frontier communities, and the Inner Witness;
- challenge abstraction when continuity doctrine begins to treat people as numbers or symbols only.

See: [[Voice of the Throne]]

### Shield of Continuity

Vel's formal office as protector, commander, judge, and consort.

Functions:

- protect Amber's person;
- command elite continuity defense forces;
- guard the witness-chain;
- judge or testify in continuity review;
- stand publicly as consort without reducing the role to ornament.

See: [[Shield of Continuity]]

## Civil Administration

### Ministry of Provision

Manages food, habitat necessities, medical reserves, disaster relief, refugee support, and frontier sustenance.

### Stellar Tithe Authority

Regulates energy obligations from rings, stellar infrastructure, fusion grids, and high-output habitats.

### Continuity Archives

Maintains identity, law, treaties, ecological baselines, disaster records, memory vaults, and names of the dead.

### Imperial Memory Courts

Adjudicate continuity crimes such as archive erasure, disaster-testimony falsification, succession fraud, unlawful emergency action, and ecological amnesia.

## Federal Counterweight

### Federal Branch

The Federal Branch is the lawful constitutional counterweight to Amber's absolute power.

Functions:

- resist Amber without being treated as an enemy;
- preserve procedural legitimacy;
- force review, witness, and record;
- speak for law, representation, and institutional continuity;
- prevent the Dominion from collapsing into pure personal rule.

This institution is not anti-Amber by default. Its right to resist exists because Amber's chosen restraint needs a public, durable body capable of exercising it.

See: [[Lawful Federal Resistance]]

### Federal Agencies

Federal agencies are the practical machinery of the Federal Branch.

They run the services citizens actually touch: identity records, franchise review, schools, clinics, transit, housing, labor standards, courts administration, food security, environmental enforcement, public works, emergency response, finance, resource allocation, science regulation, and bounded security work.

Their legitimacy depends on budget visibility, court review, legislative oversight, public appeal paths, and inspector-general audit.

They are not Royal ministries. They may coordinate with the Royal Branch during continuity emergencies, but they do not become sacred, personal, or unreviewable by invoking Amber's name.

See: [[Federal Agencies]]

## Sacred-Civic Institutions

### Sanctuaries of Continuity

Public spaces combining archive, memorial, court ritual, civic worship, school, and emergency shelter.

Core sanctuaries may be marble and gold. Frontier sanctuaries may be practical structures built around water, seed vaults, and local names.

### Office of Rites

Maintains ritual calendars, oath language, sanctuary standards, public processions, and legal forms of imperial image use.

Risk:

This office becomes dangerous if it values the image of continuity more than actual continuity.

## Military and Security

### Guardians of Transit

Protect skyhooks, corridors, rings, convoys, VFF-stabilized routes, evacuation chains, and archive transfers.

### Continuity Exemplars

Elite military-ceremonial officers whose legitimacy comes from defense of continuity rather than conquest.

### Emergency Continuity Command

Activated during invasion, infrastructure collapse, VFF crisis, succession crisis, or mass evacuation.

Risk:

The emergency chain can preserve civilization or become a quiet coup mechanism.

## Frontier Governance

### Provincial Stewards

Govern worlds, habitats, sectors, or restoration zones under the [[Continuity Code]].

They are measured by order, provision, repair, memory preservation, and ecological viability.

### Local Compacts

Agreements between Dominion authority and local populations.

These allow autonomy inside imperial continuity and are especially useful for frontier worlds such as Harmina.
