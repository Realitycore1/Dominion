# Domain Flesh Cores

## Classification

- **Type:** Biological command, metabolic, and allocation organ
- **Primary Function:** Regulation of [[Domain Flesh Pits]] and [[Biospawn Chambers]]
- **Secondary Function:** Local command-voice generation, inventory control, contamination screening, and production scheduling
- **Typical Intelligence:** Advanced non-sapient to limited tactical intelligence
- **Command Authority:** Local infrastructure and assigned bioforms
- **Typical Locations:** Hive foundries, Womb Worlds, Supernals, siege complexes, living vessels, planetary conversion sites
- **Domain Designation:** Flesh Core
- **Technical Classification:** Metabolic Reclamation and Biospawn Command Organ

---

# Overview

A **Flesh Core** is the biological command organ that governs the conversion of harvested matter into viable Domain life.

It occupies the functional position of:

- industrial controller;
- chemical regulator;
- genetic inventory;
- contamination authority;
- production scheduler;
- command transmitter;
- and local logistics intelligence.

A Flesh Core does not usually design civilization-scale strategy. It interprets orders from an [[Overseer]], [[Prime]], Sovereign, ship-mind, or local hive and converts those orders into measurable biological production.

It determines:

- what matter is digested;
- what must be preserved;
- what is toxic;
- what genetic material is valuable;
- what bioforms are needed;
- how much biomass can be allocated;
- and when production must stop to prevent collapse.

> **The Flesh Pit reduces matter. The Flesh Core decides what that matter may become.**

# Structure

## Central Cortex

The central cortex is a dense biological processor composed of:

- Wetwire-derived neural tissue;
- layered memory ganglia;
- chemical-recognition organs;
- endocrine command glands;
- and mineral-reinforced support plates.

It receives data from every connected pit, chamber, vessel, and transport artery.

The cortex does not think like a human mind.

It processes:

- mass;
- chemistry;
- heat;
- urgency;
- production priority;
- contamination;
- and command hierarchy.

## Metabolic Crown

The upper structure forms a crown of glandular lobes and sensory nodules.

It regulates:

- acidity;
- enzyme concentration;
- fluid pressure;
- oxygenation;
- thermal load;
- nutrient balance;
- and waste removal.

Each lobe can specialize in a different chemical environment.

## Genetic Archive Lobes

Flesh Cores maintain local genetic libraries containing:

- approved body plans;
- tissue templates;
- immune profiles;
- armor matrices;
- neural scaffolds;
- metabolic variants;
- and repair sequences.

These archives are not always complete.

A field Flesh Core may store only a limited set of military or labor forms. A Womb World or Supernal Core may maintain millions of lineages and evolutionary experiments.

## Command Voice Organ

A specialized organ translates production decisions into the hive's local command language.

Depending on the branch, the signal may be:

- neural;
- hormonal;
- electromagnetic;
- acoustic;
- biochemical;
- memory-encoded;
- or later Aether-assisted.

This command voice coordinates:

- Flesh Pit digestion;
- nutrient routing;
- Biospawn Chamber development;
- maintenance organisms;
- and nearby defensive Thralls.

## Vascular Manifold

The lower body of the Core branches into massive vascular trunks.

These carry:

- processed nutrients;
- acids;
- precursor chemicals;
- genetic packages;
- hormones;
- oxygen;
- coolant;
- and waste products.

The manifold physically links the Flesh Core to every chamber it governs.

# Core Functions

## 1. Material Recognition

The Flesh Core identifies incoming matter by:

- elemental composition;
- molecular structure;
- biological origin;
- toxicity;
- genetic usefulness;
- infection risk;
- and structural potential.

It distinguishes between:

- food;
- armor feedstock;
- neural substrate;
- metabolic reserve;
- mineral reinforcement;
- and waste requiring further treatment.

## 2. Personhood Screening

Orthodox Flesh Cores are required to identify signs of:

- active cognition;
- recoverable memory;
- continuity-bearing neural structure;
- integrated Domain identity;
- or potential restoration.

This safeguard reflects the Domain's legal distinction between available matter and recoverable persons. îfileciteîturn47file6î

A Core may suspend digestion and isolate a body for review by:

- an Overseer;
- a Prime;
- a continuity specialist;
- or a local legal hive.

Militant and corrupted branches may disable or redefine this function.

## 3. Chemical Governance

The Core adjusts the Flesh Pit's chemistry by controlling:

- acid strength;
- enzyme families;
- solvent production;
- temperature;
- pressure;
- and microbial composition.

Different materials require different reduction pathways.

## 4. Contamination Control

The Core directs immune colonies to identify and neutralize:

- pathogens;
- hostile Wetwire;
- nanotechnology;
- toxins;
- unstable proteins;
- mutagenic residue;
- corrupted memory tissue;
- and foreign command signals.

## 5. Inventory Management

The Core records available:

- biomass;
- metals;
- salts;
- carbon;
- water;
- neural substrate;
- reproductive material;
- and energy reserves.

This inventory determines what the connected foundry can realistically produce.

## 6. Biospawn Allocation

The Flesh Core assigns matter to specific Biospawn Chambers.

A basic production chain is:

The Core may prioritize:

- repair organisms;
- soldiers;
- workers;
- scouts;
- heat-management tissue;
- vessel organs;
- or emergency command nodes.

## 7. Developmental Oversight

The Core monitors growing bioforms for:

- structural defects;
- neural instability;
- immune failure;
- command incompatibility;
- memory contamination;
- and metabolic inefficiency.

Defective forms may be:

- corrected;
- repurposed;
- recycled;
- isolated;
- or destroyed.

## 8. Local Defense

A Flesh Core can command nearby defensive organisms.

It may:

- seal chambers;
- flood corridors;
- alter atmospheric chemistry;
- harden membranes;
- release immune swarms;
- awaken Thralls;
- or redirect digestive fluid as a weapon.

# Intelligence and Agency

## Non-Sapient Cores

Most Flesh Cores are advanced regulatory intelligences.

They can:

- adapt;
- predict demand;
- interpret commands;
- recognize anomalies;
- and choose among permitted solutions.

They do not possess stable selfhood.

## Tactical Cores

Larger Cores may develop limited local identity.

They can:

- negotiate resource priorities;
- reject contradictory orders;
- preserve a foundry during command loss;
- and formulate local defense.

## Emergent Cores

A Flesh Core that operates for long periods without higher command may accumulate:

- memory;
- preference;
- continuity;
- and independent strategy.

Such a Core may become:

- a feral foundry mind;
- a local Overseer;
- a lesser Prime node;
- or the nucleus of a new hive.

The Domain therefore watches old Flesh Cores carefully.

---

# Relationship to Command Hierarchy

A Flesh Core normally answers to:

1. local [[Overseers]];
2. ship or habitat command minds;
3. branch [[Primes]];
4. temporary Sovereigns;
5. ID during Supreme activation.

The Core itself usually controls:

- Flesh Pits;
- Biospawn Chambers;
- metabolic infrastructure;
- maintenance organisms;
- and assigned defensive Thralls.

It is an infrastructure commander rather than a political leader.

# Operational Variants

## Field Flesh Core

Compact and expendable.

Controls:

- temporary reclamation pits;
- a few Biospawn Chambers;
- and limited military production.

## Hive Flesh Core

Serves a city, fortress, station, or major vessel.

Maintains:

- broad genetic archives;
- civilian repair;
- military production;
- and long-term nutrient logistics.

## Womb Core

A continental or planetary command organ embedded within a [[Womb World]].

Coordinates:

- reproduction;
- ecosystem design;
- civic bioforms;
- planetary metabolism;
- and multiple subordinate Flesh Cores.

## Supernal Core

A vast distributed organ inside a [[Domain Supernal]].

It regulates:

- planetary digestion;
- mobile gene foundries;
- fleet docking;
- biosphere construction;
- and billions of concurrent gestation cycles.

## Martial Core

Optimized for war.

Prioritizes:

- rapid Thrall replacement;
- armor growth;
- ammunition organisms;
- repair tissue;
- and battlefield adaptation.

## Archive Core

Specialized in preserving:

- endangered genomes;
- rare neural structures;
- extinct organisms;
- and memory-compatible tissue.

# Failure States

## Command Voice Loss

Without a valid command voice, a Flesh Core may:

- repeat its last production order;
- stop all digestion;
- overproduce one bioform;
- reject all incoming matter;
- or form a local autonomous hive.

## Heat Saturation

Flesh Cores generate substantial heat through computation, chemical regulation, and gestation control.

Thermal overload may cause:

- slowed cognition;
- enzyme failure;
- tissue necrosis;
- false classifications;
- and uncontrolled chamber growth.

## Archive Corruption

Damage to genetic lobes may create:

- malformed organisms;
- unstable hybrids;
- inherited disease;
- or command-incompatible bodies.

## Metabolic Cascade

A failed Core can destabilize every connected Flesh Pit and Biospawn Chamber simultaneously.

## Prime Override Conflict

Conflicting command voices can force a Core into:

- paralysis;
- split production;
- internal immune war;
- or self-destruction.

## ID Activation

During Supreme activation, the Core may lose all local discretion and become a direct organ of ID.

# Vulnerabilities

Flesh Cores are vulnerable to:

- heat accumulation;
- command disruption;
- vascular severance;
- archive corruption;
- non-corrupt state recall;
- metabolic poisoning;
- signal spoofing;
- and simultaneous contradictory production demands.

Destroying a Flesh Core can cripple an entire Domain foundry complex, but careless destruction may also:

- release acids;
- trigger uncontrolled digestion;
- awaken feral bioforms;
- or cause chamber rupture.

# Visual Aesthetic

A Flesh Core should resemble a fusion of:

- heart;
- brain;
- glandular crown;
- industrial control center;
- and living refinery.

Common visual features include:

- ribbed bone casing;
- pulsing black-red tissue;
- luminous green sensory nodes;
- branching vascular trunks;
- suspended neural sacs;
- rotating glandular lobes;
- command filaments;
- and heat-radiating membranes.

It should look central, protected, and indispensable.

Nothing should appear decorative without purpose.

> **A Flesh Core is not the factory's brain alone. It is its judgment, metabolism, memory, and voice.**

---

# Strategic Role

Flesh Cores make Domain logistics adaptive.

They allow a hive to:

- consume local matter;
- classify resources;
- repair losses;
- alter production priorities;
- and grow new forces without conventional industrial supply lines.

A functioning Flesh Core can turn a conquered site into a self-sustaining biospawn complex.

A destroyed Core can turn that same site into an uncontrolled chemical and biological disaster.

---

# Canon Summary

- Flesh Cores govern Flesh Pits and Biospawn Chambers.
- They classify matter, screen personhood, regulate chemistry, and allocate feedstock.
- They generate local command voices.
- They are usually advanced infrastructure intelligences rather than persons.
- Long-isolated Cores may develop selfhood or become lesser Prime nodes.
- Core variants range from field organs to planetary and Supernal-scale systems.
- Heat, command loss, archive corruption, and vascular severance are major weaknesses.
- Orthodox Cores preserve recoverable persons.
- Militant and corrupted branches may disable that safeguard.
