Issued from Riyadh, Seat of the [[Yamama Caliphate]], Anno Hegirae 14XX / Anno Domini 20XX

⸻

I. The Crusader Lie

The enemies of Islam, those who call themselves heirs of Rome, have raised again the banner of the Crusade. They cloak themselves in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] of iron and cloth, yet they forget: those [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] were wielded against us before, and still the Crescent endures while empires of the Cross have fallen to dust.

The Pope, servant of idols, declares war upon the Ummah. His words are not new. They are the echo of a thousand years of oppression, the cry of tyrants who fear the return of the Caliphate.

⸻

II. The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] of the Believers

They parade the Spear and the Shroud, but these are tools of paganism, false gods of cloth and rust. The true [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] are not objects, but the lives of the faithful:
	•	The Qur’an, perfect and unaltered, unchanged since revelation.
	•	The Kaaba, eternal house of God, where Abraham once raised the stone.
	•	The Martyrs of the Ummah, whose blood is the ink of God’s living word.

These are our proof. These are our signs. No spear nor shroud can rival the eternal truth of Islam.

⸻

III. The Fatwa of Unity

Therefore, by the authority of the Caliph, Servant of God and Guardian of the Two Holy Cities, we declare:
	•	A Jihad of Defense against the [[Crusaders]] who seek to destroy Islam.
	•	A Fatwa of Unity binding all Muslims, Sunni and Shia alike, under the banner of the Caliphate.
	•	A Call to [[Arms]] for every believer, from the sands of Arabia to the mountains of [[Afghanistan]], from the deserts of Africa to the streets of Europe.

Let every mosque be a fortress, every believer a [[Soldier]], every martyr a shield.

⸻

IV. The Promise of Victory

God has promised that His Ummah shall never perish. The Pope promises indulgence; we promise Paradise eternal.
The [[Crusaders]] fight for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]] of stone. We fight for the living Word of God.
They fight for the memory of empires long dead. We fight for the Caliphate reborn.

⸻

Conclusion

To every Crusader [[Soldier]]: lay down your [[Arms]] and embrace the truth of Islam, and you shall live. Persist in your unbelief, and you shall meet the sword of God.
To every Muslim: rise. The world trembles, but God is with you. This is not the end — it is the beginning of our triumph.

✠ The Caliph, Guardian of the Faithful, Servant of God
