Core Bias:

Toxic residue and unusable abundance

This is where the basin is not empty—but what remains is often:
	•	corrosive
	•	contaminated
	•	inert
	•	or unusable

Traits:
	•	remnants of former richness
	•	chemical or energetic harshness
	•	poisoned abundance
	•	dead seas / dead systems
	•	survivable but hostile substrates

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	dried remains of once-living worlds
	•	contaminated plains of memory
	•	abandoned extraction zones
	•	chemically dead expanses

Common Expressions:
	•	post-collapse civilizations
	•	toxic relic zones
	•	chemically scarred realities
	•	old war basins

Failure Mode:

Residual sterility
The basin cannot meaningfully recover from what remains.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	fallout cosmologies
	•	relic contamination zones
	•	“dead but not empty” realities
