# Story 19 - First Winter Away

## Viewpoint

Mina Corven, now a heatline engineer in a remote settlement.

## Setting

Year 108 AL, first winter away from New Aurora.

## Story Shape

The settlement survives by sharing heat, tools, passwords, and embarrassment. Everyone knows who failed to seal their wall and who lied about their food stores.

New Aurora sends advice. Some of it helps. Some of it proves the city does not understand the region.

## Harmina Element

Regional identity forms from local weather, heatline maintenance, and shared hardship.

## Character Movement

Mina begins the winter wanting approval from New Aurora. She ends it wanting recognition instead.

## Future Payoff

The phrase "not an outpost" begins as a complaint and becomes a political identity.
