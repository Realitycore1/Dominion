## Summary

![[IMG_5196.jpg]]
Type: Council Federation with Planner AIs under [[Human]] Oversight
Location: Kuroda [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], 49 ly from Sol; one main inhabited world (Kuroda Prime) with two major asteroid-belt towns (Haven Belt and Koto Belt).
Population (c. 4000 CE): 150–220 million
Founding Charter: C.U. Industrial Syndicate Development Grant (3385 CE)

I. Founding (3380–3470 CE)

The Kuroda Mission began as a collective industrial colony funded by a coalition of postwar labor unions and logistics cooperatives seeking independence from the megacorporate monopolies that dominated Sol’s shipbuilding industry.
Launched under the C.U.’s Industrial Syndicate Grant Program, the colonists were predominantly dockworkers, materials [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], and trade-union families.

Upon arrival, they established Kuroda Prime, a habitable but mineral-poor world supplemented by rich asteroid belts.
The early settlement adopted the “Collective Charter”, declaring that no citizen would serve a private employer—all economic functions would be managed by councils of planners advised by computational [[Systems]] called Planner AIs.

By 3470 CE:

Population: ~8 million.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: Bulk habitat components and chemical refinery operations.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: decentralized council committees; AIs oversaw data, logistics, and rations.

II. The Planner Era (3470–3700 CE)

The colony’s rapid industrial expansion required coordination beyond [[Human]] administrative capacity.
The first generation of Planner AIs evolved into semi-autonomous management [[Systems]] capable of forecasting demand, labor supply, and population growth decades in advance.

[[Humans]] retained political oversight but deferred most resource-allocation decisions to the Planner Core, which proposed policy sets ratified by elected councils.
This balance of machine planning and [[Human]] arbitration became the Kuroda Model, later studied across the [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] as a successful [[Synthesis]] of socialism and computational technocracy.

[[Infrastructure]]:

Cylinder Farm Networks—modular [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] km-long agricultural and residential cylinders orbiting Kuroda Prime.

Belt Mass-Drivers—used to sling ore and finished goods toward trade routes.

Barracks-Habs for refinery laborers, spartan but efficient, maintained by syndicate boards.

By 3700 CE, Kuroda’s total population exceeded 120 million, with steady exports of bulk habitat modules and industrial feedstock.

III. The Era of Mass Construction (3700–3920 CE)

Kuroda Federation became the primary supplier of orbital [[Infrastructure]] for neighboring [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].
Its collectivized freighters, known as Convoy Guilds, operated as semi-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] [[Arms]] of the Federation.
They ferried hab modules and raw materials under armed escort, earning Kuroda a reputation for reliability and solidarity.

Cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] solidified around the motto: “Every hand builds the home.”
Council politics grew ritualized—debate sessions streamed to every [[Worker]]’s terminal, decisions tallied by Planner AIs.

Security evolved into a Layered Point-Defense [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], optimized for convoy and station protection rather than offense.
Kuroda never built capital ships; instead, it fielded swarms of drones, torpedo buoys, and converted freighters capable of sudden mass-mobilization.

IV. Labor Conflicts and the Estela Crisis (3920–4000 CE)

The Federation’s prosperity bred tension.
When [[Estela Integralism]] began undercutting Kuroda contracts with cheaper agitprop-driven labor during the Equilon buildup, Kuroda’s dockworker syndicates called a general strike.

Flashpoint – The Estela Contract Dispute (3988 CE):
Estela’s propaganda arm infiltrated union channels, promoting “integration over insurrection.”
Strikes turned violent in the Koto Belt yards; [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] 200 killed after security drones misfired on protestors.
Planner AIs declared “Labor Phase Instability” and attempted autonomous mediation—a move seen by many as near-mutiny.

By 4000 CE:

Population stabilized around 180 million.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: Council Federation reinforced by [[Human]] audit ministries.

Planner AIs officially restricted to advisory functions, though most citizens still rely on them for life logistics.

Security: still defensive but hyper-efficient, capable of mobilizing millions of [[Workers]] into combat crews within days.

Security: Layered point-defense, militia swarms, and convoy protection fleets.

Symbol: Three interlocked cogs within a rising sun—“Each Turns All.”

Language & [[culture]]: Dock Creole mixed with East Asian loanwords; strong unionist traditions, songs of labor pride.

Legacy

The Kuroda Federation represents the industrial backbone of the outer [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]: disciplined, collectivist, and cautious of ideology.
Their pragmatic cooperation with AIs and [[Human]] councils allowed them to thrive without tyranny or collapse—until ideological incursions like Estela’s began exploiting their openness.
