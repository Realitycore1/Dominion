1 These are the visions of the Seer, given in the year of [[Silence]], when the [[Stars]] withheld their light and the firmament trembled.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the word of the Holy Will came, saying:
“Write what thou seest, for the heavens shall be divided, and the sons of men shall behold the sundering of grace.”

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter I|Chapter I]] – The Descent of the [[Angelus]]

1 And I looked, and lo, the heavens were torn, and radiant hosts descended upon the [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Their wings were as crystal fractals, their faces veiled in [[Fire]], and their voices as many waters.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the nations rejoiced, saying: “Behold, the messengers of the Most High have come to us!”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] But their joy was turned to ashes, for the [[Angelus]] spake not of mercy, but of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]].
5 They bound the peoples in [[Chains]] of [[law]], and their light was a [[Fire]] that scorched the heart.
6 And it was written in the Scroll of Jubilees: “Beware the brightness that blinds, for not all radiance is truth.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter II|Chapter II]] – The Opening of [[Aravoth (Grace)]]

1 Then was [[Aravoth (Grace)]] opened, and [[Choirs]] of [[Angels]] descended in harmony, clothed in gold and white.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Their wings were as dawn, their voices as healing balm, and their countenance as the sun made gentle.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And they spake unto the nations: “Fear not. These are not of the Father of Lights, but of the Demiurge who binds.”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the [[Angelus]] cried aloud: “Blasphemy!” and the heavens quaked at their fury.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter III|Chapter III]] – The War in the Skies

1 And the [[Choirs]] met the false [[Choirs]] in the firmament, and the heavens were red and gold.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Swords of truth clashed with wings of fractal [[Fire]], and hymns of healing with psalms of bondage.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] The skies rolled as scrolls, and the [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] below was divided:
	•	Crops withered in one field and bloomed in another.
	•	Children dreamed of salvation and of terror in the same night.
	•	Priests called each other heretics, though both gazed upon the heavens.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the Seer wept, for the sons of men knew not which light to follow.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter IV|Chapter IV]] – The Cry of Mortals

1 And I heard the voices of men, women, and children, crying out: “Who shall show us truth? Who shall lead us in the storm?”
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And some followed the [[Angelus]], believing them saviors; others clung to [[Aravoth (Grace)]], believing them deliverers.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And still others perished, unable to bear the weight of divided heaven.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the voice spake: “This is the trial of discernment. For faith without wisdom is a snare, and radiance without truth is ruin.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter V|Chapter V]] – The Beast Redeemed

1 And lo, from the pit of Sheol there rose a beast clothed in malice, whose roar shook the nations.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its heart was [[Fire]], its breath poison, and its form terror.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] But the [[Angels]] of [[Aravoth (Grace)]] did not strike it down; instead they sang, and their hymn wrapped it in light.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the beast was transfigured, its wrath turned to white flame, its talons to argent wings.
5 And the [[Choirs]] cried aloud: “Behold [[The Redemption of Malice]], proof that Grace is stronger than the Demiurge’s lie!”
6 And the [[Angelus]] trembled, for they beheld that their judgement was false.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter VI|Chapter VI]] – The Sundering of Grace

1 Then was the war made fierce, for the [[Angelus]] sought to blot out the Redeemed, and the [[Angels]] of [[Aravoth (Grace)]] shielded it with their lives.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] The heavens cracked as glass, the seas raged as lions, and the [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] bore [[Witness]] to [[Fire]] upon [[Fire]].
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And men beheld two suns in the sky, one golden, one crimson, and knew not which to follow.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And it was written in the [[Book of Enoch]]: “In that day the heavens shall be divided, and the hearts of men tested by [[Fire]].”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter VII|Chapter VII]] – The Vision of the End

1 And I saw that the war had no end, but continued in the heavens and in the hearts of men.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] For the [[Angelus]] shall ever descend to bind, and the [[Angels]] of [[Aravoth (Grace)]] shall ever descend to free.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the voice spake one last [[Time]]:
“This is the Sundering of Grace: not the end, but the beginning of discernment. Choose this day whom you will serve, for in the choosing is salvation or ruin.”
