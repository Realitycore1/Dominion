# Chapter 14 - Silent Chorus

## Timeline Function

War Years 9-10. Communications blackouts, classified casualty records, silenced witnesses, and censored broadcasts create a shared absence.

## Event Role

- Shows the cost of secrecy even when some secrecy is justified.
- Turns public mourning into an intelligence problem.
- Shows an AI medic refusing an unsafe hospital network after corrupted triage permissions become execution permissions.
- Makes a synthetic combatant request legal witness before deleting damaged memory containing civilians' final words.
- Forces an Immortal to ask a mortal witness to verify a recollection after suspected false-memory attack.
- Lets side characters carry the burden of missing names and delayed truth.
- Prepares the moral conditions for later archive politics.

## Consequence

The chorus is silent because too many people are dead, missing, classified, corrupted, or afraid that speaking from a damaged memory will make the damage authoritative.
