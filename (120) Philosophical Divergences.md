# (120) Philosophical Divergences

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapters 20-24.
- Manuscript date range: Years 95-122 After Landfall.

## What Happened

Philosophical divergence began when settlements stopped asking New Aurora for permission to interpret their own conditions. Atlovaia treated abyssal sound as schedule rather than hazard. Ormova treated heat as civic identity. The Commonwealth treated routes as home. Estelia treated recordkeeping as a moral duty. The F.S.S.R. treated stacked survival labor as political legitimacy.

The Second Hymnal symbolized the shift: even shared religion no longer sounded the same everywhere.

## What Resulted

- Regional philosophies began forming around terrain, labor, risk, memory, and law.
- Amber and Emilia saw that unity could not mean sameness.
- The slow death of Earth culture became the birth of Harminan plurality.
- Settlement identity moved from local custom to political worldview.

## Consequences

These divergences made later nation states believable. Flags came later. The deeper break was that people no longer agreed on what survival had taught them.
