# (0) Misjump Event

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part II, Chapters 9-12.
- Timeline function: begins the Mesic Era context before Harmina is reached.
- Dating note: this file preserves the legacy vault number. In the manuscript, the event occurs during the Ark exile before Harmina landfall.

## What Happened

After the Ferox Revolution and the collapse of the old regime, the surviving population was forced into flight. The O'Neill cylinder was refitted into a crude faster-than-light refugee vessel and became known simply as the Ark. Its jump removed the survivors from familiar political space and stranded them in the Void, beyond reliable synchronization with Colonial Union systems.

The misjump was not a clean migration. It was a wounded escape: improvised engines, overloaded shielding, failing systems, and a population still carrying revolutionary trauma. The Ark became both ship and state because no other structure remained close enough to matter.

## What Resulted

- The Ark Provisional Authority became the first continuity government of the displaced population.
- Citizenship, rationing, labor, medicine, and survival law were rewritten around vessel reality rather than Ferox law.
- The Ark-born generation began forming before Harmina, with children treating reactor vibration, recycled weather, and enclosed sky as normal.
- Amber Zohair's legitimacy shifted from revolutionary figure to survival anchor, even while her private life was damaged by grief and trauma.
- The Mesic Era inherited its first contradiction: humanity survived by leaving everything familiar behind.

## Consequences

The misjump made later Harminan identity possible. Without the Ark years, Harmina would have been treated as a colony site. Because of the Ark years, Harmina became a second chance, a shelter, and eventually a homeland.
