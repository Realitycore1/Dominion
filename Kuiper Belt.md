The Kuiper Belt is a circumstellar disc in the outer Solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]m|System]], extending from the orbit of Neptune (about 30 AU) to roughly 50 AU from the Sun. It is similar to the asteroid belt, but far larger and more massive.

It consists mainly of icy bodies and dwarf planets, remnants from the early formation of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]]gnty/System|System]]. Well-known Kuiper Belt Objects (K[[Haumea]]de[[Makemake]]WREA~1[[Quaoar]]EOSMUL~1/THEDAR~1/TOTUMV~1/Totum/VERSES~1/SUPERC~1/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/Colonial Union/Intrastellar/Celestial Objects/Haumea]], [[Makemake]], and [[Quaoar]].

The belt is the source of many short-period comets (orbital periods under 200 years). While the asteroid belt is mostly rocky, the Kuiper Belt is composed of frozen volatiles such as methane, ammonia, and water ice.

Estimated population:
	•	Likely hundreds of thousands of objects larger than 100 km.
	•	Trillions of smaller icy bodies.

Beyond the Kuiper Belt lies the scattered disc and, farther still, the [[Oort Cloud]].

The Kuiper Belt has been explored directly by the New Horizons spacecraft, which flew past Pluto and Arrokoth (2015–2019), providing the first up-[[Close]] study of this region.
