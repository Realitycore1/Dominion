# (2) Landing

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapter 13.
- Manuscript date: Year 0 After Landfall.

## What Happened

The first descent reached Aureth through crimson fog, corrosive rain, and unfamiliar terrain. The landing did not feel like conquest or arrival. It felt like exposure. Floodlights cut through fog, the Ark hung wounded above the clouds, and the first colonists stepped into a biosphere that had no human assumptions inside it.

New Aurora began as a perimeter, not a city: portable walls, floodlights, command shelters, medical tents, contaminated soil protocols, and frightened people trying to turn survival drills into routine.

## What Resulted

- Year 0 After Landfall became the primary Harminan dating anchor.
- New Aurora became the first city by necessity rather than design.
- The Ark remained visible overhead, reinforcing that landfall was not yet safety.
- Early rituals around first breath, soil testing, and perimeter work formed the emotional foundation of Harminan settlement culture.

## Consequences

Landing established the basic Harminan truth: humanity could live here only by adapting to the planet rather than forcing the planet to behave like Earth or Ferox.
