# Chapter XV - Covenant of Syl'an

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The Covenant of Syl'an creates the legal and symbolic foundation of the United Syl'an State.

## Event Role

- Defines shared defense, archive protection, debt settlement, transit law, reconstruction duties, and limits on future secession or emergency power.
- Translates wartime coalition into state architecture.
- Transforms Amber's emergency wartime coronation into a ratified long-horizon office: Sovereign of the Syl'Cothan States.
- Preserves present-facing civil government rather than allowing emergency continuity command to absorb ordinary law.
- Contains compromises that later generations call wisdom, betrayal, or both.
- Creates the bridge between Harminan wartime unity and later Dominion continuity doctrine.

## Consequence

The covenant ends one war by creating the state that will carry its scars forward.
