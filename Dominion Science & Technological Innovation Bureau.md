# Dominion Science & Technological Innovation Bureau

## Summary

The Dominion Science & Technological Innovation Bureau funds research, certifies laboratories, manages civilian innovation policy, and translates dangerous knowledge into usable public standards.

It sits between curiosity and caution.

## Mandate

The Bureau oversees:

- research grants;
- laboratory licensing;
- scientific ethics coordination;
- public technology standards;
- frontier research stations;
- university partnerships;
- civilian spinoff programs;
- dangerous-technology reporting;
- cross-agency technical review.

## Powers

It may certify labs, suspend unsafe research, publish technical standards, fund strategic science, and refer high-risk discoveries to specialized agencies.

## Limits

It cannot classify science merely to protect political embarrassment, steal private inventions without compensation, or route every breakthrough into military use by default.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Bureau through university funding, safety approvals, public science festivals, laboratory scandals, and technologies that quietly make daily life easier.

## Story Use

Use this agency for the tension between wonder and control. The Dominion needs discovery, but every discovery carries a memory of earlier disasters.
