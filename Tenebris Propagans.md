## Summary
the Spreading Darkness

Definition:
The frontier of decay, an active gradient where Confluencia filaments are still tearing apart—the unraveling edge of multiversal connectivity.
Unlike Profundis, which is static exhaustion, Propagans moves.

Physics:
	•	Acts as an entropy wave: once a region falls below critical Confluencial density, neighboring filaments begin to destabilize.
	•	Observable speed: approximately 10^{-37}c in bulk coordinates—imperceptible locally but cosmically catastrophic over aeons.
	•	Produces “law fatigue”: constants drift randomly; fine structure constant α can vary by parts per thousand across adjacent parsecs.

Phenomenology:
	•	Appears as ghost-light: faint blue-black auroras crawling along filaments, accompanied by sub-audible harmonic drops (−70 dB from base Aether tone).
	•	Communication lag increases; messages sent through the region desynchronize—words arrive before causes.

Metaphysical interpretation:

“The Void learns to walk.”
Dominion theologians describe it as the moment when the universe begins to forget how to hold itself together—a spiritual entropy as much as physical.
Some Aetheric sects claim Propagans is a cleansing wave, resetting corrupted verses.

Practical concern:
Containment fleets monitor its boundary with Replenish Thread amplifiers, attempting to re-energize dying filaments.  These operations are known as The Lantern Effort—lighting beacons along the dark frontier.
