Junk Worlds are hypothetical objec
ts in space that are composed entirely of discarded or unwanted materials, such as space debris, abandoned spacecraft, and other types of waste. Junk Worlds could potentially form as a result of various activities in space, including failed space missions, accidents, or the accumulation of debris over [[Time]]. In a scenario where Junk Worlds were to exist, they could pose significant risks to space travel and [[Human]] activity in the solar [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|system]]. Some of the potential impacts of Junk Worlds could include collisions with satellites and spacecraft, interference with communication and navigation [[Systems]], and even the formation of unstable or dangerous orbiting structures. While Junk Worlds are purely hypothetical at this point, they serve as a cautionary tale about the need for responsible space exploration and waste management. The creation of Junk Worlds highlights the importance of maintaining a clean and safe environment in space, and the potential consequences of neglecting to properly dispose of materials once they have been used. The scientific community has begun exploring the potential risks and solutions for Junk Worlds in recent years, with ongoing research aimed at developing technologies and strategies for managing space debris and preventing the formation of dangerous objects in space.
