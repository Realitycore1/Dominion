# (3180) Earth Orbital Ring Network

Earth's first complete orbital-ring freight loop enters service in 3180.

The dynamically supported equatorial ring connects:

- surface tether ports;
- orbital customs and quarantine;
- cargo and passenger transfer platforms;
- O'Neill-cylinder routes;
- shipyards and industrial yards;
- Gilgamesh and the wider Colonial Union traffic network.

Additional passenger, utility, and industrial loops are added later.

The network reduces dependence on individual launch corridors and makes routine high-volume movement between Earth and orbit possible.

It also concentrates planetary trade in infrastructure whose interruption can close ports across Earth. During the First Solar War, ring interfaces, tether ports, control stations, and maintenance junctions become military objectives while civilian crews attempt to preserve at least one public loop.
