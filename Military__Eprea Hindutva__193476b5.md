The Sword of Purity

The [[Eprea Hindutva]] military is not a citizen’s army like the [[Austro Guard]], but a caste-born war machine, commanded exclusively by the Kshatriya and sanctified by the Brahmic elite. It is both external shield and internal enforcer, ensuring the pyramid never trembles.
	•	Caste Structure of [[Arms]]
	•	Brahmic: Provide strategic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], cosmic philosophy of war, and approve campaigns. Their writings are treated as holy texts in officer academies.
	•	Kshatriya: Commanders, officers, and elite warriors. Every [[Child]] of this caste is drilled from birth in martial discipline.
	•	Vaishya: Quartermasters and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], handling supply [[Chains]], logistics, and war [[Production]].
	•	Shudra: Mass conscripts for labor battalions, fortress construction, and cannon fodder units.
	•	Dalit: Disposable [[Soldiers]] in suicide brigades or plasma-mining combat divisions, sent where casualties are certain.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of War
	•	Purity [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: The enemy is always framed as “impure.” Warfare becomes a holy cleansing act, not just strategy.
	•	Pyramid Defense: Worlds are fortified in concentric [[Layers]] mirroring caste order—inner [[Citadels]] for Brahmic elites, outer walls defended by Shudra conscripts.
	•	Relentless Punishment: Rebellions within the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] are crushed with ritualized violence—cities are razed as public warnings.
	•	Forces & [[Arms]]
	•	The Ashen Guard: Kshatriya shock troops, clad in blackened armor inscribed with caste sigils. Known for burning rebel settlements as “[[Ritual]] purges.”
	•	Fleet of the Five [[Systems]]: A multi-[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] navy, heavily stratified. Brahmic Observers ride on command ships to ensure ideological compliance.
	•	Dalit Brigades: Disposable conscript units deployed in asteroid assaults, reactor-sealing missions, and suicide strikes. Their deaths are celebrated as “ultimate proof of purity.”
	•	Symbols of Power
	•	Iron Sigils: Branded onto Kshatriya at initiation, glowing faintly with bio-ink visible under scanners.
	•	Victory Processions: After wars, captured enemies are paraded in [[Silence]], ending in [[Ritual]] execution overseen by Brahmic judges.
	•	War Hymns: Chanted by entire fleets before battle, invoking cosmic purity and condemning the impure.
	•	Internal Role
	•	The military doubles as police. Kshatriya patrols keep lower castes in line; mass punishments are routine.
	•	Every rebellion, strike, or whisper of dissent is met with overwhelming force—often public massacres carried out in ritualized form.

Where the [[Austro Guard]] fight because all bear the burden, the [[Eprea Hindutva]] fight because only some are born worthy of the sword—and the rest exist to feed it. Their military is both battlefield engine and domestic terror.
