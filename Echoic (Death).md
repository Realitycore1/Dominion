(The Echoic Continuum / The Field of After-Resonance)

Ontological Classification
	•	Plane Type: Transitional–Residual
	•	Authority Level: Custodial, non-judicial
	•	Relation to the Empty: Pre-custody threshold
	•	Relation to Aravoth / Sheol-Gehenna: Outside their jurisdiction
	•	Relation to Axioms: Weakly bound, degrading

⸻

Core Nature

The Echoic Plane is where existence lingers after life ends but before release.

It is not punishment.
It is not reward.
It is not judgment.

It answers a single question:

“What remains once the body stops?”

This plane exists because identity does not terminate cleanly. Consciousness leaves behind resonance—patterns of memory, intent, fear, love, habit.

Those patterns echo.

⸻

What an “Echo” Is (important distinction)

An Echo is not the soul.

It is:
	•	residual self-patterning,
	•	emotional inertia,
	•	memory loops without agency.

The true soul is already drifting toward the Empty.

The Echoic Plane holds what couldn’t let go.

⸻

Structure of the Plane

The Echoic Plane resembles fading overlays of mortal reality:
	•	ruined cities made of half-memory
	•	landscapes stitched from last moments
	•	rooms repeating endlessly
	•	horizons that recede when approached

Sound behaves strangely:
	•	whispers repeat without speakers
	•	footsteps echo before they occur
	•	names lose meaning when spoken too often

Time exists as decay, not sequence.

⸻

Inhabitants

1. Echoes

The most common form.
	•	Loop fragments of behavior
	•	Cannot grow or change
	•	Fade over time unless reinforced

Most harmless. Some tragic.

⸻

2. Wraiths

Echoes strengthened by:
	•	trauma,
	•	violence,
	•	obsession,
	•	unfinished purpose.

They are not evil by default.
They are over-concentrated memory.

⸻

3. Specters

Echoes that have begun feeding on external resonance.
	•	Draw energy from fear, grief, remembrance
	•	More coherent, more dangerous
	•	Often mistaken for true souls

They are parasites on attention.

⸻

4. Revenants

Rare.

Form when:
	•	identity resists dissolution,
	•	external force anchors the Echo,
	•	or improper resurrection occurs.

They are unstable by nature.

⸻

5. Psychopomp Traces

Not beings, but paths.
	•	Residual routes left by death-guides
	•	Appear as bridges, rivers, doors
	•	Lead toward the Empty

Following one ends the Echo.

⸻

Interaction with Other Planes

With Aravoth
	•	Angels do not judge here
	•	Only rare guides intervene to stabilize dangerous Echoes
	•	Excess interference risks soul capture

⸻

With Sheol-Gehenna
	•	Hell attempts extraction here
	•	This is where possession and damnation often begin
	•	Contracts exploit Echo confusion

This is why early death rituals matter.

⸻

With the Oneiric Plane
	•	Dreams often brush the Echoic Plane
	•	Nightmares can pull Echoes into dreams
	•	Grief dreams are partial overlaps

⸻

With the Empty

The Echoic Plane empties into the Empty.
	•	All Echoes decay eventually
	•	No Echo may cross back once released
	•	The Eidolon only intervenes if extraction occurs

⸻

Mortal Interaction

Mortals encounter the Echoic Plane via:
	•	hauntings,
	•	near-death experiences,
	•	trauma-induced visions,
	•	necromancy,
	•	cursed locations.

Long exposure causes:
	•	identity erosion,
	•	memory contamination,
	•	emotional fixation,
	•	becoming an Echo while alive (extremely dangerous).

⸻

Why the Echoic Plane Is Necessary

Without it:
	•	death would be instantaneous annihilation,
	•	resurrection would be trivial,
	•	ghosts would be souls,
	•	the Empty would be overcrowded.

The Echoic Plane is the cooling period of existence
