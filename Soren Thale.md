Birth: 2044 – Oslo, Norway
Profession: Cold-climate survivalist, polar researcher
Survival specialty: Arctic logistics, long-range navigation
Base: Skygrave Republics

Soren is the most feral of the [[Immortals]].
Friendly, but sharp-edged.
He spent decades alone in the Arctic after the Fall—talking to ice, wind, and memories.

Known as:
The Northbound
The Winter Walker

He is honest to a fault and incapable of political games.
Everyone trusts him.
And yet he trusts no one.
