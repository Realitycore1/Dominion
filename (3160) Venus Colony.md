# (3160) Venus Colony

The first permanent Venusian colony is established in 3160 as a network of floating aerostat habitats.

The colony does not begin on the surface. Its cities operate roughly fifty to sixty kilometers above it, where pressure and temperature are manageable compared with the ground below. An oxygen-nitrogen breathing mixture is buoyant in the dense carbon-dioxide atmosphere and serves as both habitat air and lifting gas.

## Floating-City Engineering

Venusian cities use:

- compartmented lift envelopes able to survive the loss of individual cells;
- acid-resistant ceramic, glass, and fluoropolymer exterior layers;
- continuous sulfuric-acid aerosol monitoring and automated wash systems;
- ballast, compressed atmosphere, and variable lift cells for altitude control;
- wind-lane forecasting and limited aerodynamic steering;
- high-altitude solar arrays;
- closed-loop water recovery supplemented by imported hydrogen and Belt ice;
- high-temperature robotic surface landers and recoverable sample systems.

The cities drift. A Venusian address therefore includes altitude band and drift circuit.

## Extraction Economy

Venusian industry processes atmospheric carbon dioxide, nitrogen, and sulfur compounds into oxygen, carbon feedstocks, fertilizers, industrial chemistry, and acid-resistant materials.

Robotic surface systems recover limited mineral samples and ore without exposing permanent human crews to surface conditions.

Water and hydrogen remain the colony's central strategic vulnerability.

## Civil Government

The Venus Colonial Committee is established with the colony. It governs lift certification, altitude and drift corridors, local docking, water and breathing reserves, civil law, and surface-robot licensing.

No employer may use control of lift, water, or docking access to expel a resident.
