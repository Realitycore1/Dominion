# (2210) The remaining 12 follow

## Canonical Event

Twelve additional people complete stable immortalization between 2205 and 2210:

1. Yasmin Sayegh;
2. Anika Mirek;
3. Amari Adebayo;
4. Samira al-Khatib;
5. Tomas Keene-Cole;
6. Nkiru Okafor-Bhandari;
7. Lucien Moreau;
8. Farid Volkov-Rao;
9. Asha Said;
10. Mateo Quispe Alvarez;
11. Keiko Tanaka;
12. Noa Bell-Reyes.

Each becomes associated with one of the twelve Great Cities. Association does not grant political office or sovereignty.

Elian Voss remains the thirteenth member of the original cohort until his death in 2232.

See: [[Sol Immortal Ensemble and Twelve Dynasties]]
