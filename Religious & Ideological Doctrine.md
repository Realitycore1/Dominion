The [[Imperial Truth|Imperial Truth]] is applied as [[law]]. Harmony is truth. Discord is treason.

## Role of the Empress
- When she speaks directly: decree is binding.  
- When silent: her will is interpreted by philosophical schools.

## Heresy
Heresy is discord:
- Disharmony = eccentricity.  
- Discord = destabilization.  
- Corruption = sabotage.  
- Silencing = erasure.
