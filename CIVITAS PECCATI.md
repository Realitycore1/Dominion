The City That Embraced Sin

Location:
Former megacity ruins reclaimed and ritualized.

Anchor:
A Guilt Sink — a massive Akashic drain that absorbs shame, allowing people to live without Sin feedback loops.

Doctrine:

“We are guilty. And therefore free.”

Society:
	•	Radical honesty
	•	No punishment, only consequence
	•	Confession is public and immediate
	•	No prisons

Relationship to Archontes:
Actively hostile — but shielded by conceptual ambiguity.

Narrative Role:
A direct challenge to Archontes Sin and Law.
