Classification

Death-Gaze Megafauna / Swamp-Born Malignancy / Biological Curse-Engine

⸻

CORE IDEA

The Catoblepas is death that looks down.

It is not a predator in the usual sense, nor a thinking malice. The Catoblepas kills by existing incorrectly—its gaze is lethal not because it intends harm, but because its physiology collapses the boundary between sight and extinction.

It does not hunt.
It invalidates proximity.

⸻

ORIGIN

Catoblepas arise where:
	•	wetlands stagnate beyond recovery
	•	Beast Rot or curse layering saturates ecosystems
	•	decay becomes self-sustaining
	•	death outpaces renewal

Some traditions claim:
	•	they are failed megafauna warped by rot
	•	living condensations of miasma
	•	proto-monsters predating sanitation
	•	nature’s immune response gone wrong

They are symptoms, not invaders.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

The Catoblepas is grotesque and heavy, built around its own burden.

Common traits:
	•	Massive, bloated body resembling a diseased bull or boar
	•	Short, pillar-like legs barely supporting its weight
	•	Hide thick, cracked, and pustule-scarred
	•	A grotesquely overdeveloped head, dragged downward by its own mass
	•	Neck so heavy it cannot raise its skull easily

Its eyes are small, dull, and sunken—but terminal.

The stench alone can kill lesser animals.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Catoblepas are lethargic, irritable, and territorial.
	•	Move slowly, conserving energy
	•	Graze on toxic vegetation, carrion, and mineral sludge
	•	React violently to disturbance
	•	Rarely pursue fleeing prey

Most deaths occur accidentally—travelers step into the wrong sightline.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Low.
	•	Animal-level awareness
	•	No strategy, no malice
	•	Reacts to pain and intrusion

Its danger lies in automatic lethality, not intent.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Thanoptic Gaze: eye contact causes immediate cellular collapse, necrosis, or soul-snap
	•	Miasma Aura: surrounding air becomes poisonous
	•	Grace-resistant: divine suppression blunts but does not nullify its effect
	•	Malice-compatible: corruption increases lethality radius

Mirrors, blindfolds, and indirect viewing are the only defenses.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Anchors death-zones in swamps and bogs
	•	Presence sterilizes surrounding land
	•	Other monsters avoid its territory
	•	Death of a Catoblepas leaves a long-lasting poisoned basin

They are ecological dead ends.

⸻

ALPHA CATOBLEPAS

Rarely, a Catoblepas stabilizes into an Alpha:

Traits:
	•	Gaze lethality extends through reflection and shadow
	•	Miasma expands into kilometer-scale kill zones
	•	Body partially fossilized while still alive
	•	Often worshipped as a death-god by rot cultists

An Alpha Catoblepas turns wetlands into permanent exclusion zones.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

Severe / Environmental Catastrophe

You do not fight a Catoblepas directly.
You contain, isolate, or erase the land around it.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Catoblepas represent:
	•	nature poisoned past correction
	•	death without intent
	•	the danger of neglect
	•	ecosystems that have given up

Where Khan Worms erase structure,
Catoblepas erase life.
