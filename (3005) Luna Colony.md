# (3005) Luna Colony

## Canonical Clarification

The 3005 marker records passage of the Lunar Colony Charter and the beginning of permanent-settlement construction.

It does not mark an inhabited, self-governing colony. Luna has possessed automated mines, mass drivers, factories, and rotating crew habitats since its industrial return, but those systems remain extensions of terrestrial institutions.

The charter requires:

- family-rated habitats;
- schools and clinics;
- civil records;
- burial and inheritance law;
- elected representation;
- redundant supply and rescue;
- a path from federal administration to local government.

The charter also forbids an employer from owning a resident's civil identity, but enforcement remains unresolved until a local government exists.

Gilgamesh opens as the Terran Federation's orbital home in 3007. The first permanent Lunar colony and its Lunar Colonial Committee are established in 3008.
