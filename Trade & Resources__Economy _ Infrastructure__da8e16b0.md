## Summary

GalFed trade is built around safe corridors, certified translation, restricted technology, and resource dependency. Trade is never just economic. It is a measure of trust.

## Details

Major exports:

- Restoration medicine.
- Aether shielding systems.
- Transit corridor access.
- Translation and legal canonization.
- Archive certification.
- Disaster response logistics.
- Educational accreditation.
- Climate and biosphere stabilization.
- Predictive governance tools.
- Founder-grade technical licenses.

Major imports:

- Species-specific medicines and biochemistries.
- Rare field-stable materials.
- Archive submissions.
- Cultural works for preservation.
- Local ecological data.
- Labor for corridor maintenance.
- Strategic minerals.
- Sensor access from frontier systems.
- Compliance data from stewarded worlds.

Restricted resources:

- Aether-reactive materials.
- Cognition lattice substrates.
- Founder-grade AI cores.
- Planetary-scale energy collectors.
- Temporal field components.
- Gate keys and route vaults.
- Restoration archives containing intact dead civilizations.

Trade enforcement uses denial more often than seizure. Worlds are "temporarily delayed," "awaiting recertification," "under safety review," or "ineligible for corridor priority." GalFed's most common embargo looks like paperwork.
