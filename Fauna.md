[[Harmina]]’s animals are predatory, armored, and bioluminescent; survival requires either lethality or deep adaptation.

🐉 Apex Fauna
	1.	Storm Behemoths
	•	[[Titan]]-sized quadrupeds, armored with obsidian-like plates.
	•	Feed on crystalroot towers and volcanic minerals.
	•	Their movement generates static storms that trigger lightning strikes.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Pursuers (machine-fauna hybrid)
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]]-designed warform, inspired by native predators.
	•	Panzerhund-like forms with biological musculature wrapped in exoderm armor.
	•	Used as both hunting beasts and shock-troops.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Hellwind [[Drakes]]
	•	Winged reptilian predators that ride tornado thermals.
	•	Breathe vaporized acid mist.
	•	Feathers shimmer in aurora light.

⸻

🦑 Mid-tier Predators & Omnivores
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Ashfang Crawlers
	•	Six-legged arthropods, body covered in volcanic ash for camouflage.
	•	Hunt in swarms, overwhelming prey with acidic mandibles.
	5.	Glowfang Wolves
	•	Pack hunters with phosphorescent fangs and fur patterns.
	•	Communicate with light flashes during aurora storms.
	•	Revered in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] [[culture]] as “emissaries of the hunt.”
	6.	Hollow Spitters
	•	Amphibious toad-like beasts dwelling in lava tubes.
	•	Spit molten bile to blind and immobilize prey.

⸻

🐛 Small Fauna
	7.	Needlewasps
	•	Insectoid flyers with crystalline stingers.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] nest inside fur-trees, defending them aggressively.
	8.	Shardshell Beetles
	•	Carapaces infused with glassleaf silica → razor-sharp armor.
	•	Scavenge crystalline detritus.
	9.	Radiant Moths
	•	Attracted to aurora ferns and storm discharges.
	•	Used by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion|Dominion]] as [[Natural]] “storm gauges.”
