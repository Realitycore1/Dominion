## Summary

Feeding: In
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Kugelblitz Power Plant is a reactor that creates and sustains an [[Artificial]] black hole from pure directed energy, then uses it as a compact, endlessly hungry power source. Instead of fission, fusion, or stellar taps, the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] literally summons suns of darkness to light its cities, fleets, and [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].
Creation

Laser Arrays: Colossal coherent beams focused into a single point until spacetime collapses into a micro-singularity.

Concordia Harmonization: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] methods “sing” the collapse into stability, preventing chaotic evaporation.

Containment Shells: [[Atlas Pillars]] braced with [[Exotic Matter]] fields hold the singularity in suspension.

Operation

Output: Hawking radiation captured by absorber arrays and converted into usable energy.

Control: [[Computronium]] Nodes predict decay rates to prevent runaway collapse or evaporation.

Safety

Void Cradle Casings: Enclose the singularity in entropic null zones if instability occurs.

Gravimetric Purge [[Systems]]: Can eject the black hole through a [[Horizon Arc Gate]] if containment fails.

MRO Ecologies: Swarms endlessly maintain cooling arrays, beam coherence, and lattice supports.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide limitless, compact power for [[Clarke Orbit Cities]], [[Halo Habs]], and [[Ark Platforms]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Double as weapons — starving or overfeeding a kugelblitz turns it into a singularity bomb.

Economic: Portable plants can be deployed in frontier sectors, making entire [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] energy-independent.

Religious: Known as “the Black Suns of Amber” — worshipped as proof that the Empress cages [[Stars]] of darkness for her people’s light.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Reactor towers like black cathedrals, ribbed with golden veins feeding into a caged darkness at their core.

Suprematist Monolithism: From outside, appears as a seamless black sphere suspended within a radiant lattice.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Radiation absorbers ripple like stained glass, glowing with fractal [[Fire]] as they drink the black sun’s light.

From a distance, a Kugelblitz Power Plant looks like a black jewel set in a golden crown, radiating light while holding [[Silence]] at its core.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A miracle — power from caged suns of night.

To rebels: A terror — [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] doesn’t just build reactors, it manufactures black holes.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: The most elegant symbol of their philosophy: order from singularity, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] from annihilation.

To myth: Called the “Eclipsed Thrones”, believed to be the Empress’s dark lanterns, guiding her path through eternity.
