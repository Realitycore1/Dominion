Classification: Elementals & Primals
Subtype: Abyssal Water Sovereigns
Status: Active, Deep-Realm Bound

⸻

Core Description

Marid are colossal, semi-humanoid or serpentine beings formed of living ocean, abyssal pressure, and moonlit darkness. Their bodies appear fluid but impossibly dense—water held together by will alone. Eyes glow like distant stars seen through miles of black sea.

Where Efreeti dominate through flame and tyranny, Marid dominate through inevitability.

They do not rage.
They outlast.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Marid originate where Fundamentum Elementalis (Water) overlaps with deep-time accumulation and pressure beyond mortal comprehension.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Fundamentum Elementalis (Water)
	•	Secondary Influence: Vital Expanse (endurance), Echoic Plane (memory)
	•	Realm Interface: Tempest Realms, Abyssal Oceans, R’lyeh-adjacent zones

Their water is not merely liquid—it is compressed history, bearing the weight of ages.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Marid are fully sapient, often vastly more intelligent than mortals.

Their cognition is:
	•	slow,
	•	layered,
	•	recursive.

They remember:
	•	drowned civilizations,
	•	forgotten species,
	•	oceans that no longer exist.

A Marid does not act quickly—but once it does, nothing short of cosmological force stops it.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Marid rule vast submerged dominions:
	•	abyssal palaces,
	•	trench-kingdoms,
	•	living coral citadels.

They cultivate:
	•	aquatic elementals,
	•	enslaved sea-beasts,
	•	mortal cult-cities built along coasts and deltas.

They rarely surface unless:
	•	summoned,
	•	provoked,
	•	or responding to ecological imbalance.

Floods, tsunamis, and slow continental erosion are often side effects of Marid politics, not accidents.

⸻

Interaction with Mortals

Marid bargains are:
	•	long-term,
	•	generational,
	•	and mercilessly fair.

A Marid may grant:
	•	prosperity,
	•	safe seas,
	•	forgotten knowledge—

—but always in exchange for:
	•	tribute,
	•	bloodlines,
	•	or eventual submersion.

Mortals who betray a Marid are not destroyed immediately.
They are erased slowly, culture first, land second, memory last.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Vulnerable to extreme desiccation or null-pressure fields
	•	Lose coherence in fire-dominant or pure malice realms
	•	Severely weakened in Dream-Wastes (memory dilution)
	•	Can be bound by ancient harmonic seals tied to tides and lunar cycles

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Efreeti: Ideological rivals; fire consumes, water endures
	•	Ifrit: Barely acknowledged—too brief, too loud
	•	Undines: Viewed as lesser kin or servants
	•	Leviathans: Sometimes allies, sometimes rivals
	•	Gods: Some Marid predate known deities and remember it

Marid do not seek worship.
They expect recognition.
