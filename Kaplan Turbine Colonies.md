Spinal Axis
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Kaplan Turbine [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] are habitats constructed directly into or around colossal turbine [[Systems]], designed to generate continuous energy while providing living space. They function as energy-cities, where power [[Production]] and habitation are inseparable.
Power Core

Oceanic: Turbine-arcologies anchored in deep-sea trenches, extracting energy from abyssal currents.

Atmospheric: Floating turbine-habs suspended in gas [[Giant]] jet streams or upper planetary atmospheres.

Stellar: Fusion-scale turbines spinning inside plasma flows near [[Stars]].

Void-Currents: At higher tiers, they can be built into [[Confluencia]] streams, drawing energy from exotic spacetime flows.

Colony Design

Rotors: Each blade a living district — with farms, cities, and [[Temples]] set into reinforced latticework.

Atlas Pillar Roots: Anchor the turbine against planetary or stellar stresses.

MRO Ecologies: Drone swarms maintain blade surfaces against erosion, plasma burns, or storm shear.

Output

Energy: Supplies entire sectors or arcology networks.

Habitability: Supports millions-to-billions in turbine-sprawl cities.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Powerhouses that double as metropolises — citizens literally live inside the engine that sustains them.

Economic: Anchor trade corridors by supplying constant energy, often linked to [[Beamed-sail commerce lanes]].

Religious: Known as “The Spinning Thrones” — turbines interpreted as wheels of the Empress’s [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]], turning endlessly to drive creation.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, the colony appears as a vast black wheel spinning against sea, sky, or star.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: The blades ripple with fractal geometry, flexing in impossible patterns as they spin.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The turbine glow carries glyphs across its surface — scripture written in motion, prayers visible as the wheel turns.

Standing inside one feels like living in the ribs of a god-machine — constant vibration, eternal hum, the world itself always in motion.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A home defined by rhythm — life measured in the hum of the turbine.

To myth: Called the “Amber Wheels”, said to be the turning gears by which the Empress drives the Omniverse itself.
