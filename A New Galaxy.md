The Galaxy is around 12.8 b years old, it has a mass of 6m ly and contains around 600 T [[Stars]].
2b ly away from [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] The the ark was stranded in space completely unknown to [[Man]]. As the Ark descends to the galaxy they name their new home the [[Odysseus Galaxy]]

The [[Odysseus Galaxy]] is located on the far end of the Horologium-Reticulum Supercluster which neighbors the [[Laniakea]] Supercluster (The home of the [[Milky Way Galaxy]]).

The Galaxy map is portioned into four quadrants (Alpha, Beta, Gamma and Delta). From the Core outward is listed in Roman alphabet, from left to right is listed in numeric. Each section is 10kly in length and 5kly in width.

The first colony named New Aurora began settlement of [[Harmina]] only to struck with harsh weather conditions. New Aurora is under constant snow and below freezing conditions. Under constant hardship the colonist slowly breakdown as the loss of hope quickly spreads. 

Soon groups of colonists begin to fraction and go their separate ways into the new world. Those that choose to stay still endured hardship but soon prospered and began to expand outwards.

The [[Flora]] of Harmonia was peculiar even when compared to [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] and Equilon. What resembled trees covered in lush red leaves, Instead of bark the trees were covered in fur with a deep space blue hue. The grass is a shade of crimson red.

Many environments are unique such as many of the beaches to [[Harmina]] or differ slightly such as sand, grass and rock formations which have a black metallic shade.
