> [!canon] Canon chronology
> [[Luna Colonial Development to Craterheart]] and [[Colonial Committees - Canonical Development 3008-3600]] control the current 3008–3600 chronology. Conflicting dates in the older development material below are retained only as legacy concepts.

Phase I: First Colony (c. 2120–2170)

Marius Hills Dome (First City)
	•	Built into a massive lava tube complex in the Marius Hills skylight.
	•	Function: survival colony and “civilizational backup.”
	•	Population: [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]],000 → 25,000 within two decades.
	•	Legacy: the “New Plymouth” of post-Relic War [[Humanity]].

⸻

Phase II: Polar Expansion (2170–2200)

[[Shackleton Base]] (South Pole)
	•	Leveraged water ice and nearly continuous sunlight.
	•	Became Luna’s primary power and water hub.

[[Peary Base]] (North Pole)
	•	Smaller than Shackleton, but strategically positioned for northern comms and ice mining.

[[Selene Comm Station]] & Mene Relay
	•	Orbital and surface comms [[Infrastructure]] built to support expanding settlements.

⸻

Phase III: Industrial & Research [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] (2200–2250)

[[Aristarchus Plateau]] City
	•	Settlement focused on thorium extraction, rare [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] mining, and geological research.
	•	Known for “dust towers” of regolith-processing spires.

South Pole–Aitken Basin Outposts
	•	Mining complexes exploiting exposed mantle and exotic heavy metals.
	•	Dangerous but profitable frontier.

Lunar Test Domes & P.H.U. Clusters
	•	Scattered across mid-latitudes for habitation trials, including uplift-integration modules.

⸻

Phase IV: Governance and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] (2250–2300)
	•	Luna Colonial Committee (LCC):
	•	Established under the oversight of [[Gilgamesh Station]] / [[Colonial Union]].
	•	Coordinated resource distribution, arbitration between [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]], and trade with [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].
	•	Functioned as an administrative extension of the CU rather than sovereign authority.
	•	Cultural [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]:
	•	By late 2200s, “Lunars” saw themselves as distinct.
	•	Generations born under domes or in tubes felt alien to [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] politics.
	•	Common ethos: self-reliance, engineering pragmatism, suspicion of relic-myth [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]].
