Definition
Alteration is the reshaping of existing matter, energy, or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] into new forms. It does not create from nothing (that’s [[Creation magic]]) nor annihilate into void (that’s Destruction), but reconfigures the given world into another shape.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Malleability – all things have hidden potential; alteration taps it.
	Conservation – matter and essence are not destroyed but redirected.
	
Sympathetic Resonance – likeness invites transformation: frog to [[Man]], lead to gold, flesh to stone.
	Anchors – transformations must stabilize or they collapse violently (rebound effect).

⸻

Techniques of Alteration
	•	Transmutation – changing one substance to another (alchemy, lead to gold, stone to flesh).
	•	Polymorphy – reshaping bodies: [[Humans]] into beasts, giants into birds, self-shifting.
	•	Metamorphic Enhancement – strengthening attributes: stone skin, iron bones, wings of flame.
	•	Temporal Alteration – slowing or hastening processes (aging, decay, regrowth).
	•	Environmental Alteration – altering weather, terrain, or gravity on a local scale.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] Weaving – changing not only form but concept (a blade enchanted to “always be sharp,” or a person altered so they are “always lucky”).

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: [[Elves]] once mastered alteration in the Baletish states (before Velda conquest), using it for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Military/Technology/Technology|Technology]] and comfort. Many drifted into [[Blood Magic]] when transmutation could not sustain their ambitions ￼.
	•	Concordia: Alteration is woven into the tier [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] — especially at Argent and Amber levels, where shapeshifting and elemental reforging become common practice. Adamant-tier mages are said to alter the laws of density and hardness themselves.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]] Labs: Specializes in axiomatic alteration, where laws of a [[Verse]] are “tuned” — changing what counts as mass, energy, or causality within a controlled sandbox.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Practices harmonic alteration — resonant magic that tunes reality like an instrument, shifting the “frequency” of matter into more useful states.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Demiurgic alteration often corrupts; mortals are twisted into mockeries, cities “recast” into fractal ruins, landscapes rewritten as prisons.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Practicality – alteration is useful in everyday life (healing, farming, metalworking).
	•	Universality – nearly every [[culture]] rediscovers it independently.
	•	Versatility – from alchemy to shapeshifting, it adapts to all other [[Domains]].

⸻

Perils
	•	Instability – poorly anchored changes unravel: polymorphed creatures may explode back into their original form.
	•	Overreach – trying to alter fundamental laws destabilizes whole [[Regions]] (e.g., Concordian tessellated exoderm experiments).
	•	Loss of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] – repeated self-alteration can erode memory, personality, or soul-continuity.
	•	Contamination – partial transformations create hybrids and monstrosities.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Wards of Constancy – magic that “locks” forms and prevents polymorph effects.
	•	Null Fields – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] suppression zones revert alterations to [[Natural]] states.
	•	Blood Anchors – tying a mage’s form to their own blood, preventing forced metamorphosis.
	•	Stone Sigils – dwarven geomantic stabilizers that prevent environmental shifts.
