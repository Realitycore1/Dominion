	•	Chthono- → deep, hidden, underworld (used in geology & biology)
	•	-vermis → worm

This immediately frames them as:

Deep-space / deep-structure organisms, not predators in the usual sense.

Formal Species Descriptor (common baseline)

Chthonovermis vacuumalis
(void-dwelling deep vermiform organism)

Optional sub-types if you want taxonomy later:
	•	C. graviphaga – feeds on gravitational shear / tidal gradients
	•	C. radiovora – consumes high-energy radiation fields
	•	C. lithotica – interfaces with asteroids, crusts, megastructures

⸻

II. Dominion / Operational Terminology

VW-Class Entities

(Void Worms)

Sub-designations:
	•	VW-Passive – migratory, non-interactive
	•	VW-Reactive – responds to ships, reactors, or FTL wakes
	•	VW-Catastrophic – interacts with spacetime structures

Dominion doctrine classifies them as hazardous phenomena, not fauna.

Any report calling one an “animal” gets quietly re-reviewed.

⸻
