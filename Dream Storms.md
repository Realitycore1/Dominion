# Dream Storms

## Ontological Classification
- **Phenomenon Type:** Oneiric Weather / Self-Manifesting Dream Event
- **Plane:** [[Oneiric Plane]]
- **Authority:** Non-sovereign, naturally emergent
- **Primary Risk:** Dream overwrite, archetypal destabilization, material imposition
- **Governance:** Monitored by the [[Council of Archetypes]] under the jurisdiction of the [[Lord of Dreams]]

---

# Core Definition

Dream Storms are unstable concentrations of dream matter, archetypal pressure, symbolic contagion, and cross-planar interference.

They occur when dreams cease behaving as private or localized experiences and begin acting as a self-propagating environment.

A Dream Storm does not merely contain chaos.

It is:

> **dream-chaos attempting to become reality.**

Most dreams dissolve, recur, or return to the consciousness that generated them.

Dream Storms do not.

They persist.

They absorb.

They overwrite.

---

# Fundamental Principle

A normal dream belongs to the dreamer.

A Dream Storm emerges when the boundary between individual dreaming and shared Oneiric space begins to fail.

Once sufficiently dense, the storm may:
- overwrite nearby dreams
- absorb symbolic structures
- reroute dreamers into itself
- merge incompatible archetypes
- distort established Oneiric geography
- generate independent dream entities
- exert pressure on the [[Material Continuum]]

The greater the storm becomes, the less dependent it is on any single dreamer.

At sufficient scale, it becomes a **self-manifesting dream ecology**.

---

# Formation

Dream Storms usually form from several interacting causes.

## 1. Natural Dream Congestion

Large populations experiencing similar emotional states can produce extreme Oneiric pressure.

Common triggers include:
- war
- plague
- famine
- mass grief
- civilizational collapse
- apocalyptic expectation
- ecstatic religious experience
- collective terror
- mass displacement

Millions or billions of similar dreams begin flowing toward the same archetypal basin.

If that flow becomes too intense, the region destabilizes.

---

## 2. Cross-Planar Interference

The [[Oneiric Plane]] does not exist in isolation.

Interference from other planes may alter dream behavior.

### [[Aravoth]]
May generate:
- revelation storms
- overwhelming lucidity
- dreams of impossible harmony
- recursive beauty
- symbolic saturation

### [[Sheol-Gehenna]]
May generate:
- torment loops
- predatory nightmares
- guilt labyrinths
- infernal archetype contamination
- persistent terror dreams

### [[Fundamentum Elementalis]]
May generate:
- flood dreams becoming literal water systems
- firestorms formed from panic imagery
- living stone landscapes
- atmospheric dream phenomena

### [[Axiomatic Stratum]]
Damage or instability may cause:
- impossible dream logic
- identity fragmentation
- symbolic contradiction
- regions where dreams lose causality entirely

---

# 3. Archetypal Destabilization

The [[Council of Archetypes]] maintains the flow of recurring dream patterns.

When an archetypal domain becomes overfed, disrupted, or contaminated, its influence may spill into adjacent regions.

Examples:

- [[The Hunter]] causes pursuit dreams to consume unrelated dreamscapes.
- [[The Judge]] begins turning all dreams into trials.
- [[The Beast]] transforms otherwise peaceful dreams into predator-prey structures.
- [[The Threshold]] causes dreamers to become trapped between states of arrival and departure.
- [[The Returning Dead]] may overwhelm grief-dream systems and produce recursive ancestral encounters.

Archetypes do not own these dreams.

They act as concentrations around which similar dreams gather.

---

# 4. External Manipulation

Dream Storms may also be created deliberately.

Known or theoretical causes include:
- psychic warfare
- ritual dream amplification
- memetic weapons
- magical experimentation
- relic activation
- mass lucid dreaming
- hostile planar entities
- corrupted archetypal beings
- civilizations attempting to mine Oneiric matter
- artificial consciousness networks generating synchronized dream-patterns

Artificial storms are considered especially dangerous because they may lack normal self-limiting mechanisms.

---

# Structure of a Dream Storm

Most mature Dream Storms contain several recognizable regions.

## Outer Distortion Front

The first region encountered.

Effects include:
- shared symbols
- déjà vu
- repeating dreams
- altered sleep cycles
- subtle environmental inconsistency

The storm may not yet be visible as a coherent phenomenon.

---

## Archetypal Convergence Zone

Multiple dream patterns begin collapsing together.

Examples:
- schools becoming battlefields
- childhood homes becoming cathedrals
- oceans appearing inside bedrooms
- dead relatives serving as judges
- predators speaking in familiar voices

Identity and symbolism become unstable.

---

## Dream Eye

At the center of larger storms exists a zone of apparent calm.

This region often contains:
- the original dream pattern
- a dominant symbolic structure
- an emergent dream entity
- a repeating event
- an external contaminant
- or no identifiable source at all

Some Dream Eyes develop awareness.

---

# Effects Within the Oneiric Plane

Dream Storms can radically alter Oneiric geography.

They may:
- consume local dreamlands
- fracture pathways between archetypal regions
- redirect dream traffic
- erase established landmarks
- merge incompatible environments
- trap dreamers in recurring loops
- create temporary new territories

After major storms, the landscape may never fully return to its previous state.

---

# Effects on Dreamers

Individuals exposed to a Dream Storm may experience:

- identical dreams shared with strangers
- recurring nightmares
- false awakenings
- involuntary lucid dreaming
- sleep paralysis
- memory contamination
- symbolic fixation
- altered fears
- altered desires
- compulsive dream repetition
- inability to distinguish dream memory from waking memory

Prolonged exposure may allow storm-patterns to persist after waking.

---

# Material Continuum Imposition

The greatest danger occurs when a storm develops enough coherence to affect waking reality.

This process is known as:

## Oneiric Imposition

Possible manifestations include:
- mass hallucination
- impossible weather
- symbolic architecture appearing physically
- temporary manifestation of dream creatures
- altered local gravity
- spontaneous language convergence
- sleep epidemics
- recurring spatial loops
- shared waking visions
- spontaneous cult formation
- reality behaving according to dream logic

At extreme levels, entire regions of the [[Material Continuum]] may become temporarily indistinguishable from the Oneiric Plane.

---

# Classification

## Class I — Murmur Storm

Minor symbolic disturbance.

Typical effects:
- shared motifs
- recurring dreams
- localized sleep disruption

Usually resolves naturally.

---

## Class II — Archetypal Storm

Dominated by a single recurring dream-pattern.

Examples:
- pursuit
- falling
- judgment
- exposure
- transformation
- return of the dead

May destabilize a regional dreamland.

---

## Class III — Convergence Storm

Multiple archetypes collapse into one another.

Highly unpredictable.

Dream logic becomes increasingly nonlinear.

---

## Class IV — Imposition Storm

The storm begins influencing waking reality.

Requires active intervention by the [[Council of Archetypes]].

---

## Class V — Self-Manifest Storm

A storm capable of sustaining itself independently of its original dreamers.

It may:
- consume other dreams
- create independent entities
- persist between sleep cycles
- reshape Oneiric geography
- actively seek entry into waking reality

These are considered major existential hazards.

---

# Council Response

The [[Council of Archetypes]] does not control what beings are allowed to dream.

Its role is to maintain the integrity of shared Oneiric space.

During Dream Storm events, the Council may:
- redirect dream currents
- isolate unstable regions
- establish symbolic barriers
- evacuate dreamers
- separate colliding archetypes
- quarantine autonomous dream entities
- prevent material imposition
- sever hostile cross-planar connections

The Council governs flow.

It does not own the dreams flowing through it.

---

# Role of the Lord of Dreams

The [[Lord of Dreams]] holds sovereign jurisdiction over the shared structures of the Oneiric Plane.

The Lord does not own dreams.

The Lord does not dictate what consciousnesses may imagine.

Its authority applies when private dreaming becomes a shared ontological hazard.

Core principle:

> **The Lord of Dreams rules where dreams meet, not those who dream them.**

---

# Dreambreaks

Dreambreaks are symbolic containment structures used to interrupt storm propagation.

They may appear as:
- walls
- rivers
- silent corridors
- blank skies
- labyrinths
- sealed doors
- fields without symbols

Their purpose is not to destroy dreams.

Their purpose is to prevent one dream from consuming another.

---

# Dream Wreckage

After major Dream Storms, remnants may persist.

These include:
- abandoned dream cities
- fossilized nightmares
- severed memory roads
- broken archetypal masks
- empty houses belonging to no dreamer
- landscapes that repeat fragments of extinct dreams

Such regions may remain unstable for centuries.

---

# Ecological Role

Dream Storms are destructive, but not entirely unnatural.

They serve as pressure-release events within the Oneiric Plane.

Small storms:
- redistribute symbolic pressure
- prevent archetypal stagnation
- break overloaded dream basins
- generate new symbolic structures

Only when they become self-propagating or begin imposing on waking reality do they become existential threats.

---

# Canon Principle

> **Dreams are private in origin, shared in resonance, and dangerous when they cease recognizing the difference.**

---

# Summary

Dream Storms are the natural and unnatural weather systems of the [[Oneiric Plane]].

They emerge when dream density, archetypal pressure, or planar interference overwhelms the normal flow of dreamspace.

Most pass.

Some reshape the plane.

A few learn to continue dreaming without a dreamer.

Those are the storms the Citadel fears.
