# Shared Origin of the Oneiric and Omen Planes

## Overview

The [[Oneiric Plane]] and the [[Plane of Omens]] were not always separate.

In the earliest stages of metaphysical development, they existed as a single prefigurative continuum in which imagination, anticipation, instinct, and emerging significance were not yet distinct enough to form separate planes.

Across [[Tempus Profundis]], these functions differentiated.

What began as one shared substrate gradually divided into two autonomous but closely related realities:

- the [[Oneiric Plane]], concerned with possibility, symbolic formation, dream, and proto-expression;
- the [[Plane of Omens]], concerned with convergence, significance, causal pressure, and pre-event attention.

Their separation was not a sudden catastrophe.

It was an ontological differentiation.

> **They were once one plane because the earliest minds could not distinguish imagining what might happen from sensing what was about to matter.**

---

# The Primordial Continuum

## The Prefiguration

The oldest known state of the combined plane is commonly described as:

> **The Prefiguration**

Other possible names include:
- The First Veil
- The Proto-Phenomenal Continuum
- The Undivided Reverie
- Somnium Praesignum
- The Dream-Omen Continuum

The Prefiguration was neither fully dream nor fully omen.

It was the realm in which:
- primitive sensation accumulated;
- instinct anticipated danger;
- memory recombined with expectation;
- possibility developed symbolic shape;
- significance began appearing before events resolved.

There was no clean separation between:

> imagining danger

and

> sensing that danger was approaching.

For early consciousness, these were effectively aspects of the same process.

---

# Why the Planes Diverged

The combined continuum became increasingly unstable as consciousness and causality grew more complex.

Dreaming developed functions that were not dependent on immediate causal pressure.

It began to support:
- memory recombination;
- impossible scenarios;
- symbolic identity;
- self-reflection;
- archetypal structures;
- shared dream environments;
- unrealized possibilities.

At the same time, another class of phenomenon emerged independently.

Causal reality began producing:
- converging outcomes;
- narrowing possibilities;
- pre-event significance;
- probability bias;
- symbolic correspondence;
- pressure toward increasingly constrained futures.

These two processes became less compatible over deep metaphysical time.

The shared plane was being pulled toward two different functions.

---

# The Two Emerging Tendencies

## Oneiric Tendency

The proto-Oneiric function increasingly asked:

> **What could take form?**

It became associated with:
- imagination;
- memory;
- symbolism;
- possibility;
- identity;
- dreams;
- archetypes;
- unrealized realities.

---

## Omen Tendency

The proto-Omen function increasingly asked:

> **What is becoming difficult to avoid?**

It became associated with:
- convergence;
- causal pressure;
- pre-event significance;
- narrowing futures;
- attention;
- probability bias;
- consequential thresholds.

---

# The Symbolic Schism

As symbolic consciousness became more sophisticated, the distinction between the two functions became impossible to ignore.

Dreams were capable of creating things with no current causal basis.

Omens, however, became increasingly tied to real causal structures already developing within the [[Material Continuum]].

This produced what later scholars call:

> **The Symbolic Schism**

During this period:
- dreams became increasingly generative;
- omens became increasingly diagnostic;
- symbols began serving different purposes in each plane;
- dream geography became increasingly archetypal;
- omen structure became increasingly relational and causal.

The planes were still connected, but no longer functionally identical.

---

# Harmonic Fission

Across [[Tempus Profundis]], the distinction eventually became sufficiently stable that the shared continuum separated into two independent ontological harmonics.

This event is commonly described as:

> **Harmonic Fission**

It was not a single chronological moment.

Instead:

> **the distinction became increasingly true until reality treated the two functions as separate planes.**

The process occurred at a level deeper than ordinary chronological time.

This is why no single date or event can accurately define when the split occurred.

---

# The Sister Planes

After Harmonic Fission, the two planes became fully autonomous.

They are best understood as sibling realities descended from the same primordial continuum.

| Shared Function | [[Oneiric Plane]] | [[Plane of Omens]] |
|---|---|---|
| Possibility | Expands possibilities | Detects narrowing possibilities |
| Symbol | Generates symbolic meaning | Expresses significance through symbols |
| Consciousness | Major shaping force | Major interpreter, not always required |
| Causality | Loosely constrained | Strongly dependent upon causal development |
| Dreams | Native phenomenon | Common delivery system |
| Future | Imagines alternatives | Detects convergence |
| Geography | Archetypal recurrence | Causal correspondence |
| Primary Danger | Manifesting imagination | Self-reinforcing anticipation |

---

# Residual Harmonic Affinity

The separation was never absolute.

The [[Oneiric Plane]] and the [[Plane of Omens]] retain deep harmonic compatibility because both developed from the same primordial substrate.

This explains why dreams remain one of the most common methods through which omens are perceived.

The relationship typically functions as follows:

1. Causal pressure develops.
2. The [[Plane of Omens]] registers the convergence.
3. Omen pressure interacts with the [[Oneiric Plane]].
4. The Oneiric translates that pressure into symbolic imagery.
5. Dreamers experience recurring or shared dreams.
6. Those dreams may influence behavior.
7. Behavior changes the causal field.
8. The omen pattern adjusts accordingly.

This creates a dynamic relationship rather than a deterministic one.

---

# Omen and Dream Are Not the Same

A dream containing an omen is not itself the omen.

The distinction is:

> **Omen provides pressure. Oneiric provides imagery.**

For example:

A city approaches collapse.

The [[Plane of Omens]] registers:
- narrowing futures;
- rising instability;
- escalating causal convergence.

The [[Oneiric Plane]] may translate this pressure into dreams of:
- collapsing towers;
- dark water;
- broken clocks;
- dead birds;
- bells ringing underwater;
- roads ending at walls.

These images are symbolic interpretations.

They are not guaranteed literal predictions.

---

# Shared Ancestry of Archetypes and Harbingers

The shared origin of the planes explains why certain [[Council of Archetypes|Archetypes]] and Omen entities resemble one another.

Before Harmonic Fission, a single proto-structure may have embodied both:
- the experience of fear;
- and anticipation of approaching danger.

After separation, these functions could divide.

For example:

### Proto-Pattern
Fear of approaching predator.

### Oneiric Descendant
[[The Hunter]] or [[The Pursued]].

### Omen Descendant
A Harbinger associated with pursuit, danger, or imminent predation.

They share ancestry without being the same being.

---

# Relict Convergence Zones

Some regions retain traces of the original Prefiguration.

These zones are places where the separation between dream and omen remains incomplete.

Possible names include:
- Prefigurative Wells
- Vestigial Veils
- Dream-Omen Confluences
- Old Correspondences
- Praesomnia
- First-Veil Remnants

Within these regions:
- dreams may become unusually predictive;
- omens may become highly symbolic;
- anticipation may alter dream geography;
- shared dreams may form almost instantly;
- symbolic actions may influence causal convergence;
- nightmares may produce measurable probability shifts.

These regions are considered unstable.

---

# Dream-Omen Confluences

Rare events can temporarily recreate aspects of the old combined continuum.

These are known as:

> **Dream-Omen Confluences**

They occur when strong Oneiric activity and extreme Omen convergence begin feeding one another.

A typical feedback sequence is:

1. A major omen develops.
2. Large populations begin experiencing similar dreams.
3. The shared dreams influence behavior.
4. Behavior strengthens the causal convergence.
5. Stronger convergence intensifies the omen.
6. The omen further amplifies the dreams.
7. The dreams begin imposing upon waking reality.
8. Reality increasingly resembles the imagined event.

This is not fate.

It is a:

> **causal-symbolic runaway loop.**

Dream-Omen Confluences are among the most dangerous natural feedback phenomena associated with either plane.

---

# Relationship to Dream Storms

[[Dream Storms]] are normally Oneiric phenomena.

They overwrite local dream structures and can eventually affect the [[Material Continuum]].

When a Dream Storm overlaps with strong Omen pressure, its behavior changes.

The storm may begin:
- selecting symbols associated with actual causal convergence;
- synchronizing dreamers toward the same interpretation;
- altering behavior in predictable directions;
- intensifying real-world instability.

This produces a particularly dangerous class of Dream-Omen Confluence.

---

# Philosophical Distinction

The split between the planes reflects a deeper distinction between two aspects of unrealized reality.

The [[Oneiric Plane]] represents:

> **possibility becoming imaginable.**

The [[Plane of Omens]] represents:

> **possibility becoming consequential.**

Before their separation, these functions were entangled.

After Harmonic Fission, they became distinct enough to develop independent ecologies, entities, and laws.

---

# Historical Sequence

## I. The Prefiguration
Dream and omen remain undifferentiated.

Primitive sensation, instinct, anticipation, memory, and proto-symbolism occupy a shared phenomenological substrate.

---

## II. The First Divergence
Memory, identity, and increasingly complex causality begin pulling the continuum into different functions.

Dreaming becomes more generative.

Omen activity becomes more causally specific.

---

## III. The Symbolic Schism
Dreams develop:
- archetypes;
- impossible scenarios;
- symbolic environments;
- shared imagery.

Omens become increasingly tied to:
- actual causal pressure;
- convergence;
- narrowing futures;
- pre-event significance.

The shared substrate begins separating.

---

## IV. Harmonic Fission
Across [[Tempus Profundis]], the two functions stabilize into distinct ontological harmonics.

The [[Oneiric Plane]] becomes the realm of proto-expression and unrealized possibility.

The [[Plane of Omens]] becomes the realm of convergence and pre-event significance.

---

## V. The Age of the Sister Planes
Both planes become fully autonomous while retaining deep residual affinity.

Dreams remain one of the principal channels through which Omen pressure becomes perceptible to conscious beings.

---

# Core Canon Principles

> **The Oneiric Plane and the Plane of Omens were once one prefigurative continuum.**

> **Their separation emerged gradually across Tempus Profundis rather than through a single catastrophic event.**

> **Oneiric became the field of possibility.**

> **Omen became the field of significance.**

> **Omen provides pressure. Oneiric provides imagery.**

> **Dreams may carry omens without becoming prophecy.**

> **The planes are autonomous, but never entirely unrelated.**

---

# Canon Summary

The [[Oneiric Plane]] and the [[Plane of Omens]] are sibling planes descended from a primordial state known as [[The Prefiguration]].

In the earliest ages, imagination and anticipation were functionally indistinguishable.

As consciousness, symbolism, and causality became more complex across [[Tempus Profundis]], the shared continuum differentiated.

The Oneiric increasingly became the domain of:

> **what might take form.**

The Plane of Omens increasingly became the domain of:

> **what is beginning to matter.**

Their separation created two independent but permanently related planes.

Even now, their ancient connection remains visible whenever an approaching event first appears not as a prediction, but as a dream.
