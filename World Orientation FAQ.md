# World Orientation FAQ

This page answers the setting-level questions a reader is likely to ask before choosing a subject portal. Blank answers are intentional canon placeholders.

## What is the Dominion?

Answer:
The Dominion is one of the archive's central civilizational anchors. It connects character material, government, military structure, historical development, and large-scale cosmological stakes.

Read next:
- [[Civilizations Portal]]
- [[Characters Portal]]

## Is the Dominion good, evil, necessary, or something more complicated?

Answer:
The archive frames the Dominion as more complicated than a simple good-or-evil faction. It should be read as a civilization and survival state with power, ideology, protection, violence, continuity, and moral tension all present at once.

Read next:
- [[The Dominion as Survival State|The Dominion as Survival State]]

Answer:
Amber Andreia Zohair is a major Tier 1 character anchor for the archive. She is one of the clearest starting points for readers who prefer entering the setting through people before institutions or cosmology.

Answer:
The Pleroma is the archive's highest-level frame for existence and specialist cosmology. New readers should approach it through the Tier 4 route rather than as their first page.

## What is a Verse?

Answer:
A Verse is one complete, self-contained reality. It is larger than a world or galaxy; it is the enclosed reality structure that contains its own continuity, rules, and timeline structure.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is a Multiverse?

Answer:
A Multiverse is a collection of Verses that share similar foundational assumptions, such as related physics, continuity patterns, or structural behavior.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is Eos?

Answer:
Eos is the local multiverse to which Totum belongs. It is the home multiverse of the setting's primary human continuity.

Read next:
- [[VI - Q&A/Part 1|Reader Q&A - Part I]]

## What is Totum?

Answer:
Totum is the local Verse that contains the primary human continuity. In the existing Q&A, it is described as the setting's local verse within Eos.
