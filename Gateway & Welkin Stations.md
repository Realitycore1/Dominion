The space stations still serve their old purpose of experimentation and the product of knowledge. With more stations being commissioned each can specialize and also can accommodate more permanent residents.

The gateway station serves as the commercial station for civilian and corporate use. The welkin station serves as the primary R&D facility for both federal and corporate [[Entities]]. Both stations are zero G stations and operate within low terra orbit.
