## Rare & Specialized Castes
- **Siege Strains ("Hive Behemoths")** – colossal war-beasts for planetary assaults.  
- **Void Navigators** – translucent caste with oversized entanglement organs for FTL navigation.  
- **Aquatic Variants ("Brine Swarmers")** – finned, gilled forms adapted to ammonia oceans.  
- **Sporecasters** – plague-brooders, releasing pathogen clouds.  
- **Memory Keepers ("Lore-Caste")** – delicate priestly archivists preserving hive memory.  
- **Strain-Hybrids ("Chimeric Castes")** – unstable but potent multi-caste fusions.  
- **Apex Brood ("Highborn Daughters")** – princess-like lieutenants and future [[Queens]].
