Technocratic Constitutional Confederation
Founding Principle: Balance between individual liberties, technological advancement, and interstellar cooperation.
	•	Initial Founders: Post-[[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]] diaspora settlers from multiple factions seeking neutral ground.
Branches of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]
	•	Commonwealth Council – a multi-body legislative assembly comprised of elected representatives from Proxima B, Prime, and the orbital habitats.
	•	The Archive – an AI-advised judicial body with oversight on ethics, [[Rights]], and advanced technologies.
	•	The Directorate – an executive body composed of scientists, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]], and logisticians selected by meritocratic council votes.
Ethos: “Truth through Unity, Liberty through Knowledge”
	•	Civic Traits:
