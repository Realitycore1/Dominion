# The Inquisition / Integrity Corps

## Current Canon Guardrail

The Inquisition, publicly designated the Integrity Corps, is a continuity-threat bureau, not ordinary police and not a lawful tool for suppressing dissent.

Its authority is legitimate only while bounded by warrants, courts, Inspector-General review, legislative oversight, whistleblower protection, audit logs, and continuity-record preservation. Lawful dissent, private worship, minority culture, criticism of Amber, and criticism of the Royal Government are not valid targets without concrete evidence of continuity risk.

Memory erasure, indefinite detention, political policing, torture, parallel courts, and mass surveillance without individualized warrants are prohibited in current canon. If those practices occur, they represent corruption, emergency abuse, or institutional failure.

See: [[Integrity Corps Guardrails]]

## Dominion Continuity Security Directorate (v2.0)

> “Ignorance is Chaos. Doubt is Disease. Truth is Mercy.”

> — Triumvirate Precept 01:01:01

# I. Overview

The **Inquisition**, publicly designated as the **Integrity Corps**, serves as the principal continuity-defense and anti-subversion institution of the Royal Government of the Dominion.

Unlike conventional intelligence or policing agencies, the Inquisition exists to defend:

- civilizational continuity,

- constitutional integrity,

- metaphysical stability,

- and long-horizon societal coherence.

The organization functions as a hybrid:

- counterintelligence service,

- anti-corruption directorate,

- ontological threat bureau,

- judicial investigator,

- and continuity preservation institution.

The Inquisition does not officially govern.

It safeguards the conditions under which governance remains possible.

---

# II. Historical Foundation

The Inquisition emerged gradually from the Collapse Histories following repeated human civilizational failures across:

- Earth,

- the Drift Era,

- early Harminan expansion,

- interstellar fragmentation periods,

- and later Severance catastrophes.

Dominion doctrine concluded that civilizations most often die through:

- internal corruption,

- ideological capture,

- memetic destabilization,

- bureaucratic decay,

- institutional paralysis,

- and unmanaged existential threats.

The Inquisition was therefore designed not as a tool of conquest,

but as an institutional immune system.

Its central mandate became:

> Prevent recurrence.

---

# III. Public Identity vs Legal Identity

## Legal Designation

**The Inquisition**

## Public Designation

**Integrity Corps**

The public-facing terminology exists intentionally to reduce:

- religious connotation,

- authoritarian association,

- and historical stigma.

Within legal doctrine and classified continuity frameworks,

the original title remains preserved.

---

# IV. Core Mission

The Inquisition investigates and suppresses threats classified as:

## Continuity Threats

Threat categories include:

- systemic corruption,

- large-scale institutional infiltration,

- constitutional subversion,

- Dracoshian cult networks,

- Severance contamination events,

- ontological instability,

- prohibited metaphysical technologies,

- hostile memetic structures,

- continuity sabotage,

- timeline destabilization,

- and catastrophic anti-civilizational movements.

The Inquisition does **not** officially police:

- private belief,

- lawful dissent,

- cultural identity,

- or nonviolent ideological disagreement.

Action threshold requires demonstrable continuity risk.

---

# V. Philosophical Foundation

The Inquisition operates under the Dominion principle:

## “Faith Without Franchise”

Religious belief is permitted.

Religious governance is forbidden.

The organization therefore treats:

- political absolutism,

- cultic hierarchy,

- ideological extremism,

- and metaphysical authoritarianism

as historically recurring collapse vectors.

The Inquisition views itself not as sacred authority,

but as custodians against recurrence.

---

# VI. Organizational Structure

## The Triumvirate Model

The organization is intentionally divided into specialized authority bodies to prevent concentrated power accumulation.

---

## 1. High Inquisitor

### (Triune Crown)

Senior continuity authority.

Responsibilities include:

- strategic continuity oversight,

- inter-branch coordination,

- existential threat classification,

- Severance-level emergency authorization,

- and relic convergence authority.

The High Inquisitor may not issue unilateral permanent verdicts without tribunal concurrence.

---

## 2. Judicants

### (Judges)

Investigative and judicial officers.

Responsibilities:

- interrogation,

- tribunal oversight,

- evidence authentication,

- constitutional review support,

- truth verification procedures,

- warrant authorization chains.

Judicants wield the **Eye of Veritas** during formal proceedings.

---

## 3. Praetors

### (Enforcers)

Field enforcement and containment personnel.

Responsibilities:

- raid execution,

- cult suppression,

- continuity breach response,

- high-threat apprehension,

- relic enforcement operations,

- ontological quarantine support.

Praetors carry the **Logos Blade** during sanctioned verdict operations.

---

## 4. Archivists

### (Scholars)

Knowledge preservation and forbidden archive custodians.

Responsibilities:

- historical continuity maintenance,

- memetic analysis,

- translation,

- classified doctrine preservation,

- Severance pattern study,

- anti-corruption intelligence synthesis.

Archivists are considered among the psychologically highest-risk personnel due to prolonged exposure to hazardous knowledge.

---

## 5. Wardens

### (Relic Keepers)

Containment and preservation specialists.

Responsibilities:

- relic security,

- vault operations,

- continuity seal maintenance,

- quarantine systems,

- metaphysical object transport,

- resonance stabilization.

Wardens are often attached to deep vaults and frontier containment stations.

---

# VII. The Relics of the Triumvirate

The Inquisition maintains several continuity relics combining:

- symbolic authority,

- advanced technology,

- resonance instrumentation,

- and metaphysical interface systems.

---

## 1. Eye of Veritas

A truth-resonance orb used during:

- interrogations,

- continuity hearings,

- memory authentication,

- and causality review.

Capabilities include:

- emotional resonance analysis,

- memory pattern correlation,

- contradiction detection,

- and localized cognitive harmonics mapping.

Use is heavily regulated.

---

## 2. Scepter of Correction

A containment and adjudication instrument.

Operational modes include:

- resonance bind,

- stasis anchoring,

- localized Aether suppression,

- and geo-seal stabilization.

Typically carried by Judicants and High Inquisitors.

---

## 3. Logos Blade

Ceremonial and enforcement weapon.

Functions include:

- execution of final verdicts,

- metaphysical severance,

- ward disruption,

- and hostile resonance destabilization.

The blade is used sparingly due to ontological risk.

---

# VIII. Oversight & Restrictions

The Dominion recognizes that institutions tasked with continuity enforcement possess intrinsic authoritarian risk.

The Inquisition is therefore constrained by:

- judicial warrant requirements,

- live court oversight,

- legislative review committees,

- Inspector-General authority,

- protected whistleblower channels,

- telemetry and body-log mandates,

- constitutional review systems,

- and emergency sunset provisions.

Explicit prohibitions include:

- torture,

- indefinite detention,

- parallel courts,

- mass warrantless surveillance,

- and ideological policing of lawful expression.

The organization itself is considered a monitored institution.

---

# IX. Psychological Doctrine

Inquisitors are trained under the principle:

> “The guardian may become the breach.”

Personnel undergo:

- historical tyranny education,

- cognitive resilience conditioning,

- anti-absolutism doctrine,

- memetic contamination screening,

- and continuity ethics instruction.

Institutional drift is treated as a catastrophic threat vector.

---

# X. Dracoshian Threat Doctrine

The Inquisition considers Dracoshian cult structures among the most severe continuity threats known to the Dominion.

These groups often engage in:

- Severance worship,

- collapse acceleration ideology,

- ontological corruption rites,

- identity dissolution doctrines,

- and anti-coherence metaphysics.

Post-Severance,

Dracoshian activity increased exponentially due to:

- reality instability,

- public uncertainty,

- metaphysical fragmentation,

- and widespread continuity trauma.

The Inquisition’s anti-cult divisions became increasingly militarized during this period.

---

# XI. Post-Severance Transformation

Before Severance,

the Inquisition primarily functioned as:

- continuity investigators,

- anti-corruption auditors,

- and counter-subversion specialists.

After Severance,

their mandate expanded into:

- ontological defense,

- reality contamination response,

- continuity fracture containment,

- and existential stabilization.

This period marked the gradual transformation of the organization into a more ritualized,

grim,

and psychologically burdened institution.

---

# XII. Public Perception

Public opinion regarding the Inquisition remains divided across Dominion history.

## Supporters view them as:

- civilization protectors,

- anti-corruption guardians,

- continuity custodians,

- and defenders against existential collapse.

## Critics view them as:

- overly secretive,

- dangerously powerful,

- psychologically isolating,

- and perpetually at risk of authoritarian drift.

Both interpretations persist simultaneously.

---

# XIII. Cultural Identity

The Inquisition is heavily associated with:

- monastic discipline,

- funerary symbolism,

- judicial ritual,

- silence,

- memory preservation,

- and burdened duty.

Their presence within a region usually signifies:

- severe corruption,

- continuity instability,

- or existential threat activity.

Among civilians,

they are simultaneously feared,

respected,

and mythologized.

---

# XIV. Core Doctrine

The Inquisition ultimately operates under a singular premise:

> Civilization collapses faster than it realizes.

Its purpose is not perfection.

Its purpose is preservation.

---

> Truth is maintenance.

> And maintenance is mercy.”

>
