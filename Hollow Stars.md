I. Physical Definition

A Hollow Star is a stellar-scale object whose core is absent or replaced with a stable void, surrounded by a luminous outer shell of plasma, gas, or [[Exotic Matter]].
The central cavity may be true vacuum, exotic negative-energy space, or a stabilized [[Artificial]] environment, maintained by internal magnetic, gravitational, or [[VFF]]-lattice forces.
