# Terra 110k Scene-Level Expansion Outline

> [!note] Historical planning document
> This outline describes the pre-decompression structure. The active manuscript now contains approximately 99,600 words in a prologue and forty-seven chapters. Its old chapter numbers are retained as planning history. Use [[Terra Chapter Story Engine]] and [[Terra Chapter Decompression Audit]] for current navigation.

## Scope

This plan analyzes:

- [[Lithic Manuscript 1 - Terra - Rough Draft]];
- [[Lithic Manuscript 1 - Terra]];
- every dated Lithic-era note directly linked by the Terra planning file;
- the directly linked timeline, continuity, national-ensemble, and companion-manuscript notes;
- the older source profile for Abdulrahman bin Ali Al-Saud, which is linked by the rough draft but absent from the copied vault.

The plan does not add new wars, governments, scientific breakthroughs, perpetrators, victories, territorial changes, or historical outcomes.

Where a linked event note contains only a title and generated impact scaffold, the event is treated as a fixed marker with unresolved details. Recommended scenes show already established consequences without defining new military or political history.

## Executive Assessment

Current manuscript length: **29,325 words**.

Target manuscript length: **approximately 110,000 words**.

Required expansion: **approximately 80,675 words**.

Current structure:

- prologue;

Primary draft condition:

The current manuscript is a complete compressed narrative. It contains the correct causal chain and several effective lived scenes, but most chapters operate at one-quarter of publishable chapter length. Major events often receive one representative scene followed by years of summary. The expansion should not add more events. It should add:

- anticipation;
- aftermath;
- domestic routine;
- institutional process;
- relationship change;
- work;
- travel;
- waiting;
- recovery;
- consequences that persist into the next chapter.

Technology guardrail:

Confirmed knowledge does not imply a prototype. Terra possesses repeatable effects, bounded mechanisms, and speculative engineering horizons. It does not possess human brain transfer, viable trans-genetic people, macroscopic force fields, controlled chaos engineering, or practical access to an Ethereal domain.

Secondary-state guardrail:

The Constantinople Corridor Protocols are not a sixth bloc. Akkad, Assyria, Biafra, Kurdistan, Danubia, Katanga, Zanzibar, Occitania, Tibet, Nubia, Sapmi, Ruthenia, Circassia, Pontus, Armenia, Carthage, and Phoenicia may share standards, routes, currencies, or defense arrangements while retaining distinct governments. Exact borders, founding conflicts, rulers, and constitutions remain open unless fixed by a dedicated canon note.

1. Preserve every dated event currently assigned to a chapter.

4. Do not add battles to scaffold-only wars.
5. Treat Operation Anvil, cognitive anomalies, and deep-state interpretations through records, testimony, or factional analysis unless a linked note explicitly confirms an event.
6. Keep Wetwire biological and infrastructural; do not convert it into recurring monster action.
7. Keep Accerisma medically necessary and environmentally damaging.
8. Keep cognitive technologies inferential and neurological rather than literal supernatural mind reading.
9. Keep national viewpoints internally diverse.
10. Let ordinary people experience policy without making them secretly responsible for world events.

## Recurring Arc Expansion

### Varga-Sayegh Archive Arc

The atlas should change physical and emotional meaning in each generation:

- Selene uses it to resist clean borders.
- Daniel uses it to preserve rejected models.
- Miriam uses it to preserve ethical context.
- Leila uses it to preserve patients erased by statistics.
- Nadiya uses it to preserve conflicting national records.
- First-winter survivors use it as a practical map again.

Add one atlas interaction every two or three chapters. Each interaction must alter, damage, annotate, hide, copy, or reinterpret the object.

### Designed Generation Arc

The five designed adults need sustained development between childhood introduction and 2077 convergence:

- Mara: corporate benefit becomes medical ownership, then labor resistance.
- Sade: public-health commons becomes compulsory service, then community stewardship.
- Abdulrahman: dynastic design becomes military legitimacy, then chosen restraint.
- Kavya: civic investment becomes occupation assignment, then systems dissent.
- Elise: universal access becomes citizenship hierarchy, then unauthorized evacuation.

Each should receive:

- one childhood domestic scene;
- one adolescent conflict with the designing institution;
- one adult work scene unrelated to war;
- one personal relationship affected by their status;
- one decision during scarcity;
- one Exchange decision that exceeds the role designed for them.

### Ordinary-People Arc

Recurring ordinary-life characters should include:

- Emil and the customs family;
- Joseph Alvarez or another veteran family;
- Helen Cho and the medical-ownership case;
- Mrs. Fallow's family;
- Tomas and the AccerStick survivor community;
- Eve Mercer and her mother;
- Jude and Lena Keene;
- Binta Adebayo and market families;
- Fariha and Tariq;
- Dev and Liu;
- Luc Laurent;
- Lin Wei and his missing daughter.

Do not promote all of them into geopolitical actors. Their function is to carry memory, work, care, and cost.
