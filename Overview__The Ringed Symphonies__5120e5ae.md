The Ringed Symphonies are a unique magicktech-fantasy cosmology where myth, resonance, and sacrifice intertwine.
	•	Nine colossal [[Ringworlds]] orbit a sentient star known as [[The Starmaker]].
	•	Each ring embodies a distinct mythology (Norse, Abrahamic, Greek, Egyptian, Mesoamerican, Celtic, Polynesian, Chinese, Andean).
	•	Magic operates through harmonic runes, where spells form chords that demand resonance as fuel.
	•	[[The Starmaker]] is both sun and conductor, maintaining the fragile harmony of myth and reality.
