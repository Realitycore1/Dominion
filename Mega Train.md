Power [[Systems]]
	•	Nuclear Thermal Reactors: Compact fission reactors drive electric turbines, providing thousands of megawatts of power.
	•	ArcCell Integration: Can be fitted with modular ArcCells for redundancy or for [[Regions]] wary of nuclear use.
	•	Hybrid Drive: Some designs mount auxiliary maglev induction pads, drawing power wirelessly from V.E.I.N. arrays when available.

⸻

⚙️ Design Features
	•	Scale: Comparable to seafaring container fleets. A single mega train can haul tens of thousands of tons of raw materials, food, or weapons.
	•	Track [[Systems]]:
	•	Superheavy Rails: Reinforced alloy tracks that can handle nuclear-weight locomotives and kilometer-scale trains.
	•	Maglev Corridors: For higher speed — useful on developed worlds where speed > raw capacity.
	•	Modular Cars:
	•	Resource Containers (ore, fuel, foodstuffs).
	•	Civilian Haulers (for mass evacuations).
	•	Armored War-Cars (mobile fortresses, gun platforms).

⸻

🛡️ [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] Uses
	•	Rolling Fortress Trains: Heavily armored mega trains with gun turrets, missile silos, and drone launch bays — essentially “land [[Battleships]].”
	•	Mobile Garrisons: Can serve as moving bases for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]], allowing fast redeployment across continents.
	•	Supply Arteries: Used to keep frontline [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] alive during sieges, with Cerberus Vault-Cars carrying critical archives, weapons, or command cores.

⸻

🌍 Strategic Role
	•	On frontier [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]]: Mega trains provide the only way to move bulk cargo between inland crater [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]], [[Hive Cities]], and orbital elevators.
	•	On [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] core worlds: They become symbols of order — colossal, unstoppable machines binding the planet together like veins of steel.
	•	On warfront worlds: They’re both lifelines and targets — sabotage of a mega train can cripple entire campaigns.

⸻

🎭 Cultural & Symbolic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]
	•	Colonists view them as “iron rivers”, vital arteries of civilization.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] propaganda depicts them as unstoppable engines of progress — mankind’s will literally rolling across worlds.
	•	Some worlds even treat them like holy [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], with rituals for the “First Train” that brought survival cargo to a colony.
