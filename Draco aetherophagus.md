Class:
Macro-Aetheric Plasma Organism (MAPO)

Hazard Designation:
Aether-Class Stellar Interface Lifeform

This keeps them distinct from:
	•	Stellar Devourers (heliophagic, star-terminal)
	•	Void Worms (gravitational mass movers)
	•	Spectral Wraiths (energy-resonant parasites)

⸻

II. What Aether Drakes Are

Aether Drakes are semi-coherent plasma–aether lifeforms that exist between matter, energy, and field states.

They are not made of flesh.
They are not made of plasma alone.
They are self-organizing aetheric vortices that borrow dragon-like morphology because it is structurally optimal, not symbolic.

Think:
	•	Magnetic confinement
	•	Plasma flow stabilization
	•	Energy intake and exhaust
	•	Gravitational shear navigation

Dragons aren’t mythic here.
They’re efficient.

⸻

III. Habitat & Range

Aether Drakes are found near:
	•	Aether-rich stellar zones
	•	Pulsars, magnetars, and exotic stars
	•	Aetherstorms and Dark Eternities
	•	Regions with heavy verse-field interference

They do not inhabit normal space comfortably.

They graze reality.

⸻

IV. Behavior

Feeding
	•	Consume aetheric flux, not matter
	•	Draw energy from:
	•	Stellar magnetic fields
	•	Aether currents
	•	Verse-field turbulence
	•	Leave behind:
	•	Localized energy depletion
	•	Reality “cooling scars”
	•	Distorted causality gradients

Movement
	•	“Swim” along field lines
	•	Appear to coil around stars, nebulae, or anomalies
	•	Motion looks serpentine because that minimizes shear stress

Aggression
	•	Non-hostile
	•	Extremely dangerous
	•	Will pass through ships without noticing them—unless disturbed

⸻

V. Intelligence (Critical Distinction)

Aether Drakes are not sapient, but they are adaptive.

They possess:
	•	Pattern memory
	•	Environmental responsiveness
	•	Long-horizon behavioral inertia

They do not think.

They persist.

This makes them:
	•	Unpredictable
	•	Unnegotiable
	•	Uninterested in observers

⸻

VI. Relationship to Stellar Devourers

This is important:
	•	Stellar Devourers end stars
	•	Aether Drakes stabilize or destabilize fields

In some systems:
	•	Drakes delay stellar collapse by bleeding off excess energy
	•	In others, they accelerate Devourer attachment by reshaping fields

Dominion xenologists debate whether:
	•	Drakes are precursors
	•	Or counterbalances

No consensus exists.

That’s unsettling—and correct.

⸻

VII. Cultural Names (Multiple Civilizations)

Different cultures interpret them wildly differently:
	•	Star Seraphs
	•	Void Dragons
	•	Sky Leviathans
	•	Aether Wyrms
	•	The Coiling Light
	•	The Long Flame

Religious groups claim:

They are angels of cosmic order.

Others insist:

They are omens of collapse.

Both are wrong in useful ways.

⸻

VIII. Why They’re Called “Drakes” Universally

Even advanced civilizations default to “dragon.”

Why?

Because:
	•	Long, articulated bodies
	•	Limb-like plasma protrusions
	•	Radiant cores
	•	Wing-analog energy fins
	•	Breath-like energy exhausts

Across cultures, dragon imagery emerges independently.

This is not coincidence.
It’s convergent cognition.

⸻

IX. Dominion Doctrine

Dominion policy regarding Aether Drakes:
	•	Observe only
	•	Never engage
	•	Never attempt capture
	•	Never anchor megastructures nearby

One classified failure involved attempting to harvest a Drake.

The system no longer exists.

⸻

X. Canonical One-Liner

From a Dominion field manual:

“If you see an Aether Drake, the universe is doing maintenance.”
