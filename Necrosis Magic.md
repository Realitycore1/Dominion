Definition
Necrosis Magic manipulates [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], undeath, and the lingering energies of decay. It does not honor the dead, nor release them; it shackles them in twisted continuity, or infuses rot into the living until they resemble the dead.

⸻

Core Principles
	1.	Persistence of Essence – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] does not end the soul; necrosis binds it unnaturally.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Corruption of Cycle – life, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]], and [[Rebirth]] are broken; decay is arrested or redirected.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Entropy Harvesting – necrotic power feeds on rot, dissolution, and despair.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Parasitism – necrosis does not create its own fuel; it drains from living or dead hosts.

⸻

Techniques of Necrosis
	•	Reanimation – raising corpses as puppets or thralls.
	•	Lichdom – anchoring one’s own soul to persist beyond [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]].
	•	Soul Binding – trapping spirits in objects, constructs, or revenant shells.
	•	Blight Weaving – spreading decay through land, water, or flesh.
	•	Wraithcraft – summoning bodiless dead, ghosts, or shades.
	•	Hybridization – fusing necrosis with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Military/Technology/Technology|Technology]] or magic to create war-beasts and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]]-machines ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] and [[Yondor]] excel here).

⸻

Cultural Expressions
	•	Balintis: The Blood Mage heresies flirted with necrosis, animating [[Soldiers]] bound in blood-armor. The Veldar outlawed such practices, though remnants still lurk in ruins.
	•	Concordia: Necrosis is inseparable from [[Yondor]] and the [[Abyss]]. [[Undead]] rise not as anomalies but as ecosystems — corpses bound with machinery, souls separated from bodies, ghost-[[Soldiers]] deployed like scouts.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]]: Studies necrosis clinically. FAID agents classify it as “[[Eminentem]] contamination” and deploy [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] purges against [[Undead]] outbreaks. Yet [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Physics/MOT/Eternum|Eternum]] labs also dissect necrotic [[Systems]] to replicate their persistence.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/Xenos/Synarchate|Synarchate]]: Views necrosis as disharmony, a fundamental dissonance. Necrotic outbreaks are quarantined in resonance-crystals, where the dead can no longer sing.
	•	Gnostic Expanse: Necrosis is sanctified. Demiurgic cults teach that [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] is liberation and persistence in rot is holiness; whole civilizations willingly submit to becoming hollow thralls.

⸻

Why It Persists
	•	Utility – armies of the dead don’t eat, sleep, or rebel.
	•	Fear – necrosis terrifies, making it an effective weapon.
	•	Defiance – refusal to accept mortality or loss drives many to embrace it.
	•	Corruption – once seeded, necrosis often spreads like a disease.

⸻

Perils
	•	Loss of Self – [[Undead]] lose memory, soul, or [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].
	•	Blight – necrosis poisons land and waters, birthing wastelands.
	•	Dependence – necromancers often require constant [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] to fuel their craft.
	•	Infection – necrosis spreads; what begins with one corpse ends with a kingdom.

⸻

Countermeasures
	•	Purifying Flame – [[Fire]] severs necrotic bindings.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] Purge – [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] storms burn out necrosis like infection.
	•	Holy Wards – angelic blessings banish restless souls.
	•	Salt, Iron, and Silver – common anchors that resist necrotic resonance.
	•	Mythic Resistance – heroes whose stories are too strong cannot be bound in undeath.
