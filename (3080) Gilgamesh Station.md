# (3080) Gilgamesh Station

## Canonical Clarification

Gilgamesh opens in 3007 as the orbital home of the Terran Federation, before the first permanent Lunar colony.

The 3080 marker records its mature expansion into the principal federal seat for Solar administration.

By 3080, Gilgamesh contains expanded legislative chambers, federal courts, Space Force command, colonial ministries, public archives, diplomatic quarters, transit docks, and multiple civilian habitation districts.

It is praised as neutral ground above the twelve Great Cities and criticized as a government physically removed from ordinary Earth.
