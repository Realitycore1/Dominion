Spells are hungry harmonics. They do not care where power comes from, only that they are fed.
	•	Life → efficient, burns bright, costs health, vitality, or soul.
	•	Unlife → slower, heavier, but still fuel; entire necropolises can be drained.
	•	[[Artificial]] ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Batteries) → industrialized sacrifice, cold and efficient.
	•	Environment → ley lines, auroras, Noise storms, Anchors.

Cost Tiers:
	•	Low ([[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]-rune) → fatigue, stamina, fainting.
	•	Medium (5-rune) → lasting injury, scarring, shortened lifespan.
	•	High (7-rune) → [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] or permanent soul damage.
	•	Extreme (9-rune) → civilizations, necropolises, or rings sacrificed.
