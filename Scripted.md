Core Bias:

Law-bound or code-written continuity

This subtype is where reality behaves as if its governing laws have been:
	•	written
	•	encoded
	•	constrained
	•	legally or computationally enforced

Traits:
	•	rule-heavy environments
	•	strong logic constraints
	•	codified cause-effect pathways
	•	enforced consistency
	•	reality behaving like executable law

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	reality is running on written doctrine
	•	there are rules beneath the rules
	•	continuity is less discovered than authored

Common Expressions:
	•	code-law universes
	•	doctrinal machine realities
	•	ritual-operating cosmologies
	•	executable governance worlds

Failure Mode:

Rule brittleness
The basin cannot survive what it was not explicitly written to handle.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	highly controlled universes
	•	law-obsessed civilizations
	•	sacred machine theocracies
	•	MOT-style engineered axiom systems
