I. Physical Definition

A Dark Energy Star is a stellar-scale object whose internal pressure is dominated by dark energy, creating a repulsive gravitational effect within its core.
Instead of collapsing under gravity like a neutron star or black hole, the dark energy core counteracts compression with spacetime expansion at small scales, stabilizing the object in an exotic equilibrium.
