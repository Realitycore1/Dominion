A Gas [[Giant]] Compressor is a macro-engineered planetary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] that harvests, compresses, and reconfigures the atmosphere of a gas [[Giant]]. Its purpose is to extract hydrogen, helium, and exotic isotopes, and—at advanced stages—collapse the planet into a denser object: a super-Jovian resource bank, a brown dwarf, or even a controlled stellar embryo.
Harvest [[Systems]]

[[Atmospheric Skimmers]]: Vast fleets of orbital siphons and flying arcologies that strip hydrogen/helium in constant streams.

Ring Collectors: Orbital belts that filter captured gases into compressor stations.

Compression [[Systems]]

Gravitic Pressors: Atlas Pillar-scale arrays squeezing mass inward.

Magnetic Containment: Superconducting shells holding compressed hydrogen as stable blocks.

Fusion-Boost Reactors: Channel gas into controlled burning to drive the process.

Transformation Outcomes

Fuel Worlds: Compressed into hyperdense resource planets, mined for millennia.

Brown Dwarf Embryos: Gas [[Giant]] ignited into a low-mass “furnace star” to power a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

[[Artificial]] [[Stars]]: In extreme cases, a full ignition — deliberately birthing new suns for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] use.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Can be weaponized — deliberately igniting a gas [[Giant]] into a star or triggering collapse as a “planet bomb.”

Economic: Serve as sector banks of matter — compressed giants as vaults of usable hydrogen, helium-[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]], and strange isotopes.

Religious: Revered as “the Forges of the Empress” — worlds remade in her hand, planets pressed into suns like jewels set into a crown.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Planetary compression towers — colossal buttresses diving into clouds, glowing like cathedrals piercing a storm.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, compressors appear as seamless black rings encasing parts of the planet, like [[Chains]] binding a god.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Swirling vortex arrays sculpt gas flows into fractal storm-patterns, shimmering like stained glass cyclones.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Planetary poles inscribed with glyph-rings, entire aurorae bending into scripted halos.
