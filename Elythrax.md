Classification: Colossus Class – Tier XV / Field Designate “Elythrax” ([[The Void]] Weaver of Narthus Reach)

Observation:
Detected within the high polar voids of [[Harmina]]’s moon-facing hemisphere, where perpetual darkness and cryogenic storms form an environment hostile to nearly all known life. Elythrax measures an estimated 480 meters in span with leg structures extending over a kilometer when fully deployed. Its body temperature remains consistently below ambient levels, suggesting an internal energy [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]] reliant on vacuum resonance rather than chemical metabolism. The organism constructs massive silica-carbon weaves across mountain ridges, each filament thinner than light yet strong enough to trap meteorite fragments. These structures hum faintly when planetary magnetic tides shift—an audible resonance known as The Choir of the Pale Sky.

Anatomical Notes:
Eight primary limbs act as multi-jointed manipulators, each tipped with gravitic anchors allowing stability in low-gravity environments. The dorsal carapace contains reflective organelles that refract cosmic radiation, serving both as camouflage and potential energy absorption matrices. At the core of its thorax, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] field observers recorded a dense “hive cavity” lined with gestation pods — suggesting Elythrax may function as a solitary brood-[[Queen]], spawning self-replicating larvae known as “Threadlings.”

Contextual Analysis:
Ecologically, Elythrax maintains equilibrium within [[Harmina]]’s polar region by ensnaring airborne megafauna and celestial debris, recycling both into its silk webbing. These webs stabilize the upper ionosphere by grounding static discharges from the planet’s aurora [[Systems]] — a process theorized to prevent electromagnetic collapse during [[Harmina]]’s periodic solar alignments. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] reconnaissance drones that enter the web are rarely recovered intact; those retrieved show partial disassembly through molecular-scale slicing.

Doctrinal Interpretation:
In the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of the Deep Heavens, Elythrax is known as the “Saint of Stillness.”
To the faithful, its endless weaving embodies divine patience — the eternal act of ordering chaos strand by strand.
To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] tacticians, it represents the ideal of self-sufficiency, an organism that generates its own ecosystem through [[Silence]], solitude, and design.

Religious symbology often depicts Elythrax in celestial murals as a black weaver over a silver sphere, representing the Empress’ hand restraining entropy. The [[Infernariate]] cite its mythic [[Origin]] as the first creature to stitch the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] sky back together after the Night of Fracture, when the heavens themselves tore open.
