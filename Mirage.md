Core Bias:

False pattern under scarcity

This is one of the most useful and dangerous Desert subtypes.

Reality under scarcity begins producing:
	•	false signals
	•	phantom structures
	•	misread opportunities
	•	misleading coherence

Traits:
	•	illusion-like patterning
	•	unstable navigation
	•	deceptive meaning clusters
	•	informational heat haze
	•	false abundance

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	horizons that lie
	•	structures that vanish when approached
	•	civilizations built around misread truth
	•	realities where signal and noise blur

Common Expressions:
	•	anti-memetic zones
	•	false holy sites
	•	deceptive civilizations
	•	unstable trade or communication corridors

Failure Mode:

Pursuit collapse
Systems waste themselves chasing non-real continuity.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	antimemetic cosmology
	•	information-degraded basins
	•	false utopias
	•	“meaning deserts”
