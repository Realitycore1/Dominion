## Summary

Economic: P
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Gas [[Giant]] Shellworld is a mega-engineered habitat built inside and around a gas [[Giant]], with multiple habitable [[Layers]] (shells) suspended in its atmosphere or anchored to its deep pressure bands. It is essentially a stack of concentric [[Artificial]] worlds, each one floating on oceans of gas, with the [[Abyss]] of the Jovian core far below.
Structure

Concentric Shells: Dozens to hundreds of rigid [[Layers]], each thousands of km [[Wide]].

Atlas Pillar Struts: Titanic supports braced from layer to layer, “roots” plunging into storms.

Pressure Anchors: Strange-matter lattices suspending shells at precise [[Depths]].

Habitability

Upper Shells: Earthlike environments — oceans, forests, arcologies beneath translucent skies.

Middle Shells: Industrial forges, fusion harvesters, biosphere labs.

Lower Shells: High-pressure [[Layers]] lit by fusion lamps, often bizarre ecosystems engineered to thrive in extreme density.

Core Vault: At the very center, a cathedral-vault or [[Computronium]] bank — the “heart” of the Shellworld.

Energy & Resources

Geothermal Convection: Harnessed from heat gradients.

Fusion Feed: Extracted hydrogen routed into reactors.

Storm Harvesting: Lightning and jet streams siphoned into power conduits.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Lower [[Layers]] double as Legion fortresses, hidden beneath kilometers of storms.

Religious: Treated as “Thrones of Storms” — monuments proving the Empress sits even within the wrath of gas giants.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Ribbed [[Atlas Pillars]] piercing downward like cathedral spines, connecting shell to shell.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Each shell patterned into fractal landscapes, like living mandalas stacked in the deep.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs inscribed into each layer’s inner rim, glowing as aurorae through the gas.

Narrative Function

To citizens: A miracle of abundance — a single planet housing entire civilizations in stacked heavens.

To myth: Called the “Storm Thrones”, where the Empress builds palaces inside the lungs of giants.
