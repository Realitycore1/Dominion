I. Origins (c. 3500–3530)
	•	By this [[Time]], Luna (Cynthia Protectorate), Mars (Kindred/Red Clan), Venus (Cloudborn), Mercury (Sunborn), and the Belt (Chainfolk Confederacy) have all emerged as distinct powers.
	•	The [[Colonial Union]] (CU), anchored in [[Gilgamesh Station]], tries to maintain oversight but is increasingly irrelevant — its member states divided, its fleets outdated.
	•	Into this vacuum rise [[Corporate Fleets]], cartels, and PMCs, many richer and more powerful than the CU itself.
	•	First sparks: disputes over ice shipments from [[Ceres]], raids on Belt smelters, and “accidents” involving rival solar mirror arrays.

⸻

II. Actors of the Solar War
	1.	Corpo Fleets:
	•	Megacorporations controlling vast convoys and defense flotillas.
	•	Fight proxy battles over asteroid mining [[Rights]] and orbital trade lanes.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	PMCs (Private [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] Companies):
	•	Hire out to anyone with resources.
	•	Known for sudden allegiance shifts and false-flag operations.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] & Independents:
	•	Mars: Red Clan resists corpo extraction on principle.
	•	Venus: Cloudborn defend balance but quietly trade with whoever funds their aerostats.
	•	Mercury: Sunborn sell metals but retaliate hard if rails are threatened.
	•	Belt: Chainfolk constantly caught in the middle — some guilds resist, others collude.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Cartels & Syndicates:
	•	Control the shadow [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]: smuggling, organics, art, oxygen.
	•	Often align with corpo fleets or PMCs, blurring war with crime.

⸻

III. Methods of Conflict
	•	Resource Raids: Hijacking ice shipments, fusion fuel caches, or organics freighters.
	•	Sabotage: Habs vented in “accidents,” maglev rails disrupted, comm satellites destroyed.
	•	Proxy Wars: Corpo-backed PMCs fight “local disputes” inside domes or between habs.
	•	Economic Warfare: Flooding markets with cheap metals or counterfeit organics to destabilize rivals.
	•	Information War:
	•	Propaganda from Mars and Venus framing corpo fleets as “slavers.”
	•	Corporates label Red Clan and Chainfolk as “[[Pirates]]” or “terrorists.”

⸻

IV. Cultural Cold War (3550–3650)

This isn’t just resources — it’s ideology.
	•	Corpos: Claim legitimacy through “efficiency” and “stability.”
	•	Red Clan (Mars): Push ethos of survival, guild honor, and rejection of exploitation.
	•	Cloudborn (Venus): Advocate balance and neutrality, but quietly play all sides.
	•	Sunborn (Mercury): Glorify endurance and labor, respected but feared as harsh negotiators.
	•	Chainfolk (Belt): Celebrate independence, smuggling, and trade webs — but are most vulnerable to proxy wars.

V. Outcome & Legacy (c. 3650)
	•	No single victor. The First Solar War ends much like [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]]’s Cold War — through exhaustion and shifting priorities rather than clear defeat.
	•	Corpos weakened: After centuries of proxy wars, their fleets remain powerful but no longer unchallenged.
	•	[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] hardened: Mars, Venus, Mercury, and the Belt emerge with stronger cultural unity, forged by resisting corpo exploitation.
	•	CU irrelevant: The [[Colonial Union]] fades into obscurity, remembered as a relic of the Ash Century.
	•	Legacy: The Solar War sets the stage for the Second Solar Expansion, when [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] begin pushing into the [[Outer System]] (Jovians, Saturnians, Kuiper frontier) — not for corpo profit, but for independence.
