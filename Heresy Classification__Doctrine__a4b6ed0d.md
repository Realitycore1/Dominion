## Summary

GalFed classifies dangerous dissent as continuity risk. The word heresy is used informally by officials and openly by enforcement orders, though legal documents prefer terms like memetic hazard, noetic deviance, and civilizational instability.

## Details

Common classifications:

- Echo-Heresy: unauthorized preservation or dissemination of historical records that contradict Beacon archives.
- Null Heresy: rejection of Aether monitoring, cognitive indexing, or noetic health audits.
- Sovereignty Extremism: belief that worlds possess unconditional independence from GalFed stewardship.
- Restoration Defiance: refusal to submit silent populations or damaged archives to GalFed recovery review.
- Unlicensed Uplift: teaching restricted technology or cognition frameworks to probationary worlds.
- Singularity Denial: minimizing the Aether Singularity to undermine Federation authority.
- Founder Impiety: public argument that the Council of Primacy has no special mandate.
- Corridor Apostasy: creation of unauthorized FTL routes outside GalFed traffic law.
- Memory Secession: removal of cultural records from the Archive Gate System.
- Harmonic Contagion: art, music, language, or ritual believed to destabilize shared cognition.

Punishments range from correction orders and trade restrictions to leadership replacement, archive seizure, quarantine, population relocation, or memory restructuring.

The key horror is procedural softness. A culture can be dissolved by forms, hearings, model scores, and compassionate language long before troops arrive.
