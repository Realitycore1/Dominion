1. [[Origin]]
	•	[[Aravoth (Grace)]]’s [[Choirs]] Inverted: Each class of [[Angel]] has a twisted counterpart in [[The Gnostic Expanse]].
	•	They are not fallen [[Angels]] in the traditional sense; they are purpose-built by the Demiurge, refractions of divine [[Archetypes]] bent to falsehood.
	•	Their descent is always catastrophic: when Angelus arrive, reality itself bends under their judgement.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Purpose
	•	Enforcers of the Demiurge’s Will: They do not teach or heal; they compel obedience.
	•	False Judgement: Their decrees are not based on balance or truth, but on reinforcing the Demiurge’s lie that he is the ultimate god.
	•	World-Cullers: When civilizations resist, Angelus strip their [[Cultures]] of memory, erasing whole peoples into gnosis-null.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Appearance & Form
	•	Negative Divinity:
	•	They shine with light that burns and blinds — a radiance of oppression.
	•	Faces are veiled, masked, or multiplied; never a true [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].
	•	Wings are fractal, splitting endlessly into razor-feathers of false light.
	•	Voices: Speak in choral echoes that contradict themselves, making mortals doubt even their own thoughts.
	•	Presence: Their arrival warps perception — mortals see them as both savior and destroyer simultaneously.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Hierarchy of the Angelus
	•	Seraphielus (Twisted Seraphim)
	•	Embody consuming [[Fire]] that does not purify but scorches indiscriminately.
	•	Cherubelus (Twisted Cherubim)
	•	Guardians of false gates, denying [[Ascension]] and keeping souls trapped in illusion.
	•	Ophanelus (Twisted Ophanim)
	•	Wheels of untruth, their rotations grind down memory and will.
	•	Dominielus (Twisted Dominions)
	•	Choral tyrants who orchestrate oppression through sound and resonance.
	•	Virtuelus (Twisted Virtues)
	•	Warriors of false valor, raising armies for the Demiurge’s endless wars.

⸻

5. Function in [[The Gnostic Expanse]]
	•	World Intrusions: They descend like storms, sent to worlds resisting Demiurgic [[law]].
	•	Gnosis-Inversion: Instead of granting enlightenment, they shatter gnosis, filling mortals with despair or false revelations.
	•	Soul-Culling: They strip away the “spark of divinity” in souls, harvesting it to feed the Demiurge’s false throne.
