Located at brane-fold convergence points or causal singularities Maintains quantum coherence and gravitational symmetry across FTL gate nodes Evolves via phased entanglement upgrades to support higher-bandwidth transport Rare, ancient artifacts from a pre-[[Human]] Type III civilization or engineered by post-biological [[Entities]] 1.	Stabilizes Gateways Across Branes
	•	The QBLN maintains causal and spatial synchronization between star [[Systems]]. Without it, relativistic drift or quantum decoherence would make FTL gate travel collapse.
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Stores and Projects Exotic Metrics
	•	Projects controlled metric tensors that reshape spacetime locally—effectively sustaining an [[Artificial]] wormhole throat or traversable Einstein-Rosen bridge.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Energy Source
	•	Uses ambient zero-point flux or Planck-scale [[Graviton]] interference for indefinite operation—making it effectively immortal unless disrupted by catastrophic [[Physics]] violations.
	[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].	Strategic Role
	•	Acts as a relay of interstellar logistics, a control node, and a keystone for any gate-based empire. Losing it collapses local gate [[Infrastructure]]. •	QBLN-A (“Atlas Node”) – Basic brane anchor: sustains local gates and provides FTL logistics stability
	•	QBLN-S (“Siva Node”) – Defense-optimized: deflects attacks via spacetime lensing and houses guardian drones
	•	QBLN-O (“Orchid Node”) – Cognitive variant: integrates sentient quantum computation for reactive gate expansion and self-replication
