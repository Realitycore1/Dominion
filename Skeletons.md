Classification: Undead Constructs & Necromantic Servitors
Subtype: Bone-Anchored Automata / Residual Will Frames
Status: Common, Low-Threat Individually

⸻

Core Description

Skeletons are reanimated skeletal remains, stripped of flesh, emotion, and most identity. They move through necromantic force bound directly to bone structure, animated either by external command or by simple internal loops.

They are quiet.
They are tireless.
They are useful.

A skeleton is not a person.
It is a tool built from what remains.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Skeletons are artificial undead constructs, not souls.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Death (mechanical application)
	•	Secondary Influence: Arcane Law
	•	Realm Interface: Material Continuum, Shadow Realms (indirect)

Most skeletons contain:
	•	no soul,
	•	no personality,
	•	no memory.

Any trace of identity is residue, not consciousness.

⸻

Creation & Animation

Skeletons are created via:
	•	necromantic reanimation rituals,
	•	battlefield corpse processing,
	•	industrialized undead labor systems,
	•	curse fallout in death-saturated zones.

Key requirement:

Bones must be intact enough to hold structural coherence.

Skeletons animated improperly collapse into inert piles.

⸻

Intelligence & Behavior

Skeletons operate on instructional logic.

They can:
	•	follow commands,
	•	patrol routes,
	•	perform repetitive tasks,
	•	fight in formations.

They cannot:
	•	improvise meaningfully,
	•	strategize independently,
	•	feel fear or loyalty.

Any skeleton that displays initiative is either:
	•	improperly bound,
	•	possessed,
	•	or something worse.

⸻

Physical Capabilities
	•	Tireless endurance
	•	No pain response
	•	Resistant to poison and disease
	•	Fragile to blunt force
	•	Vulnerable to dismemberment

Skeletons excel at:
	•	guarding,
	•	labor,
	•	attrition warfare.

They are poor shock troops.

⸻

Variants
	•	War Skeletons: Armed, shielded, disciplined
	•	Labor Skeletons: Mining, hauling, construction
	•	Ritual Skeletons: Static, sigil-bound
	•	Composite Skeletons: Reinforced with metal or stone
	•	Bone Golems: Aggregated skeleton constructs

Bone Golems blur the line between skeleton and construct.

⸻

Weaknesses & Termination
	•	Blunt force damage
	•	Structural collapse
	•	Anti-necromantic fields
	•	Grace-saturated environments
	•	Phoenix-grade fire (total erasure)

Skeletons do not flee.
They stop only when unable to continue.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Necromancers: Tools
	•	Liches: Infrastructure
	•	Ghouls: Sometimes prey
	•	Revenants: Ignore skeletons entirely
	•	Ghosts: Cannot possess properly bound skeletons
	•	Domovoi: Disrupt household skeletons easily

Skeletons exist below the notice of higher undead.

⸻

Distinction from Similar Entities
	•	vs Zombies: Skeletons lack flesh and decay
	•	vs Constructs: Skeletons require death-origin materials
	•	vs Wights: No territory or will
	•	vs Liches: No autonomy
	•	vs Revenants: No purpose

This keeps the undead hierarchy clean and scalable.
