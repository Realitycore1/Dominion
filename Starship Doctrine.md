## Starship Doctrine
- **Design:** Hybrid bioships—[[Ameise]]-grown structures fused with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] alloy.  
- **Classes:**  
  - **[[Brood Drones]]** – small fighters, swarm tactics.  
  - **Carapace [[Frigates]]** – escorts with chitin-alloy plating.  
  - **Hive [[Carriers]]** – launch platforms with caste-nests.  
  - **Crown [[Cruisers]]** – command ships anchored by resonance lattice.  
  - **Hive-Behemoths** – cathedral-sized bioships, always with a [[Queen]] or highborn aboard.  
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:** Mirror their ground swarms: drone clouds screen heavier ships, Crown [[Cruisers]] anchor hive minds.
