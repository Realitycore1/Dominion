Micro-Scale (Nanite & Swarm Tier)

Nanobots that crawl over hulls, sealing microfractures.

“Biofilm” [[Layers]] of programmable matter that can reconfigure as insulation, armor, or conduit.

Meso-Scale (Drone & Skimmer Tier)

Spider-like drones that crawl megastructural ribs, welding and replacing panels.

Skimmer craft that ferry raw materials along the surface of Dyson shells or orbital rings.

Repair [[Corvettes]] patrolling fleet formations, carrying modular toolkits.

Macro-Scale (Pillar & Platform Tier)

Entire [[Atlas Pillars]] can host sub-ecologies of repair [[Systems]].

[[Ark Platforms]] carry drone swarms that maintain both their interior city and exterior hull.

Ecological Dynamics

Predator/Prey Analogues:

“Predator drones” disassemble malfunctioning or corrupted units.

“Scavenger swarms” digest waste and recycle it back into usable alloys.

Niches:

Some specialize in vacuum repair, others in plasma conduit maintenance, others in biological integration (e.g., cleaning bio-domes or hydrofarms).

Reproduction:

Larger factories create mid-scale drones. Mid-scale drones fabricate nanoscale bots.

It’s a closed-loop ecology: maintenance that maintains itself.

Governance & Autonomy

Overseen by layered AI (“Custodians”), each tuned to a specific range of tasks but with distributed autonomy to prevent single-point failure.

Operates on concordant protocols — music-like harmonics that keep the swarm in sync, preventing runaway mutation.

Still, rogue “maintenance ecologies” sometimes drift into feral states, becoming techno-plagues.

Architectural Aesthetic

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Drones and swarms adaptively reconfigure like modular organs.

Suprematist Monolithism: Macro-scale repair hives look like stark, blocky nests attached to [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Biological Overtones: To observers, an MRO ecology resembles coral reef life — constant buzzing, drifting, patching — a living skin.

Potential threat: feral MRO ecologies can become hostile, treating living beings as “corrosion” to be removed.
