Interstice Time
	•	Non-linear: Time in the City is not a line but a weave. The City has its own temporality, neither faster nor slower but different.
	•	Traveler’s Paradox: Two people from the same world could enter Interstice at the same instant and yet emerge centuries apart, or never meet inside despite walking the same streets.
	•	Local Streams: Different districts may run on different currents of Interstice time. A market may replay a single hour endlessly, while the Labyrinth eats whole weeks in minutes.
	•	Practical Effect: There is no single “now” in Interstice. Every encounter is a negotiation of whose time you are currently sharing.
