(The “living geometry” problem)

These are more dangerous conceptually—because readers instinctively assume crystals = ancient intelligence. You can weaponize that expectation.

⸻

Scientific Classification

Proposed clade:
Lithovitae
(Living mineral-based systems)

Order:
Photostructura
(Structure-driven life)

Formal designation:
Lithovitae photostructura

Key characteristics:
	•	Self-assembling crystal lattices
	•	Grow via energy-state resonance, not consumption
	•	Information stored in defect patterns and harmonic resonance
	•	Appear static, but are computationally active
	•	Operate on timescales ranging from minutes to millennia
	•	Can survive vacuum, radiation, and stellar proximity

They are not machines.
They are not organisms.
They are processes that happen to persist.

⸻

Cultural / Common Names
	•	“Star Crystals” – lazy civilian shorthand
	•	“Glass Gods” – early explorers (wrong, but telling)
	•	“The Choirs” – some Dominion scholars (they resonate)
	•	“Frozen Minds” – fringe philosophers
	•	“Light Wounds” – poetic term from deep-space crews
	•	“Cathedrals” – because of their structure and silence

Dominion official terminology would likely be chillingly sterile:

Crystalline Autonomous Structures (CAS)
Status: Life-adjacent, Non-Communicative

Which is bureaucratic denial in action.

⸻

Why the Public Calls Them “Entities” or “Beings”

Because they:
	•	React to observation
	•	Change configuration near ships
	•	Occasionally mirror sensor pulses
	•	Appear to “grow toward” energy sources

Even if they aren’t sentient, they behave too meaningfully to be dismissed.

These will vary wildly by culture and era:
	•	The Choirs – because they resonate
	•	Frozen Minds
	•	Glass Gods (early explorers, later mocked)
	•	Star Cathedrals
	•	Light Fossils
	•	The Rememberers (fringe philosophy)
	•	Dead Angels (popular among frontier crews)
