1. Common Harpies
	•	Definition: The baseline—part woman, part bird, shrieking predators of the skies.
	•	Traits: Wings for [[Arms]], clawed talons, piercing voices.
	•	Symbolism: Predatory [[Hunger]], the corruption of beauty.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Siren Harpies
	•	Definition: Harpies that use song and allure to lure victims.
	•	Traits: Mesmerizing voices, illusions of beauty, sudden brutality.
	•	Symbolism: Desire turned to doom; temptation with talons.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Carrion Harpies
	•	Definition: Scavenger harpies that feast on the dead.
	•	Traits: Filthy feathers, hooked beaks, live in graveyards or battlefields.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]’s scavengers, nature’s vultures made monstrous.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Storm Harpies
	•	Definition: Harpies born of tempests and coastal cliffs.
	•	Traits: Wind-borne flight, lightning screams, storms follow their flocks.
	•	Symbolism: Sudden destruction, the cruelty of nature unleashed.

⸻

5. Desert Harpies
	•	Definition: Harpies adapted to arid wastes.
	•	Traits: Dust-colored wings, shrieks that cause disorientation, scavengers of bones.
	•	Symbolism: Survival in desolation, the predator of emptiness.

⸻

6. Frost Harpies
	•	Definition: Harpies of tundras and frozen peaks.
	•	Traits: White plumage, razor-ice feathers, frigid auras.
	•	Symbolism: Cold [[Hunger]]; merciless winter given wings.

⸻

7. Shadow Harpies
	•	Definition: Harpies tied to curses, graveyards, or the [[Abyss]].
	•	Traits: Batlike wings, spectral talons, voices that cause despair.
	•	Symbolism: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]-omen, predator of the soul.

⸻

8. Noble Harpies
	•	Definition: Rare harpies who have structured societies or courts.
	•	Traits: More humanoid features, use of tools or weapons, political intrigue.
	•	Symbolism: Civilization layered over predation; the predator in silks.

⸻

9. Beast Harpies
	•	Definition: More animalistic, monstrous forms—nearly feral.
	•	Traits: Bird heads, monstrous claws, animal cunning.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]] stripped away entirely; instinct triumphant.

⸻

10. Arch-Harpies (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary harpies said to embody the very storm or curse itself.
	•	Traits: Towering size, storm-winged forms, voices that shatter armies.
	•	Symbolism: Harpy as apocalypse; the scream of destruction itself.
