## Summary

Religious: 
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
![[Pasted image 20250830153100.png]]
A Shellworld is a planetary-scale megastructure where the [[Natural]] core (or an [[Artificial]] anchor) is wrapped in multiple concentric shells. Each shell is a habitable surface with its own ecosystems, cities, and climates, separated by structural voids, machine [[Layers]], or oceanic volumes. A single Shellworld can hold dozens of “worlds stacked atop one another.”
Structure & Scale

Core Anchor: Planetary core or an inserted Matter Core stabilizes gravity.

Shell [[Layers]]: Concentric spheres or nested strata, each tens to hundreds of kilometers apart.

Access Wells: [[Titan]]-scale shafts (Atlas Pillar tunnels) connecting [[Layers]].

Support Machinery: [[Mantle Pistons]], [[Deep-Well Geo-Taps]], and [[Computronium]] overseers keep the structure stable.

Habitability

Each shell can sustain a different climate or function: farmland, cities, oceans, industrial strips, sanctums.

Atmospheres trapped between [[Layers]], or replaced with void oceans for energy harvesting.

Populations can number in the trillions, even on modest Shellworlds.

Variants

Civil Shells: Peaceful, stacked habitats, each layer optimized for population and [[culture]].

Industrial Shells: Entire strata devoted to strip-mining, forging, and megafabrication.

Sacral Shells: Each layer a sanctum, with reliquaries, [[Temples]], or Concordia hymn-chambers.

War Shells: Armored strata, each layer its own Magnus Bastion — a world that’s also a fortress onion.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Serve as eternal homes, perfect for housing civilizations across millennia.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Unassailable bastions — invading one layer only exposes the next.

Economic: Provide incomprehensible space for farming, industry, and trade hubs.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Each shell ribbed with cathedral-like supports, vast buttresses stretching horizon to horizon.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, a Shellworld appears as a flawless black orb — featureless perfection.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Inside, every layer spirals into fractal geometry, with [[Artificial]] sunlines and sky-mandalas.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Walls between shells inscribed with glyph-light; when seen from access wells, they glow like stained glass stacked into infinity.

Standing in a Shellworld, you could travel “up” through a tower shaft and emerge into an entirely new world, its own skies, seas, and [[Cultures]].

Narrative Function

To citizens: The ultimate security — if one layer falls, many remain.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic mastery — planets turned into onion-[[Citadels]] and scripture-worlds.

To myth: Called the “Amber Strata”, believed to be the multilayered thrones of the Empress’s wisdom.
Known as the “Amber Strata” — myths say they are worlds stacked by the Empress like pages in a scripture-book.

To rebels: A nightmare — resistance is smothered in layer after layer of prisons.
