## Summary

GalFed officially separates governance from religion, but its institutions carry religious weight. The Aether Singularity is remembered as wound, warning, and almost-sacred proof that consciousness must be guarded.

## Details

Major orders:

- The Silent Vigil: caretakers of worlds whose populations remain physically alive but cognitively unreachable.
- The Restoration Orders: clinicians, harmonic specialists, and memory engineers who attempt noetic recovery.
- The Beacon Archivists: custodians of origin records, cultural submissions, and trauma histories.
- The Wound Cantors: Soa'lain-descended ceremonial choirs that perform remembrance harmonics on Singularity anniversaries.
- The Null Monastics: communities that train in low-Aether cognition and mental isolation.
- The Ordinate Chaplaincies: moral officers attached to enforcement fleets, tasked with framing coercion as prevention.

Common rituals:

- Silence Hour: galaxy-wide comms dimming in memory of the Mindfall.
- Name Return: ceremony for restored individuals who reclaim a stable identity.
- Archive Offering: submission of language, art, genome, or myth to Beacon.
- Stewardship Oath: pledge taken by officials assigned to recovering worlds.
- Wound Fast: temporary disconnection from high-bandwidth noetic networks.

GalFed faith is rarely theistic. Its reverence is institutional: memory, continuity, recovery, and the terror of silence.
