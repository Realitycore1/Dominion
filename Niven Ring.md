A Niven Ring is a rigid megastructure encircling a star, a band placed in the habitable zone (~1 AU), spinning fast enough to create Earthlike gravity on its inner surface. Its habitable area dwarfs any planet — [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] million Earths’ worth of surface — making it both the largest single-world habitat and a literal halo of life.
Structure & Scale

Radius: ~150 million km ([[Earth (Terra)]]’s orbital distance).

Width: Tens of thousands of km.

Stabilized by active station-keeping [[Systems]] (gravitic jets, Concordia harmonics, mass-balancing satellites).

Habitability

Inner band lined with oceans, continents, mountain [[Chains]].

Day/night created by shadow squares — [[Giant]] panels orbiting between ring and star.

Atmosphere held in place by sheer walls tens of thousands of km high.

Climate, geography, and [[culture]] vary along millions of km — entire civilizations can rise and fall in one small stretch of the band.

Energy & [[Infrastructure]]

Dyson Swarm integration: Solar collectors anchored around the ring.

[[Computronium]] Nodes: Embedded across the rim to coordinate climate, traffic, and Concordia harmonics.

Cornucopia Machines: Supply endless material and food [[Production]].

Launch Loops & Mass Drivers: Built into the rim as transportation arteries.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: The ring itself can become a fortress-habitat — artillery spires along the rim capable of interstellar [[Fire]].

Economic: Serves as an entire sector capital — a single ring can anchor trade, fleets, and commerce for hundreds of [[Systems]].

Religious: Known as the “Amber Crown” — the greatest symbol of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] order, a literal halo around the sun.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Interior rib-mountains shaped like cathedral buttresses, framing seas and skies.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Cities spiral upward along the rim, fractal geometries carved into landscape.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: The inner band inscribed with glyphs visible from orbit — scripture written in continents and seas.

From the inside, the sky curves upward into infinity — land and ocean climbing until they vanish in the distance, like living stained glass arched into heaven.
