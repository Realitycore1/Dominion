⸻

Executive summary

In late 2036 a small, gold-sheathed reliquary (hereafter “the Reliquary”) was opened under provincial conservation conditions in southern [[Ethiopia]] after being retrieved from a remote, monastic cave complex. The opening produced an energetic event characterized by an intense, vertically-oriented thermal/ionization column, immediate failure of electronic [[Systems]] in the local area, and atypical radiological signatures. The event precipitated rapid politicization and proxy involvement, serving as the proximate catalyst for regional destabilization that fed into broader gray-zone competition among major powers.

This dossier synthesizes field [[Reports]], satellite and HF/VLF monitored data, medical triage logs, intelligence intercepts, and recorded testimony into a single reference entry for historians, policy analysts, and archivists.

⸻

Physical description (non-technical, observational)
	•	Form: small chest (approximate palm-to-shoulder sized as preserved in inventory photos), external gilding, hammered gold leaf finish with no modern fastenings. Heavy for its size (reported by moving crew). Interior lined with organic textiles (charred, brittle). A sealed inner capsule observed after forced opening; capsule later rendered physically degraded by the energetic event.
	•	Surface features: patina consistent with prolonged subterranean burial; ancient metallurgical repair marks (blackened patches). No modern maker’s marks or inscriptions visible in plain light.
	•	Context: recovered from a sealed recess within a rock chamber beneath a [[Forgotten]] mountain temple/monastic complex in the Bale/Harenna highlands. Local oral tradition recalled an itinerant brotherhood; no modern custodial lineage.

⸻

Chain of custody (2033 – 2040)
	•	2033: Regional mining company secures exploratory concessions in the district under contract with a provincial council. Informal salvage crews and local prospectors begin limited bore operations near undocumented ecclesiastical ruins.
	•	2034 (approx.): Treasure/salvage driller discovers gilded chest in a collapsed cleft adjacent to cave shrine. Item is recovered clandestinely by the driller and transferred to base camp. Two local witnesses (monastery orphan and elder) observe removal and report unusual sensory phenomena (hair-raising, avian flight).
	•	2035: Mining consortium discreetly routes the chest to a provincial conservator for assessment. Conservator documents chest and requests formal authorization for transfer to national authorities. Authorization stalled by competing local claims and contractual pressures.
	•	Late 2036 (T0): Provincial team opens the chest in a modest conservation room. Immediate energetic event occurs (see Incident Log). Local authorities initiate cordon and notify national security channels; mining consortium places private security around facility.
	•	Post-incident (48–72 hrs): Multiple actors assert custody: Ethiopian Orthodox Church (spiritual/heritage claim), provincial governor (state patrimony and security), the mining consortium (finder’s [[Rights]] and liability), and an international salvage contractor (contractual claim via shell company). Concurrently, covert inquiries by regional intelligence services and foreign private [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] [[Entities]] are documented within days.
	•	2037–2040: The Reliquary becomes a contested object; salvage/safe removal attempts, clandestine sample trades, and proxy [[Skirmishes]] occur. The object is ultimately removed under ambiguous circumstances during a chaotic transfer attempt (classified [[Reports]] differ about intervening actors). The removal marks the transition from localized incident to a regional security crisis.

⸻

Incident log — opening (T0 = local [[Time]], DD.MM.2036)

T0–00:00: Conservator’s assistant pries open chest lid in provincial facility. Observers report instantaneous white-hot column of light extending vertically through ceiling. Heat, pressure wave, and ionization effects noted in immediate vicinity.
T0+00:01–00:10: Local electronics (mobile networks, vehicle ignitions, street lighting) fail inside an area extending several kilometers. HF/VLF radio bands register intense ionospheric noise during the period. Satellite IR registers a transient hot spot coincident with opening.
T0+00:30: First-response medics arrive. Multiple casualties report nausea, vomiting, burns, and neurological symptoms consistent with acute exposure to ionizing radiation and thermal insult. Field Geiger counters show elevated counts with non-standard spectral dispersion (mixed emissions).
T0+06:00 – T0+72:00: Provincial authorities institute cordon. National security teams, intelligence detachments, and private security contractors appear. Conflicting statements issued to press. Rumors of “dirty bomb” spread; initial international media frames the event as either terrorist radiological attack or industrial accident.

Measured signals & forensic notes (declassified summary)
	•	Satellite/Remote sensing: short-duration IR spike at T0 with a vertical thermal plume signature; no classical mushroom cloud morphology.
	•	Communications: HF/VLF propagation anomalies and localized radio blackout consistent with ionization events; significant packet loss and cellular outages recorded in regional telco logs.
	•	Radiological: hand-held and mobile survey units reported elevated gamma counts. Spectral analysis by national labs (later leaked) indicated heterogeneous energy distribution — not conforming to a standard fission-yield detonation signature. Analysts described readings as “smeared and composite.” No immediate evidence of a preexisting weapon detonation, but widespread contamination of surfaces and short-lived volatile radioisotopes were present.
	•	Medical: cluster of ARS-like presentations (nausea, vomiting, acute dermatitis, neurological disorientation) among high-exposure cohort. Hospitals rapidly overwhelmed; international medical teams later corroborated triage logs. Psychological shock and mass hysteria amplified civil disruption.

⸻

Actors of immediate interest
	•	Ethiopian Orthodox Church: asserted spiritual custodian status; attempted to secure facility and claim immediate jurisdiction. Internal divisions noted between conservative clerical leadership and reformist younger monks (leaks of unauthorized footage attributed to the latter).
	•	Provincial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]]: declared public health emergency; later criticized for perceived opacity and slow handover to international technical teams.
	•	Mining consortium / salvage contractor: legal claimants; evidence later suggests ties to a foreign logistics front (documented shell company linkages).
	•	Private [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]] company (PMC) elements: multiple PMCs surfaced offering “secure transfer” services; at least one engagement between PMC escorts and local militia documented (clashes, casualties).
	•	Foreign intelligence services (covert): intercepts indicate early rapid interest by several regional powers (cabling, commercial assets, research outreach), consistent with attempts to obtain samples or technical data covertly.
	•	Religious/paramilitary mobilizers (local & external): charismatic preachers used the event to recruit; diaspora religious NGOs stage demonstrations and legal claims, intensifying politicized dynamics.

⸻

Escalation vectors & consequences (2036–2040)
	•	Political: rapid internationalization of custody dispute; diplomatic entanglements exploited by external powers via front companies and covert operators.
	•	Security: privatized security operations and proxy militia involvement convert a heritage incident into kinetic engagement.
	•	Information: competing [[II - Companion Narratives]] (terrorist attack, industrial accident, sign of divine intervention) proliferate; misattribution accelerates interstate posturing.
	•	Medical & economic: cordons and export restrictions imposed; local agriculture and trade disrupted; refugee flows initiated from cordoned districts.
	•	Strategic risk: the incident is used as grounds for limited deniable kinetic actions by interested states; norms against tactical radiological or low-yield countermeasures erode incrementally.

Field testimony excerpts (selected, declassified)

“It looked not like a bomb but like the night pulled inside a torch. The radios died and then the men fell like ash.” — Provincial responder, audio log 23.11.2036.

“We were trying to pry it open for cataloguing…then light like a spear. The boy who opened it collapsed and began to vomit. I have never seen anything like that.” — Conservator’s assistant, statement, 24.11.2036.

Confidence, uncertainties, and recommendations for archival use
	•	Confidence: High in the sequence of events as recorded (discovery → opening → energetic incident → politicization). Moderate confidence in the precise physical mechanisms due to mixed spectral data and incomplete chain-of-custody of samples prior to removal.
	•	Uncertainties: The exact internal composition of the Reliquary’s sealed capsule was never fully forensically characterized in public records; several samples were removed clandestinely. Attribution of intent (deliberate trigger versus accidental tampering) remains contested in the archive.
	•	Archive recommendations: Preserve all remaining chain-of-custody documentation, intercept logs, medical triage records, and satellite feed captures. Curate oral histories from local witnesses as primary source testimony. Maintain red-teamed historiography to assess misattribution dynamics that followed the incident
