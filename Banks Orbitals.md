A Banks Orbital is a massive [[Artificial]] ring — thousands to millions of kilometers in diameter — rotating to provide centrifugal gravity on its inner surface. Unlike full Dyson constructs, they’re open worlds, with habitable bands of ocean, land, and sky wrapping around the ring’s inside.
Structure

A thin but vast ring, typically 1–[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] million km in diameter, [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]],000–5,000 km [[Wide]].

Rotation provides ~1g across the habitable inner band.

Outer hull armored with smart composites, atmospheric retention walls kilometers high.

Habitability

Land/ocean distribution can mimic Earthlike environments or exotic biospheres.

Climate regulated by mirrorshades — vast reflectors and [[Sunshields]] tilting around the orbital plane.

Supports trillions of inhabitants comfortably.

Stability

Kept aligned by active mass-drivers and Atlas Pillar tethers to planetary bodies or [[Artificial]] anchors.

Autonomous MRO ecologies swarm along the superstructure, maintaining equilibrium and patching breaches.
