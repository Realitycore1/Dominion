1. Hyperactive Stellar Magnetosphere (Artemis Star)
	•	Artemis has abnormally strong and variable solar magnetic cycles, producing frequent stellar superflares.
	•	These bombard [[Harmina]] with charged [[Particles]], leading to:
	•	Global auroras (not just polar).
	•	Severe electromagnetic disruptions.
	•	Amplification of weather [[Systems]] → megastorms, flaming tornadoes (caused by high electrical potential).
