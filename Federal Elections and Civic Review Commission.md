# Federal Elections and Civic Review Commission

## Summary

The Federal Elections and Civic Review Commission administers elections, civic review periods, franchise audits, recall processes, and public legitimacy reporting.

The Dominion is not a simple mass democracy. Its Federal Branch uses service-franchised representation, merit filters, military-administrative competency thresholds, and local compacts. That complexity makes election administration a major institution.

## Mandate

The Commission manages:

- Federal elections;
- provincial certification;
- local compact voting rules;
- service-franchise verification;
- campaign conduct;
- recall procedures;
- public referendum logistics;
- emergency election postponement review;
- civic-access standards for isolated habitats and frontier worlds.

## Powers

The Commission may certify results, nullify corrupted precincts, sanction campaigns, audit voting systems, and order reruns when procedural legitimacy fails.

## Limits

It cannot decide who deserves rights, suppress lawful opposition, rewrite compact law without court review, or use service status to silence political minorities.

## Public Face

Most citizens encounter the Commission during franchise renewal, candidate forums, public review days, and mandatory civic-literacy updates.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show lawful Federal resistance to the Royal Branch, public frustration with service-franchise rules, and the tension between expertise and popular legitimacy.
