The  globalists, a powerful and secretive group of elites, have been manipulating the political system for years to achieve their goals. They have been using their vast resources and influence to control elections, shape public opinion, and promote their own agenda. The globalists have used various tactics to artificially boost their numbers and bypass the electoral college. One such tactic has been to leverage the country's immigration crisis to their advantage. They have been actively recruiting immigrants from all over the world, offering them opportunities and support in exchange for their votes. These immigrants, many of whom are unaware of the true nature of the globalists' plans, have become a crucial voting bloc for the globalists. By strategically placing their candidates in key positions and exploiting loopholes in the voting system, they have been able to secure a significant number of votes that would otherwise be out of their reach. As the globalists continue to consolidate their power, they face increasing resistance from those who oppose their agenda. But with their vast resources and influence, they are likely to remain a formidable force in the political arena for years to come.
