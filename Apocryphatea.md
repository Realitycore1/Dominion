I. The Library of Unwritten Histories

A realm containing:

* civilizations that never happened
* timelines erased before completion
* dead futures
* abandoned destinies

Entire worlds exist only as:

* drafts
* incomplete memory
* partially written possibility

Books rewrite themselves constantly.

Some inhabitants know they were “cut” from reality.

⸻

II. The Hollow Heaven

A dead divine realm.

The gods are gone.

But:

* prayers still arrive
* angels still perform routines
* bells still ring
* rituals still function

The system persists long after purpose vanished.

This realm should feel hauntingly procedural.

⸻

III. The Ash Cartography

A universe burned by total axiomatic collapse.

Only outlines remain.

Cities appear as:

* charcoal silhouettes
* ember geometry
* memory impressions

Nothing fully exists,
yet nothing fully disappears.

Travelers report hearing entire extinct civilizations speaking beneath the ash storms.

⸻

IV. The Kingdom of the Last Name

A realm where:

* identity persists longer than matter

Bodies decay.

Worlds erode.

But names remain physically real.

Dynasties wage war through:

* titles
* lineage
* remembered authority

To forget something there is equivalent to killing it.

⸻

V. The Veiled Basilica

A reality hidden behind self-censoring perception.

You cannot fully remember it after leaving.

Maps rewrite themselves.

Descriptions decay.

Entire expeditions return with:

* missing members
* altered memories
* contradictory journals

The realm actively suppresses stable documentation.

⸻

VI. The Choir of Static Saints

A broken communication-realm.

Contains:

* corrupted prophets
* failed transmissions
* divine messages fragmented by cosmic interference

The inhabitants constantly broadcast:

* prayers
* warnings
* prophecies
* screams

Reality there resembles:

* cathedral radio architecture
* signal storms
* luminous static oceans

⸻

VII. The Grave of Forgotten Species

This could tie beautifully into the Beast Chronicles.

A realm containing:

* extinct archetypes
* erased evolutionary branches
* forgotten mythic fauna

Not undead.

Forgotten.

Creatures there weaken when unremembered.

Some actively invade realities to preserve themselves through myth.

⸻

VIII. The Black Census

A realm obsessed with classification.

Everything is catalogued:

* lives
* thoughts
* species
* sins
* histories
* possibilities

The inhabitants believe:

unrecorded existence is indistinguishable from oblivion.

Entire wars are fought over archival deletion.
