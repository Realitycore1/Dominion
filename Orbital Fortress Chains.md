Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Orbital Fortress [[Chains]] are linked networks of superfortresses strung in orbital arcs around planets, moons, or [[Stars]]. Each fortress is a Magnus-class emplacement or larger, and when linked together, they form an unbroken belt of defense, firepower, and surveillance.
Structure

Chain Lattice: Linked by gravitic tethers, beam [[Relays]], and mass-driver couplers.

Anchor Towers: [[Atlas Pillars]] sometimes extend downward, connecting fortresses to planetary crusts.

Defensive [[Systems]]

Whipple-Shield Swarms & [[Kinetic Interdiction Nets]]: Layered over the chain like a cocoon.

Gravitic Curtains: Bend spacetime along the chain’s arc to deflect incoming barrages.

Distributed [[Fire]] Control: [[Computronium]] nodes synchronize fortress [[Fire]] into coherent kill-fields.

Offensive [[Systems]]

Solar Lances & Nicoll–Dyson Beams: Directed through chain-links for coherent stellar [[Fire]].

[[Mass Driver Rings]]: Integrated into the chain, firing barrages from orbit.

Resonant Choir Weapons: [[Gravitational-wave resonators]] spread along the chain to destabilize enemy fleets.

Variations

Planetary [[Chains]]: Crown a single world, turning its orbit into an armored halo.
