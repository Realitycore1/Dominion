# Chapter 18 - First Contact

## Timeline Function

War Years 14-15. Verified contemporary GalFed pressure changes the strategic meaning of the war.

The inherited title *First Contact* refers to the first signal or observation that can no longer be dismissed as old history. It is not the arrival of a GalFed delegation. Formal contact follows the Covenant of Syl'an.

## Event Role

- Matches contemporary observation, missing probes, or an administrative signal to sealed *Ardent Mercy* pursuit records.
- Brings the old GalFed discovery clock back into public and high-command awareness.
- Forces factions to consider whether their war has exposed Harmina or merely made an old approach visible.
- Reframes Project Ra, orbital defense, the space elevator, GalFed schools, biological defense, and anti-synthetic doctrine as preparation rather than paranoia.
- Reveals that some programs had public Harminan purposes and compartmentalized external-defense requirements.
- Leaves enough uncertainty for factions to accuse one another of manipulation.
- Makes planetary unity a survival argument rather than only a political one.

## Consequence

Harmina's war is no longer only about Harmina, but proof of an approaching power does not make Harminans agree who should answer it.
