Elite champions of [[Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)]], forged from the souls of the greatest betrayers, tyrants, and warlords.

Clad in infernal armor that fuses flesh, bone, and metal.

They serve as Hell’s swords, riding out when Archdemons require conquest or suppression.

[[Origin]]

Said to be chosen directly by the Throne of [[Chains]] in Gehenna.

A mortal soul condemned for supreme [[Betrayal]] or cruelty may be reforged into a Cavalieri.

They are bound by oaths of ash and sealed in armor that burns their former [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] away.

Ranks

Equites di Cenere (Ash Knights)

The most common rank.

Weapons: barbed lances, ash-forged blades.

Role: shock troops of Hell’s cavalry.

Legati di Fiamme (Flame Legates)

Commanders of infernal hosts.

Wield banners of burning scripture that sap hope from enemy [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Legions|Legions]].

Often lead [[Crusades]] into the mortal realm.

Cavalieri dell’Abisso (Abyssal Knights)

Bound to the Abyssal Vault, serving Aeonic [[Demons]] directly.

Weapons: void-forged spears that unravel matter.

Ride mounts of shadow that bleed [[Stars]].

Il Nero Cavaliere (The Black Knight)

Singular title. Said to be the first knight of Hell, who betrayed Heaven itself.

Armor: a prison of iron thorns.

Weapon: a broken angelic spear, forever burning in shadow.

Mounts

Hellsteeds: skeletal horses wreathed in black flame.

Chimeric Beasts: hybrids of wolf, serpent, and bat.

Nightmares: shadows given form, leaving trails of ash.

Function

Enforcers of Archdemons.

Raiders of the Dead [[Realms]] (harvesting souls).

Champions in Hell’s endless wars of suspicion and [[Betrayal]].

Symbolism

If [[Angels]] ride as heralds of the Holy Will, the Cavalieri di Inferno ride as mocking reflections: knighthood without honor, loyalty without trust.

Their oaths bind them not in faith, but in endless treachery — each knight waiting for the chance to usurp the other.
