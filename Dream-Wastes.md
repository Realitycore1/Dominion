# Dream-Wastes

## Alternate Names
- The Somnial Fractures
- The Night Beyond Sleep
- The Oneiric Ruins
- The Shifting Wastes
- The Sleepless Deep
- The Dreaming Scar
- The Hollow Reverie

---

# Ontological Classification

Oneirogenic Fracture Realm  
Subconscious Overlap Zone  
Archetypal Manifestation Continuum

---

# Core Definition

The Dream-Wastes are unstable overlap regions between:
- mortal consciousness
- the Oneiroverse
- and autonomous dream-space.

They are formed where:
- collective subconscious pressure
- unresolved archetypes
- nightmares
- emotional residue
- and dream logic

bleed into coherent reality.

The Dream-Wastes are not dreams themselves.

They are:
> dreams that failed to remain unreal.

---

# Fundamental Nature

Reality within the Dream-Wastes is governed primarily by:
# cognitive resonance.

Meaning:
- perception affects environment
- emotion alters geography
- fear attracts predators
- memory reshapes structure
- symbolic logic overrides physical law

The realm continuously reorganizes itself around:
- thought
- subconscious expectation
- archetypal pressure
- and narrative association.


---

# Environmental Structure

The Dream-Wastes possess no stable geography.

Landscapes constantly mutate according to:
- nearby consciousness
- emotional density
- dream cycles
- archetypal dominance
- and subconscious conflict.

Examples include:
- forests made from sleeping faces
- endless stairways
- drowned cities beneath moonlit skies
- impossible carnivals
- upside-down oceans
- labyrinths built from forgotten memories
- deserts of drifting teeth
- silent suburbs repeating infinitely

The environment often behaves symbolically rather than physically.

---

# Temporal Instability

Time barely functions coherently.

Instead:
## dream cycles dominate progression.

Events may:
- repeat
- skip
- merge
- reverse
- or occur simultaneously.

A traveler may:
- spend years inside while hours pass outside
- forget entire journeys instantly
- relive the same encounter endlessly

Chronology becomes:
> emotionally structured rather than causally structured.

---

# Forgotten Archetypes

One of the defining phenomena of the Dream-Wastes.

Archetypes abandoned by civilizations:
- myths
- fears
- symbols
- cultural memories
- suppressed identities
- forgotten gods
- primal images

do not disappear completely.

Instead:
they accumulate within the Dream-Wastes until:
> symbolic pressure crystallizes them into autonomous existence.

These entities are not always sentient.

Some behave like:
- recurring psychological storms
- instinctive symbolic predators
- or self-repeating narratives.

---

# Native Entities

---

# Night-Terrors

Predatory dream-entities formed from concentrated fear.

Characteristics:
- unstable anatomy
- shifting forms
- symbolic traits
- emotional feeding behavior

They often resemble:
- childhood fears
- cultural monsters
- distorted human forms
- impossible predators

Their appearance changes depending on observer psychology.

---

# Shapeshifters

Highly adaptive oneirogenic organisms.

They imitate:
- loved ones
- authority figures
- forgotten memories
- or desired forms

Unlike ordinary mimics,
they feed on:
- trust
- recognition
- emotional attachment
- and identity certainty.

Some lose their original form permanently.

---

# Sleep Parasites

Small but catastrophic entities that invade dreaming minds.

They consume:
- memory
- emotional coherence
- imagination
- symbolic association

Long-term infestation causes:
- personality erosion
- dream contamination
- waking hallucinations
- and eventual Somnial Dissolution.

Some civilizations consider them more dangerous than physical predators.

---

# The Hollowed

Mortals who remained too long within the Dream-Wastes.

They become:
- partially unreal
- emotionally fragmented
- dream-reactive
- and psychologically unstable.

Some continue wandering endlessly as:
- lucid husks
- memory scavengers
- or narrative-dependent beings.

---

# The Oneiroverse Connection

The Dream-Wastes are not the full Oneiroverse.

They are:
> fractured shoreline regions where mortal cognition touches deeper dream cosmology.

Beyond the Fractures lie:
- deeper dream-realms
- archetypal oceans
- conceptual entities
- and primordial subconscious structures far older than mortal civilization.

Most never return from deeper descent.

---

# Daily Existence

Existence within the Dream-Wastes revolves around:
# adaptation through uncertainty.

Nothing remains stable long enough for conventional civilization.

Survival depends on:
- symbolic awareness
- emotional control
- lucid cognition
- narrative manipulation
- and resistance to dream absorption.

Native beings thrive through:
- confusion
- deception
- misdirection
- emotional destabilization
- and recursive psychological traps.

Trust becomes extremely dangerous.

---

# Dominion Interpretation

The Dominion classifies the Dream-Wastes as:
## cognitively hazardous reality-overlap zones.

Major concerns include:
- archetypal contamination
- subconscious instability
- memetic propagation
- narrative infection
- dream-entity manifestation
- and continuity erosion.

Several Dominion black-sites study:
- weaponized dream logic
- symbolic warfare
- and oneirogenic entity stabilization.

Most projects end catastrophically.

---

# Philosophical Significance

The Dream-Wastes imply something deeply unsettling:

> thoughts do not vanish harmlessly.

Fear leaves structure.

Memory leaves residue.

Myth leaves pressure.

And eventually:
the subconscious may become ecological.

---

# Canonical Summary

> “The Dream-Wastes are not places you visit.  
> They are places your mind accidentally teaches reality how to become.”
