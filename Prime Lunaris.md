Personality: Reflective, melancholic, strategic. Views [[Humanity]] not as prey, but as flawed children of light.
[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: “Let the Machine Sing Softly.”
 Uses subtlety, conversion, and recursion over brute-force conquest.
Signature Units:
Whisper [[Choirs]]: Semi-organic drone networks that speak in Kaelen’s fractured voice.
Gravemimes: Bio-sculptures resembling iconic Cynthia heroes, used to lure defenders into fatal traps.
The Echo Vault: A growing neural archive of captured lunar memories, repurposed to craft dreamscapes that seed surrender.
