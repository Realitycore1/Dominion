# Story 23 - The Body Question

## Viewpoint

Rema Sorith, a clinic counselor handling enhancement consent sessions.

## Setting

Years 189-190s AL, regional clinic during the Body Question debates.

## Story Shape

Rema meets people who want pain reduction, permanent muscle retention, engineered attractiveness, fertility support, and maintenance-cycle relief. She also meets people who fear that refusing enhancement will make them socially obsolete.

The policy debate outside sounds abstract. Inside the clinic it is a teenager asking if natural features will make them unemployable, a parent asking if pain reduction is mercy, and a clone worker asking why their body is treated as public infrastructure.

## Harmina Element

Biological immortality does not end human vulnerability. It creates new forms of pressure.

## Character Movement

Rema stops treating consent as a signed form. She begins treating it as a social condition the clinic cannot guarantee alone.

## Future Payoff

This story ties daily life to later Dominion bioethics, clone rights, labor policy, and engineered beauty debates.
