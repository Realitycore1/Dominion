## Summary

GalFed iconography is built from trauma, recovery, and controlled unity. Its symbols present the Federation as a sheltering intelligence: many worlds held inside one stabilizing field.

## Details

Primary emblem: The Sevenfold Halo.

The Sevenfold Halo is a ring divided into seven luminous arcs around a dark central point. The arcs represent the founding survivor civilizations. The dark point represents the Aether Singularity and the silence at the center of GalFed memory. Official interpretations say the ring contains the wound. Dissident interpretations say the wound controls the ring.

Secondary symbols:

- Open hand over a star: relief, stewardship, humanitarian intervention.
- Closed ring around a world: quarantine, protected status, or restricted development.
- White archive flame: preserved memory and official history.
- Broken eye restored with a line of light: cognition recovery.
- Seven descending rays: Founder authority.
- Black starburst: Aether hazard, forbidden technology, or Singularity remembrance.

Colors:

- White: restoration, medical neutrality, official truth.
- Deep blue: continuity, law, transit space.
- Gold: Founder mandate and institutional authority.
- Black: the Singularity, silence, quarantine, and unprocessed trauma.
- Soft green: approved uplift and ecological stabilization.

Common phrases:

- "No World Stands Alone."
- "Continuity is Mercy."
- "Memory Prevents Silence."
- "Stewardship Before Sovereignty."
- "The Wound Must Not Reopen."

Public GalFed spaces avoid overt militarism. Even prisons, courts, and quarantine gates are decorated as clinics, memorials, or transit sanctuaries. The aesthetic lesson is simple: control should feel like rescue.
