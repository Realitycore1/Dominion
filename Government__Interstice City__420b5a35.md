•	Council of Custodians:
	•	A rotating body of representatives from countless civilizations and factions.
	•	No permanent seats; custodianship is temporary to avoid domination by any single power.
	•	Keepers of the Anchors: Enigmatic guardians (some believe constructs, others say ancient beings) who enforce neutrality and prevent axiomatic collapse.
	•	The Open Charter: A codified set of rules ensuring equality of entry, sanctuary, and trade.
