# Tessellated Exoderm of Concordia

**Registry Code:** CON-ARC-EXO-01
**Domain:** Concordia • Mixed-tier magitech infrastructure
**Status:** Canon proposal • Draft v1.0

---

## 1) Executive Summary

The **Tessellated Exoderm** is a modular, self-healing outer skin used across Concordia—on cities, fortresses, vehicles, habitats, and select megafauna/warforms. It fuses lithic composites, living lattice materials, and runic/field conduits into hex‑dominant tessellations. Each tile (“derm‑cell”) is a micro‑infrastructure unit: it senses, regulates climate, channels power/mana, and acts as localized armor and actuator. Arrays of derm‑cells coordinate as a distributed organism to reconfigure façades, seal breaches, and morph aerodynamic/defensive profiles in real time.

---

## 2) Nomenclature & Taxonomy

* **Exoderm:** Exterior protective and regulatory layer.
* **Derm‑cell:** The fundamental tile. Sizes: **S** (8–15 cm), **M** (25–40 cm), **L** (0.8–1.5 m), **XL** (3–6 m).
* **Mesh‑plexus:** Sub‑surface conduit web for energy, fluids, and control signals.
* **Holochoric seam:** Inter‑tile seam that can open, flex, or seal with zero-gap tolerances.
* **Stratum keys:** Layer‑locking keystones that set the array’s mechanical mode (rigid, viscoelastic, shear‑slip, etc.).

---

## 3) Function Overview

* **Protection:** Multi-hit ablative + phase‑biased warding; impact and thermal diffusion across local clusters.
* **Regulation:** Thermoregulation, humidity and ion balance, pollutant capture, radiation attenuation.
* **Metabolic/Power Handling:** Bidirectional channels for mana, charge, heat, and reagent gases.
* **Morphodynamics:** Controlled camber, ribbing, and surface roughness for aerodynamics/hydraulics; deployable spines and vanes.
* **Sensing:** Pressure, strain, EM spectrum (UV–IR), thaumic flux, bio‑chems; per‑cell sensor fusion.
* **Fabrication/Repair:** In-situ growth from feedstock inks, mycelial binders, and field‑guided crystallization.

---

## 4) Architecture

**4.1 Unit Geometry**
Default tiling: hexagonal core with interspersed pent/quad “defect” tiles to enable curvature. Edges use adaptive rhombi for seam control.

**4.2 Layer Stack (outer → inner)**

1. **Sacrificial Albedo Film:** Tunable emissivity; sheds soot, acids, and spores.
2. **Hardface Lamina:** Ceramic‑carbide with micro‑scales; accepts ward‑runes.
3. **Metabiotic Lattice:** Living fiber‑gel with piezo‑thaumic transducers; heals and routes signals.
4. **Field Conductor Mesh:** Superionic rails + mana veins; contains shunting nodes and surge vaults.
5. **Root‑substrate:** Anchors to substructure; integrates stratum keys and mesh‑plexus ports.

**4.3 Stratum Modes**

* **Bastion:** Lock rigid; maximum hardness/ward density.
* **Quench:** Max thermal drawdown; radiative fluting blooms.
* **Vesper:** Low‑signature; RF/thaumic dampening; matte biolume off.
* **Torrent:** Hydrodynamic riblets; anti‑fouling secretion.
* **Wing:** Camber and vortex control; aero vanes extend.
* **Suture:** High‑elasticity for sealing/repair and organismal interfaces.

## 6) Energetics & Conduits

* **Mana Veins:** Directional permeability; prevent backwash during ritual surges.
* **Heatways:** Phase‑change micro‑channels; dump to radiators or geothermal sinks.
* **Charge Rails:** Superionic lines with per‑tile fuses; burst‑rated for spellcasting recoil.
* **Chem Lines:** Nutrient and reagent feed to the metabiotic layer; doubles as foggers.

## 7) Interfaces

* **Portals & Hatches:** Tiles fold into irising rings; ward continuity preserved with sliding sigils.
* **Viewport Tiles:** Spectrally tunable; switch from opaque armor to refractive sensor windows.
* **Organismal Grafts:** Suture mode binds to living hulls/warforms without necrosis; supports symbiosis.

---

## 8) Behaviors (Swarm Control)

* **Local Autonomy:** Each derm‑cell runs a reflex kernel (fallback behaviors).
* **Plexus Consensus:** Cluster‑level negotiation sets mode per sector; resolves conflicts (e.g., Quench vs Bastion).
* **Healing Protocols:** Tri‑phase—clot (gel seal), knit (fiber growth), temper (re‑crystallize hardface).
* **Growth:** L‑ and XL‑cells nucleate curvature and load paths; S‑cells infill detail and seams.

---

## 9) Defensive Systems

* **Kinetics:** Graded density; shear‑slip seams; shock shunts to root‑substrate.
* **Thermal/Chem:** Ablatives and phase‑draw; acid‑resistant exudates.
* **Thaumic:** Ward stacks (hex net + spiral tracks); resonant counter‑glyphs.
* **Countermeasures:** Pop‑out chaff‑spines; EM/thaumic misting; decoy biolume patterns.
* **Siege Morphs:** Deployable buttresses, mantlets, and scale‑flaps to protect artillery mouths.

---

## 10) Environmental & Civic Systems

* **Climate Skin:** Seasonal morphs; harvests dew, vents heat, grows shade canopies.
* **Sanitation:** Biofilms that digest soot and molds; UV‑biolume sweeps.
* **Harvest:** Micro‑algal luster tiles for power/biomass; spore gardens for air polishing.
* **Signage:** Living glyphs display status; emergency wayfinding animates along flow paths.

## 11) Variants

1. **Civic Exoderm (CX):** Emphasis on climate and sanitation; Russet→Amber tiers.
2. **Fortress Exoderm (FX):** Bastion‑biased; Amber→Adamant tiers; heavy wards.
3. **Aero‑Exoderm (AX):** Wing/Torrent modes; Argent→Platina; anti‑icing runes.
4. **Naval Exoderm (NX):** Anti‑fouling toxins suppressed; hydrophilic/‑phobic cycling.
5. **Warform Exoderm (WX):** Organismal grafts; pain‑avoidance heuristics; reflex hardening.
6. **Relic‑Vault Exoderm (RVX):** Null‑flux seams; de‑enspirited corridors; Adamant grade.

## 12) Governance & Safeties

* **Keymaster Sigils:** Civic‑level control of mode changes; tamper logs etched in crystalline memory.
* **Ethical Locks:** WX variants refuse to seal living beings into voidless spaces; override requires tri‑sigil authority.

## 13) Manufacturing & Deployment

* **Print‑Grow Lines:** Hybrid additive + biogrowth; tiles matured in vats, tempered on‑site.
* **Seeding:** Drone‑laid root‑substrate; mesh‑plexus spun like mycelium through structure.
* **Commissioning:** Grid attunement ritual binds wards; calibration storm (wind/thermal) to set baselines.

## 14) Failure Modes & Counters

* **Salt Bloom:** Mineral ingress disrupts piezo channels → periodic desal cycles.
* **Glyph Rot:** Mis‑scribed wards accumulate phase debt → scheduled re‑inking.
* **Plexus Spam:** Malcode floods cluster consensus → air‑gapped reflex kernels take over.
* **Opposition Tactics:** Resonant peening (anti‑tiling harmonics), solvent fogs, void‑salt darts, glyph inversion.
