True Symmorphica ecosystems emerge.

Life converges toward:

* hollow anatomy,
* luminous cores,
* distributed nervous systems,
* and adaptive shell morphologies.

Mass die-offs occur among:

* traditional fauna,
* rigid biosystems,
* and non-adaptive engineered species.

Harmina’s old biosphere effectively collapses.
