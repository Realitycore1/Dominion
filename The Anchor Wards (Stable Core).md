•	Nature: These are the “hearths” of Interstice. Anchors (guilds, artifacts, ancient bloodlines) stabilize reality enough for permanent buildings, markets, and homes.
	•	Feel: Brick streets, markets, and spires that look almost normal — but every few blocks, geometry doesn’t quite line up.
	•	Function: Trade hubs, embassies of other factions, rumor markets. Anyone seeking Interstice starts here.
	•	Story Hook: A character might grow up here thinking it’s “just a weird city,” only later realizing it’s the last stable island in a sea of madness.
