## Communication with Silentium
- **Resonance:** [[Ameise]] harmonics = forward keyed; [[Silentium]] = inverted. To each other, it feels like conversing with reversed, mirrored speech.  
- **Pheromones:** Nearly identical but chiral-misaligned. A [[Silentium]] distress call may read as a war-cry to an [[Ameise]].  
- **Tragedy:** Both can almost understand—but never without distortion, creating kinship tinged with revulsion.
