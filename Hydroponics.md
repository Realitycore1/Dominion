a soil-less agricultural method that grows plants using nutrient-rich water solutions. This method has critical advantages in both terrestrial and space-based environments, especially for civilizations like the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] that require scalable, efficient, and adaptable food [[Systems]].

Terrestrial Hydroponics
Used extensively on colony worlds and domed ecologies.

Applications:
	•	Urban Arcologies: Vertical farms built into [[Hive Cities]] and arcologies produce fresh food where land is scarce.
	•	Barren Worlds: Planets with toxic or infertile soil use hydroponic vaults to produce agricultural output.
	•	Disaster Recovery: Deployed rapidly in warzones or post-catastrophe areas for emergency food generation.

[[Systems]]:
	•	Nutrient Film Technique (NFT): Thin nutrient film flows over roots suspended in a sloped channel.
	•	Ebb & Flow: Roots are periodically flooded and drained, useful in low-power environments.
	•	Deep Water [[culture]] (DWC): Roots float in oxygenated nutrient solution—simple, scalable.

Space Based Hydroponics
Critical to long-duration missions, orbital stations, and deep-space arks.

Applications:
	•	Ark Ships: Maintain closed-loop biospheres aboard long-range colony vessels.
	•	Orbital Habitats: Supply food, oxygen, and psychological benefit to personnel.
	•	Terraforming Outposts: Early-stage support for atmospherically unprepared planets.

	•	Psycho-Botanical Optimization: Plants genetically tuned to influence crew morale, reduce stress, or regulate oxygen with emotional resonance factors.
