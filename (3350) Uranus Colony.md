# (3350) Uranus Colony

Permanent Uranian colonies are recognized in 3350.

They center on Titania, Oberon, Ariel, and orbital facilities rather than the atmosphere of Uranus itself.

The colonies specialize in:

- cryogenic engineering;
- magnetic confinement;
- volatile extraction;
- long-duration fuel and biological storage;
- low-temperature materials;
- navigation and communications for deep-system routes.

Uranian laboratories pioneer industrial antimatter containment, cartridge handling, remote transfer, and emergency disposal.

Antimatter production remains limited until Neptunian accelerator complexes open in 3400, but Uranian engineering makes storage and transport possible.

The colonies join the Outer Systems Compact while remaining internally divided over whether independence justifies placing containment facilities under military command.
