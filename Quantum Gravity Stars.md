I. Physical Definition

A Quantum Gravity Star is a stellar-scale object whose structure and stability are governed not by classical degeneracy pressure or thermal dynamics, but by quantum gravitational effects operating at or near the Planck scale.
In such [[Stars]], spacetime geometry itself becomes quantized, and the star’s equilibrium emerges from the balance between gravitational collapse and quantum spacetime repulsion.
