# Belt Colonial Committee

The Belt Colonial Committee is the Colonial Union’s regulatory layer across asteroid concessions, habitats, navigation routes, and extraction claims. It develops during the Inner-System boom of 3050–3180 rather than through one canonical founding event.

Its formal functions include claim registration, habitat recognition, navigation standards, concession oversight, and support for Areopagus rescue and salvage courts. Its practical reach is limited by distance and mobility.

Mining cooperatives, corporations, family stations, mafia utilities, cartels, gangs, pirates, and mobile fleets often provide air, rescue, credit, or enforcement before committee law arrives. No central authority controls the Belt.

Around 3230, mega-stations receive semi-independent charters governing schools, air, rescue, and local courts. Corporations retain claims, criminal houses retain infrastructure, and fleets continue to ignore borders that do not move.

The Committee’s lasting importance is procedural: it creates enough shared claim, pressure, docking, and rescue language for incompatible communities to trade without becoming one state.

For the resource economy, factions, patchwork culture, wars, and interstellar contribution, see [[Belt Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
