Psychological warfare is a method of mentally attacking chosen targets. The method of attack used is infrasound which is sound emitted at 30hz. ( most of the energy is emitted below the [[Human]] hearing range ). This attack is used to condition the brain by send code through the sound produced. Side effects of Indoctrination at stage 1 follows paranoia, hearing slight voices. Stage [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] follows pushing away friends and family, Strange behavior, moderate paranoia, moderate hallucinations and Forgetfulness. Stage [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] follows severe paranoia, actively hostile, severe hallucinations, speaks in third person. 

The stages of indoctrination can be notable around the second stage, as you get closer the signal gets stronger and the effects go faster.
