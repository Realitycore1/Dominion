## Summary

GalFed is formally a federation of member species and restored civilizations. In practice it is a custodial hierarchy ruled by permanent Founder primacy, layered ministries, predictive governance, and stewardship law.

## Details

Highest authority: The Council of Primacy.

The Council of Primacy is composed of the seven founding survivor civilizations of the Accord of Seven Harmonies. Membership is hereditary at the civilizational level, not personal. No new civilization can become a full Founder, though exceptional cultures can receive honorary adjunct status after long compliance.

Second authority: The Synod of Member Worlds.

The Synod debates budgets, relief quotas, corridor rules, legal harmonization, and cultural petitions. It gives member worlds a voice, but Founder vetoes, predictive risk rulings, and Aether safety orders can override Synod decisions.

Third authority: Stewardship Courts.

Stewardship Courts govern worlds not considered fully stable. They issue cultural compliance orders, technology permissions, archive access rulings, restoration mandates, quarantine judgments, and leadership suitability reviews.

World status ladder:

- Silent World: population physically survives but coherent sapience is absent or unreachable.
- Recovery World: restoration underway under direct GalFed supervision.
- Stewarded World: partially autonomous but assigned a Founder sponsor.
- Member World: recognized participant in GalFed law and trade.
- Restricted World: access controlled due to Aether, ecological, or political risk.
- Stability Risk: subject to sanctions, observation, or intervention.
- Quarantine World: isolated from transit and trade.
- Extinction Memory Preserve: protected ruin, archive, or dead civilization site.

GalFed law assumes that sovereignty is conditional. A world earns autonomy by proving cognitive stability, memetic safety, economic reliability, and compliance with Aether restrictions.

## Consolidated Lock - Managed Baseline and Founder Triage

Source: [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience Workbook Consolidated Locks v1.0]]

GalFed's uplift model is capped by managed baseline doctrine.

The baseline gives a civilization enough capacity to function safely inside GalFed systems:

- medicine;
- sanitation;
- translation;
- standard education;
- safe transit;
- administrative legibility;
- stable trade;
- limited technical competence;
- dependency on GalFed infrastructure.

It withholds or restricts the capacities that would let a civilization walk away:

- uncontrolled Aether maturity;
- independent high science;
- strategic parity;
- relay autonomy;
- cultural divergence beyond approved range;
- true interstellar sovereignty.

The hidden logic of crisis triage favors the seven Founder civilizations first. When GalFed overstretches, Founder worlds and high-value corridor hubs receive protection before dependent, uplifted, stewarded, or client worlds.

This exposes the central hierarchy:

> The Seven Founders are protected first, while dependent worlds serve as strategic insulation.
