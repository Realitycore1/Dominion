# (103) Convoy Season

## What Happened

In Year 103 AL, convoys began leaving New Aurora in a sustained wave. The departures were quiet and procedural: machines rolling into rain, families carrying route bags, teachers carrying slates, engineers carrying tools, and children learning that home could move outward.

## What Resulted

- Future national cores began as convoy cultures.
- Atlovaia, Ormova, Estelia, the Commonwealth, Toldax, the F.S.S.R., and other powers gained their founding populations.
- The emotional center of Harmina spread beyond New Aurora.

## Long-Term Consequence

Convoy Season marks the beginning of planetary civilization in practice. Harmina was no longer a first city with outposts. It was becoming a world of regions.
