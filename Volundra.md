# The Dragon Cycle Sandbox

## Overview
A dark, post-industrial fantasy world where civilizations rise and fall through cyclical apocalypses caused by the rebirth of the Dragon-God — a cosmic entity of shadow, fire, and divine speech. Each victory against it doubles its strength in the next cycle, while surrender resets the cycle but destroys civilization. Knowledge of the cycles is fragmented; only broken demigods and cursed survivors recall glimpses of the truth.

---

## Cosmology of the Cycle
- **The Dragon-God**: Speaks Fundamental Enochian, reshaping reality with each word. It is plague, beast, and prophet — both destroyer and renewer.
- **Law of Rebirth**: Every defeat multiplies its next form’s power (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x…), while surrender resets the scale but annihilates civilization.
- **The Archangels**: Zeal, Ardor, Freedom, and Confidence lead divine crusades to contain each rebirth. Their power burns the world as much as it saves it.
- **Demigods**: Mortal champions fused with relics, angelic grace, or plague essence. Each burns brightly and briefly before collapse.

---

## The Cycles
1. **First Cycle (1x)** – Baseline dragon; victory at immense cost.
2. **Second Cycle (2x)** – Escalation; fractured world memory.
3. **Third Cycle (4x)** – Angels die, demigods fall, civilization barely endures.
4. **Fourth Cycle (8x)** – Global ruin, near-surrender; victory dooms the next.
5. **Fifth Cycle (16x)** – The current era; the world trembles in anticipation of the next awakening.

If mortals and angels fall, the Dragon rules until it burns itself out — the world resets to 1x, beginning anew in ash.

---

## Current Era (14k–15k AT)
### The Hair-Trigger Age
- Monster populations grow restless and territorial.
- Plague outbreaks and relic awakenings spark mass paranoia.
- Angels enforce holy surveillance while demons whisper surrender.

### The Black Season → False Dragons → The Great Shattering
- The dragon-plague spreads through dreams, relics, and blood.
- False Dragons — plague-born hybrids and Wyrm-Titans — devour nations.
- Ley-lines collapse, angels and demons wage open war, and the Dragon-God begins to reform.

### The Awakening (15,000 AT)
- The Dragon-God returns, speaking Enochian that fractures reality.
- Demigods and Archangels rise for one last stand.
- The world teeters between annihilation, reset, or escalation.

---

## Factions
- **Angelic Bloc** – Theocratic crusaders upholding the Doctrine of Struggle. Resist at any cost.
- **Demonic Syndicates** – Cartels and cults embracing the Doctrine of Fire. Fall and reset.
- **Ashborn Faith** – Heretical sects preaching surrender as purification.
- **Relic Corporations** – Elven, dwarven, and human megacorporations monetizing relics and fear.
- **Orc & Goblin Movements** – Ashborn legions and Ember Dust cartels thriving in chaos.
- **Human Nations** – Divided, pragmatic, and perpetually caught between extremes.

---

## Bestiary of the False Dragon Era
1. **Plague-Spawned Drakes** – Winged shadow predators born from infection.
2. **Wyrm-Titans** – City-sized wyverns fused with steel and stone.
3. **Ashborn Hybrids** – Dragon-blooded mortals forming plague cults.
4. **Shadow-Wraith Dragons** – Spirits of the infected dead.
5. **Ember Leviathans** – Sea serpents that boil oceans.
6. **Corpse-Dragons** – Necrotech monstrosities built from plague corpses.
7. **False Dragon-Lords** – Mortal rulers transformed into demigod tyrants.

---

## Relics & Myths
- **Cradle Chalice** – A mythical artifact said to sever the Dragon Cycle. All seekers are corrupted or destroyed.
- **Relic Weapons** – Angelic forges, demonic engines, and ancient runes capable of killing gods — temporarily.
- **Survivor Texts** – Fragments of scripture and ravings from previous cycles. Contradictory, heretical, and dangerous.

---

## Geography
- **Veythar** – Ground Zero megacity, now half-dragon lair, half-metropolis.
- **Dragon Zones** – Corrupted neon cities where the plague and shadow-fire reign.
- **The Umbral** – A shadow-realm overlapping reality; the echo of the Dragon’s dreams.
- **Corpse-Lands** – Fossilized remnants of prior Dragon-Gods.

---

## Themes & Play Modes
- **Street-Level Survival** – Wardens vs plague gangs, Ember Dust addicts, relic black markets.
- **Faction Politics** – Angelic propaganda, demonic deals, relic wars.
- **Epic Myth** – Archangels and demigods battling apocalypse.
- **Aftermath** – Reconstruction, heresy, and broken memory after reset.

---

## Story Seeds
- *The Last Warden*: A detective policing plague outbreaks in Veythar.
- *Demigod’s Curse*: A hero burning alive under their relic’s power.
- *The Heretic Prophet*: A cult leader claiming the reset is salvation.
- *Angel’s Bargain*: A crusader forced into atrocities to contain corruption.
- *The Cradle Quest*: A pilgrimage to find the mythical relic that could end the cycles forever.
