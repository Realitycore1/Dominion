Core Bias:

Collective adaptation through turbulence

This is where Storm merges with Hive logic.

Meaning:

Instability is not merely survived — it is collectively metabolized, coordinated, and used as an adaptive advantage.

Traits:
	•	swarm coordination under chaos
	•	collective adaptation to repeated disruption
	•	distributed response systems
	•	communal turbulence management
	•	societies that are strongest during crisis

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the storm does not scatter the swarm, it organizes it
	•	crisis is the basin’s natural order
	•	collective intelligence emerges through repeated shock

Common Expressions:
	•	war-swarm civilizations
	•	emergency-adapted machine societies
	•	eusocial conflict basins
	•	crisis-born intelligence systems

Failure Mode:

Perpetual mobilization
The collective becomes incapable of existing outside emergency mode.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	militarized hive societies
	•	adaptive swarm ecologies
	•	civilizations born from constant crisis
	•	war-state realities
