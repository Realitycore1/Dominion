Classification

Petrification Predators / Curse-Bearers / Threshold Guardians

⸻

CORE IDEA

Gorgons are punishment that remembers being wronged.

They are not mindless monsters and not simple victims. A Gorgon’s power is not just the petrifying gaze—it is the conversion of violation into permanence. Where boundaries are crossed and cannot be restored, Gorgons arise to make transgression irreversible.

They do not kill for hunger.
They end stories mid-sentence.

⸻

ORIGIN

Gorgons emerge through layered injustice:
	•	divine punishment misapplied
	•	curses inflicted to contain trauma
	•	relic exposure tied to forbidden sight
	•	cultural myths hardened into biology

Some Gorgons were once mortals.
Others were born into the role—raised to be warnings.

What unites them is this:
their gaze is not malice—it is boundary enforcement.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Gorgons are humanoid, beautiful or terrible by turns, always unsettling.

Common traits:
	•	Serpentine hair—living, independent, watchful
	•	Scaled skin along neck, shoulders, or limbs
	•	Eyes reflective like polished stone or molten glass
	•	Fangs or elongated canines
	•	Movements precise, coiled, deliberate

Their presence subtly stiffens the environment—plants grow rigid, air feels heavier.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Gorgons are territorial, proud, and deeply selective.
	•	Avoid unnecessary conflict
	•	Issue warnings through posture, sound, or indirect sightlines
	•	Attack decisively when boundaries are crossed
	•	Rarely pursue fleeing prey

They do not enjoy their power.
They accept it.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

High.
	•	Acute situational awareness
	•	Deep understanding of fear and intent
	•	Long memory for trespass and respect
	•	Capable of negotiation—but not forgiveness

A Gorgon may spare those who avert their eyes willingly.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Petrifying Gaze: converts organic matter into stone-like states
	•	Grace-Ambivalent: angelic presence does not negate their power
	•	Malice-Reactive: corruption spreads petrification radius
	•	Statue Memory: petrified victims retain faint psychic echoes

Breaking a Gorgon statue often releases unfinished consequences.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Dwell in ruins, borderlands, sacred exclusions
	•	Landscapes around them fill with statues—warnings and memorials
	•	Rare reproduction; many lineages end violently
	•	Their absence causes guarded zones to collapse

A Gorgon’s lair is a museum of mistakes.

⸻

ALPHA GORGONS

An Alpha Gorgon is not stronger—it is absolute.

Traits:
	•	Petrification occurs without direct eye contact
	•	Reflections and shadows carry effect
	•	Territory becomes semi-lithic over time
	•	Can selectively restore victims—at terrible cost

Alpha Gorgons become living seals on forbidden places.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (individual) / Existential (Alpha or sanctum-bound)

You don’t rush a Gorgon.
You approach with humility—or not at all.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Gorgons represent:
	•	violated consent made lethal
	•	punishment without appeal
	•	beauty weaponized by injustice
	•	boundaries that will not move

Where Catoblepas erase life,
Gorgons freeze it as testimony.
