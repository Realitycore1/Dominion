# Chapter XIII - Battle for Artemis

## Timeline Function

Late War / Unification Years. The Artemis theater decides who controls the sky, the weapons grid, and the exit routes.

## Event Role

- Orbital platforms, spacecraft security, autonomous defense, and long-horizon artillery converge.
- Civilian populations far from the battle understand that the result will govern their lives.
- The winning side gains military leverage but also inherits responsibility for external defense.
- GalFed pressure shapes the battle's urgency without erasing the danger of placing domestic surveillance beneath an external-defense mandate.
- Victory establishes the first deliberately unified planetary defense command.

## Consequence

Victory at Artemis does not end the war by itself, but it makes the covenant possible.
