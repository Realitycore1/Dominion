1. Core Principles
	•	Uniformity → Weapons are recognizable across all [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]]; a [[Soldier]] can pick up any Federal arm and know how it functions.
	•	Modularity → Each weapon has enhanced fittings (bio-interface, NLPC ports, [[Aetheric]] couplers) so standard and enhanced [[Soldiers]] can use the same chassis with different augmentations.
	•	Symbolism → Every Federal Branch weapon carries the crest of the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] — arms are not just tools but extensions of mandate.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Primary Weapons

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Pattern Rifle (DPR-01 “Verdict”)
	•	Form: Sleek bullpup plasma-mass accelerator hybrid.
	•	Modes:
	•	Standard: Gauss-propelled flechettes (for [[Humans]]).
	•	Enhanced: Plasma-bloom channeling through NLPC (for augmented).
	•	Symbolism: Double-barrel rails shaped into a cross motif, glowing with faint [[Aetheric]] tracery.
	•	Use: The universal infantry rifle; every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Soldier]] carries one.

⸻

Sidearm – “Concord Pistol”
	•	Design: Compact gravitic-pulse pistol with failsafe ballistics fallback.
	•	Enhanced Mode: Generates a micro-singular pulse, stabilized by a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] seal.
	•	Role: Standard officer’s sidearm, also issued to auxiliary troops.
	•	Symbolism: Always etched with the Federal seal — used in executions as much as in combat.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Melee Standards

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Saber (Pattern-Imperial)
	•	Standard Form: Monomolecular edge sword, durable and balanced.
	•	Enhanced Upgrade: Can channel [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] charge → becomes a radiant blade capable of slicing through powered armor.
	•	Role: Formal and ceremonial as well as practical. Carried by officers, [[Verarch]] adjutants, and executioners.

Gladius Imperium (Trooper Standard)
	•	Form: Short, broad vibroblade designed for brutal [[Close]] combat.
	•	Enhanced Mode: Resonant edge vibrates at supra-sonic frequencies, cutting through composites and bone alike.
	•	Role: Trench, ship, and urban combat.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Heavy Weapons

“Oathkeeper” Support Cannon
	•	Standard: Portable coilgun with high-velocity penetrators.
	•	Enhanced: Can channel [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] energy cores to [[Fire]] aurora beams (aurora-like plasma tracers that sear flesh and disrupt shielding).
	•	Role: Carried by shock troops and enhanced squads; doubles as anti-vehicle weapon.

Judicator Pattern Launcher
	•	Design: Smart-munition rocket [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].
	•	Enhanced Mode: Can deploy self-guiding, micro-AI missiles.
	•	Symbolism: Seen as the Federal Branch’s answer to rebellion — heavy, indisputable punishment.

⸻

5. Defensive Standards

Aegis Shield Projector
	•	Form: Wrist-mounted defense projector, collapsible into an energy shield.
	•	Standard: Small barrier, used for breaching.
	•	Enhanced: Large, dome-like defense grid, linked to NLPC for squad protection.

Standard Federal Armor (SFA-01 “Bastion”)
	•	Standard: Layered ballistic-ceramic with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] seal.
	•	Enhanced: Integrated NLPC couplers, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] regulators, and resonance amplifiers for [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|Legions]] and [[Verarch]]-command.

⸻

6. Symbolic and Psychological Weapons
	•	“Mandate Pike”: Used by ceremonial guards; doubles as shock-lance in combat. Represents the authority of the Federal Branch over both citizens and [[Soldiers]].
	•	Executioner’s Pattern Blade: Broad, heavy, designed for public punishment. Often carried by [[Infernariate]] detachments.
