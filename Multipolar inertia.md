I. Definition — The Stalemate Century (2035–2075)

By mid-century, no single nation or ideology can project undisputed dominance.
Power splinters into overlapping blocs—economic, digital, cultural, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Military/Military|Military]]—each strong enough to prevent change, none strong enough to lead.
The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] functions like a planetary version of trench warfare: every actor dug into its sphere, endlessly maneuvering without movement.

“The balance of weakness became our last peace.” — Global Strategic Review, 2104

⸻

II. Causes of Inertia

1. [[Energy plateau]] Economics

Every bloc hits the same thermodynamic wall.
Energy costs suppress industrial expansion, making conquest unprofitable and reconstruction impossible.
Wars become unaffordable in peacetime budgets but inevitable once panic sets in.

2. Data Balkanization

[[Information saturation]] fragments the global narrative.
Each bloc lives in its own algorithmic echo chamber; diplomacy becomes translation between incompatible truths.
AI-mediated foreign ministries interpret allies as much as adversaries.

3. AI Bureaucratization

Automation removes charismatic decision-makers.
[[Policies]] are outputs of committee-trained models—optimized for stability, allergic to risk.
Governments think faster, decide slower.

4. Post-Ideological Cynicism

Liberalism, communism, nationalism—all metabolized into marketing languages.
The population no longer believes in [[Systems]], only in survival.
This kills the utopian drive that once fueled space programs and revolutions alike.

5. Climate and Resource Lock-Ins

Water, arable land, and rare minerals harden borders.
[[Infrastructure]] scarcity forces localism; trade agreements become hostage treaties.
Every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Government|Government]] manages decline like accountants of entropy.
