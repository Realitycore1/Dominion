# (2996) AI Astronaut Corps

The Phaeton Program deploys embodied artificial astronauts for orbital construction, inspection, rescue preparation, and swarm coordination.

AI astronauts can move between compatible robotic bodies and retain mission memory across transfers. Their legal status becomes controversial because the Federation describes them as crew during danger and licensed systems in procurement law.

The personhood dispute centers on whether continuity of memory, self-direction, and civic responsibility can coexist with institutional ownership of hardware.

Noa Bell-Reyes, uplifted constitutional jurists, AI crew, labor advocates, and federal procurement offices become the principal parties.
