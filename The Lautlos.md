The Lautlos is a 150 ft tall mammalian with a sleek black appearance and consists of 8 appendages, two hind legs, four long [[Arms]] and two short [[Arms]]. 
The Lautlos uses Infrasound as a means of communication which consists of high and low frequency roars. And uses Ultrasounds so locate nearby [[Entities]].
