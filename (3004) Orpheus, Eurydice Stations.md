# (3004) Orpheus, Eurydice Stations

## Canonical Event

Orpheus and Eurydice are paired Stanford torus stations.

### Orpheus

- industry;
- administration;
- performance and media;
- political influence;
- orbital commerce.

### Eurydice

- agriculture;
- ecological reserves;
- medicine;
- family residence;
- closed-loop research.

Orpheus presents itself as the voice of orbital civilization. Eurydice resents being treated as its support habitat despite maintaining much of the paired system's living reserve.
