## Summary

Erasure: Ou
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Quarantine Veils are semi-permeable field membranes deployed around planets, moons, or star [[Systems]] to isolate, filter, and suppress. They block movement, filter signals, and alter perception — not just containment, but erasure. Veils differ from Curtains in that they are energetic and symbolic, often invisible or auroral, rather than mechanical lattices.
Field Structure

Membrane Layer: A vast surface of energy-fields projected by orbital nodes.

Veil Anchors: Orbital or surface spires maintain lattice harmonics.

Signal Filters: Veils selectively block comms, radiation, or even Concordia resonance.

Perception Warping: To those inside, the veil appears as sky-[[Fire]], false constellations, or endless aurorae.

Functions

Containment: Stops unauthorized ships, signals, or transmissions.

Suppression: Blocks cultural, memetic, or magical contamination.

Weaponization: Veils can contract, collapsing into sterilization fields.

Variants

Planetary Veils: Surround a single world — the population sees the veil as a second, false heaven.

Systemic Veils: Span entire star [[Systems]], creating “zones of [[Silence]]” in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] map.

Sacral Veils: Used to guard [[Dark Eternity Vaults]], relic worlds, or condemned sanctums.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Deployed in emergencies — plague, ecological collapse, or runaway Concordia resonance.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Serve as soft blockades — rebels find themselves erased from maps, sealed under shimmering skies.

Economic: Preserve resources “on hold” until reclamation or strip-mining.

Religious: Known as the “Veils of [[Silence]]” — said to be the Empress drawing her veil across her face, refusing to look upon the impure.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Veil towers shaped like black ribbed cathedrals, beaming light into heaven.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Veils ripple in fractal harmonics, like living stained glass across the sky.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs bloom across the veil in auroral [[Fire]], scripture stretching horizon to horizon.

From the surface, a Veil looks like false [[Stars]] trembling in the sky, auroras that hum like hymns. To those trapped, it feels like the world itself has been shrouded in [[Silence]].

Narrative Function

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Efficient and symbolic — not only quarantine, but obliteration from perception.

To myth: Called the “Amber Veils of [[Silence]]”, believed to be the Empress’s mourning shrouds, draped over fallen worlds.
