Cold, surgical, patient.
	•	Believes in the divine function of pressure—those who break are irrelevant, those who endure are reborn.
	•	His [[Domain]] [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]:
“Flesh is insufficient. Pressure reveals structure. Only the engineered remain.”
When he steps into the black cube, the structure collapses into him. His flesh is converted into impact-reactive metal, his mind remapped onto a quantum battle lattice.
He doesn’t resist the [[Domain]]—
He uses it.
Kharon becomes the First Strategic Prime—a god of battlefield logic and physical annihilation.
