## Summary

GalFed ministries began as emergency recovery organs after the Aether Singularity. Ten thousand years later they form a dense administrative lattice that controls relief, restoration, transit, memory, technology, and member-world legitimacy.

## Details

Major councils and ministries:

- Council of Primacy: permanent Founder authority and final arbiter of existential risk.
- Synod of Member Worlds: deliberative assembly for recognized member civilizations.
- Ministry of Continuity: coordinates disaster planning, social stability, and long-term civilizational survival.
- Ministry of Aether Safety: regulates Aether research, Singularity hazard zones, and field-engineering permissions.
- Ministry of Restoration: oversees cognition repair, silent population care, and noetic medicine.
- Archive Gate Authority: controls official memory records, cultural submissions, and history certification.
- Corridor and Transit Ministry: licenses FTL routes, jump windows, rescue corridors, and interdiction zones.
- Founders' Clearing Houses: regulate chronocredits, trade access, debt settlement, and economic sanctions.
- Office of Uplift and Stewardship: assigns sponsor civilizations to recovering or emergent worlds.
- Bureau of Language Canonization: certifies diplomatic language, translation law, and trade syntax.
- Custodial Harmonium: law enforcement, deterrent fleets, quarantine fleets, and disaster security.
- Central Predictive Engine Directorate: converts risk models into advisory or mandatory intervention orders.

GalFed ministries deliberately overlap. No single ministry can act entirely alone, but almost any ministry can delay a world, classify it, or force review. This makes the state resilient and nearly impossible to escape.
