# New Reader FAQ

This page answers the questions a first-time reader is most likely to ask.

## What is the Pleroma Archive?

Answer:
The Pleroma Archive is the public-facing reference system for the Dominion Project. It works like an encyclopedia and atlas: part world guide, part timeline map, part cosmology reference, and part archive-management system.

- [[Tiered Reader Path - Pleroma Archive]]

## What is the Dominion Project?

Answer:
The Dominion Project is the larger setting and archive being organized here. It includes civilizations, characters, history, technology, locations, cosmology, and specialist reality-architecture material.

- [[History Portal]]

## Do I need to understand the cosmology first?

Answer:
No. The archive is easier if you begin with orientation, characters, history, and civilizations before moving into MOT, Pleroma, axioms, or deep cosmology.

## What are the four tiers?

Answer:
The four tiers are a reading-difficulty system. Tier 1 is for new readers, Tier 2 is for civilization and institutional material, Tier 3 is for infrastructure and applied systems, and Tier 4 is for specialist cosmology and reality architecture.

- [[Tiered Reader Path - Pleroma Archive]]

## Why are some notes short or empty?

Answer:
Some notes are placeholders, imports, drafts, or structural anchors created during earlier organization passes. They can still be useful because their titles, folder placement, links, and metadata show where the concept belongs.

Read next:
- [[Archive and Canon FAQ]]

## What does CANON or DRAFT mean?

Answer:
CANON means the note is currently treated as authoritative. DRAFT means it is still under development. LEGACY means it is preserved for historical reference. SUPERSEDED means it has been replaced by newer material.
