## Summary

GalFed thought is not monolithic. Its arguments are ancient, technical, and emotionally rooted in the Aether Singularity. Every school claims to protect consciousness. They disagree over how much freedom consciousness can survive.

## Details

Restorationists believe the Federation exists to recover every silent or damaged mind, even at enormous cost. They oppose abandonment of Silent Worlds and often clash with economic ministries.

Continuity Legalists believe law must prevent another Singularity before compassion acts. They support strict permissions, archive submission, and long probationary stewardship.

Harmonic Humanitarians believe GalFed has become too coercive and should return to voluntary aid, transparent influence, and member-world consent.

Primacy Conservatives believe the Founders survived because they were suited to lead. They defend permanent Council authority as historical necessity.

Predictive Determinists trust the Central Predictive Engine more than political deliberation. They argue that freedom is valuable only after risk has been modeled.

Archive Absolutists believe official memory must be unified. Competing histories are seen as seeds of civilizational fracture.

Null Autonomists believe post-Singularity society should reduce Aether dependency and protect cognition through decentralization rather than control.

Border Secessionists reject the stewardship model entirely. GalFed classifies most of them as sovereignty extremists or corridor apostates.
