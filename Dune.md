Core Bias:

Slow-moving structural migration

This subtype is sparse, but not static.

Its structures move slowly over time.

Traits:
	•	migratory landscapes
	•	shifting stable zones
	•	drifting civilizations or habitats
	•	long-wave change
	•	slow but relentless reconfiguration

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	reality is always moving beneath you
	•	stability exists, but only temporarily
	•	civilizations must adapt to gradual migration

Common Expressions:
	•	nomadic basin civilizations
	•	mobile habitats
	•	drifting trade systems
	•	migratory sacred zones

Failure Mode:

Burial or dislocation
Important structures are lost through gradual shift.

This is very useful for:
	•	mobile megastructures
	•	desert cultures at cosmological scale
	•	wandering basin civilizations
