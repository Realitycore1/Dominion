# Saturn Colonial Committee

The Saturn Colonial Committee coordinates traffic, ring safety, moon access, rescue, medical standards, and supply obligations across Saturn’s distributed settlements. The first formal foothold is recognized by 3250.

Saturn rejects a single-capital Jovian model. Its moons, chandelier cities, Titan settlements, convoy families, and route communities retain distinct councils. The Committee functions as a coordinating network rather than a government that erases those differences.

Chandelier cities hang residences, farms, and transit beneath orbital industrial frames. Their load-balancing architecture shapes Saturnian law: when one structural member changes, every connected district must respond. Civic obligations likewise travel through households, routes, and reciprocal care.

Titan adds a cold-world culture organized around methane weather, patient construction, and hazards that enter slowly.

Saturn becomes a founding power of the Outer Systems Compact in 3425 while continuing to defend distributed moon authority. For its political and cultural development through 3600, see [[Saturnian Colonial Development to Craterheart]].
