Virek was stationed at Bastion Theta, a remote kinetic artillery facility defending Luna’s equator from rogue asteroid [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
	•	The [[Domain]], having already seeded Shaki’s influence into Luna’s information grid, orchestrates a coordinated misfire—triggering a retaliatory strike on allied Protectorate units.
	•	Virek is left isolated, surrounded by enemies and branded as a traitor by his own command.
Turning Point:
Rather than flee, Virek digs in—preparing for a siege, certain he’s been betrayed.
Over 17 days, he kills everything the [[Domain]] sends.
No infection, no absorption—until a massive Wombborn delivers a single biovault: a black cube that emits war songs in his own voice.
It doesn’t fight him.
It agrees with him.
