Core Features


[[Structures Index]]

![[Pasted image 20250830154226.png]]
A StarForge is a mega-industrial structure built directly around or within a star, designed to harness stellar plasma as raw feedstock for forging ships, habitats, weapons, or even new [[Stars]]. It is both factory and altar: industry scaled to stellar [[Fire]], sanctified as a hymn of creation.
Structure

Furnace Mantle: Dyson-like collector shells wrapping portions of the star, channeling plasma into forges.

Crucible Rings: Orbital factories that spin plasma into ships, habitats, and war engines.

Anchor Spires: Atlas-class struts braced into the stellar corona, stabilizing flows.

Functions

Civil:
– Mass-produce [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]], [[Ark Platforms]], and orbital habitats directly from starstuff.
– Seed [[Artificial]] [[Stars]] or power [[Beamed-sail corridors]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Forge entire fleets of [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] in months, not centuries.
– Weaponize: overdrive stellar plasma into directed energy or collapse the star as a weapon.

Religious:
– Serve as holy foundries — “forges of the Empress’s [[Fire]],” where industry becomes liturgy.

Variants

Civil StarForges: Industrial suns, factories of empire.

War StarForges: Furnace-fortresses, mass-producing [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] and weapons.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: An inexhaustible resource — the empire literally grows from [[Stars]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Strategic keystones — whoever controls a StarForge commands fleets without end.

Economic: The ultimate industrial engine, tying galactic economies to the forges of suns.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Forge spires like cathedral chimneys, venting plasma hymns into [[The Void]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, the Forge appears as a dark geometry haloed by stellar [[Fire]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Plasma flows spiral in fractal paths, factories glowing like living mandalas.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Every emission plume inscribed with glyphs — scripture written in [[Fire]], drifting across the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

From nearby, a StarForge looks like a halo of black cathedrals drinking from a sun, rivers of gold plasma flowing upward into crucibles that glow with unending prayer.

Narrative Function

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic necessity — industry scaled to suns ensures eternal supremacy.

To myth: Called the “Amber Anvils”, imagined as the places where the Empress hammers suns into swords.
