## Summary

To rebels: 
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Megastructure Tugs are colossal propulsion constructs designed to push, pull, or tow [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]], ranging from orbital habitats to planetary shells. They are part engine, part anchor, part shrine — embodiments of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] mastery over inertia itself.
Propulsion [[Systems]]

Gravitic Harpoons: Strange-matter anchors that “bite” into mass without physical contact.

Fusion-Wind Jets: Plasma plumes that redirect thrust onto attached [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Halo-Drive Couplers: Use nearby compact objects as reactionless slings.

Concordia Resonance Bands: Stabilize stress across fragile structures so they don’t shear apart in tow.

Harness & Frame

Atlas Spines: Ribbed [[Arms]] kilometers [[Wide]], clamping or tethering to megastructure surfaces.

Void Nets: Distributed drone webs that wrap around structures, spreading the pull evenly.

Matter Cores: Provide anchor-mass, balancing thrust against enormous inertia.

Operations

Relocation: Move [[Banks Orbitals]], Clarke Cities, or Bishop Rings into new orbits.

Assembly: Tow raw panels and struts into place for Dyson Fortresses or [[Hypercivilization Shells]].

Emergency: Haul wounded [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] away from collapsing [[Stars]] or hostile fleets.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Reshape orbital infrastructures; move [[Halo Habs]], launch Lozenge Stations, ferry [[Ark Platforms]] across [[Systems]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Deploy Bastion-move capability — an entire Magnus Bastion or Dyson segment can be dragged into battle.

Economic: Speed up construction timelines by centuries; no sector without tugs can sustain expansion.

Religious: Known as “The Oxen of Amber” — myth frames them as sacred beasts pulling the Empress’s chariot of worlds.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Spinal frames ribbed like cathedral arches, [[Arms]] stretching like divine scaffolds.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, they appear as seamless black crosses clamped to [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Tow-fields shimmer in fractal geometries, spreading stress across glowing nets.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Harpoon bands and thrust jets leave script-like trails across void — glyphs in plasma and gravitation.

When seen in action, a Tug looks like a beast dragging a sun’s crown across the heavens, leaving glowing script in its wake.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Beasts of burden that literally pull their cities and thrones across the sky.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic necessity — no empire that moves [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]] lacks permanence.

To myth: Called the “Oxen of Creation”, beasts harnessed by the Empress to rearrange the heavens.
The nightmare of futility — even whole habitats can be ripped from orbit and carried away.
