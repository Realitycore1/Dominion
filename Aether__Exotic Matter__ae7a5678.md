## Summary
# Aether Field Guide

**Aether (Φ)** is the effective medium of the Overfold—an order parameter telling you how “tunable” local reality is. Instrument it with ClarkeTech; cohere it with licensed rites.

## The four dials
- **Φ — Density:** higher means easier attunement, sturdier windows.  
- **Ζₑ — Impedance:** resistance to change; high Ζₑ “stiffens” reality.  
- **ℓᶜ — Coherence length:** how far a crafted pattern holds.  
- **η — Noise:** turbulence (“aether weather”).

## Site classes (rule-of-thumb)
- **Sanctified:** Φ↑, ℓᶜ↑, Ζₑ↓, η↓ — best for windows/rites (RAC chartered).  
- **Normal:** mid Φ/ℓᶜ; ClarkeTech fine; [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Technomagic|Technomagic]] limited.  
- **Damped (Null):** Φ≈0, ℓᶜ≈0 — rites fail; safe for testing/counter-chorus.  
- **Storm:** η↑↑, Φ jitter — avoid windows; expect PFN flakiness.

## Tools
- **Aetherometers** (ClarkeTech): map Φ, Ζₑ, ℓᶜ, η.  
- **Anchors**: raise Φ, extend ℓᶜ.  
- **Dampers**: raise Ζₑ/η to blunt rites.  
- **Foils/Sails**: trim micro‑thrust in high‑Φ corridors.

## Protocols
- **Null‑[[Verse]] test:** verify [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Technomagic|Technomagic]] dependencies.  
- **Light‑hygiene:** avoid sharp flicker; protect Silentiums/[[Kapik]].  
- **[[Radiator Neutrality]]:** don’t target radiators outside total war.
