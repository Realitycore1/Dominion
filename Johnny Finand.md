Role:

Cargo Tram Operator / Logistics Worker

Function:

Ordinary civilian life anchor.

Johnny is NOT exceptional.

That is why he matters.

⸻

Personality

* social,
* talkative,
* adaptable,
* emotionally transparent,
* younger than the founders,
* born shortly after landfall.

He represents:

normalized Harminan life.

⸻

Why He Matters

Your setting currently risks becoming:

* too philosophical,
* too leadership-focused.

Johnny fixes this.

He lets readers see:

* cafeteria life,
* work gossip,
* storm complaints,
* civilian culture,
* evolving slang,
* relationship normalcy.

⸻

Important Trait

Johnny genuinely likes Harmina.

Not romantically.

Practically.

To him:

* storms are annoying,
* fungal dust is irritating,
* abyssal sounds are creepy—

—but normal.

That difference unsettles older colonists.

⸻

Best Use Scenes

* tram conversations,
* overhearing rumors,
* civilian reactions to politics,
* adapting food culture,
* joking during dangerous work.

⸻

Narrative Purpose

Johnny helps show:

Harmina becoming home accidentally.
