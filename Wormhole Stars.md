I. Physical Definition

A Wormhole Star is a stellar-mass object whose core singularity or [[Exotic Matter]] configuration generates and sustains one or more stable, traversable [[Wormholes]] within its gravitational well.
It is both a luminous stellar body and a spacetime transit hub, with its envelope functioning like a normal star while its interior serves as a spacetime bridge.

Possible structures include:
	•	Core-Integrated Wormhole Throat — The star’s center contains a stabilized wormhole mouth surrounded by an accretion or fusion-powered envelope.
	•	Event-Horizon-Embedded Passage — A black-hole-like interior whose [[Exotic Matter]] prevents collapse, keeping the wormhole open.
	•	Distributed Micro-Wormhole Network — The stellar plasma lattice contains countless sub-throat connections enabling multi-point transit.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Wormhole [[Stars]] are classified as Transit–Energy Dual Nodes, functioning simultaneously as navigation beacons and matter-energy transfer points.
