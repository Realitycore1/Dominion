I. Physical Definition

In standard speculative [[Physics]], a tachyon is a hypothetical particle that always travels faster than the invariant speed c. In conventional relativistic theory, tachyons possess imaginary rest mass (m² < 0) and cannot be slowed to subluminal speeds. While their existence has not been experimentally confirmed, they appear in certain quantum field theory solutions, string theory [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Frameworks|frameworks]], and as instability indicators in vacuum states.

Within the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), tachyons are superluminal harmonic excitations of the ΩC causality field, responsible for phase-advanced interactions between events. Rather than violating causality outright, they enable information and energy transfer through non-linear temporal pathways, operating along [[Verse]]-spanning harmonic nodes outside conventional spacetime metrics.
	•	Spin: Variable; scalar (spin-0) tachyons dominate in pure signal transmission, vector (spin-1) tachyons in structured superluminal currents.
	•	Mass: Imaginary rest mass (quantified in [[VFF]] by complex-valued inertial coefficients).
	•	Velocity: > c, increasing with decreasing energy (inverse to normal [[Particles]]).
