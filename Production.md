What you’re making: almost always antiprotons (later combined with positrons → antihydrogen).
How it’s made (high level only):

Spallation/fragmentation in high-energy proton rings → collect antiprotons in accumulator rings → cool → trap (Penning traps).

Pair production (γ→e⁻e⁺) for positrons; combine later for neutral antihydrogen (better storage prospects).

In-setting upgrades: solar-powered rings near [[Close]]-in orbits; dedicated “[[Antimatter]] farms” with industrial radiators; never aboard a transport.

Storage & handling (only high-level):

Charged: Penning traps (strong B fields, UHV); good for lab quantities.

Neutral antihydrogen: magnetic minimum-B traps (mK temperatures); research-grade today, fiction-grade scalable in your setting.
