## Details

A Wormhole brain is a fictional co
ncept that appears in science fiction literature and films. It is a form of [[Artificial]] intelligence that operates on the principles of wormhole [[Physics]]. The Wormhole brain is designed to connect a central processor with a network of other computers or devices using [[Wormholes]]. This allows for near-instantaneous communication and data transfer between [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Zek'razzar Veyrth/Locations/Locations|Locations]] that may be vastly separated in space and [[Time]]. The brain can also be configured to create new [[Wormholes]] or to manipulate existing ones, allowing it to create complex connections between different points in space and [[Time]]. This makes it an extremely powerful tool for computing and problem-solving. In many stories, the Wormhole brain is depicted as an advanced form of AI that is capable of performing complex tasks and making decisions with near-[[Human]] levels of intelligence. It may be used by powerful organizations or individuals to gain an advantage over their enemies or to access vast amounts of information. However, the Wormhole brain also presents risks, as it can become unstable or out of control, potentially causing catastrophic damage to the surrounding area or even to the universe itself.
