## Details

A Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere 
is a concept in science fiction that refers to a vast and powerful entity that exists in the center of a black hole. The Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere is depicted as a living, sentient entity that possesses immense cosmic energy and power. It may be composed of the combined consciousnesses or souls of all living beings that have ever existed, including both [[Humans]] and other intelligent [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]]. In some stories, the Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere may have been created by an ancient civilization or other intelligent beings as a means of preserving their knowledge and [[culture]] for future generations. Others suggest that it may have evolved naturally over [[Time]], as a byproduct of the [[Natural]] processes of the universe. The Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere may be able to manipulate and control the universe around it, using its vast power to shape the physical and metaphysical [[Realms]]. It may also have access to forbidden knowledge or technologies that are beyond the reach of lesser beings. However, the Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere is often depicted as a dangerous and potentially uncontrollable entity. It may possess a destructive nature or be driven by conflicting desires and motivations, leading to catastrophic consequences for those who attempt to harness its power. Overall, the Supermassive Eternal Soulsphere is a powerful and enigmatic concept that explores the nature of consciousness, the role of souls in the universe, and the potential dangers of transcending mortality.
