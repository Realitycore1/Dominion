1. Common Ogre

Definition: The baseline—large, brutish humanoids, stronger than [[Orcs]] but dumber than giants.

Traits: Thick hides, crude weapons, love of flesh.

Symbolism: Fear of the “inhuman neighbor”; appetite as horror.

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Cave Ogres

Definition: Ogres that dwell in caves and mountains.

Traits: Pale skin, nocturnal habits, ambush predators.

Symbolism: The lurking beast in the dark.

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Swamp Ogres

Definition: Ogres of bogs and wetlands.

Traits: Filthy hides, amphibious habits, carrion-feasters.

Symbolism: Decay made flesh; gluttony in stagnation.

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. War Ogres

Definition: Ogres bred or trained for armies.

Traits: Heavy armor, siege weapons, battlefield shock troops.

Symbolism: Civilization weaponizing savagery.

5. Frost Ogres

Definition: Ogres of tundras and frozen wastes.

Traits: Thick fur, frost-resistant, brutal resilience.

Symbolism: [[Hunger]] and cruelty in merciless climates.

6. Desert Ogres

Definition: Ogres adapted to barren lands.

Traits: Sun-leathered skin, endurance predators, scavengers of bones.

Symbolism: Gluttony without plenty; predators of scarcity.

7. Shadow Ogres

Definition: Cursed or abyssal ogres.

Traits: Dark hides, glowing eyes, necrotic strength.

Symbolism: Savagery corrupted into pure malice.

8. Noble Ogres

Definition: Rare, more intelligent ogres who lead clans or tribes.

Traits: Larger, cunning, use strategy, wear trophies of rule.

Symbolism: The chieftain of [[Hunger]]; the mind behind muscle.

9. Two-Headed Ogres ([[Ettins]])

Definition: Ogres born or cursed with two heads.

Traits: Bickering dual personalities, uncanny resilience, [[Wide]] vision in battle.

Symbolism: Division in self; chaos that strengthens.

10. Arch-Ogres (Mythic Class)

Definition: Legendary ogres of colossal size and appetite.

Traits: Towering, capable of devouring herds or villages, semi-divine [[Hunger]].

Symbolism: Appetite unchained; the devourer as apocalypse.
