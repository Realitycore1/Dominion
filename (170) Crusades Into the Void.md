The Secundus Chapters launch massive retrieval campaigns across Eos.

Objectives:

* locate Amber,
* recover continuity fragments,
* and identify the source of Severance architecture.

The campaigns push:

* beyond stable transit regions,
* through dead verses,
* into emerald-scarred Dracoshian territories.

Many fleets are lost permanently.
