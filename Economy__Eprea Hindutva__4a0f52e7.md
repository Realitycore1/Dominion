The Engine of Hierarchy

The [[Eprea Hindutva]] economy is not driven by markets or efficiency, but by commanded labor organized through caste duty. Productivity is sacred, surplus is hoarded, and inequality is institutionalized.
	•	Caste-Based Labor
	•	Brahmic – monopolize high-value knowledge work: research, medicine, astroengineering. They dictate industrial priorities, ration knowledge, and control interstellar trade networks.
	•	Kshatriya – command the fleets, [[Man]] orbital fortresses, and oversee Shudra/Dalit labor in mines and shipyards. They are both managers and executioners.
	•	Vaishya – run commercial exchange, logistics, and [[Infrastructure]] maintenance. They are allowed some profit, but every transaction is taxed heavily upward.
	•	Shudra – toil in planetary farms, hydroponic towers, refineries, and orbital manufactories. Their output keeps the colony fed and armed.
	•	Dalit – condemned to lethal industries: asteroid cracking, reactor-core cleaning, plasma waste processing. Life expectancy is short, but the regime calls their sacrifice “the deepest purity.”
	•	Resource Extraction
	•	Each of the five [[Systems]] is exploited for a specialty:
	•	One world supplies raw ore,
	•	another energy,
	•	another agricultural output,
	•	one dedicated to shipbuilding,
	•	and one functioning as the administrative/spiritual hub.
	•	Resource quotas are enforced by Kshatriya officers; failure is punished by collective penalties on entire caste neighborhoods.
	•	Currency & Trade
	•	Official scrip is the Rashtra Mark, but value is stratified by caste—one Brahmic Mark buys more than one Shudra Mark.
	•	Black markets thrive in Dalit zones, where barter and smuggled goods are the only relief.
	•	Exports focus on [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] hardware, starship hulls, and disciplined mercenary cadres, while imports are luxuries reserved for upper castes.
	•	Guilds vs. Castes
	•	Unlike [[Austro Guard]]’s guild syndicates, here guilds exist only as caste extensions. For example:
	•	The Shipwright Guild is Vaishya-run, overseen by Brahmic supervisors, and worked by Shudra hands.
	•	The Mining Guild is officially Shudra, but Dalits make up the majority of its workforce.
	•	Economic Philosophy
	•	The regime claims that inequality is efficient, arguing that caste purity prevents social “contamination” of skill and labor.
	•	“One hand wields, one hand builds, one hand feeds”—a phrase taught in schools to sanctify division of labor.
	•	Surplus and Hoarding
	•	Surplus is stored in orbital vault-stations under Brahmic control. Lower castes see little beyond subsistence.
	•	In times of famine, the regime distributes food symbolically by caste order: Brahmic feasts first, Shudra rations last, Dalit scraps if any remain.
