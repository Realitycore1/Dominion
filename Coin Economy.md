•	Medium: metal coinage (gold, silver, copper) or minted tokens backed by guilds/[[Temples]].
	•	Scope: peasants, merchants, [[Soldiers]], lower guilds, and most Ferrous–Argent casters.
	•	Features:
	•	Supply and demand function normally (grain, tools, textiles, iron).
	•	Guild monopolies can warp local markets (e.g., Hydramancers control irrigation taxes).
	•	Magic is a service — you pay a Russet caster to heal a cow, or an Argent to light a district.
	•	Weakness: utterly dependent on density — in [[Deadlands]]/null zones, magic services vanish, prices skyrocket, and coinage collapses into barter.
