# Resource Allocation Authority

## Summary

The Resource Allocation Authority coordinates industrial quotas, energy distribution, emergency rationing, strategic reserves, and interregional supply equity.

It is one of the most politically contested Federal agencies because it decides who receives scarce things first.

## Mandate

The Authority manages:

- energy quotas;
- strategic material reserves;
- emergency rationing;
- industrial priority schedules;
- settlement supply guarantees;
- freight priority with transit agencies;
- restoration-zone material allocation;
- public scarcity reports.

## Powers

It may issue allocation orders, reserve critical materials, redirect supplies during emergency, and penalize hoarding or false reporting.

## Limits

It cannot permanently privilege core worlds, use scarcity to punish political enemies, conceal shortages, or override courts except under lawful emergency authorization.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Authority through ration credits, energy schedules, delayed construction, emergency food packets, and public anger when a distant province receives supplies first.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show moral triage. Resource policy is where survival becomes arithmetic and arithmetic becomes political.
