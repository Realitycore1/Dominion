# Sol Recurring POV Cast and Family Lines

## Use

This document defines the recurring viewpoint ensemble for *Sol*. It replaces anonymous chapter carriers with people whose families, work, errors, and institutions continue across historical transitions.

Dates marked **provisional** are drafting controls rather than locked chronology. Established Immortal birth and stabilization dates remain canon.

The cast contains thirty-eight recurring POV-capable characters:

- twenty-five mortal, uplifted, or synthetic people;
- Elian Voss;
- the twelve surviving Immortals.

Not every listed appearance must become a full viewpoint scene. It must, however, contain a consequential appearance, letter, recording, testimony, remembered action, or direct relationship continuation.

## Family-Line Architecture

The family trees include off-page generations. Those generations create plausible time depth without requiring every descendant to become a named character.

### Sayegh Archive Line — Survivor and Constitutional Family

```text
Nadiya Sayegh (2038-2124)
├── collateral archive branch
│   └── two off-page generations
│       └── Yasmin Sayegh (b. 2137, Immortal)
│           └── House Sayegh by children, adoption, marriage, and archive apprenticeship
│               └── later constitutional branch
│                   └── Tomas Sayegh (2442-2620)
└── Sayegh Archive as nonbiological inheritance
    └── Yesterday archive practice
        └── Solar navigation collection
```

Recurring tension: the family claims transparency while controlling access to the record.

### Vale Route Line — Caravan, Postal, and Outer-System Family

```text
Rami Vale (2070-2156)
└── corridor-surveyor descendants
    └── Hana Vale (2327-2418)
        └── off-page orbital and freight branches
            └── Imani Vale (3094-3298)
                └── outer-system branch
                    └── Cael Vale (b. 3374)
```

Recurring objects: the blue pencil, corrected route marks, undelivered private messages.

Recurring tension: every Vale generation believes a better route can solve a political separation.

## Aging and Continuity Rules

- Give each mortal line at least one childhood, work, partnership, illness or injury, inheritance, and death or departure scene.
- Do not use descendants only to repeat an ancestor's politics.
- A family object changes meaning each time it passes hands.
- An Immortal must sometimes misidentify a descendant's gesture, relationship, or expectation.
- Uplifted and synthetic continuity may be organized through clutch, memory house, copy lineage, school, union, habitat, or chosen household rather than a human surname.
- Long life extension does not equal Immortality. Exact late-era lifespans remain provisional until the medical chronology is locked.
- Surviving into a later manuscript means physical, memory-continuous, or legally recognized survival; note which form applies when Aster-3 or copied minds are involved.
