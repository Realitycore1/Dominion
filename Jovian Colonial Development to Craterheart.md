# Jupiter: a system that refuses one capital

## From foothold to home, 3180–3250

The Jovian Colonial Committee coordinates the Colonial Union’s first permanent presence around Jupiter: radiation shelter, convoy schedules, medical authority, fuel extraction, atmospheric access, and relations among the moons.

The Committee inherits a political impossibility. “Jupiter” is useful as a system name, but it does not describe one inhabited territory. Callisto, Ganymede, Europa, the atmospheric cities, and the other settlements face different hazards and develop different laws.

Callisto shows how the foothold becomes a home. Union plans classify its early medical annexes for short service. Children are nevertheless born there, attend school under shielding, and grow into professionals while their files still call them outer-assignment dependents. A five-year structure becomes a multigenerational district before distant administration changes the form.

The Committee’s practical work therefore shifts from managing temporary crews to recognizing households that exist outside inner-system legal assumptions.

## Local necessity

Radiation and communications delay turn household authority into constitutional authority. A Callisto child requires immediate marrow treatment while the registered guardian is too distant to answer. Local adults authorize surgery; the child survives; Union review upholds the treatment but retains the remote guardian rule.

This conflict reaches Harmonium. Jovian medical delegate Sena Okafor-Kallis argues that authority required to keep a person alive cannot remain entirely where the answer arrives late. The resulting local-necessity principle requires interplanetary agreements to identify who can act before remote authority answers.

The principle does not grant independence. It makes delayed sovereignty visible.

## Jupiter’s atmospheric cities

Jovian atmospheric settlement does not land. Buoyant platforms move through pressure bands while storms larger than Earth turn below and around them. Bridges connect neighborhoods only when relative wind allows. Airships transfer people and cargo between platforms moving at different rates. Pressure elevators descend toward industrial layers.

Danger is vertical. A city rises away from pressure and heat only to encounter radiation and poor lift. It descends away from radiation only to find denser chemistry and stronger pressure. Residents measure home through pressure bands, convoy routes, and the platforms willing to remain connected.

Gas extractors hang below residential cities. Pipelines descend toward hydrogen and helium-3. Workers occupy pressure clinics where refinery vibration never stops.

## Life in the clouds and beneath Europa

The discovery of native Jovian cloud ecologies changes extraction law. Floating organisms migrate through bands industry had treated as empty resource volume. The first atmospheric treaty creates moving biological corridors. Extractors track migrations, alter depth, and close intakes during bioluminescent blooms.

Europa produces a different restraint. Native life is found in the ocean beneath the ice. Europa’s first ocean law prohibits extraction until observers can distinguish organism, colony, habitat, and process. Human settlements use sealed ecologies and do not mix human circulation water with the alien sea.

The Jovian Committee consequently becomes more than a resource office. It must coordinate inhabited safety, native ecologies, and moon governments that reject uniform rules.

## Outer independence

By 3425, Jupiter demands independence as a major Outer-System power. Its moons refuse to disappear inside that demand. Ganymede’s delegates object that Jupiter has no single resident population capable of consenting for them all; atmospheric residents, Callisto households, Europan researchers, and the other moons need direct recognition.

The Outer Systems Compact forms around shared convoy, defense, trade, and antimatter control without making the Jovian system culturally or constitutionally uniform.

During the Second Solar War in 3450, Jupiter fights as an outer power while individual moons disagree with its capitals. The postwar settlement recognizes outer independence but preserves the argument over who may speak for a system.

## Position at the *Deliverance* launch

By 3600, Jovian civilization contributes helium-3, pressure-band navigation, radiation medicine, moving-habitat engineering, biological corridor law, and experience governing households separated by delay.

Its message to the departing ship is practical: “Every roof has a number.” Survival begins by knowing exactly which layer, field, and authority holds the people beneath it.
