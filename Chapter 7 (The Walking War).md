# Chapter 7 - The Walking War

## Timeline Function

War Years 3-5. The war spreads across Harmina's terrain through slow, mobile, and environmentally constrained campaigns.

## Event Role

- Walking Reefs shift maps and invalidate older front lines.
- Red deserts, fungal jungles, storm belts, and hollow-crust routes shape strategy.
- Power armor and autonomous platforms become normal battlefield tools.
- Mega-train corridors become lifelines and targets.

## Consequence

Harmina itself becomes a participant in the war. No faction can simply impose Earthlike military logic on the planet.
