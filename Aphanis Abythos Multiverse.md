1. Negative Geometry Constructions
	•	Hollow Polyhedra
	•	Buildings that are only their outlines — perfect cubes, spheres, and spires made of emptiness.
	•	You can see them, walk around them, but the inside is missing.
	•	Impossible Solids
	•	Shapes that cancel themselves out (e.g., stairs you descend that bring you higher).
	•	Whole [[Citadels]] exist as Escher-like contradictions, stable in paradox because paradox is [[law]].
	•	Subtractive Monuments
	•	Instead of being carved into stone, they are carved out of [[(2030) Existence]].
	•	Monuments shaped as voids in the landscape, the outline of gods or lords made by removing matter itself.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Negative [[Physics]] Architectures
	•	Entropy Engines
	•	Towers that radiate decay, pulling stability out of surrounding space.
	•	Cities cluster near them, feeding on the collapse they produce.
	•	Reverse Bridges
	•	Pathways that exist only when you deny them — the more you walk, the less they exist.
	•	Crossing requires forgetting each step, otherwise the bridge disappears beneath you.
	•	Unbuildings
	•	Ruins that predate their construction. They “collapse first, exist later.”
	•	To inhabitants, they look ancient, but in truth they are always in the process of never having been.

⸻

	•	The Hollow Citadel
	•	A fortress outlined in radiant absence, walls that cannot be touched but still obstruct.
	•	Inside is only void; yet armies march its ramparts as if it were solid.
	•	The Negasphere
	•	A Dyson-like construct, but inverted: instead of harvesting a star’s light, it erases it, leaving only its rim-glow.
	•	Used as a throne-world for Paradox Lords.
	•	The Vanishing Spiral
	•	A colossal spiral galaxy-like structure where each arm fades as you approach.
	•	Pilgrims wander eternally, never reaching the center because it is always decaying away.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Environmental Formations
	•	Anti-Mountains
	•	Valleys shaped like inverted peaks — the absence of a mountain creates a towering hollow.
	•	Black Aurorae
	•	Skies lit with rivers of negation-light: bands of uncolor weaving across void skies.
	•	Horizon Abysses
	•	Oceans of darkness where the further you sail, the less there is; ships vanish into decreasing [[(2030) Existence]].

⸻

5. Living Structures
	•	Absence Beasts: Cities grown from the bodies of creatures that are now only void-outlines.
	•	Hollow Thrones: Seats of Paradox Lords, shaped by the rotted imprints of dead civilizations.
	•	Unmirrors: Negative reflective surfaces — instead of showing you, they erase your reflection bit by bit.
