1. Common Succubus
	•	Definition: The baseline—demonic seductresses who drain vitality through intimacy or temptation.
	•	Traits: Alluring humanoid form, wings (batlike or feathered), hypnotic eyes.
	•	Symbolism: Desire as corruption; beauty hiding ruin.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Dream Succubi
	•	Definition: Succubi who invade dreams to lure and feed.
	•	Traits: Mist-like forms, appearing as ideal lovers, feeding on emotions and life essence.
	•	Symbolism: The violation of the subconscious, safety turned into danger.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Noble Succubi
	•	Definition: Aristocratic temptresses who move in courts and high society.
	•	Traits: Regal bearing, mastery of intrigue, ability to disguise their nature.
	•	Symbolism: Corruption through refinement; poison in velvet gloves.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. War Succubi
	•	Definition: Succubi who weaponize desire and charm to influence armies.
	•	Traits: Amazonian builds, enthralling aura, commanders of enthralled warriors.
	•	Symbolism: Lust that leads to bloodshed; desire as a war machine.

⸻

5. Vampiric Succubi
	•	Definition: Succubi with traits of blood-drinkers.
	•	Traits: Fangs, crimson eyes, blend of seduction and sanguine [[Hunger]].
	•	Symbolism: The merging of lust and consumption.

⸻

6. Elemental Succubi
	•	Definition: Succubi infused with primal forces ([[Fire]], storm, water, shadow).
	•	Traits: Ember hair, storm-veins, watery caress, shadows that cling.
	•	Symbolism: Desire as a [[Natural]] element—inevitable and overwhelming.

⸻

7. Fallen Angelic Succubi
	•	Definition: Succubi who were once celestial beings of love or beauty.
	•	Traits: Burned halos, wings torn but radiant, sorrowful allure.
	•	Symbolism: Beauty twisted into [[Sin]]; the fragility of virtue.

⸻

8. [[Trickster]] Succubi
	•	Definition: Mischief-driven succubi who sow discord through charm rather than feeding.
	•	Traits: Shape-shifters, masters of disguise, laughter in their wake.
	•	Symbolism: Seduction as chaos, the temptation of disorder.

⸻

9. Matron Succubi
	•	Definition: Elder succubi who serve as [[Queens]], mothers, or brood-mistresses of demonic lineages.
	•	Traits: Larger, more monstrous forms hidden beneath glamours.
	•	Symbolism: Desire turned into domination and control.

⸻

10. Abyssal Succubi (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary succubi embodying lust itself as a metaphysical force.
	•	Traits: Appear as the perfect object of desire, form shifting with perception.
	•	Symbolism: The bottomless [[Hunger]] of passion—devouring until nothing remains.
