Authoring Body: Dominion Unified Research Directorate – Division of Metaphysical Systems
Purpose:
To establish a complete and modular framework through which any magical or energetic paradigm can be defined, compared, or integrated.
The Universal Magic Architecture (UMA) unifies metaphysical and physical systems under shared axioms of energy, cognition, and conservation.

⸻

Abstract

The UMA treats “magic” as structured interaction between consciousness and field reality.
It defines eight progressive layers describing how metaphysical effects arise, stabilize, and transfer across paradigms.
The model applies to worlds, verses, and civilizations with differing physical laws.
Each layer operates independently yet harmonizes through the universal constants of comprehension, alignment, and conciseness.

⸻

Layer Summary
	1.	Root Axioms – The foundational truths governing energy-mind interaction.
	2.	Field Mediums – The substrate through which those truths manifest.
	3.	Interfaces – The biological, cognitive, or technological mechanisms of control.
	4.	Expressions – Observable results of field manipulation.
	5.	Governance – The moral, entropic, or systemic limitations maintaining balance.
	6.	Translation Matrix – The framework allowing cross-system comparison.
	7.	Intent–Reality Coupling – The cognitive metric that reconciles subjective belief with objective physics.
	8.	Energy Transformation – The laws enabling cross-field conversion between magic, science, and hybrid systems.

⸻

System Principle

All worlds operate under the same underlying conservation of informational energy.
Magic, science, faith, and technology are translation dialects of a single universal field.
UMA formalizes that unity.
