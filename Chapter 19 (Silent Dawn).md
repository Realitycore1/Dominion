# Chapter 19 - Silent Dawn

## Timeline Function

End of Book III. The first phase of the New World War ends in exhausted silence.

## Event Role

- Major weapons pause because no side can absorb the next escalation.
- Cities count their dead and discover some records are missing.
- The first postwar negotiators inherit a silence that is not peace.
- Book IV begins from the debts, secrets, and unfinished surrender terms left here.

## Consequence

Harmina survives the first war phase, but the prewar planetary order is gone.
