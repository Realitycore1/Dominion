Subsurface Bubblehab
Designed to bore into planetary regolith or ice for protection and insulation.

Each Bubblehab comes equipped with a limited AI mind known as a “Habitensor”:
	•	Tracks internal health metrics
	•	Interfaces with external [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] networks
	•	Can be upgraded into semi-autonomous structure control cores
	•	Wields limited authority to lock/unlock chambers based on threat analysis
