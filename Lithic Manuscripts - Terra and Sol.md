# Lithic Manuscripts - Terra and Sol

## Purpose

The Lithic Era should be split into two manuscript projects.

The era is too large for one clean novel because it contains two different emotional movements:

1. Earth diverges, destabilizes, and breaks.
2. Humanity survives, rebuilds, and leaves Earth's shadow.

The split gives each manuscript a clear job.

## Manuscript 1 - Terra

See: [[Lithic Manuscript 1 - Terra]]

Scope:

- early historical divergence;
- old powers surviving into modernity;
- protoscience;
- Cold War pressure;
- black-budget programs;
- biological and cognition research;
- Wetwire, Accerisma, plagues, ecological stress;
- political fracture;
- resource collapse;
- final alliance blocs;
- The Exchange.

Core movement:

Descent into ruin.

Working ending:

2078, The Exchange.

Optional epilogue:

2079, first year of nuclear winter.

## Manuscript 2 - Sol

See: [[Lithic Manuscript 2 - Sol]]

Scope:

- nuclear winter survival;
- megacity remnants;
- refuge-polities;
- trade caravans;
- surviving city-states;
- Immortals and the Quiet Council;
- reforestation;
- regional federations;
- Terran Federation;
- Earth restoration;
- Lunar colonization and the Lunar Colonial Committee;
- reformation of the Terran Federation into the Colonial Union;
- Mars foothold mission and permanent colony;
- O'Neill cylinders, fusion drives, fueling stations, Earth's orbital rings, and the Colonial Union Postal Service;
- asymmetric asteroid-belt frontier, extraction economy, criminal sovereignties, mining fleets, and semi-independent mega-stations;
- Venusian floating cities and Colonial Committee;
- Mercurian shade settlements and Colonial Committee;
- Cynthia's autonomy within the Colonial Union and the independent Kindred of Mars;
- Jovian and Saturnian foothold colonies;
- mature Jovian-Saturnian outer powers and their independence compact;
- Uranian containment and Neptunian antimatter production;
- Kuiper Watchtower stations and Pluto-Charon interstellar staging;
- the First and Second Solar Wars;
- first interstellar voyage and the crash-founding of Craterheart.

Core movement:

Reclamation into expansion.

Main Solar climax:

3600, first interstellar voyage.

Working ending:

3608, the Proxima Commonwealth establishes the first durable political community beyond Sol. The Colonial Union's continuity and limits prepare the path toward Equilon.

## Shared Structural Rule

Do not treat these manuscripts as encyclopedias.

Each should use a limited set of recurring families, institutions, field teams, politicians, scientists, soldiers, engineers, archivists, and civic workers to carry the timeline.

The vault timeline is the source.

The manuscripts should show what it costs to live through that timeline.
