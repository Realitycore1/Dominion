# Seat Sanctum SOP v1.0 — GEZ, Operations & Siege Playbook

> **Seat Sanctum:** a licensed, guaranteed-effect zone (GEZ) anchored to a Seat. This SOP defines start-up, normal ops, breach/taboo handling, siege response, and shutdown.

## 1) GEZ Budgets & Timers (accepted dials)
| Tier | GEZ Budget G / hr | Spool | Cool | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Axiarchal Anchor** | **10,000** | **120 s** | **10 min** | Root Seat; RAC live audit |
| **Verarch Seat** | **2,500** | **90 s** | **7 min** | 25 Seats; taboo-backfire armed |
| **Lance-Sanctum** | **250** | **45 s** | **3 min** | Field micro-sanctum; triage/muster only |

**Rite guidance:** Class-III ≈ 50 G • Class-II ≈ 10 G • Class-I ≈ 2 G.

## 2) Roles
- **Seat Warden (SW):** operational control and logs.  
- **RAC Auditor (RAC):** on-site or live-remote audit; may hard-stop on breach.  
- **Harmonic Liaison (HL):** light-hygiene & public comms.  
- **Legion Liaison (LL):** security & muster under lawful call.

## 3) Startup Checklist
- [ ] Pre-op survey: Φ/η/ℓᶜ within bounds; ΔS baseline recorded.  
- [ ] Radiator Neutrality posted; reject-area reserved.  
- [ ] Taboo board armed; **backfire** hooks tested.  
- [ ] Exit-keys distributed & tested (choirs).  
- [ ] PFN logging to rolling buffers.  
- [ ] Public notice issued (Harmonic).

## 4) Normal Ops
- **Duty:** obey Ω_c throttles; never exceed posted G/hr.  
- **Consent:** κ-keys recorded; *renewals only* during curfews or Null adjacency.  
- **Choirs:** M-load caps enforced; exit-keys honored on request.  
- **Audits:** PFN every 60 s under load; publish summaries daily.  
- **Neutralities:** radiators/medical sancta/light-hygiene remain protected.

## 5) Breach Protocol (DE-G/F/B)
1) **Alarm & classify:** η spike + Δ𝒦; tag **DE-G/F/B**.  
2) **Quarantine:** raise Ζₑ; portable **Veil** cells; light-hygiene ON; mark radiators neutral.  
3) **Stabilize:** drop temporary **Anchors/Motes**; suspend non-essential rites.  
4) **Assess:** Reason Sheet; decide **Bridge of Writ** if ΔS won’t recover.  
5) **Restore:** gradual reopen; spool/cool windows; post aether-weather hourly.

## 6) Taboo / Backfire Handling
**Taboo triggers (illustrative):** coercive choir on civilians; denial of exit-keys; Σ fraud; oath breach; unauthorized rite above class; non-neutral radiator use.
- **Auto-responses:** GEZ clamps to zero; **Seat ejection** (where applicable); Veil hard-drop; RAC seizure of logs.  
- **Case precedent:** *3212 House Vesper*—Seat locked for one cycle after civilian coercion incident.

### Taboo Incident Report (template)
- Event ID • Time • Triggered taboo • Actions auto-fired • Injuries/Σ impact • PFN hashes • AAR due by (72 h)

## 7) Siege Playbook — First 5 Minutes
**Minute 0–1**  
- Lock sanctum; Veil corridors; flip to defense presets; public sirens (HL).  
**Minute 1–2**  
- Evacuate nonessential; muster **Lance**; deploy portable radiators & veil bubbles.  
**Minute 2–3**  
- Cut non-critical rites; dedicate G to shields/med; publish port advisories.  
**Minute 3–4**  
- Counter-breach: drop Anchors/Motes; jam hostile motif vectors (light/music).  
**Minute 4–5**  
- Status report to Conclave; decide **Rebirth-Through-Collapse** prep if ΔS degrading.

## 8) Shutdown Checklist
- [ ] GEZ to idle; spool/cool complete.  
- [ ] Σ ledger reconciled; neutral radiator dump done.  
- [ ] Choir exit-keys exercised/verified.  
- [ ] PFN & telemetry archived; ombuds review scheduled.  
- [ ] Public after-action posted within **72 h**.

## 9) KPIs & Posting
- **Ξ_cont**, **window jitter**, **PFN compliance**, **loop-debt residual**, **rights-restoration SLA**.  
- Post **aether-weather** hourly during incidents; archive 1 cycle.

# Dominion Glossary Rail (20 Terms)

> Pin this at the top of modules. Each term is a single-line, reader-first definition.

1. **Axiom Anchor (AA):** Device that projects a portable **law-bubble** so ships/gates stay coherent across verses.  
2. **GEZ (Guaranteed-Effect Zone):** Licensed area where specified rites/devices are guaranteed to work within posted limits.  
3. **Veil Cell:** Portable **null** pocket used to isolate reality effects; safe dwell strictly time-limited.  
4. **Ω_c (Verse Fatigue Index):** Running load on the local verse; high Ω_c forces throttles/cooldowns.  
5. **ΔS (Axiom Stability):** Measure of rule-set stability; large negative drops signal verse collisions.  
6. **η (Noise):** Aether noise level; spikes raise jitter and risk at gates/sancta.  
7. **ℓᶜ (Coherence Length):** Distance over which fields/phases stay aligned; longer is better for windows.  
8. **Ζₑ (Impedance):** Effective resistance to propagation; swings signal boundary trouble.  
9. **PFN (Proof & Forensics Net):** Cryptographic logging of ops (configs, timings, hashes) for audit and safety.  
10. **Reason Sheet:** Public, proofed plan of action—used for risky ops and policy exceptions; no paradox debt allowed.  
11. **Window Proof Sheet:** Engineering disclosure for a gate: Φ/η/ℓᶜ/𝒦, arc geometry, jitter, spool/cool.  
12. **Radiator Neutrality:** Legal protection for heat-reject systems; first-come by priority tiers; no factional control.  
13. **Choir:** Temporary group-mind fuse for labor or care; strictly consent-bound with **exit-keys**.  
14. **Exit-Key:** Personal kill-switch to break choir/fuse or withdraw ongoing consent.  
15. **Loop-Debt:** Residual cost of acausal operations; must settle to within safe bounds after threads end.  
16. **AIF (Axiom-Invariant Format):** Minimal proof encoding that verifies across incompatible physics.  
17. **Witness Capsule:** Sealed, tamper-evident courier of AIF proofs + continuity seals + Σ ledger.  
18. **Entropy Bank (Σ Bank):** Onboard store for heat/entropy accumulated when radiators aren’t available.  
19. **Breadcrumb Mote:** Boundary-layer beacon that aids navigation without relying on destination physics.  
20. **Confluencia Field:** Managed field array that harmonizes multiple axiom influences in shared spaces.

# Micro‑Scenes for Onboarding

## Scene 1 — **Port Checkpoint (HAG Jitter)**
The queue shuffles forward under the blue-gray glow of the ring.  
“Window jitter twelve picoseconds,” the officer says, sliding a slate to the trader.  
The slate shows **Φ=1.20, η=0.18, ℓᶜ=22 km** and a green check next to **Window Proof Sheet**.  
“Any rites on board?”  
“Just loaders, all **Class‑II**,” the trader replies, tapping their **PFN**.  
A ripple crosses the arc; the **Axiom Anchor** bleeds a little light and the display ticks **spool 00:37**.  
“Radiator slot booked?”  
“Thirty MW‑equivalent, neutral field.”  
“Good. If η crawls past point‑two‑two, duty drops to sixty. You’ll feel the drag. Move smart.”  
The trader nods as the ring breathes open, a soft **noon** where there was none.

## Scene 2 — **Null Docking (Σ Dump)**
The ferry ghosts in on a quiet vector, sheath still bright around the hull.  
“Hold at one kilometer,” Control says. “**[[Radiator Neutrality]]** engaged. Dump schedule posted.”  
The captain watches the **Σ bank** creep to the line, thumb resting on the **exit‑[[Key]]** for the crew choir—unused today.  
Three **Radiator Spires** unfurl like night-blooming flowers; the graph turns from red to steady white.  
“Commence dump,” Control says. “PFN cadence sixty seconds.”  
Heat haze ripples over the spires. The hull fogs; then clears.  
“Ledger reconciled. Quarantine class **Q2**. AIF [[Witness]] Capsule routed to **Reimposition**.”  
The captain exhales, finally. “We brought the laws with us,” she whispers, “and left nothing else behind.”
