1. Common Chimera
	•	Definition: The baseline—lion’s body, goat’s head, serpent tail, [[Fire]]-breathing.
	•	Traits: Hybrid monstrosity, chaotic anatomy, savage predator.
	•	Symbolism: Disorder made flesh; nature’s laws broken.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Beast-Chimeras
	•	Definition: Hybrids made from common animals (wolf-bear-serpent, stag-lion-eagle, etc.).
	•	Traits: Animalistic minds, unpredictable attacks.
	•	Symbolism: The terror of the unnatural.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Draconic Chimera
	•	Definition: Chimera infused with [[Dragon]] parts.
	•	Traits: [[Dragon]] heads, wings, breath weapons.
	•	Symbolism: The apex predator stitched with chaos.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Avian Chimera
	•	Definition: Bird-infused hybrids.
	•	Traits: Eagle wings, talons, shrieking calls, serpent tails.
	•	Symbolism: The horror of the sky predator.

⸻

5. Elemental Chimera
	•	Definition: Hybrids infused with elemental forces.
	•	Variants: Flame-born, Storm-fused, Frost-fanged, Stone-backed.
	•	Symbolism: Nature’s primal forces twisted together.

⸻

6. Abyssal Chimera
	•	Definition: Infernal or [[Abyss]]-touched hybrids.
	•	Traits: Demonic horns, shadow wings, venomous tails.
	•	Symbolism: Hell’s corruption; flesh warped into blasphemy.

⸻

7. [[Human]]-Spliced Chimera
	•	Definition: Hybrids including [[Human]] parts (faces, limbs).
	•	Traits: Disturbing, uncanny blends; remnants of mortal intellect.
	•	Symbolism: Hubris of creation; horror of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]] lost.

⸻

8. Regal Chimera
	•	Definition: More stable hybrids with pride and cunning.
	•	Traits: Symmetrical features, intelligent eyes, predatory nobility.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos refined into a predator-king.

⸻

9. War-Bred Chimera
	•	Definition: Alchemically or magically created for battle.
	•	Traits: Multiple breath weapons, armored hides, unstable but devastating.
	•	Symbolism: Weapons of hubris; the laboratory turned battlefield.

⸻

10. Arch-Chimera (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Legendary chimeras embodying entire ecosystems of hybrid beasts.
	•	Traits: Colossal patchwork [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans|Titans]], multiple shifting forms, semi-divine.
	•	Symbolism: The chaos of creation unbound; flesh as nightmare.
