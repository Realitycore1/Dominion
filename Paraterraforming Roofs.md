[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Paraterraforming Roofs are planetary-scale domes or canopies — vast engineered ceilings stretched across entire worlds or regional zones, holding in an atmosphere and regulating climate. They allow near-instant habitability on otherwise airless, toxic, or thin-atmosphere worlds, bypassing deep terraforming.
Structure

Roof Membranes: Ultra-strong composites, metamaterials, or Concordia glass, kilometers thick.

Atlas Pillar Supports: Mega-spires anchoring the canopy to crustal plates or orbital rings.

Functions

Atmospheric Containment: Holds breathable air over planetary surfaces.

Climate Control: Panels regulate light, heat, and precipitation.

Radiation Shielding: Roof [[Layers]] block harmful stellar and cosmic rays.

Aesthetic Projection: Interior surface can be projected with dynamic skies, glyphs, or constellations.

Variants

Full-World Roofs: Entire planets capped under a single sky.

Regional Roofs: Cover specific valleys, basins, or domes — city-sized heavens.

Stacked Roofs: Multi-tiered atmospheres, different [[Layers]] for different biomes or functions.

Religious: Known as the “Amber Skies” — citizens believe the Empress paints heavens where none exist.
