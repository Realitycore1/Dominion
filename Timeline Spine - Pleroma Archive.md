# Timeline Spine - Pleroma Archive

The Timeline Spine is the public route through the archive's historical material. It does not replace detailed era files, event notes, or civilization timelines. It tells readers which timeline layers are ready to browse, which are scaffolds, and where to enter without losing the larger map.

## Purpose Summary

This page exists because the archive contains several kinds of chronology at once: historical-era folders, civilization timelines, dated event notes, alternate histories, and specialist cosmological time. A reader needs one stable spine before opening hundreds of dated notes.

## Reader Rule

Read timelines from broadest to narrowest:

1. Start with [[Timeline FAQ]] for how dated notes work.
2. Use this spine to choose the era or civilization layer.
3. Read overview pages before individual dated notes.
4. Treat empty or very short dated notes as draft anchors unless a page clearly says otherwise.
