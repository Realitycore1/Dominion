## Summary

Projector C
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Wormhole Projector is a megastructural engine that generates, stabilizes, and directs [[Artificial]] [[Wormholes]], allowing controlled passage of matter, fleets, or energy between distant points in space — or even between verses. Unlike passive Horizon Arc Gates, Projectors are active, directional instruments: cosmic cannons that [[Fire]] bridges through reality.
Structure

Singularity Core: [[Exotic Matter]] / negative energy anchors that stabilize the wormhole mouth.

Aim Array: Vast [[Arms]] or lens-like structures to steer the exit point.

Choir Mantle: Concordia harmonics woven into the wormhole, preventing collapse.

Functions

Civil:
– Provide flexible transport corridors between [[Systems]].
– Connect temporary [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] or nomadic fleets to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] web.
– Act as emergency evacuation arteries.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]:
– Deploy [[I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Royal Military/Legions/Legions|legions]] directly into enemy heartlands — “to open a sky-door above their citadel.”
– Turn energy weapons into instant-range attacks: beams projected through [[Wormholes]] onto targets.
– Weaponize collapse: destabilize a wormhole to shred fleets or planets.

Variants

Civil Projectors: Modest-scale transport nodes, stable and reusable.

War Projectors: Siege engines, built into [[Magnus Bastions]] or [[World Stations]].

Sacral Projectors: Reliquary-engines used in [[Ritual]] openings to holy sites or forbidden verses.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Expand and knit [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] across impossible distances.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: The ultimate in surprise and overwhelming force — arrival from nowhere, everywhere.

Economic: Keep trade flowing across sectors without requiring permanent gate [[Infrastructure]].

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Projector frames shaped like organ pipes, ribbed vaults glowing with void-[[Fire]].

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, appears as a cruciform or pyramid, dark until it “sings” a wormhole into being.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Apertures ripple with fractal mandalas, shimmering with glyph-light.

When fired, a Wormhole Projector looks like a cathedral cannon tearing the sky open, a circular wound lined with golden [[Fire]], hymns echoing as fleets march through.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Wonder — proof that no distance separates them from the Empress’s light.

To rebels: Terror — the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] arrives anywhere, without warning.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Strategic keystone — fast response, omnipresent reach.

To myth: Called the “Amber Horns”, said to be the trumpets by which the Empress proclaims her [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] to new worlds.
