## Summary
## Core Motifs
- **Tri‑Chord Sigil:** three interlocking arcs ([[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] • Scale • Craft), forming a balanced trefoil.  
  - *Geometry:* three 120° arcs on a common radius; line weight 1:32 of emblem diameter.
- **Accord Knot:** continuous over‑under loop symbolizing consensus without end points.
- **Resonance Ring:** concentric thin rings (phase lines) indicating harmony bandwidth.

## Color System (vector‑friendly)
- **Aurora Base:** `#1E2A78` (deep indigo), `#2FB6D0` (cyan), `#7A4BE1` (violet), `#E7C64B` (gold accent).  
- **Neutral Frames:** `#111418` (charcoal), `#C8CDD2` (light alloy).
- **Accessibility:** Maintain [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].5:1 contrast; gold used for highlights, not fields.

## Chambers & Marks
- **Chamber of [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] (Co‑Equal):** solid **circle** + Tri‑Chord core.
- **Chamber of Scale (Proportional):** **square** frame with phase‑line inset.
- **Chamber of Craft (Functional):** **triangle** frame with knot overlay.
- **Harmonic Ombuds:** circle‑square‑triangle compound, thin gold ring.

## Flags & Banners
- **Common Flag:** aurora gradient field (indigo→violet→cyan), centered **Tri‑Chord Sigil**, thin gold border.  
- **Sector Pennants:** add left hoist band in sector color; right fly carries Resonance Ring watermark.  
- **Embassy Banner:** white field, gold Tri‑Chord, charcoal Accord Knot watermark.

## Uniform & Hull Markings
- **Civic:** left chest Tri‑Chord ([[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] cm), chamber mark on right sleeve; Ombuds ring on collar for [[Rights]] officers.
- **Defense Hull Codes:** `SYNA-[Sector]-[Guild]-[Unit]-[Hull#]` in charcoal; chamber [[Iconography]] near dorsal spine.
- **Humanitarian:** white hull stripe, gold knot; protected under **Order of Sanctuary**.

## Writing & Glyphs
- **Accord Script:** glyphs laid on **three‑line stave** (upper = [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] phonetics, middle = semantic root, lower = scale/craft modifiers).  
  - *Syllable Node:* filled circle on middle stave; modifiers as wavelets above/below.
  - *Numerals:* base‑12 marks using node count + ring ticks.
- **Usage:** Script is ornamental on civic assets; legal signage uses multispecies pictograms + local scripts.

## Usage Rules
- **Do not** place Tri‑Chord on weapon barrels or munition housings.
- **Display order** in chambers rows: [[I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]] • Scale • Craft.
- **Memorial copies** of cultural artifacts use the gold ring watermark to indicate custodial co‑ownership.

## Export
Vector master recommended in **SVG** with separate [[Layers]]: frames, sigils, rings, text. Provide monochrome fallback (charcoal on white).
