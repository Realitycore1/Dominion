# (3175) Sea of Tranquility Corporate Conflict

In 3175, rival Lunar corporations deploy security fleets over competing extraction, mass-driver, fuel, and docking claims around the Sea of Tranquility.

The conflict escalates through:

- contradictory docking exclusions;
- seizure of maintenance and fueling depots;
- sabotage of mass-driver schedules;
- armed interception of ore packets;
- competing corporate emergency declarations;
- damage to civilian pressure corridors and power distribution.

Both corporations hold valid Colonial Union contracts. Each accuses the Lunar Colonial Committee of favoring the other, slowing Union intervention.

The committee responds by forming a unified Lunar Civic Guard and placing local traffic control, emergency docking, mass-driver safeties, and life-support reserves under public command.

The conflict proves that Lunar civil government remains incomplete while corporations command armed traffic infrastructure.

It becomes the immediate constitutional cause of the 3180 Cynthia Protectorate.
