I. Physical Definition

A Sentient Star is a stellar-scale object that possesses self-awareness, cognition, and adaptive decision-making ability, existing as both a massive energy source and a conscious entity.
Sentience may arise naturally through emergent complexity in plasma flows, magnetic fields, and quantum-level interactions, or artificially via Omniversal engineering or magical endowment.

[[Key]] features include:
	•	Neuroplasmic Convection Patterns — Plasma flows and magnetic structures function analogously to neural networks.
	•	Quantum-Coherent Core [[Regions]] — Allow stable long-term memory and thought processes.
	•	Electromagnetic Signaling — [[Stars]] communicate through modulated light output, neutrino pulses, or gravitational wave patterns.
	•	Adaptive Energy Regulation — Actively control fusion rate, mass ejection, and radiation spectra based on intentions.

In the [[VFF]] ([[Verse]] Field Framework), Sentient [[Stars]] are recognized as Conscious Energy Anchors, bridging the roles of astrophysical body and autonomous intelligence.
