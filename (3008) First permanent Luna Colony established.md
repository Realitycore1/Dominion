# (3008) First permanent Luna Colony established

The first permanent, self-governing Lunar colony is established after Gilgamesh opens.

It is distinct from the automated mines, mass drivers, factories, and rotating crew habitats operating since 2650.

The colony begins with:

- family-rated habitats;
- schools;
- clinics;
- local civil records;
- burial and inheritance law;
- elected representation;
- redundant supply and rescue;
- a defined path toward local self-government.

For the first time, people move to Luna expecting their children to remain there.

The Lunar Colonial Committee is established as the colony's provisional elected government. Its first major ordinance separates domicile from employment so an employer can no longer end a resident's legal right to remain by terminating a work contract.
