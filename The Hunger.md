Core Description

The Hunger is one of the largest naturally occurring megafauna documented within the Vital Expanse.

It is classified not merely as an organism, but as:

a migratory ecological collapse event.

Where it moves:

* ecosystems disappear
* predator hierarchies collapse
* biomass density plummets
* migration cascades begin across entire regions

Entire species have evolved specifically to detect its distant approach.

⸻

Physical Structure

The Hunger possesses no fully stable anatomy.

However, recurring traits include:

* immense quadrupedal or mass-crawling locomotion
* layered jaw-clusters
* continent-scale biomass density
* constantly shedding black filamentous matter
* internal furnace-like glow visible through wounds and mouths
* continuously regenerating tissue

Its mouths are believed to serve specialized ecological functions:

* consumption
* atmospheric filtering
* spore dispersal
* territorial signaling
* biomass liquefaction

Some observations suggest individual mouths operate semi-independently.

⸻

Scale

Estimated dimensions vary due to unstable morphology.

Average confirmed manifestations:

* Height: mountain-scale
* Length: several kilometers minimum
* Ecological footprint: hundreds to thousands of square kilometers

Its approach can alter:

* weather systems
* migration patterns
* airborne spore density
* local aggression responses in fauna

⸻

Behavior

The Hunger does not hunt traditionally.

It advances slowly through ecosystems while:

* consuming
* absorbing
* uprooting
* liquefying
* and metabolizing entire biological regions

Smaller organisms frequently follow in its wake:

* scavengers
* parasites
* symbiotic predators
* opportunistic supercolonies

Entire mobile ecosystems travel alongside it.

⸻

Ecological Function

Despite its catastrophic appearance, The Hunger is not considered “evil” within Expanse ecology.

It serves a role analogous to:

* wildfire
* extinction cycles
* ecological reset
* biomass redistribution

The regions consumed by The Hunger often experience:

* explosive evolutionary diversification afterward
* rapid mutation blooms
* emergence of new apex species

The creature may function as:

a macro-evolutionary pressure engine.

⸻

Presence Effects

Its presence induces:

* heightened aggression in wildlife
* accelerated mutation rates
* increased reproductive instability
* blood-rich atmospheric precipitation
* mass migration events

Long exposure causes:

* predatory behavioral shifts
* compulsive feeding instincts
* identity erosion in weaker organisms

Some organisms begin evolving mouth-like structures after prolonged proximity.

Theoretical Origins

Several theories exist regarding The Hunger:

1. Native Apex Theory

A naturally evolved Bleeding Wilds superpredator.

2. Crucible Spawn Theory

A failed or escaped organism from the Crucible Spiral.

3. Ecological Correction Theory

A living regulatory mechanism produced by the Vital Expanse itself.

4. Archetypal Predator Theory

An organism strengthened by omniversal fear of being consumed.

This final theory is taken disturbingly seriously by some Dominion researchers.

⸻

Psychological Effect

Observers frequently report:

* intrusive hunger sensations
* predator-prey paranoia
* recurring dreams of teeth
* auditory hallucinations resembling chewing beneath the earth

Long-range exposure alone has caused:

* cannibalistic outbreaks
* mass hysteria
* feeding frenzies in native fauna
