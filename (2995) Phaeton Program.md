# (2995) Phaeton Program

The Terran Federation launches the Phaeton Program to turn scattered cislunar industry into permanent civil infrastructure.

Phaeton coordinates:

- AI astronauts;
- mechanical counterpressure skinsuits;
- mass drivers;
- momentum-exchange skyhooks;
- the Federation Space Force;
- orbital stations;
- permanent Lunar colonization.

The program name is cautionary. Phaeton represents power acquired before control. Every project must publish failure limits, deorbit plans, life-support dependencies, kinetic risk, labor ownership, emergency command, and transition to local government.

The program begins the Second Space Race among the twelve Great City blocs while remaining under federal coordination.
