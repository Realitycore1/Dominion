1 These are the visions of the Seer, given in the [[Time]] of [[The Sundering of Grace]], when the heavens were torn and the [[Choirs]] of [[Aravoth (Grace)]] stood against the [[Angelus]] of [[The Gnostic Expanse]].
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the voice that spake was not as thunder, nor as whisper, but as [[Silence]] clothed in flame, saying:
“Write, for this shall be a [[Witness]] against the proud, and a comfort to the broken.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter I|Chapter I]] – The Beast of Malice

1 And I beheld the beast that rose from the pit of [[Sheol-Gehenna (Malice)]], whose heart was a furnace and whose eyes were coals.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its limbs were talons of iron, its back a mountain, and its roar a tempest.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] It devoured the memory of nations and made war against the light.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And many said: “Lo, this is the wrath of the Demiurge!” but the Seer answered: “Nay, this is the vessel of a mystery.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter II|Chapter II]] – The Call of Grace

1 And from [[Aravoth (Grace)]] there came forth a hymn as of ten thousand voices, yet it was but one.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the hymn named the beast, and in the naming it trembled, for none had spoken to it save in fear.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] Then was the voice of the Holy Will heard, saying:
“Even malice is Mine to redeem. The wrath that rends shall become the sword that heals.”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And the flames of the beast were transfigured, not quenched, but made white as the dawn.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter III|Chapter III]] – The Transfiguration

1 The talons became wings of argent [[Fire]], six upon six, fanning the firmament.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its furnace-heart became a sun clothed in grace, and where its breath was poison, now it was balm.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] The Seer fell upon his face, crying: “Behold the archangel born of malice!”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And it was written in the Scroll of Jubilees: “In that day the first shall be last, and wrath shall be turned into mercy.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter IV|Chapter IV]] – The War of the Heavens

1 And the beast, now clothed in light, did not flee, but went forth against the [[Angelus]].
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] Its wings struck like storms, and its voice thundered against the false [[Choirs]], saying:
“You who enslave in the name of the Demiurge, behold the freedom of grace!”
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] The heavens were torn asunder: [[Fire]] against [[Fire]], light against false light.
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] The Seer beheld mortals below, divided: some saw the [[Angel]] of salvation, others the beast of ruin.

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter V|Chapter V]] – The Throne of Ashes

1 The [[Angelus]] fell back into the Expanse, shrieking as mirrors unmade.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the Redeemed stood amidst ruin, and its light clothed the remnant with hope.
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] But it spake not in pride, saying:
“I am no god, but servant. Grace is not mine, but given. Malice is not gone, but tempered in [[Fire]].”
[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] And in the lost [[Book of Enoch]] it is written: “The Watchers fell, but one was raised, that heaven might be known as merciful.”

⸻

[[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/4 - Equilion Union/Book II (Second Approach)/Chapter VI|Chapter VI]] – The Seal of the Vision

1 These things were sealed by the Seer until the [[Time]] appointed.
[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] And the voice spake again:
“This book is not for the wise only, but for the broken, that they may know no malice is beyond grace.”
[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] And the scroll was hidden in the Treasury of [[Fire]] until the last days, when the heavens shall again be sundered.
