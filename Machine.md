Core Bias:

Mechanical and infrastructural continuity

This is the baseline Synthetic subtype.

Reality behaves as if it is being held together by:
	•	machinery
	•	process chains
	•	active infrastructure
	•	industrial or computational systems

Traits:
	•	mechanical persistence
	•	systems interdependence
	•	maintenance-heavy structure
	•	visible or hidden machine substrate
	•	civilization-as-infrastructure

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	the world is running on engines
	•	systems are alive because they are being kept alive
	•	continuity is an industrial process

Common Expressions:
	•	machine worlds
	•	infrastructural megaverses
	•	automated habitat systems
	•	civilization-scale process ecologies

Failure Mode:

Maintenance collapse
Once upkeep fails, continuity degrades rapidly.
