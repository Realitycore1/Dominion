# (3450) Second Solar War

The Second Solar War begins in 3450 after the Colonial Union rejects the Outer Systems Compact's demand for full independence.

Unlike the fractured First Solar War, the second conflict has a clearer inner-versus-outer axis.

It does not divide every moon, settlement, or household cleanly.

## Inner-System Coalition

- Colonial Union core institutions;
- Earth and its orbital-ring network;
- Cynthia under its defense compact;
- Venusian and Mercurian governments;
- Union-aligned Belt charter polities.

## Outer Systems Compact

- Jovian and Saturnian system governments;
- most Uranian and Neptunian authorities;
- outer convoy and defense fleets;
- aligned Kuiper settlements and Watchtower crews.

## Neutral and Divided Powers

- the Kindred of Mars mediates protected transit;
- Belt polities divide by route and ideology;
- individual moons resist system governments claiming to speak for them;
- postal, rescue, scientific, and Watchtower crews attempt limited neutrality.

## Causes

- outer-system independence;
- Union emergency claims over antimatter production;
- control of Watchtower data and command keys;
- convoy taxation and fuel requisition;
- fear of an outer antimatter monopoly;
- outer fear that the Union will militarize hazard-warning infrastructure.

## Character of the War

The war centers on blockade, command-key seizure, convoy interception, cyber intrusion, labor refusal, and control of transfer routes.

Direct attacks on antimatter sites are limited because destruction threatens attackers, defenders, and civilian settlements alike.

Watchtower crews continue tracking natural hazards while both coalitions use their sensors to track fleets.

## Outcome

The Outer Systems Settlement recognizes the Compact as a sovereign associated bloc.

It preserves:

- mutual rescue and navigation standards;
- postal exchange;
- trade arbitration;
- recognition of mixed households and citizenship;
- joint hazard defense;
- divided oversight of Watchtower;
- a jointly funded interstellar mission.

The Colonial Union loses direct government over the major outer systems but preserves a common civil framework.
