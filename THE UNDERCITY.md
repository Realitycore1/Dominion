The Strata
	1.	Ringfire Collapse Layer
Melted stone, fused bones, Fire-scarred scripture
	2.	Pre-Dominion Faith Layer
Competing shrines, heretical symbols, unfinished rituals
	3.	Akashic Fault Layer
Where Silence fails intermittently
	4.	The Sealed Vault Layer
Forbidden to all but the highest Custodes

This undercity is not destroyed because it cannot be.

It is:
	•	too resonant
	•	too contradictory
	•	too foundational

The Dominion builds over it, not through it.

⸻

What Happens Below
	•	Whispers that don’t translate into words
	•	Old prayers that still work
	•	Artifacts that refuse cataloging
	•	People who remember things they shouldn’t
	•	Children born with immunity to certain Chains

Officially, the undercity does not exist.
