# Story 04 - The Fabricator Queue

## Viewpoint

Sada Amri, a biolab apprentice temporarily reassigned to fabrication intake.

## Setting

Year 0 AL, New Aurora emergency fabrication hall.

## Story Shape

Fusion-fed fabricators and biolabs keep the colony alive, but every printed meal has a cost in energy, time, maintenance, and morale. Sada spends the day telling people no: no extra sealant, no second food brick, no replacement toy, no private request ahead of medical need.

The machines look like salvation from a distance. Up close they are queues, priority charts, overheating warnings, and citizens learning that technology can prevent death without removing fear.

## Harmina Element

Kardashev-level tools are grounded through scarcity, maintenance, and public access.

## Character Movement

Sada begins proud of the machines. She ends protective of the people waiting in front of them.

## Future Payoff

The story establishes a key Harminan belief: no machine is sacred if it cannot be repaired, audited, and shared.
