The Planetary strip mine is a large [[Orbital Ring]] the extracts all resources from a planetary body until nothing is left.
Designed to harvest entire planetary bodies, including crust, mantle, and in some rare cases, core.
	•	Used on lifeless terrestrial worlds, post-life sterilized targets, or planets deemed nonessential by [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] edict.
	•	Primary outputs include:
	•	Rare heavy metals
	•	Fusion fuels
	•	Crystalline matrices
	•	Organic remnants for chemical refinement
Orbital Disassembly Ring (ODR)
	•	A massive orbital belt encircling the target planet, equipped with:
	•	Mass driver claws
	•	[[Graviton]] shears
	•	Focused thermal lances
	•	Functions like a guillotine ring, shaving planetary [[Layers]] in circumferential passes.
