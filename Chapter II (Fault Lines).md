[[Narrative]]
Tensions (Leslarut / [[Aurora Station]])

Era: CU pre-Hubfall (Years 0–40)
Scope: Station [[culture]], surface ops, faction formation

Early survey friction
Orbital and surface surveys flag anomalous biosignatures—bioluminescent reef complexes with hints of coordinated behavior.
Mid-level staff begin questioning isolation [[Policies]] and top-down planning.
Rumors spread that the Prefect has denied multiple leave requests and quarantined three [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]] for “ideological drift.”

Founding: Leslarut Outpost & [[Aurora Station]]
Mission classification: Strategic Resource Forward Outpost
Designation: Operation AURORA ASCENT
Objective:
Pre-deploy a full [[O’Neill Cylinder]] ([[Aurora Station]]) in Leslarut orbit to enable long-term ISRU and staged colonization.
Status: Priority-[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] (CU Central Logistics Directorate)
Stated pretext: Resource extraction and orbital relay expansion
Operational reality: Early projection of CU power into an isolated region; lock down high-value extraction zones; test long-duration orbital habitation.
[[Aurora Station]] — pre-boot design & arrival
Build chain: ~35–45 years of autonomous fabrication (barges + pre-fab modules) staged from CU industrial nodes; final auto-unfold and crewed commissioning at Year 0.
Design philosophy: Secure, expandable, modular; favors surveillance coverage and durability over comfort.
Initial population (~1,200 CU personnel):
Defense Corps: ~300 (fixed garrison)
Admin/logistics/research: ~500
Rotating extraction/tech specialists: ~400

Governance: Prefect Commander Dal Enver and the Orbital Control Council hold final authority.
Comms: Single PFN paired link to the CU hub; no colony↔colony FTL. Content E2E-encrypted; headers/byte counts auditable.
Leslarut surface operations (ISRU-first)
First touchdowns: Suborbital skiffs from Aurora’s hangars (short-stay crews, tight evac windows).

Primary sites
Blackrock Chain: Island cluster fortified as command relay + sensor outpost (met/ionosphere/seismic + anomaly detection).
Equatorial Test Spire: Anchor/test array for a future tether; materials and rotation constraints keep the [[Space Elevator]] in pilot status only.
Vanthe Seamounts: Undersea mining pilot zone (high corrosion/volatility; rare-isotope yield).

Station [[culture]] & command
Daily cycle
Regulated rotations: 6-hour work blocks, [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]-hour mandatory instruction (safety/[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]), 16-hour cycle.
1 g at rim; sensitive labs use vibration isolation / acoustic baffling (no “magic dampeners”).
Stratification
Core Habitat Ring 1: Officers/advisors (stable climate, botanical galleries, private cabins).
Peripheral Rings: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]]/techs/science (denser berthing, utilitarian fit-out, glare-control panels).
Chain of command
Prefect Commander (governor equivalent)
High Surveyor-General (science & exploration)
Security Liaison Officer (oversight & dissent control)
Command [[culture]]: authoritarian-efficiency blend; emergency powers concentrate in the Prefect.
Early instabilities
Environmental pressures
Ionospheric/magnetospheric storms degrade comms windows.
Power fluctuations at geothermal skids.
Psych strain from isolation and bureaucratic rigidity.
Policy flashpoints
Over-regulation of scientific access to bio-reefs.
Minimal local autonomy in logistics decisions.
Heavy-handed suppression of minor protests and data leaks.
Polarization of [[Aurora Station]] (Years 10–30)
Foundations of discontent
Autocratic isolation: Council holds hard controls over comms, travel, even reproduction policy (limited births; pairing approvals).
Resource inequity: Officers enjoy pristine, solar-rich sectors; labor/science crews work in older, colder rings.

Cultural drift:
Official CU programming (from Core Command) is ceremonial and tone-deaf.
Unofficial slates circulate banned histories and reconstructions of [[Earth (Terra)]]’s past republics and rebellions.
Surface mystique: Reef-team [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]] start treating Leslarut as quasi-alive, spawning semi-mystical currents among surface [[Workers]].
Emerging factions (≈Year 20)
Purists (CU loyalists)
Premise: CU mandate = the only path to [[Human]] survival.
Base: Council families, senior officers, security divisions.
Practice: Loyalty screenings; ritualized ceremonies; weekly [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] classes.
Reformists (Grey Cadres)
Premise: Aurora must achieve autonomy; CU abandoned its founding ideals.
Base: Technicians, junior [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]], [[Hydroponics]], civic planners.
Practice: Encrypted salons; maintenance-bot mods to bypass surveillance; draft civic self-rule [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Frameworks|frameworks]].
Drifted (surface isolationists)

Premise: True adaptation is planet-first, not orbital stasis.
Base: Surface mining crews, deepwater harvesters, independent [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Ameise/Castes/Engineers]].
Practice: Unregistered submerged habitats; rogue submersibles; hydrothermal siphons.
Accelerants / flashpoints
[[Child]] Repatriation Edict (Year 24)
Children born in peripheral rings reassigned to Core sectors to prevent “cultural contamination.”

Forced separations trigger riots in Ring-[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].
Resource Blackout (Year 27)
Claimed solar-flare disruption collapses grid segments; surface logs show no flare.
Purists suspect Drifted sabotage → retaliatory raids on surface depots.
The Grey Hymnal Leak (Year 29)
Anonymous docs expose corruption, planned Reformist purges, and hidden exploitation plans.

Station-[[Wide]] morale fractures; by Year 30, no sector can be assumed fully loyal.
[[Eve]] of colonist arrival (Year 40)
Purists hold command center and weapons stations but are losing public order.
Reformists dominate life support, resource distribution, and civilian admin.
Drifted control multiple surface/seabed installs, off CU monitors.
The first ark’s arrival will not be a celebration—it’s a lit match in a habitat already soaked in fuel.
