[[The Mandate]] of Prosperity

The [[Kuomin Empire]]’s economy is a party-command [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] wrapped in the trappings of dynastic tradition. Wealth is framed not as personal gain, but as proof of [[The Mandate]] of Heaven renewed in the [[Stars]].
	•	Centralized Command
	•	The Ministry of Prosperity directs [[Production]] quotas, resource allocation, and trade.
	•	Five-year plans (styled Cycles of [[The Mandate]]) set long-term industrial and agricultural goals.
	•	Guilds exist, but only as party-controlled syndicates—extensions of the state.
	•	Agriculture & Food Security
	•	Planetary farms are designed as both sustenance and spectacle: terraces arranged in imperial patterns, [[Hydroponics]] towers draped with banners.
	•	Farmers are celebrated as “the roots of [[The Mandate]],” though heavily taxed.
	•	Surplus is stored in Heavenly Granaries—vast orbital vaults ritually sealed by governors and blessed by Brahmic-style party priests.
	•	Industrial Focus
	•	Heavy industry dominates: starship yards, weapons foundries, and orbital construction.
	•	Civilian goods are produced in abundance, but always stamped with party emblems and imperial insignia.
	•	Factories double as ideological theaters—murals of the Emperor-President line their walls, and [[Workers]] chant party slogans before shifts.
	•	Labor Organization
	•	[[Workers]] are grouped into Mandate Brigades, each tied to a specific industry or [[Infrastructure]] project.
	•	Brigades march in parades, compete in [[Production]] contests, and receive honor-banners for exceeding quotas.
	•	Strikes are unthinkable—failure is framed as a spiritual [[Betrayal]] of Heaven itself.
	•	Currency & Trade
	•	The Heavenly Credit is the official currency, pegged to state [[Production]].
	•	Party officials and loyal citizens receive privileges in purchasing power, creating tiers of access rather than wealth inequality.
	•	Exports: ships, weapons, and cultural artifacts (they export Confucian texts and propaganda as “civilization kits”).
	•	Imports: rare exotics and luxury goods, which are reframed as tribute from lesser [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
	•	Economic Philosophy
	•	Prosperity is framed as proof of divine legitimacy: if the people thrive, [[The Mandate]] of Heaven shines on the Emperor-President.
	•	Economic failure is reinterpreted as moral failure of local governors, who may be ritually stripped of office and exiled.

The [[Kuomin Empire]]’s economy is not a free market—it’s a cosmic stage, where labor, [[Production]], and trade are all performances of loyalty to [[The Mandate]]. Prosperity isn’t just material wealth—it’s a sign of Heaven’s continued favor.
