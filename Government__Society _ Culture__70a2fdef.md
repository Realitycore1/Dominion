Mandate by Oath

The [[Austro Guard]] does not crown monarchs, elect presidents, or enthrone generals. Its legitimacy flows from the Oath of Guardianship, sworn by every citizen upon reaching adulthood. The Oath is a binding civic and cultural contract, pledging life and labor to the colony’s survival and security.
	•	Oathkeepers – Governance is conducted by Oathkeepers, citizens selected by random lot (sortition) from the pool of all oath-fulfilled adults.
	•	Rotation – Terms last three local years and are non-renewable. Once a citizen has served as an Oathkeeper, they are forever exempt from future selection, ensuring [[Wide]] distribution of civic burden.
	•	Council of Oaths – Roughly 200 Oathkeepers sit in a rotating assembly, empowered to legislate, appoint officers, and direct long-term projects.
	•	[[Continuity]] Staff – Permanent professional bureaucrats (scribes, logisticians, recorders) maintain the machinery of administration, but ultimate authority rests with the temporary Oathkeepers.
