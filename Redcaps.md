Classification

Fae Predators / Blood-Bound Tricksters / Territorial Killers

⸻

CORE IDEA

Redcaps are violence made habitual.
They are not soldiers, not demons, not berserkers—they are murder as maintenance. Where bloodshed becomes routine and unremarked, Redcaps appear to keep it that way.

They don’t fight for causes.
They kill to remain real.

⸻

ORIGIN

Redcaps arise where:
	•	killings repeat over generations
	•	executions become normalized
	•	battlefields never cool
	•	castles, towers, or prisons develop a taste for blood

They are born from places that learned cruelty.

The name comes from their defining ritual:
they must soak their caps in fresh blood to survive.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Redcaps are small but terrifyingly dense.

Common traits:
	•	Short, stocky humanoids (child-sized, but heavy)
	•	Weathered, leathery skin—often ruddy or corpse-grey
	•	Iron boots or greaves, impossibly heavy
	•	Jagged teeth and predatory grins
	•	A woolen cap stained permanently dark red with blood

They smell of iron, rot, and damp stone.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Redcaps are sadistic, cunning, and fearless.

They:
	•	lurk in ruins, keeps, tunnels, stairwells
	•	attack from close range
	•	use knives, picks, clubs, or their iron boots
	•	mock victims relentlessly

They do not flee easily.
They hate cowardice—especially their own.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

Low-to-moderate, but viciously practical.
	•	Excellent ambushers
	•	Understand terrain intimately
	•	Poor planners, excellent finishers
	•	Remember insults and victories

They are not mindless; they are purpose-built.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Blood Dependency: must refresh their caps with blood or wither
	•	Iron Affinity: iron empowers rather than harms them
	•	Grace-hostile: holy presence provokes aggression
	•	Malice-compatible: corruption makes them larger, faster, worse

They weaken rapidly without violence.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Feed on bloodshed, fear, and enclosed spaces
	•	Reproduce rarely; some say new Redcaps form from mass executions
	•	Cannot exist long outside violent territories
	•	Die violently when denied blood long enough

A Redcap infestation marks a place as irredeemable.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

High (localized)

Individually manageable.
In groups, they turn ruins into slaughterhouses.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Redcaps represent:
	•	normalized brutality
	•	violence as tradition
	•	cruelty without ideology
	•	places that learned to kill

Where Boggarts annoy,
Redcaps finish.
