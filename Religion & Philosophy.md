- **Mythic [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]]:** Remember themselves as the **apex civilization** of the Myrmidon Galaxy, betrayed and nearly exterminated.  
- **[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Salvation:** Revered for saving them at their lowest ebb; devotion to [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] is as absolute as devotion to [[Queens]].
