The [[Austro Guard]] economy is not free-flowing or speculative; it is fortress-directed, designed to sustain the Oath by ensuring survival first, defense second, and prosperity only when the first two are secure.
	•	Industrial Backbone
	•	Heavy industries dominate: shipyards, orbital foundries, and mineral refineries orbit Eridani’s gas giants and asteroid belts.
	•	Civilian manufacturing is integrated into defense [[Production]]—clothing guilds make militia uniforms on the side, food processors maintain rations alongside feasts.
	•	Efficiency is prized over novelty. Tools and consumer goods are rugged, repairable, and standardized across the colony.
	•	Resource Discipline
	•	Eridani ore (iron, nickel, rare earths) is refined in rotating shifts, with output carefully logged into public ledgers much like honor records.
	•	Families often volunteer for Burden-Taking Shifts, working in hazardous zones to earn prestige.
	•	Strict quotas prevent over-extraction, since the colony views exhaustion of resources as a [[Betrayal]] of future generations’ Oaths.
	•	Guild Structure
	•	Instead of [[Corporations]], [[Production]] is organized into Guild Syndicates (shipwrights, miners, [[Hydroponics]] farmers).
	•	Each guild negotiates with the Council of Oaths, trading goods for civic priorities rather than profit margins.
	•	Guild masters gain respect not by wealth but by how much of their output is allocated to defense and communal projects.
	•	Currency & Exchange
	•	The colony issues Guard Marks, a scrip backed by labor-hours rather than rare metals.
	•	Honor cannot be directly bought, but high-honor families often receive first pick of luxury imports.
	•	Barter and sponsorship are common, with families trading services and access as much as goods.
	•	Agriculture & Food
	•	Hydroponic towers and subterranean fungal farms provide staples, with surplus stored in Honor Granaries (communal reserves opened only during crisis).
	•	Feasts are communal and honor-coded: the number of dishes a family can serve at public events is tied to its honor score.
	•	Exports & Imports
	•	Exports: warship hulls, Eridani alloys, disciplined mercenary cadres.
	•	Imports: luxury goods, cultural artifacts, exotic foods from other [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] (seen as indulgence, but tolerated if balanced with duty).
	•	Economic Philosophy
	•	Prosperity is never private—any surplus is seen as a communal shield against famine, siege, or war.
	•	Citizens measure wealth less in coin and more in “Years Guarded”—the number of years one’s family labor has contributed to keeping the colony self-sufficient.

The [[Austro Guard]] economy is both martial and moral—a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] that equates productivity with protection, and survival with honor. To grow fat while the Guard hungers is the worst form of dishonor.
