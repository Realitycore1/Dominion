## Details

Deep Well Industrial Zones, also k
nown as DWIZ, are fictional settings that are typically found in dystopian and post-apocalyptic works. They are industrial [[Regions]] that have been dug deep into the [[Earth (Terra)]], often hundreds of meters or more, to create massive factories and processing plants. The DWIZs are often depicted as dangerous and oppressive places where [[Workers]] are forced to live and work in extreme conditions. The factories within the DWIZs may produce anything from weapons and machinery to food and pharmaceuticals, depending on the specific setting. Due to their location deep underground, the DWIZs can be isolated from the rest of society and from [[Natural]] resources, making them reliant on the resources they produce. This can create a closed-loop [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]] where [[Workers]] live, work, and die within the confines of the DWIZ without ever seeing the surface. In some stories, the DWIZs are also heavily polluted and toxic, with the air, water, and soil contaminated by the chemicals and waste products produced by the factories. This adds to the oppressive atmosphere of the DWIZs and often leads to social unrest and revolt.
