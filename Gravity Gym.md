# Gravity Gym — Spec

## Purpose
Maintain musculoskeletal & cardiovascular fitness in micro/low‑g; train EVA movement, balance, and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species|Species]]‑specific tasks; support preflight prep and postflight rehab for mixed [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]/Harmonic/Athearn crews.

## Core Modules
- **Spin Ring Pods (0.[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]–1.0 g)** — [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]–6 m radius centrifuges for bone loading, G‑tolerance intervals.  
- **Vector Field Court** — programmable down‑vector (grid‑coupled/LPG) for footing/agility drills.  
- **Force Deck** — instrumented plates, EM/flywheel racks; EPM rails for mag‑boot lifts.  
- **EVA Lane** — mag‑rail walkway, tether maze, ladder bay; Harness Wall for free‑fall tasks.  
- **Hydro Module ([[Kapik]])** — resistance pool, water treadmill; quiet‑lights & humidity control.  
- **Flight Bay (Dracomari)** — radiator‑safe heat/humidity, thermal fans, UV‑safe lighting.  
- **Hive Mezzanine ([[Ameise]])** — tarsal wall/ceiling routes, scent‑rail etiquette lanes, drum court.  
- **Quiet Chamber ([[Silentium]])** — low‑light flicker cues, anti‑strobe; polarization agility drills.  
- **Rehab/Med Pods** — isokinetic rigs, BFR‑safe protocols, vibro‑tread, ultrasound bone stim.

## Ops & Controls
IMU‑gait assist; PFN logging; field map dashboards. Modes: Walk 0.[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]–0.6 g; Work 0.1–0.[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] g; Strap‑down spikes ≤1 g (≤[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] s); Free 0 g. Surfaces mix ferrous rails and gecko lanes.

## Facility Specs
**Footprint:** 30×18 m bay fits [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] spin pods, 1 court, 1 force deck, [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]–[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] specialty rooms.  
**Power:** 60–250 kW peak (spin + field). **Thermal:** liquid loop to hull radiators.  
**Noise:** <60 dBA bays; <40 dBA Quiet Chamber. **EMI:** <5 mG at avionics height.

## Safety
Field ceilings hard‑limit at 1.0 g; dead‑[[Man]] on harness/boots; dual tethers in EVA lane. Audit pings on grid coupling. Light‑hygiene (no UV strobe). Water [[Rights]]/containment per [[Kapik]] charter. Wing/rail clearance for Dracomari. Scent‑rail protection for [[Ameise]].

## Schedules (templates, 45–60 min)
- **Strength:** Force Deck + Spin (5×5 EM‑squat; [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]×10 pull; 10‑min 0.6 g run).  
- **Agility:** Vector Court ladders + EVA maze ([[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]] rounds).  
- **G‑Tolerance:** Spin intervals (5×[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] min @ 0.8 g with [[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]‑min 0.[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] g recovery).  
- **Recovery:** Hydro Module flow + Quiet Chamber breathwork (20 + 20 min).  
- **Mixed‑crew EVA:** Rails, tethers, harness wall; timed tool tasks.

## Species Adapters
- **Dracomari:** tail‑balance fin, radiator‑safe straps, thermals.  
- **[[Ameise]]:** tarsal pads, scent‑safe materials, drum‑sync metronomes.  
- **[[Silentium]]:** flicker protocol lanes; anti‑strobe lighting.  
- **[[Human]]/[[Kapik]]:** standard harness; hydro rehab compatibility.

## Integration
Pairs with **[[Mag Boots]]** and **[[Gravity Harness]]** kits; PFN logs for mishap forensics; optional Harmonic de‑escalation dampers.
