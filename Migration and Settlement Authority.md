# Migration and Settlement Authority

## Summary

The Migration and Settlement Authority governs lawful movement between worlds, provinces, habitats, frontier zones, restoration territories, and compact regions.

Its work is political because migration changes culture, labor markets, local identity, and voting power.

## Mandate

The Authority handles:

- migration permits;
- refugee placement;
- frontier settlement lotteries;
- compact-region entry rules;
- resettlement after disaster;
- family reunification;
- skill-based relocation;
- quarantine migration holds;
- settlement suitability reviews.

## Powers

It may coordinate transport, assign emergency housing, approve settlement charters, restrict entry into hazardous zones, and negotiate with local governments on intake limits.

## Limits

It cannot use migration control to punish political belief, break lawful families without review, erase minority communities through forced dispersal, or ignore local compact rights.

## Public Face

Citizens know this agency as the office that says yes, not yet, wrong form, unsafe region, approved convoy, or family reunited.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show frontier dreams, core-world crowding, refugee pressure, cultural anxiety, and the slow formation of new regional identities.
