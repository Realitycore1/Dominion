🧭 Scientific / Archaeological Report

Relic: Expanded Dead Sea Scrolls
Discovery Location: Qumran, West Bank, 2035–2040, during renewed digs after a regional conflict exposed collapsed caves. Additional fragments found in the Judean desert cliffs.
Summary:

Material: Scrolls and codices on parchment, papyrus, and copper sheets.

Dating: Radiocarbon dating places manuscripts from 200 BCE – 70 CE.

Content:

Newly discovered “Scroll of the Two Kingdoms”: an apocalyptic war prophecy describing “the Sons of Light and the Sons of Darkness” with uncanny parallels to Crusade/Jihad rhetoric.

Expanded Isaiah Commentary with messianic passages absent in canonical Bible.

Fragmented Gospel parallels (Jesus-like figure teaching cosmic judgment, but with divergent parables).

Astrological tables linking Hebrew prophecy with Babylonian sky-watching, unexpectedly precise lunar-solar calendars.

Scientific Notes:

Ink analysis suggests multiple scribal schools, some linked to Essenes.

Unique “Copper Scroll II” lists hidden temple [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Mirror Earth (2076)/Relics|Relics]], fueling relic-hunting expeditions.

Archaeological Significance: Blurs [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Boundary]] between Jewish apocalyptic sects, early Christianity, and proto-Islamic angelology. Offers fuel for competing theologies.
