1. Persona [[Realms]] (Surface Dream-Worlds)
	•	Correspondence: Persona, Ego.
	•	Nature: Shallow dreamscapes — personal fantasies, social masks, fragmented daydream worlds.
	•	Stability: Fragile; collapse quickly when not observed.
	•	Inhabitants: Dream-figures, reflections of mortals, “echo people.”
	•	Danger: These are seductive but hollow — they feel safe, but collapse when deeper truths intrude.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Shadow Belt (Nightmare Corridors)
	•	Correspondence: Shadow, Personal Unconscious.
	•	Nature: Dark [[Realms]] of suppressed fears, guilt, trauma.
	•	Stability: Chaotic, volatile; these dream-verses change violently.
	•	Inhabitants: Nightmares, phantoms, vampiric [[Entities]] feeding on unresolved fears.
	•	Danger: True monsters of the Beast Chronicle often emerge here — shadow made flesh.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Archetypal Psyche [[Realms]]
	•	Correspondence: Anima/Animus, [[Archetypes]].
	•	Nature: Mythic landscapes where [[Archetypes]] rule (the Mother, the Hero, the [[Trickster]], the Tyrant, etc.).
	•	Stability: More consistent than shadows, because [[Archetypes]] repeat across minds and [[Cultures]].
	•	Inhabitants: Archetypal beings — gods, [[Demons]], giants, lovers, tricksters — living embodiments of story roles.
	•	Danger: These [[Realms]] can empower mortals (becoming archetypal avatars) but can also consume [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]].

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. The Self (Central Sun of Dream)
	•	Correspondence: The integrated Self.
	•	Nature: The radiant axis of the Oneiroverse — a core “dream sun” that all archetypal [[Realms]] orbit.
	•	Stability: Highly stable; nexus of coherence in dream-space.
	•	Inhabitants: Guiding figures, perfected [[Archetypes]], ascendant dreamers.
	•	Danger: Exposure strips away falsehoods — many mortals fear this more than nightmares.
	•	Function: Serves as the gravity well around which all dream-verses organize.

⸻

5. The Psychoid [[Realms]] (Deep Dream / Massa Confusa)
	•	Correspondence: The psychophysical layer, where matter and spirit blend.
	•	Nature: Infinite chaotic dream-depth where all opposites blur: image and instinct, matter and spirit, past and future.
	•	Stability: Nearly none; constant flux.
	•	Inhabitants: Proto-[[Entities]] — half-symbol, half-being; dream-stuff crystallizing into potential verses.
	•	Danger: Prolonged exposure dissolves [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Identity|Identity]]. But it’s also where new dream-verses are born.
	•	Function: A primordial sea at the bottom of the Oneiroverse.
