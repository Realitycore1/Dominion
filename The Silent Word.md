The chamber of [[Harmina]] had not known [[Silence]] in an age.

Verarchs arrived one by one, summoned from distant fronts, their banners still stinking of alien ichor. Ares came first, blood still drying on his armor. Fenrir followed, cloak torn and helm dented where some beast had bitten at him. Ogun carried the smell of forge and [[Fire]], his gauntlets still hot. Others came — the [[Judge]], the Huntress, the Stormbearer — each a [[Titan]], each a war unto themselves.

The Great Table shuddered as they gathered, the floor etched with runes of a thousand victories.

At first, they spoke as kin. Fenrir laughed, booming, asking Ares how many bones he’d broken before the recall. Ogun mocked him with a half-smile, claiming numbers meant nothing without steel to weigh them. Even [[Atham]], cold and austere, offered a dry remark about the folly of endless campaigns. Their voices mingled like clashing weapons — harsh, sharp, but familiar.

And still, She did not speak.

The Empress sat at the head of the chamber, veiled in her light, gaze lowered. Her [[Silence]] was heavier than a thousand words. One by one, the Verarchs fell quiet, laughter dying, boasts crumbling into uneasy hush. The room grew colder. The walls seemed to bend inward.

Only when the last [[Verarch]] entered — cloak of void trailing, helm wreathed in pale [[Fire]] — did She rise.

The Empress stood, and inevitability filled the air.

“My children,” Her voice rang, not as sound but as [[law]] written on the soul. “The [[Crusades]] you wage, the worlds you cleanse, the xenos you scatter — all of this has been preparation. For today, a truth has revealed itself.”

Her eyes burned white and blue, searing even the armor of gods.

“The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]] have stepped from shadow. They are no mere swarm, no tide of beasts or faithless heretics. They are born outside reality, and they desire but one thing: mankind’s annihilation. To scour the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] from every root, to erase us until not even memory remains.”

A murmur rippled through the Verarchs — not fear, but the rare taste of unfamiliarity. Fenrir growled low. Ogun’s fingers clenched on the table. Even Ares, who had not flinched since [[Harmina]]’s first war, stiffened like a [[Man]] struck.

The Empress raised a hand, and [[Silence]] crushed the chamber once more.

“This is not a war of [[Crusades]]. It is not a campaign of cycles. This is the dawn of a new frontier. [[Eos]] — our home multiverse — has become the battleground. And it must be ours. Not simply defended. Taken.”

The banners along the walls ignited in golden [[Fire]], each bearing the sigil of a Legion, each flaring with unquenchable light.

“From this day,” She said, every word heavy with eternity, “we do not march into the [[Stars]] alone. We march into the multiverse. Against the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]]. Against annihilation. And [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/History]] shall name this the First Multiversal Crusade.”

The Verarchs bowed their heads, steel [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military Overview/Vessels/Space/Titans|Titans]] made children before Her. Their laughter was gone, their rivalry burned away. What remained was only blood, oath, and inevitability.

Fenrir rose first, spear howling with wolf-[[Fire]].
“Then let us hunt the hunters.”

One by one, they raised their weapons.
The chamber shook.
And the multiverse trembled.
