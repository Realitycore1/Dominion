I. Exo-Assist Layer (Infantry Enhancers)
	•	Power-Assist Exoskeletons
	•	Light skeletal frames worn under armor.
	•	Amplify strength, speed, load-bearing.
	•	Standard issue for infantry, logistics crews, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]].
	•	Linked to Resonance Net, recording Collapse histories with each deployment.
	•	Cultural Note: Treated as a [[Soldier]]’s “first step” into warforms — a rite of entry.

⸻

II. Legion Frames (Personal Warforms)
	•	Resonant Panoplies
	•	Medium-weight mechs ~[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]–4m tall.
	•	Piloted internally or semi-autonomous.
	•	Armament: modular rail repeaters, collapse-blades, micro-resonant shields.
	•	Deployed in squads, operate like enhanced infantry.
	•	Purpose: To blur the line between [[Soldier]] and warform — making every legionnaire a node of amplified [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]].

⸻

III. Piloted War-Mechs (Hunter Scale)
	•	Hunter-Class Equivalents (≈ 30–50m tall).
	•	Siege and anti-fortress.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Pilots|Pilots]] sit within resonance cradles, co-conscious with the mech’s pack-AI.
	•	Fast, predatory silhouettes — leaps, charges, fortress-breaching.
	•	Comparable to “Metal Gear” [[Archetypes]], but layered with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] resonance seams and Collapse inscriptions.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: Hunters are deployed in pairs, leading Pursuers and [[Tribus]] swarms.

⸻

IV. Federal Colossi (Mechoids)
	•	Towering Mechoids (≈ 100m+ tall).
	•	Walking cathedrals, mobile bastions.
	•	Equipped with field projectors, resonance lances, drone bays.
	•	Used to break entrenched [[Planetary Fortifications]] or act as moving command [[Citadels]].
	•	Pilotage: Typically multiple — primary pilot + oracle choir integrated for metaphysical stability.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: Rare, symbolic as much as practical. Their stride alone can decide battles.

⸻

V. Esoteric / Final Directive Mechs
	•	Non-Recallable Machines like [[The Mèya Zis]].
	•	Operate as metaphysical siege weapons more than mechs.
	•	Deployed only under federal mandate with doctrinal sanction.
	•	Embody Final Scripts — once launched, cannot be recalled.
	•	Philosophy: Not “mech suits” in the [[Human]] sense, but walking liturgies of destruction.

⸻

Cultural Role
	•	Meritocratic Ladder:
	•	Every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] [[Soldier]] begins with power-assist exoskeletons.
	•	Proving themselves allows [[Ascension]] into Legion Frames, Hunters, and potentially Colossi.
	•	To command a Federal Colossus is to be recognized as both [[Warrior]] and scholar of the Collapse.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Oneirotheon Pilgrimage/Doctrine|Doctrine]]: Each scale of mech is inscribed with Collapse verses, meaning piloting is a spiritual as well as [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Estela Integralism/Military|Military]] act.
