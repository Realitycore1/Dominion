Core Bias:

Dense clustered life and structure within flow

This is where Ocean behaves less like open sea and more like:

complex living or civilizational density anchored inside a moving medium.

Traits:
	•	clustered habitats
	•	biodiversity hotspots
	•	interdependent localized systems
	•	hard-soft ecological layering
	•	intense local complexity within larger flow

Cosmological Feel:

A basin where universes feel like:
	•	islands of life in larger continuity
	•	dense nodes surrounded by vast medium
	•	beauty and danger intertwined

Common Expressions:
	•	city-reef civilizations
	•	coral-like cosmological megastructures
	•	high-density ecologies
	•	local basin crossroads

Failure Mode:

Overconcentration
The node becomes too fragile or overdependent on local complexity.

This subtype is excellent for:
	•	coral sub-basin expressions
	•	clustered civilizations
	•	vibrant but vulnerable cosmologies
