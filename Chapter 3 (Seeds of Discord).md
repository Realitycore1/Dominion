# Chapter 3 - Seeds of Discord

## Timeline Function

War Year 0-1. Settlement blocs, route communities, and emerging nation-states interpret the Equilon Mandate through their own histories.

## Event Role

- Atlovaia frames the strike as a necessary blow against founder centralization.
- The F.S.S.R. frames the war as labor and survival justice.
- The Commonwealth prioritizes route safety and refuses easy obedience.
- Ormova, Estelia, and Toldax begin calculating what victory would cost.

## Consequence

The war expands because no faction believes the others are merely mistaken. Each believes its own survival lesson is the one Harmina must follow.
