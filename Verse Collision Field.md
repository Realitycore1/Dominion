# Verse Collision Field SOP v1.0 — Detect, Quarantine, Reimpose, Restore

> **Definition.** A *verse collision* is contact between domains with different or drifting **axiom roots**. Diagnostics shift (η↑, ℓᶜ warp, Ζₑ seesaw, Δ𝒦 ripple), windows knot/pinch, and conservation may misprice until a rule-set is reimposed.

## 1) Detection & Classification

**Field tells (minimum 2 to alarm):**
- **η spike** ≥ +0.06 in 5 min
- **ℓᶜ** distortion ≥ −20% (or ridge flip)
- **Ζₑ** alternating frictionless/absolute (two-state)
- **Δ𝒦** (sheath curvature) step > 2.5σ
- **Window jitter** > 2× baseline for >60 s
- **Paradox basins:** duplicate pasts / loop debt that won’t close

**Classes (pick highest observed):**
- **G – Graze:** superficial shear; small ΔS drop; no stable pockets
- **P – Pierce:** puncture; null pockets present; pinch-off risk
- **M – Merge (laminate):** laminated axiom coexistence with local taboos
- **O – Overwrite:** foreign axiom attempts sovereign re-root (DE beachhead risk)

## 2) Immediate Response (first 15 minutes)

**Minute 0–3 — Quarantine**
- Raise **Ζₑ** (impedance) to zone limits; post *light-hygiene* patterning
- Declare **Radiator Neutrality**; mark radiators & medical sancta neutral
- Deploy **portable Veil cells** to critical corridors & seats
- Halt **acausal threads**; suspend nonessential rites/choirs (medical/SAR exempt with caps)

**Minute 3–8 — Stabilize**
- Drop **temporary Anchors/Motes** at seam; reorient HAG arc (α ±3°)
- Reduce **duty** per Ω_c policy; move bulk traffic sublight
- PFN to 60 s cadence; publish **aether-weather** bulletin

**Minute 8–15 — Assess**
- Convoke **Athearn Reason Sheet** (proofed plan; no paradox debt)
- Decide **Bridge of Writ** if **ΔS** fails to recover within 30 min window
- Seat Sancta: clamp GEZ to **Lance** presets if taboo proximity increases

## 3) Operating Rules (until clear)

- **Null-first:** all peril ops executed from **Veil** or Null adjacency
- **Proof discipline:** new arcs require **Window Proof Sheet** + public PFN hashes
- **Choir policy:** M-load caps; **exit-keys** honored on demand; logs sealed
- **Corridors:** no new gates across seam; existing windows **spool/cool** to 0.6 duty
- **Radiators:** neutrality strict; Σ dumps prioritized; anti-surge baffles engaged

## 4) Clearance & Restoration

**Clear if *all* hold for ≥ 2 hours:**
- **ΔS(t)** trending to baseline; **η variance** down 50% from peak
- **ℓᶜ** within −10% of baseline; **jitter** < 1.2× baseline
- **Loop-debt** residual |Δ| < 0.01; no new paradox basins

**Restore (staged):**
1) Duty 0.6 → 0.8 over 3 h; reopen civilian rites
2) Lift portable veils; keep **Anchors/Motes** hot for 24 h
3) Publish **AAR** with redacted diagrams; rights-restoration **SLA** begins

## 5) KPIs & Thresholds

- **ΔS(t)** axiom stability index
- **η variance** & **ℓᶜ recovery time**
- **Window jitter** & **incident rate**
- **Loop-debt residual** (|Δ|)
- **PFN compliance** (% under load)
- **Rights-restoration SLA** (days to full franchise)

## 7) Interplay with Dark Eternities (DE)

- **DE-G/F:** dream venues capped **Λ_narr ≤ 0.20**; choirs medical/SAR only  
- **DE-B/S:** initiate **Rebirth-Through-Collapse** readiness; sequester sancta; public *quiet festival* (light-hygiene civic action)  
- **Native DE entities:** personhood triad applies; causal reach bounded to Primis-safe corridors

## 8) Edge Cases

- **Multi-anchor cities:** anchor count ≥4 → raise knotting risk; drop to 3 if F_v > 1.4  
- **HAG pinch-off:** if interlocks not stable in 30 s, retire lane and publish reroute  
- **Proof conflict:** if continuity seals disagree, sandbox and arbitrate in **Null-certified timebox**
