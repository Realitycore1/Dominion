1. Common Angels
	•	Definition: The baseline—winged emissaries of divine will.
	•	Traits: Humanoid form, radiant presence, wings of light or feather, voices of command.
	•	Symbolism: Purity, obedience, higher [[law]].

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Seraphim
	•	Definition: Burning angels of divine [[Fire]], closest to the source of holiness.
	•	Traits: Six wings, radiant flames, cannot be looked upon directly.
	•	Symbolism: Purity that consumes, overwhelming divine truth.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Cherubim
	•	Definition: Guardians of sacred places and knowledge.
	•	Traits: Multi-faced, many-eyed, wheels within wheels in older traditions; or childlike in later myth.
	•	Symbolism: Mystery, guardianship, paradox of innocence and terror.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Archangels
	•	Definition: The commanders and messengers of highest rank.
	•	Traits: Glorious humanoid forms, weapons of light, personal names (Michael, Gabriel [[Archetypes]]).
	•	Symbolism: Divine authority, leadership, the [[Warrior]]-messenger.

⸻

5. Thrones
	•	Definition: Angelic manifestations of divine justice and judgment.
	•	Traits: Wheels of [[Fire]] covered in eyes, voices like thunder, living chariots of divinity.
	•	Symbolism: Cosmic order, the machinery of heaven.

⸻

6. [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] Angels
	•	Definition: Overseers of the cosmos, administrators of divine [[law]].
	•	Traits: Regal, commanding presences, wield scepters or seals.
	•	Symbolism: Authority, hierarchy, the structure of divine rule.

⸻

7. Guardian Angels
	•	Definition: Assigned protectors of mortals.
	•	Traits: Humanoid, less radiant, able to walk among mortals unnoticed.
	•	Symbolism: Comfort, protection, personal fate.

⸻

8. Fallen Angels
	•	Definition: Angels who rebelled or strayed from their divine path.
	•	Traits: Broken halos, darkened wings, still radiant but corrupted.
	•	Symbolism: Pride, tragedy, the fragility of perfection.

⸻

9. Elemental Angels
	•	Definition: Angels tied to elemental dominions of creation ([[Fire]], water, storm, stone).
	•	Traits: Forms woven from elemental forces, wielding primal powers.
	•	Symbolism: The divine reflected in nature’s fury.

⸻

10. Aeonic Angels (Mythic Class)
	•	Definition: Cosmic-scale angels embodying abstract concepts ([[Time]], [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]], Hope, Memory).
	•	Traits: Incomprehensible size or form; appear as constellations, fractals, or voices in eternity.
	•	Symbolism: The divine abstracted—beyond [[Human]] comprehension.

•	Watchers – angels who “fell” to mingle with mortals, producing [[Nephilim]] ([[Book of Enoch]]).
	•	Virtues – angels of miracles and blessings.
	•	Powers – [[Warrior]] angels defending against [[Demons]].
	•	Principalities – guardians of nations and rulers.
	•	Ophanim – “wheels within wheels,” radiant beings of movement and order.
	•	Irin – “watchers” in Kabbalistic lore, standing before the throne.
	•	Zophiel – [[Angel]] of knowledge and watchers.
	•	Camael – [[Angel]] of strength, sometimes depicted as rival to Satan.
