THE MARTIAN BRAIN

Areopagus Station occupies a high Mars orbit, deliberately positioned so it is:
	•	above atmospheric interference
	•	outside Phobos’ immediate control zone
	•	within continuous line-of-sight to major surface settlements
	•	optimally placed for traffic coordination

It is named not for mythology, but function:
Areopagus was a court, not a palace.

This station governs Mars without living on it.

⸻

Primary Functions of Areopagus Station

1. Civil–Orbital Governance Interface

Areopagus acts as the arbitration layer between:
	•	Martian surface governments
	•	Phobos security and customs
	•	Deimos logistics buffers
	•	Offworld authorities

Surface Mars governs people.
Areopagus governs flows.

No law is enforced planetside unless it can be enforced orbitally.

⸻

2. Traffic & Jurisdiction Control

All ships entering or leaving Martian space are:
	•	tracked
	•	queued
	•	authorized
	•	routed

Areopagus decides:
	•	which ships dock at Phobos
	•	which are diverted to Deimos
	•	which are denied access entirely

Mars does not control its own sky.
That is intentional.

⸻

3. Strategic Neutral Ground

Areopagus is explicitly:
	•	demilitarized in appearance
	•	heavily defended in practice
	•	neutral in tone
	•	procedural in culture

It hosts:
	•	arbitration chambers
	•	treaty review councils
	•	logistics courts
	•	inter-polity negotiations

This prevents Mars from becoming a unilateral power center.

⸻

Architecture & Internal Design

Areopagus Station is not sprawling.

It is:
	•	dense
	•	layered
	•	compartmentalized

Key features:
	•	rotating gravity sections for long-term staff
	•	zero-g administrative cores
	•	hardened data vaults
	•	redundant comm arrays
	•	independent life-support sectors

No one “owns” Areopagus.
Everyone uses it.
