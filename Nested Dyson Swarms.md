Nested Dyson Swarms are multi-layered shells of energy-collecting structures orbiting a star, arranged in concentric bands, each optimized for a different function (habitat, energy collection, computation, defense). They are cathedrals of light, tiered like cosmic onion skins, embodying [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] order in orbital geometry.
Structure & [[Layers]]

Inner Swarm: Closest to the star — ultra-high-temperature collectors and plasma forges.

Middle Swarm: Habitats, solar farms, and Cornucopia forges.

Outer Swarm: Defensive veils, [[Computronium]] lattices, Whipple-Shield nets.

Exoswarm: Loose halo of relay satellites and [[Mega-Constellation Relays]] projecting light-script across the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]].

Scale

Each layer may number in the billions of collectors.

Spans entire AU ranges, with gaps carefully orchestrated to let light filter outward in fractal glyphs.

MRO ecologies drift endlessly, repairing and replacing nodes.

Functions

Energy Harvest: Captures near-total stellar output.

Habitat: Houses trillions in ring habitats, Bishop Rings, Clarke Cities woven into the middle [[Layers]].

Computation: Outer [[Layers]] double as [[Computronium]] matrices, “thinking” the empire’s will.

Defense: Nested Swarms serve as Whipple-shields and kinetic nets, absorbing incoming [[Fire]].

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Provide abundant energy and habitat for entire sectors.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: The outer [[Layers]] act as fortress-shields, inner [[Layers]] as weapons — beams, plasma jets, even star-lances.

Economic: Anchor trade lanes and beam-powered highways.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Crowns” — myth frames each swarm layer as another halo the Empress places upon the star.
