# Social Cohesion & Control

## Summary

The Dominion maintains cohesion through provision, memory, service, rights, ritual, public education, infrastructure reliability, and lawful oversight.

Control exists, but the current canon should not present unchecked control as automatically virtuous. The Dominion is strongest when citizens trust its systems enough to cooperate without needing constant coercion.

The question is not whether the Dominion shapes society. It does.

The question is whether that shaping remains accountable to rights, witness, and the distinction between lawful dissent and continuity threat.

## Sources of Cohesion

### Provision

Citizens trust the Dominion because basic needs are met. Food, water, medicine, shelter, transit, emergency relief, and education create practical loyalty before ideology does.

### Memory

Continuity Archives, family ledgers, public names, collapse histories, and memorial rites teach citizens that survival depends on remembering accurately.

### Service

Public service creates civic identity and franchise. It tells citizens that stewardship must be earned through contribution.

### Ritual

Rituals bind the civilization emotionally. The healthiest rites preserve grief, responsibility, and repair. The most dangerous rites turn obedience into beauty.

### Rights

The rights floor prevents cohesion from becoming mere submission. Citizens must be able to petition, dissent, worship privately, gather lawfully, and challenge state action.

## Legitimate Control

Legitimate control includes:

- emergency drills;
- evacuation orders;
- infrastructure safety rules;
- public health restrictions under review;
- classification of concrete operational secrets;
- warrants for demonstrated continuity threats;
- service standards for sensitive offices;
- disaster rationing when provision systems are endangered.

These controls should be narrow, recorded, and reviewable.

## Illegitimate Control

Illegitimate control includes:

- punishing lawful dissent;
- treating criticism of Amber as treason by default;
- using ritual attendance as loyalty proof;
- surveillance without individualized suspicion;
- indefinite secrecy;
- memory erasure as punishment;
- clerical or ideological tests for office;
- classifying embarrassment as security;
- treating non-franchised civilians as lesser persons.

When these appear, they should signal corruption, crisis, or overreach.

## The Integrity Corps Boundary

The Integrity Corps may act against continuity threats, not ordinary disagreement.

Valid continuity threats include systemic corruption, hostile infiltration, ontological instability, archive sabotage, forbidden technologies, organized subversion, or mass-harm memetics.

Invalid targets include private worship, minority culture, lawful protest, investigative journalism, peaceful anti-monarchism, and criticism of the Royal Government.

See: [[Integrity Corps Guardrails]]

## Social Pressure

Not all control is legal. Much of the Dominion's pressure is social:

- service expectations;
- honor grading;
- family reputation;
- civic shame;
- public memory;
- professional credentialing;
- access to high-trust roles;
- suspicion of people who refuse all public duty.

This pressure can create disciplined citizens. It can also create quiet cruelty if shame becomes entertainment or class sorting.

## Healthy Cohesion

Healthy cohesion looks like:

- citizens cooperating during emergency without panic;
- public systems admitting error;
- dissent remaining lawful;
- religious minorities worshiping privately without fear;
- service honored but not used to degrade civilians;
- courts resisting overreach;
- Royal authority accepting witness and review;
- Federal authority preparing for future threats it cannot personally see.

## Failure Modes

The Dominion is failing if:

- people obey because attention from the state is frightening;
- families avoid petitions for fear of classification;
- local compacts become decorative;
- ritual replaces provision;
- service becomes a caste gate;
- the Integrity Corps polices opinion;
- Federal resistance is treated as betrayal;
- Royal continuity becomes a permanent excuse for daily intrusion.

## Story Use

Social cohesion scenes should show both comfort and pressure.

A citizen may love the clean water, safe transit, stable clinic, and names preserved in the archive while still fearing what happens if a classification mark enters their record.

That contradiction is the Dominion.
