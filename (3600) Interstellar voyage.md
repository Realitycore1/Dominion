# (3600) First interstellar voyage

The *C.U.C. Deliverance* departs Pluto-Charon for Proxima in 3600.

The mission is jointly authorized and funded by the Colonial Union and Outer Systems Compact as part of the settlement ending the Second Solar War.

The ark carries:

- human, uplifted, and synthetic colonists;
- closed-loop ecologies and seed archives;
- embryos, genomes, and cultural records;
- fabrication systems and industrial feedstock;
- medical, educational, and civic institutions;
- Watchtower navigation archives;
- representatives of Solar polities.

Neptunian-produced antimatter and Uranian containment engineering support sustained acceleration and deceleration.

Families remain awake for most of the voyage. Rotating habitation and selective medical suspension supplement ordinary generational and domestic life rather than replacing it.

The mission reaches Proxima in 3608, where deceleration damage forces the controlled crash that founds Craterheart Colony.
