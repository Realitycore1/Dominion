## Core Belief
[[Humanity]]’s [[Hunger]] for gods comes from a real but missing **Divine Spark**.  
The Oneirotheon’s entire civilization exists to find and reassemble it through **mythic recapitulation** and **[[Aetheric]] dream-rituals**.  
They wield **magenta [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Science/Aether|Aether]]** as their sacred signature, and remain one of the few [[Human]] civilizations that never assimilated into [[The Dominion]].

---

## Major Factions

### 1. The Dracoshian Oneirotheon
- Believe the **Dracoshia** embody or safeguard the Divine Spark.  
- See the Draconic lineages as the **living archetype of transcendence**, a shard of divinity incarnate.  
- Advocate pilgrimage *towards Dracoshian systems*, establishing shrine-worlds in their orbit.  
- Militantly suppress any “false cycles” that deny Draconic prophecy.  

### 2. The Seekers
- Reject the Dracoshia as *false divinity*.  
- Continue the **endless quest**, harvesting myths, scriptures, and Aether loci to refine the divine cartography.  
- Sub-factions include:  
  - **Singularity Doctrine**: The Spark is the **Aetheric Singularity** itself — the convergence of Aether and consciousness.  
  - **God-Empress Doctrine**: The Empress is nearer to the Spark than any other, but unfinished — a candidate for Divinity, not yet complete.  

---

## Internal Struggles
- **Sleeper Cells**: Both sides embed agents within rival fleets, waiting to sabotage Consensus Slumber or assassinate hierophants.  
- **Mass Violence**: Entire Dream-Arks have been destroyed in internecine conflict, with populations annihilated in mythic re-enactments of betrayal and martyrdom.  
- **Dream Corruption**: Conflicting myths fracture Consensus Slumber, replaying multiple “truths” simultaneously and destabilizing Aether currents.  

---

## Ritual & Cleansing
- **Purging Dogma**: All factions agree that mortal dogma is corruption — the Spark must be found through myth, not creed. Rituals of “sin-cleansing” include fasting, magenta Aether baths, and dream-exorcisms.  
- **Role Enforcement**: Dissenters are cast into archetypal roles — branded as Betrayers, Tyrants, or Martyrs — and executed or exiled to fulfill the myth-cycle.  

---

## Dominion Context
- The Dominion tolerates the Oneirotheon but monitors them closely:  
  - **Dracoshian faction** threatens to elevate an alien lineage to divine status.  
  - **Seeker faction** edges into heresy: some revere the God Empress but refuse submission, others deify the Aether itself.  
- The Infernariate likely views them as **walking heresy engines** — valuable for relic-hunting, but always on the edge of purgation.  

---

## Visual Aesthetic
- **Magenta Aether** marks all factions, but with distinct motifs:  
  - **Dracoshian faction**: draconic imagery — wings, scaled armor, spires shaped like claws.  
  - **Seeker faction**: abstraction — singularity spirals, shattered mirrors, glyph constellations.  
- Pilgrimage fleets clash in space, leaving wrecked Dream-Arks adrift like desecrated reliquaries.  

---

## Timeline of Schisms
- **Early Unity**: At first, all Oneirotheon agreed on the Spark’s absence and the mission to reassemble it.  
- **First Fracture**: Upon contact with Dracoshia myths, a sect declared them the true Spark — forming the Dracoshian Oneirotheon.  
- **Singularity Doctrine (Later)**: Some Seekers began identifying the Aetheric Singularity itself as the divine locus.  
- **God-Empress Doctrine (Recent)**: A controversial wing of the Seekers proclaimed the Empress a candidate for Sparkhood, sparking violent purges.  
- **Ongoing State**: The pilgrimage remains divided, with sleeper cells, ritual killings, and dream-corruption endemic to their culture.  

---

## Summary
The Oneirotheon are a wandering, magenta-lit, dream-governed zealot civilization fractured into **two great factions**:  
- **Those who believe the Spark is already found** in the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Dracoshia/Dracoshia]].  
- **Those who believe it remains lost**, whether in the Singularity, the Empress, or beyond.  

They remain one of the only [[Human]] civilizations outside [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] assimilation, a living pilgrimage whose journey is both sacred and violent, forever caught between myth and heresy.
