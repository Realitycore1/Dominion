Classification

Premonitory Phenomena / Fate-Indicators / Non-Hostile Harbingers

⸻

CORE IDEA

Death Omens are warnings without agency.

They are not killers, not judges, not curses. A Death Omen appears when probability spikes—when a life, a place, or a lineage is about to cross a terminal threshold. They don’t cause death. They announce that the path has narrowed to one ending.

They don’t speak.
They signal.

⸻

ORIGIN

Death Omens manifest where causality compresses:
	•	imminent violent death
	•	irreversible illness or injury
	•	mass-casualty convergence
	•	execution of long-standing fate contracts
	•	large-scale malice or grace realignment

They emerge before death, not after.
If you see one clearly, time is almost gone.

⸻

PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION

Death Omens take symbolic, culturally resonant forms.

Common manifestations:
	•	shadowed figures at the edge of vision
	•	black birds perched where they shouldn’t be
	•	red or eclipsed moons appearing briefly out of cycle
	•	candles extinguishing simultaneously
	•	reflections moving out of sync
	•	tolling bells with no source

They are visible only to those within the probability cone.

⸻

BEHAVIOR & TEMPERAMENT

Death Omens are passive, persistent, and unavoidable.
	•	Appear repeatedly as the moment approaches
	•	Do not react to threats or attempts to communicate
	•	Intensify in clarity as death nears
	•	Vanish instantly once the outcome resolves

They do not chase.
They wait.

⸻

INTELLIGENCE

None in the conventional sense.
	•	No planning
	•	No intent
	•	No memory

They are closer to alerts than beings.

Rarely, prolonged exposure creates false patterns—people begin assigning intent where none exists.

⸻

METAPHYSICAL TRAITS
	•	Fate Synchronization: bound to a specific death-event
	•	Grace-Neutral: angels acknowledge but do not command
	•	Malice-Resistant: corruption amplifies symbolism, not lethality
	•	Perceptual Selectivity: only relevant observers perceive them
	•	Temporal Narrowing: each appearance marks fewer remaining paths

Attempting to destroy a Death Omen does nothing.
Attempting to avoid it usually fulfills it.

⸻

ECOLOGY
	•	Appear singly or in small clusters
	•	Common near battlefields, hospitals, execution sites, plague zones
	•	Do not accumulate or persist after death resolves
	•	Leave no physical trace

Places with frequent Omens feel anticipatory, not haunted.

⸻

ALPHA DEATH OMENS

There is no true Alpha—but there are Convergence Omens.

Traits:
	•	Signal mass-death or civilization-scale events
	•	Appear as large symbols: blood-red skies, mass animal silence, synchronized failures
	•	Perceived across wide populations simultaneously
	•	Often misinterpreted as divine wrath

Convergence Omens don’t warn individuals.
They warn history.

⸻

THREAT ASSESSMENT

None directly / Severe psychologically

Death Omens don’t kill you.
They tell you you’re out of time.

⸻

NARRATIVE ROLE

Death Omens represent:
	•	inevitability without malice
	•	fate without morality
	•	warning without salvation
	•	the cruelty of knowing

Where Death Echoes replay the past,
Death Omens announce the future.
