## Surveillance
Surveillance is subtle, resonance-based. Disharmony is detected as distortion in civic harmonics.

## Rooting Out Discord
The [[Infernariate]] act before discord spreads. They are feared and revered as necessary guardians.

## Inquisition Doctrine
When treachery is proven, punishment is absolute — execution and erasure from memory.

## Information Doctrine
Knowledge flows freely but is monitored. Poison is studied in quarantine, never erased blindly.
