This is the emergency diagnosis policy.

It applies where population is below viability thresholds.

A world can be underpopulated even if the empire overall has too many people.

Examples:

* a frontier colony cannot maintain its reactors
* a military border world lacks enough reserve personnel
* an Ice World lacks enough maintenance crews
* a Tomb World restoration project lacks settlers
* an Agri-World has too few skilled agricultural engineers
* a postwar region lost too many family lines
* a low-birth Core World has too many immortals and too few children
* a Xeno-integrated world lacks enough compatible specialists
* a Forge/Refinery World cannot sustain production crews

The issue is not just numbers.

It is population structure.

A world with ten million people can still be underpopulated if it lacks:

* children
* technicians
* medics
* farmers
* engineers
* defense personnel
* teachers
* reproductive-age citizens
* cultural continuity carriers
* local administrative talent

Useful phrasing:

Underpopulation is not merely low headcount. It is the failure of a population to sustain its infrastructure, institutions, defense, reproduction, and cultural continuity.
