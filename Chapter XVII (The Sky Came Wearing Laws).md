# Chapter XVII - The Sky Came Wearing Laws

## Timeline Function

After the Covenant of Syl'an ratifies Amber's wartime continuity office, GalFed enters formal contact with a Harmina capable of returning one planetary answer.

## Event Role

- GalFed arrives calmly rather than opening with military invasion.
- Its delegation offers aid, observers, legal harmonization, development guidance, education agreements, cultural classification, and demilitarization proposals.
- Every offer answers a real Harminan wound created by the New World War.
- The agreements distribute authority through procedure, standards, dependency, and administrative review.
- Amber recognizes Ferox's logic at civilizational scale: protective, orderly, patronizing, and willing to erase consent through structures described as benevolent.
- A clause governing education, guardianship, or civil development reveals a claim on future Harminan children.
- Harmina's refusal or resistance carries immediate costs because GalFed's assistance would save actual lives.
- The formal-contact source expands across Chapters 38–44 of [[Amber Book II - The Final Line - Definitive Skeleton]].
- Open GalFed war continues in [[Amber Book III - Operation Prescience - Development Spine]].

## Consequence

The old warning becomes literal.

The sky does not first arrive with fire.

It arrives with forms.
