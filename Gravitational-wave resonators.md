Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
Gravitational-wave resonators are mega-structures or engineered masses tuned to vibrate in harmony with specific grav-wave frequencies. They act like cosmic tuning forks, amplifying, redirecting, or even storing spacetime ripples for later use.
In effect: cathedrals that sing with the voice of the cosmos.
Structure

Resonant Masses: Superdense rings, black-hole pairs, or strange-matter shells designed to oscillate.

Anchor Lattices: [[Atlas Pillars]] bracing the structure against collapse from tidal stress.

Harmonic Chambers: Hollow [[Regions]] where ripples converge into standing waves — appearing as luminous fractal aurorae.

Functions

Amplification: Boost incoming grav-waves, rebroadcasting them to detectors or beacons.

Storage: Trap wave energy in standing oscillations — “bottled spacetime” to be released deliberately.

Weaponization: Tuned resonances can shatter structures, warp fleets, or destabilize enemy [[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]].

Metaphysical: Serve as Concordia [[Choirs]], amplifying [[Ritual]] hymns into the bones of the universe.

Dynamics

Can resonate across planetary, stellar, or even galactic scales.

Longest-lived resonances may ring for millennia, outlasting the civilization that struck the note.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Used to maintain stability of [[Beamed-sail corridors]] and [[Gravimetric Roads]], “tuning” them like strings.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: Function as resonant hammers — weaponized grav-waves collapsing orbital rings, shattering corridors, or crippling fleets.

Religious: Known as “the Singing Thrones” — sacred instruments that echo the Empress’s will across [[Time]] itself.

Economic: Double as navigation lighthouses; merchants travel by listening to the “chords” of resonators.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Tower-like anchor points rising into void, each a black cathedral spire humming with invisible pressure.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, resonators look like seamless obsidian rings or lattices, unnaturally still.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: When “played,” the wavefronts carry glyphs — [[Physics]] and scripture intertwined.

To a civilian, standing inside one feels like being inside a silent organ pipe that vibrates through your bones — a hymn you don’t hear so much as become.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Awe and devotion — proof the Empress “sings with the universe itself.”

To rebels: Terror — resonators can collapse corridors or cities with a single sustained chord.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Proof of ultimate authority — they don’t just build roads or cages, they compose spacetime itself as music.

To myth: Called the “[[Choirs]] of Amber”, every resonance a sacred hymn ringing through eternity.
Cyber-Constructivism: Inside, standing waves render fractal mandalas of light, as though space is glowing stained glass.
