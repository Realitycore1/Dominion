## Details

An Orbital Drydock is a type of sp
ace station that is designed to facilitate the repair and maintenance of large spacecraft, such as [[Starships]] or space stations. Like a drydock on [[Earth (Terra)]], an Orbital Drydock would provide a secure and stable platform for docking and servicing spacecraft, allowing for access to facilities and equipment necessary for repairs and upgrades. The drydock would likely be equipped with various types of docking [[Arms]] and mooring [[Systems]] to secure the spacecraft in place, as well as specialized tools and equipment for carrying out repairs and modifications. The Orbital Drydock would be located in orbit around a planet or other celestial body, providing easy access to supplies and resources from the surface below. It would also offer the advantage of being located in a low-gravity environment, which would make it easier to carry out delicate repairs or adjustments to sensitive equipment. The Orbital Drydock concept has been explored in various science fiction works, often as a central location for the maintenance and repair of [[Starships]] in space battles or other conflict situations. Despite the challenges associated with constructing such a complex structure in orbit, Orbital Drydocks remain a compelling idea for future space industries and transportation [[Systems]].
