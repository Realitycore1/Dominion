The primary source of nitrogen for ecosystems is from atmospheric deposition, where nitrogen compounds such as nitric acid and ammonia are deposited on the ground through rainfall and snow. These nitrogen compounds are also released into the environment through industrial and agricultural activities, such as fertilizer use and waste management. The decline of nitrogen has been observed in several ways. For example, nitrogen levels in the air and soil have decreased over [[Time]] due to increased atmospheric deposition and pollution.
