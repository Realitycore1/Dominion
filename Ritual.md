# Entities and Artifacts of the Empty

### **The Eidolon**
See [[The Eidolon]].

### **Reapers**
Shades bound to enforce the laws of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]. Appear as wingless, faceless figures whose touch dissolves sound.

### **Echo Sleepers**
Residual consciousnesses that drift too long. Whisper in [[Forgotten]] languages before fading.

### **Relics**
- **The Still Pebble:** when dropped in water, produces no ripples.  
- **Veil Dust:** ash gathered from Reaper crossings; used in [[Silence]] rites.
