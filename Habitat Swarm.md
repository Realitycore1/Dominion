## Details

A Habitat Swarm is a hypothetical 
type of space-based settlement that is designed to be made up of a large number of individual habitats or structures, all arranged in [[Close]] proximity to one another. The Habitat Swarm would likely be constructed in a low-[[Earth (Terra)]] orbit, where it could benefit from the planet's gravity for energy conservation and to create [[Artificial]] gravity within the habitats. The individual habitats would be designed to support a variety of different environments and lifestyles, ranging from research facilities and living quarters to recreational and leisure spaces. The benefits of a Habitat Swarm design include the ability to support a large population of residents in a relatively compact and efficient manner, as well as the potential for greater flexibility and adaptability than would be possible with traditional station designs. By combining different habitats with specialized functions, the Swarm could provide a range of capabilities and services, allowing for more diverse and complex social and economic [[Systems]]. However, there are also significant challenges associated with Habitat Swarms, such as the need for advanced engineering and construction techniques to create the necessary habitats and [[Infrastructure]], as well as the potential for instability and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Union of Recresh/System|system]] failures in such a complex structure.
