# Dominion Education & Integration Commission

## Summary

The Dominion Education & Integration Commission governs schooling, civic education, service preparation, cultural integration, adult retraining, and frontier curriculum standards.

It is one of the main ways the Federal Branch reproduces the Dominion as a culture rather than merely an administration.

## Mandate

The Commission oversees:

- public schools;
- service-track preparation;
- civic literacy;
- provincial curriculum standards;
- frontier education networks;
- adult retraining;
- integration programs for refugees and restored populations;
- GalFed, VFF, MOT, and continuity-risk literacy where approved;
- language access and compact-region education rights.

## Powers

The Commission may certify schools, issue curriculum standards, fund local education, suspend abusive academies, and approve service-track testing.

## Limits

The Commission may not erase local culture, convert education into political worship, force religious conformity, or use service scores as a hidden caste system.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Commission through school exams, apprenticeship placement, service aptitude notices, archive lessons, language classes, and public arguments over what children should remember.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show cultural drift, service pressure, youth ambition, and the long fight over whether education teaches citizens to think or to endure.
