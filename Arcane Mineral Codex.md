Arkanite

Type: Mana capacitor crystal

Appearance
Translucent blue-violet crystal with faint internal lightning patterns.

Properties

• stores magical energy
• slowly releases stored mana
• extremely stable lattice structure

Behavior

When exposed to magical fields the crystal accumulates energy until it reaches saturation. Overload can cause violent discharge.

Uses
	•	spell batteries
	•	wand cores
	•	arcane reactors
	•	enchanted tools

Arkanite becomes the magical equivalent of lithium batteries.

⸻

Aether Quartz

Type: Magical oscillator crystal

Appearance

Clear crystal with faint golden filaments.

Properties

• resonates with leyline frequencies
• stabilizes spellcasting
• amplifies magical signals

Uses
	•	spell amplifiers
	•	communication arrays
	•	ritual circles
	•	magical sensors

If Arkanite is a battery, Aether Quartz is a signal processor.

⸻

Umbra Crystal

Type: Anti-magic mineral

Appearance

Matte black crystal that absorbs light.

Properties

• drains magical energy
• suppresses enchantments
• destabilizes spell matrices

Uses
	•	anti-magic prisons
	•	magical dampening fields
	•	containment chambers

Essentially the magical version of a radiation absorber.

⸻

Pyroclast Gem

Type: Thermal magic crystal

Appearance

Red crystal with internal glowing veins.

Properties

• stores heat energy
• releases controlled bursts of fire magic
• extremely stable at high temperatures

Uses
	•	elemental weapons
	•	volcanic reactors
	•	forge enhancements

Properties

Uses

Luminar Crystal

Type: Light magic mineral

Appearance

White crystalline structure emitting soft light.

Properties

Uses

	•	illumination systems

Gravite

Appearance

Dense metallic-black stone that subtly distorts nearby objects.

Properties

Uses

	•	megastructure anchors
