The Party-Imperial State

The [[Kuomin Empire]] blends two currents: the Kuomintang’s party-structure and the imagery of restored imperial sovereignty. The result is a hybrid [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]]—part bureaucracy, part dynasty, part ideological state.
	•	The Emperor-President
	•	Styled as the Son of Heaven Restored, the head of state is both Emperor (symbolic-mythic ruler) and President (party commander).
	•	The office is not hereditary—[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] are selected from within the Kuomin Party elite—but once enthroned, they claim semi-divine legitimacy as continuation of imperial destiny.
	•	Portraits show them in both [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] dress and imperial robes, embodying modernity and tradition.
	•	The Kuomin Party
	•	A centralized political party forms the backbone of governance, modeled after the historical Kuomintang.
	•	The Party controls all organs of state: [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]], education, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]], and propaganda.
	•	Party loyalty is mandatory; citizenship requires membership.
	•	Imperial Senate
	•	A ceremonial body of appointed officials drawn from provincial governors and [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] commanders.
	•	The Senate is presented as “echoes of the mandarinate,” a symbolic link to the ancient Confucian bureaucracy.
	•	In practice, it advises but does not restrain the Emperor-President.
	•	Provincial Governors
	•	Each colony world is overseen by a Governor-General, a Kuomin Party officer with near-absolute authority.
	•	Governors must perform annual Mandate Rituals—public ceremonies declaring the Emperor-President’s divine right to rule.
	•	[[law]] & Justice
	•	Legal codes are drawn from a blend of Confucian philosophy, party [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], and militarized enforcement.
	•	Trials are political spectacles, framed around loyalty rather than evidence.
	•	Punishments are tiered: rehabilitation for loyal citizens, exile for dissenters, execution for “traitors to [[The Mandate]].”
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] of [[The Mandate]]
	•	The [[Kuomin Empire]] proclaims itself the restoration of China’s cosmic destiny in the [[Stars]].
	•	Its ideology fuses nationalism with cosmic order: “As under Heaven, so among the [[Stars]].”
	•	Stability, hierarchy, and expansion are justified as proof of [[The Mandate]] of Heaven renewed.
