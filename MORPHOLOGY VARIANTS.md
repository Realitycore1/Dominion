1. The Basaltiform (Low-Temp Silicon Organisms)

These are the closest to “solid-form” beings.

Body:
• Dense basalt-like composites
• Dark mineral skin with glowing thermal veins
• Slow metabolic rate, strong physical durability
• Trauma heals like rock repairing fractures through micro-melts and resolidification

Behavior:
Deliberate, patient, contemplative.
They serve as archivists, diplomats, and memory stewards.

⸻

2. The Siloxen (Mid-Temp Fluidic Silicon Life)

The most common “citizen-class” of Ternesj.

Body:
• Gel-like or rubbery siloxane tissues
• Semi-translucent bodies shifting from amber to violet hues
• Can flex, stretch, alter shape moderately
• Conduct light and heat as part of thought-language

Behavior:
Adaptive, expressive, mobile.
These are the traders, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], travelers.

⸻

3. The Magmavore (High-Temp Semi-Molten Giants)

Evolved near rifts, mantle vents, or crystallization pools.

Body:
• Torso-shaped magma shells held together by magnetic and lattice scaffolding
• Internal temperatures so high they glow white-gold
• Each movement leaves flickers of cooled glass fragments

Behavior:
Intense, philosophical, occasionally volatile.
They serve as planetary guardians, heavy-labor specialists, and deep-core explorers.

⸻

4. The Zeolite [[Choirs]] (Modular, Colonial Beings)

These aren’t individuals but harmonic collectives.

Body:
• Swarms of millimeter-scale crystalline motes
• Can assemble into humanoid forms, serpents, geometric patterns
• Each mote acts as a “syllable” of intelligence — the pattern is the person

Behavior:
Brilliant, alien, mathematically spiritual.
Serve as judges, theoreticians, or cosmic-map keepers.
