Horizon Arc Gates are macro-architectural portals built along the curvature edge of a [[Verse]], enabling transition between topological [[Regions]], realities, or alternate omniversal strata. They use stabilized spacetime horizons — often black hole throats, [[Confluencia]] tethers, or matter cores — to create passageways across [[Domains]] that would otherwise remain sealed.

Transition Mechanism

Arc Geometry: A great sweeping curve of lattice spanning parsecs, “drawing” the gate’s outline.

Horizon Film: A translucent event-surface, rippling with auroral light. From one side, it appears like a mirror of eternity.

Concordia Harmonics: The gate “sings” itself open; frequencies must align with [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] codes to traverse.
