The Dracoshia initiate a precision strike against Amber during the late Primus Dominion.

The attack is not aimed at:

* territory,
* fleets,
* or infrastructure.

It targets:

* continuity architecture,
* VFF cohesion,
* and Amber’s identity lattice directly.

Consequences:

* Interversal transit collapses.
* Large regions of Eos become causally unstable.
* Entire fleets disappear mid-transition.
* Chronological drift events begin.
* The Primus Dominion fractures almost instantly.

Amber vanishes.

Not killed.
Not destroyed.

Partitioned.
