1. What Psychic Surgery Actually Is

The real definition that fits your worlds:

Manipulating internal mental structures—beliefs, memories, emotional imprints, trauma loops, sensory maps, partitions, or resonance patterns—without directly modifying the physical brain.

It is NOT:
	•	real-world medical practice
	•	a replacement for physical surgery
	•	miraculous healing
	•	anything involving touching a person’s body

It is:
	•	cognitive architecture adjustment
	•	emotional circuit rewiring
	•	trauma extraction
	•	memory stabilization
	•	partition realignment
	•	resonance cleansing
	•	Aether pattern modification

Think of it as neuroscience + metaphysics + cognitive engineering.

⸻

2. Cognitive Anatomy of Psychic Surgery

A functioning psyche has structures:
	•	memory threads
	•	identity partitions
	•	trauma nodes
	•	belief scaffolds
	•	emotional anchors
	•	fear loops
	•	sensory imprints
	•	predictive models
	•	time-binding structures
	•	narrative frameworks

Psychic surgery modifies these without harming the person.

It’s more like debugging software than cutting flesh.

Done correctly, it:
	•	dissolves trauma triggers
	•	stabilizes identity fractures
	•	removes intrusive patterns
	•	rebalances emotional signatures
	•	restores continuity
	•	harmonizes partitions
	•	strengthens the core self

Done badly, it:
	•	shatters identity
	•	introduces false memories
	•	destabilizes time perception
	•	causes partition drift
	•	erodes emotional coherence
	•	creates psychic “scar tissue”
	•	opens the psyche to external influence

This is why the Dominion treats it like neurosurgery, not mysticism.

⸻

3. Psychic Surgery in Your Universes

Now the part that matters: how each setting interprets the same underlying phenomenon.

A. Dominion

The Dominion does not call it “psychic surgery.”

They call it:

Pattern Recomposition
Axiomatic Correction
Identity Realignment
Causal Resonance Editing

To Dominion practitioners:
	•	a mind is a structure
	•	identity is a pattern
	•	memory is architecture
	•	emotional states are harmonics

Psychic surgery is a clinical procedure performed by:
	•	Ascendants
	•	Pattern Architects
	•	Aether Technicians
	•	Thought-Form Engineers

They use:
	•	harmonic scalpel-fields
	•	Aether compression lattices
	•	axiomatic stabilization grids
	•	memory-thread cutters
	•	partition restart pulses

Dominion psychic surgery can:
	•	purge indoctrination
	•	repair trauma
	•	stabilize Aether-touched minds
	•	anchor a consciousness during ascension
	•	remove harmful identity echoes
	•	restructure clones’ personalities
	•	integrate multi-self architectures

It is brutal in precision and frighteningly effective.

⸻

B. Concordia

Psychic surgery becomes ritual psychomancy.

Concordians treat the psyche as:
	•	a soul
	•	a grace vessel
	•	a resonance matrix

Their psychic surgeons are:
	•	mind-mages
	•	empathy clerics
	•	dreamweavers
	•	soulbinders
	•	resonance shamans

They manipulate:
	•	emotional knots
	•	Aether scars
	•	grace patterns
	•	malice infections
	•	dream-echo residues
	•	ancestral memory imprints

Concordian psychic surgery feels mystical, but underneath the ritual:
	•	memory
	•	trauma
	•	identity
	•	resonance

…are being manipulated.

⸻

C. Post-Severance Earth

Psychic surgery becomes a dangerous survival tactic, because Severance breaks:
	•	memory
	•	time
	•	identity coherence
	•	emotional boundaries
	•	self-other distinctions

A “psychic surgery” here is:
	•	stitching a mind back together
	•	removing lingering trauma echo
	•	patching temporal self-fragmentation
	•	sealing identity leaks
	•	reinforcing partitions to withstand anomaly zones

Survivors learn crude forms:
	•	mental compartmentalization
	•	trauma excision visualization
	•	emotional recalibration
	•	reality-check anchors
	•	dissociation re-binding

Humanity becomes its own psychic surgeons because no one else is left to do it.

⸻

D. Xeno Civilizations

Different species have wildly different psychic anatomies, so their surgeries vary:

Krexolites

Psychic surgery via sensory ritual—removing scent-trauma and taste-memory imprints.

Draconids

Resonance-based emotional surgery performed through harmonic roaring and controlled pressure waves.

Synarchate

Collective psychic surgery—communal rebalancing inside shared harmonic fields.

Aether Entities

Their version is incomprehensible:
	•	they recombine identity-lattices,
	•	merge memory threads,
	•	and reshape emotional topology.

Their surgery is creation.

⸻

4. Tools of Psychic Surgery (Non-Physical)

Not scalpels.

Instead:

A. Harmonic tools

Used by Dominion and Draconids.

B. Symbolic tools

Used by Concordian mind-mages.

C. Cognitive tools

Used by post-Severance survivors.

D. Aetheric tools

Used by Ascendants.

E. Ethereal-interface tools

Used when repairing identity scars or trauma imprints.

Psychic surgery operates on patterns, not matter.

⸻

5. Failure Modes

The psyche can be harmed by bad surgery:
	•	memory perforation
	•	emotional flatlining
	•	identity liquefaction
	•	trauma resurfacing
	•	echo bleed (Severance-style)
	•	partition collapse
	•	false identity overlay
	•	harmonic instability
	•	personality revision drift

Dominion prevents these with axiomatic safeguards.
Concordia prevents them with ritual structure.
Severance Earth… rarely prevents anything.
