# (3500) Pluto-Charon Station

The Pluto colony becomes a permanent dual-body Pluto-Charon station complex in 3500.

It is neutral associated territory under the postwar settlement and the principal staging ground for the first interstellar mission.

## Pluto

Pluto provides:

- volatile extraction and storage;
- buried habitats;
- mission archives and biological reserves;
- long-duration crew training;
- Kuiper navigation and Watchtower coordination.

## Charon

Charon provides:

- low-gravity shipyards;
- ark assembly;
- antimatter-cartridge transfer;
- drive testing;
- departure control;
- sacrificial emergency-ejection corridors.

Pluto-Charon is chosen because it lies beyond dense inner-system traffic, near Watchtower coverage, and close to the Neptunian antimatter supply route.

The Colonial Union and Outer Systems Compact must jointly authorize the *C.U.C. Deliverance* fuel transfer and departure.
