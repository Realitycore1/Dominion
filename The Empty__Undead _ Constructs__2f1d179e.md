(Oblivion’s Womb)

Absolute void beyond light and shadow.

Souls drift here when [[Forgotten]], erased from both Heaven’s ledgers and Hell’s [[Chains]].

Consciousness dissolves into [[Silence]] — neither torment nor peace, only absence.

Aeonic [[Demon]] of Oblivion is thought to slumber here.

Major Dead Zones

The Ashen Lands 

A desolate plain of ash and bone, scattered ruins of dead civilizations.

Souls wander aimlessly as fading shades.

Symbolism: memory of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] without meaning.

The Black River (Apocryphal echo of Styx/Jordan)

A stagnant, tar-like river where souls are ferried only if remembered by the living.

[[Forgotten]] souls drown, remembered ones pass to other [[Realms]].

Guardians are skeletal boatmen who never speak.

The Hollow Halls (Purgatory)

Endless forests of bone-trees and blood-soaked [[Chapter XXII (Earth Primordium)]].

A realm of struggle where souls fight endlessly, purging [[Sin]] through violence.

Demonic scavengers feed here; [[Angels]] rarely intervene.

The Mausoleum of [[Silence]]

A vast necropolis, endless corridors of sealed tombs.

Souls locked in crypts replay their lives until they accept their fate.

Some learn clarity; others go mad and shatter into fragments.

The Twilight Shore

A liminal zone between life and afterlife, where newly dead awaken.

Gray sands, black ocean, and a sky of dying [[Stars]].

Here, psychopomps ([[Angels]], [[Demons]], or [[Reapers]]) come to claim them.
