## Summary
Emerged directly from the collapse of the Tirany triarch [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], when their rigid triadic governance failed.
	•	Early [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]] hybrids looked back to the First Form (the Tirany) and said:
“We fractured because we forgot to honor unity above all.”
	•	The Orthodoxy formed around preserving [[Continuity]] with the Tirany past, reframing it as a sacred covenant: unity must be absolute, [[Divergence]] must return to harmony.

⸻

🕊 [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]
	•	Unity as Sacrament – All differences must reconcile into one Accord.
	•	The First Form was Divine – The Tirany are remembered as proto-saints; their dissolution seen as a lesson, not a failure.
	•	[[The Accord]] is Eternal – Once differences are reconciled under [[The Accord]], they are considered permanent. Breaking it is heresy.
	•	Sacred Triads – The number three remains holy. Councils of three preside over religious and political decisions.

⸻

⛪ Practices & Rituals
	•	Choral Binding – groups chant or resonate together until their vocalizations fall into one harmonic sound, symbolizing unity.
	•	Blood Accords – hybrid offspring are ritually presented to [[The Accord]] Temple, their lineage recorded as proof of reconciliation.
	•	Convergence Festivals – once per cycle, entire populations pause to renew their vows to [[The Accord]], reenacting the Tirany’s “Last Triarchs” who gave up power to their children.

⸻

🛡 Organization
	•	High Triarchate – three [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Government/Leaders|Leaders]] presiding at once, never one alone.
	•	[[Temples]] of Accord – vast cathedral-like stations, often built around inherited Tirany [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Economy & Infrastructure/Megastructures|Megastructures]].
	•	Clerics of Accord – religious judges who settle disputes, always in triads.

⸻

⚔ Role in [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]]
	•	Acts as the stabilizing center of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]] politics.
	•	Often the mediators when other factions clash (e.g. Path of Thousand Forms vs [[Edge of Accord]]).
	•	Provide moral legitimacy to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]]’s expansion, declaring “unity” as not just political, but sacred.
	•	Critics: seen as too conservative, clinging to Tirany nostalgia.

⸻

🜏 Symbolism
	•	The Triple Knot – three interwoven rings, representing distinct forms fused into one Accord.
	•	Colors – deep blue (faith), white (purity), gold (unity).
	•	Sacred Architecture – triadic geometry everywhere: three spires, three halls, threefold gates.

⸻

📖 Fragment, Orthodoxy of Accord Hymn:

“From One came Three.
From Three came Many.
From Many, Unity Eternal.”
