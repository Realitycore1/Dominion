1. Common Goblins
	•	Definition: The baseline—small, wiry humanoids known for mischief, cruelty, or cunning.
	•	Traits: Green or grey skin, sharp features, quick reflexes, scavenger instincts.
	•	Symbolism: [[Humanity]]’s fear of the “other” lurking in shadows and ruins.

⸻

[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]]. Hobgoblins
	•	Definition: Larger, stronger goblins with martial [[culture]].
	•	Traits: Disciplined, organized, militaristic—often leading goblin warbands.
	•	Symbolism: Order from chaos; the dangerous “serious” goblin.

⸻

[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]]. Bugbears
	•	Definition: Hulking, brutish goblin-kin, blending goblin cunning with trollish strength.
	•	Traits: Ambush predators, hairy hides, fearsome presence.
	•	Symbolism: The lurking monster in the dark, primal terror.

⸻

[[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]]. Cave Goblins
	•	Definition: Subterranean goblins adapted to dark tunnels.
	•	Traits: Pale skin, oversized eyes, experts at traps and ambushes.
	•	Symbolism: Survival in tight places, claustrophobic menace.

⸻

5. Forest Goblins
	•	Definition: Goblins dwelling in woods, often tied to spiders or poisonous [[Flora]].
	•	Traits: Camouflage, venom use, symbiotic beasts (spiders, bats, snakes).
	•	Symbolism: The wild turned hostile, the predator hidden in greenery.

⸻

6. River Goblins
	•	Definition: Swamp and river-dwelling goblins.
	•	Traits: Amphibious, slimy skin, expertise in fishing, ambush from reeds.
	•	Symbolism: Corruption, pestilence, dangers of stagnant water.

⸻

7. Ash Goblins
	•	Definition: Goblins born in wastelands or volcanic lands.
	•	Traits: Blackened skin, resistance to [[Fire]], scavengers of ruined worlds.
	•	Symbolism: Survival in deathscapes; cockroaches of fantasy worlds.

⸻

8. Shadow Goblins
	•	Definition: Goblins tied to darkness or [[Fae]]-shadow [[Realms]].
	•	Traits: Can slip into shadows, whispers in the dark, tricksters of fear.
	•	Symbolism: Night terrors, faerie mischief turned cruel.

⸻

9. Tinker Goblins
	•	Definition: Goblins obsessed with machines, alchemy, or unstable inventions.
	•	Traits: Scrap-collectors, explosive [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], reckless geniuses.
	•	Symbolism: Chaos harnessed in tools; invention without foresight.

⸻

10. Noble Goblins
	•	Definition: Rare high-born goblins who rule over tribes with wit and cunning.
	•	Traits: Shrewd [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Government/Royal Branch/Leaders/Leaders|Leaders]], political manipulators, sometimes posing as kings of ruins.
	•	Symbolism: Intelligence hidden in small packages; underestimated rulers.
