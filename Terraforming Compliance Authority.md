# Terraforming Compliance Authority

## Summary

The Terraforming Compliance Authority regulates planetary alteration projects, atmospheric engineering, biome seeding, ocean stabilization, climate control, and long-cycle environmental transformation.

It exists because terraforming can become conquest by chemistry.

## Mandate

The Authority oversees:

- terraforming permits;
- climate-engineering safety;
- atmospheric conversion;
- biome introduction;
- ocean and hydrological alteration;
- indigenous biosphere protection;
- settlement-phase environmental limits;
- long-cycle monitoring.

## Powers

It may suspend terraforming activity, require impact review, mandate reversal measures, and certify completion stages.

## Limits

It cannot approve irreversible transformation without public record, ignore local compact rights, or declare a living biosphere worthless because it is inconvenient.

## Public Face

Citizens know the Authority through climate notices, settlement restrictions, terraforming jobs, protests, and the slow change of sky color over generations.

## Story Use

Use this agency to explore whether survival requires remaking worlds, and what moral debt follows when humanity decides a planet must become easier to live on.
