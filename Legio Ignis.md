## Summary
LEGIO IGNIS — The Worldfire Hosts

Classification: Thermal Warfare • Planetary Purge Operations • Atmospheric Devastation • Stellar Assault
Status: Active (Heavily Restricted Deployment Authority)

⸻

Profile

Where Bellator conquered through endurance,
Mortis survived catastrophe,
and Aurelis broke fortresses,
Legio Ignis existed to reduce resistance to ash.

They were the Dominion’s most feared large-scale destruction legion:

* thermal annihilation specialists
* atmosphere-burn strategists
* stellar assault operators
* planetary purification forces
* anti-biological extermination hosts

Ignis campaigns were rarely subtle.

Entire worlds announced their arrival through:

* burning skies
* artificial dawns
* atmospheric ignition
* oceans boiling along coastlines
* orbital firestorms visible from space

To the Dominion,
Ignis represented:

controlled devastation.

To enemies,
they represented apocalypse.

⸻

Origins

Legio Ignis formed during the Pyre Expansion Wars, when several hostile civilizations weaponized:

* biological superorganisms
* planetary jungles
* regenerative ecosystems
* subterranean hive continents
* adaptive biospheres

Conventional warfare failed repeatedly.

Destroyed regions regrew.
Hive organisms reconstituted themselves.
Entire continents behaved like living immune systems.

The Dominion concluded:

some environments cannot be conquered.
they must be sterilized.

Ignis was created to wage:

ecological annihilation warfare.

Over time their role expanded beyond anti-biological combat into:

* planetary cleansing
* orbital thermal operations
* atmosphere denial campaigns
* anti-fleet stellar engagements

⸻

Doctrine

Core Principles

1. Purification Through Fire

Ignis doctrine viewed fire not merely as destruction —
but transformation.

Flame:

* sterilizes
* reshapes
* denies recovery
* consumes contamination
* resets hostile environments

Their operational philosophy became:

ashes cannot rebel.

This phrase became controversial even inside the Dominion.

⸻

2. Escalation Dominance

Ignis specialized in overwhelming thermal superiority.

If an enemy adapted:
Ignis escalated.

If resistance hardened:
Ignis intensified.

They believed hesitation prolonged suffering.

Better:

* brutal certainty
    than
* endless attritional collapse.

⸻

3. Atmospheric Control

Ignis warfare heavily emphasized planetary atmosphere manipulation.

They weaponized:

* oxygen density
* pressure systems
* thermal layering
* ignition chains
* orbital mirror systems
* aerosol accelerants

Entire skies became weapons.

⸻

4. Psychological Catastrophe

Ignis understood:
fire terrifies organisms instinctively.

Thus their operations intentionally cultivated:

* overwhelming visual destruction
* heat pressure
* environmental panic
* sensory overload

Enemy morale often collapsed before ground engagement began.

⸻

Tactics

Skyburn Operations

Ignis fleets deployed atmospheric ignition bombardments capable of transforming cloud layers into expanding thermal fronts.

These operations:

* blinded defenses
* disrupted sensors
* consumed air support
* collapsed infrastructure

Worlds under Skyburn resembled living furnaces from orbit.

⸻

The Ember Advance

Unlike Bellator’s trench tides, Ignis offensives moved through:

* advancing fire walls
* mobile cremation armor
* thermal bombardment corridors

Their infantry followed devastation directly.

This made them terrifying urban warfare forces.

⸻

Sunspears

Massive orbital thermal lances designed for:

* fortress liquefaction
* glacier annihilation
* shield overload
* anti-orbital defense strikes

Entire mountain ranges reportedly became molten during prolonged Sunspear campaigns.

⸻

Ash Zones

Ignis established containment regions where:

* oxygen content altered
* atmosphere poisoned with thermal particulates
* temperatures artificially elevated
* ecological recovery denied

These zones sometimes remained lifeless for centuries.

⸻

Armor & Appearance

Ignis armor resembled:

* volcanic plating
* furnace shells
* walking reactors

Common features:

* blackened heat-resistant plate
* glowing thermal seams
* reinforced respirator systems
* exhaust venting towers
* ember-red optics
* insulated trench cloaks

Unlike elegant Dominion gold,
Ignis armor looked:

* scorched
* industrial
* brutal

Their symbol:

a burning solar disc split by a descending black spear.

⸻

Weapons

Ignis specialized in:

* plasma projection systems
* thermal accelerators
* incendiary saturation weapons
* fusion flamers
* atmospheric ignition charges
* directed stellar mirrors
* magma-breach warheads

Their close-combat weapons often incorporated:

* thermal edges
* reactor-fed blades
* combustion emitters

Many Ignis weapons continued burning after impact.

⸻

Culture

Ignis culture developed around:

* transformation
* intensity
* controlled fury
* purification

Outsiders often misunderstood them as mindless destroyers.

This was inaccurate.

Ignis officers were frequently:

* highly disciplined
* mathematically precise
* environmentally analytical

Because uncontrolled fire is wasteful.

Ignis believed:

proper destruction requires restraint.

This paradox became central to their identity.

⸻

Traditions

The Ember Rite

Before campaigns, legionaries marked armor with ash from previous battlefields.

Each layer represented:

* survival
* continuity
* remembered destruction

Veteran armor eventually became almost completely gray-black beneath accumulated ash.

⸻

The Furnace Vigil

Prior to major offensives, Ignis hosts gathered around massive thermal reactors in complete silence.

Heat levels intentionally rose to painful thresholds.

The ritual reinforced:

* stress tolerance
* thermal acclimation
* collective focus

⸻

The Last Flame

When an Ignis legionary died, a portion of battlefield fire was preserved within sealed thermal reliquaries.

These flames were archived within:

Pyre Vaults.

⸻

Verarch

Founding Verarch [[Pyraxis Dominor]]

Pyraxis Dominor established Ignis as the legion of controlled fire, reactor denial, and thermal cleansing. Later field chronicles preserve Solkan Pyre as:

Perhaps the most feared Ignis commander in Dominion history.

Descriptions portray him as:

* immense
* calm
* unnervingly controlled
* emotionally volcanic beneath restraint

Unlike rage-driven warlords, Solkan Pyre viewed destruction almost scientifically.

He reportedly studied:

* fire ecology
* atmospheric mathematics
* thermal psychology
* collapse propagation systems

His most infamous statement:

“Fire is honest.
It destroys without pretending otherwise.”

Pyre strongly believed prolonged wars created more suffering than overwhelming devastation.

Critics argued this philosophy justified atrocity.
Supporters argued it ended extinction wars faster.

The debate never resolved.

⸻

Relationship With Other Legions

Bellator respected Ignis operational reliability.
Mortis often despised their aftermath.
Aurelis viewed them as tactically excessive.
Umbra considered them strategically dangerous.

Ignis frequently clashed politically with:

* ecological restoration councils
* civilian continuity agencies
* preservationist factions

Because many Ignis victories permanently damaged worlds.

⸻

Relationship With The Dominion

The Dominion treated Ignis carefully.

They were too effective to remove.
Too catastrophic to deploy casually.

Thus Ignis deployments often required:

* Continuity Council authorization
* multi-legion oversight
* ecological risk projection
* long-term reconstruction planning

Despite this,
some commanders still invoked Ignis prematurely during desperate wars.

History judged several of those decisions harshly.

⸻

The Severance

Ignis campaigns during the Severance became almost mythological in scale.

They fought:

* reality-consuming biospheres
* unstable stars
* collapsing atmospheric systems
* unreality infestations
* world-eating growths

Some operations reportedly ignited entire fragmented realities simply to prevent contamination spread.

Post-Severance records describe:

* eternal firestorms
* burning dead worlds
* drifting ember fleets
* isolated Ignis hosts continuing sterilization campaigns long after command collapse

Some may still operate autonomously.

⸻

Legacy

Across the Dominion, one warning became associated with Legio Ignis:

“When the sky burns,
surrender immediately.”

Ignis became symbols of the Dominion’s willingness to choose catastrophic survival over extinction.

Not because they loved destruction.

Because they believed some threats could not be reasoned with,
contained,
or endured.

Only burned away.

Legio Ignis embodies one of the Dominion’s harshest philosophical questions:

At what point does preservation become indistinguishable from annihilation?
