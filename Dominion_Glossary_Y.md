# Dominion Omniverse: Glossary (Y)
YAML — “Yet Another Markup Language.” Data serialization format used within [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] archives and [[Aetheric]] computation to maintain [[Human]]-legible configuration of machine, [[Ritual]], or hybrid [[Systems]]. Serves as a lingua franca between synthetic and organic codices.
Yahweh — Proper name for the singular deity of the Abrahamic tradition. Within Aravothian theology, regarded as a Supreme Arch-Aspect of Creation and [[law]]; interpretations vary between pure monotheism and manifestation of the Logos field.
[[Yauturn Sovereignty]] — Cold-[[Systems]] technate composed of [[Warrior]]-clan houses unified under a civil charter. Population ~80–120 million; specializes in cryogenic [[Infrastructure]], long-duration logistics, and metallurgy for outer-rim colonization.
Year — Standard orbital period of a reference world. In [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Gnostic Expanse/Demiurgos/The Archontes/Dominion|Dominion]] chronology, “Year” defaults to the Sol-based standard (365 days = 1 Cycle) unless otherwise specified by local planetary constant.
Years — Plural of Year; used for extended chronological measures and epochal accounting (e.g., “12 000 Years H.C.”).
Yeenoghu — Mythic or extra-versal [[Demon]]-lord archetype of [[Hunger]] and predation. In Concordian bestiaries, classified as an Apex Malic Entity whose resonance drives gnollic or feral corruption.
Yeti — Cryptid or upland hominid adapted to high-altitude cold zones; in Concordia and Telluric records, serves as a template for bio-adaptive endurance morphs.
[[Yondor]] — Realm of [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and entropy within the Concordian cosmology. A cold necro-technological empire where souls and machines coexist in decay-preserving equilibrium; ruled by necro-archons who manage the flow between [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]] and unlife.
[[Yondor Necrotics]] — Hybridized [[Undead]] constructs combining organic remains with necro-mechanical grafts. Function as labor, soldiery, or conduits of the Abyssal Current; capable of limited autonomy through soul-link networks.
Yondor Tech — Collective term for necro-industrial [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Military/Technology|Technology]] derived from [[Yondor]] research—bone-mechanisms, vitae reactors, ectoplasmic processors—bridging biological decay and machinic persistence.
Your — Second-person possessive pronoun; in [[Ritual]] speech, sometimes invokes immediate relational binding between speaker and addressed.
Yours — Possessive pronoun denoting belonging; in liturgical contracts, marks final consent clause.
Ysirael — Legendary seraphic or nephilic figure regarded as the First [[Witness]] of Light. Said to have recorded the early laws of [[Aravoth (Grace)]] before [[The Sundering of Grace]]; invoked by theologians as patron of revelation and mercy.
