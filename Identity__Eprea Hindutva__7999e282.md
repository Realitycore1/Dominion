The Frozen Hand of Dharma
•	Self-Perception
	•	[[Eprea Hindutva]] believes itself the truest inheritor of cosmic order, a society where every being is slotted into their destined place.
	•	Their ideology frames expansion into the [[Stars]] as proof that caste is eternal: “Even in [[The Void]], Dharma abides.”
	•	Citizens are taught that without hierarchy, chaos and impurity would consume the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
	•	View of Other [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]]
	•	They see oath-driven or guild-based [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] (like [[Austro Guard]] or [[Proxima Commonwealth]]) as dangerously naive, letting “the impure” share in power.
	•	[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]] with looser structures are viewed with outright contempt, described in propaganda as “degenerate, rootless, and doomed.”
	•	Their relationship to the [[Colonial Union]] is transactional—they provide [[Soldiers]] and ships, but see themselves as the only stable pillar among fractious peers.
	•	Collective Ethos
	•	Stability is prized above all, even more than prosperity.
	•	Purity is elevated as both spiritual and biological [[law]]; intermixing of castes or [[Cultures]] is portrayed as a cosmic blasphemy.
	•	Sacrifice by lower castes is sanctified as the highest service to the order.
	•	Symbols of Self
	•	The Pyramid: ubiquitous in architecture, logos, and banners—representing eternal stratification.
	•	The Flame of Purity: a stylized [[Fire]] that burns upward, symbolizing suffering at the base feeding brilliance at the top.
	•	Iron Sigils: caste marks branded onto warriors and [[Workers]] alike, making the body itself a living emblem of hierarchy.
	•	Foreign Reputation
	•	To outsiders, [[Eprea Hindutva]] is feared and reviled: efficient, disciplined, but cruel beyond measure.
	•	Some admire its stability compared to volatile [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]], but even allies whisper of its brutality.
	•	Rebels and exiles describe it as a “machine that eats its children and calls it devotion.”

[[Eprea Hindutva]]’s identity is that of the eternal hierarchy—a civilization that claims to have tamed chaos by freezing destiny, even if it means grinding countless lives into fuel.
