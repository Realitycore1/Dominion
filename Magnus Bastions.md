Defensive [[Systems]]

Layered Whipple-Shield Swarms: Intercept kinetics and beams.

Solar Lances: Planetary-to-orbit energy cannons, drawing power from Geo-Taps or Kugelblitz cores.

Relativistic Rail Mass Drivers: Hurl projectiles at fractions of light-speed.

Concordia Choir Arrays: Harmonic weapons that destabilize enemy fleets by disrupting their resonance.

Support [[Systems]]

Autonomous MRO Ecologies: Endless drone swarms repairing walls and spires.

[[Cornucopia Machine]] Forges: Self-replenishing armories.

[[Computronium]] Nodes: Embedded command cores running tactical simulations.

Economic: Protect trade arteries, planetary cores, and megastructure hubs.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Sheer black walls ribbed with cathedral spires, mountain-buttresses rising into clouds.

Suprematist Monolithism: From orbit, they look like obsidian wedges carved into the land or void.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Glyphs etched into their surfaces glow during battle — the fortress itself becomes scripture.

Standing before one, you feel like an insect at the foot of a mountain that watches you back.

Civil: To citizens, Magnus Bastions are sanctuaries — the safest places on any world.

Religious: Known as “The Stone Thrones” — monuments where the Empress herself is said to rest her hand upon the world.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Defensive fields ripple in fractal geometries, as though the air itself is armor.

To citizens: Bastions are safety — if all else fails, the Magnus still stands.

To rebels: Symbols of futility — how can one topple a fortress that is also a mountain?
