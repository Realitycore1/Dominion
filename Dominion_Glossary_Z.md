# Dominion Omniverse: Glossary (Z)

Zealot Hosts — Congregational war-formations bound by oath and liturgy; troops whose morale, [[Pain]] response, and coordination are amplified by shared rite ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Galactic Federation/Science/Aether|Aether]]-backed hymns, sigils, or relic fields). Highly coherent soulflux, tactically relentless, brittle under creed fracture.
Zealotry — Doctrinal fervor that converts belief into sustained action; produces high but narrow-band resonance. Increases resolve and miracle frequency while raising risks of bias lock, heresy purges, and Axiom Imposition against outsiders.
Zero — The null [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Attea Matil/Culture & Society/Identity|Identity]] in arithmetic; in fieldwork, shorthand for baseline/no-signal state. In axiomics, a local marker for Nullity (unexpressed value) and the reference floor for measurement.
Zohair — Dynastic surname recurring in primary archives; associated with high-clearance operators, patrons, and founders across multiple eras. See dossiers for individuals (e.g., [[Armand Zohair]], Amber Zohair) for role-specific detail.
[[Zombies]] — Reanimated corpses or shell-bodies animated by Vitae, Aethertech, pathogen, or imprint. Low-agency, task-limited [[Entities]]; variants include hive-directed thralls, necromantic servitors, and psionic echoes. Standard countermeasures: decapitation, [[Fire]], severing animus tether.
Zone — Bounded region defined by coherent field conditions (physical, [[Aetheric]], or axiomatic). Examples: quarantine zone, APZ (Axiomatic Permeability Zone), storm cell, sanctum.
Zones — Plural of Zone; mosaic of adjacent [[Regions]] with distinct rule-sets or field behaviors.
