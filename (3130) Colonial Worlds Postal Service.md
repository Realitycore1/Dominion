# (3130) Colonial Union Postal Service

The Colonial Union Postal Service is established in 3130 to provide authenticated communication, physical delivery, and civic continuity among settlements whose routes are too irregular for ordinary planetary administration.

*Colonial Worlds Postal Service* remains its common frontier name and appears in many early records.

The service carries:

- encrypted message bundles and delayed family recordings;
- medicine and small emergency parts;
- court orders, claim filings, and inspection records;
- ballots and verified election returns;
- civil records, custody documents, and inheritance notices;
- physical evidence whose chain of custody cannot be trusted to a commercial carrier.

Laser and radio relays move data at light speed when geometry and infrastructure permit. Postal vessels carry authenticated archives, physical cargo, and messages for settlements outside reliable beam contact.

In the Belt, a postal delivery can prove that a mobile habitat remains occupied, that a rescue request was received, or that a company terminated residents before dissolving.

The service is publicly chartered but relies on commercial routes. This creates a permanent conflict between civic priority and profitable cargo.

During the First Solar War, postal depots and vessels become evacuation corridors, intelligence sources, and targeting references. The service's neutrality survives as law more often than as practice.
