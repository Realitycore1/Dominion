Classification: Aquatic Predators & Sea Spirits
Subtype: Malice-Tainted Marine Humanoids / Reef-Hunters
Status: Active, Regionally Endemic

⸻

Core Description

Adaro are humanoid aquatic entities with sleek, muscular builds, elongated limbs, webbed extremities, and faces that combine human familiarity with piscine menace. Their skin ranges from deep blue-black to coral-gray, often patterned like reef stone or shark hide. Eyes glow faintly in low light, adapted for twilight waters.

Unlike deep-sea sovereigns, Adaro are littoral predators—they hunt where land and sea meet.

They are not monstrous gods.
They are coastal nightmares.

⸻

Ontological Nature

Adaro originate where marine life, predation, and malice-tainted hunger converge.
	•	Primary Plane Affinity: Vital Expanse (predatory life)
	•	Secondary Influence: Malice-adjacent resonance
	•	Realm Interface: Shadow Realms (coastal overlaps), Bleeding Wilds (aquatic zones)

They are not elemental water beings.
They are biological predators warped by spiritual corruption.

Adaro often arise after:
	•	mass drownings,
	•	ritual blood offerings to the sea,
	•	failed Marid pacts,
	•	prolonged exposure to cursed coastal waters.

⸻

Intelligence & Will

Adaro possess low-to-mid sapience.

They understand:
	•	tools,
	•	ambush tactics,
	•	coastal geography,
	•	fear responses.

They do not possess:
	•	long-term planning,
	•	stable societies beyond loose shoals,
	•	abstract ideology.

Adaro think like hunters who remember being people.

⸻

Behavior & Ecology

Adaro operate in:
	•	reefs,
	•	mangrove swamps,
	•	ship graveyards,
	•	drowned cities,
	•	shallow island chains.

They hunt:
	•	swimmers,
	•	fishermen,
	•	coastal travelers,
	•	isolated villages.

They are particularly drawn to:
	•	blood in water,
	•	fear responses,
	•	ritual sites near shorelines.

Adaro often keep:
	•	bone charms,
	•	drowned relics,
	•	stolen tools.

They rarely leave the sea for long—but can raid shorelines briefly.

⸻

Transmission & Creation

Adaro are not a species in the conventional sense.

They are created through:
	•	transformation of drowned victims,
	•	curse contamination,
	•	deliberate rituals invoking predatory sea spirits.

Bites alone do not convert victims—fear and surrender are required components.

This keeps Adaro outbreaks localized rather than pandemic.

⸻

Weaknesses & Constraints
	•	Severely weakened away from saltwater
	•	Vulnerable to fire, desiccation, and Grace-saturated zones
	•	Lose cohesion in Null-dominant waters
	•	Disrupted by harmonic wards tied to tides and lunar cycles

Freshwater rapidly destabilizes them.

⸻

Relationship to Other Entities
	•	Marid: Often despised or enslaved by Marid
	•	Leviathans: Avoid unless driven into frenzy
	•	Undines: Hostile; Undines represent life-flow, Adaro represent predation
	•	Sirens: Occasional allies or rivals
	•	Coastal Kami: Actively oppose Adaro incursions

Adaro are not rulers of the sea—they are its knives.

⸻

Variants
	•	Reef Adaro: Camouflaged, patient ambushers
	•	Deep Adaro: Larger, more feral, short-lived
	•	Ashwater Adaro: Post-collapse, polluted coastlines
	•	Storm Adaro: Appear during cyclones and squalls

Storm Adaro are the most aggressive and hardest to repel.
