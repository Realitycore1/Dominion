Name & Etymology

The Ephyrian Spiral (Galaxy E-7-Δ)

“Ephyrian” from epíphyra (Greek: “foam, fringe”), evoking the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]]’s philosophy of unity emerging from countless fragments.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] observers catalogue it as E-7-Δ, part of a filament node along the outer [[VFF]] lattice.

Morphology

Type: Grand-design barred spiral galaxy.

Diameter: ~220,000 light-years (slightly larger than the Milky Way).

Stellar Count: ~1.8 trillion [[Stars]], with a high proportion of early F-type and Population II [[Stars]].

Cosmological Location

Positioned [[Chapter (The Old War)|Chapter (The Old War)]].[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]] billion light-years from [[Earth (Terra)]], within the same filament cluster as the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]’s anchor galaxy.

[[Close]] enough to the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] to eventually become ensnared in its historical trajectory, yet far enough to foster independent development.

Symbolic/Cultural Role

The [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]] viewed their galaxy as a living symphony, with each [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/Structure/Science/Species/Species]], star, and world being one “note” in a grand cosmic composition.

Their factions drew meaning from its structure:

[[Orthodoxy of Accord]] → the central bar, seen as the “spine of order.”

Silent Chorus → faint halo of dim red [[Stars]], “ghost voices of the past.”

[[Path of the Thousand Forms]] → [[Wide]] peripheral clusters, symbolizing change and [[Divergence]].

Distinct Features

Halo Anomalies: Thick outer halo enriched with exotic [[Particles]] (some akin to [[Axion]] condensates), fueling both spiritual myths and practical megastructure design.

Central SMBH: Named Aevon’s Heart, radiating unusually stable relativistic jets, harnessed in myth as the “pulse of [[The Accord]].”

Satellite [[Systems]]: At least 37 dwarf galaxies orbit, many absorbed into the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]]’s cultural/territorial fabric.

Historical Importance

The Ephyrian Spiral gave rise to the Tirany, who seeded much of the genetic and cultural substrate later absorbed by the [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Synarchate/Synarchate]].

When [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] forces made contact, the [[Edge of Accord]] faction betrayed the galaxy’s coordinates, leading to the collapse of its autonomy. Survivors were relocated to their progenitor homeworld, locked away, remembered only as an “inconvenience.”
