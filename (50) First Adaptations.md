# (50) First Adaptations

## Manuscript Source

- Primary source: [[Manuscript]] Draft 2, Part III, Chapters 18-20.
- Manuscript date range: Years 41-93 After Landfall.

## What Happened

The first adaptations were cultural before they were biological. Children learned storm maps before Earth geography. Holidays drifted. Songs changed tempo to match local weather. Ark hymns gained Harminan cadence. Regional slang, route marks, cloak styles, food habits, and school arguments became visible signs of identity.

Amber experienced this as both hope and loss. Earth and Ferox were not being formally rejected. They were being reduced into story, lesson, symbol, and warning.

## What Resulted

- Language drift and holiday drift became normal.
- Harminan-born children treated the biosphere as home rather than threat.
- GalFed knowledge schools began turning hidden strategic fear into curriculum.
- Religious practice, including Celestialist mass, adapted to frontier materials and local grief.

## Consequences

These adaptations seeded the later Dominion tendency to absorb rather than merely preserve: old culture survives by being rebuilt for new conditions.
