a weapon created in noetic/meta-space and launched to strike other verses, metaphysical [[Entities]], or the anchors that bind [[Realms]] together. Not simply a bomb — it’s an axiomatic payload: a concentrated packet of resonance, formal [[Axioms]], and destructive narrative that can unwrite rules, collapse metaphysical anchors, or sear the mind of an entity.

How it could work (in-setting mechanics)

Think of two [[Layers]]: the carrier medium (how it travels) and the payload (what it does on impact).
	1.	Carrier / Launching medium
	•	Fired from an oracle choir or a sanctified [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Domain/Science/Aether|Aether]] lattice (a tuned node in “meta-space”).
	•	The launch requires a bridge — a tesseract-fold or [[Verse]]-gate that temporarily couples the source [[Verse]] to the target.
	•	Travel is through noetic topology rather than Euclidean space: the missile follows similarity / doctrinal adjacency, not distance.
	•	Travel may be instantaneous (if the bridge holds) or take metaphorical [[Time]] (narrative tension).
	[[Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)|Chapter XXIX (Verse Concursus)]].	Payload types
	•	Axiomatic Disruptor: corrupts local metaphysical [[Axioms]] (e.g., flips right/left-handedness, inverts sanctity fields).
	•	Resonance Destructor: collapses resonant harmonics — removes the “glue” that holds certain [[Entities]] together.
	•	Scripted Obliterator (Final Script): a [[Ritual]]-coded sequence that enforces an ending condition (sterilize, erase, rewrite).
	•	Meme-virus: narrative payload that infects minds, causing [[Cultures]] to self-collapse.
	[[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]].	Firing [[Ritual]] / cost
	•	Requires oracular chorus, rare fuel (Aetherbone, Fornax Cores), or sacrifices (energy, souls, archival memory).
	•	Some missiles require a Final Script [[Key]]—a moral or liturgical act to bind the payload so it won’t return and infect the sender.

Practical & metaphysical effects (what it feels like)
	•	Impact visuals: a tear in reality, a choir of palimpsest-sound, [[Axioms]] burning like ink.
	•	Side effects: [[Verse]]-echoes (reverberations back to sender), sanity loss for nearby oracles, permanent censorship of certain metaphysical operations.
	•	Collateral: can blind or sterilize neutral territories, collapse local metaphysical maps, or spawn aberrant ghosts.

Risks & ethical calculus
	•	Feedback loops: firing across verses risks backlash; the missile could return inverted, turning on the sender.
	•	Moral cost: using a missile that kills metaphysical beings or rewrites worlds marks the user as a potential Collapse initiator.
	•	Non-recallable scripts: a missile with Final Script may be unstoppable once launched — plausible trigger for horror/plot.
	•	Political fallout: deploying one is tantamount to declaring metaphysical genocide; justification, secrecy, or desperation required.

Defenses & counters
	•	Anchor Shields: oracle-built lattices that “catch” or refract the carrier (like prism armor).
	•	Axiom Locks: local [[Axioms]] can be hardened to resist rewriting ([[Ritual]] fortifications).
	•	Noetic Firewalls (Retribution Nerve Lace analogs): fields that collapse the missile’s resonance envelope.
	•	Counter-script: an immune Final Script that neutralizes the payload — but these risk creating dialectic [[Arms]] races.
	•	Oracle sacrifice / choir dissonance: intentionally creating noise to confuse the missile’s tuning.
