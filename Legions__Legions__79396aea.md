## Consolidated Lock - Legion Relics

Source: [[Primus Legion Relic Status]]

Legion relics begin as field objects before they become sacred or ceremonial artifacts.

The standard lifecycle is:

1. field object;
2. witnessed object;
3. legion token;
4. ceremonial relic;
5. continuity relic.

The earliest relics are not created by priests, propagandists, or museum curators. They are created by survival. A legion keeps a damaged object because it proves what happened, who paid for it, and what must not be repeated.

Later, these relics become:

- oath objects;
- succession symbols;
- memorial anchors;
- proof of legion legitimacy;
- post-Severance continuity evidence;
- and dangerous political claims.

Custody principle:

> A legion may carry a relic. It does not own what the relic remembers.
