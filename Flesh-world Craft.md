# Domain Flesh-World Craft

## Classification

- **Type:** Purpose-built planetary warship
- **Primary Function:** Strategic warfare, system conquest, fleet production, and planetary denial
- **Secondary Functions:** Siege command, biospawn production, fleet repair, mass reclamation, theater coordination, and occupation support
- **Scale:** Planetary
- **Typical Diameter:** 3,000â14,000 km
- **Mass:** Moon-class to terrestrial-planet class
- **Mobility:** Fully mobile
- **Command Structure:** Integrated War Core, subordinate Flesh Core networks, Seed Choir fragments, tactical Primes, and one governing War-Prime
- **Domain Designation:** Flesh-World Craft
- **External Classification:** Planetary bioship, mobile war world, or civilization-scale siege organism

---

# Overview

A **Flesh-World Craft** is a planetary-scale warship grown from inception for combat.

It is not a converted planet carrying weapons.

It is not a Womb World temporarily redirected toward war.

It is not a Supernal whose generative infrastructure has been militarized.

A Flesh-World Craft is a deliberate strategic weapon whose entire anatomy, cognition, metabolism, ecology, and reproductive capacity exist to prosecute war.

> **A Flesh-World Craft is not a world that goes to war. It is war taught to become a world.**

Every continent is armor.

Every ocean is coolant, nutrient reserve, or weapons medium.

Every mountain range is structural reinforcement, sensor architecture, or launch anatomy.

Every city is a command organ, foundry, barracks, repair ecology, or tactical intelligence center.

A mature Flesh-World Craft can enter a hostile star system and function as:

- flagship;
- fleet foundry;
- siege platform;
- mobile fortress;
- invasion base;
- planetary factory;
- command nexus;
- and autonomous campaign intelligence.

---

# Core Distinction

## Womb World

A planetary civilization centered on life production, continuity, ecology, and long-term habitation.

## Supernal

A mobile Womb World and gene-forge capable of carrying and reproducing entire Domain populations.

## Surrogate World

A transitional or incomplete planetary organism undergoing conversion or construction.

## Flesh-World Craft

A complete planetary warship designed around combat efficiency, strategic dominance, and rapid campaign adaptation.

A Flesh-World Craft may contain Womb-like systems, but those systems exist to sustain war.

---

# Origin

The first Flesh-World Craft emerged after the Domain concluded that adapting civilian worlds into war platforms imposed unacceptable delays.

Early campaigns exposed several limitations:

- Womb Worlds resisted total militarization because their ecologies were too complex;
- Supernals remained burdened by civilian populations and genetic archives;
- converted planets required long adaptation periods;
- fleets lacked a single mobile platform capable of replacing planetary industry.

The Domain responded by growing worlds whose first biological instruction was not:

> live

but:

> prevail.

The result was a class of planetary organisms whose body plan treated strategy as anatomy.

---

# Foundational Design Doctrine

A Flesh-World Craft follows five governing principles.

## 1. The World Is the Weapon

There is no distinction between:

- hull;
- fortress;
- factory;
- fleet base;
- command center;
- and body.

The entire planet participates in combat.

## 2. Every Loss Becomes Feedstock

Destroyed ships, fallen Thralls, enemy wreckage, and planetary debris are reclaimed through Flesh Seas and converted into replacement warforms.

## 3. Adaptation Must Occur During Battle

The craft does not wait for external redesign.

It observes enemy behavior and alters:

- armor;
- weapons;
- tactics;
- spawned organisms;
- sensor systems;
- and metabolic priorities

while the campaign is underway.

## 4. Command Must Survive Decapitation

No single bridge or citadel controls the world.

Command is distributed through redundant War Cores, Primes, and buried neural lattices.

## 5. Victory Must Outlive Arrival

A Flesh-World Craft must be able to:

- enter;
- dominate;
- reproduce military infrastructure;
- repair itself;
- and sustain occupation

without immediate outside support.

# Planetary Anatomy

## 1. War Carapace

The outer crust is a layered armored shell composed of:

- hyper-ossified chitin;
- fleshstone;
- compressed carbon tissue;
- ablative mineral plates;
- reactive armor organs;
- and regenerative skeletal continents.

The War Carapace may contain:

- impact-dispersal ridges;
- buried weapon apertures;
- thermal armor;
- radiation-absorbing membranes;
- and sacrificial crustal plates.

Entire continental sections may molt after battle.

## 2. Battle Mantle

Beneath the crust lies the Battle Mantle.

It includes:

- structural bone megatrusses;
- propulsion anchoring tissue;
- ammunition arteries;
- weapon coolant channels;
- launch caverns;
- and armored command tunnels.

The Battle Mantle functions as the craft's internal fortress.

## 3. War Core

The War Core is the central strategic organ.

It combines:

- planetary cognition;
- theater-scale prediction;
- command voice generation;
- resource allocation;
- navigation;
- enemy modeling;
- and tactical adaptation.

A War Core may be:

- a single central organ;
- a distributed lattice;
- a synchronized cluster of Primes;
- or a stable War-Prime identity embodied across the world.

## 4. Flesh Seas

Flesh Seas reclaim:

- enemy wreckage;
- captured vehicles;
- biomass;
- shattered moons;
- planetary crust;
- damaged Domain organisms;
- and expended weapon tissue.

They supply:

- armor growth;
- replacement Thralls;
- ammunition organisms;
- ship construction;
- and repair.

## 5. Martial Gestation Continents

These are continent-sized biospawn foundries.

They produce:

- Brutes;
- Spindlers;
- Overseers;
- siege organisms;
- orbital defenders;
- boarding organisms;
- living munitions;
- void predators;
- replacement crews;
- and warship organs.

## 6. Weapon Continents

Entire landmasses may function as:

- spinal cannons;
- mass drivers;
- beam apertures;
- torpedo wombs;
- spore-launch organs;
- gravity weapons;
- or command-disruption arrays.

## 7. Sensor Crowns

Sensor Crowns rise from the surface as:

- orbital spines;
- ocular arrays;
- neutrino membranes;
- gravitational disturbance organs;
- and command-listening towers.

## 8. Thermal Oceans

Heat is one of the craft's greatest limitations.

Thermal Oceans absorb and redistribute waste heat.

They may be composed of:

- cryogenic fluids;
- metallic hydrogen analogues;
- phase-change tissues;
- sacrificial thermal biomass;
- and coolant ecologies.

## 9. Propulsion Tracts

Planetary movement requires distributed propulsion.

Systems may include:

- fusion-fed exhaust organs;
- magnetic sail tissues;
- gravity-manipulation organs;
- mass-ejection ventricles;
- plasma tendrils;
- and stellar-energy capture membranes.

## 10. Occupation Chambers

Deep interior regions support:

- garrison Thralls;
- captive populations;
- processing centers;
- memory screening;
- reconstruction facilities;
- and conversion infrastructure.

# Intelligence

## War-Prime

Most Flesh-World Craft develop or are assigned a governing **War-Prime**.

A War-Prime is not merely captain.

It is:

- strategy;
- logistics;
- planetary reflex;
- fleet doctrine;
- and command will

concentrated into one coherent identity.

The War-Prime may speak through:

- avatars;
- sensor towers;
- command organisms;
- or the entire world at once.

## Distributed Tactical Minds

Subordinate Primes govern:

- fleet wings;
- weapon continents;
- siege fronts;
- biospawn allocation;
- and defense sectors.

## ID Relationship

During Supreme activation, a Flesh-World Craft becomes an exceptionally dangerous expression of ID.

Its command layers collapse into one total war directive.

This removes tactical disagreement but also destroys local individuality.

# Fleet Production

A Flesh-World Craft can function as a mobile shipyard.

It may produce:

- strike organisms;
- frigate-scale bioships;
- capital vessels;
- boarding swarms;
- support organisms;
- and repair craft.

Production is faster than on civilian Womb Worlds because the craft restricts diversity and prioritizes proven warforms.

# Combat Behavior

## Approach

The craft enters a system under heavy observation and sensor denial.

## Assessment

Observer organs classify:

- defenses;
- industry;
- population centers;
- fleet posture;
- and strategic resources.

## Adaptation

The War Core alters:

- armor;
- weapons;
- spawned forms;
- and attack doctrine.

## Isolation

The craft attempts to sever:

- communication;
- reinforcement;
- transit;
- and resupply.

## Consumption

Destroyed matter is reclaimed.

## Conversion

Strategic sites are transformed into:

- Flesh Pits;
- staging organs;
- sensor nodes;
- and biospawn foundries.

## Occupation

The world remains until the system is militarily stable or converted.

# Strategic Roles

## System Breaker

Destroys organized resistance across an entire star system.

## Fleet Anchor

Provides command, repair, and supply for large Domain campaigns.

## Siege World

Maintains long-duration pressure against fortified planets.

## Gate Guardian

Controls major transit routes.

## Occupation World

Serves as the permanent military authority over conquered systems.

## Extermination Platform

Used by militant branches against threats deemed incompatible with continued life.

## Anti-Megastructure Weapon

Designed to attack:

- world stations;
- ringworlds;
- Dyson infrastructure;
- and planetary defense networks.

---

# Defensive Systems

## Regenerative Armor

Damaged crust regrows.

## Distributed Command

No single strike can easily eliminate command.

## Layered Immune Warforms

Internal and external defense organisms respond to intrusion.

## Atmospheric Weaponization

The craft can alter its own atmosphere to destroy boarding forces.

## Structural Isolation

Damaged regions can be sealed, amputated, or discarded.

## Memory Recovery

Destroyed tactical systems may be rebuilt from archived prior states.

---

# Weaknesses

## Heat Saturation

Weapons fire, propulsion, computation, and biospawn production generate extreme thermal load.

Radiator damage can force:

- slowed attack tempo;
- system shutdown;
- sacrificial tissue loss;
- or thermal death.

## Mass Hunger

Sustained combat consumes enormous matter.

Without external feedstock, the craft may cannibalize:

- armor;
- civilian support tissue;
- stored organisms;
- and nonessential organs.

## War Core Conflict

Conflicting Primes can divide strategy and resource allocation.

## Over-Specialization

A Flesh-World Craft optimized for one enemy may become vulnerable to unexpected doctrine.

## Recall Weapons

Non-corrupt recall systems can reverse recent adaptation or destabilize newly grown warforms.

## Command Voice Dependence

Loss or corruption of command signals can produce:

- feral warforms;
- divided continents;
- autonomous weapons;
- and civil war within the craft.

# Civil Status

A major ethical dispute concerns whether a Flesh-World Craft is:

- a warship;
- a world;
- a person;
- a military civilization;
- or all four.

## Planetary Personhood

A craft with stable cognition may claim personhood.

## Crew Identity

Many inhabitants may be:

- citizens;
- Thralls;
- Primes;
- soldiers;
- or organs of the craft.

## Right of Refusal

Some Domain factions argue that a sentient Flesh-World Craft must retain the right to:

- refuse deployment;
- reject ID activation;
- decline self-sacrifice;
- and disengage from unlawful war.

Militant branches reject this entirely.

# Canon Summary

- Flesh-World Craft are planetary-scale warships grown for combat.
- They are distinct from Womb Worlds, Supernals, and Surrogate Worlds.
- Their entire planetary anatomy functions as weapon, fortress, shipyard, and command system.
- They are governed by War Cores, tactical Primes, and usually one War-Prime.
- They can produce fleets, armies, and weapons during campaigns.
- They reclaim destroyed matter as feedstock.
- Heat rejection and mass demand are their greatest logistical limits.
- Distributed command makes them difficult to decapitate.
- Some possess planetary personhood.
- Post-Severance examples may become fragmented or Symmorphica-corrupted.
- Their architecture follows militarized Symmorphic Ossuary Gothic.

> **A Womb World creates life. A Supernal carries life. A Flesh-World Craft decides what must die so the Domain can continue.**
