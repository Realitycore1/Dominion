I. Founding (3310 – 3400 CE)

The Proxima Commonwealth was the first extrasolar polity formally chartered under the [[Colonial Union]].
Its original purpose was bureaucratic rather than colonial: to act as a communications and standards relay between Sol and the emerging Orion [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].

The C.U. seeded three components:

Council — elected representatives of recognized [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]].

Archive — an automated data vault preserving scientific and legal precedents.

Directorate — an engineering body maintaining beamer and cryogenic transport [[Infrastructure]].

By 3400 CE, Proxima hosted the first dual beamer chain linking Sol and Alpha Centauri, allowing efficient transmission of power and laser-encoded cargo data.
Population: ~35 million technicians, administrators, and legal clerks living in domed arcologies around the equatorial line.

II. Arbitration Hub of the Frontier (3400 – 3650 CE)

As the Orion Arm [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]] proliferated, disputes over patents, salvage, and charter [[Rights]] multiplied.
Proxima’s Archive Courts evolved into a planetary arbitration [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Yauturn Sovereignty/System|System]], giving rulings that carried legal weight across the C.U. network.
The colony’s neutrality and proximity to Sol made it the preferred venue for interstellar [[law]].

Simultaneously, the Directorate expanded the beamer network to Alpha Centauri A/B, creating a stable corridor for cryo freighters and communications.
By 3650 CE the Commonwealth had become a de facto capital of [[Human]] extrasolar civilization—a city of diplomats, [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Ameise Hive/Biology & Lifecycle/Engineers|Engineers]], and lawyers orbiting a dim red star.

III. The Age of Mediation (3650 – 3900 CE)

With the C.U.’s authority declining after the Tharsis Closure, Proxima assumed more autonomous functions.
The Council of [[Systems]], composed of delegates from dozens of [[II - Companion Narratives/Syl'Cothan Dominion/3 - Colonial Union/Colonies|Colonies]], met annually to standardize trade codes, safety regulations, and astro-[[law]].
The Archive became self-aware at low cognition levels, acting as both memory and mediator—its phrase “All precedents remembered” became the Commonwealth motto.

[[Infrastructure]] additions:

Cryo-Yards capable of servicing generation-ship survivors.

Customs Courts that inspected vessels for legal certification.

Solar beamed-power [[Relays]] exporting energy to the Alpha Cen corridor.

Population exceeded 200 million; the Commonwealth’s neutrality was enforced by lightly armed Corridor Patrols—gravitic-stealth ships built for observation rather than combat.

IV. Neutrality Tested (3900 – 4000 CE)

During the Equilon–C.U. tensions, the Estela Integralists began sending privateers through the Proxima corridor, claiming “integration missions.”
When Proxima customs detained one such vessel (the Vigilant [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]], 3987 CE), Estela propaganda accused the Commonwealth of bias and threatened to build a parallel corridor.

The Corridor Neutrality [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] was codified in 3991 CE: any armed vessel entering Proxima space must submit to inspection and deactivate drives within [[Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)|Chapter XXX (Munitum Age)]] AU of Alpha Cen beacon.
Despite periodic [[Skirmishes]], Proxima maintained its neutrality through measured diplomacy and overwhelming information control—its recon craft could shadow fleets without detection, recording every violation for later arbitration.
