# Chapter 9 - Siege of New Aurora

## Timeline Function

War Years 5-7. New Aurora enters prolonged siege conditions.

## Event Role

- Tests Equilon logistics, civil morale, and founder-linked legitimacy.
- Forces underground transit, ration courts, shelter governance, and archive triage.
- Makes orbital surveillance and anti-siege weapons central.
- Turns the original first city into a living argument over Harmina's memory.

## Consequence

If New Aurora falls, the old settlement order dies. If it survives unchanged, its enemies believe Harmina will never be free of it.
