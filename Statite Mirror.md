## Summary

Civil: Proo
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Statite Mirror is a solar-sail mirror array, held aloft at arbitrary points in space by balancing stellar radiation pressure against gravity. Unlike orbital reflectors, they can hover indefinitely in “impossible” positions — e.g., directly above a planet’s pole or offset from a star — making them powerful tools for illumination, climate control, and projection.
Structure

Mirror Sails: Ultrathin metamaterial films hundreds to thousands of km [[Wide]], polished to near-perfect reflectivity.

Support Frames: Lattice spars inscribed with glyph-light, rigidifying the sails.

Stability [[Systems]]: [[Computronium]] nodes fine-tune sail angling to counter solar pressure.

Swarm Integration: Often deployed as fleets of mirrors in fractal arrangements.

Functions

Civil:
– Provide constant light to polar [[Regions]] or dark-side [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Colonies]].
– Fine-tune planetary climates.
– Extend habitable [[Regions]] on marginal worlds.

– Concentrate beams into solar lances to burn cities, fleets, or habitats.
– Create blinding flares to disrupt sensors.
– Act as defensive decoys, dazzling incoming energy weapons.

Religious:
– Align into visible glyphs — entire scriptural texts shining down on planets.
– Create “holy eclipses” by veiling suns with living stained glass.

Variants

Single Statite Mirrors: Used for local illumination or targeting.

Constellation Mirrors: Fleets of statites woven into aurora-like mandalas.

Sacral Mirrors: Deliberately shaped into scripture visible in the sky.
