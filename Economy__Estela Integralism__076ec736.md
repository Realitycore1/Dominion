The Engine of the Integral State

[[Estela Integralism]]’s economy is a war-forged machine: centralized, militarized, and wholly subordinated to the party. Productivity is celebrated not for wealth, but as a measure of loyalty.
	•	Centralized Command
	•	All industry falls under the Ministry of Expansion, which sets quotas, allocates resources, and directs [[Production]] toward [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Empyrean Continuum/The Empyrearchy/Military|Military]] and propaganda needs.
	•	Private enterprise technically exists, but only as state concessions. Profits are heavily taxed, and owners must prove ideological purity to keep their licenses.
	•	Militarized Industry
	•	Factories churn out war materiel first: starship hulls, orbital cannons, small [[Arms]], and troop transports.
	•	Civilian goods are designed for dual use—tractors double as artillery haulers, transport ships can be refitted as gunboats.
	•	The colony’s motto: “Every tool a weapon, every [[Worker]] a [[Soldier]].”
	•	Labor Organization
	•	[[Workers]] are organized into National Syndicates (modeled after fascist corporatism), each tied to a specific industry: shipbuilding, mining, agriculture, [[Arms]].
	•	Syndicates parade in uniforms, march in rallies, and report directly to party commissars.
	•	Strikes or protests are unthinkable; dissenters are sent to Ash Camps, orbital labor prisons with lethal conditions.
	•	Exploitation of Territory
	•	The Estela [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/United Authority of Systems/System|System]] is strip-mined aggressively. Planets are drained of resources with little regard for long-term sustainability.
	•	Entire moons are hollowed into factories or turned into fortress-vaults.
	•	Colonization of new worlds is celebrated as ideological victories—settlers march under party banners, planting black sun emblems on alien soil.
	•	Currency & Trade
	•	The colony issues the Integral Credit, pegged to labor-hours and party loyalty.
	•	Higher ranks in the party receive enhanced purchasing power, making currency itself a reward for zeal.
	•	Exports: weapons, warships, mercenary cadres.
	•	Imports: food, luxuries, and cultural artifacts—though these are often seized and reframed as trophies of conquest.
	•	Propaganda Through Economy
	•	Factories display banners with slogans like “[[Production]] is Victory” and “The Hammer is a Weapon.”
	•	[[Workers]] earn Purity Tokens, awarded for meeting or exceeding quotas; tokens grant access to better rations or festival privileges.
	•	Economic failure is framed as treason—entire syndicates have been purged after missing quotas.

The Estela Integralist economy is less a market and more a theater of labor, where factories are stages, [[Workers]] are actors, and every output is proof of loyalty to the Caudillo.
