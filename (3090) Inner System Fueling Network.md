# (3090) Inner System Fueling Network

Permanent fusion-fuel, reaction-mass, maintenance, and rescue stations begin linking the inner worlds and the Belt gateways in 3090.

Stations are placed at:

- inhabited Lagrange and halo-orbit complexes;
- cycler and transfer-route intersections;
- Mars departure and capture nodes;
- Areopagus and other Belt gateway orbits;
- high-traffic rescue and postal junctions.

They supply standardized fusion-fuel cartridges, reaction mass processed largely from water, radiator maintenance, ignition inspection, crew shelter, and emergency reserves.

Fueling stations do not form a straight physical chain between worlds. They occupy useful transfer orbits whose value changes with planetary geometry.

Control of a station determines departure priority, rescue range, and which cargo can move. Fueling stations therefore become strategic objectives during the First Solar War.
