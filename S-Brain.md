cept in science fiction that refers to an [[Artificial]] intelligence that operates on the principles of stellar [[Physics]]. However, the Stellar brain also presents risks, as it can generate massive amounts of heat and radiation, potentially endangering the planets or other celestial bodies in its vicinity. It may also be vulnerable to outside attacks or interference, making it important to keep it under tight control. Overall, the Stellar brain is a fascinating concept that explores the potential for advanced AI [[Systems]] to harness the power of the universe itself, creating a new era of technological advancement and scientific discovery.
