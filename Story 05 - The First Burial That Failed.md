# Story 05 - The First Burial That Failed

## Viewpoint

Tomas Revik, a temporary burial registrar who expected recordkeeping to be clerical work.

## Setting

Years 0-2 AL, New Aurora burial zone.

## Story Shape

The first burial fails because the soil is wrong, the markers corrode, and the grave team cannot agree whether preserving the body matters more than preserving the name.

Tomas spends the story cleaning damaged tags, arguing with grieving families, and inventing a double record: one for the body, one for the memory. It feels cold until the first family returns and finds the name still readable.

## Harmina Element

Even death must be adapted to Harmina's chemistry.

## Character Movement

Tomas begins ashamed that the dead cannot be treated according to old customs. He ends understanding that fidelity is not copying the old rite. It is protecting what the rite was for.

## Future Payoff

Harminan memorial culture begins here: names first, bodies second, testimony always.
