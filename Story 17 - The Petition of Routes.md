# Story 17 - The Petition of Routes

## Viewpoint

Pell Varek as a young dispatcher helping his family assemble route records.

## Setting

Year 101 AL, New Aurora transit office and route shelters.

## Story Shape

The petition is not written like a manifesto. It is a bundle of shelter maps, food-sharing contracts, repair logs, birth records, and testimony from families who live between cities.

Pell expects officials to laugh at the idea that a route can be home. Instead, they ask for more documents.

## Harmina Element

Mobility becomes a civic identity shaped by megatrains, shelters, weather windows, and mutual obligation.

## Character Movement

Pell learns that paperwork can be a form of self-defense.

## Future Payoff

The Commonwealth seed appears here: belonging to a network rather than one settlement.
