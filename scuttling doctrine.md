Scuttling [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]] is the codified [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] strategy of denial through destruction, where fleets, stat[[Megastructures Portal|megastructures]]ures]], or even entire worlds are systematically rendered useless if they cannot be held. It is not treated as failure but as a sacred duty — preventing enemies from ever wielding what once belonged to the Empress.


Denial of Utility

Fleet Protocols: Every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] ship carries self-immolation codes — engines can be overdriven into miniature nova events, or hulls dissolved by onboard nanite disassemblers.

World & [[Infrastructure]] Protocols

Planetary Scuttling: [[Mantle Pistons]] or [[Deep-Well Geo-Taps]] overdriven to crack continents.

Strip-Mining Accelerants: Atmospheres vented, oceans boiled, crusts peeled into slag.

Quarantine Curtains / Veils: If unrecoverable, veils collapse inward, sterilizing a planet entirely.

Doctrinal Ethos

“Amber to Ashes, Ashes to Amber.” [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] theology frames scuttling not as destruction but return — breaking false forms back into raw matter for remaking.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Religious: Known as “The Ash [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Interstice City/Doctrine|Doctrine]]” — framed as the Empress’s [[Fire]] of cleansing, turning loss into liturgy.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Scuttle-cores built as black cruciform vaults in every structure.

Suprematist Monolithism: From afar, scuttled constructs appear as geometric ruins — precise, deliberate wreckage.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Final detonations inscribe glyphs in [[Fire]] and light — scripture as last words.

When a [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] fleet scuttles, it doesn’t look like a rout. It looks like a synchronized hymn of annihilation — every ship burning, collapsing, or fragmenting in the same sacred pattern.

Narrative Function

To rebels: Terror — they cannot capture [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] assets, only inherit poisoned ashes.

To [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]]: Tactical advantage — loss becomes denial, denial becomes purification.

To myth: Known as the “Ash Hymn”, the idea that when the Empress withdraws her hand, all things return to raw [[Fire]].

Megastructure Protocols: Dyson constructs, Orbital Rings, or Rungworlds can be shattered into debris nets, forming instant kill-zones.

To destroy is to purify. The Empress reclaims what is hers by unmaking it.
