A layered field of psionic static surrounding the Empress’s body—prevents psychic intrusion, prophecy, or hostile [[(1980) Retrocognition]].
Mutes all speech in her presence unless sanctioned.
Can be expanded as a "Blank Zone", severing lesser [[Entities]] from their consciousness or shared hive-states.
Scriptures claim this veil is woven from the last breath of a dying god.
