# Public Works and Habitation Authority

## Summary

The Public Works and Habitation Authority governs the ordinary built environment: housing, water, waste, civic utilities, arcology maintenance, local roads, storm shelters, structural inspections, and public repair schedules.

Where the Expansion Authority builds grand projects, Public Works keeps people from freezing, flooding, suffocating, or being priced out of shelter.

## Mandate

The Authority manages:

- public housing standards;
- arcology maintenance;
- water and waste systems;
- hazard shelters;
- local roads and utility tunnels;
- structural safety inspections;
- habitation rights during reconstruction;
- accessibility standards;
- repair labor allocation.

## Powers

It may condemn unsafe structures, mandate repairs, prioritize emergency shelter, regulate habitation density, and coordinate with local governments on civic maintenance.

## Limits

It cannot clear neighborhoods for prestige projects without compensation and court review. It cannot use safety codes as a weapon against poor, frontier, minority, or dissident communities.

## Public Face

Citizens know this agency when heat fails, flood doors jam, lifts break, fungus enters a wall system, or a family receives a new housing allotment.

## Story Use

Use this agency to show class, competence, neglect, and care. A civilization's ideals become believable when the plumbing works and morally visible when it does not.
