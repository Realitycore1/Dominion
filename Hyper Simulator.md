## Summary

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Military/Military]]: U
Core Features


[[Structures Index]]
[[Vault Index]]
A Hyper Simulator is a [[Computronium]]-driven construct capable of running fully realized, high-fidelity simulations of entire star [[Systems]] — planets, moons, atmospheres, biospheres, even civilizational dynamics — all evolved in accelerated [[Time]]. They are both tools of analysis and [[Temples]] of control.
Substrate

[[Computronium]] Nodes: Dense cores running exascale+ processing.

Infant-[[Verse]] Cores: Pocket universes used as simulation substrates, each containing billions of simulated years compressed into seconds.

Onto-Computatium Interfaces: Allow projection of consciousness directly into simulations ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] citizens can “walk” the worlds).

Simulation Capabilities

Astrophysical: Stellar dynamics, orbital mechanics, radiation belts.

Ecological: Climate cycles, evolution of [[Fauna]] and [[Flora]], biosphere collapse or recovery.

Civilizational: Populations, economies, rebellion probabilities, memetic drift.

Metaphysical: Concordia harmonics modeled as laws, testing magical or symbolic permutations.

Temporal Control

[[Time]] scales adjustable: from 1:1 real-[[Time]] to millions of years simulated in a single second.

Reverse modeling allows “what if” scenarios of past alterations.

[[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Role

Civil: Hyper Simulators forecast climate, migration, and population growth across entire sectors.

Economic: Predict long-term commerce and resource flow; trade networks tested centuries ahead.

Religious: Known as “The Amber Mirrors” — sacred engines where the Empress sees futures bloom and collapse.

Design Language ([[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Civilizations/Dominion/Dominion]] Aesthetic)

Neo-Gothic Stratobrutalism: Simulator cores built as massive rib-vaulted towers, each “chamber” a simulated sun or world.

Suprematist Monolithism: From outside, they look like simple obsidian spheres — featureless, absolute.

Parametric Cyber-Constructivism: Inside, simulated [[Systems]] unfold as fractal holographies, spiraling like luminous mandalas.

Hyper-Sanskrit Imperialism: Each simulation run inscribes glyphic hymns, a scripture of futures written in [[Fire]] and geometry.

Narrative Function

To citizens: Awe — “the Empress rehearses our futures before we live them.”

To rebels: Terror — “every rebellion was already simulated, every [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death]] pre-ordained.”

To myth: Called the “Mirror Thrones”, as if the Empress sits upon seats of glass that reflect all possible suns.

To stand before one is to see suns and worlds being born and dying within translucent glass-like orbs, galaxies in miniature.
