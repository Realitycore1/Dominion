I. The Collapse of the Green Horizon (2035–2060)

The period historians later call The Great Blight isn’t one disease but a perfect storm of factors: climate volatility, nutrient depletion, microbial imbalance, and data-driven overconfidence.

Each year looks survivable. Each decade looks fatal.

⸻

II. The Anatomy of Failure

1. Fertilizer Dependency and the Nitrogen Crash
	•	Haber–Bosch [[Synthesis]] runs on [[Natural]] gas; during the [[Energy plateau]], prices skyrocket.
	•	Governments subsidize synthetic fertilizer [[Production]], but distribution AIs ration output based on yield forecasts—models skewed by outdated climate data.
	•	By 2038, entire [[Regions]] miss planting cycles waiting for shipments that never come.
	•	Soil nitrogen levels plummet; harvests decline 15–25% over a decade.

2. Genetic Homogeneity
	•	Global agriculture relies on a handful of genome-edited “universal crops” optimized for AI-managed environments.
	•	When the Ark’s EMP event causes data corruption, entire seed libraries sync to the wrong genome variant.
	•	The resulting monocultures are robust—until an airborne fungus mutates to exploit a shared vulnerability.
	•	One season’s blight kills multiple continental harvests.

3. Pollinator Collapse
	•	Drone pollination programs replace bees by 2040.
	•	When lithium shortages limit drone battery [[Production]], whole ecosystems experience sterile seasons.
	•	Real bees can’t recover; synthetic pollination collapses when energy rationing hits.

4. Soil [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Oneiroverse Multiverse/Chorus of Archetypes/Death|Death]]
	•	Decades of precision farming strip microbial diversity.
	•	AI irrigation cuts water “waste,” removing the anaerobic intervals soils need to replenish.
	•	The earth itself becomes inert—fertile only under constant machine care.

5. Climate Extremes
	•	Monsoon shifts and jet-stream instability produce consecutive “global years without summer.”
	•	Crop belts migrate poleward faster than [[Infrastructure]] can follow.
	•	Satellites watch green turn to brown from orbit; governments call it “ecological adjustment.”

⸻

III. Socioeconomic Consequences
	1.	Food Inflation Spiral
	•	Commodity markets behave like cryptocurrency—wild swings daily.
	•	“Food futures” become the planet’s most-traded asset, enriching speculators while bread lines form.
	2.	Synthetic Food [[New Readers Start Here/I - Pleroma Archive/World/Locations/Eos Multiverse/The Dark Forest Zone/Local Cluster/Totum/Verse Structure/Superclusters/Laniakea/Milky Way Galaxy/Sol System/Earth (Terra)/2079-4000/Colonial Union/Interstellar/Colonies/Kuomin Empire/Economy|Economy]]
	•	Protein vats and algae farms fill the calorie gap but can’t match taste or nutrition.
	•	“Vat food” becomes class marker: elites eat vintage grains, everyone else eats paste.
	3.	Corporate Feudalism
	•	Agro-tech giants merge with state ministries. Ownership of viable farmland equals political sovereignty.
	•	PMCs guard seed vaults and vertical farms like nuclear facilities.
	4.	Cultural Trauma
	•	Agrarian holidays and cuisines vanish.
	•	Rural populations disperse to arcologies, bringing superstition and resentment—perfect fuel for populist insurgencies.
	5.	Biological Illegality
	•	Unauthorized seed saving is outlawed; gene patents enforced by drone sweeps.
	•	Underground “seed priests” become the new resistance—hiding pre-modification stock in monasteries and caves.
