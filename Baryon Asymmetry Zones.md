Think “patchy baryogenesis” rather than a perfect[[Antimatter]]ter era.

How they arise (theory hooks).
	•	Inhomogeneous baryogenesis: CP-violating fields/phase transitions imprint spatial variation in η₍ᴮ₎.
	•	Topological remnants: [[Domain]] walls/defects seed matter vs [[Antimatter]] catchments.
	•	Late seeding: exotic decays or [[Verse]] collisions inject local baryon excess/deficit.

What they look like (phenomenology).
	•	Mild zones (Δη₍ᴮ₎ ≠ 0): altered BBN yields (D/He/Li), slightly different gas cooling histories, subtle CMB/21-cm isocurvature signatures.
	•	Polarity flips (anti-[[Domains]]): large-scale p–p̄ annihilation fronts at boundaries → MeV–GeV gamma haze, hot/ionized rims; cosmic-ray antinuclei tails; radio/[[X-Ray]] excess where filaments touch.

Detection toolkit.
	•	Spectral: diffuse gamma maps, annihilation line searches; 21-cm tomography of hot rims.
	•	Abundance: deuterium/helium gradients; metal patterns in dwarf satellites.
	•	[[Particles]]: antiproton/antinuclei outliers; neutrino/antineutrino asymmetry hints.
	•	Geometry: “warm” void walls where filaments of opposite polarity meet.
