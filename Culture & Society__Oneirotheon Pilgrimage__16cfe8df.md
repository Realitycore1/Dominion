# Cultural Incorporation & Conversion  
**The Oneirotheon Doctrine of Myth Assimilation**

The Oneirotheon do not convert species.  
They **convert myths**.

Individuals—human or alien—are accepted not because of belief, origin, or loyalty, but because their cultures carry **usable fragments of the Divine Spark**. Conversion is not submission. It is **translation**.

This doctrine is the primary reason the Oneirotheon remain nomadic, internally volatile, and epistemically dangerous.

---

## Core Principle

> **No myth is false.  
> Some myths are incomplete.  
> All myths are fragments.**

Alien cultures are not judged by truth-claims. They are evaluated by **mythic utility**.

If a civilization can contribute symbolic structure, archetypal novelty, or alternative metaphysical framing, it is considered **eligible for incorporation**.

---

## I. The Process of Incorporation

Incorporation unfolds in four formal stages. Failure at any stage halts conversion permanently.

---

### I.a Mythic Contact (First Resonance)

Initial contact is conducted by:
- Somnarch Hierophants
- Specialized Salvager-Fleet ethnographers
- Dream-linguists and myth-cartographers

The goal is not diplomacy.  
It is **resonance testing**.

Evaluators assess:
- Presence of recurring archetypes
- Cultural handling of transcendence, loss, and authority
- Whether the species’ myths *compress* or *explode* under paradox

Species whose myths collapse into dogma or incoherence under stress are rejected.

---

### I.b Translation & Mapping

Eligible myths are translated into **Oneirotheon symbolic grammar**.

This involves:
- Converting alien deities into archetypal functions
- Reframing cosmologies as narrative topologies
- Stripping moral absolutes while preserving symbolic force

No alien myth is imported whole.  
Every myth is **deconstructed and reassembled**.

Alien contributors are required to *witness* this process.

This is often the breaking point.

---

### I.c Ritual Recontextualization

Translated myths are enacted within Consensus Slumber.

- Alien participants experience their own myths retold through foreign archetypes
- Contradictions are deliberately amplified
- Emotional dissonance is treated as data, not failure

If a myth survives recontextualization—if it still produces meaning—it is deemed **Spark-compatible**.

---

### I.d Archetypal Integration

Successful myths are assigned one of three statuses:

1. **Reinforcing** — strengthens an existing archetype  
2. **Variant** — alters expression of an existing archetype  
3. **Novel** — introduces a new archetypal role (rare and dangerous)

Only after this step are individuals offered **conversion into the Pilgrimage**.

---

## II. Conversion Without Erasure

Joining the Oneirotheon does not require abandoning prior identity.

Converted individuals:
- Retain species-specific biology and culture
- Continue ancestral rituals in modified form
- Are never required to deny former beliefs

What changes is **context**.

Former gods become:
- Archetypal expressions
- Failed Spark-attempts
- Localized mythic solutions to universal Hunger

This reframing is traumatic—but honest.

---

## III. Mixed-Species Fleets

Living Fleets are intentionally **poly-mythic and poly-species**.

Design principles include:
- Rotational cohabitation to prevent myth ossification
- Separate Consensus Slumber chambers for incompatible myth-structures
- Periodic cross-slumber experiments under Somnarch supervision

Species are not segregated by biology, but by **symbolic load**.

Two species may live side-by-side but never dream together.

---

## IV. Alien Roles in Governance

Alien converts are not marginalized.

They may serve as:
- Somnarch Hierophants
- Council of the Spark members
- Salvager Fleet myth-interpreters
- Eternal Cast candidates (exceptionally rare)

However:

> **No Eternal Cast role may originate entirely from a single alien myth.**

This prevents foreign cosmologies from dominating the Pilgrimage.

All archetypes must be **cross-species intelligible**.

---

## V. Cultural Tension & Internal Risk

Incorporation is the Oneirotheon’s greatest strength—and greatest liability.

### Risks Include
- Myth overload leading to Consensus Slumber fragmentation
- Alien doctrines reasserting absolutism
- Archetypal inflation or collapse
- Covert memetic warfare disguised as myth contribution

Entire fleets have fractured after incorporating a single incompatible cosmology.

These losses are recorded, not hidden.

---

## VI. Dominion Perspective

The Dominion views Oneirotheon incorporation practices as:

- **Culturally invasive**
- **Memetically destabilizing**
- **Politically subversive**

Yet also:
- Exceptionally effective at intelligence gathering
- Unmatched in cross-species translation
- A reliable sink for otherwise volatile cultures

This ambiguity is why the Oneirotheon are tolerated—but surveilled.

---

## VII. Why Aliens Join Anyway

Alien converts are rarely seeking salvation.

They are seeking:
- Preservation of meaning in collapsing civilizations
- A framework where their myths are not dismissed as primitive
- A chance to matter beyond extinction or assimilation

The Oneirotheon offer no comfort.

They offer **continuance through relevance**.

---

## Final Doctrine

> The Pilgrimage is not human.  
> Humanity is merely where it began.

The Divine Spark, if it exists, is not bound to species, gods, or worlds.

It is assembled from **everything that ever tried to explain why it hurts to exist**.

That is why the Oneirotheon accept aliens.

And that is why they are never safe.
